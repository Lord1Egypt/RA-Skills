## Use Case:

External travelers and travel planners use this skill to prepare language-barrier strategies, phrase frameworks, communication aids, cultural considerations, and emergency communication plans before a trip.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Travel, embassy, medical, payment, and safety details can become outdated because the skill does not fetch real-time data.

Mitigation: Verify destination-specific details with official or current sources before acting on recommendations.

Risk: Cultural and language guidance is general and may not fit every local situation.

Mitigation: Confirm key phrases, etiquette, and emergency procedures with local contacts, official tourism resources, or qualified professionals when stakes are high.
