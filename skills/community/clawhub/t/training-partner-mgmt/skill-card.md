## Description:

Training Partner Mgmt helps agents structure external training partner records, cooperation agreements, settlement records, partner evaluations, and contact maintenance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Training operations teams and business administrators use this skill to maintain a structured lifecycle view of external institutions, venues, associations, agencies, contracts, settlements, evaluations, and partner contacts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill describes records containing sensitive business, financial, tax, contract, and contact data.

Mitigation: Store only necessary fields, restrict access, redact exported views where practical, and define retention and deletion rules before deployment.
