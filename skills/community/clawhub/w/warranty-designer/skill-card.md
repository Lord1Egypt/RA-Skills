## Description:

Design product warranty and guarantee programs that increase buyer confidence and conversion rates while managing liability exposure, including policy language, claim workflows, fulfillment costs, and disclosure requirements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand, ecommerce, operations, finance, support, and legal teams use this skill to design or update warranty and guarantee programs, estimate claim reserves, define claim workflows, and draft customer-facing warranty copy for legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Warranty terms may conflict with jurisdiction-specific consumer rights or translation requirements.

Mitigation: Have counsel validate market-specific statutory rights, disclosures, and translations before publishing customer-facing terms.

Risk: Claim-rate and reserve estimates may be inaccurate for a specific product or market.

Mitigation: Use the skill's financial outputs as planning drafts and reconcile them against actual RMA, failure-rate, remedy-cost, and support data.
