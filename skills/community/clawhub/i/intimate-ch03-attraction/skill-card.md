## Description:

Chinese-language study assistant for Chapter 3, "Attraction," of Rowland S. Miller's Intimate Relationships, using progressive disclosure and chapter-specific reference notes to answer study, review, and application questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners, instructors, and study assistants use this skill to explain, review, and apply Chapter 3 attraction concepts from Intimate Relationships. It keeps answers scoped to the chapter and introduces concepts gradually in Chinese with English terms on first use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill discusses attraction and relationships, so users may treat educational chapter summaries as personal relationship advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses framed as Chapter 3 study support, avoid value judgments or prescriptions, and point users back to the course or source text for decisions beyond the chapter.

Risk: The skill includes many study names, dates, and textbook figures that could be misstated if the agent extrapolates beyond the supplied references.

Mitigation: Use only the provided reference files for research details and state uncertainty instead of inventing citations, years, study names, or numerical results.
