## Description:

Write detailed, academically rigorous Chinese literature review chapters or literature review sections for theses, dissertations, and undergraduate papers from a user's topic, research problem, variables/concepts, theory, method, population, references, and extra information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junwugit](https://clawhub.ai/user/junwugit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and academic writing assistants use this skill to draft, expand, polish, organize, synthesize, or structure Chinese thesis literature review chapters and related review sections. It is designed for literature review work only and uses placeholders when exact references are not supplied.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated literature review text may include citation placeholders or unverified claims if the user does not provide real sources.

Mitigation: Users should replace placeholders with verified sources, check all citations, and confirm factual claims against primary literature before academic submission.

Risk: The output may not match a specific school's academic integrity, citation, or formatting rules.

Mitigation: Users should review the draft against institutional requirements, required citation style, and academic integrity policies before use.
