## Description:

Trades structural mispricings in crypto price-threshold markets by reconstructing the implied probability distribution curve across multiple strike levels and detecting mathematical violations such as monotonicity breaks and range-sum inconsistencies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[diagnostikon](https://clawhub.ai/user/diagnostikon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and trading-agent operators use this skill to scan Polymarket crypto price-threshold markets for implied probability curve inconsistencies and, when configured, place simulated or live trades through Simmer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can place real trades when run with the live trading flag and a scoped Simmer or Polymarket trading key.

Mitigation: Run in paper mode first, use a scoped trading key, and enable live trading only after reviewing strategy thresholds and position limits.

Risk: The security summary notes missing or inconsistent safety limits, including liquidity filtering, position-limit semantics, and threshold defaults.

Mitigation: Correct or explicitly accept those limits before live use, and monitor configured tunables such as maximum position size, maximum open positions, spread, volume, and minimum violation.

Risk: The release depends on simmer-sdk for trading execution.

Mitigation: Use a pinned and reviewed simmer-sdk version before running the skill against live markets.
