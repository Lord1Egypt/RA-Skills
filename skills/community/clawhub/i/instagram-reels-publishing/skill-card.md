## Description:

Helps an agent prepare a finished Instagram Reel for spec-clean publishing by producing publish settings, safe-zone checks, a cover brief, caption and audio guidance, and WoopSocial scheduling guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and publishing agents use this skill when a Reel has already been created and needs final publishing guidance. It produces checks and instructions for Instagram display specs, crop-safe cover planning, WoopSocial scheduling, and native in-app actions such as trending audio and tags.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide publication of public Instagram content through WoopSocial, so incorrect account, asset, caption, cover, or schedule choices could publish the wrong material.

Mitigation: Confirm the Instagram account, finished video, caption, cover, and schedule before posting.

Risk: Instagram Reels specifications, safe zones, cover behavior, and native publishing controls can change over time.

Mitigation: Re-verify Instagram publishing requirements before release and treat trending audio, Share-to-Feed, tags, media-quality settings, and Reel Insights as native in-app actions.

Risk: Publishing guidance could imply unsupported outcomes, such as fabricated reach or automated access to native-only Instagram features.

Mitigation: Keep metrics factual, avoid reach guarantees, and clearly separate WoopSocial-publishable fields from native-only actions.
