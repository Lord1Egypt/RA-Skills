## Description:

Clone someone's writing tone from sample text and produce a ready-to-use voice skill.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[couriard](https://clawhub.ai/user/couriard)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and content creators use this skill to analyze writing samples across linguistic dimensions and create a reusable voice profile plus a deployable voice skill.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Writing samples may contain private, identifying, financial, medical, or third-party information.

Mitigation: Use only writing the user has the right to analyze and imitate; redact sensitive details before providing samples.

Risk: Generated voice-skill files may overwrite an existing skill or reference profile.

Mitigation: Check whether the target skills/[name]-voice/SKILL.md or references/voice-profile.md already exists before writing files.
