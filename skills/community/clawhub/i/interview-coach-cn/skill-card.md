Mitigation: Treat salary figures as general planning references and ask the agent to adapt recommendations to the user's region, role, experience level, and timing.

Risk: Legal or employment-contract topics such as non-competes, arbitration, probation terms, or equity terms may require jurisdiction-specific advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for general preparation only and consult a qualified professional before making legal or binding employment decisions.
