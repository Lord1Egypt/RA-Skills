Students, tutors, and education-focused agents can use this skill to explain Chinese K-9 math word problems with structured reasoning, direct answers, verification, and optional Socratic guidance.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is oriented toward Chinese K-9 math wording and examples, which may not match other curricula, languages, or grade levels.

Mitigation: Ask for the target language, curriculum, or grade level when it matters, and review final explanations for age and context fit.

Risk: Math tutoring responses can still contain reasoning or calculation mistakes.

Mitigation: Use the built-in verification step and independently check equations and final units before relying on an answer.
