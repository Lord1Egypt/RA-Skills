## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security summary reports that the skill gives staff sensitive minor-record and dormitory-search guidance without enough privacy, legal, or access-control safeguards.

Mitigation: Review carefully before using in a real school and rewrite operational guidance to require approved school policy, local legal compliance, documented authorization, least-intrusive searches, role-based access, audit logs, retention and deletion rules, and safeguards for minors' sensitive information.

Risk: Pastoral-care and risk-level guidance may be mistaken for professional clinical, legal, or final administrative advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as general guidance only and route self-harm, suicide, violence, sexual assault, severe psychological crisis, student-record access, and inspection decisions through authorized school leaders and qualified professionals.
