## Description:

Draft a public-records request (FOIA / FOI / state open-records) that's specific enough to get records and hard to deny.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, journalists, researchers, and advocates use this skill to draft focused public-records requests with specific records, date ranges, custodians, delivery preferences, fee language, and jurisdiction-specific statutory framing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The drafted request may cite an incorrect statute, deadline, agency address, portal, fee-waiver basis, or expedited-processing basis for a specific jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Verify the cited law, agency contact path, response deadline, fee language, and expedited-processing rationale before sending the request.
