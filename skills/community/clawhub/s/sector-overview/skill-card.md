## Description:

Produces Chinese-language industry sector overviews and rotation analysis when users ask for sector analysis, sector rotation, or sector opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dzy11650](https://clawhub.ai/user/dzy11650)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and market analysts use this skill to generate Chinese-language sector rotation reports, compare industry performance and valuations, and summarize sector allocation signals without making individual stock recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sector analysis and rotation signals may be incomplete, stale, or misleading if source data is unavailable or interpreted incorrectly.

Mitigation: Review the generated report, confirm cited neodata-financial-search data, and avoid treating the report as a recommendation to buy or sell individual stocks.

Risk: Reports and IMA notes may reveal sensitive sector interests, prompts, or analysis topics in the workspace.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to confirm before saving reports or creating reminders when prompts or sector interests are sensitive.
