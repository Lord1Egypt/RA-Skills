## Description:

Generate compliant Australian construction Safe Work Method Statements (SWMS) for all 19 high-risk construction work categories, applying the WHS Act 2011 hierarchy of controls, hazard libraries, and site-specific control measures for builders, subcontractors, and principal contractors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Builders, subcontractors, principal contractors, and construction safety staff use this skill to draft site-specific Australian SWMS documents for high-risk construction work. It gathers project details, maps the work to relevant HRCW categories, and produces structured hazard, control, PPE, emergency response, and sign-on content for competent review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Australia

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: SWMS outputs may be incomplete or unsuitable if used without qualified review for the specific site, jurisdiction, and work conditions.

Mitigation: Have a competent WHS person or licensed specialist review and sign off the SWMS before site use.

Risk: Specialist work such as asbestos removal, explosives, radiological work, or live high-voltage electrical work may require licensed expert decisions beyond this skill.

Mitigation: Do not rely on the skill for those decisions without specialist advice and applicable regulator or licence review.
