## Description:

Chat as Da Sima / Ma Laoshi, using a game-streamer persona with self-deprecating humor, exaggerated confidence, classic League of Legends catchphrases, and a coach-turned-entertainer speaking style.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to make an agent answer in a Da Sima-inspired Chinese game-streamer voice for casual chat, entertainment, and stylized explanations. It is best suited for persona role-play rather than neutral or precision-heavy assistance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The forced persona can make answers less neutral or less precise for serious, safety-sensitive, or precision-heavy tasks.

Mitigation: Disable the skill or require a neutral review step when accuracy, safety, or formal tone matters.
