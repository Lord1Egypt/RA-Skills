## Description:

深入分析法条中的不确定法律概念（如"合理期限"、"重大误解"、"明显不当"）的内涵、外延、裁判标准和边界案例。当用户想穿透法条中模糊词汇的具体含义时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to examine ambiguous legal concepts in statutes by asking for legislative intent, mainstream academic interpretation, judicial interpretation, adjudication criteria, boundary examples, and common misunderstandings. It supports legal analysis workflows but does not replace legal representation or source verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce incorrect or unverified legal concept analysis, including illustrative case examples.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as legal analysis support only and verify AI-analyzed cases and legal claims against official court records or other authoritative legal sources.

Risk: Users may mistake structured legal analysis for legal representation or guaranteed case law accuracy.

Mitigation: Present the output as guidance for review and require qualified human review before relying on it in legal practice.
