## Description:

Evaluates whether an early-stage AI startup is worth joining as a technical contributor using a structured checklist, red-flag review, and decision scorecard.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orangon](https://clawhub.ai/user/orangon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and technical contributors use this skill to evaluate AI startup offers, team health, resource gaps, and whether to join, conditionally join, or avoid a startup.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce opinionated career advice from limited or one-sided startup context.

Mitigation: Treat the output as decision support, verify claims with independent due diligence, and avoid relying on the verdict as the sole basis for a career decision.

Risk: The checklist includes broad judgments about team composition, schools, funding, and equipment that may not apply equally across regions or startup stages.

Mitigation: Adjust the scorecard to the user's local market, role, company stage, and available evidence before accepting red-flag conclusions.
