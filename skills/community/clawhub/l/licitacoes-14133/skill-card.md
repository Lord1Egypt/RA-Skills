## Description:

Portuguese-language guidance for Brazilian public procurement and administrative contracts under Lei 14.133/2021, including bidding modalities, direct contracting, contract management, challenges, appeals, sanctions, PNCP publication, and practical drafting support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Public procurement, legal, compliance, and municipal administration users can use this skill to draft and review procurement guidance, edital analysis, direct-contracting reasoning, contract-management checklists, impugnação and recurso responses, and sanction procedure support under Lei 14.133/2021. Outputs should be treated as drafting support and checked against current law, decrees, TCU/TCE precedent, and local procedures before official use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Brazil

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal or procurement guidance may become outdated or may not reflect current thresholds, decrees, TCU/TCE precedent, or local procedures.

Mitigation: Verify current legal sources, applicable decrees, tribunal precedent, and local rules before using outputs for official procurement decisions.

Risk: Drafted analyses, pareceres, impugnações, recursos, or checklists may omit facts from the specific procurement process.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer compare the output against the complete administrative record and document any required adjustments before adoption.
