## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Healthcare compliance details may be outdated or specific to a clinic's emirate, license, patient consent workflow, data residency posture, or vendor contract.

Mitigation: Verify current UAE regulator requirements, Malaffi or Nabidh connectivity, consent wording, recording disclosure, retention controls, and vendor processing terms before implementation.

Risk: Users could mistake operational guidance for legal, regulatory, or clinical approval.

Mitigation: Use the skill for planning support only and obtain qualified clinical, legal, and regulatory review before adopting EMR, telemedicine, AI, or data-sharing workflows.
