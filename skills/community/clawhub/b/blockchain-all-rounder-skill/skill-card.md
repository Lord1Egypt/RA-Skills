## Description:

Blockchain All-Rounder is a Chinese-capable multi-chain blockchain assistant for BTC, ETH, BSC, Solana, and major L2s, covering market analysis, on-chain address tracing, DeFi yield and risk strategy, Solidity contract development and audit support, and security risk controls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and security reviewers use the skill for blockchain market interpretation, wallet and contract address review, DeFi risk comparison, Solidity code generation, and smart-contract audit guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can provide financial-adjacent blockchain and DeFi guidance.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational and independently verify live prices, on-chain facts, yields, and protocol conditions before acting.

Risk: The skill may produce Solidity code or smart-contract audit guidance that affects asset safety if used directly.

Mitigation: Test generated code, review dependencies and permissions, and obtain independent audit review before any mainnet deployment.

Risk: Users may be tempted to share wallet secrets while discussing address analysis or signing risk.

Mitigation: Never provide private keys, seed phrases, wallet passwords, keystores, or signing authority to the agent.
