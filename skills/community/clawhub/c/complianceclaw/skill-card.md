## Description:

Regulations change 4,000+ times per year. Your clients can't track them all. complianceclaw monitors federal and state regulatory changes, maps them to your clients' obligations, generates compliance checklists, and produces audit-ready reports before the enforcement action arrives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External legal, regulatory, and compliance teams use this skill to monitor regulatory changes, map them to client obligations, generate compliance checklists, analyze gaps, track deadlines, and produce audit-ready reports.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill installs an unpinned third-party Homebrew executable.

Mitigation: Review the Homebrew tap, publisher, release version, and source before installing, and pin or otherwise control the executable version before production use.

Risk: The CLI may process sensitive client compliance records and obligation data.

Mitigation: Use test or non-production compliance records first, and avoid granting access to sensitive client data until data handling and update provenance are clear.

Risk: Calendar synchronization could expose regulated deadlines or client matter details.

Mitigation: Do not grant calendar access until the CLI's permission scope, data retention, and sharing behavior have been reviewed.
