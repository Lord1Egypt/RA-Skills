## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automatic publishing behavior could post to a live Xiaohongshu account without the user's intent being clear.

Mitigation: Confirm the logged-in account, require human review of the final post, and require an explicit publish confirmation before any browser automation publishes content.
