## Description:

Optimize weekly promotion calendars across content, live sessions, and paid pushes while accounting for inventory, capacity, and budget constraints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Ecommerce operators and marketing planners use this skill to build balanced weekly promotion plans, avoid campaign collisions, and keep execution within inventory, creator, and budget limits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The calendar is advisory and could be mistaken for an approved campaign execution plan.

Mitigation: Require human review before posting, purchasing media, or launching promotions.

Risk: Aggressive scheduling can exceed team capacity or overexpose inventory-critical SKUs.

Mitigation: Check capacity, inventory, budget windows, and creator availability before using the plan, and keep fallback options for content delays.
