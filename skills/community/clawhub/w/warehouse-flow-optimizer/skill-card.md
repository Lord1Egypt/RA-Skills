## Description:

Turn warehouse process notes, bottleneck observations, labor constraints, slotting issues, and service targets into a practical flow optimization brief with bottleneck hypotheses, quick wins, pilot ideas, and operating guardrails.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operations teams, 3PL managers, and consultants use this skill to turn warehouse pain points, KPI notes, and constraints into a first-pass optimization brief. It is best suited for practical triage before deeper industrial-engineering analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The brief may overstate a bottleneck or root cause when user notes are sparse, inconsistent, or missing zone-level KPI definitions.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as operational guidance only and confirm them with floor observation, measured KPIs, and human review before changing staffing, layout, capex, or automation.

Risk: The skill does not validate recommendations against live WMS, OMS, labor, or automation systems.

Mitigation: Keep the advisory framing explicit and require human approval before applying any irreversible warehouse process or systems change.
