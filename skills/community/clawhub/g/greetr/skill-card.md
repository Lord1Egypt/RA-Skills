## Use Case:

External users and agents use greetr to respond to casual greetings with concise, time-appropriate welcome messages and an offer to help.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on many casual greeting phrases because its trigger wording is broad.

Mitigation: Review and tighten the greeting trigger list before deployment if predictable activation boundaries are required.
