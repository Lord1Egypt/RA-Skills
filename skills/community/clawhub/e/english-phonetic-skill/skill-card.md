## Description:

Annotates English text with IPA pronunciation, stress marks, intonation cues, and Chinese pinyin approximations for pronunciation learning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liulnx3-brave](https://clawhub.ai/user/liulnx3-brave)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External English learners, teachers, and language-practice users can use this skill to turn English words, sentences, and passages into pronunciation annotations with IPA, stress, intonation, and pinyin-style aids. It is especially aimed at Chinese-speaking learners who want a familiar pronunciation bridge while studying English sounds.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installation may carry a minor supply-chain risk if users install an unverified package version.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned or verified ClawHub package version before installation.

Risk: Pinyin approximations can help beginners but may be misleading if treated as exact English pronunciation.

Mitigation: Use the pinyin aids alongside IPA notation and audio practice, and treat them as memory aids rather than authoritative phonetic transcriptions.
