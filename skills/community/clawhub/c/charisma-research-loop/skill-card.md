## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks the agent to maintain a persistent charisma-insight ledger in workspace memory.

Mitigation: Review the ledger location before installation and prefer a skill-specific storage path with appropriate access controls.

Risk: The skill asks the agent to send email briefs and create or update calendar tasks or events.

Mitigation: Require explicit recipients, calendar targets, event IDs, and user confirmation before any email send or calendar update.

Risk: Recurring social influence guidance could become repetitive, misleading, or inappropriate for the user's context.

Mitigation: Review generated insights before delivery and preserve the required failure mode or misuse risk field for each insight.
