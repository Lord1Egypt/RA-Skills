## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can provide general fitness programming but cannot assess exercise form, spot the user, diagnose injuries, or guarantee results.

Mitigation: Use conservative exercise choices, stop painful movements, use safety equipment when training alone, and seek a qualified professional for persistent pain or medical concerns.
