## Description:

Plans a week or month of social media content in one session, producing a balanced, on-brand calendar with post briefs that are ready for review and scheduling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and social media operators use this skill to turn brand, voice, and content-pillar inputs into a realistic batch content plan for a defined period. It creates an editable calendar and per-post briefs, then asks for confirmation before any scheduling handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad planning phrases may activate the skill and lead it to read brand, voice, or content-planning files.

Mitigation: Review which planning files are available to the agent and confirm the intended period, platforms, goals, and capacity before relying on the generated plan.

Risk: Generated calendars or post briefs could contain unsuitable timing, cadence, promotional balance, or brand guidance.

Mitigation: Review and edit the calendar before approval; the skill is designed to present the plan for review before any scheduling handoff.

Risk: Timing suggestions are general guidance rather than personalized analytics.

Mitigation: Treat suggested posting times as defaults unless separate analytics or platform data is available.
