## Description:

A Chinese career-coaching agent that helps users with career planning, resume improvement, mock interviews, workplace communication, salary negotiation, promotion planning, and job-change decisions using structured methods such as GROW, Ikigai, SWOT, and STAR.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lzh1998-ui](https://clawhub.ai/user/lzh1998-ui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill as a Chinese-language career coach for structured career planning, resume rewrites, interview practice, workplace conflict guidance, promotion planning, and salary negotiation scripts. It is intended to produce options, tradeoffs, templates, and next-step actions rather than make major life decisions for the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may retain personal career context without clear consent or deletion controls.

Mitigation: Users should avoid sharing unusually sensitive details, anonymize employers and coworkers where possible, and provide salary or job-search timing details only when needed.
