External travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to build practical Paraguay itineraries, choose bases, handle border and payment logistics, and adapt plans for heat, seasonality, family needs, food, nature, and safety.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional local memory may contain travel preferences, budget, trip history, or other personal context.

Mitigation: Keep sensitive personal or financial details out of ~/paraguay/memory.md and review the file before sharing logs, backups, or device access.

Risk: Travel prices, opening hours, border delays, booking terms, and local conditions can change after the skill content was written.

Mitigation: Verify current logistics and terms with official or booking sources before committing money or fixed travel plans.

Risk: Heat, rural routing, border crossings, and emergency access can materially affect safety and trip feasibility.

Mitigation: Build plans with hydration, daylight travel, transfer buffers, document separation, and escalation to major urban care when needed.
