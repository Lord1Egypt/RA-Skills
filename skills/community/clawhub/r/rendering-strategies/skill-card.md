## Description:

Guides rendering strategy selection and optimization for search engine and AI crawler visibility.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers, SEO practitioners, and site owners use this skill to choose SSG, SSR, ISR, CSR, or dynamic rendering strategies and check whether critical content is available in initial HTML for crawler visibility.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Dynamic rendering guidance can create SEO or policy risk if prerendered crawler content differs from the user-visible page.

Mitigation: Validate any prerendered bot content against the framework implementation and search-engine requirements before deployment.
