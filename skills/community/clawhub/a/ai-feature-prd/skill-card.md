## Description:

Write a PRD for an AI-powered feature, covering the things normal PRDs miss.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, developers, and AI feature teams use this skill to draft PRDs for AI-powered capabilities such as assistants, generators, summarizers, or classifiers. It emphasizes uncertainty UX, model approach, evaluation criteria, guardrails, fallback behavior, data feedback, rollout, and cost or latency budgets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The PRD may contain incorrect or incomplete product assumptions if the user provides weak or outdated feature context.

Mitigation: Review the generated PRD with product, engineering, safety, and privacy stakeholders before using it for implementation or launch decisions.

Risk: High-stakes AI features may require stronger evaluation, guardrail, privacy, or compliance review than a generic PRD can guarantee.

Mitigation: Calibrate quality thresholds, fallback behavior, rollout gates, and data handling requirements to the actual risk level of the feature.
