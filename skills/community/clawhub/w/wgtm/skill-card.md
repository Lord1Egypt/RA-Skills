## Description:

Generates go-to-market sales, PR, competitor-positioning, customer Q&A, and messaging materials from new feature descriptions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, sales, product, and customer-facing teams use this skill to turn new feature descriptions into GTM materials for enterprise AI platform audiences, including sales FAQs, PR drafts, competitor talking points, customer Q&A, and messaging templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated GTM, PR, sales, or competitor-comparison content may be inaccurate, overstated, or misaligned with desired external tone.

Mitigation: Review generated materials for factual accuracy, competitive claims, positioning, and tone before external use.

Risk: The skill may be invoked for a broad range of new-feature marketing and sales-writing requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the request is intended for customer-facing GTM or sales enablement before relying on the output.
