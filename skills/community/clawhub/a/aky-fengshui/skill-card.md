## Description:

Comprehensive Feng Shui (风水) analysis skill integrating three major schools: Eight Mansions (八宅法), Flying Stars (玄空飞星), and Forms School (形峦派). Supports life trigram calculation, house-person compatibility, directional analysis, flying star chart interpretation, landscape assessment, interior layout recommendations, and Feng Shui cures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A full analysis may involve private home details, floor plans, birth years, gender, and household relationships.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed for the specific question and avoid unnecessary personal or location information.

Risk: Traditional Feng Shui interpretations may be mistaken for deterministic predictions.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as cultural and reference guidance, and review practical decisions independently.
