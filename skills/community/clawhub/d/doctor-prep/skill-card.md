## Description:

Help users prepare for doctor appointments by organizing symptoms, questions, medication lists, and medical history.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiwenbing](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiwenbing)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to prepare for upcoming medical visits by organizing symptoms, questions, medications, allergies, and relevant medical history into a pre-visit checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may involve personal health details.

Mitigation: Share only information useful for the appointment and avoid unnecessary sensitive details.

Risk: Appointment-preparation output could be mistaken for medical advice.

Mitigation: Use the output as an organization aid and confirm medical decisions with a qualified clinician.
