## Description:

Find and fix keyword cannibalization: queries where two or more of the site's own pages get impressions in Google, which of those splits actually cost traffic, and the fix for each (merge and 301, retarget, canonical, internal links, noindex, or de-optimize). Use when the user mentions cannibalization, two pages competing for the same keyword, pages swapping positions in Search Console, asks which page should rank for a query, or wants to consolidate overlapping content. For mapping a keyword list to pages before they exist, use keyword-clustering instead.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
