## Description:

A focused Business English coach for Vietnamese tech professionals that improves workplace English for Slack messages, emails, standups, code reviews, 1-on-1s, meetings, and client communication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, developers, and tech professionals use this skill to rewrite, practice, and improve business English for daily workplace communication. It is especially focused on Vietnamese-English phrasing, tone, and reusable language patterns for professional contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may trigger broadly for workplace English editing or practice requests.

Mitigation: Users should state the desired dialect, tone, audience, and context when they need a specific communication style.

Risk: Coaching output can produce wording that is fluent but not aligned with a user's intended professional voice.

Mitigation: Review important external, managerial, or client-facing messages before sending and adjust wording to match the relationship and context.
