## Description:

Generates Chinese-language emerging industry opportunity reports that use current public-source research to assess policy support, investment activity, technical maturity, growth drivers, competitive geography, regional fit, and future trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow depends on current public web sources, so industry, policy, financing, and market claims may become stale or vary in reliability.

Mitigation: Require current authoritative sources for material claims, cite the source and date, and explicitly mark unavailable or weak evidence rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

Risk: The skill is Chinese-language and oriented toward China policy and regional industry planning, which may not fit multilingual or non-China decisions without adaptation.

Mitigation: Review the report framing for the target jurisdiction and audience before using it for cross-border planning, investment, or policy decisions.
