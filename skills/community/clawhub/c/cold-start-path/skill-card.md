## Description:

Choose the best first growth path for a new product, new shop, or weak early-stage offer based on product shape, margin room, content readiness, and channel constraints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External builders, operators, and growth teams use this skill to choose the first practical channel for a cold-start product or weak early-stage offer. It sequences search, merchant content, creator content, paid ads, and live selling based on economics and readiness.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Store exports, ad spend data, product sheets, search trend exports, sheets, or CSVs may contain sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Provide only data that is appropriate to use for business analysis and remove unnecessary confidential details before using the skill.

Risk: Recommendations may be weak when product economics, asset readiness, creator access, ad budget, or active channels are missing.

Mitigation: Clarify the key product and channel facts first, and label any remaining assumptions in the recommendation.
