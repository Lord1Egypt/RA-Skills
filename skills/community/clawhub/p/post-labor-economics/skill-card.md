## Use Case:

External users, policy analysts, planners, and strategy teams use this skill to analyze automation transitions, design post-labor policy portfolios, and stress-test distribution, ownership, services, work coordination, and legitimacy tradeoffs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional local memory may store policy preferences, assumptions, drafts, indicators, and scenarios that shape future sessions.

Mitigation: Ask for explicit approval before writing memory files, allow users to decline persistence, and direct users to delete ~/post-labor-economics/ if they no longer want saved planning context.

Risk: Policy guidance can become misleading if assumptions, values, or political constraints are presented as settled empirical facts.

Mitigation: Maintain an evidence ledger that labels claims as empirical findings, modeling assumptions, normative preferences, or political constraints, and require appropriate institutional fiscal or legal review before adoption.
