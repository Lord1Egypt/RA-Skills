## Description:

Audit the mobile shopping experience of an ecommerce store for friction points including page load speed, thumb-reach interactions, form length, checkout flow, and pay-method breadth.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce, product, design, and engineering teams use this skill to audit mobile shopping funnels, diagnose conversion friction, and produce prioritized remediation findings before redesigns or during periodic UX reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audit work can involve private business metrics, checkout details, and session-recording identifiers.

Mitigation: Use the skill only on stores and analytics tools the user is authorized to review, and keep sensitive audit evidence out of broadly shared outputs unless it is necessary.
