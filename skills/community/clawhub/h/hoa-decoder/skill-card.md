## Description:

Decode HOA covenants (CC&Rs) and the fee structure before purchase, including lifestyle-impact restrictions, special-assessment exposure, enforcement and fine mechanics, and records to request.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Home buyers, real-estate advisors, and agents use this skill to turn HOA governing documents into a plain-language due-diligence readout. It helps compare HOA rules against a buyer's plans, financial exposure, enforcement path, and records still needed before purchase.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: HOA and home-buying documents may contain personal or financial details.

Mitigation: Share only the document content needed for review and remove irrelevant personal or financial details before use.

Risk: Plain-language HOA analysis can be incomplete or jurisdiction-dependent.

Mitigation: Verify important legal, financial, lien, foreclosure, or enforceability questions with qualified local professionals before relying on the result.

Risk: Missing reserve studies, budgets, meeting minutes, insurance records, or assessment history can hide financial exposure.

Mitigation: Treat unavailable records as due-diligence gaps and request the specific records identified by the skill before purchase.
