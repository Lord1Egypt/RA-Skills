## Description:

Create, inspect, and edit Microsoft Word documents and DOCX files with reliable styles, numbering, tracked changes, tables, sections, and compatibility checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, editors, and document automation agents use this skill when creating, inspecting, or editing Word and DOCX files where styles, numbering, tracked changes, comments, fields, tables, templates, or layout stability matter.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional related-skill installs or ClawHub commands may be unnecessary for the current document task.

Mitigation: Confirm that the related skill or command directly supports the task before installing or running it.

Risk: Word and DOCX edits can leave stale fields, unresolved review metadata, broken anchors, or layout drift that is not visible in a text-only check.

Mitigation: Inspect relevant DOCX structure when needed and verify round-trip compatibility before delivering layout-sensitive documents.
