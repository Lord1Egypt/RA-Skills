## Description:

yanji-tech-indicators calculates common technical indicators from built-in example K-line data for educational demonstrations, not real market analysis.

This skill is for demonstration purposes and not for production usage.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Educators, learners, and developers use this skill to demonstrate MACD, RSI, KDJ, Bollinger band, moving average, volume-price, pattern, and support/resistance calculations with fixed sample data. Outputs should be treated as teaching examples, not financial advice or live market conclusions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake fixed demonstration indicator outputs for live market data or financial advice.

Mitigation: Keep the educational data-source disclosure visible and verify any real investment decision against independent, current market data and qualified financial guidance.

Risk: The skill is useful only when a Chinese-language educational stock-indicator demo matches the user's need.

Mitigation: Install or enable it only for teaching and demonstration workflows where fixed sample calculations are acceptable.
