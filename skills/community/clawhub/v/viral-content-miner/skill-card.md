## Description:

热点分析与爆款挖掘 helps Chinese-language content creators, operators, brands, and strategy teams scan recent public trends, mine viral content patterns, compare platform signals, and generate content angle recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content creators, social media operators, brand teams, and strategy teams use this skill to research recent public trends, identify viral content patterns, and plan topic angles across Chinese social and news platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause the skill to be used for trend questions where narrower research would be sufficient.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's target topic, platform, audience, and time window before producing recommendations when the request is ambiguous.

Risk: Search snippets and platform metrics can be incomplete, estimated, or time-sensitive.

Mitigation: Verify important engagement metrics and source dates manually, label uncertain data, and treat outputs as a point-in-time snapshot.
