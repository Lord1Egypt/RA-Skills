## Description:

フリーランス・個人事業主のための確定申告サポート。所得分類、経費整理、控除確認、消費税・インボイス制度の知識アシスタント。令和7年度税制改正（基礎控除95万円段階・160万円の壁）対応。API接続なし、マイナンバー取扱なし。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, especially freelancers, sole proprietors, and side-business earners in Japan, use this skill to organize tax filing information, classify income and expenses, check deductions, and understand consumption tax and invoice-system considerations before filing or consulting a tax professional.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Japan

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tax calculations, deadlines, and deductions can be outdated or approximate.

Mitigation: Verify current rules with the National Tax Agency or a qualified tax professional before filing.

Risk: Tax workflows may involve sensitive identifiers or detailed financial information.

Mitigation: Avoid entering My Number or unnecessary sensitive financial details into persistent notes or shared agent memory.
