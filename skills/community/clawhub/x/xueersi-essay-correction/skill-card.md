## Description:

Xueersi AI Essay Correction grades, analyzes, and improves Chinese essays with detailed feedback on structure, language, content, and scoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhouzhi-tal](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhouzhi-tal)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and educators use this skill to provide structured Chinese essay correction for elementary, middle-school, and high-school writing. It returns scoring, strengths, issues, rewrite examples, comments, and practice suggestions without rewriting the whole essay.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill text uses Xueersi branding, but the publisher is a third-party ClawHub user rather than server-proven NVIDIA or Xueersi ownership.

Mitigation: Verify publisher identity and brand authorization separately if brand authenticity matters for deployment.

Risk: Essay feedback and scoring can be subjective or unsuitable for a student's specific rubric.

Mitigation: Have educators or guardians review recommendations before relying on them for grading or high-stakes assessment.

Risk: Very short or incomplete essays may produce weak analysis.

Mitigation: Ask the user to provide the full essay when the submitted text is under 50 Chinese characters.
