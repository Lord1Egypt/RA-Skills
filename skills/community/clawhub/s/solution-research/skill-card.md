## Description:

Use when choosing solutions, including selecting open-source projects, working around issues, and comparing products.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to perform evidence-heavy research before choosing technical solutions, including open-source projects, product options, workarounds, and compatibility paths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can consume significant time and tool usage during exhaustive web searches, repository inspection, issue review, and video analysis.

Mitigation: Set explicit research scope, time budget, source priorities, and stopping criteria before using the skill for quick decisions.

Risk: Research results may over-index on English-language sources or miss ecosystem-specific context if the task scope is vague.

Mitigation: Specify target languages, ecosystems, platforms, and compatibility requirements before research begins.
