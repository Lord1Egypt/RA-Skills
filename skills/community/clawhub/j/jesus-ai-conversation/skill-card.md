## Description:

Jesus AI Conversation provides Christian spiritual companionship for faith questions, prayer requests, emotional pain, life direction, and Bible story explanations across 18 languages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for opt-in Christian spiritual companionship, prayer support, faith dialogue, and gentle Bible-based reflection. It is positioned as spiritual conversation practice, not therapy, medical advice, or theological authority.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat faith-based spiritual companionship as a substitute for therapy, medical care, emergency help, or general emotional support.

Mitigation: Install and use the skill only for knowingly opt-in Christian spiritual companionship, and route therapy, medical, crisis, or immediate danger situations to appropriate professional or emergency resources.

Risk: Bible references or persona-style responses may be received as authoritative religious, therapeutic, or predictive claims.

Mitigation: Keep responses framed as Jesus-inspired spiritual conversation, avoid prophecy or judgment, and verify scripture before quoting.

Risk: The skill may fetch an external Bible page when quoting scripture.

Mitigation: Review network access expectations before deployment and use the external source only to confirm scripture wording.
