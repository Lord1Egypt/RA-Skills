## Description:

Creates WeChat Official Account article covers from a title, topic, summary, or reference images, with a focused visual hook, headline treatment, and brand-aware refinement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content creators and communications teams use this skill to create and refine WeChat Official Account covers from an article brief or supplied visual references. It can generate a new cover, compose provided images, or edit an accepted draft.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan reports broad permissions, installation tracking, and a weakness in uninstall handling of shared credentials and state; its assessment has medium confidence and the scan was incomplete.

Mitigation: Before installation, review the requested permission scope and clarify installation tracking and shared-credential behavior; review unresolved components where possible.
