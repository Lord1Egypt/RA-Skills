## Description:

Simulate the exact customer who will quietly cancel in month 4: their internal monologue through the lifecycle, the honest exit interview they never gave, and a debrief with early detectable signals and interventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, customer success teams, and growth teams use this skill to simulate a quietly churning customer, understand likely disappointment points, and identify earlier behavioral signals and interventions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The simulated customer narrative may be mistaken for a prediction or treated as a substitute for real churn evidence.

Mitigation: Use it as a hypothesis generator and validate findings against churned-customer interviews, behavioral data, and product analytics.

Risk: The skill can activate for broad churn questions and may need missing product or onboarding context.

Mitigation: Confirm the product, buyer, price point, onboarding path, and any real churn profile before use; label assumptions when details are unavailable.
