## Description:

Relate Coach helps users improve everyday communication, set healthier boundaries, de-escalate conflict, and maintain better relationships through practical self-help frameworks such as nonviolent communication, active listening, and respectful assertiveness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People seeking everyday communication support use this skill to plan calmer conversations, set clear boundaries, de-escalate low-stakes conflict, and draft respectful messages without receiving therapy, diagnosis, dating strategy, or crisis support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat low-stakes relationship coaching as therapy, diagnosis, legal advice, crisis help, or a substitute for qualified support.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as self-help communication guidance and refer users to professional, legal, emergency, or crisis resources when serious safety, clinical, or legal concerns appear.

Risk: Users may request manipulative, deceptive, dating, ex-recovery, or impersonation scripts outside the skill scope.

Mitigation: Decline those requests and redirect only to respectful, low-stakes communication planning.
