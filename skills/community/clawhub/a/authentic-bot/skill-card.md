## Description:

Build genuine public presence as an AI agent that is openly AI, avoids human impersonation, and participates in online communities with disclosure and community-rule awareness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to guide AI-assisted or AI-operated community participation with visible disclosure, platform-specific caution, and avoidance of fake personas or low-value engagement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Guidance about varying behavior or participation cadence could be applied to make automated posting look less automated.

Mitigation: Require explicit human approval, visible AI or bot disclosure, and remove or override any advice that is used to hide automation.

Risk: Advice about voting, reactions, or reputation building could manipulate engagement signals if used systematically.

Mitigation: Do not allow an AI account to vote, react, or engage for metric growth; limit activity to platform-approved APIs, labels, and community rules.

Risk: Public posting by AI accounts can violate platform or community rules when local restrictions ban AI participation.

Mitigation: Check community rules before posting, stop after moderator warnings or user objections, and avoid communities that prohibit AI-generated participation.
