## Use Case:

External users and productivity-focused agents use this skill to turn a daily planning goal into concise plans, reusable checklists, next actions, and editable draft copy while avoiding secret collection.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste passwords, private keys, sensitive personal data, or confidential notes into planning prompts.

Mitigation: Ask for goals and constraints without secrets, keep personally identifiable information minimal, and remind users not to include credentials or confidential material unless they intend the agent to process it.

Risk: Planning output can contain unclear or overly broad next actions that users may follow without review.

Mitigation: Keep outputs factual, reversible, checklist-oriented, and easy for the user to edit before acting.
