## Use Case:

External users, investment researchers, and analysts use this skill to evaluate listed companies across A-share, Hong Kong, and US markets by identifying the company's core moat, scoring six competitive-advantage dimensions, and producing a structured investment research report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated ratings and investment conclusions may be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research assistance, verify conclusions against current source documents, and consult qualified financial professionals before making investment decisions.

Risk: Uploaded financial reports, research notes, or meeting records may contain confidential information.

Mitigation: Avoid uploading confidential or restricted documents unless the user is comfortable having them processed for this analysis.
