## Description:

Guides an agent through creating ecommerce spokesperson videos with portrait prompts, production recipes, and a script-to-video workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers, marketers, and video creators use this skill to plan digital spokesperson videos for product listings and livestream commerce. It provides reusable portrait prompts, narration guidance, and CLI steps for image, voice, avatar, and B-roll generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow uses a third-party CLI and may send identifiable or confidential media to external services.

Mitigation: Consider the listed global npm install command before installing, and avoid sending identifiable or confidential images, audio, or video unless you are comfortable sharing them.
