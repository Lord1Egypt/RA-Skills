## Description:

Searches for movie and TV titles by Chinese or English name and returns grouped resource links for streaming platforms, subtitles, and download-oriented search sites.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent to locate movie or TV resources from a title, optional year, and inferred media type. The skill formats results as grouped links for legal streaming platforms, subtitle sites, and download-oriented search sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may surface torrent or unofficial download resources for copyrighted movies and shows.

Mitigation: Prefer licensed streaming platforms and ask before showing unofficial download-oriented resources.

Risk: Links to unofficial download sites can expose users to malware or unsafe browsing destinations.

Mitigation: Review links before use and avoid directing users to untrusted download pages when a licensed source is available.
