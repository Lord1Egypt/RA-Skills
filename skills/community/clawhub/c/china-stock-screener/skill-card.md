## Description:

Screens A-share and Hong Kong-listed stocks using technical, capital-flow, and fundamental criteria, with multi-factor filtering and ranked results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tingdall](https://clawhub.ai/user/tingdall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to screen A-share and Hong Kong-listed stocks by technical signals, capital-flow indicators, fundamentals, market, and industry filters. It helps produce ranked informational screening results and stock-level rationale, not personalized investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stock scores, rankings, and recommendation labels may be mistaken for personalized investment advice or trading authorization.

Mitigation: Treat results as informational screening only, verify data independently, and do not rely on the output as personalized investment advice.

Risk: Financial, capital-flow, and market data used in screening may be incomplete, stale, or inconsistent across sources.

Mitigation: Check current market data and issuer filings before making financial decisions, and review the screening rationale against trusted data sources.
