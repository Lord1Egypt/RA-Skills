## Description:

Tracks global research, essays, discourse, and media discussion about "mommy-type" characters, mommy issues, daddy issues, mothering ideology, gendered care, emotional labor, parasocial intimacy, and digital intimacy, then converts findings into Chinese-ready article ideas, source packs, and weekly briefs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Writers, researchers, editors, and external agents use this skill to discover high-quality sources, prepare weekly briefs, assemble topic research packs, and turn English and Chinese source material into structured Chinese article plans on care, intimacy, gender, and pop-culture archetypes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research outputs may overstate weak discourse evidence or imply unsupported causality.

Mitigation: The skill instructs agents to mark confidence, distinguish source types, avoid overstating causality, and separate empirical findings from cultural interpretation and inference.

Risk: Sensitive terms such as mommy issues or daddy issues could be presented as diagnoses.

Mitigation: The skill explicitly tells agents not to diagnose real people and not to present these terms as formal clinical diagnoses unless a source explains their status.

Risk: The default Chinese output and broad English and Chinese search scope may not match every deployment need.

Mitigation: Ask the agent for a different output language, narrower source scope, or specific databases when needed.
