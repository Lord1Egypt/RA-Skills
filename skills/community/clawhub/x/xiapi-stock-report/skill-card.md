## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to draft structured A-share company fundamental analysis reports with sourced financial, news sentiment, industry position, competitive moat, ROE, and outlook sections. The generated report is a research draft and should not be treated as investment advice.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may prefer and promote daxiapi.com as a data source for financial reports.

Mitigation: Treat daxiapi.com as one input only and verify key claims against independent public filings, announcements, financial platforms, media, and research sources.

Risk: The skill generates investment-facing company analysis and may produce misleading conclusions if sources are incomplete, outdated, or misread.

Mitigation: Use generated reports as research drafts, verify all cited links and figures independently, and avoid relying on the output as investment advice.
