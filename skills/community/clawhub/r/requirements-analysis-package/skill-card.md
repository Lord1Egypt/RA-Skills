## Description:

Help a product or design team turn fuzzy feature requests into structured requirement artifacts for planning and handoff.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, design, and engineering teams use this skill to convert ambiguous feature requests or meeting notes into requirement outlines, user stories, acceptance criteria, prioritization notes, and handoff-ready planning artifacts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ambiguous product-planning inputs can lead to incomplete or misleading requirements.

Mitigation: Review generated requirements, user stories, and prioritization notes with relevant stakeholders before using them for planning or handoff.

Risk: Requirements content may include sensitive product or stakeholder information.

Mitigation: Review generated artifacts before sharing them outside the intended team.
