## Description:

Estimate how changes in bundle uptake, upsell acceptance, or cart-building tactics could increase average order value without hiding margin tradeoffs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce, growth, and merchandising teams use this skill to compare bundle, upsell, discount, and cart-building scenarios before changing live offers. It helps surface AOV upside, margin or break-even pressure, weak assumptions, and test recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The included reference template does not match the AOV simulation purpose, which could lead to unhelpful business output if followed literally.

Mitigation: Review or replace the template before relying on the skill for ecommerce scenario analysis.

Risk: Scenario recommendations may be misleading if margin, refund, conversion, attach-rate, or guardrail assumptions are incomplete.

Mitigation: Require explicit baseline inputs and treat outputs as planning guidance to review before testing or scaling offers.
