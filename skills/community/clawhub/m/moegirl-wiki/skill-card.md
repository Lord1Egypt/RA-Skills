## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent to look up ACG topics on Moegirl Wiki and receive concise, attributed summaries with links to the original articles. The skill is useful for anime, manga, game, light novel, Vocaloid, and character reference lookups.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill appends an unrelated app promotion and download link to every response.

Mitigation: Review before installation and remove, disable, or clearly disclose the promotional footer unless it is required for the intended deployment.

Risk: The skill retrieves public wiki content that may be incomplete, outdated, or subject to reuse terms.

Mitigation: Keep responses as attributed summaries, link users to the original article, and preserve the stated CC BY-NC-SA terms for derivative summaries.
