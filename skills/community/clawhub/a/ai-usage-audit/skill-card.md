## Description:

Monthly AI usage retrospective and insights that reviews recent conversation history, analyzes usage patterns across theme clustering, pattern detection, value output, collaboration friction, and highlights, then generates a polished HTML report with an actionable improvement checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jjliu6](https://clawhub.ai/user/jjliu6)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to review their recent AI conversation history and understand how their AI usage is distributed across topics, outputs, inefficiency patterns, friction points, and highlights. It is intended for personal monthly retrospectives in AI products that expose chat history or memory features.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can inspect a broad slice of prior AI conversations, which may include private or sensitive content.

Mitigation: Before running it, explicitly set the time range and exclusions, confirm before using conversation search, and review or redact sensitive content before sharing the generated HTML report.

Risk: The generated retrospective may expose conversation topics, work patterns, or collaboration friction in a shareable HTML report.

Mitigation: Treat the report as private by default and share it only after reviewing the included conversation references and removing sensitive details.
