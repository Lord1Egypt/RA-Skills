## Description:

Turn sales notes, trend signals, seasonal context, promo plans, and inventory constraints into a practical demand forecast brief with base, upside, and downside scenarios, leading indicators, and replenishment cues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External planners, ecommerce operators, founders, and consultants use this skill to turn user-provided sales notes, trend signals, seasonal context, promo plans, and inventory constraints into a scenario-based demand forecast brief for planning and replenishment decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat heuristic forecast scenarios as guaranteed demand or as approval for inventory commitments.

Mitigation: Keep outputs advisory and require human review before purchase orders, budgets, or inventory transfers.

Risk: Users may assume the skill fetched live trend, ERP, BI, ads, marketplace, or competitor data.

Mitigation: State that trend metrics, marketplace observations, and search-volume exports must be user-provided, and downgrade confidence when inputs are incomplete or distorted.
