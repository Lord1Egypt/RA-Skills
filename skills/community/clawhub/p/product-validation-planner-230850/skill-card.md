## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, and actionable checklists before investing heavily.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to evaluate whether a product idea is worth building and to produce validation workflows, checklists, plans, prototypes, and positioning artifacts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill for unrelated product, SaaS, startup, or validation requests.

Mitigation: Review the trigger wording before deployment and narrow it when precise routing matters.

Risk: Product validation outputs may be incomplete when user goals, constraints, inputs, or success criteria are underspecified.

Mitigation: Require visible assumptions, limits, required inputs, and a short verification note in user-facing outputs.
