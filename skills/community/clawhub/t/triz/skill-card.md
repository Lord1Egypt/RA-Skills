## Description:

Helps engineers and product managers analyze technical problems and generate innovation options using TRIZ's Five Bridges methodology.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[homerzhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/homerzhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and product managers use this skill to structure technical problems, identify contradictions, apply TRIZ bridges, and produce candidate solutions with evaluation notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installer instructions use an unpinned latest package reference.

Mitigation: Review before installing and prefer a pinned or verified installer version.

Risk: Generated solution rankings and validation labels may be mistaken for engineering proof.

Mitigation: Treat results as brainstorming output and validate recommendations through normal engineering review.

Risk: Analysis runs can save local Markdown reports that may contain sensitive business or technical details.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive information unless local report storage is acceptable for the environment.
