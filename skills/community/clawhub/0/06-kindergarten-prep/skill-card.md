## Use Case:

External caregivers use this Chinese-language skill to assess whether a 2-3 year old child is ready for kindergarten across social interaction, self-care, and emotional separation readiness. The skill produces a readiness report, gap analysis, preparation plan, and checklist from caregiver-provided context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Caregiver prompts may include sensitive child development details and family context.

Mitigation: Share only details needed for the readiness assessment and avoid including information the caregiver is not comfortable processing through the agent.

Risk: The assessment can provide parenting guidance but is not a substitute for professional support when readiness concerns are significant.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a preparation aid and seek qualified professional support when the output flags substantial gaps or sustained separation distress.
