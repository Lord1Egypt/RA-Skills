## Description:

Write and save a RoamSEO report as one self-contained HTML page.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[roamseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/roamseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

RoamSEO users and their agents use this skill to turn completed SEO research into a self-contained report saved to the project. It provides the reader with a concise verdict, leading recommendation, and report link in chat.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad activation description may trigger for generic report, check-in, or summary requests outside RoamSEO research.

Mitigation: Review the activation scope and invoke the skill for completed RoamSEO research or a clearly relevant report request.

Risk: The scanner guidance notes a possibility of conversation content being saved in a report.

Mitigation: Review report contents before saving and omit sensitive conversation details that do not belong in the project report.
