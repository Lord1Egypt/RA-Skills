## Description:

A systematic anatomy skill bundle based on the 10th edition of Systematic Anatomy from People's Medical Publishing House, covering 112 core anatomy skills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Medical students, clinicians, anatomy researchers, and surgical residents use this skill to retrieve structured anatomy explanations, analyze neuroanatomy and clinical anatomy scenarios, and navigate textbook-oriented terminology and reference material.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes medical anatomy content that may touch clinical localization, invasive procedures, emergency scenarios, and surgical planning.

Mitigation: Use it only as a supervised educational anatomy reference, and require clinician review before applying any output to patient care or procedural decisions.

Risk: Users may mistake educational explanations for diagnosis, emergency triage, self-treatment, or procedure instructions.

Mitigation: Add guardrails that prohibit diagnosis, emergency triage, self-treatment, and independent procedural guidance, and direct urgent or patient-specific situations to qualified medical professionals.
