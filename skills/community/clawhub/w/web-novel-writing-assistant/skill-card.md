## Description:

Assists web novel authors with planning, outlining, chapter drafting, pacing diagnosis, continuity checks, plot-thread tracking, foreshadowing management, and reader feedback analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openlark](https://clawhub.ai/user/openlark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External writers and writing assistants use this skill to plan serial web novels, convert outlines into chapter briefs, check pacing and continuity, track foreshadowing and plotlines, and turn reader feedback into revision decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for a wide range of web-novel writing workflow requests and may use story drafts, outlines, reader comments, or performance data that the user supplies.

Mitigation: Provide only the creative material, reader feedback, and performance data that should be used for the writing task.

Risk: Feedback-driven guidance may overweight incomplete or unrepresentative reader data if the supplied inputs are narrow or noisy.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against the story outline, setting rules, and broader reader signals before changing published or planned material.
