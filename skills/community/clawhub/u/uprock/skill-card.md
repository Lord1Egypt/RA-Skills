## Description:

Crawl live web pages and run multi-engine web research from real devices in a chosen country, and search a shared public video index by moment. Use when a fetch is blocked or bot-walled, when the answer depends on location (local prices, local search results), or when the answer is inside public video (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and research agents use UpRock when they need to retrieve a live page blocked to ordinary fetchers, compare web results from selected countries, check regional availability, or find and describe moments in public video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: URLs or research queries may be sent to UpRock, including sensitive URL parameters or confidential query content.

Mitigation: Before sending a URL or query, remove sensitive URL parameters and avoid including confidential information.
