## Description:

Make a person, portrait, or avatar talk on camera by guiding an agent through script-driven text-to-speech or audio-driven lip-sync video workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[runware](https://clawhub.ai/user/runware)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use this skill to prepare talking-head presenters, explainers, spokesperson videos, and re-voiced footage from a portrait, registered avatar, script, supplied audio, or source video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real people's images, voices, or footage may be used without rights or consent.

Mitigation: Use only media the user owns or is authorized to use, and confirm consent before generating talking-avatar or re-voiced videos.

Risk: Generated presenter videos can enable impersonation, misleading endorsements, or fabricated on-screen claims.

Mitigation: Avoid impersonation and misleading endorsements, and keep spoken or on-screen claims limited to copy the user supplied and approved.

Risk: Requests may send biometric likeness and voice material to external video-generation providers.

Mitigation: Treat faces, voices, and source footage as sensitive media and avoid submitting them to external providers without approval.
