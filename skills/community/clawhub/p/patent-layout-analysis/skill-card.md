## Description:

Generates a patent and market landscape analysis for a specified product or technology field, covering market size, competitors, technology hotspots and gaps, applicant jurisdiction coverage, opportunities, risks, and a self-contained HTML report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, strategy, patent, and innovation teams use this skill to evaluate a product or technology field before project initiation. It guides retrieval and analysis of patent and market evidence, checks leading domestic and international applicants, and produces a structured HTML report with opportunities, risks, and differentiated recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requires authorization to the Zhihuiya/PatSnap MCP service and may send patent and market research queries to external services.

Mitigation: Confirm MCP configuration, account authorization, and data-sharing expectations before use.

Risk: The skill is primarily documented in Chinese, which may affect setup and review by non-Chinese-speaking users.

Mitigation: Request a different output language explicitly and review generated reports before relying on them.

Risk: Patent and market findings may be incomplete or time-sensitive because the workflow depends on retrieved samples and external search results.

Mitigation: Validate cited sources, search queries, sample sizes, and key competitor coverage before making business or filing decisions.
