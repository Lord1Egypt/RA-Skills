## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broader homepage or conversion-focused web copy requests beyond strict landing pages.

Mitigation: Use narrower trigger wording during installation when activation should be limited to landing-page-only work.

Risk: Generated landing page claims, metrics, testimonials, or proof points could be inaccurate if the user supplies weak or incomplete inputs.

Mitigation: Review generated copy against verified product facts, approved customer proof, and legal or brand requirements before publication.
