Insurance brokers, MGAs, carriers, and insurtech teams use this skill to structure agent-assisted underwriting, claims triage, policy administration, renewals, compliance checks, and broker operations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is a high-stakes insurance automation guide that encourages automated claims and underwriting decisions without enough guardrails for regulated data or human review.

Mitigation: Treat it as planning material unless the organization adds licensed human approval, permissible-factor controls, claims authority limits, fraud review, audit logging, data minimization, retention, and jurisdiction-specific insurance and privacy compliance.
