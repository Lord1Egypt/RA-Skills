## Description:

Full-stack B2B marketing execution skill for building ICP and brand messaging, generating multi-platform content, creating cold email sequences, planning SEO topic clusters, monitoring competitors, and producing marketing reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, founders, and go-to-market operators use this skill to define B2B audience strategy, create reusable campaign assets, repurpose content across Western and Chinese social platforms, and draft reporting materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing content may include unrelated company branding or messaging that does not match the user's brand.

Mitigation: Review generated posts, hashtags, SEO keywords, emails, and reports before publishing; remove hard-coded Yucheng or Mingyang terms before use.

Risk: The skill can send prompt content and referenced file material to an external LLM provider through an API key.

Mitigation: Do not provide confidential inputs unless approved for external AI processing, and document any required privacy controls before deployment.

Risk: Outbound email and platform publishing suggestions may not reflect applicable compliance rules or platform policies.

Mitigation: Add explicit privacy, consent, and outbound-email compliance checks to the workflow before using generated campaign material.
