## Description:

Optimize ecommerce product listings for Amazon A9, Taobao/1688 search, JD search, TikTok Shop discovery, Xiaohongshu SEO, and Shopify storefront search using keyword density frameworks, attribute-completeness checklists, and conversion-trap detection.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketplace sellers, listing specialists, and ecommerce teams use this skill to audit provided product listing notes, refresh keyword strategy, check listing attributes, and produce a practical optimization brief without live marketplace analytics or seller portal access.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketplace wording may be treated as ranking or sales advice even though the skill is a static heuristic helper.

Mitigation: Review generated copy before publishing and keep ranking or sales expectations directional, not guaranteed.

Risk: The brief does not use live SEO analytics, ad console data, seller portal data, or platform APIs.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against current marketplace data and account-specific performance signals before operational changes.

Risk: Product categories with regulated claims may require stricter review than the skill can provide.

Mitigation: Route regulated listing copy through the operator's compliance or legal review process before publication.
