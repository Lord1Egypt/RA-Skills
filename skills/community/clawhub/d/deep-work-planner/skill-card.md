## Description:

Transforms a messy task list or brain dump into a structured daily deep work schedule using time-blocking and priority scoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tetsuakira-vk](https://clawhub.ai/user/tetsuakira-vk)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Knowledge workers, freelancers, founders, writers, researchers, and creators use this skill to turn raw task lists into prioritized daily plans with protected deep work blocks. It helps users classify tasks, score priorities, identify low-value work, and produce a copy-pasteable schedule.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include sensitive task, client, or calendar details in prompts.

Mitigation: Avoid entering highly sensitive details unless sharing them with the assistant fits the user's privacy requirements.

Risk: Strict environments may require supply-chain controls for skill installation.

Mitigation: Use a pinned or otherwise trusted installer and version when installing the skill.

Risk: The generated schedule may misprioritize work if the input task list or context is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the schedule before moving it into a calendar or planner, especially for deadlines and delegated tasks.
