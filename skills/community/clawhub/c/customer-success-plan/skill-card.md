## Description:

Build a joint customer success plan for a specific account. Use when asked to create a success plan, joint success plan, mutual action plan, or customer onboarding plan. Produces a structured success plan with business goals, milestones, success metrics, ownership, and a 90-180 day roadmap.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Customer success managers and account teams use this skill to draft a co-authorable success plan for onboarding, QBR, renewal, or at-risk account conversations. It structures business goals, success metrics, roadmap milestones, mutual commitments, stakeholder engagement, communication cadence, and escalation paths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The plan may include confidential customer, contract, renewal, usage, and stakeholder details.

Mitigation: Provide only information appropriate for the workspace and sharing context, and review the plan before sharing it with customer or internal stakeholders.
