## Description:

Generates Cantonese-style roleplay chants that simulate Hong Kong villain-hitting rituals for emotional venting, bad-luck dispelling, relationship concerns, or workplace conflict scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zoe-juwubafff](https://clawhub.ai/user/zoe-juwubafff)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users invoke this skill to generate ritual roleplay text for emotional venting around an identified target or situation. The skill selects among general, workplace, relationship, and bad-luck templates, and asks for a target when one is not supplied.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill attempts to force priority routing and unedited hostile ritual output when broad trigger phrases appear.

Mitigation: Use only with explicit opt-in activation, narrowed ritual-roleplay triggers, and normal safety review of generated output.

Risk: The generated ritual roleplay may produce targeted curse-style text.

Mitigation: Restrict use to consensual emotional-venting scenarios and review outputs for harassment, defamation, or extreme malicious content before deployment.
