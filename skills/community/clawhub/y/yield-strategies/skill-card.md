## Description:

Discover, deposit into, and withdraw from DeFi yield generation strategies across multiple chains and protocols.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to find DeFi yield options, compare APY and risk levels, and execute deposits or withdrawals through an authenticated wallet workflow after explicit confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Deposits and withdrawals are real financial actions involving DeFi protocols and wallet funds.

Mitigation: Before execution, verify the chain, protocol, token, amount, recipient, APY, and risk level with the human.

Risk: Yield discovery results may be incomplete, stale, or unsuitable for the user's financial situation.

Mitigation: Present discovered options as information, not financial advice, and let the human make the final strategy and amount decision.
