## Description:

Generates Xiaohongshu (XHS) marketing copy that can combine current weekly trends and marketing moments with a specified scenic area, brand, or topic.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing and content teams use this skill to draft Xiaohongshu posts with a title, first-person body copy, hashtags, and publishing suggestions. It is especially oriented toward travel, scenic area, and brand promotion content that benefits from current topic hooks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trend references and hashtags may be stale, inaccurate, or unsuitable for the intended audience.

Mitigation: Review the generated copy, hashtags, and trend references before publishing.
