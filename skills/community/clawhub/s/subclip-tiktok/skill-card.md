## Description:

Post, schedule and review TikTok videos for a connected TikTok account through Subclip MCP (beta). Use for TikTok drafts and direct posts, scheduling, and TikTok analytics. The user chooses privacy, interactions and disclosures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users with a connected TikTok account can use this skill to prepare drafts or direct posts, schedule and review publication status, and inspect available TikTok analytics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing can send content to an external TikTok account with visibility and interaction settings that affect who can see or engage with it.

Mitigation: Confirm the account, exact caption, privacy, comments/duet/stitch settings, disclosures, and timing with the user before publishing.

Risk: While the TikTok app is unaudited, direct posts are limited to private accounts with Only me visibility; published videos cannot be edited or deleted through the public API.

Mitigation: Check creator information and account settings first; offer a draft or a permitted Only me direct post, and direct users to the TikTok app for edits or deletion.
