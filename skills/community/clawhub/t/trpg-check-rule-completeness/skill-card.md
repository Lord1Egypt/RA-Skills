## Description:

Audits completed tabletop role-playing game rule systems for major gaps, missing rules, and logical inconsistencies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ice26985850](https://clawhub.ai/user/ice26985850)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

TRPG designers, editors, and agents use this skill to review finalized rule documents for missing rules, incomplete subsystems, and inconsistencies that could block play.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may surface incorrect or overstated gaps if the reviewed rule files are incomplete or not the finalized rule set.

Mitigation: Run the review only against finalized rule documents and have a human designer confirm critical playability findings before changing the rules.
