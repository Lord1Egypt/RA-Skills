Designers, developers, and other external users use this skill to choose mood-matched color palettes and receive practical role-based colors for UI and design work.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked for broad color-related design requests where the user did not explicitly ask for Happy Hues palettes.

Mitigation: Review trigger wording during deployment and narrow it if the intended use is limited to explicit Happy Hues lookups.
