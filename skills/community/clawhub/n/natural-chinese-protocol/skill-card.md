## Description:

Edits and rewrites general Chinese text so it reads more natural, idiomatic, and human while preserving the original intent and context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[garden-cosmos](https://clawhub.ai/user/garden-cosmos)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese writers, editors, and agents use this skill to revise general Chinese text for more natural native phrasing, reducing formulaic AI-style patterns while preserving meaning and matching the original writing context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be misused to hide required AI-use disclosures or alter legal and compliance statements.

Mitigation: Do not apply it to required disclosure, legal, or compliance wording unless the user explicitly confirms the exact text may be edited; preserve required statements verbatim.

Risk: A rewrite could drift from the user's requested tone, language, or original meaning.

Mitigation: Confirm the writing context before rewriting, preserve the core intent, and review the result against the user's requested tone and language.
