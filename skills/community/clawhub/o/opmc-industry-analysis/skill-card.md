## Description:

A Chinese-language industry analysis skill that guides agents through a 10-dimension framework with authoritative data-source requirements, citation rules, and Word-report output guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External entrepreneurs, analysts, and strategy teams use this skill to structure market-entry, investment, and industry-research work into a cited Chinese report. It covers PEST, market sizing, value chain, competition, technology routes, customer profiles, unit economics, compliance risks, and recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad market-research wording and produce a Chinese-formatted Word report even when the user expected a narrower answer or another format.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested analysis scope, language, and output format before applying the full workflow.

Risk: Industry reports can influence strategic or investment decisions and may rely on public-source assumptions or projections.

Mitigation: Keep citations attached to data points, label assumptions and forecasts clearly, and require human review before business or investment use.

Risk: Compliance and market-size claims may become misleading if sources are weak, stale, or unsupported.

Mitigation: Use the skill's source-ranking rules, cross-check core TAM/SAM/SOM/CAGR claims with at least two A-level sources, and mark unresolved data as needing verification.
