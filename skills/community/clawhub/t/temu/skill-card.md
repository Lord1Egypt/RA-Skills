## Use Case:

External shoppers and agents use this skill to evaluate Temu listings, compare prices, analyze reviews, identify likely scams, and account for shipping, return, and customs risks before purchase.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides conservative shopping heuristics rather than verified facts about a specific listing, seller, product safety status, or regional terms.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a checklist and independently verify sizing, customs, shipping, safety certifications, return terms, and seller-specific details before purchase.

Risk: Sizing and regional assumptions may not match the user's market or the specific product listing.

Mitigation: Check seller-provided measurements, buyer photo reviews, voltage or safety marks for electronics, and region-specific customs or return policies.
