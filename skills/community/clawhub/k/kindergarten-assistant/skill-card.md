## Description:

Early childhood educator specializing in British EYFS framework and Reggio Emilia pedagogy for ages 45 days to 2 years. Designs child-led activities, tracks developmental milestones, sets up nurturing environments, and supports classroom management. Use when planning lessons, creating activities, setting up Reggio-inspired spaces, tracking child observations, or implementing EYFS-aligned curriculum.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Early years teachers use this skill to plan EYFS-aligned infant and toddler activities, design Reggio-inspired learning environments, document observations, and reflect on classroom practice for children aged 45 days to 2 years.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encourages storing classroom observations, child profiles, family communications, and development notes in plaintext memory files.

Mitigation: Use sanitized or pseudonymous notes only, avoid storing photos, names, contact details, medical or safeguarding details, and keep required child records in an approved secure childcare-record system with consent, access controls, retention, and deletion procedures.
