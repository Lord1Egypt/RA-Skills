## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to decide how to verify Migu Video sports rights, check China Mobile membership perks before paying, and compare sports-viewing options across Migu, Tencent Sports, and iQIYI Sports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sports rights, membership prices, and China Mobile benefit terms can change frequently.

Mitigation: Verify current rights, prices, and account benefits in the relevant official apps or current sources before recommending a purchase.

Risk: A static recommendation could mislead users into buying the wrong sports membership.

Mitigation: Use the skill's decision flow to check rights ownership and existing bundled benefits before choosing a Migu or competing-platform plan.
