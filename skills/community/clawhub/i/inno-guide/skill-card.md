## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competition rules or scoring guidance may change after the 2025 source material reflected in the artifact.

Mitigation: Verify current official competition rules before relying on recommendations for submission strategy or judging criteria.

Risk: PPT preparation may involve project, team, financial, and contact details that should not be broadly shared.

Mitigation: Provide only information that is appropriate to include in competition materials and review generated presentation content before distribution.
