## Use Case:

Managers and people-operations users use this skill to convert precise Zi Wei Dou Shu chart inputs and workplace context into structured people-management reports for onboarding, promotion support, communication planning, team relationship diagnosis, and manager-employee fit analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill processes sensitive birth details and workplace profiling context.

Mitigation: Use it only with explicit consent from every person whose birth or workplace data is processed.

Risk: External charting sites may receive identifying personal or employment data.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting names or employment identifiers to external charting sites; use only the minimum data required for chart generation.

Risk: Default report archival and MEMORY.md updates may retain sensitive employee profiles without an approved retention basis.

Mitigation: Disable MEMORY.md and default report archival unless an approved retention policy is in place.

Risk: The generated profiles could be over-weighted in employment decisions.

Mitigation: Do not use these profiles as the sole basis for hiring, promotion, performance, compensation, or other employment decisions.
