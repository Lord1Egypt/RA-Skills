## Description:

Copywriting Hooks helps agents generate and refine opening hooks and article titles for long-form English or French writing, offering varied options and asking the user to choose before continuing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samber](https://clawhub.ai/user/samber)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, marketers, and developer-relations teams use this skill to draft article openings and titles for long-form blog posts, newsletters, opinion pieces, reported features, and technical deep dives. It is designed for English, French, or bilingual copywriting workflows where the user chooses among distinct rhetorical approaches.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence reports that the skill requests broader file-editing, search, and agent-delegation powers than its copywriting task requires.

Mitigation: Review before installing and prefer a version limited to reading reference material and asking the user clarifying questions, or run it only in a contained workspace.
