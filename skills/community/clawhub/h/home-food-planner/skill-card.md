## Description:

Family meal planning tool that provides weekly menu planning, nutrition analysis, shopping list generation, ingredient management, and recipe recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to plan household meals, manage available ingredients, generate shopping lists, analyze general nutrition, and receive recipe recommendations based on family needs and available food.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Nutrition, allergy, or medical-diet guidance may be too general for users with health constraints.

Mitigation: Treat nutrition outputs as planning help and confirm important allergy or medical-diet advice with an appropriate professional.
