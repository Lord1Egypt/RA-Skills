## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Data-room and due diligence workflows can involve sensitive corporate, financial, customer, employee, legal, and investor materials.

Mitigation: Use an access-controlled data room, share only what is needed at each diligence stage, redact unnecessary personal or account details, require appropriate legal review or NDA coverage, and revoke access when diligence ends.

Risk: Fundraising, valuation, dilution, and term-sheet guidance may not fit a company's jurisdiction, financing structure, or legal obligations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance and have qualified legal and financial advisors review investor materials, financing terms, and diligence disclosures before relying on them.
