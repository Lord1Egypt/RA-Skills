## Description:

Reads a contract from the counterparty's lawyer's perspective to identify leverage, draft a plausible adversarial demand or position letter, and provide clause-level fixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, business users, and legal teams use this skill to stress-test contracts before sending, signing, or escalating a dispute. It helps surface exploitable language, missing protections, and redline-ready fixes for qualified legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat adversarial contract analysis or drafted position letters as legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as drafting and risk-identification support only, and have a qualified lawyer review high-value or legally sensitive agreements.

Risk: Contracts or dispute facts can contain sensitive business or personal information.

Mitigation: Provide only contract text and dispute details the user is comfortable sharing with the agent.
