## Description:

Use when a landlord withholds or deducts from a security deposit, when moving in or out and a room-by-room condition inventory is needed as evidence, or when deciding whether a claimed deduction (repainting, carpet replacement, cleaning) is fair wear-and-tear or chargeable damage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External renters, landlords, and tenant-support reviewers use this skill to organize move-in and move-out condition evidence, classify deposit deductions, calculate depreciation-adjusted amounts, and draft itemized dispute letters. It is decision support, not legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with built-in deadline examples for selected US states, England and Wales, Germany, New South Wales, and Ontario.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated letters, deadline citations, and deduction calculations may be wrong if local law has changed or differs from the built-in examples.

Mitigation: Verify current jurisdiction rules and review all facts, dates, and dollar amounts before relying on or sending generated output.

Risk: The skill provides decision support and may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Use it to organize evidence and draft arguments, and consult a qualified tenant adviser or attorney for material disputes.

Risk: Incorrect user-supplied inventories, dates, deposit amounts, or landlord deductions can produce misleading conclusions.

Mitigation: Check source documents, photos, receipts, the tenancy agreement, and claimed deductions before acting on the analysis.
