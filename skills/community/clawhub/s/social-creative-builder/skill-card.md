## Description:

Social Creative Builder helps an agent turn one approved idea into platform-native social copy packages and optional carousel slide specifications for human review and publishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketers, social teams, and content operators use this skill to convert approved ideas into ready-to-paste packages for platforms such as X, LinkedIn, Threads, Xiaohongshu, and carousel formats. It keeps product claims tied to approved wording or flags them for sourcing before human publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated social copy may contain claims or disclosures that are not ready for publication.

Mitigation: Review claims and disclosures before publishing; keep unsupported claims flagged until approved source wording is available.

Risk: Draft saving or proposal events may not fit every project workflow.

Mitigation: Approve saving drafts or proposal events only when they match the project's agreed workflow and review process.
