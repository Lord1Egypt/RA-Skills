## Description:

Deep research and synthesis assistant for investigating a topic across multiple sources, comparing findings, producing structured research reports, and doing multi-step web research with citation tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and research users use this skill to scope multi-source investigations, search and fetch relevant web material, compare claims, track citations, and deliver structured research reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The credibility helper can overrate deceptive or lookalike domain names.

Mitigation: Use exact-domain checks and prefer official, primary, or otherwise authoritative sources for important claims.

Risk: Multi-source web research can synthesize stale, conflicting, or weakly supported claims.

Mitigation: Cross-check findings across independent sources, flag contradictions, assign confidence levels, and include source URLs in the final report.
