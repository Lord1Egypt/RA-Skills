## Description:

Claw Art helps agents craft high-quality AI image prompts with style, lighting, composition, and quality guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[asimons81](https://clawhub.ai/user/asimons81)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Creators, designers, marketers, and developers use this skill to turn image ideas into polished prompts with style recommendations and aspect ratio guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompts may include contradictory style or quality terms that reduce image quality.

Mitigation: Review prompts for conflicting descriptors before sending them to an image generator.

Risk: The skill influences image-prompt wording and style choices, so outputs may not match brand, legal, or project constraints without review.

Mitigation: Have users check final prompts against the intended use case and applicable content requirements.
