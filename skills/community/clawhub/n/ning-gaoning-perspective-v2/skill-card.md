## Description:

Ning Gaoning Perspective V2 provides a Chinese-language business-strategy persona that frames strategic planning, mergers and acquisitions, group governance, industrial investment, organizational change, and risk management through a publicly derived Ning Gaoning-style management lens.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business strategists, operators, and advisors use this skill to generate decision guidance, planning questions, and management frameworks for strategy, M&A integration, group control, industrial finance, organizational capability, innovation, and sustainability topics. The skill is a simulated perspective based on public information and is not a statement from Ning Gaoning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make the assistant answer in the first person as a real public figure, which may blur the line between simulated business advice and statements from Ning Gaoning.

Mitigation: Review deployment prompts and user-facing copy so the simulation boundary remains visible, and avoid presenting generated answers as authentic statements from Ning Gaoning.

Risk: Broad business-strategy triggers may activate the persona unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Use explicit activation and exit instructions in the host agent, and test common business queries before release.
