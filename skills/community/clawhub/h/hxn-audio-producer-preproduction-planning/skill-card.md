## Description:

Provides Chinese-language preproduction planning workflows for audiobook and multi-role audio projects, including project initialization, reading notes, source packages, episode planning, scripts, recording workbooks, recap handling, and trial-recording calibration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External audio producers and production teams use this skill to turn usable source text into structured pre-recording planning artifacts for audiobooks or multi-role performances. It helps organize episode plans, role/color responsibilities, source-text questions, recording scripts, and calibration records before recording begins.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Planning records may contain unconfirmed assumptions about source text, characters, timing, or production requirements.

Mitigation: Mark unknowns as pending confirmation and keep source questions in project records before using outputs for recording.

Risk: Generated scripts, episode plans, or role assignments can affect recording quality if accepted without production review.

Mitigation: Have the producer or recording lead review plans, role/color responsibilities, and trial-recording calibration before final recording.
