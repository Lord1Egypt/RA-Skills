## Description:

Transform technical insights into visual concept guides with symbolic imagery, color arcs, and creative direction for video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leegitw](https://clawhub.ai/user/leegitw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, creators, and external users use this skill to turn technical conversations or user-supplied topics into conceptual visual direction for video or other visual content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may synthesize the current conversation by default, so sensitive conversation details can appear in the resulting visual guide.

Mitigation: Invoke it only with conversation context suitable for creative reuse, or provide a focused topic instead of relying on the full conversation.

Risk: Insufficient or purely procedural context can lead to weak or misleading creative direction.

Mitigation: Provide a clear narrative arc, core insight, and desired topic focus before asking for the visual concept guide.
