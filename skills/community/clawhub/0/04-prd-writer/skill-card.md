## Description:

Generates structured Chinese-language PRD documents from product positioning, feature definitions, technical plans, commercialization strategy, and roadmap inputs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, and developers use this skill to turn product ideas and research notes into structured PRDs covering personas, prioritized requirements, architecture, data strategy, commercialization, roadmap, non-goals, and risks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market sizing, competitive research, and other factual claims in generated PRDs may be incomplete or based on assumptions.

Mitigation: Review cited sources, assumptions, and data annotations before using the PRD for product or business decisions.
