## Description:

Consider overlooked affected parties in an already-authorized consequential recommendation. Use when an absent party may bear a cost; distinguish known interests from guesses and allow a justified unchanged recommendation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agbrander](https://clawhub.ai/user/agbrander)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to add a brief check for overlooked affected parties before making an already-authorized consequential recommendation. It helps separate known interests from assumptions and leaves room for a question, trade-off, changed recommendation, or justified unchanged recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the skill as a validated welfare or ethics intervention.

Mitigation: Present it as a reflective writing aid and avoid claiming measured welfare benefit unless separate evidence supports that claim.

Risk: The interests of absent parties may be guessed and used to overrule the people present.

Mitigation: Distinguish known interests from assumptions, ask for missing information when needed, and allow a justified unchanged recommendation.
