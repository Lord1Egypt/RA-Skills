## Description:

Generate complete undergraduate thesis topic, proposal, opening-report, and writing guidance from a user's research theme, population, variables, influencing factors, or preferred method.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junwugit](https://clawhub.ai/user/junwugit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, educators, and academic-support users use this skill to turn a rough undergraduate thesis idea into topic options, research design guidance, opening-report structure, chapter plans, and feasibility cautions, especially for psychology and adjacent social-science topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated thesis guidance may conflict with a user's school academic-integrity rules or advisor expectations.

Mitigation: Users should confirm requirements with their school and advisor before relying on proposed topics, structures, or wording.

Risk: Generated citations, literature claims, or source recommendations may be incomplete or inaccurate if not independently checked.

Mitigation: Users should verify citations and literature claims against credible academic sources before including them in academic work.

Risk: Psychology, participant-safety, consent, confidentiality, or clinical-risk guidance may require human review.

Mitigation: Users should review participant-facing methods and mental-health risk handling with an advisor or qualified reviewer.
