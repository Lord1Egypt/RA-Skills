## Description:

Calculates estimated lawsuit filing fees and litigation costs for civil cases in China, including property and non-property disputes, while reminding users that outputs are fee estimates and not legal advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and legal-support practitioners use this skill to estimate PRC civil court filing fees, case acceptance fees, preservation fees, and common litigation cost scenarios before filing. It is intended for calculation assistance and should be checked against the filing court's current schedule.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Court fee schedules, local court practices, or applicable rules may vary or change.

Mitigation: Verify estimates against the filing court's current fee schedule before relying on them.

Risk: Users may mistake fee estimates for legal advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as calculation assistance and advise users to consult qualified legal counsel for legal strategy or filing decisions.
