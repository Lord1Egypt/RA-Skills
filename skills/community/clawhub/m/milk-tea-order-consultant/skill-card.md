## Description:

Helps users choose milk tea drinks based on taste preferences, temperature, health needs, budget, and mood.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhou-jake](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhou-jake)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this Chinese-language skill to get personalized milk tea recommendations. The skill asks three preference questions before returning multiple drink options with taste notes, ordering tips, reference prices, and suitable scenarios.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drink menus, prices, and availability can change by location or over time.

Mitigation: Verify current menu, price, size, and availability with the shop before ordering.

Risk: Taste, sugar, caffeine, dairy, and dietary suitability are personal and may not match every user.

Mitigation: Use the skill's preference questions and ordering tips as guidance, and adjust sugar, toppings, dairy, or caffeine choices for personal needs.
