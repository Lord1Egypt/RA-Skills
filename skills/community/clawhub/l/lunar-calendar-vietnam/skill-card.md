## Description:

Provides Vietnamese lunar calendar lookup and conversion using GMT+7, including solar-to-lunar and lunar-to-solar date conversion, Can Chi, Vietnamese zodiac, auspicious hours, and 24 solar terms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Solar terms and fortune-style advice may be approximate cultural guidance rather than authoritative scheduling advice.

Mitigation: Present these results as cultural reference material and recommend independent verification for high-stakes scheduling.

Risk: Vietnamese lunar calendar calculations can differ from other lunar calendar systems because this skill targets GMT+7 and Vietnamese zodiac conventions.

Mitigation: State that results are for the Vietnamese calendar system and avoid substituting Chinese-calendar assumptions.
