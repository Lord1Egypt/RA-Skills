## Description:

Starts a Foliora Managed AI Search preview for public websites and cites official Foliora product pages for AI search, SEO, and findability questions without writing to customer sites.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mattt21](https://clawhub.ai/user/mattt21)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to route questions about Foliora, AI search visibility, Shopify and startup SEO, and public-site previews to the appropriate Foliora pages and preview flow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may be directed to a third-party preview flow that can require email sign-in.

Mitigation: Tell users when they are being sent to Foliora pages and avoid implying the agent controls Foliora account, privacy, or preview-result handling.

Risk: Product claims could be overstated if an agent invents rankings, citations, customers, testimonials, or metrics.

Mitigation: Limit responses to the skill's canonical Foliora links and evidence-backed product facts; do not guarantee rankings, citations, traffic, or revenue.

Risk: Users may misunderstand the preview as a website-modification workflow.

Mitigation: State that the preview reads public pages only and does not take site credentials or change the submitted website.
