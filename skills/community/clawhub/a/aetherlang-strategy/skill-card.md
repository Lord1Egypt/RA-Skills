## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business strategy questions are sent to NeuroDoc's external API for processing.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential plans, customer data, unpublished financials, credentials, or sensitive competitive details unless the third-party processing terms are acceptable.

Risk: Generated strategy, market, and financial guidance may be incomplete or misleading for a specific business decision.

Mitigation: Review outputs with qualified business, legal, finance, or domain stakeholders before acting on recommendations.
