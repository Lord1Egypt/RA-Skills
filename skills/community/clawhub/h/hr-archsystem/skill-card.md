## Description:

HR ArchSystem helps HR and management teams design organization structures, job level systems, competency dictionaries, and role evaluation workflows, including updates for AI-era work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wuritu](https://clawhub.ai/user/wuritu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

HR leaders, HR business partners, and managers use this skill to build aligned organization architecture, job levels, competency standards, and evidence-based evaluation reports. It is intended for structured HR system design and review rather than automated employment decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used with sensitive employee, performance, feedback, and AI-use data without defined privacy or retention controls.

Mitigation: Limit use to authorized HR or management users and define data access, employee notice or consent, logging, redaction, retention, deletion, and review requirements before using real employee data.

Risk: Evaluation outputs could be mistaken for final employment decisions.

Mitigation: Require human HR review, evidence checks, calibration, and an appeal process before using any generated evaluation, promotion, compensation, or role-level recommendation.

Risk: Role evaluation can be inconsistent or unfair if competency standards are missing or evidence is weak.

Mitigation: Apply the artifact's stated safeguards: do not evaluate without a competency dictionary, do not score without behavioral evidence, and do not confirm results without calibration.
