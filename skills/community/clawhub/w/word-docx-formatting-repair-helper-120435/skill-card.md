## Description:

Helps knowledge workers automate Word/DOCX formatting cleanup, find-and-replace, style repair, and repeatable document editing workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Office workers, legal and operations teams, students, and documentation maintainers use this skill to turn Word/DOCX formatting, style cleanup, and find-and-replace requests into practical workflows, checklists, scripts, templates, or guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad automatic activation may invoke the skill for loosely related Word, document, or productivity prompts.

Mitigation: Narrow invocation to explicit Word/DOCX repair, formatting cleanup, find-and-replace, style cleanup, or document automation requests when many skills are installed.

Risk: Formatting or automation guidance can change document content or styles incorrectly when constraints are incomplete.

Mitigation: Restate the user's outcome, constraints, available inputs, and success criteria before producing scripts, templates, or cleanup steps, then validate the result against those criteria.
