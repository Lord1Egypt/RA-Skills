## Description:

A comprehensive AI agent skill for anyone working with AI models. Helps you choose the right model for any task, write effective prompts, evaluate model outputs critically, understand capabilities and limitations, compare models across providers, and build workflows that get consistently useful results from AI without the trial and error.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad scope and may be invoked for general model-selection or AI-workflow advice when narrower guidance is needed.

Mitigation: Narrow the skill description or invocation criteria when deploying it for a specific model-selection workflow.

Risk: Model capabilities, pricing, and provider comparisons can change quickly, so guidance may become stale.

Mitigation: Verify current model capabilities, pricing, and provider claims against authoritative sources before using recommendations for consequential decisions.

Risk: Advice about evaluating AI outputs may still miss factual errors or overconfident model responses.

Mitigation: Use independent review and external verification for high-impact factual, legal, medical, financial, or security-sensitive outputs.
