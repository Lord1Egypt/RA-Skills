Generates production-grade website page copy and reusable page templates with SEO, GEO, schema, FAQ, internal-link, crawl/index, and launch QA guidance.

## Use Case:

External marketers, content teams, SEO leads, product managers, designers, and frontend teams use this skill to turn a page idea, product position, or content brief into a complete website page deliverable. It is especially suited for Chinese-first homepage, landing page, feature page, category page, collection page, blog, documentation, FAQ, comparison, pricing, and local/service page drafts that need search and AI-answer discoverability constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated product, competitor, policy, pricing, rating, or other time-sensitive claims may be inaccurate if published without verification.

Mitigation: Review generated claims before publishing and use current web research with citations for time-sensitive facts.

Risk: Structured data can mislead search systems if it includes ratings, prices, offers, authors, reviews, or dates that are not present in visible page content.

Mitigation: Keep JSON-LD aligned with visible content and omit unsupported schema fields.

Risk: SEO/GEO drafts can drift into misleading optimization tactics if users ask for ranking guarantees, hidden text, keyword stuffing, fake reviews, or similar search-spam patterns.

Mitigation: Reject deceptive tactics and replace them with clear page structure, visible text, real FAQs, accurate metadata, and compliant crawl/index guidance.
