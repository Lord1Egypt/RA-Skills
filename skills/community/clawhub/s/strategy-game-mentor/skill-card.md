## Description:

Reframes messy real-life situations as strategy game boards so users can see victory conditions, resource lines, phases, tradeoffs, and next moves.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to reframe planning, study strategy, project sequencing, family logistics, or life-review situations into a clear board state with goals, constraints, resources, phases, and decision tradeoffs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-treat coaching-style reframing as legal, medical, financial, or other high-risk professional advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as planning guidance and defer high-risk professional decisions to qualified experts.

Risk: The skill can imply more certainty than the situation supports when user inputs are incomplete or uncertain.

Mitigation: Name uncertainty explicitly, offer scenarios for high-uncertainty cases, and avoid formal forecasting claims.
