## Description:

Generates cash flow projections and scenario analysis for small businesses and freelancers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Small business owners, freelancers, and their supporting agents use this skill to structure cash-flow forecasts, scenario analyses, templates, recommendations, and next-step checklists from user-provided financial context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat heuristic cash-flow recommendations as authoritative financial advice.

Mitigation: Present results as informational planning support and recommend professional review for material decisions.

Risk: Broad finance keywords may activate the skill outside an intentional cash-flow forecasting context.

Mitigation: Invoke it intentionally for cash-flow forecasting, scenario planning, and related checklist generation.
