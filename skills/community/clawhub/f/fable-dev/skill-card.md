## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The guidance is specific to the Fable codebase and may be inappropriate for unrelated projects.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when working on the Fable app and review generated changes against the local codebase.

Risk: Incorrectly applying mutation, IPC, storage, or i18n guidance could introduce regressions in Fable project behavior.

Mitigation: Run the documented checks and relevant E2E tests before committing changes.
