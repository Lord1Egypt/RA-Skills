## Description:

Cluster keywords by intent and map them to existing or proposed pages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[roamseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/roamseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO practitioners use this skill to group keywords by search intent and map each group to a relevant existing page or a proposed new page. It produces a structured report with page briefs and next steps grounded in the project's goals and available keyword and URL data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cluster and page recommendations may not fit the site's goals when keyword, URL, or project context is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the mapping against current business goals and available search results before changing pages or applying keyword tags.
