## Description:

Helps founders and builders turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototype scopes, reusable checklists, and user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to convert product ideas into validation workflows, prototype plans, positioning, checklists, and decision support before committing major effort.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad startup, SaaS, prototype, and validation triggers may route general product-planning requests to this skill when a narrower skill would be more appropriate.

Mitigation: Review installed trigger policy and narrow invocation keywords when precise routing matters.

Risk: Product validation outputs may be mistaken for automatic business advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance and review assumptions, constraints, and success criteria before acting.
