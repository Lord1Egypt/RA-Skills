## Description:

Helps agents create community strategies, platform recommendations, engagement playbooks, ambassador programs, and health audits for online communities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External builders, community managers, founders, and marketing teams use this skill to design, launch, grow, and audit online communities that support product growth, retention, feedback, support deflection, and advocacy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Advice may overgeneralize across community platforms, stages, or audiences if the user supplies limited context.

Mitigation: Ask for the product, platform, community stage, goal, ideal member, seed audience, and management capacity; flag assumptions when details are missing.

Risk: Ambiguous trigger phrases can activate the skill during broad business or marketing discussions.

Mitigation: Narrow activation to explicit community-building requests or confirm intent before applying the playbooks.

Risk: Existing product-marketing context may be outdated or mismatched to the community plan.

Mitigation: Use product-marketing context for relevance, but ask the user to confirm key audience, positioning, and business-goal assumptions before final recommendations.
