## Description:

Build a privacy-conscious daycare waitlist status board with provider contacts, licensing checks, policy questions, availability signals, follow-up dates, and decision notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize daycare, preschool, nursery, or early-childhood waitlist logistics into privacy-conscious comparison boards, follow-up messages, and decision checklists. It supports administrative tracking only and directs users to verify licensing, policies, fees, enrollment terms, and availability with official provider or regulator sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The board could expose unnecessary child or family-sensitive details if users include them in shared artifacts.

Mitigation: Keep shared versions limited to initials, age range, classroom category, and operational waitlist details; avoid full child names, medical records, custody documents, home addresses, payment details, and identity documents.

Risk: Users could mistake administrative organization for childcare, safety, legal, medical, licensing, or parenting advice.

Mitigation: Use the board as a tracking aid only and verify licensing status, inspections, policies, ratios, fees, deadlines, and availability directly with providers or official regulators.
