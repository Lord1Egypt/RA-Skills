## Description:

E-commerce operations workflow for high-repeat small goods stores such as cosmetics, phone cases, accessories, small jewelry, and daily FMCG, producing executable playbooks and table templates for store operations, repeat purchase, acquisition, membership, campaigns, assortment, content conversion, customer-service SOPs, reviews, post-purchase support, ad ROI, GMV, CVR, AOV, and repeat-rate work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External e-commerce operators and growth teams use this skill to convert store goals and operating data into weekly action plans, campaign briefs, conversion checklists, repeat-purchase cadences, customer-service SOPs, and review templates for high-repeat small-goods businesses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad store-operations topics outside narrow repeat-purchase small-goods workflows.

Mitigation: Use it when the requested work fits high-repeat small-goods e-commerce operations, and ask for scope clarification when the store category or purchase cycle is unclear.

Risk: Some referenced helper files are absent from the artifact, so template-generation shell commands may not work as written.

Mitigation: Treat the commands as optional convenience examples and rely on the Markdown templates and guidance in the skill text when helper files are unavailable.

Risk: The skill advises on commercial offers, claims, reviews, customer service, and post-purchase incentives where misleading claims or unclear coupon rules can create business or compliance risk.

Mitigation: Review generated offers, claims, materials or ingredient statements, asset usage, after-sales terms, and coupon rules before publishing or sending them to customers.
