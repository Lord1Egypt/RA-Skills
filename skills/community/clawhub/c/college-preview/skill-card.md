## Description:

College Exam Sprint is a Chinese-language study-planning skill for university exam revision that organizes course intake, a 20-day plan, daily tasks, quizzes, progress feedback, and local adjustments across courses such as computer networking, data structures, and advanced mathematics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yedda1117](https://clawhub.ai/user/yedda1117)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and study-support agents use this skill to structure a university exam sprint from course information, materials, available study time, weak areas, daily progress, and quiz results. It helps produce revision plans, task lists, explanations, practice questions, progress records, and short-term adjustment guidance while keeping final academic work and decisions with the student.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private course files, notes, or exam materials may be organized in the disclosed study workspace.

Mitigation: Keep private course files inside the disclosed study workspace and avoid adding unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Risk: Generated plans, quizzes, explanations, or progress records may be mistaken for authoritative academic requirements or final answers.

Mitigation: Review generated plans, records, and quizzes yourself and follow the applicable course and academic integrity rules.

Risk: A user may ask for homework, lab reports, exam answers, fabricated study records, or unsupported claims about instructor-designated重点.

Mitigation: Refuse requests that replace the student's own academic work or fabricate records, and redirect to explanations, hints, practice, and evidence-based revision guidance.
