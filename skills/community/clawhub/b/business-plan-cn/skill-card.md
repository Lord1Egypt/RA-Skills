## Description:

Generates Chinese business-plan materials including full business plans, lean canvases, SWOT analyses, financial projections, elevator pitches, and market-analysis templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Entrepreneurs, founders, and business operators use this skill to draft Chinese-language business-plan sections, financial projections, lean-canvas templates, SWOT analyses, market-analysis frameworks, and short pitches.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Server security evidence reports a confirmed command-execution vulnerability.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installing, use it only with trusted inputs, and avoid letting untrusted users or generated text populate numeric financial fields until input validation is added.

Risk: Server security evidence reports undisclosed local logging of user input.

Mitigation: Avoid passing sensitive business ideas, drafts, financial assumptions, or confidential planning details to the bundled content script unless logging is made explicit and controllable.
