## Description:

Audit, track, and optimize an organization's software licenses by identifying waste, surfacing compliance risks, and organizing renewal planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers, operations teams, finance teams, and software asset managers use this skill to audit license inventories, find unused or duplicate subscriptions, assess compliance exposure, plan renewals, and prepare vendor negotiation briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: License, vendor, usage, or dependency inputs may contain sensitive commercial or compliance data.

Mitigation: Provide sensitive data deliberately and limit inputs to information needed for the license or spend review.

Risk: Compliance and savings conclusions can affect renewals, cancellations, or legal exposure.

Mitigation: Verify recommendations with qualified internal stakeholders before acting on renewal, cancellation, procurement, or compliance decisions.
