## Description:

Turn a static business website into a living site with an animated video hero, in plain words, with any AI agent. Your AI agent drafts the design system, the brand image brief, and the 5 to 6 second loop plan, then checks the hero with evidence you paste back, and gives a fix draft for owner review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Small business owners and people building websites with an AI agent can use this workflow to plan one animated video hero, then check the design, image, loop, performance details, and handoff evidence before an owner approves changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A proposed design or fix draft may not match the business or evidence provided.

Mitigation: Treat drafts as proposals, check each handoff item against quoted user-provided evidence, and get owner approval before changing or deploying a site.
