## Description:

Guides ecommerce creators through avatar-led product videos with portrait recipes and a script, voice, avatar, B-roll, and assembly workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Ecommerce creators and marketers use this skill to plan and produce avatar-led product videos for channels such as TikTok Shop, Douyin, Amazon, Shopee, and brand stores.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Selected local images and other media may be sent to dLazy and its model providers; the security scan notes repeated gaps in warnings about this external processing.

Mitigation: Use only media you are authorized to share, and review the services’ retention terms before submitting it.

Risk: The scan was partial because some local references could not be resolved, so the security evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved references and their behavior before relying on the scan assessment.
