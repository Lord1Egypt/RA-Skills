## Description:

Transform a dated business website in 7 steps, in plain words, with any AI agent. Your AI agent audits the site, studies competitors, drafts the brand and wireframes, then gives a redesign plan with a before and after audit sheet, for owner review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Small-business owners and website builders use this workflow with an AI agent to organize a site audit, competitor notes, redesign drafts, and a before-and-after checklist for owner review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audit findings or competitor claims could be misleading if they are not supported by supplied evidence.

Mitigation: Require pasted evidence for each Checked or Missing status, quote it, and mark unsupported items Not confirmed.

Risk: A redesign plan could be mistaken for an approved live-site change.

Mitigation: Keep the plan labeled as a draft for owner review and require separate owner approval before changes or deployment.
