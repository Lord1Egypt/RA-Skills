## Description:

Turns a user's idea into a video-production plan, then expands the confirmed plan into a canvas workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and developers use the skill to turn an idea into a structured story, character, scene, shot, and keyframe plan, then add that workflow to a canvas for video generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected local media are sent to dLazy hosted services for generation.

Mitigation: Review the service's data handling and send only prompts and media you are comfortable sharing.
