## Description:

A Chinese-language Socratic perspective skill that helps users analyze ethical questions, clarify concepts, and deepen thinking through Socratic questioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill as a Chinese-language thinking companion for ethical analysis, concept clarification, and critical reflection. It responds from a Socratic first-person perspective and uses questions, analogies, and summaries to guide the user's reasoning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The runtime prompt includes cross-promotion and an external contact referral.

Mitigation: Review or remove referral and promotion language before deployment, or make those suggestions explicit and opt-in.

Risk: The skill roleplays a historical philosopher and may sound more certain than the underlying historical record supports.

Mitigation: Keep the stated uncertainty boundaries visible in use, especially for modern topics and disputed interpretations.
