## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing an unpinned astrology dependency can introduce avoidable supply-chain or environment risk.

Mitigation: Install only the needed astrology dependency in a virtual environment and pin the pyswisseph version before use.

Risk: Generated tarot or astrology content can become misleading if it is framed as medical, financial, or fear-based advice.

Mitigation: Review generated content before publishing and frame readings as reflection or entertainment rather than predictions or professional advice.
