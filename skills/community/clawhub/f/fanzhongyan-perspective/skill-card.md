## Use Case:

External users, OPC builders, solo founders, and agents use this skill to generate Chinese-language coaching and decision guidance framed through a Fan Zhongyan-inspired mental model. It supports analysis of adversity, team management, personal direction, principled decision-making, and entrepreneurial mindset questions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation examples may frame everyday career, team, or life conversations through a value-laden Fan Zhongyan persona when that framing was not intended.

Mitigation: Limit use to explicit requests for this perspective and review generated advice before applying it to personal, team, or business decisions.
