## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validation plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision-support artifacts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before major investment. It produces practical validation workflows, checklists, lightweight analyses, prototypes, positioning support, and next-step guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording and implicit invocation may make the skill appear for general startup, prototype, or SaaS requests.

Mitigation: Invoke a more specific skill explicitly when the task needs narrower expertise or a different workflow.

Risk: Product validation advice can be misleading when user goals, constraints, evidence quality, or success criteria are incomplete.

Mitigation: Ask only for missing information that materially changes the output, make assumptions visible, and validate the result against the stated success criteria.
