## Description:

Generates system acceptance reports by consolidating test case execution results, functional acceptance conclusions, and performance and security assessments into structured delivery documentation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA, delivery, and engineering teams use this skill to turn acceptance evidence into a complete report with test overview, result summaries, module details, defects, performance findings, security findings, and a final acceptance recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may include secrets, internal hostnames, exploit details, sensitive architecture information, or vulnerability specifics that are not appropriate for the audience.

Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive content before sharing reports, and tailor technical detail to the intended audience.

Risk: The acceptance conclusion is a generated recommendation and may be treated as final without accountable review.

Mitigation: Have the responsible owner review the evidence and confirm the final acceptance decision before delivery.
