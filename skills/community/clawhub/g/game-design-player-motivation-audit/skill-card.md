## Description:

Audits game features, live-ops systems, progression loops, social features, and monetization surfaces using a Self-Determination Theory-inspired motivation framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and live-ops teams use this skill to evaluate what kind of motivation a design creates, diagnose hollow or exhausting engagement patterns, and rebalance systems toward healthier short-, medium-, or long-term player motivation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The audit can be mistaken for empirical player research even though it is an opinionated design-analysis framework.

Mitigation: Validate major product decisions with playtesting, analytics, user research, or other evidence before production use.
