## Description:

Helps ecommerce operators compare platform promotion programs, assess directional eligibility, and produce markdown enrollment briefs without live seller portal or API access.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers, brands, and marketplace operators use this skill to compare promotion types, plan enrollment timelines, prepare checklist items, and understand common rejection reasons across platforms such as Amazon, TikTok Shop, JD, Taobao, Shopify, and Xiaohongshu.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Promotion deadlines, eligibility rules, fees, terminology, and approval criteria can vary by platform, account, category, and event window.

Mitigation: Verify the generated checklist, timeline, fees, and eligibility assumptions against the relevant platform before acting.

Risk: Directional eligibility guidance could be mistaken for guaranteed enrollment or deal placement.

Mitigation: Treat the brief as an operator planning aid and make final decisions only after platform review or official seller-center confirmation.
