## Description:

Build a translation/terminology glossary so a product's key terms render consistently everywhere.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Localization teams, product writers, and agents use this skill to create termbase-style glossaries for products or domains so translators can apply approved terms consistently across target locales.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Machine-generated or draft translations could be mistaken for approved terminology.

Mitigation: Keep uncertain translations marked as draft and require native-speaker or localization reviewer approval before importing the glossary into production workflows.

Risk: Product UI text, documentation, brand names, or terminology supplied to the skill may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Review input material for confidentiality requirements before sharing it with an agent or downstream localization process.
