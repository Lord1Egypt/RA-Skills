## Description:

Evaluates overseas distributors, agencies, resellers, and ecosystem partners with structured scorecards, due-diligence checklists, commercial model comparisons, pilot plans, and exit guardrails.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, business development teams, channel managers, and international expansion leaders use this skill to evaluate local go-to-market partners for overseas market entry. It produces planning frameworks, weighted assessments, validation tasks, and decision guardrails rather than professional legal, financial, tax, anti-corruption, or compliance due diligence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat partnership assessments as legal, financial, tax, anti-corruption, or compliance due diligence.

Mitigation: State that outputs are planning frameworks and require qualified professional review before agreements, exclusivity, sensitive disclosures, or market commitments.

Risk: Outputs may rely on incomplete, stale, or locally invalid assumptions about partners, market conditions, or regulations.

Mitigation: Require users to validate local laws, platform policies, consumer expectations, market facts, references, and pilot results with reliable sources and local experts.

Risk: Users may include sensitive partner, customer, commercial, or financial information in prompts.

Mitigation: Encourage minimization or redaction of sensitive information before sharing it with any AI tool.
