## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Static travel, relocation, transport, healthcare, visa, safety, and business setup details may become outdated.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive claims against current official or local sources before relying on the advice for bookings, legal status, healthcare access, transport fares, personal safety, or company setup.

Risk: Neighborhood, lodging, restaurant, and cost recommendations may not fit a user's budget, accessibility needs, risk tolerance, or travel dates.

Mitigation: Use the skill's guidance as a starting point and confirm final decisions with current local conditions, official venue information, and user-specific constraints.
