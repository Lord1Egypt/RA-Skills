## Use Case:

External users and writers use this skill to plan fiction narratives, including characters, relationship maps, three-act plot structure, alternate endings, and writing suggestions. It is especially oriented toward Chinese-language novel genres and customizable story inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated fiction plans may contain weak logic, derivative ideas, or language that does not match the user's intended genre or audience.

Mitigation: Review and revise generated characters, plot structure, endings, and writing suggestions before using them in a manuscript or publication workflow.

Risk: The artifact is Chinese-oriented, and users expecting strict activation boundaries or non-Chinese output may receive less suitable guidance.

Mitigation: State the desired language, genre, tone, story length, and output constraints explicitly when invoking the skill.
