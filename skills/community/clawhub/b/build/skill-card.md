## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill frames itself as an autonomous builder across code, infrastructure, and organizations without clear approval boundaries.

Mitigation: Require explicit user scope, approval checkpoints, and review before allowing agents to make impactful code, infrastructure, or organizational changes.

Risk: Security evidence marks the release as suspicious because broad instructions could be treated as permission for impactful changes.

Mitigation: Review the skill carefully before installation and apply local policy limits for execution, deployment, and privileged operations.
