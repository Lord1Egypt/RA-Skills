## Description:

Standardized grading and feedback prompt for elementary school English written homework in grades 3 through 6.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, parents, and teachers use this skill to review elementary English homework, assign scores, correct mistakes, and provide age-appropriate feedback in Chinese with English examples.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill for general English editing that is not children's homework.

Mitigation: Narrow the trigger wording during installation or use the skill only when the request is clearly for grades 3-6 English homework.

Risk: Homework text may contain student-provided personal information.

Mitigation: Review and minimize submitted homework text before sharing it with an agent or model.
