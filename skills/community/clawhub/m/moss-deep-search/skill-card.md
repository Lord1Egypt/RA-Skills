This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill when they need an agent to plan multi-round web searches, synthesize multiple sources, and produce a structured research report with sources and recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad trigger wording may route lightweight lookup requests into a more expansive multi-round research workflow.

Mitigation: Use the skill when comprehensive research is intended, and narrow the request when a quick lookup is sufficient.

Risk: Multi-source synthesis can include outdated, irrelevant, or conflicting source material.

Mitigation: Review cited sources and treat recommendations as analysis that may require human verification before use.
