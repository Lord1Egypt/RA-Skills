## Description:

Systematically analyzes scientific papers by mapping figures to discussions, identifying logical flow, and tracking evidence sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[larry-of-cosmotim](https://clawhub.ai/user/larry-of-cosmotim)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, reviewers, and technical readers use this skill to deconstruct a scientific paper's argument by inventorying figures, mapping each figure to its discussion, tracing the logic chain, and assessing evidence support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential or unpublished manuscripts may be included in the analysis workflow.

Mitigation: Handle manuscripts according to the user's confidentiality requirements and avoid sharing sensitive paper content with systems that are not approved for that data.

Risk: The analysis may misstate a paper's logic if figures, discussions, methods, or citations are not checked against the source paper.

Mitigation: Verify claims against the paper text, methods, figures, and citations, and explicitly flag weak support, missing evidence, contradictions, or speculation.
