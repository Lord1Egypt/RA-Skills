## Description:

Create a sort list and prep checklist for safely bundling small electronics for recycling, with reminders to remove personal data without handling credentials or promising data recovery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize small electronics for recycling, donation, mail-back, or local e-waste drop-off. It produces a practical prep card that separates ready items from data-bearing, battery-sensitive, and special-handling items.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake recycling prep reminders for secure erasure, certified destruction, data recovery, account removal, or recycler acceptance.

Mitigation: State that users should follow manufacturer wipe steps and recycler rules, and avoid making guarantees about data removal, recovery, or acceptance.

Risk: Sensitive personal, work, school, legal, medical, financial, or identity data may remain on devices.

Mitigation: Tell users to use official instructions and organization-approved or qualified professional processes for sensitive devices without requesting credentials or private files.

Risk: Damaged, swollen, leaking, hot, or smoking batteries may require special handling.

Mitigation: Tell users to stop handling problem batteries and follow local hazardous waste guidance.
