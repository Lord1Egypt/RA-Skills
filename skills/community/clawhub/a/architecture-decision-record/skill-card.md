## Description:

Create an Architecture Decision Record (ADR) for technical decisions, producing structured documentation with context, options, decision, consequences, risks, and tradeoffs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and engineering teams use this skill to document architectural and technical decisions in ADR format, including the decision context, considered options, tradeoffs, consequences, and revisit triggers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on some general decision-writeup requests.

Mitigation: Use it when the user intends to produce architecture or technical decision documentation.

Risk: ADR quality depends on accurate user-provided context, options, rationale, and consequences.

Mitigation: Review the generated ADR for factual accuracy, honest tradeoffs, rejected alternatives, and concrete revisit triggers before relying on it.
