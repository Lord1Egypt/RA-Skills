## Description:

Discover, register, and analyze a brand's competitors on PostKing: probe rival domains, classify candidates, and pull comparison intelligence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, brand operators, and agents use this skill to discover rival domains, register and analyze tracked competitors, and retrieve head-to-head comparison or landscape intelligence for a PostKing brand.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can manage PostKing competitor records, including a documented destructive delete command.

Mitigation: Verify the competitor ID and obtain explicit user confirmation before any delete operation.

Risk: Adding or analyzing competitors can change the brand's tracked competitive landscape.

Mitigation: Review classified candidates with the user before registration and check competitor status after async analysis completes.
