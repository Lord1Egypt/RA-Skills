## Description:

Write effective prompts for Runware's image and video models with model-appropriate phrasing, in-image text handling, negation, and cinematic grammar.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and agents composing Runware image or video generation prompts use this skill to adapt wording to the selected model family and handle text, negation, camera motion, and shot structure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Mitigation: Review those related skills independently before installing or using them together.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected model's current strengths and schema before relying on model-specific prompt phrasing.
