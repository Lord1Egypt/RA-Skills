## Description:

通过自我描述帮助匹配最相似的历史人物。用户提供关于自身性格、成就、兴趣或生活态度的描述，该技能会分析并推荐最贴合的历史人物，并解释匹配理由。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for entertainment and self-reflection by comparing their personality, interests, achievements, and values with similar historical figures. The skill returns matched figures with scores, reasons, and optional learning references.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share personal self-descriptions that include unnecessary sensitive details.

Mitigation: Ask only for the minimum personality, interest, achievement, and value details needed for the match, and avoid requesting sensitive personal information.

Risk: Historical-person matching may be mistaken for a serious psychological or historical assessment.

Mitigation: Frame matches as entertainment and self-reflection, and present reasons as similarity signals rather than authoritative judgments.
