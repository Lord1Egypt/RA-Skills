## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be misused for bulk collection or attempts to bypass SegmentFault protections.

Mitigation: Review SegmentFault terms and rate limits before use, keep collection lightweight, and do not use the skill for bulk scraping or protection bypass.

Risk: Dynamic pages and human checks can make automated extraction incomplete or unreliable.

Mitigation: Open pages manually when needed and review extracted summaries, metrics, and source links before relying on the output.
