## Description:

Generates Chinese business data visualization reports with chart recommendations, structured analysis, key insights, and action recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business analysts, operators, marketers, finance teams, and developers use this skill to turn tabular business data into concise Chinese reports with appropriate chart choices, trend analysis, anomaly notes, and next-step recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business datasets may contain personal, regulated, financial, or confidential information.

Mitigation: Remove or mask sensitive fields before sharing data with the agent session unless that disclosure is acceptable for the workflow.

Risk: Incorrect or incomplete source data can produce misleading charts, insights, or recommendations.

Mitigation: Validate source data quality and key assumptions before using the generated report for decisions.
