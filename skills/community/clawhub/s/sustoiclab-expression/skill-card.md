## Description:

This Chinese-language expression framework toolbox helps users organize speeches, articles, reports, proposals, and ideas with KongYuSan, 3C persuasion, pyramid, and timeline frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[susstoiclab](https://clawhub.ai/user/susstoiclab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to clarify motivation, understand an audience, select a suitable communication framework, and produce a concise content skeleton for speaking or writing tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked for broad writing or communication requests where the selected framework does not fit the task.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's communication goal and review the chosen framework before drafting the final structure.
