## Description:

Provides Chinese-language WhatsApp private-domain operations planning, including account health diagnosis, user segmentation, compliant message strategy, and operating checklists under a Meta compliance framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

WhatsApp private-domain operators, cross-border commerce teams, outbound marketing teams, and SCRM practitioners use this skill to plan account-health reviews, user lifecycle segmentation, compliant messaging, and operational improvement plans. Its recommendations are advisory and intended for human review before use with real customers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated plans or templates may become noncompliant if Meta/WhatsApp rules, privacy requirements, or anti-spam laws change or vary by region.

Mitigation: Verify current Meta/WhatsApp policies and applicable privacy or anti-spam laws before using recommendations with real customers.

Risk: Marketing guidance could be applied without adequate user consent, unsubscribe handling, or human approval.

Mitigation: Require human review, documented opt-in, clear opt-out handling, and compliance checks before executing any customer-facing plan.
