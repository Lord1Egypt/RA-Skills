## Description:

Audit mathematical proofs or proof-like arguments for Korpi's 63 fake proof methods and explain exact proof defects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, reviewers, educators, and mathematically technical users use this skill to inspect proofs or proof-like arguments, identify defects against Korpi's numbered fake proof taxonomy, and describe the minimum repair needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat analytical labels and repair suggestions as authoritative mathematical validation.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a review aid and have important proofs checked by a qualified human expert.

Risk: The Korpi taxonomy may be forced onto ordinary proof errors where no special fake-proof method fits.

Mitigation: Report ordinary proof defects separately and avoid assigning a Korpi label unless the evidence supports it.
