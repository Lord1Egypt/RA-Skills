## Description:

Reviews and improves homepage copy, positioning, and messaging for startups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[c0ldsmi1e](https://clawhub.ai/user/c0ldsmi1e)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, startup teams, and marketers use this skill to turn discovery answers or existing homepage copy into clearer positioning, landing page structure, headline options, copy improvements, messaging guidance, and prioritized next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share business-sensitive positioning, competitor, testimonial, pricing, or launch details while requesting copy feedback.

Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential information that should not be processed in chat, and redact sensitive business details before using the skill.

Risk: Generated landing-page recommendations may be inaccurate, overstated, or misaligned with actual customer evidence.

Mitigation: Review proposed claims, testimonials, pricing language, and differentiators against verified product and customer data before publication.
