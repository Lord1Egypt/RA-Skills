## Description:

Generates a comprehensive privacy policy in Markdown covering GDPR, CCPA, and APPI based on a user's business details and data practices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[noahcraft-open](https://clawhub.ai/user/noahcraft-open)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Website and application operators use this skill to draft privacy policies that document data collection, cookies, third-party services, user rights, and region-specific privacy obligations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated privacy policies may not fully match the user's jurisdiction, actual data practices, or legal obligations.

Mitigation: Treat generated policies as drafts and review them for the applicable jurisdiction and business practices before publishing.
