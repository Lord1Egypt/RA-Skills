## Description:

Evaluate supplied technology packages or scoped technology portfolios, identify and rank evidence-backed potential licensees, acquirers, commercialization partners, or joint-development partners, assess transfer readiness and risks, and generate auditable HTML decision-support reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Technology-transfer teams, research commercialization staff, and IP commercialization analysts use this skill to structure evidence, score potential recipients, assess readiness and transfer risks, and generate traceable decision-support reports for licensing, acquisition, or joint-development prioritization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential technology-transfer materials, unpublished IP details, or private search sources may be mishandled if authorization is unclear.

Mitigation: Confirm confidential-material handling and authorized external sources before use, and do not upload or search private systems without approval.

Risk: Report files may be saved to an unintended or shared location, or an existing report may be replaced unintentionally.

Mitigation: Use an exact private output path approved by the user, avoid shared writable folders, and enable overwrite only when replacement is intended.

Risk: Recipient rankings, valuation context, legal/IP conclusions, or commercialization recommendations may mislead readers when evidence coverage or qualified review is insufficient.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support, verify evidence IDs and coverage, preserve missing-data limits, and route legal, FTO, valuation, and transaction decisions to qualified reviewers.
