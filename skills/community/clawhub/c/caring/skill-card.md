## Description:

Caring applies a structured quality review to drafts, plans, code, reports, analyses, replies, and other deliverables before they ship.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[minerva-care](https://clawhub.ai/user/minerva-care)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external contributors, and developers use this skill to review work products before release, with attention to whether the work solves the real problem, is complete, and reaches a clear standard of quality.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad activation language and may be selected for generic review or checking requests.

Mitigation: Use a more specific skill or explicit instruction when a domain-specific review standard is required.

Risk: The skill produces opinionated qualitative guidance that may not fully reflect domain-specific acceptance criteria.

Mitigation: Compare the review against the relevant project, legal, safety, or technical requirements before treating it as final approval.
