## Description:

Automate accounts receivable workflows including aging reports, collection prioritization, payment reminders, cash application, DSO tracking, and bad debt forecasting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Finance teams and business operators use this skill to analyze receivables, prioritize collection work, draft payment follow-ups, match payments, monitor DSO, and estimate bad debt exposure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Collection emails or final notices may contain inaccurate, inappropriate, or unapproved content if sent without review.

Mitigation: Review and approve all payment reminders, final notices, and collection communications before sending them.

Risk: Cash-application recommendations, credits, or write-off decisions can affect financial records if applied without business review.

Mitigation: Confirm recommendations against source invoices, payments, customer records, and accounting policy before applying them in business systems.
