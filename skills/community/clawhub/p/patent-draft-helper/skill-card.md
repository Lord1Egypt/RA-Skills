## Description:

专利文案撰写助手 helps draft Chinese patent disclosure materials, claims, specifications, and novelty or creativity self-assessments for motor and mechanical inventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this Chinese-language skill to structure motor and mechanical patent drafts, including technical disclosure forms, claim sets, specification sections, and preliminary novelty or creativity checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential invention details could be exposed if entered into an AI environment that is not approved for that information.

Mitigation: Use only approved environments for confidential inventions, and redact sensitive technical or ownership details when approval is not clear.

Risk: Generated patent text or novelty and creativity assessments may be incomplete, jurisdiction-specific, or legally incorrect.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting assistance and have a qualified patent professional review claims, disclosures, and filing strategy before use.
