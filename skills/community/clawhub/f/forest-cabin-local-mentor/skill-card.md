## Description:

Locate and activate relationships with experienced local humans to gain practical knowledge, safety advice, regulatory guidance, and occasional hands-on support when building your own cottage from self-cut forest timber.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users building or planning a self-cut timber cabin use this skill to identify local mentors, ask practical site-specific questions, and build a support network for safety, permitting, construction, and long-term off-grid living.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks the agent to maintain notes about local people and conversations, which can expose private contact details or consent-sensitive information.

Mitigation: Keep notes local and anonymized where possible, avoid storing names, private contact details, photos, or conversation logs without clear consent, and delete records when no longer needed.

Risk: Cabin-building planning can involve hazardous tree felling, construction decisions, and local permitting requirements.

Mitigation: Treat mentor advice as input, verify safety-critical steps with qualified professionals and local authorities, and do not cut timber or build until local legal requirements are confirmed.
