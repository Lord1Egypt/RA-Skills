## Description:

Offers, compensation framing, and negotiation planning. Use when evaluating offers or raises.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and external users use this skill to structure offer, raise, pay, and negotiation planning. It helps clarify goals, compare compensation components, plan negotiation scripts and BATNA, and prepare written follow-ups.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Compensation-adjacent planning may be mistaken for legal or tax advice.

Mitigation: Keep guidance framed as planning support, preserve the skill's not-legal-or-tax-advice boundary, and consult qualified advisors for legal, tax, or jurisdiction-specific questions.

Risk: Negotiation guidance can create relationship risk, miscommunication, or unrealistic asks if used without context.

Mitigation: Clarify goals, constraints, BATNA, trade-offs, and follow-up wording before acting on recommendations.
