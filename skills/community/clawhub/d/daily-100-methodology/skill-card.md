## Use Case:

External creators, founders, marketing teams, and operator-led businesses use this skill to turn brief business inputs into a reusable short-video content operating plan. It produces positioning guidance, narrative strategy, publishing cadence, risk reminders, topic ideas, and reusable script templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill defaults to saving personalized business or content plans under a user-controlled filename.

Mitigation: Use it only in workspaces where plan persistence is acceptable, require confirmation before saving files, and sanitize or replace user names in filenames.

Risk: Prompts and generated plans may include confidential strategy, product, customer, or staffing details.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive details unless retention is intentional, and redact confidential identifiers from saved plans and filenames.

Risk: The skill can produce high-frequency and controversy-led publishing strategies that may create platform, brand, or factual-accuracy risk.

Mitigation: Review generated topics, scripts, claims, and publishing cadence against platform rules, brand standards, and available evidence before publication.
