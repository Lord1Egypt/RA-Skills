## Description:

Designs, prompts, critiques, and integrates publication-ready research-paper inspiration figures that explain why a paper is needed through motivating cases, failures, contrasts, scenarios, or reviewer-facing limitation examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[c-narcissus](https://clawhub.ai/user/c-narcissus)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

Researchers, paper authors, and technical communicators use this skill to turn a paper's motivation into a credible visual narrative for introductions, talks, rebuttals, and figure handoff text. It guides material intake, figure subtype selection, candidate comparison, image-generation prompting, critique, captioning, and integration text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paper content, unpublished claims, or reference images may be sent to the selected image-generation provider.

Mitigation: Use only material the user is allowed to share with that provider, and avoid proprietary or sensitive content unless sharing has been approved.

Risk: Generated figures can introduce unsupported cases, data, claims, logos, or visual details if prompts are not tightly grounded.

Mitigation: Anchor prompts, captions, and legends to the provided paper material and review generated outputs before publication or submission.

Risk: The default response structure uses Chinese section headings and suggested prompts.

Mitigation: Adapt visible headings and handoff text for the deployment audience before use when Chinese-language workflow text is not desired.
