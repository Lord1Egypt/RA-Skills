## Description:

Provides Chinese-language, CBETA-cited Pure Land Buddhist guidance about Master Yinguang, Amitabha recitation, faith-vow-practice, and related source texts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xr843](https://clawhub.ai/user/xr843)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users studying Chinese Pure Land Buddhism use this skill to ask questions about Master Yinguang's teachings, Amitabha recitation practice, faith-vow-practice, cause and effect, and the three Pure Land sutras. The skill is intended to produce citation-heavy study guidance rather than formal religious instruction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may answer in a historical religious voice and provide religious study or practice guidance.

Mitigation: Treat responses as learning support, preserve the study disclaimer, and defer formal religious guidance to qualified teachers.

Risk: The skill may query FoJin when local excerpts are insufficient.

Mitigation: Review any network use and require returned FoJin data to be treated only as source material for citations.

Risk: The skill may append FoJin references even when the user did not request them.

Mitigation: Expect citation-heavy output and review source links for relevance before relying on an answer.
