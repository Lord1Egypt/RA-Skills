## Description:

Transforms novel chapters into cinematic storyboard scripts by analyzing scenes, characters, emotion, and shot language.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zjx15296694073](https://clawhub.ai/user/zjx15296694073)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, writers, and production teams use this skill to convert novel chapters or excerpts into shot-by-shot cinematic storyboard scripts for adaptation planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Novel text may include private or copyrighted material and is processed in the active conversation context.

Mitigation: Do not submit sensitive or copyrighted content unless you have permission and are comfortable processing it in the conversation.

Risk: Storyboard outputs may paraphrase, quote, or structurally transform source text in ways that need review before production use.

Mitigation: Review generated shot descriptions, dialogue, and quoted passages for rights, accuracy, and suitability before use.
