## Description:

Cross-platform PPC strategy planner for ecommerce businesses that recommends Google Ads, Meta Ads, and TikTok Ads strategy, calculates ROAS and CPA targets, allocates budget, and generates campaign briefs with ad copy and creative direction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[phheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/phheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators and marketing teams use this skill to plan new paid advertising programs or optimize existing cross-platform campaigns. It turns product, margin, budget, and campaign-performance inputs into budget allocation guidance, platform-specific campaign briefs, ad copy, creative direction, and prioritized optimization actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Install examples use unpinned global skill installation commands.

Mitigation: Use a pinned CLI version and a reviewed commit or release when available, and avoid global installation unless the skill should be available across sessions.

Risk: The skill may process commercially sensitive metrics such as margins, budgets, and campaign performance.

Mitigation: Share only the business metrics needed for the planning task and avoid unnecessary account, customer, or confidential operational details.

Risk: Benchmark-based estimates can produce guidance that does not match actual account performance.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current platform data before changing budgets, bids, or campaign structure.
