## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Amazon Prime pricing, delivery availability, benefit scope, and promotional details can change by region and over time.

Mitigation: Verify current Amazon Prime terms and local availability before making purchase or subscription decisions.

Risk: The value calculator uses approximate assumptions that may not match an individual user's actual costs or usage.

Mitigation: Review and replace calculator inputs with current membership fees, local service prices, and personal usage data.
