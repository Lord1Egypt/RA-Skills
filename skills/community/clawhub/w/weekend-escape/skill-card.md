## Description:

Weekend Escape helps city dwellers plan short weekend trips with Friday evening departures, Sunday evening returns, destination comparisons, transport, lodging, activities, food recommendations, checklists, and backup plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yunhanli7](https://clawhub.ai/user/yunhanli7)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to compare nearby weekend destinations and generate a practical 48-hour travel plan based on departure city, preferences, dates, budget, and travel range.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Casual phrases about wanting to get away may trigger the skill and lead to travel searches using location, dates, budget, or preferences.

Mitigation: Confirm vague travel intent and the user-provided trip details before running flyai travel searches.
