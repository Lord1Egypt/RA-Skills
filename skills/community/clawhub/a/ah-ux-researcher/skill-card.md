## Description:

Expert UX researcher specializing in user insights, usability testing, and data-driven design decisions to uncover user needs, validate designs, and drive product improvements through actionable insights.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mtsatryan](https://clawhub.ai/user/mtsatryan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product teams, designers, researchers, and developers use this skill to plan and synthesize UX research, including interviews, usability tests, surveys, analytics review, personas, journey maps, accessibility research, and research recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: UX research work may involve sensitive participant, analytics, or customer data.

Mitigation: Use only project-authorized materials and confirm consent, privacy, and compliance requirements before providing sensitive research data.

Risk: Research recommendations can be misleading if inputs are incomplete, biased, or statistically weak.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against source evidence, sample quality, and stakeholder context before using them for product decisions.
