## Description:

Book driving-lessons services through Lokuli by searching providers, checking availability, and creating bookings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to find driving-lesson providers near a ZIP code, check appointment availability, and create a booking after confirming provider, service, time slot, and contact details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Searches and bookings involve Lokuli and may include personal contact details.

Mitigation: Confirm the provider, service, time slot, and that the user's name, email, and phone number will be sent to Lokuli before creating a booking.
