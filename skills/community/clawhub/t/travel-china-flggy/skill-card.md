## Description:

综合旅行规划、预订和管理技能。当需要规划国际旅行、管理多目的地行程、处理家庭旅行事务、优化旅行成本以及协调复杂旅行安排时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External travelers, especially Chinese citizens planning international or family trips, use this skill to structure itineraries, compare transport and airline options, prepare travel documents, estimate costs, and manage pre-trip and in-trip checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill advises storing scans of passports or identity documents in cloud storage and email, which can expose sensitive personal data if accounts or files are not protected.

Mitigation: Use encrypted storage, strong account protection, and avoid sending unencrypted identity scans through ordinary email.

Risk: Travel requirements such as visa policies, health requirements, flight prices, weather, exchange rates, and attraction availability can change after the skill content was authored.

Mitigation: Verify current travel requirements and prices with official or current sources before booking or traveling.
