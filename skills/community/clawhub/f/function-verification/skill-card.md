## Description:

Compares acceptance checklists and test cases item by item to produce pass, conditional pass, or fail conclusions for functional verification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, QA engineers, and product owners use this skill to run structured functional acceptance reviews against acceptance checklists, test cases, and P0/P1/P2 module priorities, then record conclusions and defects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Functional acceptance conclusions could be mistaken for final approval.

Mitigation: Treat the skill output as advisory and have the project owner confirm final acceptance decisions, especially for business-critical releases.
