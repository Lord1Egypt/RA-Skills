## Description:

Run, audit, or improve an OpenClaw/ClawLite-style AI marketing team as a coordinated multi-lane operating system for research intel, content packaging, social publishing, blog publishing, lifecycle activation, QA, truth-gate review, and Mission Control state management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External teams, founders, and developers use this skill to run a repeatable AI-agent marketing operating system across research, asset synchronization, publishing, lifecycle activation, QA, and same-day truth closure. It is suited to OpenClaw or ClawLite-style growth teams that need coordinated handoffs and receipts rather than isolated content drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may publish social posts, blogs, or lifecycle messages that affect public communications or customers.

Mitigation: Require human approval for public posts, blog publishes, and customer email sends where correctness, brand fit, or customer impact matters.

Risk: Reusable marketing packages can accidentally include private credentials, campaign data, customer email addresses, or internal-only account handles.

Mitigation: Keep credentials and customer data out of reusable packaging and generalize private paths, campaign details, and internal account handles before sharing.

Risk: Lane completion can be overstated when drafts, attempted sends, or unverified publishes are treated as delivery.

Mitigation: Use dated receipts, ASSET_CHECK evidence, live URLs, visibility proof, and explicit truth states before marking a lane complete.
