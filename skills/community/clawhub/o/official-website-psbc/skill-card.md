## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may present banking, wealth-management, recruitment, or login destinations as fully official even when some URL mappings are under-verified.

Mitigation: Confirm returned links through PSBC-controlled sources before signing in, transferring funds, purchasing financial products, or submitting personal information.

Risk: A stale or incorrect link could route a user to the wrong service context for sensitive banking tasks.

Mitigation: Review the returned domain and service name against current PSBC public materials before acting on the result.
