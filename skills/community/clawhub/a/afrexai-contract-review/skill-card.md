## Description:

Analyzes business contracts to identify risks, flag problematic clauses, check for missing protections, and generate negotiation checklists with an overall risk score.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Founders, operations teams, freelancers, agencies, and other business users use this skill for first-pass review of contracts to identify risky clauses, missing protections, and negotiation points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide privileged, regulated, or highly confidential contract details.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in agent environments whose data handling, retention, and access controls are acceptable for the contract material.

Risk: The review can be mistaken for legal advice or a complete legal analysis.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a first-pass business review and have qualified counsel review important or high-risk contracts.
