## Description:

Analyzes video advertising creatives and produces evidence-based tags, summaries, reusable script outlines, and iteration recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tencent-adm](https://clawhub.ai/user/tencent-adm)

### License/Terms of Use:

Apache 2.0

## Use Case:

Developers and creative analysts use this skill to review user-provided video ads, tag their visual and narrative features, and prepare structured reports with reusable script outlines and practical iteration advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:
