## Description:

Write or adapt original lyrics and generate sung music in Subclip using MiniMax Music 2.6, Google Lyria 3 Pro or ElevenLabs Music. Use for songs, sung hooks, jingles, custom lyrics, generated lyrics, vocal language/style and verse/chorus structure; not spoken narration or instrumental background beds.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Risk: Lyrics, prompts, or reference material may be sent to an external music provider.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential material unless you have permission to share it.

Risk: The benign security assessment has medium confidence because review coverage is partial.

Mitigation: Read the skill and confirm its external service use before installing.
