## Description:

Thesis Writer is a backward-compatible Chinese thesis-writing helper that routes users to the integrated paper thesis workflow for chapter drafting, consistency revision, advisor-feedback changes, and terminology management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Graduate students and academic writers use this skill to draft and revise Chinese thesis sections, check consistency, apply advisor feedback, and keep terminology aligned while preserving the legacy thesis command path.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow is tailored to Chinese academic thesis conventions and may not match other language, institution, or citation-style requirements.

Mitigation: Review and adapt the writing rules, terminology conventions, and citation expectations before using it for a different academic context.

Risk: Academic writing assistance can introduce unsupported claims, fabricated data, or nonexistent references if outputs are accepted without review.

Mitigation: Verify all data, claims, terminology, and citations against the user's source materials before including generated text in a thesis.
