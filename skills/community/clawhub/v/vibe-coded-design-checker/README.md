## The design quality bar

1. One clear main point per page, easy to see in seconds.
2. One color palette, used the same way on every page.
3. One type system, readable, with clear headings.
4. Motion with a purpose, it helps the visitor read, or act.
5. Fast to load, and easy to use on a phone.
6. Readable by everyone, including people who use a keyboard, or a screen reader.
7. Real content, real photos, real numbers, in the voice of the business.
8. No sign the site was built by AI with no human check.

The 17 AI design tells in the skill come from a source reel by Strategy Fox, dated 21 Oct 2026. They are one section in the check, not the whole skill.

## How to use it

1. Install the skill in your AI agent (see below).
2. Give your agent the site, the goal of the site, and the audience, in plain words.
3. Ask: "Check this design with vibe-coded-design-checker."
4. Read the design check first. Every Pass needs evidence pasted in the message.
5. Read the AI design tells. A tell with no evidence is Not confirmed, not found.
6. Read the fix draft. Approve the first change before anything goes live.

If you already give the site, the goal, and the audience up front, the skill does the full check in the same reply. It never stops at questions alone.

## What it will not do

- It will not change a live site, publish anything, or redesign a page, without a separate approval from the owner.
- It will not invent a tool name, a price, a link, a stat, a partner, a testimonial, or a result.
- It will not mark a check Pass without evidence pasted in the message.
- A fix draft is not a promise of more sales, more visitors, or a higher rank.
