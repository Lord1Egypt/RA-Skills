## Description:

深度洞察引擎 — 对任何分析结论执行穿透式深度审查，输出带有因果链、反事实场景和张力暴露的洞察报告。不与领域知识耦合，只负责分析方法论。Use when 需要深化分析结论、穿透表层判断、暴露战略建议的可执行性风险。不适用于领域数据查询、简单事实确认。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers use this skill to deepen strategic or analytical conclusions after an initial analysis by testing causal chains, counterfactuals, tensions, failure modes, and executable next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make responses longer and more adversarial when deep review is requested.

Mitigation: Use it only for explicit DeepInsight, Chief-review, or high-risk strategic-review contexts when that posture is desired.

Risk: Strategic critique may surface assumptions or counterarguments that are not backed by current domain data.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's data-request workflow and treat unverified claims as assumptions until the needed evidence is supplied.
