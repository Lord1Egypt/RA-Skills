## Description:

Policy Sea helps users query Southeast Asia trade taxes, certifications, market-entry requirements, and FTA rules for Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, the Philippines, and Singapore.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External cross-border sellers, trade operators, and investment researchers use this skill to compare Southeast Asia customs costs, certification requirements, import qualifications, and market-entry barriers before making business decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tariff, certification, and customs guidance may be incorrect or outdated, and the artifact states that its content is not formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Verify important answers against official government, customs, or certification authority sources before relying on them for business decisions.

Risk: The inspected artifact does not show a live data-source implementation for policy updates.

Mitigation: Treat answers as decision support and confirm time-sensitive quota, FTA, and import-license details with current official sources.
