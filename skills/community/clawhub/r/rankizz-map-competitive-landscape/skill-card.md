## Description:

Map an organic-search market by identifying recurring ranking domains, competitor types, keyword coverage, winning content formats, authority patterns, and underserved opportunities. Use when entering a category, planning SEO strategy, or determining which search competitors matter. Rankizz is optional until live SERP, domain, keyword, or backlink data is needed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SEO strategists, marketers, and site owners use the skill to map which domains repeatedly win relevant searches, understand the content and authority patterns behind those results, and identify evidence-backed opportunities for their own site.
