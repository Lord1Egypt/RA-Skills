## Description:

Agente Historia Geografia is a Portuguese-language tutoring agent for FUVEST first-phase History and Geography preparation, focused on Brazilian history, geopolitics, biomes, current events, and interdisciplinary vestibular study.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad History, Geography, Brazil, geopolitics, biomes, or historical-event conversations.

Mitigation: Review activation triggers during installation and tune routing if the agent should only respond inside exam-preparation workflows.

Risk: The skill provides educational tutoring guidance on current geopolitics and exam topics that may need human review for accuracy before high-stakes study use.

Mitigation: Have educators or maintainers review current-event examples and topic coverage before relying on the skill for exam preparation.
