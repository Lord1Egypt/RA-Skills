## Description:

A Chinese-language business model analysis skill that uses an OPC-adapted business model canvas, profitability matrix, LTV/CAC unit economics, and validation scoring to assess startup profitability and sustainability.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Entrepreneurs, founders, and business advisors use this skill to structure business model information, compare OPC revenue models, calculate LTV/CAC unit economics, and generate an actionable analysis report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial scores and unit-economics calculations can be misleading when user-provided business data is incomplete or speculative.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory, validate assumptions with real customer and revenue data, and review results before making business decisions.
