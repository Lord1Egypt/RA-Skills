## Description:

Helps OpenClaw Clinical Hackathon participants get started quickly building clinical and healthcare apps with Lovable.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External developers and hackathon participants use this skill for quick-start guidance on building clinical or healthcare MVPs in Lovable, including project scoping, component-by-component development, authentication, data handling, and PHI-safe prompt practices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers may mistake clinical hackathon build guidance for medical, legal, HIPAA compliance, diagnosis, or treatment advice.

Mitigation: Present the skill as app-building guidance only and require qualified review for medical, legal, privacy, and compliance decisions.

Risk: Clinical app builders may expose PHI through prompts, Knowledge uploads, or logs.

Mitigation: Use placeholders in prompts, avoid real patient data in model-facing context, and keep sensitive data only in appropriately secured application storage.
