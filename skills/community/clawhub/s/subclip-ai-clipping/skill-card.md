## Description:

Find strong, standalone social clips from video transcripts and assemble them as editable candidates with appropriate framing and captions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[prosamik](https://clawhub.ai/user/prosamik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video editors and creators use this skill to find complete moments in a transcript and assemble them into an editable short-form reel with captions and evidence-based framing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Clips-only assembly removes original full-length timeline placements without warning.

Mitigation: Confirm clips-only output is intended; explicitly request a source-plus-candidates review timeline if the original placements should remain. Source files are preserved in Project Media.
