This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing and growth teams use this skill to turn paid-media objectives, budgets, and channel scope into market-aware strategy snapshots, channel roles, budget plans, test matrices, and scale or kill rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked for broad marketing or growth questions because of its trigger terms.

Mitigation: Use it for paid-media planning requests where the objective, budget frame, and channel scope are relevant.

Risk: Budget and performance recommendations could be inaccurate if treated as facts without business data.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current campaign data, tracking quality, platform policy constraints, and internal budget controls before acting.
