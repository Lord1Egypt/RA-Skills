## Description:

Helps users turn software-improvement requests about 工具操作前置安全校验（edit/write/shell 防护） into a practical workflow, artifact, checklist, analysis, or implementation-support response.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and maintainers use this skill to turn software-improvement requests about edit, write, and shell safety checks into clear workflows, deliverables, checklists, analyses, code changes, or decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill's implicit invocation wording is broad and may appear during ordinary creative, feature, edit, write, or shell requests.

Mitigation: Installers should narrow or disable implicit invocation when they do not want this workflow helper triggered for general requests.
