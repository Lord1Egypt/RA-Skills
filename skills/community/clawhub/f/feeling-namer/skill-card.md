## Description:

把“烦、累、焦虑、说不上来”这类模糊感受，拆成 2—3 个具体情绪，配“它在保护什么”、一个 5 分钟微行动和一句可说的话。强触发词为“情绪命名”“帮我理理情绪”“我说不清什么感受”。当用户说“我最近很烦”“心里堵得慌”“什么都不想干”时也可触发。不做心理诊断，不替代专业帮助，内置危机识别与转介流程。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People who want help identifying and expressing an unclear emotional state can use this skill to explore two or three possible emotions, what each may be protecting, and a small next action. It also provides clear boundaries and crisis referral guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake emotion-labeling suggestions for a diagnosis or treatment plan.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's boundary statement and recommend qualified professional assessment when symptoms persist or affect daily life.

Risk: Crisis resources vary by location; the skill gives 120 only as a mainland China example.

Mitigation: Direct users to emergency services or a crisis line available in their location.
