## Description:

Integral Hermeneutics is a self-contained interpretation methodology for analyzing texts, statements, systems, behaviors, artifacts, and phenomena through reflexive, empathic, systemic, dialectical, empirical, and boundary-aware lenses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and developers use this skill when they want an assistant to perform nuanced interpretation, meaning analysis, worldview comparison, ideological analysis, cultural translation, conflict analysis, or meta-interpretation. It is intended for reflective analysis tasks where surface summaries are insufficient and uncertainty, perspective, and evidentiary limits need to be explicit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce longer, more reflective answers and may be a poor fit when users need short factual responses.

Mitigation: Use it for meaning, intent, ideology, conflict, or perspective analysis, and ask for a compressed mode when brevity matters.

Risk: Interpretive analysis of motivations, inner states, trauma, ideology, or hidden intent can overstate uncertain claims.

Mitigation: Use the skill's confidence tags and boundary acknowledgments to separate observed facts from inferences, speculation, and empathic reconstruction.

Risk: High-stakes topics such as crisis, abuse, medical, legal, or similar domains require normal safety judgment beyond interpretive framing.

Mitigation: Apply domain safety policies and avoid treating the interpretive framework as a substitute for professional or emergency guidance.
