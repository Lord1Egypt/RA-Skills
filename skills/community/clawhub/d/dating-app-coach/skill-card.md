## Description:

Dating App Coach is an AI strategist for Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, and other dating apps that helps users optimize profiles, photos, openers, follow-ups, date conversion, and rejection recovery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive personal conversations, contact details, or dating-app content while seeking coaching.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive personal data unless comfortable with the agent processing it.

Risk: The install command is unpinned, which can make reproducible installation harder.

Mitigation: Use a trusted ClawHub installation path and prefer pinned or verified installer versions when available.

Risk: Dating advice could be misused to pressure uninterested people or misrepresent a user.

Mitigation: Keep guidance focused on truthful profiles, respectful exits, and safety-first behavior.
