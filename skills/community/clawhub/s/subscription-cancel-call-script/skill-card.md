## Description:

Builds a firm, honest cancellation call script with refusal phrases, evidence checklist, and confirmation log for recurring services.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to prepare a clear, honest cancellation packet for recurring services, including a call script, refusal phrases, evidence checklist, confirmation log, and follow-up checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may provide credentials or sensitive account details while preparing the cancellation script.

Mitigation: Do not ask for passwords, one-time codes, full payment details, security answers, sensitive identity documents, or private account credentials.

Risk: The script could be misused to impersonate another account holder or make false claims during a cancellation call.

Mitigation: Use only truthful authorization and facts; do not advise impersonation, fabricated hardship, fabricated service defects, false legal threats, or unsupported dispute claims.

Risk: Users may mistake cancellation preparation for legal, refund, chargeback, or regulatory advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as call preparation, avoid promising outcomes, and suggest checking written terms and local consumer rules when relevant.
