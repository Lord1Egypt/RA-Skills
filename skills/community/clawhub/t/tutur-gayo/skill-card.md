## Description:

Produce, adapt, or review text for Bahasa Gayo with an offline-first regional-language workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to draft, adapt, or review Bahasa Gayo wording for ordinary community and educational contexts. It helps preserve source discipline by marking uncertain vocabulary, dialect-sensitive phrasing, and formal-use risks for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Formal, legal, ritual, educational, or public-facing Bahasa Gayo text may require local authority, dialect, or native-speaker judgment beyond the skill's evidence base.

Mitigation: Have a competent speaker or relevant language authority review such outputs before use.

Risk: Dialect-sensitive or undocumented vocabulary could be misleading if treated as verified translation.

Mitigation: Use verified KBDA, dictionary, or user-provided samples; preserve Indonesian terms and mark uncertainty when no reliable Gayo equivalent is available.
