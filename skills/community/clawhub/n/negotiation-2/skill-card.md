## Description:

Navigate negotiations as a solopreneur across pricing, scope, terms, and contracts, including price pushback, scope changes, deal structure, objection handling, BATNA preparation, anchoring, and contract-term negotiation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and solopreneurs use this skill to prepare for and conduct client negotiations involving price objections, discounts, scope changes, payment terms, IP ownership, termination clauses, liability caps, and non-competes. It provides structured conversational guidance and response scripts, not professional legal or financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat generic negotiation and contract-term guidance as professional legal or financial advice.

Mitigation: Present the skill as general business guidance and have users consult qualified legal or financial professionals before relying on it for contract, pricing, liability, IP, or payment-term decisions.

Risk: Negotiation scripts may be applied without enough context about the client's constraints, the user's BATNA, or local contract requirements.

Mitigation: Encourage users to adapt the scripts to the deal facts, document agreed terms in writing, and review material contract changes before use.
