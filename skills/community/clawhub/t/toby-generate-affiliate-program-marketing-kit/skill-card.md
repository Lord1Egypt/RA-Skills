This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketers, agencies, and growth teams use this skill to draft affiliate launch or scaling materials, including banner concepts, email templates, social posts, and tracking guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated affiliate claims or promotional copy may be inaccurate, noncompliant, or inconsistent with brand rules.

Mitigation: Review claims, required affiliate disclosures, brand rules, and partner-facing copy before publishing or distributing materials.

Risk: Tracking guidance may be incomplete or inconsistent with the affiliate program's measurement policy.

Mitigation: Validate tracking links, parameters, disclosure placement, and partner instructions against the program's approved tracking requirements before release.
