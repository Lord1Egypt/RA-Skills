## Description:

Helps an agent turn user-provided native social analytics and GA4/UTM metrics into honest, goal-mapped reports and next actions without fabricating missing data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media practitioners and teams use this skill to interpret native platform analytics, map metrics to goals, build outcomes-first reports, and decide which content to scale, cut, or test next.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide sensitive or unnecessarily granular social analytics data.

Mitigation: Use aggregate metrics where possible and avoid platform passwords or personal-level tracking data.

Risk: Missing or noisy analytics could lead to misleading reports if treated as complete or precise.

Mitigation: Flag missing numbers, cite the native source for each figure, and present small-sample or attribution limits as caveats.
