## Description:

E-commerce growth strategy advisor that diagnoses unit economics, identifies opportunities across traffic, conversion, average order value, retention, and expansion, and builds a prioritized 90-day roadmap for channel, product, and market growth.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
