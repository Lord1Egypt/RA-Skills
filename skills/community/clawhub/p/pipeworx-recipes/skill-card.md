## Description:

Search and retrieve detailed recipes, find meals by ingredient, or get a random meal suggestion with step-by-step instructions and video links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[brucegutman](https://clawhub.ai/user/brucegutman)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and external users use this skill to connect an agent to a recipe lookup gateway for meal search, ingredient-based discovery, random suggestions, and full recipe instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recipe searches and tool calls are sent to the disclosed Pipeworx recipe gateway.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive personal information in recipe searches and disclose the external gateway before installation.
