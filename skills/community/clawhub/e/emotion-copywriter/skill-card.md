## Description:

Generates Chinese social media copy tailored to target emotions, scenarios, and platform conventions using emotion psychology patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Social media writers, marketers, and creators use this skill to draft emotionally resonant Chinese posts for platforms such as Weibo, WeChat Moments, Xiaohongshu, public accounts, and Instagram. It helps adapt emotional angle, copy structure, rhetorical technique, and platform length or tone constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated copy may use anxiety, anger, loneliness, or other high-arousal emotions in ways that feel manipulative, especially for vulnerable audiences or commercial campaigns.

Mitigation: Review outputs before publication and revise emotional pressure, claims, and calls to action so the copy remains appropriate for the audience and use case.

Risk: Commercial use can over-prioritize emotional resonance over brand tone or platform expectations.

Mitigation: Balance emotional hooks with brand voice, platform constraints, and the artifact's own guidance to avoid over-selling anxiety or misaligned tone.
