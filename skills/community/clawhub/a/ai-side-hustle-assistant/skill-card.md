## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance for evaluating AI side-hustle paths, including skill publishing, content creation, tool distribution, automation services, platform selection, and weekly action planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[marson2016](https://clawhub.ai/user/marson2016)

## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill to compare AI monetization options, estimate effort and income potential, identify suitable publishing platforms, and create a practical weekly plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The referenced ClawHub publishing example uses a token-based CLI login command that could expose credentials if pasted into shell history or logs.

Mitigation: Use safer authentication methods such as interactive login, stdin, or a credential store when available, and rotate any token that may have been exposed.

Risk: Monetization guidance may create unrealistic expectations or omit market-specific constraints.

Mitigation: Treat revenue estimates as planning inputs, validate platform rules and market demand independently, and review progress before committing significant time or money.
