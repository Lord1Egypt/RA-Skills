## Description:

Run a brand's TikTok and Instagram week after week with Hookbird. Each week it reads what is trending in the niche, writes and scores hooks, makes the posts in Hookbird Studio, schedules them after one approval, then learns from what sold and what got views. Use when someone asks an agent to run, grow or automate their social content, TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
