## Description:

Assess and implement obligations under Lei nº 15.211/2025 (ECA Digital), Decreto nº 12.880/2026, and ANPD's Oct 2026 Guide for IT Providers for digital products directed to or likely accessed by children and adolescents. Use when user mentions 'ECA Digital', 'menores', 'crianças e adolescentes', 'verificação de idade', 'sinais de idade', 'acesso provável', 'controle parental', 'loot box', 'uso compulsivo', 'denúncia de conteúdo', or when the product may have users under 18 — including social networks, games, generative AI, app stores and e-commerce of age-restricted goods. Modulates controls by ecosystem role and risk.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
