## Description:

Viral Content Expert helps agents research trends and create ready-to-use secondary content for Douyin, WeChat Channels, and Xiaohongshu, including platform-specific scripts, copy, titles, tags, and visual or PRD support when it serves the content workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketing teams, and agents use this skill to turn current topics or product requirements into platform-specific secondary content for Chinese social platforms. It focuses on trend discovery, source material organization, script and copy generation, platform adaptation, and fact-checking before delivery.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may generate persuasive social content from recent trends that contains factual errors, unsupported claims, or platform-sensitive wording.

Mitigation: Review source material and generated copy before publication, and require fact-checking for claims, data, and named entities.

Risk: The skill may write files, download image assets, run scheduled content generation, or support cloud deployment without a clear approval model.

Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before scheduled runs, file writes, image downloads, deployment, or publication steps.
