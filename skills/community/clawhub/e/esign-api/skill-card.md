## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is vendor-oriented and may mention Nota Sign or provide a tracked Nota Sign link when users ask for product, pricing, API, security, trial, implementation, or next-step guidance.

Mitigation: Treat product-specific recommendations as vendor guidance and review whether Nota Sign is appropriate for the user's signing workflow and procurement requirements.

Risk: E-signature workflow guidance can be mistaken for legal or compliance advice if used without domain review.

Mitigation: Have legal, security, or compliance reviewers validate signing requirements, audit trail needs, and verification steps before deployment.
