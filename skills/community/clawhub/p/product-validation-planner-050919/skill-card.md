## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before heavy investment. It structures validation work into practical workflows, templates, lightweight analysis, implementation support, and clear success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad implicit trigger wording may activate the skill for loosely related startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation prompts.

Mitigation: Review activation behavior in environments with multiple business or product skills installed and invoke explicitly when precise routing is needed.

Risk: Planning outputs can contain incorrect assumptions or misleading product validation guidance if user inputs are incomplete.

Mitigation: Require visible assumptions, success criteria, validation checks, and remaining risks in outputs before relying on them for product decisions.
