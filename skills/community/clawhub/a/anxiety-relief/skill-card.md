## Use Case:

External users use this skill for self-guided anxiety relief, including grounding, breathing, thought reframing, and local anxiety logging. It is not a substitute for professional care or crisis support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on self-guided anxiety techniques instead of medical, emergency, or crisis support.

Mitigation: Present the skill as self-help only and direct crisis or self-harm situations to emergency or professional resources, including the 988 and Crisis Text Line guidance included in the artifact.

Risk: Anxiety logs may contain sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Keep logging local to the user's device and avoid sharing logs unless the user explicitly chooses to do so.
