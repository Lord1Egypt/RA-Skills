## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether product ideas are worth building before heavy investment. It helps produce validation workflows, prototype plans, user-facing positioning, checklists, lightweight analyses, and verification notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for general product, SaaS, startup, or prototype conversations where a narrower skill would be more appropriate.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants product-validation planning before applying its workflow, and defer to a narrower skill when the request is outside validation planning.

Risk: Generated validation plans, prototypes, positioning, or business checklists may reflect incomplete assumptions and could mislead product decisions if treated as validated facts.

Mitigation: Make assumptions explicit, ask for material missing inputs, and validate outputs against stated success criteria and real user evidence before relying on them.
