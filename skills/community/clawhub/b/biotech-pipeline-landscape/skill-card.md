## Use Case:

Biopharma teams, investors, CRO/CMO users, and analysts use this skill to map company or modality pipelines, compare competitors, structure CAR-T and gene-therapy evaluations, and produce research reports with source-marked findings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sequence, product, or pipeline details may be sensitive when used with external research tools.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with data approved for the configured research tools and follow the user's data-handling policy before submitting sequence or product details.

Risk: FTO and design-around outputs are preliminary research and may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat patent conclusions as advisory, retain source markings, and have qualified patent counsel confirm FTO and design-around decisions.

Risk: Generated HTML reports may preserve sensitive biotech strategy, sequence, or patent analysis.

Mitigation: Review report contents and storage location before sharing or retaining versioned report files.
