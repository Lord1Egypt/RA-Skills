## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Analytics tracking can create privacy or compliance obligations if added without proper authority or consent.

Mitigation: Confirm authority to add Google Analytics before installation and use the consent option where law or policy requires it.

Risk: Custom analytics events can accidentally send personal information, credentials, sensitive search terms, full error details, or unnecessary transaction details.

Mitigation: Review event parameters before deployment and avoid sending sensitive or unnecessary data to analytics.
