## Description:

鲁迅先生写作手法 v2.0 helps agents rewrite or draft Chinese prose in a Lu Xun-inspired critical style, emphasizing cold analysis, spectator structures, structural harm, skeptical endings, and rejection of sentimental writing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bfchain2-hub](https://clawhub.ai/user/bfchain2-hub)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External writers and agent users use this skill to transform drafts into Chinese essays, commentary, speeches, eulogies, and reflective prose with a Lu Xun-inspired critical posture. It is best suited for serious writing that needs structural critique, self-examination, restrained emotion, and non-sentimental endings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally applies a strong Lu Xun-inspired critical tone that may be unsuitable for neutral editing, marketing copy, warmer user-facing text, or non-Chinese writing.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly only for that style and review the output for audience, language, and tone fit before publication.

Risk: Style-focused rewriting can overemphasize critique or alter the emphasis of user-provided factual material.

Mitigation: Preserve factual claims from the source text and perform human review before using outputs in public, academic, or sensitive contexts.
