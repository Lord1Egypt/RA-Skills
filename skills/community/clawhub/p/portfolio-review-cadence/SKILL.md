# Portfolio Review Cadence

## Overview

Portfolios drift quietly and concentrate accidentally. This workflow produces the same review every period — same tables, same questions — so changes over time are visible and decisions are deliberate instead of reactive.

## When to use

Monthly or quarterly, and after any major market move or life change.

## Workflow

1. **Snapshot the present** — balances by asset and venue, valued the same way as last time
2. **Compare to target** — allocation now vs target allocation; flag anything off by more than your band
3. **Concentration check** — single-asset, single-venue, and single-custodian shares; name the biggest
4. **What changed** — buys, sells, deposits, withdrawals since last review; no unexplained deltas
5. **Income and yield** — what the portfolio actually paid out this period, per position
6. **Decide deliberately** — rebalance, hold, or research more; write the decision and the reason down

## Guardrails

- Never rebalance on a feeling — the review produces the decision, the decision cites the numbers
- Keep valuation methods identical across periods or the comparison is fiction
- This is a review process, not financial advice; the owner makes the calls

## Output format

A review note: allocation table (now vs target), concentration flags, period changes, income, decisions with reasons.
