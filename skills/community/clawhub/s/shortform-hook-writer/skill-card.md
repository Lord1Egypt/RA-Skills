## Description:

Create natural, high-retention hooks and opening text for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and slideshow/carousel-style short-form content in English and German.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wotaso-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/wotaso-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, founders, and content teams use this skill to generate and audit short-form video or slideshow hooks, visual openings, continuations, and A/B test variants for major vertical video platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may return both English and German hooks when the user expects one language.

Mitigation: Specify the desired language, platform, format, and audience before generation.

Risk: Marketing hooks can imply unsupported product, medical, financial, or performance claims if the prompt lacks restrictions.

Mitigation: Provide claim boundaries, proof limits, and legal or brand restrictions; review generated hooks before publication.
