## Description:

S2 品牌价值动态演算模型 uses a dual Python-based valuation method to estimate cumulative media value (V1) and conversion expectation value (T) for a brand.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[spacesq](https://clawhub.ai/user/spacesq)

## Use Case:

External brand, finance, and strategy users can use this skill to estimate intangible brand value from industry sales, maturity, media, and brand-score assumptions. Its outputs are suitable for exploratory valuation analysis and should be reviewed before business or financial reliance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce valuation estimates that may be mistaken for formal financial valuations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as estimates and review assumptions, formulas, and results before using them for financial or business decisions.

Risk: The security evidence notes incomplete valuation guidance, including a missing V1 implementation.

Mitigation: Check the input assumptions and formula coverage, especially the cumulative media value calculation, before relying on the report.
