## Description:

Build a calm, factual log for repeated home noise, including timestamped incidents, proof notes, pattern summary, and a neutral message draft for management, an HOA, a landlord, or a neighbor.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize repeated home noise concerns into a factual documentation packet and a neutral first-contact message for a landlord, property manager, HOA, roommate, or neighbor.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the documentation packet or neutral message as legal or tenant-rights advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs factual and neutral, avoid legal conclusions, and direct users to official rules or qualified local support when legal interpretation is needed.

Risk: Noise documentation can expose private names, unit numbers, contact details, or other personal information.

Mitigation: Use only user-provided proof, preserve originals, and recommend redacting private details when preparing a shareable packet.

Risk: Attempts to gather proof could become unsafe, intrusive, or policy-violating.

Mitigation: Do not suggest secret recording, surveillance, trespass, identification of private occupants, or evidence collection that may be unsafe or restricted.

Risk: Noise disputes may involve threats, violence, stalking, property damage, domestic conflict, child safety concerns, or immediate danger.

Mitigation: Route immediate safety concerns to local emergency services or qualified local support instead of drafting a routine message.
