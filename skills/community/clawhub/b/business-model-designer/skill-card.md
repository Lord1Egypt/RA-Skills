## Description:

Business Model Designer helps users analyze, design, evaluate, and iterate business models with Business Model Canvas and Value Proposition Canvas frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[taihongliu](https://clawhub.ai/user/taihongliu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, entrepreneurs, product managers, consultants, and business teams use this skill to convert business descriptions or ideas into canvases, structured evaluations, risk notes, and iteration options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill repeatedly adds the author's paid consulting advertisement to completed outputs.

Mitigation: Remove the promotional footer or make it opt-in before broad deployment.
