## Description:

Expert in cooling circulating water treatment for industrial plants, providing design, optimization, and troubleshooting guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nickliu54](https://clawhub.ai/user/nickliu54)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External industrial water-treatment users and operations engineers use this skill to obtain cooling circulating water system design guidance, chemical dosing recommendations, operating optimization, and troubleshooting support. The skill is intended as a domain reference assistant, not an authoritative engineering sign-off.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chemical formulations, equipment sizing, or compliance guidance may be incorrect for a specific site if local water analysis and regulatory constraints are not verified.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a domain reference assistant and require qualified review against site-specific water data and local environmental regulations before implementation.

Risk: Non-Chinese users may misunderstand important cautions because much of the skill body is Chinese.

Mitigation: Review translated guidance carefully with a qualified domain expert before using it for project decisions.
