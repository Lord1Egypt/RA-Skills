## Description:

Harvard author-date referencing (Cite Them Right baseline): reference lists, in-text citations, Accessed dates, chapters, web sources. Use for papers requiring Harvard style.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vhsgreed](https://clawhub.ai/user/vhsgreed)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, writers, and agents use this skill to produce Harvard author-date reference list entries and in-text citations for papers that require Harvard style. It helps apply Cite Them Right baseline conventions while checking local institutional variants.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce citations that follow the Cite Them Right baseline but differ from a user's required institutional Harvard variant.

Mitigation: Confirm the required module, institution, or publisher guide before generating citations, then apply that variant consistently throughout the document.
