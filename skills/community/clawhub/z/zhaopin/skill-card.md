## Description:

Provides lightweight guidance for summarizing public Zhaopin job postings and company pages, including role details, salary ranges, experience requirements, and company information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[codekungfu](https://clawhub.ai/user/codekungfu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to organize public Zhaopin job search results, job details, and company summaries for lightweight comparison and internal analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Use beyond public Zhaopin pages could conflict with platform rules or human-verification controls.

Mitigation: Keep usage limited to public pages, avoid account login and job applications through the skill, and respect platform prompts.

Risk: Bulk collection of job or company information could create compliance or operational risk.

Mitigation: Use lightweight manual review workflows, avoid batch scraping, and record collection time when summarizing results.
