## Description:

Scans email and LinkedIn for stalled commercial relationships and surfaces re-ignition candidates with scored briefs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rednix](https://clawhub.ai/user/rednix)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business development, sales, partnerships, and founder teams use this skill to identify existing commercial relationships that have stalled and decide which contacts are worth re-engaging. It produces scored relationship briefs and tailored opening lines for up to five promising contacts per week.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may perform recurring analysis of connected business communications and retain derived relationship notes.

Mitigation: Configure narrow source access and private delivery, review pipeline and context notes regularly, and confirm how to disable the weekly job and delete retained notes.

Risk: Suggested re-engagement messages may be inappropriate if personal threads, resolved conversations, or clearly closed business relationships are included.

Mitigation: Keep the scope limited to existing commercial relationships, exclude ambiguous personal threads, and have the user review each suggested message before sending.
