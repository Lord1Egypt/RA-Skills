## Description:

Generates Chinese Xiaohongshu-style content drafts with titles, body copy, and hashtags for topics such as parenting, knowledge sharing, and lifestyle.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators and marketing teams use this skill to draft Xiaohongshu posts, including candidate titles, structured body text, and platform-style hashtags.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated posts may contain unsupported or sensitive claims, especially around medical, legal, financial, political, or pricing topics.

Mitigation: Review and edit each draft before publication, and require subject-matter review for regulated or sensitive topics.

Risk: Generic Xiaohongshu writing prompts may activate the skill even when the user expected a different content-writing workflow.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended platform, audience, and topic before using or publishing the generated copy.

Risk: High-volume or insufficiently reviewed posting can create platform-compliance or account-quality issues.

Mitigation: Use the output as draft assistance, avoid automated bulk publishing, and follow the target platform's content and posting policies.
