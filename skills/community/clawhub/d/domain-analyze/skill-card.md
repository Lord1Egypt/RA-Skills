## Description:

Analyze a specific domain using registration, DNS, website, safety, backlink, cross-TLD, and market and legal-risk evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and domain operators use this skill to perform evidence-based due diligence on a single domain before acquisition, technical review, or reputation assessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill performs public domain, DNS, safety, web, backlink, and market or legal lookups, and may fetch domain-related web pages during analysis.

Mitigation: Use it only where public network lookups are permitted, keep safety checks ahead of web fetches, and do not fetch domains flagged as malicious, phishing, or malware-hosting.

Risk: Market and legal findings can be incomplete or time-sensitive and should not be treated as professional legal or purchasing advice.

Mitigation: Treat conclusions as research context, preserve source attribution and observation dates, and seek qualified review before relying on the report for acquisition or legal decisions.
