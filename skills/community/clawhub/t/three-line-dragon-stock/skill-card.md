## Use Case:

Traders, analysts, and developers use this skill to structure educational stock screening around MA5, MA10, and MA20 alignment, limit-up momentum, platform breakouts, and predefined position sizing and stop-loss rules.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the stock-screening criteria for personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as educational screening guidance, encourage independent verification, and recommend consulting a qualified financial professional before trading.

Risk: Actionable trading signals can still lead to losses if applied without market context or risk controls.

Mitigation: Surface the skill's position sizing, stop-loss, volume confirmation, and market-environment checks whenever explaining screening results.
