## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to turn CryptoRank free-layer data into Chinese Web3 market, funding, upcoming-sale, airdrop, and daily-brief summaries for research, content planning, and workflow handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Routine text and markdown outputs may include publisher-controlled Telegram and YouTube support links.

Mitigation: Review outputs before reusing them in reports, posts, automations, or customer-facing summaries, and remove links that are not appropriate for the deployment context.

Risk: Market, funding, public-sale, and airdrop summaries depend on CryptoRank free-layer website data that may change or be incomplete.

Mitigation: Check source links and timestamps before acting on or publishing time-sensitive Web3 information.
