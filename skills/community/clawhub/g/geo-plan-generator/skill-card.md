## Description:

Generate a complete AI-GEO marketing plan for a client and deliver it as a detailed Word teleprompter plus a minimal display-focused PPTX.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and consultants use this skill to create client-ready GEO marketing proposals from company information, target keywords, or source plan documents. It researches the market, develops audience-driven keywords, drafts an 11-module proposal, and produces separate detailed Word and presentation PPTX deliverables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated client documents may include publisher or service-provider promotional claims without explicit user opt-in.

Mitigation: Review and edit the service-provider advantages section before client delivery, removing unsupported or unwanted promotional claims.

Risk: Generated market claims, certifications, and performance numbers may be inaccurate or stale if not checked against sources.

Mitigation: Verify all cited numbers, platform qualifications, client product claims, and certification wording before sharing the Word or PPT deliverables.

Risk: The skill may perform web research and create local .docx and .pptx files using Node document-generation dependencies.

Mitigation: Run it in an approved workspace and inspect generated files and dependency activity according to local security policy.
