## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Insurance recommendations may be wrong or incomplete if policy details, premiums, exclusions, claim rules, insurer ratings, or legal terms are inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Use current policy documents and verify premiums, exclusions, claim rules, insurer ratings, and legal terms with the insurer or a qualified advisor before relying on a recommendation.
