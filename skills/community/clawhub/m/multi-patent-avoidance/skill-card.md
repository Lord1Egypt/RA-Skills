## Description:

Multi Patent Avoidance is a Chinese-language eight-step FTO workflow for analyzing and designing around two or more patent risks using application requirements, patent intelligence matrices, safe-zone identification, cross-scans, and targeted remediation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External patent, IP, and product teams use this skill to structure multi-patent freedom-to-operate analysis, identify candidate safe zones, compare candidate designs against patent sets, and draft review materials for professional assessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: FTO conclusions and design-around recommendations may be incorrect or incomplete if treated as final legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as analytical drafts and have a qualified patent professional review conclusions before business use.

Risk: Without the optional PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP account configuration, the skill may not retrieve database-backed patent records.

Mitigation: Confirm the MCP account is configured when current patent retrieval is required; otherwise use the skill only as an analysis framework.
