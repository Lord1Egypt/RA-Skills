## Description:

A targeted web research workflow for finding and summarizing hard-to-locate public information through cross-language search, source triangulation, and document review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, and agents use this skill to investigate cross-border business, policy, competitor, and technical-standard questions, then summarize facts with sources, freshness, and confidence labels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Expansive public-source research can involve sensitive competitive, regulatory, or regional topics.

Mitigation: Keep research within legal and ethical boundaries and avoid using the workflow for confidential or restricted information gathering.

Risk: Cross-language and third-party source checks can surface stale, conflicting, or low-confidence claims.

Mitigation: Prefer current official or audited sources, preserve source URLs or document names, and label freshness and confidence in the final summary.
