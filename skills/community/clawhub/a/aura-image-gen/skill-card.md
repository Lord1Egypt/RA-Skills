## Description:

Multi-platform infographic card generator for Xiaohongshu, Pinterest, Instagram, and more, with 8 visual styles, 8 layouts, platform-specific aspect ratios, and prompt templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[luoboask](https://clawhub.ai/user/luoboask)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to turn social content topics into platform-aware image-card series, outlines, and AI image prompts for Xiaohongshu, Pinterest, Instagram, YouTube, and related social formats.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may route broad image requests into a social-media-card workflow and adapt prompt text for platform defaults.

Mitigation: Review generated prompts, platform choices, and on-image text before submitting them to an image-generation provider.

Risk: User-provided text may be sent to an external image-generation tool selected by the operator.

Mitigation: Avoid including private or sensitive text unless the chosen provider's handling of that content is acceptable.
