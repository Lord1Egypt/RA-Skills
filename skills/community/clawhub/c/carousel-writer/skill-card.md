## Description:

Use to write swipeable multi-slide carousel posts for Instagram, LinkedIn document/PDF, and TikTok photo formats, including slide copy, design direction, image-generation prompts, and handoffs for captions and scheduling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to turn an idea or source material into a concise, platform-aware carousel with a strong cover, one idea per slide, a save-leaning CTA, and visual production prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated social media claims, platform specs, or AI-image disclosure recommendations may be inaccurate or stale by publication time.

Mitigation: Review claims, platform requirements, and disclosure language before publishing carousel content.

Risk: The skill may read local brand-profile and voice files when available.

Mitigation: Provide only approved brand and voice files, and review generated slides against brand, compliance, and disclosure guardrails.

Risk: AI-generated slide images can render text incorrectly or inconsistently across a carousel.

Mitigation: Verify each generated slide for exact wording and legibility, and use a Canva, Figma, or designer template when exact text and brand type are required.
