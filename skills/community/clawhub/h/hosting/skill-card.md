## Description:

Choose and manage web hosting services for websites and apps without server administration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, site owners, and operators use this skill to choose web hosting approaches, compare managed hosting tradeoffs, and plan domains, DNS, backups, costs, and migrations without managing servers directly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hosting provider names, prices, free-tier limits, and capabilities can change after publication.

Mitigation: Verify current provider terms, pricing, limits, and product capabilities before purchasing or migrating.

Risk: Hosting setup work can expose credentials or environment secrets if copied into repositories or shared prompts.

Mitigation: Keep hosting credentials, API keys, and environment secrets out of repositories and store them only in the hosting provider's secret management controls.
