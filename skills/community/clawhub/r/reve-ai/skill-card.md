## Description:

Generate, edit, and remix images using the Reve AI API.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dpaluy](https://clawhub.ai/user/dpaluy)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers and creators use this skill to have an agent generate new images, edit existing images, or remix reference images through the Reve AI API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected edit or remix images are sent to Reve's external API.

Mitigation: Do not use sensitive, confidential, regulated, or private prompts or images unless that use is approved under the user's data-handling requirements.
