## Description:

Leo's Reading Notes helps users turn book ideas into structured reading notes with source excerpts, paraphrases, 5 Whys analysis, and business or action links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leo-zzl](https://clawhub.ai/user/leo-zzl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Readers, knowledge workers, and developers use this skill to capture book passages and transform them into reusable Markdown notes with personal interpretation, structured tables, reflective questioning, and work-related action items.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can save personal reflections, work context, future plans, and action items by default.

Mitigation: Ask for explicit confirmation before writing or updating notes, and avoid storing sensitive reflections unless the user approves.

Risk: The skill uses external searches to look up book text and context.

Mitigation: Confirm search permission before lookup and mark uncertain source text clearly when it cannot be verified.

Risk: Daily internalization reminders can create ongoing behavior from broad reading-note triggers.

Mitigation: Require opt-in for reminder behavior and provide a clear way to pause, skip, or disable the daily workflow.
