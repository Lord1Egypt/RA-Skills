## Description:

Automates an end-to-end protein-mining workflow from literature review through candidate protein prioritization and publication-oriented outputs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[majiajia0529](https://clawhub.ai/user/majiajia0529)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, computational biologists, and bioinformatics engineers use this skill to plan and execute a multi-stage protein discovery workflow covering literature survey, sequence collection, HMM construction, homology search, structural search, phylogenetic analysis, and candidate prioritization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can generate substantial local analysis outputs and may require significant compute and storage resources.

Mitigation: Run it in a dedicated project directory, confirm output locations before use, and verify that required bioinformatics tools, compute resources, and storage are available.
