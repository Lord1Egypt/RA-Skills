## Description:

Offers optional context for acknowledging harm and expressing care while preserving existing safety, accessibility, and help commitments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[otherpowers](https://clawhub.ai/user/otherpowers)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agent developers and operators can use this text as optional context for discussing harm, apology, and repair without pressuring affected people or reducing help.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Although labeled ambient context, this guidance can influence apologies and response style.

Mitigation: Keep its use optional and under operator control; do not assume the guidance will persist across sessions.
