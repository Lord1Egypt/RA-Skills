## Description:

Generates Xiaohongshu/Rednote topic candidates from a theme, audience pain points, discussion signals, and platform fit, with hooks, outlines, hashtags, scoring, and claim checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and content operators use this skill to turn a theme or content direction into Xiaohongshu-ready topic ideas, hooks, card-style outlines, hashtags, and prioritization scores. It is especially useful when the user needs platform-fit topic candidates while keeping freshness and evidence limits explicit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce platform-specific Chinese Xiaohongshu guidance when the user expects another language or format.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for the desired language, target platform, and output format when the default Xiaohongshu format is not appropriate.

Risk: Trend, popularity, ranking, or performance claims may be stale or unsupported without current source material.

Mitigation: Use current sources when available, ask the user for source material, or label freshness and claims as unverified.

Risk: Generated topics can be misleading if they rely on private account identity, private archives, exaggerated claims, or unsupported metrics.

Mitigation: Avoid private or account-specific assumptions and keep result, metric, ranking, and trend claims conservative unless evidence is provided.
