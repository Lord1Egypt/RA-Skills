## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill describes automating paid ad creation and budget optimization without clear built-in safeguards or confirmation steps.

Mitigation: Use explicit ad-account scoping, preview or dry-run behavior, budget caps, and human confirmation before creating campaigns, changing targeting, or increasing spend.

Risk: Optimization recommendations could affect real advertising spend, targeting, or campaign performance.

Mitigation: Review generated campaign changes against business policy, platform rules, and current budget limits before applying them to live accounts.
