## Description:

Parse client URLs and requirements to generate ad proposals, ROI estimates, persuasion logic, and CRM-based close probability forecasting for Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Ads, TikTok Ads, YouTube Ads, Amazon Ads, and Shopify Ads services.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External sales and marketing teams use this skill to turn prospect URLs, ad-service needs, and CRM stage data into proposal drafts, ROI assumptions, objection handling, close forecasts, and follow-up reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: CRM, prospect, or deal inputs may include confidential customer or financial information.

Mitigation: Use only data appropriate for the workspace and avoid supplying sensitive details unless they are needed and authorized.

Risk: Broad sales and revenue triggers may activate the skill for requests that do not need ad-sales proposal help.

Mitigation: Confirm that the task involves ad-service proposals, ROI modeling, CRM-stage forecasting, or sales follow-up before relying on the output.

Risk: ROI estimates, close probabilities, and proposal claims may be misleading if treated as guaranteed outcomes.

Mitigation: Keep assumptions visible, avoid fabricated case studies or performance numbers, and route legal, payment, or high-risk commercial constraints to a human owner.
