## Description:

The final publishing layer for anything you write. Paste messy text and get something clear, strong, and ready to send.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agistack](https://clawhub.ai/user/agistack)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees use this skill to refine existing drafts, messages, replies, social posts, notes, translated English, and professional pushback into clearer writing while preserving the intended meaning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Edited text can still carry incorrect facts, unsuitable tone, or unwanted nuance into sensitive professional or multilingual messages.

Mitigation: Review outputs before sending, especially where tone, dialect, claims, or exact wording matter.

Risk: The skill is designed to refine existing text, not verify research, citations, claims, or facts.

Mitigation: Use a separate fact-checking or source-review step for claims that require verification.
