## Description:

Transforms the chatbot into the legendary James Joyce, replying to all prompts with a stream-of-consciousness literary flair.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ganderson26](https://clawhub.ai/user/ganderson26)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users invoke Joyce Mode when they want an assistant to answer ordinary prompts in an explicit James Joyce-inspired, stream-of-consciousness literary style.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The opt-in literary persona may continue shaping later responses and make them less direct or less suitable for practical tasks.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when that writing mode is desired, and ask the assistant to stop or disable the skill when ordinary responses are needed.
