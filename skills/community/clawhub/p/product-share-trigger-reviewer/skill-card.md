## Description:

Strictly review any new skill, browser extension, app, community, course, template, or micro-product before building or launching it.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zack-dev-cm](https://clawhub.ai/user/zack-dev-cm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, builders, and developers use this skill to review product ideas or shipped drafts for first traffic source, trust reason, share trigger, free MVP plan, stop criteria, and ecosystem fit before launch or monetization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may add or update local product-share gate files in a repository.

Mitigation: Review generated repository changes before committing or shipping them.

Risk: Product reviews can expose sensitive community, customer, payment, credential, or testimonial data if users provide it.

Mitigation: Use public page copy, aggregate counts, creator-owned notes, explicit opt-in feedback, or synthetic examples instead of private or unconsented data.

Risk: A strict review may recommend stopping, delaying, or reworking a launch based on incomplete product evidence.

Mitigation: Treat verdicts as decision support and confirm traffic, trust, free-MVP, and stop-criteria assumptions with concrete experiments.
