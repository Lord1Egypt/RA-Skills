## Description:

Qb Travel Expert provides Chinese-language, globally benchmarked destination critiques that assess authenticity, aesthetics, over-commercialization, exploration freedom, and cultural depth.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External travelers and travel planners use this skill through /travel to get rigorous, critical assessments of destinations or travel topics. It is aimed at experienced travelers who want blunt evaluation rather than generic itinerary filler or tourism-board language.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Travel logistics and conditions such as opening hours, safety, visa rules, weather, and seasonal suitability may change after the skill response.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to verify current operational, safety, visa, weather, and seasonal details before making travel decisions.

Risk: The skill's intentionally harsh and culturally specific perspective may not match every user's preferred tone or travel values.

Mitigation: State language preference, travel constraints, experience level, and desired evaluation posture when requesting an assessment.
