## Description:

Generates SEO-optimized affiliate reviews, comparison posts, email sequences, and social scripts to promote products and monetize content effectively.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitflopez](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitflopez)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and affiliate publishers use this skill to generate publish-ready affiliate marketing assets for a product, niche, and target audience. It supports search, email, and social promotion workflows with disclosure language included in each format.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The email sequence prompt can encourage deadline-based urgency when no real deadline exists.

Mitigation: Use only real, verifiable deadlines or clearly stated self-imposed bonus windows, and review the email copy before publishing.

Risk: Generated affiliate content may include claims, prices, disclosures, or platform-specific wording that require review before publication.

Mitigation: Verify product claims, pricing, affiliate disclosures, and platform rules before using the generated content publicly.
