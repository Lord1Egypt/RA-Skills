## Description:

通用 SMART 原则目标设定助手；将模糊目标优化为具体、可衡量、可达成、相关、有时限的清晰表述，并生成关键结果与行动计划。适用于个人发展、团队管理、项目、质量改进、学习、业务等多领域，不限定质量管理。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this Chinese-language skill to convert vague personal, team, project, learning, quality, or business intentions into SMART goals. It helps produce measurable key results, staged action plans, and review notes for common goal-setting gaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Referenced support files such as validators, templates, and detailed SMART references are not bundled in the package.

Mitigation: Treat those resources as unavailable unless separately provided, and rely on transparent SMART reasoning plus user confirmation before finalizing goals.

Risk: The skill can produce planning guidance that may be incomplete when goal context, resources, or strategy are missing.

Mitigation: Mark missing inputs as pending confirmation and avoid presenting feasibility, relevance, or timelines as final without user-supplied context.
