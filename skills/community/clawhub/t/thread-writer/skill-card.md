## Description:

Helps an agent draft cohesive X/Twitter or Threads thread copy with a strong opener, one clear idea, post-by-post momentum, and a single closing CTA.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, founders, and social media teams use this skill to turn a multi-beat idea into a native social media thread, or to recognize when a single post is the better format.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated copy may include inaccurate claims, regulated-topic issues, unsafe links, or brand-sensitive language.

Mitigation: Review generated copy before posting publicly, with extra attention to claims, regulated topics, links, and brand-sensitive wording.

Risk: A requested thread may be weaker than a single post when the idea lacks multiple real beats.

Mitigation: Use the skill's format check and accept a single-post recommendation when padding would reduce quality.
