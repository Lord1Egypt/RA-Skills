## Description:

Find and contact verified Alibaba suppliers for Amazon FBA products, draft outreach, check replies, and manage negotiations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[blockchainhb](https://clawhub.ai/user/blockchainhb)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Amazon FBA sellers use this skill to source products, compare Alibaba suppliers, send approved outreach, monitor replies, and manage follow-up negotiations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can use a logged-in Alibaba session to read messages and send supplier inquiries or replies.

Mitigation: Require explicit user supplier selection and message approval before sending, and review the Alibaba form or conversation before submission.

Risk: Local negotiation notes may contain supplier names, pricing targets, and message history.

Mitigation: Use the skill on trusted machines and remove local conversation logs when they are no longer needed.
