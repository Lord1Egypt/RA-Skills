## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes one uncensored racial slur in historical context.

Mitigation: Review audience and content policies before enabling the skill, and consider whether filtering or contextual handling is needed for users.

Risk: Several Krump history files state that their information is based on knowledge available up to 2017 and may be outdated or inconsistent.

Mitigation: Treat historical and event details as reference material that may need confirmation before use in current-facing guidance.

Risk: Users may mistake the static persona and reference material for executable software or live research.

Mitigation: Present outputs as conversational guidance from static Markdown evidence, not as command execution, live lookup, or authoritative current cultural records.
