## Description:

Seven complete motion formats, each with a beat structure in seconds, a shot list and presets: a found-footage analog-horror trailer of a website, an authentic VHS bouncing-logo screensaver, a pixel-art runner on a CRT, a wordmark with a wave running through it, a one-shot music video that follows a DAW timeline, a launch trailer for an import feature, and a kinetic lyric video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Video creators and designers use these beat-synced formats and presets to plan and build VHS, analog-horror, pixel-art, lyric, launch, DAW-timeline, and wordmark videos for their own music or brands. The skill provides build guidance and preset data for local rendering with HyperFrames or Remotion.
