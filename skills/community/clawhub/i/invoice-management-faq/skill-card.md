## Description:

Provides quick, source-grounded answers to common Chinese-language invoice application, issuance, and refund process questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[longtel-skill](https://clawhub.ai/user/longtel-skill)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Sales staff and new employees use this skill to retrieve approved invoice management FAQ answers for opening invoices, handling special invoice cases, and requesting invoice refunds. Questions outside the documented FAQ should be referred to the finance department.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The FAQ is intended for a specific Chinese-language finance workflow and may not match other invoice processes.

Mitigation: Confirm the workflow applies before installation or use.

Risk: Invoice, customer, contract, or payment information may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Use approved internal channels when sharing or discussing sensitive finance information.

Risk: Questions outside the documented FAQ may receive incomplete guidance if treated as covered policy.

Mitigation: Escalate unlisted questions to the finance department instead of inferring an answer.
