## Description:

Build a simple daily water habit around the user's schedule using personalized cues, a bottle plan, and a 7-day check-in sheet.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn their ordinary day pattern into a simple hydration routine with practical cues, a bottle or container plan, and a copyable 7-day check-in sheet. The skill is for habit design and health literacy support, not medical fluid advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat hydration habit guidance as medical fluid advice despite fluid restrictions, relevant health conditions, pregnancy-related guidance, severe dehydration symptoms, or urgent symptoms.

Mitigation: Keep guidance general, defer to clinician instructions for medical fluid limits or relevant conditions, and direct urgent symptoms to urgent medical care or local emergency help.

Risk: A hydration habit plan can collect more personal health and routine context than needed.

Mitigation: Gather only the schedule, water pattern, dry spells, bathroom-access constraints, drink preferences, bottle options, tracking preference, and clinician-guidance details needed to design the routine.
