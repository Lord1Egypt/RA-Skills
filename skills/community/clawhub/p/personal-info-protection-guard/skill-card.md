## Description:

Reviews privacy policies, user agreements, app permission prompts, personal information collection notices, marketing copy, and related China-focused data protection materials for compliance issues and suggested revisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1003429073](https://clawhub.ai/user/1003429073)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Privacy, legal, compliance, product, and app teams use this skill to review China-focused personal information protection materials before release. It identifies compliant items, warnings, and blocking issues, then provides revision suggestions in a structured review report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides compliance assistance and may be incomplete or incorrect for high-stakes legal decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as review guidance only and consult a qualified privacy, legal, or data compliance professional before relying on them for material decisions.

Risk: The skill focuses on China privacy and data protection requirements, so its guidance may not cover all obligations in other jurisdictions or specialized sectors.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable jurisdiction, sector rules, and current legal requirements before publishing reviewed materials.
