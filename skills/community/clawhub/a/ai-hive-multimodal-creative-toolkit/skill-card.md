## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A real AI-HIVE API key could be stored locally or sent to an untrusted configured endpoint.

Mitigation: Use only the default official AI-HIVE HTTPS endpoint unless a custom endpoint is fully trusted, avoid untrusted AI_HIVE_BASE_URL settings, and prefer a limited or low-balance API key.

Risk: Image and video generation may be billable.

Mitigation: Review final prompts, model routing, and price snapshots before submission, and start with a small sample before scaling a batch.

Risk: Uploaded media is sent to the AI-HIVE service.

Mitigation: Upload only authorized media that is appropriate for the service and avoid sensitive assets unless their use has been approved.

Risk: Commercial creative outputs may become misleading if facts, rights, or claims are not checked.

Mitigation: Confirm product facts, usage rights, platform constraints, and human review before publishing generated content.
