## Description:

Conception et animation d'ateliers collaboratifs professionnels (renoncement, modelisation de processus, diagnostic d'equipe, cartographie des flux, priorisation, retrospectives).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thibautnext](https://clawhub.ai/user/thibautnext)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

French-speaking facilitators, consultants, managers, and transformation teams use this skill to prepare collaborative workshops, create facilitation materials, document outcomes, and define follow-up action plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Workshop plans, summaries, and action plans may be incomplete or misleading if the agent fills gaps without enough project context.

Mitigation: Review generated materials with the facilitator and stakeholders before sharing or using them to make decisions.

Risk: The skill is written in French and may not match teams that need another working language.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to translate or adapt templates before use, then review terminology and local facilitation norms.

Risk: Generated workshop outputs may include sensitive team feedback or organizational information supplied by users.

Mitigation: Apply normal confidentiality review before distributing workshop notes, diagnostics, or action plans.
