This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Educators and academic support staff use this skill to turn calculus homework and submission data into class-level learning analytics, individual student profiles, targeted practice recommendations, and lesson planning guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill profiles identifiable student names, IDs, scores, submission behavior, rankings, and risk predictions.

Mitigation: Use only with authorized educational staff, prefer de-identified inputs, restrict exports and screenshots, and follow institutional student-record handling policies.

Risk: Generated predictions and recommendations may influence student support or instructional decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support and require educator review before interventions, placement decisions, or classroom changes.

Risk: Dashboards and exported reports can expose sensitive learner records.

Mitigation: Limit access, retention, and sharing of generated dashboards and reports to approved staff and systems.
