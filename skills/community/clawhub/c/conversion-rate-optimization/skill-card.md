## Description:

Audits a landing page or funnel step and produces a prioritized CRO test plan with heuristic findings, diagnosed friction, ICE-scored hypotheses, sample-size-aware test designs, and measurement guardrails.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, product, growth, and ecommerce teams use this skill to audit landing, signup, checkout, or other funnel steps and turn performance data or research signals into a prioritized CRO testing plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real analytics, session recordings, or survey data may include sensitive business or user information.

Mitigation: Provide only data approved for agent use, redact sensitive identifiers, and summarize raw user-level data where possible.

Risk: CRO recommendations may be misleading if based on incomplete traffic, audience, or funnel data.

Mitigation: Review the proposed diagnosis, sample-size assumptions, and guardrail metrics before running experiments or shipping changes.
