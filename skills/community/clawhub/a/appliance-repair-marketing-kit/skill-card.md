## Description:

Creates compliant digital marketing materials for appliance repair businesses, including ads, SEO pages, review systems, and B2B outreach templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitflopez](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitflopez)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Appliance repair business owners, operators, and marketers use this skill to generate local ads, SEO service pages, review/referral copy, and B2B outreach materials while keeping business claims aligned with provided authorization, warranty, pricing, and certification facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated ads, service pages, review requests, or outreach may contain inaccurate authorization, OEM parts, EPA 608, warranty, pricing, review, or affiliation claims if the supplied business facts are wrong or incomplete.

Mitigation: Review all generated copy before publishing and use only verified authorization, certification, warranty, pricing, review, and parts-sourcing claims.

Risk: Cold outreach, review requests, referral rewards, and dealer referral programs can create compliance issues if contacts are sourced unlawfully, opt-outs are missing, incentives are unclear, or platform rules are ignored.

Mitigation: Source outreach contacts lawfully, include required opt-outs and sender details, disclose referral or review incentives where applicable, and follow anti-spam, FTC, TCPA, CAN-SPAM, and platform requirements.
