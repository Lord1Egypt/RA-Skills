## Description:

Rewrites Chinese and English text to reduce common AI-writing patterns and make prose read more naturally.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaoyu-157](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoyu-157)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and writing-focused agents use this skill to edit drafts, diagnose AI-like writing patterns, and produce revised prose with a quality report. It is most relevant for Chinese and English rewriting, polishing, and style naturalization workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rewriting can alter meaning, facts, quotations, punctuation, or document structure beyond what the user expects.

Mitigation: Review outputs before publishing or overwriting originals, and explicitly require preservation of facts, numbers, quotations, punctuation, and structure when those details matter.

Risk: Text in legal, academic, technical, marketing, testimonial, factual, or multilingual contexts may require stricter fidelity than a style-humanizing pass provides.

Mitigation: Use the rewritten text as a draft and have a qualified reviewer compare it against the source for accuracy and intended meaning.
