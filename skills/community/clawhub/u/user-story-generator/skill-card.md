## Description:

Creates well-structured agile user stories with acceptance criteria for product development.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huajianjiu000](https://clawhub.ai/user/huajianjiu000)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, product owners, and development teams use this skill to turn feature requests, user needs, personas, and constraints into agile user stories with acceptance criteria, story point estimates, priorities, technical considerations, and dependencies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated story point estimates and technical considerations may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a specific engineering context.

Mitigation: Review generated stories with product, engineering, and delivery stakeholders before using them for planning or implementation.
