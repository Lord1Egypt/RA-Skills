## Description:

Generates Amazon A+ content plans with module recommendations, scenario-based copy, multilingual localization, and compliance checks for marketplace listings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangm-a3](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangm-a3)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Amazon sellers and ecommerce content teams use this skill to turn a product category, key selling points, and a target language into Amazon A+ module structures, localized copy, and review checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with documented support for DE, JP, FR, ES, IT, US, and UK marketplaces.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad ecommerce and Amazon copywriting prompts may activate the skill outside the intended marketplace or language context.

Mitigation: Provide explicit target marketplace, target language, product category, and content objective in the prompt.

Risk: Generated product claims, certifications, comparison data, and policy guidance may be inaccurate or unsuitable for publication.

Mitigation: Verify claims, certifications, comparison sources, and Amazon policy compliance independently before publishing.
