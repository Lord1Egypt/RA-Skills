## Description:

Quill guides agents through planning, researching, drafting, and reviewing evidence-based long-form nonfiction with structured notes for claims, evidence, audience, and style.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cgartlab](https://clawhub.ai/user/cgartlab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers use this skill with an AI agent to turn ideas or existing drafts into evidence-grounded long-form nonfiction, while keeping final editorial choices with the writer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
