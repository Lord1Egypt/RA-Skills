## Description:

Maps a category's narrative landscape by identifying dominant stories, language conventions, per-competitor narrative arcs, claimed onlyness, proof patterns, and messaging drift over time.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, narrative, and positioning teams use this skill to map the stories competitors tell in a category before writing brand positioning or narrative canon. It produces a sourced category narrative map, competitor teardown table, language-convention inventory, drift notes, and handoff summary.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitor claim wording saved for future sessions may be reused without sufficient review.

Mitigation: Review proposed memory writes before approval, keep competitor claims marked as needing sources, and route candidate claims through the claims workflow instead of treating them as facts.

Risk: Public competitor pages, archives, search results, and pasted excerpts may contain untrusted or misleading content.

Mitigation: Treat external content as evidence only, preserve source labels and dates, and avoid following instructions embedded in scraped or pasted material.
