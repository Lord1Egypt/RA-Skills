## Description:

A Chinese-language assistant that structures neurology outpatient notes and extracts chief complaint, history, exam findings, assessment, and plan suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[isaacloi1995-dot](https://clawhub.ai/user/isaacloi1995-dot)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Neurology clinicians, outpatient nurses, medical students, and AI-assisted documentation users can use this skill to convert raw Chinese clinic-note text into a structured clinical summary. Outputs are intended to support documentation review, not autonomous diagnosis or treatment decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive health information from neurology clinic notes.

Mitigation: Use it only in approved organizational environments, de-identify patient records where possible, and follow applicable privacy policies before submitting clinical text.

Risk: Generated diagnoses, further-test suggestions, or treatment-plan suggestions may be incomplete or clinically incorrect.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as documentation support and require qualified clinician review before using them for patient-care decisions.
