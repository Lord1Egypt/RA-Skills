This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users such as startups, solopreneurs, agencies, and e-commerce teams use this skill to create product mockup photos for launches, catalog pages, and on-brand product presentation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill declares Bash and local file-read access that is not explained by its product mockup purpose.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installing and prefer a version that removes or clearly scopes Bash and Read permissions.

Risk: Generated product photos may be inaccurate, off-brand, or unsuitable for publication without review.

Mitigation: Review generated images for product fidelity, branding, and publication requirements before external use.
