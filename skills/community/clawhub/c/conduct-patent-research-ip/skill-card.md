IP professionals, R&D engineers, project leaders, product teams, and strategy teams use this skill to turn a technical question and preliminary solution into traceable patent evidence, bounded screening analysis, and decision-ready reports. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent research can involve unpublished inventions, product details, or FTO-oriented facts that may be confidential.

Mitigation: Confirm the confidentiality boundary before live searches and share only information authorized for the relevant PatSnap connectors.

Risk: The skill performs patent research and screening, but it does not provide legal opinions or dispositive FTO, infringement, validity, or patentability conclusions.

Mitigation: Use calibrated screening language and route dispositive legal questions, claim interpretation, and final risk decisions to qualified counsel.

Risk: Patent status, family, citation, litigation, and transaction data are dated signals that may change.

Mitigation: Preserve source, retrieval date, jurisdiction, family basis, and data-cutoff information for all material findings.
