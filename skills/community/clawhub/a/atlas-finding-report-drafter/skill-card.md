## Description:

Drafts structured, severity-ranked smart contract audit finding reports for Solidity/EVM protocols from raw vulnerability notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[n8gendegen](https://clawhub.ai/user/n8gendegen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, security auditors, and bug bounty participants use this skill to turn Solidity/EVM vulnerability notes into submission-ready finding reports with severity scoring, exploit narratives, proof-of-concept sections, impact analysis, remediation guidance, and CWE mapping.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may include Atlas attribution or promotional links.

Mitigation: Review generated reports before submission or publication and remove that branding unless it is intentionally retained.

Risk: Drafted findings may contain incorrect severity, impact, exploit, or proof-of-concept details.

Mitigation: Manually verify the exploit path, severity rationale, affected components, impact claims, and PoC before relying on the report.
