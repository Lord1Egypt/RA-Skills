## Description:

Generate customer-facing answers for common market research questions. Use when working on FAQ content for analysts, founders, strategy teams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[modestyrichards](https://clawhub.ai/user/modestyrichards)

## Use Case:

Analysts, founders, and strategy teams use this skill to draft and refine customer-facing FAQ content about market research topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated market research FAQ answers may contain inaccurate, incomplete, or poorly sourced claims.

Mitigation: Review answers for accuracy, sourcing, readability, and publication readiness before external use.
