## Description:

Helps agents create, optimize, and audit customer story and case study pages, including structure, proof points, metrics, SEO metadata, and conversion elements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, founders, and content practitioners use this skill to plan and draft customer stories, case studies, testimonial pages, and customer proof sections that emphasize challenges, solutions, quantified results, quotes, calls to action, SEO metadata, and internal links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated customer stories may include unsupported metrics, quotes, customer claims, or logo usage.

Mitigation: Verify metrics, quotes, customer permissions, and logo rights before publishing.

Risk: Broad routing language may select this skill for testimonial component work that belongs to a more specific testimonial skill.

Mitigation: Use this skill for customer story and case study pages; route standalone testimonial components to the dedicated testimonials generator when available.
