## Description:

Product Recall helps quality and compliance teams assess recall triggers, draft recall levels, notification matrices, timelines, and exercise plans, and produce text and Markdown reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Quality directors and compliance teams use this skill to turn recall trigger information into a draft recall plan with level recommendation, notification responsibilities, milestones, and exercise checkpoints for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recall levels and timelines are templates and may not match jurisdiction-specific law or company policy.

Mitigation: Have qualified legal or compliance staff verify applicable rules, deadlines, and reporting obligations before acting.

Risk: Generated plans can be incomplete when product, batch, risk nature, affected region, or root-cause details are missing.

Mitigation: Require review of missing fields marked for enterprise input before approval, filing, or operational use.

Risk: Users may mistake draft recall plans for final legal decisions or regulatory submissions.

Mitigation: Use outputs as drafts for responsible teams to approve and submit through official company and regulator channels.
