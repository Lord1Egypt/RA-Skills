## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may trigger on broad CI/CD or automation-testing mentions when the user does not need pipeline-focused advice. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the requested workflow needs CI/CD testing or quality-gate guidance before applying the skill. <br>
Risk: The skill provides Chinese-language guidance that may not match every user's expected working language. <br>
Mitigation: Use it when Chinese output is acceptable, or translate and review recommendations before adoption. <br>
Risk: Suggested quality-gate thresholds may not fit every project's complexity, risk tolerance, or runtime budget. <br>
Mitigation: Tune thresholds, stage ordering, and runtime targets against project-specific release criteria before deployment. <br>
