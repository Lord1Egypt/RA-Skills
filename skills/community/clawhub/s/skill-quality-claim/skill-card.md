## Description:

Quality Claim helps SQE and customer quality teams draft customer claim letters and supplier chargeback requests, calculate PPM and losses, and produce plain text and Markdown reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Quality, SQE, procurement, finance, and customer quality teams use this skill to assemble claim or chargeback drafts, calculate PPM and loss amounts, and maintain an evidence chain for review and approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive supplier, customer, pricing, defect, and loss data.

Mitigation: Use it under the organization's data handling controls and avoid sharing confidential inputs outside approved systems.

Risk: Claim letters and chargeback drafts can affect business, legal, finance, or procurement decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and route them for business, legal, finance, or procurement review before sending claims or applying chargebacks.

Risk: Incomplete enterprise-specific facts such as contract numbers, unit prices, stop-line rates, or standards can make a claim inaccurate.

Mitigation: Keep missing values marked for enterprise completion and verify supporting contracts, prices, rates, and evidence before finalizing.
