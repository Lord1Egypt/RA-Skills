## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill instructions are corrupted and unclear for a sensitive legal and personal-data workflow. <br>
Mitigation: Install only after the publisher restores valid UTF-8 content, fixes the frontmatter, and provides readable scope, data-handling, legal-limitation, and network-access details. <br>
Risk: Compliance reports and response workflows could be mistaken for legal determinations. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as compliance assistance and require qualified privacy or legal review before relying on them for regulatory decisions. <br>
