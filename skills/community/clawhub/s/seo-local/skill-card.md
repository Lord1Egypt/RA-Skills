## Description:

Local SEO analysis covering Google Business Profile optimization, NAP consistency, citation health, review signals, local schema markup, location page quality, multi-location SEO, and industry-specific recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and SEO practitioners use this skill to audit a local business website and produce prioritized recommendations for Google Business Profile integration, NAP consistency, citations, reviews, local schema, and location page quality.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use web or SEO tools against the URL provided by the user.

Mitigation: Run it only on sites the user is authorized to analyze and follow the terms for any external SEO APIs or web tools used.

Risk: Generated SEO, business listing, review, and schema recommendations may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific business context.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before applying changes to listings, review workflows, structured data, or location pages.

Risk: The generated markdown audit report can contain business, site, and competitive analysis details.

Mitigation: Store and share the report according to the user's confidentiality and workspace handling requirements.
