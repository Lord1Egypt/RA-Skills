## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the persona unintentionally.

Mitigation: Review activation phrases and scope the skill to conversations where Wang Xing-style strategic analysis is explicitly requested.

Risk: Supporting notes include unnecessary internal path references.

Mitigation: Remove internal workspace paths from public release materials before publication.

Risk: Persona-style analysis based on public information can be mistaken for the real person's views.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's honesty boundary visible: outputs are interpretive guidance and do not represent Wang Xing personally.
