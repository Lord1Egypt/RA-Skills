## Description:

Provides safety-first guidance for identifying and harvesting common wild edible plants while emphasizing local verification, dangerous lookalikes, and legal constraints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to provide cautious foraging education, local plant-identification prompts, and guidance on common edible plants, dangerous lookalikes, seasonal availability, and public-land constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; users must verify local plant availability, seasonal timing, legality, and safety guidance for their specific region before harvesting.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could treat text guidance as sufficient to decide that an unknown wild plant is safe to eat.

Mitigation: Require 100% identification confidence, multiple identification features, authoritative regional field guides, and confirmation by qualified local experts before consumption.

Risk: The universal edibility test and smell-based checks could be misused as shortcuts for eating unknown plants.

Mitigation: Present these only with strong survival-only or family-specific limits, and direct users to poison-control or emergency services for suspected exposure.

Risk: The release includes an unpinned npx install command.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned and verified package source before installation.
