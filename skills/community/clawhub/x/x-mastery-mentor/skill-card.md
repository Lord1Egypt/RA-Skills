## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate broadly for X/Twitter drafting or growth prompts and steer output toward its preferred workflow.

Mitigation: Use it when X/Twitter coaching is intended, and review recommendations before applying them to live account strategy.

Risk: Users may be tempted to share confidential business metrics, customer personal information, credentials, or private account data during account reviews.

Mitigation: Keep inputs limited to public content or anonymized performance metrics.

Risk: Platform and algorithm guidance can become stale as X/Twitter changes its ranking systems or policies.

Mitigation: Treat algorithm-specific guidance as time-sensitive and verify current platform rules before making operational decisions.
