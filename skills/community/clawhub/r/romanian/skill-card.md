## Description:

Helps agents write casual, natural Romanian that sounds human rather than formal or robotic.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to adapt Romanian responses toward casual, native-sounding phrasing, including register, fillers, common expressions, and warmth. It is best suited to informal or internet-style Romanian unless the user asks for a formal register.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The casual default may be inappropriate for business, legal, official, or elder-facing Romanian communication.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to use a formal register or dumneavoastra for those contexts.
