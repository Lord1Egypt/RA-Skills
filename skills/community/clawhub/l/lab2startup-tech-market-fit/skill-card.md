## Description:

Helps teacher-student startup teams compare technology-led and market-led paths, choose application scenarios, and plan technology-market fit transitions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[earthwalking](https://clawhub.ai/user/earthwalking)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, educators, and student startup teams use this Chinese-language advisory skill to assess whether a project should start from technical advantages or validated market needs. It guides scenario exploration, market validation, transition planning, and production of a technology-market fit strategy report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill gives business strategy guidance that may be misleading if applied without current customer, competitor, and cost evidence.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as planning support, validate assumptions with customer interviews and market data, and review decisions before acting.

Risk: The skill is written for Chinese-language technology-market fit contexts and may trigger too broadly in multilingual business workflows.

Mitigation: Narrow activation triggers or adapt the language and examples before deployment in broader business contexts.
