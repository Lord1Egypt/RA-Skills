## Description:

Guides SEO content strategy by helping plan topic clusters, pillar pages, cluster articles, editorial calendars, internal linking, and update plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, marketers, and content teams use this skill to turn SEO goals, keyword research, existing content, and publishing capacity into topic clusters, content calendars, internal linking plans, and refresh plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask the agent to read local project context files when present, which can surface product, audience, and proof-point details in generated strategy output.

Mitigation: Review available project context before use and avoid including sensitive or unpublished details in final content plans.

Risk: SEO planning guidance may be inaccurate or overfit to incomplete keyword, audience, or capacity information.

Mitigation: Validate topic priorities, keyword intent, and publishing commitments with current business goals and trusted SEO data before execution.
