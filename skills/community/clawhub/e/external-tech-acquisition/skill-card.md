## Description:

Guides Chinese central state-owned enterprise technology teams through external technology acquisition, from strategic demand and patent intelligence scanning to asset evaluation, due diligence, transfer-path selection, and decision evidence packaging.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Technology management teams use this skill to identify external technology opportunities, evaluate suppliers and patent assets, assess IP and transfer-path risks, and assemble auditable Markdown evidence packs for acquisition or project approval decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External MCP use may expose confidential internal templates, budgets, due-diligence notes, or proprietary technology descriptions.

Mitigation: Install only in an environment approved for PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP use, redact sensitive details where possible, and follow organizational confidentiality and data-governance rules before submitting materials.

Risk: If the required MCP services are unavailable or unauthorized, outputs may be limited to general analysis frameworks rather than current patent, paper, company, or legal-status findings.

Mitigation: Complete MCP authorization and initial self-check before relying on data-backed reports, and label framework-only outputs clearly when live data access is unavailable.

Risk: Generated reports may influence technology acquisition, IP, compliance, and investment decisions.

Mitigation: Have legal, IP, compliance, and business owners review source-tagged findings and decision evidence before approval or negotiation use.
