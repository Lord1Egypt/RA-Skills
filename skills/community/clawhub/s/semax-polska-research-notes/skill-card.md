## Description:

Laboratory research notes helpers and Semax Polska shop links for chemical reagents and research peptides. Homepage https://semaxpolska.com/

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External researchers and lab users use this skill to format research highlights into structured notes and retrieve official Semax Polska homepage, shop, shipping, and contact links while staying within laboratory-use framing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated notes may include the Semax Polska brand or homepage even when the research-note content comes from another source.

Mitigation: Review notes for source attribution and brand references before using or sharing them.

Risk: Requests about human use of reagents could lead to medical, dosing, or treatment advice outside the skill's intended laboratory framing.

Mitigation: Keep responses factual and scientific, refuse human-use instructions, and redirect to laboratory-use framing and the Semax Polska contact page.
