## Description:

Generate professional voicemail greeting scripts from your terminal using Claude Code, with industry-specific templates, five greeting types, and a direct link to Solvea for AI voice and MP3 export.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and business operators use this skill to generate concise voicemail greeting scripts for business, personal, holiday, after-hours, and out-sick scenarios. The skill also guides users through converting an approved script to an MP3 with Solvea.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill directs users to paste voicemail scripts into an external Solvea service for MP3 generation.

Mitigation: Do not include confidential, medical, legal, or sensitive personal information in scripts intended for external conversion.

Risk: The skill is vendor-specific and may steer users toward Solvea account or plan options after free generations are exhausted.

Mitigation: Review the external service terms, account requirements, and plan choices before using it for business workflows.
