## Description:

Responds to brand or executive impersonation incidents by producing a verification protocol, takedown sequence, communications plan, and hardening plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External response, security, communications, and product teams use this skill to plan and document responses to fake apps, spoofed domains, cloned support channels, deepfakes, and other impersonation campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Response plans may include takedown filings, fraud referrals, or public statements that affect customers or third parties.

Mitigation: Have legal, security, and communications owners review filings, referrals, and public statements before sending them.

Risk: Premature public responses can amplify low-reach impersonation attempts.

Mitigation: Use reach thresholds, preserve evidence first, and avoid linking to or repeating scam content in communications.
