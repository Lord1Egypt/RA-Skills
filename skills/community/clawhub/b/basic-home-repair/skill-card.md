## Description:

The 15 most common home repairs that anyone can do with basic tools, aimed at people who currently call someone for routine repairs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, including new homeowners and renters, use this skill to diagnose and attempt simple low-risk home maintenance tasks before paying for routine professional service calls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installer commands can expose users to supply-chain or privilege-escalation risk if run unverified or with elevated permissions.

Mitigation: Pin or otherwise verify the ClawHub installer before installation, and do not run the install command with elevated privileges.

Risk: Home-repair guidance may be unsafe when applied to electrical work, gas lines, structural issues, or repeated breaker trips.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for low-risk tasks the user is competent to perform, shut off water or breakers as directed, and prefer a licensed professional for higher-risk work.

Risk: Renters may attempt repairs that should be handled through habitability or landlord processes.

Mitigation: For rental habitability repairs or major unresolved issues, document the problem and use the landlord or local tenant-rights process instead of continuing DIY work.
