## Description:

Produces current, evidence-graded pharmaceutical and biotechnology analysis for biopharma diligence, pipeline reviews, clinical-data interpretation, competitive landscapes, catalysts, risk, and valuation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sciminer](https://clawhub.ai/user/sciminer)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External analysts, developers, and biopharma research teams use this skill to produce evidence-graded reports on pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies, assets, mechanisms, trials, competitors, catalysts, risks, and valuation scenarios. It supports research and diligence workflows while requiring outputs to avoid medical, legal, patent, or personalized investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Biopharma reports may be mistaken for medical, legal, patent, or personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research support only and verify important claims against cited primary sources before relying on them.

Risk: Time-sensitive pipeline, trial, regulatory, financing, management, market, or competitor information may become stale.

Mitigation: Require a clear data cutoff and confirm material claims against current primary sources.
