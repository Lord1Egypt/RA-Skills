## Description:

Transform feature briefs into structured design briefs that give designers the context they need before opening Figma.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers and product teams use this skill to turn feature briefs or PRDs into concise design handoff briefs for designers. It organizes user goals, emotional context, success criteria, constraints, edge cases, and out-of-scope boundaries before design work begins.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to engage on general requests to write design briefs or translate PRDs.

Mitigation: Review when the skill activates and provide only product or business context appropriate for the active workspace.

Risk: A generated design brief may contain incorrect constraints or vague edge cases when the source PRD is incomplete.

Mitigation: Have the product or engineering owner validate constraints, edge cases, success criteria, and out-of-scope boundaries before design work begins.
