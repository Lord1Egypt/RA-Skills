## Use Case:

Property buyers, advisors, and agents use this skill to investigate Indonesian housing projects and developers with public-source due diligence, registry checks, location evidence, and buyer-facing reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Indonesia

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Setup installs browser automation and reporting dependencies that may affect the execution environment.

Mitigation: Install in a dedicated sandbox or virtual environment, avoid administrator privileges, and review dependency setup before use.

Risk: Generated reports and evidence archives may include screenshots, addresses, coordinates, and registration details.

Mitigation: Review PDFs and ZIP files before sharing, especially in Telegram or group chats.

Risk: Public-source due diligence can be incomplete or stale for property transactions.

Mitigation: Use the report as screening input and confirm legal, land, permit, tax, and transaction documents with the appropriate Indonesian authorities and professional reviewers.
