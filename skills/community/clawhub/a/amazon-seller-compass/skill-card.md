## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public web research and third-party marketplace data can be incomplete, stale, or unavailable.

Mitigation: Review cited sources, dates, and any estimates before making sourcing, pricing, advertising, or inventory decisions.

Risk: The skill may save a local Markdown report containing sensitive product ideas, costs, or launch plans.

Mitigation: Specify an appropriate save location and avoid sharing confidential business inputs in untrusted workspaces.

Risk: FBA fees, referral fees, storage charges, freight costs, and advertising benchmarks can change over time.

Mitigation: Confirm current Amazon fee schedules and logistics costs before relying on margin or ROI calculations.

Risk: Product recommendations may overlook brand, patent, regulatory, or platform-policy constraints.

Mitigation: Perform brand, intellectual property, compliance, and Amazon policy checks before launch.
