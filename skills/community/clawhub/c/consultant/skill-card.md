## Description:

Diagnose business problems, scope engagements, and deliver decision-ready recommendations with measurable outcomes and executable plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External consultants, operators, and business teams use this skill to frame ambiguous consulting requests, diagnose root causes, scope engagements, and produce decision-ready memos, workplans, roadmaps, stakeholder plans, and implementation sequences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local consulting memory can retain durable client context or engagement notes that may include sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Review files under ~/consultant/ periodically and avoid storing confidential, regulated, credential, or unrelated personal information.

Risk: Consulting recommendations can be weakened by incomplete evidence, unclear decision rights, or unstated assumptions.

Mitigation: Use the skill's discovery prompts, scope contract, quality gates, and final risk label before acting on recommendations.
