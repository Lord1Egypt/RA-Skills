## Description:

Personal relationship manager that helps you stay in touch with important people through gentle nudges, birthday reminders, and conversation tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to maintain a personal relationship network, remember birthdays and standing events, and receive gentle prompts for staying in touch. It also helps capture conversation notes and touchpoints in local relationship records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create durable local records containing personal contacts, birthdays, emails, phone numbers, and relationship history.

Mitigation: Use the skill intentionally, store only information you want retained, and periodically review or remove outdated contact records.

Risk: Optional calendar, email, Facebook export, or transcript-derived imports may add third-party personal data to local records.

Mitigation: Review imported or forwarded entries before saving them and avoid importing data that should not be retained.
