Marketing teams, CPA firm operators, and tax advisory partners use this skill to draft service comparison landing pages for CPA tax advisory offers, including offer positioning, pricing cues, trust signals, and SEO keywords.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated tax, pricing, or trust-claim content may be inaccurate, misleading, or unsuitable for the firm's compliance obligations.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer verify tax advisory claims, pricing cues, testimonials, and trust signals before publication.

Risk: Generated landing-page layouts or visual directions may not satisfy accessibility, brand, or rights-clearance requirements.

Mitigation: Review final page design, images, and calls to action against brand, accessibility, and publication standards before external use.
