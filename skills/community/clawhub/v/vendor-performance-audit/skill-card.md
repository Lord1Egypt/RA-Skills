## Description:

Provides a quarterly vendor review system with weighted KPI scoring, incident review, improvement planning, and offboarding triggers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[flynndavid](https://clawhub.ai/user/flynndavid)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Operations, procurement, vendor management, and business owners use this skill to evaluate significant vendors on a regular cadence, after incidents, or before renewals, then decide whether to renew, issue an improvement plan, renegotiate, or replace a vendor.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Vendor scoring can be misleading if based on memory, incomplete records, or unverified assumptions.

Mitigation: Use vendor-specific evidence such as ticket data, delivery logs, SLA records, and incident history before assigning scores.

Risk: Improvement plans, renegotiation decisions, and offboarding recommendations can affect commercial relationships.

Mitigation: Review recommendations with the appropriate internal owner before sharing externally or taking contractual action.

Risk: Vendor reviews may expose confidential operational or commercial information.

Mitigation: Limit inputs to records relevant to the vendor audit and avoid adding unrelated confidential records.
