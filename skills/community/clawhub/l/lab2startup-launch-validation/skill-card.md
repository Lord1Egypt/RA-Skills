## Description:

Guides teacher-student startup teams through launch and validation, including commercialization assessment, team formation, technical validation, market validation, MVP testing, and early planning documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[earthwalking](https://clawhub.ai/user/earthwalking)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, educators, founders, and student teams use this skill to structure the earliest stages of teacher-student commercialization projects. It helps them assess translational potential, define team roles, plan financing, prototype, interview customers, run MVP tests, and document validation results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat startup equity, IP, fundraising, or contract guidance as professional legal, financial, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a planning checklist and have qualified local professionals review equity, IP, fundraising, and contract decisions.

Risk: The artifact is written in Chinese, which may lead to misunderstanding for non-Chinese readers.

Mitigation: Use accurate translation or bilingual review before applying the guidance in a team or investment context.
