## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be used with sales, review, competitor, or shop data that may be proprietary or sensitive.

Mitigation: Use it only with ecommerce data you are authorized to analyze and limit access to generated reports.

Risk: Generated Excel, CSV, or PDF reports may expose sensitive business or competitor information if shared broadly.

Mitigation: Review and classify generated exports before storage, transfer, or publication.
