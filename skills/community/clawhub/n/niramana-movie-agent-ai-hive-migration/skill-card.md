## Use Case:

Developers, media production teams, and agency operators use this skill to evaluate whether a Niramana AI film-production workflow can be rebuilt with host agents and AI-HIVE MCP. It produces role boundaries, agent handoffs, MCP work orders, cost and quality checks, approval gates, and rollback criteria for a controlled migration pilot.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Niramana AI or AI-HIVE capabilities, pricing, availability, or product names may change after the recorded 2026-08-31 verification.

Mitigation: Recheck the official sources before production use and record a current capability and price snapshot before comparing migration results.

Risk: Uploading or generating with unauthorized people, trademarks, IP, music, products, or reference media could create rights and brand-safety exposure.

Mitigation: Use only owned or licensed assets, preserve source and hash records, and stop the workflow when unauthorized materials are detected.

Risk: Paid generation, external sharing, publishing, or data writing could happen before budget and quality review.

Mitigation: Require explicit budget approval and human review before any paid task, external send, publishing action, or data write.

Risk: The migration could be overstated as a full replacement without same-day, same-input comparison evidence.

Mitigation: Use the same input, duration, dimensions, and acceptance rubric for both systems, and avoid superiority or full-replacement claims without completed comparative testing.
