## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project reports, recordings, code archives, or customer examples may contain sensitive information when users adapt the templates.

Mitigation: Review and desensitize customer data, test data, recordings, code archives, and reports before storing or sharing them.

Risk: The operations templates may be applied to projects whose delivery model, KPI targets, or risk profile differ from the original scenario.

Mitigation: Have the project owner review task priorities, KPI targets, reporting cadence, and risk controls before rollout.
