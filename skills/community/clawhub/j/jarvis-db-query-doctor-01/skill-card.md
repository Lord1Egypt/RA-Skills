## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on the SQL-themed description for performance tuning, safe query rewrites, schema changes, or database-specific safety checks.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a planning and quality-gate checklist only, and require separate expert review before applying database changes.

Risk: The skill can produce advisory plans and commands that may be incomplete or incorrect without environment-specific validation.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against the actual schema, logs, query plans, test data, and acceptance criteria before execution.
