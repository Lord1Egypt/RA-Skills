## Description:

Build a same-day notary readiness packet with document inventory, ID checklist, signer logistics, and verification prompts while avoiding legal advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users preparing for urgent notarization use this skill to organize documents, signer identification, witness questions, appointment logistics, and verification prompts before meeting with a notary or receiving institution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce jurisdiction-specific notary guidance or ID acceptance details that a user could mistake for authoritative requirements.

Mitigation: Use the output as preparation only and verify requirements with the notary, receiving institution, or a qualified legal professional.

Risk: Example behavior may encourage drafting notarial wording, choosing the notarial act, or creating official journal records.

Mitigation: Keep use limited to readiness checklists, logistics, and questions to ask; do not rely on the skill for legal-document wording or official record creation.
