## Description:

Free AI receipt generator for limo, chauffeur, rideshare, taxi, and delivery drivers that creates professional receipts, invoices, and income summaries via ReceAI.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bptravel2017](https://clawhub.ai/user/bptravel2017)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External drivers, small transportation businesses, and independent contractors use this skill to generate passenger receipts, branded ride invoices, delivery receipts, and monthly income summaries for records and tax preparation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Receipt generation may send passenger emails, route details, payment methods, amounts, and driver business details to ReceAI.

Mitigation: Enter only the details needed for the receipt and review ReceAI privacy and retention terms before using account history or branded receipt features.

Risk: Emailing receipts can expose passenger information or send transaction details to the wrong recipient.

Mitigation: Get passenger consent before emailing receipts and verify the recipient address and receipt contents before sending.
