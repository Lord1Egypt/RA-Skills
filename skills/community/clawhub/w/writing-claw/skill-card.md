## Description:

Writing Claw helps agents develop fiction by organizing characters, settings, motifs, plots, themes, scene work, and gap analysis across stories or larger narrative universes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[scottginsberg](https://clawhub.ai/user/scottginsberg)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors, narrative designers, and agents assisting them use this skill to plan, draft, and maintain consistent fiction from individual scenes to series bibles. It supports character records, plot and theme tracking, gap analysis, and structured outputs for story planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad trigger scope may apply this narrative framework when a narrower style, genre, or writing method is intended.

Mitigation: Use explicit task instructions or a more specialized writing skill when the work needs a narrower creative posture.

Risk: Generated registries, matrices, and gap analyses can add continuity assumptions that the author has not approved.

Mitigation: Review proposed records and gap-priority recommendations before treating them as story canon.
