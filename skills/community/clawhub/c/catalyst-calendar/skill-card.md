## Description:

Catalyst Calendar generates a Chinese-language forward-looking market catalyst calendar covering macro policy, earnings, industry events, and technical market events.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dzy11650](https://clawhub.ai/user/dzy11650)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors and market analysts use this skill to scan an upcoming time window, usually 14 days, for market-moving events and receive an impact-ranked Markdown calendar with investor response suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial catalyst calendars can contain incomplete or stale event information.

Mitigation: Verify high-impact events against official schedules or primary market sources before acting on the report.

Risk: Saved reports and reminder notes may expose a user's market watchlist or investment focus.

Mitigation: Review or disable report and reminder behavior when working with sensitive watchlists or when only an ephemeral answer is needed.

Risk: Investor response suggestions may be mistaken for individualized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat suggestions as general planning prompts and apply independent financial review before making decisions.
