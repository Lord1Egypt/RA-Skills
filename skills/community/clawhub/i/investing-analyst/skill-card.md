## Description:

Thesis-driven equity and options trading analyst. Use when: (1) evaluating a trade idea before entering a position, (2) analyzing options pricing via IV vs Historical Volatility, (3) planning earnings catalyst plays, (4) reviewing a losing position and deciding whether to hold or exit, (5) checking for emotional/FOMO trading patterns, (6) running a pre-trade checklist. NOT for: general financial education, portfolio-level asset allocation, or tax advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and investing-focused agents use this skill to structure equity and options trade analysis before entry, review losing positions, compare implied and historical volatility, and check for emotional or FOMO-driven trading patterns. It is decision-support only and is not a substitute for independent financial advice or verified market data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake trade-structuring output for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as decision-support, verify market data independently, and apply personal suitability and risk controls outside the skill.

Risk: Options analysis can rely on stale or incorrect volatility, pricing, or catalyst assumptions.

Mitigation: Verify implied volatility, historical volatility, expected move, and current pricing with trusted market-data sources before acting.

Risk: Users may expose brokerage credentials or account details while extending the workflow.

Mitigation: Do not connect or share brokerage credentials unless a separate trusted tool explicitly requires and secures them.
