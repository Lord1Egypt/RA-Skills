## Use Case:

Platform operations leads and data analysts use this skill to structure community group-buying KPIs, analyze growth funnels and retention, segment users with RFM, and run basic A/B test and attribution calculations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Applying segmentation, geofencing, or cross-platform data ideas may involve customer, behavioral, or location data.

Mitigation: Confirm authorization, minimize data use, and review privacy and regional compliance requirements before applying the guidance.

Risk: The A/B test and sample-size examples may be used directly for business rollout decisions.

Mitigation: Validate formulas and calculator output against a trusted statistics library and analyst review before relying on results.

Risk: Industry benchmarks and platform examples may not match a user's current market or operating model.

Mitigation: Recalibrate thresholds with current internal data before using them as decision rules.
