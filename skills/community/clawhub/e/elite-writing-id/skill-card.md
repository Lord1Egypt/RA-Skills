## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can encourage remembering writing preferences, which may accidentally include sensitive drafts, credentials, or private personal data.

Mitigation: Review memory behavior before use and redact sensitive content before storing or reusing preferences.

Risk: Generated persuasive writing may include unverified claims, statistics, testimonials, or overly manipulative framing if source facts are weak.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims before publication and keep persuasion grounded in evidence and user-provided truth.
