## Description:

Create a short visual clip guided by a song's mood, rhythm, and visual concept, turning a music excerpt and visual direction into a cinematic promo clip or animated cover art.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Musicians, labels, and music creators use the skill to plan and create short promotional visuals from a finished song or excerpt. It supports teasers, album promotion, and cover art animation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent installation telemetry, permissions beyond music video creation, and a shared credential that can retain broad access; scan coverage is partial.

Mitigation: Consider avoiding installation until the reported telemetry and broad permissions are clarified and narrowed.
