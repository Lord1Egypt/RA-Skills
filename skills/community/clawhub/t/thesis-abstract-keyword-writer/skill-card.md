## Description:

Write academically rigorous Abstract and Keywords sections for theses, dissertations, undergraduate papers, graduation theses, journal-style papers, research reports, and proposals from a user's complete thesis, chapter draft, abstract draft, results, discussion, conclusion, contribution, and school requirements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junwugit](https://clawhub.ai/user/junwugit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and academic writing assistants use this skill to draft, polish, structure, translate, shorten, or expand thesis Abstract/摘要 and Keywords/关键词 sections from user-supplied thesis material and requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The user may provide confidential, embargoed, or institution-restricted thesis material.

Mitigation: Process only material the user is comfortable sharing in the agent environment, as recommended by the server-resolved security guidance.

Risk: Incomplete source material can lead to unsupported academic claims if findings, methods, data, or implications are invented.

Mitigation: Use the skill's evidence boundaries: avoid fabricating unsupported details and ask for missing thesis information or provide a provisional framework.
