## Description:

Guides users through recording life stories, collecting local and web materials, and generating a personal biography-style Life Book.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sunnyyao2222-eng](https://clawhub.ai/user/sunnyyao2222-eng)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to preserve personal life events in structured chapter files and generate a Markdown biography. It is intended for personal biography and life-story organization rather than diary, scratch-note, or temporary memo workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill automatically stores sensitive life-story content in local files.

Mitigation: Use it only with consent, avoid sensitive or third-party details unless they are intentionally included, and review chapter files before sharing generated books.

Risk: Imported URLs and local paths may add untrusted or unintended material to the Life Book workspace.

Mitigation: Import only trusted files or URLs and inspect imported materials before using them in generated output.

Risk: The security guidance says the [私密] marker should not be relied on to filter generated books in this version.

Mitigation: Manually remove or redact private content from chapter files and generated Markdown before distribution.
