## Description:

Establish a nonprofit coordinator remit, privacy and consent boundaries, operating briefs and honest integration readiness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jjjhenriksen](https://clawhub.ai/user/jjjhenriksen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Nonprofit organizers and coordinators use this skill to define authorized work, privacy and consent boundaries, and the scope of event, volunteer, and organization knowledge tasks. It produces a source-labelled operating brief and integration readiness plan for human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A planning brief could expose sensitive volunteer details or imply consent or authority that was never granted.

Mitigation: Use only relevant authorized records, collect minimal information, keep private access needs out of shared briefs, and require current scoped authority before external actions.
