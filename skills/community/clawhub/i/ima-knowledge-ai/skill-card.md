## Description:

AI content brief, script, and outline generator for video, image, and audio production that provides research guidance for workflow design, model selection, parameter optimization, and visual consistency.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dai-shuo](https://clawhub.ai/user/dai-shuo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, content creators, designers, video producers, developers, and AI agents use this skill to plan multimedia content workflows, draft briefs, scripts, and outlines, choose IMA Studio models, tune parameters, and maintain visual or brand consistency before calling execution skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Model-selection guidance may steer sensitive or real-person image requests toward providers described as more permissive.

Mitigation: Review the model-selection guidance before installation and apply the strictest applicable content-safety rules regardless of provider.

Risk: The skill relies on model and parameter guidance that can change after publication.

Mitigation: Verify current model availability, costs, and parameter support with live provider or IMA Studio model listings before acting on recommendations.
