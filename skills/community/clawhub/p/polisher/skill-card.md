## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to rewrite user-provided text for clearer grammar, punctuation, phrasing, and readability while preserving the original meaning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Polishing can unintentionally alter exact wording, tone, or meaning when precision matters.

Mitigation: Review the polished text before use and compare it with the source text for meaning preservation.

Risk: The skill may trigger on broad rewrite or polish requests where the user expects exact wording control.

Mitigation: Use it for wording, grammar, punctuation, and readability improvements, and ask for clarification when the requested change is ambiguous.
