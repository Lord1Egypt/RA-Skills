## Description:

Create an evidence-backed weekly monitor of newly published antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) patent applications. Use when a user asks for an ADC patent weekly, new WO/PCT publications, emerging ADC targets or technologies, priority claim comparisons, or a shortlist of records that warrant claim review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

IP, R&D, competitive-intelligence, and business-development teams use this skill to monitor newly published ADC patent applications, screen out predictable noise, and identify records that warrant claim review. It supports monitoring and triage, not legal freedom-to-operate, infringement, validity, patentability, ownership, clinical, or commercial conclusions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated patent-monitoring reports could be mistaken for legal, clinical, or commercial advice.

Mitigation: Review outputs as monitoring intelligence only and route freedom-to-operate, infringement, validity, patentability, ownership, clinical, or commercial decisions to qualified reviewers.

Risk: Live retrieval may access authorized patent, drug, or pharmaceutical news connectors.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with intended PatSnap connectors or supplied patent exports, confirm connector authorization, and avoid placing API keys or credentials in reports.
