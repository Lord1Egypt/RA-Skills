## Description:

A local AI accounting assistant for recording income and expenses, querying transactions, generating reports, and analyzing financial activity with local data storage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chuhemiao](https://clawhub.ai/user/chuhemiao)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Individuals and small-team users can use this skill to maintain local income and expense records, search transactions, and generate summaries or reports from their accounting data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial records are stored and edited locally, so accidental updates, deletes, or data-directory mistakes could affect the user's accounting history.

Mitigation: Choose the local accounting data directory before use, keep independent backups for important records, and review update or delete requests before applying them.
