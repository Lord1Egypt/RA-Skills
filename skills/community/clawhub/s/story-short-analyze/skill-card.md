## Description: <br>
Analyzes legally provided short web fiction by extracting story core, plot structure, emotional arcs, reversals, writing techniques, and reusable report artifacts for downstream writing. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[worldwonderer](https://clawhub.ai/user/worldwonderer) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, editors, and agent workflows use this skill to deconstruct user-provided short fiction into structured Markdown analysis, plot-node notes, technique notes, and JSON metadata that can guide follow-on short-story drafting. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill saves a local copy of the user-provided story text under the analysis output directory. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only with material you own or are allowed to analyze, and delete the generated original-text backup when it is no longer needed. <br>
