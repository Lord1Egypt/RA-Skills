## Description:

Helps users prepare OEM/ODM manufacturing inquiries for Genichi Tool Co., Ltd. for hand tools, hardware, metal products, CNC machining, forging, custom tooling, and quote requests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and business teams use this skill to prepare manufacturing quote requests, including drawings, specifications, quantities, timelines, certification needs, and packaging or branding preferences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Manufacturing drawings, CAD files, specifications, regulated technical data, or customer-confidential details may be externally disclosed when sent for a quote.

Mitigation: Verify the vendor and email address, confirm NDA/privacy/export-control requirements, and share only the minimum authorized information needed for the initial quote.
