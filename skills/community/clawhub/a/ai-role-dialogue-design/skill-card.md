## Description:

This skill helps agents design Chinese higher-mathematics classroom dialogues using five recurring roles, cognitive conflict, and structured introduction, development, and wrap-up patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daigxok](https://clawhub.ai/user/daigxok)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Educators, course authors, and agents preparing higher-mathematics lessons use this skill to generate multi-role classroom dialogue that introduces topics, resolves misconceptions, breaks down difficult concepts, and creates supporting exercises.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is optimized for Chinese-language higher-mathematics classroom dialogue and may not directly fit English, bilingual, or non-mathematics lessons.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to adapt the language, roles, and examples for the target audience and subject before using the output.

Risk: Generated classroom dialogue may contain mathematical explanations or examples that need instructional review.

Mitigation: Have a qualified instructor review mathematical statements, examples, and exercises before classroom use.
