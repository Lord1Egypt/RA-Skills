## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Applying funnel guidance to real customer analytics can expose unnecessary personal or sensitive business data.

Mitigation: Keep analytics and customer data scoped to the specific project, follow applicable privacy rules, and avoid sharing unnecessary personal or sensitive data.

Risk: Business-process recommendations may be adopted without confirming they fit the team, platform rules, or local compliance requirements.

Mitigation: Review the proposed SOP, metrics, and rollout steps with responsible business and compliance owners before operational use.
