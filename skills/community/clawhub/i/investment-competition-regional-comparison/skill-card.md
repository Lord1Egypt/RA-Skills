## Description:

Generates a Chinese regional investment-promotion comparison report using public, legal, and verifiable data across industry base, policy support, business environment, costs, factor availability, park capacity, enterprise clustering, project landing efficiency, and differentiated competition strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and business analysts use this skill to compare multiple regions for investment promotion, project siting, park positioning, and policy optimization. It produces structured conclusions, competitiveness scoring, regional strengths and weaknesses, and practical differentiation strategies for Chinese-language reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce regional rankings, competitiveness scores, or policy recommendations that depend on the availability and freshness of public data.

Mitigation: Verify sourced data, confidence labels, and scoring assumptions before using outputs for policy, investment, or site-selection decisions.

Risk: Some policy兑现, project-landing, cost, or factor-availability evidence may be incomplete or uneven across regions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision-support analysis and request explicit caveats where regional data is limited or only weakly supported.
