## Description:

Generates up to three high-ROI, actionable ideas tailored to a user's goal, with rationale and practical starting steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, creators, founders, and operators use IdeaForge to brainstorm concise, practical opportunities around a goal and quickly compare why each idea may work and how to start.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Money-making or ROI suggestions may be infeasible, noncompliant, or misleading if treated as advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as brainstorming prompts and independently verify feasibility, costs, legality, and platform rules before acting.
