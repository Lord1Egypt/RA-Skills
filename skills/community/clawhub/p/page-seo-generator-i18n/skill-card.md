## Description:

Generates culturally adapted SEO landing-page copy in Japanese, Chinese, and other non-English languages from user-provided page details and TDK.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[notegpt-official](https://clawhub.ai/user/notegpt-official)

## Use Case:

Content teams and marketers use this skill to create localized SEO landing-page content for Japanese, Chinese, and other non-English markets. It collects the target language, keyword, supplied TDK, page path, and brand details before producing structured Markdown copy.
