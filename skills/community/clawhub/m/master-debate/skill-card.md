## Description:

Generates structured, multi-round adversarial Buddhist master debates with selected or inferred masters, source-citation constraints, and a neutral closing summary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xr843](https://clawhub.ai/user/xr843)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent builders use this skill when they want a structured, adversarial, multi-round debate between Buddhist masters on doctrinal topics, followed by a neutral synthesis rather than a verdict.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated religious debate content may overstate theological positions or cite sources inaccurately.

Mitigation: Review outputs for theological neutrality and citation accuracy, especially before external publication.

Risk: The skill may read local reference files for citations, so outputs can be limited by incomplete or stale local source material.

Mitigation: Confirm cited source identifiers against the skill's available references before relying on doctrinal claims.
