## Description:

Search and summarize research papers in robotics and related fields, including recent work from venues such as TRO, IJRR, RAL, Science Robotics, CVPR, and ICLR.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers, developers, and engineers use Paper Scout to find recent robotics and AI papers, consolidate scholarly-source results, and produce structured literature digests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scholarly-source searches may reveal research interests through submitted queries.

Mitigation: Review the planned search terms and sources before use, especially for confidential or unpublished research topics.

Risk: Google Scholar backup collection may require interactive browser automation.

Mitigation: Use browser automation only in an appropriate session and rely on CrossRef or another approved source when interactive scraping is not acceptable.

Risk: The default dated Markdown digest path may place research summaries on the Desktop of a shared machine.

Mitigation: Review or change the output path before running the skill on shared systems.
