## Description: <br>
Helps agents classify growth initiatives as incremental, leap, or split, then align goals, metrics, resources, stage gates, and governance to the chosen growth mode. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external strategy teams, and AI agents use this skill to diagnose stalled or transformative growth initiatives and produce a growth-mode diagnostic that separates incremental execution from leap-stage planning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may influence strategic or financial decisions through advisory growth-mode framing. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support and independently verify important strategic, financial, market, and resource assumptions before acting. <br>
