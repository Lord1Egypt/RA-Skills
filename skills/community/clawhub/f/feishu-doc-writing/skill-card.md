Developers, employees, and agent operators use this skill to turn chats, meetings, and research notes into structured Feishu documents that preserve context, hierarchy, and key conclusions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill recommends granting Feishu document access to loosely defined roles such as bosses, group members, and requesters.

Mitigation: Review exact recipients and access levels before sharing, and require explicit confirmation before granting or changing document permissions.

Risk: Generated documents may include sensitive meeting, research, or internal operational details.

Mitigation: Check generated content for API keys, internal system addresses, unpublished data, and other sensitive information before external sharing.
