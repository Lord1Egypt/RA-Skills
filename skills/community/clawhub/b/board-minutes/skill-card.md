## Description:

Write formal board meeting minutes from an agenda, notes, transcript, or discussion summary, producing structured minutes with attendees, agenda items, resolutions, decisions, an action register, and approval-ready wording.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, company secretaries, governance leads, and board support teams use this skill to draft formal board or committee minutes from agendas, notes, transcripts, or discussion summaries for review and approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated minutes may include confidential, legal, financial, employment, conflict-of-interest, or regulated information from the source material.

Mitigation: Review the draft before circulation and route sensitive items to the appropriate governance, legal, or secretarial owner.

Risk: Missing source details can leave official-record fields such as quorum, attendees, votes, resolutions, or action owners unconfirmed.

Mitigation: Keep unknown items marked for confirmation and verify them before approving or circulating the minutes.
