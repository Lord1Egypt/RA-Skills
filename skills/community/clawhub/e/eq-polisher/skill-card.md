## Description:

Eq Polisher helps agents rewrite Chinese messages into tactful wording and draft context-aware replies for praise, difficult questions, awkward moments, and incoming messages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chiling7](https://clawhub.ai/user/chiling7)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees can use this skill for Chinese-language communication polishing, including softening direct wording, responding to praise or awkward situations, and drafting appropriate replies to incoming messages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated wording may be unsuitable for sensitive work or relationship contexts if sent without review.

Mitigation: Review and adjust the suggested message before sending, especially where tone, commitments, or interpersonal stakes matter.
