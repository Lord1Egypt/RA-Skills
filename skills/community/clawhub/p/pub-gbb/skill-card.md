## Description:

Searches Chinese and international academic literature through the SmartLib Open Platform, returning bibliographic details and available full-text downloads.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j-levee](https://clawhub.ai/user/j-levee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External researchers, students, and other literature searchers can use the skill to find Chinese and international papers, patents, standards, and other scholarly records, inspect bibliographic details, and request available full text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence marks the package suspicious and reports a gateway authentication secret in the package; scan coverage was partial.

Mitigation: Verify the packaged credential before use, remove or rotate it if it is genuine, and keep active credentials in protected configuration.

Risk: The security evidence notes that research queries and account activity may be shared externally or associated with a configured email address.

Mitigation: Tell users that their email and search requests may be sent to the service, and obtain their agreement before registering or searching.
