## Description:

Fanbozhe Clean helps an agent provide guided decision review by default and direct red-team critique when the user explicitly asks to be challenged.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to test business ideas, plans, and decisions with a structured opposing view. It is suited for decision review and adversarial critique, not crisis support or emotionally vulnerable situations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce direct or harsh critique that may be inappropriate for crisis, mental-health, or emotionally vulnerable situations.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when the user explicitly wants adversarial critique, and avoid invoking it for crisis support or emotionally vulnerable conversations.

Risk: Red-team critique can overstate certainty when the user provides incomplete or embellished context.

Mitigation: Ask for concrete facts before giving a strong judgment, and state uncertainty when evidence is incomplete.
