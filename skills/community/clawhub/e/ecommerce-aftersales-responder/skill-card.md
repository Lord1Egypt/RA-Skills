## Description:

Generates standardized Chinese-language ecommerce after-sales customer service replies for return or exchange requests, logistics issues, and compensation negotiations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Customer support teams and agents handling Chinese-language ecommerce after-sales interactions use this skill to draft brand-consistent replies for return or exchange requests, shipping exceptions, and compensation requests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer order identifiers, logistics details, or damage photos could expose personal or sensitive support data if handled outside approved channels.

Mitigation: Use the skill only inside approved ecommerce support workflows and apply local privacy rules for minimizing, storing, and sharing customer data.

Risk: Refund, replacement, shipping upgrade, or compensation language could exceed brand policy or authorized limits.

Mitigation: Require operator review against current brand policy and escalate requests that exceed approved compensation thresholds.

Risk: Replies can be inaccurate if order status, carrier status, return eligibility, or customer history has not been verified.

Mitigation: Confirm order, product, carrier, and policy facts before sending the generated response.
