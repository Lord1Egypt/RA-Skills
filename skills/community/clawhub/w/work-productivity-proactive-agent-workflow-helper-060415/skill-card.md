## Description:

Helps AI-agent users and skill maintainers turn Proactive Agent-style productivity demand into practical workflows, checklists, implementation support, and validation notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to convert Proactive Agent-style productivity needs into concrete local workflows, artifacts, checklists, analysis, code changes, or decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for generic productivity or task-planning requests because its invocation wording is broad.

Mitigation: Narrow the trigger terms or require explicit skill selection when precise routing is important.

Risk: Workflow outputs may contain incorrect or overgeneralized plans, checklists, or implementation advice.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the user's success criteria and validate any proposed code, commands, or configuration before use.
