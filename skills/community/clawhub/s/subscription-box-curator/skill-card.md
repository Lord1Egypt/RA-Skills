## Description:

Curate recurring subscription box concepts, item mixes, pricing tiers, add-on ideas, and replenishment warnings for DTC brands, merchandising teams, and subscription operators.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

DTC brands, merchandising teams, and subscription operators use this skill to plan monthly or quarterly subscription boxes with theme options, item architecture, indicative pricing guidance, add-on ideas, and operational caveats.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing, retention, inventory, and supplier recommendations are directional rather than live business intelligence.

Mitigation: Review the generated plan against current SKU margins, inventory cover, supplier constraints, shipping costs, and customer data before acting on it.

Risk: Sensitive customer, margin, or supplier details may appear in prompts or generated planning briefs.

Mitigation: Review sensitive business details before sharing them with the agent or forwarding generated output.
