## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Freelancers, agencies, and automation developers can use this skill to find marketplace jobs, draft tailored proposals, track applications, and contact potential clients for RPA, automation, web scraping, and data-entry work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can direct an agent to apply for jobs or send outreach messages without clear confirmation controls.

Mitigation: Require explicit user approval before submitting applications, sending marketplace messages, or contacting leads.

Risk: Lead-tracking data may include marketplace contacts, job details, and application status.

Mitigation: Clarify where tracking data is stored and avoid recording unnecessary personal or client information.

Risk: Generated proposals or outreach may be inaccurate, misleading, or poorly matched to a job posting.

Mitigation: Review and edit every generated proposal or outreach message before use.
