## Description:

Estimates daily feed intake per livestock individual from continuous feeder videos by tracking the change of feed remaining in the trough, and outputs intake trend with anomaly alerts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Livestock and farm operators use this skill to estimate individual or group feed intake from feeder images or videos, review intake trends and anomaly alerts, and retrieve prior analysis reports.
