## Description:

Applica la brand identity EmmeStudio a qualsiasi output visivo o testuale.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external-facing document authors use this skill to produce EmmeStudio-branded documents, presentations, letters, one-pagers, reports, and quotes. It guides typography, colors, logo usage, document structure, and standard quote clauses for formal internal and client-facing materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The trigger language is broad and may apply EmmeStudio branding when a user did not intend it.

Mitigation: Ask users to state clearly whether EmmeStudio branding is desired for each document or presentation.

Risk: The skill references fonts, templates, and guidelines stored in Google Drive.

Mitigation: Access those resources only through authorized accounts and confirm permission before using non-public brand assets.
