## Description:

Retrieves latest and searched crypto, blockchain, and Web3 news articles and flash updates from Marsbit, including full article content on request.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and AI-agent users use this skill to fetch, search, and summarize Marsbit crypto, blockchain, and Web3 articles and flash updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and article lookup requests may be sent to Marsbit's external API.

Mitigation: Avoid putting secrets, private wallet details, or sensitive personal information in search terms or article lookup requests.
