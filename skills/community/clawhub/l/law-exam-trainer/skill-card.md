## Description:

Law Exam Trainer helps users turn Chinese legal exam videos, documents, and handouts into practice question banks with immediate feedback, wrong-answer explanations, and knowledge-point references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lujun2508](https://clawhub.ai/user/lujun2508)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners preparing for China's legal professional qualification exam use this skill to convert study materials into multiple-choice, multi-select, and true/false practice sets. It supports practice sessions with immediate answer feedback and mistake review tied to relevant legal provisions or knowledge points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided legal study materials may include client-confidential, personal, or copyrighted content.

Mitigation: Before installing or using the skill, confirm that the save directory is changed to the user's own workspace and avoid uploading sensitive or copyrighted materials unless transcription, OCR, and analysis are acceptable.
