## Description:

Miaoji Video Script Pro helps Amazon sellers plan multi-length video script matrices with platform-specific hooks, A/B test angles, and thumbnail copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangm-a3](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangm-a3)

## Use Case:

External sellers and marketing teams use this skill to turn product, audience, platform, and duration inputs into video content matrices for Amazon, TikTok, YouTube, and independent storefronts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Predicted metrics and benchmark numbers may be mistaken for guaranteed business outcomes.

Mitigation: Treat generated metrics as planning estimates and validate scripts against real campaign data before budget or production decisions.

Risk: Some referenced documentation files are not included in the package.

Mitigation: Use the included README and skill instructions as the available documentation, and confirm missing references before relying on unsupported workflow details.

Risk: The skill provides strategy and script guidance but does not create finished video assets.

Mitigation: Review scripts, visual claims, platform fit, and compliance requirements before publishing or commissioning production.
