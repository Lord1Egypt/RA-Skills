An AI-powered podcast production skill for end-to-end episode creation, including scripting, guest preparation, audio production, show notes, distribution optimization, analytics, and content repurposing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Podcast creators, producers, marketers, and agent users can use this skill to plan, script, record or synthesize, edit, package, distribute, promote, and repurpose podcast episodes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Guest research, transcripts, quotes, and audience analytics can involve personal information.

Mitigation: Use appropriate consent, avoid collecting unnecessary personal details, and limit retained information to what is needed for production.

Risk: Generated show notes, clips, quotes, or promotional copy may misrepresent speakers or episode content.

Mitigation: Review public-facing assets against the recording or transcript before publishing.

Risk: Audio production and distribution guidance may not match every platform, brand, or legal requirement.

Mitigation: Check final settings, metadata, music rights, sponsorship disclosures, and publishing requirements before release.
