## Description:

Automates generation of short-video storyboard scripts by opening DeepSeek in Chrome, enabling Deep Thinking and Smart Search modes, and converting user-provided narration into segmented scene descriptions with matching narration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[infiniteask](https://clawhub.ai/user/infiniteask)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to turn narration text into a short-video storyboard with numbered shots, visual descriptions, and aligned narration segments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Narration text is sent to DeepSeek through a browser session.

Mitigation: Do not submit confidential, proprietary, regulated, or highly personal narration unless the user accepts DeepSeek processing for that content.

Risk: Generated storyboard content may change or misrepresent the user's intended creative direction.

Mitigation: Review the generated storyboard before production use and confirm that narration segments remain accurate and appropriate.

Risk: The skill relies on Chrome access to DeepSeek and specific UI modes.

Mitigation: Confirm the browser session, DeepSeek availability, and selected modes before submitting narration.
