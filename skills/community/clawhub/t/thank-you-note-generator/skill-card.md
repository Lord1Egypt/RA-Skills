Write heartfelt and professional thank-you notes for interviews, gifts, help, business partnerships, and other occasions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and professionals use this skill to draft thank-you notes for interviews, gifts, personal help, business partnerships, text messages, and social media comments. It helps adapt gratitude messages by tone, length, occasion, and specific personal details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated notes may include inaccurate, overly personal, or context-inappropriate wording.

Mitigation: Review and personalize each note before sending, especially for professional or sensitive relationships.

Risk: Prompts may include sensitive personal or business details.

Mitigation: Provide only the details needed for the note and avoid unnecessary confidential information.
