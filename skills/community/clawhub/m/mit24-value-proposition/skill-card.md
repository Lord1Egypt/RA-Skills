## Description:

Guides users through MIT 24 Steps phase 2 tasks for value proposition design, value quantification, potential customer identification, core value definition, and business model canvas creation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External founders, startup teams, and business planners use this skill to turn a target customer segment into a quantified value proposition, interview plan, one-sentence value statement, and business model canvas.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The prospect-list workflow can collect personal contact details for potential customers.

Mitigation: Limit entries to necessary business outreach details, use consent-based or lawfully sourced contacts, and redact personal identifiers when a profile or role description is enough.
