## Description:

Structured desk research workflow for market, company, policy, product, and competitor questions using public information, source triangulation, and evidence-based synthesis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[draco-kzn](https://clawhub.ai/user/draco-kzn)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, founders, product teams, and policy or market observers use this skill to turn secondary public-source research questions into concise reports with sourced claims, confidence tags, risks, falsification checks, and next verification steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research outputs can be misleading if important claims rely on stale, weak, single-source, or unattributed evidence.

Mitigation: Require source dates, reliability labels, at least two independent sources for key claims, explicit uncertainty, and falsification checks before delivery.

Risk: Some reference guidance is written in Chinese, which can affect output language expectations.

Mitigation: Set the preferred output language explicitly when using the skill.
