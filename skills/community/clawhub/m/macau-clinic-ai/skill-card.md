## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill promotes AI handling of patient records and messaging workflows without clear privacy, consent, or compliance safeguards.

Mitigation: Use it for real clinic planning only after local healthcare privacy and legal review, explicit patient consent design, compliant AI and messaging vendors, secure storage, access controls, and retention/deletion policies are defined.

Risk: Identifiable clinical details could be sent through general chat or AI services during workflow design or operation.

Mitigation: Do not send identifiable patient information to chat, AI, or messaging services unless the deployment has been assessed and approved for that clinical use.
