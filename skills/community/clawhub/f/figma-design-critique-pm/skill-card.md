## Description:

Runs a PM-perspective design critique focused on product outcomes and user goals, not aesthetics. Use when asked for a PM design critique, a product review of a Figma design, or feedback from a product perspective without needing to be a designer. Produces structured outcome-based feedback tied to user goals, business metrics, and requirement coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product managers and product teams use this skill to critique Figma designs from a product perspective, checking whether screens support user goals, business outcomes, requirements, and key metrics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share product goals, requirements, metrics, or design details while requesting critique.

Mitigation: Provide only business information that is approved for disclosure in the current agent environment.

Risk: Feedback may rely on hypotheses when no research, metric, or constraint evidence is supplied.

Mitigation: Keep evidence basis explicit and validate important product decisions with appropriate user research or product data.
