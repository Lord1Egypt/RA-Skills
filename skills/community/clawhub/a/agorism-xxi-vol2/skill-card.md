## Use Case:

External users use this skill as a reference for querying topics, essays, and frameworks from Agorism in the 21st Century: A Philosophical Journal Vol. 2. It helps agents summarize chapter material and apply concepts such as dark forest anonymity, death athletic, squad wealth, minor calendars, sousveillance, necroprimitivism critique, and lunar epistemology.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill frames privacy, treasury, deletion, and governance ideas through agorist and lunarpunk source material.

Mitigation: Treat those concepts as philosophical reference material, not legal, financial, compliance, or data-retention advice; require explicit user confirmation before applying them to real systems or records.

Risk: The source volume contains internal tensions and ideological claims that can be flattened into overconfident recommendations.

Mitigation: Preserve chapter-level attribution and disagreements when using the material, and combine it with project-specific context before operational decisions.
