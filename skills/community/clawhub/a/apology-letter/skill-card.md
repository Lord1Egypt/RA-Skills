## Description:

Writes sincere, accountable apology messages for customers, groups, or public audiences, including acknowledgement, responsibility, empathy, concrete remedy, prevention, and a short version.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external-facing teams, and support writers use this skill to draft complete apologies after mistakes, complaints, or public incidents. It helps produce ready-to-send apology text with assumptions and confirmation notes when details are incomplete.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for general apology-writing requests and steer the answer toward a full apology format.

Mitigation: Review the drafted apology for the intended audience, situation, and communication channel before sending.

Risk: The skill may infer impact, remedy, or prevention details from an incomplete brief.

Mitigation: Confirm bracketed or labeled assumptions, especially names, dates, compensation, and prevention commitments.
