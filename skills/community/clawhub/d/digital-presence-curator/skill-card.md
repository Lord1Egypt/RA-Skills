## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat reflective digital-wellbeing output as professional mental health, legal, business, parenting, or medical advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for self-reflection and personal development only, and consult qualified professionals for health, mental health, legal, business, parenting, or medical concerns.

Risk: User prompts may include sensitive details about digital habits, relationships, work, family, or online identity.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive personal details unless the user is comfortable with them appearing in the session output.
