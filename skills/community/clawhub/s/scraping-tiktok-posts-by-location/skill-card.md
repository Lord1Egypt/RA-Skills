## Description:

Extracts TikTok posts tagged at a specific location using apidojo's TikTok Location Scraper on Apify, returning post text, engagement counts, channel details, hashtags, timestamps, and video links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[apidojo-io](https://clawhub.ai/user/apidojo-io)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, local marketing teams, and event researchers use this skill to collect public TikTok posts associated with a specific TikTok place or location URL for downstream review and analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can export location-linked public TikTok creator data at broad scale.

Mitigation: Use it only for lawful, user-directed collection, set explicit result limits, and avoid targeting or monitoring individuals.

Risk: Use of TikTok and Apify data may be subject to platform terms, privacy rules, and billing constraints.

Mitigation: Confirm compliance with TikTok, Apify, and applicable privacy requirements before running the skill, and protect APIFY_TOKEN as a billing-capable credential.
