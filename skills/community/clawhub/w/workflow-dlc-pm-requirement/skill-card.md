## Description:

Guides product managers from an initial product requirement idea to a review-ready PRD using project classification, material readiness checks, structured dialogue, PRD drafting, and gated iteration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mistyhuqwq-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/mistyhuqwq-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers and product teams use this skill to structure PRD creation for new projects, iterative requirements, and small optimizations. It helps gather prerequisite materials, clarify goals and constraints, draft PRD sections, and drive unresolved items toward review readiness.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may export PRD content to Feishu by default.

Mitigation: Review before installation and ask the agent to confirm before creating external documents.

Risk: The skill may persist project details and document links in local workflow logs.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential roadmap, partner, or customer data unless logging is disabled, redirected, or sanitized.
