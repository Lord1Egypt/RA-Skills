## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad Google, Workspace, CLI, and bug-fix triggers may activate this workflow helper for unrelated productivity tasks.

Mitigation: Confirm that the requested task is a Gog-style Google Workspace workflow, reliability, setup, or adjacent-skill task before applying the skill output.

Risk: Workflow, setup, or safety advice may be incomplete for a user's specific environment.

Mitigation: Review generated steps against the user's stated constraints and validate the result before deployment or reuse.
