## Description:

Guides agents in creating, optimizing, or structuring Privacy Policy pages covering data collection, cookies, user rights, page placement, indexing, and legal review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External site owners, marketers, product teams, and developers use this skill to draft or refine privacy policy page structure and content for websites or applications. It helps identify jurisdiction, business model, collected data, cookies, user rights, third-party sharing, contact details, and the need for legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Privacy-policy drafts may be incomplete or inaccurate for a specific jurisdiction, business model, or data-processing practice.

Mitigation: Treat generated content as drafting support and review the final policy with qualified counsel for the relevant jurisdiction and business practices.

Risk: The broad privacy-related trigger may activate for adjacent legal-page requests.

Mitigation: Confirm whether the user needs a privacy policy page and redirect broader legal overview or terms requests to a more specific skill when available.

Risk: GDPR, CCPA, cookie, and data-rights guidance can become misleading if the agent lacks details about data collection, third parties, retention, and user rights.

Mitigation: Gather jurisdiction, business model, data categories, cookie and analytics usage, third-party recipients, retention needs, and contact information before drafting.
