## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill relies on current web search and policy interpretation, so generated policy guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or misapplied.

Mitigation: Verify cited policy sources before using outputs for official, legal, fiscal, or procurement decisions.

Risk: The skill can discuss fiscal investment, data rights, cross-border data flow, authorization operations, and procurement-sensitive public-sector topics.

Mitigation: Route outputs involving these topics through local legal, finance, development-and-reform, and data-governance review before action.
