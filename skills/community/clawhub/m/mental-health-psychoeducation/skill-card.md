## Description:

Provides educational psychoeducation on common mental health conditions, therapy modalities, coping techniques, psychiatric medications, and self-assessment frameworks while emphasizing that it is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, therapy, or crisis support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to learn about mental health concepts, therapy options, coping strategies, psychiatric medication categories, self-assessment frameworks, and when to seek professional help. It is for education only and should not be used as a substitute for licensed professional care or crisis services.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake psychoeducation for professional mental-health advice, diagnosis, therapy, medication guidance, or crisis support.

Mitigation: Preserve the educational-only disclaimers and direct users to licensed mental health professionals for diagnosis, treatment planning, medication decisions, and care decisions.

Risk: Crisis-related content may be insufficient for users at immediate risk of self-harm or harm.

Mitigation: Direct users in crisis to local emergency services or appropriate local crisis lines; US users can call or text 988.
