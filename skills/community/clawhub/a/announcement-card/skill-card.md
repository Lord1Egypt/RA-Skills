## Description:

Writes short, punchy announcements designed for image cards or social posts about launches, milestones, features, hires, funding, or wins.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, product, recruiting, and community teams use this skill to turn announcement inputs into a concise card-ready message with a headline, one-line summary, proof points, call to action, alternate headlines, caption, and channel note.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Announcement drafts may include confidential or embargoed information intended for public channels.

Mitigation: Review content before external sharing and remove confidential details, unapproved claims, or embargoed information.

Risk: Promotional copy can overstate claims or omit concrete support.

Mitigation: Check that the final card leads with the actual news, includes at least one concrete proof point, and avoids unsupported hype.
