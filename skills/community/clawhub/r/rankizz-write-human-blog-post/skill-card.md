## Description:

Research, outline, draft, edit, or rewrite a source-backed blog post that sounds natural and useful rather than formulaic or AI-generated.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[prosamik](https://clawhub.ai/user/prosamik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and content teams use this skill to research and produce source-backed blog posts for a defined audience. It supports the brief, evidence ledger, outline, draft, editorial review, and publication package.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Current or high-stakes claims in a draft may be inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Check claims against reliable current sources, link evidence near each claim, label uncertainty, and obtain qualified human review for high-stakes topics.

Risk: A draft may imply experience, quotations, tests, or results that the author did not provide.

Mitigation: Use only author-supplied experience and documented evidence; remove unsupported specifics before publication.
