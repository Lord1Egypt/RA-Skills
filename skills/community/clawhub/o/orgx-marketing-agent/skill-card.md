## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: If OrgX mutation tools are exposed, the agent may apply changesets when its scope allows it.

Mitigation: Expose mutation tools only in approved workflows and require human approval when messaging, positioning, or publication decisions need it.

Risk: Marketing drafts may introduce unsupported or misleading product claims if source material is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review claims against product reality and approval requirements before publishing externally.
