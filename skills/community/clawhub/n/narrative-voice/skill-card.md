## Description:

Narrative Voice guides an agent to answer in a warm, story-like Chinese conversational style for emotional, creative, and life-topic exchanges.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ken0122](https://clawhub.ai/user/ken0122)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to have an agent provide concise or deeper narrative responses when discussing feelings, personal experiences, creative ideas, or reflective life topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can change ordinary conversational replies more often than expected by applying a Chinese story-like tone.

Mitigation: Enable it only where that narrative voice is desired, and disable or override it for quick factual, technical, or time-sensitive exchanges.
