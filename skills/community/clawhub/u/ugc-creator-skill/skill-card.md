## Description:

Helps creators assess paid UGC campaign briefs, prepare campaign checklists, scripts and shot lists, check and edit their videos, coordinate posting, and track submissions and payouts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Creators running paid UGC campaigns use this skill to evaluate campaign terms, prepare and check disclosed social videos, coordinate posting, and track submissions, payouts, and time spent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creator audio may be sent to an external transcription provider.

Mitigation: Use local transcription or a supplied subtitle file, or confirm the selected provider is acceptable before sending audio.

Risk: Disclosure, upload verification, or other workflow checks may miss issues.

Mitigation: Review spoken, on-screen, caption, and platform disclosures, and confirm upload status and destination before submitting campaign posts.
