## Description:

Checks academic paper titles for keyword quality and searchability, including core terminology, generic versus proprietary terms, abbreviation use, and keyword coverage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[3492027682](https://clawhub.ai/user/3492027682)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic writing agents use this skill to evaluate whether a paper title contains searchable, discipline-appropriate keywords and to receive a keyword score, identified core terms, a searchability assessment, and improvement suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has a narrow Chinese-language scope for academic paper title keyword searchability.

Mitigation: Use it for that focused review task, and pair it with broader title editing or multilingual review when those needs are present.
