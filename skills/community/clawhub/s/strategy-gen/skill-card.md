## Description:

strategy-gen helps an agent turn goals and constraints into structured strategy canvases using SWOT, OODA, scenario planning, first principles, and OKR closure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to convert business, product, or personal planning goals into structured strategy options, tradeoffs, roadmaps, metrics, and review checkpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Strategy outputs may influence business or personal decisions without complete context.

Mitigation: Treat generated strategies as decision support and review major choices independently before acting.

Risk: Local learning notes and preferences may retain confidential business or personal details in learned_patterns.json.

Mitigation: Avoid recording sensitive details in learner notes or preferences unless local retention is intended.

Risk: Markdown output is written to a path selected by the user.

Mitigation: Choose output paths deliberately and avoid overwriting important files.
