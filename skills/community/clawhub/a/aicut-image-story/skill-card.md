## Description:

Make a narrated AI image story video with the aicut CLI: the user's script is cut into scenes, each one an AI image, read aloud with captions. Use for facts, history, horror, storytime and explainer videos built from a written script.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users can use the skill to turn a written script into a narrated, captioned AI image story video for factual, historical, horror, storytime, and explainer content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Narration scripts may be sent to aicut and selected voice providers during generation.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive or confidential text unless you are comfortable sharing it with those services.

Risk: The security scan was partial because a local path-like reference could not be matched to a bundled artifact.

Mitigation: Review the unmatched referenced content before relying on the scan assessment.
