## Description:

Reviews finished long-form technology, AI, and industry analysis through an eight-role expert panel and an evaluate-then-optimize workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors and editors use the skill to review finished technology, AI, and industry research articles for factual reliability, originality, depth, style, reader fit, and distribution before publication. It provides recommendations for the author to apply.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Originality checks may involve searching material from an unpublished or confidential draft.

Mitigation: Before sending draft-derived text to an external search service, confirm authorization; otherwise use minimal redacted factual queries or mark originality unverified and skip the search.

Risk: The security analysis is incomplete because a local reference could not be resolved, and a benign verdict does not guarantee safety.

Mitigation: Review the supplied skill and its local references before use, and limit invocation to finished long-form technology, AI, or industry analysis where the panel applies.
