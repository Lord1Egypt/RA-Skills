## Description:

Ideogram helps agents write structured prompts for design-grade social images where typography, layout, brand colors, transparency, and app-editor workflows matter.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, social-media operators, and brand-content teams use this skill to draft Ideogram prompts for quote cards, carousel covers, posters, logo concepts, ads, signage, and other social images that need accurate text and deliberate layout.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated image prompts can produce inaccurate text or misleading social content.

Mitigation: Review rendered text and composition before publishing, disclose AI-generated images, and use C2PA signing where appropriate.

Risk: Separate Ideogram or WoopSocial integrations may require API credentials or publishing permissions.

Mitigation: Review integration files before granting credentials or enabling automated posting.

Risk: Requests involving real identifiable people, copyrighted characters, logos, or unowned brand styles can create rights and policy concerns.

Mitigation: Decline those requests and offer original or owned/licensed alternatives.
