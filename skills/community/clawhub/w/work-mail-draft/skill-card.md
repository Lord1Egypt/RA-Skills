## Description:

按场景起草或改写工作邮件/企微长消息，包括请示、催办、道歉、同步进度、对外答复。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and business users use this skill to draft or rewrite Chinese-first workplace emails and long business chat messages for approvals, follow-ups, apologies, progress updates, invitations, refusals, and external replies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain incorrect commitments or sensitive business statements if the user provides incomplete facts.

Mitigation: Review drafts before sending, especially for salary, disciplinary, legal, pricing, deadline, or responsibility statements.

Risk: The generated tone or request may not match the recipient or business context.

Mitigation: Confirm the recipient, purpose, required facts, and desired tone before relying on the final message.
