## Use Case:

Marketing, operations, and partnership teams use this skill to plan, pilot, measure, and standardize KOL collaboration workflows. It helps define goals, metrics, data dashboards, review cycles, compliance checks, and reusable campaign materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: KOL campaign plans may require legal, platform, and privacy review before real-world use.

Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers confirm local legal obligations, platform rules, and personal-information handling before using the SOP for live campaigns.

Risk: General SOP guidance can be misapplied if campaign goals, metrics, or data sources are unclear.

Mitigation: Define owners, metric definitions, reporting cadence, and data minimization requirements before pilot launch, then review results before standardizing.
