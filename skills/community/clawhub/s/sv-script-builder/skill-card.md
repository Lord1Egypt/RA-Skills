## Description:

Creates timed, structured short-video scripts using proven frameworks with hooks, visual notes, and CTA for content creators and marketers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, video marketers, educators, storytellers, and scriptwriters use this skill to turn topics, raw ideas, or long-form material into short-video scripts with structure, timing, visual notes, hook options, and CTA variations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger keywords may select this skill for adjacent script or video-writing requests where a different writing skill would fit better.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants short-video script structure, timing, hooks, visual notes, or CTA guidance before applying the skill.

Risk: Template-based script guidance may produce content that needs audience, brand, factual, or platform-policy review.

Mitigation: Treat generated scripts as drafts and have the creator review final claims, tone, platform fit, and responsibility for publication.
