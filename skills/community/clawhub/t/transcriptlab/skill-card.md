## Description:

Use when the user wants a YouTube transcript, a transcript of an audio or video file, caption languages for a video, a search inside a transcript, their TranscriptLab credit balance, or to revisit a transcript they pulled before.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hussamsi2040](https://clawhub.ai/user/hussamsi2040)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users use TranscriptLab to retrieve YouTube captions, transcribe audio or video, search and revisit transcripts, check their credit usage, and get transcript export links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media submitted to TranscriptLab is processed for transcription, which may raise privacy concerns.

Mitigation: Submit media only when you are comfortable having it processed by TranscriptLab.
