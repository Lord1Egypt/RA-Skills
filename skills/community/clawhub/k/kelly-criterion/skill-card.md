## Description:

Helps an agent explain Kelly Criterion position sizing, estimate bet or trade allocation from win rate and payoff ratio, and present conservative Kelly variants.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to produce educational Kelly Criterion calculations, stock position-sizing examples, and conservative allocation guidance from estimated win rate and payoff ratio.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Position-sizing examples can be interpreted as personalized investment advice or encourage large allocations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational math, verify win-rate and payoff assumptions independently, apply conservative Kelly fractions or caps, and consult a qualified financial professional before real trading.

Risk: Historical or subjective win-rate estimates may not predict future outcomes.

Mitigation: Require current evidence for input assumptions, stress test unfavorable scenarios, and disclose that future market behavior can invalidate the calculated allocation.
