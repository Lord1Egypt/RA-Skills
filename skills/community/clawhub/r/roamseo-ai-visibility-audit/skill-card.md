## Description:

Audit how a brand shows up in AI answers about its market and deliver a short report on the few changes most likely to get it mentioned or cited, such as a third-party page to get onto, an owned page to improve, or an access problem to fix.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[roamseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/roamseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand owners and SEO marketers use this skill to understand how AI answers mention and cite their brand, identify competitor gaps, and prioritize practical content, access, and third-party visibility improvements grounded in observed answers and cited pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
