## Description:

Plan and orchestrate a product launch or social media campaign end to end, including the multi-week arc, channel plan, STORY framework, and coordinated social posts scheduled through WoopSocial.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External teams and agents use this skill to plan launch campaigns around one primary goal, three phases, credible urgency, and coordinated platform timing. It drafts the brief, week-by-week map, post plan, and social posts while keeping waitlists, email, checkout, ads, press, livestreams, and conversion measurement in their native tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coordinated public social posts could publish incorrect timing, platform choices, or copy if the plan is accepted without review.

Mitigation: Review the campaign plan, platforms, timing, and post copy before allowing connected social accounts to publish.

Risk: Launch messaging can become misleading if it uses fake urgency, invented scarcity, fabricated anticipation, or unsupported performance results.

Mitigation: Use only real scarcity, honest deadlines, verified metrics, and native analytics or store data for conversion and ROI claims.

Risk: The skill may be asked to operate systems outside its stated scope, such as waitlists, email campaigns, checkout, paid ads, press outreach, livestreams, or conversion measurement.

Mitigation: Keep those workflows in their native tools and use the skill to plan, draft, coordinate, and schedule only the social side through WoopSocial.
