## Description: <br>
Tech Manager Office is an OPC-SPARK workflow agent for technology commercialization, combining TRL maturity assessment, technical due diligence, project management, resource matching, and five role-based workflows. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Technology transfer teams, incubators, research organizations, and commercialization consultants use this skill to evaluate technology readiness, perform structured due diligence, plan conversion paths, and prepare handoff reports for projects moving from research toward market adoption. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can guide business workflows that collect sensitive project, contact, intellectual property, financial, contract, and customer data. <br>
Mitigation: Use clear privacy notices, consent requirements, retention and deletion rules, role-based access controls, and audit logs before applying it to real projects. <br>
Risk: The skill describes proactive outreach, customer replies, project handoffs, resource matching, and recurring monitoring that could affect external parties or business commitments. <br>
Mitigation: Gate autonomous daily runs, external outreach, customer replies, project handoffs, and resource matching behind explicit human approval. <br>
Risk: Server security evidence flags the release for human review before use as a governed business-process assistant. <br>
Mitigation: Install only in governed environments and review generated assessments, decisions, and recommendations before acting on them. <br>
