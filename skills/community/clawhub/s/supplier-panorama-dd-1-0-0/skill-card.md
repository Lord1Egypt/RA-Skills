## Description:

This Chinese-language skill helps agents produce supplier and enterprise due-diligence reports, including general enterprise panorama analysis and pre-award winner review for procurement and partnership decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Procurement, sourcing, compliance, and partnership teams use this skill to review suppliers or target companies from public information and receive structured due-diligence findings, risk ratings, and decision guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated legal-risk ratings or due-diligence conclusions may be incorrect, incomplete, or stale.

Mitigation: Review official source citations and obtain human legal or procurement review before making final procurement, contracting, or investment decisions.

Risk: The skill could be misused for personal investigations, retaliation, or competitive targeting.

Mitigation: Use it only for legitimate supplier, enterprise, procurement, or partnership risk review and refuse malicious or retaliatory requests.

Risk: The skill includes a fixed recommendation to consider another specialized bidding-compliance skill.

Mitigation: Treat that recommendation as contextual guidance and independently decide whether specialized follow-up review is appropriate.
