## Description:

AI Piano Teacher covering music theory, technique, repertoire analysis, practice plans, exam prep (ABRSM/CM/CCM), sight reading, ear training, mistake diagnosis, and repertoire recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bettermen](https://clawhub.ai/user/bettermen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners and piano educators use this Chinese-language skill to get level-aware piano tutoring, practice plans, exam preparation, repertoire recommendations, sight-reading and ear-training exercises, and mistake diagnosis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated HTML reports may include content supplied by other people.

Mitigation: Sanitize or review inserted text before sharing or opening generated reports in a browser.

Risk: Practice-plan content may be sent to Tencent Docs when that connector is connected.

Mitigation: Confirm user intent before using the connector and avoid including sensitive student information.

Risk: Piano instruction, practice, and exam guidance may be incomplete or mismatched to a learner's actual level.

Mitigation: Use the output as educational support and review important plans with a qualified teacher.
