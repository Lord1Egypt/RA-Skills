## Description:

Helps create, optimize, or audit giveaway, contest, sweepstakes, and promotional campaign pages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, founders, and developers use this skill to draft or review contest landing page copy, entry mechanics, legal checklist items, and tool integration notes for promotional campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad promo trigger may activate for promotional work that is not a contest, giveaway, or sweepstakes.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking for contest-page planning, optimization, or audit before applying the skill.

Risk: Contest and sweepstakes pages can require jurisdiction-specific legal terms, eligibility rules, and disclosures.

Mitigation: Treat legal checklist output as drafting support and have qualified reviewers confirm official rules, disclosures, and eligibility language before publication.

Risk: Project-context notes may contain campaign goals or product details that influence the generated page guidance.

Mitigation: Review any context-derived recommendations for accuracy and avoid including sensitive internal details in public campaign copy.
