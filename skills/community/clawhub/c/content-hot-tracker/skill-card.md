## Description:

Tracks hot topics across Chinese social platforms, analyzes trend momentum, and suggests content angles and headline ideas for creators.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators and social media operators use this skill to find current Chinese-platform topics, assess trend timing, and turn trends into content angles, competitor research prompts, and headline options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trend freshness and platform visibility may vary because the skill relies on public web-search results.

Mitigation: Check important topics against current platform hot lists before publishing or making time-sensitive decisions.

Risk: Topic and headline suggestions can overstate popularity or fit poorly with a creator's audience.

Mitigation: Review suggested angles against editorial standards, audience expectations, and any available first-party performance data.
