## Description:

Plans knowledge and explainer short-video visuals by turning text into high-information storyboard scenes with noun visualization and entrance animation design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[a1024708231](https://clawhub.ai/user/a1024708231)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, developers, and content automation agents use this skill to plan vertical knowledge-video scenes from a topic and speaking points. It produces storyboard structure, visual element choices, animation timing, and rendering-ready prompts for follow-on video production.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated storyboards may include a default clawhub.ai promotional ending unless the ending text, title, and description are overridden.

Mitigation: Review the storyboard before rendering or publishing, and provide explicit endingText, endingTitle, and endingDesc values when promotional copy is not desired.
