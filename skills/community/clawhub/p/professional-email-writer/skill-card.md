## Description:

Craft professional emails for any business situation, including formal, persuasive, or friendly emails with proper structure and tone for follow-ups, introductions, requests, apologies, and thank-you notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and external business users use this skill to draft, revise, and structure professional email messages for common workplace communication scenarios.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Email prompts may include confidential business or personal information.

Mitigation: Provide only the context needed for the draft and avoid unnecessary sensitive details.

Risk: Generated email drafts may not fully reflect the user's intent, relationship with the recipient, or organizational communication standards.

Mitigation: Review and customize the subject line, tone, facts, and placeholders before sending.
