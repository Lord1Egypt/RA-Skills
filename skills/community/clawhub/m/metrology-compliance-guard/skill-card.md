## Description:

计量检测合规护栏。在检定证书、校准报告、检测文书、计量授权文件发布前，实时检测是否符合《计量法》《计量法实施细则》《法定计量检定机构监督管理办法》及JJF 1069等规范要求，拦截不合规内容并给出修改建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Compliance teams, metrology laboratories, and document reviewers use this skill to review Chinese metrology certificates, calibration reports, inspection documents, authorization files, and related promotional text before release. It flags missing required elements, personnel or authorization issues, out-of-date standards, nonstandard terminology, and publish-blocking compliance concerns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be applied too broadly to documents that are not related to Chinese metrology compliance.

Mitigation: Users should clearly identify unrelated documents so the skill is not applied outside its intended metrology-review scope.

Risk: Regulatory conclusions may be incomplete, outdated, or inappropriate for a specific organization or jurisdictional situation.

Mitigation: Users should have qualified compliance staff verify legal and regulatory conclusions before relying on them for publication or operational decisions.
