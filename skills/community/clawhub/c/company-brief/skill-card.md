## Description:

Builds a role-specific one-page company research brief for candidates preparing for an application or interview.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External candidates and job seekers use this skill to turn company, role, and research inputs into a concise interview-preparation brief with business model, recent trajectory, competitors, role-relevant challenges, culture signals, questions to ask, and a personal angle.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include private employer documents or confidential personal information in the input.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting confidential materials unless the user intends the agent to use that information in the brief.

Risk: Company facts, recent news, funding, metrics, and leadership details may be incomplete or time-sensitive.

Mitigation: Verify current company facts before relying on the brief and label unsupported inferences as assumptions.

Risk: A generic or fabricated brief could mislead interview preparation.

Mitigation: Ground the brief in provided information and flag unclear business model, trajectory, or culture signals instead of inventing details.
