## Description:

Interactive planning assistant for end-to-end Quality Month activities, guiding users through proposal planning, scheduling, publicity copy, summary, and awards materials while marking missing or unconfirmed information for review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Quality managers, operations teams, and internal communications staff use this skill to plan a Quality Month program and prepare review-ready schedules, copy drafts, and summary or awards materials. It is intended to guide organizational decisions without inventing themes, budgets, dates, responsibilities, or performance metrics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated slogans, publicity copy, budgets, dates, responsibilities, or performance metrics may be incorrect or unsuitable for the user's organization.

Mitigation: Review and approve generated planning materials before use, especially fields that affect commitments, costs, ownership, or reported results.

Risk: The skill intentionally leaves uncertain items for confirmation and does not verify real-world facts.

Mitigation: Provide authoritative organizational data and keep unconfirmed items marked until a responsible reviewer validates them.
