## Description:

Extracts and summarizes public Modao prototype project and share pages, including page structure, interactions, annotations, and resource links without requiring login.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mike47512](https://clawhub.ai/user/mike47512)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers, product teams, and developers use this skill to organize public Modao project and share pages into concise summaries of page structure, interaction flows, annotations, and resource links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public Modao share pages may still expose project metadata, annotations, links, assets, owners, or update times.

Mitigation: Use the skill only on public pages you are authorized to review, and avoid confidential prototypes or restricted team content unless permission is confirmed.

Risk: The skill summarizes visible public content and may repeat sensitive details that were unintentionally exposed through a share link.

Mitigation: Review generated summaries before sharing them outside the authorized audience.
