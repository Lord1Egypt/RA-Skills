## Description:

Review support quality for chat, email, phone, social DM, or marketplace service teams and produce QA scorecards, failure-mode analysis, coaching plans, calibration checklists, or service-improvement briefs from user-provided inputs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Support managers, team leads, and service operations teams use this skill to convert support quality concerns into a practical QA and coaching brief. It is best suited for defining rubrics, diagnosing likely failure modes, planning coaching actions, and setting sampling or calibration rhythms without connecting to live support systems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste unnecessary customer personal data or sensitive support details into the prompt.

Mitigation: Share only the notes needed for QA review, minimize customer personal data, and keep privacy and compliance-sensitive judgments under human review.

Risk: Heuristic outputs may be mistaken for verified agent performance facts or disciplinary evidence.

Mitigation: Use the brief as a planning aid, verify conclusions against actual reviewed samples, and keep HR, refund, compliance, and disciplinary decisions human-approved.

Risk: Small or anecdotal samples may lead to overconfident conclusions about team-wide quality.

Mitigation: Treat tiny samples as signals for follow-up review and calibrate against representative samples before changing scorecards or coaching policy.
