## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated fee, payment-channel, or API details could lead to incorrect Youzan implementation plans.

Mitigation: Verify current pricing, payment rates, API parameters, and platform policies against official Youzan sources before acting.

Risk: Distribution commissions or stored-value programs can create legal and compliance exposure.

Mitigation: Keep commission structures within documented limits and obtain legal review for multi-level distribution or stored-value fund designs.
