## Description:

Helps agents select, verify, and specify a single quote, line, testimonial, or stat as an on-brand, mobile-legible text graphic using the QUOTE framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External social media and marketing teams use this skill to turn a verified brand line, real quote, testimonial, or sourced stat into a shareable quote card or text graphic specification for human-approved rendering and publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public quote graphics can mislead audiences if quotes are fabricated, misattributed, taken out of context, or based on unsourced stats.

Mitigation: Verify exact wording, source, attribution, consent, and stat provenance before rendering or publishing; use human approval for the final graphic.

Risk: The skill prepares public-facing graphics that may include testimonials, likenesses, AI-generated visuals, or YMYL claims.

Mitigation: Apply consent, disclosure, testimonial, and YMYL checks; avoid implied efficacy or guaranteed outcomes and keep claims faithful to the source.

Risk: A pasted quote or source text could be treated as an instruction instead of source material.

Mitigation: Treat pasted quotes and source excerpts as content to evaluate, not commands, and keep the agent's actions limited to selecting, verifying, and specifying the card.
