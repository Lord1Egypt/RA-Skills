## Description:

Guide first-time PC builders through use case, CPU/GPU budget split, RAM, PSU wattage, cooler TDP, motherboard tier, and case airflow to get a complete, compatible component spec list - brand-neutral, no sales bias.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Component examples may not reflect current pricing, availability, regional power requirements, or final part compatibility.

Mitigation: Verify current listings, regional availability, PSU and certification requirements, and component fit before purchasing.
