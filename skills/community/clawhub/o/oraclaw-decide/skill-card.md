## Description:

Decision intelligence for AI agents. Analyze options, map decision dependencies with PageRank, detect when information sources conflict, and find the choices that matter most.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to compare competing options, model decision dependencies, identify bottlenecks and critical paths, and assess whether multiple sources agree or conflict.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requires an ORACLAW_API_KEY and may trigger paid analysis calls.

Mitigation: Confirm API-key handling, pricing, and usage limits before installing or invoking the skill.

Risk: Decision-analysis outputs can be misleading if graph inputs, source estimates, or assumptions are incomplete or incorrect.

Mitigation: Review inputs and investigate outliers or high-impact recommendations before acting on the results.
