## Description:

Map how a company works from its public website (customer journey, teams, processes, handoffs), find the gaps that make it hard to buy from or work with, ask questions about the map, and check public ease-of-doing-business scores and AI-agent readiness. Use when the user asks how a company operates, who owns what, where a business loses customers, how easy a company is to deal with, or whether AI agents can use a website.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
