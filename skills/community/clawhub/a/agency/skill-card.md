## Use Case:

External agency operators and service-business teams use this skill to structure client onboarding, pricing, project tracking, deliverables, communication, team coordination, and continuous learning workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The local agency workspace can contain client, financial, and personnel records.

Mitigation: Keep ~/agency out of shared or synced folders unless approved, use restrictive file permissions where possible, and avoid storing passwords or API keys there.

Risk: Draft proposals or client communications could be inaccurate or sent before business approval.

Mitigation: Require human review before sending proposals or client communications, and keep revision limits, scope changes, and budget impacts explicit.
