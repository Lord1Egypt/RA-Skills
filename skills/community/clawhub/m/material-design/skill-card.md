## Description:

A Chinese-language practical reference for applying Google Material Design 3, covering Material You dynamic color, typography, component specifications, shape, motion, dark theme adaptation, and Jetpack Compose/Flutter comparisons for Android, web, and cross-platform UI design decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
