## Description:

Use when judging an early product idea, MVP, AI tool, creator product, or launched demo with Baoqingtian-style demand evidence, verdicts, seven-day validation tasks, and a visual verdict card.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders, builders, and product teams use this skill to evaluate early ideas, MVPs, AI tools, creator products, or demos against concrete demand evidence. It produces a verdict, four scoring dimensions, seven-day validation tasks, a rendered HTML verdict card, and a Markdown verdict report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product idea details may be saved temporarily and rendered into local output files.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive inputs unless local storage is approved, and remove temporary case data or generated outputs when they are no longer needed.

Risk: Generated HTML includes a hardcoded third-party branding/domain footer.

Mitigation: Review generated HTML before external sharing and confirm the footer is acceptable for the intended audience.

Risk: The verdict report can influence product decisions even when demand evidence is weak.

Mitigation: Treat verdicts as guidance and complete the recommended validation tasks with real user or payment evidence before committing product investment.
