## Description:

Search and browse 100 curated Japanese udon noodle recipes from Cookpad.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to find Japanese udon recipe ideas by ingredient, style, category, or recipe number and receive concise recipe summaries with source links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recipe summaries may be incomplete or unsuitable for a user's dietary, allergy, or food-safety needs.

Mitigation: Treat search results as recipe references and verify ingredients, allergens, cooking steps, and safe handling practices before preparing food.

Risk: Displayed Cookpad links lead to external content outside the bundled skill.

Mitigation: Open external links only when needed and verify the destination and recipe details before relying on them.
