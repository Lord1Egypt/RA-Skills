## Description:

Generate and evaluate ad creatives for Meta (Facebook/Instagram), TikTok Ads, YouTube Ads, Google Ads, and Amazon Ads with script generation, performance scoring, fatigue timing, and inspiration references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketing teams, growth operators, and creative strategists use this skill to generate ad concepts, score existing creatives, diagnose fatigue, and plan replacement tests across major ad platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ad copy can include unrealistic or policy-sensitive claims.

Mitigation: Verify claims and route policy-sensitive creative through the appropriate ad compliance review before publishing.

Risk: Creative scoring and fatigue assessments may be provisional when performance data is absent or incomplete.

Mitigation: Label confidence clearly and tie recommendations to measurable thresholds such as CTR trend, conversion rate, and frequency.

Risk: Prompts may include sensitive business metrics or campaign context.

Mitigation: Share only the metrics needed for the review and avoid highly sensitive business data unless it is required for the task.
