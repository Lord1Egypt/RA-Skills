## Description:

Viral Copywriting is a Chinese-language guide for creating social-platform marketing copy, including titles, covers, content structure, persuasion frameworks, sales copy, platform-specific tactics, and Xiaohongshu operations guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and marketers use this skill to draft and improve Chinese social-platform marketing copy, especially titles, hooks, story-driven posts, sales copy, and platform-specific content for Xiaohongshu, Toutiao, CSDN, WeChat Official Accounts, Zhihu, Douyin, and video channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Promotional or lead-generation suggestions may be inappropriate for a specific audience, product, or publication goal.

Mitigation: Review generated posts before publishing and adapt calls to action to the user's actual campaign and compliance needs.

Risk: Follow, comment, or private-message prompts may create unwanted engagement pressure or platform-policy risk.

Mitigation: Remove follow, comment, and private-message prompts when they do not fit the goal or the target platform's rules.

Risk: Lead-collection tactics can involve consent and privacy obligations.

Mitigation: Add clear consent language and privacy handling before using any lead-collection tactic.
