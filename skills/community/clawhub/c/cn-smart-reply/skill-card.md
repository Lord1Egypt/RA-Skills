## Description:

Generates canned Chinese reply suggestions for common social messaging, email, Zhihu, and Xiaohongshu scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agents and users can use this skill to draft short, socially appropriate Chinese replies for common messaging, email, Zhihu, and Xiaohongshu contexts. It is best suited for canned reply suggestions rather than general multilingual response generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expect multilingual or open-ended reply drafting beyond the skill's canned Chinese reply suggestions.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for the listed Chinese reply scenarios and choose a different assistant for broader multilingual drafting.

Risk: A canned social reply can be inappropriate for sensitive or context-specific communication.

Mitigation: Review and tailor the selected reply before sending it to another person.
