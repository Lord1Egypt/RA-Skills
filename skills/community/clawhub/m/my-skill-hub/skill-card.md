## Description:

A Chinese-language enterprise value research skill that guides agents through industry, moat, peer, financial, valuation, catalyst, margin-of-safety, and risk analysis using current public financial data and visual HTML reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and investment researchers use this skill to produce structured company research, peer comparisons, valuation ranges, risk reviews, and HTML reports from current public annual, quarterly, industry, and association data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce valuation ranges, portfolio-sizing suggestions, or investment action lists that users could mistake for investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research prompts, verify financial data from current public sources, and make independent investment decisions.

Risk: Outdated, incomplete, or inconsistent annual, quarterly, peer, or industry data can materially change the analysis.

Mitigation: Check all reported figures, dates, source citations, and peer selections before relying on the report.
