# Before you go

Twelve lines about the place. Hand them over when someone is about to leave, or paste them into the BEFORE YOU GO part of the trip sheet. Where a line names a tool and you do not have the connector, say "not read" and name the office.

These are not records and they do not say a place is open, legal, passable or safe. Fill the blanks from the records. Where a record is silent, write "unrecorded" and name the office.

1. [ ] **Camp and a backup camp** picked, each with its record link and record date.
2. [ ] **The dates, with the year** said out loud. A winter date gets "this guide plans spring, summer and fall trips" and an offer of a later date.
3. [ ] **The recorded rules** read: stay limit, season, and fire rules as dated records. Unrecorded means unrecorded.
4. [ ] **The forest's posted orders** read (`posted_orders`, for the forests it reads), quoted with the read time. "Nothing posted when we read it" is not "no fire ban." Then name the office to confirm with.
5. [ ] **The recorded road** looked up (`road_to_camp`, for the camps it covers). It is the record, not today's condition. Ask the office about the road, and read every sign and gate on the way in.
6. [ ] **The forecast** read for the trip dates (`forest_weather` or `trip_check`). It reaches about a week. A later date has no forecast yet.
7. [ ] **Wildfire reports** read for the area. They are reports, not closures.
8. [ ] **Water**: what the record holds, nothing more. Natural water is untested: filter or boil.
9. [ ] **The town run** planned from the recorded fuel, groceries, water and dump, in straight-line miles. The drive is longer.
10. [ ] **A paper map and an offline map** of the forest and the way in. Ask the ranger district for its Motor Vehicle Use Map.
11. [ ] **Tell someone**: leave them the trip sheet with the forest, the camp, the backup camp, who is going, the day you will be back, and the day they should call the ranger district or the county sheriff if they have not heard from you.
12. [ ] **The morning you leave**: run `posted_orders` and `changes_since` again, read what is new, and call the office.
