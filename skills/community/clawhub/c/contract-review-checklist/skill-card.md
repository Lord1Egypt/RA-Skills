## Description:

Contract risk review checklists — clause-by-clause red-flag scanning by contract type, risk grading with rewrite suggestions, missing-clause detection, and a negotiation-ready review report. Not ”AI lawyer”: structured triage before counsel review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chinabizskills](https://clawhub.ai/user/chinabizskills)

## Use Case:

Business users and contract reviewers use the skill to screen agreements for counterparty, clause, missing-term, and consistency issues, then prepare a risk-graded issue list and rewrite suggestions for negotiation or counsel review.
