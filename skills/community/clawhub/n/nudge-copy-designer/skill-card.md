## Description:

Designs behavior-economics-based nudge copy and choice architecture plans for posters, prompts, defaults, app onboarding, public-policy language, and similar behavior-change scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, designers, policy writers, educators, and product teams use this skill to create transparent behavior-change copy, choice architecture adjustments, and evaluation metrics for public, campus, workplace, app, and policy settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persuasive behavior-change copy can be misused for deceptive, coercive, or dark-pattern designs.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for transparent nudges that preserve user choice and support user or public benefit.

Risk: Generated copy may include behavioral claims, metrics, or examples that are not yet validated for the deployment context.

Mitigation: Review factual claims, localize metrics, and test outcomes before publishing or operationalizing the copy.
