## Description:

Provides injection-molding engineering guidance for material selection, process parameters, defect diagnosis, DFM review, mold design, simulation, tolerances, and cost estimation.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Manufacturing engineers, mold designers, and plastic part developers use this skill to get structured technical guidance for injection-molding design reviews, troubleshooting, simulation setup, process tuning, material choice, and cost estimation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated external files may automatically include MoldYang attribution and personal contact details.

Mitigation: Review generated files before sharing, and control or remove personal contact details when the intended distribution does not require them.

Risk: Injection-molding parameters, material data, simulation setup, and cost estimates are technical references rather than validated production instructions.

Mitigation: Confirm recommendations against supplier datasheets, simulation results, trial molding, local standards, and qualified engineering review before production or purchasing decisions.
