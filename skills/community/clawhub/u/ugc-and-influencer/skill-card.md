## Description:

UGC & Influencer helps agents plan brand-to-creator and UGC campaigns by vetting creators, drafting briefs and usage-rights checklists, enforcing disclosure rules, and preparing rights-cleared brand posts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing and social media operators use this skill to plan paid or gifted creator partnerships, customer UGC pipelines, briefs, rights terms, disclosure requirements, and rights-cleared brand reshare posts. It is for advising, drafting, and planning; outreach, contracts, payments, paid media, creator posts, and legal approval remain human or native-platform workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Campaign guidance may be mistaken for legal approval of usage rights, disclosures, or contracts.

Mitigation: Treat drafts as planning aids and have qualified human or legal reviewers confirm rights, disclosure, creator authorization, and platform requirements before publishing.

Risk: UGC or creator content could be reposted, whitelisted, or used in ads without sufficient consent or usage rights.

Mitigation: Confirm written usage scope, likeness rights, duration, channels, paid-use rights, and native creator authorization before reshare, whitelisting, or paid media use.

Risk: Sponsored, gifted, affiliate, employee, or otherwise materially connected creator content could omit clear disclosure.

Mitigation: Require upfront platform-native and textual disclosure on every materially connected post, and decline requests to skip, hide, or soften disclosure.
