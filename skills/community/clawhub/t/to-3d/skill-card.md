## Description:

将服装平铺图转换为具有体积感和版型的隐形模特式商品图。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce teams use it to turn flat-lay clothing photos into dimensional ghost-mannequin product images for product listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The selected image provider may receive the garment images and prompts.

Mitigation: Before sending them, confirm which provider will receive the data and whether its handling is acceptable.
