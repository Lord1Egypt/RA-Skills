## Description:

Generate platform-specific social posts from a single brief for Twitter/X threads, LinkedIn, Instagram, Reddit, and Mastodon.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[theshadowrose](https://clawhub.ai/user/theshadowrose)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to turn one short campaign brief into platform-specific social media drafts with suitable length limits, hashtags, thread splitting, and format conventions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated social media drafts may be inaccurate, off-brand, or unsuitable for a platform audience.

Mitigation: Review and edit all generated posts before publishing.

Risk: Documentation describes local data processing, so user-provided campaign briefs may remain on the local system.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive information unless local handling is acceptable, and maintain appropriate local backups.
