## Description:

Create trip-specific packing and pre-departure checklists for family, road, outdoor, international, and general travel requests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[onlydreams](https://clawhub.ai/user/onlydreams)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and travel-planning assistants use this skill to produce destination-, traveler-, transport-, activity-, and luggage-style-specific packing lists and pre-departure checklists. It is intended for preparation guidance, not booking, full itinerary planning, medical diagnosis, or binding legal, visa, customs, airline, venue, or safety determinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Travel rules for visas, customs, medicines, airlines, cruises, venues, and attractions can change or depend on the responsible authority.

Mitigation: Verify current official sources before stating a rule; otherwise describe the rule as unverified and provide only non-rule packing precautions.

Risk: High-risk outdoor trips may involve route, weather, altitude, rescue, or evacuation factors that a checklist cannot make safe.

Mitigation: When route or conditions are unknown, provide general risk controls, avoid route-specific kit or go/no-go advice, and ask one focused question about the highest-impact missing factor.

Risk: Users may rely on the checklist as complete for medical or special-needs travel.

Mitigation: Include concrete medication and health preparation items while avoiding diagnosis or prescribing, and direct users to qualified or official sources for medical and import decisions.
