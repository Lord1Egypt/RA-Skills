## Description:

Plan trending social content-marketing topics and headline directions from account positioning and target audience, producing hot topic ideas, viral title angles, and content frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wsd-mj](https://clawhub.ai/user/wsd-mj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content creators, marketers, and social media operators use this skill to plan topic ideas, headline directions, and content frameworks for niche social accounts. It is useful when planning a content calendar or addressing topic burnout, repeated ideas, or low-traffic content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account positioning, niche strategy, and audience descriptions are sent to wsdsocial.com.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when that transfer is acceptable, and avoid entering confidential campaign plans, customer data, or regulated information.

Risk: The skill requires a WSD_API_KEY for access.

Mitigation: Keep WSD_API_KEY private, scoped to the intended use, and out of prompts, logs, shared files, and public repositories.
