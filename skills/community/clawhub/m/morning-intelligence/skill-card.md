## Description:

Interviews you across 15 questions to capture your role, topics, sources, exclusions, and format preferences, then writes a master prompt you can paste into a scheduled task or Claude Code Routine.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to create a personalized daily news briefing prompt tailored to their role, topics, preferred sources, exclusions, recency needs, and desired format. It produces a confirmed preference summary, a reusable master prompt, and setup guidance for scheduled briefing workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated briefing prompts may contain confidential role, company, deal, product, regulatory, or source information that could be saved or committed accidentally.

Mitigation: Review the generated master prompt before saving it, store routine files privately or in ignored local files, and avoid committing sensitive tracking priorities to Git history.
