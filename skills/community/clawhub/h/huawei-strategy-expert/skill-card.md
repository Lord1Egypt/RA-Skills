## Description:

华为管理战略专家 provides Chinese-language strategic consulting, methodology explanation, and report generation using Huawei management frameworks such as DSTE, BLM, BEM, Five-Look-Three-Set, Iron Triangle, LTC, and IPD.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bettermen](https://clawhub.ai/user/bettermen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business leaders, consultants, and strategy teams use this skill to diagnose enterprise strategy and management problems, explain Huawei-style frameworks, and produce structured recommendations or strategic reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated HTML strategy reports may load Chart.js from a CDN, which can be inappropriate for confidential strategy work.

Mitigation: For confidential reports, bundle charting code locally or omit remote JavaScript before sharing or deploying the report.
