## Description:

Build a lightweight competitor monitoring brief across DTC sites, marketplaces, social channels, and promo surfaces. Use when a team needs pricing or promo scans, assortment watchlists, messaging comparison, threat assessment, or response options without live scraping, BI, or market-intelligence platform access.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External commercial operators, ecommerce teams, marketplace sellers, founders, and analysts use this skill to turn supplied competitor observations into practical watch briefs, threat assessments, response options, and monitoring cadence recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill relies on user-supplied competitor notes and does not verify live market data.

Mitigation: Validate pricing, legal, assortment, and public-response decisions with current evidence and human review before acting.

Risk: Heuristic interpretation can overstate competitor intent when notes are incomplete or anecdotal.

Mitigation: Keep assumptions visible in the brief and treat recommendations as directional until supported by stronger, recent evidence.
