## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installation and cloud-privacy controls require review before use with sensitive student or institutional data.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation, configure only trusted cloud endpoints and API keys, and keep sensitive materials in the local workflow unless a reviewed request is necessary.

Risk: Bundled setup flows may depend on FFmpeg or environment preparation steps that should be trusted before execution.

Mitigation: Use skip-ffmpeg where available or preinstall a trusted FFmpeg build before running setup commands.

Risk: Zero-upload privacy claims may not cover every final payload until redaction and executable integrity checks are verified.

Mitigation: Avoid placing PII in intent or context fields and treat local-first privacy claims as requiring operational validation before production deployment.
