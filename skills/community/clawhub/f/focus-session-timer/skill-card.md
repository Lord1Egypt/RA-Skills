## Description:

Builds a focused work session card that selects a session mode, defines a finish line, and provides start, distraction, break, interruption recovery, and end review prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn one work or study task into a realistic focus session with a clear finish line, break plan, and restart path after interruptions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the generated focus card for an actual timer, notification system, or attention-treatment tool.

Mitigation: Present the output as personal productivity guidance and use separate timer, notification, or medical support tools when needed.

Risk: A suggested session mode or break plan may not fit the user's real energy level or interruption risk.

Mitigation: Review the plan before use and adjust the session length, objective, and break plan to the user's current context.
