## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommended community skills may be unaudited or require permissions, dependencies, or environment variables that affect the user's workspace.

Mitigation: Inspect each skill before installation, review its ClawHub security report and source, and prefer workspace installs.

Risk: Using npx clawhub@latest or bulk updates can introduce mutable CLI or dependency supply-chain changes.

Mitigation: Use a pinned or trusted local clawhub CLI when feasible and avoid bulk updates without review.
