## Description:

Helps users plan, prioritize, position, submit, and track software product listings across startup, SaaS, AI, agent, MCP, no-code, review, and launch directories for backlinks and discovery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, marketers, and go-to-market teams use this skill to prepare directory submissions, adapt positioning by directory type, plan launch sequencing, and track listing status and backlink verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may submit irrelevant or low-quality listings that resemble spam or violate platform rules.

Mitigation: Use only relevant directories, avoid spammy venues, and review each platform's rules before posting or asking users for reviews.

Risk: Public directory or review listings may expose private customer data or sensitive product information.

Mitigation: Review listing copy before submission and keep private customer data out of public listings and review requests.
