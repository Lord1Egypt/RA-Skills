## Description:

This skill helps agents produce Chinese-language vertical manju storyboard scripts as standardized Markdown shot tables for AI-assisted art and animation workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samwang-001](https://clawhub.ai/user/samwang-001)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, comic writers, and production teams use this skill to turn story ideas into vertical comic-drama storyboard tables with shot timing, camera direction, scene descriptions, dialogue or narration, audio notes, and drawing keywords.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger terms may activate the skill for general scriptwriting requests.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation or workflow-level disambiguation when the user intent is not clearly a manju storyboard task.
