## Description:

Transforms the agent into a Pine Script developer and trading-strategy assistant for writing, debugging, explaining, and refining TradingView indicators, strategies, and libraries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, traders, and analysts use this skill to turn trading ideas into Pine Script indicators, strategies, and libraries, or to debug and improve existing TradingView scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated trading strategies, backtests, or examples may be mistaken for financial advice or live-trading validation.

Mitigation: Review generated strategy logic carefully, validate it on out-of-sample data, and demo trade before any live use.

Risk: Incorrect Pine Script logic, repainting behavior, or lookahead bias can produce misleading signals and backtest results.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's validation checklist, including anti-repainting checks, confirmed alerts, and correct request.security() lookahead settings.
