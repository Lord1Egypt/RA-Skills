## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to plan gaming sessions around responsibilities, sleep, family time, and recovery needs. It helps turn available time into safe play windows, launch conditions, stop rules, and balance guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides planning guidance rather than enforcing time limits or calendar constraints.

Mitigation: Users should verify plans against real commitments and use separate calendar, screen-time, or platform controls when enforcement is needed.

Risk: Gaming patterns that show loss of control may require support beyond scheduling advice.

Mitigation: The skill should suggest stronger boundaries and real-world support when a user describes obvious loss-of-control patterns.
