## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language Liuyao/Yijing divination assistant that simulates coin or number-based hexagram casting, applies Jing Fang Najia calculation rules, and produces interpretive readings with practical guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[eamanc-lab](https://clawhub.ai/user/eamanc-lab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to ask Liuyao/Yijing questions, generate a hexagram through coin casting or reported numbers, and receive a structured traditional interpretation for self-reflection. It is not intended to replace professional medical, legal, financial, or major life-decision advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on divination output for finance, health, legal, or major life decisions.

Mitigation: Present readings as entertainment or self-reflection and keep the disclaimer that professional advice should guide high-stakes decisions.

Risk: The skill may store explicit user preferences such as divination method or response style in local memory.

Mitigation: Only record preferences when the user explicitly provides them, and avoid storing sensitive personal details.
