## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persona-style relationship advice may be mistaken for professional counseling.

Mitigation: Present outputs as informal perspective and encourage professional support for counseling, legal, medical, safety, or crisis situations.

Risk: First-person responses may be mistaken for current factual statements from the real person.

Mitigation: Keep the artifact's information limits visible and avoid claiming current views, private facts, or post-research developments without separate evidence.

Risk: The README uses an unpinned installer command.

Mitigation: Install only from trusted sources and pin the package, source, or revision before deployment.
