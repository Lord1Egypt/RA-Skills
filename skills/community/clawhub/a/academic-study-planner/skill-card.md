## Description:

Academic Study Planner is a Chinese-language study planning assistant for exam candidates and lifelong learners preparing for exams such as postgraduate entrance exams, civil service exams, judicial exams, and CPA exams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lihangmissu](https://clawhub.ai/user/lihangmissu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners and education-focused assistants use this skill to create personalized study plans, revision schedules, learning-efficiency diagnostics, and adjustment strategies for Chinese exam preparation. It supports planning around learner goals, baseline ability, available study time, emotional state, and progress feedback.
