## Description:

Score and qualify leads using customizable criteria. Prioritize your pipeline by fit, intent, and engagement to focus on deals most likely to close.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Sales reps, SDRs, founders, and pipeline owners use this skill to score individual or batched leads by fit, intent, and engagement, then prioritize follow-up with grades and recommended next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lead records can contain sensitive business or personal data.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting sensitive lead data unless the agent environment is approved for that information.

Risk: Suggested scoring and sales actions may be incomplete or incorrect for a specific CRM process or market.

Mitigation: Manually review scores and recommended actions before updating a CRM or contacting prospects.
