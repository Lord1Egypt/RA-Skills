## Use Case:

External users and messaging-app agents use Claudius to ask cryptocurrency price, technical analysis, market trend, investment risk, and crypto education questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User crypto questions and IP address are sent to Claudius-operated servers.

Mitigation: Do not submit wallet seed phrases, private keys, exchange credentials, proprietary trading data, or personal financial details.

Risk: The skill may return buy, sell, or investment-related guidance for cryptocurrency assets.

Mitigation: Treat responses as informational analysis only and review financial decisions independently before acting.
