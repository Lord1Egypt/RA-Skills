## Description:

Activate when the user asks a question that requires judgment, choice, or decision-making. This skill helps provide structured decision support by analyzing from both AI perspective and user's perspective, with confidence levels and confidence ratings to help users assess the certainty of conclusions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[patrtiox](https://clawhub.ai/user/patrtiox)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to structure subjective decisions, trade-offs, predictions, and risk assessments into evidence-aware recommendations with explicit confidence levels, caveats, and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Decision guidance can be incorrect, incomplete, or outdated for time-sensitive topics.

Mitigation: Verify current facts with authoritative sources and treat the confidence rating and caveats as decision support rather than a final determination.

Risk: High-stakes legal, medical, financial, or similar decisions may require expertise beyond the skill output.

Mitigation: Consult an appropriate professional before relying on the recommendation for high-stakes choices.
