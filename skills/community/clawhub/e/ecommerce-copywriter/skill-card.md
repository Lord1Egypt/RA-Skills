## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated sales figures, efficacy claims, discounts, testimonials, certifications, and scarcity language may be inaccurate or unsupported if used as final copy.

Mitigation: Treat those items as placeholders and verify they are truthful, evidenced, and compliant with the target platform and applicable advertising rules before publication.

Risk: Platform-specific marketing copy may include terms that a platform or advertising policy restricts.

Mitigation: Review generated copy against the provided platform-rules reference and the current rules for the target marketplace before publishing.
