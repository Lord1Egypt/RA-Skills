## Description:

Audit a game, feature, progression system, social system, live-ops loop, onboarding flow, monetization surface, or multiplayer space through the lens of the Big Five personality traits (OCEAN): Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and researchers use this skill to review games, features, progression systems, social systems, live-ops loops, onboarding flows, monetization surfaces, or multiplayer spaces through an OCEAN personality-fit lens. It helps identify which design pressures feel comfortable, energizing, exhausting, or alienating for different trait expressions without treating the framework as a diagnostic assessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: OCEAN trait language could be mistaken for clinical assessment or definitive labeling of real players.

Mitigation: Use the framework only as a product-design fit lens and avoid diagnosing, labeling, or making psychometric claims about individuals.

Risk: Audit recommendations could overgeneralize player comfort, stress, or social preferences when applied without product context.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the specific feature scope, intended audience, and available player research before making design decisions.
