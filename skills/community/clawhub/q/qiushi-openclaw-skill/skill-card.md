## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A bundled skill may globally steer assistant reasoning and auto-run at the start of top-level conversations.

Mitigation: Review the package before installation, scope activation to appropriate tasks, and disable or narrow automatic invocation where the host platform allows it.

Risk: A bundled instruction-priority rule may place user requests above platform safety, privacy, and authorization rules.

Mitigation: Correct or override that rule so platform, safety, privacy, and authorization requirements always take precedence over user requests and skill guidance.
