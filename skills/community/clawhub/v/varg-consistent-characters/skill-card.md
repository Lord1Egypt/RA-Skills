## Description:

Creates multi-scene ad videos with consistent characters, outfits, and locations using actor or generated-character references, approved stills, and cloud rendering.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and video creators use this skill to turn a scene script and actor photos into multi-scene ads with consistent people, outfits, and locations. It also supports reviewing stills before video rendering and turning a reference video into a scene script.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may send identifiable actor photos to VARG and its generation providers; scan coverage was partial and retention or deletion controls were not established.

Mitigation: Confirm permission to upload and use each person's likeness, and review VARG and provider retention and deletion terms before sending photos.
