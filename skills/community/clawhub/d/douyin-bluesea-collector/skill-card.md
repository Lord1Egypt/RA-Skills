## Description:

Finds emerging Douyin product and content opportunities by combining hot rankings with keyword searches and public video, creator, and comment data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[why20261](https://clawhub.ai/user/why20261)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content operators and market researchers use the skill to discover niche Douyin topics and products, review public trends and creators, and analyze comments for demand or customer-acquisition signals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search keywords and supplied links or IDs are sent to the third-party API at guaikei.com.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with public Douyin data and submit only queries and links you are comfortable sharing with that service.
