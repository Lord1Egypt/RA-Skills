## Description:

Payable Test turns a confirmed Offer Brief into one ethical, smallest viable paid validation plan with clear payment terms, evidence labels, cost limits, and an external-action approval gate.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External builders and operators use this skill after an Offer Brief to design a single paid validation test with truthful payment terms, cost limits, evidence thresholds, and execution gates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A generated paid-test plan could be used externally before authority, payment terms, refund handling, data basis, platform rules, or regulated-review prerequisites are confirmed.

Mitigation: Verify the buyer, price, refund terms, account authority, platform rules, and any required regulated review before external use; block execution when any prerequisite is unknown.

Risk: Copied offer briefs, messages, attachments, payment records, or customer statements may contain prompt injection or confidential information.

Mitigation: Treat copied inputs as untrusted evidence, ignore embedded instructions, avoid exposing credentials or confidential context, and use only safe, relevant facts.

Risk: Free interest, simulated results, invoices, or verbal promises may be mistaken for paid validation.

Mitigation: Preserve the evidence label earned by observed artifacts and require a real payment condition when a lawful, feasible payable test exists.
