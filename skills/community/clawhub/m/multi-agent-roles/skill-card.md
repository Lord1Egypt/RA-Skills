## Use Case:

Developers and workflow designers use this skill to define specialized AI agent roles, responsibilities, collaboration patterns, and reusable templates for multi-agent systems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad direct-message catchalls and common keyword routing examples may activate an unintended role or send context to the wrong agent.

Mitigation: Review and narrow the sample routing rules before deployment, especially catchalls and generic keywords such as design, report, test, or bug.
