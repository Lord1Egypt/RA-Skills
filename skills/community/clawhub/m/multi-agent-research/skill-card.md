## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-language documentation may be misunderstood by readers who cannot confidently review the instructions.

Mitigation: Confirm the documentation meaning before installing or running the referenced commands.

Risk: Research artifacts may be synchronized to an unintended cloud storage destination if project storage or credentials are misconfigured.

Mitigation: Use only cloud storage locations and credentials intentionally selected for the project, and verify the destination before execution.
