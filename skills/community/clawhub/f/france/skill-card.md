## Use Case:

External travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to plan France trips with city guides, regional routing, dining context, accommodation strategy, transport planning, and practical safety guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trip preferences and personal travel details may be stored in a local plaintext memory file.

Mitigation: Avoid storing passport numbers, payment data, reservation login links, or sensitive confirmation codes, and delete or edit the memory file when it is no longer needed.

Risk: Travel logistics such as transport disruptions, opening hours, weather, reservation availability, and seasonal pricing can change quickly.

Mitigation: Confirm high-value bookings, live schedules, weather, and operator notices directly before relying on a plan.
