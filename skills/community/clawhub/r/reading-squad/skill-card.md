## Description:

Reading Squad simulates a platform-agnostic multi-expert book discussion inside any chat-based AI tool, covering setup, member definitions, multi-round discussion flow, and consensus synthesis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Readers, students, facilitators, and knowledge workers use this skill to structure multi-perspective discussions of books, chapters, or reading topics. It helps an AI leader coordinate expert-style viewpoints, debate at least five rounds, and synthesize consensus points with actionable reading guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The ready-made templates are Chinese-first and may not match every user's preferred language or locale.

Mitigation: Adapt the prompt language and member roster before use when working in another language or cultural context.

Risk: The default roster uses named public-person style personas, which can blur the line between simulated viewpoints and real statements.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as simulated discussion only, avoid presenting generated views as real quotes or endorsements, and replace named personas with role-based experts when appropriate.
