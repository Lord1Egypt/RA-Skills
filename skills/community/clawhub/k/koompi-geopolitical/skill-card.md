## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to monitor geopolitical developments, evaluate regional and country risk, structure OSINT collection, and produce concise intelligence briefings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Geopolitical and sanctions conclusions can affect sensitive operational or compliance decisions.

Mitigation: Verify sensitive conclusions against authoritative sources before acting on them.

Risk: Current events and social-media signals may be incomplete, misleading, or fast-changing.

Mitigation: Use corroborated sources, state confidence clearly, and update assessments when new evidence changes the picture.
