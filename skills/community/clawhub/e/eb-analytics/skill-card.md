### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Analytics tracking can send unnecessary personal or sensitive data to third-party analytics providers.

Mitigation: Confirm what data will be sent, avoid unnecessary personal data, use pseudonymous identifiers where possible, and align consent, privacy notices, and retention rules with the product and jurisdiction.

Risk: Poorly chosen events or dashboards can produce misleading metrics that drive ineffective product decisions.

Mitigation: Start with a north star metric, document a tracking plan before implementation, and keep dashboards tied to decisions and accountable owners.
