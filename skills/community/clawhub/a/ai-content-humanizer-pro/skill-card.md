## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to detect AI-writing patterns and rewrite text in academic, casual, professional, or SEO-oriented modes while preserving the intended meaning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Whole-document rewrites may change meaning or tone more broadly than intended.

Mitigation: Review proposed diffs before saving broad rewrites, especially for academic, legal, business, or published materials.

Risk: Humanized text could be used to misrepresent authorship or bypass academic, workplace, or platform rules.

Mitigation: Use the skill only where rewriting assistance is allowed and disclose or attribute AI assistance when required.

Risk: Paid calls may be triggered after the free tier.

Mitigation: Confirm intended use and pricing before running repeated or batch humanization requests.
