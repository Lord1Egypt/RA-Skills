## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read profile data and store tax, expense, and payment records in workspace files.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installing, use it only in private workspaces, and avoid storing full confirmation numbers or unnecessary personal financial details.

Risk: The skill can set up recurring Telegram reminders, which may expose tax deadlines or financial context in shared messaging channels.

Mitigation: Create reminders only after explicit user consent, use private channels, and remove reminders when they are no longer needed.

Risk: Tax law changes frequently, and incorrect or outdated guidance could affect filing decisions.

Mitigation: Verify current-year rules with official tax sources and consult a licensed CPA or tax attorney before making material tax decisions.
