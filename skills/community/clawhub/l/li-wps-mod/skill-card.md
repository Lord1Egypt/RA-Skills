## Description: <br>
Unified WPS Office assistant for Excel, Word, and PPT that helps agents operate WPS document, spreadsheet, presentation, and conversion tools through natural-language requests. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, developers, and office automation agents use this skill to operate WPS Office files across Excel, Word, and PPT. It supports spreadsheet formulas and data work, document formatting and template filling, presentation creation and beautification, file conversion, saving, and related office workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Document automation can change, delete, save, export, or convert important business documents. <br>
Mitigation: Review requests carefully before batch edits, deletions, global replacements, saves, exports, or file conversions; confirm high-impact operations and keep backups for important documents. <br>
