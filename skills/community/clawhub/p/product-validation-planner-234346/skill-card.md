## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, checklists, analyses, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It turns the user's context into a repeatable validation workflow, checklist, template, lightweight analysis, or implementation plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad product, startup, SaaS, or validation wording may invoke the skill when the user intended a narrower workflow.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants a product-validation workflow before applying the planning process.

Risk: Business planning outputs are advisory and may depend on incomplete assumptions or user-supplied context.

Mitigation: Keep assumptions visible and validate recommendations against user-defined success criteria before acting on them.
