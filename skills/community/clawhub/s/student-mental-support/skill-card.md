## Description:

Provides Chinese-language emotional support, employment-anxiety triage, stress-management techniques, career-planning guidance, and professional resource referrals for university students facing graduation and job-search pressure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaowuheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaowuheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and support-facing agents use this skill to respond to job-search anxiety, interview stress, career uncertainty, and related emotional distress with empathetic conversation, coping exercises, resource referrals, and actionable career plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake supportive coping guidance for emergency support, diagnosis, or treatment.

Mitigation: Keep the skill framed as non-clinical support and route self-harm risk, suicidal thoughts, severe symptoms, or inability to stay safe to emergency services, crisis hotlines, campus counseling, or trusted people immediately.

Risk: Career and stress plans may be too general for a student's medical, cultural, financial, or academic situation.

Mitigation: Use the plans as self-help starting points, ask only necessary context, and encourage review with campus counseling, career services, or qualified professionals for high-impact decisions.

Risk: The skill may handle sensitive student mental-health and employment details.

Mitigation: Avoid unnecessary personal data collection and respect user privacy when exploring background, stressors, and support needs.
