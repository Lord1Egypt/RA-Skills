## Description:

Write clear customer-facing outage and service-disruption notifications, including phased status-page updates, customer emails, in-app banners, and resolved summaries in plain language.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Customer success, support, operations, and incident-response teams use this skill to draft customer-facing outage notices across status pages, emails, banners, and post-incident summaries. It helps turn incomplete incident briefs into clear updates with bracketed assumptions for human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft notices may include inferred outage scope, timing, ETA, or impact details.

Mitigation: Replace bracketed or inferred facts with verified incident data before publishing customer-facing communications.

Risk: Using a general outage notice for a security incident, data incident, or PR crisis may omit required review steps.

Mitigation: Use the organization's specialized security, data-incident, or crisis-communications process for those cases.
