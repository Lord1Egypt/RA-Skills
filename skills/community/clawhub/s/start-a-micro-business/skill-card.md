## Description:

Helps users identify a practical service- or skill-based micro-business and take first-customer, pricing, tax, and legal basics steps with minimal upfront capital.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to choose a low-capital service or skill-based micro-business, find an initial customer, and understand basic pricing, tax, legal, and scaling considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States; general service-selection guidance may transfer elsewhere, but legal and tax references are U.S.-focused.
