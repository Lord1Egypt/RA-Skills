## Description:

Meeting Mastery helps an agent prepare meeting briefs, create tailored agendas, capture structured notes, track action items, draft follow-ups, maintain relationship history, and assess meeting ROI.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, operators, sales teams, managers, and developers can use this skill to prepare for meetings, document decisions, manage follow-up work, preserve relationship context, and audit recurring meetings for value.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent meeting and relationship records may contain personal details, confidential notes, or regulated business data.

Mitigation: Use a protected storage location, minimize sensitive details, mark confidential meetings clearly, and define retention and deletion practices before use.

Risk: Attendee research and relationship memory may collect personal information without enough consent or privacy guidance.

Mitigation: Limit records to meeting-relevant information and follow organizational privacy, consent, and data-handling policies.

Risk: Generated summaries, action items, and follow-up drafts may misstate decisions or commitments.

Mitigation: Review notes and drafts before sending, and confirm owners, deadlines, and decisions with meeting participants.
