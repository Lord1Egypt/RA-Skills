## Description:

Email Assistant helps classify email, draft professional replies, extract action items, summarize long messages, and prioritize follow-up.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crazypatient0](https://clawhub.ai/user/crazypatient0)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business users provide email text or descriptions to classify messages, draft replies, extract tasks and deadlines, summarize long emails, and prioritize follow-up. The skill is intended for manually supplied email content, not direct inbox access.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Email content can include sensitive personal or business information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with email text intentionally provided for assistance, and avoid adding content that should not be shared with the agent.

Risk: Generated replies, summaries, priorities, or extracted tasks may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review generated outputs before sending replies or acting on extracted tasks, especially for important email.

Risk: The skill is text-only and does not provide permission controls for live mailbox operations.

Mitigation: Do not connect it to a live inbox or automate sending, deletion, or mailbox changes without additional authorization and review controls.
