## Use Case:

External users, BD teams, R&D strategists, and analysts use this skill to assess oncology targets or targeted drugs across biology, pipeline, clinical, patent, literature, and transaction evidence. It supports project initiation, partnership screening, and due diligence while separating observed facts from strategic interpretation.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for medical, legal, valuation, or formal freedom-to-operate advice.

Mitigation: Treat reports as decision-support material and route medical, legal, valuation, and formal FTO conclusions to qualified reviewers.

Risk: External search or domain-tool evidence may be incomplete, stale, or inconsistently sourced.

Mitigation: Preserve source, date, entity, region, and verification status for key facts, and mark unsupported figures as unverified.

Risk: Deep-report mode may create an HTML file in session outputs.

Mitigation: Review generated files before sharing and ensure included evidence, assumptions, and disclaimers match the intended audience.
