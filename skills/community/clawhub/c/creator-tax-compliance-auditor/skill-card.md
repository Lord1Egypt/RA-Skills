## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, developers, digital entrepreneurs, and independent professionals use this skill to audit income, expenses, licensing agreements, and jurisdiction-specific tax obligations. It produces quarterly compliance reports, deduction checklists, documentation gap analysis, and filing action plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive financial, tax, or customer data may be exposed through connected accounts, spreadsheets, or pasted records.

Mitigation: Use read-only and least-privilege access where possible, redact unnecessary personal data, and avoid sharing live payment-account secrets unless strictly required.

Risk: Tax, licensing, and compliance guidance may be incomplete, stale, or unsuitable for a user's specific jurisdiction and business structure.

Mitigation: Treat generated reports as analysis only and verify conclusions with a qualified tax, accounting, or legal professional before filing or acting.

Risk: Credentialed integrations can broaden account access beyond the immediate audit task.

Mitigation: Prefer scoped API keys, rotate credentials after use, and review connected services before enabling optional payment or accounting integrations.
