## Description:

Turns raw git logs, PR title lists, and changelog drafts into polished, audience-targeted release notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[charlie-morrison](https://clawhub.ai/user/charlie-morrison)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, product managers, and product marketers use this skill to convert raw release artifacts into publishable changelog entries, emails, developer notes, marketing posts, and status-page copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad release-note trigger phrases may invoke the skill during general changelog or versioning discussions.

Mitigation: Review generated outputs before publishing and confirm the skill was intentionally applied to the release task.

Risk: Drafting from unreleased release artifacts may expose sensitive product, customer, or roadmap details.

Mitigation: Provide only details intended for release-note drafting and remove confidential content before publishing.
