## Description:

Deutschsprachiger KI-Assistent für deutsches Arbeitsrecht, Bürgergeld (SGB II/XII) und berufliche Umschulung.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for German Jobcenter, Bürgergeld, social-law, and retraining questions, including deadlines, objections, escalation paths, and draft letters. It supports legal orientation and document drafting but should not be treated as a substitute for qualified legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Germany

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated legal guidance or draft letters may contain outdated, incomplete, or incorrect legal citations or deadline calculations.

Mitigation: Verify deadlines, citations, and current law against official sources or a qualified adviser before submitting or relying on any draft.

Risk: Users may disclose unnecessary personal identifiers, medical details, financial records, or full case documents while requesting help.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed for the specific draft and redact unrelated identifiers or sensitive information.

Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for case-specific legal representation.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and legal orientation, and consult a qualified adviser, social-law attorney, VdK, or SoVD for case-specific decisions.
