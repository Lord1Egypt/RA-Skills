## Description:

Transforms long-form content, outlines, transcripts, research notes, or article drafts into structured card packages, platform publishing copy, and handoff guidance for image production.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and publishing teams use this skill to convert long source material into skimmable card sequences for Xiaohongshu, WeChat inline cards, Zhihu Idea images, and generic carousels. It preserves source caveats, prepares platform-specific captions and tags, and can hand publish-ready packages to image production.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated card copy can preserve or amplify unverified source claims.

Mitigation: Review claims needing verification and source caveats before publishing.

Risk: Trend or hot-tag metadata may be stale or unsupported when current evidence is unavailable.

Mitigation: Leave verified hot tags empty or unverified unless a current source and check time are available.

Risk: Default publish-ready mode depends on downstream image production before assets are uploadable.

Mitigation: Confirm real image files and a checked cards manifest are returned before treating the package as publish-ready.
