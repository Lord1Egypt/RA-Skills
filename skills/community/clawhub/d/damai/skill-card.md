## Description:

Damai live-event ticketing guide for pre-sale preparation, real-name ticketing rules, seat selection, refund and transfer checks, and scalper-risk avoidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to plan Damai ticket purchases for concerts, theater, music festivals, and other live events, including pre-sale setup, real-name entry requirements, seat-tier selection, and secondhand-ticket risk checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Event-specific ticket prices, sale times, refund rules, and transfer rules may change after the skill's guidance is generated.

Mitigation: Check the live Damai ticket page and official event details before acting on any purchase recommendation.

Risk: Users may encounter fraudulent or unsupported secondhand-ticket offers, especially for strong real-name events.

Mitigation: Use official Damai purchase, transfer, refund, and shortage-registration flows, and avoid screenshot, PDF, low-price, or off-platform ticket offers.
