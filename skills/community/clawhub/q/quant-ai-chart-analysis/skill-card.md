## Use Case:

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chart screenshots may include brokerage credentials, account balances, or other sensitive personal information when uploaded to Quant AI.

Mitigation: Use screenshots that omit sensitive account details and credentials before uploading them for analysis.

Risk: Generated trade setups or risk zones may be mistaken for financial advice or guaranteed outcomes.

Mitigation: Present outputs as educational technical analysis and avoid claiming that any setup guarantees a result.
