## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, researchers, and external users use this skill to combine multiple sources into a single synthesis while preserving source attribution, surfacing conflicts, and identifying coverage gaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incomplete or conflicting sources can lead to misleading synthesis if disagreements or gaps are hidden.

Mitigation: Use the skill's source inventory, conflict resolution, and coverage verification steps before relying on the synthesis.

Risk: Input sources may contain sensitive or unsuitable information for the current workspace.

Mitigation: Provide only sources that are appropriate to analyze in the active environment and review the generated synthesis before sharing it.
