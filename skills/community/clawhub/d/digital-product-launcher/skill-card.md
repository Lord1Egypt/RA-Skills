## Description:

Digital Product Launcher helps solopreneurs and creators plan and launch digital products by guiding ideation, positioning, pricing, product page copy, sales funnels, email sequences, platform setup, and launch checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, solopreneurs, and business users use this skill to turn a digital product idea into launch-ready positioning, pricing, sales copy, funnel steps, email copy, platform setup guidance, and an operational launch checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business and marketing guidance may be outdated or may not reflect current platform policies, fees, listing rules, or market conditions.

Mitigation: Users should verify platform-specific requirements, fees, and policies before publishing or purchasing services.

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate this skill for general online-selling requests where a narrower skill would fit better.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's goal and product type before applying the full launch workflow.

Risk: Pricing and sales recommendations are not guaranteed financial outcomes.

Mitigation: Treat pricing and funnel suggestions as planning guidance and validate them against audience research, costs, and business constraints.
