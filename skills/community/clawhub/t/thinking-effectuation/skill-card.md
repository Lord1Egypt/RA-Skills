## Description:

Under genuine uncertainty with no reliable forecast, inventory means, cap downside at affordable loss, act for commitments, and let goals emerge from controllable moves.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this skill to structure decisions when forecasts are unreliable, starting from available means, affordable loss, and real commitments. It helps produce an effectuation brief that identifies current means, downside caps, the next controllable action, commitments, contingencies, the emerging goal, and whether to continue, switch planning modes, or halt.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reasoning framework can be misapplied to predictable situations that are better handled with causal planning.

Mitigation: Use the skill's over-application guard: switch to causal planning when reliable forecasts, known playbooks, or fixed goals are available.

Risk: A recommended action could exceed the user's stated time, money, reputation, or opportunity-cost loss caps.

Mitigation: Run the loss-cap check before action: shrink or stop any step that does not fit inside the stated affordable loss.

Risk: The brief may treat opinions, predictions, or uncommitted interest as progress.

Mitigation: Require either a real commitment from another party or a cheap information gain before counting the cycle as valid progress.
