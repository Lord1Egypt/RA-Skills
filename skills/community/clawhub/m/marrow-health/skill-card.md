## Description:

Read and log your own health and fitness data from the Marrow iPhone app (Apple Health metrics, sleep, meals, water, weight, workouts, strength sets, mood). Use when the user asks how they slept, how today compares with their usual, what they ate, about training, steps, heart rate or weight, wants a weekly review, or asks to log something.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
