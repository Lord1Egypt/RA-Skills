## Description:

Generates compliance-aware marketing prompts and content packages for auto repair, auto body, and collision repair businesses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitflopez](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitflopez)

## Use Case:

Shop owners, service department managers, multi-location operators, and automotive marketing agencies use this skill to generate campaigns, website copy, review responses, local SEO plans, fleet outreach, and compliance-oriented marketing guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing may include state-specific legal, warranty, credential, or advertising language that is inaccurate for a particular shop.

Mitigation: Verify all regulatory language, certifications, warranty terms, prices, and license claims against the shop's records and applicable state requirements before publishing.

Risk: Review responses, referral campaigns, and fleet outreach could expose customer information or create misleading endorsement, review-gating, or commercial email issues.

Mitigation: Remove customer PII, ask for honest reviews only, separate referrals from reviews, include required commercial email disclosures, and review platform policies before use.
