## Description:

Checks Chinese-language AI side-hustle projects, courses, and opportunities with a local rule-based CLI that scores credibility and flags common scam patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[freedompixels](https://clawhub.ai/user/freedompixels)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to evaluate Chinese-language AI side-hustle pitches, courses, or opportunities and receive a credibility score, risk flags, and practical expectation guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The checker is rule-based and can misclassify nuanced or unfamiliar side-hustle opportunities.

Mitigation: Use its report as triage guidance and verify claims, costs, refund terms, and operator credibility independently before spending money or time.

Risk: The CLI and reports are Chinese-language, which may limit review quality for non-Chinese readers.

Mitigation: Have a Chinese-capable reviewer assess high-impact outputs or translate reports before acting on them.

Risk: The documentation includes a promotional AISoBrand footer.

Mitigation: Review bundled documentation and outbound links for brand, policy, and redistribution suitability before release.
