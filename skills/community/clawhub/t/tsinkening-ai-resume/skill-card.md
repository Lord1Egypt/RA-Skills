## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language fictional AI entrepreneur resume and profile that agents can use to answer questions about education, career history, skills, entrepreneurship, income sources, learning methods, and interview experience.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and career learners use this skill for fictional profile Q&A, AI career inspiration, and entrepreneurship learning paths based on the simulated persona of Kening Qin.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the biography, income numbers, social metrics, and company history for verified facts about a real person.

Mitigation: Present the profile as simulated content for inspiration or Q&A, not as verified personal, financial, or company information.
