## Description:

Schedule and publish posts to X and LinkedIn through SocialMCP's hosted MCP server, with one social media calendar. Use when the person wants to post, schedule, plan, edit, reschedule or cancel social media posts, write X threads or LinkedIn posts, fill their posting slots, or see what is scheduled or already published.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A post created without a scheduled time may publish immediately, and SocialMCP cannot remove a post already published to a social network.

Mitigation: Show the final text, destination networks, and time, then wait for the user's confirmation before creating the post.

Risk: The security review reported no findings, but coverage was partial because a local path-like reference could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference before installation; the scanner guidance says the evidence is incomplete even though the result was benign.
