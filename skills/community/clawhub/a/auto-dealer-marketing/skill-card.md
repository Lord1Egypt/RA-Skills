## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: CRM, DMS, equity-mining, SMS, email, and direct-mail workflows may involve customer records or contact data.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting unnecessary customer records into prompts and confirm authorization, privacy compliance, channel consent, opt-out handling, and applicable advertising rules before use.

Risk: Generated dealership marketing claims, payment offers, and outreach scripts can be inaccurate, incomplete, or non-compliant if used without review.

Mitigation: Review all creative against OEM co-op rules, Truth in Lending, TCPA, CAN-SPAM, FTC endorsement guidance, fair lending obligations, state advertising rules, and dealership approval processes before publishing.
