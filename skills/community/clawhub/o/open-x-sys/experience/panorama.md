# Panorama: Know Where You Are, However Deep You Go

A complex system lets a person enter different levels, times, and relations. The panorama maintains direction throughout the exploration: what is open now, where it belongs, why we came here, and how to return to the whole.

## A complex work provides

- **The whole world**: the object's main levels, relations, change over time, and current boundary;
- **Current position**: the system, relation, evidence, or simulation open now;
- **Path in**: the discovery or question that led here;
- **Local-to-whole effect**: how this part changes a higher-level result;
- **Way back**: an immediate return to overview, central discovery, and main narrative;
- **Basis**: sources, assumptions, and dates when a judgment needs to be checked.

## Principles of expression

Give the person the whole first, then unfold local detail as needed. One view carries one primary cognitive task: a relation map reveals structure, a timeline reveals change, comparison reveals difference, and narrative or space lets a person enter a world. Every visual structure exists because it makes a central relation visible.

Interaction carries comparison, verification, simulation, or movement across scale. Static media can also give a panorama through a contents map, persistent overview, current-position marker, and return anchors. On desktop and mobile alike, the central discovery remains visually dominant.
