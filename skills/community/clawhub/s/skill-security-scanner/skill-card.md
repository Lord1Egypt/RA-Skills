## Description:

Scan OpenClaw skills for security risks, suspicious permissions, and provide a trust score to help evaluate skill safety before use or installation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[steffano198](https://clawhub.ai/user/steffano198)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers and OpenClaw users use this skill to review skills before installation, audit existing skills, and produce lightweight trust-score guidance for untrusted skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The scanner may present stronger safety ratings than its lightweight checks can support.

Mitigation: Treat scan output as an initial lint signal and review untrusted skills manually before installation or execution.
