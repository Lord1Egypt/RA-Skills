## Description:

Designs or improves web, app, extension, community, course, video-ideation, marketing landing, SEO content hub, and public policy-page UI with a DESIGN.md-backed design system, media assets, responsive screenshots, accessibility checks, conversion copy, and generated raster imagery when useful.

## Use Case:

Developers, product builders, and design-focused agents use this skill to create or refine DESIGN.md-backed product surfaces, landing pages, extension UIs, media assets, and public review pages. It emphasizes real screenshots, accessibility checks, SEO readiness, claim alignment, and concrete quality gates before final delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad UI and landing-page triggers may lead to extensive repo changes or public-page edits.

Mitigation: Review diffs carefully before publishing or deploying public pages, especially when the skill edits files or changes product claims.

Risk: Generated media or polished marketing copy can overstate product behavior or imply unsupported affiliation.

Mitigation: Use real screenshots for product evidence, keep affiliation boundaries visible, and verify that claims match shipped behavior.

Risk: Design changes can introduce accessibility, responsive layout, SEO, or policy-page regressions.

Mitigation: Run the skill's screenshot, accessibility, layout, SEO, link, and critic-score gates before final delivery.
