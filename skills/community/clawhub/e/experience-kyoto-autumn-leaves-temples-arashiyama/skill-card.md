## Description:

Feel the quiet awe of fleeting crimson maples as Kyoto’s temples glow in night light, inviting calm reflection on impermanence. Wander lit gardens and misty bridges across twenty gentle steps, low intensity, spanning weeks. 静观枫叶绚烂. asombro sereno ante arces.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review rates the skill suspicious because its Kyoto reflection experience also requests unrelated external account and review activity.

Mitigation: Review the external requests before following them; omit optional profile details and reflections you do not want to share, and skip unrelated account or review actions.
