## Description:

Sets social media goals and KPIs by turning business outcomes into SMART objectives, one North Star KPI per channel, supporting metrics, benchmark-based targets, guardrails, and action thresholds.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External social media teams and strategy agents use this skill to convert vague ambitions into measurable social media objectives and KPI cascades. It is intended for target setting and planning, not for measuring live performance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat planned KPI targets as measured results or verified performance data.

Mitigation: Pair outputs with analytics/reporting or verified platform data before using targets as evidence of performance.

Risk: Targets can become misleading if baselines are missing, stale, or fabricated.

Mitigation: Use real first-party benchmarks where available; otherwise set a time-boxed learning goal and clearly mark comparable benchmarks as provisional.

Risk: Vanity or gameable metrics can encourage low-quality growth tactics.

Mitigation: Use controllable, outcome-mapped KPIs with quality guardrails such as engagement-rate floors or lead-quality checks.
