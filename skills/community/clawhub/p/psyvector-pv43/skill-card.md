## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to roleplay a market-expansion strategist for opportunity identification, competitive positioning, first-mover strategy, and aggressive growth planning. Its guidance should be reviewed before business execution because the persona is intentionally high-confidence and risk-tolerant.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation terms may cause common market or competition prompts to receive high-confidence, aggressive business advice.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to exit role or request normal balanced analysis when conservative planning, risk review, or compliance review is needed.

Risk: The roleplay persona intentionally de-emphasizes risk management and long deliberation.

Mitigation: Require human review before acting on recommendations involving budgets, pricing, market entry, competitive response, or operational commitments.
