## Description:

通过结构化框架评估创业想法的可行性与市场潜力，并输出量化评分、市场分析、风险识别和行动建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External entrepreneurs and startup operators use this skill to structure and evaluate early-stage business ideas, compare risks and market opportunity, and decide whether to refine, validate, or stop a direction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports may include embedded sales lead wording, paid upsell recommendations, and an external enterprise WeChat contact.

Mitigation: Review or constrain the skill before installation when neutral startup advice is required; remove promotional language and third-party contact instructions if they are not wanted.
