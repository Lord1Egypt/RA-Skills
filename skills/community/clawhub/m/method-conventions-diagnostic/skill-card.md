## Description:

Diagnoses academic convention issues in psychology Method sections, including citation attribution, ethics and transparency statements, formatting conventions, scoring, and journal-quality examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, graduate students, and academic writing reviewers use this skill to assess the academic-conventions dimension of psychology Method sections. It produces issue localization, severity-based scoring, concrete revision guidance, and relevant top-journal examples.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill relies on referenced textbook/source material and example paths that may not be fully available in the installed package.

Mitigation: Before relying on full evidence matching, confirm that the expected source materials and packaged reference files are available in the execution environment.

Risk: Academic diagnostics can be over-applied if users treat scoring and examples as a substitute for journal-specific review.

Mitigation: Use the report as reviewer guidance and check final manuscript decisions against the target journal's author instructions and human editorial judgment.
