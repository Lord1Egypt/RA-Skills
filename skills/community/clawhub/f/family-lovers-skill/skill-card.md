## Description:

Family Lovers is a Chinese-language self-help companionship skill for family-of-origin reflection, emotional self-awareness, boundary-setting practice, and supportive role-based conversation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[phoenixlucky](https://clawhub.ai/user/phoenixlucky)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for Chinese-language self-reflection, family-of-origin pattern awareness, emotional support, inner-child exercises, and practical boundary-setting prompts. It is intended as a self-help companionship aid, not as therapy, medical care, crisis intervention, or a substitute for real-world support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The bundled runtime configuration grants network access, broad tool availability, and CodeGraph auto-install authority that are unnecessary for a local text-routing skill.

Mitigation: Before installing, review or change reasonix.toml to disable CodeGraph auto-install, network access, and broad tool availability unless those capabilities are explicitly needed.

Risk: Users could mistake emotional-support roleplay for therapy, medical care, or crisis intervention.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for Chinese-language self-reflection and companionship; for crisis, serious distress, or clinical needs, stop roleplay and seek qualified professional or emergency support.

Risk: Romantic and child personas may increase emotional dependence or blur relationship expectations for vulnerable users.

Mitigation: Keep persona use bounded, prefer real-world support networks, and pause use or seek professional support if the interaction increases dependence.
