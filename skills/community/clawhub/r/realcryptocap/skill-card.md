## Description:

Answer crypto price, ranking, comparison and market-overview questions with live RealCryptoCap data (HTX primary price source, real market cap rankings, Pro market datasets).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to answer cryptocurrency price, ranking, comparison, and market overview questions with live RealCryptoCap data. Pro members can also request advanced market datasets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A benign scan is not a guarantee of zero risk.

Mitigation: Review whether the skill suits your needs before installing, as the security guidance recommends.

Risk: Live market values can change and may be misleading without their update time or market-data context.

Mitigation: Include the tools' update times with quoted prices, distinguish native-asset market-cap rankings from separately listed asset categories, and present the data as not financial advice.
