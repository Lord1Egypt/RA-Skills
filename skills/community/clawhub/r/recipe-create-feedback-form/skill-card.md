## Description:

Create a Google Form for feedback and share it via Gmail.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Google Workspace users and automation agents use this recipe to create a feedback form and email its link to recipients through Gmail.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The recipe can create a Google Form and send email from the connected Google account.

Mitigation: Confirm the Gmail recipient list, subject, body, and form URL before execution.
