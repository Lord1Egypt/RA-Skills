## Description:

OPC Mentor Team is a Chinese-language startup mentoring agent that coordinates AI technology, community operations, project coaching, incubator upgrades, IP building, and follow-up scheduling across the entrepreneur lifecycle.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External entrepreneurs, startup mentors, and incubator or community operators use this skill for Chinese-language coaching that structures project assessment, diagnostics, 90-day plans, weekly check-ins, milestone tracking, and retrospectives. The skill frames outputs as reference advice while final decisions remain with the entrepreneur.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger keywords could activate the skill during general entrepreneurship discussions.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants OPC startup mentoring before applying the skill's coaching workflow.

Risk: Users may share confidential startup, financial, or personal information during ongoing coaching.

Mitigation: Ask users to limit sensitive details and avoid retaining or restating confidential information unless needed for the current coaching task.

Risk: Business coaching outputs may be mistaken for final investment, legal, financial, or operational decisions.

Mitigation: Present outputs as reference guidance and recommend human review before consequential decisions.
