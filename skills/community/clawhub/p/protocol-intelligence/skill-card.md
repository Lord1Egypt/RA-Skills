## Description:

Produces structured research briefs on AI governance, agent protocols, decentralized infrastructure, competitive landscapes, regulatory signals, and strategic implications.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[filipbl4gojevic](https://clawhub.ai/user/filipbl4gojevic)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, product leaders, governance teams, and strategy teams use this skill to turn a topic in AI governance, agent protocols, or decentralized infrastructure into a cited Protocol Intelligence Brief with technical context, competitive positioning, regulatory signals, strategic implications, and a 90-day watch list.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated briefs may include incorrect, outdated, or unverifiable factual, regulatory, deadline, or credential claims.

Mitigation: Verify cited primary sources and current legal or standards status before relying on a brief for decisions.

Risk: Fixed Resomnium branding and experience claims could mislead report readers about authorship or firsthand expertise.

Mitigation: Review and edit attribution, credentials, and branding before sharing generated briefs externally.
