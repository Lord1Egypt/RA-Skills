## Description:

Generate a 7-day LinkedIn content plan from a theme, audience, and pillars. Produces per-day post pillar, format, hook type, CTA, posting time, daily comment targets, and a weekly inbound-readiness check. Use when the user wants to plan a week or month of content, not draft a single post.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users such as founders, executives, and marketing teams use this skill to plan a week or month of LinkedIn content around audience, theme, content pillars, posting cadence, comment targets, and inbound-readiness checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce LinkedIn posting, comment, and DM strategy that users could apply without review.

Mitigation: Review generated plans before use and keep all LinkedIn posting, commenting, messaging, and scheduling as user-controlled actions.

Risk: Voice sample files may contain personal or sensitive writing style examples.

Mitigation: Provide only voice samples that are appropriate for style calibration and acceptable to share with the agent.
