## Description:

Ai Slides Zh helps agents create complete Chinese-oriented PowerPoint presentations from a topic, outline, or notes, including title, agenda, body, summary, and professionally formatted slide content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[olina1ye](https://clawhub.ai/user/olina1ye)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external business users use this skill to quickly draft customer proposals, product presentations, training materials, financial or annual summaries, and roadshow decks in PowerPoint format.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated slide content can contain inaccuracies or unsuitable wording in business, financial, HR, or customer-facing presentations.

Mitigation: Review and edit the generated deck before sharing it, especially for external or decision-making use.

Risk: The skill is Chinese-oriented and may produce language that does not match the intended audience if the desired language is ambiguous.

Mitigation: Specify the target language and audience when requesting the presentation.
