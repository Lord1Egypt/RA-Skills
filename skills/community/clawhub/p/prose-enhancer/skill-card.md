## Description:

A Chinese-oriented prose enhancement skill that helps users polish, rewrite, proofread, and adjust the style of text provided in chat.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chenkek901](https://clawhub.ai/user/chenkek901)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees use this skill to refine Chinese drafts, including grammar correction, tone adjustment, rewriting, shortening, expansion, and clearer presentation of edits. It is intended for text already provided in the conversation and does not access external resources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may apply polished legal, medical, financial, or other specialized text as substantive advice.

Mitigation: Treat the skill output as wording assistance only and have a qualified professional review the underlying substance before use.

Risk: Rewriting may unintentionally shift meaning, tone, or emphasis from the original draft.

Mitigation: Compare the revised text with the original and review the listed changes before publishing or sending it.
