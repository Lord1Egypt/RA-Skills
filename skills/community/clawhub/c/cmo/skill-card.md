## Description:

Provides chief marketing officer guidance for marketing strategy, pipeline planning, budget allocation, channel selection, launches, positioning, lifecycle, measurement, communications, international expansion, and compliance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, marketing leads, and executives use this skill to diagnose marketing performance, make CMO-level allocation decisions, and draft plans, budgets, briefs, and public-facing recommendations. It is intended for strategy and allocation work rather than single-asset execution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may load and save local preference or memory files containing business facts and user preferences.

Mitigation: Review ~/Clawic/data/cmo/ and ~/Clawic/profile.yaml before use, and store only information intended to personalize future marketing guidance.

Risk: Marketing recommendations can affect spend, public statements, price changes, crisis responses, and substantiation-sensitive claims.

Mitigation: Keep money commitments, public statements, price changes, crisis responses, and competitor or guarantee claims subject to human review and documented substantiation before use.
