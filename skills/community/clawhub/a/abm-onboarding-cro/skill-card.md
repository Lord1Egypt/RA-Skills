## Description:

Helps agents optimize post-signup onboarding, user activation, first-run experience, empty states, onboarding checklists, and time-to-value.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mariokarras](https://clawhub.ai/user/mariokarras)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External product, growth, and lifecycle teams use this skill to assess post-signup onboarding, define activation, reduce time-to-value, design onboarding flows, and plan measurement for activation and retention.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be applied to adjacent signup, retention, or email-sequence work where a more specific skill may be a better fit.

Mitigation: Use it for post-signup first-run activation; route signup form optimization to signup-flow-cro and onboarding email sequence work to email-sequence.

Risk: Recommendations may be too generic if product context, activation definition, and drop-off data are missing.

Mitigation: Read product marketing context when available and ask for activation goals, current onboarding state, drop-off points, and cohort evidence before making recommendations.
