## Description:

Guides agents in advising film photographers on negative versus reversal film exposure strategy, especially for high-contrast scenes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and photography assistants use this skill to answer film exposure questions, distinguish negative-film and slide-film latitude behavior, and choose a practical exposure bias for analog high-contrast scenes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is mainly written for Chinese-speaking film photographers, which can limit accurate use by non-Chinese users.

Mitigation: Provide localized guidance or human review before relying on it in non-Chinese workflows.

Risk: Film-era exposure guidance may be misapplied to pure digital photography, where sensor latitude behavior differs.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is shooting negative or reversal film before applying the exposure-asymmetry rule.
