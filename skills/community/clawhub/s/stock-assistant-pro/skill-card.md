## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stock signals and strategy suggestions could be mistaken for professional financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational, verify market data and assumptions independently, and make investment decisions based on personal risk tolerance or qualified financial advice.

Risk: Real-time analysis depends on any market data source supplied by the host agent or user.

Mitigation: Confirm data freshness, ticker identity, market, and source reliability before acting on generated analysis.

Risk: Watchlist and strategy features may involve user preferences if the host application implements storage.

Mitigation: Review host-app storage behavior and avoid entering sensitive portfolio details unless the deployment's privacy controls are acceptable.
