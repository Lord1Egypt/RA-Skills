## Description:

天台藏全集2026 helps agents search and verify Tiantai Buddhist literature, with source citations and CBETA line-level links when available.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and researchers use this skill to retrieve Tiantai Buddhist texts, verify doctrinal claims, and prepare cited answers or structured research notes grounded in the Tiantai Canon 2026 knowledge base.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tiantai-related questions may be routed to a narrow specialized knowledge base even when the user needs broader Buddhist studies coverage.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants Tiantai Canon 2026 coverage, and ask explicitly before using web search or non-Tiantai sources.

Risk: The skill can produce incomplete answers when the subscribed knowledge base does not cover a requested text or claim.

Mitigation: State coverage limits clearly and avoid presenting unsupported doctrine, quotations, or source locations as verified.
