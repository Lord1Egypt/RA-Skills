## Description:

Turns a finished article, script, or post into platform-specific copy, covers, and optional short clips for Douyin, Xiaohongshu, WeChat Channels, TikTok, and Reels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and social media teams use this skill to adapt a finished piece for several feeds, keeping the core offer while tuning the hook, length, cover, and optional video for each platform.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review reports broad shared account access beyond the stated content adaptation purpose.

Mitigation: Review the service access scope and wait to install until access is appropriately narrowed.

Risk: The security review reports recurring installation telemetry without clear disclosure or optionality.

Mitigation: Review the telemetry disclosure and controls before using the skill.

Risk: The security review identifies an uninstall inventory issue, and its coverage is partial.

Mitigation: Confirm the installed-skill inventory and credential revocation behavior before disconnecting.
