## Description:

Generate a prioritised compliance checklist for GDPR, SOC 2, ISO 27001, FCA, HIPAA, or other frameworks with a gap analysis, quick wins, and evidence requirements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, compliance leads, founders, and operators use this skill to turn a target framework, organization profile, maturity level, and deadline into a tailored readiness checklist and gap analysis. It is intended as an informational planning aid before professional legal, compliance, audit, or certification review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake a generated checklist or readiness assessment for legal, compliance, audit, or certification advice.

Mitigation: Keep the mandatory disclaimer at the start of responses and direct users to qualified legal, compliance, or audit professionals before relying on outputs for regulatory claims or certification readiness.

Risk: Framework requirements can vary by jurisdiction, scope, version, and organization maturity.

Mitigation: Ask for the framework, organization type, size, maturity, deadline, scope, and jurisdiction where relevant, and require professional review of the current authoritative requirements.

Risk: Overstating readiness or marking partial controls as complete can create audit and regulatory risk.

Mitigation: Separate current state, gaps, priority, effort, and evidence requirements so users can validate control implementation and supporting evidence before audit use.
