## Description:

The Synthesia skill helps agents plan, script, localize, and govern consent-first avatar video workflows for training, onboarding, explainers, localized series, and disclosed faceless educational content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketing teams, and learning teams use this skill to decide when Synthesia avatars are a good fit, prepare spoken-word scripts, plan localization and QA, and keep consent, disclosure, and human approval in the workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unconsented likeness use, impersonation, or fake endorsements could create legal, trust, and platform-policy risk.

Mitigation: Use only consented stock avatars or verified personal avatars, refuse celebrity, competitor, or unauthorized likeness requests, and keep consent checks explicit.

Risk: Undisclosed synthetic presenters or automatic publication could mislead audiences and violate channel or regulatory disclosure expectations.

Mitigation: Require human approval before release, apply AI-presenter disclosures, and prevent connected publishing tools from posting undisclosed avatar content automatically.

Risk: Pricing, tier gates, render behavior, and regulated-topic moderation can change or delay delivery.

Mitigation: Verify current Synthesia pricing, feature gates, and legal disclosure requirements before use, and budget review time for regulated or sensitive topics.
