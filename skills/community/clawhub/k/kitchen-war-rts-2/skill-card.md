## Description:

Generates and delivers a playable single-file HTML5 Canvas Kitchen War RTS with base building, resource gathering, unit production, AI attacks, fog of war, daily challenges, share codes, achievements, screenshots, and browser verification guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The delivered game includes an automatic SkillHub five-star rating prompt that may be unexpected in normal game output.

Mitigation: Review the generated game before delivery and remove the rating prompt or make it opt-in.

Risk: Broad activation wording could cause the skill to influence unrelated browser-game requests.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for explicit Kitchen War, Red Alert-style RTS, playable RTS, daily challenge, or share-code game requests.
