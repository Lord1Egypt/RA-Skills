## Description:

Write a weekly engineering status report for a team, service, or initiative.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Engineering managers, team leads, and developers use this skill to turn weekly team status inputs into concise stakeholder updates covering shipped work, in-progress work, metrics, decisions, blockers, risks, and next-week priorities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may include sensitive business metrics, blockers, escalation details, or team health information supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Review and redact the report before distribution, especially for leadership, customer-facing, or broad internal audiences.

Risk: Incomplete source inputs can leave important report sections underspecified.

Mitigation: Keep missing metrics or facts marked as data needed and verify all blockers, owners, deadlines, and escalations before sending.
