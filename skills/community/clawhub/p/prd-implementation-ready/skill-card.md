## Description:

Use when writing, reviewing, or filling gaps in PRDs, product requirements, BRDs, functional specs, or SaaS/admin workflows that must become implementation-ready.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wair56](https://clawhub.ai/user/wair56)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, operations teams, and implementation teams use this skill to turn rough PRDs, BRDs, and SaaS/admin workflow notes into requirements that are complete enough for engineering handoff. It is especially suited to business flows involving object states, permissions, data sources, exceptions, finance, reconciliation, formulas, and post-event writebacks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated PRD assumptions could be incorrect or incomplete, especially for money movement, permissions, lifecycle states, cross-system behavior, or formulas.

Mitigation: Review generated assumptions with the business owner before treating them as final requirements, and keep unresolved items explicitly marked for confirmation.

Risk: Bilingual Chinese/English guidance may introduce terminology mismatches for teams that standardize on one language.

Mitigation: Normalize business terms, field names, statuses, and acceptance criteria into the team's implementation language before handoff.
