## Description:

A Chinese-language novelty divination skill that generates playful fortune readings from bundled Bagua, scene-specific, or custom fortune data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tiandaox](https://clawhub.ai/user/tiandaox)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for light entertainment, social interaction, and humorous Chinese-language fortune-style responses. It is intended for novelty readings only, not factual, religious, medical, legal, financial, or life-planning advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake playful fortune-style output for factual advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses clearly framed as entertainment and avoid presenting outcomes as medical, legal, financial, religious, or life-planning guidance.

Risk: Broad fortune-related or scene-related phrases may trigger the skill during casual conversation.

Mitigation: Use the skill when the user asks for divination, fortune, or one of the listed scenes, and preserve the visible entertainment disclaimer.
