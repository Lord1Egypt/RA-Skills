## Description:

Labor Arbitration helps workers in China assess labor dispute evidence, prepare arbitration materials, calculate claim amounts, find submission guidance, and plan mediation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangyue71](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangyue71)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, especially workers in China, use this skill to prepare for labor arbitration involving unpaid wages, unlawful termination, missing contracts, overtime, annual leave, or related claims. It helps organize evidence, generate Markdown application materials, and identify regional submission considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may handle highly sensitive labor, wage, address, phone, and ID information.

Mitigation: Collect only the case details needed, store generated files in a controlled folder, and avoid sharing more documents than necessary.

Risk: Labor arbitration rules, filing channels, and deadlines can vary by region and change over time.

Mitigation: Verify current requirements with 12333, 12348, the local arbitration commission, or a qualified lawyer before filing.

Risk: Generated arbitration documents may contain incomplete case facts, incorrect amounts, or guidance that is not suitable for complex matters.

Mitigation: Review all generated materials before submission and seek qualified legal help for complex, collective, or high-value disputes.
