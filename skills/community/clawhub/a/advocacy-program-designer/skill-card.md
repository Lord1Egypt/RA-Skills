## Description:

Designs employee advocacy and founder-led sharing programs with opt-in roster specs, varied share kits, disclosure lines, staggered posting windows, anti-pod guardrails, and Slack/Teams distribution plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, communications, and founder-led growth teams use this skill to plan employee advocacy programs, prepare compliant share kits, and avoid coercive or coordinated engagement patterns. It is intended for human-operated publishing workflows, not automated posting or paid creator campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may work with employee handles, disclosure lines, opt-in evidence, and internal content plans.

Mitigation: Users should provide only roster data and internal plans they are allowed to use, and should review any proposed memory or registry changes before confirming them.

Risk: Assigned sharing, identical reshares, or engagement rings can create coercion, disclosure, or inauthentic-engagement risk.

Mitigation: Use opt-in participation by default, require per-person variation and disclosure lines, stagger posting windows, and prohibit coordinated identical reshares, engagement rings, and automated replies.
