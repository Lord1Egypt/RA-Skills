## Description:

This prompt-only skill helps e-commerce after-sales support teams draft policy-aligned Chinese responses for returns or exchanges, logistics exceptions, and compensation negotiations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Customer service agents and after-sales operations teams use this skill to generate compliant draft replies for common e-commerce support scenarios. It supports returns and exchanges, delayed or damaged logistics cases, and compensation negotiations while requiring current brand policy details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated replies may rely on placeholder refund, shipping, timing, or compensation values from examples.

Mitigation: Ground each response in current brand policy and review it before sending to customers.
