## Description:

Design statistically rigorous A/B tests for product features, UI changes, onboarding flows, and pricing experiments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External product teams, product managers, analysts, and growth practitioners use this skill to plan A/B tests with a clear hypothesis, variant definitions, sample size, duration, guardrail metrics, rollback triggers, and result interpretation guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sample-size lookup values may be treated as precise enough for business-critical or revenue-impacting experiments.

Mitigation: Verify sample-size and duration calculations with a statistics tool before making business decisions.

Risk: Experiment plans can be misleading when baseline rate, minimum detectable effect, traffic, or guardrail inputs are missing or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Require concrete experiment inputs and define guardrail metrics and rollback triggers before launch.
