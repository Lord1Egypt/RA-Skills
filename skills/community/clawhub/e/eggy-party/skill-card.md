## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be misused to request private account data, login-only assets, or non-public test content.

Mitigation: Limit use to public official pages and exclude private, authenticated, or unpublished information.

Risk: The topic includes possible requests for cheating, account trading, payment bypasses, or anti-addiction bypasses.

Mitigation: Refuse those requests and keep outputs to official public information, rules, safety notices, and help resources.
