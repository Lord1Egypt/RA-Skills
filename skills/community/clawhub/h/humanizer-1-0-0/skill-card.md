## Description:

Removes signs of AI-generated writing from text and rewrites it to sound more natural and human-written.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lion504](https://clawhub.ai/user/lion504)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and developers use this skill to revise text so it reads less like AI-generated prose while preserving the original meaning and intended tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be used to misrepresent authorship where disclosure rules, academic policies, workplace policies, or publication standards require transparency.

Mitigation: Use it only on documents you are authorized to edit, and follow any applicable disclosure or authorship requirements.

Risk: Rewriting can unintentionally change meaning, tone, or attribution.

Mitigation: Review the rewritten text before publishing or submitting it, especially for policy, academic, legal, or workplace content.
