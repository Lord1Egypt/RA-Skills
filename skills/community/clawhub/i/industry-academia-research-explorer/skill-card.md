## Description:

Analyzes a company's collaborations with universities, research institutes, joint labs, expert workstations, and major research projects to assess industry-academia research depth, technical reserves, and potential technology moat.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Forward-looking moat and investment-style judgments may be analytical estimates rather than verified outcomes.

Mitigation: Treat forecasts and strategic judgments as decision support only, and verify material business decisions against primary sources.

Risk: Public collaboration announcements can overstate the depth of research activity.

Mitigation: Prefer official university, research-institute, government, and company sources, and distinguish framework agreements from technical projects, patents, funding, or commercialization evidence.
