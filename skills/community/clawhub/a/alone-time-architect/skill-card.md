## Description:

Design intentional alone time with a clear purpose, realistic duration, phone boundaries, nourishing activities, and a gentle transition back.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to turn a desired solitude block into a practical markdown blueprint with purpose, boundaries, activity options, re-entry, and reflection prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Malformed or unexpected purpose values may cause the skill to fail instead of returning a graceful response.

Mitigation: Validate and normalize purpose inputs, and provide a safe fallback for unsupported purpose values.

Risk: Solitude planning may become avoidance for lonely users or users with caregiving constraints.

Mitigation: Keep the built-in re-entry, reflection, connection-planning, and micro-solitude guidance visible in generated plans.
