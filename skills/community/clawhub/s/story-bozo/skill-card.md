## Description:

Chinese-language writing skill that turns web-novel chapters, outlines, or scene notes into vivid storyboard-style prose with stronger visual rhythm, suspense, and satisfaction beats.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bozoyan](https://clawhub.ai/user/bozoyan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External writers and creative-writing agents use this skill to transform Chinese web-novel chapters, outlines, or plot descriptions into cinematic storyboard scripts and to refine scenes for clearer tension, contrast, and reader satisfaction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad creative-writing requests and may respond in Chinese unless the user asks otherwise.

Mitigation: Use it for Chinese web-novel chapter, outline, or storyboard work, and specify another language or scope when needed.

Risk: Generated creative-writing guidance can still produce flat pacing, excessive exposition, or genre-inappropriate satisfaction beats if the input lacks context.

Mitigation: Review the output against the included scene and satisfaction checklists before using it in a manuscript.
