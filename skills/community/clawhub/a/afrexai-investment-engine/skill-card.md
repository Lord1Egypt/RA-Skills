## Description:

Performs structured investment thesis development, fundamental and technical analysis, portfolio risk management, and trade execution across asset classes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill as an investment-analysis playbook to structure theses, evaluate assets, size positions, manage portfolio risk, document trades, and generate review checklists. Its outputs should be treated as educational research support, not personalized financial, legal, or tax advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce actionable trading, allocation, and tax-harvesting suggestions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational research support, verify data independently, and require explicit human approval before trades, allocation changes, or tax actions.

Risk: The artifact provides frameworks and templates but does not fetch or verify current market, portfolio, or tax data.

Mitigation: Use current, reliable data sources and review assumptions before relying on any analysis.

Risk: Tax guidance may vary by jurisdiction and personal circumstances.

Mitigation: Review tax-related outputs with a qualified professional before acting.
