全面分析黄金价格走势的技能。整合多数据源（金十数据、金价网站、财经新闻），提供技术面、基本面、情绪面三维分析，生成可操作的投资建议。

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to produce gold market analysis reports that combine price action, technical indicators, macroeconomic factors, sentiment, geopolitics, and time-horizon outlooks. The skill can also generate trading-oriented suggestions such as support, resistance, stop-loss, and position-size guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce buy/sell, stop-loss, and position-size suggestions that could be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Verify live market data from authoritative sources and treat generated suggestions as informational analysis, not individualized investment advice.

Risk: Gold market reports can become misleading when based on stale prices, macroeconomic data, or news events.

Mitigation: Cross-check current prices, economic releases, central bank decisions, and news before relying on the analysis.
