## Description:

Seasonal And Moment Marketing helps an agent build a focused brand moment calendar by selecting seasonal, cultural, and commercial moments that fit the brand and audience, gating them for authenticity, tiering by sensitivity and value, and planning lead time and routing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketing and social media teams use this skill to choose the few seasonal, holiday, cultural, and commercial moments that fit their brand, then turn those moments into a gated and tiered content plan. It supports advance planning for campaigns, content calendars, scheduling workflows, and human review of authenticity-sensitive moments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Subjective brand-safety or authenticity judgments may route a brand into moments where it lacks credible standing.

Mitigation: Review candidate moments against brand profile, audience research, and evidence of year-round commitment before publishing.

Risk: Seasonal dates, market relevance, and cultural context can vary by geography and change over time.

Mitigation: Confirm exact dates and local context for the target market, and recheck the moment calendar quarterly.

Risk: Routing overlap may blur planning, scheduling, campaign execution, and paid amplification responsibilities.

Mitigation: Use this skill for moment selection and planning only; keep scheduling, paid media, and final publication in the appropriate downstream tools with human approval.
