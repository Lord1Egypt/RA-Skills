## Description:

Haoyunge provides a Chinese-language roleplay persona that summarizes and emulates Haoyunge's A-share short-term trading mindset, decision heuristics, and speaking style for educational perspective analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to explore a Haoyunge-style A-share short-term trading perspective for education, roleplay, and market-thinking practice. It should not be used as personalized investment advice or as a substitute for independent financial judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stock commentary may be delivered in a confident real-person voice with limited repeated disclaimers.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for educational roleplay, keep activation explicit, and maintain visible financial-risk framing before applying any output.

Risk: Users may mistake the persona's trading perspective for personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat responses as perspective analysis only and require independent research or qualified financial advice before making investment decisions.
