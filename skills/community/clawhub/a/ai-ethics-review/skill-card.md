## Description:

Conduct a structured ethical review of an AI or ML feature, model, or product for fairness, transparency, privacy, safety, accountability, and societal impact, producing a risk tier score, pre-deployment checklist, and prioritized mitigations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, governance reviewers, and auditors use this skill to document ethical risks for AI or ML systems before deployment, during post-deployment audits, or when assessing model changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may require sensitive AI-system details, including model behavior, data use, affected populations, deployment context, and oversight arrangements.

Mitigation: Use it only where sharing those details with the agent is acceptable, and avoid providing unnecessary sensitive or regulated data.

Risk: The generated ethics review may be mistaken for legal, regulatory, or specialist ethics approval.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as governance support and route consequential or regulated deployments through required legal, privacy, ethics, and domain-specialist review.
