## Use Case:

External creators and content teams use this skill to draft, structure, title, and review Chinese-language WeChat public-account articles for high-value commercial niches such as legal rights, finance, career development, and car ownership.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal or finance article drafts may contain inaccurate facts, outdated policy details, or unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Review facts against authoritative sources before use, and treat the output as drafting assistance rather than professional advice.

Risk: A connected publishing workflow could upload or post a draft before a person has approved it.

Mitigation: Require explicit human approval before any upload, draft submission, or publication step.

Risk: High-eCPM optimization could drift into sensitive or noncompliant topic areas.

Mitigation: Keep topics within the stated legal, finance, career, and car-ownership niches and avoid politics, social unrest, and other sensitive topics.
