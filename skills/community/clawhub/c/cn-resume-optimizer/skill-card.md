## Use Case:

External users and job seekers use this skill for Chinese-language resume diagnosis, achievement rewriting, job-description matching, cover letters, self-introductions, and interview preparation. It is intended for normal job-application writing and guidance workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume and job-application drafts may include contact details, career history, and other personal information.

Mitigation: Provide only the information needed for the task and remove unnecessary sensitive details before sharing content with the agent.

Risk: Broad career-related prompts may activate the skill when the user only wants general job-search discussion.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired resume, application, or interview-preparation task before applying detailed rewriting guidance.
