## Use Case:

Content creators, educators, and presenters use this skill to turn source material into a concise Chinese vertical tech-style PPT script and single-file presentation structure. It emphasizes one idea per screen, dark minimalist visuals, short high-impact section titles, keyboard navigation, and smooth transitions.
