This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External TikTok Shop sellers and their agents use this skill to find and read order, product, and authorized shop information through a connected account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Setup runs a changeable installer script downloaded from the internet; the security scan was partial and the installer was not available for inspection.

Mitigation: Have an administrator independently review and verify the current installer and resolve the referenced files before following the setup instructions.
