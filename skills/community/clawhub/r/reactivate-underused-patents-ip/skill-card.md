## Description:

Assess underused patent assets and related intangible assets, cluster them into decision-ready packages, evaluate internal reuse and transaction options, research candidate counterparties, and create evidence-backed portfolio activation deliverables.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Patent, IP, R&D, licensing, and portfolio-management teams use this skill to review a defined patent or related-intangible-asset population, identify evidence-backed activation options, and prepare decision-ready internal or external deliverables with diligence gates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may require access to sensitive patent portfolios, internal business records, contracts, product plans, or transaction history.

Mitigation: Install and invoke it only for approved patent or intangible-asset work, limit source access to authorized records and connectors, and confirm confidentiality boundaries before generating deliverables.

Risk: External reports could expose confidential technical detail, personal data, internal thresholds, negotiating positions, or restricted source content.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended audience and confidentiality level, then produce only the authorized internal or external-safe version with restricted details removed.

Risk: Patent scores, database signals, citations, or missing records could be mistaken for legal assurance, formal valuation, buyer interest, market demand, or transaction readiness.

Mitigation: Keep evidence states visible, distinguish model-derived inputs from professional conclusions, and require legal, finance, tax, valuation, regulatory, technical, and business approval before outreach or irreversible decisions.
