## Description:

Registers a Huya Live skill for organizing public live-stream categories, creator rules, activity information, and help documentation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to summarize public Huya Live pages, including live categories, activity pages, creator rules, safety notes, and help-center content. It is scoped to public information and excludes private account, monetization, message, evasion, cheating, and platform-abuse workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public Huya Live activity and rule pages may change frequently, making summaries stale or incomplete.

Mitigation: Include source links and retrieval times when summarizing activities, rules, help entries, or safety guidance.

Risk: The skill could be misapplied to private account, monetization, message, evasion, cheating, or platform-abuse workflows.

Mitigation: Keep use limited to public Huya Live research and summaries, and reject requests involving private data, backend access, cheating, or abuse.
