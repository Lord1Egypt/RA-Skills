## Description:

Live Commerce Agent supports end-to-end live commerce operations, including product planning, livestream scripting, host training, performance analysis, and fan operations for ecommerce teams and MCN agencies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Mitigation: Review and adapt the templates for brand voice, platform policies, and applicable advertising requirements before using them in a live commerce workflow.
