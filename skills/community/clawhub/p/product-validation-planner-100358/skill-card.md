## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototype workflows, and user-facing positioning before heavy investment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps convert rough ideas into repeatable validation workflows, checklists, lightweight analyses, prototypes, positioning, and decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad product, startup, validation, prototype, and SaaS triggers may invoke the skill for generic business requests.

Mitigation: Review whether the skill is relevant before relying on its output, especially when the request is only loosely related to product validation.

Risk: Planning artifacts can be incomplete or misleading if user goals, constraints, market assumptions, or success criteria are underspecified.

Mitigation: Use the skill's workflow to surface assumptions, request materially missing information, and validate the result against explicit success criteria before acting.
