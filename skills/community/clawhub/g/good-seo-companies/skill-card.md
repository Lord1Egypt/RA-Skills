## Description:

Query evidence-based SEO agency rankings, 7-factor scorecards, and city indexes from Good SEO Companies (goodseocompanies.com) via its public llms.txt and machine-readable JSON endpoints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fsakbas](https://clawhub.ai/user/fsakbas)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can find, compare, and verify SEO agencies by city using published rankings, seven-factor scorecards, and linked source evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review found no reported issues, but confidence is medium and one path-like reference in the skill could not be resolved to a bundled artifact.

Mitigation: Inspect the unresolved reference and verify agency claims on their cited city or profile pages before relying on the results.
