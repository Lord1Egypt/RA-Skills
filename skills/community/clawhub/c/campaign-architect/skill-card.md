## Description:

Designs paid-ads account structures by selecting campaign types, laying out ad groups or asset groups, planning targeting and match types, building negative and exclusion lists, auditing paid-organic overlap, and scoring ROAS Audience structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External marketers, performance marketing teams, and agent-skill users use this skill to plan or restructure paid-ad accounts before launch. It helps choose campaign types, organize ad groups or asset groups, define targeting and match types, prepare negative or exclusion hygiene, audit paid-organic cannibalization, and prepare a ROAS Audience/structure handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
