## Description: <br>
Design typed ontology and knowledge-graph workflows for agent memory, structured notes, domain models, and retrieval systems, including entity types, relations, schemas, examples, validation rules, migration plans, and query patterns. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
