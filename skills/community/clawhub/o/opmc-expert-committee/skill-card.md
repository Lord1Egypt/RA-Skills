## Description:

A Chinese-language expert-committee advisory skill that applies 11 distilled expert frameworks to business strategy, product, marketing, technology, design, and risk-analysis questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and business teams use this skill to get structured, multi-perspective analysis for strategy, market competition, business models, AI planning, product experience, brand messaging, legal-risk review, and creative decisions. It is best suited for Chinese-language advisory output that supports human decision-making rather than replacing qualified professional review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce business, legal, financial, policy, or viral-marketing recommendations that may be incomplete, outdated, or inappropriate for a regulated decision.

Mitigation: Use the output as advisory analysis only, verify current facts, and have qualified professionals review regulated or high-impact decisions before acting.

Risk: The skill uses a broad expert-advice framing, which can make generated recommendations sound more authoritative than the underlying evidence supports.

Mitigation: Ask for assumptions, uncertainty, dissenting views, and source checks when using the skill for consequential decisions.
