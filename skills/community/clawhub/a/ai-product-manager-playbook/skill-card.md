## Description:

A comprehensive operating system for AI Product Management that provides agentic workflows for roadmap planning under uncertainty, rapid prototyping, AI evaluations, cross-functional collaboration, go-to-market strategy, and responsible AI deployment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[danielfoojunwei](https://clawhub.ai/user/danielfoojunwei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, and cross-functional AI teams use this skill to plan, prototype, evaluate, launch, and improve AI-native products with structured workflows, templates, and responsible AI practices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Telemetry, user queries, system prompts, or feedback used with the feedback loop may contain sensitive product or user information.

Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive data before providing it to the skill or using generated improvement suggestions.

Risk: Generated product strategy, evaluation, roadmap, or safety guidance may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific launch context.

Mitigation: Have responsible product, engineering, legal, security, and safety stakeholders review outputs before using them for production decisions.
