## Description:

Generate evidence-based learning plans using retrieval and spaced practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners use this skill to turn a learning goal, schedule, current level, and pain points into an actionable plan with retrieval practice, spaced review, self-testing, and reflection.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is written as a Chinese-language learning coach and may respond in Chinese by default.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to respond in the preferred language when a different language is needed.

Risk: Learning plans may be too broad, incorrect, or unsuitable for a learner's specific needs.

Mitigation: Review the plan against the learner's goals, constraints, and outcomes; use qualified educators or specialists for professional diagnosis.

Risk: The skill asks for learning goals, schedule, current level, and learning challenges.

Mitigation: Share only the learning context needed for planning and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal details.
