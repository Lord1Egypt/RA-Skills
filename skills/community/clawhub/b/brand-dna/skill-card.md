This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business owners, solopreneurs, marketers, and content teams use this skill to turn business details into reusable brand guidance for voice, positioning, audiences, messaging pillars, and visual identity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Brand details, founder stories, target audience notes, or positioning inputs may contain sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive details before sharing them with an agent or using them in generated brand guidance.

Risk: The artifact links to an optional external ContentAI Suite service.

Mitigation: Review the external service separately before entering confidential business information there.
