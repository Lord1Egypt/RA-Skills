This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers use this skill to add structured ethical review to requests involving fairness, bias, cross-cultural policy, non-interference, data sovereignty, or other value-laden decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may cause an agent to persist unspecified ethics artifacts and metrics under the user's home directory.

Mitigation: Before use, confirm what is saved under ~/.openclaw/hands/federation_ethic_hand, whether prompt content is included, and how to disable or delete that data.

Risk: The skill may change how broad categories of ethically complex requests are handled.

Mitigation: Review its ethical guidance posture before deployment and keep user judgment authoritative for value-laden decisions.
