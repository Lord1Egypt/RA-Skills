## Description:

Extracts hidden assumptions from plans or documents, prices what happens if they are wrong versus what it costs to test them, and turns the riskiest assumptions into decisive next-week tests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product teams, strategy reviewers, founders, and operators use this skill before committing to a plan, PRD, forecast, model, or strategy. It helps identify hidden assumptions, rank them by exposure, choose the cheapest useful tests, and produce an honest confidence statement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reviewed plans, forecasts, PRDs, or spreadsheet assumptions may contain sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with documents the user is comfortable sharing in the agent environment, and redact unnecessary sensitive details before review.

Risk: Assumption pricing and test selection can influence planning decisions if treated as definitive.

Mitigation: Treat the ledger as decision support, verify high-impact assumptions with accountable domain owners, and keep tests capable of disproving the assumption.
