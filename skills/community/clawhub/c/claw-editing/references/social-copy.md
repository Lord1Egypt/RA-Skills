# Social copy for OpenClaw videos

Offer this after delivering a video. Write from the transcript, and claim only what's said in the video. Leave placeholders for handles and links you don't have, and say so in your reply (Kevin's X handle and the Discord URL were unknown last time).

## YouTube description
Structure:
1. A hook line, usually the video's title as a question ("What is OpenClaw Enterprise, and why does it matter?").
2. One or two sentences: who's talking (name plus role), and what it is or what's new.
3. One or two sentences of context (origin, partners, status).
4. "We cover:" followed by 5–8 bullets, each a concrete idea from the video.
5. A call to action: "Find the OpenClaw Foundation at openclaw.ai and join us on Discord."
6. **Chapters:**
   - Times must match the final file. Add the thumbnail lead-in (+1 s) and any title-card offset.
   - Start at `0:00`, list at least 3 chapters, each at least 10 s long, with the time floored to the second.
   - Use the section-overlay titles in sentence case.

## X / Twitter
- One main post under 280 characters: a hook question plus who and what. A 🦞 is on-brand.
- An optional 3-post thread with the strongest ideas, numbered "1/", "2/", "3/". The last post ends with a call to action and the link.
- X doesn't make timestamps clickable; use highlights instead.

## LinkedIn
- Open with a 1–2 line hook that works before "…see more" (a question about the business problem).
- Short context paragraph.
- "A few things that stood out:" followed by 4–6 emoji-led one-liners (🔒 🧱 🔌 ☁️ 🧪 work well for platform topics).
- A reflective takeaway paragraph (the speaker's big idea).
- Who should get involved, plus openclaw.ai.
- "Full conversation 👇" and 4–5 hashtags (#AIAgents #OpenSource #EnterpriseAI #PlatformEngineering #OpenClaw).
- Tips to include in your reply:
  - Tag people and companies with @ in LinkedIn's editor; pasted text doesn't make tags clickable.
  - Put the YouTube link in the first comment, since LinkedIn tends to show posts with outside links to fewer people.
