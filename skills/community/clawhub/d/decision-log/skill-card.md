This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to record decisions, rationale, alternatives, expected results, and later review outcomes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Decision records and event metadata may persist sensitive personal, business, or security decisions.

Mitigation: Use explicit prompts when recording decisions and avoid logging sensitive decisions unless that persistence is acceptable.
