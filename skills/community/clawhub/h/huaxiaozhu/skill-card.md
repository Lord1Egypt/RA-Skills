This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and travel planners use this skill to compare Huaxiaozhu ride-hailing scenarios, understand fixed-fare and discount mechanics, and keep safety checks in view before booking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fare, coupon, and policy details may be outdated or differ by location, time, or account.

Mitigation: Verify current prices, discounts, and ride policies in the official Huaxiaozhu app before booking.

Risk: Ride-hailing safety guidance may not cover every traveler, route, or emergency situation.

Mitigation: Use official in-app safety options, share trips with trusted contacts when appropriate, and follow local emergency procedures.
