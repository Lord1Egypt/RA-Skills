## Use Case:

QA engineers, product teams, and developers use this skill to turn requirements, screenshots, specifications, or brief text into structured test coverage, review questions, and execution-ready cases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Requirements or screenshots may contain confidential product details.

Mitigation: Share only information intended for agent processing and keep uploaded requirements confidential.

Risk: Generated test cases can miss context or become misleading if product details are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review generated cases before using them as release criteria and state dependencies on missing product detail.
