## Description:

系统化整合多管理体系要素，支持预置ISO标准映射、兼容性分析与文件生成；当用户需要整合质量/环境/职业健康安全/信息安全等管理体系时使用

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, consultants, auditors, and management-system owners use this skill to integrate multiple ISO-style management systems, map shared requirements, analyze compatibility, and draft reusable Markdown materials such as integrated manuals and comparison tables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide internal management-system details while using the skill.

Mitigation: Share only information appropriate for the agent environment and follow organizational data-handling rules for internal management-system material.

Risk: Generated compliance and management-system materials are templates and may not fully match a specific organization or applicable requirements.

Mitigation: Have qualified business, quality, safety, security, or compliance owners review and tailor generated materials before operational use.

Risk: Compatibility analysis is based on the skill's preset ISO-style frameworks and user-provided details.

Mitigation: Validate mappings, gaps, conflicts, and integration recommendations against current standards, certification scope, and actual business processes.
