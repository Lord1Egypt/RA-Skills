## Description:

将心理学研究兴趣或初步方案转化为理论连贯、变量明确、测量可实施、分析可检验的研究设计；适用于研究问题细化、方案设计与设计审查，不用于诊断个体或替代伦理审批。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[adecimall](https://clawhub.ai/user/adecimall)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External researchers, students, and research teams use this Chinese-language skill to turn psychology research interests or drafts into actionable study designs with research questions, variables, hypotheses, measurement plans, sampling and analysis guidance, and design-risk review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may mention literature, measurement scales, legal requirements, ethics requirements, or institution-specific approval obligations that require current external verification.

Mitigation: Verify cited literature, scale details, legal or ethics requirements, and local approval obligations before relying on the output.

Risk: Psychology research design guidance could be mistaken for clinical diagnosis, treatment advice, or formal ethics approval.

Mitigation: Use the output only as research design support and obtain appropriate clinical, legal, ethics, and institutional review where applicable.
