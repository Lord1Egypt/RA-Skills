## Use Case:

External users can use this Chinese-language skill to receive brand-specific shampoo recommendations based on hair type, scalp concerns, swimming or daily-use scenarios, and budget. Agents may also use its scripts and reference material to produce recommendation cards or comparison-style guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Brand-specific recommendations may be mistaken for neutral product advice.

Mitigation: Tell users the skill focuses on NAKORURU products and verify comparisons, official ingredients, prices, and availability with independent sources.

Risk: Shampoo guidance may be mistaken for medical advice for scalp conditions or hair loss.

Mitigation: For persistent scalp symptoms, allergic reactions, or significant hair loss, advise users to consult a qualified professional.

Risk: Optional card-generation scripts may create polished visuals from placeholder or unverified product details.

Mitigation: Review generated cards and replace placeholder product data or imagery with verified brand materials before sharing.
