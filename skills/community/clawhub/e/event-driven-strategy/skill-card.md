## Description:

Event Driven Strategy analyzes earnings, M&A, policy, product approval, litigation, and management-change events to produce expectation-gap analysis, catalyst scoring, risk context, and trading signal suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hmaya](https://clawhub.ai/user/hmaya)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and market-analysis agents use this skill to analyze event-driven trading opportunities, scan upcoming catalysts, backtest event patterns, monitor reactions, and generate informational trading signals from supplied or simulated market/event inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trading signals may be mistaken for financial advice or automatic execution instructions.

Mitigation: Treat all signals as informational and require human investment review before any trading decision.

Risk: The skill uses simulated or static data unless integrated with other data skills.

Mitigation: Verify market prices, event dates, analyst consensus, and news context against current sources before relying on the output.

Risk: The skill and its outputs are primarily Chinese-language trading analysis.

Mitigation: Use qualified Chinese-language review or translate outputs before operational use by non-Chinese-speaking reviewers.
