## Description:

Generates Chinese-language enterprise investment value assessment reports using public information to analyze industry position, growth signals, capital attention, risk constraints, and decision recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business analysts, investment teams, and economic development users can use this skill to draft Chinese-language enterprise investment or due-diligence reports from public, verifiable information. It is intended as decision support for investment screening, industry research, partnership diligence, and investment promotion workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs may contain public-information analysis that is incomplete, stale, or unsuitable as a sole basis for investment or diligence decisions.

Mitigation: Verify cited facts independently and treat the report as decision support rather than a substitute for professional due diligence.

Risk: Users may misread the report as securities advice or an investment return recommendation.

Mitigation: Preserve the skill's stated disclaimer that outputs are not securities trading advice and do not promise investment returns.
