## Description:

Review and analyse a whole Subclip timeline in bulk.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Video editors and project creators use this skill to review pacing, layers, captions, and visual style across a composed timeline, then target fixes and verify edits in batches.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contact sheets marked HF OFF do not show HyperFrames layers, so those tiles may not represent the complete composition.

Mitigation: Do not assess HyperFrames layers from HF OFF tiles; include HyperFrames in a follow-up render before making a decision about those layers.

Risk: The security review advises checking flagged instruction context before installation when it is relevant to the intended use.

Mitigation: Review the flagged instruction context in the security report before installing when it matters to your use.
