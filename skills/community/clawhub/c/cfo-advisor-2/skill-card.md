## Description:

Provides CFO-style financial leadership guidance for startups and scaling companies, including financial modeling, unit economics, fundraising strategy, cash management, and board financial packages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vasan-rajesh](https://clawhub.ai/user/vasan-rajesh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, finance leaders, and agents supporting startup leadership use this skill to analyze runway, unit economics, fundraising readiness, budgets, and board financial materials. Outputs should support planning and review, not replace professional financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on CFO-style outputs for financial, legal, tax, or investment decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning support and verify assumptions, calculations, and recommendations with qualified reviewers before making decisions.

Risk: The skill may read company-context.md for relevant finance tasks, which can contain sensitive company information.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary company context and review generated outputs before sharing them outside the intended audience.
