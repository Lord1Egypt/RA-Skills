## Description:

Explains OpenClaw’s local security audit results in plain language, prioritizes findings, and points to OpenClaw’s remediation guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ismyagentsafe](https://clawhub.ai/user/ismyagentsafe)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

OpenClaw users can use the skill to understand their plain security audit results, decide which reported settings to review first, and find the relevant fix guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The available security review had incomplete coverage, so its benign verdict does not establish that all risks were assessed.

Mitigation: Consider the limited coverage when deciding whether to install, and review the skill before use.
