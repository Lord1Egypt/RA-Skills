## Description:

Find link-building prospects for a page or asset: resource pages, listicles, publishers, and local sites from live SERPs and competitor backlinks, with contact paths and personalized outreach drafts. Use when the user wants backlinks, link building, guest post or PR targets, outreach emails for links, or the sites that link to a competitor.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[winsley-jeune](https://clawhub.ai/user/winsley-jeune)

## Use Case:

SEO professionals, marketers, and site owners use the skill to identify relevant pages that may reference a linkable asset, then find public contact paths and prepare personalized outreach. It can also help assess competitor backlink patterns when HarborRank is connected.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment is incomplete because two local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review those references if they are relevant to the intended use before relying on the skill.

Risk: Prospect pages and public contact paths can change or may not suit a particular outreach message.

Mitigation: Recheck each prospect and contact path at its cited source, personalize messages, and avoid mass outreach.
