## Description:

Use Subclip's desktop tools to navigate web pages and apps, interact with controls, capture screenshots or recordings, and follow guidance on permissions, browser use, checkout, and safe handling of page and window text.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and agents use Subclip to navigate web pages and apps, interact with controls, and inspect or record desktop activity. The guidance supports permission checks, screenshot-based verification, and safer handling of sign-in, checkout, and untrusted page content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A screen recording could capture sensitive sign-in information; the security analysis was partial.

Mitigation: Do not record login, password, or verification screens. Limit capture to the intended app window or area, and review the recording instructions before use.
