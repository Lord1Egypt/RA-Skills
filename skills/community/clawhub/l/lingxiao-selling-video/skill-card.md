## Description:

Guides agents to create product-selling video concepts, shot lists, and prompts across pain-point, identity/emotion, and promotion angles, with practical checks for AI-generated video scenes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and commerce operators use this skill to plan short product-selling videos, generate shot-by-shot prompts, and diagnose why product video material may not convert. It is especially aimed at TikTok-style commerce assets and AI video generation workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer finished video generation to lingxiaochuhai.com services that require login and token consumption.

Mitigation: Treat those services as optional, review cost and privacy terms before uploading product assets, and use another video-generation provider when preferred.

Risk: AI-generated people, music, or brand elements may create compliance or rights issues for finished product videos.

Mitigation: Have the user or responsible reviewer check generated assets, brand usage, and campaign claims before publication.
