## Description:

Audit ecommerce listings, ad copy, and creator content against TikTok Shop policies to catch violations before they trigger penalties.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators and compliance reviewers use this skill to audit TikTok Shop listings, creator deliverables, ads, promotional pricing, restricted-category documentation, and violation responses before publication or appeal.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: TikTok Shop policies and restricted-category requirements can change and vary by market.

Mitigation: Verify current TikTok Shop rules, category requirements, and the target market before relying on final recommendations.

Risk: Platform-policy review does not replace local advertising, consumer-protection, or product-regulatory legal review.

Mitigation: Use qualified legal review for high-risk categories, regulated claims, and market-specific advertising obligations.

Risk: Incomplete listing, creator, pricing, market, or substantiation inputs can produce incomplete audit findings.

Mitigation: Collect the full audit surface, product category, target market, certifications, claim evidence, offer terms, and creator materials before finalizing an audit.
