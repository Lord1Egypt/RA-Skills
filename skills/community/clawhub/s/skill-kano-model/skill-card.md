## Description:

Guides agents through Kano model requirements analysis by collecting functional and dysfunctional ratings, classifying needs, calculating Better-Worse coefficients, and producing a priority matrix.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, service, process, and quality teams use this skill to structure Kano interviews or questionnaire summaries, classify requirements, and prepare prioritization outputs for planning or review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The inspected package references a helper script and reference files that are not included.

Mitigation: Confirm the referenced files are supplied before use, or perform Kano classification and Better-Worse calculations manually with reviewed formulas.

Risk: Single-person interview inputs may be exploratory and may not represent broader user needs for important decisions.

Mitigation: Use questionnaire summary mode with an appropriate sample and review the resulting classifications before relying on the priority matrix.
