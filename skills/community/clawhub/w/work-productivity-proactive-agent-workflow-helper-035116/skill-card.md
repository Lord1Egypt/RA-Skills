## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording and implicit invocation may cause the skill to activate more often than intended.

Mitigation: Review invocation settings before deployment and tighten trigger terms when predictable activation is required.

Risk: Workflow recommendations or generated artifacts may be incomplete if the user's outcome, constraints, or success criteria are underspecified.

Mitigation: Require the agent to restate assumptions, validate the result against explicit success criteria, and list residual risks or follow-up work.
