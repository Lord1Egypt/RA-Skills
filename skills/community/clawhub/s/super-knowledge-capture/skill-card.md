## Description: <br>
Converts conversations, meeting notes, and discussion transcripts into structured Notion documentation with summaries, decisions, action items, and related links. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, team leads, and knowledge managers use this skill to turn meetings, chats, interviews, customer calls, and architecture discussions into organized Notion pages. It is intended for preserving institutional knowledge and tracking decisions and follow-up work. <br>
