## Description:

Provides Chinese-language educational guidance for applying the 《股票操盘宝典》 A-share trading methodology, including market phase assessment, stock-selection criteria, technical buy/sell points, risk controls, position management, and trading-system discipline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer Chinese-language questions about the 《股票操盘宝典》 trading framework and to route requests to the appropriate capability card. It is intended for educational methodology guidance, not deterministic stock prediction, personalized investment advice, or leveraged trading recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake educational buy/sell or position guidance for personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as educational methodology, require users to verify current market data and personal risk tolerance, and recommend consulting a licensed professional for actual trading decisions.

Risk: The source methodology is based on 2014-2016 A-share market experience and may not fully fit newer market structures.

Mitigation: Surface the skill's stated limitation that registration-system, institutional, and quantitative-market changes can weaken historical pattern assumptions.

Risk: Users may ask for deterministic stock predictions, stock recommendations, leverage, or aggressive full-position trades.

Mitigation: Refuse or redirect those requests to risk-control guidance, position limits, stop-loss discipline, and non-personalized framework explanation.
