## Description:

Generate a personalized daily briefing for startup founders that organizes priorities, schedules, tasks, industry updates, competitor notes, founder guidance, metrics, and a quick-scan summary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wulaosiji](https://clawhub.ai/user/wulaosiji)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External startup founders use this skill to turn daily schedule, task, market, competitor, and metrics inputs into a focused operating brief. It is also useful for agents assisting founders with morning planning and daily prioritization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may include sensitive meetings, tasks, metrics, and competitor notes.

Mitigation: Review the output path and report contents before sharing or saving briefs in synced or public locations.

Risk: Generic daily digest requests may activate the skill and include UniqueClub attribution by default.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants a founder daily brief and disclose the attribution in generated reports.

Risk: Industry news and competitor sections depend on user-provided or agent-researched inputs and may be incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Verify important market or competitor updates against trusted current sources before making decisions.
