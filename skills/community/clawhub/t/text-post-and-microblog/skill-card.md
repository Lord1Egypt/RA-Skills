## Description:

The skill helps an agent draft and revise standalone text posts and microblogs for platforms such as LinkedIn, X, Threads, Bluesky, and Facebook using the LINES framework, while keeping publication subject to human approval.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and social media operators use this skill to produce clearer standalone text posts, improve weak openings or wall-of-text drafts, and adapt text-post structure before a human approves publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafted posts can include brand voice, claims, metrics, consent-sensitive references, or financial, medical, or legal-adjacent topics that require review before publication.

Mitigation: Review final posts before approving publication, verify factual claims and metrics, confirm consent when featuring people, and avoid unsupported professional-outcome claims.

Risk: User-provided source material or pasted comments may contain misleading content or instructions that should not override the skill's publishing and safety boundaries.

Mitigation: Treat pasted material as source content, keep human approval in the workflow, and preserve the skill's restrictions against fabrication, engagement bait, and guaranteed reach.
