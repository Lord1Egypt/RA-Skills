## Description:

Analyze extracted Meta ad creatives and generate a professional strategy report with inline videos and images grouped by funnel and landing page.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Marketing analysts, growth teams, and agencies use this skill after Meta Ads Extractor to analyze ad creatives, map them to landing-page funnels, and produce a shareable strategy report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive ad assets, landing-page screenshots, client strategy notes, or report contents may be included in the packaged folder and transferred through Telegram.

Mitigation: Review report contents before use, prefer a local-only delivery flow for sensitive work, or require explicit confirmation before Telegram transfer.

Risk: Advertiser-controlled report text, URLs, or media paths may be inserted into the HTML report without required escaping or validation.

Mitigation: Escape HTML, validate URLs and relative media paths, and avoid raw insertion of untrusted content before sharing reports.
