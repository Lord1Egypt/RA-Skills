## Description:

Extracts tasks, deadlines, and responsible owners from meeting minutes, transcripts, or notes, then returns an organized action-item list with priority and owner.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[andreataide86](https://clawhub.ai/user/andreataide86)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Meeting participants, project managers, coordinators, agile teams, freelancers, and other professionals use this skill to turn Portuguese meeting notes or transcripts into action-item lists, meeting summaries, and follow-up status.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved follow-up history may include sensitive meeting details, identities, or action history.

Mitigation: Confirm what will be stored before saving follow-up history, avoid highly sensitive meeting content unless appropriate for the workspace, and request one-time extraction when persistence is not needed.

Risk: Ambiguous notes may lead to missing owners, uncertain deadlines, or inferred priorities.

Mitigation: Keep uncertain owners or deadlines explicit and ask clarifying questions before treating the generated action-item list as final.
