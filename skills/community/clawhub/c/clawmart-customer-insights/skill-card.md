## Description:

Analyzes customer behavior and transaction data to provide customer segmentation, deal forecasting, follow-up timing, and personalized marketing recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales, customer success, and marketing users can use this skill to analyze WeChat, email, and optional CRM customer records for prioritization, conversion forecasting, and follow-up recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intended to process customer communications and business records that may contain sensitive personal or commercial information.

Mitigation: Use it only with authorized WeChat, email, and CRM data; limit inputs to necessary records and define retention and deletion expectations for any locally stored analysis.

Risk: Customer segmentation, deal forecasts, and follow-up recommendations may be inaccurate or incomplete if source data is stale, sparse, or biased.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before customer action and compare them with current account context and business judgment.
