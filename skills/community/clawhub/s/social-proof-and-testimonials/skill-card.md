## Description:

Turns real customer reviews, testimonials, UGC, case studies, and results into believable, objection-matched social proof content using the VOUCH framework while keeping consent, disclosure, and authenticity requirements explicit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketing and social media teams use this skill to turn real, consented customer proof into quote cards, captions, carousels, UGC reshares, case-study cuts, and placement guidance. It helps match proof to buyer objections while avoiding fabricated testimonials, deceptive suppression, undisclosed material connections, and unsupported results claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Proof content can become misleading or noncompliant if it uses fabricated testimonials, lacks consent or rights, omits material-connection disclosures, or makes unsupported regulated claims.

Mitigation: Use only real customer material, confirm consent and usage rights, disclose paid, gifted, or insider relationships, and review legal or FTC-related details for regulated claims before anything is scheduled or published.
