## Description:

Video Script AI helps short-video teams generate ideas, hooks, outlines, shooting-ready scripts, shot lists, platform-specific copy, and CTAs for ads, product demos, creator content, explainers, and repurposed shorts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[imwyvern](https://clawhub.ai/user/imwyvern)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, creators, marketers, and video teams use this skill to draft short-form video scripts with hooks, beat-by-beat timing, visual direction, on-screen text, copy variants, and calls to action for platforms such as TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Douyin, and Xiaohongshu.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated scripts may include marketing claims or platform-specific advice that is inaccurate, exaggerated, or unsuitable for publication.

Mitigation: Review generated claims, calls to action, and platform guidance before publishing or using the script in a campaign.
