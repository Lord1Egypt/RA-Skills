## Description:

Hutian Opc Patent Search helps agents provide China-focused IP novelty search and filing workflow guidance across patents, trademarks, copyright, application compliance, and post-grant maintenance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use this skill to structure IP pre-review, search planning, filing preparation, agent selection, maintenance tracking, and risk-oriented checklists for China-focused patent, trademark, and copyright workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides broad legal filing guidance that may be incomplete, jurisdiction-specific, or outdated.

Mitigation: Verify filing rules, fees, deadlines, and legal conclusions against official sources or a qualified IP professional before relying on them.

Risk: The workflows may involve sensitive identity documents, business licenses, signatures, sealed documents, or source code.

Mitigation: Do not paste sensitive materials into chat unless an approved secure handling process is in place.

Risk: The security review marked the release suspicious because privacy and scope controls are limited for legal and IP workflows.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation and constrain use to informational workflow support rather than definitive legal advice.
