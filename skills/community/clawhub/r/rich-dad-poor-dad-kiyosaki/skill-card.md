## Description:

Robert Kiyosaki's "Rich Dad Poor Dad" is an executable toolkit that reframes money mindset, assets, passive income, financial education, and entrepreneurship through the book's 6 Lessons framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to receive book-framed financial literacy guidance based on Rich Dad Poor Dad concepts such as assets versus liabilities, the Rat Race, passive income, and entrepreneurship. It should be treated as general educational commentary rather than personalized financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can present broad financial, debt, investment, tax, and business-entity guidance as actionable advice.

Mitigation: Require responses and deployment notes to frame outputs as general educational commentary and to direct users to qualified financial, legal, or tax professionals for personalized decisions.

Risk: Broad activation phrases may route ordinary personal-finance questions into this opinionated book framework.

Mitigation: Constrain activation and routing so the skill is used when the user asks for Rich Dad Poor Dad, Kiyosaki, or book-framed financial-literacy commentary.

Risk: The pay-yourself-first and minimum-payment guidance could be harmful if applied to debt, bills, taxes, or emergency obligations without context.

Mitigation: Add safeguards that discourage skipping required payments and encourage assessing obligations, emergency needs, interest rates, and professional advice before acting.

Risk: Every response is instructed to include a promotional Heardly watermark.

Mitigation: Review distribution requirements and remove or clearly disclose promotional content where platform policy or user expectations require neutral output.
