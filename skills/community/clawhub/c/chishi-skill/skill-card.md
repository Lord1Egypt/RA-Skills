## Description:

Provides Chinese writing in the 赤石TV style: pseudo-serious, internet-native, absurd, low-budget, and dryly funny cultural commentary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill to draft Chinese 赤石-style copy, micro essays, vibe summaries, and deadpan internet-culture observations while staying within boundaries against targeted harassment or fabricated allegations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The style guidance can encourage cold or mean judgments about real people.

Mitigation: Keep outputs focused on fictional examples, internet-culture patterns, or critique of content rather than personal attacks.

Risk: The skill could be used to fabricate allegations or harassment-style copy.

Mitigation: Do not generate doxxing, protected-class targeting, or unsupported claims about real people.
