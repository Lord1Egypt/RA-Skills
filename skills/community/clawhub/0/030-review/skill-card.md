## Description:

030复盘 provides a Chinese-language seven-stage A-share short-term trading review framework for daily trade review, pre-market scanning, auction and open validation, intraday observation, closing summaries, market-health scoring, and next-day scenario planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to structure daily A-share short-term market reviews, compare market scenarios, and prepare trading plans from a risk-first checklist. Outputs should be treated as educational trading-review guidance, not personalized investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes position sizing, buy signals, sell rules, stop-loss triggers, and liquidation rules that could be mistaken for personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Use the outputs as educational review guidance only; independently verify data and consider personal risk tolerance or a licensed professional before acting.

Risk: The framework depends on timely A-share market data and subjective signal interpretation, so stale or inaccurate inputs can produce misleading plans.

Mitigation: Cross-check market data, news, and calculated scores against reliable sources before making trading decisions.
