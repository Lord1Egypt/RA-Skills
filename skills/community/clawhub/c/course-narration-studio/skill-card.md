## Description:

Turn lesson materials into polished course narration audio. This course narration studio organizes lessons into speakable scripts, records the teacher-voice narration for each section, and delivers slide-ready audio for courses, training, and knowledge-product audio.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Course creators, trainers, and internal enablement teams use this skill to turn lecture manuscripts into ordered lesson narration with a consistent teacher voice, checking a pilot before expanding to the full course.
