## Description:

Use when a user wants a practical audit for a backpack, work bag, school bag, purse, messenger bag, or daily carry that feels heavy, awkward, or uncomfortable. Produces a bag weight audit, fit checklist, discomfort map, repacking plan, and weekly reset routine while routing severe pain, numbness, weakness, or injury signs to medical care without diagnosing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, including commuters, students, parents, travelers, and office workers, use this skill to audit an uncomfortable daily bag and create a practical repacking, fit, and reset plan. The skill supports organization and comfort observations only, while routing severe or persistent symptoms to qualified medical evaluation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat bag comfort guidance as medical advice for severe pain, numbness, weakness, injury signs, or persistent symptoms.

Mitigation: Screen for red flags first, avoid diagnosis or treatment language, and route severe or persistent symptoms to qualified medical evaluation.

Risk: Repacking advice could remove essential safety, medication, accessibility, weather, school, or work items.

Mitigation: Preserve must-carry constraints and prioritize removing rarely used or relocatable items before changing necessary items.
