## Description:

The narrative-craft layer applies the BEATS framework to help agents find real brand, founder, or customer stories, structure arcs, anchor specifics, tune emotion and meaning, and route the resulting story brief to format writers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing and social content teams use this skill to turn real brand, founder, customer, or audience material into story briefs with a clear arc, concrete specifics, emotional meaning, platform framing, and downstream routing to format-specific writers. It is especially useful when a flat update needs narrative structure without inventing testimonials or guaranteeing virality.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer stories, testimonials, founder struggles, or user-generated content can mislead audiences if invented, exaggerated, or used without consent.

Mitigation: Use only true source material, confirm consent for customer or testimonial details, and review outputs before downstream publishing.

Risk: Brand voice, profile, testimonial, UGC, and audience-research context may contain sensitive business or customer information.

Mitigation: Provide only the context needed for the narrative task and review generated story briefs before sending them to publishing or scheduling tools.

Risk: Platform framing and marketing-performance claims can become stale as social platforms and audience behavior change.

Mitigation: Reverify platform guidance and cited performance claims quarterly before treating them as current operating guidance.
