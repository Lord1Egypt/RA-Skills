## Description:

Generates Xiaohongshu-style 3:4 vertical image notes with high-resolution PNG pages and ready-to-post captions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kpdmiao](https://clawhub.ai/user/kpdmiao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to turn knowledge material into Xiaohongshu-style image-note posts with concise pages, visual themes, and publish-ready Chinese captions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local rendering creates PNG exports and a temporary themed HTML file, which can overwrite similarly named outputs in the export directory.

Mitigation: Review the target export path before rendering and inspect generated images for layout overflow or incorrect content before publishing.
