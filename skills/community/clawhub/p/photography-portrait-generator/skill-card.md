## Description:

融合经典人像摄影理论，为主流 AI 生图平台生成摄影级人像提示词、参考图分析和质量修复建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators and prompt-writing agents use this skill to turn simple portrait ideas or reference images into structured AI image-generation prompts with camera settings, lighting, film style, negative prompts, and quality checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encourages exact reference-image and facial-likeness replication without clear consent or rights safeguards.

Mitigation: Use only images the user owns or has permission to reuse, especially for real people.

Risk: Full-fusion and facial-identity templates can increase likeness, privacy, and rights risk when a reference image depicts a real person.

Mitigation: Edit or disable those templates so reference images are treated as style or pose inspiration unless the user explicitly confirms consent and rights.
