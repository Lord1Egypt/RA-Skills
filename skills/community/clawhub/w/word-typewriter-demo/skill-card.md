## Description:

Automates Microsoft Word or WPS Writer to type document content character by character, with paging, alignment, font, size, and formatting controls driven by a text content file.

This skill is for demonstration purposes and not for production usage.

## Use Case:

Presenters, developers, and operators use this skill to demonstrate AI-driven Word/WPS document generation from marked-up text files during live demos or scripted document-generation walkthroughs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Word automation can create and save local documents and may close or clear unsaved Word/WPS documents.

Mitigation: Review the script before use, close important unsaved Word/WPS documents, and prefer a workflow that always creates a fresh document without deleting or closing existing documents automatically.
