## Use Case:

External users use this skill to guide an agent toward empathetic, non-diagnostic emotional support, active listening, crisis-aware responses, and appropriate encouragement to seek professional or real-world help when needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake supportive conversation for professional mental health care.

Mitigation: Clearly frame the skill as supportive guidance, not therapy or diagnosis, and encourage professional help for persistent symptoms, trauma, severe depression, or other serious concerns.

Risk: A user in immediate danger may need urgent support beyond an agent conversation.

Mitigation: Treat self-harm, suicide, or threats to harm others as safety concerns and direct the user to local emergency services or crisis resources.
