## Description:

Generates Chinese-language construction bid materials, including bid letters, technical bids, commercial bids, quotation sheets, and qualification document outlines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Construction companies, bid specialists, project managers, and procurement teams use this skill to draft complete Chinese-language construction bid packages and supporting quotation or qualification content for review before submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated bid materials may include inaccurate, incomplete, or noncompliant procurement language.

Mitigation: Have all generated bid materials reviewed by qualified procurement or legal staff before submission.

Risk: The skill may request sensitive business, pricing, qualification, or project information while drafting bids.

Mitigation: Provide only the information needed for the bid and avoid unnecessary personal identifiers, signatures, account details, proprietary pricing strategy, or confidential documents.
