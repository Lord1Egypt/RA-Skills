## Description:

Strategy Thinking helps users clarify a desired result, identify the real problem, assess conditions and resources, choose a path, and define executable next steps for planning-oriented tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jhonnylau](https://clawhub.ai/user/jhonnylau)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this Chinese-language planning skill to turn an intended goal, adjustment, judgment, or stalled initiative into a structured planning conversation. It guides result definition, problem diagnosis, option selection, and immediate next actions while marking assumptions and facts that still need verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Planning advice may be treated as a final decision or as verified market research.

Mitigation: Use the skill as planning support, and independently review recommendations, assumptions, and market facts before acting.

Risk: The skill may need to reason with incomplete or uncertain project facts.

Mitigation: Keep unresolved inputs marked as [假设] or [需确认] and verify them before committing resources.
