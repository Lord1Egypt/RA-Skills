## Description:

Lightweight LinkedIn prospecting and outreach workflow for researching qualified leads, applying simple prioritization, drafting concise personalized messages, exporting clean CSV or Google Sheets-ready lead lists, and summarizing campaign activity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gaelbuenobarthe](https://clawhub.ai/user/gaelbuenobarthe)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and business development users use this skill to define ICP-based LinkedIn targeting, qualify visible or user-provided prospects, draft review-ready outreach, and prepare CSV or Google Sheets-ready lead lists and summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prospect exports may contain business contact data that should not be retained or shared unnecessarily.

Mitigation: Limit exports to necessary fields, store them securely, and delete them when no longer needed.

Risk: Externally sourced lead data can create spreadsheet import risk when opened in Google Sheets or Excel.

Mitigation: Sanitize CSV cells before spreadsheet import and review generated rows before sharing.

Risk: Weak evidence can lead to unsupported personalization or misleading outreach claims.

Mitigation: Mark weak evidence clearly, prefer role-based messages when personalization is limited, and review messages before use.
