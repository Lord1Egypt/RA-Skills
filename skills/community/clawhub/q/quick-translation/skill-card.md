## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers can use this skill to translate everyday messages, documents, learning materials, generated content, and business communications across languages. It is also useful when users need terminology notes, tone adjustments, or batch translation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Translation history behavior is not explained, so sensitive text may be retained in ways users cannot assess.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential, legal, medical, or business-sensitive text unless the retention behavior is reviewed and acceptable.

Risk: Important translations can be inaccurate, incomplete, or miss domain-specific nuance.

Mitigation: Use human review for important translations, especially legal, medical, business, or other professional content.
