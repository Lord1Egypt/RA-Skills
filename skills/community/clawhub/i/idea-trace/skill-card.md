## Description:

Don't just save ideas - trace how they mature. Use when a user wants to follow how an important idea was shaped by later conversations, work, projects, and lived experience; decide whether it deserves long-term tracking; classify what changed it; and explain how it became a more mature view.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to trace how an important idea, stance, method, or judgment matured through later conversations, work, projects, and lived experience. It helps distinguish trackable core propositions from passing thoughts and explains what changed the idea over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided ideas, projects, conversations, or life events may include sensitive personal context.

Mitigation: Share only the context needed for the trace and review outputs before storing or sharing them.

Risk: The skill may overstate weak relationships between later events and an earlier idea.

Mitigation: Keep weak, partial, or speculative links explicitly labeled with confidence.
