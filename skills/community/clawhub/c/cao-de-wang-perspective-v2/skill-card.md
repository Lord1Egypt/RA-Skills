## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language Cao Dewang-inspired business decision perspective focused on manufacturing strategy, cost discipline, quality, compliance boundaries, philanthropy, and resilience.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and agent users apply this skill when they want business, manufacturing, strategy, governance, or philanthropy questions framed through a Cao Dewang-inspired perspective. The skill is intended as perspective-taking guidance, not neutral legal, financial, compliance, or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases such as "专注主义", "敬天爱人", or "曹德旺模式" may activate the perspective unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Use explicit activation language when requesting this perspective and review outputs for unwanted persona framing.

Risk: The skill frames advice through a business persona and may be mistaken for neutral legal, financial, compliance, or investment guidance.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as perspective-taking guidance and require qualified human review for regulated or high-impact decisions.
