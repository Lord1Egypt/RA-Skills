## Description:

Navigate e-commerce privacy regulations including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, cookie consent, data collection policies, email marketing compliance, and customer data handling to protect your business from fines and build customer trust.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

E-commerce operators, founders, and compliance teams use this skill to audit privacy practices and plan implementation for cookie consent, privacy policies, data-subject requests, email marketing, breach response, and ongoing maintenance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides practical privacy-compliance guidance that may involve customer data flows, vendor details, revenue thresholds, and marketing practices, but it is not legal advice.

Mitigation: Share only details appropriate for the agent session and consult qualified counsel for binding privacy-law decisions.
