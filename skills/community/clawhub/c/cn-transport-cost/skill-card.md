## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance for transportation engineering cost consulting budgets, including service classification, rate calculations, budget ratios, and compliance checks for highway, railway, and waterway projects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to answer Chinese-language questions about transportation engineering cost consulting budgets, classify consulting service types, apply fee formulas, and surface compliance checkpoints. It is intended for guidance and review support, not as a substitute for qualified professional judgment or contract-specific verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-language guidance may be misunderstood by non-Chinese users in compliance or contract work.

Mitigation: Use qualified translation or bilingual review before relying on outputs for compliance, procurement, or contracts.

Risk: Budget estimates, coefficients, and service classifications may be misapplied to project-specific scopes, optional work, or add-on services.

Mitigation: Have a qualified cost consultant verify source data, service classification, coefficients, and contract terms before use.
