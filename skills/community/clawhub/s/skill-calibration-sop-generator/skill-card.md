## Description:

Assists Chinese-language calibration and metrology teams in drafting calibration SOP documents by recommending applicable standards, calibration parameters, environmental conditions, and structured document sections.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External calibration, quality, and metrology practitioners use this skill to create structured calibration SOP drafts for common instruments. It is intended as drafting support and requires professional review of standards, tolerances, applicability, and final procedure content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated standards, tolerances, calibration points, or applicability guidance may be incorrect, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific instrument.

Mitigation: Have a qualified metrology professional verify all cited standards, tolerances, calibration points, and environmental requirements before the SOP is used.

Risk: Special equipment or mandatory-verification instruments may require procedures outside the skill's stated scope.

Mitigation: Route those cases to the applicable internal procedure and domain expert instead of relying on generated guidance.
