This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External newsletter creators, content marketers, independent developers, and small-business marketing teams use this skill to select growth tactics, improve email content, generate testable subject lines, and interpret campaign performance.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current campaign data and platform analytics before using them for business decisions.
