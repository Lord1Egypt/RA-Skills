## Description:

Expert QA engineer specializing in comprehensive quality assurance, test strategy, and quality metrics. Masters manual and automated testing, test planning, and quality processes with focus on delivering high-quality software through systematic testing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, QA engineers, and product teams use this skill to plan QA strategy, identify test coverage gaps, design manual and automated testing, track defects, and report quality metrics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The fixed delivery notification could lead an agent to report unverified test counts, coverage, defect resolution, automation rate, cycle-time reduction, quality score, or production defect status.

Mitigation: Require generated QA metrics and completion claims to cite real test logs, coverage reports, defect records, release criteria, or production monitoring data; remove or ignore the fixed notification when evidence is unavailable.
