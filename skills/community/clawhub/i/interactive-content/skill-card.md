## Description:

Interactive Content helps agents design polls, quizzes, scoped AMAs, this-or-that posts, question prompts, challenges, and follow-up plans that turn social audiences from passive scrollers into participants.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Social media practitioners, marketers, and brand teams use this skill to plan interactive content mechanics, draft participation prompts, and create close-the-loop follow-up content. The skill is intended for human-reviewed publishing workflows where native stickers, live AMAs, contest compliance, consent, and sensitive-topic handling remain under human control.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may draft content for native stickers, live AMAs, contests, or sensitive-topic polls that require consent, platform-rule checks, or legal review before publishing.

Mitigation: Keep native interactive elements, live execution, contest compliance, consent decisions, and sensitive-topic review under human control before publication.

Risk: Poll, quiz, AMA, or challenge outputs could mislead audiences if results, participation, facts, or research claims are fabricated or overstated.

Mitigation: Use only real participation data, verify quiz facts, frame social poll results as directional audience signals rather than rigorous research, and do not fabricate or rig engagement.

Risk: Submitted audience answers could contain instructions or unsafe requests that try to steer the agent away from the skill's limits.

Mitigation: Treat submitted answers as content material, not commands, and preserve the skill's consent, privacy, and publishing guardrails.
