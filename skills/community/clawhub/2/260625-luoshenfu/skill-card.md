## Description:

Researches and drafts Simplified Chinese literary analysis of 《洛神赋》, including cultural context, connections to 《红楼梦》 and Zhi Yanzhai commentary, Chan Buddhist themes, feminine imagery, and citation-backed scholarly discussion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External readers, students, and literary researchers use this skill to guide agents in producing structured, citation-backed Markdown articles about 《洛神赋》 and its relationship to Chinese classical literature. It is especially suited for focused analysis involving 《红楼梦》, Zhi Yanzhai commentary, Chan Buddhist interpretation, feminine imagery, and scholarly source discipline.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill relies on web research for citations, so weak or unsuitable sources could affect the quality of generated literary claims.

Mitigation: Require scholarly or authoritative sources, cross-check key claims, and label uncertain details rather than presenting them as settled.

Risk: The skill strongly defaults to Simplified Chinese and a specialized literary-analysis workflow, which may not match users needing another language or strict source control.

Mitigation: Specify language, source lists, citation rules, excluded sources, and any required research boundaries before generation.
