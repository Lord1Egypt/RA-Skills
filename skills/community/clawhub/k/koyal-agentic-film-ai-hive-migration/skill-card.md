## Description:

Chinese-language skill that helps agents evaluate a Koyal-to-AI-HIVE MCP workflow-rebuild migration for agentic film and video generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wubin1836](https://clawhub.ai/user/wubin1836)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and creative operations teams use this skill to plan a neutral migration assessment from Koyal-style agentic film workflows to an AI-HIVE MCP-based workflow. It guides official capability checks, pilot sample design, role handoffs, cost and quality tracking, human approval, and rollback decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger terms may cause the skill to activate during general AI video platform comparisons.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to stay outside this skill unless the task is specifically about Koyal-style migration, AI-HIVE MCP planning, or the listed agentic filmmaking workflow.

Risk: The workflow may involve paid generation, asset upload, external publishing, or other actions that affect budget and rights.

Mitigation: Require manual approval for paid generation, uploads, publishing, or data writes, and use only owned or authorized reference assets.

Risk: Claims about platform superiority, pricing, availability, or full replacement can become misleading without current side-by-side tests.

Mitigation: Re-check official sources before release and require same-day, same-input, same-duration pilot testing before making comparative conclusions.
