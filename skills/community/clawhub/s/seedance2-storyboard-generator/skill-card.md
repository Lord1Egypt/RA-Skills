## Description:

Professional AI video script and storyboard generator for the Seedance 2.0 platform that converts stories and articles into four-act scripts, episode breakdowns, asset prompts, and formatted storyboard scripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creative professionals, developers, and external users can use this skill to turn novels, articles, outlines, or story concepts into production-oriented Seedance 2.0 video planning assets. It helps plan multi-episode AI video series with scripts, continuity notes, visual asset prompts, and time-axis storyboard prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes guidance that may help users avoid copyright-related refusals when adapting protected characters or stories.

Mitigation: Use only original, licensed, or clearly permitted source material; ignore or remove character-renaming guidance intended to bypass copyright checks.

Risk: Chinese-language templates and Seedance-specific output conventions may be misunderstood or applied inconsistently.

Mitigation: Review generated storyboard prompts and asset lists before use, especially for source-material rights, platform suitability, and continuity.
