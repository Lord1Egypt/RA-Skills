## Description:

创建和维护可逐篇续写的中文知识库、系列教程、学习手册与案例库。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qingkaiwu](https://clawhub.ai/user/qingkaiwu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and knowledge workers use this skill to turn ambiguous topics, existing materials, and author experience into structured Simplified Chinese knowledge bases, tutorial series, study guides, and case libraries. It supports staged requirement confirmation, research, outline approval, article drafting, review, and ongoing maintenance of project records and cross-document references.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read existing project materials and write or update drafts in connected document tools such as Feishu.

Mitigation: Confirm the target knowledge base, folder, or document before use, and review generated updates before accepting or publishing them.

Risk: Generated knowledge-base content can include unsupported claims, stale details, or links that do not support the surrounding text.

Mitigation: Review cited sources, current facts, article links, and project records before treating the output as authoritative.
