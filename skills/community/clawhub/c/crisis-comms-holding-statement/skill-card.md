## Description:

Helps PR, communications, and executive teams draft first-hour crisis holding statements for incidents such as breaches, outages, recalls, safety events, or viral events, including tiered channel statements, spokesperson Q&A, and a stakeholder notification timeline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

PR, communications, legal, and executive teams use this skill to produce a legally cautious crisis communications packet when facts are incomplete and a first response is needed quickly. It supports internal, customer, press, and social statements, plus spokesperson preparation, stakeholder notification timing, and red-flag review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft crisis statements could include incorrect or misleading claims if facts are unconfirmed.

Mitigation: Confirm all incident facts carefully; the skill flags unknowns as [UNCONFIRMED] and requires review before release.

Risk: External customer, press, social, or regulator-facing output could create legal or reputational exposure.

Mitigation: Route all external-facing drafts through legal and executive review before publication.

Risk: Sensitive personal, customer, or incident data could be exposed in drafts.

Mitigation: Avoid entering unnecessary personal data and remove any PII or victim identity from examples and final statements.
