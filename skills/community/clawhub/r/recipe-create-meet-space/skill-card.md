## Description:

Create a Google Meet meeting space and share the join link.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[googleworkspace-bot](https://clawhub.ai/user/googleworkspace-bot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and developers use this recipe to create an open Google Meet space and email its join link to meeting participants.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An open Google Meet join link may be sent to the wrong recipients or shared beyond the intended audience.

Mitigation: Confirm the Gmail recipient list and verify that OPEN meeting access matches organizational policy before sending the link.
