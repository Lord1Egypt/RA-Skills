## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to compare marketplace options, prepare listings and pricing, detect scams, evaluate resale opportunities, and understand platform, tax, and compliance considerations.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketplace tax, legal, fee, and platform-policy guidance can be outdated or jurisdiction-specific.

Mitigation: Verify current rules with official sources before acting and keep account actions, transactions, listings, and messages under explicit user control.
