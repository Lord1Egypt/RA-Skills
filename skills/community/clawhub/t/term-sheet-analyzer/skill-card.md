## Description:

Analyze startup financing term sheets, including SAFEs, convertible notes, and priced equity rounds, by decoding economic terms, flagging founder-unfriendly clauses, modeling dilution impact, calculating effective pre/post-money valuations, and comparing competing term sheets side by side.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samledger67-dotcom](https://clawhub.ai/user/samledger67-dotcom)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, CFOs, and investors use this skill to understand the economic and control implications of startup financing term sheets before signing. It produces plain-language risk summaries, red-flag checks, dilution scenarios, and side-by-side comparisons, while directing users to counsel for legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The analysis may be mistaken for legal or investment advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs as financial analysis support only and consult qualified counsel before signing, drafting, or redlining legal documents.

Risk: Term sheet reviews can involve confidential deal data.

Mitigation: Provide only the deal terms needed for the analysis and omit unnecessary confidential information.

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the skill during general deal discussions.

Mitigation: Invoke it specifically for startup financing term sheet analysis when SAFE, convertible note, or priced round review is intended.
