## Description:

Provides Chinese-language AI news briefings with current headlines, industry events, viewpoints, international updates, neutral summaries, and source links when the user asks for recent AI news or trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pppig1357](https://clawhub.ai/user/pppig1357)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to gather and organize recent AI news into a concise Chinese briefing with links to supporting sources. It is intended for user-triggered current-events requests such as AI news, hot topics, industry events, and international AI updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad routing may activate the skill for vague AI questions about recent developments.

Mitigation: Phrase non-news AI questions clearly when current-events search or a news briefing is not desired.

Risk: News summaries can be incomplete or stale if source search results are limited or conflicting.

Mitigation: Review cited source links for important decisions and ask for additional sources when coverage seems thin.
