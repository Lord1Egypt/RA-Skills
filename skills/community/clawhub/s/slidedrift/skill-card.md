## Description:

Turn notes, articles, links and ideas into LinkedIn posts in your brand with SlideDrift (carousels, image posts, captions), then export them or schedule them to LinkedIn. Use when the user wants a LinkedIn carousel or post made from their own material, wants to edit an existing SlideDrift carousel, or wants to plan, export or schedule LinkedIn content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can turn their own notes, articles, links, or ideas into on-brand LinkedIn posts and carousels, then edit, export, organize into campaigns, or schedule them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generating or exporting content can consume credits.

Mitigation: Show the quoted credit cost and wait for the user's approval before generating or exporting.

Risk: Scheduling or publishing can send content to an external LinkedIn account.

Mitigation: Obtain separate, explicit user approval before scheduling or publishing.

Risk: A share link can make a carousel accessible to others.

Mitigation: Explain that the link is public before creating it, and let the user review or disable the link.
