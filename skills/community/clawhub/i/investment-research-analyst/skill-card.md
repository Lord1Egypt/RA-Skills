## Description:

Multi-agent investment research framework that performs stock analysis across fundamentals, news, sentiment, technicals, bull and bear debate, risk assessment, fact checking, and dashboard reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gudai1972](https://clawhub.ai/user/gudai1972)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts, investors, and research-oriented agents use this skill to gather market data, compare bullish and bearish views, assess risks, verify key facts, and produce structured investment research reports. It can also generate an interactive dashboard for reviewing the analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated investment research may be incomplete, incorrect, or mistaken for investment advice.

Mitigation: Verify financial data independently and treat reports as research support rather than investment advice.

Risk: A deployed dashboard may expose analysis, company research, or portfolio context.

Mitigation: Review generated dashboard content and deployment settings before publishing or sharing it.
