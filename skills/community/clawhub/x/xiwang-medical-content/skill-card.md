## Description:

A Chinese-language skill for retrieving Xiwang Group graphene medical research materials and generating platform-specific health education or marketing copy for video, Xiaohongshu, and WeChat public account formats.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content and marketing teams use this skill to search a bundled Chinese-language Xiwang graphene medical research corpus and draft consumer-facing medical science copy in the requested platform style. Outputs should be reviewed carefully before publication because the source evidence flags health and medical marketing risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags disease treatment instructions and strong medical claims without sufficient safety guardrails for public use.

Mitigation: Require expert review before consumer-facing publication and add clear informational-only language, clinician-consultation guidance, contraindication and adverse-event warnings.

Risk: The skill may rely on unpublished or preliminary medical evidence in generated content.

Mitigation: Clearly label unpublished or preliminary evidence and avoid presenting generated claims as established medical advice.
