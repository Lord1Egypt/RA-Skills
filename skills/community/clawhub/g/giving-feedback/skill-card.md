## Description:

Giving Feedback turns vague concerns into specific, kind, actionable feedback using situation-behavior-impact, with a clear ask, opening line, and dialogue prompts calibrated for reinforcing or constructive feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and team leads use this skill to prepare ready-to-deliver feedback for peers, reports, or managers from a specific situation, observed behavior, impact, and relationship context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for general requests about giving feedback.

Mitigation: Review the generated feedback before sharing it, and provide specific situation, behavior, impact, type, and relationship context to keep the response grounded.

Risk: Feedback guidance can be inappropriate if the user's context is incomplete or emotionally charged.

Mitigation: Check that the final message stays behavior-focused, timely, private when constructive, and invites the other person's perspective.
