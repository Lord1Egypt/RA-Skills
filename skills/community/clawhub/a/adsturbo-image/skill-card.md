## Description:

Text-to-image generation and image editing, plus e-commerce image tools for background removal, product imagery, campaign posters, watermark removal, and upscaling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[adsturbo](https://clawhub.ai/user/adsturbo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can create or edit images and prepare e-commerce and marketing assets, including product image sets, posters, cutouts, retouched images, and upscaled images.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Images sent for processing may contain private or commercially sensitive information.

Mitigation: Before uploading, consider the image's privacy implications and use only images the user is authorized to share.

Risk: Image generation and processing consume account credits, and some high-resolution outputs require a subscription.

Mitigation: Check current pricing and plan requirements before submitting paid or high-resolution work.
