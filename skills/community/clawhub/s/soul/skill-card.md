## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive personal details while following social or dating platform guidance.

Mitigation: Keep privacy reminders visible and advise users not to disclose location, workplace, school, financial, or other sensitive personal information.

Risk: Soul platform features, matching behavior, or community rules may change after release.

Mitigation: Ask users to verify current Soul platform rules and feature behavior before relying on platform-specific advice.

Risk: Social interactions can involve scams, harassment, or manipulative behavior.

Mitigation: Encourage blocking, reporting, avoiding transfers or investment requests, and keeping advice oriented toward sincere, respectful interaction.
