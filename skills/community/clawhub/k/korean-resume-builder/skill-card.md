## Description:

Builds Korean-style resumes and cover letters for job seekers by collecting applicant details, target role, experience, skills, education, awards or certificates, and personal statement material.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jochoonhwan](https://clawhub.ai/user/jochoonhwan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees can use this skill to draft Korean job application materials, including a resume and a structured self-introduction or cover letter tailored to a target role.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for resume-relevant personal details such as contact information, education, and work history.

Mitigation: Provide only the details needed for drafting, and use placeholders for phone, email, school, or employer details until the final version is ready.
