## Description:

OpenClaw 成长追踪器记录用户在 OpenClaw 中的对话、任务、能力、效率和成就，并生成数据化的个人成长报告。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[alexduming](https://clawhub.ai/user/alexduming)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External OpenClaw users use this skill to initialize local growth tracking, review daily or weekly progress, and record achievements across tasks, capability areas, and efficiency metrics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may retain a local history of personal OpenClaw activity, task metrics, achievements, and progress reports.

Mitigation: Review auto_track and retention_days before use, avoid continuous tracking for sensitive sessions, and adjust retention or disable tracking when local history is not desired.
