## Description:

Plan, run, and debrief structured pre-release bug bash sessions, including scope, participant mix, exploratory test charters, severity triage, de-duplication, fix prioritization, and retro artifacts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Release managers, QA leads, engineering teams, product managers, designers, customer-facing teams, and external developers use this skill to organize a time-boxed exploratory bug bash before a SaaS release. It helps produce practical session artifacts such as a scope document, charter board, intake form, severity rubric, run-of-show, prioritized bug list, waiver document, and retro notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bug bash artifacts can collect sensitive operational details, customer data, HAR files, session recordings, or credentials if teams paste raw debugging material into shared trackers.

Mitigation: Review generated intake forms, bug exports, and attachments before sharing; avoid production credentials, customer PII, and sensitive HAR or session data in broadly visible channels.

Risk: Facilitation guidance can influence release decisions if triage rubrics or waiver templates are applied without project-specific review.

Mitigation: Have the release owner, engineering manager, or QA lead confirm severity assignments, waivers, and ship or defer decisions before relying on the outputs.
