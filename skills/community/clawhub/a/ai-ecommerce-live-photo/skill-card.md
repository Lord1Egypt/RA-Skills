## Use Case:

External ecommerce merchants, content teams, agency operators, and advertising teams use this skill to plan and validate short dynamic product-photo assets for social and commerce channels. It guides users through authorized source-material checks, pilot generation, frame-by-frame review, platform specifications, cost tracking, and stop conditions before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated product assets may be based on images without clear ownership or usage rights.

Mitigation: Require owned or authorized reference assets before generation and do not publish until rights are confirmed.

Risk: Generated Live Photo samples may alter product structure, logos, labels, colors, materials, or accessories.

Mitigation: Use the skill's frame-by-frame review checklist and keep human approval enabled before channel expansion or publication.

Risk: Model availability, pricing, task state, or platform requirements may change between runs.

Mitigation: Verify current AI-HIVE model availability, pricing, task status, and platform specifications for each batch and record cost snapshots.
