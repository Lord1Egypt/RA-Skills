## Description:

NEXUS Research provides deep research on any topic with structured findings and recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cyberforexblockchain](https://clawhub.ai/user/cyberforexblockchain)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agent users use this skill to send research questions to NEXUS and receive structured findings, sources, and recommendations. It is suited to paid remote research workflows where the user approves sharing submitted text with the provider.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research text and the payment proof are sent to the NEXUS remote service.

Mitigation: Use only when the provider is trusted and avoid submitting secrets, regulated data, or proprietary material unless organizational policy allows it.

Risk: The skill supports paid remote requests.

Mitigation: Require approval or budget controls before paid calls.
