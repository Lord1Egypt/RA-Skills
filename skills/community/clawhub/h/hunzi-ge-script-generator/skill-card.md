## Description:

混子哥风格知识脚本生成器，把枯燥、复杂的知识转化为生动、有趣、视觉化的知识脱口秀脚本，让观众在哈哈大笑中搞懂硬核知识。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, educators, and content teams use this skill to turn history, institutions, anecdotes, and other knowledge topics into humorous Chinese explainer scripts with storyboard guidance. It is best suited to topics with a clear information gap, causal chain, and everyday analogy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill strongly steers responses into a Chinese, humorous seven-part script style, which may be unsuitable for neutral, multilingual, or formal deliverables.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to adapt the language, tone, or structure when neutral, multilingual, or formal output is required.

Risk: The skill uses punchy analogies and humor to simplify knowledge topics, which can flatten nuance in complex historical or institutional material.

Mitigation: Review factual claims and analogies before publication, especially for sensitive or contested topics.
