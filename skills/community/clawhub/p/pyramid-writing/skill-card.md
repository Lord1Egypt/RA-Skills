## Description:

金字塔原理（Pyramid Principle）写作与结构化表达工作流，用于把散乱内容改写成先结论、后论据的可汇报文稿，并输出关键结论、分组论点、证据、行动项和目标达成评估。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking employees, developers, and external communicators use this skill to turn raw notes into conclusion-first reports, reviews, weekly updates, emails, PRD sections, presentations, and technical communication. It helps structure messages around audience goals, evidence, risks, trade-offs, action items, and final goal evaluation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make draft recommendations look more decisive than the user's evidence supports.

Mitigation: Keep assumptions, missing evidence, and items needing confirmation visible, then review decision-critical or external-facing text before use.

Risk: The workflow favors Chinese headings, Pyramid Principle structure, and predefined templates that may be too formal for casual or non-Chinese writing.

Mitigation: Specify the target language, audience, tone, and desired format, and select compact, standard, or decision templates according to the communication need.
