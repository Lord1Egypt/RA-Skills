## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill covers personal finances and investment-related allocation guidance.

Mitigation: Verify account data directly in Alipay and do not treat generated allocation suggestions as professional financial advice.

Risk: Large transfers can create account security exposure if acted on without additional checks.

Mitigation: Use double identity verification before high-value transfers.
