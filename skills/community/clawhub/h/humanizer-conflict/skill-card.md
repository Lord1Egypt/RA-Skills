## Description:

Remove signs of AI-generated writing from text and revise drafts to sound more natural and human-written.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beelkic](https://clawhub.ai/user/beelkic)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and writing editors use this skill to review drafts for common AI-writing patterns and produce a more natural rewrite while preserving the intended meaning and tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make AI-written text appear human, which may conflict with academic, publication, journalistic, employment, legal, or compliance disclosure rules.

Mitigation: Use it only where revising the text is authorized and required AI-use disclosures have already been considered.

Risk: The skill encourages adding first-person voice, opinions, or personality, which could introduce claims the author does not actually hold.

Mitigation: Keep revisions faithful to the author's intent and remove any added opinion, feeling, or personal claim that is not explicitly approved by the author.
