## Description:

Generate emotionally driven, platform-native 16-second AI-avatar scripts, image prompts, and video-production guidance for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and Facebook.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to operate a short-form AI-avatar content workflow for social platforms. It helps produce structured scripts, avatar image prompts, reference-grid guidance, voice and lip-sync planning, and platform-specific creative variants.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide paid media-generation actions that may consume credits.

Mitigation: Confirm cost before committing to image, voice, or video generation.

Risk: Avatar workflows may involve uploading reference media, audio, or cloning a real person's voice.

Mitigation: Confirm rights, consent, and privacy requirements before uploading media or creating voice clones.

Risk: AI-avatar content in finance, health, privacy, or security categories can carry higher trust and brand risk.

Mitigation: Review sensitive-category outputs before publication and use lighter categories for initial validation when possible.

Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause an agent to use this specialized pipeline for loosely related social-video requests.

Mitigation: Override the skill when the task only needs general social-content advice.
