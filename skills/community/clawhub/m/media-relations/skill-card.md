## Description:

Provides practical media relations guidance and SOPs for planning, executing, measuring, and standardizing media relations work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[clawkk](https://clawhub.ai/user/clawkk)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Communications, marketing, and operations teams use this skill to create media relations SOPs, define goals and metrics, set up reporting, run small pilots, and standardize reusable public-relations workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media relations plans may mishandle personal data, business-confidential data, local legal requirements, or platform rules.

Mitigation: Review generated plans against applicable laws, platform policies, privacy obligations, and internal approval processes before use.

Risk: Guidance-only SOPs can be applied with incorrect goals, metrics, dashboards, or rollout assumptions.

Mitigation: Assign a responsible owner to validate goals, metric definitions, reporting cadence, pilot results, and standardization decisions.
