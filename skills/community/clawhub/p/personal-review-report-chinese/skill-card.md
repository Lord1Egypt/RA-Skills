## Description:

Generates structured Chinese personal-development review reports from user-provided text or accessible local files.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[westwood-lin](https://clawhub.ai/user/westwood-lin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Individuals and agents use this skill to turn journals, notes, or accessible local records into concise Chinese review reports covering career, relationships, health, finances, habits, strengths, gaps, and suggested improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive journals, notes, or personal records supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Use it only with the specific personal files intended for review, and avoid credential files, browser profiles, SSH keys, system configuration, or unrelated private documents.

Risk: Personal-review conclusions can be incomplete or misleading when source records are sparse, one-sided, or ambiguous.

Mitigation: Review the generated report against the original inputs before acting on recommendations or sharing the report.
