## Description:

Compares BOM versions, splits mechanical and electrical materials, merges multi-supplier BOMs, and helps generate BOM change reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[paudyyin](https://clawhub.ai/user/paudyyin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Manufacturing, procurement, and engineering users can use this agent skill to process BOM Excel files for version comparison, mechanical/electrical split, supplier consolidation, and change-report drafting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: BOM outputs may affect procurement or engineering decisions if used without review.

Mitigation: Review generated comparisons, splits, merged totals, and change reports before relying on them.

Risk: BOM files may contain sensitive supplier, cost, or engineering information.

Mitigation: Provide only BOM files you are comfortable with the agent reading for the requested task.
