## Description:

Guides agents through Dow Theory trend classification, confirmation and non-confirmation, volume validation, and decisive reversal checks for market trend analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and market-analysis agents use this skill to classify market trends by scale and stage, validate signals across independent sources, and decide whether reversal evidence is strong enough to treat a trend as ended. It is an educational analysis framework, not an automated trading or financial-decision tool.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the Dow Theory framework for automated trading or financial-decision advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as educational market-analysis guidance and avoid issuing trade instructions or portfolio decisions.

Risk: Non-Chinese users may miss key scope and boundary details because the artifact is primarily Chinese with bilingual summary text.

Mitigation: Ask for or apply a clear response language preference before relying on the skill in multilingual settings.

Risk: The framework can be over-applied to support/resistance drawing or chart-pattern recognition tasks outside its stated scope.

Mitigation: Route support and trend-line drawing to trend-tools and reversal-pattern recognition to reversal-patterns when those are the user's actual task.
