## Description:

Helps developers and QA teams plan, add, and validate useful unit tests and coverage improvements for existing codebases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers, QA engineers, open-source contributors, and product teams use this skill to plan and produce tailored unit-test additions, coverage workflows, checklists, and validation notes for existing codebases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for general software-quality discussions where unit-test help was not intended.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for adding unit tests, improving coverage, or creating regression-test workflows, and confirm the testing goal before applying outputs.

Risk: Proposed test plans, code, or shell commands may not fully match project-specific behavior or tooling.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the repository's requirements and run the relevant test and coverage commands before merging changes.
