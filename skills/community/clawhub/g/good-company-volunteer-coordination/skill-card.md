## Description:

Plan opt-in volunteer task matches, invitations, shifts and follow-through using eligibility, availability and workload evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jjjhenriksen](https://clawhub.ai/user/jjjhenriksen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Volunteer organizers use this skill to plan opt-in task matches and shifts, draft invitations, and surface staffing gaps using authorized eligibility, availability, and workload records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Volunteer availability, qualifications, and preferences may be exposed in planning outputs.

Mitigation: Use only authorized records, keep individual proposals private, and build shared views from an allowlist that excludes comparative fit and decline reasons.

Risk: A draft invitation or waitlist may be mistaken for confirmed consent, signup, or attendance.

Mitigation: Label proposals, offers, acceptances, withdrawals, and completion separately; update status only from authorized, verified evidence.
