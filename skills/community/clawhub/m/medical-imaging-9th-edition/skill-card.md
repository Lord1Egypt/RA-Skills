External clinicians, radiology teams, medical students, and medical educators use this skill as an educational and clinician-reviewed reference for medical imaging interpretation, modality selection, report review, and interventional radiology decision support.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides high-stakes medical diagnosis and invasive-procedure guidance that could be mistaken for independent clinical advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs only for education or tightly supervised clinician workflows, with qualified clinician review before any real-case use.

Risk: Recommendations may not reflect current official guidelines, local protocols, patient-specific constraints, or complete clinical context.

Mitigation: Verify recommendations against current guidelines, local protocols, radiology specialists, and the complete patient record.

Risk: Patient images or metadata may introduce privacy and compliance obligations.

Mitigation: Apply privacy controls before using real patient data and keep any patient-specific workflow within approved clinical systems.
