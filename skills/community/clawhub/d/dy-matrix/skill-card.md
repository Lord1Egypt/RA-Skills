## Description:

Read-only Douyin data skill for keyword search, creator posts, post details, comments, and hot lists, returning structured JSON for analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[guaikei](https://clawhub.ai/user/guaikei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Social media operators, analysts, and marketing teams use this skill to retrieve public Douyin search, creator, post, comment, and hot-list data as structured JSON. They can compare accounts and content and summarize engagement for internal analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Douyin links or identifiers are sent to a third-party API.

Mitigation: Before use, decide whether sharing that data fits your privacy needs.
