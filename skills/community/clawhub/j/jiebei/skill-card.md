## Description:

Jiebei helps agents explain Ant Cash Now borrowing costs, credit-report impacts, and responsible borrowing decisions, with a local calculator for daily-rate and installment APR comparisons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer Jiebei borrowing-cost, credit-report, and debt-risk questions in plain language. Developers can also run the included zero-dependency calculator to convert daily rates or installment fees into annualized cost comparisons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake educational borrowing-cost guidance for professional financial advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as educational support, require users to verify current rates in the actual loan app, and direct high-risk debt situations to qualified financial or legal help.

Risk: Calculator runs with invalid or unrealistic numeric inputs may crash or produce unusable results.

Mitigation: Use realistic positive inputs and review calculator output before relying on it for borrowing comparisons.

Risk: Borrowing guidance could be misused to seek credit-limit increases, risk bypasses, cash-out methods, or debt cycling.

Mitigation: Keep responses within the skill's stated boundaries: cost comparison, credit-report facts, and responsible borrowing; do not provide evasion, cash-out, or risk-bypass methods.
