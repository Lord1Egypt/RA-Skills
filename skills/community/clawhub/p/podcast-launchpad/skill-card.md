## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Podcast platform submission requirements can change, making directory guidance outdated.

Mitigation: Verify current platform submission requirements before claiming a show is listed, approved, ranked, or monetized.

Risk: Launch, growth, or monetization guidance may be mistaken for guaranteed audience or revenue outcomes.

Mitigation: Present recommendations as educational guidance and avoid promises of growth, rankings, sponsorships, or monetization.

Risk: Podcast planning can touch legal, financial, copyright, consent, or defamation questions.

Mitigation: Use the skill for general planning only and route legal, tax, contract, copyright, and business-structure decisions to qualified professionals.
