## Description:

Evaluate a patent package for licensing, assignment, or collaboration opportunities; identify evidence-backed candidate counterparties; rank them with a transparent and sensitivity-tested rubric; propose diligence and outreach hypotheses; and generate a self-contained HTML transfer-intelligence brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and internal IP, business development, and technology-transfer teams use this skill to screen patent packages for licensing, assignment, collaboration, option, pool, and related commercialization hypotheses. It supports evidence-backed candidate-counterparty research, diligence planning, and a self-contained HTML transfer-intelligence brief for authorized use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may handle confidential patent or transaction information.

Mitigation: Use it only in an approved environment with clear confidentiality boundaries and permission to process the relevant materials.

Risk: Users could overread generated rankings, transaction options, or outreach hypotheses as legal advice, valuation, approval, or authorization to contact a company.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research support and require legal, commercial, ownership, and internal approval checks before decisions or outreach.

Risk: Patent status, ownership, rights, and candidate signals may be incomplete, ambiguous, or stale.

Mitigation: Preserve evidence states, cite dated sources, mark unresolved items as requiring diligence, and verify material facts through authorized PatSnap connectors or credible current sources.
