## Description:

Helps SDS authors, EHS managers, industrial hygienists, product stewards, and regulatory-affairs specialists draft a 16-section Safety Data Sheet under OSHA HCS 2024 for a substance or mixture, with a draft SDS, GHS label preview, authoring-gap list, and trade-secret worksheet.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External SDS authors, industrial hygienists, EHS managers, product stewards, and regulatory-affairs teams use this skill to turn product identification, composition, and hazard data into a draft SDS authoring packet for qualified human review before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, for U.S. OSHA HCS 2024 SDS workflows unless the user names another jurisdiction for review.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft SDS content could be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for publication if source data or regulatory interpretation is wrong.

Mitigation: Require qualified SDS, EHS, or regulatory professional review and sign-off before publication or distribution.

Risk: Users may provide confidential formulations or trade-secret concentration data while drafting.

Mitigation: Use only data the user is authorized to process and keep CBI decisions under supplier or authorized-officer control.

Risk: Missing hazard, exposure, toxicology, ecological, or regulatory data may be mistaken for evidence that no hazard exists.

Mitigation: Preserve explicit authoring gaps and distinguish measured negatives from 'No data available' or 'Not applicable' entries.
