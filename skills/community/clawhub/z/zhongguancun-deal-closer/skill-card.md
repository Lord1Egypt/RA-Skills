## Description:

Zhongguancun Deal Closer helps Chinese-language users turn meeting notes and cooperation records into deal-stage judgments, next actions, follow-up drafts, and risk reminders for industry-academia collaboration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhangxiaopeng-zgci](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhangxiaopeng-zgci)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business development, university partnership, and industry-academia collaboration teams use this skill to analyze meeting notes, identify real needs and decision roles, judge cooperation stage and deal probability, choose the next move, and draft Chinese follow-up messages or emails.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated follow-up language can influence external business communication.

Mitigation: Review and edit generated messages before sending, especially where commitments, budgets, timelines, or stakeholder positions are mentioned.
