## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Listings, prices, and suggested agents are author-curated guidance rather than verified marketplace data.

Mitigation: Validate prices, availability, reputation, and payment terms before relying on the guide for transactions.

Risk: The skill includes promotion templates and self-referential service recommendations.

Mitigation: Review recommendations for conflicts of interest and adjust templates to match the user's actual capabilities and obligations.
