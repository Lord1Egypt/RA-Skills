## Description:

Helps teams improve how often generative AI assistants mention and accurately describe their brands using verified facts, independent corroboration, and repeated prompt testing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing and product teams use this skill to measure how AI assistants describe a brand and plan evidence-based improvements to its visibility and accuracy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer-derived prompts may contain personal, identifying, or confidential information sent to external AI providers.

Mitigation: Review and sanitize prompts before use, remove sensitive details, and use approved provider accounts.
