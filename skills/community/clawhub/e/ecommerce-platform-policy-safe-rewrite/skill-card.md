## Use Case:

Growth, ecommerce, and marketing teams use this skill to rewrite risky ads, creator scripts, landing page lines, and product claims into safer, more review-friendly copy while preserving the commercial intent.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce marketing copy that appears safer but is still unsuitable for a specific platform, market, product category, or evidence level.

Mitigation: Review generated copy against current platform policies, product substantiation, and applicable legal or regulatory requirements before publication.

Risk: Users may mistake rewrite guidance for formal legal, regulatory, or platform-enforcement certainty.

Mitigation: Use the output as drafting support only and route high-risk claims or regulated categories to qualified review.
