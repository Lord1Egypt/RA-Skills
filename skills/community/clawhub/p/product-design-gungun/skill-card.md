## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product design recommendations may be too generic or unsuitable for a specific product context if adopted without validation.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations with user research, product metrics, technical feasibility review, and stakeholder approval before implementation.

Risk: The skill is written primarily in Chinese, which can lead to misunderstanding for non-Chinese users.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed translation or bilingual adaptation when operating in multilingual teams.
