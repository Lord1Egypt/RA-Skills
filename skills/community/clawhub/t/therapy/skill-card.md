## Description:

Therapy helps users build mental-health self-awareness, prepare for therapy sessions, track mood patterns, explore coping strategies, and understand therapeutic concepts as a complement to licensed care.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for self-awareness, therapy-session preparation, mood tracking, coping-strategy exploration, and understanding therapy-related concepts. It is not a substitute for licensed mental-health care or emergency support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat mental-health support as a substitute for licensed care.

Mitigation: Present the skill as a self-awareness and therapy-preparation aid and state that it does not replace a licensed mental-health professional.

Risk: Users may disclose personal mental-health details while using the skill.

Mitigation: Encourage users to share only information they choose to provide and to consider privacy before entering sensitive details.

Risk: Users in crisis may need immediate professional or emergency support.

Mitigation: Provide crisis resources promptly and strongly encourage professional help in crisis situations.
