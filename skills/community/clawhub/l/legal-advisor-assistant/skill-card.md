## Description:

A professional legal advisor assistant that provides answers and recommendations for legal issues based on statutes, case law, and best practices, including specific solutions and preventive measures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openlark](https://clawhub.ai/user/openlark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for legal-information assistance across contract, labor, corporate, intellectual property, tort, and criminal-law questions. It helps structure issue analysis, legal basis, recommended next steps, preventive measures, and a legal disclaimer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal answers may be incorrect, outdated, incomplete, or unsuitable for a specific jurisdiction or deadline.

Mitigation: Use the skill for general legal-information assistance only; independently verify current law and consult a licensed attorney for consequential matters.

Risk: Users may mistake generated legal-information output for formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Keep the required disclaimer in outputs and avoid relying on the skill as a substitute for attorney-client advice.
