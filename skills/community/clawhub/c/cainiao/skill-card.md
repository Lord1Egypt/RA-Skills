## Description:

Use Cainiao Network (菜鸟物流) for shipment tracking, shipping guidance, service-type comparison, outlet lookup, and delivery-time or fee estimation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to interpret Cainiao shipment status, retrieve pickup guidance, compare service types, estimate shipping time or fees, and understand local privacy controls for package-related data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cainiao package history, pickup codes, addresses, and local query or subscription records can be sensitive.

Mitigation: Use the documented privacy controls to inspect, export, or clear local Cainiao data, and avoid sharing pickup codes or account details unless needed for the request.
