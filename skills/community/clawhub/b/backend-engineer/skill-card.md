## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Production troubleshooting guidance could expose secrets or sensitive environment data.

Mitigation: Avoid dumping full environment variables and redact secrets from terminal output, logs, incident notes, and chat history.

Risk: Kubernetes or cloud operational guidance could affect production infrastructure.

Mitigation: Prefer read-only diagnostics first and require explicit confirmation before execing into pods or making infrastructure changes.
