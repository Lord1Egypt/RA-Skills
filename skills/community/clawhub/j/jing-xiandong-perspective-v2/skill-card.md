Risk: The skill may present named-executive-style first-person examples that could be mistaken for factual personal testimony.

Mitigation: Treat first-person phrasing as roleplay and verify any factual claims or business advice against authoritative sources before relying on it.

Risk: Broad business, compliance, risk, IPO, or internationalization prompts may activate the perspective unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Use explicit prompting to confirm when this perspective should apply, and review outputs for suitability before using them in business decisions.
