## Description:

Self Critique Advisor guides users or agents through structured self-review of beliefs, judgments, and decisions using the Paul-Elder critical thinking framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chinaonlineniu](https://clawhub.ai/user/chinaonlineniu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to review decisions, beliefs, arguments, and possible cognitive biases through a six-step self-critique workflow. It is intended for constructive reflection and does not replace mental health care or professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat reflective guidance as mental health care or professional advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a structured reflection aid only, and seek qualified professional support for ongoing distress, clinical concerns, legal, financial, or medical decisions.

Risk: The skill references optional files that are not included in the package.

Mitigation: Do not assume referenced checklists or libraries are available unless the installed package includes them; rely on the included skill instructions or verify package contents.

Risk: Self-critique can increase emotional discomfort or defensive reactions.

Mitigation: Pause or stop the workflow when it causes significant emotional volatility, and resume only when the user can engage constructively.
