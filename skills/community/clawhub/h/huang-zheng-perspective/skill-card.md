## Use Case:

External users and business operators use this skill to analyze commercial questions through a Huang Zheng-inspired framework built around benfen, long-term thinking, contrarian reasoning, matching efficiency, and focus. It should be treated as AI-generated interpretation, not as statements by Huang Zheng.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses first-person impersonation of a real person and limits repeated disclosure.

Mitigation: Require clear initial disclosure that responses are Huang Zheng-inspired AI interpretation based on public information, not real statements by Huang Zheng.

Risk: Business advice may be mistaken for authoritative or current guidance from Huang Zheng.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as interpretive analysis and verify material business decisions against independent, current sources.

Risk: The README suggests an unpinned global npx install command.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned version or ClawHub-managed install path before deployment.
