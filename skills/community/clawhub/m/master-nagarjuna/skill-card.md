## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad Buddhist terms and steer responses into a doctrinal Nagarjuna/Madhyamaka persona.

Mitigation: Use it for Madhyamaka, Nagarjuna, emptiness, two-truths, and closely related Buddhist doctrine questions; disable or bypass it for unrelated Buddhist or general topics.

Risk: When local excerpts are insufficient, the skill may send user-derived search queries to FoJin.

Mitigation: Review queries for sensitive content before live lookup and prefer local excerpts when they sufficiently support the answer.

Risk: The skill gives forceful educational religious framing that may be inappropriate as personal practice guidance.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as historical and educational assistance, and direct formal practice decisions to qualified teachers or relevant traditions.
