## Description:

Designs a sustainable social engagement cadence with time-boxed windows, triage priorities, goal-linked signals, and clear boundaries against inbox automation or fake engagement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media operators, founders, and marketing teams use this skill to turn engagement into a repeatable daily and weekly routine. It helps plan when to reply, what to prioritize, how to measure progress with native signals, and how to keep human review and posting in control.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expect the skill to access inboxes, analytics, auto-replies, or mass commenting tools.

Mitigation: State that the skill is advisory: WoopSocial has no inbox or analytics surface, and final review and posting remain under human control.

Risk: Overuse could encourage always-on engagement habits or burnout.

Mitigation: Use time-boxed engagement windows, notification discipline, response windows, and capacity-based scaling.

Risk: Engagement guidance could be misused for fake or automated interactions.

Mitigation: Refuse automation, bots, mass auto-comments, bought interactions, and fabricated performance claims; support only genuine draft-for-review workflows.
