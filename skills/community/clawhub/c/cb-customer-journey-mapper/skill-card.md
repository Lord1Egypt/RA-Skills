## Description:

Map complete customer journeys for any overseas market \u2014 with localized touchpoints, cultural buying triggers, and conversion barriers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, marketing, customer experience, and growth teams use this skill to map international customer journeys, diagnose trust and friction points, align local proof and channel strategy, and define journey-stage measurements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Journey maps may be mistaken for validated market research even though they are planning hypotheses.

Mitigation: Validate cultural assumptions, benchmark numbers, and conversion recommendations with customer research, funnel analytics, and experiments before business decisions.

Risk: Generalized cultural or channel assumptions can produce misleading localization recommendations.

Mitigation: Require market-specific inputs, identify evidence gaps, and mark assumptions or confidence levels for each journey stage.
