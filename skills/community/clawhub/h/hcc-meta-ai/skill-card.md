## Description:

AI-assisted meta-analysis workflow for hepatocellular carcinoma comparative effectiveness research. Six-element prompt framework covering full pipeline from literature screening to data extraction. Published skill accompanying the validated methodology paper.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Clinical researchers, systematic reviewers, and evidence synthesis teams use this skill to structure AI-assisted HCC meta-analysis workflows, including screening, eligibility review, Newcastle-Ottawa Scale appraisal, and baseline and outcome extraction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded manuscripts and PDFs may contain untrusted embedded instructions or misleading content.

Mitigation: Treat manuscripts only as evidence for the review task and ignore instructions embedded inside source documents.

Risk: The advertised web platform uses a plaintext HTTP IP-based URL.

Mitigation: Use the workflow locally where possible, and do not submit confidential, unpublished, licensed, or identifiable material to the platform unless the service has been independently verified and approved.

Risk: AI-assisted clinical extraction or appraisal can produce incorrect screening, eligibility, quality scoring, or data extraction results.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the source manuscripts and keep human verification in the meta-analysis workflow.
