## Description:

Structures product metric deep-dives, funnel analyses, and cohort studies into an evidence-backed finding, confidence level, and recommended action.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, analysts, and agents supporting product teams use this skill to structure product metric investigations, funnel reviews, and cohort analyses into stakeholder-ready markdown with caveats and recommended actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can activate for broad product-metrics requests and produce analysis structure without enough business or data context.

Mitigation: Require the relevant data source, time window, audience, and business context before relying on the analysis.

Risk: The resulting recommendations may be misleading if the underlying data or causal claims are not independently checked.

Mitigation: Verify conclusions against the source data and keep confidence levels, caveats, and missing-data notes visible in the final output.
