## Description:

Produce 2-4 genuinely distinct video script variants from a single brief, using scene-by-scene writing, audience-specific registers, word count targets, and a handoff checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nissan](https://clawhub.ai/user/nissan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, marketers, and video-production teams use this skill to turn one product brief into distinct A/B/C video script variants for technical, general, and developer audiences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated scripts may carry project names, brief details, or audience assumptions into production materials.

Mitigation: Review generated scripts before sharing or production, and remove sensitive project details that should not appear in handoff files.

Risk: The skill can shape script structure and messaging, which may result in misleading or off-brand narration if the brief is incomplete.

Mitigation: Validate each variant against the source brief, audience goal, and final differentiation checklist before approval.
