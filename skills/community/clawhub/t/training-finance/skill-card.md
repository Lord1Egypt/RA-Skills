## Use Case:

Training operators and finance reviewers use this skill to manage course enrollment records, confirm payments, track costs, calculate instructor or partner revenue shares, and draft period or annual profit reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student contact details and payment information may be entered into prompts or reports.

Mitigation: Use only authorized records, minimize unnecessary personal or payment fields, and verify where outputs are stored or shared.

Risk: Generated settlement and profit reports may contain incorrect or incomplete financial calculations.

Mitigation: Treat all generated finance reports as drafts and require human financial review before executing payments or finalizing accounts.
