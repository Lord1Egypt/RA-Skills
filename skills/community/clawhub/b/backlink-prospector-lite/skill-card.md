## Description:

Free version: find guest post opportunities and draft one outreach email; covers opportunity discovery and basic pitching.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and marketing teams use this skill to identify up to five guest post targets for a website and niche, then draft one personalized outreach email for a selected publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The agent may return outdated, low-quality, or pay-to-post publication targets from web searches.

Mitigation: Review each target's current editorial standards, publication quality, and fee policy before using it in an outreach campaign.

Risk: Generated outreach copy may include inaccurate personalization or overstate the sender's credentials.

Mitigation: Verify publication details, article references, and sender credentials before sending any email.

Risk: Users may share private campaign details or credentials while preparing outreach.

Mitigation: Provide only the website, niche, and public writing samples needed for the task; avoid credentials and sensitive campaign data.
