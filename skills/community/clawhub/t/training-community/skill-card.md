## Description:

Training Community helps operators convert one-time training participants into an ongoing learning community with cohort setup, follow-up activity planning, referral programs, resource pushes, and repurchase tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Training program operators and community managers use this skill to turn post-training rosters and records into community profiles, operation calendars, referral tracking, and conversion funnel summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process personal student or customer records, including rosters, participation history, referrals, and spending details.

Mitigation: Use only authorized data, limit fields to what is necessary, avoid exposing rosters or spending details in shared chats, and define consent, retention, deletion, and access-control practices.

Risk: Community invitations, referral rewards, and repurchase analysis may affect customer communications and incentives.

Mitigation: Have the program owner review generated plans and metrics before acting on invitations, rewards, or follow-up sales workflows.
