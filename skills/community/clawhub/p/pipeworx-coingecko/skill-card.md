This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to configure access to CoinGecko-style cryptocurrency market data, including coin lookup, market rankings, trending assets, and search by name or symbol.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cryptocurrency requests and returned market data route through a Pipeworx-hosted MCP gateway rather than an official CoinGecko endpoint.

Mitigation: Review the gateway before installation and verify important price or market data against another trusted source before making financial decisions.
