## Description:

Chinese-language expert assistant for construction engineering tendering and procurement agency work in China, covering planning, tender document preparation, bid evaluation, risk assessment, contracts, cost control, schedules, and project management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External procurement and construction tendering professionals use this skill to draft tender agency service plans, plan bid packages, analyze client needs, prepare or review bidding documents, organize bid evaluation, assess procurement risks, and manage contracts, costs, schedules, and quality for China-focused construction projects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal or procurement guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or not tailored to a specific project.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting aids, verify current law and project requirements, and have qualified legal or procurement professionals review major decisions.

Risk: Tender documents, evaluation plans, or contract drafts may affect procurement fairness, compliance, cost, or schedule outcomes if used without review.

Mitigation: Review generated materials for legality, completeness, fairness, and consistency with the project's tendering method, bid package plan, budget, and approval requirements before release.
