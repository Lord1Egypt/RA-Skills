## Use Case:

CPS promoters and social commerce operators use this skill to draft product promotion copy and group-chat sales scripts for WeChat, QQ,朋友圈, and similar community channels. It helps turn verified product facts such as name, price, offer details, and purchase link into concise promotional text or staged conversation scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can generate staged group-chat personas, purchase confirmations, or urgency that may be mistaken for real customer activity.

Mitigation: Use it only for transparent promotional copy or clearly labeled role-play examples; do not impersonate customers or invent purchases.

Risk: Promotional copy may include scarcity, price, certification, return, inventory, or testimonial claims that are not verified.

Mitigation: Require those facts to come from user-provided or server-resolved product information before including them in output.
