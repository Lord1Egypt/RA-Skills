## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill activates broad planning, orchestration, and verification behavior for high-level agent tasks.

Mitigation: Review the scope before enabling it, especially when narrow task-specific behavior is preferred.

Risk: Much of the guidance is written in Indonesian, which may reduce reviewer or operator comprehension.

Mitigation: Ensure intended users can understand the guidance or review a translation before relying on it.
