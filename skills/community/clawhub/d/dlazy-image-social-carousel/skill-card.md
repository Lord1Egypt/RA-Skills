## Description:

A structured workflow skill dedicated to social-media carousel design. The core method is 'decide intent first, then execute,' using a 'single-confirmation + cover-first' two-phase flow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

## Use Case:

Content creators and social media teams use this skill to plan carousel structure, slide headlines and references, then guide cover-first slide generation.
