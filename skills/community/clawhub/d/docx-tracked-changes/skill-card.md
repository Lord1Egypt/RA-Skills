## Description:

Produce properly-formatted tracked changes for a Word document, including redline summaries, top-level changes, line-by-line tracked changes, margin comments, stylistic edits, pattern flags, review completeness, and application instructions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external reviewers, and document authors use this skill to draft structured redlines for Word documents, contracts, compliance reviews, copy edits, substantive rewrites, and plain-English revisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft redlines and review comments may be incorrect, incomplete, or inappropriate for legal, compliance, or other high-impact decisions.

Mitigation: Have qualified counsel or the responsible reviewer verify legal, compliance, and high-impact edits before relying on them.

Risk: Tracked deletions can become misleading if the original source text is not preserved exactly.

Mitigation: Compare proposed deletions against the source document and confirm that original wording is preserved character for character before applying changes.
