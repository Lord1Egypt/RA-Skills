## Use Case:

External job seekers and resume reviewers use this skill to compare Chinese resumes against target job descriptions, identify fit gaps, and receive honest wording, keyword, formatting, and STAR-framework improvement suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume content and job-search context can contain personal data.

Mitigation: Ask users to share only information they are comfortable using in the current chat, and do not save or disclose resume content.

Risk: Aggressive resume optimization can produce misleading claims or create interview risk.

Mitigation: Keep edits grounded in the user's real experience, clearly mark quantities or claims that need user confirmation, and provide honest gap analysis when job requirements are unmet.
