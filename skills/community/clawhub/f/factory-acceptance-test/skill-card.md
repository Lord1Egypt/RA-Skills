## Description:

Writes factory acceptance test plans or reports with a test coverage matrix, AQL sampling plan, pass/fail criteria, golden-sample handling, deviation log, and sign-off structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Manufacturing, quality, sourcing, and inspection teams use this skill to draft auditable factory acceptance test plans or reports for outgoing quality inspection, pre-shipment inspection, and lot disposition decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated AQL sampling tables, regulatory interpretations, or lot acceptance decisions may be incorrect for a specific product, jurisdiction, or quality system.

Mitigation: Verify AQL tables, regulatory requirements, and final acceptance decisions with qualified quality personnel before use.

Risk: A FAT plan or report may omit product-specific requirements, misclassify defects, or leave deviation and sign-off authority unclear if input specs are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the coverage matrix, defect classifications, deviation log, golden-sample handling, and sign-off block against the product spec, lot history, and named approvers.
