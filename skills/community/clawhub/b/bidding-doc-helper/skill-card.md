## Description: <br>
Helps agents create Chinese-language bidding document frameworks and technical proposal content for competitive negotiation or tender projects. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[powerzzjohn](https://clawhub.ai/user/powerzzjohn) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and bid-response teams use this skill to analyze user-supplied .docx tender documents, extract project and scoring requirements, and draft structured Markdown for bid frameworks, technical proposal sections, commercial scoring strategy, pricing guidance, and submission reminders. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Tender documents may contain confidential business, pricing, qualification, or project information. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide documents the user is authorized to process, and avoid sharing sensitive tender materials with agents or systems that are not approved for that data. <br>
Risk: The skill can activate on broad bidding-related terms and may read user-provided documents. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user's intended workflow before reading documents or generating bid-response content. <br>
Risk: Generated proposal language, scoring analysis, and pricing strategy may be incomplete, inaccurate, or noncompliant with a specific tender. <br>
Mitigation: Have qualified bid, legal, and commercial reviewers verify all generated content against the original tender before submission. <br>
