## Description:

Govern SEO findings and requirements through implementation, review, release gates, regression repair, and production verification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and SEO practitioners use this skill to turn SEO audits, search-facing release requirements, and SEO regressions into scoped implementation, review, release-gate, and production-verification work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be used to propose SEO-related implementation, release, deployment, search-console, URL-submission, or third-party setting changes beyond the user's intended authority.

Mitigation: Keep each task scoped to the specific SEO work and require explicit approval before deployments, external writes, search-console changes, URL submissions, or use of sensitive project data.

Risk: SEO analysis may be mistaken for proof of delayed external outcomes such as indexing, ranking, traffic, rich results, advertising approval, or AI citations.

Mitigation: Separate engineering verification from search-platform outcomes and record delayed external results as pending until verified by the appropriate source.
