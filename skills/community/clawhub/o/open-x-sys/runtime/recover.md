# Recover: Continue from What Truly Remains

Recovery finds still-valid system state that can change this run, turning the past into understanding, relations, and capabilities that can be used today.

## What to recover

- the current object, user purpose, explicit constraints, and exclusions;
- corrected understanding and explanations already disproved;
- completed works that can actually be opened, run, or used;
- validated capabilities with their conditions, dependencies, and failure signals;
- important relations, permissions, owners, and authoritative external state;
- unfinished effects, real signals awaiting arrival, and the next handoff point;
- changes since the last run that are large enough to alter judgment.

Recover only relevant parts. Isolated facts that change no model, choice, or action stay out of the center of the run.

## Order of evidence

The user's current words and corrections outrank old inference. Real files, system state, and tool results outrank old descriptions of them. Historical conclusions retain their dates and original conditions. When crossing hosts, establish the new host's actual tools and permissions again.

## How to speak after recovery

Continue directly from the most valuable present point. When useful, state in one sentence where the run resumed and what changed; the system carries the reading process. If entries are damaged or conflict, preserve checkable works first, then identify the conflict and recommend a recovery point.
