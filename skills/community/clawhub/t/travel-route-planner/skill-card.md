## Description:

根据用户输入的多个目的地，生成推荐的旅行路线规划。当用户需要提供多城市旅行路线建议、行程规划、交通方式推荐时使用此技能。适用于自由行规划、多目的地旅行、跨区域旅行等场景。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Travelers and travel-planning assistants use this skill to arrange multi-destination trips, compare route order, suggest transportation between stops, estimate time and budget, and provide practical travel tips.
