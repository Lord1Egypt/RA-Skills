## Description:

基于前六步分析结果，为法条生成完整的适用决策流程图（SOP），标注判断节点、证据要求和切换条件。这是法条分析的最终沉淀，形成可复用的实战操作指南。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users working in Chinese legal analysis use this skill after earlier statute-analysis steps to convert findings into a reusable decision-tree SOP. The skill helps draft text-based flowcharts that capture decision nodes, evidence requirements, burden of proof, dispute handling, and statute-switching conditions.
