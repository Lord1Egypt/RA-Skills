## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validation plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision support before making large investments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before committing significant time or budget. It helps convert rough ideas into repeatable validation workflows, practical artifacts, checklists, lightweight analysis, and next-step guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate in broad product, startup, or SaaS discussions because its trigger language is loose.

Mitigation: Use it as a planning aid for explicit validation or product-planning requests, and confirm intent when activation is ambiguous.

Risk: Demand evidence may be mistaken for proof of market demand.

Mitigation: Treat demand evidence as background context and base business decisions on current customer interviews, experiments, sales signals, and user data.

Risk: Generated plans, checklists, code, shell commands, or configuration may be incomplete or unsuitable for the user's operating context.

Mitigation: Review outputs before execution or adoption, verify assumptions against the stated success criteria, and test any code or commands in a controlled environment.
