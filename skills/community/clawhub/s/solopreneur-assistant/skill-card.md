## Use Case:

Solo business operators and external agent users use this skill to configure an AI assistant for inbox triage, task prioritization, revenue and expense tracking, decision journaling, opportunity scoring, and recurring reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can involve privacy-sensitive business, email, calendar, revenue, and persistent memory data.

Mitigation: Decide which files and integrations the agent may read before installation, and grant only least-privilege access.

Risk: Agent actions on inboxes, calendars, or external communications could alter business records or expose sensitive information.

Mitigation: Require human confirmation before the agent sends, deletes, archives, or externally shares anything.
