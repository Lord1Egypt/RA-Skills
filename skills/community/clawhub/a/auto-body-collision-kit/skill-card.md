## Description:

Generates compliance-audited marketing content for Nevada auto body and collision repair shops, with copy screened for OSHA, EPA, FTC, credential, parts-disclosure, warranty, and Nevada salvage-title concerns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Auto body shop owners, collision repair managers, fleet repair operators, and marketing agencies use this skill to draft localized marketing campaigns, service pages, ads, reputation responses, referral materials, and local SEO content while avoiding unsupported regulatory and credential claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Nevada, United States (Clark County primary; principles transferable nationwide)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing copy could be treated as legal or regulatory advice, especially for Nevada and Clark County compliance claims.

Mitigation: Verify current regulations and have qualified reviewers confirm OSHA, EPA, FTC, Nevada, and local advertising obligations before publishing.

Risk: Shop-specific claims about licenses, certifications, EPA/OSHA status, warranties, parts policies, or location-specific service coverage may be inaccurate if placeholders are not replaced with verified facts.

Mitigation: Confirm shop licenses, certifications, EPA/OSHA status, warranty terms, parts sourcing policies, and local claims against current records before use.
