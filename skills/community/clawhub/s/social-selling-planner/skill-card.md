## Description:

Social Selling Planner helps founders and sellers plan a human-run B2B social selling routine with daily engagement blocks, warm-touch thresholds, trigger-response plays, and quarterly diagnostics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, sellers, and go-to-market teams use this skill to convert target-account lists, watchlist signals, and platform exports into a daily social-selling operating block and follow-up decision rules. It is for planning and diagnostics; pitch/DM mechanics and cold email are out of scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Plans may rely on user-provided account lists, exports, screenshots, watchlist items, and local memory records.

Mitigation: Treat those inputs as untrusted, verify important trigger signals at their source, and review outputs before saving cadence proposals or plans.

Risk: Social selling guidance could be misused as automated outreach or engagement.

Mitigation: Keep outreach, DMs, connection requests, and engagement actions under human control; use the skill only for planning, diagnostics, and handoff guidance.
