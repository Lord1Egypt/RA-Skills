## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The artifact references external Teneo/agent-console services and crypto-native payments.

Mitigation: Review any connection to those services separately before providing accounts, wallet details, credentials, or payment authorization.

Risk: Travel information such as visas, airport status, exchange rates, events, and emergency contacts can become outdated or vary by traveler circumstances.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive or official travel details against authoritative sources before acting on the guidance.
