## Use Case:

Biopharma patent, product, and business development teams use this skill to structure preliminary freedom-to-operate screening for drug candidates, platforms, or licensing targets across ADCs, antibodies, oligonucleotides, cell and gene therapies, and small molecules. It supports claim mapping, jurisdiction status checks, risk grading, evidence gap tracking, and attorney review preparation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake preliminary FTO screening output for a legal opinion or freedom-to-operate guarantee.

Mitigation: Require review by a qualified patent professional before relying on high-impact findings, infringement conclusions, validity positions, or launch decisions.

Risk: Confidential product, sequence, structure, process, or market details may be exposed if entered into external search tools without approval.

Mitigation: Apply organizational confidentiality rules before using external databases or tools, and redact sensitive details when policy requires it.

Risk: Incomplete product facts, market scope, or legal status evidence can produce overconfident risk ratings.

Mitigation: When required facts are missing, produce a search plan and assumption register instead of definitive risk grades.
