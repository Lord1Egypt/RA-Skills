## Description:

Fiverr Gig Optimizer helps Fiverr and similar gig-marketplace sellers diagnose sales funnel issues and draft optimized gig titles, descriptions, pricing tiers, tags, image briefs, and 30-day action plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External freelancers and gig-marketplace sellers use this skill to improve gig packaging and sales funnel performance on Fiverr, Creative Market, Etsy, and similar platforms. It supports funnel diagnosis, copy improvement, pricing and package structure, Fiverr SEO, image brief creation, and repeat-buyer planning.
