## Description:

Support users with interpersonal communication practice, social scenario rehearsal, and relationship problem framing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and assistants use this skill to prepare clear, respectful wording for difficult interpersonal conversations, including boundary setting, apologies, needs expression, interview or work practice, and roleplay rehearsal.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat communication coaching as therapy, legal advice, crisis support, or a guarantee of relationship outcomes.

Mitigation: Present outputs as practice and framing, and direct users to appropriate professional, crisis, legal, or trusted support for high-risk situations.

Risk: The skill could be misused to pressure, deceive, harass, coerce, or emotionally control another person.

Mitigation: Keep responses respectful and boundary-oriented, and refuse requests involving coercion, emotional blackmail, stalking-style persistence, deception, or harassment.

Risk: Conversation scripts may miss context in sensitive interpersonal conflicts.

Mitigation: Ask only necessary clarifying questions, offer concise drafts, and prioritize safety concerns over communication tactics when abuse or immediate risk appears.
