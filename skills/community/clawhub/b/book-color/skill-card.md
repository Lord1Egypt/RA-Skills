## Description:

Book color services through Lokuli MCP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to find color service providers by ZIP code, check availability, and create Lokuli bookings after confirming appointment details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can create external appointments through Lokuli and may send customer contact details.

Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the provider, service, time, and that the user is comfortable sharing their name, email, phone number, ZIP code, and appointment details with Lokuli.
