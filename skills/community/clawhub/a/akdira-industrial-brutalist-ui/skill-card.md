## Description:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated interfaces may reduce readability, accessibility, or localization quality when using all-caps text, very small metadata text, tight line-height, or visual noise effects.

Mitigation: Review generated UI for readability, accessibility, and localization before deployment.
