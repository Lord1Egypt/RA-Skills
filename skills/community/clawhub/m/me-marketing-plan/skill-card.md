## Description:

Create a minimalist marketing plan focused on building an audience through content, not ads.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External founders, operators, and business advisors use this skill to create a content-led marketing plan after early product-market fit, including platform focus, content ideas, email capture, build-in-public topics, and paid advertising thresholds.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides strategic marketing advice that may not fit every business model, audience, or industry constraint.

Mitigation: Review the Minimalist Entrepreneur framing, bilingual documentation, and recommendations against the user's business context before adopting the plan.

Risk: Marketing recommendations can lead to ineffective spend if paid advertising is used before organic traction and customer profile clarity.

Mitigation: Use the skill's spend-money-last posture and require review of organic traction, customer profile, and expected customer value before paid acquisition.
