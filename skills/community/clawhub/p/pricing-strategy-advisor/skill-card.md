## Description:

Turn pricing notes, margin pressure, competitor context, discount behavior, and channel constraints into a practical pricing strategy brief with objective framing, move options, test ideas, and guardrails.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce teams, brand operators, category managers, founders, and consultants use this skill to turn partial pricing, margin, competitor, discount, and channel context into a practical pricing strategy brief. It is best suited for advisory planning before launches, quarterly reviews, margin recovery work, discount discipline, and pricing architecture decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides heuristic business guidance and may be mistaken for live pricing, competitor, ERP, or analytics evidence.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory only and require users to supply or verify current market, cost, conversion, and channel data before acting.

Risk: Pricing recommendations could imply elasticity or conversion certainty that has not been proven.

Mitigation: Keep confidence caveats visible and require direct experimental or analytics evidence before presenting elasticity as proven.

Risk: Permanent price changes, MAP exceptions, discount changes, or channel-policy moves can create commercial or contractual harm.

Mitigation: Require human approval and escalation review before implementing real price, discount, MAP, or channel-policy changes.
