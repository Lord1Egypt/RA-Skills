## Description:

Provides HR-focused guidance for talent reviews, leadership assessment, succession planning, career path design, high-potential identification, competency models, individual development plans, and leadership pipeline planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tuobadaidai](https://clawhub.ai/user/tuobadaidai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

HR leaders, managers, and employee development specialists use this skill to structure talent reviews, manager evaluations, succession plans, career paths, retention risk checks, and individual development plans. It is intended for authorized HR or management use and excludes compensation design, labor-law compliance, termination risk, training course design, performance metric design, and organization structure design.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive employee information could be exposed or over-shared during talent assessment, succession planning, or retention analysis.

Mitigation: Use only in authorized HR or management workflows, provide the minimum necessary employee information, and anonymize personal identifiers when detailed identities are not required.

Risk: The skill may produce advisory HR recommendations that touch adjacent compensation, labor-law, termination, or pay-equity issues outside its stated scope.

Mitigation: Route compensation design, pay-equity, termination, and labor-law questions to qualified HR/legal workflows and review recommendations before acting on them.
