## Description:

Generate personalized cloud career development roadmaps for AWS, Azure, and GCP support roles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd](https://clawhub.ai/user/krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, career coaches, support engineers, and training organizations use this skill to generate personalized cloud support learning roadmaps, certification paths, skill gap analysis, practical exercises, and next steps from assessment data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career assessment details, session identifiers, timestamps, and optional user identifiers may be sent to an external API.

Mitigation: Use anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers where possible, and avoid submitting sensitive personal, employer-confidential, or account data unless the service's privacy terms are acceptable.
