## Description:

Gaokao City Budget helps students and families estimate university living costs in Chinese cities across housing, food, transportation, and related expenses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zulirrrr](https://clawhub.ai/user/zulirrrr)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and families use this skill when comparing university destination cities in China or preparing a four-year living-cost budget. It gathers public cost signals and returns tiered budget reports or focused answers for housing, dining, transportation, and related expenses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, for China-focused university city cost research.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated HTML reports may include web-sourced text or links that are unsafe or misleading.

Mitigation: Escape cited text, allow only safe http/https source links, and review reports before opening or sharing.

Risk: Public cost data can be stale, inconsistent, or specific to a neighborhood, campus, or platform.

Mitigation: Cite the platform, collection time, and scope for each cost figure; flag older or uncertain data and direct users to current school, transport, and rental sources.
