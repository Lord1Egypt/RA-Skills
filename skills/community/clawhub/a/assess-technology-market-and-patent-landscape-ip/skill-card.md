## Description:

Assesses a defined product or technology field by combining market evidence, global patent landscape analysis, validated key-player research, technical themes, risks, and project options into a self-contained HTML report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, IP teams, and R&D strategists use this skill to screen a technology or product field, map evidence-backed patent and market landscapes, and identify project-entry options for further diligence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent and market research queries may be sent to referenced PatSnap/OpenClaw connectors and other approved research sources.

Mitigation: Confirm data-sharing permissions, connector access, and acceptable-use requirements before running sensitive research.

Risk: Generated reports may be mistaken for legal, investment, market forecast, or freedom-to-operate advice.

Mitigation: Require qualified human review before relying on outputs for legal, investment, filing, or project-entry decisions.

Risk: Patent samples or incomplete connector aggregations can produce misleading landscape conclusions.

Mitigation: Use only verified full-population metrics or explicitly mark metrics unavailable, and keep representative records separate from population claims.
