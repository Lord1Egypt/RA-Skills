## Description:

A Chinese-language structured novel-writing workflow that guides an agent through prewriting analysis, drafting, self-checks, prose polishing, revision, and metadata updates for chapter-by-chapter fiction projects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wuxiao00j](https://clawhub.ai/user/wuxiao00j)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and writing assistants use this skill to draft chapters from an existing outline while preserving timeline, character state, foreshadowing, continuity, and prose quality. It is most relevant for Chinese-language novel projects that maintain state-tracking files between chapters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow and templates are Chinese-focused and may not fit projects in other languages or narrative conventions.

Mitigation: Review and adapt the outline, state-tracking fields, and prose-polishing criteria before using the skill for non-Chinese writing projects.

Risk: The skill asks the agent to produce chapter metadata and state updates that may affect continuity records.

Mitigation: Review generated state updates before applying them to project files, especially timeline, character state, inventory, and foreshadowing records.
