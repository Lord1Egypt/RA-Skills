## Description:

Research, plan, revise, and deliver lodging-anchored travel guides as HTML/PDF with verified route data, hotel selection, fallback hotel swaps, curated screenshots, and formal copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Travel planners, agents, and external users use this skill to research lodging-first itineraries, verify route and venue data, choose hotel fallbacks, and produce formal HTML/PDF-ready travel guides.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Travel prices, hours, routes, and hotel availability may change after the guide is drafted.

Mitigation: Re-check schedule-critical route, lodging, scenic, and dining data before relying on or distributing the final guide.

Risk: Screenshots or generated local HTML/PDF files may contain stale, cluttered, or irrelevant evidence.

Mitigation: Review outputs for accuracy, remove screenshots that do not support decisions, and verify the rendered HTML before PDF export.
