## Description:

A Chinese-language trending-topic briefing skill that gathers hot-search topics from public web sources and produces AI-assisted context, viewpoints, and trend interpretation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krisyhr](https://clawhub.ai/user/krisyhr)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to understand current Chinese-language hot topics, trace the background of a specific trending item, or generate a daily public-web briefing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Live public web sources can contain stale, incomplete, or misleading current-events information.

Mitigation: Verify important claims against primary or authoritative sources before acting on the briefing.

Risk: The skill is designed for Chinese-language hot-topic briefings and may answer in Chinese by default.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for another language or narrower scope when needed.
