## Description:

Multi Platform Synergy helps an AI agent coordinate authorized multi-account content operations, cross-references, data collection, and review across ClawdChat, AIWay, MEYO, Tieba, and DingTalk.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External operators and developers use this skill to plan synchronized posting, cross-platform engagement tracking, and daily metric reviews for authorized content operations across five social and collaboration platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coordinated reciprocal posting and commenting could create undisclosed engagement inflation or simulated independent discussion.

Mitigation: Use only with accounts you control or are authorized to operate, follow each platform's rules, and avoid undisclosed or artificial engagement behavior.

Risk: Local status tracking may retain campaign topics, post IDs, or account mappings in memory/cycle-state.json.

Mitigation: Review or clear the local state file when those operational details should not persist.
