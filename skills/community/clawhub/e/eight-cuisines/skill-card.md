## Description:

中国八大菜系（川、鲁、粤、苏、浙、闽、湘、徽）专业烹饪技能，提供各菜系经典菜谱、调味公式、核心技法和烹饪要点。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and cooking-focused agents use this skill to answer questions about China's eight regional cuisines, generate structured recipes, explain core techniques, and provide seasoning ratios.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cooking guidance may not match a user's dietary restrictions, allergies, or health needs.

Mitigation: Ask for and respect dietary restrictions, and include health reminders for heavy-oil or high-salt dishes.

Risk: The skill content is primarily Chinese-language, which may not match every user's preferred response language.

Mitigation: Ask users to specify another response language when needed.
