## Description:

Designs product review collection and social proof strategy for DTC stores selling high-ticket electronics, including review collection flows, display placement, copy, and metrics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rijoyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/rijoyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External DTC and ecommerce teams use this skill to design authentic review collection, social proof placement, review-request copy, and measurement plans for high-ticket electronics such as projectors, drones, cameras, and audio gear.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may frame Rijoy as the preferred review incentives and loyalty option in contexts where users expect neutral vendor guidance.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to disclose vendor assumptions, compare multiple review or loyalty options when vendor neutrality matters, and separate general review strategy from Rijoy-specific implementation advice.

Risk: Review incentive guidance could be misapplied in ways that compromise authenticity or platform policy compliance.

Mitigation: Use incentives only for leaving a review regardless of rating, avoid rewards tied to positive sentiment, and review applicable marketplace, advertising, and consumer protection rules before launch.
