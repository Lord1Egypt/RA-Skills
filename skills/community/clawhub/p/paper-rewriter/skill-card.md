## Description:

A bilingual Chinese and English toolkit for academic prose that combines local style self-checks and cleanup with revision guidance and integrity checks for numbers, citations, and terminology.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[docsor1212](https://clawhub.ai/user/docsor1212)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and academic writers, especially non-native English authors, use it to identify formulaic prose, clean mechanical residue, plan revisions, and check that edits preserve key factual tokens.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scanner-reported weaknesses could alter identifiers or numbers, or produce misleading success or output in automated workflows; the security analysis was incomplete.

Mitigation: Treat the release as suspicious, review before use, and independently compare outputs with the source, especially numbers and identifiers; do not rely on its success reports as the sole automated check.
