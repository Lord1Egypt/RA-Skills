## Description:

Chan Theory is a Chinese-language technical-analysis skill for applying Chan Theory concepts to A-share market structure, including strokes, line segments, centers, divergence, and buy/sell points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[danpian1](https://clawhub.ai/user/danpian1)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to apply a Chinese-language Chan Theory framework to A-share technical analysis workflows. It helps structure educational market analysis around fractals, strokes, line segments, centers, divergence, and three classes of buy/sell points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake Chan Theory signals or buy/sell point labels for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat all outputs as educational technical analysis, verify market data independently, and do not rely on the skill as an instruction to trade.
