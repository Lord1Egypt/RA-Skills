## Description:

Builds a modern hashtag and discoverability strategy for social posts by prioritizing keyword and social SEO signals, then selecting tight, platform-specific hashtag sets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External social media operators, marketers, founders, and content teams use this skill to turn a post, audience, and brand context into platform-aware hashtag guidance. It is intended for practical social publishing workflows where hashtags support categorization and search rather than promise reach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill reads brand, audience, and post context to tailor hashtag guidance.

Mitigation: Provide only the brand, audience, and post details needed for the current recommendation, especially when working with unpublished campaigns or sensitive launches.

Risk: The skill focuses on hashtag strategy and may be too narrow for broad reach or social SEO planning.

Mitigation: Use a more specialized strategy skill for full social SEO or growth planning unless the task specifically needs hashtag guidance.

Risk: Platform hashtag behavior and limits change over time.

Mitigation: Re-check platform limits and tag pages before publishing, especially for Instagram caps, banned or spam-associated tags, and campaign hashtags.
