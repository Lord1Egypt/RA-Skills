## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat business-coaching output as HR, legal, medical, compliance, or other professional advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as decision-support guidance and require users to consult qualified professionals or internal policy owners for regulated, legal, medical, HR, or compliance decisions.

Risk: Team-building guidance may encourage overly aggressive discipline, hiring, or termination decisions if applied without context.

Mitigation: Apply fair process, documentation, bias checks, company policy, and HR or legal review before acting on workplace recommendations.

Risk: Framework outputs can be persuasive even when based on incomplete user context or weak assumptions.

Mitigation: Ask clarifying questions, state assumptions explicitly, and validate recommendations against facts, constraints, and user-specific risk tolerance before execution.
