## Description:

Academic paper summarization with dynamic SOP selection based on paper topic classification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nomorecoding](https://clawhub.ai/user/nomorecoding)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Researchers, reviewers, and developers use this skill to generate structured academic paper summaries, methodological critiques, experimental assessments, and reproducibility-focused prompts for papers classified by topic.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can save summaries and full prompts under local research directories.

Mitigation: Confirm local storage is acceptable before use, review output paths, and avoid processing sensitive or unpublished paper content unless the workspace is approved for it.

Risk: Default output instructions are Chinese-focused.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to adapt the template language or edit the SOP template when English-only or another output language is required.

Risk: Generated critiques may contain incorrect interpretations of a paper's methods, experiments, or claims.

Mitigation: Review summaries against the source paper before relying on them for research decisions, peer review, or reporting.
