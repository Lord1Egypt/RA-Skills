## Description:

Generate a ~10000-word research article in Simplified Chinese investigating the identity of Zhiyanzhai (脂砚斋), the anonymous commentator on "Dream of the Red Chamber" (红楼梦), covering literary, historical, and poetic dimensions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External users, students, educators, and researchers use this skill to draft a citation-backed Simplified Chinese research article about Zhiyanzhai's identity and Redology scholarship.
