## Description:

Draft professional follow-up emails to contacts made at conferences - not too pushy, but memorable.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aipoch-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/aipoch-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers use this skill to draft professional conference follow-up emails or LinkedIn connection messages from a contact name, conference, discussion topic, sender name, and tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated follow-up text may be inaccurate, too assertive, or mismatched to the real conversation.

Mitigation: Review and edit every generated email or LinkedIn message before sending.

Risk: Empty or whitespace-only contact names can crash the script instead of returning a clean error.

Mitigation: Validate that contact names contain non-whitespace text before execution.
