## Description:

B2B Lead Generation Assistant that identifies competitors, discovers potential customers through connection mining, scores and enriches leads, and generates personalized business development materials through a six-phase workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ericn26-star](https://clawhub.ai/user/ericn26-star)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sales, marketing, and business development users use this skill to turn a product description into competitor research, lead discovery, lead scoring, contact enrichment, outreach drafts, and a daily lead-generation report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide collection of business contacts, inferred emails, and prospect profiles from public or platform-sourced activity.

Mitigation: Use only lawful and permitted contact sources, respect platform and email-service rules, avoid private or scraped data, and retain only the personal data needed for the outreach workflow.

Risk: Repeated LinkedIn and email outreach may create compliance, consent, or opt-out risks.

Mitigation: Keep suppression and opt-out lists, review outreach before sending, and apply applicable email and privacy compliance requirements before deployment.
