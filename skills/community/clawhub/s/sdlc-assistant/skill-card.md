## Description:

A guided assistant for software development teams across roles including developers, testers (QA), product owners (PO), project managers (PM), and business analysts (BA), supporting SDLC phase guidance, Agile and Waterfall planning, requirements, testing, release management, and related delivery artifacts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pattzaks](https://clawhub.ai/user/pattzaks)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, QA testers, product owners, project managers, and business analysts use this skill to get role-specific SDLC guidance and generate practical planning artifacts such as user stories, acceptance criteria, test cases, risk registers, RTMs, definitions of done, and release checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad SDLC activation wording may route general software planning questions to this skill.

Mitigation: Review and narrow activation wording during installation if stricter routing is required.

Risk: Generated SDLC templates and checklists may not match an organization's internal process, compliance gates, or delivery standards.

Mitigation: Have project leads review generated guidance before using it for delivery decisions or official project artifacts.
