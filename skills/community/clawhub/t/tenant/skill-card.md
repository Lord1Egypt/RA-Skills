## Use Case:

External renters use this skill to evaluate apartments, understand leases, document move-in and move-out condition, communicate with landlords, and prepare for common tenant disputes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tenant rights, remedies, deadlines, and lease obligations vary by state, city, and lease language.

Mitigation: Confirm guidance against the user's local law, lease terms, a tenant organization, or a licensed attorney before relying on it for eviction risk, major habitability issues, or significant money disputes.

Risk: Lease files, dispute records, and rental communications may contain personal or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum information needed and avoid unnecessary personal identifiers when asking the skill to review documents or draft communications.
