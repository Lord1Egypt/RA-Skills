Learn the 48 Laws of Working with Claude, in Plain Words

Give your AI agent one prompt, or one workflow. It teaches the 48 laws in plain words, then checks your prompt or workflow against the laws, law by law, with evidence, then gives a fix draft for owner review.

Most people write one prompt, get a weak answer, and write another prompt, and another, and never know why the answers stay weak. The problem is not the agent. The problem is the way the work is asked. No role, no outcome, no context, no example, no check, no saved system. The 48 laws fix that, one law at a time, in plain words.

## What you get

- A teach and check for the 48 laws, in 7 groups: start right, organize knowledge, reuse and do not repeat, think and check, write well, connect and learn, and build systems
- A laws check, each law Followed, Broken, or Not confirmed, with evidence from the pasted text, so you know what your prompt follows and what it breaks
- A fix draft, labelled DRAFT, NOT SENT: laws check summary, laws broken, laws followed, what to change first, and what to keep
- A missing checks list at the end, so nothing is hidden

## The 48 laws bar

1. Every prompt has a role, an outcome, and full context.
2. Knowledge lives in Projects and folders, not in lost chats.
3. A prompt used twice becomes a Skill, a SOP, or a template.
4. Every answer is checked before it is used.
5. Every prompt states the audience, the tone, and the format.
6. Knowledge is connected before a hard task.
7. Work is built as a system, not as one off chats.

The 48 laws in the skill come from a post by Sarvesh Thakur, dated 26 Oct 2026. They are the whole skill, rewritten in plain words, not one section inside it.

## How to use it

1. Install the skill in your AI agent (see below).
2. Give your agent the prompt or workflow, the business, the goal, and the audience, in plain words.
3. Ask: "Teach me the laws and check my prompt with claude-laws-skill."
4. Read the laws check first. Every Followed and every Broken needs evidence pasted in the message.
5. Read the laws that are Not confirmed. A law with no evidence is Not confirmed, not broken.
6. Read the fix draft. Approve the first change before anything goes live.

If you already give the prompt, the business, the goal, and the audience up front, the skill does the full teach and check in the same reply. It never stops at questions alone.

## What it will not do

- It will not send a message, publish a post, or change a live workflow, without a separate approval from the owner.
- It will not invent a tool name, a price, a link, a stat, a partner, a testimonial, or a result.
- It will not mark a law Followed or Broken without evidence pasted in the message.
- A fix draft is not a promise of more sales, more calls, more visitors, or a higher rank.

Copy the `SKILL.md` file into your skills folder, in a folder called `claude-laws-skill`, then restart your agent. The skill file itself is just instructions. It collects no data and sends nothing anywhere.
