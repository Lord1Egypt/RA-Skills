## Description:

Cognitive copilot for people with ADHD that helps detect states such as overwhelm, planning, focus, transitions, reflection, and brain dumps, then provides concrete support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[betancur](https://clawhub.ai/user/betancur)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill for ADHD-oriented productivity support, including getting unstuck, planning a day or week, starting focused work, transitioning between tasks, reflecting without shame, and externalizing mental clutter.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could mistake mental-health-adjacent paralysis or crisis-mode support for clinical or emergency mental-health care.

Mitigation: Frame the skill as productivity support only, and direct acute safety concerns or clinical needs outside the skill to appropriate professional or emergency resources.

Risk: Medication-related discussion could drift into medical advice.

Mitigation: Do not suggest starting, stopping, or changing medication; recommend that users discuss medication questions with a healthcare professional.

Risk: Focus-mode timer support could overstep into running commands, notifications, or system actions.

Mitigation: Keep all timers user-side; instruct the user to set a phone or browser timer and do not run shell commands or notifications.
