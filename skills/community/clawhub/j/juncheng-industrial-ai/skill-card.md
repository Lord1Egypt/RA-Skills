## Use Case:

Industrial automation developers and operations engineers use this skill to review PLC ladder, SCL, and STL logic; draft PLC program frameworks from process flowcharts; investigate alarms with historical data; and produce SCADA daily, weekly, or monthly reports from sensor data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: PLC programs, SCADA reports, alarm histories, network details, credentials, and production telemetry can contain sensitive operational data.

Mitigation: Use only authorized, redacted, or test data when possible, especially in industrial or critical-infrastructure settings.

Risk: Industrial automation analysis and generated PLC frameworks may be incomplete or incorrect for a live process.

Mitigation: Have qualified personnel review and validate outputs in a controlled environment before applying them to production equipment.
