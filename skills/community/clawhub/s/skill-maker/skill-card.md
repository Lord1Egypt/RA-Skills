## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated or modified skills may contain unclear activation descriptions, unsafe dependency choices, or scripts that do not match the installer's intent.

Mitigation: Review generated skill files, metadata, scripts, dependencies, and activation descriptions before deployment.

Risk: Because the package provides authoring guidance rather than strict enforcement, generated skills may need additional safety review.

Mitigation: Scan and test generated skills in the intended agent environment before publishing or operational use.
