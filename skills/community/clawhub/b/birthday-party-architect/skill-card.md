## Description:

Helps plan a child's birthday party with an age-appropriate schedule, activities, food quantities, budget, and home-versus-venue comparison.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Parents and caregivers use this skill to turn a child's age, guest count, venue, and budget into a practical party plan, including a timeline, activity ideas, shopping quantities, and budget guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General party advice may miss a child's specific allergies, medical or accessibility needs, supervision requirements, or venue safety rules.

Mitigation: Confirm individual needs with caregivers and the venue, collect and label allergy information, and arrange age-appropriate adult supervision.
