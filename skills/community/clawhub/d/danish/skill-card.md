## Use Case:

Developers, writers, and agents use this skill to produce Danish text that reads more casual and native-like, with informal register, natural particles, concise phrasing, dry humor, and common expressions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The default casual, native-like register may be inappropriate when formal, neutral, or business Danish is required.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for formal, neutral, or business Danish when that register matters.
