## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Complaint workflows may involve personal information or jurisdiction-specific regulatory requirements.

Mitigation: Adapt the SOP to applicable local law and platform rules, and apply data minimization before operational use.

Risk: Generic SOP guidance may not match an organization's escalation paths, metrics, or reporting cadence.

Mitigation: Review the workflow with support, operations, and compliance owners before deployment and update the knowledge base after pilots.
