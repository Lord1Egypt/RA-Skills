## Description:

Learn to cook real meals from basic ingredients on a tight budget, with meal plans, foundational techniques, and a pantry system for people who never learned to cook or who rely on takeout.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to learn basic scratch cooking, reduce takeout spending, stock a low-cost pantry, follow simple meal plans, and prepare inexpensive meals from common ingredients.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for filesystem access even though the evidence describes a cooking guidance workflow.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installing and ask the publisher to remove the unnecessary filesystem requirement or limit file access before deployment.

Risk: The installer command is unpinned or unverified.

Mitigation: Use a pinned, verified release or publisher-confirmed installation path before installing in a managed environment.

Risk: Cooking advice may not cover every food-safety situation involving meat, eggs, rice, leftovers, refrigeration, or cross-contamination.

Mitigation: Follow current food-safety guidance from authoritative sources when applying the skill's meal preparation advice.
