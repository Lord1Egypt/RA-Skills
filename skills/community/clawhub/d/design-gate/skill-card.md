## Description:

A design gate checker for architecture validation, feasibility analysis, and impact scope assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terr123123](https://clawhub.ai/user/terr123123)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to check whether a proposed design includes reasonable architecture, a feasible technology stack, and a documented impact scope before implementation proceeds.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill's pass/fail decisions are based on simple scoring heuristics and may not replace a full architecture review.

Mitigation: Review the scoring rules and treat results as a lightweight design-review aid before relying on them for release decisions.
