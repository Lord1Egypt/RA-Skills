This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Construction sustainability teams, architects, engineers, and developers use this skill to calculate embodied and lifecycle carbon for materials, assemblies, and projects, compare results against building-type benchmarks, and identify carbon reduction opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill depends on pandas and may behave differently across dependency versions.

Mitigation: Install it in a dedicated Python virtual environment and pin pandas in a requirements file before use.

Risk: The included carbon factors appear to be sample data and may not match authoritative EPDs or project-specific sources.

Mitigation: Validate carbon factors against authoritative EPD or project-specific sources before using results for compliance or reporting.
