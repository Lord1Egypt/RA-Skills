## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and workspace administrators use this skill to plan least-privilege Feishu document and folder sharing, audit collaborator access, and prepare remediation plans before changing permissions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Permission recommendations may be incomplete or incorrect for the organization's actual Feishu policy and document sensitivity.

Mitigation: Treat the skill output as planning guidance and verify access changes through owner approval and the normal audit process before applying them.

Risk: Elevated or external Feishu access could remain broader or longer-lived than intended if recommendations are applied without review cadence.

Mitigation: Document why access was granted, time-box elevated external access, and schedule periodic access reviews.
