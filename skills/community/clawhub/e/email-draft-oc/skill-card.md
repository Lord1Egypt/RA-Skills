## Use Case:

External users use this skill to generate email drafts from keywords, adjust tone, manage reusable email templates, and draft emails in multiple languages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Email drafts and templates may be saved locally in drafts.json and templates.json, which can expose sensitive email content if users store confidential material.

Mitigation: Avoid saving sensitive email content unless local persistence is acceptable for the deployment environment.
