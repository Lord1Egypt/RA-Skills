## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Use the skill only where retaining chat content is acceptable, and clear history or cache data when retention is no longer needed.

Risk: Broad activation phrases can trigger the skill in more situations than intended.

Mitigation: Prefer narrower trigger wording when the agent environment supports trigger customization.

Risk: The skill documentation is mainly in Chinese, which may make operational details harder to review for non-Chinese readers.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reader review the skill behavior and usage notes before deployment.
