## Description:

Conduct a structured operational audit to identify friction points, map workflows, quantify waste, and produce a priority-ranked automation blueprint with ROI projections.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales and operations teams use this skill to turn discovery calls, process descriptions, and operational pain points into a client-ready automation blueprint with workflow scoring, waste quantification, ROI modeling, and implementation priorities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may include DynoClaw-specific attribution, recommendations, and calls to action that are not suitable for neutral client deliverables.

Mitigation: Review and edit the report for vendor attribution, recommendation framing, and the fixed call to action before sharing it externally.

Risk: ROI estimates and automation priorities may be misleading when the provided workflow details, volumes, costs, or error rates are incomplete.

Mitigation: Use the skill's discovery questions and human checkpoints to collect missing inputs, then review calculations before relying on the blueprint.
