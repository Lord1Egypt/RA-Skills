## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad activation wording around finding information or learning new knowledge.

Mitigation: Review and narrow activation criteria if deployment should only trigger on explicit web-research or knowledge-gathering requests.

Risk: Research workflows may involve external source lookup for sensitive topics.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive topics or require user confirmation and source review before external lookup is used.

Risk: Generated research reports or learning notes can contain incorrect or outdated information.

Mitigation: Use the skill's source traceability, cross-verification, and reliability-assessment guidance before acting on outputs.
