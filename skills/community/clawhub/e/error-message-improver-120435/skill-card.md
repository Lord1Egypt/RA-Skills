## Description:

Helps developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users turn vague error messages into clearer troubleshooting copy that explains what failed, why it failed, and what to do next.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users use this skill to rewrite or design clearer error messages and troubleshooting aids. It helps produce tailored guidance, reusable checklists, workflows, analysis, code-oriented suggestions, and validation notes for error-handling improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause the skill to appear for general debugging or support tasks beyond error-message wording.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for error-message copy, troubleshooting text, or user-facing failure explanations, and review generated wording before shipping.
