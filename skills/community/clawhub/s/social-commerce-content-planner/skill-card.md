## Description:

Social Commerce Content Planner helps Chinese-language social commerce teams plan content themes, calendars, livestream scripts, short-video copy templates, and compliance checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Social commerce operators, livestream sellers, and content creators use this skill to create structured planning outputs for ecommerce content workflows. It is suited to small teams that need topic ideas, weekly or monthly calendars, livestream templates, short-video copy structures, and self-checklists for marketing content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing plans may include outdated platform timing, seasonal references, or promotional assumptions because the skill uses static guidance and no real-time APIs.

Mitigation: Review dates, platform requirements, and current trends before publishing or scheduling generated content.

Risk: Generated marketing copy may make claims that require evidence or special care for regulated products.

Mitigation: Verify product claims, avoid unsupported guarantees or absolute language, and apply extra review for medical, drug, financial, or similarly regulated categories.
