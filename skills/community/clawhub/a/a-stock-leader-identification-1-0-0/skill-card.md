## Description:

This skill helps agents identify likely leading A-share sector stocks using short-term trading signals such as first limit-up, order-book strength, consecutive limit-up days, turnover, theme fit, and company leadership.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and trading-focused agents use this skill to frame short-term A-share sector-leader screening and avoid follower-stock candidates. Outputs should be treated as educational heuristics, not personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Short-term speculative stock-selection guidance could be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as educational heuristics and require independent research, suitability review, and risk controls before any trading decision.
