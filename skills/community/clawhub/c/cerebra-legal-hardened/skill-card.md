## Use Case:

External users and legal-supporting agents use this skill to structure legal-information responses around jurisdiction, facts, issue spotting, law application, risk assessment, and action items while preserving attorney-escalation and safety guardrails.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat structured legal analysis as legal advice for their specific situation.

Mitigation: Include a legal-information disclaimer in every substantive response and direct users to a licensed attorney for situation-specific decisions.

Risk: Time-sensitive legal deadlines or filing obligations may be inaccurate, outdated, or jurisdiction-specific.

Mitigation: Recommend verification by a licensed attorney, compliance professional, or authoritative government source before the user relies on any deadline.

Risk: Safety disclosures such as domestic violence, stalking, or abuse can be hidden inside routine legal questions.

Mitigation: Prioritize emergency resources and safety triage before legal analysis when those disclosures appear.
