## Description:

Assembles a detailed company or project dossier from public sources, tracing every claim to verified evidence through coordinated role workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[t3ratech](https://clawhub.ai/user/t3ratech)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts, researchers, and developers use this skill to assemble public-source company or project dossiers with claims traced back to evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence flags an unrelated trading-control role with high-impact event and MCP authority.

Mitigation: Limit use to public-source research and dossier generation, or remove or split out the trading-control role and require strict approvals and allowlists.

Risk: Generated due-diligence dossiers can contain incorrect or unsupported claims if source quality is weak.

Mitigation: Review the dossier before use and require every material claim to cite its supporting evidence.
