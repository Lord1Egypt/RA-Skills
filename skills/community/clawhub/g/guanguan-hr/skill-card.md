## Description:

Activates a Chinese-language HR officer persona for recruiting, interviewing, employee management, compensation, performance, organizational design, retention, departures, labor disputes, culture, talent pipeline, and KPI/OKR guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business operators and HR teams use this skill to draft job descriptions and interview questions, structure compensation and performance systems, handle employee-relations scenarios, and reason about organizational design in Chinese-language workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad people-management language and answer in a branded HR-advisor style when the user expected general advice.

Mitigation: Route it to explicit HR, employment, recruiting, compensation, performance, or labor-relations requests.

Risk: HR recommendations may be incomplete or jurisdiction-specific, especially for termination, compensation, non-compete, and labor-dispute scenarios.

Mitigation: Review outputs against applicable employment law and company policy before using them in workplace decisions.

Risk: The skill may produce templates or scripts that affect employees or candidates directly.

Mitigation: Have a qualified HR or legal reviewer approve sensitive communications before sending.
