## Use Case:

VC fund teams, investor relations staff, and internal research users use this skill to draft LP-facing quarterly updates, fund summaries, fundraising materials, and cited knowledge-base answers. It emphasizes traceable numbers, authorized knowledge sources, Markdown delivery, and explicit confirmation before optional external publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: LP reports and fundraising materials can contain sensitive fund, portfolio, or LP information.

Mitigation: Review, classify, and redact confidential content before sharing or publishing outside the current conversation.

Risk: Knowledge-base answers or report figures may be unsupported if source material is missing or incomplete.

Mitigation: Use authorized source material, require citations for knowledge answers, and state when there is no evidence.
