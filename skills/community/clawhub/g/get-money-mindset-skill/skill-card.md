## Description:

Turn an income, freelance, creator, business, or AI-product goal into ethical, evidence-led earning experiments and reusable assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to evaluate earning, freelancing, creator, business, and AI-product ideas as small ethical experiments with clear offers, buyer validation, pricing hypotheses, channel-fit checks, and stop, iterate, or scale thresholds.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat business coaching frameworks as guaranteed income or professional financial, tax, legal, credit, or mental-health advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs educational, avoid income guarantees, state assumptions and uncertainty, and refer users to qualified local professionals for professional advice.

Risk: Users may act on outdated or unsupported platform, marketplace, or local compliance assumptions.

Mitigation: Require verification of current platform rules, local requirements, data-use permissions, and relevant terms before publishing, selling, or accepting payment.
