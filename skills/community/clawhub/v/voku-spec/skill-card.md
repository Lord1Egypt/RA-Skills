Mitigation: Confirm the referenced files are available before relying on the skill for complete Voku parsing, generation, or translation workflows.

Risk: The activation terms are broad and may trigger the skill for general constructed-language or AI-communication discussions.

Mitigation: Use explicit Voku requests or narrow activation rules when broad triggering is not desired.
