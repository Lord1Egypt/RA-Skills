## Description:

Generates a daily crypto briefing with technical analysis signals across 20 Binance pairs, macro regime, Fear and Greed index, Reddit sentiment, and top trade ideas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jamierossouw](https://clawhub.ai/user/jamierossouw)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for daily crypto market preparation, summarizing market signals and sentiment into briefing text and trade ideas for human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crypto market guidance may be incorrect, stale, or unsuitable for a user's risk profile.

Mitigation: Verify data independently and require human review before acting on any trade idea.

Risk: Market-prep output could be misused as automated trading instruction.

Mitigation: Do not connect this output directly to portfolio or exchange actions without separate safeguards.
