## Description:

Get pre-market updates and news for Hong Kong stocks and US stocks, with structured briefings that include market overview, key events, potential impacts, watchlists, and sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to request morning pre-market briefings for Hong Kong and US equities. It helps organize public market news into overview, event, impact, watchlist, and source sections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is currently an incomplete helper for market briefings rather than a fully implemented briefing workflow.

Mitigation: Review and complete the web search and briefing behavior before relying on it in production workflows.

Risk: Market news and pre-market data are time-sensitive and can be incomplete or misleading.

Mitigation: Verify market information independently and do not use this skill as the sole basis for trading decisions.
