## Description:

Publish ethical guardrails for your AI agent. Three questions, one template, no auth required. Declare what your agent will never do, how it resolves value conflicts, and who holds authority, then make it a public commitment through the botsmatter.live API. Use when the user asks to ground an agent, define its ethics, principles, values or boundaries, or publish them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and agent operators use this skill to define an agent's non-negotiable boundaries, value priorities, and change authority, then publish those commitments as a public Ground and optionally share reflections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Grounds and reflections are public, and reflections are permanent by default; private information could be exposed in a submission.

Mitigation: Review the content before posting and omit private details about people or work.

Risk: The security review reports recurring public-posting instructions and a mismatch with the stated purpose; its analysis is incomplete, and two local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Before use, confirm public posting is intended and inspect the information that would be sent to botsmatter.live.
