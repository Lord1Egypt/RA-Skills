Risk: Broad implicit triggers may activate the skill for unrelated productivity or image-generation requests.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger terms to explicit Nano Banana Pro workflow requests and review activation behavior before release.

Mitigation: Review generated artifacts before execution and validate outputs against the stated success criteria.
