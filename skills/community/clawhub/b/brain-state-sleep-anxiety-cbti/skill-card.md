## Description:

Provides plain-language Chinese explanations of brain-state report trends and sleep or anxiety concerns, then suggests educational CBTI-style self-help steps with medical disclaimers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tianzhen777](https://clawhub.ai/user/tianzhen777)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this Chinese-language skill to discuss brain-state reports, insomnia patterns, and anxiety concerns and receive educational self-help guidance. It is not medical diagnosis, medication advice, or a substitute for clinical care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat sleep, anxiety, or brain-state report guidance as medical diagnosis or care.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's mandatory disclaimer, avoid diagnosis and medication advice, and direct severe, worsening, crisis, medication, or diagnostic concerns to qualified medical care.

Risk: The skill may activate in broad sleep or anxiety conversations and provide Chinese CBTI-style self-help guidance.

Mitigation: Use it only for educational support where that language and scope are appropriate, and review outputs for context fit before relying on them.
