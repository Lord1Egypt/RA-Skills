## Description:

Helps agents advise on turning an audience into a genuine community by diagnosing member-to-member interaction, choosing the right space, designing recurring rituals, nurturing superfans, and keeping community-building honest.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External social media and community teams use this skill to shape community strategy, plan member-to-member engagement, and draft community rituals or prompts. It is advisory: the human runs the actual community spaces and reviews any member-facing content before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Advice may be shaped by sensitive or incomplete brand, social strategy, or audience files provided by the user.

Mitigation: Review input files and generated recommendations before use, especially where member privacy, consent, or audience assumptions are involved.

Risk: Community guidance could be misused to fake activity, buy members, run engagement pods, or expose member information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for genuine community-building; get consent before spotlighting members and keep humans responsible for closed spaces and moderation.

Risk: The skill is advisory and does not operate Discord, Slack, groups, inboxes, analytics, or superfan identification systems.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as strategy and draft content; run community spaces and measure results in the native platforms with human oversight.
