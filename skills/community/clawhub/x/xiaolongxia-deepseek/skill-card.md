## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, code, and prior conversation history are saved locally in plaintext and resent to DeepSeek on later requests.

Mitigation: Clear history before discussing sensitive code, credentials, private logs, or regulated data, and install only when that data flow is acceptable.
