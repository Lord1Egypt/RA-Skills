## Description:

Truecaller helps agents organize public Truecaller product information, including features, platform availability, download entry points, permissions, privacy notes, and help content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[codekungfu](https://clawhub.ai/user/codekungfu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content or support teams use this skill to summarize public Truecaller website, download, feature, privacy, and help-center information while staying within publicly available sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be misused for device access, private data extraction, unofficial package discovery, or bypass activity.

Mitigation: Constrain use to public Truecaller information and decline device control, bypass, private-data, or non-public content requests.

Risk: Privacy, permissions, platform support, and download details can be misstated if the agent over-interprets public pages.

Mitigation: Base summaries on official Truecaller pages and clearly distinguish Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS information when available.
