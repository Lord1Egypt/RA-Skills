## Description:

Create a content style guide / voice & tone guide so everyone writes consistently.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content designers, product teams, documentation teams, support teams, and marketing teams use this skill to create practical voice and tone guidance, editorial mechanics, terminology rules, and accessibility guidance for a brand or product.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may infer brand voice, terminology, locale, or audience details from a short brief, which could turn unsupported assumptions into team policy.

Mitigation: Review and confirm inferred voice, terminology, and mechanics before adopting the generated guide.
