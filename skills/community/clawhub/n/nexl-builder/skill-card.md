## Description:

Nexl Brand Site Generator helps agents create interactive brand sites, international homepages, and landing pages through a guided brand-profile workflow, template selection, budget check, and deployment handoff.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, brand teams, founders, creators, and developers use this skill to turn brand positioning into a polished one-page website or landing page, including a reusable brand profile, interaction guidance, and deployment-oriented handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can store brand-profile files that may contain pricing strategy, unreleased product details, compliance boundaries, or private contact data.

Mitigation: Review brand-profile contents before saving or sharing, keep sensitive fields out of generated examples, and avoid entering credentials or confidential launch details.

Risk: Generated pages may include third-party links or external font requests.

Mitigation: Inspect generated HTML before publishing and replace, self-host, or remove external resources that do not meet the user's privacy, security, or licensing requirements.

Risk: The workflow is oriented toward public deployment of generated brand sites.

Mitigation: Require explicit final approval before publishing, validate all brand fields and claims, and confirm the deployment target is appropriate for public release.
