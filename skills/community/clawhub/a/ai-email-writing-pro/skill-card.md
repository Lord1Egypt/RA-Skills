## Description:

Professional email writing assistant with tone adjustment, template library, grammar checking, and response suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[banxian87](https://clawhub.ai/user/banxian87)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business users, developers, and agents use this skill to draft, revise, and evaluate professional emails, including outreach, follow-ups, meeting requests, status updates, apologies, and responses. It helps select tone, apply templates, improve clarity and conciseness, and produce subject-line suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes mostly Chinese-language templates despite an English general-purpose description.

Mitigation: Review generated emails for the intended recipient language, locale, and business context before use.

Risk: Future versions could request email-account, tracking, or sending permissions that are not present in this release.

Mitigation: Do not grant email-account, analytics, tracking, or send-mail permissions unless the integration is explicitly disclosed, user-controlled, and reviewed.
