## Description:

Guides Chinese passport holders through arrival-card, electronic declaration, and visa-preparation steps for common Asian destinations, including document checklists, country-specific instructions, and optional automation examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivy-ting](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivy-ting)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External travelers and travel-support agents use this skill to identify required arrival-card or visa steps, prepare passport and itinerary information, follow official form workflows, and generate user-reviewed guidance for supported Asian destinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may handle passport, booking, and itinerary documents containing sensitive personal data.

Mitigation: Avoid uploading passport or booking images unless necessary, minimize retained data, and review privacy handling before use.

Risk: Arrival-card, visa, and immigration requirements can change and incorrect guidance may affect travel.

Mitigation: Verify every requirement against official government sites before relying on the generated guidance.

Risk: Automation examples could submit official immigration or visa forms before the traveler has checked the declarations.

Mitigation: Keep official submissions under explicit user control and manually review every declaration before submission.
