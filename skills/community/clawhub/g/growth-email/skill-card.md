## Description:

Growth Email helps agents draft and improve SaaS onboarding email sequences using timely welcomes, behavior-triggered messaging, personal voice, single-action goals, and subject-line testing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wellyxy](https://clawhub.ai/user/wellyxy)

## Use Case:

Growth, marketing, and product teams use this skill to create or refine SaaS onboarding email flows for activation and retention. It focuses the agent on concise, behavior-aware emails with one clear user action per message.
