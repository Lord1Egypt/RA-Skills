## Description:

Audit a game, feature, failure loop, combat encounter, reward system, progression wall, or high-variance mechanic for perceived fairness and frustration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, producers, and design reviewers use this skill to audit game systems for perceived fairness, frustration, attribution, randomness, flow mismatch, and retry willingness.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce blunt or subjective design feedback that overstates fairness problems when the input is incomplete.

Mitigation: Anchor conclusions to concrete player complaints, telemetry, playtest observations, or clearly stated assumptions before applying design changes.
