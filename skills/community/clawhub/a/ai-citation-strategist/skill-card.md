## Description:

AI Citation Strategist audits brand visibility across AI recommendation engines and provides content optimization strategies to improve AI citation likelihood.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openlark](https://clawhub.ai/user/openlark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, content, and growth teams use this skill to benchmark brand citation rates across AI answer engines, analyze competitor citation wins, and plan prioritized AEO/GEO content fixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Brand and competitor prompts may expose confidential business strategy, private customer data, or credentials to third-party AI platforms.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive prompt data unless the user intends those third-party services to receive the information.

Risk: AI citation results are non-deterministic and can shift across platforms and model updates.

Mitigation: Treat findings as point-in-time measurements, benchmark before changes, avoid guaranteeing outcomes, and re-run the same prompt set after fixes.
