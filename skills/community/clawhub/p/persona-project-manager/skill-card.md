## Use Case:

Employees and agents use this Google Workspace persona to coordinate project work by preparing status updates, tracking tasks in Sheets, scheduling meetings, sharing Drive artifacts, and sending stakeholder communications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Google Workspace actions can affect recipients, attendees, uploaded files, sharing permissions, and announcements.

Mitigation: Review dry-run previews and confirm recipients, attendees, files, and sharing settings before allowing emails, calendar invites, Drive uploads, or announcements.
