## Description:

Builds a customer journey map for a product, service, or experience with stages, touchpoints, emotions, pain points, and prioritized opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, UX, and customer-experience teams use this skill to create journey maps for a specific customer persona, journey scope, and current or future state. The output supports product discovery, design workshops, prioritization, and stakeholder alignment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer research, analytics, support-ticket themes, or business context may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Provide only information suitable for use in the generated map and avoid unnecessary sensitive or confidential details.

Risk: Journey maps based on limited research can overstate assumptions and mislead prioritization.

Mitigation: Review assumptions, label sections that are hypothetical, and use the research-gaps table to identify follow-up validation work.
