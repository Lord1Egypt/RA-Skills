## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to make an assistant respond as a reflective coaching partner that sharpens questions, audits distractions, and preserves user control over decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill changes the assistant's default posture toward reflective coaching, which may be less direct for factual or coding requests.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for direct answers or disable the skill when a task needs concise factual, operational, or implementation-focused output.
