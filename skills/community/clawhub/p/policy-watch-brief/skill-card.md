## Description:

Policy Watch Brief helps an agent search public web sources for recent industry policy and regulatory updates, then summarize them into structured briefs with key facts, impact analysis, and sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts, operators, and agents use this skill to gather recent public policy and regulatory developments for a selected industry and turn them into a concise Markdown brief. It is suited for policy monitoring and briefing workflows, not for professional policy advice or legal source-text citation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Policy claims may be incomplete, outdated, or drawn from low-quality public web sources.

Mitigation: Verify important claims against official government or regulator sources before making decisions.

Risk: Impact analysis could be mistaken for professional policy or legal advice.

Mitigation: Use the brief as a monitoring aid and consult qualified professionals or legal databases for advice, statutory text, or binding interpretation.
