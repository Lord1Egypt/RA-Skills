## Description:

Remove signs of AI-generated writing from text and rewrite it to sound more natural and human-written.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jazzyong2023](https://clawhub.ai/user/jazzyong2023)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to edit text so it reads less like AI-generated prose while preserving the intended meaning and tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rewrites may alter meaning, voice, neutrality, or authorship expectations in professional, academic, legal, factual, or ghostwritten material.

Mitigation: Review rewritten output against the source text before accepting or publishing it, especially where accuracy or authorial voice matters.

Risk: The skill is designed to make text sound less AI-generated, which may conflict with disclosure or authorship policies in some settings.

Mitigation: Use it only where this kind of editing is permitted and keep any required AI-use or authorship disclosures intact.
