## Description:

Builds a creator sponsorship media kit and brand-deal outreach package, including a one-pager, personalized pitch email, defensible rate card, and negotiation notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, creator managers, and marketing teams use this skill to prepare sponsor-ready media kits, brand outreach emails, rate cards, and negotiation notes from real creator stats and clearly labeled placeholders when details are missing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated rates and outreach can be misleading if the creator stats supplied by the user are inaccurate or incomplete.

Mitigation: Review generated rates and outreach text against the creator's actual platform metrics, audience data, usage rights, exclusivity terms, and brand fit before sending.
