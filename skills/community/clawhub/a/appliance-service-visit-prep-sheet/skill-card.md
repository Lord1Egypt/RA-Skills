## Description:

Prepare a technician-ready appliance service visit sheet with model and serial details, symptom timeline, photo checklist, access notes, questions, and visit-day prep.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this prompt-only skill to prepare a concise appliance repair appointment handoff with appliance identifiers, symptom history, safe evidence checklist, access notes, technician questions, and visit-day follow-up fields.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the prep sheet as appliance diagnosis or repair advice.

Mitigation: Keep the output focused on documentation and technician handoff; defer diagnosis, repairs, and safe-use decisions to the manufacturer, utility provider, landlord, or qualified technician.

Risk: Users may include sensitive access or payment information in the visit notes.

Mitigation: Omit permanent gate codes, alarm codes, payment card numbers, full account numbers, and unnecessary private information; share sensitive access details separately with the service provider when needed.

Risk: Routine visit preparation could be inappropriate during urgent hazards such as gas smell, smoke, sparks, shock risk, active flooding, or carbon monoxide alarms.

Mitigation: Stop routine prep for urgent hazards and direct the user to leave the area if needed and contact emergency services, the utility provider, landlord, building management, or a qualified professional.
