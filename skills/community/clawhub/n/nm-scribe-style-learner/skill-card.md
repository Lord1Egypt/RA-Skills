## Description:

Extracts writing style patterns from exemplar text into a reusable profile

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[athola](https://clawhub.ai/user/athola)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and writers use the skill to analyze representative writing samples and create a reusable style profile for consistent content generation or editing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A profile based on too few or mismatched exemplars may not capture the intended style.

Mitigation: Use at least 1,000 words from samples in the same genre, then validate the profile against new content and review the result.
