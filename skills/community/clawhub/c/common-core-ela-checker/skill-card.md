## Description:

Common Core State Standards ELA alignment checker. Analyzes lesson plans, unit plans, and assessments against CCSS ELA standards for K-12.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gowanginc](https://clawhub.ai/user/gowanginc)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Educators, curriculum designers, and reviewers use this skill to check lesson plans, unit plans, assessments, and curriculum maps against K-12 Common Core ELA standards and identify coverage gaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad lesson-planning or curriculum requests where Common Core ELA alignment is not desired.

Mitigation: Users should state whether they want Common Core ELA alignment help when making broad education-planning requests.

Risk: Standards-alignment suggestions may be incomplete or may not match local curriculum policy.

Mitigation: Educators or curriculum reviewers should compare outputs against the applicable grade-level standards and local requirements before use.
