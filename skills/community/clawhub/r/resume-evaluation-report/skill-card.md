## Description:

Generates structured resume evaluation reports from an HR perspective, including candidate profile, strengths, risks, pass or hold recommendation, and follow-up interview questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kongkong827](https://clawhub.ai/user/kongkong827)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Recruiters, HR teams, and hiring managers use this skill to turn candidate-provided resumes into concise hiring decision support, structured candidate summaries, risk checks, and interview questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume reports may be over-used as an automated hiring decision.

Mitigation: Treat pass or no-pass recommendations as decision support and require human recruiting review before action.

Risk: Candidate strengths or risks may be stated too strongly when resume evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Ground claims in the resume, mark uncertain points for interview verification, and avoid inventing unsupported facts.
