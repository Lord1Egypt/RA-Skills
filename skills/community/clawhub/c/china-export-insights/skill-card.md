## Description:

Provides professional China export data analysis and market intelligence for international traders, including export trends, emerging hot products, market growth analysis, trade opportunities, visualization recommendations, and business context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External traders, sourcing managers, and market researchers use this skill to turn China export data into market intelligence, including trend analysis, hot product discovery, partner shifts, regional comparisons, and sourcing recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Export-market recommendations may be inaccurate if based on stale trade data, preliminary statistics, tariff changes, or regulatory changes.

Mitigation: Verify current official trade data, tariffs, and regulations before acting; treat the output as analytical guidance rather than legal or financial advice.

Risk: Source quality can affect market conclusions when the skill uses web search, industry reports, or international trade databases.

Mitigation: Use current, credible sources, cite source names and dates, and compare important conclusions across multiple data sources.
