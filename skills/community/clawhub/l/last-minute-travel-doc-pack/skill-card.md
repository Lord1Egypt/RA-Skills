## Description:

Organize urgent travel papers into a destination-ready packet and print/download checklist without storing private ID numbers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External travelers use this skill when they are close to departure and need a destination-ready document packet organized by traveler, destination, transit point, and order of use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may paste passport numbers, national ID numbers, visa numbers, ticket numbers, payment card details, or full birth dates while preparing documents.

Mitigation: Ask only for document types and trip context, use labels instead of private identifiers, and remind users not to paste sensitive ID numbers into the chat.

Risk: Entry, transit, carrier, health, or customs requirements may be outdated or incomplete if treated as verified legal or travel advice.

Mitigation: Direct users to verify current rules with official destination, transit, carrier, embassy, consulate, or transport sources before departure, and avoid guaranteeing boarding or entry.

Risk: Missing urgent documents can block boarding or entry under time pressure.

Mitigation: Label missing items by urgency, assign next action, owner, and deadline, and recommend contacting the carrier or official authority for likely blockers.
