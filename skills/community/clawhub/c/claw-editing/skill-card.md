## Description:

Edit OpenClaw videos into publishable, branded cuts with motion graphics, quality checks, and social copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[grahammcbain](https://clawhub.ai/user/grahammcbain)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and video editors use this skill to turn raw or rough OpenClaw release updates, feature shorts, and interviews into branded, publishable videos. It also prepares editable motion graphics, quality checks, and social copy for the finished video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cleanup guidance could delete project data.

Mitigation: Confirm the target before cleanup, limit deletion to a dedicated scratch or output folder, and keep source footage and earlier versions intact.

Risk: Crafted plan values could change FFmpeg filter behavior.

Mitigation: Review and validate plan JSON values before running edit or composite operations.

Risk: The security scan was partial and could not fully interpret a referenced image.

Mitigation: Review the relevant files and referenced image manually before relying on the scan verdict.
