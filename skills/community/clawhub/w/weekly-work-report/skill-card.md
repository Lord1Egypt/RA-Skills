## Description:

Transforms user bullet points into polished, manager-ready weekly reports with structured summaries, completed work, blockers, and next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huajianjiu000](https://clawhub.ai/user/huajianjiu000)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and individual contributors use this skill to turn weekly activity bullets into concise status reports for team updates, manager reviews, and progress tracking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports can include overstated or unsupported numbers, dates, completion percentages, business impact, customer impact, or technical details.

Mitigation: Review the draft before sending and replace any unsupported content with verified facts or placeholders.

Risk: Broad auto-activation may invoke the writing helper when a user mentions weekly reports or status updates.

Mitigation: Install only when passive report-writing assistance is desired and confirm that the generated draft matches the user's intended context.
