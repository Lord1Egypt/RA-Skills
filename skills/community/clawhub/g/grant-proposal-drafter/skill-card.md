## Description:

Grant Proposal Drafter helps nonprofit grant writers, program managers, and development directors turn funder RFPs or NOFOs into structured, compliance-checked proposal drafts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[archlab-space](https://clawhub.ai/user/archlab-space)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Nonprofit grant writers, program managers, and development directors use this skill to convert RFP or NOFO guidance and project context into a proposal draft, eligibility screen, compliance matrix, unresolved-information list, and pre-submission checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive grant, budget, personnel, and organizational information in-session.

Mitigation: Use it only with materials the user is comfortable sharing in the active session, and keep confidential funder or organizational data out of external searches and examples.

Risk: Draft proposal content may be incomplete, inaccurate, or missing required funder evidence before submission.

Mitigation: Review the final proposal carefully, verify citations and statistics, reconcile the budget narrative, and resolve all unresolved-information flags before submission.

Risk: The skill may append a GitHub feedback link when a result is unsatisfactory or outside scope.

Mitigation: Treat the feedback link as optional and avoid sharing confidential grant materials through public issue or pull request channels.
