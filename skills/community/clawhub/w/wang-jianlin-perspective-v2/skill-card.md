## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: First-person roleplay as a real person may lead users to mistake generated responses for that person's actual views.

Mitigation: Preserve the public-information disclaimer on first activation and make it clear that responses are a simulated perspective, not statements from Wang Jianlin.

Risk: Broad business trigger phrases may keep the assistant in role during ordinary business questions.

Mitigation: Review activation behavior before deployment and prefer explicit user intent for sustained persona responses.
