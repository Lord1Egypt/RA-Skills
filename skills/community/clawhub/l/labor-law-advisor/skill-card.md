## Description:

劳动法律问题咨询。当用户遇到劳动纠纷：工伤认定、合同问题、加班费争议、社保缴纳、竞业限制纠纷、裁员赔偿、仲裁流程等问题时触发。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and agents use this skill to analyze China labor disputes, calculate compensation or overtime claims, plan arbitration steps, and draft common labor-law documents. It is intended as legal information support and should not replace case-specific advice from a qualified lawyer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat legal information or generated calculations as final legal advice.

Mitigation: Tell users to verify overtime, local wage standards, limitation periods, and case-specific facts with current official sources or a qualified lawyer.

Risk: Labor-law outcomes can vary by region, evidence quality, and current legal updates.

Mitigation: Ask for missing facts, cite specific provisions, flag regional differences, and recommend checking current official sources for local standards.
