## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, analyses, and implementation-ready next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to decide whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps convert early ideas into practical validation workflows, prototypes, user-facing positioning, and decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be selected automatically for broad product or business-planning requests.

Mitigation: Review whether invocation is appropriate for the user's request before relying on its output.

Risk: Business-planning outputs may be incomplete or mismatched to the user's market, constraints, or success criteria.

Mitigation: Check assumptions, constraints, validation criteria, and next steps before acting on product decisions.
