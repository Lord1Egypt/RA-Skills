## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad keywords and implicit invocation may cause the skill to activate more often than intended.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's outcome, constraints, available inputs, and success criteria before producing detailed workflow guidance.

Risk: Guidance for setup hardening, reliability work, or code-oriented changes can be misapplied if assumptions are unclear.

Mitigation: State assumptions and remaining risks, and have proposed changes reviewed before use in production workflows.
