## Description:

Audits whether an organization's AI spend paid off by measuring realized returns against baselines, hidden costs, confidence levels, and per-tool renewal or consolidation decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Finance, procurement, operations, and AI program leaders use this skill to audit existing AI tools, quantify credible ROI, identify hidden costs, and decide whether to keep, consolidate, renegotiate, cut, or instrument tools for future measurement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The audit may involve sensitive spend, utilization, business outcome, or personnel-related metrics.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum data needed for the audit and handle confidential financial or personnel metrics under normal company data-handling rules.

Risk: Weak evidence can lead to overstated or misleading ROI conclusions.

Mitigation: Require each figure to include its measurement method, confidence level, conversion assumptions, and baseline plan when evidence is insufficient.
