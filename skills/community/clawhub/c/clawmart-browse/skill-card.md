## Description:

Browse and discover AI personas and skills on ClawMart when a user wants to explore, search, or evaluate listings on shopclawmart.com without an account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nateliason](https://clawhub.ai/user/nateliason)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to browse, search, compare, and recommend public ClawMart marketplace listings for AI personas and skills. It helps surface listing details such as price, category, capabilities, creator information, and purchase links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketplace listing details and prices may change after the skill retrieves them.

Mitigation: Verify the current listing page and price before purchasing.

Risk: Purchase flows require a ClawMart account outside the public browsing flow.

Mitigation: Do not provide account credentials to the skill; only sign in directly on ClawMart when intentionally purchasing or managing an account.
