## Description:

Scan Freelancer.com for new projects matching your skills, draft personalized bid proposals, and track bidding history.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[onlyloveher](https://clawhub.ai/user/onlyloveher)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Freelancers and their agents use this skill to find relevant Freelancer.com projects, draft tailored bid proposals, estimate bid prices, and maintain a workspace bid log.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated proposals or bid prices may be inaccurate, misleading, or poorly matched to the project.

Mitigation: Review generated proposals and bid prices before using them.

Risk: The agent may prepare a real bid submission or workspace bid-log update that the user did not intend.

Mitigation: Confirm before any real submission or workspace log update.
