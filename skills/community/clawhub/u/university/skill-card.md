## Use Case:

External learners, students, career changers, exam candidates, and tutors use this skill to create structured curricula, study plans, practice assessments, flashcards, and progress tracking for long-term learning goals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may keep long-term local records of education goals, progress, assessment history, and learning preferences.

Mitigation: Ask for confirmation before creating or archiving files, store only necessary details, and let users review or delete records.

Risk: Calendar connections, voice recordings, and parent or tutor sharing can expose personal study or student information.

Mitigation: Require explicit opt-in before connecting calendars, recording voice, or sharing reports, and confirm recipients and visibility before sharing.

Risk: Generated curricula, exam readiness predictions, and practice assessments may be inaccurate or outdated for high-stakes programs.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as study guidance, verify requirements against official schools or exam bodies, and review plans before relying on them.

Risk: Free-form degree names used for folders could produce confusing or unintended local paths.

Mitigation: Use safe slugged folder names and confirm the target path before creating or moving study records.
