## Description:

Provides Chinese-language AI legal compliance guidance for AI creation, enterprise AI office use, and public AI service scenarios, with professional and plain-language conclusions, checklists, redlines, case references, and document templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Individuals, businesses, service providers, and compliance teams use this skill to evaluate AI-related legal and operational compliance questions under PRC-focused AI regulations. It helps produce structured compliance judgments, risk checks, remediation suggestions, filing guidance, content-labeling guidance, and supporting templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI legal and regulatory information can become outdated or may not fit a user's specific facts.

Mitigation: Verify current official sources before acting and treat the skill output as legal education and checklist support, not formal legal advice.

Risk: Users may rely on checklist output for enforcement exposure, litigation, penalties, or major business decisions.

Mitigation: Consult qualified counsel for high-impact decisions or complex legal matters.
