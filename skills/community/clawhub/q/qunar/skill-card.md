## Description:

Helps users make better Qunar travel-booking decisions by comparing public platform trade-offs for flights, hotels, and other travel bookings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to compare public Qunar booking options, weigh price against flexibility and service trade-offs, and decide what to verify before paying.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expect account-state, live page, or checkout actions that the skill does not perform.

Mitigation: Use it for public decision support only; switch to an appropriate browser workflow for live account pages, order retrieval, coupon claiming, or checkout.
