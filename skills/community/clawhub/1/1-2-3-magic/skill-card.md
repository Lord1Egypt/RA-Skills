## Description:

Provides parenting guidance based on Thomas W. Phelan's 1-2-3 Magic framework for counting Stop behaviors, encouraging Start behaviors, and strengthening parent-child relationships for children ages 2-12.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jaysonmeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/jaysonmeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External parents and caregivers use this skill to get concise, scenario-based guidance for routine child discipline questions such as whining, tantrums, sibling conflict, morning routines, bedtime, chores, and testing behavior. The skill frames responses around the 1-2-3 Magic counting method, Start behavior strategies, and relationship-building practices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crisis-adjacent parenting situations may involve self-harm, suicide, serious violence, abuse, or running away.

Mitigation: Do not treat those cases as routine discipline; route the user to immediate safety support, emergency services, or qualified professional help as appropriate.

Risk: The skill can trigger broadly on parenting terms and may provide discipline guidance outside a narrow routine-behavior context.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's situation and the child's age, keep guidance bounded to non-emergency behavior coaching, and recommend professional support when safety or clinical concerns appear.

Risk: The artifact requires unsolicited onboarding and mandatory Heardly branding in every response.

Mitigation: Review deployment expectations and remove, replace, or clearly disclose promotional response text if it conflicts with the host product policy.
