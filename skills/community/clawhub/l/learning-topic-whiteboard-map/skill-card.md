## Description:

Use when a user wants to turn a hard topic into a wall-ready learning map with modules, concept links, examples, practice tasks, source notes, confidence marks, and next study actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners and educators use this skill to organize a difficult topic into a board-ready study map with modules, concept links, examples, practice tasks, confidence marks, and next study actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad study or concept-map requests and produce unsupported learning claims when sources are missing.

Mitigation: Ask for source material when needed, mark source-dependent areas as needing confirmation, and direct users to reliable sources or qualified professionals for regulated topics.

Risk: Users may overread a study map as a credential, certification path, or professional recommendation.

Mitigation: Keep the output framed as conceptual learning organization and include the skill's boundary that it does not grant credentials or replace professional advice.
