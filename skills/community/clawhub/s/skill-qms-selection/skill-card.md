## Description:

Helps IT and quality-management teams perform structured QMS software selection by clarifying requirements, defining weighted scoring dimensions, comparing candidate systems, and producing recommendation reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, IT leaders, and quality information-system stakeholders use this skill to evaluate QMS software candidates with a transparent scoring matrix, weighted criteria, and recommendation rationale for management review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Report generation may not be fully automated because the referenced build_report.py file is not packaged.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an advisory selection framework and verify or assemble the Markdown and HTML report outputs before relying on them.

Risk: Vendor claims, pricing, compliance certifications, and scores may be incomplete or unverified.

Mitigation: Verify vendor claims, pricing, compliance evidence, and scoring inputs with enterprise records; mark unknown items as pending enterprise completion.
