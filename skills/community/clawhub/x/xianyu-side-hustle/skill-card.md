## Description:

A Chinese-language coaching skill that guides beginners through a low-cost, low-risk Xianyu side-hustle workflow from positioning and product selection to listing, customer service, fulfillment, and review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Chinese-speaking marketplace sellers use this skill for step-by-step coaching on starting and validating a Xianyu side business. It helps the agent keep users focused on the current stage, avoid high-risk beginner mistakes, and produce compliant listing, customer-service, fulfillment, and review guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, for users operating on Xianyu and subject to current Xianyu platform rules.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Xianyu rules, fees, or enforcement practices may change after the bundled reference material was last verified.

Mitigation: Verify current Xianyu rules, prohibited categories, qualification requirements, and fees before following marketplace guidance.

Risk: Buyer chat logs or order context can contain personal information such as names, phone numbers, or addresses.

Mitigation: Redact buyer personal information before sharing chat logs or fulfillment details with an agent.

Risk: Income examples and staged growth paths could be mistaken for guaranteed results.

Mitigation: Treat all income figures as educational examples, validate demand with small tests, and avoid large inventory commitments before evidence supports them.

Risk: Marketplace coaching can lead to compliance issues if users ask for prohibited goods, fake activity, off-platform transactions, or misleading listings.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's compliance checks, refuse prohibited tactics, mark unknown product facts as UNKNOWN, and keep transactions on-platform.
