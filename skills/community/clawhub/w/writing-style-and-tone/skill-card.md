## Description:

Writing Style And Tone helps agents edit drafts for human-facing style and tone by applying the STYLE framework for specificity, register, concision, rhythm, and careful AI-tell review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media teams, writers, brand operators, and agent workflows use this skill to revise AI-assisted or human drafts so they preserve brand voice, adjust tone to the moment, remove generic filler, and leave human-owned specifics for final approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Edited public content could be posted, scheduled, or used in sensitive brand contexts without adequate review.

Mitigation: Require separate explicit user approval of final text before scheduling, publication, crisis response, sales use, or other brand-sensitive use.

Risk: The agent could appear to create authenticity by inventing anecdotes, credentials, feelings, metrics, or staged imperfections.

Mitigation: Keep human-specific material as visible placeholders until the user supplies and approves real details; do not fabricate personal experience, vulnerability, claims, or metrics.

Risk: AI-detector or punctuation-based advice could be overapplied and harm legitimate writing, especially for non-native writers.

Mitigation: Use the tell review as a cluster-based editing pass and prioritize reader trust, substance, disclosure where required, and human judgment over detector scores.
