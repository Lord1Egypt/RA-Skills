## Description:

Build a welcome email or welcome series for new subscribers or customers: a first welcome email, then follow-up emails over the first days, triggered by a signup form or a new list member. Use for welcome emails, onboarding emails and new subscriber journeys.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nitrosend](https://clawhub.ai/user/nitrosend)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams use this skill to plan and draft a welcome email or short onboarding series for new subscribers or customers. It helps select a signup trigger, avoid duplicating an existing flow, and save the series as an inactive draft for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A welcome series could reach the wrong contacts if its trigger or audience is misconfigured.

Mitigation: Review the account, brand, trigger, and saved target; launch or schedule only after the user explicitly approves the audience.
