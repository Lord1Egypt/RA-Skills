## Description:

Upload product documents, FAQs, or historical chat logs to generate a structured customer-service knowledge base with intent recognition, response optimization, sensitive-word checks, and multi-turn dialogue design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[amaxclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/amaxclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Customer-service teams and product managers use this skill to turn product documentation, FAQs, and support transcripts into reviewable knowledge-base entries, intent maps, dialogue flows, and coverage recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded source material may include secrets, regulated data, or unredacted customer chat logs.

Mitigation: Submit only data the user is authorized to process in this environment, and redact sensitive information before use.

Risk: Generated customer-service knowledge bases may contain incorrect, overbroad, or industry-sensitive guidance.

Mitigation: Require human review before publishing, especially for finance, medical, legal, or customer-specific content.
