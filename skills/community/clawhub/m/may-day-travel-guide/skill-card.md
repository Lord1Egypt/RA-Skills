## Description:

A Chinese-language May Day travel planning guide for China domestic trips, covering destination selection, itinerary planning, booking preparation, crowd avoidance, and practical travel cautions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to plan China domestic May Day trips by gathering traveler constraints, recommending suitable destination styles, and producing practical itineraries with booking and crowd-avoidance guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill targets Chinese-speaking users planning China domestic May Day trips, and broad activation metadata may make it appear for unrelated May Day travel questions.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's destination geography and language needs before applying the guidance.

Risk: Travel prices, opening hours, ticket policies, weather, and crowd levels can change after the static guidance was authored.

Mitigation: Verify current transport, lodging, attraction, weather, and booking details with authoritative live sources before finalizing a trip.

Risk: Destination advice includes consumer-safety and scam-avoidance guidance that may not cover all local conditions.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as planning support and defer to official venue, transport, emergency, and local authority guidance during travel.
