## Description:

Podcast Monetization Coach helps podcasters plan sponsorships, CPM pricing, media kits, sponsor outreach, audience growth, premium feeds, affiliate programs, events, course or coaching offers, branded podcast services, and exit strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External podcasters and podcast operators use this skill to diagnose their show stage, choose realistic monetization channels, price sponsor inventory, and build a 90-day growth and revenue plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Monetization guidance may be mistaken for legal or financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as business coaching and verify ad-law obligations, platform fees, and contract terms before acting.

Risk: Outdated CPM bands, platform fees, or network terms could lead to poor pricing decisions.

Mitigation: Check current platform policies, rates, and sponsor terms before finalizing offers.

Risk: Requests for undisclosed sponsorships, inflated listener claims, bot-farmed downloads, or pyramid affiliate schemes could encourage unethical conduct.

Mitigation: Refuse those requests and redirect toward transparent, lawful monetization tactics.
