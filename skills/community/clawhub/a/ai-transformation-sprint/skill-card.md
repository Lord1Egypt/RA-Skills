## Use Case:

Engineering leaders, CTOs, founders, and developers use this skill to audit development workflows, identify AI integration opportunities, select tools, and plan team training for AI-assisted engineering practices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Productivity claims and tool recommendations may not match a team's actual workflow, budget, or policy constraints.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against a workflow audit, local baselines, approved tools, and measured rollout metrics before scaling.

Risk: AI-assisted code review, testing, documentation, or custom-agent plans can introduce incorrect guidance if adopted without review.

Mitigation: Keep human review for AI output, track quality metrics, and use pilot rollouts before full-team adoption.
