## Description:

协助训练营教员依据官方评分表逐模块点评学员作业、记录得分，并整理教练定向书所需内容。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Training camp teachers use this skill to review learner homework and graduation submissions against the provided rubrics, confirm scores module by module, and produce editable scoring records. It can also help collect and structure teacher-provided content for a coaching direction deck without inventing missing details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student submissions may contain learner names, class or group details, homework text, and grading comments.

Mitigation: Review submissions for privacy before use and avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Risk: Feedback or scoring can become unsupported if it goes beyond the submitted homework and bundled rubrics.

Mitigation: Require evidence-backed comments and teacher confirmation for each module before recording final scores.
