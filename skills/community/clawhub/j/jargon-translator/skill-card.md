## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and writers use this skill to translate Chinese internet and workplace jargon into plain language, or to rephrase plain language into polished workplace wording for reports, PPT titles, OKRs, interviews, and internal communication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger examples may activate the skill during casual translation requests where the user did not intend workplace jargon rewriting.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired direction and tone when the request is ambiguous, especially whether the user wants plain-language translation or workplace-style packaging.

Risk: Workplace jargon rewrites can obscure meaning or make communication less direct when used with audiences outside the intended context.

Mitigation: Prefer plain-language output for external, cross-industry, or non-specialist audiences unless the user explicitly asks for jargon-heavy phrasing.
