## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Speculative cross-domain composition can produce incorrect or misleading guidance if used for high-stakes decisions.

Mitigation: Use the skill for low-risk exploratory brainstorming; for medical, legal, financial, security, or other high-stakes decisions, rely on domain-standard guidance or qualified experts.
