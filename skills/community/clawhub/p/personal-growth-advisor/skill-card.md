## Use Case:

External users use this skill to receive structured self-improvement guidance, including SMART goal plans, habit-building strategies, procrastination support, review templates, motivation prompts, and time-management suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general self-improvement suggestions as professional mental-health, medical, legal, financial, or guaranteed career guidance.

Mitigation: Keep the skill positioned as general personal-growth support, preserve its stated boundaries, and redirect users to qualified professionals for serious mental-health issues, addiction, crises, medical, legal, financial, or guaranteed career decisions.

Risk: The skill's examples and templates are mostly Chinese-language, which may limit suitability for users expecting another language.

Mitigation: Set language expectations before use and review generated plans for clarity, cultural fit, and user-specific applicability.

Risk: Goal, habit, and time-management plans can be overconfident or poorly matched to a user's circumstances.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning drafts, ask clarifying questions when context is thin, and encourage users to adapt milestones, workload, and review cadence to their constraints.
