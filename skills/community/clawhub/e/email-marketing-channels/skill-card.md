## Description:

Guides email marketing, newsletter strategy, campaign planning, and deliverability configuration for AI/SaaS products.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to plan email marketing, EDM, newsletters, onboarding campaigns, and deliverability work. It helps structure content mix, cadence, SPF/DKIM/DMARC considerations, article delivery, and KPI tracking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on incidental mentions of email marketing or deliverability terms.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants email marketing or deliverability guidance before applying the skill's recommendations.

Risk: Bulk sender and unsubscribe guidance may need verification against the user's current email platform, jurisdiction, and provider requirements.

Mitigation: Review provider-specific requirements and applicable email compliance rules before using the guidance for a live campaign.
