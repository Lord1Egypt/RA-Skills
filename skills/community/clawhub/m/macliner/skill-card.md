## Description:

Measures, cleans, updates, and checks a Mac, showing a plan, asking before discretionary deletions, and verifying the results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Mac owners can use a terminal-capable assistant to review cleanup plans, remove selected leftovers, check updates and security settings, and verify the results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan reports risky automatic and privileged deletions, unsafe command handling, and incomplete analysis; the verdict does not establish malicious intent.

Mitigation: Avoid installing until the reported deletion and privileged-execution risks are independently reviewed and addressed; review the proposed plan before cleanup.
