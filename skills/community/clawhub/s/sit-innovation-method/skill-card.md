## Description:

Guides Chinese-language SIT brainstorming with five structured innovation strategies: subtraction, division, multiplication, task unification, and attribute dependency.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and facilitators use this skill to turn product, service, marketing, business model, operations, or organizational challenges into structured SIT innovation proposals and comparison tables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Brainstorming templates can produce inaccurate, unrealistic, or misleading innovation recommendations if the user's system description is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review generated proposals against domain knowledge, user research, feasibility constraints, and business requirements before acting on them.

Risk: Attribute dependency exercises may involve demographic or health-related user attributes.

Mitigation: Use sensitive attributes only when necessary, appropriate, and compliant with the project's privacy, fairness, and legal requirements.
