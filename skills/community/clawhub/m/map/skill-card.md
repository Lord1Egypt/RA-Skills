## Description:

Turn complex business goals into navigable strategic maps that identify milestones, resource nodes, risk points, bottlenecks, and route logic so users can see the terrain before they move.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders, operators, strategists, and teams planning complex initiatives use Map to turn ambiguous business goals into route logic with milestones, dependencies, bottlenecks, risks, and next steps before execution begins.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Strategic maps can be mistaken for execution plans or domain expert advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs as planning guidance and obtain legal, financial, operational, or domain-specific review before committing resources.

Risk: Broad business-planning prompts may select this skill when the goal or starting point is underspecified.

Mitigation: Clarify the destination, current position, constraints, resources, risk tolerance, and success criteria before producing a detailed map.

Risk: Route recommendations may rely on incomplete assumptions about resources, dependencies, timing, or market conditions.

Mitigation: Validate critical dependencies, risk zones, and first moves against current business evidence before execution.
