## Description:

Generates copy-paste-ready Chinese PPT structure, slide copy, speaker notes, and visual guidance for government digitalization presentations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[boboy-j](https://clawhub.ai/user/boboy-j)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales consultants, solution architects, and project managers use this skill to draft Chinese government digitalization PPTs for product or solution pitches, planning and construction proposals, and project status reports. It structures the narrative, writes slide-ready titles, bullets, data statements, speaker notes, and visual recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide sensitive project, policy, company, or planning materials while requesting slide drafts.

Mitigation: Provide only the materials needed for the slides and avoid unnecessary confidential or personal data.

Risk: Inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated source materials can lead to misleading presentation claims.

Mitigation: Review policy citations, figures, project claims, and customer examples before presenting or distributing the output.

Risk: The default style is formal Chinese government-sector prose and may not fit other languages, audiences, or tones.

Mitigation: Specify the desired language, tone, audience, and presentation context when invoking the skill.
