## Description:

Suanming provides entertainment-style Liuyao and Bagua fortune-telling analysis from a user's birth date and optional birth time.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for entertainment-oriented Chinese fortune-telling prompts that generate Bagua-based personality and fortune readings from birth details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks users to provide birth details in chat.

Mitigation: Use only when users are comfortable sharing those details, and avoid treating the chat as a private record system.

Risk: The generated fortune-telling output may be mistaken for authoritative life, health, financial, or relationship advice.

Mitigation: Keep the entertainment disclaimer in every response and present readings as non-authoritative.

Risk: The implementation is simplified and template-based rather than a verified Liuyao or Bazi charting system.

Mitigation: Do not rely on outputs for consequential decisions; use them only for light entertainment.
