## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Copywriting workflows may involve customer records, support tickets, call transcripts, or platform content that contains sensitive or regulated data.

Mitigation: Use only authorized inputs and redact private, regulated, or confidential details before invoking the skill.

Risk: Persuasive copy can overstate proof, urgency, guarantees, or outcomes if source claims are not checked.

Mitigation: Review generated copy against approved claims, available evidence, brand standards, and applicable marketing rules before publishing.

Risk: Broad natural-language commands may cause an auto-routing agent to invoke the skill when persuasion guidance is not intended.

Mitigation: Require explicit invocation or route only clearly marketing-focused writing and review requests to this skill.
