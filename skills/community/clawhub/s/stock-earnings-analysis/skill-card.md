## Description:

Organizes U.S. stock earnings analysis by fiscal quarter, combining reported results, guidance, call summaries, filing changes, market context, and price reactions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thesentitrader](https://clawhub.ai/user/thesentitrader)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Financial researchers and people following U.S. stocks use this skill to review quarterly earnings, compare management guidance and filing changes, and prepare earnings previews using cited data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review is incomplete because some local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the referenced material before relying on the skill's analysis.

Risk: API content can include AI-generated summaries of public company disclosures.

Mitigation: Treat summaries as data, check cited disclosures where needed, and preserve the stated coverage and limitations.

Risk: Earnings analysis may be mistaken for investment advice.

Mitigation: Present it for research and education only, with the source disclaimer, and do not frame it as a buy or sell recommendation.
