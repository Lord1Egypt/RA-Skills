## Description:

Seo Listing helps cross-border ecommerce sellers turn basic product claims or Chinese-style English into localized, SEO-friendly listing copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zzhimin](https://clawhub.ai/user/zzhimin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers and marketers use this skill to generate SEO titles, product bullet points, long descriptions, and A/B ad copy for marketplace or storefront listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated listing copy may invent or overstate product details, certifications, guarantees, or SEO claims when source input is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review all claims, keywords, certifications, guarantees, and marketplace policy requirements before publishing the listing.
