## Description:

Helps technical commercialization consultants plan, write, optimize, format, publish, and review WeChat public-account content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, consultants, and operators use this skill to run a WeChat public-account workflow for technology commercialization topics, from topic planning and article drafting through SEO, formatting, publication support, and analytics review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags guidance for AI-detection evasion and weak disclosure of native advertising.

Mitigation: Do not use evasion guidance; clearly label sponsored or affiliate content and review posts against platform and legal requirements before publishing.

Risk: Generated HTML previews from untrusted Markdown may be unsafe to open directly.

Mitigation: Preview only trusted Markdown or HTML, inspect generated output before opening it, and use an isolated browser context for review.

Risk: Helper scripts may read or write files based on user-provided paths.

Mitigation: Check input and output paths before running helper scripts, and work on copies of important files.
