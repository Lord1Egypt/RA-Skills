## Description:

Break a goal into an ordered, checkpointed task plan an agent can execute without supervision drift.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[manteclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/manteclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and people planning multi-step or delegated work can use this skill to turn a goal into a concise, ordered plan with constraints, dependencies, checkpoints, approval gates, and stop conditions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Planning guidance may activate for broadly defined tasks without an explicit planning request.

Mitigation: Before applying it to delegated or longer work, decide whether a written plan is wanted and whether the skill's planning steps fit the task.
