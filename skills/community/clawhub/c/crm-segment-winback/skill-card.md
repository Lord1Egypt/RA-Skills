## Description:

Define CRM segments (new, active, lapsed, VIP, at-risk, churned), build winback triggers, and generate personalized re-engagement campaign ideas across email, SMS, WeChat, and app push for CRM managers, operators, or founders who need a segment health brief, winback strategy, or lifecycle campaign calendar without live CRM API, marketing automation, or analytics platform access.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

CRM managers, ecommerce operators, and founders use this skill to turn rough lifecycle notes into a practical CRM segment brief, winback trigger plan, channel matrix, campaign sequence ideas, and KPI framework.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat advisory campaign ideas as live CRM automation or guaranteed performance guidance.

Mitigation: Use the output as planning guidance only, and review campaign strategy before launch.

Risk: Prompts may include unnecessary personal customer data.

Mitigation: Provide aggregate segment notes and omit personal or sensitive customer records.

Risk: Discounts, message frequency, and consent assumptions may not match the user's business or legal requirements.

Mitigation: Have a human approve offers, cadence, and opt-in or consent rules before using generated campaign ideas.
