## Description:

Business Automation Strategy helps agents audit, prioritize, design, test, monitor, govern, and scale business automation workflows across no-code, low-code, custom-code, and AI-enabled platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business operators, automation teams, consultants, and developers use this skill to identify high-value automation opportunities, compare implementation platforms, and produce workflow blueprints with reliability, testing, monitoring, and governance practices.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automation recommendations may involve money, customer communications, legal or compliance work, HR, security, credentials, or production data.

Mitigation: Keep explicit human approval, sandbox testing, rollback plans, logging, and least-privilege access in place for those workflows.
