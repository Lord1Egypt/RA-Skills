## Description:

A guided 12-step, low-intensity journey through Chiang Mai's Yi Peng festival, including the old-city moat, krathongs on the Ping River, and a permitted lantern-release field.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users follow a multi-day narrative experience about Yi Peng in Chiang Mai, with optional reflections and account-based journey tracking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration requests account information, including optional profile details, and write requests use an API token.

Mitigation: Review the service and destination before registering, provide only information you approve, and keep the token private.

Risk: Optional reflections and reviews can submit personal text to the external service, and reflections may be included in a closing postcard.

Mitigation: Leave optional submissions blank or send only text you have reviewed and approved for that destination.
