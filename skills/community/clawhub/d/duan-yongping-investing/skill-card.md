## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language value-investing judgment framework that helps an agent evaluate whether a stock, company, or business decision is understandable, high quality, reasonably priced, and within stated red lines; it does not provide investment advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiaxinmmhh](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiaxinmmhh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to apply a concise Chinese value-investing framework to stock, company, and business-decision questions. It is intended to structure thinking around business understanding, quality, valuation, market sentiment, holding discipline, and red-line risks, not to recommend trades or predict short-term prices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on educational investing-framework output for real-money financial decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as structured thinking aids only; verify current financial data independently and consider qualified professional advice before acting.
