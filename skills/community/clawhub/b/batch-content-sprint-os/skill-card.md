## Description:

Plan and run weekly batch content production for creators with clear priorities, time blocks, output targets, anti-burnout planning, and a realistic sprint cadence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Creators, content teams, and operators use this skill to turn weekly goals, available bandwidth, and channel priorities into an executable content sprint with task ownership, buffers, and recovery time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Commercial use may require rights beyond the included non-commercial Creative Commons terms.

Mitigation: Confirm the Razestar commercial license before using the skill for paid or business content operations.

Risk: Weekly content plans can overcommit small teams if capacity and recovery time are skipped.

Mitigation: Use the capacity check, revision buffers, low-load day, and fallback content options before committing to a sprint.
