## Description:

Draft a firm, professional demand letter that states the facts, the legal or contractual basis, the specific demand, and a deadline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and business operators use this skill to draft ready-to-review demand letters for payment disputes, contract breaches, cease-and-desist requests, refunds, return of property, or other pre-action resolution requests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A drafted demand letter may contain incorrect facts, amounts, dates, deadlines, or legal basis if the user's brief is incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify all factual details, figures, contractual clauses, notice periods, and delivery requirements before sending.

Risk: Demand letters can create legal or reputational exposure if they include improper threats, defamatory statements, or jurisdiction-specific claims.

Mitigation: Have qualified counsel review the draft before sending, especially where litigation, statutory remedies, or cease-and-desist demands are involved.
