## Description:

Generate professional resignation letters from templates. Use when preparing to resign.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xueyetianya](https://clawhub.ai/user/xueyetianya)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees use this skill to draft resignation letters, offboarding checklists, handover documents, farewell messages, and resignation timing notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated resignation, benefits, timing, or legal-process guidance may be inaccurate for a user's employer, jurisdiction, or contract.

Mitigation: Review and adapt generated text before use, especially legal, benefits, timing, and notice-period sections.

Risk: Handover templates may lead users to include account or access details in generated documents.

Mitigation: Document systems, owners, and transfer steps without including passwords, secrets, or unnecessary sensitive information.
