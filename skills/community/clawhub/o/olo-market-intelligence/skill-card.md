## Description:

Competitive landscape and market intelligence for M&A due diligence - TAM/SAM/SOM, competitor mapping, and industry analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aniebyl](https://clawhub.ai/user/aniebyl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External analysts, deal teams, and business users use this skill to structure market sizing, competitive landscape analysis, industry dynamics review, customer and channel analysis, and recent M&A benchmarking for acquisition diligence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market sizing, competitive intelligence, and financial estimates may be incomplete, stale, or misleading if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Independently verify financial estimates, date-stamp competitive intelligence, cite sources, and cross-reference market size claims before using outputs for diligence decisions.

Risk: The skill may activate for broad market research or industry analysis prompts.

Mitigation: Review whether the user intends to perform diligence work before including sensitive deal context or confidential company data.
