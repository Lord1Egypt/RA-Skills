## Description:

AI long-form novel writing assistant based on the Biling seven-stage creation workflow, covering novel setup, story architecture, chapter blueprinting, drafting, automated review, and knowledge-base management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External writers and creative-writing agents use this skill to structure long-form Chinese novel projects, generate outlines and character/world details, draft chapters, review manuscripts, and maintain continuity notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may strongly steer broad creative-writing requests into its seven-stage web-novel workflow.

Mitigation: Review the trigger wording and category labels before installation if a lighter or more neutral writing assistant is desired.

Risk: The skill may suggest saving novel project files in the user workspace.

Mitigation: Confirm desired file locations with the user before creating or updating project files.
