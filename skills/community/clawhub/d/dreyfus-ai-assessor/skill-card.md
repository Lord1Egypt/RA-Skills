使用德雷福斯五阶段模型定位某项具体技能，并叠加 AI 替代风险，区分规则执行、独立判断、流程编排和专家洞察，生成基于真实项目的升维路径。

## Use Case:

External ClawHub users use this skill to assess one clearly defined ability against Dreyfus stages, identify AI-era replacement risk, and plan evidence-based 30/60/90 day development steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include sensitive employer, client, project, or personal details while describing recent work.

Mitigation: Ask for only the details needed for assessment and avoid unnecessary sensitive identifiers or confidential facts.

Risk: Users may treat the result as a validated psychological or hiring assessment.

Mitigation: Frame the output as coaching guidance based on supplied project evidence, not as a validated psychometric test or employment decision.

Risk: AI replacement risk guidance may be misleading if the user provides vague or unverified project evidence.

Mitigation: Tie stage and risk conclusions to observable project outcomes, decision records, review evidence, and clearly stated missing evidence.
