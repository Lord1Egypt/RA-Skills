## Description:

Generates compliance-gated marketing templates for asphalt sealing and driveway maintenance contractors, with Nevada and Clark County prompts for service pages, SEO content, ads, follow-up emails, referrals, and SMS.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External home-services marketers, contractors, and agency operators use this skill to draft Las Vegas and Clark County asphalt sealing marketing assets while prompting for licensing, HOA, storm-water, warranty, SMS, and advertising compliance checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States, with specific guidance for Nevada and Clark County / Las Vegas

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing copy may be treated as compliance assistance even though local contractor licensing, environmental, SMS consent, ad-platform, and legal requirements can vary by business and jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Confirm generated claims, disclosures, permits, consent language, and license numbers with qualified local review before publishing or sending customer communications.

Risk: The artifact is Nevada and Clark County-oriented, so users outside that market may receive copy that is over-specific or incomplete for their location.

Mitigation: Use the prompts only after replacing bracketed business and location details, and adapt regulatory references to the user's actual service area.
