## Description:

Helps define ICP, draft cold emails and DMs, and plan outreach sequences for startup sales.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[c0ldsmi1e](https://clawhub.ai/user/c0ldsmi1e)

## Use Case:

Startup founders and go-to-market operators use this skill to answer a discovery questionnaire and receive a tailored cold outreach plan, including ICP definition, prospecting strategy, email and DM templates, outreach operations, and a one-week launch checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated cold outreach content may be used in ways that violate anti-spam laws, platform rules, privacy expectations, or truthful marketing practices.

Mitigation: Review and adapt generated emails or DMs for applicable law, platform policies, consent and privacy obligations, and accurate claims before sending.
