## Description:

Creates a simple 7-day meal rhythm card for people who want steadier eating without detailed dieting, calorie tracking, or rigid menus.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to build a practical weekly meal routine with simple anchors, fallback meals, a short prep list, and an end-of-week review point. It supports general routine planning only and avoids calorie tracking, weight-loss coaching, macros, medical diets, or rigid menus.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general meal-routine output as medical nutrition advice or as suitable for medically restricted diets.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as general routine-planning support and direct users with allergies, eating disorders, pregnancy needs, diabetes, kidney disease, heart disease, or other medically restricted diets to a qualified professional.
