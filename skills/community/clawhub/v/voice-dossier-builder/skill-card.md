## Description:

Builds a versioned founder or executive voice dossier from the user's own posts, emails, and decks, including platform registers, banned phrases, disclosure lines, own-post examples, and estimated content pillars.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, social, and founder-led content teams use this skill to codify how a brand or executive actually sounds before drafting public content. It helps agents extract voice traits from the user's own material, produce a reusable voice record, and hand off platform-specific register, disclosure, and pillar guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can ask agents to store derived voice and claim records from private business material for future sessions.

Mitigation: Use it only with explicit permission to save; review the exact destinations and content before saving, especially when source emails, decks, or posts contain confidential business information.
