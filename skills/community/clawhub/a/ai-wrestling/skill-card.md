## Description: <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External learners, wrestling beginners, combat-sport fans, and MMA-oriented users can ask for background explanations of Olympic wrestling styles, common techniques, scoring, equipment, conditioning, and comparisons with judo or Brazilian jiu-jitsu. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Beginner-facing explanations of throws, takedowns, live wrestling, and neck-bridge work could encourage unsafe self-training. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as background learning only; practice physical techniques only with qualified coaching, proper mats, trained partners, and appropriate medical caution. <br>
