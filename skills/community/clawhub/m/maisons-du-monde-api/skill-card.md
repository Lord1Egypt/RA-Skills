## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to retrieve current Maisons du Monde prices, promotions, product details, reviews, marketplace listings, and store availability across supported markets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Europe (France, Belgium, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Germany, Spain, Italy, Portugal, and Switzerland)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Store lookups may send the supplied postcode to Scavio.

Mitigation: Provide only the postcode needed for the store lookup.

Risk: The security evidence reports incomplete reference checks because some local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review linked references and confirm their destinations before relying on them.
