## Description:

Find the questions people ask about a market, how ChatGPT answers them, and which sites get cited in the answers. Use when the user asks what people ask AI about their niche, wants prompt ideas, or wants to know where their brand is missing before choosing prompts to track. Research only; it never saves tracking or starts answer collection.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SEO and marketing teams use this skill to research questions and cited sources related to their market, identify where their brand appears or is missing, and choose neutral prompts for possible tracking. It produces research findings only and does not save tracking or start answer collection.
