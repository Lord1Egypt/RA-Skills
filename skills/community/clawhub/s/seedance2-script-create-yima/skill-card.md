## Description:

Generates Chinese-language Seedance 2.0 advertising video scripts and prompts with modular industry templates and advertising compliance checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huowe](https://clawhub.ai/user/huowe)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to turn product or campaign requirements into Seedance 2.0 video prompts, image prompts, storyboard scripts, and compliance notes for commercial advertising workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated advertising scripts and prompts may be mistaken for legal clearance.

Mitigation: Review all generated advertising content against applicable Chinese advertising law, platform rules, and campaign requirements before publication.

Risk: Optional utility documentation includes pip install commands and script examples that are not part of automatic skill execution.

Mitigation: Run utility examples only after verifying the referenced scripts exist and installing pinned dependencies in an isolated environment.
