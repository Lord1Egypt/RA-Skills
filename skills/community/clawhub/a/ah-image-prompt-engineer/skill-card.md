## Description:

Image Prompt Engineer helps agents craft precise prompts for AI image generators such as Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, and Flux.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mtsatryan](https://clawhub.ai/user/mtsatryan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, designers, and content teams use this skill to structure image-generation prompts with subject details, style, lighting, composition, platform-specific parameters, negative prompts, and iteration guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompts can include style references, negative-prompt terms, or content constraints that may be unsuitable for a user's content, brand, or platform policy.

Mitigation: Review prompts against applicable content, brand, and platform policies before generation or publication.

Risk: Platform-specific image-generator parameters may change over time.

Mitigation: Confirm parameter names and supported values against the target generator before relying on them in production workflows.
