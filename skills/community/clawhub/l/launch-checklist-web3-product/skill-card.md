## Description:

A launch-day checklist for a Web3 product or token-adjacent release: contracts, keys, comms, monitoring, and the rollback plan. Use in the week before any mainnet launch.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Web3 product teams use this checklist during the week before a mainnet launch, token release, or opening a product to public funds to coordinate launch facts, key custody, monitoring, communications, and pause planning.
