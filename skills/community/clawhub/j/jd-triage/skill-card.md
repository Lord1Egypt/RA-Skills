## Description:

Evaluates job postings against a job seeker's preferences and qualifications, then recommends a concrete next action.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bwancoding](https://clawhub.ai/user/bwancoding)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers use this skill to weigh a posting's desirability against their candidacy, decide whether to apply, and review patterns across past evaluations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local career criteria and evaluation history may reveal sensitive job-search details.

Mitigation: Keep the workspace private, avoid retaining full confidential recruiter postings, and review or delete the local Markdown files when no longer needed.
