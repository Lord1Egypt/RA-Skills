## Description:

去除中文文本中的 AI 味、官话味、模板味，让文字更自然、具体、更像真人表达。适用于博客、公众号、邮件、演讲稿、产品文案等中文文本润色。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[antony232](https://clawhub.ai/user/antony232)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and writers use this skill to make Chinese drafts sound more natural, specific, and human-written while reducing boilerplate, official tone, and business jargon. It is suited for blogs, public-account posts, emails, speeches, product copy, project descriptions, self-introductions, and AI-generated Chinese drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive legal, academic, medical, financial, or compliance writing could lose needed formality or precision if rewritten too aggressively.

Mitigation: Use light edits for sensitive material and review the rewritten text against the original before relying on it.

Risk: The skill may flag unsupported claims, missing sources, or unverifiable citations but cannot supply verified evidence by itself.

Mitigation: Check any source, data, DOI, ISBN, or factual-change suggestion before publishing or submitting the text.
