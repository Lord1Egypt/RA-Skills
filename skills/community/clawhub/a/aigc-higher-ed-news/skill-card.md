## Use Case:

External users and education-market analysts use this skill to generate a daily Chinese digest of higher-education AIGC news, AI micro-major updates, relevant procurement needs, and university AI policy signals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Remote web pages and search results may contain inaccurate, outdated, promotional, or misleading claims.

Mitigation: Review generated links and summaries before relying on or redistributing the digest.

Risk: The skill may save digest files locally and sync reports to a knowledge base when permission is granted.

Mitigation: Grant storage or knowledge-base sync permissions only in workspaces where those generated reports are expected.

Risk: The digest focuses on recent Chinese web sources, so coverage can be incomplete when sources are unavailable or a category has no timely results.

Mitigation: Treat empty or sparse sections as search results for that run, and verify high-impact procurement or policy items against their original source.
