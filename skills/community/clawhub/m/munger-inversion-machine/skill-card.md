## Description:

Stress-test ideas, businesses, investments, startups, products, strategies, plans, careers, offers, and decisions by inverting them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to pressure-test decisions before committing time, money, reputation, or effort. It identifies failure modes, bad incentives, second-order effects, kill criteria, and the smallest high-signal test.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat skeptical decision analysis as professional financial or business advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as a reasoning aid; for investment or acquisition analysis, include the educational-only disclaimer and avoid buy, sell, valuation, or price-target instructions.

Risk: Blunt downside analysis may be mistaken for certainty or may overstate negative conclusions.

Mitigation: Separate fatal and manageable risks, name the assumptions behind the analysis, and recommend the smallest high-signal test before larger commitments.
