## Description:

Use this skill when creating, reviewing, or extending interfaces inspired by Evangelion monitor, tactical screen, and control-room aesthetics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sullivangu](https://clawhub.ai/user/sullivangu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, designers, and agent builders use this skill to create, review, or extend EVA-inspired monitoring interfaces and UI systems with dense instrumentation, hard-edged framing, red-orange status colors, and tactical control-room patterns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill strongly steers outputs toward red-orange instrument panels, which may not fit every product brand.

Mitigation: Review generated or revised interfaces for brand fit before use.

Risk: Japanese title typography references may introduce localization or cultural-fit concerns.

Mitigation: Review typography choices for the target locale and replace title fonts when needed.
