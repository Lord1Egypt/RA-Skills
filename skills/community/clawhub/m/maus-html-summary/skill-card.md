## Description:

Turn articles, transcripts, long posts, and raw text into a self-contained illustrated HTML explainer with simple language, memorable structure, source-grounded extraction, and explanatory SVG metaphors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nefas11](https://clawhub.ai/user/nefas11)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to turn an article, transcript, long post, or raw text into a self-contained HTML explainer with source-grounded claims, adapted inline SVG metaphors, and an explicit audit of omitted material.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive or proprietary source material may appear in the generated HTML summary.

Mitigation: Only provide source text that is approved for summarization and review the generated HTML before sharing it.

Risk: Ambiguous or incomplete source text may lead to misleading summary emphasis.

Mitigation: Review the output against the source text, especially the main ideas, omitted-material box, and final takeaways.
