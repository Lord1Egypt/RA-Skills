## Description:

Structures AI and ML product decisions by producing a product canvas covering problem definition, model approach, data requirements, evaluation framework, UX design, responsible AI checks, and launch monitoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product managers, AI/ML leads, and product teams use this skill to evaluate AI-powered features and create a structured canvas before build or launch.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste sensitive training data, private customer information, or confidential launch details into the planning canvas.

Mitigation: Share only information intended for the agent to process, and redact private or confidential details unless they are required for the planning task.

Risk: Canvas recommendations may be incomplete if required product, data, or ownership inputs are missing.

Mitigation: Collect the required feature description, user problem, available data, and ML or AI lead before using the canvas for launch decisions.
