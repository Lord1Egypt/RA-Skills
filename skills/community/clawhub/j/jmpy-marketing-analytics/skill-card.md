## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses a JMPY API key and may access link, QR, campaign, domain, and traffic analytics data.

Mitigation: Install only for trusted JMPY accounts, store JMPY_API_KEY as a credential, and avoid exposing the key in prompts, logs, or shared output.

Risk: Delete, alias, domain, and destination-change requests can affect existing public links or QR campaigns.

Mitigation: Review the target link, QR code, campaign, domain, and destination before confirming destructive or URL-changing actions.
