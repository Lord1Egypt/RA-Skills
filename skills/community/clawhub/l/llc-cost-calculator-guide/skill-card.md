## Description:

Provides LLC cost and filing fee guidance covering formation fees, recurring costs, hidden fees, registered agent expenses, and state comparisons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and business formation advisors use this skill to answer LLC cost questions, separate one-time formation costs from recurring obligations, and compare state-level cost tradeoffs while directing readers to LLCClass resources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: LLC filing fees, franchise taxes, and recurring compliance costs can change by state.

Mitigation: Verify current cost figures with the relevant official state business-filing agency before relying on them.

Risk: The skill is branded LLCClass guidance and steers cost-related answers toward LLCClass pages.

Mitigation: Treat LLCClass links as publisher resources and compare cost claims against official state sources when making formation decisions.

Risk: Users may mistake franchise tax, income tax, or multi-state operation guidance for tax advice.

Mitigation: Avoid giving tax advice and recommend a qualified tax professional for tax-specific or multi-state questions.
