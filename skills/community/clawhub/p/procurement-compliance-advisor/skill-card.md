## Description:

Provides Chinese-language practical guidance for PRC Government Procurement Law compliance, including Q&A, procurement document review, case search, compliance checkups, policy implementation review, and protest or complaint assistance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Public procurement practitioners, suppliers, reviewers, and compliance teams use this skill to interpret PRC government procurement requirements, check procurement materials, assess policy implementation, and prepare reference guidance for questions, complaints, and process reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal and policy guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific high-stakes procurement matter.

Mitigation: Verify current law, official sources, and case-specific facts before relying on outputs; consult qualified counsel or the responsible finance authority for consequential decisions.

Risk: The skill is scoped to PRC Government Procurement Law and may be misapplied to tendering and bidding law, enterprise self-procurement, or non-fiscal-fund procurement.

Mitigation: Confirm procurement subject, funding source, object, and method before use; redirect excluded matters to an appropriate tendering and bidding compliance workflow.

Risk: The package is intended for Chinese-language government procurement use and may be less accessible to users outside that language and legal context.

Mitigation: Use Chinese-language legal source material and have domain specialists review translations, summaries, or cross-border interpretations.
