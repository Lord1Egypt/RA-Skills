## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to look up and compare public China social security, medical insurance, and housing fund policy rules, procedures, conditions, dates, and source references. It is for policy research and summaries, not personal eligibility decisions, guaranteed outcomes, or optimization advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: China social policy rules can vary by locality and change over time.

Mitigation: Check the cited dates and local authority pages, and treat the skill output as a summary of official public sources.

Risk: Users may mistake public policy summaries for personal eligibility, benefits, or compliance decisions.

Mitigation: Use official agencies, local service windows, official customer service, or qualified professionals for case-specific decisions.
