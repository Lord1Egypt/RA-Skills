A practical daily planning assistant for university students.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External university students use this skill to turn classes, assignments, exams, activities, appointments, and deadlines into realistic daily or weekly schedules with priorities, breaks, and reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for general planning requests, not only explicit university scheduling requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is seeking student scheduling help and ask for class, task, deadline, time, duration, and availability details before building a plan.

Risk: A plan can be unrealistic or miss urgent work when the user provides incomplete deadlines, times, durations, or availability.

Mitigation: Do not invent missing times; ask clarifying questions or clearly label assumptions, and tell the user when the workload exceeds available time.
