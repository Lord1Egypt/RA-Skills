# YouTube thumbnail specs and policy

Source: YouTube Help, "Add video thumbnails on YouTube" (read Nov 2026). Rules can change; recheck before a launch.

## Specs

| Item | Video thumbnail | Shorts thumbnail |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 | 9:16 |
| Recommended | 3840x2160 | 2160x3840 |
| Minimum | width 640 px | height 640 px |
| Formats | JPG or PNG | JPG or PNG |
| Max file size | 2 MB on mobile; 50 MB on desktop | same rules as video |

- This skill defaults to 1280x720 JPG under 1.9 MB: sharp enough for most uses and safe for mobile uploads. For a higher-resolution master use `"size": "youtube_hd"` (1920x1080) or a custom `WxH` such as `3840x2160`, and keep the file under the limit that applies.
- Shorts thumbnails can only be added in YouTube Studio on a computer.
- A vertical video with a 16:9 custom thumbnail is replaced by an auto-generated 4:5 thumbnail on some surfaces (home, explore, subscriptions).
- The account must be verified to upload custom thumbnails; daily upload limits apply and can vary by country or channel history.

## Policy

- Thumbnails must follow the Community Guidelines. Nudity or sexually provocative content, hate speech, violence and harmful or dangerous content can be rejected and can earn a strike.
- Repeated violations can remove custom-thumbnail privileges for 30 days or lead to account termination; strikes can be appealed.
- YouTube may turn off custom thumbnails in certain search results if it considers them inappropriate.
- Beyond the rules: do not use misleading imagery. A thumbnail that overpromises raises clicks and loses viewers.
