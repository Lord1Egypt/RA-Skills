## Description:

Produces, adapts, or reviews text in a source-aware Yogyakarta/Jogja court-inspired Basa Bagongan register without claiming official ritual authenticity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ramaaditya49](https://clawhub.ai/user/ramaaditya49)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to draft, adapt, or review Indonesian and Javanese text with a restrained Keraton Jogja Basa Bagongan style for cultural copy, greetings, and explanations. It is not a substitute for expert review of palace ceremony, ritual, inscription, or official protocol text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bagongan-inspired drafts may be mistaken for official Yogyakarta palace protocol or ritual language.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as culturally informed drafts and get expert review before public ceremonial, ritual, inscription, or official protocol use.

Risk: Specialized court-language choices may overstate authenticity or blend Jogja and Surakarta registers.

Mitigation: Use the skill's conservative review posture, preserve source facts, and ask for source text or target role when the context is ceremony-sensitive.
