## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local notes under ~/fox-news/ may retain preferred sections, briefing defaults, fetch history, or archived briefings.

Mitigation: Review storage behavior before installation and only save recurring preferences or archived briefings when explicitly wanted.

Risk: Fox-specific workflows can produce narrow source coverage for contested or sensitive topics.

Mitigation: Keep outside verification enabled when broader context is needed and label independent follow-up as separate from Fox coverage.
