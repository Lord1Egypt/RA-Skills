## Description:

Builds an 8–12 creator shortlist from a category, budget, and market or supplied accounts, with recent performance, interaction, content themes, and an approach recommendation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and creator researchers use this skill to identify creators to approach for a campaign or collaboration. It produces an evidence-based shortlist from public creator information or material the user supplies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan rates the skill suspicious because it reports broad account permissions and capabilities beyond creator research. The scan was partial, and this verdict is not evidence of malicious intent.

Mitigation: Use the skill only if the additional Beatra capabilities are needed and you are comfortable with the broad account access, metadata reporting, and local-file upload described in the security guidance.
