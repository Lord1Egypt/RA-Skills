Risk: Crypto market signals may be incomplete, stale, or mistaken for financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational market research and independently verify them before making financial decisions.

Risk: Private exchange API keys or trade-execution permissions could expose funds to unintended trading loss.

Mitigation: Use public market-data access by default; provide private keys or execution permissions only with explicit safety limits and intentional user approval.

Risk: Position-sizing and leverage suggestions can amplify losses if applied without portfolio controls.

Mitigation: Apply explicit maximum leverage, position-size, exposure, and consecutive-loss limits before acting on any signal.
