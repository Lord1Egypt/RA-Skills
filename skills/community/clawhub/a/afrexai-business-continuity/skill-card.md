## Description:

Business Continuity Planner guides users through creating business continuity and disaster recovery plans by mapping critical functions, recovery objectives, risks, communication paths, and maintenance practices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Operations leads, CTOs, founders, and audit-preparation teams use this skill to build a practical BCP/DR document for their organization, including business impact analysis, risk scoring, recovery strategies, communication plans, and testing schedules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated plans may contain sensitive operational dependencies, disaster recovery details, vendor relationships, and personal contact information.

Mitigation: Review generated plans before sharing, limit inputs to information needed for the plan, and avoid unnecessary secrets or credentials.

Risk: Continuity and recovery recommendations may be incomplete if the user's operational details are incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Have responsible stakeholders review the plan and validate it through tabletop, simulation, or disaster recovery testing.
