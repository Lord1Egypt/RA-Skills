## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read sensitive company context when producing CEO-level advice.

Mitigation: Avoid placing confidential company data in company-context.md unless the user is comfortable having the assistant use it for this skill's guidance.

Risk: Strategic, financial, fundraising, legal, staffing, and investor-relations outputs can affect high-stakes business decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support and require review by appropriate company leaders, finance, legal, or investor-relations owners before acting.

Risk: The included financial and strategy analyzers use simplified assumptions and default example data when run directly.

Mitigation: Use validated company inputs, inspect model assumptions, and compare results with authoritative financial and operating data.
