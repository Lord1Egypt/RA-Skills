## Description:

Creates engaging, example-driven long-form articles with strong hooks, clear argument structures, reader-retention strategies, and platform-specific adaptations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aiepco](https://clawhub.ai/user/aiepco)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to draft publishable articles, blog posts, newsletters, and social media threads with research-first framing, concrete examples, and platform-specific structure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated articles or posts may include unsolicited follow requests, account handles, creator attribution, promotional calls to action, or endings the user did not explicitly request.

Mitigation: Review drafts before publishing and remove any CTA, attribution, handle, or follow request that was not explicitly provided.

Risk: The skill may lead the agent to perform web research for recent studies, data, controversies, and case studies.

Mitigation: Tell the agent not to browse or constrain sources when web research is not desired, and verify cited studies and claims before publication.
