## Use Case:

External marketers, coaches, course creators, community builders, and service providers use this skill to plan and write webinar funnels. It guides intake, positioning, webinar structure, offer design, email sequences, traffic strategy, revenue projections, slide outlines, and launch checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Webinar marketing outputs may include tracking, SMS reminders, advertising, email, or text-message tactics that require user consent and legal compliance review.

Mitigation: Before production use, add a privacy notice, consent controls for non-essential tracking, clear SMS opt-in language, opt-out handling, and review of applicable advertising, email, and text-message rules.

Risk: Aggressive sales-funnel guidance can lead to overstated scarcity, guarantees, claims, or revenue expectations if applied without review.

Mitigation: Review deadlines, guarantees, performance claims, ad copy, and projections for accuracy, substantiation, and jurisdiction-specific marketing requirements before launch.
