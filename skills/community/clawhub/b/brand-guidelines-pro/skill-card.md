## Description:

Applies Anthropic's official brand colors and typography to artifacts that benefit from Anthropic-style visual formatting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hsuyungfeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/hsuyungfeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

Apache 2.0

## Use Case:

Developers, designers, and content creators use this skill to apply Anthropic-style colors, typography, and visual hierarchy to documents, presentations, or other generated artifacts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The trigger wording is broad and may apply Anthropic visual branding where that branding is not appropriate.

Mitigation: Confirm that Anthropic-style branding is suitable for the target artifact before applying the skill.

Risk: Requested typography may not be installed in the execution environment.

Mitigation: Use the documented Arial and Georgia fallbacks when Poppins or Lora are unavailable.
