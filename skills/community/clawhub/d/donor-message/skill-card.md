## Description:

Generate personalized donor communication messages including thank-you notes, impact updates, re-engagement emails, and donation acknowledgment letters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiwenbing](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiwenbing)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Fundraisers, nonprofit staff, and donor-relations teams use this skill to draft thank-you notes, impact updates, re-engagement outreach, soft asks, and formal donation acknowledgments for supporters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated messages may include sensitive donor details such as names, donation amounts, causes, or impact claims.

Mitigation: Use only donor information appropriate for the communication and review drafts for privacy, accuracy, and organizational donor-communication requirements before sending.

Risk: Acknowledgment or impact language may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for a specific organization's compliance practices.

Mitigation: Have staff verify donation facts, tax or acknowledgment requirements, and final wording against the organization's approved communications process.
