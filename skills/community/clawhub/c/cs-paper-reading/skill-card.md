## Description:

Guides agents through Keshav's three-pass method for reading computer science research papers and producing structured summaries, critical analysis, and an integrated reading report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[overdue-lin](https://clawhub.ai/user/overdue-lin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, researchers, students, and reviewers use this skill to analyze computer science papers for literature review, paper review, research implementation, or study. It structures reading into overview, content grasp, deep understanding, and final report phases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad paper-related requests where a full three-pass computer science paper workflow is not desired.

Mitigation: Review and narrow trigger wording before deployment when strict routing is important.

Risk: Structured academic-paper guidance can still produce incomplete or unsupported critiques if the source paper is not read carefully.

Mitigation: Require users or reviewers to check generated summaries, scores, and criticism against the paper text before relying on the report.
