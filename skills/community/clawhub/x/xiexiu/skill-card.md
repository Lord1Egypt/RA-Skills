Teaches unconventional problem-solving methodology for lateral thinking, first principles reasoning, and creative approaches to problems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to frame low-stakes problems with a five-step unconventional problem-solving method, including deconstructing standard approaches, identifying the real goal, brainstorming alternatives, testing quickly, and iterating.
