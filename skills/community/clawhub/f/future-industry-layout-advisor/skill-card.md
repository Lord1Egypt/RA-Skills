## Description:

Provides Chinese-language, evidence-based recommendations for regional future-industry layout by evaluating local resources, industrial base, innovation capacity, talent supply, market demand, and future industry trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Regional planners, economic development teams, and investment-attraction advisors use this skill to produce customized future-industry layout reports for a target region. It supports industry planning, strategic positioning, staged cultivation paths, and targeted investment-attraction recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public web research and industry forecasts may be incomplete, outdated, or uncertain for policy and investment decisions.

Mitigation: Ground conclusions in current official regional statistics, government planning documents, and authoritative industry research; treat the report as decision support rather than final policy.

Risk: The skill is optimized for Chinese-language regional industrial planning and may not fit other languages or jurisdictions without adaptation.

Mitigation: Explicitly request language, jurisdiction, and policy-context adaptation before using it outside Chinese-language regional planning work.
