## Description:

Provides a production-focused methodology for building, optimizing, and debugging Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems from architecture assessment through deployment and evaluation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill as a RAG engineering playbook to design, diagnose, evaluate, and productionize retrieval-augmented generation systems. It supports decisions across ingestion, preprocessing, chunking, embeddings, vector stores, retrieval, prompting, evaluation, security, scaling, and monitoring.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommended cloud AI providers, vector databases, logging, ingestion, and evaluation tools can introduce privacy, cost, or compliance exposure when used with sensitive data.

Mitigation: Review provider, storage, logging, data residency, access control, and budget choices before applying the playbook to sensitive or regulated corpora.

Risk: Broad methodology may be applied without enough evidence from the user's own corpus and traffic patterns.

Mitigation: Benchmark retrieval and generation quality on representative data, maintain a golden test set, and validate latency, cost, and hallucination targets before production rollout.
