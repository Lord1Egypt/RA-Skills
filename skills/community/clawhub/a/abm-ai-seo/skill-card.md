## Description:

Helps users optimize content for AI search engines, get cited by LLMs, and appear in AI-generated answers across platforms such as Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, marketers, content teams, and SEO practitioners use this skill to audit AI search visibility and plan content changes that make pages more extractable, authoritative, and citable by AI answer engines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketing statistics or platform-specific SEO claims may be outdated or misleading if applied without review.

Mitigation: Review claims, dates, and cited sources before using recommendations in public marketing or content strategy.

Risk: Robots.txt recommendations may broaden AI crawler access to public content.

Mitigation: Confirm crawler access choices with the site's content, legal, and business owners before changing robots.txt.
