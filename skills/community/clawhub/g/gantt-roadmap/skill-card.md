## Description:

Turn a project plan or milestone set into a dated Mermaid Gantt roadmap with dependencies, milestones, critical path notes, risks, buffers, and assumptions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Project managers, developers, and planning teams use this skill to convert phases, tasks, dates, dependencies, and milestones into a roadmap that can be rendered as a Mermaid Gantt chart. It also helps call out critical path items, schedule risks, buffers, and date assumptions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Estimated or inferred dates could be mistaken for committed delivery dates.

Mitigation: Review generated dates, buffers, and assumptions before using the roadmap for planning commitments.

Risk: Missing or inaccurate dependency information can make the critical path or parallel work misleading.

Mitigation: Confirm task dependencies and milestone timing before sharing or exporting the chart.
