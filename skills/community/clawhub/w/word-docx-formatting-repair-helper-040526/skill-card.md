## Description:

Repair and automate Word DOCX formatting, styles, find-and-replace, numbering, tables, and conversion workflows while preserving document structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Office workers, legal teams, students, documentation maintainers, and operations teams use this skill to plan and verify DOCX formatting repairs, style cleanup, numbering fixes, safe find-and-replace, table handling, and conversion workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad document-formatting terms outside a Word or DOCX cleanup task.

Mitigation: Use it when the request is specifically about Word, DOCX, formatting repair, styles, numbering, find-and-replace, tables, or conversion workflows.

Risk: Batch document edits can unintentionally alter protected content, tracked changes, fields, links, or layout.

Mitigation: Keep backups, use dry-run reports for batch changes, apply repairs in reversible stages, and verify page flow, headings, numbering, tables, links, and conversion fidelity.
