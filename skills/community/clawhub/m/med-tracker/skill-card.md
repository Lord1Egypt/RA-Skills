## Description:

Medication tracker for logging prescriptions, dosages, schedules, and reminders. Use when users want to track what medications they take, set reminders, or check drug interactions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiwenbing](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiwenbing)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize medication names, dosages, schedules, missed-dose concerns, and reminder preferences. It provides general tracking help and directs dosing or interaction questions to a pharmacist or doctor for verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medication and condition details may be sensitive personal health information.

Mitigation: Install only if users are comfortable entering those details into the agent environment.

Risk: General interaction, dosing, or missed-dose guidance may be incomplete for a user's clinical situation.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an organizer and reminder aid, and verify interaction or dosing questions with a pharmacist or doctor.
