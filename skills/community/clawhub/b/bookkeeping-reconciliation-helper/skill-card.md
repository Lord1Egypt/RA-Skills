## Description:

Helps bookkeeping and finance teams create repeatable workflows for reconciling bank feeds, invoices, receipts, payment processor exports, and accounting ledgers while tracking unmatched exceptions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Bookkeepers, accountants, finance operations teams, small business owners, and developers use this skill to structure reconciliation workflows, produce checklists or implementation support, and preserve unmatched exceptions for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked implicitly from broad bookkeeping terms and could receive sensitive bank, invoice, receipt, or ledger details.

Mitigation: Use it intentionally for reconciliation tasks and share only the financial details needed for the requested workflow or analysis.

Risk: Reconciliation guidance can affect financial records if applied without review.

Mitigation: Review generated workflows, exception handling, and any code or commands before using them with accounting systems or production data.
