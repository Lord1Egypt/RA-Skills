## Description:

Etsy Seller helps sellers improve listing SEO, tag selection, pricing strategy, competitor analysis, descriptions, and shop audits with Etsy-specific guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Etsy sellers use this skill to review and optimize shop listings, tags, titles, descriptions, pricing, fees, shipping strategy, reviews, and competitor positioning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Etsy algorithm weights, fee amounts, and third-party tool recommendations may become outdated or may not match a seller's current shop context.

Mitigation: Verify business-critical Etsy fees, marketplace rules, and tool recommendations against current Etsy and vendor sources before changing pricing, listings, or advertising strategy.
