## Description:

Screens proposed business strategies, side projects, and monetization plans against China-focused compliance red lines, operational checks, and revenue transparency criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kuanghs](https://clawhub.ai/user/kuanghs)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to pre-screen China-facing business plans, side projects, and monetization ideas for legal red lines, gray-zone concerns, required qualifications, data-use issues, platform-rule risks, tax reminders, and revenue transparency concerns. It is a compliance aid and not a substitute for legal counsel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be mistaken for legal advice because it produces compliance conclusions and recommendations.

Mitigation: Use it as a preliminary screening aid and have qualified legal counsel verify decisions for specific matters.

Risk: Broad trigger wording can activate the skill on generic compliance, legal, or risk language.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking for a China-focused business compliance screen before applying the checklist.

Risk: The artifact documents optional installs for related skills that are not verified in this release.

Mitigation: Review the related skills separately before installing or running their install commands.
