## Description:

为初中教师提供省级和市级教育课题申报全流程AI辅助，涵盖选题推荐、申报书生成、竞争力优化及实施指导。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Teachers, education researchers, and school leaders use this skill to plan provincial or municipal education research applications, draft proposal materials, improve proposal competitiveness, and prepare implementation guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill depends on unpinned external education-project packages that were not included for review.

Mitigation: Pin and review the provincial and municipal education project dependencies before installation or deployment.

Risk: Generated proposals, biographies, achievements, school-support materials, and implementation guidance may contain inaccurate or unverifiable claims.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and verify every factual, institutional, eligibility, deadline, and compliance claim against official application requirements before submission.

Risk: Official education research applications may require local formatting, signatures, seals, attachments, or review steps that the skill cannot guarantee.

Mitigation: Use the generated checklist as a planning aid and have the responsible teacher or school administrator complete a final review against the relevant education department's current requirements.
