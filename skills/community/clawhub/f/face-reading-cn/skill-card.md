## Description:

Chinese traditional face-reading skill that combines cultural reference material, image-based face analysis, mole reading, and psychology-oriented self-improvement prompts for entertainment and learning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to produce entertainment-oriented Chinese face-reading explanations, quick reference answers, image-analysis reports, and self-improvement guidance. It should not be used for medical, psychological, hiring, dating, financial, or other consequential decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill processes face photos and may infer sensitive personal traits from appearance.

Mitigation: Use only with consent, treat outputs as entertainment and cultural reference, and do not use results for medical, psychological, hiring, dating, financial, or life decisions.

Risk: The security evidence notes under-scoped external execution and weak privacy safeguards around image analysis.

Mitigation: Prefer local-only processing, review or remove the VITA integration before use, pin dependencies, and verify any model downloads.

Risk: The artifact includes upgrade and external execution paths that could change behavior outside the reviewed release.

Mitigation: Do not follow remote upgrade procedures without a full security diff plus checksum or signature verification.
