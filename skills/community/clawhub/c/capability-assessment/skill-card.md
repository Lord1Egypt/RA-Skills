## Description:

Assess organizational capabilities using radar charts for competitive analysis, gap identification, and strategic planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linuszz](https://clawhub.ai/user/linuszz)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business strategists, operators, consultants, and planning teams use this skill to assess organizational capabilities, compare them with benchmarks or competitors, identify gaps, and prioritize improvement actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Capability assessments may include confidential business strategy, competitive positioning, or operational performance details.

Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential business details unless they are appropriate for the active agent session and its data handling environment.

Risk: Scores and priorities can be subjective or misleading if based on incomplete inputs.

Mitigation: Use clear scoring definitions, benchmark evidence, and stakeholder review before relying on the assessment for planning decisions.
