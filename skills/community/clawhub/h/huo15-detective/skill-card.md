## Use Case:

Compliance, operations, and due diligence users can use this skill to structure authorized company or personnel background checks, compare registry sources, identify business and legal risk signals, and draft investigation reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide personal background checks and lookup of risk records or partial identifiers without built-in privacy limits.

Mitigation: Use it only for legitimate, authorized due diligence, confirm a lawful basis before investigating private individuals, and avoid collecting unnecessary identifiers.

Risk: Investigation results can be incorrect or misleading if third-party registry data is stale, incomplete, or matched to the wrong person or company.

Mitigation: Verify findings against authoritative sources, confirm identity through company associations, cite data sources and access dates, and request supporting records before making decisions.
