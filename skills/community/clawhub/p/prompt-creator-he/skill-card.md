## Description:

Prompt Creator Hebrew helps agents draft polished, Hebrew-first prompts for AI image and video generation, with emphasis on realistic cinematic style and respectful Jewish or Torah-related content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jojobok](https://clawhub.ai/user/jojobok)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to create copy-ready prompts for image and video tools in Hebrew or English. It is especially suited for realistic, cinematic, social-media-oriented, Jewish, Torah-related, or biblical-era visual prompt requests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill answers in Hebrew by default, which can be unsuitable for workflows that require English prompts.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for English or specify the target language before requesting a prompt.

Risk: Prompts for Jewish or biblical scenes may be treated as historically authoritative by downstream users.

Mitigation: Review generated prompts for cultural and historical accuracy, and keep requests grounded in respectful, authentic period details.
