## Description:

Health Habit Builder / 健康习惯养成师 helps users build sustainable health habits by evaluating habit difficulty, breaking goals into micro-habits, tracking check-ins, and analyzing motivation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and wellness-focused agents use this skill to create and adjust habit plans, evaluate habit difficulty, record check-ins, review progress, and receive motivation-oriented coaching suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat generic habit, motivation, or progress analysis as medical or mental-health advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as general wellness coaching and direct users to qualified professionals for medical, mental-health, or safety-critical decisions.

Risk: Prototype-style habit tracking may not preserve authoritative long-term state across agent sessions.

Mitigation: Use the output as planning and coaching support unless the deploying system supplies durable tracking, validation, and review controls.
