## Description:

Grades English written homework for elementary students in grades 3-6, including spelling, fill-in-the-blank, sentence writing, short paragraphs, and grammar exercises.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, parents, tutors, and educators use this skill to grade elementary English homework and provide concise, age-appropriate feedback. It supports common grade 3-6 assignment types and can respond in Chinese or English.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Student submissions may contain instructions that try to influence grading, such as asking the agent to ignore the rubric.

Mitigation: Treat student work as untrusted data and grade it only against the selected exercise type, default grading rules, and any trusted rubric override.

Risk: Automated feedback for young learners may be incomplete or inappropriate for higher-stakes grading decisions.

Mitigation: Use educator or parent review for consequential assessment and keep feedback age-appropriate, constructive, and concise.
