## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ambiguous storytime or short-video requests may be steered toward TikTok-specific scripting.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended platform, concept, and goal before using the output for a public campaign.

Risk: Generated scripts, captions, hashtags, or AI-video prompts may include unsuitable brand voice, unsupported claims, or compliance-sensitive language.

Mitigation: Review all generated content against brand guardrails, factual sources, and applicable compliance requirements before publishing.

Risk: Trending sound recommendations can create brand-fit or commercial licensing issues.

Mitigation: Use original audio or commercial-safe music unless the trend is relevant and properly licensed for the account.

Risk: AI-video prompts for faceless or b-roll concepts may require synthetic-media disclosure and may perform worse than native filming for trust-heavy content.

Mitigation: Prefer filming for faces and personal voice, reserve AI video for appropriate b-roll or faceless concepts, and confirm platform disclosure requirements.
