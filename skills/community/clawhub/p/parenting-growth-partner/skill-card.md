## Description:

Parenting Growth Partner provides parents and caregivers with informational child-development milestone checks, age-appropriate activity recommendations, communication guidance, behavior analysis, and routine suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents and caregivers use this skill to get structured, age-aware parenting guidance for children from infancy through preschool, including developmental milestone checks, play activities, positive communication scripts, behavior-support suggestions, and daily routines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Health-adjacent developmental guidance may be mistaken for medical or developmental diagnosis.

Mitigation: Treat milestone results as informational and consult a pediatrician or developmental specialist for real concerns.

Risk: Chinese-first guidance may be difficult to evaluate for users who do not read Chinese.

Mitigation: Use only when the reviewer and intended users are comfortable with Chinese-language parenting guidance, or review translations before relying on outputs.

Risk: Artifact evidence notes syntax errors in several Python files, which may prevent the package from running correctly.

Mitigation: Run functional tests and fix package execution issues before deployment.
