## Description:

Turns Chinese product details, supplier specs, or 1688 copy into three polished English Amazon listing variations optimized for Western buyers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kiwi-phantomworks](https://clawhub.ai/user/kiwi-phantomworks)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External cross-border sellers and ecommerce operators use this skill to convert Chinese product information into Amazon-ready English listing drafts. It produces multiple listing angles with titles, bullet points, descriptions, backend keyword suggestions, and usage notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated listing copy may include unverified warranty, certification, safety, support, measurement, or Amazon policy claims.

Mitigation: Confirm all factual claims, measurements, support terms, and Amazon policy fit before publishing.
