## Description:

Tenant rights knowledge and actionable defense templates for repair disputes, eviction concerns, security deposit recovery, habitability issues, and renter-rights questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to prepare tenant-rights guidance, documentation checklists, repair demand letters, security deposit recovery steps, and eviction-defense planning while first confirming the user's jurisdiction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tenant law varies by jurisdiction, and incorrect assumptions about local rules can lead to missed deadlines or harmful legal steps.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's jurisdiction, dates, court deadlines, notice requirements, and rent-withholding rules against local legal aid or official tenant resources before action.

Risk: Installer commands can change over time or be run with more privileges than needed.

Mitigation: Use a pinned or otherwise trusted ClawHub installer command and run it with normal user privileges.
