## Description:

Selling-point health check for ecommerce copy that runs six checks to judge whether a claim is distinctive or easily copied, then returns pass/reject findings and concrete fixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[handsomeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/handsomeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to evaluate existing selling-point copy for distinctiveness, defensibility against competitor claims or live-host challenges, and concrete revision paths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local archive files may contain commercially sensitive brand profiles, conclusions, or deliverables.

Mitigation: Use archive behavior only in private project directories, exclude those directories from source control or sharing, and skip archiving for confidential material.

Risk: First use displays off-platform WeChat support contacts.

Mitigation: Treat those contacts as unaudited support information and avoid sharing sensitive operational data through off-platform channels.

Risk: A passing selling-point review could be mistaken for advertising, platform, category, or regulatory clearance.

Mitigation: Run separate compliance review before publishing or using claims in live commerce, advertising, or regulated product contexts.
