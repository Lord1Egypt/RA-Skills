## Description:

Use this skill when a Certified General appraiser or MAI designee needs to draft a USPAP 2026-2027 narrative appraisal report for an income-producing commercial property; it develops highest-and-best-use analysis, Sales Comparison, Income, and Cost approaches, and produces a DRAFT report for the signing appraiser to certify.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Certified General appraisers, MAI designees, supervised trainees, review appraisers, report-production analysts, lenders, special servicers, or counsel use this skill to draft or review a USPAP 2026-2027 narrative report for an income-producing commercial property. The output remains a draft that a qualified signing appraiser must verify, certify, and sign.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States by default; non-US assignments require confirmation of the governing appraisal standard.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A draft appraisal narrative could be mistaken for a signed appraisal or a substitute for licensed professional judgment.

Mitigation: Keep the output labeled as DRAFT and require the signing appraiser to verify every value input, certify the report, and sign before delivery.

Risk: Assignment data may include confidential client, property, rent-roll, lease, transaction, or operating-statement information.

Mitigation: Use only tools and services authorized for the assignment and avoid sharing confidential appraisal work product with unauthorized systems.

Risk: The governing standard or jurisdiction may differ from the skill's USPAP 2026-2027 default.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable standard and jurisdiction for each assignment, and verify current USPAP requirements before relying on the draft.
