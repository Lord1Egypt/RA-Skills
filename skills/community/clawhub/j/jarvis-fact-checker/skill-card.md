## Description:

Jarvis Fact Checker is a Russian-language fact-checking checklist that guides an agent to verify legal references, dates, figures, quotations, court cases, and source claims before responding.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to add a Russian-language pre-response fact-checking routine for legal and factual claims, including document numbers, statutes, dates, figures, quotations, and case references.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may make an agent more cautious and may cause source-verification or uncertainty statements when sources are unavailable.

Mitigation: Use it where a conservative factual or legal-answering posture is desired, and review final answers against authoritative sources when precision matters.
