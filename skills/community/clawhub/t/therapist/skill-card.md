## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for supportive coaching with CBT, ACT, mindfulness, grounding, reframing, and behavioral activation techniques. It is intended to complement professional care, not replace licensed mental-health services.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat supportive coaching guidance as a substitute for licensed mental-health care.

Mitigation: Use it as complementary support and refer trauma, medication questions, crisis situations, or suicidal thoughts to qualified human professionals immediately.
