## Description:

Social media copywriting and distillation skill. Transforms any news, dev logs, or external articles into high-engagement, opinionated tech posts with authentic voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hacksing](https://clawhub.ai/user/hacksing)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers, founders, and technical communicators use this skill to turn articles, README files, dev logs, and architecture notes into concise opinionated social posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated social posts may be inaccurate, overstate source material, or sound more opinionated than intended.

Mitigation: Review each generated post against the source before publishing.

Risk: Private logs or business details supplied as input may be transformed into public-facing copy.

Mitigation: Use only information that is approved for public release.
