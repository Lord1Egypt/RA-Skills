## Description:

Aggregates 28 Chinese-language education skills and routes teacher or student learning requests to matching sub-skills with a unified response format.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chenleimap](https://clawhub.ai/user/chenleimap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers and students use this skill to route Chinese-language education, learning, exam preparation, lesson planning, homework, and subject-improvement requests to appropriate education sub-skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad education trigger terms may activate this routing skill in workspaces that contain many education-related skills.

Mitigation: Review and narrow the trigger terms before deployment if activation should be limited.
