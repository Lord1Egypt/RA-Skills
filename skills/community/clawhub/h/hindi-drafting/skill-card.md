## Description:

Professional Hindi drafting for business documents, government applications, HR, legal, and marketing content for Indian startups and SMEs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and business teams use this skill to draft polished Hindi documents, translate English content into professional Hindi, and create government, business, HR, legal, investor, and marketing content with suitable tone and vocabulary.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill tends to produce polished Devanagari Hindi even when a user expects Hinglish, bilingual output, or a different style.

Mitigation: Ask for Hinglish, bilingual output, or a specific style explicitly when that is required.

Risk: Business, HR, government, legal, investor, or financial drafts may include placeholders or require factual and domain review before use.

Mitigation: Review all placeholders, figures, obligations, names, dates, and formal salutations before sending or relying on the document.
