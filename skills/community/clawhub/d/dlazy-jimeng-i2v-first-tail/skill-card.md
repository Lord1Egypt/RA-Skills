## Description:

Generate coherent transition videos with Jimeng models from a provided first and last frame.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and creators provide a prompt and beginning and ending images to request a transition video from Jimeng. They can review the returned media URL or save the generated asset locally.
