## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to research historical or military conflicts and produce mobile-friendly, single-screen tactical briefing images. It is suited for battle reports, opposing-force summaries, command-structure snapshots, and concise visual briefings grounded in multiple cited sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Historical casualty figures, commander quotes, and tactical claims may be inaccurate, disputed, or biased.

Mitigation: Review outputs for historical accuracy and bias, and require multi-source cross-checking before use.

Risk: Generated HTML/PNG artifacts may include incorrect layout, colors, flags, or source presentation.

Mitigation: Inspect the rendered briefing image before delivery and confirm that cited sources and visual identifiers match the requested conflict.
