## Description:

Audit LinkedIn post drafts for algorithm compliance, AI cues, structure, and length; provides pass/fail results with detailed fixes and posting advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sergebulaev](https://clawhub.ai/user/sergebulaev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content authors and social media teams use this skill before publishing LinkedIn drafts to flag AI cues, structural weaknesses, algorithm-risk patterns, and length issues, then receive specific fixes or optional rewrites.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Algorithm-fit and AI-detection scores may be inaccurate because they are heuristic writing advice, not guaranteed platform or detector results.

Mitigation: Treat scores as editorial signals and review the underlying draft, blockers, and suggested fixes before publishing.

Risk: Suggested rewrites may over-optimize a post and weaken the author's intended voice or context.

Mitigation: Review any rewrite manually against the author's voice, audience, and public posting goals before use.
