## Description:

Takes an existing LinkedIn draft and returns an optimized version with a change log explaining the edits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zepoldani](https://clawhub.ai/user/zepoldani)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and professionals use this skill to improve existing LinkedIn drafts before publishing. It focuses on hook strength, mobile-friendly structure, CTA clarity, hashtag restraint, voice preservation, and an explanatory change log.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optimized LinkedIn copy may contain inaccurate claims, overstate the author's intent, or miss the desired tone if published without review.

Mitigation: Review the generated post for accuracy, voice, and tone before publishing.

Risk: Users may mistake the skill for a LinkedIn integration or publishing tool.

Mitigation: Treat it as a draft-editing aid only; it does not post to LinkedIn, access accounts, use credentials, or persist data.
