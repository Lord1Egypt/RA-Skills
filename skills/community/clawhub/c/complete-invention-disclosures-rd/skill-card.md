## Description:

Guide inventors and R&D teams through a seven-step English invention-disclosure workflow, organize rough technical notes, identify missing technical support, and generate a structured draft for inventor and qualified patent-professional review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Inventors, R&D teams, in-house IP teams, and patent professionals use this skill to turn fragmented technical notes, product discussions, experiment records, or incomplete disclosures into a structured invention-disclosure draft for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unpublished invention details may be confidential and sensitive.

Mitigation: Use the guide only on approved devices and browsers, avoid unnecessary personal data, and move exported drafts into approved matter storage.

Risk: Clipboard, downloads, browser extensions, screen sharing, backups, or endpoint controls may expose disclosure content outside the skill's local workflow.

Mitigation: Review organizational browser and endpoint controls before entering confidential material, and handle copied or downloaded drafts under approved retention and access rules.

Risk: The generated draft may be mistaken for legal advice, a patent application, or a patentability assessment.

Mitigation: Require inventor and qualified patent-professional review before using the draft for filing, inventorship, ownership, disclosure timing, patentability, infringement, or freedom-to-operate decisions.
