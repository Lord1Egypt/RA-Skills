## Description:

Find free and cheap things to do in Charlotte, NC, including daily event listings with times, prices, venues, searchable deals, festivals, kids activities, and local guides from Charlotte On The Cheap.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer Charlotte-area event and deal questions, compare free or low-cost options by date or topic, and retrieve source article details when needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Event prices, dates, availability, and venue details may change after the site is read.

Mitigation: Treat event and deal details as website-derived information and encourage checking the source article before acting on time-sensitive plans.

Risk: Monthly overview results are previews and may omit many events for a day.

Mitigation: Use the reported total for volume and fetch the full daily listing before presenting a day as complete.

Risk: Expired deals can be shown if historical results are explicitly requested.

Mitigation: Label expired offers clearly and do not present them as currently available.
