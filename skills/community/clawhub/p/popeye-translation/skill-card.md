## Description:

Popeye Translation helps agents translate between Chinese and English, polish translations, and adapt text for localization.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to translate Chinese and English text, improve existing translations, and localize content for target markets or audiences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad Chinese trigger words for translation, polishing, and localization may activate this skill during ordinary translation requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intended translation task and target language or market before applying the skill's detailed templates.

Risk: Localized or polished translation output may not fit the user's intended audience without context.

Mitigation: Ask for intended use, target audience, tone, and market when those details are missing.
