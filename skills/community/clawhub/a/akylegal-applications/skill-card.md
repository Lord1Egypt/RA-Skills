## Description:

A PRC-focused legal practice skill for litigation workflows, trial preparation, legal document drafting, arbitration, evidence review, and litigation risk assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, legal professionals, and developers use this skill to draft PRC-focused litigation and arbitration materials, plan trial and evidence strategy, and identify procedural deadlines and filing requirements. Outputs should be reviewed against current law and by a licensed attorney before formal use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: PRC-specific legal procedure, deadlines, or forms may be incorrect, outdated, or unsuitable for the user's jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Confirm the jurisdiction and current law before relying on deadlines, forms, or strategy, and require licensed attorney review before formal use.

Risk: Legal document workflows may request sensitive personal, financial, property, or case information.

Mitigation: Use placeholders or redacted values for ID numbers, addresses, phone numbers, bank accounts, property records, and similar data unless a lawyer confirms the details are necessary.
