## Description:

Design, optimize, and batch-produce video covers and thumbnails for Bilibili, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and other platforms using title strategy, reference analysis, portrait consistency guidance, AI image prompts, layout specs, and quality scoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, designers, and agent users use this skill to turn video topics, titles, reference covers, portraits, and platform constraints into executable thumbnail strategies, no-text image prompts, post-production layout directions, and quality checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Portrait and reference-image workflows can create rights, consent, or similarity concerns if users provide assets they are not allowed to use.

Mitigation: Confirm consent and usage rights before using portraits or references, and review outputs to avoid copying protected brand assets, distinctive layouts, logos, or third-party likenesses.

Risk: Thumbnail optimization guidance can become misleading if it overstates outcomes or encourages clickbait beyond the underlying content.

Mitigation: Review generated titles, prompts, and layouts for factual alignment, avoid guaranteed performance claims, and keep emotional hooks proportional to the content.

Risk: The skill is primarily written for Chinese-language cover workflows, so default outputs may not fit every locale.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for localized language, platform conventions, and layout expectations when producing non-Chinese covers.
