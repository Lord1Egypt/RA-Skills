## Description:

Parallax helps agents review significant product and engineering decisions through user, maintainer, operator, adversary, and sponsor perspectives to expose blind spots and tradeoffs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jcools1977](https://clawhub.ai/user/jcools1977)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and engineering leads use this skill during architecture reviews, RFC discussions, and other decisions where multiple stakeholder perspectives need to be considered before implementation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked in more design discussions than a narrowly scoped architecture-only skill.

Mitigation: Use it for significant decisions where stakeholder tradeoffs matter, and keep routine changes lightweight.

Risk: The output is a reasoning aid and may not validate project-specific facts or constraints.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current product, security, operations, and business context before acting.
