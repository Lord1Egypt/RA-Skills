## Description:

A specialized skill for tracking the US-Iran conflict in English, focusing on intelligence published within the last 6 hours for tactical situation reports on military actions, official statements, and international reactions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to produce English-language, near-real-time situation reports on the US-Iran conflict. It emphasizes a six-hour search window, source triangulation, explicit attribution, and structured timelines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports about an active conflict can include propaganda, stale claims, or unverified primary-source statements.

Mitigation: Use the skill's triangulation workflow: treat primary-source claims as claims, corroborate across opposing or neutral sources, and separate verified facts from competing claims.

Risk: The skill is intentionally scoped to English, long-form reports and a recent six-hour reporting window.

Mitigation: Use a different skill variant or explicitly request another workflow for non-English output, shorter summaries, or historical analysis.
