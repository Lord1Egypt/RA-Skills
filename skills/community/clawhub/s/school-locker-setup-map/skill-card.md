## Description:

Create a student locker layout map with zones, supplies, reset routines, photo-checklist prompts, and safety rules that keep valuables and private schedules off visible labels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and caregivers use this skill to create a practical, privacy-safe school locker setup for a new term, new routine, or specific locker constraint. It helps plan zones, supplies, labels, routines, and a photo checklist without exposing sensitive student information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive student details such as locker combinations, student IDs, addresses, medication details, private schedules, payment cards, or valuables.

Mitigation: Ask only for practical setup constraints, omit sensitive details from visible maps, and replace any supplied sensitive information with generic placeholders.

Risk: Visible locker labels or photo checklist outputs could reveal private schedules or valuable items.

Mitigation: Use generic labels and include photo prompts that explicitly avoid showing combinations, IDs, private schedules, payment cards, or valuable items.
