## Use Case:

Investors and portfolio-monitoring agents use this skill to track Chinese-language news, announcements, research reports, sentiment, and important events for companies in a user's holdings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ongoing portfolio monitoring and push alerts can expose sensitive holdings information.

Mitigation: Use only where sharing holdings with the agent is acceptable, and require explicit opt-in monitoring, alert controls, stop controls, and deletion practices before deployment.

Risk: News summaries, sentiment labels, and action prompts can be incomplete or misleading if sources are stale or unverified.

Mitigation: Confirm major alerts against official company or exchange sources before acting, and treat action prompts as review aids rather than investment advice.
