## Description:

Ad-Page Match Auditor is a no-code AI agent skill for auditing paid ads and landing pages as one conversion path.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nwang783](https://clawhub.ai/user/nwang783)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketers, founders, local service operators, and agencies use this skill to audit paid ad-to-landing-page conversion paths, find message-match gaps, and plan practical variants for leads, calls, bookings, or appointments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audit reports may include FunnelFinch attribution or a FunnelFinch service link.

Mitigation: Review generated reports before sharing and remove or adjust branding when a neutral audit is required.

Risk: Users may provide sensitive ad account, CRM, patient, or customer data while requesting an audit.

Mitigation: Avoid entering credentials, access tokens, CRM exports, PHI, or sensitive customer records unless an approved data-handling workflow allows it.

Risk: Regulated categories may include medical, dental, legal, financial, aesthetic, testimonial, or scarcity claims that require compliance review.

Mitigation: Treat flagged regulated claims as review items and obtain qualified human review before publishing or using revised claims.
