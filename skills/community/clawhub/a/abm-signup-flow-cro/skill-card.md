## Description:

Provides structured guidance for auditing and improving signup, registration, account creation, and trial activation flows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mariokarras](https://clawhub.ai/user/mariokarras)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External product, marketing, design, and engineering teams use this skill to audit signup friction, prioritize conversion improvements, design lower-friction registration flows, and generate A/B test hypotheses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad signup-related wording could trigger the skill for adjacent onboarding or lead-capture tasks.

Mitigation: Confirm the task is about account creation or trial activation; route post-signup onboarding to onboarding-cro and non-account forms to form-cro.

Risk: The skill may read local product marketing context if one exists.

Mitigation: Keep product marketing context limited to information appropriate for signup-flow CRO work.
