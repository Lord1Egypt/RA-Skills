Turn a Business Into Reels That All Look the Same, in Plain Words

Give your AI agent one business. It drafts a Playbook.md with your colors, fonts, logo, and tone, picks one of three reel formats, Explainer, Challenge, or Stage Talk, then drafts the script, the caption, and the edit brief for owner review. Every reel follows the same playbook, so every reel looks like it came from the same business. The agent drafts, you record and post, the agent never posts a reel itself.

Most small business reels look different every time. One reel is bright, the next is dark, one uses one font, the next uses another, and no one can tell they came from the same shop. This skill gives you a short playbook first, colors, fonts, logo, and tone, written down once, then every reel follows it.

## What you get

- A Playbook.md template: business, audience, colors, fonts, logo, tone, words to use, words not to use, reel formats, caption style
- Three reel formats: Explainer, Challenge, Stage Talk, with one point per reel
- A three step editing workflow: break down a viral reel into layers, build the Playbook.md, feed a raw clip plus the playbook for an edited reel
- A script draft, a caption and hashtags draft, and an edit brief, all checked against the playbook
- A handoff check, script, caption, brand kit, and edit brief, each Checked, Missing, or Not confirmed, with pasted text quoted as the evidence
- A fix draft, labelled DRAFT, NOT SENT: handoff check summary, items checked, items missing, what to change first, and what to keep
- A missing checks list at the end, so nothing is hidden

## Who this is for

Small business owners who post reels and want every reel to look like it came from the same business. People who make reels with an AI agent, in plain words, and want a script, a caption, and an edit brief to review before they record anything. People who hand a raw clip to an editor, and want a clear brief, so the editor knows the colors, fonts, logo, and tone to use.

## Why this saves money

A playbook written once stops you from starting from zero for every reel. A script checked against the playbook stops a reel that does not sound like your business. A caption drafted first stops a post that says the wrong thing. An edit brief, reviewed first, stops an edit that uses the wrong colors, fonts, or tone, and has to be done again.

## Technical, for coders

Structured reel brand playbook for one business: Playbook.md brand kit, three reel formats, script draft, caption and hashtags draft, edit brief, handoff check, plus a fix draft for owner review. No guarantee of more views, more followers, or more sales. The fix draft is for owner review, not a posted reel. This skill is a playbook template and edit brief only. It does not render video, it does not edit video, and it does not post a reel.

## What a good playbook looks like

- The colors, fonts, logo, and tone are written down once, in plain words.
- Every reel uses one format, one point, one script, one caption, and one edit brief.
- Every Checked item has pasted text quoted as the evidence.
- Missing items are listed first in the fix draft, in order.
- Nothing is called done until the owner has reviewed the draft.

## How to use it

1. Install the skill in your AI agent (see below).
2. Give your agent the business, the goal of the reel, and the audience, in plain words.
3. Ask: "Plan a reel with reel-brand-playbook."
4. Read the playbook first. Every reel must follow it.
5. Read the handoff check. Every Checked item needs pasted text quoted as the evidence. A short description alone is not evidence.
6. Read the fix draft. Approve the script and the caption before recording or posting.

If you already give the business, the goal, and the audience up front, the skill does the full playbook and the reel plan in the same reply. It never stops at questions alone.

## What it will not do

- It will not record a reel, edit video, render video, or post a reel itself. Those steps are yours, the agent drafts the words and checks the text you paste back.
- It will not change a live account, publish anything, or buy a tool, without a separate approval from the owner.
- It will not invent a tool name, a price, a link, a stat, a partner, a testimonial, a view count, or a result.
- It will not claim a reel is posted or viral if you only saw a script or a caption.
- A fix draft is not a promise of more views, more followers, or more sales.
