## Description:

Builds educational debt-payoff plans across multiple debts using avalanche or snowball methods, comparing payoff order, time to debt freedom, total interest, and a recommendation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to model educational payoff options for credit cards or loans, compare avalanche and snowball outcomes, and choose an ordered repayment plan based on cost savings or quick-win preference.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide sensitive financial details such as debt balances, APRs, and minimum payments.

Mitigation: Share only the planning inputs needed for the model and avoid including account numbers, credentials, or unnecessary personal data.

Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Present results as educational modeling, state assumptions clearly, and consult a qualified financial professional for personal decisions.

Risk: Plan accuracy depends on assumptions such as fixed APRs, no new debt, and continued minimum payments.

Mitigation: Verify the inputs, flag the assumptions in the output, and recalculate the plan when rates, balances, spending, or payment amounts change.
