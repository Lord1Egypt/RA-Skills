## Description:

Create mixed-cut promotional videos for art supplies, drawing tools, sketching lessons, or painting material clips with stable vertical editing, Chinese male AI voiceover, synchronized Chinese subtitles, low original background audio, and product-promotion copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators and marketers use this skill to turn user-provided art-supply or drawing-practice videos into a finished vertical promotional short with narration, subtitles, and a clear timing plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow defaults to Chinese male narration and Chinese subtitles, which may not match every requested audience or language.

Mitigation: Tell the agent the desired narration language, voice style, and subtitle language before invoking the skill.

Risk: Promotional copy, subtitles, or voiceover timing can become inconsistent with the visible source segment if the timing plan is not reviewed.

Mitigation: Review the timeline table and final playback for segment duration, voice-subtitle alignment, source uniqueness, and decode errors before using the output.
