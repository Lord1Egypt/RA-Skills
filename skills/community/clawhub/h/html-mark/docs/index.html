Is there a caffeine-free option?

Yes — flip the "low & no caffeine" toggle in preferences and we'll build your box from herbals and decaffeinated lots only.

Can I gift a subscription?

Three, six, and twelve-month gift passes ship in a letterpress box with a handwritten note. No auto-renew surprises for the recipient.

Do you ship outside the US?

Canada and the UK today; EU is on the roadmap for autumn. Join the waitlist and we'll email when your country opens up.
