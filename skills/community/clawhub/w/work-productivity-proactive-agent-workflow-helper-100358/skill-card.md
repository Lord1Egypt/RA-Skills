## Description:

Helps agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn Proactive Agent-style workflow demand into practical plans, checklists, artifacts, analysis, code changes, and verification notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to clarify proactive workflow requirements, produce concrete work artifacts, and validate results without needing the original demand sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked too broadly because its keywords and implicit invocation policy are generic.

Mitigation: Use it for explicit proactive-agent workflow design, reliability, hardening, or adjacent skill-planning requests, and review trigger wording before deployment.

Risk: Workflow guidance may produce incomplete or misleading plans if the user's constraints are underspecified.

Mitigation: Restate outcomes, constraints, available inputs, and success criteria, then surface assumptions and remaining risks in the final output.
