## Use Case:

External users, individual investors, long-term holders, and learning-oriented investors use this skill to generate structured stock research reports covering macro context, industry dynamics, company fundamentals, valuation, scenario probabilities, contradictory evidence, and decision guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce buy, add, reduce, or clear-style investment recommendations that users could over-trust.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research support, not licensed financial advice, and require independent review before acting.

Risk: Market data, financial metrics, currencies, or growth rates may be inaccurate, stale, or fabricated by the agent using the skill.

Mitigation: Verify key numbers against current primary or reputable market sources and use the skill's own cross-checking and source-labeling requirements.
