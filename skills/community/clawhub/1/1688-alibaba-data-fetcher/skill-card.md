## Description:

Collects 1688 seller workspace and Business Advisor data through a bundled browser extension, generates a daily operations report, and can push the report to Feishu.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howerlin0329](https://clawhub.ai/user/howerlin0329)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers, operators, and agents use this skill to gather 1688 store traffic, product, inquiry, transaction, customer, fulfillment, channel, and keyword metrics. It turns those metrics into a daily Markdown operations report and can send the report to a configured Feishu chat.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad browser and messaging privileges could expose login QR codes, seller analytics, or Feishu tokens beyond the requesting user.

Mitigation: Review before installing and run only in a dedicated, low-privilege environment with a separate browser profile and private storage for QR images, tokens, and raw reports.

Risk: Extension access that is broader than the 1688 workflow needs could collect or expose unrelated browser data.

Mitigation: Restrict extension permissions to 1688 origins and remove wildcard external extension access before deployment.

Risk: Automated Feishu delivery can send sensitive login or seller data to an unintended group.

Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation of the Feishu recipient before forwarding login QR codes or seller reports.

Risk: Downloaded browser or runtime installers may be tampered with before use.

Mitigation: Verify downloaded installers by hash or signature before installation.
