## Description:

Generate visually unified image-based PPT/PPTX decks from articles, reports, papers, notes, or outlines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[notegpt-official](https://clawhub.ai/user/notegpt-official)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other users can turn articles, reports, papers, notes, or outlines into visually unified slide decks for presentations. The skill is suited to image-based slides; individual text, charts, and shapes are not separately editable.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Source material or images may be sent to an external image provider without an explicit disclosure or authorization check.

Mitigation: Before use, confirm the selected provider and obtain authorization for the material; avoid submitting confidential content unless the provider and its handling terms are approved.
