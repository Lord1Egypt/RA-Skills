## Use Case:

Food inspection personnel, testing institutions, meat processors, and slaughter enterprises use this skill to classify fresh or frozen pig by-products, verify source documents, apply sampling rules, and prepare standards-based pass/fail judgments under GB/T 9959.4-2019.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat standards guidance as a substitute for official regulatory review or accredited laboratory procedures.

Mitigation: Use the skill as reference guidance and confirm operational decisions against official standards, local regulatory requirements, and qualified inspection or laboratory procedures.

Risk: The skill is focused on Chinese GB/T pig by-product inspection standards and does not provide multilingual guidance.

Mitigation: Require review by qualified users who can read the source-language standard and understand the applicable inspection context.
