## Description:

Tracks recent public reporting on the US-Iran conflict to help agents produce attributed situation reports on military actions, official statements, and international reactions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and analysts can use this skill to guide high-tempo public-source monitoring of the US-Iran conflict and generate structured, attributed reports. It is intended for recent situational awareness, not for treating agent output as confirmed operational fact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for confirmed operational intelligence in a fast-moving conflict.

Mitigation: Treat reports as public-source situational awareness, preserve attribution, and verify important claims against reliable sources before acting on them.

Risk: Recent multilingual reporting may include translation errors, propaganda, or uncorroborated claims.

Mitigation: Check translations and source reliability, and separate verified facts from competing or unconfirmed claims.
