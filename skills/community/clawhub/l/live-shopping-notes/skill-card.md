## Description:

Capture and summarize shopping notes during livestream commerce sessions, including product records, deal tags, and post-stream decision lists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External shoppers and agents can use this skill to record items, prices, deal notes, highlights, and interest levels during livestream shopping sessions, then produce summaries and purchase decision lists after the stream.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shopping notes can include product preferences, links, prices, and other personal buying context retained locally.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal or financial information, and review the host environment's local storage retention and deletion behavior before use.
