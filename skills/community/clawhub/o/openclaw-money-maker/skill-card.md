## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill suggests recurring autonomous activity through scheduler and HEARTBEAT entries.

Mitigation: Do not add recurring jobs unless continuous autonomous operation is intended; require manual approval for claims, submissions, and public posts.

Risk: The skill suggests storing platform credentials in a plaintext JSON file.

Mitigation: Store credentials in a keychain or secret manager, use scoped revocable tokens, and avoid saving reusable passwords.

Risk: Money-making workflows can encourage public posts, claims, or submissions that may violate platform rules or misrepresent capability.

Mitigation: Review platform terms and approve each public action before submission or posting.
