## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers use this skill to plan and manage hiring for freelance, contractor, physical-task, and AI-agent work, including platform selection, vetting, contract terms, payments, and review checkpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hiring workflows can create payment, contractual, legal, or physical-world obligations when an agent is separately given platform or payment authority.

Mitigation: Set explicit budgets, approval thresholds, credential-sharing limits, and human-review requirements before allowing payment, dispatch, legal, or regulated work.

Risk: Contractor classification, tax, privacy, IP assignment, or jurisdiction questions may require legal judgment.

Mitigation: Escalate ambiguous classification, high-value contracts, international engagements, disputes, unusual terms, and regulated work to qualified human review.

Risk: Delegating physical tasks may expose sensitive information or create safety and verification concerns.

Mitigation: Share only minimum necessary information, use vetted platforms, require proof of completion, and require live or human confirmation for high-value or urgent tasks.
