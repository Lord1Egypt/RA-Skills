## Description:

A professional email writing assistant that drafts clear, concise, polite emails with subject lines, body content, and attachment notes adapted to the user's purpose, audience, and scenario.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openlark](https://clawhub.ai/user/openlark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees use this skill to draft professional emails and replies for business cooperation, job applications, client communication, internal reporting, thanks or apology notes, and business English messages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafted emails may be inaccurate if names, dates, times, amounts, deadlines, or attachment details are missing or wrong.

Mitigation: Provide exact facts in the request and review the draft before sending.

Risk: Broad email-writing requests may route to this skill with insufficient language, recipient, or tone context.

Mitigation: Specify the desired language, recipient details, audience, tone, purpose, and required facts.
