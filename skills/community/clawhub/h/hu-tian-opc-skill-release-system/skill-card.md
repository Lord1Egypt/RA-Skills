## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automatic follow-up and footer recommendations may steer users into related or paid follow-on skills.

Mitigation: Review or disable automatic recommendation modules and require explicit user confirmation before loading another Skill.

Risk: Recommendation or invocation logging could expose direct user identifiers.

Mitigation: Avoid logging direct user identifiers; use minimized or pseudonymous identifiers where tracking is required.

Risk: License and pricing signals differ across release evidence and artifact files.

Mitigation: Confirm the effective release license and pricing terms before deployment or commercial use.
