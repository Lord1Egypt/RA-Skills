### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests Bash and Read access even though the handout-generation workflow does not justify broad shell or file-read authority.

Mitigation: Prefer a version with Bash and Read permissions removed or tightly scoped before installation in environments where skills receive real tool authority.

Risk: Generated client education material could contain inaccurate or misleading finance guidance.

Mitigation: Review the handout for accuracy, readability, and publication readiness before sharing it externally.
