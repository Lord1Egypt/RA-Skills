## Use Case:

External users and travel-planning agents use this skill to produce concise cycling-route recommendations, check-in route guidance, filtering criteria, safety notes, and practical trip details for trending cycling outings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cycling-route guidance can become unsafe or inaccurate when weather, closures, permits, crowding, or on-site rules change.

Mitigation: Verify real-world conditions, permits, route closures, weather alerts, crowding, and local safety requirements before relying on recommendations.
