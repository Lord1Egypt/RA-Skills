## Description:

Write detailed, academically rigorous Chinese methodology chapters or methodology sections for theses, dissertations, and undergraduate papers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junwugit](https://clawhub.ai/user/junwugit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, academic writers, and advisors use this skill to draft or revise Chinese thesis methodology sections after classifying the study as quantitative, qualitative, mixed methods, literature-review-based, or intervention-scheme design.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated methodology text may omit local school requirements, research ethics details, or unresolved placeholders.

Mitigation: Review the draft against institutional guidance, study-specific ethics requirements, and all placeholder fields before using it in academic work.
