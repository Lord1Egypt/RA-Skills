## Description:

Drafts or reviews an OECD TPG benchmarking memo for one intercompany transaction, covering FAR analysis, tested-party and method selection, comparables search logs, adjustments, interquartile-range conclusions, and reviewer sign-off boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Transfer-pricing professionals use this skill to draft a defensible benchmarking memo for a single controlled transaction inside a BEPS Action 13 Local File. It structures intake, FAR analysis, tested-party and method selection, comparables review, adjustments, range conclusions, and sign-off checks for qualified tax review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the draft memo as tax advice or rely on unverified transfer-pricing positions.

Mitigation: Use the output only as a drafting aid for a qualified transfer-pricing professional and require independent review before any return position, filing, APA, or MAP submission.

Risk: Confidential intercompany agreements, company financials, or comparables extracts may be shared without appropriate authorization.

Mitigation: Confirm confidentiality posture before intake and share only information that is appropriate for the session.

Risk: Incorrect citations, calculations, comparables, or jurisdiction-specific tax overlays could make the memo unreliable.

Mitigation: Independently verify citations, calculations, comparables data, and country-specific tax positions before relying on the memo.
