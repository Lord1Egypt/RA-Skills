## Description:

Audits the numeric balance of completed tabletop RPG rule systems, including level curves, economy, combat pacing, equipment, skills, consumables, and other numeric subsystems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External tabletop RPG designers and developers use this skill to review whether completed rule systems have stable, playable numeric balance across progression, combat, economy, equipment, skills, consumables, and custom subsystems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The checklist can surface balance concerns but does not prove a tabletop RPG is balanced without design review and playtesting.

Mitigation: Use the skill's findings as review guidance, then validate proposed changes through human review, scenario simulation, and playtesting.

Risk: The skill text is Chinese-language, which may reduce clarity for users who do not read Chinese.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to provide the review in the user's preferred language when needed.
