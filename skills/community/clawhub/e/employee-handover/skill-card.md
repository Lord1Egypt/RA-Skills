## Description:

Generates structured employee handover materials from authorized task records, work chats, contacts, and local work-folder information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and operations staff use this skill to turn departing employee work records into a signable handover package with business sources, data storage locations, ongoing items, appendices, and coverage gaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive work chats, task records, contacts, and local work-folder metadata.

Mitigation: Confirm authorization, data scope, time range, participants, and recipients before use; keep intermediate files in a controlled location and review or delete them after completion.

Risk: Handover content assembled from screenshots, pasted text, or manually supplied records may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review generated tables against source records, disclose coverage gaps, and have the handover parties validate critical dates, owners, and open items.
