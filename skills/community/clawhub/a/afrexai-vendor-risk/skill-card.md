## Description:

Evaluate and score vendors on security, financial stability, compliance, operational dependency, and data handling to classify risk and manage remediation plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Procurement teams, CISOs, and operations leaders use this skill to assess critical vendors, assign risk tiers, and plan review cadence or remediation across a vendor portfolio.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Vendor financials, contracts, security questionnaires, customer concentration, spend, and compliance details may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Treat assessment inputs as confidential, minimize shared details, and keep outputs within approved storage and access controls.

Risk: The skill includes promotional external AfrexAI links that are not required for the assessment to function.

Mitigation: Review optional external links under the organization's normal third-party content policy before using them.

Risk: Vendor scoring can be incomplete or misleading when based on stale questionnaires, missing financial data, or unverified compliance claims.

Mitigation: Confirm high-impact inputs with current vendor documentation and route high or critical findings through the organization's vendor risk process.
