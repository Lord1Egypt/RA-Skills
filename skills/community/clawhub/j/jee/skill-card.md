## Description:

Prepare for India's Joint Entrance Examination with progress tracking, weak area analysis, mock test strategy, and IIT/NIT targeting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Students preparing for JEE Main or JEE Advanced use this skill to plan study schedules, track subject and chapter progress, analyze mock tests and mistake patterns, manage wellbeing, and evaluate realistic IIT/NIT targets. Parents, tutors, and coaching teachers can use its guidance for progress summaries and support planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide the agent to store exam goals, category, mock results, mistake logs, wellbeing notes, and parent or tutor reports in local plaintext files under ~/jee/.

Mitigation: Keep only necessary preparation records, avoid sensitive category or mental-health details unless they are needed, and periodically review or delete the local files, especially on shared devices or synced backups.

Risk: Rank, cutoff, and college targeting guidance is based on historical or approximate data and can become outdated.

Mitigation: Use the skill's targeting output as planning guidance and verify final eligibility, cutoffs, counseling rules, and deadlines against official exam and JoSAA sources before making application decisions.

Risk: High-intensity study plans can increase stress or burnout if followed rigidly.

Mitigation: Apply the documented wellbeing protocols, reduce workload when warning signs appear, and involve parents, counselors, or appropriate support resources for severe stress.
