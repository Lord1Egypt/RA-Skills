## Description: <br>
Generate and create UGC-style product review and testimonial videos using SJinn Seedance 2.0, with 9-layer prompt architecture for authentic selfie-style content. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jax-explorer](https://clawhub.ai/user/jax-explorer) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to collect campaign inputs, create structured Seedance 2.0 UGC video prompts, and submit generation jobs through SJinn when available. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts, product images, private likenesses, unreleased campaign details, or audio preferences may be sent to SJinn during generation. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid supplying confidential or sensitive campaign assets unless the user intends that material to be processed by SJinn. <br>
Risk: Generated testimonial-style prompts can imply product claims or endorsements that need review before publication. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated script, visuals, and claims for accuracy, permissions, and campaign compliance before using the video. <br>
