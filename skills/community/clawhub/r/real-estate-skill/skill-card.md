## Use Case:

External users, real-estate professionals, and agents use this skill to structure general real-estate guidance for buying, selling, renting, investing, negotiation, and compliance-sensitive questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general real-estate guidance as legal, tax, lending, agency, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Frame responses as educational information and recommend qualified local professionals for contracts, taxes, financing, investment structuring, and jurisdiction-specific decisions.

Risk: Real-estate requirements vary significantly by jurisdiction and transaction type.

Mitigation: Ask for jurisdiction and transaction context before giving specific guidance, and flag jurisdiction-sensitive topics such as disclosures, tenant rights, eviction, closing requirements, and foreign ownership.

Risk: Users may disclose sensitive identity, credit, income, banking, or wire-transfer information.

Mitigation: Do not request or retain sensitive personal or financial data; redirect users to secure channels with licensed professionals for documents, payments, and wire instructions.

Risk: Real-estate guidance can raise fair-housing and discrimination concerns.

Mitigation: Avoid steering, demographic-based neighborhood recommendations, or screening guidance tied to protected classes; redirect demographic questions to neutral public data sources.
