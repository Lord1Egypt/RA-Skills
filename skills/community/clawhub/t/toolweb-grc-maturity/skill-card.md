## Description:

Conducts Governance, Risk & Compliance maturity assessments and returns maturity scores, gaps, recommendations, maturity-level definitions, and GRC building-block guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Compliance officers, risk managers, internal audit teams, and GRC program owners use this skill to benchmark organizational maturity, identify capability gaps, and prioritize improvement actions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitted GRC maturity scores, session identifiers, timestamps, and optional user IDs are sent to an external service.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential control weaknesses or regulated data unless the organization has approved the provider and reviewed retention and processing terms.
