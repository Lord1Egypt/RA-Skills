## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for a birthdate and may use a name to generate deterministic results.

Mitigation: Use only the minimum personal details needed, avoid entering another person's information without consent, and do not share generated output when it exposes personal information.

Risk: Lottery-number output and fortune language could be mistaken for financial advice or a claim of improved odds.

Mitigation: Treat the generated numbers as entertainment and do not use them as a basis for financial decisions.
