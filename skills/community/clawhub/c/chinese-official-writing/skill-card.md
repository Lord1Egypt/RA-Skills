## Description:

Helps users draft, revise, review, and format Chinese official documents, workplace materials, and news copy from supplied facts and requirements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gongyu0918-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/gongyu0918-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Employees and external users can draft or revise Chinese official documents, operational materials, and news copy from supplied facts, existing drafts, and templates. They can also check genre, structure, tone, and Word layout.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The host runtime's permissions are unknown from the inspection results.

Mitigation: Review the permissions available to the skill in the host environment before enabling it.

Risk: Generated official or operational copy may rely on factual details or procedures that need current, authoritative confirmation.

Mitigation: Check material facts, dates, figures, and applicable procedures against the supplied records and current authoritative sources before use.
