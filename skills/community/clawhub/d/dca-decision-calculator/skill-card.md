## Description:

A finance calculator skill that uses user-provided index levels, yearly highs, holding cost, exchange rates, and budget inputs to produce structured DCA, exchange-cost, and portfolio-diagnosis reports with risk warnings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to calculate DCA trigger status, add-on multiples, exchange-cost comparisons, and portfolio health from user-supplied financial inputs. It is intended as an informational calculator and not as personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs may sound like actionable financial guidance because the skill covers investment and currency-exchange decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational calculations, verify inputs and formulas independently, and do not rely on them as personalized financial advice.

Risk: Broad finance-related triggers may activate the skill during ordinary finance conversations.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation or review whether the skill is appropriate before applying its output to a decision.
