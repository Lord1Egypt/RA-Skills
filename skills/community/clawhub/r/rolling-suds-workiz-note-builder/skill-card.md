## Description:

Build concise internal Rolling Suds Workiz notes for exterior cleaning sales and estimating workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mwdearing](https://clawhub.ai/user/mwdearing)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Rolling Suds sales or office users use this skill to turn quote intake, estimator results, appointment details, and messy job notes into concise internal Workiz notes for appointments, estimates, comparisons, and follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated notes may include customer contact, address, appointment, and pricing details.

Mitigation: Review each note before pasting it into Workiz and keep only the operational details needed for the internal workflow.

Risk: Summarized notes may omit or overstate job-scope, pricing, or follow-up assumptions.

Mitigation: Confirm unresolved scope, schedule, material, photo/access, and pricing details before using the note for sales or estimating decisions.
