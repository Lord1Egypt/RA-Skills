## Description:

Enrich and score sales leads using AI-powered analysis, data validation, and CRM integration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales teams, demand generation professionals, and marketing operations users use this skill to enrich contact records, validate lead data, score prospects, and prepare or sync qualified leads into CRM workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad CRM and enrichment authority can transmit or modify business contact data at scale.

Mitigation: Use approved API credentials, confirm datasets and destinations before sync, and keep automatic updates or merges disabled until field mappings and rollback procedures are reviewed.

Risk: Lead, CRM, or Slack data may contain sensitive business or personal data.

Mitigation: Review consent, retention, and audit obligations before enrichment; use audit logging and limit access to approved credentials and destinations.
