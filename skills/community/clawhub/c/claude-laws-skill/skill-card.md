## Description:

Learn the 48 laws of working with Claude, in plain words, with any AI agent. Your AI agent teaches each law in simple words, checks your prompt or workflow against the laws with evidence you paste in, then gives a fix draft for owner review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users who write prompts or run AI workflows can learn the 48 laws in plain language and assess a prompt or workflow using evidence they provide. The skill produces a reviewable fix draft for the owner.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A prompt or workflow assessment may be incomplete when the user provides limited evidence.

Mitigation: Treat laws without supporting pasted evidence as Not confirmed, and review the quoted evidence before acting on the assessment.

Risk: A suggested fix may not suit the owner's workflow or business context.

Mitigation: Review and approve the fix draft before applying any changes.
