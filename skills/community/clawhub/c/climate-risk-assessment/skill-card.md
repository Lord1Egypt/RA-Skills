## Description:

Assess physical and transition climate risk for a site, product, or portfolio with scenario-based structure, producing scenario-based hazard-exposure-vulnerability matrices, financial-impact ranges, confidence labels, and adaptation options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, sustainability teams, and business decision-makers use this skill to structure climate risk assessments for sites, products, or portfolios. It supports decision-making and reporting preparation by separating physical and transition risks across 2030, 2040, and 2050 scenarios.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Climate risk outputs may be mistaken for compliance-grade, actuarial, engineering, insurance, investment, or capital-planning conclusions.

Mitigation: Validate scenario selection, assumptions, and any figures intended for high-stakes use with qualified climate, engineering, actuarial, or compliance specialists before relying on them.

Risk: Assessments may require sensitive business data such as asset values, revenue dependence, supply chain nodes, workforce exposure, or insurance history.

Mitigation: Provide only the minimum sensitive business information needed for the assessment and label uncertain or assumed inputs for later review.
