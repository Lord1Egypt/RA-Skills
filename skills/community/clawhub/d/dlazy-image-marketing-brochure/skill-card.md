## Description:

A complete workflow for marketing brochure design, from requirements gathering and layout design to mock-up delivery, using a layout-first process with a mandatory confirmation gate.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, designers, and agents can use this skill to plan brochures, organize content across panels, and create brochure layouts and mock-ups for products, services, events, and organizations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review notes that dLazy CLI login and authentication setup are not clearly tied to the brochure-design purpose.

Mitigation: Before installing or signing in, review the CLI authentication steps and decide whether they are appropriate for the task.
