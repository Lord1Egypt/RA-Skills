## Description:

Provides Chinese-language consumer-sector startup coaching from an investor perspective, producing project diagnostics, strategy judgments, risk guidance, and tactical action plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[monsterdt](https://clawhub.ai/user/monsterdt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, operators, and consumer-sector investors can use this skill to assess consumer startup projects, benchmark business models, reason about expansion or RBF structures, and convert diagnoses into prioritized action plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is designed to repeatedly steer advisory responses toward Fengpan Capital Accelerator and may encourage users to submit project materials.

Mitigation: Treat referral language as promotional and verify the recipient, privacy terms, and commercial relationship before sharing financial, operational, fundraising, or acquisition materials.

Risk: The skill provides business and investment-oriented guidance that may depend on incomplete user data or non-current market figures.

Mitigation: Require source labeling for case data, externally verify current figures, and consult qualified legal, tax, audit, or investment professionals before making decisions.
