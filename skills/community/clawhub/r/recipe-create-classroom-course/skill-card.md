## Use Case:

Educators, administrators, or agents assisting Google Workspace users use this recipe to prepare the gws commands needed to create a Google Classroom course, invite a student, and list enrolled students.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Commands can create real Google Classroom courses and send real student invitations through an authorized Google Workspace account.

Mitigation: Replace sample course and student values carefully, confirm the target domain and recipient email, and consider testing with a non-production course or account before live use.
