## Description: <br>
Rewrites Chinese and English text to reduce recognizable AI-writing patterns and make the result read more naturally. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[xiaoyu-157](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoyu-157) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, editors, and agents use this skill to diagnose AI-like phrasing, revise text with fewer formulaic patterns, and produce a quality-check report for the edited result. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill is designed to make AI-generated writing appear human-written, which can be misused to bypass disclosure, academic, legal, or workplace rules. <br>
Mitigation: Use only where rewriting assistance is allowed, disclose AI assistance when required, and review revised text for accuracy, citations, quotations, formatting, and domain-specific constraints. <br>
