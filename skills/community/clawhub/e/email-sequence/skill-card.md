## Description:

Writes multi-email nurture, onboarding, launch, or re-engagement sequences with a mapped trigger, cadence, goal, subject, body, and CTA for each email.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketers, product teams, and founders use this skill to draft behavior-aware email sequences that move an audience toward activation, purchase, launch conversion, or re-engagement. The skill produces both the campaign structure and the full copy for each email.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated campaign copy could contain unverified marketing claims or inappropriate targeting assumptions.

Mitigation: Review generated emails against product facts, audience consent, targeting policy, and applicable marketing rules before sending.

Risk: Email sequences can continue after a recipient converts if exit conditions are not implemented.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's exit and suppression guidance so converted users stop receiving conversion prompts.
