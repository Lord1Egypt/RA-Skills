## Description:

AI X Twitter Post Generator creates editable, X-ready posts and slides with generated captions, images, text blocks, storylines, backgrounds, and structured visual layouts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ai-hub-admin](https://clawhub.ai/user/ai-hub-admin)

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to generate editable social media cards, posters, carousels, and X-ready visual posts from prompts, reference images, and layout settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, reference images, design settings, and the OneKey credential are sent to a hosted DeepNLP/OneKey service.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with content and credentials appropriate for that service, and avoid sensitive or private material unless its terms meet your requirements.

Risk: The artifact includes an unpinned npx CLI example that could change behavior between runs.

Mitigation: Prefer the documented HTTPS API call, or pin and review the CLI package before executing it.
