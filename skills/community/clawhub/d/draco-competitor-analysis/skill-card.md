## Description:

Write the competitor-analysis section of research reports, strategy decks, and product/brand studies, especially when the goal is to extract usable conclusions, recommendations, and strategic implications rather than summarize features.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[draco-kzn](https://clawhub.ai/user/draco-kzn)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and strategy or marketing teams use this skill to turn competitor information into decision-useful report sections, strategy deck content, product comparisons, campaign analysis, launch analysis, and brand upgrade recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may influence the structure and style of business analysis outputs, which can lead to misleading recommendations if source facts or assumptions are weak.

Mitigation: Review the evidence, competitor selection logic, assumptions, and recommendations before using the analysis for business decisions.
