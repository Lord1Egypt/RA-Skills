## Description:

Explains cap tables, dilution, SAFEs, option pools, and financing round mechanics in plain English with step-by-step math and worked before/after ownership breakdowns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External founders, operators, and startup advisors use this skill to understand ownership changes from priced rounds, SAFEs, convertible notes, and option pool changes. It helps turn incomplete financing terms into clearly labeled illustrative assumptions, ownership tables, dilution math, and watch-outs for founder-impacting terms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat educational dilution output as legal, tax, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Keep the disclaimer visible and verify legal, tax, and financing decisions with qualified counsel before acting or signing documents.

Risk: Incomplete financing terms can produce misleading ownership math if assumptions are not clear.

Mitigation: Ask for key deal details when available, label illustrative figures explicitly, and state every assumption used in the calculation.

Risk: Cap table results can be materially wrong when pre-money versus post-money treatment, SAFE conversion terms, or option pool timing are confused.

Mitigation: Show pre/post-money math step by step, compare SAFE cap versus discount terms explicitly, quantify option-pool impact, and check ownership tables sum to 100%.
