## Description:

Discover and run Subclip cloud image, video, video-avatar, music, and speech generation, then inspect, reuse, import, or delete persistent AI outputs. Use for prompts that ask Subclip to generate new media or revisit previous AI generations; do not use for ordinary timeline edits to existing assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to discover suitable Subclip models, plan and request image, video, avatar, music, or speech generation, and manage completed outputs in a project. It also guides previewing, importing, reusing, and deleting persistent generations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The scanner flags approval-related guidance that could encourage overriding independent approval safeguards.

Mitigation: Review the approval-related instructions before use and keep applicable independent approval safeguards in force.

Mitigation: Consider whether Subclip's external media services fit your privacy expectations before sending prompts or source media.
