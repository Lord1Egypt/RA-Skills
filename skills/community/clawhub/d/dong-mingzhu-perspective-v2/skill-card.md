## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic business phrases such as product-first or technology independence may unintentionally activate the persona.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly when that style is desired and review outputs before using them in decisions.

Risk: The perspective is distilled from public information and cannot predict Dong Mingzhu's actual response to new situations.

Mitigation: Treat responses as a strategic lens rather than an authoritative personal statement, and verify time-sensitive business facts separately.
