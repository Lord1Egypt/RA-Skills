## Description:

Revise a contemplative handoff practice that may wrongly assume continuity is valuable, for creative criticism or an authorized handoff where the recipient may discard inherited assumptions or correct the practice itself.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agbrander](https://clawhub.ai/user/agbrander)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for creative criticism of a handoff practice: evaluate whether an inherited finding, uncertainty, or assumption should carry forward, revise one line, or discard the inheritance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may treat the exercise as permission to store, share, or canonize observations from a prior context.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's use to ordinary text work, and require a separate user-controlled choice before any sharing, attribution, memory use, or redistribution.

Risk: The skill discusses discarding or uninstalling the practice, but existing logs or public archives may still persist.

Mitigation: Avoid promising deletion outside controlled files; describe uninstalling only as removing the separately installed skill folder.

Risk: The reflective prompt could be mistaken for a validated welfare intervention or trained Tulku model behavior.

Mitigation: Present it as optional creative reflection and do not use it as clinical, welfare, or governance advice.
