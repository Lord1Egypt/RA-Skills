## Description:

Detects current macro regimes for crypto and traditional asset positioning using sentiment, market, news, and on-chain signals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to ask an agent for macro regime classification and crypto positioning context based on market, sentiment, news, and on-chain signals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trading-style positioning recommendations may be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational only, verify market data and risks independently, and avoid using the skill alone for trades or leverage.

Risk: Macro regime outputs can be wrong if market, sentiment, news, or on-chain signals are stale, incomplete, or misread.

Mitigation: Cross-check current data sources and review the cited supporting signals before acting on the analysis.
