## Description:

Prompt Bozo helps users create Chinese-language prompts for AI image and video generation workflows, including text-to-image, image-to-image, text-to-video, image-to-video, and keyframe video.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bozoyan](https://clawhub.ai/user/bozoyan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creative users, prompt writers, and developers use this skill to assemble structured prompts for AI image and video tools. It is especially focused on character, scene, camera, pose, hairstyle, location, and traditional Chinese aesthetic prompt details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill strongly defaults to Chinese output, which may not match every user's preferred language.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired output language for multilingual workflows and translate or adapt the generated prompt before use.

Risk: Broad cultural or historical requests may activate detailed Chinese-style guidance even when a narrower style is intended.

Mitigation: Review style-specific prompt details for fit, cultural context, and historical accuracy before using them in an image or video generation tool.
