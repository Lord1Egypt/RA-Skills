## Description:

The pre-production system turns a video idea into a shootable, editable plan using a two-column AV script, storyboard or shot table, numbered shot list, batch-shoot plan, timing pass, and paper edit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, social media teams, and agents use this skill to convert a video idea or script spine into practical pre-production artifacts for filming, generative-video planning, editing, and publishing handoff. It is useful when shoots are chaotic, scripts run long, videos feel visually static, or content needs to be batch-filmed efficiently.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Planning output could be treated as approval to film, generate, edit, or publish without checking consent, disclosure, originality, or feasibility.

Mitigation: Require human review before production or publication, especially for real-person likeness, AI previz frames, staged scenes, timing claims, and production feasibility.

Risk: The skill may produce directional storyboards or AI-video shot briefs that users mistake for final footage assessment.

Mitigation: Keep the skill limited to pre-production planning; the human must shoot or generate, judge every take or clip, and approve final use.

Risk: Story or script planning can drift into staged-as-candid endorsements or copied creator scripts.

Mitigation: Use disclosed dramatizations or consented real reactions, and write original scripts based on structure study rather than verbatim copying.
