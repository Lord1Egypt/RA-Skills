## Description: <br>
Digital Persona helps agents research and draft SOUL.md persona files for deceased figures using seven-dimensional personality, appearance, mannerism, voice, language, and emotional-response modeling. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and content teams use this skill to turn sourced material about deceased figures into a structured SOUL.md persona file for agent roleplay or interaction, while marking speculative boundaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated personas may be mistaken for authentic statements from deceased people. <br>
Mitigation: Present generated personas as simulations, distinguish sourced facts from inferred patterns, and avoid claiming the person actually said unsupported content. <br>
Risk: Persona workflows can be misused for deception, harassment of relatives, living-person impersonation, or legal-signature scenarios. <br>
Mitigation: Do not use the skill for living people, deceptive contact, harassment, legal signatures, or any output that hides its simulated and speculative nature. <br>
