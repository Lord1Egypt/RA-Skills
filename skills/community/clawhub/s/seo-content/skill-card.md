### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional SEO tooling or URL fetching may involve third-party services and user-provided URLs.

Mitigation: Review DataForSEO or seogeo setup separately and only fetch URLs the user authorizes.

Risk: Referenced companion files or skills may be unavailable when the skill is installed alone.

Mitigation: Confirm companion references are installed or note the limitation in reports.

Risk: Heuristic content scores may be interpreted as guaranteed ranking outcomes.

Mitigation: Label scores as advisory and validate findings against official Google guidance and first-party data.
