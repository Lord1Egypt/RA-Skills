## Description:

Generates structured Chinese information-flow advertising video scripts from product name, selling points, and duration, including scenes, voiceover, subtitles, and timing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[suwangsen](https://clawhub.ai/user/suwangsen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing and creative teams use this skill to turn product details into shoot-ready Chinese ad video scripts for short-form information-flow placements. It helps produce timed scenes, voiceover, subtitles, shooting notes, and platform suitability from a compact product brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated advertising scripts may include commercial claims such as prices, stock limits, sales volume, or guarantees that do not match the actual offer.

Mitigation: Review generated claims before use and align them with product facts, campaign terms, and applicable advertising requirements.
