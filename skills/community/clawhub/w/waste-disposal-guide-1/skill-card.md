## Description:

Guide for disposing specific chemical wastes into the correct colored waste containers, with safety precautions and regulatory compliance notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Laboratory users and support agents use this skill to look up standard chemical waste container categories, disposal guidance, and safety notes for named chemicals or waste types. It is not intended for emergency spill response, radioactive waste, biological waste, controlled substances, or final regulatory certification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chemical-mixture disposal guidance can produce unsafe recommendations that contradict the skill's own instructions.

Mitigation: Do not rely on this skill for mixed, ambiguous, incompatible, or high-risk waste streams until mixture parsing and safety validation are fixed; consult the institution's EHS office for disposal decisions.

Risk: Users may treat the guidance as final disposal authorization for regulated laboratory waste.

Mitigation: Use the output as preliminary guidance only and verify local regulatory and institutional requirements before disposal.
