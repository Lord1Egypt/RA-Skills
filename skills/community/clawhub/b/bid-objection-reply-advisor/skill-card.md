## Description:

Helps tender owners and procurement agencies register bid objections, assess whether each objection is founded, draft correction or rejection replies, and prepare internal complaint-risk forecasts for projects governed by China's tendering and bidding framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Procurement, legal, and tender-agency staff use this skill after receiving a bidder or stakeholder objection to check intake requirements, calculate response deadlines, analyze facts and law, draft a formal reply, and prepare internal next-step and complaint-risk materials. It is scoped to tendering and bidding objections, not government-procurement challenges or bidder-side complaint drafting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Deadline, suspension, or local-platform rule errors could create procurement compliance exposure if the draft is used without review.

Mitigation: Confirm the governing law, local trading-platform rules, received date, response deadline, and suspension obligation with legal or procurement staff before sending a reply.

Risk: The skill drafts legal and procurement guidance that may be incomplete for the specific jurisdiction or project record.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting support and verify cited statutes, facts, project documents, and administrative-supervision authority before acting.

Risk: External replies or internal risk notes could disclose bidder confidential information if project evidence is copied too broadly.

Mitigation: Review generated text for commercial secrets, bidder identities, bid details, evaluation committee information, and other protected content before release.
