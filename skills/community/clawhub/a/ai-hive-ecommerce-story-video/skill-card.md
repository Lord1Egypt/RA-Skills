## Description:

A Chinese-language ecommerce video workflow that turns product facts, user pain points, evidence-backed selling points, and brand rules into story-driven shopping videos, product shorts, ad variants, scripts, prompts, captions, voiceover copy, AI-HIVE task records, and platform adaptations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to plan evidence-backed product videos and ad variants for marketplaces and social commerce platforms. The workflow emphasizes verified product facts, brand rules, asset rights, platform constraints, and claim review before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated ecommerce copy or video plans could publish inaccurate claims, prices, promotions, endorsements, or product specifications if the inputs are not verified.

Mitigation: Confirm all claims, prices, promotions, certifications, endorsements, inventory statements, and product parameters against user-approved or traceable sources before production.

Risk: Product references, user reviews, music, voices, portraits, logos, or platform assets may be used without sufficient rights.

Mitigation: Use only authorized assets and keep an asset-rights record for product images, footage, reviews, sounds, voices, likenesses, and brand marks.

Risk: AI-HIVE generation may alter product appearance, packaging, logos, proportions, or other details that need to remain exact.

Mitigation: Use authorized product reference images when accuracy matters, generate small batches first, and review product identity before scaling production.

Risk: Platform-specific versions may violate channel rules or imply unsupported third-party affiliation.

Mitigation: Review each platform adaptation for safe areas, CTA language, restricted claims, disclosure requirements, and neutral use of third-party platform names.
