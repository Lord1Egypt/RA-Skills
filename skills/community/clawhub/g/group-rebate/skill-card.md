## Use Case:

External shoppers, group-buy leaders, and community commerce operators use this skill to compare group-buy rebate opportunities, estimate leader commissions and member rebates, and draft daily recommendation lists for shopping groups.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce rebate recommendations or earnings estimates that are outdated, incomplete, or promotional.

Mitigation: Verify product prices, rebate rates, commission terms, and availability against the relevant shopping platform before buying or sharing recommendations.

Risk: Generated group-buy messages, shared links, or earnings tracking could affect other shoppers if used without review.

Mitigation: Keep group-message sending, link sharing, and rebate or commission tracking under explicit user review.
