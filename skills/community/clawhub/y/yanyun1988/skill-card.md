## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Speculative or stale stock commentary could be mistaken for personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat the skill's stock commentary as historical opinion and verify dates, prices, filings, rumors, and personal risk tolerance before acting.

Risk: The artifact includes concrete trading actions, holdings, and timing opinions that may no longer be current.

Mitigation: Surface dates when summarizing the material and avoid presenting archived positions or opinions as current recommendations.
