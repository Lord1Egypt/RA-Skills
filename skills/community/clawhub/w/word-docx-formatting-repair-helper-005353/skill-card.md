## Description:

Helps users plan, automate, and verify Microsoft Word DOCX formatting, find-and-replace, style cleanup, and related document repair tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Office workers, legal and operations teams, students, and documentation maintainers use this skill to turn DOCX cleanup requests into concrete workflows, checklists, templates, automation outlines, code, or validation steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bulk formatting or find-and-replace guidance can unintentionally damage important documents.

Mitigation: Work on copies or backups first, then validate formatting and content before applying changes broadly.

Risk: Broad implicit trigger settings may cause the skill to appear for loosely related productivity prompts.

Mitigation: Confirm the request is about Word, DOCX formatting, document cleanup, styles, or find-and-replace before relying on the specialized workflow.
