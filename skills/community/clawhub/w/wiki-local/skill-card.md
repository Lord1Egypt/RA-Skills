## Description:

Personal knowledge wiki managed by your agent. Create, link, search articles. Build a searchable knowledge base through conversation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[theshadowrose](https://clawhub.ai/user/theshadowrose)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to let an agent create, update, link, search, and export a local personal knowledge wiki made of markdown articles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local wiki edits can accidentally add, replace, or change important notes.

Mitigation: Keep independent backups and ask the agent to confirm before adding or replacing articles when accidental edits would matter.
