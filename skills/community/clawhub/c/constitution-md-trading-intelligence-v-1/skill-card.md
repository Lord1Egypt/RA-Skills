## Description:

Delivers high-confidence intraday trading intelligence on select pairs using AMD, FVG, and multi-timeframe analysis with strict risk and news filters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Traders and market analysts use this skill to generate manual intraday market briefs, session updates, setup alerts, and risk warnings for XAU/USD, EUR/USD, GBP/JPY, USD/JPY, and BTC/USD. It is intended as decision-support text for human review, not automated trade execution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market or news data used with the skill may be incomplete, stale, or incorrect.

Mitigation: Verify market structure, prices, spreads, and high-impact news independently before acting on any brief or setup alert.

Risk: Users may treat generated setup alerts as automatic trade instructions.

Mitigation: Use outputs only for manual analysis and human review; do not connect the skill directly to broker execution.

Risk: Messaging automation could distribute unreviewed trading guidance.

Mitigation: Separately review any WhatsApp or other messaging integration and keep a human approval step before sending alerts.
