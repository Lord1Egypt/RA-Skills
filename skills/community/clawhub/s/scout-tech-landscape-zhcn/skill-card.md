External users, analysts, and strategy teams use this skill to produce Chinese-language materials technology landscape reports from user queries and optional patent, literature, publication, or industry context.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Materials landscape reports may include unsupported or stale market, patent, or investment claims when source material is incomplete.

Mitigation: Provide reliable source material and verify market, patent, and business claims before using the output for business or investment decisions.

Risk: The skill may answer in Chinese by default, which can be unsuitable for audiences expecting another language.

Mitigation: Specify the desired response language or request translation when needed.
