## Description:

Helps founders and builders mine real pain points from Reddit and turn them into buildable MVP plans for idea validation, market analysis, and business scoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ange-web3](https://clawhub.ai/user/ange-web3)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, indie hackers, SaaS builders, cross-border entrepreneurs, and AI tool developers use this skill to identify target subreddits, extract recurring pain points, design a seven-day MVP, draft Reddit validation posts, and score commercial viability.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated Reddit validation posts may violate subreddit rules or read as promotion.

Mitigation: Review each subreddit's rules and edit posts for community fit before posting; do not auto-post generated content.

Risk: Reddit-derived research can include privacy-sensitive details or unsupported conclusions.

Mitigation: Preserve source links, avoid collecting unnecessary personal data, and add privacy and consent checks before following analytics or session-recording recommendations.

Risk: MVP and commercial scoring can overstate demand when based on limited Reddit samples.

Mitigation: Treat scores as planning guidance and validate with additional sources, direct user conversations, and human review before investing.
