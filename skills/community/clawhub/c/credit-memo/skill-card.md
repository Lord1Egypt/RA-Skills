## Description:

Write a credit memo for a lending decision: borrower story, facility structure, repayment sources, financial-ratio spread with covenant headroom, risk factors with mitigants, risk-rating rationale, and a recommendation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Credit analysts, relationship managers, and lending teams use this skill to draft committee-ready credit memos for loan requests, including borrower context, facility terms, repayment analysis, covenants, risks, mitigants, rating rationale, and recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Borrower and financial information may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Use the skill only where sharing that information with the agent is approved.

Risk: Assumed figures, ratios, covenant headroom, ratings, or recommendations may be inaccurate or inconsistent with institutional policy.

Mitigation: Review all assumptions and outputs against source financials, credit policy, approval authority, and applicable regulation before committee use.
