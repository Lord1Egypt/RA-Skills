## Description:

Use when commercial yarn, thread, or fiber becomes unreliable, expensive, or unavailable and you need custom-strength, colorable, repairable yarn from local wool, flax, or plant fiber for knitting, weaving, sewing, or trade goods.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to plan drop-spindle yarn production from local wool, flax, or plant fiber while the agent handles fiber assessment, twist and yardage calculations, schedules, logs, reminders, inventory cards, and barter templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The install command executes remote package code through npx.

Mitigation: Use a trusted registry, avoid elevated privileges, and prefer a pinned or platform-verified installer when available.

Risk: The skill may store spinning logs, recipes, photos, reminders, and inventory-style templates on the filesystem.

Mitigation: Store only intended project data, review generated files before sharing, and avoid adding sensitive personal or location details to logs or templates.

Risk: Drop-spindle work involves repetitive motion and fiber dust exposure.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's break prompts, stop if wrist or shoulder pain occurs, work in a ventilated area, and use a mask when dust is a concern.
