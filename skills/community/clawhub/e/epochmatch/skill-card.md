## Description:

Cross-matches star catalogues from different epochs by propagating positions with proper motions before matching.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raktikoo](https://clawhub.ai/user/raktikoo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Astronomers and developers use EpochMatch to compare catalogues observed at different epochs, account for stellar proper motion, and review matched, unmatched, and ambiguous candidates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
