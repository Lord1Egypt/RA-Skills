## Description:

将主题、课堂 PDF 或转录稿转化为结构化学习笔记，包含关键概念、定义、示例、大纲、概念关系和复习问题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students, educators, and knowledge workers use this skill to turn educational source material into concise, organized study notes with outlines, definitions, examples, concept relationships, and review questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated notes may omit context or present source material too confidently.

Mitigation: Review notes against the original material and treat [需要扩展] markers as prompts for additional source context.

Risk: Users may submit educational content they do not want summarized or retained in an agent conversation.

Mitigation: Provide only content appropriate for summarization in the target environment.
