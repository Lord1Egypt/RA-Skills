## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad comparison wording such as "versus" or "alternative to."

Mitigation: Use it for SEO competitor comparison or alternatives-page drafting, and confirm the requested task matches that intent before relying on the output.

Risk: Competitor feature and pricing claims can be inaccurate or become outdated.

Mitigation: Verify claims against public sources, cite those sources, and include an "as of" date for pricing before publication.

Risk: Comparison content can become misleading if it omits competitor strengths or product affiliation.

Mitigation: Review for balanced presentation, disclose affiliation, and remove unsupported or defamatory claims.
