## Use Case:

External users and solo-business builders use this skill to map personal capabilities, industry experience, hidden assets, and market demand into a differentiated one-person-company position. The skill produces SWOT analysis, recommends expert, tool, or platform paths, and summarizes next actions in a Markdown report.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may collect sensitive personal career history, strengths, network resources, and business goals, then persist them in a Markdown report.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to confirm before saving the report, omit sensitive details when possible, and review the generated file before sharing it.

Risk: Market opportunities, income ranges, and positioning recommendations can be misleading if based on stale or weak evidence.

Mitigation: Use current official or primary sources for market checks where browsing is available, and treat the report as planning guidance rather than a guaranteed business outcome.
