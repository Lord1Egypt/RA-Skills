## Description:

Writes targeted 180-220 word freelance proposals from job postings or URLs by analyzing client needs, scoring opportunity fit, and drafting a problem-led proposal.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kevdogg102396-afk](https://clawhub.ai/user/kevdogg102396-afk)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Freelancers and consultants use this skill to evaluate job postings, skip poor-fit opportunities, and generate paste-ready proposals that lead with the client's problem and a concrete solution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use broad shell access while processing job URLs.

Mitigation: Prefer pasted job descriptions or web retrieval; review any proposed shell command before allowing it, especially for untrusted URLs.
