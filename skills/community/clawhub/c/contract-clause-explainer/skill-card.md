## Description:

Explain a contract clause in plain language: what it obligates, what it permits, and what to negotiate — without pretending to be a lawyer.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

People reviewing freelance agreements, SaaS terms, partnership documents, or other contracts can use this skill to understand a clause's obligations, permissions, triggers, exit terms, and negotiation options. It explains contract language and flags when high-stakes questions should go to a lawyer.
