## Description:

Your AI think tank. Five domain-expert Fellows debate any idea, decision, or creative work. Three rounds. One verdict that's actually useful.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers use FlowCouncil to stress-test decisions, brainstorm ideas, or review existing work through a structured five-fellow debate. Deep mode can add web research before the debate for topics involving meaningful time or money commitments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Significant user-derived decisions may be written to persistent memory without clear consent or retention controls.

Mitigation: For sensitive sessions, instruct the agent not to save the verdict unless you explicitly approve the exact memory entry.

Risk: Deep mode uses web research and may expose or derive sensitive strategy, legal, finance, architecture, or customer context during research.

Mitigation: Use Quick mode for confidential material, or provide only sanitized context before allowing Deep mode research.
