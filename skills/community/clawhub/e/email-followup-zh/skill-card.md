## Description:

Email Followup Zh drafts polite, appropriately firm Chinese follow-up emails for sales, business development, and customer service teams when customers or partners have not replied.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[olina1ye](https://clawhub.ai/user/olina1ye)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales, business development, and customer service teams use this skill to draft Chinese follow-up emails for stalled customer, partner, quote, contract, order, or collaboration conversations. It supports first follow-ups, second reminders, and urgent nudges with tone adapted to waiting time and relationship context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafting follow-up emails may require customer, contract, or pricing context.

Mitigation: Provide only the details needed for the draft and avoid unnecessary sensitive business information.

Risk: A generated follow-up may use a tone or business claim that is inappropriate for the recipient relationship.

Mitigation: Review and edit the draft before sending so the tone, urgency, and claims match the actual relationship and deal status.
