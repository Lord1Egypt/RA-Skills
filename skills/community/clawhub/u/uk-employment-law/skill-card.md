## Description:

Calculates UK statutory employment entitlements and generates HR letter templates using 2025/26 rates and ERA 2025 reforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

UK small business owners, office managers, startup founders, bookkeepers, and other employers use this skill to calculate statutory employment entitlements and draft HR letters for common workplace situations. Outputs are intended as templates and calculations for review, not legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United Kingdom

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Employment law calculations or HR letter drafts may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific contract, workplace policy, or legal dispute.

Mitigation: Review outputs against current law, employment contracts, workplace policies, and qualified HR or legal advice before use.

Risk: Dismissal, redundancy, grievance, disciplinary, settlement, immigration, and discrimination-sensitive matters can create material legal exposure if handled only from generated text.

Mitigation: Use outputs as drafts or checklists and obtain specialist review before sending formal communications or making employment decisions.
