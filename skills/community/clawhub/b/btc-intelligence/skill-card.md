## Description:

Crypto market data for agents from AgentReeve - prices, candles, derivatives (funding, basis, open interest), long/short positioning, order-book liquidity and a one-call snapshot for any coin on Binance, Bybit, OKX and Hyperliquid, scanners for extreme funding and crowded trades, and Bitcoin depth (options, liquidations, whales, similar setups). Paid per request over x402, free samples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Developers and agents use this skill to retrieve crypto market prices, candles, derivatives, positioning, and liquidity data across supported exchanges, and to obtain Bitcoin focused market summaries. It supports market research with free samples and paid live data requests.
