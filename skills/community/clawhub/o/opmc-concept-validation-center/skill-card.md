## Description:

OPC Concept Validation Center guides agents through six-dimension concept validation for TRL 1-4 ideas, covering technical, commercial, resource, team, risk, and conclusion review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad concept-validation or feasibility-review phrasing.

Mitigation: Review trigger behavior during installation and scope usage to concept validation, feasibility assessment, TRL review, and related evaluation requests.

Risk: Advisory validation output could influence business or technical decisions if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Use the output as structured guidance and require human review before making investment, pilot, or handoff decisions.
