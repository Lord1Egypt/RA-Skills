## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cashback rates, coupon codes, promotional activities, and rebate links may be stale or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Verify rates, coupons, activities, and links against Rakuten or merchant pages before acting on them.

Risk: The skill may activate for broad U.S. or overseas cashback queries where Rakuten is not the intended source.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking about Rakuten cashback before using the generated guidance.
