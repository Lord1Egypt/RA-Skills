## Description:

A plain-English portfolio reading skill that turns a holdings list into understandable risk patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this prompt-only skill to review approximate crypto holdings and understand concentration, overlap, stablecoin assumptions, exposure imbalance, and missing information before making portfolio changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake educational portfolio risk framing for financial, tax, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as explanatory risk patterns and reflection questions, and require independent professional review before acting on portfolio, tax, or investment decisions.

Risk: Approximate or incomplete holdings can lead to uncertain concentration and exposure analysis.

Mitigation: Label uncertainty clearly, work with ranges when needed, and state what information is missing.

Risk: The skill cannot calculate live valuations, taxes, or actual correlations.

Mitigation: Avoid price prediction and direct users to current market, tax, or correlation data when those calculations are required.
