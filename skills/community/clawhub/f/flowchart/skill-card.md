## Description:

Turn a process, workflow, or decision logic into a clean flowchart.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, operators, and other external users use this skill to turn described processes, workflows, or decision logic into readable Mermaid flowcharts with legends and assumptions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad charting requests may trigger this skill when another diagram type is intended.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants a flowchart for process, workflow, or decision logic before applying the skill.

Risk: Ambiguous process descriptions can lead to incorrect branches or assumptions in the generated diagram.

Mitigation: State assumptions explicitly, label all decision branches, and review the rendered Mermaid flowchart before use.
