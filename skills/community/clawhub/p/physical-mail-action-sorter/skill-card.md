## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may accidentally share sensitive identifiers from bills, notices, healthcare mail, insurance mail, or government mail.

Mitigation: Use only sender names and general envelope clues; do not share account numbers, IDs, payment details, passwords, one-time codes, or private case numbers.

Risk: Mail organization could be mistaken for legal, tax, medical, insurance, credit, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Keep the skill to organizing paper mail and action steps, and direct users to professional or official-channel help for high-stakes notices and disputes.

Risk: Official, ambiguous, or deadline-bearing mail could be discarded before the user verifies its contents.

Mitigation: Place uncertain items in Review This Week, avoid discarding official notices without review, and make the user responsible for verifying contents and deadlines.
