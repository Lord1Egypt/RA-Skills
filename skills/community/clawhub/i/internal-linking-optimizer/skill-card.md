## Description:

Plans website information architecture and internal linking by producing page hierarchies, URL taxonomy, navigation specs, hub-and-spoke link plans, Mermaid site maps, orphan-page diagnostics, anchor-text checks, and structure scores.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO practitioners, marketers, and site owners use this skill to design or restructure site architecture and to improve internal-link flow from a sitemap, page inventory, domain, or target article.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Redirect, noindex, deletion, or navigation recommendations could affect search visibility if applied without review.

Mitigation: Treat structural changes as proposals and have an authorized site owner review them before implementation.

Risk: Domains, page inventories, crawl outputs, or analytics exports may contain sensitive site or business information.

Mitigation: Provide only data you are allowed to analyze and redact confidential details that are not needed for structure planning.

Risk: Saved handoff summaries may retain client strategy, page inventories, or unresolved decisions.

Mitigation: Approve memory saves only when retaining the results for future work is intended.
