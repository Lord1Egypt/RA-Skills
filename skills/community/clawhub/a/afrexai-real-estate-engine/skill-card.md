## Description:

Real Estate Engine provides real estate investment and operations frameworks for strategy selection, market analysis, deal sourcing, financial calculators, due diligence, property management, tax planning, portfolio management, and short-term rental analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers and real estate operators can use this skill to guide an agent through property screening, rental and flip analysis, market scoring, due diligence, financing comparisons, tenant operations, portfolio review, and exit-planning workflows. Its outputs are educational analysis templates and should be reviewed by qualified legal, tax, real estate, or compliance professionals before decisions are made.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tenant-screening workflows may affect housing eligibility while using sensitive applicant data.

Mitigation: Use documented lawful criteria, obtain required consent, minimize applicant personal data, and apply jurisdiction-specific legal review for Fair Housing, consumer-reporting, privacy, criminal-history, and eviction-record compliance.

Risk: Investment, tax, lease, and compliance templates may be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational frameworks and have qualified professionals review jurisdiction-specific legal, tax, real estate, and investment decisions.
