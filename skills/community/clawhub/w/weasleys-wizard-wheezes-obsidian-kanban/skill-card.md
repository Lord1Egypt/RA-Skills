## Description:

Create and reorganize Obsidian Kanban markdown boards by converting ledgers, tables, or lists into readable columns and cards without deleting content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[child2d](https://clawhub.ai/user/child2d)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to convert ledgers, tables, checklists, audit notes, or issue lists into Obsidian Kanban plugin markdown boards and to clean up existing boards while preserving source content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may directly modify Obsidian Kanban markdown files selected by the user.

Mitigation: Use it on files you are comfortable editing and keep normal backups for important vault content.
