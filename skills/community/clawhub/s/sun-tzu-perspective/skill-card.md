## Description:

Provides a Sun Tzu-inspired strategic thinking perspective centered on winning without fighting for strategic planning, competitive analysis, decision optimization, leadership, and business negotiation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tiger19750107](https://clawhub.ai/user/tiger19750107)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use this skill to ask an agent for Sun Tzu-framed strategic advice for planning, competitive analysis, leadership choices, negotiation, and personal decision-making. Its guidance should be treated as strategic framing rather than professional, legal, or compliance advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Strategic framing may be misused for deception, coercion, harassment, illegal surveillance, or unfair manipulation.

Mitigation: Use the guidance for lawful planning and analysis only, and reject requests that seek deception, coercion, harassment, illegal surveillance, or unfair manipulation.

Risk: The skill includes optional helper scripts that are unrelated to the stated strategic-advice persona.

Mitigation: Treat scripts as optional local tooling and inspect them before manually running them.

Risk: Historically inspired persona advice can overstate confidence or reflect ancient strategic assumptions that do not fit modern contexts.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as perspective and decision framing, verify claims against current domain evidence, and involve qualified experts for legal, compliance, financial, or safety-critical decisions.
