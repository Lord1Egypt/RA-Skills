## Description:

Review contracts for risks, unfair terms, and missing clauses. Generates plain-English summaries, risk flags, and negotiation talking points. Use when someone needs a contract reviewed, wants to understand terms, or needs to negotiate better conditions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users such as freelancers, sole traders, and SME owners use this skill to review UK-oriented contracts before signing. It summarizes key terms, flags risky or missing clauses, and drafts plain-English negotiation points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United Kingdom

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks users to paste contracts that may contain personal data, trade secrets, privileged material, or commercially sensitive details.

Mitigation: Users should avoid pasting unnecessary sensitive information unless they are comfortable with how their agent environment handles that content.

Risk: The skill applies UK-oriented contract risk standards and may be unsuitable for non-UK agreements or unfamiliar jurisdictions.

Mitigation: For non-UK contracts, treat the output as a checklist only and consult qualified local counsel.

Risk: The output may miss legal nuance in high-value, complex, or unusual agreements.

Mitigation: Use the review as risk-flagging support, not legal advice, and consult a qualified solicitor before relying on it for high-value or complex contracts.
