## Description:

Qualify sales prospects from company lists with social profiling, trigger event research, and personalized outreach angles. Saves 10+ hours/week on manual prospect research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tktk-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/tktk-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales teams, founders, agencies, and SDRs use this skill to turn company or contact lists into ranked prospect profiles with trigger events, likely pain points, and personalized outreach angles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lead research may involve personal or professional contact data and public social-profile information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for lawful B2B prospect research, provide authorized company or professional contact data, avoid sensitive personal data, and define retention, opt-out, and deletion practices for lead lists.

Risk: Prospecting workflows can conflict with platform or CRM terms if users collect or reuse data outside allowed purposes.

Mitigation: Respect LinkedIn, X, CRM, and other source terms before using generated prospect profiles or outreach recommendations.
