## Description:

A Chinese productivity-coaching persona that uses randomized nagging characters to prompt users or agents when they procrastinate, while backing off for rest, leave, and urgent incidents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dataelf](https://clawhub.ai/user/dataelf)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and agent operators use this skill to add a Chinese, deliberately pushy productivity coach that nudges progress on code, meetings, documents, reviews, and replies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad procrastination triggers and a deliberately harsh coaching tone may activate when unwanted or feel stressful in sensitive contexts.

Mitigation: Enable the skill only where a pushy Chinese productivity coach is desired, and avoid use in support, wellness, crisis, or other stress-sensitive settings.

Risk: Escalation rules can produce high-pressure language when users repeatedly delay or say they forgot a task.

Mitigation: Keep the artifact's limits active: do not nag between 2:00 and 7:00, when the user is resting or on leave, or during urgent production and emergency incidents.
