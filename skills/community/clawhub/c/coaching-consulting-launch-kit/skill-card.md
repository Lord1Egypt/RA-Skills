## Description:

Generates a complete launch kit for coaches and consultants including a signature offer, enrollment script, content plan, and case study assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitflopez](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitflopez)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Coaches, consultants, fractional executives, and expertise-based service providers use this skill to turn their positioning, sales conversation, content plan, and proof assets into a launch-ready business kit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Case studies and testimonials may expose identifiable client details, quotes, employer names, revenue figures, salary figures, or private circumstances.

Mitigation: Get documented client permission before publishing identifiable material; use aliases, composites, or aggregated results by default, and clearly label reconstructed or hypothetical content.
