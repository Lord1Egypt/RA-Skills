## Description:

Generates data-backed enterprise patent strategy research reports in HTML format using PatSnap data, covering company overview, core competitiveness, competitive landscape, market opportunities, key risks, strategic recommendations, and PatSnap solution sections, with core claims supported by patent or literature evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External business, patent, and IP strategy users can use this skill to create executive patent strategy reports for enterprises. The skill structures patent, literature, competitive, risk, strategy, and PatSnap service evidence into a browser-readable and print-ready HTML report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill steers generated patent strategy reports toward PatSnap commercial services, pricing, and Eureka/TRIZ positioning.

Mitigation: Review outputs as vendor proposal material and use the skill only when PatSnap service recommendations are desired; otherwise remove or make the commercial solution section optional before use.

Risk: Patent strategy claims, market figures, and competitive conclusions may be misleading if they are not supported by current patent, literature, policy, or attachment evidence.

Mitigation: Require every core claim to include patent or literature evidence and mark unsupported data as Unverified before sharing the report with executives.
