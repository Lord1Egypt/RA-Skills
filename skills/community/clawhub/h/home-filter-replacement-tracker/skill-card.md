## Description:

Build a household filter inventory with locations, sizes, replacement intervals, next due dates, shopping list, and reminders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External household users use this skill to organize routine home filter replacements, calculate due dates, build shopping lists, and keep a reusable replacement log.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the tracker for repair, diagnostic, or safety advice.

Mitigation: The skill directs users to follow manuals, landlord or building rules, and qualified professionals for unsafe or uncertain equipment access.

Risk: Unknown filter sizes, model numbers, access rules, or replacement dates can lead to incorrect purchases or unsafe inspection attempts.

Mitigation: The skill marks unknowns for verification and avoids asking users to open sealed systems, remove safety panels, access live components, or perform risky maintenance.
