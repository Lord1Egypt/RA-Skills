## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer agents toward an unofficial Dutch Bros menu guide for prices, availability, ingredients, nutrition, and allergens.

Mitigation: Use the guide as a convenience reference and verify important details with official Dutch Bros sources or a local store.

Risk: Menu details can vary by location or season, which may make exact prices or item availability inaccurate.

Mitigation: Present prices and availability as approximate unless current local confirmation is available.

Risk: Nutrition-related answers could be mistaken for dietary or medical advice.

Mitigation: Frame nutrition information as menu reference material and advise users to verify ingredients, allergens, and dietary needs with official sources.
