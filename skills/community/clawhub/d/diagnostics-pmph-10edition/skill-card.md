## Description:

A clinical diagnostics skill collection based on People's Medical Publishing House Diagnostics, 10th Edition, covering 183 skills for history taking, physical examination, laboratory interpretation, ECG and imaging analysis, differential diagnosis, procedures, and clinical reasoning.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Clinicians, medical students, residents, laboratory and imaging technicians, nursing staff, and public health professionals use this skill as an educational reference and clinical training aid for diagnostic reasoning, test interpretation, physical examination, and procedural checklists. It should not replace qualified clinical judgment or current local medical guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: High-risk clinical procedure and treatment guidance may be used without qualified oversight.

Mitigation: Limit use to education or clinician-supervised decision support, and require escalation of diagnosis, procedures, medications, anesthesia, and emergency care decisions to qualified medical professionals.

Risk: Medical content may not reflect the latest clinical guidelines or local protocols.

Mitigation: Cross-check outputs against current official guidelines, local safety policies, and specialist advice before clinical use.

Risk: Non-clinicians may treat educational material as patient-specific medical advice.

Mitigation: Review before installation in non-clinician settings and present outputs as training or reference material, not a replacement for professional medical judgment.
