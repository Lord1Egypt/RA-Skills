## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language travel and cultural guide to Gaozhou, Guangdong, covering geography, lychee culture, Lady Xian heritage, attractions, food, transportation, and practical visitor information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Travelers, tourism planners, and agents serving Gaozhou-related queries use this skill to summarize local geography, cultural history, attractions, food, transportation, seasonal travel timing, and practical contact information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: English-language users may not be fully served because the inspected skill body is Chinese-only despite metadata listing English.

Mitigation: Use Chinese-capable agents or add reviewed English content before advertising English coverage.

Risk: Travel details, seasonal recommendations, contact information, and transportation guidance may become outdated because the skill is static.

Mitigation: Verify current visitor information against official local sources before relying on the guidance.
