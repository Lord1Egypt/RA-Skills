## Description:

Guides the creation, critique, and redesign of audience-centered charts, slides, dashboards, and reports using the Storytelling with Data method.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ricardodpalmeida](https://clawhub.ai/user/ricardodpalmeida)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts, developers, and other people communicating data use this skill to choose and improve visuals, clarify the main takeaway, and shape evidence into decision-focused slides and reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A chart or narrative may mislead if its source, units, denominator, or contradictory evidence are unclear.

Mitigation: Check the data source, period, units, and denominator; preserve missing values and acknowledge evidence that contradicts the takeaway. Leave unsupported claims open.
