## Description:

Provides daily accountability coaching with two modes: supportive encouragement or savage roasts, mixing humor and motivation to boost habit consistency.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People who want habit accountability use this skill for morning check-ins, evening reviews, and motivational feedback in either a supportive or opt-in roasting tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sharp teasing or high roast intensity may demotivate or distress vulnerable users.

Mitigation: Use supportive mode as the default, require explicit opt-in for savage mode, and keep intensity low for users who are struggling.

Risk: Scheduled check-ins may feel intrusive if enabled without clear consent.

Mitigation: Enable morning and evening reminders only after user consent and provide an easy way to turn them off.
