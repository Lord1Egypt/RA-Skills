## Description:

生成、改写和润色校园学生场景的规范中文邮件与书面公文，覆盖课程或活动请假、导师沟通与毕业设计进度汇报、实习岗位投递、奖学金申请与自述、调课申请、校园活动正式通知与群发邮件等。

## Use Case:

Students, campus staff, and activity organizers use this skill to create polished Chinese campus emails, applications, self-statements, reports, and notices from their scenario details and personal information. It is suited for copy-ready drafts that preserve required unknown fields as clear placeholders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated drafts may still contain placeholders or details that require user confirmation before sending.

Mitigation: Review every draft, replace all `【待填写】` placeholders, and confirm dates, recipients, attachments, and requested actions before use.

Risk: Campus emails and applications can involve sensitive student, medical, family, contact, or academic-record information.

Mitigation: Include only details the recipient truly requires and avoid unnecessary sensitive information unless the user explicitly chooses to provide it.
