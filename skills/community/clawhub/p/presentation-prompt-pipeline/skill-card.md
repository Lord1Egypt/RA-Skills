## Description:

Use this skill when building or repairing a presentation with an AI model — the six staged prompts (blueprint → structure → story frame → visual direction → content → build) that turn an idea into a deck with one message, each stage producing an artifact and a checkable gate, with prompt templates, eight named failure modes, and a deck_lint.py script that mechanically enforces short titles, tight bullets, speaker notes, a two-font and three-colour ceiling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Presentation authors use this skill to turn a brief or source material into a focused deck through six staged prompts, with a checkable gate at each stage. Reviewers can use its linter to check slide text and formatting rules before reviewing rendered slides.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested claims, examples, or chart choices in a presentation may be inaccurate or misleading.

Mitigation: Check factual claims, figures, and chart choices against the source material before presenting the deck.
