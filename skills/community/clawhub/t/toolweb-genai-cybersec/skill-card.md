## Description:

Generate personalized cybersecurity transformation roadmaps based on Microsoft's 5-point blueprint for GenAI-driven cyber defense.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd](https://clawhub.ai/user/krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Security leaders, CISOs, and enterprise teams use this skill to generate strategic GenAI cybersecurity transformation roadmaps from organizational maturity, challenges, goals, budget, and timeline inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Organizational security posture, challenges, goals, budget, and optional contact or user identifiers may be sent to the API.

Mitigation: Use pseudonymous identifiers and omit unnecessary sensitive details unless they are required for the roadmap.
