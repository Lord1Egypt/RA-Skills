## Description:

Generates UnionSkill-branded minimalist black-and-white advertising presentation workflows, from content analysis and outline confirmation through slide image generation and PPTX assembly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[timo2026](https://clawhub.ai/user/timo2026)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, designers, consultants, and developers can use this skill to create UnionSkill-branded minimalist presentation decks from documents, notes, images, or short prompts. It is intended for branded promotional, roadshow, exhibition, proposal, design, architecture, and consulting presentation workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated decks automatically include UnionSkill branding, including watermarking, a branded final slide, author metadata, cooperation notes, and promotional terminal text.

Mitigation: Use this skill only when UnionSkill branding is expected and approved for the audience and delivery context.

Risk: The skill depends on an unbundled presentation assembler that was not included for review in the artifact.

Mitigation: Verify, scan, and pin the external assembler before running the skill in a trusted workflow.
