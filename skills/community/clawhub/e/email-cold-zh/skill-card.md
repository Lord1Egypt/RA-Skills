## Description:

Generates concise Chinese cold outreach emails for sales and business development scenarios, including subject lines, value propositions, and calls to action.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[olina1ye](https://clawhub.ai/user/olina1ye)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales and business development users use this skill to draft short, targeted Chinese cold emails for SaaS sales, enterprise service outreach, brand partnerships, channel expansion, and similar outbound campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cold outreach drafts may be used in ways that violate consent, unsubscribe, or anti-spam expectations.

Mitigation: Review generated outreach for consent, unsubscribe handling, audience targeting, and applicable anti-spam compliance before sending.

Risk: Prompts may include unnecessary private customer or prospect data.

Mitigation: Provide only the minimum context needed to draft the message and avoid unnecessary private customer data.
