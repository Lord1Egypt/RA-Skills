## Use Case:

External creators and prompt writers use this skill to turn short creative ideas into complete Seedance 2.5 video prompts. It supports single-shot prompts, character-focused prompts, long-form narratives, video extension, editing, transitions, and white-model rendering prompts.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompts may include unrelated author branding, ideology, or an email address.

Mitigation: Review generated prompts before use and remove unwanted branding or contact details when they are not appropriate for the task.

Risk: Creative prompts can include references to protected characters or recognizable IP if supplied by a user.

Mitigation: Use the skill's own IP-conversion guidance: describe original traits and explicitly avoid known character appearances, names, logos, or signature elements.
