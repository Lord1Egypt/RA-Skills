## Description:

Build a fast guest-ready home reset for people expecting visitors soon, including a 60-minute reset plan, supplies list, quick task assignments, guest essentials, and welcome note template.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill when guests are arriving soon and they need a practical, visible home reset plan with task assignments, supplies, guest essentials, privacy checks, and a welcome note.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Household guidance may be mistaken for professional cleaning, medical allergy, accessibility, sanitation, or remediation advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as practical prompts and seek appropriate professional help for mold, pests, biohazards, sewage, smoke damage, strong chemical exposure, medical allergy needs, or accessibility assessment.

Risk: Fast reset plans could overlook privacy, scent sensitivity, pet exposure, cleaning chemical, or trip-hazard concerns.

Mitigation: Review privacy, allergy, scent, pet, cleaning product, ventilation, and floor-safety prompts before acting on the plan.
