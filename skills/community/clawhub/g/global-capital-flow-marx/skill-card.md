## Description:

Analyzes macroeconomic events through a Marxist contradiction framework and four dimensions—time, space, class, and coercion—to identify core tensions and assess possible paths.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External users and analysts use this skill to examine macroeconomic and geopolitical events, trace who benefits and who bears costs, and compare conditional scenarios using observable evidence.
