## Description:

Full EPA and state environmental regulatory compliance guidance for manufacturing, construction, energy, logistics, and other operations with environmental obligations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Operations, environmental health and safety, and compliance teams use this skill to identify applicable EPA and state requirements, track permits and reporting deadlines, prepare inspection checklists, assess violation risk, and plan corrective actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Facility and operations details may include unnecessary identifiers or sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Provide only the facility and operations details needed for the compliance question and redact unnecessary identifiers where possible.

Risk: Regulatory and penalty guidance may be incomplete or unsuitable as a final legal determination.

Mitigation: Use the skill's output as planning support and have qualified environmental compliance or legal personnel verify obligations, deadlines, and penalty exposure.
