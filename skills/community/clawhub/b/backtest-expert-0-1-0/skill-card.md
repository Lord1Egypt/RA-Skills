## Description:

Expert guidance for systematic backtesting of trading strategies, including robustness testing, slippage modeling, bias prevention, and interpretation of backtest results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, quantitative researchers, and traders use this skill to design, validate, stress-test, and troubleshoot systematic trading strategy backtests. It is intended as methodology support, not financial advice or authorization to place trades.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trading-related guidance could be mistaken for financial advice or authorization to place live trades.

Mitigation: Use the skill as backtesting methodology support only; users should make independent trading, compliance, and risk decisions.

Risk: Backtest results can be misleading when affected by overfitting, look-ahead bias, survivorship bias, unrealistic fills, or insufficient sample size.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's stress tests, bias checks, pessimistic friction modeling, and out-of-sample validation before relying on results.
