## Description: <br>
Decompose business scenarios into Agent capability modules, tool chains, and implementation roadmaps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[bettermen](https://clawhub.ai/user/bettermen) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, developers, and agent builders use this skill to turn business scenarios or PRDs into structured capability modules, tool/API plans, user stories, implementation phases, and risk notes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sensitive roadmap, customer, architecture, or operational details may be written into local Markdown output. <br>
Mitigation: Preview generated documents and require explicit approval before saving files that contain confidential planning content. <br>
Risk: When project tracking or document connectors are available, generated planning content may be synced to Linear, Jira, Notion, or Feishu. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the target workspace and content scope before external sync, and disable connectors when working with restricted information. <br>
Risk: Requirement decompositions and implementation roadmaps may be incomplete or misaligned with actual delivery constraints. <br>
Mitigation: Have product and engineering reviewers validate capability modules, tool dependencies, priorities, and risks before execution. <br>
