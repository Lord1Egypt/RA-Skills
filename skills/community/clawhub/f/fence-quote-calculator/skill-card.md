## Description:

Calculate material takeoffs and quotes for wood, vinyl, and aluminum fence projects, including posts, pickets, concrete, gate hardware, and material costs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[reesepj](https://clawhub.ai/user/reesepj)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External contractors or estimating users can use this skill to turn fence job details into a material takeoff for quoting and purchasing materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fence quantities may be inaccurate when the layout, corners, gates, fence type, or section spacing differ from the skill's assumptions.

Mitigation: Confirm the project layout, gate configuration, fence type, and local material specifications before using the takeoff for purchasing.

Risk: The skill excludes labor costs, so its output is not a complete job bid.

Mitigation: Use the material takeoff as input to a separate labor and pricing workflow.
