## Description:

A Chinese-language personal-growth skill that uses multiple mentor-style perspectives to diagnose growth questions and provide concise, actionable advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[web3dog8](https://clawhub.ai/user/web3dog8)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for Chinese-language personal-growth reflection across cognition, decisions, habits, emotions, business, and interpersonal situations. It returns a structured diagnosis, selected mentor-style viewpoints, a memorable principle, and up to three concrete actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat persona-style personal-growth advice as professional medical, mental-health crisis, legal, financial, or other regulated advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for general reflection only, and seek qualified professional support for medical, crisis, legal, financial, or similarly high-stakes decisions.

Risk: The package publicly includes a phone number for contact.

Mitigation: Review the published contact information before redistribution and remove or replace it if it should not remain public.

Risk: Persona style references could be mistaken for statements by the named people.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as style-inspired guidance and avoid presenting generated advice as the named individuals' actual positions.
