## Use Case:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encodes a particular Bible-study methodology and some doctrinal framing.

Mitigation: Compare interpretations with the user's preferred tradition, sources, or study materials when doctrinal nuance matters.

Risk: Bible-study output may contain incorrect citations, mismatched verse ranges, unsupported quotations, or proof-texted interpretations.

Mitigation: Verify references, translation labels, quoted text, and surrounding context before relying on the response.
