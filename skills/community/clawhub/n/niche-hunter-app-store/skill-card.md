## Use Case:

Developers, founders, and product researchers use this skill to run bounded App Store market research, analyze competitors, identify niche gaps, estimate monetization signals, rank opportunities, and generate an MVP PRD after selecting an opportunity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce heuristic proxy revenue estimates from ratings, pricing, and conversion assumptions.

Mitigation: Treat proxy revenue numbers as estimates, preserve the stated confidence level, and validate important business decisions against direct market or financial sources.

Risk: The workflow performs bounded web research against public App Store and competitor sources, so source quality and availability can affect conclusions.

Mitigation: Review cited sources, compare multiple signals when confidence is medium or low, and rerun research when App Store listings or competitor data change.
