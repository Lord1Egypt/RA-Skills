## Description:

Generates academic technology roadmap diagrams from ten templates using a research topic or custom JSON content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and proposal writers use this skill to turn research topics or structured study content into editable academic roadmap SVG diagrams.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated roadmap SVGs may include undisclosed promotional or social-media text in templates 24 and 27.

Mitigation: Review generated SVG output before academic or public use, and avoid or edit templates 24 and 27 when promotional text is present.

Risk: The release claims PNG export, but the included CLI only exposes SVG output.

Mitigation: Treat this package as SVG-only unless a separate trusted PNG conversion step is added.
