## Description:

Yi Jing helps an agent provide entertainment-oriented Chinese divination and cultural readings using I Ching hexagrams, Mei Hua Yi Shu, Ba Zi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Qi Men Dun Jia, prophecy texts, calendar calculations, and classical reference material.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent for entertainment and cultural readings based on I Ching divination, birth-chart style fortune analysis, calendar timing, and related classical Chinese reference texts. The skill is not appropriate for medical, mental-health, emergency, dietary, medication, postoperative, or other consequential decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could mistake divination, ritual, or health-related content for medical or mental-health guidance.

Mitigation: Present outputs as entertainment and cultural material only, and direct users to qualified professionals for medical, mental-health, emergency, dietary, medication, or postoperative decisions.

Risk: Birth date, birth time, location, and similar details can be personally identifying.

Mitigation: Ask only for details needed for the requested reading and avoid retaining or sharing identifiable birth information.

Risk: Calendar-related functionality may require package installation or local execution in some agent environments.

Mitigation: Review and approve any package installation separately, and run it in an isolated environment.
