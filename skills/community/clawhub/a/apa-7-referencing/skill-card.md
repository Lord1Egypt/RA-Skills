## Description:

Clean generic APA 7 referencing (English conventions): reference lists, in-text citations, DOIs, secondary sources, quotes, tables. Use for any paper requiring APA 7 style.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, editors, and writing assistants use this skill to format generic English APA 7 reference-list entries and in-text citations from source details they provide.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill formats APA 7 citations but does not verify source facts such as DOI values, dates, page numbers, or author details.

Mitigation: Confirm all source metadata against the original sources before using generated citations.

Risk: The guidance targets generic English APA 7 conventions and may not reflect institution-specific deviations.

Mitigation: Compare output with any applicable course, publisher, or institutional style requirements.
