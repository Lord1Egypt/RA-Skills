## Description:

End-to-end dropship product lifecycle pipeline covering CJ Dropshipping sourcing, margin checks, AI hero image generation, WooCommerce publishing, and CJ supplier mapping for auto-fulfillment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External developers and store operators use this skill to plan an agent-assisted dropshipping flow from product sourcing through WooCommerce listing and supplier mapping. It is intended for users who can provide CJ Dropshipping, WooCommerce, WordPress, FAL, and OpenAI credentials and review outputs before publishing.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The artifact references a pipeline script that is not included in the package.

Mitigation: Confirm the script source and contents before execution; do not assume the documented commands are runnable from this artifact alone.

Risk: The workflow can affect a live store, WordPress media library, CJ account, and paid AI APIs.

Mitigation: Use dry-run mode or a test store first, limit credentials to the minimum required access, and verify product details, pricing, and supplier mapping before publishing.
