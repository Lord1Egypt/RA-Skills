## Description:

Find and rank suitable academic journals for a manuscript using multi-source semantic matching, metrics, OA options, indexing, and predatory-risk checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[comeingwind](https://clawhub.ai/user/comeingwind)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, authors, and publication-support teams use this skill to identify, compare, and prioritize suitable journals for a manuscript based on title, abstract, keywords, publication preferences, fit signals, indexing, metrics, open access options, fees, timelines, and legitimacy checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unpublished manuscript details may be shared with external journal matching or publisher tools during matching.

Mitigation: Use the minimum necessary text, such as title, abstract, keywords, and preferences, and avoid sharing a full unpublished manuscript unless needed.

Risk: Journal metrics, acceptance rates, review timelines, and legitimacy signals can be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Cross-check recommendations against current publisher pages, indexing sources, open access directories, and journal legitimacy checklists before submitting.
