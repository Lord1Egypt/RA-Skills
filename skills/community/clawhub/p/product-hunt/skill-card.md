## Use Case:

Founders, makers, marketers, and agents supporting them use this skill to plan Product Hunt launches, coordinate with hunters, engage with the community, and interpret Product Hunt activity during competitive research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product Hunt platform practices and community rules may change or differ by context.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as operational guidance and verify current Product Hunt and community rules before posting, promoting, or asking others to engage.

Risk: Promotion, email, or community sharing can be perceived as manipulation or spam if handled carelessly.

Mitigation: Keep outreach user-directed, compliant with community rules, and focused on authentic feedback rather than soliciting votes.
