## Description:

This Chinese-language skill helps users structure business ideas with a nine-part Business Model Canvas, unit-economics checks, moat analysis, comparable cases, and MVP validation plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qqyougitcom](https://clawhub.ai/user/qqyougitcom)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users such as entrepreneurs, product managers, and business analysts use this skill to evaluate whether a business idea can work, identify key assumptions, and produce a Chinese-language business model canvas with validation and optimization guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is designed to produce Chinese-language business model guidance, which may not fit English or multilingual workflows.

Mitigation: Confirm that the agent can override or translate the output language before using it in non-Chinese workflows.

Risk: Business model recommendations may rely on estimates or comparable examples rather than validated company-specific data.

Mitigation: Review assumptions, unit-economics estimates, and validation plans against current business data before making decisions.
