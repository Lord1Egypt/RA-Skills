## Description:

Launch Tier Planner helps agents size product or feature launches, declare launch type, set D0/W1/M1 targets, build a risk register with kill criteria, and outline a T-8w to T+4w launch timeline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, product, and launch teams use this skill to decide whether a launch should be Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3 and to turn that decision into effort scope, KPI targets, risk ownership, rollback thresholds, and a timeline skeleton.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Launch history, analytics baselines, or partner launch details may contain sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive inputs where possible and confirm before saving launch plans or proposal records to memory paths.

Risk: Estimated KPI targets or rollback thresholds may be misleading if not grounded in the user's own baseline data.

Mitigation: Label estimates clearly and anchor KPI targets and kill criteria to user-provided or measured trailing baselines.
