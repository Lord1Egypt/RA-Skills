## Description:

Humanize Vietnamese ecommerce copy to reduce robotic AI phrasing while keeping the message persuasive and platform-appropriate.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers, marketers, and support teams use this skill to rewrite Vietnamese product pages, ads, seller replies, customer replies, captions, and selling scripts so they sound more natural while preserving the offer and call-to-action intent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used outside its Vietnamese ecommerce scope, such as translation or bilingual localization.

Mitigation: Use it only for Vietnamese ecommerce copy rewriting and route translation or localization work to a dedicated workflow.

Risk: Generated marketing language may still contain exaggerated, risky, or context-inappropriate claims.

Mitigation: Review the rewritten wording before publishing and use the skill's risk-safe wording swaps to replace problematic phrases.

Risk: Users may mistake the wording review for legal, medical, or regulated compliance review.

Mitigation: Treat the output as copywriting guidance only and have regulated claims reviewed through the appropriate compliance process.
