## Description:

Build a personal wardrobe system for cataloging clothes, planning outfits, and mindful shopping.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Individuals use this skill to organize a local wardrobe catalog, plan outfits from owned items, manage seasonal rotation, pack for trips, and make more intentional shopping or decluttering decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Wardrobe notes and photos may include personal style, purchase, donation, and storage details in local files.

Mitigation: Approve the ~/wardrobe/ location before setup and omit details or photos you do not want stored locally.

Risk: Outfit, shopping, decluttering, or donation suggestions may be incomplete if the wardrobe catalog is outdated.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against the physical wardrobe and update item records when clothing is bought, removed, repaired, or rotated seasonally.
