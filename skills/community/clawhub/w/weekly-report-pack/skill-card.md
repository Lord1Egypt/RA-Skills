## Description:

Auto-generate structured weekly, daily, and monthly reports from scattered work traces such as chats, todos, calendars, commits, and notes, with quantified wins, actionable risks, deliverable-focused next steps, and versions for different audiences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
