## Description:

流量重组洗稿虾 helps agents rewrite text content into original variants that preserve viral hooks, adapt style and platform fit, and include a brief compliance assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tujinsama](https://clawhub.ai/user/tujinsama)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content creators, marketers, and account operators use this skill to transform rights-cleared text into platform-adapted rewrites, alternate content angles, and compliance-review summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be misused to disguise copying, bypass plagiarism checks, or republish third-party content without rights.

Mitigation: Use it only for lawful, rights-cleared transformation, original drafting, or compliance review, and review outputs for attribution and originality before publication.

Risk: Platform-sensitive, regulated, or misleading claims may survive the rewrite process.

Mitigation: Apply the bundled compliance checklist, remove absolute or sensitive claims, and verify platform-specific disclosure, medical, financial, advertising, and copyright requirements before posting.
