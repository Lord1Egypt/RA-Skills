## Description:

Turns user-provided text into a structured infographic plan, validates it, and renders a self-contained static HTML infographic with optional PNG export.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and content authors use it to turn articles, notes, or conversations into a single-page infographic plan, check layout and numerical consistency, and render it for sharing or printing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
