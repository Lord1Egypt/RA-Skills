## Description:

Batch-grades English written homework for Chinese elementary students in grades 3-6 with rubric-based scores, corrections, and age-appropriate feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers, tutors, and learning-support agents use this skill to grade elementary ESL writing for Chinese students in grades 3-6. It supports sentences, paragraphs, dialogues, reading comprehension, and workbook exercises with configurable grade level, assignment type, rubric, score ceiling, and feedback language.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Homework submissions may contain student identifiers or sensitive personal details.

Mitigation: Remove unnecessary identifiers and personal details before submitting student work for grading.

Risk: Automated grading feedback may miss classroom context or produce marks that need teacher judgment.

Mitigation: Have a teacher or tutor review final scores and comments before sharing them with students or guardians.
