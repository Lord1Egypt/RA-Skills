## Description:

Expert change management advisor for enterprise transformations. Assess readiness, diagnose adoption risks, build sponsor plans, and drive stakeholder adoption in cybersecurity, cloud, AI, and compliance initiatives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and external practitioners use this skill to plan and assess enterprise change initiatives, including readiness diagnostics, sponsor action plans, adoption reviews, stakeholder impact analysis, and resistance response planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can invoke Bash to run its included Python assessment helper.

Mitigation: Review the command before execution and run only the bundled local helper for the intended readiness, sponsor, adoption, impact, or resistance task.

Risk: Change-management prompts may include sensitive business details.

Mitigation: Share only the business context needed for the advisory task and avoid unnecessary sensitive organizational, personnel, or compliance details.

Risk: The default output style uses Canadian English and a senior advisor voice.

Mitigation: Override locale or style requirements in the prompt when a different regional or organizational writing standard is needed.
