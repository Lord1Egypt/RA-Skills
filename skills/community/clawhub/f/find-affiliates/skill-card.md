## Description:

Find affiliates, publishers, review partners, content partners, and performance-based promotion opportunities for ecommerce offers. Use when the user wants help identifying affiliate candidates, shortlist likely partners, or compare affiliate fit by audience, content type, and commercial intent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators, marketers, and partnership teams use this skill to identify, compare, and prioritize affiliate or publisher prospects by audience fit, content fit, geography, and commercial intent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Affiliate recommendations may rely on public signals that are incomplete, outdated, or not directly validated by the publisher.

Mitigation: Review suggested partners and verify identity, audience fit, claims, and partnership terms before outreach.

Risk: Shortlists can be weak or misleading when the offer, audience, geography, payout preference, or exclusions are underspecified.

Mitigation: Clarify the product, target audience, market, affiliate model, and must-have criteria before prioritizing prospects.
