## Description:

视频脚本主笔虾 creates Chinese-language short-video shot scripts, podcast outlines, and content series plans from an account style profile and topic.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tujinsama](https://clawhub.ai/user/tujinsama)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, social media teams, and podcast producers use this skill to turn topics, articles, or trends into structured Chinese-language scripts, shot-by-shot production plans, podcast outlines, and multi-episode content plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use web search for topic research, so generated facts, statistics, and trend claims can be stale, incomplete, or source-sensitive.

Mitigation: Review factual claims against reliable sources before production use, especially for scripts that cite numbers, news, or current events.

Risk: The skill is optimized for Chinese-language content and broad content-writing prompts may activate it when another writing workflow was intended.

Mitigation: Confirm the target language, platform, account style, and output format before relying on the generated script.
