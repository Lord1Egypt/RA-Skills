## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on generated financial calculations without checking the assumptions or Python script.

Mitigation: Review the generated script, formulas, and return-cost assumptions before using the results for business decisions.

Risk: The skill is written primarily in Chinese, which may create review or operational misunderstandings for teams that do not use Chinese.

Mitigation: Confirm language fit before installation or translate the skill guidance before operational use.

Risk: Return impact can be understated if the scope excludes refunds, reverse logistics, resale recovery, support cost, or loss categories relevant to the business.

Mitigation: Clarify the return-impact definition and included cost categories before calculation.
