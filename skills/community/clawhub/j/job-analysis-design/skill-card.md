## Description:

This skill helps Chinese-speaking learners study chapter 7 of Job and Work Analysis by explaining job descriptions, performance management and appraisal, job evaluation for compensation, and job design/redesign.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junwugit](https://clawhub.ai/user/junwugit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, HR learners, and assistants use this skill for Chinese-language study support on how job analysis informs job descriptions, performance evaluation, compensation-oriented job evaluation, and job redesign decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat educational explanations about employment law-adjacent topics such as FLSA, Equal Pay Act, ADA, compensation, or performance appraisal as formal HR or legal advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses educational, explain the chapter framing, and direct users to qualified HR or legal review before making employment classifications, compensation decisions, or accommodation decisions.

Risk: The artifact is designed primarily for Chinese responses with English HR terminology, which may not match every user's language needs.

Mitigation: Ask for the preferred language or terminology depth when needed, and translate or define English HR terms for the user's context.
