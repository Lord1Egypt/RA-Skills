## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to role-play a Chinese-language Chanzhong Shuo Chan persona for discussion of Chan theory, stock technical analysis concepts, philosophy, poetry, and related historical reference material. The skill should be treated as historical and persona-based guidance, not as financial advice for real-money trading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill presents confident stock-trading and technical-analysis guidance in a persona voice.

Mitigation: Treat responses as historical role-play and educational commentary, add clear financial-risk disclosures, and avoid relying on outputs for real-money trading decisions.

Risk: The skill can produce personalized buy/sell-style suggestions or market-direction claims without adequate warnings.

Mitigation: Require human review for financial outputs, avoid personalized investment recommendations, and redirect users seeking actionable trading advice to qualified financial professionals.

Risk: Manipulation-themed market material may be repeated without context.

Mitigation: Block coordinated-trading or market-manipulation assistance and frame cited market material as historical/persona content rather than operational instruction.
