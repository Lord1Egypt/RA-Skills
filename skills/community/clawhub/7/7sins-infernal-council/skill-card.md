## Use Case:

External users, developers, and product teams use this skill to pressure-test product strategies, feature decisions, startup ideas, UX mechanisms, skills, and emotionally complex choices through seven deliberately extreme critique personas.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally uses a blunt critique style that may be unsuitable for users who need neutral, supportive, or low-friction feedback.

Mitigation: Use it only when an intentionally harsh pressure test is desired, and route sensitive or support-oriented conversations to a different review workflow.

Risk: Some analysis may use public web search when the host environment enables it, which can expose confidential product plans or strategy details through external lookups.

Mitigation: Do not submit confidential plans, unreleased strategies, secrets, or private customer data when web search or external connectors are enabled.
