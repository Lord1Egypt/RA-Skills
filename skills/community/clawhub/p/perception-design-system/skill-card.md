## Description:

A perception-driven product experience design methodology for mapping user psychology into trust, safety, transparency, and quality cues across software product experiences.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[fanleod](https://clawhub.ai/user/fanleod)

## Use Case:

Designers, product teams, and agents use this skill to evaluate or design product experiences with a four-level perception intensity matrix, four design dimensions, and MustBe/NotToBe constraints. It is especially relevant for finance, SaaS, e-commerce, tools, health, and other software products where trust, safety, transparency, and decision clarity matter.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes conversion-oriented UX guidance for finance, credit, repayment, health, and other sensitive workflows, where persuasive design can affect user autonomy, disclosures, fairness, or regulatory obligations.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as design prompts, not compliance guidance; require domain, legal, and accessibility review before using final designs in sensitive workflows.

Risk: The skill is primarily Chinese-language methodology content, so readers or agents may miss context or nuance when applying it across languages or regions.

Mitigation: Use bilingual or domain reviewers to confirm interpretation before applying the methodology to production design decisions.
