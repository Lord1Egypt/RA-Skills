## Description:

Helps automotive software quality and process improvement engineers prepare ASPICE assessments, analyze evidence-based gaps across SWE, SUP, SYS, and MAN process areas, and produce text and Markdown improvement reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External automotive software quality, engineering, and process improvement teams use this skill for ASPICE self-assessment preparation, customer audit readiness, gap analysis, and process improvement planning. It requires user-provided evidence and frames outputs as preparation support rather than official assessor conclusions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat assessment-preparation output as an official ASPICE assessment conclusion.

Mitigation: Use outputs only for self-assessment support and validate conclusions with qualified assessors and the organization's official PAM.

Risk: The skill asks users to provide enterprise process evidence that may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Share only evidence appropriate for the agent environment and follow internal data-handling rules before use.

Risk: Chinese-language guidance may not fit every review team or deployment context.

Mitigation: Confirm stakeholders are comfortable using a Chinese-language ASPICE preparation helper before installation.
