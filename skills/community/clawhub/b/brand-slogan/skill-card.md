## Description:

Generates Chinese brand slogans using a Huayuhua-style workflow that inventories brand value, creates a 7 by 7 strategy and rhetoric matrix, scores the results, and recommends the strongest options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cocokkxy](https://clawhub.ai/user/cocokkxy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External marketing, brand, and copywriting users can provide a brand name and supporting brand details to generate concise Chinese slogan candidates. The skill returns a value inventory, 49 slogan options, a scored Top 10, and Top 3 recommendation notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use web search when brand details are missing, which can introduce stale or inaccurate brand information.

Mitigation: Provide current brand facts and review generated slogans for accuracy before use.

Risk: Uploaded PPT or PDF brand materials may contain confidential launch plans, customer data, or unreleased positioning.

Mitigation: Avoid uploading sensitive brand documents unless agent processing of that information is acceptable.
