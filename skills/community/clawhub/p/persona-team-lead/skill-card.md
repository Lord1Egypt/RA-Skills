## Use Case:

Employees and team leads use this persona to run standups, prepare meetings and weekly digests, delegate email action items, and track OKRs with Google Workspace tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide an agent to post team information to Chat or append shared Sheet data without explicit confirmation or destination checks.

Mitigation: Before posting or appending content, verify the target Chat space or Sheet, review the exact content, and sanitize sensitive team data.
