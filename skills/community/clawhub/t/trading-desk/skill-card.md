## Description:

The Trading Desk provides options strategy templates, a strategy router, IV analysis guidance, and portfolio-management templates for AI-assisted options education.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nogravedev](https://clawhub.ai/user/nogravedev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent builders use this skill to guide AI agents through educational options strategy selection, IV analysis, portfolio review, and trading journal workflows. Outputs should be independently reviewed and not treated as professional financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Options strategy recommendations may be mistaken for professional financial advice or automated trading instructions.

Mitigation: Use the skill for education only; require independent human review before making trades or portfolio changes.

Risk: Market conditions, IV rank, and account constraints can be incomplete or stale, leading to unsuitable strategy selection.

Mitigation: Validate market data, assumptions, position sizing, and risk limits before acting on any strategy.
