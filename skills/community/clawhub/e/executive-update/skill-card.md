## Description:

Transform detailed product updates into concise executive briefings. Use when asked to write an executive update, leadership update, product update for the exec team, or a C-suite product briefing. Produces a structured 250-word briefing with headline, key metrics, progress, risks, decisions needed, and next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Product managers, operators, and leadership-facing contributors use this skill to turn detailed product notes into concise executive updates focused on metrics, progress, risks, decisions, and next steps.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Executive-update prompts may include confidential product, financial, or personnel information.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential details unless the agent environment is approved for that content; redact or aggregate sensitive information before use.

Risk: A compressed briefing can misstate metrics, risks, or recommendations if the source notes are incomplete or ambiguous.

Mitigation: Review the draft against source notes before sharing and confirm that metrics include comparison points, risks include mitigations, and decisions include recommendations.
