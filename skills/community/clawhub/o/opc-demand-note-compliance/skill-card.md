## Description:

Provides end-to-end demand promissory note compliance support, including contract element checks, document validation, case-library classification, and institution mapping for demand note, promissory note, SWIFT validation, endorsement, and fraud-risk scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External compliance, finance, and legal users use this skill to review demand promissory note workflows, validate note and contract elements, classify fraud cases, and prepare institution research briefs. It is intended as compliance assistance for demand note scenarios, including cross-border and SWIFT-related checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive financial, contract, KYC, UBO, and SWIFT-related information supplied by users.

Mitigation: Handle inputs under the user's data governance policy and require confirmation before saving or sharing case-library updates.

Risk: Compliance conclusions may be incomplete, jurisdiction-specific, or unsuitable as final legal advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs as compliance assistance and verify legal conclusions with qualified counsel before relying on them.
