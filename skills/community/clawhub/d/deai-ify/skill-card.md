## Description:

Edits or reviews text to identify and reduce common signs of AI-generated writing while preserving the intended meaning and voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hazy2go](https://clawhub.ai/user/hazy2go)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and reviewers use this skill to revise user-provided prose so it sounds more natural and less formulaic while preserving the original message.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be used to disguise AI-generated writing or misrepresent authorship.

Mitigation: Use it as a transparent editing aid and avoid using it for undisclosed AI-generated academic, professional, or platform-policy-violating work.

Risk: The skill's detector-focused guidance may encourage attempts to bypass AI detection systems.

Mitigation: Review outputs for policy compliance and require users to disclose AI assistance where rules, contracts, or institutional policies require it.
