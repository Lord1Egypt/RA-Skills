## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Illustrative assumptions or regulatory details may be outdated or unsuitable for a specific product or reporting context.

Mitigation: Verify current official requirements and company experience, document assumptions, and obtain qualified actuarial review before applying results or filing reports.

Risk: Insurance, health, and claims data can contain sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Use only authorized minimum-necessary data and de-identify or aggregate personal information before including it in prompts or drafts.

Risk: The security review was benign with medium confidence and did not cover three referenced files that were unavailable to the scan.

Mitigation: Review those referenced materials if they are important to the intended use.
