## Description:

Streamline approval for non-standard deals by enforcing discount limits, validating deal value, and managing custom terms to protect margin and speed closing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Revenue, sales, finance, and deal desk teams use this skill to review non-standard B2B deals, apply discount guardrails, assess margin impact, and route approvals for custom terms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing, margin, or approval advice could conflict with an organization's finance controls or authority matrix.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an advisory framework and require normal finance, management, and deal desk approval before finalizing discounts or terms.

Risk: Custom contract, liability, SLA, or data-processing guidance may be incomplete for a specific deal.

Mitigation: Route non-standard legal terms through legal review before customer acceptance.

Risk: External resource links may lead to optional commercial materials outside the skill artifact.

Mitigation: Review external links before adoption and keep vendor materials separate from internal policy.
