## Description:

提供应急方案结构化编制框架与模板，支持场景描述、措施制定、责任分工和流程设计；当用户需要编制突发事件应急预案、编写应急处置方案或完善应急管理体系时使用

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and organizational staff use this skill to draft or improve Chinese emergency response plans, including incident scenarios, responsibilities, response workflows, support measures, and drill plans. Real emergency plans should be reviewed by qualified local personnel before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated emergency plans may be mistaken for official legal, medical, or safety authority.

Mitigation: Review any real emergency plan with qualified local personnel before use.

Risk: Draft plans may omit site-specific hazards, contacts, resources, or local requirements.

Mitigation: Customize the template with current local information and complete the skill's operational checks before approval.
