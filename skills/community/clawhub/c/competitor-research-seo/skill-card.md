## Description:

Guides competitor research for SEO, content, backlinks, pricing, metrics, and positioning when planning content, auditing articles, building links, or evaluating market position.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, marketers, SEO specialists, and content teams use this skill to structure competitor analysis and produce actionable keyword, content, backlink, pricing, and positioning recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitor research may involve private business files, analytics exports, or project context supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Review and minimize any private business data before sharing it with an agent or external tools.

Risk: SEO and competitor findings can become stale or incomplete if based on old rankings, limited pages, or third-party metrics.

Mitigation: Confirm important recommendations against current search results, competitor pages, and authorized SEO data before acting on them.
