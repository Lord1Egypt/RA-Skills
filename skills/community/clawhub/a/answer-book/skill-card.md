## Description:

The Answer Book / 答案之书 lets a user hold a question in mind, flip to a random page, and receive a short philosophical answer in English or Chinese.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[enihsago](https://clawhub.ai/user/enihsago)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this entertainment skill to flip a bilingual Answer Book page for a short oracle-style response to a yes/no or guidance prompt.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat oracle-style responses as ordinary advice or decision support.

Mitigation: Present answers as entertainment-oriented prompts for reflection, and ask users to be explicit when they want practical advice instead.
