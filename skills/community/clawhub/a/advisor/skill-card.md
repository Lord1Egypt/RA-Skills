## Description:

Advisor provides balanced, objective guidance on important decisions across business, personal finance, career, and strategy when users need a sounding board, objective perspective, or trusted second opinion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiwenbing](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiwenbing)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill as a structured sounding board for important decisions, including business strategy, career moves, investments, partnerships, and major personal choices. It helps surface assumptions, compare tradeoffs, assess risks, and form a recommendation while leaving the final decision to the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be invoked for finance, legal, medical, mental-health, or other high-impact personal decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as structured thinking support and seek qualified professional advice before acting on regulated or high-stakes matters.

Risk: Broad advice prompts may produce recommendations that miss context or overweight incomplete user-provided information.

Mitigation: Use the skill to ask clarifying questions, surface assumptions, and compare options before relying on any recommendation.
