## Description:

Email Assistant Pro helps users classify pasted or described emails, draft professional replies, extract tasks and deadlines, summarize long messages, and prioritize follow-up.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crazypatient0](https://clawhub.ai/user/crazypatient0)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, business users, and support teams use this skill to process pasted or described email content, identify priority messages, draft replies, and turn email threads into tasks for manual review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pasted emails may contain secrets, credentials, personal data, or confidential business details.

Mitigation: Redact sensitive details before using the skill when possible.

Risk: Drafted replies, summaries, classifications, or task lists may be incomplete or incorrect.

Mitigation: Manually review generated outputs before sending replies or acting on tasks.
