Mitigation: Use the skill when metaphorical framing is appropriate, and keep task-specific technical requirements explicit during use.

Risk: Conceptual guidance can be misapplied as a substitute for project-specific safety review.

Mitigation: Review generated recommendations against the target system's requirements, threat model, and deployment context before adoption.
