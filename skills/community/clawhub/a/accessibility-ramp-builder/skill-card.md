## Description:

Plans residential access ramps from the measured rise, user mobility, available space, and doorway width, including slope checks, layout fit, materials, and cost estimates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Homeowners, caregivers, and accessibility planners use the skill to estimate a residential ramp layout and assess whether an existing ramp and doorway suit a wheelchair or walker.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may bring ramp-planning guidance into loosely related mobility discussions.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's trigger wording focused on residential ramp planning and review its scope before use.

Risk: Incorrect measurements or unverified construction guidance can lead to an unsafe ramp or costly rework.

Mitigation: Confirm measurements, check local permit and code requirements, and have the final design reviewed by a qualified ramp professional.
