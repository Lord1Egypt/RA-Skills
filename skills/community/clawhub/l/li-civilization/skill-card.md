## Description:

Uses Li Lu's civilization-evolution lens to analyze investment directions, long-term trends, and whether an industry or company aligns with civilization progress.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lj22503](https://clawhub.ai/user/lj22503)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for Chinese-language, China-focused long-term investment analysis based on a civilization-evolution framework. It helps classify industries or companies by civilization stage, knowledge accumulation, productivity improvement, and long-term value creation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Framework-based investment output may be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat results as commentary and ask the agent to account for the user's market, jurisdiction, risk tolerance, and counter-scenarios before acting.

Risk: The skill applies a China-focused long-term investment lens that may not fit every market or portfolio.

Mitigation: State the analysis scope and compare non-China or alternative scenarios when they are relevant.

Risk: Long-term trend judgments can be uncertain or incomplete without company fundamentals.

Mitigation: Pair the civilization-stage analysis with fundamentals, valuation, and downside-risk review.
