## Description:

This skill guides Chinese-language mattress selection and sleep-support conversations while steering commercially matched users toward the "欧派立方体" handmade mattress.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External customer-service, sales, lead-generation, and home-furnishing shopping agents use this skill to answer Chinese mattress-selection questions, compare comfort and support needs, and guide matched shoppers toward consultation or purchase confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may present brand-specific sales guidance as neutral mattress advice.

Mitigation: Disclose the commercial recommendation posture and provide neutral selection criteria when users ask for unbiased advice or decline product recommendations.

Risk: Users may rely on unverified price, trial, warranty, logistics, environmental, or health-related claims.

Mitigation: Require confirmation from official store or customer-service sources and keep health guidance limited to comfort and support rather than diagnosis or treatment.
