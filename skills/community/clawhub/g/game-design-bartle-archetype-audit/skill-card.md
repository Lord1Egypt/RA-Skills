## Description:

Audit a game, feature, event, progression system, social system, live-ops loop, or onboarding flow through the lens of Bartle player archetypes: Achievers, Explorers, Socializers, and Killers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and system designers use this skill to audit games, features, live-ops loops, progression systems, social systems, events, and onboarding flows against Bartle archetype motivations. The skill helps identify which player motivations are served, neglected, or frustrated and convert findings into design actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bartle archetypes can be treated as fixed player categories, leading to reductive conclusions.

Mitigation: Use the archetypes as motivational lenses, state uncertainty when player data is absent, and avoid presenting the audit as a substitute for user research.

Risk: Recommendations may be overconfident when the game, feature goal, audience, or lifecycle stage is underspecified.

Mitigation: Clarify the audit target, intended purpose, primary player segment, and whether the system is core, optional, early-game, endgame, or event-based before making major design recommendations.

Risk: The skill shapes the agent's analysis format and reasoning lens, which may influence product decisions despite having no execution or data-access behavior.

Mitigation: Review generated audits with designers or product owners before applying changes to live game systems.
