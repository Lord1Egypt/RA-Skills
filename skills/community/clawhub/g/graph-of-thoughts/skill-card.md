## Description:

Graph-based reasoning with thought combination and feedback loops for exploring multiple solution paths, combining insights, and synthesizing solutions for synthesis, optimization, creative combination, and complex multi-dimensional problems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tobisamaa](https://clawhub.ai/user/tobisamaa)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and other agent users use this skill to structure complex reasoning tasks by generating multiple paths, evaluating them, combining complementary insights, iterating with feedback, and aggregating a final answer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional caching, memory logging, or example creation may retain prompt details locally.

Mitigation: Disable or avoid caching, memory logging, and example creation for confidential prompts unless local retention is acceptable.

Risk: The skill produces structured reasoning and recommendations that may be incorrect or overconfident.

Mitigation: Review outputs against requirements, constraints, and domain expertise before using them for consequential decisions.
