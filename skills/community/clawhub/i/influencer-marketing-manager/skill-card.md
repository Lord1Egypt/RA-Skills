## Description:

Provides expert management for influencer, creator, KOL, UGC, and ambassador partnerships-from goal framing and evidence-led qualification through outreach, negotiation, fulfillment, measurement, and iteration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and business operators use this skill to manage creator partnerships across strategy, discovery, qualification, outreach, negotiation, fulfillment, measurement, and iteration. It is intended for work that needs business judgment, relationship progress, or autonomous execution under approved rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External outreach could be sent to the wrong recipient or without adequate approval.

Mitigation: Define approved outreach rules, sender accounts, allowed recipient classes, opt-out handling, and confirmation requirements before use.

Risk: Creator fit, contact readiness, or performance may be misread from incomplete or stale evidence.

Mitigation: Use stable creator or channel identifiers, dated evidence, recent format-specific review, and verified contact routes before prioritizing or sending.

Risk: The agent may prepare terms that affect spend, rights, exclusivity, payment, schedule, or contract language.

Mitigation: Require user confirmation before any material commitment or external message that changes commercial terms.
