## Description:

Lianjia is a home-buying and renting guidance skill for reading listing pages, estimating tax components, understanding second-hand home transaction workflows, and checking rental contract pitfalls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to structure home-buying or renting conversations around Lianjia and Beike listings, transaction steps, tax components, funds supervision, and lease-contract checks. It supports informational decision guidance and should be paired with current official or professional advice before real-estate decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real-estate tax, loan, contract, and housing-policy details can vary by city and change over time.

Mitigation: Verify current details with local official sources, Lianjia transaction advisors, contract documents, or qualified professionals before acting.

Risk: The skill may be used as if it were legal, tax, financial, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat responses as general informational checklists and avoid using them to make final transaction or investment decisions without professional review.
