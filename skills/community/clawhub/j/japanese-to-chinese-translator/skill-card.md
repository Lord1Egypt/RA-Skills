## Description:

Translates Japanese business and legal documents into Chinese while preserving tone, terminology, numbers, dates, and document structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaoxuan1917](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoxuan1917)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Translators, legal teams, and business users use this skill to translate Japanese contracts, LOIs, NDAs, emails, meeting minutes, and reports into Chinese while preserving formatting and professional terminology.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the skill for generic translation requests.

Mitigation: Confirm that the source language is Japanese and the target language is Chinese before translating sensitive documents.

Risk: Business and legal translations can carry confidentiality and interpretation risk.

Mitigation: Review translated terms, dates, amounts, and legal clauses before relying on the output.
