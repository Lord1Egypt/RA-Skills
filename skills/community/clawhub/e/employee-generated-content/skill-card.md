## Description:

When the user wants to plan, implement, or optimize employee-generated content (EGC) or employee advocacy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, brand, and go-to-market teams use this skill to plan employee-generated content and employee advocacy programs for AI, SaaS, B2B, and professional-services contexts. It helps select channels and formats, identify employee participant tiers, define governance and disclosure practices, and plan measurement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read existing project-context files to tailor employee advocacy advice.

Mitigation: Review project-context files before use and remove confidential or sensitive business information that should not influence generated marketing guidance.

Risk: Employee advocacy recommendations can create brand, disclosure, or compliance issues if applied without review.

Mitigation: Have marketing, legal, or communications reviewers confirm disclosure language, approval flow, and brand guidance before employees publish content.
