## Description:

Remove signs of AI-generated writing from text by identifying and rewriting common AI-style patterns while preserving meaning and matching the intended tone.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[runesleo](https://clawhub.ai/user/runesleo)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and agents use this skill to revise AI-sounding prose into more natural text while preserving the original meaning, tone, and voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Opinionated rewrites can change tone, emphasis, or wording in sensitive, formal, quoted, legal, academic, medical, or compliance text.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to preserve meaning, citations, neutrality, and original voice before invoking the skill, then review the final text before use.
