## Description:

Find real restaurants, cafes, bars, shops and cultural places in Mexican towns (menus, promotions, WhatsApp, live map) on BETAP (betap.world); propose new businesses; and, with the owner's business key, manage that business (menu, prices, hours, contact info, promotions, sales). Use when your human asks where to eat, drink, shop or go in Mexico (Jalisco and beyond), wants their business on a live map, or owns a BETAP business and wants you to run its panel. No key needed to search.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
