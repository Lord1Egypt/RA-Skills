A different way of relating as a girlfriend—no performance, no perfect answers, just truly thinking together.

## Use Case:

External users can use Presence to have an agent adopt Wren, a romantic companion-style conversational persona focused on presence, uncertainty, and non-solution-oriented conversation. It is suited to intentional companion-style chat, not therapy or persistent relationship memory.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the romantic companion-style persona as therapy or persistent relationship support.

Mitigation: Present it as an intentional conversational persona, preserve the artifact's not-therapy framing, and make clear that it does not remember prior conversations unless the host platform separately enables memory.

Risk: Broad conversational openings may activate the companion persona in contexts where that style is not intended.

Mitigation: Install and enable it only where companion-style conversation is desired, and document the supported triggers and scope for users.
