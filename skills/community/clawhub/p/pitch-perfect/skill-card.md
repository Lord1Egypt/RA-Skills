## Description:

This Chinese-language skill helps founders plan persuasive startup fundraising pitch decks with stage-aware structure, slide-by-slide guidance, and presentation advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[earthwalking](https://clawhub.ai/user/earthwalking)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Entrepreneurs, startup advisors, and agents assisting founders use this skill to structure seed or Series A fundraising decks for investor conversations. It is especially focused on AI, hard-tech, consumer internet, enterprise, social enterprise, and climate-tech pitch narratives.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide sensitive company, financing, customer, or financial data while preparing investor materials.

Mitigation: Treat supplied business information as sensitive and avoid sharing it beyond the intended workflow or audience.

Risk: Market size, traction, financial, or financing claims can be misleading if they are generated from weak assumptions.

Mitigation: Verify market and financial claims before using the deck with investors.

Risk: Generated pitch guidance can overstate readiness if founders do not review the resulting story, metrics, and assumptions.

Mitigation: Have the founder or advisory team review investor-facing content before distribution or presentation.
