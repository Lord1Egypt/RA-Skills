# Platform notes and adaptation

Limits and features change, so treat this as a starting point and confirm in the app before a launch.

## Length and format (checked 23 Nov 2026)

| Platform | Maximum length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts | 3 minutes | Must be square or vertical; uploads from 15 Oct 2024 that are up to 3 minutes and vertical or square count as Shorts (YouTube Help) |
| Instagram Reels | 3 minutes per a May 2026 guide; some sources list longer | Check what the app allows for your account |
| TikTok | Sources differ (about 10 minutes recording in-app, longer uploads) | Check the app |

Practical rule: most short-form scripts work best at 15 to 60 seconds, and a well-paced 30 to 90 seconds suits stories and how-tos. Choose the length by how much value the idea needs, not by the maximum.

## Adapting one idea across platforms

Keep the core idea and the evidence; change the packaging.

| Element | YouTube Shorts | Instagram Reels | TikTok |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook phrasing | Clear promise, often search-friendly wording | Visual and aesthetic first, short punchy line | Conversational, native-feeling, trend-aware |
| Tone | Informative or entertaining | Polished or personal | Casual, unscripted feel |
| Title or caption | The title matters (it is searchable): put the keyword in it | Caption supports the hook; keywords help discovery | Caption short; keywords help search |
| CTA | Subscribe or watch the next video; link to a long-form video if relevant | Follow, save, share, DM a keyword | Follow, comment, part 2 |
| Captions | On, high contrast, safe area | On, matches the visual style | On, native caption style |
| Audio | Original voice or YouTube-allowed music | Licensed or original audio per account type | Platform sounds where allowed for the account type |

When adapting, change the first line and the CTA first, then the pacing. Do not republish the identical file with another platform's watermark; most platforms deprioritize that, and the audience differs anyway.

## Series and repeatable formats

A repeatable format (same structure, new topic) is cheaper to produce and easier for viewers to recognise. Suggest one when the user plans to post regularly: a fixed opening line, a fixed number of beats, and a consistent CTA.

## Search and discovery notes

Put the main keyword in the spoken hook, the on-screen text and the title or caption, since platforms index all three. Use `research_helper.py suggest` to find the phrases people actually search.
