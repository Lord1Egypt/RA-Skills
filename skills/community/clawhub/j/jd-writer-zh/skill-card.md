## Description:

Jd Writer Zh helps HR and recruiting teams generate structured Chinese recruiting job descriptions from a role title and core requirements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[olina1ye](https://clawhub.ai/user/olina1ye)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

HR staff, recruiters, and hiring teams use this skill to draft Chinese job descriptions for roles across industries such as internet, manufacturing, finance, and education. It produces sections such as company introduction, responsibilities, requirements, compensation, benefits, location, and employment type for review before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated job descriptions may contain inaccurate details, legally sensitive language, or requirements that do not match company hiring policy.

Mitigation: Review generated JDs for accuracy, legal compliance, and company-specific hiring requirements before publishing.

Risk: The skill may activate on broad HR drafting phrases and produce recruiting copy when the user intended a different HR writing task.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested role, requirements, and intended recruiting use before relying on or distributing the generated text.
