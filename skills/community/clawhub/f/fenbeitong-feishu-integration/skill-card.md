## Use Case:

Sales, solution, and delivery teams use this skill to answer prospect or customer questions about Fenbeitong-Feishu integration support, select market versus embedded editions, and route unknown or urgent requirements for follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer requirement details and integration questions may include sensitive business or personal data sent through Feishu escalation workflows.

Mitigation: Use only in environments with approved customer logging and Feishu escalation; apply consent, redaction, recipient verification, access control, and retention rules.

Risk: Broad emergency notification paths can expose customer information to unintended recipients.

Mitigation: Remove broad @everyone escalation and verify recipients before sending urgent follow-up messages.
