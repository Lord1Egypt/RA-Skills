## Description:

AI assistant for creating, scheduling, and optimizing social media content with hashtag research, multi-platform posting, and engagement analytics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hughtan93-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/hughtan93-dev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, creators, and developers use this skill to draft platform-specific social posts, plan content calendars, generate hashtag bundles, and review engagement metrics for Twitter/X, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated posts, hashtags, and engagement recommendations may be inaccurate, off-brand, or unsuitable for a platform.

Mitigation: Review and edit generated content before publishing, and compare recommendations against current platform rules and brand standards.

Risk: Platform limits, best posting times, and social media guidance can become outdated.

Mitigation: Confirm current platform documentation and audience analytics before relying on limits, schedules, or optimization advice.
