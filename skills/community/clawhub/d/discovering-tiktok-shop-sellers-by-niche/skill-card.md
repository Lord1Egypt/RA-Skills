## Description:

Discovers TikTok Shop sellers and product listings in a specific niche using apidojo's TikTok scraper on Apify.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[apidojo-io](https://clawhub.ai/user/apidojo-io)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External e-commerce sellers, product researchers, affiliate marketers, and TikTok Shop entrants use this skill to map sellers and products in a TikTok Shop niche, rank sales signals, and identify competitors or product gaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: TikTok search terms, target URLs, and collected public seller or product results are processed by Apify/apidojo under the user's Apify account.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when that third-party processing is acceptable, and avoid entering confidential research targets.

Risk: Broad or unbounded runs can increase Apify usage and cost.

Mitigation: Keep runs scoped with a maxItems limit and focused keywords or start URLs.
