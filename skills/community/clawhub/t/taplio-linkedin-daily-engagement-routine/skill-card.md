## Description:

Runs a daily LinkedIn engagement session by reviewing recent replies, drafting comments, getting batch approval, and scheduling approved items through Taplio.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[porenes](https://clawhub.ai/user/porenes)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

LinkedIn creators and professionals use this skill to manage a daily engagement session: review comments on their own posts, draft replies and comments for selected feed posts, and schedule approved items through Taplio.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review is incomplete because two references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Consider whether the incomplete review coverage is acceptable before installing.

Risk: Scheduled replies and comments are made from the user's LinkedIn account.

Mitigation: Review, edit, or drop each draft before approving the batch for scheduling.
