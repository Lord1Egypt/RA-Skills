## Description:

Help analyze recurring conflict patterns on software or game projects by identifying likely collaboration archetypes, behavior patterns, project risks, and practical response strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, engineering managers, producers, founders, and project stakeholders use this skill to analyze recurring software or game team conflict, identify likely non-clinical behavior patterns, assess project risk, and choose practical next moves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Archetype labels could be treated as facts about a person or as clinical diagnosis.

Mitigation: Frame labels as rough collaboration patterns, distinguish observed behavior from interpretation, state uncertainty, and avoid mental health diagnosis.

Risk: Advice about workplace escalation may be risky in power-imbalanced, retaliation-sensitive, or HR-sensitive situations.

Mitigation: Prefer documentation, witnesses, careful escalation, and project-impact framing over direct confrontation when safety or role risk is present.

Risk: Incomplete user stories can lead to overconfident or misleading conflict analysis.

Mitigation: Ask for roles, recurring behavior, project impact, prior attempts, desired outcome, and power dynamics; when details are missing, state assumptions and present options.
