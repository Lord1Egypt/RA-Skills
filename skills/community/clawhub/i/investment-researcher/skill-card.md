## Use Case:

Investment teams, analysts, and finance users can use this skill to draft A-share and Hong Kong equity research, industry due diligence, portfolio analysis, valuation work, risk cases, and monitoring triggers. Its outputs should be treated as research assistance that requires independent review of primary sources.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce securities research or investment recommendations that users could mistake for professional financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research assistance only, verify cited primary sources independently, and require qualified human review before investment decisions.

Risk: Broad Chinese trigger terms such as finance, analysis, reports, or budget may activate the skill for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the user intends securities research before relying on the persona, especially when the request is general finance or reporting work.
