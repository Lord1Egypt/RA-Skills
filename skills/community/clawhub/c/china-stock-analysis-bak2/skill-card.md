## Description:

Analyze Chinese stock prices (A-shares, HK stocks) and provide investment recommendations for Chinese companies, including buying or selling recommendations and market trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[make453](https://clawhub.ai/user/make453)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to structure market-data lookup, technical analysis, and buy/hold/sell-style commentary for A-shares, Hong Kong stocks, and selected US stocks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce buy/hold/sell-style stock opinions from web-searched market data, which may be inaccurate, stale, or mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational, verify prices and sources independently, and include the stated investment-risk disclaimer.
