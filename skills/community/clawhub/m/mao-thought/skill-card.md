## Description:

Provides Chinese-language Q&A support about Mao Zedong Thought, related writings, core concepts, and Chinese Communist Party history topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jennyfun](https://clawhub.ai/user/jennyfun)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Learners, researchers, and agents use this skill to answer academic or historical knowledge questions about Mao Zedong Thought, Mao's major works, and related party history. It is intended for source-aware responses, especially for sensitive historical topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Responses to sensitive historical questions may reflect an official-authoritative source framing.

Mitigation: Ask for multiple perspectives, alternative sources, and explicit citations when balanced historiography is required.

Risk: Broad trigger terms may activate the skill for loosely related Mao Zedong or party-history questions.

Mitigation: Review whether the response is relevant to the user question and whether sources are cited or described clearly.
