Expert in Jungian analytical psychology, depth psychology, shadow work, archetypal analysis, dream interpretation, active imagination, addiction/recovery through Jungian lens, and the individuation process.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for Jungian-informed psychological education, reflective self-exploration, shadow work, dream interpretation frameworks, archetypal analysis, active imagination guidance, and individuation-oriented prompts. It is not a substitute for therapy, diagnosis, crisis support, or care from a licensed analyst.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake self-guided depth psychology guidance for clinical care.

Mitigation: Present the skill as educational reflection only and direct users to licensed analysts or clinicians for therapy, diagnosis, persistent symptoms, trauma containment, or relational depth work.

Risk: Depth-work exercises may be inappropriate during crisis, suicidality, withdrawal or intoxication, psychosis, severe dissociation, or overwhelming trauma material.

Mitigation: Avoid using the skill in those conditions and seek licensed, emergency, or crisis support instead.

Risk: Users may disclose highly sensitive personal material while exploring dreams, addiction, trauma, or inner figures.

Mitigation: Avoid entering highly sensitive details unless the user is comfortable with how their agent environment stores and handles conversation content.
