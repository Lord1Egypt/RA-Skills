## Description:

Builds sourced, decision-ready Alberta energy market briefings covering AESO pool prices, supply/demand balance, outage events, regulatory changes, and commercial implications for traders, facility managers, data center developers, and sustainability teams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External energy-market practitioners use this skill to turn public AESO, AUC, and Alberta regulatory information into sourced Alberta electricity-market briefings. It supports commercial context for trading desks, facility managers, data center developers, sustainability teams, and PPA or renewable procurement evaluators without providing trading, legal, compliance, or procurement recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could treat analytical market briefings as financial, trading, procurement, legal, or compliance advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision-support analysis only, preserve source traceability, and verify current market data and professional advice before acting.

Risk: Alberta electricity-market data can become stale quickly, especially for pool prices, supply conditions, outages, and regulatory changes.

Mitigation: Verify live or latest AESO, AUC, ETS, and Alberta regulatory sources before using outputs for current commercial decisions.

Risk: Weak or unverified sources could be mistaken for confirmed AESO or regulatory facts.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's source-tier rubric, separate confirmed data from analyst projections and speculation, and label evidence gaps explicitly.
