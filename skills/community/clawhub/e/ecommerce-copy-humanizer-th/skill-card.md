## Description:

Humanize Thai ecommerce copy to make it sound more natural, local, and less machine-written while preserving selling intent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators, marketers, and sellers use this skill to rewrite Thai product pages, social captions, seller replies, and ecommerce scripts so they sound more natural while preserving offer intent and safer wording.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be misapplied to translation, bilingual localization, or regulated compliance review.

Mitigation: Use it only for Thai ecommerce copy rewriting and route legal, medical, or regulated claims to qualified review.

Risk: Rewrites could introduce exaggerated product benefits or unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Preserve the original offer and proof direction, review risk-safe wording swaps, and remove any invented claims before publication.
