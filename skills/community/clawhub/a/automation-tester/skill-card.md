## Description:

Professional automation testing career roadmap generation platform that creates personalized learning paths based on experience, skills, and career goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

QA engineers, automation testing professionals, team leads, training coordinators, and career coaches use this skill to generate structured automation testing career roadmaps from experience, skills, and goals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Assessment requests can include automation testing career details, session identifiers, timestamps, and an optional user ID sent to the provider's external API.

Mitigation: Avoid including unnecessary personal, confidential employer, or sensitive project information in assessment fields.

Risk: Generated career roadmaps may be incomplete or may not match a user's workplace requirements, available tools, or actual readiness.

Mitigation: Review generated phases, skills, resources, and milestones before relying on them for training plans or career decisions.
