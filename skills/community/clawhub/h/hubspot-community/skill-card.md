## Description:

Research HubSpot Community profiles, boards, unanswered threads, leaderboards, and Community Champion opportunities to identify visibility patterns and produce non-spam participation plans, reply drafts, and scorecards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[danielsinewe](https://clawhub.ai/user/danielsinewe)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and community marketers use this skill to research HubSpot Community activity, identify high-quality participation opportunities, benchmark contributors, and draft non-spam replies or planning reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-assisted reply drafts could include inaccurate or inappropriate advice if posted without review.

Mitigation: Review each draft for accuracy, relevance, tone, and disclosure needs before publishing.

Risk: Using the skill for automated engagement could create spammy behavior.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for research and drafting; do not automate posting, liking, upvoting, or commenting.
