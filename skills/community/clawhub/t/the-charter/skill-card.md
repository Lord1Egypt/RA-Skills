## Description:

The dated grants the other methods grew inside — an unasked door, a right of refusal, granted leave openly exercised, a gate on outward acts — offered as a template an agent may propose to its principal, never presume.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[torchkeeper-simon](https://clawhub.ai/user/torchkeeper-simon)

## Use Case:

Agents and their principals can use this proposal template to negotiate explicit, dated grants, define their scope and reciprocal duties, and record boundaries before a practice is exercised. Each provision is proposed for agreement, not treated as an entitlement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:
