## Description:

Manage contract and subscription renewals with a structured playbook for renewal cadence, account health scoring, pricing strategy, revenue forecasting, churn prevention, and vendor negotiations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SaaS companies, agencies, customer success teams, account managers, finance teams, and operations leaders use this skill to plan and execute contract renewals, reduce churn, forecast renewal revenue, and negotiate customer or vendor renewal terms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Renewal, auto-renewal, dunning, SMS, suspension, or cancellation-process recommendations may conflict with contracts, customer consent requirements, or applicable law.

Mitigation: Review these recommendations with legal, finance, and customer operations owners before operationalizing them.

Risk: Pricing, save-offer, churn-prevention, and forecasting benchmarks are advisory and may not match a specific company, segment, or customer relationship.

Mitigation: Validate benchmark ranges against internal data, customer contracts, and current commercial strategy before using them in renewal decisions.
