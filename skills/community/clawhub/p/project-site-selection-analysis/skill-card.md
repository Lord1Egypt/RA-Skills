## Description:

项目选址分析报告 helps agents produce Chinese-language reports that evaluate how well a project fits a target region across land, industry base, policy support, logistics, talent, costs, supporting services, and implementation risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, government teams, park operators, investment teams, and enterprise site-selection analysts use this skill to assess project landing feasibility, compare regional carrying capacity, identify location risks, and draft structured site-selection recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated site-selection reports may rely on web research, regional policy data, and assumptions that affect investment or location decisions.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against official regional sources, current policy documents, and qualified local professional advice before using them for decisions.
