## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic writers use this skill to evaluate a proposed paper title across contribution, structure, clarity, claim strength, keyword quality, and style, then receive revision guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The diagnostic report could provide incomplete or overly generic academic-title advice if the user supplies only a title without research context.

Mitigation: Ask for an abstract, background, or contribution summary when precision matters, and treat the final title choice as the author's decision.
