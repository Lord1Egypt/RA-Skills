## Description:

Build a lightweight risk register for a chosen or leading option with risk statements, likelihood, impact, warning indicators, mitigations, contingencies, owners, and review dates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to risk-check a chosen or leading decision option before acting. It helps managers, founders, project leads, students, household decision-makers, and similar users produce a structured risk register with warning indicators, mitigations, contingencies, owners, review dates, and a non-forced confidence note.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the risk register as professional legal, medical, financial, employment, safety, or other specialized advice.

Mitigation: Frame the output as a planning aid and recommend qualified expert input for serious, regulated, specialized, safety-critical, or irreversible decisions.

Risk: Qualitative probability, impact, detectability, and urgency labels may be mistaken for precise predictions.

Mitigation: Describe scores as judgment aids, preserve uncertainty, and include questions or evidence that would improve confidence.

Risk: A confidence note could be interpreted as forcing a final decision.

Mitigation: Use non-forced outcomes such as go, go with mitigations, review-first, delay, or no-go, and explain the main uncertainty.
