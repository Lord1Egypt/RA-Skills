## Use Case:

Developers, architects, and engineering leaders use this skill to assess legacy systems, select a modernization strategy, plan incremental migrations, and structure review checkpoints before production changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Modernization recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific production system.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as a structured starting point and require architecture review, security review, compliance review, production-scale testing, and human sign-off before making system changes.
