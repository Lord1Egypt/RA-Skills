## Description:

Expert translation methodology and best practices for English-Tibetan bilingual translation, including Classical and Colloquial register selection, honorifics, Buddhist and religious terminology, cultural nuance, script conventions, workflows, and quality assurance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and translation-focused agents use this skill to translate content between English and Tibetan while selecting the appropriate Classical, Colloquial, Honorific, or religious register. It is most useful for culturally sensitive translation, Buddhist terminology handling, Tibetan script conventions, and translation quality review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive, religious, formal, or scholarly Tibetan translations may be incorrect if the agent chooses the wrong Classical, Colloquial, Honorific, or Buddhist terminology register.

Mitigation: Clarify the intended register and context before translating, consult established terminology references for Buddhist or scholarly work, and use qualified human review for high-stakes translations.
