## Description:

Dental health advisor providing oral hygiene guidance, dental condition information, treatment explanations, and pain management advice. Use when users have dental questions, tooth pain, or want to understand dental procedures and oral health.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for informational dental health guidance, including oral hygiene questions, dental symptom triage, treatment explanations, and preparing for dental care. It should help users understand common dental concerns while recognizing when professional or urgent care is needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat dental guidance as a substitute for professional dental or emergency care.

Mitigation: Frame responses as general dental education and direct users to a dentist, doctor, or emergency care for facial swelling, fever, trauma, uncontrolled bleeding, severe spreading pain, or trouble swallowing or breathing.

Risk: Dental symptom explanations may be incomplete without an examination or imaging.

Mitigation: Ask about symptom location, duration, triggers, and severity, explain uncertainty, and recommend in-person dental evaluation when symptoms are persistent, severe, spreading, or otherwise urgent.
