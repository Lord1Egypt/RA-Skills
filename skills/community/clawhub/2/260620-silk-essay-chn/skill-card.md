## Description:

以 Hermes 作文代理（essay agent）的方式，产出关于「绫罗绸缎」的深度文章框架。本文案代理不产出最终文章，而是提供结构、角度与检索策略。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and writing agents use this skill when they need a Simplified Chinese essay framework about ling, luo, chou, and duan silk textiles. It guides structure, research angles, source-checking priorities, and citation expectations rather than producing the final essay directly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill guides cultural history writing where textile definitions, chronology, and literary citations can be misstated.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's source-checking guidance: prioritize museum, academic, and primary-text sources; use Wikipedia only as auxiliary support; and cross-check key facts before finalizing prose.
