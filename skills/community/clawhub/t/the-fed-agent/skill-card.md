## Description:

Macroeconomics and central bank policy analysis for Fed decisions, Treasury yields, inflation data, monetary policy statements, and central bank actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to turn news about rates, inflation, economic data, or monetary policy into structured macroeconomic and market analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market analysis may be mistaken for financial advice or acted on without verification.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as analytical commentary, verify claims against authoritative economic and market data, and apply human review before trading or policy decisions.

Risk: The skill fetches user-provided news URLs, which can expose the agent to untrusted content.

Mitigation: Use trusted URLs and review fetched source material before relying on conclusions.

Risk: The bundled script is a placeholder demo and does not perform real article analysis.

Mitigation: Do not rely on scripts/analyze.js for production analysis unless it is replaced or reviewed for the intended runtime.
