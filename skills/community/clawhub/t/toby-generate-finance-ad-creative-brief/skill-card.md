## Description:

Create targeted ad creatives and hooks for finance campaigns to support paid campaign planning by finance teams and analysts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External finance teams, analysts, and operators use this skill to plan paid campaign creative briefs, including audience goals, visual direction, messaging hooks, and refinement steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broad shell and file-read access that the described creative-brief workflow does not justify.

Mitigation: Review before installing and rely on platform sandboxing and approval controls, or use a version with those tool permissions removed.

Risk: Finance ad briefs may include inaccurate, noncompliant, or misleading claims if published without review.

Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers check audience assumptions, claims, disclosures, and policy compliance before external publication.
