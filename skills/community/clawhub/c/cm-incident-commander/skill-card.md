## Description:

Guide incident response with structured communication, timeline tracking, severity assessment, stakeholder updates, and post-incident review - a virtual incident commander for on-call teams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

On-call, SRE, and operations teams use this skill to coordinate incidents, assess severity, assign response roles, draft stakeholder updates, maintain timelines, and facilitate post-incident reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incident updates may contain inaccurate or incomplete operational details if generated from partial context.

Mitigation: Review generated stakeholder communications, status updates, and resolution summaries before sending.
