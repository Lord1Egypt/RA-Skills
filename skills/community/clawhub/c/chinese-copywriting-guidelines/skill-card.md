## Description:

Chinese copywriting typesetting guidelines that help agents standardize spacing, punctuation, fullwidth and halfwidth characters, and common naming conventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dizhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/dizhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, localization teams, and agents use this skill to review or improve Chinese copy so spacing, punctuation, numeric formatting, quote style, and proper-name casing follow the documented conventions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encodes specific Chinese typesetting preferences, including quote style and spacing, that may conflict with locale, brand, or house style.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the target locale or brand guide and override rules such as quote style or spacing when needed.

Risk: Style suggestions may introduce unintended wording or formatting changes before publication.

Mitigation: Have a human reviewer approve suggested edits before using them in published copy.
