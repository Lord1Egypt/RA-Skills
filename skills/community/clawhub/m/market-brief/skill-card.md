## Description:

Produces structured, decision-useful market briefs for tokens, sectors, equities, FX, and macro questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oonyl](https://clawhub.ai/user/oonyl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, investors, and market analysts use this skill to generate concise market briefs with drivers, scenarios, uncertainties, price targets, probabilities, and practical reads. It is intended for analysis support, not personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce price targets, probabilities, and position-sizing language that may be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as market analysis, require citations for load-bearing claims, and make independent decisions or consult a qualified financial advisor before acting.

Risk: Broad prompts such as 'analyze' may activate the market-brief format when an asset or market context is present.

Mitigation: Confirm intent for ambiguous requests and bypass this format for simple price lookups or purely factual questions.
