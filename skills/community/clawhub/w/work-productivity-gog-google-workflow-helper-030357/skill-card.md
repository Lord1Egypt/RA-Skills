## Description:

Helps agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams create practical Gog-style Google workflow support, including artifacts, checklists, analysis, implementation help, bug fixes, setup hardening, and reliability improvements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad productivity and Google-related trigger wording may route unrelated requests to this helper.

Mitigation: Narrow the activation keywords or require explicit invocation when maintainers want stricter routing.

Risk: Generated workflows, code, shell commands, or configuration guidance may not fit the user's local environment.

Mitigation: Review generated artifacts and commands before use, keep assumptions visible, and validate outputs against the stated success criteria.
