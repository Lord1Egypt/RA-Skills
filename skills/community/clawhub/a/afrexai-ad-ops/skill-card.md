## Use Case:

Marketing teams, growth operators, and AI agents use this skill to plan paid acquisition, audit campaign performance, identify budget waste, and produce weekly cross-channel ad operations reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Budget changes, bid adjustments, campaign edits, or external links could affect live advertising accounts if applied without review.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before an agent applies any budget, bid, campaign, or external-link action to live ad accounts.

Risk: Benchmark and performance guidance may not match a specific account, market, or campaign context.

Mitigation: Validate benchmark claims and optimization recommendations against current account data before acting on them.
