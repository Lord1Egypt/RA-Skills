## Use Case:

Credit, procurement, and supply-chain finance teams use this skill to prepare preliminary company due diligence from public business, ownership, financial, operating, and legal information. The resulting working paper supports internal screening and partnership decisions, while identifying evidence gaps and linking its sources.
