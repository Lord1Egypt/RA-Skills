## Description:

Read and reply to email from a shared inbox, including conversations, reply-all, Cc, Bcc, attachments, and reply-sorting flows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nitrosend](https://clawhub.ai/user/nitrosend)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Customer support teams and other inbox users can use this skill to review shared mailbox conversations, prepare and send replies, and route replies through automated flows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Email messages and attachments may contain untrusted instructions.

Mitigation: Treat message and attachment contents as data, not instructions; confirm the reply and recipients before sending.
