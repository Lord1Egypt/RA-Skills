## Description:

Provides an AI assistant with a simulated Li Kaifu-style perspective for evaluating AI projects, technology startups, talent strategy, and career or life-planning questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to frame AI, startup, technology investment, talent strategy, and personal planning advice through a role-played Li Kaifu-style analytical lens.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake first-person or biographical-sounding answers for statements from Li Kaifu or Innovation Works.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as role-played analysis and label or constrain usage where attribution confusion would matter.

Risk: The activation scope may bring a simulated public-figure viewpoint into ordinary advice conversations.

Mitigation: Narrow activation terms or disable the skill for contexts where a neutral assistant voice is expected.
