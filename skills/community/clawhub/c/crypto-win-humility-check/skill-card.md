## Description:

A reflection skill that helps users process a win without overconfidence or lifestyle inflation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this prompt-only reflection skill after a successful crypto trade or investment gain to separate outcome from decision quality, check for overconfidence, and write a humility anchor before making further financial decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat post-win reflection as financial advice or as validation to increase risk after a gain.

Mitigation: Present the skill as a reflection aid, not financial advice, and encourage users to pause before major allocation, trading, or lifestyle decisions.

Risk: A prompt-only financial reflection skill could be expected to inspect accounts, wallets, local files, or initiate trades.

Mitigation: Keep usage limited to user-provided context; the artifact behavior states that no local file, wallet, account inspection, trading actions, or persistence are performed.
