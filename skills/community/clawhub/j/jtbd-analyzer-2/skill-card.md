## Description:

Uncover the real "job" customers hire your product to do, going beyond features to understand functional, emotional, and social motivations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[marjoriebroad](https://clawhub.ai/user/marjoriebroad)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External product, research, and strategy users use this skill to analyze customer motivations through Jobs-To-Be-Done statements, functional, emotional, and social dimensions, alternatives, underserved needs, and priority.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can trigger on broad customer or product-analysis phrasing.

Mitigation: Review whether JTBD analysis is the intended posture before applying its framework to ambiguous customer, product, or user-needs requests.

Risk: JTBD outputs may overstate customer motivations when based on limited or assumed user evidence.

Mitigation: Treat generated job statements and priority labels as hypotheses to validate with interviews, behavioral data, or additional research.
