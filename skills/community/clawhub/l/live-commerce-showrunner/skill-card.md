## Description:

Plans live commerce sessions across Douyin, TikTok Shop, Taobao Live, Amazon Live, and similar channels by producing run-of-show, offer, staffing, moderation, risk-control, and debrief guidance without live platform or GMV data access.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External brand, marketplace, and ecommerce operations teams use this skill to create an operator-ready live commerce show brief before a livestream selling session. It is best suited for pre-live planning of show flow, offer structure, crew roles, moderation plays, risk controls, and debrief questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing, inventory, compliance, and sales guidance may be incomplete because the skill is heuristic and does not access live commerce systems.

Mitigation: Treat the output as draft planning support and require human approval for final pricing, inventory, legal, moderation, and compliance decisions.

Risk: Users may overread a generated show plan as a performance forecast or sales promise.

Mitigation: Keep the no-live-data limitation visible and avoid claiming access to traffic, GMV, comments, stock systems, or guaranteed outcomes.
