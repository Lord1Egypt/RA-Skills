## Description:

Turns a product image into a short 3–5 second product video with a chosen camera move and motion prompt.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers and product content creators use the skill to turn an existing product image into a short listing video with controlled motion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security verdict is suspicious, scanner inspection was incomplete, and using a provider sends images and prompts to that provider.

Mitigation: Review the verdict before installing; if proceeding, confirm provider data sharing is acceptable and account for incomplete evidence about partially inspected files.
