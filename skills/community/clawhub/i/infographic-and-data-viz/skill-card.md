## Description:

Guides agents to turn real, sourced data into honest, scannable charts and infographics by choosing the right chart, writing takeaway headlines, preserving honest scales, reducing clutter, citing sources, and adding accessibility support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, content, and analytics teams use this skill to turn real research or performance data into publishable chart and infographic specs that a design/chart tool can render and a human can approve.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may guide an agent to use brand materials and real analytics or research data that could be confidential.

Mitigation: Use only data intended for public output and require human review before rendering or publishing.

Risk: A chart or infographic can mislead readers if the source data, scale, context, or accessibility treatment is wrong.

Mitigation: Review the visualization spec for source and date, honest scale, sufficient context, and accessibility before publishing.
