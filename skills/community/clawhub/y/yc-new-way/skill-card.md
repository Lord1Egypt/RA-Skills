Founders, operators, and startup-focused agents use this skill to turn a vague idea into a 7-day validation sprint with a narrow wedge, one distribution channel, a lightweight MVP, metrics, and a weekly iteration loop.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated launch plans can include community posting, analytics, email capture, or outbound links that may create privacy, disclosure, or platform-rule obligations.

Mitigation: Review plans before publication, check community and site rules, disclose affiliate or advertising relationships when applicable, and confirm email collection and analytics comply with applicable privacy requirements.

Risk: Evidence-first startup recommendations may still rely on incomplete, stale, or weak market signals.

Mitigation: Require cited sources, inspect the underlying evidence, and treat early metrics as experiment inputs rather than final proof of demand.
