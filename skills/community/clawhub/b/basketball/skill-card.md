## Description:

Analyze basketball games, lineups, players, and practice plans with film-room structure, scouting grids, and possession-based coaching tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External coaches, analysts, scouts, players, and basketball staff use this skill to turn game prep, post-game review, player scouting, lineup design, and practice planning into coach-ready reports and decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local basketball notes may contain team context, roster notes, practice constraints, or analysis preferences.

Mitigation: Enable memory only after explicit user approval and keep saved notes lean.

Risk: Basketball guidance can mislead users if it presents guessed live stats, injuries, lineup certainty, betting picks, or medical clearance as verified information.

Mitigation: Separate observations, assumptions, and recommendations; decline betting picks and medical clearance; verify public facts when requested.
