## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Platform-compliance wording may be incomplete or become outdated.

Mitigation: Review generated copy against current Xiaohongshu policies before posting and edit or remove any questionable claims.

Risk: Crystal energy claims may be interpreted as proven, medical, supernatural, or guaranteed effects.

Mitigation: Keep claims framed as cultural context, aesthetics, personal experience, or self-reflection, and include clear disclaimers that effects are not proven guarantees.

Risk: Rephrasing restricted crystal claims to appear compliant could enable misleading marketing.

Mitigation: Use the output as draft copy only, avoid hard-sell promises, and ensure final posts do not imply guaranteed health, wealth, relationship, safety, or destiny outcomes.
