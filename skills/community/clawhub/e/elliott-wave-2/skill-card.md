## Description:

Guides agents through Elliott Wave analysis for counting market waves, identifying extensions, applying Fibonacci retracement and target ratios, and enforcing core counting constraints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and market-analysis agents use this skill to structure Elliott Wave counts, compare wave alternatives, apply Fibonacci levels, and avoid mixing wave analysis with time-cycle or reversal-pattern methods.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial technical analysis may be interpreted as guaranteed trading advice.

Mitigation: Cross-check outputs against independent market data and present conclusions as analytical guidance, not certainty.

Risk: The skill is mostly Chinese-language, which can affect reviewability for non-Chinese readers.

Mitigation: Use bilingual review or translated summaries before deployment in environments where reviewers do not read Chinese.

Risk: Elliott Wave counts can be ambiguous or overfit unclear charts.

Mitigation: Require Fibonacci ratios and independent signals such as trend lines, moving averages, patterns, or divergences before relying on a wave count.
