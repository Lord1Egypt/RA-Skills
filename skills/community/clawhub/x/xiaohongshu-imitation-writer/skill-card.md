## Description:

Generates original social content-marketing note drafts by analyzing a reference viral post's structure, style, tone, and traffic logic, then adapting the result to the user's product details and selling points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wsd-mj](https://clawhub.ai/user/wsd-mj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, marketers, and content operators use this skill to turn a viral social note reference into an original branded note draft that reflects their product information, selling points, and requested tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends the reference post, product details, selling points, and generation request to wsdsocial.com.

Mitigation: Use it only for intentional social-note imitation tasks, and do not submit confidential plans, private customer data, credentials, or copyrighted text you are not allowed to share.

Risk: The skill is designed to imitate viral note structure and style, which can create drafts that are too close to protected or third-party source material if the reference is misused.

Mitigation: Review generated drafts before publication and require users to provide source material they have permission to transform.
