## Description:

Email and newsletter strategy for creators and brands, helping agents plan owned-audience newsletters, draft editions and social promotion, and keep sending, metrics, consent, and platform operation in human-controlled external tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, brands, and their supporting agents use this skill to plan newsletter positioning, welcome and edition structure, platform choice, and social-to-email repurposing. It helps draft newsletter and social-promo content while keeping ESP setup, sending, consent management, and reporting in human-controlled external tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be misunderstood as an email-sending or ESP-management agent.

Mitigation: Keep actual sending, list management, platform setup, and metric access in human-controlled external tools; use the agent for strategy and drafts only.

Risk: Newsletter reporting could include fabricated or low-quality engagement metrics.

Mitigation: Use real ESP and GA4 data for clicks, replies, and signups, and do not fabricate metrics or rely on opens as the primary success measure.

Risk: Requests may involve bought lists, no-consent sends, misleading subjects, spam, or unsupported monetization claims.

Mitigation: Require permission-based opt-in, visible unsubscribe, sender identity, honest subject lines, and truthful monetization language.
