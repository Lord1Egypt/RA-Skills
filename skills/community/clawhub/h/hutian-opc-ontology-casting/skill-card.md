## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language, five-dimension framework for assessing an enterprise's OPC transformation potential on a 50-point scale.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

OPC mentors and enterprise transformation consultants use this skill to assess whether an organization is ready for OPC-style transformation, then plan ontology extraction, skill distillation, and agent deployment work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on the scoring framework as a business decision authority.

Mitigation: Treat the output as advisory guidance and have qualified stakeholders validate recommendations against current enterprise data and constraints.

Risk: The methodology reflects Chinese-language OPC ecosystem assumptions that may not fit every region, industry, or platform.

Mitigation: Review regional, contractual, and operational assumptions before applying the framework to a specific transformation plan.

Risk: Enterprise diagnosis can involve sensitive business, customer, or employee information.

Mitigation: Use approved internal systems and complete privacy, security, and contract review before sharing sensitive data with external platforms.
