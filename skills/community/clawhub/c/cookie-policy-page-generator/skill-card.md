## Description:

Helps agents create or optimize cookie policy pages covering cookie types, consent flows, jurisdictional context, policy structure, and legal-review disclaimers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External site owners, content teams, and developers use this skill to draft or improve cookie policy pages that explain cookie categories, third-party cookies, consent management, and user choices. It supports GDPR, ePrivacy, and CCPA-oriented planning while recommending legal review for final policy text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated cookie policy content may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific jurisdiction, consent setup, or third-party tracking stack.

Mitigation: Review final policy text with qualified legal or compliance stakeholders before publication.

Risk: The skill may activate on broad cookie-related language when the user means a non-compliance cookie topic.

Mitigation: Clarify whether the user is asking about website cookie compliance before producing policy guidance.
