## Description:

Kitchen remodeling budget planner: split a kitchen remodel budget into line items (cabinets, labor, appliances, countertops, flooring, lighting, plumbing and more), add sales tax, a contingency reserve and a permit allowance, or price a minor, major or upscale remodel from Cost vs Value averages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and remodel planners use the skill to divide an all-in kitchen budget into construction line items or estimate a project's total cost, including contingency, sales tax, and permit allowances.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Default tax, permit, and cost figures may not match the user's location or project.

Mitigation: Use the user's local sales tax rate, confirm permit fees with the city, and present totals as planning numbers rather than a bid.
