## Use Case:

External users and employees can use this skill for general investing education, including account-type orientation, portfolio concepts, allocation basics, and international investing considerations. It is not for personalized investment recommendations, tax planning, estate planning, or real-money decisions without current official sources and qualified professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake educational investing content for personalized investment, tax, estate, or fiduciary advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as general education only and consult qualified financial, tax, or legal professionals before making decisions involving real money.

Risk: Financial rules, account features, tax treatment, and regulations may be outdated or jurisdiction-specific.

Mitigation: Verify current requirements with official sources and local authorities before acting on any account, tax, or regulatory information.

Risk: Historical market examples and common allocation frameworks may be interpreted as predictions or recommendations.

Mitigation: Present historical context as non-predictive, emphasize that investments can lose value, and avoid recommending a specific allocation without professional review.
