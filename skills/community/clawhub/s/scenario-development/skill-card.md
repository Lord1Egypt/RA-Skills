## Description:

Develop multiple future scenarios to prepare for uncertainty for long-term strategic planning, risk management, and organizational foresight.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linuszz](https://clawhub.ai/user/linuszz)

## Use Case:

Employees, external strategists, and planning teams use this skill to structure scenario-planning work around a focal question, critical uncertainties, narrative futures, early warning signposts, and strategic implications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad scenario or uncertainty terms and provide planning guidance outside the user's intended context.

Mitigation: Confirm the focal question, time horizon, scope, and intended audience before relying on outputs.

Risk: Scenario narratives and strategic implications can be misleading if treated as forecasts.

Mitigation: Review outputs with domain experts, validate assumptions against current evidence, and use scenarios for stress-testing rather than prediction.
