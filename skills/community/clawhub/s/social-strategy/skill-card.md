## Description:

Use to set a brand's overall social media strategy, including channel focus, objectives, strategic angle, trade-offs, and operating cadence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and business teams use this skill to create or reset a focused social media strategy before planning content pillars or calendars. It helps choose 1-3 channels, define what to drop, set a business-tied objective, and produce a sustainable social-strategy.md plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated strategy may contain incorrect, incomplete, or misleading business guidance.

Mitigation: Review social-strategy.md before using it for business decisions or downstream content planning.

Risk: The skill reads existing brand, audience, and content planning documents when available.

Mitigation: Run it only in workspaces where those planning files are appropriate for the agent to read.
