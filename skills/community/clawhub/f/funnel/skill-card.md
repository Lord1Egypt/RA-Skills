## Description:

A comprehensive AI agent skill for building and optimizing marketing and sales funnels for SaaS founders, marketers, and growth teams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agistack](https://clawhub.ai/user/agistack)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SaaS founders, growth marketers, product managers, and marketing teams use this skill to diagnose funnel drop-offs, design measurement frameworks, write conversion copy, plan onboarding and nurture sequences, and structure conversion experiments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Common marketing terms may trigger the skill in broad contexts where funnel advice is not the user's actual intent.

Mitigation: Confirm that the request is about a marketing or sales funnel and review the advice against the user's business model before acting on it.
