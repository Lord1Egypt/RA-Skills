## Description:

Researches SEO keywords for a topic and generates an article brief with primary and secondary keywords, competitor analysis, article structure, and SEO recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kryzl19](https://clawhub.ai/user/kryzl19)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content strategists, SEO writers, and developers use this skill to plan SEO articles, cluster keywords, compare ranking pages, and create markdown article briefs for content production.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill performs web searches and fetches competitor pages, so results may reflect incomplete, outdated, or ranking-biased sources.

Mitigation: Review the cited pages and validate keyword and competitor claims before using the brief for publication or strategy decisions.

Risk: Broad content-planning prompts may route to this SEO workflow when the user intended only general brainstorming.

Mitigation: Use explicit topic, audience, and article-brief prompts when invoking the skill, and confirm the desired SEO workflow before applying recommendations.
