## Description:

A Chinese-language local CLI helper for planning multi-platform content schedules, linking posts to holidays or events, tracking manually entered engagement metrics, and generating weekly or monthly Markdown reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nh5gntnf78-oss](https://clawhub.ai/user/nh5gntnf78-oss)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, content operators, and agents use this skill to draft social content calendars for Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, and WeChat, then record post performance and produce review reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill stores planning data and manually entered engagement metrics in ~/content-calendar.

Mitigation: Install only if local retention is acceptable, and review or delete ~/content-calendar when the retained planning or performance data is no longer needed.
