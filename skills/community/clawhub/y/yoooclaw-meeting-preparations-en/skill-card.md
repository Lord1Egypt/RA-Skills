## Description:

Generates concise pre-meeting briefings by organizing recent notification content and, when useful, recent web-search findings around a meeting topic, key findings, and suggested focus points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vivalavida-say-hi](https://clawhub.ai/user/vivalavida-say-hi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and other meeting participants use this skill to prepare for upcoming meetings by summarizing recent updates about specified people or topics, grouping findings into themes, and identifying points to discuss.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read recent notification content about people or topics supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Use only with meeting topics and people whose notification content the user is comfortable processing, and review the briefing output for sensitive information before sharing.

Risk: The skill may search the web and incorporate recent external findings into the briefing.

Mitigation: Check source labels and verify important claims before using them for business decisions or meeting commitments.

Risk: The skill can automatically add an unpinned remote web-search dependency when the dependency is missing.

Mitigation: Prefer a reviewed version that asks before installing dependencies and uses pinned, verified sources.
