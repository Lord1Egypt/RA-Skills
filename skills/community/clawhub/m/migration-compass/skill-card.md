## Description:

Migration Compass generates step-by-step migration plans for framework, library, language, database, architecture, and version transitions with rollback points, parallel-run strategies, and ordered change sequencing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jcools1977](https://clawhub.ai/user/jcools1977)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to plan incremental migrations across libraries, frameworks, languages, databases, architectures, and major versions. It helps produce ordered rollout steps with validation gates, rollback points, risk areas, effort estimates, and parallel work opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause an agent to produce migration plans during vague replacement discussions.

Mitigation: Confirm that a migration plan is desired and define the source, target, and scope before following suggested project changes.
