## Description:

Build a structured account plan for any key customer or target account, including relationship map, growth opportunities, risks, and a 90-day action plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Account managers, sales leaders, and customer success teams use this skill to create key account plans, strategic account reviews, and territory planning documents. It structures customer inputs into account snapshot, relationship map, retention anchors, growth opportunities, risks, 90-day actions, and measurable success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated plans may include confidential account, customer, revenue, relationship, renewal, or expansion information.

Mitigation: Handle inputs and generated plans according to the user's normal data policies before sharing or storing them.
