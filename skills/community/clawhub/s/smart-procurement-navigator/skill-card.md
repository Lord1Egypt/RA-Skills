## Description: <br>
智慧招采通（Smart Procurement Navigator）覆盖从商机甄别、投标研判、采购文件解读、评审模型构建，到应答方案架构设计、文稿生成、内容润色与提交前合规审查的完整招采作业流程。 <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Procurement, business development, proposal, and bid decision teams use this skill to analyze procurement opportunities, extract tender requirements, build evaluation models, plan bid responses, draft structured materials, and review submission readiness. It supports human decision-making for Chinese government procurement, engineering construction, service procurement, and related electronic bidding workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
China <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Procurement files, company qualifications, pricing, historical bids, and commercial strategy may contain confidential or personal information. <br>
Mitigation: Install only in authorized environments, redact unnecessary personal details, and process bid materials only where the organization permits agent access. <br>
Risk: Generated bid analysis, legal timing guidance, pricing strategy, commitments, or submission recommendations may be incomplete or incorrect. <br>
Mitigation: Require qualified human review for legal interpretation, pricing, commitments, signatures, complaints, final bid decisions, and final submissions. <br>
Risk: Reference bid files or examples may leave stale project names, locations, model numbers, schedules, prices, or internal strategy language in draft outputs. <br>
Mitigation: Run the skill's consistency, residual-reference, and compliance checks, then manually verify all project identifiers and commercial terms before use. <br>
