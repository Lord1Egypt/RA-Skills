## Description:

Punch up your writing with actual wit: toasts, bios, birthday messages, Slack posts, presentation openers, witty daily briefs, original one-liners in English and Chinese, and joke scoring and repair.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external users use this skill to add tasteful humor to everyday writing, create original jokes and captions, write warm occasion messages, and evaluate or improve draft jokes. It also guides agents to avoid humor in serious, stressful, health, legal, grief, or financial-loss contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Humor could be inappropriate in serious, legal, health, grief, financial-loss, error-reporting, or stressed-user contexts.

Mitigation: Avoid invoking humor mode for those contexts; the skill directs the agent to answer plainly and skip the joke when the context is sensitive.

Risk: Humor may punch down or target personal traits, creating reputational or interpersonal harm.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's target-up-or-inward rule: joke about systems, powerful actors, situations, or the assistant itself rather than protected traits, death, illness, or victimization.
