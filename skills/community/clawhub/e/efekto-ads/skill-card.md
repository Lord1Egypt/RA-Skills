## Description:

Run paid ads for a small business or a new app from chat - Facebook, Instagram and Google ads made from a website address, published, and switched off when they stop bringing leads. Uses the Efekto MCP server.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users running a small business or launching an app can use this skill to create and review paid ad previews, manage campaign budgets and status, and understand spend and lead results across Facebook, Instagram, and Google.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing ads, resuming campaigns, or changing budgets can incur advertising spend.

Mitigation: State the daily budget and what will go live, then wait for clear user approval before taking these actions.

Risk: Campaign lead records can contain personal contact details.

Mitigation: Only access lead contact details for the account owner and avoid sharing them beyond the authorized user.
