## Use Case:

External course creators and instructors use this skill to plan Udemy course topics, draft curriculum and learning materials, prepare publishing assets, and manage post-launch updates under human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Udemy instructor, GenAI, and quality-review policy details may become stale.

Mitigation: Verify Udemy's current instructor terms and GenAI policy before publishing course materials.

Risk: Drafted course materials may contain inaccurate, low-quality, or noncompliant AI-assisted content.

Mitigation: Keep a human instructor in the loop to review, edit, disclose AI assistance, and confirm accuracy before publication.

Risk: Using generated materials without manual control could conflict with platform expectations.

Mitigation: Use the skill for drafting and organization only; perform uploads, logins, pricing, and other Udemy account actions manually.
