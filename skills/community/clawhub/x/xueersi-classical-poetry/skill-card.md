## Description:

Xueersi Classical Poetry Assistant helps Chinese K-12 students memorize classical poems, analyze literary techniques, play Feihualing, and ask poetry questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External students and educators use this skill as a Chinese classical poetry tutoring aid for memorization practice, short literary explanations, Feihualing play, and poetry Q&A.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expect support outside Chinese classical poetry or in another language.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for another language or scope when needed; otherwise expect Chinese poetry-focused tutoring.

Risk: Poetry citations, source attribution, or line corrections could be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review important poem text and attribution before using outputs for graded or published work.
