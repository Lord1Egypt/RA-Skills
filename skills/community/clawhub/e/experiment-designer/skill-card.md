## Description:

Design statistically rigorous A/B tests and interpret experiment results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Product managers, growth teams, analysts, and developers use this skill to plan A/B tests, estimate sample size and run time, define success criteria, and interpret experiment results with statistical and practical significance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for business metrics such as baselines, sample sizes, and experiment results.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential data unless appropriate for the workspace and data-handling policy.

Risk: Experiment recommendations can affect product or business decisions if the inputs, assumptions, or statistical interpretation are wrong.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations with a qualified analyst or data scientist for high-stakes decisions.
