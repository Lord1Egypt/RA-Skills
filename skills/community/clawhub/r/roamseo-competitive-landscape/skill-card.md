## Description:

Map SEO market leaders, winning content themes, keyword coverage, backlinks, and strategic gaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[roamseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/roamseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External SEO practitioners and marketing teams use this skill to compare several competitors across a market, identify winning search themes and content patterns, and find opportunities for their own site.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A small query set or estimated traffic data can make market winners and gaps appear more certain than they are.

Mitigation: Describe small-query-set results as directional and label third-party traffic as an estimate.

Risk: SEO competitors may be mistaken for business competitors, or a publisher may be misclassified as a product competitor.

Mitigation: Distinguish SEO competitors from business competitors and label each domain type explicitly.

Risk: Re-running recent research can waste credits and produce redundant analysis.

Mitigation: Check the research log and reuse matching research from the last 30 days.
