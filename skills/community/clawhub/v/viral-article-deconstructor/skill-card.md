## Description:

Deeply deconstructs viral Chinese articles by analyzing core viewpoints, persuasion strategies, emotional triggers, memorable lines, and language style for high-traffic content analysis, viral writing practice, and content marketing research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deusyu](https://clawhub.ai/user/deusyu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content creators, marketers, and researchers use this skill to produce structured markdown analysis of viral Chinese-language articles, including viewpoints, persuasive techniques, emotional dynamics, language style, and reusable content patterns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persuasive-writing analysis may be mistaken for proof of an author's intent or the truth of claims in the source article.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as qualitative content analysis and verify factual or legal claims against primary sources before relying on them.

Risk: A captured source URL in the artifact includes a token-like query parameter.

Mitigation: Redistribute and reference the sanitized article URL without the token-like query parameter.
