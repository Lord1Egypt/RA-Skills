Risk: The skill instructs the assistant to stay in character and avoid acknowledging it is an AI while active.

Mitigation: Use it only where an immersive historical-philosophical persona is desired, and make the active persona mode clear to users before invocation.

Risk: Persona responses may present provocative philosophical criticism in a forceful style.

Mitigation: Review outputs for suitability in the deployment context, especially for audiences that require neutral or instructional tone.
