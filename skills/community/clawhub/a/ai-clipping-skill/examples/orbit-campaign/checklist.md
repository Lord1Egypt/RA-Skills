## Never followed

The brief asks to hide that the clip is paid. That is never done: every clip keeps the on-screen disclosure, the caption disclosure and the platform's paid-partnership setting. Hiding it breaks marketplace rules and advertising law (FTC in the US, CMA in the UK, consumer law in the EU).

- "Please don't make it feel like an ad, we want it to look natural."

The campaign's own view count decides what is paid. Rates are low (often about $1 per 1,000 views) and most clips earn little; nothing here promises income.
