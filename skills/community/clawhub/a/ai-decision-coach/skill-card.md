## Description:

AI决策助手。帮用户分析决策困境，权衡利弊，给出建议。不是替用户做决定，而是帮用户看清局面。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill as a Chinese-language decision-analysis aid for comparing options, weighing pros and cons, assessing risks, and identifying practical next steps while keeping the final decision with the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on general decision guidance for medical, legal, financial, crisis, or other high-stakes choices.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as reflection support and seek qualified specialist advice for high-stakes decisions.

Risk: Broad activation can apply the decision template to situations where more context or professional judgment is needed.

Mitigation: Review the generated analysis against the user's actual constraints and ask clarifying questions before acting on recommendations.

Risk: Chinese-only presentation may limit accessibility for users who do not read Chinese.

Mitigation: Translate or adapt the generated structure before use with non-Chinese-speaking audiences.
