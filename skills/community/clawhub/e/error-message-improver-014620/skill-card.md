## Description:

Helps developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users turn vague error messages into clearer guidance that explains what failed, why it failed, and what to do next.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and end users use this skill to draft actionable error messages, troubleshooting copy, checklists, workflows, analysis, code changes, or decision support for debugging and support scenarios.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger terms may cause the skill to appear during general debugging or support requests.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly when the desired output is error-message wording, troubleshooting copy, or failure-guidance structure.
