## Description:

Turns a research brief and source materials into an evidence-checked, reviewed Markdown industry deep-dive article with a final evidence package.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Analysts and technology writers use it to develop a single evidence-backed deep-dive for technology, AI, data, cloud, or enterprise-software audiences, from a topic brief and source materials through editorial review and a final Markdown package.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External originality searches may expose unpublished or confidential wording or claims.

Mitigation: Review and authorize material before sending it to external search providers; omit or generalize sensitive content when authorization is absent.

Risk: A validation path is reported to be able to skip a human approval gate.

Mitigation: Keep the human approval gates in the workflow and review proposed changes before accepting them or producing the final article.
