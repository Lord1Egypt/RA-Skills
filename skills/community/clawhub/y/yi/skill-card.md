## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for I Ching and Zhouyi hexagram lookup, scripted coin, number, or time divination, changing-line interpretation, and reflective decision support. It is intended as cultural and philosophical guidance, not prediction or professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat divination results as reliable predictions or professional advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as reflective cultural guidance and keep medical, legal, financial, safety-critical, pregnancy, and life-critical decisions with qualified professionals and user judgment.

Risk: Some bundled hexagram data may be incomplete.

Mitigation: Quote or summarize only bundled data that is present and clearly state when classical text is unavailable.

Risk: Simulated coin divination can be mistaken for user-provided physical coin results.

Mitigation: State when randomness is simulated unless the user supplies their own six line values.
