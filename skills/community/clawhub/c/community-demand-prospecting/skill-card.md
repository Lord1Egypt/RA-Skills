## Description:

Audit a repo or product, run market research and competitor research, choose positioning, find users, and draft safe Reddit or X outreach.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers, founders, and product teams use this skill to turn a repository, README, landing page, or product brief into launch-readiness feedback, market research, positioning, buyer-intent searches, scored community opportunities, and human-reviewed outreach drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated Reddit or X replies could be used without adequate human review.

Mitigation: Review every draft before posting and treat the skill as a research and drafting aid, not an automated posting system.

Risk: Community outreach can violate platform or subreddit rules when replies are promotional, repetitive, or off-topic.

Mitigation: Follow community rules, prefer helpful participation, skip weak-fit threads, and avoid high-volume outreach.

Risk: Product mentions can be misleading if the poster's affiliation is hidden.

Mitigation: Disclose affiliation when promoting your own product and avoid fake neutrality or deceptive personas.
