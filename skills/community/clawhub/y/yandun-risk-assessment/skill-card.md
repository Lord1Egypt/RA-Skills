## Description:

Provides five-dimensional demonstration stock risk scoring, metric calculation, and risk-level classification.

This skill is for demonstration purposes and not for production usage.

## Use Case:

Developers, educators, and analysts can use this skill to generate illustrative stock risk assessments for teaching or demonstration workflows. The output is not investment advice and does not represent a real financial risk-control conclusion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the illustrative scores for investment advice or production financial risk analysis.

Mitigation: Present outputs as demonstration-only risk scoring and require separate validation before any financial or investment use.

Risk: The skill does not use live market data and may not satisfy workflows that require current data or English output.

Mitigation: Confirm data-source and language requirements before relying on the generated assessment.
