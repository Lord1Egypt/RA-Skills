## Description:

Structured Darktable photo critique and non-destructive RAW edit workflow. Evidence-based analysis, phased collaboration loop, and restrained post-processing guidance for Darktable 5.6 modules, tone mappers, masks, and photographic-art analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and photo-editing agents use this skill to analyze photographs, confirm an editing direction, and produce restrained, non-destructive Darktable guidance. It is intended for RAW photographers who need evidence-based critique, Darktable module choices, and complete edit plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Photo files and metadata can contain sensitive personal, location, or rights-related information.

Mitigation: Use supplied images and metadata only for the requested analysis, identify visible privacy or rights risks without legal conclusions, and avoid unsupported private inferences.

Risk: Darktable controls, release behavior, or publication-ready art-historical facts may require current verification.

Mitigation: Use official Darktable or museum sources when live research is available; in air-gapped use, state the evidence gap and provide only supported guidance.

Risk: Rendered images can make RAW clipping, highlight recoverability, focus cause, or editing history uncertain.

Mitigation: Separate visible facts, judgments, and unknowns, and avoid recoverability or processing-history claims unless RAW data, scopes, history, or screenshots support them.
