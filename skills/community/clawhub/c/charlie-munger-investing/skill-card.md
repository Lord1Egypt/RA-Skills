## Description:

Charlie Munger's multidisciplinary thinking and mental models, built from rationality, inversion, patience, and the lollapalooza effect.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer investing and decision-making questions through Charlie Munger's mental models, including inversion, circle of competence, margin of safety, patience, and multidisciplinary reasoning. It is best suited for general reasoning guidance, not personalized financial, legal, or tax advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake Munger-style investing principles for personalized financial, legal, or tax advice.

Mitigation: Present responses as general decision-making and investing philosophy, and direct users to qualified professionals for personal financial, legal, or tax decisions.

Risk: Broad triggers such as mental models or inversion may activate the skill for generic reasoning prompts where the Munger frame is not desired.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger configuration during deployment if the skill should activate only for Charlie Munger, Berkshire Hathaway, or investing-specific contexts.
