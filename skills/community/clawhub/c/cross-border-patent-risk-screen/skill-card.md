## Description:

Screens cross-border e-commerce products for preliminary patent infringement risk across design, invention, and utility model patents using product image URLs, product links, or product descriptions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Cross-border e-commerce patent analysts use this skill before product launch or market entry to triage infringement risk in target markets. It produces a preliminary structured report with risk levels, relevant patent lists, legal status, similarity notes, and avoidance suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local, confidential, unreleased, regulated, or trade-secret product images may be uploaded to an external service and made available through a public URL.

Mitigation: Prefer public product links or already-public image URLs; obtain explicit user confirmation and retention/deletion details before uploading any local product image.

Risk: The report provides AI-assisted preliminary patent risk screening and may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Present results as triage only and require review by a qualified patent lawyer before making launch, sales, licensing, or design-around decisions.
