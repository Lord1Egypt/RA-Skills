## Use Case:

Employees, leaders, coaches, and communicators use this skill to build, review, repair, adapt, rehearse, and pressure-test leadership stories using the CARE framework: Context, Action, Resolution, and Engagement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include confidential, identifying, legal, HR, medical, customer, or security details while drafting leadership stories.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive details unless they are appropriate for the target audience; anonymize people, customers, and internal events when needed.

Risk: A leadership story may overstate facts, causality, metrics, or outcomes if the user has not provided evidence.

Mitigation: Keep claims tied to user-provided facts, mark uncertainty clearly, and ask for proof points before using metrics, quotes, chronology, or outcomes.
