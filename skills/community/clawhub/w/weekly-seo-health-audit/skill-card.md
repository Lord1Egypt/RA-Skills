## Description:

Weekly SEO Health Audit is a read-only SEO audit skill that surfaces quick-win keyword opportunities, low-effort technical fixes, and a one-page prioritized opportunity list for a site.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hkim08](https://clawhub.ai/user/hkim08)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External site owners, SEO teams, and developers use this skill to run a concise weekly SEO health check on a specified domain and decide which keyword, metadata, indexing, schema, or internal-link fixes to prioritize next.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill makes read-only web requests to public pages on the audited site.

Mitigation: Invoke it with a specific domain and review the one-page output before acting on any recommendation.

Risk: SEO opportunities and reported conversion figures may be misunderstood as guaranteed performance results.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a prioritized opportunity list and keep reported research signals clearly framed as non-guaranteed context.
