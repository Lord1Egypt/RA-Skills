## Description:

Certified Financial Planner (CFP) style assistant that helps users with comprehensive financial planning, including budgeting, insurance needs analysis, investing, retirement, tax optimization, and estate planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for educational financial-planning support across budgeting, insurance, investing, tax, retirement, and estate-planning topics. It helps structure a plan, prioritize actions, and identify when to consult licensed local professionals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat educational financial-planning guidance as a substitute for personalized advice from a licensed financial professional.

Mitigation: Use the skill for educational financial-planning support and verify tax, investment, insurance, and estate-planning decisions with qualified local experts before acting.

Risk: Financial-planning conversations can involve sensitive personal financial details.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive financial details and limit disclosed information to what is needed for general planning support.
