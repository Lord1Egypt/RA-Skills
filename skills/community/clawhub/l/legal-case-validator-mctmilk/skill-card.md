## Description:

This skill helps users validate statutory analysis against real legal cases by examining judicial practice, judgment divergences, frequent reasons for losing, and judge review priorities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mctmilk](https://clawhub.ai/user/mctmilk)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Legal practitioners and users reviewing Chinese legal analysis use this skill to check whether statutory interpretations align with judicial practice. It guides agents to identify common case types, judgment divergences, frequent reasons for losing, and practical judicial review points while requiring independent case verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated case numbers, case names, or judicial summaries may appear plausible but be nonexistent or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Verify every cited case and case summary in authoritative legal databases such as China Judgments Online, PKU Law, or Faxin before relying on it.

Risk: Users may mistake the generated research checklist or analysis framework for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as research support and have legal conclusions reviewed by a qualified professional before use in legal matters.
