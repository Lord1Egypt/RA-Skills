## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Solana transaction guidance can become stale or be insufficient for a specific transaction.

Mitigation: Verify addresses, simulations, current rent values, base fees, and priority fees before signing or broadcasting.

Risk: Wallet approval guidance may be misapplied to high-value or unknown-site transactions.

Mitigation: Never expose seed phrases or private keys, use burner wallets for unknown sites, and review wallet simulation prompts carefully.
