### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated depression, anxiety, stress, or similar self-tests could be mistaken for medical or mental-health diagnosis.

Mitigation: Add clear disclaimers, avoid diagnostic claims, and direct real users to qualified professional care where appropriate.

Risk: Generated quizzes, scoring rules, or result interpretations may be misleading if published without review.

Mitigation: Review questions, scoring logic, and result text for accuracy, bias, suitability, and audience impact before use.
