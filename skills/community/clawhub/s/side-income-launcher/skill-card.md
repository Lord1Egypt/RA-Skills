## Description:

Helps employed users evaluate a low-risk side-income idea, stay within workplace boundaries, and produce a Markdown launch plan for a first paid experiment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cglt2026opc](https://clawhub.ai/user/cglt2026opc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and independent professionals use this skill to assess monetizable capabilities, choose a first lightweight offer, and create a workplace-aware side-income validation plan. The plan emphasizes market feedback, limited time commitment, and avoiding use of employer resources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A side-income plan may conflict with an employer's policies, time expectations, or resource restrictions.

Mitigation: Confirm employer policies before acting, keep validation lightweight, avoid employer resources, and revise the plan around any workplace constraints.

Risk: Generated Markdown may include attribution or wording the user does not want to share.

Mitigation: Review the generated plan before use or distribution and remove unwanted attribution or promotional language.

Risk: Business guidance may not match the user's real market, pricing power, or readiness.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a planning draft, validate assumptions with a small paid experiment, and adjust pricing and scope from actual feedback.
