## Description:

Plan Figma prototype interactions and user-testing flows, producing prototype scope, interaction specifications, test task scripts, and setup guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product managers, designers, researchers, and developers use this skill to plan the minimum Figma prototype needed for a usability test, define clickable versus static areas, and align prototype behavior with test tasks and success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A prototype plan can over-scope interactions and waste usability test effort.

Mitigation: Keep scope tied to the research question and explicitly mark screens or elements as clickable or static.

Risk: Poorly framed test tasks can bias participant behavior or fail to answer the research question.

Mitigation: Use scenario-based task scripts with clear observing points, success criteria, and a reset process between participants.
