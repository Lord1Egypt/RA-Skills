## Description:

Helps agents create structured industry research reports, market analyses, business trend analyses, and professional research documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chaoliuzhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/chaoliuzhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business users, analysts, and agents use this skill to scope, research, structure, and draft industry research reports with cited data, market context, competitive analysis, risks, opportunities, and recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports may include outdated, unsupported, or misleading market claims if cited sources are not reviewed.

Mitigation: Review cited sources, confirm data recency, and cross-check key claims before using the report for business decisions.

Risk: Research prompts may include confidential business data.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential business data unless it is intended to be used in the research workflow.
