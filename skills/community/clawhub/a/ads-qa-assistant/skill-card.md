## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can activate for broad marketing or growth questions without enough campaign, platform, or KPI context.

Mitigation: Ask for clear ad-platform, campaign, timeline, and KPI context before making operational recommendations.

Risk: Ads recommendations may be applied directly to live ad accounts without review.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before applying them and use validation checkpoints for changes that affect budget, targeting, bidding, or compliance.

Risk: The skill may be asked to reason from incomplete or low-quality performance data.

Mitigation: Mark assumptions explicitly, request missing critical data, and prefer conservative recommendations when data quality is low.
