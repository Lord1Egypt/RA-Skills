## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Avoid using the skill for confidential topics unless that tool exposure is acceptable.

Risk: Research outputs may contain incorrect, outdated, or weakly supported claims.

Mitigation: Verify cited sources before relying on the output for important decisions.
