## Description:

Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams create practical PollyReach-style workflows, including plans, checklists, automation outlines, reliability improvements, and safety hardening guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and maintainers use this skill to turn a PollyReach-style productivity or workflow request into an actionable local-hardware-friendly plan, checklist, automation outline, code change, or decision aid. It is intended for practical workflow creation, bug fixing, setup hardening, reliability improvement, and adjacent skill authoring.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause accidental invocation for generic productivity or workflow requests.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation by skill name and narrow future trigger phrases to PollyReach-specific workflow creation, hardening, and reliability tasks.

Risk: The zh-CN localization may not fully match the English source content.

Mitigation: Review localized instructions before relying on the skill in Chinese-language workflows.
