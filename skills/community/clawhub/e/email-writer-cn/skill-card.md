## Description:

Helps agents draft and polish Mandarin business emails, including requests, follow-ups, apologies, proposals, internal updates, tone adjustments, subject lines, and etiquette checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[goldath](https://clawhub.ai/user/goldath)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external-facing business users use this skill to draft, polish, and structure Mandarin professional email communication for customers, partners, government contacts, managers, and internal teams.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is scoped to Mandarin business email writing and may be unsuitable for other languages or general multilingual correspondence.

Mitigation: Use it only for Mandarin professional email tasks, or choose a different skill for other languages.
