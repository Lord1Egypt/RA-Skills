## Description:

Cross-platform content strategy and operations for brands, merchants, and creators, including planning topics, structuring briefs, generating or rewriting publish-ready posts, adapting content for major platforms, creating editorial calendars, and reviewing performance for optimization steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content teams, creators, merchants, and brand operators use this skill to turn incomplete business context into platform-ready content plans, drafts, rewrites, calendars, and performance improvement recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process local source notes passed to its helper scripts.

Mitigation: Only provide files intended for content work, and review generated content before publishing.

Risk: Publish-ready content can become misleading if source material lacks verified product facts, proof, or performance data.

Mitigation: Check factual claims against supplied source material and keep unsupported claims as explicit placeholders or remove them.
