## Description:

Plan email campaigns with segmentation, A/B testing, and benchmarking. Use when designing campaigns, segmenting lists, benchmarking rates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing operators and campaign teams use this skill to record campaign configuration, benchmarks, comparisons, prompts, evaluations, tuning notes, analysis, costs, usage, optimization results, tests, and reports in local logs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Campaign notes may be stored as plaintext local files and later exported.

Mitigation: Avoid entering customer personal data, credentials, mailing-list contents, or confidential strategy unless local permissions, retention, and export handling are managed outside the skill.

Risk: Exported logs may carry sensitive campaign context outside the local data directory.

Mitigation: Review exported JSON, CSV, or text files before sharing them and delete exports that are no longer needed.
