## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Assessment answers may contain sensitive business security information, secrets, credentials, personal data, or user identifiers.

Mitigation: Submit only information appropriate for ToolWeb's external API, and avoid secrets, credentials, unnecessary personal data, and nonessential user identifiers.
