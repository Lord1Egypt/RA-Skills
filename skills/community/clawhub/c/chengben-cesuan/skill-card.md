## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may trigger on broad cost-analysis phrases outside a construction estimating context.

Mitigation: Use it for construction project costing and review whether the user's request fits that domain before applying the formulas.

Risk: The formulas, tax rate, and cost assumptions appear tailored to China-region construction practice.

Mitigation: Review local labor rates, material prices, tax rules, and procurement norms before using results for commercial decisions.
