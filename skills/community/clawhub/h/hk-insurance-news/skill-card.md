## Description:

Generates a Chinese-language weekly WeChat-style Markdown report summarizing Hong Kong insurance market news, product updates, and company developments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[castlewang](https://clawhub.ai/user/castlewang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Insurance analysts, financial content creators, and Hong Kong insurance practitioners use this skill to collect weekly public market updates and draft a categorized WeChat article with summaries, source links, and optional images.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may contain stale, incomplete, or unsupported insurance-market claims from public sources.

Mitigation: Review the generated article against its cited source links before publishing or sharing it.

Risk: Embedded links, image URLs, or local image references may be unsuitable for publication.

Mitigation: Check every link, image URL, and local file reference before using the generated Markdown.
