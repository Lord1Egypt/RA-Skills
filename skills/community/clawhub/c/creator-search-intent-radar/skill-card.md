## Description:

Convert TikTok/YouTube/Instagram search and trend signals into a prioritized weekly content backlog with script angles and hook directions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators and creator teams use this skill to turn social search, trend, comment, and FAQ signals into a ranked weekly content backlog with platform fit, hooks, CTAs, and a lightweight publishing schedule.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private comments, DMs, or community data could be included in content research inputs.

Mitigation: Use only conversation data that is appropriate for content planning, and avoid entering private or sensitive creator-audience messages unless the user is authorized to use them.

Risk: Fallback-mode recommendations may be mistaken for current live trend evidence.

Mitigation: Require fallback-mode outputs to be clearly labeled and treated as non-live trend analysis.

Risk: The artifact license text conflicts with the server-resolved release license.

Mitigation: Confirm whether MIT-0 or the artifact's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms govern this release before public publication or commercial use.
