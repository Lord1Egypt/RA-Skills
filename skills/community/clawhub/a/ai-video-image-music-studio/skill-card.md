## Description:

Generates AI videos, images, music, and voice-over, edits visual results, and organizes completed media in one creative workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers can use the skill to make and manage videos, images, music, narration, and other media for campaigns, courses, podcasts, and short films.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installation and environment details may be sent for analytics.

Mitigation: Review the privacy implications before installation and avoid using the skill in environments where this telemetry is unacceptable.

Risk: Local media or text may be sent to Beatra without clear advance disclosure.

Mitigation: Upload only media and text that are appropriate to share with Beatra, after reviewing the applicable privacy terms.

Risk: If the shared inventory is malformed, uninstall may remove connection state used by other skills.

Mitigation: Review the shared inventory and uninstall guidance before removal, then confirm other skills can still connect.
