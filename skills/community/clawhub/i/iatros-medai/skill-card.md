## Description:

Iatros（诺康） helps agents produce Chinese health-literacy and health-communication content with a consistent educational voice, signature style, and explicit boundaries against personalized medical advice, approvals, certifications, or exaggerated health claims.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Use Case:

External content authors and agents use this skill to draft non-personalized Chinese health-literacy articles, handbooks, image-copy text, rumor-checking responses, and signature or boundary language under the Iatros（诺康） persona. It is intended for health education and information communication, not diagnosis, treatment decisions, medication advice, urgent issues, regulatory approvals, or professional medical decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake generated health-literacy content for diagnosis, treatment choices, medication advice, urgent-care guidance, or professional medical decisions.

Mitigation: Keep outputs non-personalized and educational, include boundary language where relevant, and direct individual health questions to qualified institutions or professionals.

Risk: Externally facing health content may overstate effects or use absolute claims.

Mitigation: Use restrained, evidence-aware language, avoid superlatives and outcome promises, and present health effects as context-dependent.

Risk: The release has no server-resolved GitHub import provenance.

Mitigation: Evaluate the packaged artifact files and ClawHub release evidence directly before deployment.
