## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Membership offers, point values, and third-party benefit details may be stale or incorrect.

Mitigation: Verify offers, redemption terms, and privilege availability with the official provider before acting.

Risk: Real membership-account authorization or points redemption may expose sensitive account information or cause account changes.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing unnecessary personal data, review requested authorizations carefully, and confirm any redemption action before proceeding.

Risk: Third-party interfaces or links suggested during use may change or become unreliable.

Mitigation: Prefer official provider channels and check link destinations before signing in or submitting account details.
