## Use Case:

External Amazon FBA sellers use this skill to monitor inventory, plan reorders, audit listings, track advertising performance, review financials, and compare competitors. It is aimed at private label and wholesale sellers who want recurring operational checklists, calculations, and draft business outputs.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use sensitive seller business data such as supplier details, inventory levels, advertising spend, costs, and margins.

Mitigation: Provide only the data needed for the task, omit credentials from prompts, and avoid sharing unnecessary confidential business information.

Risk: Generated purchase orders, seller responses, financial templates, and operational recommendations may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a specific business decision.

Mitigation: Review generated outputs before acting on them, especially financial calculations, purchase orders, and customer-facing responses.
