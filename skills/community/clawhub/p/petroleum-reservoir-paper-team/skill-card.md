## Description:

Deploys a WorkBuddy expert team for petroleum reservoir engineering paper writing, covering topic selection, research, drafting, fact checking, style polishing, review, and formatting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External researchers, students, and developers using WorkBuddy can install this team to support petroleum reservoir engineering paper workflows, including topic selection, literature and data review, drafting, fact checking, style revision, peer review, and formatting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installation adds a persistent WorkBuddy expert team and updates marketplace.json.

Mitigation: Review the deployment target before running the script and remove the team directory plus marketplace entry if the installation is no longer wanted.

Risk: The team may process user-provided drafts, notes, cloud-note content, and unpublished research material.

Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential or unpublished material unless the user is comfortable with those materials being processed by the team agents.

Risk: Academic outputs such as literature summaries, citations, data checks, and journal recommendations can affect research quality.

Mitigation: Verify important claims, data, formulas, citation details, and current journal requirements against authoritative sources before submission.
