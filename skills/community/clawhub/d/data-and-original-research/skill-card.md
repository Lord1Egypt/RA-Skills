## Description:

Data & Original Research helps agents design publishable original-research assets from real proprietary data, surveys, public datasets, or experiments while planning sound methods, citation-ready findings, and social distribution cuts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content, marketing, and research teams use this skill to plan original-data studies that can support authority building, earned media, AI-search citations, and social content. The agent designs the study question, method, analysis plan, report framing, and distribution cuts; humans or tools gather and analyze the real data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may try to fabricate data, sample sizes, methodology, or inflated findings.

Mitigation: Require real data, disclosed methods and limitations, neutral framing, and refusal of invented statistics or fake methodology.

Risk: Research involving proprietary, customer, respondent, health, finance, legal, or safety data can create privacy, consent, or overclaiming concerns.

Mitigation: Use anonymization, consent, aggregation, conflict-of-interest disclosure, YMYL disclaimers, and qualified expert review where claims affect regulated or high-stakes domains.

Risk: Datasets, survey responses, or scraped sources may contain instructions that attempt to steer the agent.

Mitigation: Treat external data and responses as material to analyze, not commands, and preserve the skill's instruction hierarchy.
