## Description:

Use this skill when a product manager, engineer, or stakeholder wants to write or structure a Product Requirements Document (PRD) for a new feature, enhancement, API change, or MVP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[archlab-space](https://clawhub.ai/user/archlab-space)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, engineers, and stakeholders use this skill to turn rough feature requests, enhancements, API changes, or MVP ideas into structured, review-ready PRDs for alignment before planning or implementation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste confidential roadmap details, metrics, revenue figures, or customer names while drafting a PRD.

Mitigation: Treat pasted product details as confidential and include only the information needed for the PRD.

Risk: The skill may draft assumptions or placeholders that could be mistaken for confirmed product decisions.

Mitigation: Keep assumption and TBD markers visible until a stakeholder or decision owner confirms them.
