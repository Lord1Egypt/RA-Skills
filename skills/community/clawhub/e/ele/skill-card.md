Ele helps agents advise on Ele.me food-delivery ordering savings, fulfillment issues, and merchant operation basics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to compare Ele.me coupon, membership, and order-discount choices and to prepare evidence for delivery after-sales requests. Merchants use it for high-level onboarding, fee-structure, fulfillment, and ranking-priority guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ele.me prices, coupons, membership terms, delivery compensation, and merchant fees may vary by city, time, and promotion.

Mitigation: Verify current values in the official Ele.me app or merchant portal before acting on the skill's guidance.

Risk: Merchant ranking or promotion advice could be misapplied as manipulative activity.

Mitigation: Keep guidance at the operational-priority level and avoid prohibited tactics such as fake orders, fake reviews, or exploit-based discount abuse.
