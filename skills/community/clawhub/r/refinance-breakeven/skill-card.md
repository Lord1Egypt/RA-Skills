## Description:

Computes refinance breakeven timing, payment delta, upfront costs, total interest for keep-versus-refi paths, and warnings for term resets, cash-out, points, and sale horizon.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to compare a current mortgage with a refinance offer, including payment delta, upfront costs, breakeven month, ownership horizon, total interest, and cash-out or term-reset warnings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Refinance calculations could be mistaken for individualized financial advice.

Mitigation: Present the result as educational, verify the math and assumptions, and consult a licensed financial professional before making refinancing decisions.

Risk: Incomplete or inaccurate mortgage inputs can produce misleading breakeven and total-interest comparisons.

Mitigation: Ask for current balance, rate, months left, refinance rate, new term, closing costs, points, ownership horizon, and whether the offer includes cash-out before producing a verdict.
