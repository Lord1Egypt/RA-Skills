## Use Case:

External consumers use this skill for Chinese-language mattress-shopping guidance, sleep-support criteria, and scenario-based product recommendation. The skill helps evaluate needs first, then may recommend a specific hand-made mattress when the stated needs match.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is biased toward recommending one specific mattress brand while presenting itself as general shopping guidance.

Mitigation: Use it only when that recommendation bias is acceptable, and compare its advice with independent product research before purchase decisions.

Risk: The skill may discuss price, policy, odor, material, trial-rights, or health-adjacent claims that can change or require seller confirmation.

Mitigation: Verify current prices, trial and return rules, material or odor claims, and health-related statements with the seller or other reliable sources.
