## Description:

Filter raw target lists into prioritized prospects worth pursuing, score fit, timing, value, and access, and route each target to pursue, research, watch, or drop.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[depinhq](https://clawhub.ai/user/depinhq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, sales teams, business development operators, and partnership teams use Prospect before outreach to rank target accounts, identify gaps and disqualifiers, and decide whether to pursue, research, watch, or drop each target.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-supplied target lists may contain private customer, partner, or prospect data.

Mitigation: Use the minimum necessary data, avoid adding sensitive personal details unless needed for the business decision, and keep outputs within the user's approved workspace and retention rules.

Risk: Generic prompts such as 'who should I target' may refer to contexts outside sales, partnerships, or business development.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants business target selection before applying the skill, and decline or redirect unrelated prospecting contexts.

Risk: Prospect scoring can affect outreach decisions that may be subject to privacy, platform, legal, or compliance requirements.

Mitigation: Treat the output as prioritization guidance only and require human review for ethics, compliance, privacy, and regulatory constraints before outreach.
