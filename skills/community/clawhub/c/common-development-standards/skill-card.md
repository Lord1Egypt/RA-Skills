## Description:

Shared engineering skill for Weline development standards, safe change boundaries, code quality, documentation duties, and validation evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aiweline](https://clawhub.ai/user/aiweline)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineering agents use this skill to apply WelineFramework development standards, choose safe change boundaries, coordinate specialist ownership, and report validation evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill steers agents toward Weline-specific engineering process, validation, and Chinese collaboration conventions that may be too opinionated for other projects.

Mitigation: Use it for WelineFramework work or review and adapt the conventions before applying them to unrelated repositories.
