## Use Case:

External users use this skill for outfit advice, wardrobe strategy, shopping guidance, and style problem-solving across daily dressing, event preparation, travel, budget, body, climate, cultural, and accessibility constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may request personal fit, body, budget, cultural, or accessibility details to tailor styling advice.

Mitigation: Users should share only the details they are comfortable using for styling advice and can ask for less personal alternatives.

Risk: Some guidance uses inch-based measurements that may be less useful for metric-unit users.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to provide centimeter equivalents when sizing, inseam, hem, or garment-length measurements matter.

Risk: Fashion, price, sizing, and trend guidance can become stale or vary by region, retailer, and body proportions.

Mitigation: Verify current retailer policies, size charts, prices, and local dress-code expectations before purchasing or dressing for high-stakes events.
