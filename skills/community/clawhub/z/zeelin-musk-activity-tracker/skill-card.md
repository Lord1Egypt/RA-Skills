## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recent public activity can change quickly or be reported inconsistently across sources.

Mitigation: Use current primary sources and reputable news outlets, include dates and source links, and distinguish verified facts from commentary.

Risk: The skill is localized in Chinese, which may not match every user's preferred output language.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to report in the desired language and verify translated names, sources, and quotations before reuse.
