## Description:

Get-started-fast guides for accessible physical instruments for users who want to learn an instrument, build a stress relief practice, play music with others, or start an embodied creative skill.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to choose a beginner-friendly physical instrument and get practical first-session guidance for ukulele, guitar, harmonica, or hand drum.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests filesystem capability even though its behavior is primarily educational guidance.

Mitigation: Review why filesystem access is needed before installation and avoid granting it unless the publisher provides a clear need.

Risk: The install command uses an unpinned npx package.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned or trusted installer version before installing in a sensitive environment.
