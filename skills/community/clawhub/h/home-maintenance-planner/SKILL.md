# Home Maintenance Planner

## Overview

Homes fail on skipped intervals: filters, seals, detectors, drains. This planner puts every recurring task on a seasonal calendar with its interval and supplies, so maintenance happens by schedule instead of by breakdown.

## When to use

For homeowners and renters alike; set it up once, run it forever.

## Workflow

1. **Inventory the systems** — HVAC, plumbing, roof/gutters, appliances, detectors, water heater, exterior
2. **Attach intervals** — manufacturer intervals where they exist (filters quarterly, detectors tested monthly, batteries yearly), service contracts where they apply
3. **Lay out the year** — seasonal anchors: spring (AC service, gutters), summer (exterior, pests), fall (heating service, weatherproofing), winter (pipes, detectors)
4. **Build the supplies list per task** — so a task never stalls on a missing filter size; record the sizes once
5. **Log completions with dates and costs** — the log is the home's service history and raises resale value
6. **Add the emergency card** — shutoff locations (water, gas, breaker) and the two phone numbers that matter

## Guardrails

- Never defer a safety item (detectors, gas, electrical) for budget reasons — resequence luxuries instead
- Record model numbers and filter sizes the first time; future-you is grateful
- Renters: mark which tasks are the landlord's by lease, and notify rather than DIY those

## Output format

A yearly maintenance calendar: tasks by season with intervals, supplies with sizes, completion log, emergency card.
