## Description:

Use this skill when a litigator, in-house counsel, or ADR specialist needs to draft a two-part mediation brief for a civil mediation session under JAMS, AAA, FINRA, or court-annexed rules; it produces a DRAFT SHARED brief and a CONFIDENTIAL mediator-only brief with BATNA/WATNA/ZOPA analysis, settlement-authority worksheet, and a do-not-share audit for licensed counsel review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Litigators, in-house counsel, ADR specialists, and mediation advocates use this skill to prepare a two-part civil mediation brief package for a scheduled mediation session. It structures case intake, shared brief drafting, confidential mediator-only strategy, settlement-authority analysis, BATNA/WATNA/ZOPA work, and a do-not-share audit for licensed counsel review.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow asks users for privileged and highly sensitive litigation strategy, including settlement authority, walk-away analysis, and confidential mediation posture.

Mitigation: Use only with appropriate counsel oversight, avoid entering confidential material unless platform and matter controls are suitable, and review outputs carefully before sharing.

Risk: Strategy content such as BATNA, WATNA, ZOPA, authority gates, leverage candour, and work-product analysis could harm the client if included in the shared brief.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's dual-audience separation and do-not-share audit so confidential strategy remains in the mediator-only brief.

Risk: Drafted legal or settlement analysis could be mistaken for a binding legal conclusion or settlement decision.

Mitigation: Keep all outputs marked as drafts for licensed counsel review; settlement authority and final legal judgement remain with counsel and the client.
