## Description:

Get a complete financial overview of the business including balance, recent transactions, outstanding invoices, and upcoming tax obligations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanlee000](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanlee000)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and finance operators use this skill to ask for a concise business financial overview across company details, balance, recent cash flow, unpaid invoices, and upcoming VAT or tax obligations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can expose sensitive business finance data when an agent is connected to Norman Finance.

Mitigation: Install only for users who are intended to access balances, transactions, invoices, VAT deadlines, and tax statistics.

Risk: In shared or multi-business environments, the overview could be generated for the wrong company or account.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended company or account before requesting or sharing the financial overview.
