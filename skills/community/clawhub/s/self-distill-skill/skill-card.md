## Description:

A Chinese-language Persona Metadata Framework skill for structuring personality analysis with Jungian layers, archetypes, cognitive functions, development dynamics, and relationship patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[roryyu](https://clawhub.ai/user/roryyu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agent users use this skill to analyze self, fictional, historical, or public-person personas through a structured personality metadata framework. It helps produce evidence-based Markdown reports that separate observed material from speculative personality inferences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personality analysis may be mistaken for a factual psychological diagnosis.

Mitigation: Present conclusions as speculative, evidence-based inferences and avoid diagnostic claims.

Risk: Profiling private living people without consent can create privacy and consent concerns.

Mitigation: Use the skill for self-analysis, fictional characters, historical figures, or public figures based on public information, and obtain consent before analyzing private living people.

Risk: Low-quality or secondhand source material can distort personality conclusions.

Mitigation: Prefer primary materials, document source quality, and cross-check behavioral evidence before producing a report.
