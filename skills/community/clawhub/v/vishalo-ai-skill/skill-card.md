## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad triggers may activate the skill for generic AI image or video requests, which can increase the chance of sharing sensitive creative assets or unreleased campaign details with the wrong active skill.

Mitigation: Check which skill is active before providing sensitive creative assets, unreleased campaign details, or proprietary visual references.
