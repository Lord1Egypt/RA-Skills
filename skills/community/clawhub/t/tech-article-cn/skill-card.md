## Description:

Tech Article CN guides Chinese technical writers from topic framing through outline, drafting, review, polishing, and platform adaptation for technical articles, internal sharing, blog posts, postmortems, and talks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lanyasheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/lanyasheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to turn concrete technical experience, data, code, and decisions into Chinese technical articles, outlines, review reports, and presentation drafts. It is suited to internal engineering sharing, Juejin or InfoQ posts, technical postmortems, and conference talk scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broader file, shell, web, and agent capabilities than its writing workflow clearly needs.

Mitigation: Install only with approval controls, narrow shell, file, web, and agent access where possible, and review proposed commands or file changes before execution.

Risk: Drafting without concrete source material can produce generic or unsupported technical claims.

Mitigation: Require real experience, data, code, screenshots, or citations before drafting; use outline-only or review mode when evidence is incomplete.
