## Use Case:

Product owners, product managers, and delivery teams use this skill to manage backlogs, write user stories, define acceptance criteria, prioritize sprint work, and align stakeholders around product value.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill stores product context, backlog details, and sprint history locally under ~/product-owner/.

Mitigation: Avoid storing confidential business details unless local persistence is acceptable for the user's environment.

Risk: The setup flow can offer proactive backlog and sprint-planning assistance.

Mitigation: Choose the less proactive setup option when assistance should occur only on explicit request.
