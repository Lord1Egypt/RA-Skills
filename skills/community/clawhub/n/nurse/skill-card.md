## Description:

Clinical support system for nurses and frontline healthcare workers that helps draft documentation, patient communication, care plans, handover notes, medication-query support, incident reports, and professional nursing materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Nurses and frontline healthcare workers use this skill to draft clinical documentation, handover notes, care plans, patient and family explanations, incident reports, and professional communication while applying established nursing frameworks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may respond to broad clinical scenarios and produce nursing-oriented drafts that could be mistaken for clinical authority.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting support only and verify clinical content through local policy and qualified clinicians.

Risk: Medication, consent, escalation, and scope-of-practice topics can create patient-safety or professional-risk consequences if acted on without review.

Mitigation: Escalate these topics to pharmacy, senior clinicians, RN or medical staff, or local incident and consent protocols as applicable.
