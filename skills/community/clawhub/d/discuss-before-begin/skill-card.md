## Description:

Guides an agent to challenge and clarify a user's plan through structured discussion, requirement mapping, risk review, and explicit consensus before implementation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[blueriverlhr](https://clawhub.ai/user/blueriverlhr)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill when they want an agent to pressure-test an idea, plan, proposal, or implementation approach before execution. It is suited for requirement clarification, conflict resolution, blind-spot review, and consensus-gated handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad planning or review language and slow direct execution.

Mitigation: Use it when a strict planning gate is desired, and proceed only after explicit user consensus.

Risk: Assertive critique may over-constrain lightweight requests if applied outside planning contexts.

Mitigation: Keep the scope tied to requirement clarification, risk review, and decision consensus.
