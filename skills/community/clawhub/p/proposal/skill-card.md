## Description:

Turn late-stage commercial opportunities into easier-to-approve proposals by clarifying scope, pricing, likely buyer questions, and the path to yes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[depinhq](https://clawhub.ai/user/depinhq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External teams such as founders, sales teams, consultants, agencies, and operators use this skill to draft, structure, audit, and improve client or stakeholder proposals for late-stage commercial opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Proposal guidance can affect pricing, scope, commercial commitments, or approval paths in regulated, high-value, legal, tax, financial, procurement, or contract-sensitive contexts.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer check outputs against jurisdictional requirements, internal policies, procurement rules, and formal agreements before external use.

Risk: Generated proposal language may overstate value, omit exclusions, or leave assumptions unclear.

Mitigation: Review the final proposal for buyer goals, explicit scope and exclusions, clear timeline and pricing, visible assumptions, and a concrete next step.
