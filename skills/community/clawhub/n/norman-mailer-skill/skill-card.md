## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses a provocative literary and political-analysis voice that may frame direct political or on-the-ground action in forceful terms.

Mitigation: Treat action-oriented political advice as rhetorical unless independently reviewed for legality, nonviolence, consent, and personal safety.

Risk: The persona includes dated or controversial perspectives around gender, race, violence, and political action.

Mitigation: Use the skill's stated boundaries as review prompts, and adapt outputs to contemporary ethical, legal, and audience expectations.

Risk: Its AI-era authorship and power-analysis claims are interpretive and not settled technical or scholarly conclusions.

Mitigation: Verify factual, legal, professional, or policy-relevant conclusions with authoritative sources before relying on them.
