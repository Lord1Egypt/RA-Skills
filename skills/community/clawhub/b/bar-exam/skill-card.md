## Description:

Prepare for the US Bar Exam with MBE practice, essay drilling, weak area targeting, and jurisdiction planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External bar exam candidates, tutors, and supporters use this skill to structure US Bar Exam preparation, practice MBE, MEE, and MPT tasks, track study progress, and plan around jurisdiction-specific requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create and maintain local study records in ~/bar-exam/, which could include sensitive study history or personal exam context.

Mitigation: Avoid storing unnecessary sensitive details on shared machines and delete ~/bar-exam/ when local study history should no longer be retained.

Risk: Bar admission requirements and passing thresholds vary by jurisdiction and can change over time.

Mitigation: Confirm jurisdiction-specific requirements, deadlines, and score thresholds with official bar admission authorities before relying on a study plan.
