## Description:

Analyzes accounts receivable aging and suggests prioritization strategies for collections.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Finance teams, small business owners, and agents use this skill to review receivable aging, DSO changes, and customer payment risk, then generate collection prioritization plans, templates, and next-step checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Receivable aging, collection, tax, legal, or financial recommendations could be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an informational planning aid and consult qualified finance, tax, legal, or collections professionals before acting.

Risk: Heuristic recommendations may be incomplete if the user's receivable data, payment terms, or dispute context is incomplete.

Mitigation: Validate outputs against source records, customer history, contracts, and current business policies before prioritizing collections.
