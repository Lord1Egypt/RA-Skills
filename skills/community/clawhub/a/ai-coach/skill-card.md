## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive personal information about goals, habits, fitness, productivity, career, or wellbeing.

Mitigation: Users should share only information they are comfortable discussing with their agent and avoid entering secrets or unnecessary sensitive details.

Risk: Coaching responses about fitness, wellbeing, finances, career, or mental health may be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as general coaching support, and consult qualified professionals for medical, mental health, financial, legal, or career decisions.
