## Description:

Generate usable color systems for creative and design work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External users, designers, developers, and creative teams use this skill to turn a mood, medium, or design goal into a practical color palette with roles, usage guidance, variants, and accessibility notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Accessibility and print guidance may be approximate when contrast or CMYK conversion is not explicitly calculated.

Mitigation: Treat accessibility and CMYK notes as practical estimates, and verify important contrast ratios or print output with dedicated tools before production use.
