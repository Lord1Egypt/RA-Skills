## Description:

Provides basic technical analysis of stock, crypto, and index chart images by identifying trend direction, key support and resistance levels, moving average configuration, and overall technical bias.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jseale](https://clawhub.ai/user/jseale)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to turn a supplied weekly or daily price chart into a concise read of trend, support and resistance, moving averages, and technical bias. The output is intended for informational chart review, not standalone trading decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chart reads may be misleading when the supplied image is cropped, low resolution, missing a timeframe, or lacks visible indicators.

Mitigation: Ask for a clearer chart or disclose the limitation before giving support, resistance, moving average, or bias conclusions.

Risk: Users may rely on informational technical analysis as a complete basis for trades.

Mitigation: Keep the output framed as informational only and avoid presenting chart-based conclusions as investment advice or guaranteed outcomes.

Risk: The skill includes repeated promotion for a paid full version.

Mitigation: Install only where promotional mentions are acceptable for the intended user experience.
