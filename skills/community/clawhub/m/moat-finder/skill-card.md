## Description:

Moat Finder guides agents through a structured competitive-moat analysis using Xiao Jing's 5 Factors + 4 Relations framework while keeping each category boundary distinct.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raphaelxiao](https://clawhub.ai/user/raphaelxiao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business strategists, investors, founders, and analysts use this skill to interview for and synthesize company defensibility across factor monopolies, relation monopolies, and disruption vulnerabilities. It is most useful when the user wants a category-by-category moat assessment rather than a general business summary.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide confidential strategy, supplier, customer, or trade-secret information during moat analysis.

Mitigation: Use only information appropriate for the operating environment and avoid sharing confidential business details unless they are intended for the analysis.

Risk: The skill instructions reference detailed guide filenames that do not match the packaged reference filenames.

Mitigation: Confirm the packaged reference paths before deployment or update the skill instructions to point to the included reference files.
