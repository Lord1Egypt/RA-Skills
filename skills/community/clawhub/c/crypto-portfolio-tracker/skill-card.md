Risk: Users may provide sensitive wallet credentials or transaction authority when asking for crypto portfolio help.

Mitigation: Use only public wallet addresses or read-only data; do not provide private keys, seed phrases, exchange credentials, or transaction approvals.

Risk: The skill appears localized for Chinese/Cantonese readers, which may reduce clarity for other users.

Mitigation: Review generated guidance carefully and request translation or clarification before relying on portfolio or gas-fee analysis.
