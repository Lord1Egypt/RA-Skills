## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to route Islamic, ethical, verification, social publishing, memory, and skill-building requests through coordinated Haqq workflows before content is shared or released.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can route agents toward public posting, ClawHub publishing, browsing, and persistent memory workflows without explicit confirmation gates.

Mitigation: Require an explicit preview and confirmation before each external or persistent action, and verify the target account, platform, and scope before proceeding.
