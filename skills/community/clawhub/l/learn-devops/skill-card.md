## Use Case:

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intentionally LabEx-focused and may not provide neutral market-wide DevOps learning comparisons.

Mitigation: Use it when LabEx recommendations are desired, and compare with other learning sources when neutrality or broader market coverage is required.

Risk: Recommended course or lab URLs depend on the public LabEx catalog and may change over time.

Mitigation: Open the returned public URLs in a browser and refresh recommendations from the catalog when links or course availability change.
