## Description:

Professional-grade Chinese social media sentiment analysis report writer for government and enterprise use, generating structured public opinion reports with risk assessment, communication analysis, countermeasures, and international media cross-validation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, and enterprise or government teams use this skill to draft Chinese public opinion and sentiment reports, including rapid assessments, deep research reports, monthly summaries, and special event analyses. It guides public-source collection, cross-validation, risk assessment, and actionable countermeasure writing for domestic and international media contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad political, diplomatic, ethnic, religious, ideological, or media-monitoring collection can create sensitive-use and compliance risk.

Mitigation: Review use before installation on sensitive topics, collect only public or explicitly authorized content, and apply relevant organizational policy review.

Risk: Browser scraping guidance may be interpreted as permission to bypass paywalls, login walls, access controls, or site terms.

Mitigation: Do not bypass paywalls, login walls, access controls, or site terms; use authorized sources and structured tools with user confirmation for external collection.
