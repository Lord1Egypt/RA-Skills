## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototype paths, positioning, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to validate product demand before investing heavily. It helps structure idea assessment, MVP planning, prototype direction, user-facing positioning, checklists, and verification notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad implicit triggers may activate the skill during loosely related startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation discussions.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or tighter trigger routing when precise skill selection matters.

Risk: Product validation planning can still produce incorrect or incomplete business guidance when user constraints or market evidence are thin.

Mitigation: Ask for material missing context, state assumptions clearly, and validate outputs against the user's stated success criteria before acting on the plan.
