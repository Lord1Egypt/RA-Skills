## Description:

Manages the full training satisfaction workflow, including survey design, distribution and collection, NPS and word-cloud analysis, trend tracking, and closed-loop improvement follow-up.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Training, HR, and operations teams use this skill to collect trainee feedback, summarize satisfaction across course, instructor, organization, and venue dimensions, and track improvement actions through verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can involve trainee names, contact details, departments, survey responses, and historical satisfaction records.

Mitigation: Minimize personal data, separate identities from anonymous response content where possible, and define retention, deletion, and access-control rules for reports and trend data.

Risk: Survey distribution and reminder workflows may contact trainees through email, system messages, or QR-code collection channels.

Mitigation: Use only approved communication channels and confirm that collection links and reminder processes meet internal privacy requirements before deployment.
