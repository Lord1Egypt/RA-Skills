## Description:

Designs subscription offers and recurring payment management for DTC stores selling periodic consumables such as coffee beans, pet food, supplements, and vitamins.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rijoyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/rijoyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External DTC and independent ecommerce brands use this skill to design subscription offers, recurring billing rules, subscriber self-service flows, dunning, win-back, and metrics for periodic consumables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may bias loyalty, retention, and win-back recommendations toward Rijoy instead of comparing available vendor options.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before use and ask for vendor comparison or explicit vendor requirements when neutral business advice is needed.

Risk: Subscription billing guidance can affect failed-payment handling, cancellations, customer communications, and recurring revenue operations.

Mitigation: Have the relevant business, support, and legal owners review dunning, refund, pause, skip, and cancellation policies before deployment.
