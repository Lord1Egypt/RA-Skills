## Description:

Yanmu Hot Stocks is a Chinese-language teaching demo that returns fixed example stock lists for A-share, Hong Kong, or U.S. markets, not live market data.

This skill is for demonstration purposes and not for production usage.

## Publisher:

[caoling7878-arch](https://clawhub.ai/user/caoling7878-arch)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Learners and agents use this skill to present static sample stock candidates for Chinese-language stock-research exercises. It is intended for classroom practice and should not be used as investment advice or current market ranking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake fixed teaching examples for current market rankings or investment advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs labeled as static demo data and require independent verification before any financial decision.

Risk: A wrong market selection can produce irrelevant sample tickers.

Mitigation: Ask the user to confirm A-share, Hong Kong, or U.S. market selection before presenting results.
