## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Wallet private keys and session cookies are sensitive secrets.

Mitigation: Keep private keys and session cookies in secure secret storage and do not paste them into shared chats or logs.

Risk: Blockchain transactions and API-key operations can affect user assets or account access.

Mitigation: Use testnet or a low-value wallet first and manually review any transaction or API-key operation before execution.
