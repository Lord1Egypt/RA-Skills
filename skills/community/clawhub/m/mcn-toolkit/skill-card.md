## Description:

MCN Toolkit is an AI operations assistant for MCN agencies, influencer managers, and KOL teams that helps organize campaign assets, creator workflows, batch video production, script variation, and platform-ready deliveries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External MCN agencies, brand marketing teams, content studios, and talent managers use this skill to coordinate creator campaigns, generate creator-specific content variants, manage deliverables, plan schedules, and review cross-platform performance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Campaign assets, creator analytics, and social scheduling requests may contain sensitive business or account information.

Mitigation: Treat those inputs as sensitive, install only from the expected ClawHub source, and avoid running install commands with elevated privileges.

Risk: The skill may propose actions that publish, schedule, or modify content on connected platforms.

Mitigation: Confirm any publishing, scheduling, or platform modification action with the user before allowing the agent to proceed.
