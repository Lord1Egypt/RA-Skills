## Description:

Pump helps users design launch momentum before a project goes live by optimizing first-day release logic, trigger sequencing, attention concentration, and breakout timing for products, skills, campaigns, and digital launches.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External founders, operators, creators, and skill builders use Pump to diagnose launch momentum gaps and design trigger sequences, first-day attention curves, and follow-through steps for product, skill, campaign, or digital releases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad launch or marketing questions where long-term strategy, paid media execution, automation setup, or platform-policy review is needed.

Mitigation: Use it for launch-momentum design around a release event, and use a more specific marketing, advertising, automation, or policy skill for those other needs.

Risk: Incomplete launch context can lead to overconfident or generic launch recommendations.

Mitigation: Ask for missing audience, goal, launch timing, channel, proof, urgency, community, and current-plan details before producing a launch sequence.

Risk: Launch-momentum guidance could be mistaken for guaranteed virality, purchased traffic, product validation, or distribution execution.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as planning guidance and preserve the stated boundaries that the skill does not guarantee virality, buy traffic, replace product quality, or execute posting and paid distribution.
