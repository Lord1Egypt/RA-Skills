## Description:

Builds live setlists that actually work on stage — energy arc (open strong, mid-set valley, closer peak), key compatibility via circle-of-fifths distance, BPM jump caps so dancers don't stumble, timed to your slot with breaks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

## Use Case:

Bands, wedding and corporate acts, and DJs can use the skill to sequence songs for a live set, shape its energy, manage transitions, and fit the performance slot.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The BPM jump limit is soft, so generated setlists may include transitions above the advertised cap.

Mitigation: Review the transition report and adjust any BPM jumps that exceed the limit you want for the performance.
