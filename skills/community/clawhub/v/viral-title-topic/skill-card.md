## Description:

Turn a product, an account, or posts you paste into title options and a topic list grounded in what people are already publishing. This title and topic workflow reads public posts on Douyin, Xiaohongshu, TikTok, and X when you want a live read, then writes hooks and titles you can shoot today. Use it for trend titles, topic selection, and the next post on an existing account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and account managers can turn a product, account, or supplied posts into shootable topic angles, titles, and a spoken hook. They can also request a cover image and a short hook clip, or use pasted evidence for a text-only workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence marks the skill suspicious because scanners report broad account permissions, shared connection management, and telemetry beyond the stated title-and-topic purpose.

Mitigation: Review the requested account access before connecting. Use pasted posts when live social access is unnecessary, and enable additional account or connection features only when needed.

Risk: The skill can make paid media-generation calls and upload a supplied image; the security guidance notes spending and file-upload capabilities.

Mitigation: Review the current price and approve each paid call before it runs. Upload only an image you intend to send to the service.

Risk: The security analysis is incomplete because some referenced items could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the installed skill files and its access scope before authorizing the shared connection.
