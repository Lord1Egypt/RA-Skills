## Description:

Guide mattress buyers through sleeping position, body weight, and usage questions to determine exact firmness, type, and size specs they need, unbiased and brand-neutral.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[arbazex](https://clawhub.ai/user/arbazex)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to complete a structured mattress buying consultation and receive prioritized specifications for firmness, type, size, certifications, and compatible product options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The consultation may ask for personal comfort, health, weight, and location details.

Mitigation: Provide coarse location information such as country or region and skip medical details unless they are needed for the recommendation.

Risk: Product suggestions and mattress specifications can become outdated or vary by region.

Mitigation: Treat suggestions as starting points for user research and verify current availability, specifications, trial terms, and warranty terms before purchase.
