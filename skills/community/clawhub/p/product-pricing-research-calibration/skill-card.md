## Description:

Calibrate an enterprise-software or AI product-pricing research article before drafting. Use when a topic links pricing, packaging, governance, product strategy, or commercial model and there is a risk of treating broad platform pricing as direct feature pricing, using media-style vendor camps, or jumping to procurement advice before analyzing product facts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
