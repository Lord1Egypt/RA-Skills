## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ticket prices, reservation rules, photography limits, refund policies, and venue policies may change or vary by site.

Mitigation: Verify details against official venue and ticketing sources before booking or traveling.

Risk: Popular venues may have long queues during peak periods.

Mitigation: Reserve in advance when possible and choose off-peak visit windows.
