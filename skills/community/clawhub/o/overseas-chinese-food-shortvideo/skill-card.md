## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is narrowly scoped to Chinese-language marketing guidance for overseas Chinese restaurant short videos, so users outside that language or niche may receive less relevant outputs.

Mitigation: Use it for the intended overseas Chinese food short-video workflow, and ask the agent to localize or adapt recommendations when applying it to another language, cuisine, market, or platform.

Risk: Marketing scripts, competitor assessments, and monetization suggestions may be inaccurate or overbroad for a specific city, platform policy, or restaurant operation.

Mitigation: Review generated plans against local market data, platform rules, and the restaurant's actual capabilities before publishing content or making business decisions.
