## Description:

Miantiao Career Advisor is a Chinese-language workplace-advice persona that answers career, job-search, interview, negotiation, workplace-relationship, and work-stress questions in a direct, nonjudgmental conversational style.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuranliu0818](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuranliu0818)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for Chinese-language career and workplace guidance, including workplace relationships, career decisions, interviewing, salary negotiation, onboarding, emotional stress, and job-change questions. The agent responds as a concise career-advice persona that asks reflective questions rather than making decisions for the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be triggered by common workplace terms more often than expected.

Mitigation: Use it where a Chinese-language workplace-advice persona is intended and review activation behavior before deployment.

Risk: The referenced knowledge file is not included in the inspected artifact.

Mitigation: Expect responses to rely on the visible skill instructions unless the missing reference file is supplied and reviewed.

Risk: Workplace and career guidance can be mistaken for a definitive decision.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's reflective-question style and avoid presenting responses as final employment, legal, financial, or personal decisions.
