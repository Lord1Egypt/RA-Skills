## Description:

深圳中考填报全流程助手，帮助深圳初中生和家长查询政策、规划志愿、参考高中录取分数线、理解指标生和自主招生路径，并生成学校匹配建议。

## Use Case:

External users, especially Shenzhen junior high students and parents, use this skill to interpret Zhongkao policy, compare school options, plan preferences, and evaluate standard, indicator-student, and independent-admission paths. It is intended for public-information-based education planning and should be checked against current official announcements before decisions are made.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China, with primary applicability to Shenzhen

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill gives education-planning guidance based on locally documented policy and score references that can become outdated or differ from official current-year announcements.

Mitigation: Use the skill's source-year notes as context and verify policy, deadlines, admissions rules, and score lines against Shenzhen official sources before acting.

Risk: The security summary notes a repeated personal WeChat consulting promotion that could move sensitive student enrollment details off-platform.

Mitigation: Treat the contact channel as optional advertising, not an official or platform-endorsed service, and avoid sharing identity documents, full transcripts, account credentials, residency records, or other sensitive student information through it.

Risk: The skill can produce school matching or admissions-path recommendations that may be mistaken for official admissions advice.

Mitigation: Present recommendations as public-data-based planning support and remind users that final enrollment decisions should be independently verified with official schools or admissions offices.
