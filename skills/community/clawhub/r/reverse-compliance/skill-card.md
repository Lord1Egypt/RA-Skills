## Description:

Reverse Compliance helps agents generate compliance-first market-entry strategies, architecture plans, trust frameworks, and compliance documentation for cross-border businesses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangm-a3](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangm-a3)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers, founders, and compliance, product, and engineering teams use the skill to plan cross-border market entry from strict target-market requirements. It supports compliance-first architecture planning, compliance checklists, trust-building narratives, and reusable compliance documentation drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided product, market, platform, and compliance facts may be sent to OpenAI for text generation.

Mitigation: Install only if that data sharing is acceptable, and avoid entering confidential details beyond what the task requires.

Risk: Generated compliance plans may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific jurisdiction or product category.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning aids and have qualified counsel or compliance specialists verify requirements before relying on them.
