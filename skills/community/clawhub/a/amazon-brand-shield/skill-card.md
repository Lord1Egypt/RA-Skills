## Description:

Amazon brand protection toolkit that helps detect hijackers, counterfeits, unauthorized sellers, MAP violations, and trademark abuse, and generates reporting and evidence-collection templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangm-a3](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangm-a3)

## Use Case:

E-commerce brand owners and operators use this skill to monitor Amazon listings for unauthorized sellers, counterfeit signals, MAP violations, and trademark abuse, then prepare complaint templates and evidence checklists for human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The documented installer uses an unpinned global npx install from mutable remote sources.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installing, pin the package version and immutable repository commit or checksum, and avoid global installation unless cross-session availability is intended.

Risk: Generated complaints, legal notices, and enforcement recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific brand dispute.

Mitigation: Require brand-owner or legal review before sending notices, submitting Amazon reports, making test buys, or taking enforcement action.
