## Description:

Draft, check, and schedule social posts across X, LinkedIn, Bluesky, TikTok, YouTube, and Pinterest, with by-hand Patreon handoffs through Flaghand.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[flaghand](https://clawhub.ai/user/flaghand)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and their agents use Flaghand to prepare, check, and manage social content across connected platforms. Action-specific owner confirmations govern selected posts and handoffs, while ordinary future social posts can be scheduled without per-post approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Social scheduling and publishing can affect public accounts if the skill's action-specific owner confirmation rules are missed.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's confirmation rules for each action, schedule only the requested checked plan, and leave immediate posting and approval decisions to the owner.

Risk: Security scan coverage was partial, so some linked details may not have been inspected.

Mitigation: Review the relevant service guidance and confirmation steps before using the workflow with connected accounts.

Risk: Workspace tokens and sign-up claim codes could expose account access if shared or logged.

Mitigation: Keep credentials in secret storage or private files and out of command arguments, chat, and shared logs.
