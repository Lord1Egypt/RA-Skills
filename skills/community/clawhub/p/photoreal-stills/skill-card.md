## Description:

Generate images that read as real photographs rather than AI renders, including candid portraits, editorial, documentary, food, architectural, and lifestyle stills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[runware](https://clawhub.ai/user/runware)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use this skill to craft and iterate prompts for photorealistic still-image generation across portrait, editorial, documentary, food, interior, and lifestyle scenes. It helps agents choose live image models, set photographic parameters, and evaluate outputs against common AI-look artifacts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and reference images used with the skill may be sent to external image-generation services.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive private images or confidential prompt content unless the user has approved the provider handling that data.

Risk: Photorealistic output could be used to misrepresent real events or people.

Mitigation: Use the output with clear provenance and avoid presenting generated images as documentary evidence or authentic depictions of real events.
