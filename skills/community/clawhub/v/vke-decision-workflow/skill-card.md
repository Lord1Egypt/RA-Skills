## Description:

Build VKE evidence-led decision workflows and business knowledge products from a request.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wodeking](https://clawhub.ai/user/wodeking)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and analysts use this skill to turn ambiguous VKE knowledge requests into evidence-led decision packages, including briefs, research plans, evidence ledgers, analysis notes, red-team reviews, decision reports, watchlists, and retrospectives.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked implicitly for VKE-like artifact work.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for a different workflow when VKE decision artifacts are not desired.

Risk: Default templates and headings may use Chinese for case artifacts.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for another language when Chinese headings or template text are not desired.
