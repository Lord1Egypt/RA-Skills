## Description: <br>
Helps agents diagnose credit-cycle fragility, debt-structure risk, and whether prolonged financial calm is masking systemic collapse risk. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and analysts use this skill to evaluate leveraged companies, sectors, markets, or economies for Minsky-style fragility, including debt-stage classification, trigger stress, and collapse cascade mapping. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the skill's financial analysis as investment advice. <br>
Mitigation: Frame outputs as analytical guidance and verify conclusions with current cash-flow, debt-service, and market data before acting. <br>
Risk: A diagnosis may be forced when debt-service or rollover data is missing. <br>
Mitigation: Apply the skill's stop rule: name the data gap and avoid assigning a fragility stage without supporting data. <br>
