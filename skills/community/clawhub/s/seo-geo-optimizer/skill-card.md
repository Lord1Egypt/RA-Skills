## Description:

Optimizes websites for SEO and GEO by planning keyword clusters, site architecture, multilingual URL structure, page-level metadata, AI-citation-ready content, topical authority, internal links, structured data, and conversion-aligned recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[osamagweaa](https://clawhub.ai/user/osamagweaa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and site operators use this skill to audit, plan, and rewrite SEO/GEO assets for websites. It helps choose keyword clusters, structure pages and multilingual URLs, write titles and meta descriptions, recommend schema, improve AI citation readiness, and align visibility work with conversion paths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use a project's market-profile.md for positioning context, which can expose sensitive business strategy to SEO recommendations.

Mitigation: Keep confidential strategy out of market-profile.md unless it is intended to be used in optimization guidance.

Risk: Publishing incorrect SEO, GEO, schema, or internal-linking recommendations could reduce traffic quality, rankings, AI-citation potential, or conversions.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against the site's actual audience, offer, visible page content, and conversion goals before publishing.
