## Description:

Prepare for the SAT with adaptive practice, score prediction, weak area targeting, and college admissions planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners preparing for the SAT use this skill to diagnose strengths and weaknesses, create adaptive study plans, track practice results, estimate scores, and align preparation with college admissions goals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may store SAT scores, target colleges, test dates, mistake logs, and study feedback locally under ~/sat/.

Mitigation: Use it on a device and account where this study history is acceptable to store, and delete ~/sat/ when the records are no longer needed.

Risk: SAT schedules, registration details, and college testing policies can change over time or vary by school.

Mitigation: Verify current College Board information and each college's testing, score choice, and superscoring policies before making registration or application decisions.
