## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce actionable trading guidance, including buy, sell, stop-loss, target-price, and position-size suggestions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational analysis, verify market data independently, and avoid using the skill for personalized investment decisions unless appropriate financial-advice safeguards are added.

Risk: The security review notes no technical invasiveness, code execution, or persistence, but flags insufficient suitability and risk guardrails for financial decisions.

Mitigation: Require human review for investment-relevant outputs and include clear suitability, uncertainty, and risk disclosures before acting on recommendations.
