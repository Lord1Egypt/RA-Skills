## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototype paths, positioning, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It helps turn product ideas into practical validation workflows, checklists, prototypes, user-facing positioning, and next-step recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad product, startup, validation, prototype, and SaaS wording may activate the skill for general business requests outside product validation planning.

Mitigation: Use it for product validation planning and confirm the user's outcome, constraints, inputs, and success criteria before producing guidance.

Risk: Validation plans and positioning may be mistaken for proof of market demand.

Mitigation: Present assumptions clearly, tie recommendations to available evidence, and identify what still needs user or customer validation.
