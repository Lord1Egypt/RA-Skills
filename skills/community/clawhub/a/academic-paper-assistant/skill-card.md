## Description:

A Chinese-language academic paper writing assistant for drafting, revising, polishing, structuring papers, normalizing academic phrasing, organizing references, and giving plagiarism-check guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dingwenbin1978](https://clawhub.ai/user/dingwenbin1978)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and academic writers use this skill to plan paper structures, improve academic wording, polish drafts, organize references, and check formatting expectations across disciplines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat writing, citation, or formatting suggestions as authoritative for a specific journal, institution, or review process.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the target submission rules and keep the author responsible for final academic content, data, citations, and compliance.

Risk: Running the bundled packaging script on untrusted skill folders or folders containing symlinks may process files the user did not intend to package.

Mitigation: Use the writing guidance by default; only validate or package trusted skill folders and review generated package contents before distribution.
