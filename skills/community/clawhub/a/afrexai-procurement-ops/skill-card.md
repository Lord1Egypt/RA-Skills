## Description:

Conducts procurement assessments using company inputs to score maturity, identify savings opportunities, map automation priorities, and produce a 90-day improvement roadmap.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Procurement, finance, and operations teams use this skill to assess procurement maturity, estimate savings opportunities, prioritize automation, and plan near-term process improvements from supplied company details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Procurement spend, supplier, pricing, and contract details may be sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in approved environments and provide the minimum necessary procurement data, redacting supplier, pricing, or contract details when possible.

Risk: Benchmark and ROI estimates may not match a specific company's operating conditions.

Mitigation: Validate savings, automation, and investment assumptions against internal procurement and finance data before making financial or vendor decisions.
