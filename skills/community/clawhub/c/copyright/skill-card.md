This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, content users, and businesses use this skill to organize copyright protection, licensing, fair use, DMCA, and infringement-response questions in plain language.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general copyright guidance as jurisdiction-specific legal advice or rely on it for high-stakes decisions.

Mitigation: Advise users to verify jurisdiction-specific requirements and consult a qualified attorney before sending formal notices, counter-notices, settlement demands, or relying on fair use in high-stakes situations.

Risk: The skill provides legal-information workflows that may become outdated as platform policies or copyright procedures change.

Mitigation: Have users verify current platform, DMCA, registration, and licensing requirements before taking formal action.
