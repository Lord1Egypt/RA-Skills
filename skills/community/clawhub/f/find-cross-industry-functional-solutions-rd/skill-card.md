## Description:

Find transferable technical solutions from other industries by reframing a concrete R&D problem as functions, effects, constraints, and contradictions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

R&D, engineering, and patent-search teams use this skill to turn a concrete technical problem into domain-neutral function and effect queries, screen cross-industry patent and technical evidence, and produce a solution matrix with transferability and experiment recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent-search tasks may involve external services and sensitive technical problem details.

Mitigation: Use only organization-approved services and keep confidential technical details out of searches unless data handling has been approved.

Risk: The workflow can generate technical analogies and patent hypotheses that may be mistaken for feasibility, patentability, freedom-to-operate, or infringement conclusions.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations through engineering analysis and experiment, and route material IP questions to qualified patent counsel.
