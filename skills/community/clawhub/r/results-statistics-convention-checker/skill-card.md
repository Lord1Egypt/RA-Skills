## Description:

Checks English psychology Results sections for APA-style statistical reporting conventions, including descriptive statistics, test statistics, p values, effect sizes, confidence intervals, and consistency between statistical results and prose.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, reviewers, editors, and academic writing assistants use this skill to diagnose APA-style statistical reporting issues in English psychology Results drafts. It produces a structured review with a dimension score, problem evidence, example-based comparisons, revision suggestions, and prioritized fixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the skill as a statistics calculator or as validation that reported statistical values are correct.

Mitigation: Use it only for reporting-convention review; verify test statistics, degrees of freedom, effect sizes, confidence intervals, and p values with the original analysis software or data source.

Risk: The skill defaults to APA-style reporting for psychology Results sections and may not match every journal or discipline-specific style requirement.

Mitigation: Compare recommendations with the target journal's author guidelines and provide any journal-specific requirements in the prompt.
