## Description:

Use when building a manual stock watchlist, defining alert thresholds, or documenting a repeatable monitoring routine with public market data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize manual stock monitoring: building watchlists, setting threshold rules, choosing a review cadence, and recording follow-up notes from public market data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market data may be delayed, vendor-dependent, or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify important prices and volume signals against a trusted market-data source before acting on the notes.

Risk: Users may mistake monitoring guidance for financial advice or automated trading capability.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for watchlist organization and planning; keep human review before any investment or trading decision.

Risk: The skill does not send automatic alerts or execute trades.

Mitigation: Pair the generated thresholds with a real notification or brokerage system only after separately validating that system.
