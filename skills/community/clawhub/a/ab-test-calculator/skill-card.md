## Description:

A local A/B test calculator that compares control and variant data for continuous, binary, or count experiments and returns significance, effect size, statistical power, and a plain-language launch verdict.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[eelowl](https://clawhub.ai/user/eelowl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, analysts, and developers use this skill to evaluate A/B test results from raw samples or summary statistics and translate statistical outputs into a practical recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A statistically formatted verdict can be misleading when the experiment design, sample quality, or metric assumptions are weak.

Mitigation: Double-check the statistical assumptions and experiment setup before using the result for important product or business decisions.
