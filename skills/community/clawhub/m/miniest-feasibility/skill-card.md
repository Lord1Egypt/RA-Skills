## Description:

Helps agents assess the viability of projects, business ideas, or investments using market, technical, financial, operational, legal, and risk-analysis frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ethan-china](https://clawhub.ai/user/ethan-china)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, founders, and planning teams use this skill to structure feasibility studies for new projects, business ideas, investments, products, infrastructure, or technology implementations. It guides market, technical, financial, operational, legal, and risk analysis toward a go/no-go recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business, investment, market, or regulatory conclusions may rely on outdated, incomplete, or weak external sources.

Mitigation: Review cited sources, assumptions, calculations, and regulatory facts with qualified stakeholders before making funding, launch, compliance, or investment decisions.

Risk: The skill provides procedural guidance rather than an automated feasibility-analysis engine.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision-support drafts and validate them with domain experts, current data, and organization-specific feasibility criteria.
