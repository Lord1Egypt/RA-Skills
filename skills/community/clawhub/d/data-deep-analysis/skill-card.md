## Description:

遇到复杂或矛盾的信息时使用。系统性地追溯根源、交叉验证、形成有证据支撑的结论，避免草率判断。

## Use Case:

Analysts, developers, and decision makers use this skill when data is complex, contradictory, or consequential. It guides an agent through source review, cross-validation, pattern analysis, hypothesis testing, confidence labeling, and limitation reporting before conclusions are formed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make the agent slower and more methodical during complex evidence review.

Mitigation: Apply it when decisions depend on conflicting, anomalous, or high-impact data where additional verification is useful.

Risk: Unsupported or premature conclusions could still mislead decisions if the workflow is skipped or applied superficially.

Mitigation: Require source mapping, cross-validation, confidence labels, and explicit limitations before acting on conclusions.
