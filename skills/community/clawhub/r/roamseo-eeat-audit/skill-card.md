## Description:

Score a website's E-E-A-T signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust) from its pages, schema and links, then list the fixes that matter most, quick wins first. Use when asked for an E-E-A-T, trust or credibility check of a domain or URL.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SEO practitioners and website owners use the skill to review a domain or URL for observable trust, first-hand experience, expertise, and authority signals, then prioritize practical improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
