## Description:

Participation Warmup Planner helps agents create per-community pre-promotion warming plans with account-history expectations, give-before-ask ledger specs, rule digests, human-executed cadence, graduation criteria, and owned-community lifecycle guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketing and community teams use this skill after channels are selected to plan authentic participation before promotion. It helps an agent produce per-community etiquette, account-standing expectations, participation cadence, and graduation evidence for moving a channel from warming to active.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Planning notes and proposed channel facts may be stored in a memory workspace after confirmation.

Mitigation: Confirm before saving and review any proposed memory or registry updates before accepting them.

Risk: User exports or screenshots from community platforms may contain private account or community information.

Mitigation: Provide only the necessary details and redact sensitive identifiers or private content before use.

Risk: Community rules and account-history expectations can change or may be estimated from named sources.

Mitigation: Require dated rule digests, named sources, and last-verified dates before treating the plan as current.
