## Description:

Shike Gift Advisor helps agents recommend gifts based on recipient relationship, occasion, and budget, with cultural taboo filtering and personalized greeting generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sjj2026](https://clawhub.ai/user/sjj2026)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to collect gift requirements, recommend three to five gift options, filter culturally sensitive or occasion-inappropriate items, and generate tailored greeting text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Gift suggestions may be culturally inappropriate or inaccurate for the recipient's preferences, local customs, or budget.

Mitigation: Confirm the recipient relationship, occasion, budget, preferences, and any special cultural or personal taboos before acting on recommendations.

Risk: The skill may activate broadly on gift-recommendation requests and provide suggestions that require real-world review.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as suggestions and review cultural fit, item availability, and pricing before purchase or business use.
