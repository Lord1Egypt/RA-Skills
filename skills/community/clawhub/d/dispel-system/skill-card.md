## Description:

Dispel System is a bilingual relationship-control analysis skill that uses screening questions, safety boundaries, structured inquiry, and companion role models to help an agent examine user-described relationship patterns for possible coercive control or manipulation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to structure conversations about suspected controlling relationships, including screening, safety checks, behavior capture, system mapping, and reflective output modes. It should be treated as a reflective framework, not as legal, custody, financial, psychological, or emergency safety advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill operates in high-stakes relationship contexts and server security evidence flags overconfident or under-scoped safety, legal, financial, and child-custody guidance.

Mitigation: Review carefully before installing or deploying; users should rely on qualified local professionals and trusted human support for real danger, custody disputes, evidence questions, finances, protective orders, or separation planning.

Risk: Users may mistake the skill's formulas or fixed protocols for professional psychological, legal, or emergency guidance.

Mitigation: Present the skill as a reflective framework only, preserve its boundaries against diagnosis and legal advice, and direct urgent safety situations to appropriate emergency or local professional resources.
