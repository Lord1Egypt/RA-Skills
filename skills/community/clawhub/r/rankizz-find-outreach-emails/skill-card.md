## Description:

Find publicly listed, role-relevant business email addresses for one website or a list of domains by inspecting the live site and using web search, then cite the exact source for every result.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[prosamik](https://clawhub.ai/user/prosamik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, editorial, partnership, and business development teams can use the skill to research publicly listed contacts that match a specific outreach purpose. It returns cited findings for manual review and does not send outreach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A publicly listed work email may still be personal data and its publication does not establish consent or legal permission to send marketing.

Mitigation: Use the cited source and business context for manual review, collect only role-relevant details, and check applicable rules before sending. Keep opt-outs in a suppression list.

Risk: The security review was partial because four local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the referenced material and its applicability before installing or relying on the skill.
