## Description:

A Chinese-language writing assistant that helps content creators choose content types, build outlines, apply structure templates, and check article completeness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators and writing assistants use this skill to turn a topic and goal into a clear article structure, including templates for tutorials, reviews, stories, opinion pieces, lists, and Q&A content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad writing-help requests or when users expected a different language.

Mitigation: Route it to Chinese-language content-structure requests and confirm the intended content type, audience, and goal before applying a template.
