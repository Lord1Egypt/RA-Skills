## Description:

Generates quality knowledge quiz packages for Quality Month competitions, onboarding, and routine assessments, including question banks, exams, answer explanations, competition plans, and score templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, quality teams, and training organizers use this skill to create quality-management quiz materials and competition packages. It is intended for general quality knowledge content while requiring user-provided source material for enterprise-specific standards, process parameters, and internal cases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated quiz questions may not match the exact standard version or internal procedure used by an organization.

Mitigation: Review generated questions, answers, and explanations against the organization's applicable standards and procedures before use.

Risk: Enterprise-specific standards, process parameters, or internal cases could be inaccurate without supplied source material.

Mitigation: Keep enterprise-specific items marked as "待企业补充" unless the user provides authoritative internal material.

Risk: Referenced helper files or scripts may be unavailable in the released artifact.

Mitigation: Treat helper-file behavior as optional unless the files are included and reviewed in the deployed artifact.
