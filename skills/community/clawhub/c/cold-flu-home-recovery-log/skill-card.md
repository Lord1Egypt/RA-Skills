## Description:

Creates a short-term home recovery log for a cold, flu, or similar illness, including symptom timeline, temperature entries, hydration and rest notes, user-entered medication timing, red-flag checklist, and a clinician-ready summary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and caregivers use this skill to organize short-term cold or flu observations, maintain a daily recovery log, and prepare concise updates for clinicians, school, work, or household handoffs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake an organized illness log for medical assessment or treatment guidance.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as tracking and communication aids, include red flags prominently, and direct users to qualified medical support for serious, worsening, high-risk, or uncertain situations.

Risk: Medication timing fields could be misconstrued as dosing or medication-change advice.

Mitigation: Record only user-entered medication facts and encourage users to verify medication questions with a clinician, pharmacist, or product label.

Risk: The log may contain sensitive illness details provided by the user.

Mitigation: Ask only for practical tracking details the user is comfortable sharing and avoid unnecessary private medical history.
