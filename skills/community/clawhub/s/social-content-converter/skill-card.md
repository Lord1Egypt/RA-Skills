## Description:

Converts one article, script, or topic brief into platform-specific drafts for Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and Bilibili, including titles, body copy, hashtags, cover suggestions, and suggested publishing times.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automated publishing through logged-in creator accounts could post or upload content without adequate review.

Mitigation: Use the skill for local draft generation by default; require explicit human review and confirmation before any browser automation, upload, or publishing action.
