## Description:

A scam-screening skill that reviews offers, messages, or influencer claims and points out concrete red flags.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to screen pasted crypto offers, direct messages, investment pitches, or influencer claims for concrete scam red flags and safer next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on the skill as if it can inspect links, smart contracts, wallets, domains, or accounts in real time.

Mitigation: Present results as text-based scam-screening guidance and independently verify links, identities, contracts, domains, and accounts before taking action.

Risk: A pasted crypto offer or message may not contain enough evidence for a confident verdict.

Mitigation: Use cautious verdicts when certainty is impossible and recommend safer next steps such as not clicking, not signing, not sharing secrets, and verifying independently.
