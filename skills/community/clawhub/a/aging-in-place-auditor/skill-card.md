## Description:

Use when an older parent or relative is staying in their home, after a fall or near-fall, when visiting and noticing trip hazards, or when the family asks 'is the house safe for grandma / can dad keep living here'. Room-by-room fall-risk audit with scored hazards, prioritized fixes priced in cost bands, and the weekend top-5 action list.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Family members and caregivers use the skill to review an older adult's home room by room, prioritize practical fall-hazard fixes with cost bands, and identify when to seek an occupational therapy or medical review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States (costs, funding routes, and renter guidance are US-specific)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A scored home checklist may be mistaken for a clinical assessment or may not capture an individual's full fall risk.

Mitigation: Use the results to guide discussion with a qualified occupational therapist or clinician, especially after repeated falls or when mobility, vision, or medication concerns are present.

Risk: Poorly installed grab bars or home modifications can create hazards instead of reducing them.

Mitigation: Follow the documented anchoring and weight-rating guidance for grab bars, and involve a qualified contractor for structural or stair modifications.
