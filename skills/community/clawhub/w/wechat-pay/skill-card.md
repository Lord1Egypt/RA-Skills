## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may confuse this informational guidance with an official payment authorization or automation tool.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as reference guidance only, and complete passwords, verification codes, and payment authorization only through trusted official WeChat flows.

Risk: Some described workflows may involve sensitive account or payment data.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing passwords, verification codes, payment credentials, or unnecessary personal information with the agent.

Risk: Some WeChat Pay features may depend on third-party services whose availability or behavior can change.

Mitigation: Verify current availability and final transaction details in the official WeChat Pay interface before acting.
