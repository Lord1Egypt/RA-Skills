## Description:

当用户需要热点盘点、指定话题爆款挖掘、跨平台热度对比、选题推荐或话题关键词聚合时使用；能力摘要：限定公众号、抖音、小红书三平台，按 workflow 完成关键词提取、搜索、数据校验与关键词聚合。

## Use Case:

Content creators, social media operators, marketing teams, and MCN researchers use this skill to investigate trending topics, find viral examples, compare activity across WeChat public accounts, Douyin, and Xiaohongshu, and turn validated samples into keyword and angle recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trend results may contain public-search snapshots, incomplete interaction data, stale timestamps, or links that need confirmation.

Mitigation: Verify important source links, publication dates, and engagement numbers manually before publishing findings or making business decisions.

Risk: Some auxiliary guides mention platforms outside the advertised WeChat public account, Douyin, and Xiaohongshu scope.

Mitigation: Restrict searches, analysis, and final reports to the three allowed platforms and filter other platform results out.

Risk: Public trend research can involve copyrighted, platform-governed, or privacy-sensitive content.

Mitigation: Use summaries and links responsibly, follow platform rules, and avoid exposing private personal data.
