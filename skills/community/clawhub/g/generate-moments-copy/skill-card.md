## Description:

根据图片、主题与风格要求，生成自然贴合且可直接发布的朋友圈文案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to generate concise Chinese WeChat Moments captions from an image or image description, topic, and desired tone. It is intended for personal social posting rather than long-form articles or professional marketing copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be asked to caption images with missing or ambiguous context.

Mitigation: Provide accurate image details, topic, and style requirements; avoid treating generated captions as factual claims about location, date, relationships, or events unless those facts were supplied.

Risk: The skill is specialized for Chinese WeChat Moments-style captions and may be unsuitable for other platforms or professional marketing copy.

Mitigation: Use it for personal Moments captions, and explicitly request another language, channel, or marketing workflow when that is the intended output.

Risk: Implicit invocation on closely related caption requests may produce Chinese social-copy output when the user intended a different captioning task.

Mitigation: State the desired platform, language, tone, and output count when invoking the skill in mixed captioning workflows.
