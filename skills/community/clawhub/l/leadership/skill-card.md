## Description:

A leadership-coaching skill that helps leaders prepare difficult conversations, build feedback practices, make decisions under uncertainty, develop team members, and manage upward with clarity.

## Use Case:

Leaders and managers use this skill as a coaching and planning aid for difficult conversations, actionable feedback, decisions under uncertainty, team development, and managing up. It supports preparation and reflection, not personnel recordkeeping.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may enter sensitive employee details, confidential personnel matters, or information that belongs in approved HR systems.

Mitigation: Use the skill for coaching and planning only, avoid unnecessary sensitive details, and keep official personnel records in approved HR tools.
