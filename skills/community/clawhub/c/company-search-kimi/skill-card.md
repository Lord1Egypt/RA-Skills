## Use Case:

Business analysts, investors, legal teams, and other due-diligence users can use this skill to generate structured Chinese-language company research reports from public web sources. It is suited for company background checks, ownership and financing summaries, legal and operating risk review, intellectual property checks, hiring signals, news monitoring, and competitive analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Company research conclusions may be incomplete or outdated because the skill relies on live public web searches and accessible pages rather than paid business databases or internal records.

Mitigation: Verify sensitive business conclusions against authoritative sources, paid registries, official filings, or internal diligence channels before relying on them.

Risk: Single-source media, forum, or self-published claims can lead to misleading risk or reputation assessments.

Mitigation: Prefer official, regulatory, court, exchange, company, and multiple authoritative media sources, and preserve the skill's confidence labels for weakly supported claims.
