## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Media conversion settings can overwrite originals, produce unacceptable quality loss, or create outputs that do not meet platform limits.

Mitigation: Review output paths, target limits, quality settings, and file-size estimates before running compression, especially when working with original source files.

Risk: Over-compressing videos with faces, text, captions, products, or demo motion can reduce clarity and make the output unsuitable for publishing.

Mitigation: Use the skill's quality notes and further-reduction step to preserve important visual details and make tradeoffs explicit.
