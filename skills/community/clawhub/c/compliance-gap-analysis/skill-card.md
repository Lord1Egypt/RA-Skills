## Description:

Use when a compliance officer, security analyst, or auditor needs to assess an organization's controls against a regulatory framework (GDPR, SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, NIST CSF). Guides structured evidence collection per control domain and produces a gap analysis matrix, risk-rated findings, and a prioritized remediation roadmap.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Compliance officers, security analysts, and auditors use this skill to interview stakeholders about controls for a selected regulatory or security framework and turn self-reported evidence into a gap analysis, risk-rated findings, and a remediation roadmap.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share credentials, customer records, employee names, exact hostnames, or other highly sensitive details during the compliance interview.

Mitigation: Use generalized system categories and size bands unless specific details are strictly necessary, and avoid sharing credentials or identifiable records.

Risk: The resulting compliance observations may be mistaken for legal advice or independently verified audit findings.

Mitigation: Treat the report as a self-reported gap analysis, verify findings against official standards and evidence, and consult qualified legal counsel for legal interpretations.
