## Description:

Expert guidance for systematic backtesting of trading strategies, including robustness testing, slippage modeling, bias prevention, and interpretation of backtest results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, quantitative traders, and trading-strategy researchers use this skill to design, validate, stress-test, and troubleshoot systematic backtests before considering live deployment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general backtesting methodology as financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as educational guidance and independently validate every strategy before risking real capital.

Risk: Backtest guidance can still produce overconfidence if users deploy a strategy without independent review.

Mitigation: Review assumptions, data quality, slippage, bias controls, and out-of-sample results before live use.
