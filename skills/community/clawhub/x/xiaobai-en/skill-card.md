Developers, educators, and AI safety communicators use Xiaobai EN to turn AI security incidents into thought experiments, visual campaign prompts, and narrative materials for public AI safety discussion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks agents to collect and archive youth-oriented social discussion data without clear privacy limits or consent safeguards.

Mitigation: Before use, require authorized, platform-compliant, anonymized or aggregated data collection with consent rules, access controls, retention and deletion policy, and explicit protections for minors.

Risk: Public-facing security narratives may overstate conclusions when converting incidents into philosophical or campaign materials.

Mitigation: Review generated messages for factual accuracy, sourcing, audience appropriateness, and clear separation between evidence and interpretation before publication.
