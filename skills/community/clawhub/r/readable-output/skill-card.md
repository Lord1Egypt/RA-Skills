## Description:

Official 看得清 Readable Output skill. Turns dense AI replies into a clearer reading layer with overview, key points, warnings, next steps, and folded raw text.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sheygoodbai](https://clawhub.ai/user/sheygoodbai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to reformat long AI responses into concise overviews, key points, warnings, next steps, and preserved raw wording for easier review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readability restructuring may change how important decisions are perceived if users rely only on the summary.

Mitigation: Compare the readability layer against the preserved original wording before acting on important decisions.
