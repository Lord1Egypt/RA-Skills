## Description:

Helps agents evaluate or plan photographs using three composition principles: clear subject, attention guidance, and simplicity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and photography learners use this skill when asking an agent to judge photo quality, check composition before shooting, or explain what makes a good photograph. It focuses on subject clarity, visual attention, and simplicity rather than camera settings, post-processing, or product research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-first guidance may not match every user's preferred language.

Mitigation: Ask for or follow the user's preferred language when multilingual clarity matters.
