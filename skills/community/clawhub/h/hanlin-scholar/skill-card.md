## Description:

翰林学士 helps users polish, proofread, and paraphrase Chinese or English academic manuscript text by sending submitted text to DeepSeek and returning issues, revised suggestions, and explanations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and academic authors use this skill to improve academic wording, correct grammar, or paraphrase manuscript passages while preserving the original meaning and technical terminology.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitted manuscript text is sent to DeepSeek for rewriting.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential, unpublished, regulated, or third-party-restricted manuscripts unless external processing is permitted by the user's policies.

Risk: Academic edits or paraphrases may alter nuance or introduce unsuitable wording.

Mitigation: Review generated suggestions before using them in scholarly work, especially where accuracy, authorship, or citation integrity matters.
