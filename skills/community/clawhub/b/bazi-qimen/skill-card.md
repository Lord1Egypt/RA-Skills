## Description:

Bazi Qimen calculates and interprets Bazi (Four Pillars Astrology) and Qi Men Dun Jia charts as an auxiliary decision-making reference rooted in traditional Chinese metaphysics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to calculate Bazi and Qi Men Dun Jia charts and produce structured, probabilistic interpretations for traditional-cultural reference across career, wealth, relationships, health, or time-sensitive questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for birth date, birth time, and gender to calculate charts.

Mitigation: Request only the details needed for the chart and avoid retaining or sharing personal inputs outside the active task.

Risk: Users may over-weight traditional-cultural interpretations when making important life, financial, relationship, or health decisions.

Mitigation: State that outputs are probabilistic reference material and do not replace real-world due diligence or qualified professional advice.

Risk: Simplified solar-term, Major Luck, and Qi Men transition calculations can differ from precise calendrical methods.

Mitigation: Disclose simplified calculation assumptions and recommend an authoritative perpetual calendar when precision matters.
