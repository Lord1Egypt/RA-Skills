## Description:

Design & Templates helps agents specify a reusable on-brand social template system: brand-kit tokens, a small template set, hierarchy rules, and a production workflow for Canva, Adobe Express, Figma, image tools, and WoopSocial publishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, social teams, and operators use this skill to turn a brand profile and recurring content needs into a reusable brand kit, template set, and production workflow for consistent social graphics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill discusses design tool pricing, AI copyright, and indemnification claims that may change over time.

Mitigation: Verify current tool terms, pricing, copyright guidance, and indemnification coverage before making business or publishing decisions.

Risk: The skill can suggest reusable brand systems but does not produce finished graphics itself.

Mitigation: Use a design tool or image generation tool for final assets, then review the finished work for brand fit, legibility, and originality before publishing.

Risk: Overly broad routing could lead the agent to treat this skill as responsible for adjacent tasks such as copywriting, image generation, or publishing.

Mitigation: Keep this skill scoped to brand kits, template systems, hierarchy rules, and workflow guidance; route copy, generation, and publishing tasks to their dedicated tools.
