## Use Case:

External users can enter a stock ticker or company name to have an agent produce a structured fundamental-analysis report covering business basics, financial health, valuation, moat strength, risks, management quality, and margin of safety. The skill is intended for research assistance and includes a non-advice disclaimer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may send stock tickers or company names to third-party financial sites during data lookup.

Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential watchlists or sensitive company names unless third-party data sharing is acceptable for the use case.

Risk: Investment conclusions could be mistaken for professional financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research assistance, verify cited data sources, and consult qualified professionals before making investment decisions.
