## Description:

AI 应用工程师专属的每日资讯日报技能，收集 AI 行业动态并按六大工程实用板块组织中文日报。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[robusth](https://clawhub.ai/user/robusth)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and AI application engineers use this skill to produce a concise Chinese daily briefing on AI industry updates, product changes, engineering patterns, safety issues, and practical learning resources. It emphasizes source-linked items and engineering applicability over benchmark tracking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad daily-report wording.

Mitigation: Use explicit AI-news phrasing or clarify the requested topic, date, and language before generating the report.

Risk: The report relies on public web sources that may change or contain incomplete context.

Mitigation: Review cited URLs, dates, and source credibility before using the briefing for decisions.

Risk: The default output language is Chinese.

Mitigation: Ask for a different output language when the audience needs localized or bilingual reporting.
