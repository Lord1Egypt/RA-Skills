## Description:

Design and adjust Subclip canvas aspect ratio, dimensions, backgrounds, framing, layer position, size, crop, and visual hierarchy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External video creators and editors use this skill to choose canvas formats and adjust framing, layer placement, crops, and visual hierarchy in Subclip compositions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Canvas changes or repositioned layers can leave visible crops, overlaps, black bars, or unsafe margins.

Mitigation: Inspect affected frames after edits and again after corrections, including later frames when layers animate or enter later.
