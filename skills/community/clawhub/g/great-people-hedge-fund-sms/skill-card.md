## Description:

Coordinates investor-persona and analysis agents to evaluate stocks, synthesize viewpoints, and generate investment signals with conviction levels.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[smseow001](https://clawhub.ai/user/smseow001)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and market-analysis users can use this skill to coordinate multiple investor-style perspectives for stock research, comparison, signal synthesis, and risk-aware portfolio commentary. Outputs should be treated as educational analysis rather than financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Documentation can lead users toward a mismatched or unpinned installer command.

Mitigation: Prefer the server-resolved install path or a pinned, verified installer command matching the metadata slug before installation.

Risk: Trading-style signals and position-size suggestions may be mistaken for financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational analysis, verify market data independently, and consult qualified financial professionals before investment decisions.
