## Description:

Guides app advertising for app install campaigns, user acquisition, and in-app promotion across mobile ad platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, growth practitioners, and agents advising them use this skill to plan mobile app install, user acquisition, and in-app action campaigns. It helps frame platform choices, tracking setup, metrics, and launch readiness for iOS and Android app promotion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Platform-specific ad recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for a user's campaign budget, attribution setup, or target market.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current platform documentation and campaign constraints before committing ad spend.
