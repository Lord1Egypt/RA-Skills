## Description:

Build a clear chronological timeline and proof index from user-provided events and evidence. Use when the user needs to explain what happened with supporting proof, identify gaps, and draft a concise factual summary without legal advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize claims, disputes, complaints, incidents, appeals, support cases, or personal records into a chronological timeline with proof IDs, gaps, and a concise factual summary. The skill is intended for factual organization only and does not provide legal advice or predict outcomes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat an organized timeline as legal, financial, medical, or other professional advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as factual organization, avoid advice or outcome predictions, and recommend qualified professional or official resources for high-stakes matters.

Risk: Evidence packets may contain sensitive identifiers or credentials.

Mitigation: Ask users to share only needed evidence and redact account numbers, ID numbers, passwords, one-time codes, and other sensitive identifiers.

Risk: Unsupported statements may become more persuasive when placed in a polished timeline.

Mitigation: Separate directly supported facts from user interpretation and unknowns, flag unsupported statements, and ask what proof exists instead of strengthening claims.

Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill outside factual evidence organization tasks.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for user-provided event and evidence organization, and decline requests to fabricate facts, coach deception, impersonate professionals, threaten unsupported action, or make legal conclusions.
