## Use Case:

External solopreneurs and small business builders use this skill to create or refresh a coherent brand identity. It guides brand strategy, voice, visual identity choices, logo direction, imagery style, and a reusable brand guidelines document.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may request business, audience, and brand preference details during a branding workflow.

Mitigation: Share only business context and brand preferences that are appropriate for use in brand-development guidance.

Risk: Brand recommendations can be subjective or misaligned with the user's actual market positioning.

Mitigation: Review generated positioning, voice, and visual guidance against known customer research and business goals before adopting it.
