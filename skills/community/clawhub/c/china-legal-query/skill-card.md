## Description:

China Legal Query helps agents search Chinese laws, regulations, judicial interpretations, and case references for common legal domains including civil, criminal, labor, contract, and intellectual property law.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tobewin](https://clawhub.ai/user/tobewin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to look up Chinese legal provisions, judicial interpretations, and reference cases, then receive concise summaries or article-level answers. Outputs are reference material only and should be checked against official sources for consequential legal matters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal answers may be incomplete, outdated, or mistaken when based on AI knowledge or broad web search.

Mitigation: Verify important results against official Chinese legal sources and consult a qualified lawyer for consequential matters.

Risk: Network lookups through curl or web search may return unavailable or non-authoritative sources.

Mitigation: Prefer official legal databases and clearly distinguish retrieved legal text from AI interpretation.
