## Description:

Plan AI video scenes from a creator's concept into character sheets, scene plates, multi-shot prompt briefs, credit-aware run plans, and review checklists for tools such as Claude, Higgsfield, Seedance, Nano Banana Pro, and similar image/video generators.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zack-dev-cm](https://clawhub.ai/user/zack-dev-cm)

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to turn AI video concepts into production-ready prompt briefs with continuity planning, shot tables, credit-risk review, and repair prompts before using image or video generation tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompt plans may lead to unnecessary paid image or video generation attempts if they are vague or over-scoped.

Mitigation: Review the shot plan, generation order, and credit-risk rating before using paid generation services.

Risk: Prompt briefs for ads, source-inspired videos, or real-person likenesses may raise rights, disclosure, impersonation, or consent concerns.

Mitigation: Check rights to source material, include ad or affiliate disclosures when relevant, and reject non-consensual real-person impersonation or sexualized content.
