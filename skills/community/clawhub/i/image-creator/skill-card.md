## Use Case:

External users and creative teams use this skill to choose a curated visual style, provide any required inputs, and generate stylized images for characters, scenes, products, ads, logos, and visual transformations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad routing terms may steer general image-generation or weather-related prompts into this curated image workflow.

Mitigation: Review routing scope before deployment and require clarification when the user's intent is ambiguous.

Risk: Generated brand, logo, product, movie, or city imagery can be mistaken for official or factual material.

Mitigation: Have users review generated images for rights, affiliation, factual accuracy, and suitability before publication.
