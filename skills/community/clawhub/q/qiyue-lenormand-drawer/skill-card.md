## Description:

Draw Lenormand cards from QiyueAstro: single card, three-card, relationship, decision, nine-card grid, and more; browse the full 36-card Lenormand deck with meanings in English or Chinese; no API key is needed and free AI interpretations are included with a limit of two per day per IP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to draw or browse Lenormand cards, choose common spreads, and receive API-returned card meanings in English or Chinese. Users may also explicitly request a third-party AI interpretation from QiyueAstro.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Card questions, including relationship or decision details, can be sent to QiyueAstro.

Mitigation: Tell users before external AI interpretation is requested and advise them not to share highly sensitive personal, medical, legal, financial, or identifying information.

Risk: Promotional links and AI-generated readings may be included in responses as third-party content.

Mitigation: Present QiyueAstro readings and links as third-party content and avoid treating them as agent-verified advice.
