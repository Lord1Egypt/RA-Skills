## Description:

Dewell Bot helps freight forwarders query ocean container routes, rate rules, itemized shipping costs, booking details, and shipment tracking guidance for major global ports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Freight forwarders and logistics operators use this skill to compare ocean container route options, estimate freight and surcharges, prepare booking details, and understand port-specific constraints before confirming shipment information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Freight rates, route availability, surcharges, port rules, and shipment timing can change before booking.

Mitigation: Confirm routes, fees, cargo details, provider identity, and applicable restrictions with the logistics provider before submitting an order or business information.

Risk: The skill is written in Chinese and appears intended for freight logistics workflows.

Mitigation: Use it with operators who can review the Chinese guidance accurately, or translate and verify the content before relying on it for commercial logistics decisions.
