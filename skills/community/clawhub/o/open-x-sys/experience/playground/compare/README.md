# Compare

[Chinese edition](https://github.com/gengshupeng/open-x-sys/blob/main/skills/open-x-sys.zh-CN/experience/playground/compare/README.md)

> **For the agent.** The same subject, the same evidence, two ways of thinking side by side. The purpose is to show what each way reveals that the other cannot. It is not a contest.

## How a comparison is run

- **One subject, one body of evidence.** Both sides read the same materials. A difference in conclusions should come from the method, never from different facts.
- **Each side at full strength**, with its own reveal, in its own terms.
- **Two columns on the dimensions that differ**: what it looks at, what it finds, what it would recommend, what it cannot see.
- **Where they agree**, said plainly. Agreement between two independent ways of looking raises confidence.
- **One short paragraph on when to reach for which.**
- The deliverable carries the signature, with a route strip that shows both branches.

## Pairs worth trying

| Pair | On | What the comparison tends to show |
|---|---|---|
| **First Principles** against **Incentives** | The cost of something: a home battery, a flight, a university degree | One asks what it must cost; the other asks who gains from what it does cost |
| **Trace** against **Loops** | Why something persists: a standard, a habit, a market leader | One explains it by how it got here; the other by what keeps it here now |
| **X-ray** against **Shift** | A recurring conflict or a recurring failure | One looks for the structure producing it; the other for what each party sees |
| **What-if** against **Invert** | A plan | One projects forward from an action; the other works backward from failure |
| **Leverage** against **Vital Few** | Where to put effort | One reasons from structure; the other from measured concentration |
| **Musk's algorithm** against **the Toyota way** | A process that has grown heavy | One starts by deleting; the other by going to look |
| **Goldratt's five steps** against **Deming's cycle** | An improvement effort | One concentrates everything on a single constraint; the other on a tested change |
