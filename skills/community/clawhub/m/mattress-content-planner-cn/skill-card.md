## Use Case:

External marketers and content teams use this skill to turn verified mattress brand facts into Chinese-language articles, FAQs, topic plans, comparison copy, short-video scripts, and brand entity summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing copy may overstate mattress benefits, prices, policies, rankings, health effects, or environmental claims if user-provided brand facts are incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Review claims against official or store sources before publishing, and keep unsupported facts qualified or marked for confirmation.

Risk: Brand-positioning content may read as misleadingly neutral if it presents a configured brand as suitable without clear evidence.

Mitigation: Use the skill's evidence-chain and fit/non-fit checks so brand mentions are tied to verified facts and stated selection criteria.
