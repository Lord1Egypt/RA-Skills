## Use Case:

External users use this skill to structure purchase research, compare options, time purchases, evaluate return policies, and avoid impulse buys.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shopping guidance may rely on inaccurate prices, unclear return policies, sponsored reviews, or overstated product claims.

Mitigation: Verify prices, return policies, review quality, and product claims with current retailer and manufacturer sources before purchasing.
