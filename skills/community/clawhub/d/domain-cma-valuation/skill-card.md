## Description:

Apply the substitution principle to position a compound or keyword-plus-modifier domain within the current asking-price market.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abtdomain](https://clawhub.ai/user/abtdomain)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and domain-market analysts use this skill to build a current-listing Comparative Market Analysis for compound or keyword-plus-modifier domains, using verified substitute listings rather than historical sale prices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The output can be mistaken for an official appraisal or investment recommendation.

Mitigation: Present results as reference-only current listing analysis and retain the compliance statement that it is not official appraisal or investment advice.

Risk: Domain listing data may be stale, unverifiable, or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify marketplace pages for current asking prices, mark unavailable fields as Not Provided, and avoid producing a range when verified comparable evidence is insufficient.

Risk: Ongoing price monitoring may create unexpected repeated market checks.

Mitigation: Use monitoring only when the user intentionally requests ongoing price tracking.
