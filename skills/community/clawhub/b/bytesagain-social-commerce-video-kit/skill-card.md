## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketplace copy may contain unsupported claims, localization issues, or platform-policy conflicts if published without review.

Mitigation: Review generated copy against official marketplace policies, product facts, target-market requirements, and claim substantiation before publishing.

Risk: Drafted ecommerce assets can become misleading when product details, dimensions, compatibility, certifications, or package contents are missing or assumed.

Mitigation: Fill and verify source product facts before using titles, bullets, scripts, FAQs, or launch bundles in store workflows.
