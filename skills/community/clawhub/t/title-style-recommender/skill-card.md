## Description:

Recommends academic paper title styles and templates based on research type, including conceptual, empirical, exploratory, methodological, review, meta-analysis, and longitudinal studies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External researchers, students, and academic writers use this skill to identify an academic study type and receive title templates, example titles, and cautions for paper-title wording.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad Chinese trigger phrases may activate the skill for general title requests that are not about academic papers or studies.

Mitigation: Clarify whether the request is for an academic paper or study before applying the title-style recommendations.
