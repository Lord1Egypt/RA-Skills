## Description:

Supports industry analysis using the Structure-Conduct-Performance framework to assess market concentration, entry barriers, competitive dynamics, firm conduct, and market performance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panlm](https://clawhub.ai/user/panlm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, strategists, and policy researchers use this skill to structure industry-level market analysis, evaluate concentration and entry barriers, and connect firm conduct to market performance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad competition or market-analysis trigger phrases may activate this framework when a different business framework is more appropriate.

Mitigation: Review activation wording and use the skill for industry-level SCP analysis; choose other frameworks for firm-level, macro-environment, or nascent-market questions.

Risk: SCP conclusions can be misleading when market definitions are too broad, too narrow, or weakly supported by evidence.

Mitigation: Define the market, geography, time period, and evidence base before drawing conduct or performance conclusions.
