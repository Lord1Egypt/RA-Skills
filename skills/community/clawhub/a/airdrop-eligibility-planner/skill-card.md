## Description:

Plan and track airdrop eligibility work across protocols: criteria, deadlines, and cost per wallet — without sybil behavior. Use when farming announced or likely airdrops with your own wallets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[manteclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/manteclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals tracking potential protocol airdrops use this skill to record likely eligibility criteria, estimated costs per wallet, dated deadlines, and status. It helps them separate confirmed criteria from guesses and avoid deceptive or uneconomic activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scanner coverage was incomplete, so the available scan does not fully assess the skill artifact.

Mitigation: Review the skill description and contents before installing.

Risk: Guessed criteria or undated snapshot rumors may be mistaken for confirmed requirements and lead to uneconomic activity.

Mitigation: Mark criteria as confirmed or guessed, record only dated deadlines, estimate costs before starting, and stop when costs exceed a conservative reward estimate.
