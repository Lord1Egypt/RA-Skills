## Description:

Generate professional invoices in Markdown or HTML by specifying client, items, tax, currency, dates, and output format.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, freelancers, and business operators can use this skill to generate structured invoices from command-line inputs and save them as Markdown or print-ready HTML.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Untrusted invoice fields can alter generated invoices or active HTML output.

Mitigation: Use trusted invoice data, avoid HTML output for untrusted client or item fields, and prefer a fixed version that escapes HTML fields.

Risk: Numeric fields may be malformed or unexpected because the artifact does not validate tax, quantity, or rate inputs.

Mitigation: Validate numeric inputs before use and review generated totals before sending invoices.
