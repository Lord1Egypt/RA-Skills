## Description:

Generate personalized cybersecurity certification and career advancement plans based on individual assessment data and professional goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd](https://clawhub.ai/user/krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Security professionals, hiring managers, educational advisors, and workforce development teams use this skill to request personalized cybersecurity certification roadmaps, career pathways, skill-gap analysis, and salary progression guidance from an external API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Professional profile details, certifications, tools, interests, goals, timestamps, session IDs, and optional user IDs may be processed by an external API.

Mitigation: Avoid sending unnecessary identifying information and use the skill only when the provider's privacy and retention practices are acceptable.
