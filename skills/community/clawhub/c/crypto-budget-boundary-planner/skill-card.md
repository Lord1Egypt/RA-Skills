## Description:

A prompt-only budgeting skill that helps users set conservative crypto spending limits anchored to real-life finances.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for educational budget planning when deciding whether, and how much, they can afford to allocate to crypto without pressuring emergency funds, debt repayment, or monthly essentials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat crypto budget boundaries as personalized regulated financial advice.

Mitigation: Present the output as educational budgeting guidance and direct users to qualified financial professionals for individualized advice.

Risk: Users under high-interest debt, insufficient emergency savings, or acute money pressure may over-allocate to crypto.

Mitigation: Favor delay or a zero-allocation recommendation when basic financial stability signals are weak.
