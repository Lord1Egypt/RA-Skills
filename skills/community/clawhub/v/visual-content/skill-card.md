## Description:

Helps agents plan, create, and repurpose visual content such as images, infographics, social post images, and platform-specific visual specs across channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, content, and design-support agents use this skill to decide which visuals a content piece needs, choose channel-specific image specifications, and map one core visual into reusable assets for social, web, and email.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for a broad range of visual-content planning requests.

Mitigation: Review the generated visual plan and platform specs against the intended channel before using them in production content.

Risk: The skill may consult local project visual identity context when available.

Mitigation: Confirm that any local brand context used by the agent is appropriate for the specific campaign or content asset.
