## Description:

Helps draft formal academic research paper sections using common paper structures and IEEE, ACM, ASCE, or Elsevier formatting conventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill as a backward-compatible academic writing helper for drafting paper sections and applying common conference or journal formatting conventions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated academic writing can contain inaccurate, unsupported, or improperly cited claims.

Mitigation: Review drafts for factual accuracy, citation quality, venue requirements, and institutional academic-integrity rules before use.

Risk: The legacy trigger may catch generic writing prompts, and the runtime implementation appears minimal.

Mitigation: Install only when a compatibility alias is desired, and prefer the documented unified paper workflow for normal use.
