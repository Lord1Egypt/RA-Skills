## Description:

Starting from a user's own viewpoint, this skill helps an agent search online for supporting material, reorganize the argument, bind evidence to each section, and produce a personally styled public-account article or short-video script for content creators.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External content creators, bloggers, and media operators use this skill to turn rough opinions, live-stream notes, interviews, or conversations into structured articles or short-video scripts while preserving their personal voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Online searches based on user ideas may expose sensitive personal details or unpublished viewpoints.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive personal details in prompts or search queries unless the user is comfortable using them as draft material.

Risk: Drafts may include incorrect or weakly supported facts if search results are incomplete or unreliable.

Mitigation: Keep source labels with searched material, mark unsupported claims as pending verification, and review factual claims before publication.
