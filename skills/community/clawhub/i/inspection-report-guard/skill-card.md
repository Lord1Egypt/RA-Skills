## Description:

Reviews China-focused product quality inspection, commissioned inspection, supervision sampling, type-test, factory inspection, third-party testing, and testing-organization publicity materials for report-format, qualification, standards-reference, data, conclusion, wording, and CMA-use compliance issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Compliance, quality, and testing teams use this skill to review inspection-report text before publication and identify issues that should block release or be corrected. It is focused on China product inspection and testing report requirements, including CMA qualification scope, current standards, report elements, conclusions, and wording.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Compliance guidance may become incomplete or outdated because Chinese standards and regulatory requirements can change.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as compliance assistance and confirm material findings against current regulations, standards, and qualified legal or accredited laboratory review before publication.

Risk: The skill can recommend blocking a report, but it does not replace final professional approval.

Mitigation: Use the structured findings to support human review, especially for CMA scope, standard validity, report conclusions, and publication decisions.
