## Description:

Build a personal gift system for tracking ideas, occasions, and gift-giving history.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to maintain a local gift notebook for people, occasions, gift ideas, gift history, and personal wishlist notes. The agent checks saved notes before suggesting gifts and helps capture useful context over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Gift notes may include personal details about the user or other people in local files.

Mitigation: Keep notes under local control, avoid storing highly sensitive information, and review files before relying on them for gift decisions.
