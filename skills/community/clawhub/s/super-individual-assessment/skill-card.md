External users and agents use this skill to run a Chinese-language, evidence-based self-assessment of four AI super individual capability pillars and turn the scores into a 90-day action plan.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may ask for specific work examples, client context, employer details, or payment evidence.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive client, employer, or financial details unless the user is comfortable including them in the conversation.

Risk: Users could mistake the score for a psychological measurement, professional qualification, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Present conclusions as self-assessment only, avoid invented rankings or predictions, and do not use scores as a substitute for professional advice in career or financial distress.
