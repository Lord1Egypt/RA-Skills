## Description:

Novel Writer Agent helps generate novel outlines, draft chapter prose, manage writing progress, run chapter quality checks, support Chinese and English writing, and organize reusable writing materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Writers and writing-workflow agents use this skill to turn planning documents into four-act novel outlines, chapter drafts, quality self-checks, and complete writing packages with outlines, prose, and character cards.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The material-library workflow may affect how writing materials are organized or reused in a workspace.

Mitigation: Confirm the material-library behavior matches the intended writing workflow before installing or using the skill.

Risk: Generated fiction may include quality issues, sensitive content, or reuse that requires editorial review.

Mitigation: Review chapter outputs with the skill's quality checklist and verify originality, sensitivity, and publication readiness before release.
