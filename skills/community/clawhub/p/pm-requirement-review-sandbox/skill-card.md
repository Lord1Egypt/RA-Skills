## Description:

A product requirement review sandbox workflow that helps product managers and collaborators clarify ambiguous requirements, co-create PRD materials, rehearse multi-role review challenges, narrow initial scope, and produce Markdown, PDF, or Feishu-ready deliverables.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whisperbot](https://clawhub.ai/user/whisperbot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers and product collaborators use this skill to clarify product ideas, structure PRD drafts, expose assumptions and review risks, simulate feedback from engineering, design, data, operations, compliance, business, and user perspectives, and prepare review-ready documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create local Markdown or PDF files and may create or fill a Feishu document when the user requests Feishu delivery in an already logged-in environment.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended output location and review generated files or Feishu content before sharing them externally.

Risk: Generated product requirement content can include assumptions, suggested metrics, review challenges, and scope recommendations that affect business decisions.

Mitigation: Validate assumptions, metrics, constraints, compliance expectations, and cross-team dependencies with responsible stakeholders before treating the document as final.
