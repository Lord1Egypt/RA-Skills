## Description:

Detect, triage, mitigate, and communicate during outages. Use when pages fire, SEVs open, or post-incident follow-up is needed.

## Use Case:

Developers, operators, and incident responders use this skill to structure outage response from initial triage through mitigation, validation, stakeholder communication, and post-incident follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The incident workflow may be offered in adjacent contexts such as reviews, rollouts, or stakeholder updates when the user may prefer freeform help.

Mitigation: Ask whether to follow the four-stage workflow or work freeform, and switch styles if the user declines.

Risk: Incident-response guidance can be incomplete when environment, impact, constraints, or verification needs are unclear.

Mitigation: Ask targeted context questions before strong recommendations and tie advice to validation, monitoring, documentation, and follow-up steps.
