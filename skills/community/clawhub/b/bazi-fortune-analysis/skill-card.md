## Description:

Helps an agent provide Chinese BaZi fortune analysis, including chart construction, five-element analysis, ten-god interpretation, pattern assessment, luck-cycle discussion, and topical readings for relationships, career, finance, health, children, and education.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tianheihei002](https://clawhub.ai/user/tianheihei002)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to request culturally framed BaZi readings from birth date, birth time, birthplace, calendar type, and gender. The skill guides the agent to produce structured chart tables, interpretive analysis, and practical suggestions while presenting fortune-telling outputs as reference guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks users for personal birth details, including date, time, birthplace, calendar type, and gender.

Mitigation: Collect only the birth details needed for the requested reading and avoid retaining or sharing them outside the active conversation.

Risk: Fortune analysis may include health, marriage, finance, or life-event predictions that users could mistake for professional advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as cultural or entertainment guidance and direct users to qualified professionals for medical, legal, financial, or other high-stakes decisions.
