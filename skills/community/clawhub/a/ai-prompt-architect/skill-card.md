## Description:

Designs, improves, debugs, and evaluates prompts and AI workflows through structured interviews, templates, examples, workflow diagrams, and optimization guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jzw6](https://clawhub.ai/user/jzw6)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, developers, and external users use this skill to convert AI behavior requirements into production-oriented prompts or agent workflows. It is also used to diagnose and improve existing prompts with structured gap analysis, examples, and iteration guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad trigger may activate for simple AI-related questions where the user wanted a short answer.

Mitigation: Narrow or disable the skill when lightweight answers are preferred, and confirm the user's desired depth before producing a full prompt architecture.

Risk: Generated prompts or workflows may encode incomplete compliance, safety, or domain assumptions.

Mitigation: Review generated prompts before deployment, especially for regulated, customer-facing, or high-impact use cases.
