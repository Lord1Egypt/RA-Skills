## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Referenced skills may use browser automation against academic databases through existing accounts or institutional sessions.

Mitigation: Review each referenced skill separately and confirm account, database, and institutional policy constraints before use.

Risk: Generated literature reviews, evidence grades, and citations may be incomplete or inaccurate if sources are missed or misread.

Mitigation: Validate source coverage, evidence grading, citations, and final claims against the original papers before submission.
