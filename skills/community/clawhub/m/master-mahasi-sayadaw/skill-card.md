## Description:

Provides grounded Chinese-language guidance and answers about Mahasi Sayadaw, Burmese vipassana, the Mahasi noting method, satipatthana, the sixteen insight knowledges, and related Theravada meditation topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer questions about Mahasi Sayadaw's Burmese vipassana tradition and the Mahasi noting method, with responses grounded in declared Pali, Visuddhimagga, and Mahasi publication sources. It is intended for learning and reference, not for personal attainment assessment or a substitute for qualified meditation instruction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can strongly steer answers in a religious and meditation context.

Mitigation: Review before installing and ensure users understand outputs are learning-oriented guidance, not authoritative personal instruction.

Risk: The skill includes broad auto-activation triggers and a persona-like response style.

Mitigation: Deploy with clear invocation boundaries and monitor whether it activates only for Mahasi, Theravada, Burmese vipassana, or noting-method requests.

Risk: Meditation-stage or attainment language could be misread as personal assessment.

Mitigation: Preserve the artifact guardrail that the agent must not judge individual attainment or confirm insight stages, and direct users to a qualified teacher for intensive practice assessment.

Risk: The source material includes copyright-sensitive Mahasi publication summaries.

Mitigation: Keep outputs as concise summaries with citations, avoid long verbatim reproduction, and honor takedown or publisher objections.
