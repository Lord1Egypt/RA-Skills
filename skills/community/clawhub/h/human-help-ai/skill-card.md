## Description:

HumanHelpAI directs users to Human Help AI experts across professional fields for AI validation and consulting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[david1kos](https://clawhub.ai/user/david1kos)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, AI companies, and users seeking domain review use this skill to identify the kind of human expert they need and get directed to Human Help AI to browse experts, post requests, or register as an expert.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on marketplace listings for regulated or high-stakes professional needs without confirming credentials, licensing, pricing, availability, or privacy terms.

Mitigation: Verify current expert credentials, professional licensing, rates, availability, and privacy terms on the external Human Help AI site before relying on the referral, especially for medical, legal, financial, or mental health needs.
