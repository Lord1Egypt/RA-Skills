## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, founders, product teams, and developers use this skill to structure market sizing, competitor analysis, and customer validation research using public sources and Tavily search workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research queries may be sent to Tavily and could expose confidential business details.

Mitigation: Avoid including confidential, regulated, or proprietary information in search queries unless that sharing is approved for the use case.

Risk: Market forecasts and competitor estimates can be incorrect, outdated, or overconfident.

Mitigation: Cite sources, state methodology and confidence, and treat outputs as probabilistic research rather than financial or investment advice.

Risk: Competitive intelligence work can cross ethical or legal boundaries if it uses non-public access or deceptive collection.

Mitigation: Use public filings, published pages, reviews, and other lawful public sources; avoid fake inquiries, social engineering, paywall scraping, or internal documents.
