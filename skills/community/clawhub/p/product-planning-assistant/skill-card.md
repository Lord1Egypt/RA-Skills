## Description:

Helps complete high-quality product planning from requirements to implementation. Invoke when PM needs annual/quarterly/product iteration planning, reporting to leadership, or team alignment meetings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orzinfo](https://clawhub.ai/user/orzinfo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers use this Chinese-language skill to structure annual, quarterly, and product iteration plans, then prepare leadership reports or team alignment materials. It guides goal setting, demand prioritization, delivery path breakdown, risk planning, and stakeholder reporting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill instructions and templates are in Chinese, which may reduce usability for non-Chinese users.

Mitigation: Use with Chinese-speaking product teams or translate outputs before relying on them for broader stakeholder review.

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for casual planning requests.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user needs a structured product planning workflow before applying the full five-step process.
