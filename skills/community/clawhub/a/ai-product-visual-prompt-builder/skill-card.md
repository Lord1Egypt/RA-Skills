## Description:

Turn rough product image, poster, thumbnail, social creative, ad concept, or image-to-video ideas into structured prompts for AI image and video generation tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gpt-img-2](https://clawhub.ai/user/gpt-img-2)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creative teams use this skill to turn product visual, ecommerce, poster, thumbnail, social creative, and image-to-video ideas into structured prompts for AI generation tools. It helps preserve product details, organize scene and composition requirements, and call out practical constraints for generated visuals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompts may include inaccurate brand details, label text, or product claims.

Mitigation: Review prompts and resulting visuals for brand, label, and claim accuracy before commercial use.

Risk: The optional testing site is a separate third-party service.

Mitigation: Evaluate the external site's own terms, privacy practices, and account requirements before uploading product assets.
