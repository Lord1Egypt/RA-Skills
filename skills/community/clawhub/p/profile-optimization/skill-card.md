## Description:

Optimizes social media profiles as conversion landing pages by producing ready-to-apply bio, searchable-field, CTA, link, pinned-content, highlight, and visual specs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, creators, marketers, and business profile owners use this skill to turn profile visits into follows or leads through clearer positioning, platform-specific bio copy, searchable keywords, one CTA, a goal-matched link, and first-impression content specs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad profile or bio wording may route users to this skill when they expected another social media workflow.

Mitigation: Review activation routing and examples before installation so profile optimization requests are routed intentionally.

Risk: Users may assume the skill can directly edit social account profile fields.

Mitigation: Present outputs as ready-to-apply recommendations and require the user to make changes in each social platform.

Risk: Users may treat recommendations as verified performance analytics.

Mitigation: Use native platform insights or the qualitative 3-second clarity check to evaluate results; do not claim WoopSocial analytics validation.
