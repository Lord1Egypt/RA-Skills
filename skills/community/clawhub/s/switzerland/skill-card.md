This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to plan Switzerland trips with practical routing, entry-rule, rail, road, mountain, budget, safety, and regional guidance. It is most useful when a traveler needs an operational itinerary with realistic transfer windows, booking cautions, weather fallback options, and local trip continuity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local trip notes may retain travel dates, budget style, route preferences, constraints, and deadlines in ~/switzerland/memory.md.

Mitigation: Use one-off answers without memory when continuity is not needed, or ask the agent to show, edit, pause, or delete stored trip notes.

Risk: Entry rules, customs limits, transport schedules, mountain weather, avalanche conditions, and road-pass status can change close to travel.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive decisions against the official sources listed by the skill before locking non-refundable bookings or committing to mountain and border plans.

Risk: Travel guidance can be incomplete for emergencies, serious alpine activity, or high-value customs situations.

Mitigation: Use official emergency channels for acute issues and confirm visa, customs, insurance, rescue, and safety exposure with the relevant official source.
