## Description:

Assists Chinese-language thesis writers with drafting, revising, and organizing thesis chapters by combining reference literature and experiment plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaoyu4444](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaoyu4444)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and academic writers use this skill to structure thesis chapters, convert notes and experiment descriptions into academic prose, and refine Chinese thesis drafts. It prompts for missing context before expanding outlines into detailed sections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated thesis text may include inaccurate claims, citation details, or conclusions that do not match the user's actual research data.

Mitigation: Review generated content against source literature, experiment records, and required citation formats before use.

Risk: The skill may infer a supervisor's intended logic from incomplete notes and present plausible but unconfirmed interpretations.

Mitigation: Treat reconstructed thesis logic as draft options and confirm the selected framing with the supervisor or source materials.
