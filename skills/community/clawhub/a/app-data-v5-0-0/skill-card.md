## Use Case:

External learners and exam candidates use this Chinese-language skill to study for the intermediate accounting exam across accounting practice, financial management, and economic law. It provides structured explanations, practice questions, answer templates, accounting entries, formulas, legal-rule summaries, and study planning guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include sensitive personal information when discussing or maintaining an error book of incorrect answers.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive study content unless the host app clearly explains storage, deletion, and retention.

Risk: Accounting, tax, and legal exam-prep content may become outdated or be applied outside its intended study context.

Mitigation: Review outputs against current official exam materials and treat them as study guidance rather than professional accounting or legal advice.
