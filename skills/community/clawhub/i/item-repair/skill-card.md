## Description:

商品精修、去褶皱。随手拍的商品图 → 可直接上架的精修图。当用户说「精修」「去褶皱」「修图」「拍得不好看」「整理一下」时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

E-commerce sellers and product-content teams use this skill to prepare product photos for listings by reducing random wrinkles, improving layout and lighting, and cleaning the background while preserving product details.
