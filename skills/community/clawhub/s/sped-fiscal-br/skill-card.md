## Description:

Provides Portuguese guidance for Brazilian SPED accessory tax obligations, including ECD, ECF, EFD modules, eSocial, DCTFWeb, deadlines, penalties, tax regimes, digital certificates, and withholding.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, accountants, and tax operations teams use this skill to answer questions about Brazilian SPED filings, regimes, deadlines, penalties, layout concepts, certificates, and withholding obligations. It is most relevant when a user needs Portuguese-language orientation before checking current official tax sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Brazil

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Brazilian tax deadlines, penalties, and Receita Federal rules may change after the skill release.

Mitigation: Verify answers against current official Receita Federal and applicable state or municipal sources, or consult a qualified professional before acting.
