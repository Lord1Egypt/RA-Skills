## Description:

根据商品图片和卖点生成带中文文案排版的电商转化主图。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers and e-commerce content creators use this skill to turn a product image and supplied selling points into a Chinese-language listing main image with arranged benefit copy and optional promotion text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images and copy may be sent to an external image provider, and the security scan was only partial (55.6% coverage).

Mitigation: Review the selected provider, the images and copy being sent, and the provider's data handling before use.
