## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial or business facts may become stale because the skill is static reference content.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive Berkshire Hathaway, Greg Abel, market, and investment facts against current authoritative sources before relying on them.

Risk: The source base is limited because Greg Abel has a low public profile and much of the material is secondary or interpretive.

Mitigation: Distinguish direct Abel statements from Buffett, Munger, media, analyst, or other secondary interpretations, and preserve uncertainty in responses.

Risk: Users could mistake investing-style responses for current financial advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as historical and philosophical reference guidance, not personalized or current investment recommendations.
