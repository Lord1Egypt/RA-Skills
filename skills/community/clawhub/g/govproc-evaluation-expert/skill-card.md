## Description:

This Chinese-language agent skill helps users analyze PRC government procurement review questions, including invalid bid assessments, complaint handling, supplier collusion signals, procurement-document review, response-file risk analysis, and legal-basis checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users working on PRC government procurement matters use this skill for structured decision support on review procedure, invalid-bid and project-termination questions, supplier-risk analysis, procurement-document checks, and challenge or complaint workflows. It is scoped to the Government Procurement Law system and asks for missing project context before giving analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

People's Republic of China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on deadline or complaint-process guidance for filings or rights-preservation steps.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as decision support and verify advice against current rules, local practice, and qualified counsel before relying on it for formal action.

Risk: Government procurement guidance may be incomplete when current legal text, local rules, or configured knowledge bases are unavailable or stale.

Mitigation: Confirm the governing procurement documents and applicable jurisdictional rules, and require source-labeled support for legal conclusions.
