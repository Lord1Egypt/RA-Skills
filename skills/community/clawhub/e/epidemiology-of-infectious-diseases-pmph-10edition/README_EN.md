## About

This project systematically integrates core clinical skills in infectious diseases, including viral, bacterial, fungal, and parasitic infections. It covers **80+ key clinical skills** organized into 8 categories.

**Target Audience**: Infectious disease physicians, medical students, public health workers, infection control practitioners, critical care teams

**Reference Textbook**: *Infectious Disease* (10th Edition), People's Medical Publishing House

**⚠️ Risk ⚠️**: This skill covers infectious disease diagnosis, medication dosing, emergency treatment, isolation measures, and public health management, which may be misused as independent medical advice.

**Mitigation**: Use output solely as reference material for educational purposes or clinician review. Always verify recommendations against current official guidelines, local protocols, and qualified infectious disease specialists.

**⚠️ Risk ⚠️**: Source content does not consistently enforce clinician-only safety boundaries.

**Mitigation**: Deploy systemic medical safety policies requiring escalation to qualified clinicians for diagnosis, prescribing, dosing, emergency care, and self-treatment decisions.
