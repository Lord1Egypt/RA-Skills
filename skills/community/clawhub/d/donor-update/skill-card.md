## Description:

Write a warm donor update or stewardship message that makes a supporter feel their gift mattered.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Nonprofit fundraisers, communications staff, and stewardship teams use this skill to draft donor-centered thank-you updates, acknowledgements, newsletters, and concise SMS or social versions that emphasize gratitude, concrete impact, and belonging.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated donor messages may contain placeholder impact figures or example stories.

Mitigation: Review each message before sending and replace all marked examples with verified organization data.

Risk: Stewardship copy can be misused if it implies unverified outcomes or overstates donor impact.

Mitigation: Confirm that impact claims, stories, and donor attribution are accurate before external use.
