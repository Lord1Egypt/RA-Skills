## Use Case:

Media professionals, business users, technology professionals, and local readers use this skill to find and summarize Shenzhen news, policy updates, technology developments, business activity, and local information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shenzhen news, policy, finance, and business information can become outdated or be inaccurate if summarized from a single source.

Mitigation: Verify important policy, finance, or business information against official sources and multiple reputable reports before acting on it.

Risk: Optional recurring searches can repeatedly fetch and summarize news without fresh human review of the schedule or query scope.

Mitigation: Review the scheduled update example before enabling recurring searches, and keep recurring queries limited to the user's intended Shenzhen news topics.
