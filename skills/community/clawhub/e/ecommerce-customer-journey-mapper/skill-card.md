## Description:

Maps ecommerce customer journey stages and outputs friction analysis plus prioritized action briefs using built-in heuristics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce operators, DTC brands, Shopify teams, and consultants use this skill to turn provided touchpoints, reviews, FAQs, support signals, and optional funnel metrics into a journey-stage audit with friction points and prioritized next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations are heuristic and may not reflect live analytics, customer behavior, or account data.

Mitigation: Treat the report as advisory and validate recommendations against first-party analytics, customer research, and business constraints before acting.

Risk: User-provided business notes may include sensitive customer or operational information.

Mitigation: Share only the business notes needed for the audit with the agent environment running the skill.
