## Description:

Generate professional freelancer proposals, scopes of work, and project quotes from a brief description, with deliverables, timelines, pricing, and terms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jamespatrickthom2003-star](https://clawhub.ai/user/jamespatrickthom2003-star)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External freelancers, consultants, and agencies use this skill to turn a project brief into a client-ready proposal, scope of work, quote, or compact project brief. It is intended for business-development workflows where users need polished scope, timeline, pricing, payment terms, and next steps quickly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated proposals may contain pricing, terms, currency, locale, or legal language that does not fit the user's business or region.

Mitigation: Review those sections before sending a proposal to a client and adjust them for the user's business, jurisdiction, and commercial policy.

Risk: Client-ready proposal language may overstate credentials, social proof, scope certainty, or client context if the prompt is incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify claims, deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, and next steps against the actual client brief before use.
