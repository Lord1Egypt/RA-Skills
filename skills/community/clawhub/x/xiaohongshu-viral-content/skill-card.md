## Description:

A Chinese-language copywriting skill that helps generate Xiaohongshu-style viral titles, post copy, topic tags, and publishing-time suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[imgolye](https://clawhub.ai/user/imgolye)

MIT

## Use Case:

External content creators and marketing teams use this skill to draft Xiaohongshu posts, including titles, body copy, hashtags, and suggested publication timing. Users should review generated marketing claims for accuracy, platform compliance, and brand fit before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated posts may include strong viral-marketing claims or generic engagement formulas that do not match the user's product, evidence, or brand voice.

Mitigation: Review and edit all titles, body copy, hashtags, and calls to action for factual accuracy, brand fit, and platform compliance before publishing.

Risk: Suggested publishing-time and engagement guidance may not reflect current Xiaohongshu platform behavior or a specific audience segment.

Mitigation: Treat timing and optimization advice as drafting guidance, then validate it against current account analytics and campaign requirements.
