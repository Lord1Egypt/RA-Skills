## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill records study topics, weak points, review schedules, and cited resources as an ongoing study profile.

Mitigation: Use it only with consent for progress tracking, avoid sensitive personal details, and prefer deployments that let users review, edit, or delete saved study records.

Risk: The skill may use connected knowledge-base or web lookup for user questions without clear consent boundaries.

Mitigation: Confirm before sending question content to external search tools and keep queries limited to the minimum exam context needed.
