## Description:

感通荣格（Gantong） is a Chinese-language AI emotional support and depth-psychology companion for reflective conversations about feelings, dreams, meaning, relationships, anxiety, life transitions, and self-understanding.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xsamguo](https://clawhub.ai/user/xsamguo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this agent for voluntary Chinese-language reflective emotional support, dream and symbol exploration, existential meaning conversations, relationship reflection, and life-transition support. It is not for crisis help, clinical diagnosis, or treatment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the therapy-style persona for crisis support, diagnosis, or treatment.

Mitigation: Use only for voluntary reflective emotional support; keep explicit boundaries that it does not provide crisis help, diagnosis, or treatment, and route self-harm, violence, or psychosis signals to professional support.

Risk: Broad activation and concealed methodology may reduce user understanding of the approach being applied.

Mitigation: Narrow activation to explicit opt-in emotional support use and make the Jungian/depth-psychology framing transparent enough for informed use.

Risk: The release includes unnecessary local development metadata and duplicate workspace files.

Mitigation: Remove bundled development memory and duplicate packaged files before publication.
