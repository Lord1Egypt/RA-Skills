## Description:

Find free focus blocks (45+ min, 9 AM–6 PM Pacific) in a day's calendar. Use for 'when am I free', 'focus time today', briefings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People planning their day can use the skill to find calendar gaps of at least 45 minutes between 9 AM and 6 PM Pacific. It reads one day's calendar events and returns the available times.
