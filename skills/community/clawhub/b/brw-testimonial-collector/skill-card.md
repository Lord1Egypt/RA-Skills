## Description:

Systematically gather and format client testimonials. Use when someone needs social proof, wants to collect feedback, or needs to turn happy clients into public advocates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[brianrwagner](https://clawhub.ai/user/brianrwagner)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users, marketers, consultants, and business owners use this skill to ask clients for testimonials, format the responses for websites or proposals, and maintain a simple testimonial library.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Testimonials may include client-identifiable information or edited claims that require permission.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with clients who agreed to provide testimonials, send edits back for approval, confirm attribution, and protect any testimonial tracking document that contains client-identifiable information.
