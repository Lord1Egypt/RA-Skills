## Description:

Analyzes an individual stock’s short-term price and volume, money flows, trend structure, volatility, drawdown, and support or resistance levels, then produces a reviewable technical-analysis report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Traders use this skill to review one stock’s short-term technical setup, including price and volume behavior, trends, risk measures, and key levels. It provides a structured research report to inform timing decisions; the user makes any trading decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
