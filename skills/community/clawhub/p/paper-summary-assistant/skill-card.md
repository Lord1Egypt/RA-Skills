## Description:

A skill that helps students summarize and understand academic papers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use this skill to turn uploaded academic papers or research articles into structured summaries for study, understanding, and presentation preparation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Academic paper summaries may omit or misstate details if source papers are ambiguous, incomplete, or outside the provided text.

Mitigation: Use outputs as study assistance and verify titles, authors, methods, findings, and limitations against the original paper before relying on them.
