## Description:

Write a structured escalation brief for an at-risk customer account. Use when an account has escalated, when a customer is threatening churn, when a P1 customer issue needs executive attention, or when preparing an internal save play. Produces a crisp escalation brief with account context, timeline, root cause, business impact, and a clear resolution plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Customer success and revenue leaders use this skill to prepare concise escalation briefs for at-risk customer accounts, executive escalations, churn threats, P1 issues, and internal save plays. It structures account context, timeline, root cause, business impact, customer position, resolution plan, decision request, and communication plan.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Escalation briefs may include sensitive customer, revenue, legal, and executive-contact information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in an authorized business context, provide the minimum necessary details, and redact names or account identifiers unless the environment is approved for that data.

Risk: Incomplete or unreviewed inputs can lead to misleading escalation summaries or unclear ownership.

Mitigation: Collect the required account, timeline, root cause, business impact, and resolution-plan inputs, then review the brief before sharing with stakeholders.
