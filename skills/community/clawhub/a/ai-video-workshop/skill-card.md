## Description:

Turns story ideas, scripts, and other text into structured AI video workflows and production deliverables, from screenplay and storyboards through assets, editing, quality checks, and release feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjiaocheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjiaocheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and video production teams use the skill to turn a synopsis, script, ad copy, or other brief into an AI video workflow with scripts, storyboards, asset specifications, production guidance, quality checks, and delivery materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review reports that project content may be sent to external generation services without a clear disclosure or approval step.

Mitigation: Review the generation path and remove sensitive material or obtain approval before sending project content to an external service.

Risk: The skill directs project learnings to be written back into persistent skill guidance and project materials.

Mitigation: Review proposed persistent updates and approve them before applying changes.

Risk: The security scan was partial, with one static analysis degraded.

Mitigation: Review the skill's update and external-service practices and complete a fuller security review before deployment.
