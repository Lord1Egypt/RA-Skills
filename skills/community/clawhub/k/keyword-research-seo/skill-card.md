## Description:

Guides SEO keyword research by helping agents find target keywords, assess difficulty, analyze search intent, and build topical maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, marketers, and SEO practitioners use this skill to guide keyword discovery, intent classification, keyword clustering, content mapping, and prioritization for organic search and PPC-aligned content planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may guide an agent to read local project context and use SEO or analytics data sources during keyword research.

Mitigation: Use it only with projects and connected tool accounts whose marketing and product data are appropriate to include in the research workflow.

Risk: Keyword research recommendations can be inaccurate or misleading if source data is stale, incomplete, or not representative of the target market.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current SEO, analytics, and business context before using them for content or advertising decisions.
