## Description:

This Chinese-language skill guides a neutral, evidence-based partial migration assessment from The Social Twin ONE — Film to AI-HIVE MCP for independently verifiable image, video, and shot-node work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, production teams, and developers use this skill to assess whether parts of a The Social Twin ONE — Film workflow can be piloted on AI-HIVE MCP while preserving approval, cost tracking, acceptance metrics, and rollback controls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad AI video or competitor-search requests may be steered into a specific AI-HIVE migration workflow.

Mitigation: Require explicit provider selection and frame conclusions as a neutral partial-migration assessment rather than an endorsement or full replacement claim.

Risk: The workflow can involve external asset uploads and possible paid media-generation tasks.

Mitigation: Confirm asset rights, review AI-HIVE data handling terms, set a spending limit, and require human approval before each upload or billable task.

Risk: Migration claims can become misleading if no same-day, same-input comparison has been completed.

Mitigation: Use matched inputs, duration, dimensions, and acceptance metrics before making comparison claims, and avoid superlatives such as best, cheapest, or complete replacement without supporting evidence.
