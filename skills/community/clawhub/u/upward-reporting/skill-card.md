## Description:

Upward Reporting routes Chinese workplace reporting requests to focused report-writing workflows and provides shared style, audience adaptation, oral presentation, and technique guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sopaco](https://clawhub.ai/user/sopaco)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and workplace writers use this skill to identify the right reporting scenario and draft Chinese-language planning reports, incident postmortems, performance reviews, kickoff alignment documents, OKRs, and oral-reporting preparation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad requests such as writing a document may route into this workplace reporting workflow when the user intended a different writing task.

Mitigation: Confirm the reporting scenario, audience, and occasion before drafting when the request is ambiguous.

Risk: The artifact is primarily Chinese-language guidance, which may not fit users expecting another language.

Mitigation: Ask for the desired output language when the user's language preference is unclear.
