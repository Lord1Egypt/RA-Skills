## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may submit sensitive company information or contact details in chat instead of through the provider page.

Mitigation: Do not request or retain company records, contact details, financial tables, intellectual property materials, or application originals in chat; direct users to review the external page and submit information only voluntarily there.

Risk: Users may mistake the self-test for an official government determination or a guarantee of approval.

Mitigation: State that the self-test is not a government recognition result and does not guarantee application approval, subsidies, timelines, pricing, or service outcomes.

Risk: The skill sends users to an external provider page.

Mitigation: Tell users to review the external page before voluntarily submitting contact information for a full report or consultation.
