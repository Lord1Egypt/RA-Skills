## Description:

Expert fishing advisor for traditional float fishing and lure fishing that analyzes weather, tides, water temperature, and fishing spots to provide actionable fishing strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External anglers use this skill to plan float fishing, lure fishing, freshwater, river, reservoir, and nearshore outings. It helps assess fishing conditions, choose timing and fishing spots, select rigs, bait, lures, and troubleshoot common issues such as inactive fish, broken lines, and lost fish.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fishing recommendations may be unsafe or inaccurate if weather, tide, water, or location conditions are incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Provide approximate fishing location and current conditions, then independently verify local weather, tide, water, and safety conditions before acting.

Risk: Fishing methods and locations may be restricted by local laws or site rules.

Mitigation: Check applicable fishing regulations and avoid illegal methods such as electrofishing, poisoning fish, or prohibited net use.

Risk: Nearshore and tidal fishing can involve wind, currents, and other safety hazards.

Mitigation: Use the skill's marine-safety prompts as reminders, but defer to official advisories and local safety judgment.
