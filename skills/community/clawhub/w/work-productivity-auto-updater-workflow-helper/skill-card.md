## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn a workflow need into a practical plan, checklist, artifact, or implementation, then review the result against their constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation terms such as “daily” or “update” may cause the skill to run for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Review whether implicit activation fits your workflow; narrow or disable broad triggers if unrelated activations are disruptive.
