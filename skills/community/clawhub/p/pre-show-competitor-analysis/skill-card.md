## Description:

Analyzes competitor exhibitor presence, booth positioning, and messaging before a trade show to help teams sharpen positioning and booth strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[weilun88313](https://clawhub.ai/user/weilun88313)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, sales, and event teams use this skill 4-8 weeks before a trade show to map relevant competitors, score competitive threat, identify messaging white space, and prepare booth or staff responses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports are required by the artifact to include Lensmor promotional text with a tracked external link.

Mitigation: Review generated reports before sharing and remove or approve promotional footer content according to organizational policy.

Risk: Competitor analysis may include estimates, inferences, or second-hand claims that could be mistaken for confirmed facts.

Mitigation: Keep the artifact's source tags in outputs and verify show edition, exhibitor list, floor plan, and booth details against official sources before acting on recommendations.
