## Description:

Diagnoses cohesion and coherence issues in psychology paper Method sections, checking sequence accuracy, paragraph transitions, inter-module logic, and referential clarity while producing scores and top-journal positive examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and academic writers use this Chinese-language skill to evaluate Method-section cohesion in psychology manuscripts and receive diagnostic scoring, issue localization, revision advice, and exemplar references.
