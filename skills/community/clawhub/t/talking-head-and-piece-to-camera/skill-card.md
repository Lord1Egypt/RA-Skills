## Description:

This skill coaches a human creator through phone-first talking-head video delivery, including setup, beat-map or teleprompter preparation, retake discipline, batch filming, and routing to editing or publishing workflows while leaving filming and take selection to the human.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, founders, and social media operators use this skill to prepare and film authentic pieces to camera from existing scripts. It helps an agent provide setup guidance, delivery coaching, beat maps, retake rules, batch-day plans, and self-review checklists without judging footage it cannot inspect.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The agent may be asked to judge or praise a recorded take it cannot actually see.

Mitigation: State that the agent cannot inspect footage, provide a self-review checklist, and leave take selection to the human.

Risk: Publishing or filming decisions could proceed without human approval or consent from people appearing in the footage.

Mitigation: Require the human to pick the take, confirm anyone visible or audible has consented, and route publishing through the separate scheduling or WoopSocial workflow only after approval.

Risk: Camera-shy users could be pressured into appearing on camera or steered toward unnecessary gear purchases.

Mitigation: Treat not filming as valid, offer disclosed avatar or faceless routes, and recommend gear upgrades only when a named filming limit requires them.

Risk: Some current production claims, such as phone capabilities, platform safe zones, or disclosure expectations for AI enhancements, may change over time.

Mitigation: Treat time-sensitive filming and platform details as directional guidance and verify current platform rules before publishing.
