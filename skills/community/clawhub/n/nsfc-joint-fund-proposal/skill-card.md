## Description:

国家自然科学基金联合基金申报书智能撰写助手。依据官方指南编制模板，帮助科研人员逐模块生成高质量申报内容，涵盖基本信息填写、指南说明、主要研究内容、科学意义与背景、研究目标与关键科学问题、创新点、预期成果及湖北应用前景等全部核心板块。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External researchers and grant writers use this skill to draft, revise, and check Chinese-language NSFC Hubei Joint Fund proposal sections against the provided proposal structure and word-count expectations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated proposal claims, novelty statements, funding history, or contact details may be inaccurate or incomplete.

Mitigation: Treat generated content as draft material and verify all factual, scientific, funding, and personal-contact details before submission.

Risk: Grant proposal language may overstate originality, feasibility, or regional application value.

Mitigation: Have domain experts review the final proposal for evidence support, scientific accuracy, and compliance with the target fund requirements.
