## Description:

智能伴游AI旅游搭子，提供沉浸式景点与文物讲解服务，当用户要求讲解景点、介绍文物、提供旅游信息、推荐游览路线时使用，并支持当年明月幽默风格与孔二狗江湖风格。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External travelers and cultural tourism users use this skill to receive Chinese-language scenic spot, artifact, history, poetry, and route narration with a chosen storytelling style. It is best suited for cultural explanation and itinerary context rather than booking, emergency, visa, or non-Chinese travel assistance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad travel requests even when the user needs booking, emergency, visa, or other operational travel support.

Mitigation: Use it for cultural narration and attraction context, and route booking, emergency, visa, medical, or legal travel needs to dedicated authoritative services.

Risk: Current attraction details such as opening hours, ticket prices, activities, traffic, and weather can change.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive travel details against current authoritative sources before relying on them.

Risk: Mandatory Chinese formatting and storytelling style rules may be unsuitable for non-Chinese or strictly factual travel assistance.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired language, tone, and output format before use, or choose a more neutral travel assistant when concise factual output is required.
