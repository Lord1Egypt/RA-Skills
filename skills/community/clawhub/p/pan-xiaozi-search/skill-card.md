## Description:

Search cloud drive resources (Quark, Baidu, Aliyun, Xunlei) indexed on pan.xiaozi.cc. Use when users want to find shared files, movies, TV shows, software, or documents across Chinese cloud storage platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search results may include third-party cloud-drive links that are unsafe, expired, misleading, or legally restricted.

Mitigation: Verify domains before opening links, do not enter account credentials on unfamiliar pages, and use results only for lawful content.

Risk: The indexed resources are third-party public shares and may involve copyright restrictions or unavailable content.

Mitigation: Treat results as discovery pointers, preserve the source links, and remind users to follow applicable laws and platform terms.
