## Description:

Funding provides fundraising and investment guidance for startups, nonprofits, and individuals preparing capital-raising campaigns, pitches, donor outreach, and closing materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticio](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticio)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users raising capital use this skill to choose an appropriate fundraising path, frame investor or donor psychology, prepare materials such as pitch decks and executive summaries, and plan follow-up through closing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation language may apply fundraising guidance to adjacent money topics such as budgeting, debt, income, or general personal finance.

Mitigation: Clarify whether the user is raising capital from investors, donors, crowdfunding backers, or another fundraising audience before applying investor or donor guidance.

Risk: Fundraising, term-sheet, and financial-model guidance may be mistaken for professional legal, securities, tax, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Have qualified legal and financial professionals review term sheets, securities compliance, financial projections, and binding commitments before use.
