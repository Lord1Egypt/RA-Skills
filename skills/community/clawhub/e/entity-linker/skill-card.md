## Description: <br>
Entity Linker is a Discord channel plugin for OpenClaw that rewrites Entity workspace paths into hosted Entity URLs in outgoing messages. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[h-mascot](https://clawhub.ai/user/h-mascot) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and workspace operators use this skill to keep Discord-bound OpenClaw messages readable while preserving clickable access to referenced Entity workspace files. It is intended for environments where generated hosted links are appropriate for the channel audience. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>
