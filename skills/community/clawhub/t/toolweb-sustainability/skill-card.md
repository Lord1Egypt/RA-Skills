## Description:

AI-powered platform that generates personalized career roadmaps for entry-level sustainability professionals based on skills assessment and goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd](https://clawhub.ai/user/krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to generate personalized sustainability career roadmaps, compare specialization paths, and retrieve learning path guidance from an API-backed service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career assessment details, goals, skills, experience, session identifiers, timestamps, and optional user IDs are sent to an external API.

Mitigation: Submit only information needed for roadmap generation and avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information until the publisher provides clearer privacy and retention terms.
