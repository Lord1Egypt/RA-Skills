## Description:

Audit a game, feature, level sequence, combat loop, progression curve, onboarding path, event structure, or return-player journey through the lens of Flow theory.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and developers use this skill to audit gameplay journeys, onboarding, difficulty progression, events, or return-player paths for challenge-skill alignment, pacing drift, boredom, anxiety, and flow breakpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The audit can produce misleading recommendations when the input lacks gameplay details, player segment, timeline, or playtest observations.

Mitigation: State assumptions, tie conclusions to supplied evidence, and treat unsupported findings as hypotheses for designer review.

Risk: Direct design critique may influence production decisions if accepted without human review.

Mitigation: Have a responsible designer review proposed changes against product goals, player research, and implementation constraints before adoption.
