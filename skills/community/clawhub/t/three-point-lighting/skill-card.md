## Description:

Guides an agent in indoor or studio artificial-light photography setup using key, fill, and background lights, with light-ratio choices for portrait mood control.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, photographers, and photography-learning agents use this skill to explain or plan indoor and studio portrait lighting setups with artificial lights. It helps choose key, fill, and background light placement and select a light ratio such as 1:1, 4:1, or 9:1.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lighting and electrical-safety guidance may not match a user's specific equipment ratings or studio conditions.

Mitigation: Review the proposed setup against the user's lights, modifiers, power limits, and manufacturer safety guidance before use.

Risk: The skill's lighting ratios and setup sequence may be treated as universal even when the creative goal or scene calls for a different approach.

Mitigation: Use the recommendations as a starting point and adjust key, fill, and background lights after observing test results.
