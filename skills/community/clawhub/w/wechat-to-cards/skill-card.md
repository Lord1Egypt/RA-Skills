## Description:

Adapt a reviewed WeChat article or publish package into WeChat-specific inline-card requirements, Moments copy, community share text, or archive assets while reusing the shared long-to-cards and cards-to-images pipeline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, content teams, and publishing assistants use this skill to adapt reviewed WeChat articles or publish packages into inline-card requirements, Moments copy, community share text, and archive card assets while preserving approved article claims and metadata.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated card claims, copy, or visuals may be inaccurate or misleading if source claims are not checked before publishing.

Mitigation: Review generated claims and visuals before publishing, and keep human confirmation visible before WeChat upload.

Risk: Private drafts, internal notes, or unpublished context could be incorporated into reader-facing card assets if provided as input.

Mitigation: Avoid providing private drafts or internal notes unless they are intentionally meant to be used in the generated package.
