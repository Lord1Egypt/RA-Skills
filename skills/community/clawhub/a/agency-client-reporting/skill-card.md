## Description:

Generates tailored agency client reports, QBR narratives, health scorecards, and renewal proposals to prove ROI, retain clients, and grow accounts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitflopez](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitflopez)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agency owners, account managers, consultants, and managed service providers use this skill to draft client-facing reporting materials, internal health scorecards, QBR narratives, and renewal or upsell proposals from user-provided account data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Client-facing reports, ROI summaries, renewal options, and upsell proposals may overstate performance or frame pricing in a way that feels misleading if generated text is used without review.

Mitigation: Review generated materials against source metrics, contract terms, and client context before sharing; make ROI assumptions, pricing comparisons, and renewal urgency transparent.

Risk: The skill depends on user-provided account and performance data, so incomplete or inaccurate inputs can produce inaccurate recommendations or health scores.

Mitigation: Validate KPI inputs, budget figures, renewal dates, and account health signals before using the generated report, scorecard, or proposal for business decisions.
