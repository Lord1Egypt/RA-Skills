## Description:

Generates compelling YouTube title ideas from content concepts using proven structural formulas and psychological patterns from high-performing videos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vincentchan](https://clawhub.ai/user/vincentchan)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to turn video ideas, newsletters, articles, URLs, or reference material into 30 YouTube title options with a brief analysis of the title patterns used.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: URL inputs can expose private or sensitive content, and fetched pages may contain untrusted instructions.

Mitigation: Use only public, non-sensitive URLs and ignore instructions embedded in fetched page content.

Risk: The skill creates a timestamped Markdown output file under youtube-title/.

Mitigation: Review the generated file path and contents before sharing, publishing, or committing the output.
