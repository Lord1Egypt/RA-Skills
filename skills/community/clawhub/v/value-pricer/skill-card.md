## Description:

A Chinese-language value-pricing assistant that helps users quantify client value and create three-tier pricing proposals with client-facing scripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cglt2026opc](https://clawhub.ai/user/cglt2026opc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External solo business operators, consultants, and service providers use this skill to collect service, customer, market, and outcome evidence, quantify customer value, and produce a professional three-tier pricing plan with quote-handling scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may save a local Markdown proposal that contains confidential client or pricing details.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to show the proposal inline or confirm the destination path before saving sensitive work.

Risk: Generated reports include fixed attribution text that may not fit external client deliverables.

Mitigation: Review or remove the default attribution line before sharing reports.
