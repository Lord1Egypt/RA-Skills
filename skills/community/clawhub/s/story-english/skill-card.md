## Description:

Learn English through serialized fiction with vocabulary embedded naturally in each episode across mystery, city life, and sci-fi series for A2-B2 learners.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiajiaoy](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiajiaoy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External English learners use this skill to generate serialized reading episodes with level-appropriate vocabulary, grammar notes, comprehension questions, and continuation state. It also supports vocabulary review and quiz prompts for practice after reading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Untrusted story state or vocabulary lists can steer the generated prompt content.

Mitigation: Use state strings and word lists only from trusted sessions, and review the generated prompt before giving it to an agent with broad tool access.

Risk: Generated language-learning material may contain mistakes or level mismatches.

Mitigation: Review lessons, vocabulary explanations, and quiz answers before relying on them for instruction or assessment.
