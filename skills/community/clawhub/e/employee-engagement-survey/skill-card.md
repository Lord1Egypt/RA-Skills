## Description:

Designs employee engagement, pulse, and eNPS surveys and helps analyze results with questions, rating scales, action planning, and communication templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People teams, managers, and operators use this skill to draft employee engagement, pulse, onboarding, exit, or eNPS surveys and to analyze survey results into themes, scores, action plans, and employee communications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Survey result inputs may contain personally identifiable employee responses.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting identifiable responses unless the agent is intended to process that data; use aggregate tables or summary statistics where possible.

Risk: Small-team segmentation can compromise anonymity when anonymous reporting is promised.

Mitigation: Suppress or combine segments below the skill's stated threshold of five responses.

Risk: Survey outputs can reduce trust if results are collected without follow-up communication and action planning.

Mitigation: Include the communication-back date and action planning template that the skill requires.
