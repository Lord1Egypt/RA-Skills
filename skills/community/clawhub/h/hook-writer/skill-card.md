## Description:

Hook Writer helps agents write truthful opening hooks for social content, including caption first lines, short-video openings, carousel covers, thread openers, YouTube title and thumbnail concepts, and email subject or preview lines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and social teams use this skill to turn existing content into format-aware hook options that fit the intended social channel. The skill is best suited for drafting and selecting attention-earning openings while preserving brand voice and avoiding unsupported claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated hooks may overstate, omit context, or create unsupported expectations if source content is thin or sensitive.

Mitigation: Review the selected hook against the underlying content, brand voice, and any compliance requirements before publishing.

Risk: The skill may read local brand-profile.md and voice.md files when available.

Mitigation: Only provide brand and voice files that are appropriate for the agent to use in copywriting tasks.
