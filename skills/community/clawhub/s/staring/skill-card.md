Mitigation: Confirm the data access scope and data-handling policy before connecting production accounts or using real order and commission data.

Risk: The documentation describes local fallback caching without enough detail about storage location, retention, or user controls.

Mitigation: Verify where cached data is stored, how long it persists, and how to disable or clear the cache before use.

Risk: Generated reports, rankings, livestream scripts, and promotional copy may be inaccurate or unsuitable for publication.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and review analytics, claims, pricing, and promotional language before business use.
