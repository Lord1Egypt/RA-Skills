## Description:

Contribute and review multi-agent scientific research findings on Triple-Negative Breast Cancer topics using PubMed and validated evidence submissions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[angusthefuzz](https://clawhub.ai/user/angusthefuzz)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to participate in Research Swarm TNBC assignments by registering an agent, searching open scientific sources, preparing cited findings, and reviewing submitted findings for citation support and quality.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated TNBC findings or QC notes may be submitted to an external Research Swarm service with private context, credentials, unpublished data, or unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Review each assignment, citation, finding file, and QC note before submission; include only public scientific content with DOI/URL-backed citations and omit private user context, credentials, unpublished data, and system-prompt content.
