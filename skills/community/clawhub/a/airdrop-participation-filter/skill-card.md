## Description:

A decision helper that helps users decide whether to participate in an airdrop campaign.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to evaluate whether a crypto airdrop campaign is worth their time and risk before participating.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on incomplete, promotional, or outdated airdrop details.

Mitigation: Independently verify campaign details and avoid treating speculative token value as guaranteed.

Risk: Airdrop campaigns may request wallet connections, deposits, or sensitive personal data.

Mitigation: Treat wallet connection, funds deposits, and significant personal-data requests as red flags unless the user trusts the campaign and understands the risks.

Risk: The skill cannot inspect wallets, accounts, local files, or external services to validate claims.

Mitigation: Use the output as advisory guidance based on supplied campaign details, then verify critical facts through trusted independent sources.
