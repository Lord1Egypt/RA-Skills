## Description:

Write a standout rental application / cover letter to a landlord or letting agent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External renters and applicants use this skill to draft a concise, professional rental application letter, one-line renter summary, and document checklist. It helps present reliability signals such as income stability, rental history, references, pets, and move-in timing without unnecessary oversharing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for personal rental details, including employment, income stability, references, pets, and move-in timing.

Mitigation: Provide only details needed for the application, avoid exact salary or bank details unless required, and review the final letter before sending.

Risk: A generated application could include unsupported claims about rental history, references, or finances if the user does not provide complete details.

Mitigation: Keep missing specifics bracketed for confirmation and replace them only with accurate user-provided information.
