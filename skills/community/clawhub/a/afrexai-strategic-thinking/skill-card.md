## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be used for financial, legal, hiring, or other consequential decisions where poor advice could materially affect people or organizations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory, verify assumptions and evidence, and route consequential decisions through appropriate human, legal, financial, or domain review.

Risk: Broad activation phrases may cause the skill to shape common decision-making prompts even when a lighter response would be sufficient.

Mitigation: Use it when structured analysis is desired and ask the agent to limit scope or skip the framework when a concise answer is more appropriate.
