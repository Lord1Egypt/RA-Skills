## Description:

This Chinese-language skill guides an agent through structured industry value assessment using market space, strategic value, value-chain position, capital activity, competitive landscape, and regional fit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Government industry-planning teams, regional economic-development teams, consultants, and investment researchers use this skill to assess whether a target industry or set of industries has enough market, strategic, economic, innovation, investment-attraction, driving, and regional-fit value to justify focused cultivation or resource allocation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; the skill content and expected report output are in Chinese.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public market, policy, financing, and company data can be outdated, incomplete, or weakly sourced.

Mitigation: Verify cited policies, market reports, investment events, and company disclosures against authoritative sources before using the report for real planning or investment decisions.

Risk: The skill produces advisory scoring and prioritization that could be mistaken for a final resource-allocation decision.

Mitigation: Treat the scoring matrix as decision support and require human review against local constraints, budget, policy priorities, and implementation capacity.
