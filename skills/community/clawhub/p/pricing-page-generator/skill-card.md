## Description:

Guides agents in creating, optimizing, and auditing pricing page content, structure, and conversion elements for SaaS and related business models.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, growth, product, and web teams use this skill to draft or audit pricing pages, including plan structure, enterprise/contact-sales treatment, usage-based pricing, FAQs, CTAs, objection handling, and SEO metadata.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad pricing or subscription wording and produce pricing-page recommendations when the user intended pricing strategy or billing implementation work.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants pricing page content or audit guidance; route base pricing strategy to pricing-strategy and implementation work to the relevant billing or product workflow.

Risk: The skill may read local project-context files when present to tailor output.

Mitigation: Review project-context content for sensitive product, audience, or pricing assumptions before using the resulting recommendations in public-facing drafts.
