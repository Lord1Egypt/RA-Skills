## Description:

Unified whatifthen framework for early-stage product thinking, requirement clarification, and decision review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhang3xing1](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhang3xing1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, product managers, and teams use Wit to clarify vague product ideas, feature requests, process changes, or strategic choices before committing to planning or execution. It guides the conversation from problem validation through scope reduction, non-goals, and a concrete next step.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may slow planning-style conversations when the user already wants implementation or a domain-specific review.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to skip Wit and proceed directly to implementation, review, or the relevant domain workflow when the decision target is already clear.

Risk: The framework can over-constrain exploratory discussions if its structured synthesis is treated as final evidence.

Mitigation: Review the synthesis with the user and confirm observations, assumptions, and the decision state before acting on it.
