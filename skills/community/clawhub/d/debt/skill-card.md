## Description:

Debt helps agents organize a user's obligations into a complete debt map, compare payoff strategies, track payments, draft creditor negotiation scripts, and explain credit-score tradeoffs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ethagent](https://clawhub.ai/user/ethagent)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for personal debt planning, including debt inventory, payoff strategy comparison, payment tracking, creditor negotiation preparation, and credit-score impact review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the skill may involve sharing detailed debt, spending, and credit-related information with the agent.

Mitigation: Share only the financial details needed for planning, and do not provide account credentials or full payment-card numbers.

Risk: Creditor negotiation, payoff, or credit-score recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for a user's financial situation.

Mitigation: Treat the output as planning assistance and consult a qualified financial or credit professional before making major decisions.
