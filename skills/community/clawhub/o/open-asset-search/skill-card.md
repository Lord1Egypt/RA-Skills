## Description:

Searches WebSim's public asset library for images, audio, music, sound effects, videos, and 3D models, returning direct download URLs without authentication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[upintheairsheep](https://clawhub.ai/user/upintheairsheep)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers, designers, and agents use this skill to find reusable public media assets for games, prototypes, presentations, and creative projects. It helps search by descriptive terms, filter by media type, and present filenames, MIME types, relevance scores, and direct asset URLs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms are sent to WebSim.

Mitigation: Avoid entering secrets, private project names, customer data, or other sensitive terms in asset searches.

Risk: Results are direct links to community-uploaded public files.

Mitigation: Review downloaded assets before using them in a project or sharing them with others.
