## Description:

Processize helps users turn a product idea into a manual service delivery workflow they can start before writing code.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Entrepreneurs, founders, and product builders use this skill to convert an early product idea into a manually delivered service workflow, including the customer input, ordered steps, delivery artifact, starter pricing, and first automation candidate.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording could activate the coaching workflow when the user only loosely mentions starting or processizing a product idea.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants manual-delivery startup process guidance before applying the workflow.

Risk: Business guidance about pricing, customer outreach, and manual delivery may not fit every market, contract, or regulated context.

Mitigation: Treat the output as planning guidance and have the user validate pricing and outreach choices against their own business, legal, and customer constraints.
