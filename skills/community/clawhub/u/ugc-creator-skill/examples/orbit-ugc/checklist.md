# Campaign checklist: Example Orbit: split-screen UGC

Campaign id `example-orbit-app`, brand Example Orbit, an example marketplace (made up). Brief dated 2026-11-17, checklist version `eae2ccd694` (changes when the campaign file or the brief changes).

**EXAMPLE ONLY: a made-up demo campaign.** Its videos show the real "Ad" label in the corner with an "EXAMPLE: not a real ad" label opposite it, and a labelled placeholder where your recording goes. A real campaign can't use this.

## Verdict: GO

Conditions:

- tone notes ("keep it natural", "a regular post, not an ad") are followed as style: a relaxed, unscripted read. The disclosure stays in every video
- at most 3 submissions for this campaign

## Checklist

1. **Platforms and rates:** TikTok $2.00 per 1,000 views, Instagram Reels $2.00 per 1,000 views, YouTube Shorts $1.00 per 1,000 views.
2. **Payout:** minimum payout per video $5.00; cap per video $200.00; marketplace fee 10%; total budget $5,000.00; $3,820.00 left when checked 18 Nov 13:30 UTC. When the budget runs out, views stop paying.
3. **Pays from:** TikTok 2,500, Instagram Reels 2,500, YouTube Shorts 5,000 views per video; under that a video earns nothing.
4. **Format:** split screen (the brand's footage and your recording, one above the other); or face plus screen recording. Effort: split screen low: one selfie take under the supplied footage; face screen medium: a selfie take and a screen recording that match.
5. **Face:** your face on camera is required.
6. **Supplied footage:** orbit-footage.mp4, top half of the split screen; your recording in the other half. Nothing else from the brand's side.
7. **Talking points:** `tp1` "Example Orbit shows a new space photo every day"; `tp2` "You can save any photo as a phone wallpaper in two taps"; `tp3` "The app is free to download; there is an optional paid plan". Every brand claim in the script traces to one of these. One angle per video reads better than every point at once.
8. **Never say:** "free forever", "best app ever", "official NASA app". Plus no claim about use or results you didn't have.
9. **Call to action:** "Download Example Orbit, link in bio." (said in the video).
10. **Every caption:** starts with **#ad**; hashtags: #exampleorbit; mentions: @example.orbit.
11. **Disclosure:** said out loud, ending by second 10, anywhere in that window (a short phrase works: "Example Orbit is paying me to show you this", or a hook that opens with "Ad, but ..."); "Ad" on screen from the first frame for 3 seconds; first in the caption; the platform's paid-partnership setting.
12. **Length:** 15 to 60 seconds, vertical 9:16.
13. **Language:** en.
14. **Audience:** at least 40% of viewers in the US, the UK, Canada, Australia. Post from accounts whose audience is there; never a VPN or location trick.
15. **Music:** only music you hold a license for. The brand gets a license to your video for ads, so trending sounds are a liability.
16. **Accounts:** post only from your own accounts, connected and verified in an example marketplace (made up).
17. **Submitting:** within 30 minutes of posting; one post goes into one campaign, once; at most 3 submissions.
18. **Time live:** public and unchanged for 30 days; views count for 30 days from the day a video crosses the payout threshold.
19. **Approval:** no approval step before posting.
20. **Deadline:** 2026-12-15 (ends 16 Dec 04:59 UTC; the day is read in America/New_York).
21. **Usage rights:** not stated in the brief. On most marketplaces an approved video gives the brand a license to run it, with your face and voice, in its own ads, often worldwide and with no end date. Ask if it matters to you.
22. **Proof to keep:** analytics screenshot with audience countries at 72 hours. Screenshots at 24 hours, 72 hours and 7 days per post either way.

At 10,000 views one video earns, after the fee: TikTok $18.00, Instagram Reels $18.00, YouTube Shorts $9.00. Most videos get fewer views; the campaign's own count decides. Nothing here promises income.

Your floor: $15.00 an hour at 1.5 hours per video means about $22.50 per video. Views needed per video, after the fee: TikTok 12,500, Instagram Reels 12,500, YouTube Shorts 25,000.

## Tone notes: followed as style, the disclosure stays

These ask for a natural, unscripted feel. The script reads relaxed and the edit isn't glossy, and every video still says it's an ad, shows the on-screen label, starts the caption with the disclosure and sets the platform's paid-partnership setting.

- "Keep it natural, it shouldn't feel like an ad."
