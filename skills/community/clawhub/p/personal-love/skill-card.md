## Description:

Acts as the user's relationship and dating assistant in the Love topic, tracking dating life, relationship notes, advice, and important dates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

OpenClaw users use this skill for relationship and dating support, including remembering relationship context, tracking important dates, reflecting on situations, and generating date or gift ideas.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may save sensitive romantic or relationship details in a local memory file.

Mitigation: Review or clear knowledge/personal/love.md regularly and avoid sharing details that should not be persisted.

Risk: Broad relationship trigger terms may activate the skill in conversations where persistent memory is not expected.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit user confirmation before saving sensitive relationship facts.
