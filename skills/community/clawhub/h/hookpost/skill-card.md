## Description:

Draft, schedule, publish, and manage social media posts across supported networks through the user's Hookpost workspace.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jatinder14](https://clawhub.ai/user/jatinder14)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent to draft, schedule, publish, or manage posts for their connected social accounts through a Hookpost workspace. The agent can also help connect accounts, attach media, review scheduled posts, and summarize analytics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scheduling or publishing sends content to real social accounts.

Mitigation: Save drafts unless the user clearly requests scheduling or publishing; before either action, show the exact post text, channels, and time and wait for the user's confirmation.
