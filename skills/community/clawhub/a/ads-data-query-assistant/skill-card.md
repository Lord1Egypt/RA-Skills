Run natural-language data query workflows for Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Ads, TikTok Ads, YouTube Ads, Amazon Ads, and Shopify Ads reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketers, analysts, and growth teams use this skill to translate ads performance questions into precise metric pulls, decision-ready summaries, action plans, risk guardrails, and handoff payloads for campaign work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ads recommendations may be applied as budget, bidding, launch, or campaign changes without sufficient review.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before applying them in ad platforms, especially when another tool consumes the handoff payload.

Risk: Missing KPIs, low data quality, or unclear platform context can produce misleading campaign guidance.

Mitigation: Request missing inputs, mark assumptions explicitly, and use conservative recommendations when evidence is incomplete.
