## Description:

Provides Chinese-language lookup, question answering, and compliance precheck guidance for the PRC Ecological Environment Code using packaged legal articles and expert commentary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whichonewang-eng](https://clawhub.ai/user/whichonewang-eng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Legal, EHS, compliance, and policy users use this skill to retrieve PRC Ecological Environment Code articles, inspect the code structure, review transitions from abolished laws, and generate preliminary environmental compliance checklists. Outputs are legal-reference aids and should be checked against official sources and qualified legal counsel before business or regulatory decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, for PRC ecological-environment law reference use

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on compliance precheck output for business or regulatory decisions without legal review.

Mitigation: Verify results against official sources and qualified legal counsel before making compliance, filing, enforcement, or business decisions.

Risk: Packaged legal articles and commentary may become outdated or differ from current official interpretations.

Mitigation: Check current official law, regulations, and regulator guidance before using the output in formal work.

Risk: Expert commentary is useful context but does not have legal force.

Mitigation: Use commentary as background only and cite authoritative legal text in formal documents.
