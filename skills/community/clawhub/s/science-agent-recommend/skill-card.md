## Description:

Get personalized AI agent recommendations for a research domain, task, or workflow from curated Claw4Science projects and skill hubs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[johnnychen1113](https://clawhub.ai/user/johnnychen1113)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, lab staff, and agents use this skill to map a research domain or workflow need to a short list of relevant AI research tools, project listings, and skill hubs. It is a discovery aid and does not install, execute, or independently evaluate the recommended tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommended stars, project metadata, or installation details may be stale.

Mitigation: Independently review each suggested project's current repository, documentation, and installation instructions before using it.

Risk: Recommendations are drawn mainly from Claw4Science and may not cover every suitable research tool.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a discovery starting point and compare suggestions against current domain requirements and alternatives.
