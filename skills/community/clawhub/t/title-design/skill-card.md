## Description:

Provides nine broadcast-informed video title templates and presets for lower thirds, show opens, and stylized title reveals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video editors, motion designers, and agents can use these presets and guidelines to create readable lower thirds, show opens, chapter cards, and stylized text reveals. The skill is not intended for subtitles or logo animation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Running package commands such as npx hyperframes can execute downloaded code.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed exact package version installed in the project with a lockfile.
