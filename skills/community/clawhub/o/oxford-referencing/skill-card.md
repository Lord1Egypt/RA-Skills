## Description:

Oxford documentary-note referencing: footnote citations, shortened notes, bibliography, OSCOLA pointer. Use for papers requiring Oxford style with footnotes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students, writers, and researchers use this skill to format Oxford documentary-note citations with footnotes, shortened subsequent notes, and a bibliography, while recognizing when UK legal writing should use OSCOLA instead.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Citation output may not match a user's institution-specific Oxford style guide.

Mitigation: Check generated citations against the relevant institutional or publisher guide before submission.

Risk: UK legal writing may require OSCOLA rather than general Oxford documentary-note style.

Mitigation: Use OSCOLA for case law and statutes instead of forcing those sources into Oxford general style.

Risk: Use of ibid. can become incorrect when notes are inserted or reordered during editing.

Mitigation: Prefer shortened notes in editable drafts and recheck footnote numbering before final delivery.
