## Description:

Research high-performing posts across social platforms with the okdata API, then summarize their hooks, formats, and angles with examples and links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Creators and content strategists use it to find high-performing examples for a topic and develop content ideas grounded in platform posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requires an OKDATA_API_KEY, and security guidance advises considering whether you trust okdata before providing it.

Mitigation: Configure the key only for a service you trust and keep it private.

Risk: Security evidence flags a Weibo trends request as potentially mismatched with the stated content-research purpose; review coverage was partial.

Mitigation: Check that the requested platform and endpoint match the research goal, and review unresolved references before use.
