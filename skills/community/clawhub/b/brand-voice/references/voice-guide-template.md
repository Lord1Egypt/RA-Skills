# Voice guide template

Keep it to one or two pages. Replace every bracket with specifics drawn from the samples; delete anything you cannot support.

## 1. Voice in one sentence
"[Brand] sounds like [a role or person] who [does what] for [whom], [adjective], [adjective], never [adjective]."

## 2. Traits (3 to 5)
| Trait | Means | Does not mean | Evidence (quote from samples) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct | says the point first, plain words | blunt, rude | "..." |

## 3. Tone dials
Mark a position from 1 to 5 and give one example sentence each.

## 4. Language rules
- Languages used and when (for example English for product and numbers, Roman Urdu for explanation).
- Spelling choices for frequent Roman Urdu words, one form each.
- Pronouns: formal vs informal "you"; "we" vs "I"; how the brand addresses the reader.
- Numbers, currencies, dates, units (for example PKR, 24-hour time).

## 5. Vocabulary
| Use | Avoid | Never |
|---|---|---|
| "customers" | "users" | competitor bashing |

Add product names with exact capitalisation.

## 6. Sentence and format habits
Average sentence length, paragraph length, openers that work, closers, question and exclamation habits, lists vs prose, emoji (which, how many, where), hashtags (how many, branded tags), links, capitalisation.

## 8. Situation tones
Launch, discount, customer complaint, apology, delay, crisis, sensitive topics, sharing wins, replying to trolls. One or two lines each: what to do and what not to do.

## 9. Before and after (6 examples)
| Before (off voice) | After (on voice) | What changed |
|---|---|---|

Use real or realistic drafts, not strawmen. Include at least one in each language the brand uses.

## 10. "Sounds like us" checklist
- [ ] Opens with the point, not a greeting or filler.
- [ ] Uses our vocabulary; none of the avoid/never list.
- [ ] Sentence length and punctuation match section 6.
- [ ] One specific detail (number, name, example) instead of a general claim.
- [ ] Right tone for the situation (section 8).
- [ ] Read aloud: sounds like the person described in section 1.

## Rewrite checklist
1. Keep every fact, number, name and link unchanged.
2. Cut filler and stock phrases; replace with the specific thing.
3. Match sentence length, openers and punctuation habits.
4. Apply vocabulary and spelling rules.
5. Check the platform shift and length limit.
6. Show a short list of changes and flag anything that needs the user's decision.
