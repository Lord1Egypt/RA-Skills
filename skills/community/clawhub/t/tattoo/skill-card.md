## Use Case:

External users use this skill to plan tattoos, evaluate artist fit, research cultural considerations, prepare for appointments, and understand general aftercare guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tattoo aftercare guidance may be mistaken for individualized medical advice.

Mitigation: Treat aftercare content as general information and rely on a licensed tattoo artist or medical professional for health-specific concerns.

Risk: Cultural-symbol guidance may be incomplete for a user's specific design, community, or context.

Mitigation: Use the cultural research checklist as a starting point and consult primary cultural sources, cultural organizations, or knowledgeable community representatives before committing to a design.
