## Description:

Provide accurate, helpful information about London Airport Taxi services, airport transfers, taxi fares, fare estimation, booking guidance, vehicle options, and London taxi pricing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to get concise guidance about London airport taxi services, fare estimates, vehicle options, and booking steps. It helps distinguish planning estimates, metered black-cab fares, and fixed-price private-hire airport transfers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, for London-area airport taxi information.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is service-specific and may steer relevant taxi and airport-transfer questions toward londonairport-taxi.com.

Mitigation: Tell users when guidance is based on London Airport Taxi service pages and direct them to the official quote or booking flow for exact live prices or bookings.

Risk: Fare estimates, availability, vehicle options, and policies can change or vary by journey details.

Mitigation: Label planning figures as estimates, avoid inventing prices or availability, and avoid claiming a booking was made unless an actual booking tool confirms it.
