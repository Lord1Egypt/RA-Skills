## Description:

Applies 24 Chinese ancestral wisdom frameworks to help users analyze decisions, efficiency, growth, relationships, money, and life questions with automatic or manual framework selection.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liangjfblue](https://clawhub.ai/user/liangjfblue)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to analyze modern life, work, relationship, competition, and planning problems through structured Chinese wisdom frameworks. The skill can auto-match a user's state or follow a manually named framework or framework combination.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad auto-activation cues for life, work, relationship, stress, and money-related prompts.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger conditions or require explicit opt-in when users need neutral responses or do not want culturally styled coaching.

Risk: Advice-style outputs may be mistaken for authoritative financial, health, legal, or career decisions.

Mitigation: Use the outputs as reflective guidance and review important decisions with appropriate human experts or stakeholders.
