## Description:

Downloads from YouTube and 1,700 other sites with yt-dlp: video that plays on any phone or TV, audio as m4a or mp3, subtitles, transcripts, thumbnails, comments, clips, playlists and channels. Use when someone pastes a video link and wants to save it, its sound, its text or its comments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
