## Description:

Checks Arabic and bilingual right-to-left layouts for readable ordering, alignment, font support, punctuation, mirroring, and common UI/design mistakes before publishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abdullah944](https://clawhub.ai/user/abdullah944)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers, localization reviewers, marketers, and developers use this skill to review Arabic, bilingual Arabic/English, and right-to-left layouts before publishing. It helps identify readability, ordering, typography, alignment, mirroring, translation, and visual consistency issues in app screens, websites, carousels, PDFs, slides, and social posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could share secrets, private client material, or unrelated personal information when requesting layout review.

Mitigation: Provide only the layout text, screenshots, and brand rules needed for review, and avoid sharing secrets or private material unless authorized.

Risk: A layout or translation review may miss context-specific Arabic wording, dialect, or brand requirements.

Mitigation: Treat the output as review guidance and confirm publish-critical language, legal, contact, and brand details before release.
