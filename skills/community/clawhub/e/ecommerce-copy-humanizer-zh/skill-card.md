## Description:

Humanizes Chinese ecommerce copy for conversion-focused platform contexts while preserving selling intent and risk boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce operators, marketers, and customer-facing teams use this skill to diagnose AI-sounding Chinese product, ad, customer-reply, livestream, and social copy. It produces platform-tailored rewrites, alternatives, usage notes, and risk replacements that keep copy natural, commercially useful, and publishable after human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rewritten ecommerce copy may retain or introduce regulated, exaggerated, or platform-sensitive claims.

Mitigation: Review final copy against applicable platform rules and the skill's risk checklist before publishing, especially for medical, financial, pricing, scarcity, and performance claims.

Risk: The skill is not a legal, medical, financial, or full compliance-review tool.

Mitigation: Use qualified review for regulated products or high-risk claims instead of relying on copy rewriting alone.
