## Description:

Removes common AI-generated writing patterns from Chinese text so editors can make wording more natural while preserving meaning and tone.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[northwind200](https://clawhub.ai/user/northwind200)

## Use Case:

Editors, writers, and reviewers use this skill to scan Chinese text for common AI-writing patterns and rewrite affected passages while preserving the original meaning and target voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be used to make AI-generated text appear less AI-generated in contexts where disclosure or authorship rules matter.

Mitigation: Use it only where this editing is allowed, and follow academic, employment, publication, or disclosure requirements before submitting revised text.
