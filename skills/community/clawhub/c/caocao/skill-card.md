## Description:

Caocao helps agents provide ride-hailing guidance for Caocao Mobility, including service selection, EV fleet tradeoffs, fare-estimate caveats, safety practices, and corporate travel workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and employees can use this skill to compare Caocao ride-hailing options, understand EV-focused service characteristics, estimate trip costs with caveats, and plan individual or corporate travel decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fare estimates, city coverage, service details, and phone numbers may become outdated or vary by location.

Mitigation: Treat these details as advisory and verify current prices, availability, and support channels in the official app before booking.
