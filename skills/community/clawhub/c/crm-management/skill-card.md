## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: CRM recommendations may be applied to real customer data without enough review of consent, unsubscribe handling, sensitive-data permissions, or local privacy obligations.

Mitigation: Review generated SOPs with privacy, legal, and CRM operations owners before applying them to production customer data.
