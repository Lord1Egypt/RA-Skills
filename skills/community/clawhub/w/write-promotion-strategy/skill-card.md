## Description:

Write a comprehensive promotion strategy based on provided product information, including product selling point extraction, multi-channel promotion planning, goal setting, and expected outcome evaluation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Marketing, product, and growth teams use this skill to draft structured product promotion strategies from product details, target audience context, competitive context, budget, and launch timing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated promotion strategies may include unverified marketing claims, budget assumptions, KPI estimates, or channel recommendations.

Mitigation: Review the strategy before public use and validate claims, budgets, KPIs, and channel choices against current product and market evidence.

Risk: Promotion guidance can become stale as market conditions, platform norms, and launch timelines change.

Mitigation: Use the document generation date and refresh market assumptions before executing the plan.
