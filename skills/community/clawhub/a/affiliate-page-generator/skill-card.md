## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated affiliate page copy may include inaccurate commission, payout, legal, or FTC-disclosure claims.

Mitigation: Review all generated marketing and compliance claims against the actual affiliate program terms before publishing.

Risk: Existing project-context files may influence affiliate page copy with stale or unsuitable product, audience, or proof-point details.

Mitigation: Check any reused project context for accuracy and remove sensitive or outdated details before relying on generated copy.
