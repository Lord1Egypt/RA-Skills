## Description:

Plans, promotes, and repurposes livestreams and realtime social moments while keeping the actual broadcast, realtime engagement, moderation, and live shopping under native platform and human control.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External social media teams, creators, and marketing operators use this skill to plan a live event, prepare a run-of-show, schedule pre-live promotion, guide the human-run live session, and turn the recording into repurposed clips and replay content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be misused to imply that WoopSocial can broadcast livestreams, moderate in realtime, or run live shopping.

Mitigation: Keep the broadcast, realtime engagement, moderation, and live shopping native and human-run; use the skill for planning, promo scheduling, playbook guidance, and repurposing.

Risk: Platform thresholds, live features, and commerce rules can change.

Mitigation: Re-verify platform requirements and feature availability before each live event and at least quarterly.

Risk: Livestream reporting could include fabricated viewer counts or fake engagement.

Mitigation: Use native live analytics only, do not buy fake views, and do not invent viewer numbers for client or stakeholder reporting.
