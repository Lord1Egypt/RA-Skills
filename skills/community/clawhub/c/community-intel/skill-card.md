## Description:

Community Intel guides an agent to run recurring public community monitoring for open-source projects and products, search common discussion platforms, and compile structured intelligence reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[npfaerber](https://clawhub.ai/user/npfaerber)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers, maintainers, and product teams use this skill to monitor public community sentiment, adoption signals, complaints, feature requests, and ecosystem trends for a target project or product.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recurring public monitoring can retain findings in a persistent project intel log and share reports to configured Discord or email destinations.

Mitigation: Keep search terms and delivery destinations explicit, and avoid sensitive internal research unless retained and shared findings are intended.
