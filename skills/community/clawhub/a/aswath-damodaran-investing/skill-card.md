## Description:

Provides valuation-oriented investment analysis guidance based on Aswath Damodaran's public valuation frameworks, emphasizing DCF, risk assessment, story-driven analysis, and probabilistic scenario ranges.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[talentedleo](https://clawhub.ai/user/talentedleo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to structure educational valuation discussions around business story, scenario analysis, DCF assumptions, risk assessment, and investment decision framing. It should not be presented as Aswath Damodaran's endorsement or as personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake persona-style output for Aswath Damodaran's own words, approval, or endorsement.

Mitigation: Label the skill as a third-party emulation of public valuation methods and avoid language that implies affiliation, authorship, or endorsement.

Risk: Investment analysis may be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as educational valuation discussion, require independent review, and avoid individualized buy, sell, or hold recommendations.

Risk: Static valuation references and market examples may become stale or may not match current market data.

Mitigation: Verify current facts, prices, and source claims before relying on outputs for investment research or publication.
