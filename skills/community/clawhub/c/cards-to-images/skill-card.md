## Description:

Renders approved card content packages into inspected, platform-sized image files and a cards manifest for human confirmation, upload handoff, or optional public-URL preparation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, developers, and publishing teams use this skill to turn reviewed card copy into local PNG or JPG assets for Xiaohongshu, WeChat inline cards, Zhihu Idea images, or generic carousel workflows. It helps preserve review checkpoints for visual QA, human confirmation, upload decisions, and optional public URL preparation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rendered card images may still contain factual, branding, platform-compliance, layout, or legibility issues before publication.

Mitigation: Review every generated image before publishing and confirm factual accuracy, branding, platform fit, dimensions, and readability.

Risk: External image generation or public URL workflows may require additional approval before assets leave the local review flow.

Mitigation: Keep upload and URL-publication steps behind explicit human confirmation, especially when external generation or public hosting is used.
