## Description:

Transforms user-provided Mandarin text into concise nonsense literature by preserving the original topic while using tautology, circular reasoning, causal inversion, and vague philosophical modifiers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chiling7](https://clawhub.ai/user/chiling7)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and writers use this skill to rewrite short Mandarin statements into humorous, philosophical-sounding nonsense literature while retaining the original topic and avoiding added facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on casual Chinese text if the routing system interprets the message as text to transform.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation when users want a rewrite, and avoid applying it to ordinary conversation unless transformation intent is clear.
