## Description:

Prepares an analyst briefing kit for Gartner, Forrester, IDC, and similar engagements, covering objectives, narrative, differentiation, proof points, demo storyline, anticipated questions, and follow-up commitments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Analyst relations, product marketing, and go-to-market teams use this skill to prepare credible briefing materials for industry analyst calls, evaluations, and submission narratives. It helps structure the briefing objective, market framing, proof-backed differentiation, demo storyline, anticipated questions, and follow-up commitments.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include confidential analyst materials, NDA content, customer names, metrics, or roadmap details in the active agent session.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive materials unless appropriate for the session; clearly flag confidential or NDA-bound items before using them in the briefing kit.

Risk: Unverified claims, customer references, metrics, or roadmap dates could make an analyst briefing misleading.

Mitigation: Mark unconfirmed evidence as [to confirm], avoid fabricating proof points, and validate all customer, metric, and roadmap details before use.
