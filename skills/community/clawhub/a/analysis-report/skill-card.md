## Description:

Coordinates complete, structured data analysis reports by selecting report sections, delegating metric, anomaly, attribution, and forecast work to capability skills, and weaving the results into a coherent narrative.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jackyujun](https://clawhub.ai/user/jackyujun)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business analysts, operations teams, and agent users use this skill to produce complete multi-section business analysis reports rather than one-off metric queries or isolated anomaly checks. It is most useful when a report needs an agreed outline, section-by-section analysis, cross-section context, and a final Markdown narrative.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Downstream data-analysis skills may query business data based on an overly broad or misunderstood report scope.

Mitigation: Confirm the report topic, time range, audience, depth, and outline before allowing downstream metric, anomaly, attribution, or forecast work.

Risk: A generated report may overstate conclusions if sections are treated as independent fragments or if findings are not carried forward accurately.

Mitigation: Maintain report context between sections and review the final narrative for concrete data support, coherent transitions, and actionable conclusions.
