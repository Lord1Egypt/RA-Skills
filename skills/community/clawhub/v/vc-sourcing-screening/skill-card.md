## Description:

Assists VC investors with sector-based deal sourcing lists and four-dimensional startup screening scorecards with cited, traceable reasoning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhuiwensm99](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhuiwensm99)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

VC analysts and investors use this skill to generate prioritized candidate lists for an investment theme or to produce early screening scorecards for startup targets across team, market, technology, and financial dimensions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expect non-Chinese output from a Chinese-first skill.

Mitigation: Ask for the desired output language explicitly when non-Chinese deliverables are needed.

Risk: Investment research may be published externally unintentionally if the user does not notice the publication path.

Mitigation: Use external publishing only after deliberately connecting the ima connector and confirming destination and visibility.

Risk: Sourcing and screening outputs can be misleading if claims lack evidence.

Mitigation: Require cited, traceable sources for conclusions and mark unsupported gaps as needing verification.
