## Description:

Generates Chinese shuangwen web-fiction chapters with humiliation, reversal, and authority-confirmation beats, with optional comic-style short-drama video prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liuxiangjian-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/liuxiangjian-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language fiction writers, prompt-library users, and short-drama creators use this skill to draft a first shuangwen chapter from a short option-based prompt and optionally convert it into storyboard-style video prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake fictional medical or legal plot devices for real-world advice.

Mitigation: Keep medical and legal content clearly fictional and avoid actionable professional guidance.

Risk: Conflict-heavy story beats can drift into hateful, sexually explicit, or graphically violent content.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's safety boundaries and keep humiliation, reversal, and confrontation scenes non-explicit, non-hateful, and fictional.

Risk: Video prompts may be treated as automatic video generation instructions when no video tool is available.

Mitigation: Treat video conversion as user-directed prompt text unless the host platform explicitly provides and authorizes a video generation tool.
