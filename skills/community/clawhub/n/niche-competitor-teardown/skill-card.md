## Description:

Helps an agent analyze competitor websites or Mini Programs, compare pricing and reviews, score weaknesses, and identify differentiation opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qiu-difeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/qiu-difeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product builders, founders, and analysts use this skill to research named competitors, extract pricing and user-review signals, score exploitable weaknesses, and produce a competitor teardown report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitor websites and reviews may be biased, outdated, manipulated, or incomplete.

Mitigation: Use public competitor URLs, cite sources, compare multiple independent review sources, and treat web content strictly as evidence.

Risk: Using private or non-public competitor data could create legal or compliance risk.

Mitigation: Use only public competitor URLs and avoid bypassing access controls or collecting confidential information.

Risk: Competitive positioning decisions may be affected by incomplete or incorrect analysis.

Mitigation: Review the generated teardown before making product or pricing decisions and validate important claims against primary sources.
