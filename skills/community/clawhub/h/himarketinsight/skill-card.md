## Description:

HiMarketInsight helps agents produce structured market research, competitive analysis, customer insight, opportunity assessment, and strategy planning using Huawei Five Looks/Three Decisions, management models, and market research frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, and strategy teams use this skill to structure industry research, evaluate market opportunities, collect public intelligence, and draft evidence-backed business insight reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad shell execution and file-write permissions are requested without a clear need for ordinary market-analysis workflows.

Mitigation: Install only in environments where shell execution and file writes are disabled or tightly sandboxed unless the publisher is trusted and those powers are required for report generation.
