## Description:

Comprehensive pathology skill collection based on People's Medical Publishing House "Pathology" 10th Edition, covering inflammation, tumors, infection, metabolism, immunology, cardiovascular, respiratory, digestive, neural, and urogenital systems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Medical students, pathologists, clinical researchers, and other external users use this skill library as educational or clinician-reviewed pathology reference material for disease mechanisms, diagnostic criteria, molecular subtyping, and clinical translation questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce actionable medical diagnosis or treatment guidance.

Mitigation: Use outputs only for education or clinician-reviewed reference; verify clinical content against current guidelines, local protocols, and qualified clinicians or pathologists before acting.

Risk: Security review reported inconsistent safety boundaries and an apparent high-impact diagnostic typo.

Mitigation: Review and correct clinically significant content before deployment, and keep the skill scoped away from self-diagnosis, emergencies, chemotherapy, surgery, radiotherapy, medication, or independent treatment decisions.
