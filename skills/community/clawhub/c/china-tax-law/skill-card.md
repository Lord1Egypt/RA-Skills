## Description:

China Tax Law provides Chinese-language support for PRC tax-law consultation, tax planning analysis, compliance review, policy interpretation, and tax dispute preparation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Tax professionals, business teams, and agents assisting users with China-related tax questions use this skill to identify applicable PRC tax categories, compliance checkpoints, planning considerations, and dispute-preparation issues. Outputs should be treated as reference material and checked against current official guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tax-law guidance may be outdated or incomplete for current filings, disputes, or planning decisions.

Mitigation: Verify current rules with official tax authorities and consult a qualified professional for important tax matters.
