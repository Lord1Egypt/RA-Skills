## Description:

RF Engineering is a Chinese-language reference skill for RF circuit design, wireless propagation, impedance matching, RF components, PCB RF layout, test measurement, EMC, and regulatory certification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[viewway](https://clawhub.ai/user/viewway)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, RF engineers, and certification teams use this skill as a quick reference for RF propagation, circuit parameters, Smith chart matching, PCB layout, measurement workflows, common bands, and regulatory topics. It is best used for guidance and calculation support, with current standards and project constraints verified before real engineering or certification decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: RF formulas, example values, and regulatory limits may be outdated or context-specific.

Mitigation: Verify technical values against current official standards, device requirements, and project-specific constraints before real engineering or certification decisions.

Risk: The skill content is primarily Chinese, which may reduce accessibility for English-speaking or multilingual teams.

Mitigation: Use translation or bilingual technical review when applying the guidance in teams that do not primarily work in Chinese.
