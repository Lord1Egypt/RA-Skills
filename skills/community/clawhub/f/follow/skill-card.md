## Description:

Monitor content from people, topics, and sources across platforms with smart filtering, tiered alerts, and searchable archives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

People who monitor public or permitted private sources use this skill to configure followed people, topics, and feeds, filter noisy updates, archive useful summaries, receive tiered alerts, and query prior activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Monitoring people, communities, or restricted sources may create privacy, consent, legal, or platform-terms risk.

Mitigation: Only monitor public or explicitly permitted sources, confirm that platform access methods are allowed, and decide retention rules before enabling scheduled checks or integrations.

Risk: Archives and digests may retain sensitive summaries, links, timestamps, or behavior patterns longer than intended.

Mitigation: Store summaries instead of full content dumps, keep links and timestamps for traceability, and define retention and deletion practices for archived records.
