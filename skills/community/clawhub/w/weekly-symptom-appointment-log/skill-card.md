## Description:

Prepare a clear seven-day symptom, trigger, question, and visit-note sheet before a clinician appointment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize recent health observations before a clinician visit, including a seven-day symptom log, possible patterns, prioritized questions, and a concise appointment summary.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may enter sensitive health details while preparing the appointment sheet.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in an AI assistant environment acceptable for health notes and avoid entering details the user is not comfortable sharing.

Risk: Routine logging could be inappropriate when symptoms are severe, sudden, dangerous, or rapidly worsening.

Mitigation: Perform the urgent-care safety check first and direct red-flag symptoms to urgent care, emergency services, or crisis support instead of routine note-taking.

Risk: The generated sheet could contain omissions or inaccurate summaries.

Mitigation: Review and correct the output before sharing it with a clinician, and keep medical decisions with qualified professionals.

Risk: Symptom patterns could be mistaken for medical conclusions.

Mitigation: Frame patterns as observations to discuss with a clinician and avoid diagnosis, causation claims, treatment changes, medication changes, or test interpretation.
