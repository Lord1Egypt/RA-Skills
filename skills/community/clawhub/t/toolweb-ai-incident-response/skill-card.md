## Description:

Generate personalized career roadmaps for AI incident response professionals with specialized learning paths and skill assessments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd](https://clawhub.ai/user/krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd)

## Use Case:

Security engineers, incident responders, SOC analysts, threat hunters, and security certification holders use this skill to generate AI incident response career roadmaps, specializations, learning paths, resources, and milestones from assessment data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Assessment data may be sent to the listed external API.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive employer, incident, or personal data unless you trust the provider's privacy and retention practices.
