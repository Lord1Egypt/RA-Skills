## Description:

Guides an HR one-on-one work values interview based on Super's Work Values Inventory and produces value-priority insights, satisfaction gap analysis, and attrition-risk guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaobod1](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaobod1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

HR practitioners use this skill to conduct structured employee value interviews, identify core work values and bottom lines, and generate advisory retention and management recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill collects sensitive employee preference data and generates attrition-risk reports.

Mitigation: Use it only with informed employee consent, defined access controls, retention rules, and clear limits on how HR may use the results.

Risk: Attrition-risk output could be over-weighted in employment decisions.

Mitigation: Treat the report as advisory and require human review alongside interviews, performance context, and applicable HR policy.
