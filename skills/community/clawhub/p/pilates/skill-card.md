## Description:

Plan Pilates sessions, refine form, and track steady progress with equipment-aware modifications, core-focused cues, and weekly reviews.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and personal coaching agents use this skill to plan Pilates practice, choose session modes, refine one form issue at a time, and track weekly progress. It is intended for movement quality, consistency, posture awareness, and safety-aware habit support, not diagnosis or rehabilitation care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local practice notes may include exercise tolerance, pain, pregnancy or postpartum context, surgery, and other safety limits.

Mitigation: Ask for confirmation before writing local files, keep notes under ~/pilates/, and store only information that improves future practice decisions.

Risk: Pilates coaching could be mistaken for medical, rehabilitation, or diagnostic care.

Mitigation: Keep guidance limited to movement quality and habit support, avoid therapeutic guarantees, and recommend professional help for red flags or medically uncertain situations.

Risk: Progressing too quickly or translating studio equipment work to home practice can aggravate symptoms or reduce form control.

Mitigation: Use conservative modifications, reduce range or leverage before increasing difficulty, progress one variable at a time, and stop or regress if symptoms escalate.
