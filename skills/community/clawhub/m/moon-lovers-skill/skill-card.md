## Description:

Moon Lovers 白月光 generates gentle, restrained romantic chat replies and rewrites for fictional character roleplay from a role sheet, message, draft, or chat context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[phoenixlucky](https://clawhub.ai/user/phoenixlucky)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and roleplay authors use this skill to produce sendable romantic chat replies, tone-matched rewrites, emotional reading, and response strategy for fictional character roleplay. It defaults to Chinese, can respond in other requested languages, and is tuned for a soft, emotionally intelligent, pre-relationship white-moonlight tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
