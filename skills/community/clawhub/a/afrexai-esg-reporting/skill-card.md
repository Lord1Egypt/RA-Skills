## Description:

Generates structured ESG and sustainability reporting guidance, including materiality assessment, environmental, social, governance, compliance, scoring, and action recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sustainability, finance, compliance, board, investor, and consulting teams use this skill to prepare ESG reports, scorecards, due diligence responses, and regulatory-readiness materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Company ESG inputs may include sensitive workforce, DEI, legal, incident, supplier, or financial information.

Mitigation: Aggregate or anonymize sensitive metrics, avoid unnecessary personal data, and redact legal or incident details before use.

Risk: Regulatory disclosure requirements and ESG standards can change, and incorrect claims may create compliance or publication risk.

Mitigation: Verify regulatory claims against current official standards and qualified counsel before publishing externally.
