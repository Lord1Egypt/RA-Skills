## Description:

Career fit assessment through 10 occupation-specific questions that estimates success likelihood and produces a personalized report for career switches, job fit, and transition decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tonytan10086](https://clawhub.ai/user/tonytan10086)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution License (MIT-0)

## Use Case:

External users considering a career switch or uncertain about job fit use this skill to complete a structured self-assessment and receive a short narrative report with a fit range, readiness stage, and concrete next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The assessment may ask for personal career context, financial runway, family constraints, and readiness details.

Mitigation: Ask only for details needed for the assessment and remind users to review the generated report before sharing it.

Risk: A generated career-fit report could be mistaken for a deterministic prediction or a major life-decision directive.

Mitigation: Present results as self-reported planning guidance with a range, readiness stage, and validation steps rather than as a definitive verdict.
