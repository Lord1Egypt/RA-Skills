## Description:

易学命名专家「善起名」技能，当用户需要为新生儿或儿童起正式名和小名、为现有姓名做八字五行评析，或结合父母与子女生辰八字做家族五行合参调衡时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for Chinese cultural naming consultation, including newborn or child name proposals, existing-name review, and family birth-chart comparison. Outputs are framed as traditional culture reference and psychological meaning, not deterministic life or legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for family names and birth dates for cultural naming analysis.

Mitigation: Use it only when the user is comfortable providing that information in-session, and do not retain or share birth details beyond the current analysis.

Risk: Birth-chart, five-elements, and naming outputs can be mistaken for deterministic life advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as cultural reference and psychological meaning, and avoid claims that names determine life outcomes.

Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill unintentionally.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants Chinese cultural naming or name analysis before collecting personal birth information.
