## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Banner copy can become stale or misleading if offers, deadlines, or discount terms change.

Mitigation: Review active banner messaging on a regular cadence and verify promotions against current campaign terms before publishing.

Risk: Oversized or poorly adapted banner assets can degrade mobile usability and page performance.

Mitigation: Use mobile-specific layouts and optimized image assets, and check that the banner does not crowd navigation or harm key page loading metrics.

Risk: Broad trigger wording may route requests to this skill when a stricter promo or discount strategy skill is more appropriate.

Mitigation: Review routing behavior during installation and delegate promotion strategy questions to the related discount-marketing strategy workflow when needed.
