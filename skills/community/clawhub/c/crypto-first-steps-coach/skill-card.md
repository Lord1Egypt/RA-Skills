## Description:

A calm beginner guide that helps a first-time user understand what crypto is, what to learn first, and what not to rush into.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users who are new to cryptocurrency use this skill to get a calm, safety-first learning sequence, identify beginner-appropriate next concepts, and avoid rushed investment behavior.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake beginner cryptocurrency education for financial advice or investment selection.

Mitigation: Present the skill as educational guidance only, avoid coin picks or leverage recommendations, and redirect users toward risk awareness when they ask for specific trades.

Risk: Users seeking urgent recovery from losses may be vulnerable to rushed decisions.

Mitigation: Slow the interaction down, focus on foundational learning and safety basics, and avoid action-oriented investment guidance.
