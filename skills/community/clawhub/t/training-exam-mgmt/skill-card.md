## Description:

Training Exam Mgmt helps manage the exam lifecycle, including question banks, paper generation, scheduling, online or offline execution, grade entry, score analysis, and certificate generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Training, education, and certification teams use this skill to plan exams, structure question banks and test papers, track scoring workflows, analyze candidate performance, and prepare certificate issuance criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Candidate rosters, exam scores, certificate decisions, and exam content can contain sensitive or controlled information.

Mitigation: Use only authorized data and apply organization-approved privacy, retention, approval, and audit procedures outside the skill.

Risk: The prompt and operational templates are Chinese-language materials, which may be unsuitable for teams that cannot review them accurately.

Mitigation: Install and use the skill only when reviewers can understand and approve the Chinese-language exam-management workflow.

Risk: Generated exam plans, scoring analyses, and certificate criteria may affect learner outcomes if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Have qualified staff review generated exam materials, score reports, and certificate issuance decisions before use.
