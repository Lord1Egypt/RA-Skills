## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has a broad activation scope spanning legal, sales, support, product, and business requests.

Mitigation: Review the activation scope before installing and limit use to domains where the deploying team accepts the workflow coverage.

Risk: Legal-document drafts or compliance guidance could be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat legal output as drafting assistance only, include the stated disclaimer, and require review by a qualified attorney before use.

Risk: Business recommendations, benchmarks, or cited statistics may be inaccurate if not grounded in current evidence.

Mitigation: Require source citations or user-provided data for factual claims, and verify statistics before making business decisions.
