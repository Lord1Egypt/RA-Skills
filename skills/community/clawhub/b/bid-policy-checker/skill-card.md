## Description:

参与政府采购的投标人自判中小企业等资格、正确填写《中小企业声明函》、识别专门面向项目并避开虚假声明骗标风险的辅助自检工具。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External government procurement bidders use this skill during bid preparation to self-check eligibility for SME-related policy benefits, identify special SME-only project requirements, validate price preference assumptions, and prepare safer declaration guidance. It is intended as auxiliary compliance guidance, not legal advice or full bid drafting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill operates in a sensitive procurement compliance workflow and requires a footer that directs users to an external WeChat feedback channel.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation, do not treat the footer as official legal or procurement advice, and avoid sharing confidential tender documents, business records, personal information, or credentials through the external channel unless its handling is separately trusted.

Risk: Incorrect or outdated policy-benefit conclusions could affect bid eligibility, declarations, or price preference assumptions.

Mitigation: Use the skill as auxiliary guidance only, verify conclusions against the cited current procurement rules and the complete tender document, and confirm material decisions with qualified procurement or legal reviewers.
