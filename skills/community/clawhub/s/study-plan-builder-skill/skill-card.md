## Use Case:

External learners, educators, and agents use this skill to turn study goals and user-owned notes into concise study plans, reusable checklists, next actions, and editable draft copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste private personal data, secrets, or copyrighted notes they cannot share into study-planning prompts.

Mitigation: Keep inputs minimal, avoid secrets and sensitive personal data, and only paste notes the user owns or has rights to share.

Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill when a more specific study-planning workflow was intended.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger wording during review if deployment requires more precise activation.
