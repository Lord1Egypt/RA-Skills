## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated affiliate content can include incorrect or outdated claims, prices, commission details, or disclosure language.

Mitigation: Review product claims, current pricing, commission information, and affiliate disclosures before publishing.

Risk: Affiliate program access and compliance requirements may not match every publisher or region.

Mitigation: Confirm account eligibility, real-name verification requirements, platform terms, and local advertising disclosure obligations before use.
