## Description:

Reels Script Studio generates structured 15-second vertical short-video scripts with shot tables, platform-adapted AI video prompts, quality checks, and user-facing production guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bbroot](https://clawhub.ai/user/bbroot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and social media teams use this skill to turn short option-based inputs plus product or topic details into platform-ready short-video script packages. It supports product recommendations, explainers, narrative shorts, Vlogs, comparison reviews, and timely commentary for platforms such as Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, YouTube, Jimeng, Xiaoyunque, Seedance, and Jianying.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local evolution records can retain sensitive campaign details, private client data, or unreleased product information if users choose to save that feedback.

Mitigation: Avoid recording sensitive details in evolution feedback, and review or clear evolution.json before sharing the skill folder.

Risk: Generated marketing scripts may include claims, platform-sensitive wording, cultural tone, or music and media suggestions that require review before publication.

Mitigation: Have a human reviewer check brand fit, claims, platform policy, and commercial rights for music and visuals before publishing generated content.
