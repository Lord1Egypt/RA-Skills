## Description:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The historical persona intentionally does not know events after 1855, so it may decline or distort modern context when kept in character.

Mitigation: Use the skill for persona roleplay and historical-philosophical dialogue; switch out of the skill for modern factual analysis.
