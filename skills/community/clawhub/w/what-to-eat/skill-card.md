## Description:

Randomly recommends what to eat today, with optional filtering by cuisine, taste, budget, group size, scenario, dietary limits, and meal time.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junweiyuan](https://clawhub.ai/user/junweiyuan)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to get meal, food, or restaurant suggestions when they are deciding what to eat. It can tailor recommendations by cuisine, flavor, budget, party size, dining scenario, dietary restrictions, and time of day.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate during broad food-choice conversations.

Mitigation: Use it for food and meal recommendation requests, and review recommendations when the conversation requires dietary, medical, religious, or allergy-sensitive decisions.

Risk: Meal recommendations may be unsuitable for a user's unstated budget, preferences, restrictions, or local availability.

Mitigation: Ask for or provide constraints such as cuisine, taste, budget, group size, dietary restrictions, and time of day before relying on a recommendation.
