## Description:

Draft and review Brazilian LGPD privacy policy drafts against documented data flows, with versioning guidance and a required legal review before publication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[goul4rt](https://clawhub.ai/user/goul4rt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Organizations and privacy professionals use this skill to draft and version a privacy notice after consolidating their records of processing activities (ROPA). They should reconcile the draft with actual data practices and obtain legal and privacy-owner approval before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, where Brazil's LGPD applies.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A draft may not match the organization's actual data flows or may contain unsupported statements.

Mitigation: Cross-check all sections against the current ROPA and implemented practices, then obtain legal and privacy-owner review before publication.

Risk: The security review was partial because some local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Inspect the referenced materials that were not included in the scan before relying on the skill.
