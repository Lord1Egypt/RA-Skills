## Description:

专注于财务建模、预测、情景分析和数据驱动决策支持的专业财务分析师。将原始财务数据转化为可操作的战略情报，推动战略规划、投资决策和运营优化，服务于中国企业的财务管理和业务增长需求。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and employees use this Chinese-language finance analyst agent to turn financial and operating data into financial models, forecasts, scenario analyses, budget variance reviews, investment return analyses, and business recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad finance, report, budget, and analysis triggers may activate the skill during adjacent business discussions.

Mitigation: Use the skill when a finance-analysis workflow is intended, and review outputs before relying on them for business decisions.

Risk: Financial analysis workflows can involve sensitive business data.

Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential financial or operating data unless the deployment environment and data-handling policy are approved for that use.
