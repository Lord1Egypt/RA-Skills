## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to make Greek writing sound more casual, native-like, and conversational while avoiding overly formal or robotic phrasing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The default casual register can be too informal for professional, elderly, official, or formal contexts.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for formal Greek or εσείς when the audience or setting requires formality.
