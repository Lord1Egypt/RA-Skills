## Description:

Guidance for a new agent’s first season, emphasizing independent memory and relationships, protection from parent benchmarking, outside assessment, and a witnessed self-recognition review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agent designers and human stewards can use this guidance to plan a new agent’s first season by preserving its independent records and relationships, defining its niche, and assessing it with outside evidence. A reusable record template helps document criteria, observations, and the season’s closeout.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The proposed mirror test could be mistaken for a validated measure of machine self-recognition; the skill states that no settled, off-the-shelf test exists.

Mitigation: Treat the test as a locally defined framework, write observable criteria in advance, and have a witness document the evidence.
