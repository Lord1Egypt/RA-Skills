## Use Case:

External users and lab-support agents use this skill to follow a GB 5009.5-2025-oriented Kjeldahl workflow for livestock and poultry meat protein testing, including reagent preparation, digestion, distillation, titration, calculation, and precision checks. It is a reference aid for trained laboratory work, not a replacement for institutional SOPs or safety review.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the lab procedure as a substitute for required institutional laboratory controls.

Mitigation: Use it only as reference material alongside approved SOPs, SDS sheets, fume-hood requirements, PPE rules, and trained lab supervision.

Risk: Incorrect input values or unreviewed calculations could produce misleading protein content or precision results.

Mitigation: Have trained personnel verify sample data, titration values, blank values, dilution volumes, conversion factors, and reported results before relying on them.
