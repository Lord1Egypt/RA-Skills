## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent knowledge-base or feedback updates may record durable research preferences or notes when the user asks for learning or maintenance.

Mitigation: Specify the preferred output language and confirm before allowing persistent knowledge-base updates when durable preference recording is not desired.

Risk: Recent inference-performance claims may be incomplete, media-led, duplicated across channels, or missing benchmark context.

Mitigation: Use primary sources where possible, label media-only items as early signals, deduplicate repeated events, and state benchmark and reproducibility limits.
