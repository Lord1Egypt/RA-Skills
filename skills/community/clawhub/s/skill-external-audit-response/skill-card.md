## Description:

External Audit Response helps Chinese-speaking teams prepare for second-party customer audits and third-party certification audits through a mandatory three-step intake before producing preparation advice, checklist tables, and key cautions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Quality, compliance, and operations teams use this skill to prepare for customer, internal, certification, surveillance, or recertification audits by confirming audit type, industry, and audit standard. It generates practical preparation advice, tabular checklists, and response cautions for review before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create multiple txt/md report files in the current working directory.

Mitigation: Use it from an appropriate project folder and review generated files before sharing them.

Risk: Audit preparation content may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific certification contract, customer requirement, or internal decision.

Mitigation: Review the generated audit advice and checklist with responsible company personnel before using it for official communications or audit responses.
