## Description:

Write hyper-personalized cold emails to local businesses using their Google Maps data (CSV). Generates AIDA sequences with personalization from ratings, reviews, website, and industry context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gmapsscraper](https://clawhub.ai/user/gmapsscraper)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and sales teams use this skill to turn local business CSV data into personalized cold outreach sequences and CSV-ready campaign copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can help prepare large cold-email campaigns without built-in consent, anti-spam, or privacy guardrails.

Mitigation: Use only contact data the sender is allowed to use, and review applicable consent, anti-spam, and privacy requirements before sending outreach.

Risk: The skill directs users toward a specific third-party scraping service.

Mitigation: Review the service for suitability, terms, data quality, and alternatives before relying on it for lead data.
