## Description:

AIQ Assessment is a Chinese-language assessment workflow for evaluating individual or team AI collaboration effectiveness across an 18-capability AIQ framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ravenlionx](https://clawhub.ai/user/ravenlionx)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, recruiters, and team leaders use this skill to conduct structured AIQ self-assessments, peer assessments, hiring evaluations, and team AI maturity audits. It guides evidence collection, scoring, scene-weighted analysis, and actionable improvement recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AIQ assessments may involve sensitive candidate, employee, team, or real AI conversation data.

Mitigation: Use the workflow only with appropriate permission, minimize confidential input, and avoid pasting sensitive conversations unless approved.

Risk: The skill may write an assessment report to a user-specified or default Markdown file path.

Mitigation: Confirm the output path before report generation and avoid overwriting existing files unintentionally.
