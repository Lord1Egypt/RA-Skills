## Description:

Biz Retro Analyzer turns meeting transcripts, field observations, and follow-up reasoning from complex collaborative projects into evidence-backed retrospectives, audits, and next-step actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlxeva](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlxeva)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, business analysts, project leads, and operators use this skill to turn conversation-heavy project materials into structured fact records, retrospectives, stakeholder and participant-understanding analysis, reverse audits, and action recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce interpretive judgments about motives, pricing, control, or stakeholder intent from sensitive business materials.

Mitigation: Use only materials appropriate for agent processing, and review high-impact interpretations before using them for commitments, pricing, sequencing, or partner decisions.

Risk: Outputs depend on source quality, speaker attribution, and the availability of source anchors.

Mitigation: Downgrade, validate, or omit claims when evidence is thin, speaker attribution is unreliable, or source anchors are missing.
