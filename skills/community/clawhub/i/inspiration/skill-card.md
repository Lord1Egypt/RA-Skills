## Description:

Find design and AI art inspiration from curated galleries, screenshot libraries, and creative showcases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, designers, and developers use this skill to find focused visual references for UI patterns, landing pages, AI-generated images, typography, colors, icons, and related creative work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expose confidential, personal, customer, or unreleased images while browsing or uploading to third-party galleries.

Mitigation: Do not upload sensitive images or project materials to third-party galleries unless the user has reviewed the service terms and has permission to share the content.

Risk: Prompts, screenshots, models, or visual assets found through linked galleries may have licensing or reuse restrictions.

Mitigation: Review the source site's terms and asset license before reusing or publishing any referenced material.
