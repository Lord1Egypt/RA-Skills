## Description:

为小红书、X、知乎等平台诊断并重写中文内容，减少模板腔，增强口语感、观点力度和可追溯性。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lanyasheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/lanyasheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and content teams use this skill to turn drafts or source material into platform-adapted Chinese posts with AI-tone diagnosis, rewritten copy, titles, comments, tags, and pre-publication risk notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated drafts may include unverified factual claims or platform tone that needs adjustment.

Mitigation: Review every output before use, keep source labels and verification status, and mark uncertain claims as 待核实.

Risk: The skill is intended for drafting, not publishing actions or high-risk conclusion-style advice.

Mitigation: Require a human publication decision and avoid investment, medical, or other high-risk recommendations.
