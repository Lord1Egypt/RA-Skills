## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal, confidential, biometric, or customer images may be sent to external services such as Baidu, Tencent, or the listed demo website.

Mitigation: Review those services' privacy and retention terms before use, and avoid uploading sensitive images unless the user's data-handling requirements are satisfied.

Risk: Generated API, pricing, and batch-processing guidance may become outdated as third-party image services change.

Mitigation: Verify current service documentation, pricing, and quotas before implementing or reselling a workflow.
