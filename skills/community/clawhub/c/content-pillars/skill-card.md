## Description:

Defines a brand's 3-5 recurring content themes from its brand profile, point of view, audience, proof, and offers, then produces a usable content-pillars.md for downstream content planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content strategists, social media teams, creators, and agents use this skill after brand-profile to derive ownable content pillars before creating content calendars or batches. It helps avoid generic intent or format buckets by testing each pillar for ownability, relevance, strategy, and sustainability.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation for content-planning requests may generate pillars that downstream calendar or posting workflows treat as strategy.

Mitigation: Review content-pillars.md before using it in batch planning, publishing, or other downstream content workflows.

Risk: Pillars derived without a current brand profile can be speculative.

Mitigation: Load brand-profile.md and voice.md first, or create the brand profile before accepting the generated pillar set.
