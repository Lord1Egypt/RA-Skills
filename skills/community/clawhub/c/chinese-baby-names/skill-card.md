## Description:

Chinese Baby Names helps agents gather family naming preferences and suggest meaningful Chinese baby names with pinyin and explanations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xixinglong](https://clawhub.ai/user/xixinglong)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to get Chinese baby-name suggestions based on surname, gender, preferred style, desired meaning, generation-character needs, and characters to avoid.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide more sensitive family information than is needed for name suggestions.

Mitigation: Ask only for naming preferences needed to generate suggestions, such as surname, gender, desired meaning, generation character, and characters to avoid.

Risk: Generated name suggestions may include unintended cultural, phonetic, or family-specific issues.

Mitigation: Encourage review of suggested names for family preferences, local naming norms, and unwanted homophones before use.
