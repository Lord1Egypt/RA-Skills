## Description:

Analyzes Polymarket BTC prediction markets to show active market sentiment, crowd consensus, and alignment with on-chain data via Orion's oracle.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to summarize active BTC prediction-market sentiment and compare it with Orion's on-chain bias signals. It is intended for speculative market commentary and decision support, not financial advice or autonomous trading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill produces speculative BTC and prediction-market commentary that could be mistaken for financial advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as informational sentiment summaries and remind users to make independent decisions before acting on market signals.

Risk: The skill routes users toward paid Orion ACP offerings.

Mitigation: Require user confirmation before using any paid ACP service and show the relevant offering and price before purchase.
