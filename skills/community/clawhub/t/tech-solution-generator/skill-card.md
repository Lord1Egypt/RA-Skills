## Description:

Generates technical solution documents from requirements, including architecture design, technology selection, risk assessment, and schedule estimation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[imgolye](https://clawhub.ai/user/imgolye)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, architects, and product teams use this skill to turn product requirements or brief project descriptions into structured technical solution documents for reviews, feasibility analysis, and team alignment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated architecture, interface, risk, or schedule content may be inaccurate or incomplete for the target project.

Mitigation: Have qualified engineers review the generated document before using it for planning, implementation, or stakeholder approval.

Risk: The skill is Chinese-oriented, so outputs may require localization or terminology review for non-Chinese audiences.

Mitigation: Review language, technical terminology, and formatting before sharing externally or across multilingual teams.
