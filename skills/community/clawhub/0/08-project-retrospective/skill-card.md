## Description:

项目复盘与经验沉淀：重建时间线、追踪关键决策、提取可复用经验，输出结构化复盘报告。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and project contributors use this skill to run structured retrospectives for completed projects by reconstructing timelines, tracing decisions and issues, and extracting reusable lessons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow is written in Chinese, so non-Chinese users may misread prompts or apply them inconsistently.

Mitigation: Translate or adapt the retrospective prompts before use with non-Chinese-speaking teams.

Risk: Project retrospectives may include sensitive internal project details, decisions, incidents, or contributor feedback.

Mitigation: Review the generated retrospective report before sharing and remove confidential or personal information.
