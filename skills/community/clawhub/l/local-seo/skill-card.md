This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, marketers, and business operators use this skill to prepare local search guidance, GBP optimization checklists, consistent NAP formatting, citation priorities, and citation audit findings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for some general local-business questions.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to use a different SEO or business-planning workflow when local SEO guidance is not desired.

Risk: Incorrect NAP or citation guidance could lead to inconsistent public business listings.

Mitigation: Review the proposed NAP format, GBP checklist, and citation changes before updating live listings.
