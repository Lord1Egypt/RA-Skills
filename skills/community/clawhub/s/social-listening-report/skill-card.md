## Description:

Creates social listening reports, benchmarks, competitive analyses, campaign performance summaries, trend analyses, and brand health reports from social and digital media data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[renehdzgtz](https://clawhub.ai/user/renehdzgtz)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing strategists, insights researchers, brand managers, and agency analysts use this skill to turn social and digital media data into structured listening, benchmark, campaign, periodic, trend, and training reports with recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad reporting phrases that are not actually about social listening or digital/social data.

Mitigation: Confirm the user task is about brand mentions, sentiment, campaign listening, social performance, or digital/social data before relying on the workflow.

Risk: Incomplete or low-quality source data can make listening reports and benchmarks misleading.

Mitigation: Ask for the available data source, period, platforms, and format before analysis, and state data-quality limitations in the output.
