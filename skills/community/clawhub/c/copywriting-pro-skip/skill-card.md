## Description:

Helps agents write, improve, rewrite, and review persuasive marketing and sales copy for ads, landing pages, emails, sales pages, headlines, product descriptions, CTAs, and social posts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, founders, sales teams, and external creators use this skill to draft and evaluate conversion-focused copy with audience research, copywriting frameworks, readability rules, and a preflight quality checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persuasive marketing output can be misleading if urgency, scarcity, social proof, or claims are not truthful.

Mitigation: Review copy for truthfulness, proportional urgency, substantiated claims, and real limits before publication.

Risk: Copy intended for health, finance, legal, or vulnerable audiences may create heightened consumer-protection risk.

Mitigation: Apply domain-specific review and avoid using the skill without expert approval for sensitive audiences or regulated claims.
