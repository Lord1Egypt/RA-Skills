## Description:

Runs short-horizon Bitcoin sentiment analysis for 15-minute Kalshi or trading decisions, summarizing key themes, directional bias, confidence, and a quick betting angle.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill as a fast second opinion before short-term Bitcoin or Kalshi wagering decisions. It is optimized for concise sentiment, bias, confidence, and bankroll-aware bet sizing guidance on 15-minute horizons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce speculative Bitcoin/Kalshi wagering guidance on very short timeframes.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as speculative decision support, not financial or gambling advice, and require independent user judgment before any wager.

Risk: Broad triggers may activate the skill for general Bitcoin or trading questions.

Mitigation: Enable it only when short-term BTC/Kalshi betting assistance is specifically desired.

Risk: The artifact includes bankroll and bet sizing suggestions that could encourage repeated wagering.

Mitigation: Keep bankroll limits explicit, avoid use by people who do not want wagering-oriented recommendations, and track outcomes before scaling any strategy.
