## Description:

Write compelling crowdfunding and fundraising campaign copy including titles, descriptions, story narratives, and social media posts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to draft crowdfunding and charity campaign copy, including campaign descriptions, donation appeals, titles, story narratives, and social media posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fundraising copy may include sensitive personal, medical, or financial details.

Mitigation: Decide what personal details are appropriate to share before drafting or publishing campaign content.

Risk: Generated campaign claims, amounts, timelines, or beneficiary details may be inaccurate or misleading.

Mitigation: Review the generated copy for factual accuracy and trust-building details before publication.
