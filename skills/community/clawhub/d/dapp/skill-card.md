## Description:

A comprehensive AI agent skill for discovering, evaluating, and monitoring decentralized applications. Tracks on-chain activity anomalies, evaluates smart contract security, identifies emerging dApps before they go mainstream, monitors your portfolio positions across DeFi protocols, analyzes tokenomics and incentive structures, and helps Web3 users and investors make faster and better-informed decisions in a market where information advantage measured in hours translates directly into financial outcomes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Web3 users, investors, developers, and analysts use this skill to discover, evaluate, and monitor decentralized applications, including on-chain activity anomalies, smart contract risk signals, DeFi positions, and tokenomics. It is research support for faster triage and decision-making, not financial advice or a substitute for a professional smart contract audit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat Web3 research output as financial advice or as a complete smart contract audit.

Mitigation: Present outputs as research support only and require independent review before trading, investing, or committing meaningful capital.

Risk: Users may expose private keys or grant signing authority while applying related Web3 workflows.

Mitigation: Keep private keys out of agent workflows and use read-only addresses or observation-only access for portfolio and protocol analysis.
