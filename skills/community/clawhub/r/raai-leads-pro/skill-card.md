## Description:

Lead generation and qualification system: ICP, source selection, outreach, nurture, reactivation, scoring and pipeline reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raaipro](https://clawhub.ai/user/raaipro)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business owners, marketers, and sales leaders use this skill to structure lead generation workflows, qualify leads with scoring, plan outreach and nurture sequences, and report funnel economics. The artifact is especially oriented toward Russian-language and Russia-market lead generation examples.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can encourage contact scraping, lead processing, or outreach that may violate platform terms, consent requirements, or opt-out obligations.

Mitigation: Use it only with lead data the user is allowed to process, respect platform terms, honor opt-outs, and avoid unauthorized scraping or bulk unsolicited outreach.

Risk: Bundled install/build scripts have reported syntax or security issues and the release lacks the expected .env.example.

Mitigation: Review and fix the support scripts and environment documentation before running install or build commands.

Risk: Lead scoring, ROI estimates, and funnel recommendations may be misleading when business inputs are invented, stale, or incomplete.

Mitigation: Require honest configuration data and human review of generated scores, economics, and channel decisions before acting on them.
