## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft handouts may be mistaken for legal advice or may omit case-specific nuance.

Mitigation: Use only for general bankruptcy client education and require review by a qualified attorney before client delivery or reliance.

Risk: Generated content may include inaccuracies or overstate next steps for a particular client matter.

Mitigation: Confirm jurisdiction, practice policies, and client-specific facts before using any generated material.
