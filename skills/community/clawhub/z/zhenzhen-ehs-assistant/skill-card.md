## Description:

ZhenZhen EHS Assistant helps EHS officers and safety inspectors draft scenario-based checklists, templates, SOP guidance, and regulation-anchored responses for daily inspection, hazard management, training, emergency response, audits, documentation, high-risk operations, fire safety, occupational health, and related EHS workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

EHS officers, safety inspectors, project safety leads, and related workplace safety staff use this skill to produce practical inspection checklists, rectification notices, training materials, emergency plans, audit preparation lists, safety meeting records, and other EHS work guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China-focused use; reviewers should adjust if the release is intended for other jurisdictions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory citations or EHS procedures may become outdated or may not match the user's jurisdiction or company policy.

Mitigation: Review outputs against current local regulations, regulator guidance, and organization-specific EHS procedures before relying on them.

Risk: Generated EHS checklists, notices, emergency plans, or templates could be mistaken for final professional decisions.

Mitigation: Use outputs as drafting support and require qualified EHS review before field execution, audit submission, or incident response use.

Risk: Installer or package integrity could vary by distribution path.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed or pinned ClawHub installer when available, or inspect and manually place the skill folder.
