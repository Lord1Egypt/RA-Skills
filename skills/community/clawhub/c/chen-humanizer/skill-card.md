## Description:

Chen Humanizer helps agents identify and rewrite AI-sounding prose patterns so text reads more naturally while preserving meaning and tone.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cs995279497-byte](https://clawhub.ai/user/cs995279497-byte)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and agents use this skill to revise AI-sounding text into clearer, more natural prose. It is intended for editing or reviewing drafts while preserving the original meaning and matching the intended tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be misused to hide AI assistance where disclosure is required by academic rules, employment rules, platform policies, or legal duties.

Mitigation: Use the skill only where rewriting is permitted, and preserve required disclosure of AI assistance.

Risk: Automated rewrites may alter meaning, tone, attribution, or factual nuance in important documents.

Mitigation: Review the revised text before applying it to important, public, legal, academic, or employment-related documents.
