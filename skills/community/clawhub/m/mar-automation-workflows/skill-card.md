## Use Case:

Solopreneurs and business operators use this skill to identify repetitive work, choose no-code automation tools, design workflows, test automations, and maintain them over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested automations can expose sensitive customer data through connected tools, notifications, or shared workflow outputs.

Mitigation: Limit connected-account permissions and avoid sending sensitive customer fields to notifications or destinations that do not require them.

Risk: Customer-facing emails, public posts, CRM status changes, and billing or accounting updates may have business impact if an automation is misconfigured.

Mitigation: Test workflows with non-production or controlled data and require human review before enabling high-impact automations.
