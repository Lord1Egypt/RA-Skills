## Description:

Use this skill when preparing candidates for Web3 product manager interviews, especially wallet, exchange, DeFi, DEX, on-chain data, growth, AI Wallet, Agentic Wallet, senior PM, product lead, or product director roles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[alexander10011](https://clawhub.ai/user/alexander10011)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External candidates and career-coaching agents use this skill to turn a resume, target JD, company context, interview stage, and preparation timeline into Web3 product-manager interview preparation. It supports fit diagnosis, full interview battle plans, mock interview scoring, case preparation, and post-interview debriefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste private career details such as raw recordings, recruiter messages, compensation details, named interviewer analysis, internal metrics, non-public company information, or unnecessary personal data.

Mitigation: Redact sensitive information before use and convert names into role labels such as hiring manager, wallet lead, cross-functional interviewer, bar raiser, or HRBP.

Risk: Wallet-related practice can expose seed phrases, private keys, or real funds if users test with production wallets.

Mitigation: Use testnets or burner wallets with trivial funds and never disclose seed phrases or private keys.

Risk: Interview coaching output can be incomplete or overconfident if the resume, JD, company context, or interview stage is missing or outdated.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preparation guidance, label assumptions, and review the final plan against the current target role and interview process.
