## Use Case:

External quality, regulatory, and operations teams at medical-device organizations use this skill to plan an ISO 13485:2016 quality management system, including scope, process mapping, quality objectives, document structure, internal audit preparation, and management review planning.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated ISO 13485 planning or compliance documents may be incomplete, outdated, or misapplied to a specific medical-device business, product risk class, or target market.

Mitigation: Have qualified regulatory or quality professionals review outputs before using them for certification, audits, or controlled QMS documentation.

Risk: Broad requests such as audit or certification preparation may produce generic quality-system guidance that does not match medical-device ISO 13485 needs.

Mitigation: Clarify that requests concern medical-device ISO 13485 work and specify applicable markets, product scope, organization type, and regulatory constraints.
