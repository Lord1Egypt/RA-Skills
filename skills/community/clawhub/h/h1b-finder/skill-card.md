## Description:

Find H1B / H-1B sponsor companies, visa sponsorship employers, and sponsor history for US jobs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ewangchong](https://clawhub.ai/user/ewangchong)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External job seekers, OPT candidates, and professionals use this skill to research US employers with historical H1B sponsorship activity by company, role, city, state, or year. It helps narrow sponsor leads and compare filing-history signals before applying.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The install example uses an unpinned npx command, which can depend on the currently resolved ClawHub CLI package.

Mitigation: Use a pinned or otherwise trusted ClawHub CLI version before installing.

Risk: H1B sponsor results may be delayed, incomplete, or not representative of current openings or sponsorship policies.

Mitigation: Treat results as research leads and verify important job, visa, and legal details with authoritative sources before acting.
