## Description:

Turns paid short-video clipping campaign briefs into disclosed clips, posting plans, checks, and payout records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators and social media teams use the skill to turn paid clipping campaign requirements into platform-ready short videos, disclosed posts, submission records, and payout evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence reports concerns with disclosure checks, file cleanup, data handling, and tool scope; the scan was partial.

Mitigation: Review those areas before use, limit account and file access to what the campaign needs, and retain the documented disclosure and pre-post checks.
