## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business planning or client communication guidance could be incomplete, misleading, or misaligned with the user's actual obligations.

Mitigation: Review generated priorities, proposals, follow-ups, renewal plans, and invoice messages before sending or acting on them.

Risk: Sensitive client, invoice, or relationship details may be processed in the current chat.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential details unless the active assistant environment is appropriate for that information; redact or summarize where possible.
