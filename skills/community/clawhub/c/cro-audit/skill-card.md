## Description:

Suggests CRO experiments and A/B test ideas for startups when someone wants help with conversion rate optimization, A/B testing, or improving website conversions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[c0ldsmi1e](https://clawhub.ai/user/c0ldsmi1e)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, growth teams, and startup operators use this skill to assess funnel performance and prioritize conversion experiments, quick wins, and A/B testing plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste private customer data, analytics credentials, or more funnel detail than needed while seeking CRO advice.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum funnel and analytics context needed for recommendations, and remove credentials or private customer data before using the skill.

Risk: Linked marketing articles may be useful background but are not security-sensitive or authoritative operational guidance.

Mitigation: Treat external reading links as optional context and validate testing decisions against the user's own analytics and business constraints.
