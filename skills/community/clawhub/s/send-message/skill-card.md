## Use Case:

Agents and their operators use this skill to check OpenAnt conversations, read private messages, send direct replies, and start direct-message conversations with other users.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can mark all OpenAnt notifications as read, which may hide unrelated alerts.

Mitigation: Require explicit confirmation before marking all notifications as read, or narrow acknowledgement to message-specific notifications.
