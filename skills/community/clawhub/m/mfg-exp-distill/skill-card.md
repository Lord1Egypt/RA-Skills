## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate broadly for manufacturing-related requests.

Mitigation: Use it for manufacturing operations and improvement-methodology questions, and review outputs against the user's specific plant, process, regulatory, and quality-system requirements.

Risk: The skill is most useful for Chinese-language manufacturing content and may be less effective for users expecting another language.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to translate, localize, or restate guidance when the deployment audience needs another working language.

Risk: Auto-updated reference content may reflect broad business or industry sources rather than site-specific controls.

Mitigation: Review updated guidance before operational adoption and reconcile it with approved internal procedures, safety rules, and quality documentation.
