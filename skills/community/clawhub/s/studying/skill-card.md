## Description:

Plans study schedules and runs study sessions with retrieval practice, spaced review, and exam countdown protocols.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External students and certification candidates use this skill to plan deadline-bound study, choose evidence-based techniques, run retrieval-focused study sessions, diagnose weak spots, and prepare for exam day without having the agent do the assignment itself.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may keep or migrate local study preferences, session logs, and exam notes.

Mitigation: Review or back up ~/Clawic/data/studying/ and any old study-data paths before installation if automatic study-history migration is not desired.

Risk: Study coaching can surface panic symptoms, escalating stimulant use, skipped meals, or sleep deprivation that are outside normal study-planning support.

Mitigation: Follow the artifact's red-flag guidance by pausing optimization and routing the student to campus counseling, a clinician, or another appropriate support channel.
