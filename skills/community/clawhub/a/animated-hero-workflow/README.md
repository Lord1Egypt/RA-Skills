Turn a Static Business Website Into a Living Site With an Animated Video Hero, in Plain Words

Give your AI agent one business website. It drafts the design system, colors, typography, and spacing, then drafts the brief for a clean brand image, then drafts the plan for a 5 to 6 second seamless loop, then drafts how the design and the loop come together as a full bleed video hero. The agent drafts, you make the image, you animate the loop, you combine it, and you deploy it. The agent never makes the image or the video itself, and never deploys a site itself.

Most small business websites look static and flat. The top of the page is a still picture and a headline, and nothing moves, so nothing catches the eye. This skill gives you a short moving hero at the top, one clean image, animated into a short loop, in the same colors and type as the rest of the site, so the page feels alive from the first second.

## What you get

- A design system template: business, audience, colors, typography, spacing, headline, button, image, loop, mobile fallback
- A brand image brief: one clean image, on brand, no clutter
- A loop plan: 5 to 6 seconds, seamless, start and end matching, what moves and how it moves
- A combine plan: the design plus the video loop as a full bleed video hero, where the video goes, where the headline goes, how the text stays readable
- A performance check: loop length, file size, and mobile fallback to a still image
- A handoff check, design system, image, loop, hero, and performance, each Checked, Missing, or Not confirmed, with pasted text quoted as the evidence
- A fix draft, labelled DRAFT, NOT SENT: handoff check summary, items checked, items missing, what to change first, and what to keep
- A missing checks list at the end, so nothing is hidden

## What a good hero looks like

- The colors, typography, and spacing are written down once, in plain words.
- One image, one loop of 5 to 6 seconds, one headline, and one button.
- The loop end matches the loop start, so it plays without a jump.
- The headline stays readable over the video.
- Every Checked item has pasted text quoted as the evidence.
- Missing items are listed first in the fix draft, in order.
- Nothing is called done until the owner has reviewed the draft.

## How to use it

1. Install the skill in your AI agent (see below).
2. Give your agent the business, the goal of the hero, and the audience, in plain words.
3. Ask: "Plan an animated hero with animated-hero-workflow."
4. Read the design system first. Every part of the hero must follow it.
5. Read the handoff check. Every Checked item needs pasted text quoted as the evidence. A short description alone is not evidence.
6. Read the fix draft. Approve the image and the loop before combining or deploying anything.

If you already give the business, the goal, and the audience up front, the skill does the full workflow and the handoff check in the same reply. It never stops at questions alone.
