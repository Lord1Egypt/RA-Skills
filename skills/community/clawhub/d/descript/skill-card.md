## Description:

The Descript skill guides agents through transcript-based editing of podcasts, interviews, talking-head video, and related talk content, including cleanup, own-voice Overdub corrections, multicam/captions, clips, show notes, and chapters with human review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, podcast teams, and social-content operators use this skill to plan transcript-first Descript edits, repurpose long recordings into clips and notes, and route finished exports for publication. It is designed for agent-assisted planning and connected tool actions where available, with humans verifying edits by ear and approving final media.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connected Descript, MCP, or WoopSocial actions can affect media edits, publishing workflows, or billing-sensitive AI-credit usage.

Mitigation: Connect these services only when agent-assisted media operations are intended, keep credit-aware workflow steps in place, and require human approval before publishing or performing material account-impacting actions.

Risk: Transcript-first editing and voice correction can misrepresent a guest or place synthetic words in a real voice.

Mitigation: Require human listen-through review, preserve interview meaning and clip context, use Overdub only for consent-verified own-voice word or phrase corrections, and disclose synthetic speech where required.
