## Description:

Analyzes academic paper title structures, classifies titles into supported structure types, and evaluates whether the structure fits the research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External academic writers, researchers, and reviewers use this skill to classify academic paper titles by structure type and assess whether a title matches the research content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-language guidance may be difficult for non-Chinese users to apply accurately.

Mitigation: Provide translation or language-choice guidance when the skill is used by non-Chinese readers.

Risk: Title-structure analysis can produce subjective recommendations that may not match a specific discipline or venue.

Mitigation: Review suggestions against the target field, research content, and publication requirements before revising a title.
