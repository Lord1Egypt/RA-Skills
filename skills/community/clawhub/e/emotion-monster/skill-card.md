Parents, caregivers, and agents supporting them use this skill to help children ages 2-6 recognize, name, and express emotions through Traditional Chinese emotion cards, diary prompts, coaching language, and calendar views.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mood diary entries may contain sensitive child or family information stored locally at ~/.bookshelf-plus/kids/mood_diary.json.

Mitigation: Install only if comfortable with local mood diary storage; on shared or backed-up devices, review local permissions, avoid entering identifying or highly private details, and delete the file when no longer needed.
