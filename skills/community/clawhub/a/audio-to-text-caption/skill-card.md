## Description:

Turn creator audio into clean text captions for ecommerce content and reuse. Use when teams need fast transcript-to-caption workflows.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Use Case:

External creators, ecommerce teams, and content operators use this skill to turn audio from short-video, live clip, and repurposing workflows into readable transcripts, captions, and review notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may submit audio they do not have permission to process.

Mitigation: Only process audio the user is authorized to transcribe, reuse, and transform into captions.

Risk: Unclear speech may be misrepresented as confident transcript text.

Mitigation: Flag unclear segments for manual review and do not invent speech that is not supported by the audio.

Risk: Business use may conflict with the non-commercial license terms stated in the skill artifact.

Mitigation: Confirm a separate paid commercial license from Razestar before using the skill for commercial workflows.
