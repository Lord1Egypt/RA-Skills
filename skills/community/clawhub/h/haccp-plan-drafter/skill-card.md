## Description:

Drafts HACCP and FSMA Preventive Controls food-safety plans for PCQI or HACCP-team review, including hazard analysis, CCP or preventive-control determination, monitoring, corrective actions, and recordkeeping.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Food-safety professionals, including PCQIs, HACCP coordinators, QA managers, and consultants, use this skill to draft or revise HACCP and FSMA Preventive Controls plans from product, process-flow, and hazard information. The output is intended for qualified team review before operational, audit, or certification use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft food-safety content may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for a specific facility, process, product, or audit scheme.

Mitigation: Require a qualified PCQI or HACCP team to validate hazards, critical limits, citations, monitoring procedures, corrective actions, and recordkeeping before operational or audit use.

Risk: Unsupported hazards, critical limits, process parameters, or monitoring frequencies could create misleading plan content.

Mitigation: Treat missing or unsupported inputs as unknown, require validation references, and keep unvalidated values flagged for PCQI or process-authority review.

Risk: The skill may be mistaken for formal approval, certification, or regulatory advice.

Mitigation: Keep every generated plan labeled as draft-only and require HACCP-team review and signature before implementation.
