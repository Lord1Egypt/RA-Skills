## Description:

Rethink how an app works on a phone (or computer): too many buttons, several AI assistants in different places, confusing navigation, a busy live or detail screen, long flows. Use for "UX", "redesign the mobile app", "simplify the UI", "mockups", "too many buttons", "merge the assistants", "new layout", "/shipyard:ux-redesign". Not for colours and fonts (use design).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[philipposk](https://clawhub.ai/user/philipposk)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to audit an app's navigation and workflows, compare established UX patterns, and produce phone mockups with step-counted redesign proposals. It can also guide small implementation pull requests behind a beta layout setting for the app team to review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: App-derived screen designs may expose proprietary or sensitive app details to Superdesign.

Mitigation: Confirm that external sharing is authorized before using the skill with app material.

Risk: Script analysis is incomplete, so the scan does not establish that the artifact is safe.

Mitigation: Review the skill and its scripts before installation, especially the portions not covered by the scan.
