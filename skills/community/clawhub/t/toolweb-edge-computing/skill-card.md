## Description:

Professional career development platform that generates personalized edge computing engineering roadmaps based on individual skills, experience, and career goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd](https://clawhub.ai/user/krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and organizations use this skill to request personalized edge computing engineering roadmaps, specialization pathways, learning paths, skill gaps, milestones, and next steps from an API-backed career guidance service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Roadmap requests may send skills, work experience, goals, session IDs, timestamps, and optional user IDs to the provider's API.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting secrets or highly sensitive personal details, and review the provider's privacy terms when retention or account tracking matters.
