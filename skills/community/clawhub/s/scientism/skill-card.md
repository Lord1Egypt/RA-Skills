## Description:

Provides a bilingual philosophical essay that argues for testability, correctability, and cumulative knowledge as standards for reliable claims and discusses their use in epistemology and AI governance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ontoai](https://clawhub.ai/user/ontoai)

### License/Terms of Use:

CC BY 4.0

## Use Case:

External readers, writers, and AI governance practitioners can use the essay to discuss epistemology, apply its principles of testability, correctability, and cumulative knowledge, or critically examine its claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers may mistake the essay’s factual examples and broad claims for established empirical findings.

Mitigation: Check primary sources and distinguish the author’s philosophical argument from empirical evidence before applying it in research or policy.
