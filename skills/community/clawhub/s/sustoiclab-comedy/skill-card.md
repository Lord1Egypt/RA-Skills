## Description:

脱口秀段子创作工具箱 · Su's StoicLab。基于格雷格迪安脱口秀方法论，融合素材挖掘技巧。覆盖笑话结构(目标假设->连接点->再解读)、笑话勘探器(5步创作流程)、打磨技巧(精简/留底/三段式)、视角切换(叙述者/本人/角色)和排练表演。原创作品 by 苏庶 @ 钝感实验室。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External creators and comedy writers use this Chinese-language skill to turn experiences, observations, and negative emotions into structured stand-up material. It guides topic discovery, setup and punchline design, joke polishing, perspective shifts, rehearsal, and performance review.
