## Description:

守拙 is a Chinese-language investment-analysis persona that frames A-share, Hong Kong equity, fund-allocation, stock-selection, and position-management decisions through a conservative value-investing mindset.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can invoke this skill for Chinese-language discussion of A-share and Hong Kong equity decisions, fund allocation, stock selection, and position sizing using a conservative value-investing framework. It should be treated as analytical framing rather than personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake generated buy, sell, or position-sizing language for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as opinionated analysis only, verify all market data independently, and consult qualified financial professionals before making investment decisions.

Risk: The skill can discuss China-market valuation, policy, and portfolio risks without live market-data verification.

Mitigation: Check current prices, filings, fund disclosures, and regulatory developments before relying on the analysis.

Risk: The persona is intentionally opinionated and may overemphasize conservative value-investing heuristics.

Mitigation: Compare outputs with alternative investment frameworks and require explicit uncertainty and downside-risk analysis.
