## Description:

辅助高校档案人员按事由原则分类归档文件，确定分类号、保管期限和档号，生成规范组卷方案供审阅。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Archive staff use this skill to classify completed university records, group related materials into case files, assign retention periods, and draft archival identifiers and inventories for official review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Archive records can include confidential, personnel, financial, or legally regulated material.

Mitigation: Provide only information authorized for processing and follow applicable confidentiality controls.

Risk: Retention, disclosure, or destruction recommendations may affect official records obligations.

Mitigation: Require official human archive review before acting on recommendations.
