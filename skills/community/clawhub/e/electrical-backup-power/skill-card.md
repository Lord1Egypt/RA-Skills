## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Electrical guidance may be incomplete or inappropriate for a user's local code, panel type, or installation requirements.

Mitigation: Advise users to follow local electrical standards and hire a licensed electrician for panel work, new circuits, transfer switches, interlock kits, and permitted work.

Risk: Generator misuse can cause carbon monoxide poisoning or dangerous backfeeding into utility lines.

Mitigation: Emphasize outdoor generator placement away from openings, working CO detectors, and safe connection through extension cords, a transfer switch, or an approved interlock installed by an electrician.

Risk: Installation through a package command carries normal supply-chain risk.

Mitigation: Use a pinned or otherwise trusted clawhub installer version and install from a low-privilege environment.
