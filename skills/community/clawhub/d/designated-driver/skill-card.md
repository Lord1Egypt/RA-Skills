## Use Case:

External users and small teams use this skill to plan, launch, and improve a designated-driver service business. It provides a 90-day cash-flow roadmap, channel strategy, operating process, tool categories, compliance reminders, and a 30-day execution checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the business plan as complete legal, insurance, platform-policy, or safety advice.

Mitigation: Verify local laws, platform policies, insurance coverage, driver eligibility, contracts, invoices, and safety requirements before acting on the guidance.

Risk: Generic monetization advice may lead to poor execution if applied without local demand, cost, and fulfillment validation.

Mitigation: Use the skill's MVP, tracking, weekly review, and checklist steps to validate one channel before scaling.
