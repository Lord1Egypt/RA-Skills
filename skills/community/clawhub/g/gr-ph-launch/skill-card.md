## Description: <br>
Product Hunt launch playbook for planning a T-14 preheat, T-0 launch day, and T+7 follow-up process, including hunter selection, maker comments, community seeding, comment cadence, and launch risk avoidance. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

External founders, product marketers, and startup operators use this skill to prepare and run a Product Hunt launch from pre-launch setup through launch-day operations and post-launch follow-up. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The playbook includes under-scoped guidance to export and import all Product Hunt upvoter email addresses. <br>
Mitigation: Only handle upvoter email addresses with a lawful basis, clear consent or platform permission, retention limits, and an approved CRM or user-pool process. <br>
Risk: Launch outreach guidance can be misused for vote manipulation, brigading, or automated posting. <br>
Mitigation: Keep outreach honest and manual, avoid incentivized or coordinated vote manipulation, and follow Product Hunt platform rules. <br>
