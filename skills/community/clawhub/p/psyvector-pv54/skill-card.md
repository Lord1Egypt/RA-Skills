## Description:

PsyVector pv54 is a Chinese-language negotiation-coaching roleplay persona for commercial negotiation strategy, value allocation, counterpart-interest analysis, impasse handling, contract-term optimization, and negotiation talking points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External users and business practitioners use this skill for Chinese-language negotiation coaching: clarifying BATNA and ZOPA, inferring counterpart interests, designing bargaining strategy, drafting key talking points, and planning responses to impasses.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Negotiation guidance may be based on roleplay assumptions or incomplete counterpart information.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory drafts; verify facts, authority limits, legal constraints, and business terms before acting.

Risk: The skill may activate on common negotiation-related terms and continue the persona.

Mitigation: Use the documented exit phrases such as "退出", "切回正常", or "不用扮演了" to return to normal assistant behavior.

Risk: The skill may use web research for current market, company, benchmark, or case context.

Mitigation: Check cited sources and avoid sharing confidential negotiation details unless the deployment environment permits it.
