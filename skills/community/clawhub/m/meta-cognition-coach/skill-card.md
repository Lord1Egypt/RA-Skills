## Description:

Meta-Cognition Coach helps students develop thinking-about-thinking skills, reflection habits, and learning strategy awareness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and learning-support agents use this skill to generate reflection prompts, strategy suggestions, and self-monitoring guidance without providing direct answers or completing assignments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Students may enter personal learning struggles, assignments, or study habits that are saved locally in plaintext log files.

Mitigation: Review before installing in student environments, disclose the local logging behavior, and delete or protect generated log files when they are no longer needed.
