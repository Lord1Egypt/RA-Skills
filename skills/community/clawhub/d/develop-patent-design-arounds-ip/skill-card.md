## Description:

Develop and screen single-patent design-around concepts using an application-requirements baseline, claim-feature and functional reconstruction, TRIZ trimming, evidence-backed function-oriented search, differentiated concept engineering, and jurisdiction-specific claim risk review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Patent, product, and engineering teams use this skill to develop technically plausible alternatives to one target patent and produce a preliminary claim-risk screen for counsel and engineering review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent design-around outputs could be mistaken for legal opinions or freedom-to-operate conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat every output as preliminary screening and require review by qualified counsel before relying on it.

Risk: Confidential product architecture or patent strategy may be exposed through external PatSnap patent connectors.

Mitigation: Use the connectors only when organizational policy permits the data sharing, and work locally from authorized materials when confidential data cannot be sent externally.

Risk: Incomplete claims, jurisdiction, prosecution history, or engineering facts can make risk classifications unreliable.

Mitigation: Record assumptions, mark unresolved legal or engineering questions, and validate concepts with authoritative patent records, primary legal authority, and engineering tests.
