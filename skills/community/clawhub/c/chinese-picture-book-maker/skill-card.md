## Description:

Use when turning any Chinese children's story, fable, classroom reading text, or original plot into a cute picture book for preschool or primary school readers, especially when the task needs age-appropriate rewriting, page-by-page storyboard captions, consistent character design, and image-generation prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to adapt Chinese children's stories into age-appropriate picture-book storyboards. It helps produce short Chinese captions, consistent character anchors, and image-generation prompts for coherent page illustrations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill tends to produce Chinese captions by default, which may not match a user's requested language or output format.

Mitigation: Ask for or state the desired language and format before using the generated storyboard or prompts.

Risk: Generated storyboards or image prompts for children's content may still need review for age suitability, readability, and illustration consistency.

Mitigation: Review captions, scene order, character anchors, and prompts before creating or publishing final picture-book pages.
