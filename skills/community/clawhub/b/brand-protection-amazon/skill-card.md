## Description:

Amazon brand protection toolkit for detecting hijackers, counterfeits, unauthorized sellers, MAP violations, and trademark abuse, with complaint templates and test-buy evidence guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers and brand-protection teams use this skill to review Amazon listing risks, identify unauthorized seller or counterfeit signals, and draft escalation materials for Brand Registry, seller outreach, or evidence collection.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The install command depends on a package and repository outside NVIDIA control.

Mitigation: Before installing, verify the skills package and repository, prefer a pinned version or commit if available, and avoid running the install command with elevated privileges.

Risk: Generated complaint and seller-contact templates may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific legal dispute.

Mitigation: Treat generated legal and complaint templates as drafts for human review before sending them to Amazon or sellers.
