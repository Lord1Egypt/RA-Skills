## Description:

Find global B2B prospects with LeadsDaddy via MCP for importers, distributors, wholesalers, restaurant supply buyers, packaging buyers, regional channel partners, and other commercial prospects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales, export, and business development users use this skill to guide an agent through LeadsDaddy MCP workflows for finding, reviewing, and selectively unlocking likely B2B prospects, distributors, importers, wholesalers, and channel partners in target markets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External paid-service integration may expose confidential prospecting inputs or customer context if used without review.

Mitigation: Use a least-privileged LeadsDaddy token and avoid sharing confidential customer data unless authorized.

Risk: Lead unlock actions may incur costs or disclose additional lead details.

Mitigation: Confirm unlock budget and authorization, review lead fit before unlocking, and start with a small batch when quality is uncertain.

Risk: Generated prospect lists may include noisy or low-fit companies.

Mitigation: Classify results as high-fit, possible-fit, or low-fit and avoid presenting prospects as confirmed buyers without review.
