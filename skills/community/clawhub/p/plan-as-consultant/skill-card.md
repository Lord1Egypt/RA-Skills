## Description:

Plan a business research study by selecting an analytical framework, designing desk and user research, and defining a specific actionable output.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[owenrao](https://clawhub.ai/user/owenrao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business, product, marketing, and strategy users use this skill to turn ambiguous research questions into concise plans for product decisions, user understanding, market analysis, pricing, positioning, feature prioritization, or competitive strategy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad planning prompts.

Mitigation: Review the generated research plan for fit before acting on it, especially when the prompt did not explicitly request business research planning.

Risk: The skill references a separate interview-running skill that is not part of this artifact.

Mitigation: Review that separate skill independently before using it to conduct interviews or group discussions.
