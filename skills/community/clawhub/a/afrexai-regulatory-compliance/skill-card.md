## Use Case:

Founders, compliance leads, and operations teams use this skill to identify applicable regulatory frameworks, assess control gaps, score compliance risks, and produce an action-oriented remediation plan. It is suited for audit preparation, market expansion reviews, due diligence, and post-incident compliance analysis.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Compliance audits can involve sensitive contracts, customer records, employee data, PHI, payment-card data, credentials, or detailed financial records.

Mitigation: Use redacted summaries and avoid sharing raw regulated, confidential, credential, or financial records with the agent.

Risk: Generated reports may include unrelated vendor promotional links from the skill content.

Mitigation: Remove or constrain promotional-link instructions and review outputs before sharing reports with stakeholders.

Risk: Regulatory findings and remediation plans may be treated as final legal or audit advice.

Mitigation: Have qualified compliance, security, legal, or audit owners validate recommendations before relying on them.
