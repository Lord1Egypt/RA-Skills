## Description:

Geo Local Optimizer helps agents create structured local GEO plans for local businesses that want clearer visibility in AI-powered local search, map-style results, reviews, local pages, and service-area discovery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, agencies, and business operators use this skill to plan local-business GEO improvements across location pages, service-area pages, map and review profiles, structured data, crawler signaling, Q&A, and measurement workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad activation wording for local-business marketing and discoverability requests.

Mitigation: Review whether proactive invocation for local SEO, GEO, maps, listings, store pages, reviews, and service-area requests fits the workspace before enabling it.

Risk: The skill may produce local business recommendations, structured data examples, review scripts, and listing guidance that affect public marketing claims.

Mitigation: Have a responsible operator verify business facts such as address, phone, hours, service area, pricing, practitioner credentials, regulatory claims, and platform-specific listing details before publication.
