## Description:

Generates local brand-protection risk reports and Amazon-oriented complaint templates for unauthorized seller, counterfeit, MAP, image theft, and trademark scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

E-commerce brand operators and support agents use this skill to draft brand-protection reports and enforcement templates from supplied brand and listing data. Reviewers should verify actual Shopify, domain, and social-media coverage before relying on it for those workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Security evidence says the skill appears to be a local Amazon-focused demo/template generator, while its documentation substantially overstates Shopify/DTC, domain, social-media, DMCA, and Shopify abuse coverage.

Mitigation: Treat generated reports and templates as drafting aids; verify real Shopify/DTC, domain, and social-media monitoring coverage before operational use.

Risk: Security evidence notes an unpinned global npx install command.

Mitigation: Install from a pinned version or immutable commit, and avoid running global installs from a privileged account.

Risk: Generated takedown, cease-and-desist, and platform complaint text may not fit the user's jurisdiction, evidence, or platform policy requirements.

Mitigation: Have a qualified legal or policy reviewer approve templates and supporting evidence before sending complaints or notices.
