## Description:

Renovation Skill is a China-focused home renovation advisor that helps homeowners plan budgets, avoid material and construction pitfalls, review renovation contracts, and manage renovation stages from rough shell to move-in.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External homeowners and renovation decision-makers use this skill to get stage-specific advice for China-market renovation budgeting, material selection, construction acceptance checks, contract review, and process planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Price ranges, standards, and renovation practices may not match a user's city, project scope, or current local rules.

Mitigation: Treat the skill's renovation ranges and standards as reference material and verify local requirements, market prices, and contractor assumptions before acting.

Risk: Advice about structural changes, gas, electrical work, safety-critical work, or contract terms could be mistaken for qualified professional judgment.

Mitigation: Use qualified local professionals for structural, gas, electrical, safety, and legal contract decisions.
