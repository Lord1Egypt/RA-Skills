## Description:

Segment customers into groups with similar needs and behaviors. Use for targeting strategy, product development, and marketing strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing strategists, product teams, and business planners use this skill to define actionable customer segments for targeting, positioning, product development, and marketing strategy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer segmentation based on incomplete or biased customer data can produce misleading strategy recommendations.

Mitigation: Validate segments with authorized, representative, and current customer data before using the recommendations for business decisions.

Risk: Real customer data may include sensitive or regulated information.

Mitigation: Use only customer data you are authorized to share, and prefer aggregated or anonymized inputs where possible.
