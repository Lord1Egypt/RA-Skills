## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Node, validator, relayer, or IPFS setup guidance may include commands such as remote shell installers that should not be run blindly.

Mitigation: Verify official sources, checksums, or signatures before execution, and prefer isolated or test environments for setup steps.

Risk: Examples may involve private keys, seed phrases, broad JWTs, or production signing authority.

Mitigation: Never paste real secrets into copied examples; use limited test credentials and environment-specific secret handling.

Risk: IPFS uploads can be public and persistent.

Mitigation: Review content before upload and avoid publishing sensitive or private data to IPFS gateways.
