## Description:

Help patients find suitable hospitals based on medical needs, location, hospital level, and specialties.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to choose hospital options based on symptoms, location, urgency, hospital level, and specialty needs. It supports comparison and recommendation workflows while keeping treatment decisions outside scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat hospital recommendations as medical advice.

Mitigation: State that recommendations are logistical guidance only and direct users to clinicians for diagnosis and treatment decisions.

Risk: Hospital details, availability, specialties, and emergency access may change.

Mitigation: Tell users to confirm current hospital information, location, insurance acceptance, and availability before visiting.

Risk: Life-threatening situations require urgent local emergency response.

Mitigation: Direct users to call their local emergency number immediately for emergencies; for the China-oriented workflow, mention 120 when appropriate.
