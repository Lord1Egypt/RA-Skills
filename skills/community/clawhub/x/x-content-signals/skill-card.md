## Description:

Rewrite X posts for algorithm-relevant actions. Use when drafting posts to maximize dwell, replies, reposts, shares, profile clicks, follows, and low negative feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to rewrite rough X post ideas into publish-ready drafts optimized for a chosen engagement signal while checking likely negative-feedback risks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated X posts may be inaccurate, off-tone, or misaligned with platform policy.

Mitigation: Review each generated post for factual accuracy, tone, and platform-policy fit before publishing.

Risk: Engagement optimization can create negative audience feedback if the draft overuses controversy or bait.

Mitigation: Use the skill's negative-feedback risk check and avoid controversy unless the user explicitly requests it.
