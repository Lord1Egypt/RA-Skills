## Description:

Helps users design, construct, or improve sellable offers by working through value framing, bonus stacking, guarantee design, scarcity and urgency, naming, and payment structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this skill to evaluate and improve offers for services, courses, coaching, information products, high-ticket B2B, and direct-response campaigns. It guides offer diagnosis and drafting across value proposition, proof, guarantees, scarcity, bonuses, naming, pricing, and payment structure.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Offer advice may produce guarantee, refund, scarcity, or claims language that does not fit a user's brand, margins, or legal obligations.

Mitigation: Review drafted terms with appropriate commercial and legal stakeholders before publishing, and ensure claims are specific, supported, and operationally deliverable.

Risk: Scarcity or urgency tactics can mislead customers if the stated capacity, deadline, inventory, or bonus limit is not real.

Mitigation: Use only constraints the business can verify and enforce, and remove or update scarcity language when the constraint changes.

Risk: Bonus value claims can become inflated or unverifiable, reducing trust and increasing customer-risk exposure.

Mitigation: Tie stated bonus values to real standalone prices or credible comparables, and keep total claimed bonus value proportionate to the offer price.
