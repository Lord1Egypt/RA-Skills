## Description:

Guide a weekly reflection across Work, Health, Relationships, and Growth, then turn the review into one clear pattern plus Stop, Start, Continue, and one experiment for next week.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to run a structured weekly review across work, health, relationships, and growth. It helps convert concrete weekly evidence into a balanced markdown reflection with one pattern and practical next-week adjustments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill processes personal weekly notes that may contain sensitive life, health, work, or relationship details.

Mitigation: Avoid entering highly sensitive details unless the user is comfortable sharing them with the agent running the skill.

Risk: Reflection output could overstate certainty from limited notes.

Mitigation: Keep conclusions evidence-based and avoid claiming psychological certainty from the user's notes.

Risk: During crisis or overload weeks, ambitious planning may be unhelpful.

Mitigation: Prioritize stabilization before ambition and keep next-week adjustments small and practical.
