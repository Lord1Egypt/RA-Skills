## Description:

Audit mobile shopping flows, identify conversion friction, prioritize UX issues, and suggest experiment backlogs for product teams, CRO specialists, and e-commerce operators.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product teams, CRO specialists, and e-commerce operators use this skill to review mobile storefronts, PDPs, carts, checkouts, reorder flows, screenshots, or flow notes and turn conversion friction into a prioritized UX backlog.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The audit is heuristic and may be mistaken for evidence from real analytics, heatmaps, or session replay tools.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as hypotheses and validate prioritization with analytics, usability research, or experiment results before making high-impact commerce changes.

Risk: Recommendations can overstate certainty when the user provides incomplete screenshots or flow notes.

Mitigation: State evidence gaps, avoid guaranteed uplift claims, and ask for missing stage context when coverage is incomplete.
