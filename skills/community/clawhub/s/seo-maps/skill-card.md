## Description:

Maps intelligence for local SEO, including geo-grid rank tracking, GBP profile audits, review intelligence, cross-platform NAP verification, competitor radius mapping, and LocalBusiness schema generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketers, local SEO practitioners, and developers use this skill to evaluate local business visibility across maps platforms, choose an available free or paid API tier, and produce prioritized maps SEO recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid API geo-grid scans may consume DataForSEO credits.

Mitigation: Review the displayed cost estimate and require confirmation before running paid scans.

Risk: Generated reports may contain business, location, review, and competitive-analysis data.

Mitigation: Handle generated Markdown reports according to the user's data handling expectations and avoid sharing them outside the intended audience.

Risk: A broad maps-related request could invoke maps SEO analysis unintentionally.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation with /seo maps or another clear maps-SEO request.
