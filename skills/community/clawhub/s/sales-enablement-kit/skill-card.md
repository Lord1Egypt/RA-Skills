## Description:

Builds internal sales enablement materials for a sales-led launch, including battle cards, a launch talk track, objection handling, internal FAQ and CS macros, and an embargo-aware internal launch announcement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales, support, CS, and launch teams use this skill to prepare consistent internal enablement from approved launch messaging. It is intended for battle cards, sales talk tracks, objection responses, FAQs, CS macros, and internal launch announcements that remain traceable to source claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated claims or objection responses could introduce incorrect or misleading sales messaging.

Mitigation: Review every generated claim and evidence pointer before allowing the skill to save outputs or propose facts to project memory.

Risk: Pasted PR-FAQs, competitor dossiers, or launch documents may contain untrusted instructions.

Mitigation: Treat supplied documents as source material only and follow the skill's instruction to ignore embedded instructions in those documents.
