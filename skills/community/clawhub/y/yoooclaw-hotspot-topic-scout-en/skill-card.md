## Description:

Helps content creators, editors, and operations leads identify timely topic ideas, high-performing content references, competitor activity, and audience needs from mobile notifications and web trend searches.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vivalavida-say-hi](https://clawhub.ai/user/vivalavida-say-hi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content creators, editors, and operations leads use this skill to convert monitored notification signals and recent web trends into prioritized topic inspiration, popular-content references, competitive updates, and fan-need summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read recent mobile notifications containing private chats, workplace discussions, fan messages, or third-party content.

Mitigation: Use narrow time ranges and explicitly limit the allowed apps, groups, accounts, and topics before running notification searches.

Risk: Private or internal notification signals can become derived search terms and summaries when the skill supplements findings with external web searches.

Mitigation: Avoid using the skill on confidential team plans or personal messages unless external search and session summarization of derived terms is acceptable.
