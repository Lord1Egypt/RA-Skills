## Description:

Use when conducting deep product opportunity research or feature prioritization: 6 specialized agents analyze user needs, workflow, technical feasibility, system integration, compliance, and business model considerations, then cross-examine results to produce opportunity maps, priority lists, product roadmaps, and business packaging strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[joe-rq](https://clawhub.ai/user/joe-rq)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, product strategists, founders, and product teams use this skill to structure deep product opportunity research, prioritize new products or features, evaluate market and implementation tradeoffs, and turn findings into roadmap, validation, and packaging recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad market, opportunity, or strategy prompts may activate a full research workflow when a lighter analysis would be enough.

Mitigation: Confirm the research scope, assumptions, and desired depth before starting the deeper multi-agent phases.

Risk: Opportunity, compliance, and commercial recommendations may include unvalidated assumptions that affect roadmap or pricing decisions.

Mitigation: Treat generated reports as decision support and review key claims with product, legal, technical, and business owners before acting.
