## Use Case:

Supply-chain, procurement, strategy, and manufacturing risk teams use this skill to produce Chinese-language volatility watch reports for a target company's upstream materials and components. It supports monitoring recent price pressure, supply disruptions, substitution feasibility, geopolitical exposure, and likely business impact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad company stability or outlook questions that are not specifically about supply-chain risk.

Mitigation: Phrase requests with an explicit supply-chain, upstream-materials, or cost-pressure scope when that is the intended analysis.

Risk: Reports rely on current web sources for prices, policy changes, disruptions, and company disclosures, and those sources may be stale, incomplete, or inconsistent.

Mitigation: Check source dates and quality before using the report for business decisions, and keep sourced facts separate from labeled inferences.

Risk: Cost impact and resilience estimates may use industry averages when company-specific disclosures are unavailable.

Mitigation: Treat labeled inferences as decision support and replace assumptions with internal procurement, inventory, supplier, and finance data where available.
