## Description:

Check and remove hidden metadata from photos, PDFs, and Word documents before sharing files.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[warren455](https://clawhub.ai/user/warren455)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and agents use this skill when preparing to email, upload, publish, or share photos, PDFs, or Word documents that may contain hidden metadata. It guides users to review supported metadata, use the relevant RemoveMetadata cleanup page, download the cleaned file, and verify the result before sharing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may send sensitive photos or documents to an external cleanup website without reviewing its privacy terms.

Mitigation: Review the linked site's privacy terms before using it with sensitive files.

Risk: Users may overestimate metadata cleanup and assume it guarantees anonymity or removes visible content, watermarks, platform logs, or prior copies.

Mitigation: Describe the workflow as removal of supported hidden metadata, avoid anonymity claims, and verify the cleaned file before sharing.
