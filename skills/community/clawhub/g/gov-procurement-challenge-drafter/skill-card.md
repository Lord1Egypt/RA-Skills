## Description:

基于现行政府采购法律法规，帮助供应商或采购从业人员起草和检查政府采购质疑函、投诉书，并提示法定时限和不予受理风险。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External suppliers and procurement practitioners use this skill to draft or revise Chinese government procurement challenge letters and complaints, check required legal elements, and identify deadline or acceptance risks before filing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated legal citations, dates, evidence references, or local finance-authority practice may be incomplete or unsuitable for filing.

Mitigation: Review all citations, deadlines, source procurement documents, evidence attachments, and local filing requirements before submission; consult a qualified lawyer for significant rights or high-value matters.

Risk: The skill may be misapplied to procurement contract disputes or tender-bidding-law matters outside its stated scope.

Mitigation: Use it only for Chinese Government Procurement Law challenge letters and complaints, and redirect contract disputes, bid-law objections, and other legal documents to appropriate expertise.
