AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to convert Proactive Agent-style productivity requests into concrete plans, checklists, workflow artifacts, code changes, analysis, or decision support. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad keywords and implicit invocation may cause the skill to activate for loosely related productivity or task-management requests.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or tighter local routing rules when predictable activation is important.

Risk: Workflow or implementation guidance may be incomplete or mismatched to the user's actual environment.

Mitigation: Validate outputs against the stated success criteria and review assumptions, limits, and follow-up work before relying on results.
