## Description:

Combines up to eight product images into a coordinated look on one model while aiming to preserve each item's details.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers and catalog teams use this skill to create coordinated outfit imagery from multiple product photos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and reference images may be sent to a third-party image provider for generation.

Mitigation: Check which provider receives the inputs, and avoid sending sensitive or identifiable images unless authorized and comfortable with that disclosure.

Risk: The security review reports undeclared tool scope and only partial analysis of some content and scripts.

Mitigation: Review the skill's commands and permissions before running it, and account for the incomplete scan.
