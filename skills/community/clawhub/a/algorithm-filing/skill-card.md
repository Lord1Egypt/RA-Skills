## Description:

Helps users plan China CAC large-model and algorithm filings by assessing filing type, preparing material checklists, outlining security assessment reports, guiding submissions, and managing post-filing compliance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bettermen](https://clawhub.ai/user/bettermen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External developers, product teams, and compliance teams use this skill to determine whether AI services require China CAC large-model registration, algorithm filing, or related security assessment work, then prepare checklists, report outlines, submission guidance, rejection responses, and post-filing compliance plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may prompt users to prepare reviewer or unrestricted test-account credentials in filing documents.

Mitigation: Use least-privilege, isolated, short-lived reviewer accounts and transfer credentials only through an approved secure process.

Risk: The skill gives authoritative regulatory guidance for China CAC filing workflows that may change over time.

Mitigation: Verify current CAC requirements with counsel or official sources before relying on generated filing materials or submitting them.
