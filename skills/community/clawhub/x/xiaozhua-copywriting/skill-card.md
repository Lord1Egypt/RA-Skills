## Description:

Helps agents rewrite Chinese or English copy by adapting wording, tone, detail, and formality for workplace reporting, peer discussion, customer service replies, group messages, and email drafting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and other writers use this skill to turn rough communication drafts into concise, audience-aware wording for bosses, coworkers, customers, group chats, and email.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided drafts may contain sensitive or confidential text submitted for rewriting.

Mitigation: Provide only text that is appropriate to share with the agent for rewriting.

Risk: The rewritten copy may use the wrong language, tone, or level of formality when the user's intent is ambiguous.

Mitigation: Specify the target audience, role relationship, scenario, and whether Chinese or English output is required.
