## Description:

Academic Writing Custom helps agents produce scholarly Markdown for papers, literature reviews, research methods, and thesis writing with strict citation, source, structure, and quality checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangzhi-collab](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangzhi-collab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, students, researchers, and academic writers use this skill to draft and refine scholarly papers, literature reviews, research methodology sections, and thesis-style content with formal structure and citation discipline.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill enforces a rigid Markdown, citation, and <ama-doc> response format that may be unsuitable for non-academic contexts.

Mitigation: Use the skill for academic writing tasks and review outputs before using them in other communication formats.

Risk: Academic citations can still be incorrect or unsuitable even when the skill instructs the agent not to fabricate references.

Mitigation: Verify cited sources, citation order, and reference metadata against legitimate academic databases before publication or submission.
