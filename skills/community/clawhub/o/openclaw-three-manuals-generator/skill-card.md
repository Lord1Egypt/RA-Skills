## Description:

Guides users through a dialogue to generate personalized SOUL.md, USER.md, and AGENTS.md files for an OpenClaw AI companion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

OpenClaw users and teams use this skill to clarify their desired AI companion behavior through guided questions, then produce personalized SOUL.md, USER.md, and AGENTS.md configuration/manual files.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated personal preference files may contain sensitive personal details that future agents can read.

Mitigation: Review SOUL.md, USER.md, and AGENTS.md before writing them, and omit secrets, account details, and sensitive identifiers.

Risk: Suggested follow-on skills may be installed without separate review.

Mitigation: Treat follow-on skill recommendations as optional and verify each skill independently before installation.
