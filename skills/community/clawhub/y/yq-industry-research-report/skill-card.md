## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to generate full industry, market, company, or technology research reports with sourced analysis, charts, and final Markdown, DOCX, and PDF deliverables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may route broad business, market, company, or technology questions into a mandatory full-report workflow when a concise answer would be more appropriate.

Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before invoking the skill for ordinary questions or when the user has not asked for a complete report.

Risk: The skill can trigger web research, image generation, file creation, DOCX generation, and PDF output.

Mitigation: Run it in a controlled workspace and review sources, generated images, charts, and final files before sharing or relying on the report.
