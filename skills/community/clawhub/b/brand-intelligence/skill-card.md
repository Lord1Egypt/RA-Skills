## Description:

Search, compare, and analyze 5,600+ companies across 30 verticals using AI visibility scores, knowledge graph data, and competitive intelligence from geo.sig.ai.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sigaihealth](https://clawhub.ai/user/sigaihealth)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this plugin to research companies, compare competitors, find alternatives, and prepare concise brand or watchlist intelligence briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Brand search terms, company slugs, capability queries, and watchlist brand lists are sent to geo.sig.ai or to the configured baseUrl.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive internal project names or confidential watchlists unless disclosure to that service is acceptable.

Risk: Managed deployments may inherit dependency or configuration risk from unreviewed package versions or custom baseUrl settings.

Mitigation: Use reviewed dependency versions and approved configuration values before deployment.
