Developers, engineers, researchers, and technical users use this skill to explain, analyze, and evaluate technologies from scientific and engineering perspectives, especially materials science and engineering topics. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad technology-style prompts may invoke this skill when a user expects narrower domain routing.

Mitigation: Use more specific trigger wording or route this skill only for materials science, engineering technology, and related explanation requests when strict routing is required.

Risk: Generated technical explanations can be incomplete or inaccurate when no supporting context is supplied.

Mitigation: Review important scientific or engineering claims against trusted sources or supplied technical documents before using the output for consequential decisions.
