飞影数字人 V2 guides agents through digital-human video production, including script planning, avatar and voice selection, emotional expression settings, lip-sync configuration, and quality review.

## Use Case:

Developers, creators, and marketing teams use this skill to plan and configure digital-human videos for product demos, technical education, roadshows, brand promotion, and short-form content. The skill is best treated as a one-way video generation guide rather than an emotional companionship or counseling workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes emotion recognition and companion-style emotional support framing.

Mitigation: Limit use to one-way digital-human video generation and avoid counseling, virtual companion, or emotional-dependency workflows.

Risk: Digital-human workflows can involve face or voice samples.

Mitigation: Use only face or voice material collected with clear consent and appropriate rights for the intended video.

Risk: The skill recommends multiple third-party video, avatar, TTS, and synthesis services.

Mitigation: Review provider terms, privacy handling, pricing, and output quality before using recommendations in a production workflow.
