## Description:

Provides simplified single-agent stock research for A-share, Hong Kong, and U.S. equities using market data, recent news, regulatory checks, and concise rating-oriented guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tanteng](https://clawhub.ai/user/tanteng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors, analysts, and agents use this skill to produce a concise stock review with current market context, core thesis points, support and resistance levels, regulatory risk checks, and action-oriented guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may provide stock ratings and action suggestions that users could mistake for standalone investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research assistance only and verify financial data, news, and conclusions independently before making investment decisions.

Risk: Market data, news, and regulatory events may be incomplete, stale, or unavailable from the tools used during a run.

Mitigation: Cross-check recent prices, filings, penalties, and policy developments with authoritative market and regulatory sources.
