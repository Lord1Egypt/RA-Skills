## Use Case:

External users and employees use this Chinese-language skill to rehearse presentations, explanations, reviews, and negotiations. It challenges unclear claims, identifies likely audience objections, and suggests simpler wording for different listeners.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic Chinese phrasing requests such as asking how to say or explain something may route ordinary wording questions to this skill.

Mitigation: Use this skill when challenge-style review, communication rehearsal, or audience-specific explanation coaching is intended.

Risk: Communication coaching may produce advice that is incomplete, too adversarial, or mismatched to the real audience.

Mitigation: Review the suggested challenges and wording against the actual audience, relationship context, and business goals before using them.
