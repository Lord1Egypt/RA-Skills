## Use Case:

Researchers, analysts, and teams with research questions use this skill to have an agent plan and execute a full research cycle, including literature synthesis, hypothesis generation, analysis design, execution, and cited synthesis under human oversight.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-led research may produce plausible but unsupported or incorrect claims.

Mitigation: Require citations for factual claims, cross-verify critical facts across multiple sources, flag confidence levels, and have a human validate key findings before publication.

Risk: Broad research autonomy may allow the agent to choose weak methods or drift beyond the intended scope.

Mitigation: Define the research question, constraints, and success criteria up front, then require human review of methodology, intermediate results, and consequential course corrections.

Risk: Research outputs may reflect source bias or incomplete source coverage.

Mitigation: Diversify source types, document source selection criteria, check for representation gaps, and include limitations in the final synthesis.

Risk: Unclear attribution or undisclosed AI involvement can create academic integrity concerns.

Mitigation: Cite all sources, disclose AI involvement where appropriate, and ensure a human takes responsibility for final research outputs.
