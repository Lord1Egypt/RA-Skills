## Description:

Generates dot-line terminal ASCII art from short text, including Chinese input converted to pinyin.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiaosai](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiaosai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use Dotline Art to turn short text into terminal-friendly dot-line banners and CLI ASCII art. It is especially useful when a prompt asks for an ASCII art banner or Chinese text rendered as pinyin-based terminal art.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese text and broad banner requests may be transformed instead of preserved exactly.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when pinyin-style terminal art is acceptable, and review generated banners before using them in user-facing contexts.
