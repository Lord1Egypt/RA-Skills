## Description:

Help a user rehearse a necessary hard conversation with a clear opening, likely responses, steady replies, and a boundary line.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and agents use this skill to prepare calm, direct language for hard conversations. It helps clarify the goal, draft an opening, anticipate likely responses, prepare steady replies, set a boundary, and close with a next step.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be insufficient for emergencies, ongoing abuse, threats, coercion, legal strategy, or safety planning.

Mitigation: Use appropriate local emergency, crisis, legal, or specialist support for those situations instead of relying on conversation rehearsal alone.

Risk: The generated rehearsal language may not prevent a bad reaction from the other person.

Mitigation: Review the brief before use and adapt it to the user's context, safety needs, and available support.
