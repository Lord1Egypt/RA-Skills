## Description:

Choose and implement the right Circle wallet type for your application, comparing developer-controlled, user-controlled, and modular passkey wallets across custody model, key management, account types, blockchain support, and use cases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mscandlen3](https://clawhub.ai/user/mscandlen3)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill when selecting a Circle wallet model for onchain applications. It helps compare custody, authentication, account type, gas sponsorship, and chain support before delegating implementation to the appropriate Circle wallet skill.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad wallet-related prompts may steer the agent toward Circle wallet options even when a vendor-neutral comparison is intended.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for non-Circle alternatives or vendor-neutral criteria when Circle-specific guidance is not desired.
