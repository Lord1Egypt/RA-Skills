## Description:

Redesign paywall and upgrade screens for SaaS products by assessing conversion blockers and producing a conversion-optimized upgrade screen specification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[flynndavid](https://clawhub.ai/user/flynndavid)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External SaaS founders, product managers, and growth teams use this skill to diagnose a current paywall and generate upgrade screen copy, plan structure, CTA variants, risk reversal, social proof placement, and downgrade-path guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for business-sensitive marketing details such as paywall copy, pricing, target customers, objections, and social proof.

Mitigation: Share only information appropriate for the agent session, redact confidential metrics or customer names, and review outputs before use.

Risk: Paywall and pricing recommendations may not match a specific product, market, or customer segment.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a draft, validate changes with product analytics or experiments, and avoid fake scarcity or unsupported social proof.
