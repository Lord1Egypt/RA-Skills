## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use a signed-in Chrome session and user-accessible research services.

Mitigation: Confirm the browsing session and research sources before allowing the agent to proceed.

Risk: The skill may create or edit a Google Doc, including a document with an existing title.

Mitigation: Verify the exact target document or request a new document before allowing edits.

Risk: Research notes and script ideas may reflect incomplete or stale launch-video observations.

Mitigation: Review candidate links, video observations, and final script inspiration before using the plan for external marketing.
