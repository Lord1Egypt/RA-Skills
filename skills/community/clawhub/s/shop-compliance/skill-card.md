## Description:

Shop Compliance helps small and midsize e-commerce sellers in mainland China run a local checklist for product listing, consumer rights, customer data protection, and marketing compliance risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External sellers, brand operators, and individual shop owners use this skill to perform a quick informational self-check before product launches, after complaints, during data handling reviews, or before livestream and promotion campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The compliance checklist is informational and is not legal advice or proof of compliance.

Mitigation: Users should verify current legal requirements and consult qualified professionals for material compliance decisions.

Risk: The checklist is focused on mainland China e-commerce requirements and may not fit other jurisdictions.

Mitigation: Use it only for the stated jurisdiction unless a qualified reviewer adapts the checks for another legal environment.

Risk: The CLI writes report files wherever the user passes an output path.

Mitigation: Choose output paths deliberately and avoid writing reports into sensitive or shared locations unless intended.
