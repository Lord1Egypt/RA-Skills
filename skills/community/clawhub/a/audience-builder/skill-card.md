## Description:

Design targeted ad audiences for ecommerce campaigns across Meta, TikTok, and Google by combining purchase behavior, interest signals, lookalike modeling, and retargeting funnels into a unified audience architecture.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce advertisers and paid media teams use this skill to design layered audience architectures across Meta, TikTok, and Google. It helps structure customer segmentation, prospecting, retargeting, exclusions, budget allocation, frequency rules, and measurement plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill directs users to segment and upload customer identifiers and value data to advertising platforms.

Mitigation: Use the workflow only where documented consent or another lawful basis exists, exclude opted-out and sensitive-category data, and upload only the minimum identifiers needed.

Risk: Third-party platform uploads can create privacy, retention, deletion, contractual, and regional compliance obligations.

Mitigation: Confirm each platform or vendor contract, approved hashed upload method, retention policy, deletion process, and regional privacy requirement before launch.

Risk: Audience architecture guidance can lead to overlapping targeting, attribution double-counting, or misleading campaign conclusions.

Mitigation: Review proposed audiences before launch, document exclusions and attribution windows, and use incrementality holdouts where practical.
