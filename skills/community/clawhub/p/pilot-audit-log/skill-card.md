### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local Pilot trust and connection audit records may contain sensitive operational details.

Mitigation: Set restrictive permissions on ~/.pilot/audit, keep only necessary fields, and define a retention period before using the logs.

Risk: Audit records generated by shell snippets may not be forensic-grade by default.

Mitigation: Review generated records before relying on them for compliance or incident response, and use JSON-safe encoding for externally influenced values.
