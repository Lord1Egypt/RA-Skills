## Description:

Threat modeling with STRIDE, PASTA, and attack trees. Analyze architectures for security gaps, extract security requirements, build data flow diagrams, and prioritize risks. For secure-by-design planning and security architecture reviews.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, security architects, and engineering teams use this skill to structure threat modeling sessions, review system architectures, identify attack vectors, prioritize risks, and document mitigations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Threat models may contain sensitive architecture, asset, trust-boundary, or security-control details.

Mitigation: Provide only architecture details you are authorized to review and keep sensitive threat-modeling materials in workspaces with appropriate access controls.

Risk: Threat-modeling guidance can become stale after system or architecture changes.

Mitigation: Review and update threat models after architecture changes and document residual risks.

Risk: The skill provides structured security review guidance, not legal, compliance, or certification approval.

Mitigation: Use qualified human review for legal, compliance, audit, and certification decisions.
