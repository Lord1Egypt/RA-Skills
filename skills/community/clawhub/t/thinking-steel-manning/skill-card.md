## Description:

Before rejecting a proposal or reflexively agreeing, build the strongest faithful opposing case, state agreement conditions, then update or reaffirm.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, developers, and decision makers use this skill to test a proposal or preferred decision against the strongest faithful opposing case before accepting, revising, or reaffirming it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can shape the agent's answer format and may over-apply deliberation to settled facts, hard constraints, trivial choices, or emergencies.

Mitigation: Apply it only when a real decision or trade-off needs review, and skip it for factual errors, hard requirements, near-zero-cost reversible choices, and live emergencies.

Risk: A weak or unfair opposing argument could create misleading confidence in the final decision.

Mitigation: Rebuild the steel man if it is weaker than the original position, state an overturn condition, and update the decision when evidence meets that condition.
