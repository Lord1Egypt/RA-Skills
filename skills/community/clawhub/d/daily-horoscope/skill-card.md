## Use Case:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The almanac calculations appear simplified and may be inaccurate or incomplete.

Mitigation: Present the output as reference guidance and avoid treating it as authoritative advice for legal, medical, financial, safety, or high-impact decisions.

Risk: The generated guidance is Chinese-only.

Mitigation: Translate or summarize the result when serving users who cannot read Chinese.
