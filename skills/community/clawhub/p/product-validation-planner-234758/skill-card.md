## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps produce practical validation workflows, checklists, artifacts, lightweight analysis, implementation support, and decision notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger terms may activate the skill for general startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation requests where a narrower skill would be more appropriate.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's desired validation outcome, constraints, and success criteria before producing the plan or artifact.

Risk: Product validation guidance can overstate confidence if assumptions and evidence gaps are not visible.

Mitigation: State assumptions, limits, required inputs, verification checks, and remaining risks in the output.
