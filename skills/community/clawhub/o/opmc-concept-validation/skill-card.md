## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Concept validation reports could influence business, investment, or project-stage decisions with incomplete or incorrect assumptions.

Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers check the input evidence, scoring rationale, and final recommendation before relying on the report.

Risk: The skill content is Chinese-language and may not match users who need another working language.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired language and terminology expectations before generating or sharing validation reports.
