## Description:

A Chinese-language creative-writing skill that provides formulas, templates, examples, and checklists for planning and improving emotional fiction, especially reincarnation romance stories.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mumuli2021](https://clawhub.ai/user/mumuli2021)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and agent users use this skill to structure Chinese emotional fiction projects, apply reusable story formulas, adapt a seven-life reincarnation template, and evaluate draft quality with checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
