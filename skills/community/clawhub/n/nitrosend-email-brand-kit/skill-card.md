## Description:

Build an email brand kit from a website: logo, brand colours, fonts and tone of voice, so newsletters, campaigns and automated emails match the brand. Use for email branding, a brand kit, on-brand email design, brand guidelines or brand voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nitrosend](https://clawhub.ai/user/nitrosend)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Businesses and email teams use this skill to capture their brand identity from a website and keep email campaigns and automated messages consistent. Users can review the captured details and request specific corrections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A logo used for a scan is sent to Nitrosend, which may disclose a private or unreleased brand asset.

Mitigation: Use a public logo or confirm you are comfortable sharing the logo with Nitrosend before scanning.
