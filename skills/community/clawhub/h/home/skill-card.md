## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Home advice can involve safety-critical work such as gas, electrical panel, or structural changes.

Mitigation: Use licensed professionals for gas, electrical panel, and structural work; do not provide DIY tutorials for those tasks.

Risk: Personal room photos, measurements, and household details may reveal private information.

Mitigation: Share only the home context needed for the request and avoid including sensitive personal details.
