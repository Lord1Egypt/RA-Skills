## Description:

Helps users structure everyday decisions with pros and cons, alternatives, weighted factors, decision tools, and a recommended decision process.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-language users can use this skill to break down everyday choices into structured factors, alternatives, and reflection prompts. It is best suited for low-stakes personal decisions rather than professional financial, legal, medical, or career advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad decision-related triggers may activate during general Chinese-language chats about choices.

Mitigation: Install only when that behavior is desired and review whether the generated structure is relevant before acting on it.

Risk: The decision framework could be mistaken for professional financial, legal, medical, or career advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as reflection prompts and seek qualified professional advice for high-stakes or regulated decisions.
