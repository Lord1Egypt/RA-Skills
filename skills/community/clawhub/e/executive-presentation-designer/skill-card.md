## Description:

Creates or redesigns PowerPoint, slide decks, pitch decks, reports, and visual narratives into premium, modern, executive-ready presentations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nano20926](https://clawhub.ai/user/nano20926)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external users use this skill to redesign, structure, or critique executive, investor, product, report, training, and classroom slide decks. It helps select a visual system, improve story flow, tighten layout hierarchy, and check finished slides for readability and polish.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Presentation inputs may contain confidential business, investor, or classroom information.

Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive content before sharing it with an agent or generated deck workflow.

Risk: Design or storytelling recommendations may misstate source content if applied without review.

Mitigation: Review redesigned slides and final summaries against the original deck before delivery.
