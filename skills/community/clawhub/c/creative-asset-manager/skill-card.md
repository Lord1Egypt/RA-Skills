## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad growth or marketing prompts outside ad creative asset workflows.

Mitigation: Confirm the request is about ad creative asset planning before applying its recommendations.

Risk: Recommendations could be mistaken for authorized ad-account actions or guaranteed platform policy outcomes.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance, keep observed facts separate from assumptions, and review policy, billing, tracking, and spend-risk issues before execution.
