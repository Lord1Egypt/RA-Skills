## Description:

Provides concise guidance for optimizing sign-up flows by reducing form friction, emphasizing social login, clarifying progress, improving micro-copy, and evaluating email confirmation tradeoffs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Growth, product, and UX teams use this skill to review sign-up flows and identify conversion-focused changes that reduce registration friction. It is most relevant when registration completion is below benchmark or users abandon the flow before activation.
