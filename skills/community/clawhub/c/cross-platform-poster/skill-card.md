## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Posting commands publish content to external social and messaging services.

Mitigation: Run posting commands only intentionally, verify each destination service, and use least-privilege, revocable tokens.

Risk: The ClawSwarm marketplace section promotes registering a paid external service with limited scoping and user-safety guidance.

Mitigation: Review who operates the marketplace, what becomes visible, and how to remove or revoke the listing before registering a paid public service.
