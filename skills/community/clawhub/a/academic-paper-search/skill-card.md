## Description:

Conducts comprehensive academic paper searches across multiple databases, downloads PDFs, monitors new publications, and manages standardized citations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yun520-1](https://clawhub.ai/user/yun520-1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and research-support agents use this skill to coordinate academic literature discovery, PDF acquisition, monitoring, CNKI searches, and citation formatting into a consolidated research package.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scheduled monitoring or push reminders could run with unintended keywords, frequency, or destination.

Mitigation: Only enable monitoring and push reminders after choosing the keywords, frequency, and destination.
