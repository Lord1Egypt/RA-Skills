## Use Case:

External users and business owners use this skill to evaluate whether and how to scale a solopreneur business beyond solo operations. It helps plan automation, delegation, hiring, SOPs, revenue growth, and sustainable operating systems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may appear for broad hiring or revenue-growth prompts and could be mistaken for legal, tax, HR compliance, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Use it as general business planning guidance; route legal, tax, HR compliance, and financial decisions to qualified experts or more specific skills.

Risk: Scaling recommendations such as hiring, contractor use, pricing, and cash runway are context-sensitive and may not fit every business model.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against current finances, local employment rules, and the business's proven operating data before acting.
