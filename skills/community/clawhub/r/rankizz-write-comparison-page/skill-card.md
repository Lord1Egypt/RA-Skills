## Description:

Research and write a balanced product comparison or versus page using consistent criteria, current sources, normalized pricing, use-case verdicts, limitations, and clear publisher disclosure. Use when a user asks for "A vs B," "our tool vs competitor," a software comparison, feature comparison, buyer decision page, or migration comparison. Rankizz keyword and SERP data is optional; current evidence for both products is required.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Writers, marketers, and product teams use this skill to create evidence-based product comparison pages for buyers evaluating two products for a defined job. It guides balanced research, pricing comparisons, segmented recommendations, and clear disclosure of testing and commercial relationships.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product details and prices may change after the comparison is researched, leaving published claims out of date.

Mitigation: Date the research and pricing cutoff, link to sources, and refresh time-sensitive facts before publication.

Risk: Readers may mistake documentation review or a demonstration for hands-on testing, or interpret an unverified feature as absent.

Mitigation: Label evidence confidence and testing status, keep unknowns unverified, and describe only tests that were actually performed.

Risk: A publisher's ownership or affiliate relationship could bias recommendations or be unclear to readers.

Mitigation: Disclose commercial relationships before the first verdict and apply the same evidence standard to both products.
