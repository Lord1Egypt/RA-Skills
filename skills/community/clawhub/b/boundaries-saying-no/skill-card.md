## Description:

Setting and maintaining personal boundaries across all relationships. Use when someone can't say no, feels constantly drained by others' demands, is being guilt-tripped, or needs to establish limits with family, work, friends, or partners.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and assisting agents use this skill to generate concise boundary-setting explanations, refusal scripts, escalation steps, and safety-aware referrals for family, work, friends, and partner contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with cultural and local legal calibration for workplace and family-boundary advice.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The release requests filesystem permission through its OpenClaw metadata.

Mitigation: Install and run it only in a trusted, sandboxed, or least-privilege environment.

Risk: Boundary-setting guidance can be unsafe when the user reports threats, violence, or fear of physical harm.

Mitigation: Prioritize safety planning and crisis or domestic-violence resources instead of relying on boundary scripts alone.

Risk: Workplace and family-boundary advice can vary by culture and local law.

Mitigation: Calibrate phrasing and escalation recommendations to the user's cultural context and jurisdiction.
