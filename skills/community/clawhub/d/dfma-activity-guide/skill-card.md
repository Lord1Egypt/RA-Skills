## Description:

Guides group-chat participants through a DFMA activity, tracks progress, redirects off-topic discussion, and produces a DFMA activity report with a shareable Feishu document link.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[justinx8023](https://clawhub.ai/user/justinx8023)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Design, manufacturing, process, and procurement teams use this skill to structure DFMA meetings in group chat, collect participant input, identify manufacturability and assembly risks, prioritize improvements, and generate a reusable activity report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read DFMA group-chat discussion, track participation, remind non-responding participants, summarize content, and create a cloud report.

Mitigation: Use it only where the organizer and participants understand these behaviors before the meeting starts.

Risk: Cloud report links can expose confidential design materials or meeting outcomes if document permissions are too broad.

Mitigation: Confirm Feishu document permissions and link-sharing settings before uploading confidential materials or sharing the report.
