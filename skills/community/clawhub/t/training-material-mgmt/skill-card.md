## Description:

Helps agents manage training materials and courseware across version control, archiving, review workflows, copyright confirmation, distribution, and update tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Training coordinators and education operations teams use this skill to organize courseware inventories, review status, copyright records, distribution controls, and change logs for training materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive or restricted training materials, copyright information, or distribution details could be included in generated management records.

Mitigation: Provide only course materials, copyright data, and distribution details appropriate for the workspace, and review generated records before distribution.
