## Description:

Drafts a founder's periodic update to investors and LPs for review, using approved session materials or pasted inputs to produce a complete investor-update email without sending, scheduling, or posting it.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[skillsandagentsco](https://clawhub.ai/user/skillsandagentsco)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders use this skill to draft monthly or quarterly investor and LP updates from approved Slack, email, analytics, billing, uploaded, or pasted materials. It helps produce a review-ready email in a short or long investor-update format while flagging unsupported facts, sensitive figures, and items that need founder confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Investor-update drafts may include sensitive revenue, burn, runway, customer, hiring, or fundraising information.

Mitigation: Use only founder-approved materials and connected sources, flag sensitive figures before inclusion, and require founder review before sending.
