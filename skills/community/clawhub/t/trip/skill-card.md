## Description:

Trip helps users make better Trip.com or Ctrip booking decisions from public travel-booking factors such as price, flexibility, timing, and convenience without account-state actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for public travel-booking decision support when comparing flights, trains, hotels, or similar booking choices. It helps weigh price, flexibility, timing, convenience, and caveats before purchase without handling login or account-state actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share account credentials, private booking details, or checkout-state requests with a skill intended only for public travel-booking advice.

Mitigation: Keep use to public decision support and switch to an appropriate browser or account workflow for live page inspection, checkout state, or logged-in booking details.
