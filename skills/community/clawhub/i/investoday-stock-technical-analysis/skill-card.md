## Description:

Analyzes A-share stock technical indicators including trends, moving averages, MACD, Bollinger Bands, volume confirmation, and support and resistance using Investoday finance data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors, analysts, and finance-focused agents use this skill to identify A-share securities by name or code and generate structured technical analysis reports. It supports trend, position, support/resistance, and volume-confirmation questions using recent market data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public market data may be stale, unavailable, or incomplete when a report is generated.

Mitigation: Confirm data freshness and treat unsupported dimensions as insufficient data rather than making a technical conclusion.

Risk: Technical-analysis reports could be mistaken for investment advice.

Mitigation: Keep conclusions informational, cite the supporting indicators, and avoid presenting the output as a buy, sell, or hold recommendation.
