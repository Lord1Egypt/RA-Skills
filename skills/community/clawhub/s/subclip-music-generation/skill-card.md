## Description:

Generate instrumental background music, soundtracks, underscore, or original music beds for product demos, SaaS launches, ads, explainers, talking heads, stories and travel videos using Subclip cloud music models. Use for new music, music variations, image-inspired music or reusing generated tracks, and for AI sound effects (whooshes, hits, risers, UI sounds, foley, ambience). For sung lyrics, also read subclip-songwriting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Video creators and editors use this skill to brief Subclip cloud models for instrumental music, soundtracks, and sound effects, then review and place the generated audio in a timeline.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Security scan coverage is incomplete because one referenced local path could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Resolve the missing path reference and rerun review and scanning to complete coverage.

Risk: Cloud generation uses credits, and generated audio may not match the requested timing or edit on the first try.

Mitigation: Check live pricing and settings before generation, confirm the requested model and brief, then review and trim the result before placing it.
