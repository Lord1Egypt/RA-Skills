## Description:

AI Music & Sound helps social-video creators brief original or licensed music beds and sound effects, choose safer commercial sources, and track license, Content ID, and disclosure caveats.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External social-video creators and brand teams use this skill to brief background music and sound design, select licensed sources for commercial use, and understand Content ID, disclosure, and workflow hand-off limits before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill gives licensing and workflow guidance that could be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Verify current AI music terms, paid-tier commercial rights, platform disclosure rules, and license receipts before publishing brand content.

Risk: AI-generated or licensed audio may still trigger Content ID claims, platform mutes, or ownership uncertainty.

Mitigation: Keep proof of license, document human contribution, apply required AI disclosure, and avoid copyrighted or platform trending music for brand use without a license.

Risk: Users may expect the agent or WoopSocial to generate music, clear licenses, run Content ID, or add native trending audio.

Mitigation: Keep the workflow explicit: the agent briefs and advises, the selected tool generates or licenses audio, the creator bakes audio into the video, and WoopSocial publishes the finished file.
