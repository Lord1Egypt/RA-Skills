## Description:

Supports authenticity checks, quality assessment, and market value estimation for Chinese collectible jewelry such as turquoise, beeswax amber, southern red agate, Hetian jade, and jadeite.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Collectors, buyers, and appraisers use this skill to review images and physical details of Chinese collectible jewelry, estimate authenticity, quality, and value, and identify when certified gemological testing is needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Jewelry photos uploaded to public URLs may expose private possessions, identifying context, or purchase details.

Mitigation: Upload images only with explicit user consent, remove sensitive details when possible, and prefer private or access-controlled image handling.

Risk: Destructive, heated, chemical, or flame-based tests can damage valuable items and create safety hazards.

Mitigation: Present non-destructive inspection as the default and recommend certified gemological testing for valuable or disputed items.

Risk: Market value estimates can be misleading when based on volatile or non-comparable listings.

Mitigation: Frame appraisals as approximate ranges, use recent comparable sources when available, and defer high-value decisions to qualified appraisal or certification.
