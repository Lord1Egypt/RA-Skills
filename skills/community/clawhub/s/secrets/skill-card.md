## Description:

Guides agents through secrets lifecycle governance, including classification, secure storage, rotation, least privilege, developer ergonomics, audit, and incident response.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, security engineers, and infrastructure teams use this skill to review or design secret handling for code, infrastructure, and CI/CD workflows. It helps structure inventories, access models, rotation runbooks, developer ergonomics, audit checks, and incident response planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose real secrets or sensitive infrastructure details while following the workflow.

Mitigation: Use redacted examples or placeholders, and share live credentials only through approved secret-management channels.
