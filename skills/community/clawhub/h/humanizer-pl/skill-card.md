## Description:

Humanizer (Polish) helps an agent rewrite Polish text to reduce common AI-writing patterns while preserving the intended meaning and tone.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[alexwoo-awso](https://clawhub.ai/user/alexwoo-awso)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and editors use this skill to make Polish text sound more natural by revising inflated claims, promotional phrasing, vague attribution, overused connectors, formulaic structure, and other common AI-writing patterns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can cause an agent to add unsupported facts, sources, opinions, or first-person claims while making text sound more human.

Mitigation: Review every proposed rewrite before saving it, and verify facts, sources, opinions, and first-person claims before using the result in legal, academic, technical, compliance, or public-facing text.

Risk: Broad or ambiguous edit selections can change the author's intended meaning or tone.

Mitigation: Use the skill on clearly selected passages and compare the rewritten text against the original before publishing or committing changes.
