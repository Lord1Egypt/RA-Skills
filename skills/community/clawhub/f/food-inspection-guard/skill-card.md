## Description:

食品安全抽检检测合规护栏。在食品安全监督抽检报告、食品检验报告、食品快检结果发布前，实时检测是否符合《食品安全法》《食品安全抽样检验管理办法》及GB 2760/GB 2761/GB 2762等食品安全国家标准要求，拦截不合规内容并给出修改建议。

## Use Case:

Compliance, food-safety, and inspection teams use this skill to review Chinese food safety inspection reports, commissioned inspection reports, factory inspection reports, rapid-test result publications, institutional publicity materials, and risk warning text before publication. It flags noncompliant statements and provides revision guidance against the rules described in the skill.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Food safety laws, inspection procedures, and GB standards may change after the skill release.

Mitigation: Verify cited standards and regulatory requirements are current before relying on the skill for legal, regulatory, or publication decisions.

Risk: The skill may produce compliance guidance that should not be treated as final legal or regulatory approval.

Mitigation: Use the output as a pre-publication checklist and route material risks or noncompliant findings to qualified compliance, inspection, or legal reviewers.
