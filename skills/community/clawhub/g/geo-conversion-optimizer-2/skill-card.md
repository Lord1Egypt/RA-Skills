## Description:

GEO conversion optimization engine that balances AI citation readiness with real user conversion for content that ranks well in AI answers and drives sign-ups, leads, purchases, or other business outcomes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External marketing, content, product, and growth teams use this skill to redesign pages and content so AI systems can understand and cite them while preserving or improving business conversions such as sign-ups, leads, purchases, bookings, or downloads.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process confidential conversion metrics, customer data, or regulated-industry claims if users provide them as inputs.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive or regulated data unless analysis is intended, and route high-stakes marketing copy through legal or compliance review.

Risk: Conversion-focused recommendations can unintentionally encourage unsupported claims or changes that reduce trust.

Mitigation: Validate claims against approved source material, preserve required disclaimers, and test GEO changes with conversion and quality guardrails.
