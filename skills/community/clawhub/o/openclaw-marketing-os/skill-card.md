## Description:

Run, audit, or improve an OpenClaw/ClawLite-style multi-agent marketing operating system across research, asset synchronization, content packaging, social publishing, blog publishing, lifecycle activation, QA, truth-gating, and Mission Control state management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founder-led teams, agencies, and AI-agent operators use this skill to run a repeatable marketing operating system from community research through assets, publishing, lifecycle sends, QA, and same-day truth closure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can coordinate real social publishing, blog publishing, lifecycle email sends, and writebacks when connected accounts or tools are available.

Mitigation: Confirm allowed accounts, audiences, customer data, and writeback locations before execution, and require review before public posts or lifecycle sends.

Risk: Marketing completion claims can overstate delivery if URLs, visibility proof, send evidence, QA receipts, or Mission Control state are missing.

Mitigation: Require dated receipts, ASSET_CHECK evidence, public URL verification, and explicit truth states such as PASS, BLOCKED, AT_RISK, or SAME_DAY_UNRESOLVED_GAP.
