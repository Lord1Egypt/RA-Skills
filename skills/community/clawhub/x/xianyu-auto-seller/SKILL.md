## Listing Optimization Tips

### Photos
- First image: Product preview with text overlay (big bold title)
- Image 2-3: More product samples
- Image 4: After-sales policy / FAQ card
- Image 5: Customer reviews (if any)
- Use Canva or 醒图 for image creation

### Keywords
- Include search keywords in title AND description
- Research competitor listings for keyword ideas
- Common keywords: 电子版、可编辑、自动发货、网盘

### Pricing Strategy
- Round numbers work: ¥9.9, ¥19.9, ¥29.9
- Bundle deal: "拍2件减5元"
- Anchor pricing: Show higher original price crossed out

### Ranking Tips
- Post during active hours: 8-10 AM, 12-2 PM, 8-11 PM
- "擦亮" (refresh) your listing daily for visibility
- Respond quickly to buyer messages (within 5 min = ranking boost)
- Maintain high response rate and good reviews

## Batch Generation Workflow

```
1. Choose product category (e.g. PPT templates)
2. Generate 10-20 listing variations
   - Different titles for different audiences
   - Unique descriptions for each
3. Generate listing images using Canva templates
4. Prepare downloadable files (create actual content)
5. Publish to Xianyu (manually or via API)
```
