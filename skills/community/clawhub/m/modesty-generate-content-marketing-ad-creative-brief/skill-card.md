Plan campaign visuals and hooks for content marketing promotions for paid campaign planning by content teams, creators, and growth marketers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content teams, creators, and growth marketers use this skill to plan paid campaign ad creative briefs, including audience goals, visual direction, messaging hooks, and draft refinement before delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated ad copy, claims, audience targeting, or visual direction may be inaccurate, off-brand, or noncompliant with ad platform rules.

Mitigation: Review generated copy and images for brand accuracy, claims, targeting, and platform compliance before publishing or using in paid campaigns.
