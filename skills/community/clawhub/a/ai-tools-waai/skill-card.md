Mitigation: Install through a trusted ClawHub workflow and prefer pinned or verified installer versions where available.

Risk: The skill directs users to third-party AI services, which may process submitted prompts or data under their own policies.

Mitigation: Review each provider's terms and avoid entering sensitive data unless sharing it with that provider is intended.
