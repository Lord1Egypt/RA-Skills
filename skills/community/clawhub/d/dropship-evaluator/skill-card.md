## Description:

Evaluate dropshipping product opportunities and supplier reliability by scoring margin potential, shipping times, supplier communication quality, and sample quality to reject dud products before sinking ad spend on them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators and teams use this skill to screen dropshipping product ideas, vet suppliers, model margins, inspect samples, and document launch decisions before spending on ads.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Evaluation notes may contain sensitive supplier terms, business plans, customer personal data, or credentials.

Mitigation: Share only information needed for the evaluation and omit credentials, unnecessary customer personal data, and unrelated sensitive business records.

Risk: Marketplace listings, ad-library signals, supplier quotes, shipping estimates, and sample results can become outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify current product demand, competitor activity, supplier terms, shipping timelines, and sample quality before approving a launch.
