## Description:

Patent Infringement Watch helps users compare their patent portfolio against competitor products or competitor companies and produce a patent infringement risk analysis report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent, product, and legal operations teams use this skill to monitor whether competitor products may read on their own active patents. It supports either uploaded competitor product files or competitor company names, then produces claim-element comparison, risk ratings, equivalence analysis, and recommended follow-up actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent infringement conclusions may be incomplete, jurisdiction-dependent, or unsuitable for high-impact legal decisions.

Mitigation: Treat reports as research aids and have a qualified patent professional review high-risk or action-driving conclusions.

Risk: The workflow can process uploaded competitor product files and public web sources through authorized patent, web, and file tools.

Mitigation: Install only after confirming the required patent MCP authorization and data-processing expectations are acceptable.
