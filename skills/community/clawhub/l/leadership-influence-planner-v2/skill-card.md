## Description:

Combine transactional game analysis with Baker influence strategy for leadership conversations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and leadership coaches use this skill to diagnose repeated leadership conversation loops, choose an ethical influence mode, and produce a short message or meeting plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-apply influence tactics in HR, legal, safety, harassment, retaliation, or mental-health situations.

Mitigation: Follow the skill boundaries by prioritizing safety, documentation, HR/legal/professional support, and clear boundaries over influence tactics.

Risk: Workplace conversation advice may be incomplete if the user omits facts, power dynamics, or safety concerns.

Mitigation: Treat pattern labels as hypotheses, preserve agency, and review the proposed message or meeting plan against the real context before use.
