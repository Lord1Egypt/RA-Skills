## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated cloud functions may have unsafe defaults for real users, payments, orders, messages, uploads, or databases.

Mitigation: Review and harden generated functions before deployment by adding authentication, authorization, allowlists, validation, quotas, logging, and payment verification.

Risk: Generated files can affect a Tencent Cloud environment if used directly.

Mitigation: Use a sandbox Tencent Cloud environment while reviewing the scaffold and prefer a pinned installer version.
