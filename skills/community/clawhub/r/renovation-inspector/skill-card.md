## Description:

Renovation Inspector helps homeowners review renovation quotes and contracts, check construction-stage acceptance criteria, assess waterproofing and tile issues, prepare negotiation responses, and draft supplementary agreement clauses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External homeowners and renovation clients use this skill to audit renovation proposals, contracts, and construction milestones, with practical guidance grounded mainly in Chinese domestic renovation standards and templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Price ranges, construction standards, and contract clauses may not match a user's locality, project facts, or current market conditions.

Mitigation: Tell users to verify pricing, standards, and legal language locally before relying on the guidance for disputes, payments, or binding contract changes.

Risk: The skill can draft contract-like clauses and negotiation language that may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Frame generated clauses as practical templates and advise users to consult a qualified professional for major disputes or high-value commitments.
