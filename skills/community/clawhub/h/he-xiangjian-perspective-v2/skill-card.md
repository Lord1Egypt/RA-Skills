Mitigation: Use explicit invocation when this perspective is desired and review outputs for accidental roleplay activation in broader business conversations.

Risk: First-person responses can be mistaken for real advice from He Xiangjian.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as public-information-based roleplay and independently review any business recommendation before use.
