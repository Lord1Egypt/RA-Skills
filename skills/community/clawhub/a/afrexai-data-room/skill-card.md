## Description:

Generate a tailored virtual data room folder structure, document checklist, gap analysis, and access control guidance for fundraising, M&A, or due diligence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Founders, operators, advisors, and deal teams use this skill to plan a virtual data room for fundraising, M&A, due diligence, board governance, or acqui-hire preparation. It helps them organize expected documents, identify gaps, estimate preparation effort, and choose appropriate access levels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat generated data room guidance as legal or financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as organizational guidance and review deal, legal, and financial decisions with qualified professionals.

Risk: Prompts may include confidential company documents or personal data during checklist customization.

Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential documents or personal data unless the workspace, access controls, and sharing settings are appropriate.

Risk: Access-control recommendations may not match the user's transaction terms or disclosure obligations.

Mitigation: Review teaser, NDA-only, management-only, watermarking, and download settings with the deal team before sharing documents.
