## Use Case:

Strategy and research users use this skill to support industry outlook analysis, TAM/SAM/SOM market sizing, value-chain analysis, policy impact assessment, Porter five forces analysis, lifecycle assessment, and disruption risk review. It is intended for use as part of the GuanShi expert cluster, with standalone use degraded to first scoping the target industry.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Current market, policy, and industry-size claims may become stale or misleading if the agent relies on outdated sources.

Mitigation: Require recent citations for current claims and mark industry data older than two years as potentially stale, matching the skill guidance.
