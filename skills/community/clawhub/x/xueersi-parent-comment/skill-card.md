## Description:

Generates warm, appropriate parent signature comments for report cards, homework books, and school communication forms based on a child's performance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhouzhi-tal](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhouzhi-tal)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents, guardians, and education staff use this skill to draft short parent-perspective comments for homework books, report cards, and school communication forms. It can create multiple tones and optionally convert output to Traditional Chinese or English.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include child school details when asking for personalized comments.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum school-performance details needed and avoid unnecessary identifying information.

Risk: The apparent default output style is Chinese, which may not fit all schools or regions.

Mitigation: Specify Traditional Chinese or English when that language is required.
