## Description:

Generate and check privacy policy documents, including draft privacy policies, completeness checks, and GDPR and CCPA supplemental clauses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[loutai0307-prog](https://clawhub.ai/user/loutai0307-prog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and privacy reviewers can use this skill to draft baseline privacy policy text, generate GDPR or CCPA supplemental clauses, create update notices, and run a keyword-based completeness check on local policy files.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated privacy policy text may not reflect the user's actual data practices or applicable legal obligations.

Mitigation: Review and adapt generated text with qualified legal or privacy stakeholders before publication.

Risk: The completeness check is a simple keyword-based local file review and may miss substantive privacy-policy gaps.

Mitigation: Use the check result as a preliminary aid and perform a separate substantive compliance review.
