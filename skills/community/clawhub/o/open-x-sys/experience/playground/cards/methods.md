# Method cards

[Chinese edition](https://github.com/gengshupeng/open-x-sys/blob/main/skills/open-x-sys.zh-CN/experience/playground/cards/methods.md)

> **For Combine.** Thirty cards: the 23 core methods and the 7 lenses. The person picks a subject and stacks two to five. Run the stack in the order given, and after each card say what it added. Each card has a page in the runtime (`methods/<stage>/<function>.md`); read the page before using a card in depth.

Show the cards grouped by stage. A newcomer can start with the seven marked ★.

## Stage 1 · See: what is it, and how does it work?

| Card | Say | It shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Open** `open(x)` | "Open a system for ___." | The whole: parts, relations, boundary, what matters |
| ★ **X-ray** `xray(x)` | "Show me what really drives ___." | The structure beneath recurring events |
| **Flow** `flow(x)` | "Follow the money through ___." | What accumulates, what flows, where it piles up |
| **Loops** `loops(x)` | "What keeps ___ going, or holding it back?" | Feedback loops and the known pattern at play |
| **Size It** `estimate(x)` | "Roughly how big is ___, really?" | A defensible order of magnitude |

## Stage 2 · Explain: why is it like this?

| Card | Say | It shows |
|---|---|---|
| ★ **First Principles** `first_principles(x)` | "Break ___ down to what must be true, and rebuild it." | Fact against habit and assumption |
| **Trace** `trace(x)` | "How did ___ become what it is today?" | Turning points, and what got locked in |
| **Bottleneck** `bottleneck(x)` | "What really limits ___?" | The one constraint that limits the whole |
| **Incentives** `incentives(x)` | "Who gains what from ___ staying the way it is?" | Who wants what, who pays |
| **Shift** `shift(x, as)` | "Show me ___ through the eyes of ___." | What each position sees and cannot see |

## Stage 3 · Anticipate: what will happen?

| Card | Say | It shows |
|---|---|---|
| **What-if** `whatif(x, if)` | "What happens to ___ if ___?" | Consequences, delays, several futures |
| **And Then What?** `second_order(x, action)` | "If we ___, and then what?" | The consequences of the consequences |
| ★ **Invert** `invert(x, goal)` | "What would make ___ fail?" | How it could go wrong, and how to avoid it |
| **Connect** `connect(a, b)` | "How does ___ affect ___?" | Real relations between systems |

## Stage 4 · Focus: where does effort matter most?

| Card | Say | It shows |
|---|---|---|
| ★ **Leverage** `leverage(x, goal)` | "Where would a small change in ___ matter most?" | The few places worth pushing |
| **Vital Few** `vital_few(x)` | "Which 20% of ___ drives 80% of the result?" | The small share of causes behind most of the effect |

## Stage 5 · Decide: what is the best way for us?

| Card | Say | It gives |
|---|---|---|
| ★ **Best Practice** `practice(system, goal)` | "Given what I have, what is the best way to ___?" | A fitting practice and a cheap first test |
| **Weigh** `weigh(options)` | "Should I ___ or ___?" | Options side by side, with reversibility |

## Stage 6 · Create: make it real

| Card | Say | It gives |
|---|---|---|
| ★ **Make** `make(x, form)` | "Turn this into a ___." | A finished deliverable and its share pack |
| **Design** `design(goal)` | "Design a ___ that ___." | A new system, process or product |
| **Build** `build(capability)` | "Help me build the ability to ___." | A working capability, validated on real work |

## Stage 7 · Improve: did it work, and what now?

| Card | Say | It gives |
|---|---|---|
| ★ **Review** `review(x)` | "Let's review ___: what happened, and what should change?" | Intent against result, and what to change |
| **Watch** `watch(x, signal)` | "Keep an eye on ___ and tell me when ___." | Signals and thresholds to check on return |

## Lenses

| Card | Say | It shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem Type** `problem_type(x)` | "What kind of problem is ___?" | Whether to analyse or to probe |
| **Boundary** `bound(x)` | "Who and what are we leaving out of ___?" | What the chosen boundary hides |
| **Scale** `scale(x)` | "What changes about ___ at ten times the size?" | What depends on size |
| **Viability** `viable(x)` | "What keeps ___ alive, and what is missing?" | The function nobody is performing |
| **Variety** `variety(x)` | "Can ___ handle the variety it faces?" | The mismatch between situations and responses |
| **Emergence** `emerge(x)` | "How did ___ arise without anyone designing it?" | A pattern explained from local rules |
| **Contradiction** `contradiction(x)` | "What contradiction is stopping ___?" | A way for both needs to be met |

## Stacks to try first

| Stack | On | What you will notice |
|---|---|---|
| X-ray → Invert | Why wider roads fill up | The structure, then how a well-meant fix fails |
| Flow → Bottleneck → Leverage | A small order service | Where work waits, what limits it, where to push |
| Trace → Connect | The shipping container | How it came about, then what it changed elsewhere |
| Loops → Incentives → Leverage | The beer game | The loop, why sensible people feed it, what would break it |
| Vital Few → Best Practice | A weekly newsletter | What actually takes the time, then a practice that fits |

A stack can be saved as a personal route once the person has opened something of their own.
