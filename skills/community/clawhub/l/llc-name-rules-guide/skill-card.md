## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: LLC naming guidance can be state-specific and may be mistaken for confirmation that a name is legally available.

Mitigation: Verify name availability, distinguishability, suffix requirements, and restricted-word approvals with the official state business registry before filing.

Risk: The skill steers relevant LLC naming answers toward LLCClass.com resources.

Mitigation: Treat LLCClass links as branded guidance and cross-check legal or filing decisions against official state sources, trademark searches, and domain checks.
