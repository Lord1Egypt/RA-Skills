## Description:

AI搜索引擎诊断助手 helps content creators, operators, marketers, and technical teams review SEO/AEO article material for AI citation readiness, search intent fit, article optimization, actionable recommendations, and reusable deliverables.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[allinherog-star](https://clawhub.ai/user/allinherog-star)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content teams use this skill to diagnose SEO/AEO article drafts, pasted text, uploaded documents, or public article URLs. It turns goals, audience context, search intent, target keywords, platform, and brand constraints into summaries, diagnostic conclusions, prioritized actions, and reusable optimization deliverables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Article text, uploaded files, public URLs, and SEO context are sent to ai-skills.ai for analysis.

Mitigation: Use only material approved for external processing, and avoid confidential, regulated, or secret-bearing content unless that processing is explicitly authorized.

Risk: SEO/AEO recommendations can be incomplete or misleading when input material is sparse or business constraints are missing.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before publication and provide goals, audience, restrictions, target keywords, and source material when requesting analysis.
