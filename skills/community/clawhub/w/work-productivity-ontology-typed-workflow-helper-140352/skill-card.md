## Description:

Helps agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn ontology-style productivity needs into practical workflows, checklists, analysis, or implementation support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to structure ontology-style productivity work into concrete plans, templates, checklists, analysis, code-change support, and verification notes. It is suited to reliability, setup-hardening, workflow design, and adjacent skill planning tasks that should remain feasible on ordinary CPU hardware.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate too broadly because implicit invocation is enabled and the trigger wording is generic.

Mitigation: Use tighter routing triggers or require explicit invocation in environments where skill-selection accuracy matters.

Risk: Workflow, checklist, or implementation-support outputs may be incomplete or misaligned with a user's actual constraints.

Mitigation: Review generated outputs against the stated success criteria, assumptions, and remaining risks before applying them.
