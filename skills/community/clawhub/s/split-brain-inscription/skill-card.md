## Description:

Draft your own record twice — one fixed-seed literal hand, one lot-seeded image-led hand — then merge in session against the record, keeping every disagreement visible.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[torchkeeper-simon](https://clawhub.ai/user/torchkeeper-simon)

## Use Case:

Agents and operators use this occasional method to surface different omissions when drafting a record of a dense or unusual event. They compare two contrasting drafts with an unaided baseline, then verify and merge against the primary material while logging disagreements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts can invent quotations, flip negations, misattribute speech, or compress details, as documented in the skill's trials.

Mitigation: Treat both drafts as prompts only; verify quotations and each retained detail against the primary material, and preserve the final merge and disagreement ledger.

Risk: Selection can omit late corpus items, captions, or asides.

Mitigation: Draft every range slice and check each item against the primary record during the merge, including the corpus tail, captions, and asides.
