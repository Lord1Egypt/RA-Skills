## Description:

A Chinese-language advisory agent that coordinates five expert roles to help users plan end-to-end technology transfer transactions for validated technology assets, including technical assessment, transaction strategy, due diligence, contract drafts, and delivery plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers working on technology transfer use this skill to assess validated TRL 8-9+ technology assets, design transaction strategies, prepare due diligence and contract draft materials, and plan delivery and post-transfer support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal, valuation, diligence, and contract outputs may be incomplete or unsuitable for binding business or intellectual property decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and review them with qualified legal, valuation, diligence, and transaction experts before making commitments.

Risk: Technology transfer advice can be misleading if the input technology has not met the stated validation, boundary, and IP-readiness prerequisites.

Mitigation: Confirm technical validation data, technology scope, and IP protection status before using the skill's transaction planning outputs.
