## Description:

Strategic planning framework for construction claim responses that helps plan direction, scope, arguments, disclosure posture, response architecture, and risk assessment before clause selection or drafting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[redkiwi1688-prog](https://clawhub.ai/user/redkiwi1688-prog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External construction contract managers, claims consultants, quantity surveyors, commercial managers, and construction lawyers use this skill to plan claim-response strategy for EOT, delay, disruption, variation, and payment disputes before drafting formal correspondence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat strategic planning output as legal advice for a real dispute.

Mitigation: Have qualified legal or contract professionals review any real submission before it is sent externally.

Risk: Incorrect deadlines, disclosure posture, or claim strategy could affect contractual rights.

Mitigation: Verify deadlines, contract clauses, statutory requirements, and disclosure decisions against the governing contract and local law before relying on the output.

Risk: Outputs may be incomplete or misleading if the user provides incomplete facts or project records.

Mitigation: Compare the strategy against contemporaneous correspondence, notices, programme records, cost records, and expert input before using it.
