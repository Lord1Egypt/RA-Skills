## Description:

Turns ecommerce exports, KPI notes, and natural-language business questions into metric alignment notes, anomaly diagnoses, operator-ready summaries, and prioritized next actions for founders, operators, and analysts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External founders, ecommerce operators, analysts, and agency teams use this skill to turn pasted commerce KPI notes and business questions into concise markdown insight briefs with driver hypotheses, drill-downs, and next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could treat heuristic briefs as authoritative live BI analysis.

Mitigation: State that briefs are based only on pasted notes and require source-data verification before decisions.

Risk: Incomplete inputs or inconsistent metric definitions could lead to misleading KPI diagnoses.

Mitigation: Surface evidence gaps and clarify definitions for GMV, net revenue, refunds, attribution, and inventory availability.

Risk: Budget, pricing, inventory, or operational changes could be acted on without review.

Mitigation: Keep those decisions human-approved and verify the supporting data before execution.
