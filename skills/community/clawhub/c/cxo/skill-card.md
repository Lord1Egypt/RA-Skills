## Use Case:

Business leaders, CX teams, and agents use this skill for customer journey mapping, voice of customer programs, service design, employee experience strategy, and experience metrics. It supports strategy and operating guidance while leaving policy, legal, compensation, VIP, and major business decisions to human owners.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Experience strategy recommendations could be over-applied to policy, compensation, VIP, legal, or major business decisions.

Mitigation: Keep human owners in control for those decisions and use the skill as strategy support.

Risk: CX metrics, journey maps, or recovery guidance can mislead when used without current customer, employee, and business context.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against live feedback, operational data, and responsible business stakeholders before acting.
