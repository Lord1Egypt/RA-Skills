## Description:

谈判说服工具箱 · Su's StoicLab is a Simplified Chinese skill that helps users recognize persuasion pressure in negotiation, sales, relationship conflict, and workplace communication scenarios, preserve decision autonomy, and form legitimate responses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[susstoiclab](https://clawhub.ai/user/susstoiclab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees who communicate in Simplified Chinese can use this skill to analyze negotiation, sales, relationship, or workplace pressure scenarios, identify likely persuasion tactics, clarify their own reference point, and draft defensive but legitimate response language.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The material discusses influence techniques that could be repurposed for manipulative behavior.

Mitigation: Use it for defensive awareness, recognizing pressure tactics, and legitimate communication rather than manipulating others.

Risk: Users may treat a detected persuasion technique as proof of bad intent.

Mitigation: Frame detections as communication-pattern analysis and return to the user's own facts, interests, and decision criteria before responding.
