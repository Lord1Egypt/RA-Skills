## Description:

Analyze two-person conversations with Berne-style transactional game analysis and suggest antithesis options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and practitioners use this skill to analyze two-person conversations, identify possible transactional game patterns, and choose practical antithesis responses that can break or soften the loop.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be implicitly invoked on sensitive two-person conversations.

Mitigation: Use it only when conversation analysis is intended, and avoid sending highly sensitive transcripts unless that analysis is appropriate.

Risk: Transactional analysis labels can be overread as diagnosis or certainty about motives.

Mitigation: Treat labels as hypotheses, ground claims in transcript evidence, and present output as interpretive guidance rather than professional mental-health advice.

Risk: High-stakes conversations involving threats, abuse, coercion, self-harm, violence, or serious mental-health risk may be mishandled if treated mainly as game classification.

Mitigation: Prioritize safety, boundaries, support, and professional or local help before applying named-game analysis.
