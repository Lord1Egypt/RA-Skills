## Description:

Generates compliance-audited marketing content for Nevada chiropractic practices, including patient acquisition campaigns, ads, service pages, local SEO, reputation templates, and referral materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chiropractic practice owners, practice managers, and healthcare marketing agencies use this skill to draft Nevada-focused chiropractic marketing assets with compliance checks for advertising, testimonial, HIPAA, Medicare, credential, and lien-related claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Nevada, United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated healthcare marketing content may be mistaken for legal, billing, or compliance advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and have qualified reviewers verify Nevada rules, credentials, Medicare coverage language, and patient authorization requirements before publication.

Risk: Practice-specific testimonials, reviews, or case examples could expose PHI or imply atypical treatment outcomes if used without review.

Mitigation: Use de-identified examples unless proper HIPAA marketing authorization is documented, and include typical-result context for outcome-adjacent claims.

Risk: Regulatory and payer requirements referenced by the prompt kit can change over time.

Mitigation: Check current Nevada chiropractic board rules, FTC endorsement guidance, HIPAA authorization requirements, and Medicare chiropractic coverage language before using generated content.
