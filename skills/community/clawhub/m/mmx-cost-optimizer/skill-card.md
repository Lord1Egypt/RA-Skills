## Description:

MMX Cost Optimizer helps agents monitor token budgets, track multi-dimensional AI usage costs, detect diminishing returns, and generate cost-optimization guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[e2e5g](https://clawhub.ai/user/e2e5g)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agent operators use this skill to control token usage, monitor budget consumption, produce cost reports, and receive cost-saving recommendations during AI-assisted work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stop, continue, or aggressive compaction recommendations may prematurely curtail important work if applied automatically.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as advisory and keep final continuation or compaction decisions under user or operator review.
