## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes practical city guidance that may become outdated or vary by individual circumstances, especially for legal, medical, tax, immigration, cost, and safety topics.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive or regulated details with official Hungarian sources and treat the skill's output as practical orientation rather than authoritative professional advice.

Risk: Neighborhood, cultural, lodging, dining, and lifestyle recommendations can be subjective and may not fit every user's needs or risk tolerance.

Mitigation: Ask for the user's context and preferences, present tradeoffs clearly, and encourage independent checking before decisions involving travel, housing, relocation, healthcare, or business setup.
