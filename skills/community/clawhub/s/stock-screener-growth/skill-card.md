## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to screen growth stocks, analyze company fundamentals, map market trends, and produce structured equity research for informational decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial research may sound authoritative even when market data, analyst estimates, or projections are stale or wrong.

Mitigation: Verify current figures against primary sources and clearly identify data dates and sources in the output.

Risk: Users could mistake screening results or scenarios for investment advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs informational, include financial disclaimers, and avoid buy, sell, hold, or position-size recommendations.
