## Description:

Audits game scenarios and systems through attribution theory to assess whether players interpret success and failure as deserved, learnable, controllable, unfair, random, or hidden.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and developers use this skill to audit combat deaths, failure loops, onboarding mistakes, reward outcomes, and other game moments for perceived fairness, player agency, and learning quality.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can influence game-design critiques and may produce incomplete or misleading recommendations if the scenario evidence is thin.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against playtest evidence, telemetry, and designer intent before changing live game systems.
