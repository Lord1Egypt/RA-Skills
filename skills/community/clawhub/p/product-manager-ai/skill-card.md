## Description:

以专业产品经理视角分析用户需求，拆解产品功能，输出结构化的产品文档（PRD、竞品分析、用户故事、需求池、数据指标等）。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, and product teams use this Chinese-language skill to turn product ideas, feature requests, or improvement needs into structured planning artifacts. It supports PRDs, competitive analysis, user stories, information architecture and flow descriptions, metrics plans, and prioritized backlogs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is designed for Chinese-language product-management assistance and may not match teams that need another language or narrower activation behavior.

Mitigation: Adjust the skill instructions before use to change the output language, triggering criteria, or supported document scope.

Risk: Product planning documents can be incomplete or misaligned when the initial requirements are ambiguous.

Mitigation: Review the skill's assumptions, clarify target users and success metrics, and validate acceptance criteria before using outputs for delivery planning.
