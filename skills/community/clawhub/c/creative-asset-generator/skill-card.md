External marketers, growth teams, and advertising operators use this skill to turn product, audience, and placement inputs into production-ready ad creative briefs, placement-specific asset specifications, test variant plans, and QA notes.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs may include platform-policy or performance assumptions that are not verified facts.

Mitigation: Review generated asset plans against current ad-platform policies, campaign data, and compliance requirements before launch.

Risk: Generated spend or testing recommendations could be applied without adequate budget controls.

Mitigation: Use explicit rollback or stop-loss conditions and validate tracking before increasing campaign spend.
