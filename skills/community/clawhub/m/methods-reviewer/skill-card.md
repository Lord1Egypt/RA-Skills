## Description:

Review the structure, tense, voice, and details of the Methods section in academic papers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qiuxin-bit](https://clawhub.ai/user/qiuxin-bit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic authors use this skill to review Methods-section text for structure, tense, voice, detail sufficiency, justification, and problem handling before revision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill analyzes user-provided Methods-section text and returns revision guidance that may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific journal or discipline.

Mitigation: Use the report as editorial guidance and have a qualified author or reviewer verify the suggested changes before submission.

Risk: The generated report and suggestions are mainly in Chinese, which may be unclear to users expecting English-only review output.

Mitigation: Confirm the expected review language before use or translate the output before applying revisions.
