# Compliance and ethics for social content

This is a checklist, not legal advice. Rules differ by country and platform and change over time; for regulated products or large campaigns, have a qualified person review.

## Disclosure

- Paid partnerships, sponsored posts, gifted products and affiliate links must be disclosed clearly, using the platform's partnership label or tool where one exists, and plain words near the start of the post ("Ad", "Sponsored", "Affiliate link").
- Say when the brand owns or is paid by the thing being recommended.
- If content is made or heavily altered with AI in a way a viewer could mistake for real (a realistic person, event or place), use the platform's AI or synthetic-content label where it requires one and say so in the post. Do not present AI-generated people as real customers or experts.

## Claims

- Every statistic, result or comparison needs a source the user can show. Use real numbers only; round honestly; state the time frame and sample.
- No guaranteed outcomes ("double your income"), no fake scarcity or countdowns, no misleading before/after, no testimonials that are not real and permitted.
- Health, finance, legal, safety and children's topics: keep to general information, cite reputable sources, avoid individualized advice, add a short disclaimer, and flag for human review.
- Compare competitors fairly and factually; do not disparage or misrepresent them.

## Rights

- Music: use platform-licensed or royalty-free audio the user has the right to use; business accounts often have a narrower music library than personal accounts.
- Images and video: own, licensed or properly credited (see the stock licence notes in `thumbnail-design` and `long-video-generation-using-free-clips`). Do not use brand logos or other people's work without permission.
- People: get consent to feature customers, staff or children; do not post private messages, personal data or identifying details without permission.
- Quotes: attribute accurately; never invent quotes from real people.
- User-generated content: ask for permission and credit the creator.

## Community and platform rules

- Follow each platform's community guidelines and ad policies; avoid engagement bait that they discourage (rewards for tags or shares, fake giveaways, misleading links).
- Giveaways and contests: state rules, eligibility, dates and the platform's non-sponsorship statement where required; check local contest and gambling laws.
- Buying followers, comments or likes, and coordinated fake accounts, are not acceptable and can get accounts restricted.
- Political, election, social-issue and crisis content: slow down, verify facts, avoid speculation, and check ad-policy requirements before posting or promoting.

## Privacy

- Do not collect or expose personal data in posts, comments or DMs. Move account-specific help to a private channel without asking for passwords or payment details in public.
- Respect opt-outs for any messaging or email follow-up.

## Quick pre-publish check

1. Facts and numbers sourced? 2. Disclosures present? 3. Rights and permissions in hand? 4. Tone safe for the topic and audience? 5. Platform rules and limits respected? 6. Human approved?
