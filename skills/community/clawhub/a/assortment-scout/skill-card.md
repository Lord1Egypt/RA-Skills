## Description:

Audit an ecommerce catalog, spot SKU sprawl, price and attribute coverage gaps, hero dependence, long-tail bloat, and duplicate-risk clusters, then turn rough catalog notes or CSV exports into keep-add-expand-merge-retire recommendations and a 30-day merchandising brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Merchandisers, category managers, marketplace sellers, and consultants use this skill to turn catalog notes or CSV export summaries into assortment health, gap, duplicate-risk, and keep-add-expand-merge-retire recommendations for planning reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Assortment recommendations may be misleading when taxonomy, margin, demand, inventory, or return evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Present recommendations as hypotheses, downgrade confidence when evidence is thin, and require business review before acting.

Risk: Duplicate-risk or cannibalization findings may be mistaken for proven causality.

Mitigation: Validate overlap patterns with controlled analysis or stakeholder review before changing listings, prices, or assortment structure.

Risk: Users may over-apply keep, merge, retire, or repricing guidance as automated operational decisions.

Mitigation: Keep retire, merge, repricing, supplier, and catalog-change decisions human-approved.
