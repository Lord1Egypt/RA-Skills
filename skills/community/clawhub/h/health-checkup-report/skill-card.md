## Description:

Interprets routine health checkup reports and lab results in plain Chinese, highlighting abnormal items, urgency, follow-up questions, and practical next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to understand routine health checkup, lab, and imaging reports in Chinese. It groups abnormal findings, explains likely meaning and urgency, and suggests follow-up questions or care pathways without replacing clinician diagnosis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical report explanations may move from educational interpretation into diagnosis or treatment advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as informational, avoid definitive diagnoses, and advise licensed medical care for abnormal, symptomatic, urgent, or treatment-related decisions.
