## Use Case:

External users use this skill for respectful, wellness-focused intimate wellbeing guidance, including communication, lifestyle habits, stress reduction, relationship health, and when to seek medical care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general intimate wellbeing guidance as professional medical advice.

Mitigation: Frame responses as general wellness information and recommend professional care for pain, sudden changes, persistent issues, or other red flags.

Risk: The skill could be used for questions that drift into explicit sexual content.

Mitigation: Keep responses non-explicit, respectful, and focused on health, wellbeing, communication, and safety.
