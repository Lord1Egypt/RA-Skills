## Description:

Budget Optimizer helps agents allocate influencer marketing budgets across creator tiers, platforms, and content types, then compare ROI, CPM/CPE, and mid-campaign reallocation scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing operators and agents use this skill to split influencer or paid-ad budgets across tiers, platforms, content types, and scenarios. It is useful for planning new campaigns, justifying budget requests, and preparing mid-campaign reallocation recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may save campaign budget recommendations and approved budget facts into workspace memory.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive CRM or campaign-performance data unless the host memory and connector settings match the user's data-retention expectations.
