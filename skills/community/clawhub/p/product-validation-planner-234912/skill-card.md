## Description:

Helps founders and product builders turn rough ideas into validated plans, lightweight prototypes, user-facing positioning, and validation workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before significant investment. It turns rough ideas into practical validation plans, checklists, lightweight prototypes, positioning, and decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for loosely related product, SaaS, startup, validation, or prototype prompts.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly when product-validation planning is needed, or confirm the user wants a validation-planning workflow before applying it.

Risk: Planning outputs can be misleading if they rely on unstated assumptions or incomplete product context.

Mitigation: Restate the outcome, constraints, available inputs, success criteria, assumptions, limits, and remaining follow-up work in the response.
