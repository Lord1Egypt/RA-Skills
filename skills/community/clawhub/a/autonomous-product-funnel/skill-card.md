## Description:

Build and launch a complete single-product sales funnel end to end (research, offer, copy, design, checkout, payment, analytics, ad pixels, SEO, deploy, QA, handoff) from one comprehensive intake, then continue autonomously without ordinary approval gates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing and e-commerce teams can use the skill to build or rebuild a single-product funnel from intake through checkout, payment, measurement, deployment, and QA. It suits teams that want routine decisions handled autonomously within their stated constraints and permissions.
