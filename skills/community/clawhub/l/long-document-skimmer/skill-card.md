## Description:

Read a long document strategically: extract its claims, evidence, and structure before deciding what deserves a deep read.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[manteclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/manteclaw)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Readers, researchers, and professionals use this skill to map long papers, reports, technical documents, and contracts before choosing which sections need a close read. It helps identify a document's structure, key claims and evidence, obligations, unresolved questions, and priority sections.
