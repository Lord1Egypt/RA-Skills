## Description:

Plans a product feature sunset from auditable decision record through user migration, communications, data handling, code removal, and retro.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, engineering leads, support teams, and account teams use this skill to plan the retirement of product features. It helps produce a sunset plan covering the kill decision, affected-user segmentation, migration paths, staged communications, exception policy, data handling, code removal, and retro.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Feature sunset planning may involve sensitive customer, contract, workflow, or compliance details.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum necessary context with the agent and avoid pasting unnecessary sensitive data into prompts.

Risk: Generated deletion, retention, migration, or communications plans may be incomplete for contractual or regulatory obligations.

Mitigation: Review the generated plan with the responsible product, legal, compliance, support, and engineering owners before taking action.
