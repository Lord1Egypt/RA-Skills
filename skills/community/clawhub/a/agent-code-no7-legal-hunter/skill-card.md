## Description:

Assists with China-focused legal research by locating laws, regulations, judicial interpretations, and cases, then drafting structured research analysis and legal opinion frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[monarchmio](https://clawhub.ai/user/monarchmio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, legal operations teams, and legal researchers can use this skill to structure Chinese legal research requests, identify relevant authorities and cases, and draft research reports or legal opinion outlines. Outputs are research support and should be reviewed against authoritative current sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal citations, case status, or statutory validity may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect.

Mitigation: Verify cited laws, regulations, judicial interpretations, cases, and current effectiveness against authoritative current sources before use.

Risk: Users may treat generated research support as formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Keep the artifact's disclaimer that research is for reference only and does not constitute a formal legal opinion, and require qualified legal review for decisions.
