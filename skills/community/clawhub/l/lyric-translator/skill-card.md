## Description:

Translates Indonesian song lyrics into natural-sounding English while preserving rhythm, syllable flow, cultural meaning, and emotional intent.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[polacapital](https://clawhub.ai/user/polacapital)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External creators, songwriters, and collaborators use this skill to adapt Indonesian lyrics into singable English for international release, sync licensing, or English-speaking collaboration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow asks the agent to reproduce original lyrics alongside the English translation.

Mitigation: Users should only provide lyrics they have the right to translate or share.
