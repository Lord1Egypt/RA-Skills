## Description:

Build and run a recurring social content calendar: designed carousels, single-image posts, and short videos, written fresh each run and scheduled ahead to LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and Threads.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing operators, founders, and social media teams use this skill to plan, draft, design, schedule, and audit recurring posts for connected LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and Threads accounts through PostNitro.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can create future posts on connected social accounts with the wrong account, timezone, cadence, or draft versus scheduled status.

Mitigation: Review selected accounts, timezone, posting windows, cadence, and status before approving a recurring routine or scheduled publishing.

Risk: Recurring publishing can repeat topics or miss prior publication failures if calendar history is not checked.

Mitigation: Use the skill's deduplication and failure-audit steps before writing or scheduling each new batch.
