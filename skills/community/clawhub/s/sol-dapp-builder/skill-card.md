## Description:

Designs, scopes, and scaffolds production-minded Solana dApps with clear on-chain/off-chain boundaries, modern tooling choices, authority design, and implementation checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, founders, and product teams use this skill to turn Solana dApp ideas or existing designs into practical architecture, MVP scope, account and authority models, tooling choices, repo plans, and security review checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated Solana architecture or scaffold guidance could contain incorrect assumptions about authority, custody, rent, transaction limits, or upgrade controls.

Mitigation: Review the proposed architecture, threat model, and test plan before implementation or deployment.

Risk: Planning may identify wallets, keypairs, API keys, and deployment secrets needed for a project.

Mitigation: Use the guidance only to plan credential requirements; do not paste real secrets into the agent conversation, and manage production credentials in an approved secret store.
