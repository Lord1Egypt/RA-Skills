## Description: <br>
Guides agents through foreign company due diligence using public web sources, covering company identity, business activity, news, legal and sanctions checks, contact channels, physical presence, and comparable companies. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Business, sourcing, compliance, and operations users can use this skill to generate a structured Chinese-language background-check report on an overseas company from public information. It is intended for legitimate due diligence and should be reviewed before use in decisions or outreach. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The workflow can produce broad contact dossiers about company staff and public channels. <br>
Mitigation: Use only for legitimate due diligence, prefer official company-level contact channels, and avoid inferred or employee-specific contact details unless there is a lawful reason. <br>
Risk: Collected contact details could be misused for unsolicited outreach, harassment, phishing, or profiling. <br>
Mitigation: Review the generated report before use and apply organizational privacy, legal, and acceptable-use controls before acting on contact information. <br>
