## Description:

Converts user-provided text into a Zaregoto Mikoko-style voice with lively, contrast-driven absurd metaphors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and writers use this skill to rewrite supplied material in a specific character-inspired Chinese prose style, emphasizing energetic tone and absurd but coherent contrast metaphors.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the style rewrite for generic character-voice or style-conversion prompts.

Mitigation: Use explicit wording when requesting another style or when no persona transformation is intended.
