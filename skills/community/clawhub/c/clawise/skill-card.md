## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill connects agents to sensitive account-operation services including email, SMS verification, credential vault, CAPTCHA, and identity workflows.

Mitigation: Use scoped or dedicated credentials where possible, avoid unnecessary personal or account data, and review the provider's retention and security practices before installation.
