## Description:

Analyze service business websites to identify missed lead capture, follow-up, chatbot, and AI automation opportunities, then produce prioritized recommendations to improve conversions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[subham7872](https://clawhub.ai/user/subham7872)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business operators, marketers, and automation consultants use this skill to audit service business websites, landing pages, or company web presences for lead capture and follow-up gaps. It produces a conversion-focused checklist, score, problems found, priorities, and an outreach summary.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may trigger on broad references to business websites or company names.

Mitigation: Use it when a website audit for lead capture and automation opportunities is intended.

Risk: Audit recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific business context.

Mitigation: Review the score, findings, priorities, and outreach summary before using them for implementation or customer-facing outreach.
