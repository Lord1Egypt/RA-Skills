## Description:

Senior technical article editor for creating well-structured articles from research, organizing content, and publishing to a WeChat Official Account.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[viodmain](https://clawhub.ai/user/viodmain)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, technical writers, and content teams use this skill to research a technical topic, organize the article structure, create Markdown content with metadata and citations, archive it in Obsidian, and publish it to a WeChat Official Account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may save generated article drafts to an Obsidian vault without an explicit confirmation step.

Mitigation: Require preview and user confirmation before writing to the configured Obsidian vault.

Risk: The workflow may publish generated content to a WeChat Official Account when credentials and network requirements are configured.

Mitigation: Use only intentionally configured WeChat credentials and require explicit approval before invoking the publishing command.
