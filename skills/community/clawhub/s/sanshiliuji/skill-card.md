## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can turn deceptive or manipulative competitive stratagems into concrete real-world tactics.

Mitigation: Constrain use to lawful, transparent strategy analysis and refuse requests involving deception, coercion, covert data collection, corruption, sabotage, evasion of accountability, or self-harm-related strategy.

Risk: The aggressive competitive-strategy lens may encourage harmful or unethical application if user goals and constraints are vague.

Mitigation: Require clear scenario context, stakeholders, boundaries, and risk review before presenting actionable recommendations.

Risk: Installing through an unpinned npx command may retrieve a different release over time.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned ClawHub installer version when installing this skill.
