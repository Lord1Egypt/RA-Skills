## Description:

Generates a full week of social media posts with captions, hashtags, and matching images for each post.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tobeyrebecca](https://clawhub.ai/user/tobeyrebecca)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketers, solopreneurs, bloggers, and content marketers use this skill to plan a seven-day social media calendar with captions, hashtags, and matching images.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence flags broad shell and file-reading permissions that are not needed for captions, hashtags, calendars, or images.

Mitigation: Install only in a sandboxed environment or remove Bash and Read permissions before use.

Risk: Generated social media content could be inaccurate, off-brand, or unsuitable for public posting without review.

Mitigation: Review and edit generated posts and images before scheduling or publishing.
