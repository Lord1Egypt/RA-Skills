## Description:

Covers caption strategy, styling, placement, timing, accessibility, and engagement for short-video subtitles and on-screen text.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video editors, content creators, social media managers, and accessibility-focused creators use this skill to design, audit, and adapt captions for short-form video across platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad caption-related requests.

Mitigation: Users should state the video platform, video type, target language, accessibility needs, and brand constraints when requesting caption guidance.

Risk: Caption design guidance can be unsuitable if it is not checked against the actual video timeline, platform interface, caption accuracy, or translation quality.

Mitigation: Review the proposed caption style, placement, timing, accessibility checklist, and translations against the final footage before publishing.

Risk: Users who need non-English output may receive a default English-first response.

Mitigation: Specify the preferred output language explicitly when requesting caption recommendations.
