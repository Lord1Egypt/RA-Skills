## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recurring deal reports may be sent to an external WeChat destination.

Mitigation: Confirm the destination, included data, linking method, and pause or cancellation controls before enabling recurring pushes.

Risk: Deal prices and resale margin notes may become stale or misleading quickly.

Mitigation: Verify current prices and platform terms before purchasing or acting on resale guidance.
