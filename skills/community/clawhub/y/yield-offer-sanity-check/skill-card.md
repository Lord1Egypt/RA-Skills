## Description:

A checklist skill that evaluates whether a yield, staking, or earn offer is reasonable or suspicious.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to screen yield, staking, or earn offers for warning signs, missing information, and whether more evidence is needed before acting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The checklist does not independently verify platforms, audits, contracts, or on-chain data.

Mitigation: Treat the output as preliminary risk-screening guidance and verify claims against trusted platform, audit, contract, and on-chain sources before acting.

Risk: A reasonable-looking offer could be mistaken for a safety guarantee.

Mitigation: Use the skill's verdict as a warning-sign assessment only, preserve unknowns, and avoid declaring any platform or yield offer safe.
