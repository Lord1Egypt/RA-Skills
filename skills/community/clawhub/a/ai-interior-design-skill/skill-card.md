## Description:

Room redesign planning companion that turns interior design goals, constraints, and photos or text notes into palettes, layouts, lighting plans, AI image prompts, and implementation checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[eavemma5-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/eavemma5-tech)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn room photos or notes, design goals, budgets, and constraints into practical interior redesign guidance, AI image prompts, and implementation checklists. It is suited for room refresh planning, furniture layout exploration, color and material selection, and lighting guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat inspirational interior design output as professional construction, electrical, plumbing, mold, asbestos, lead paint, or code-compliance advice.

Mitigation: Keep advice informational and recommend qualified professionals for structural, electrical, plumbing, hazardous-material, and code-compliance issues.

Risk: Room photos or household details may expose private living-space information.

Mitigation: Collect only details needed for the design task and advise users to be selective when sharing room photos or household information.

Risk: Linked website and browser add-on are separate products that may have their own behavior and risks.

Mitigation: Evaluate the linked website and Firefox add-on separately before using or installing them.
