## Description:

Scan Freelancer.com for new projects matching your skills, draft personalized bid proposals, and track bidding history.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[onlyloveher](https://clawhub.ai/user/onlyloveher)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Freelancers and agents use this skill to find relevant Freelancer.com projects, draft client-specific bid proposals, and maintain a simple local bid-history log.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated bids may include inaccurate, overstated, or unsuitable claims for a client project.

Mitigation: Review and edit every proposal before submitting it on an external account.

Risk: Project search requires browsing or querying Freelancer.com and may surface stale or incomplete listings.

Mitigation: Confirm project status, budget, and client requirements on Freelancer.com before acting.

Risk: The local bids.md history can contain project or account-sensitive bidding information.

Mitigation: Keep the bid-history file in a controlled workspace and avoid storing sensitive account details.
