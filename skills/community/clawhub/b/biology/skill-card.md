## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Biology explanations can be inaccurate, incomplete, or overstate uncertain mechanisms.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims against authoritative biology sources and distinguish established consensus from emerging or uncertain findings.

Risk: Clinical correlations can be mistaken for medical advice.

Mitigation: Keep clinical examples educational, avoid diagnosis or treatment recommendations, and direct users to qualified medical professionals for health decisions.

Risk: The skill has broad tutoring scope, so activation boundaries may be unclear.

Mitigation: Review prompts and outputs for scope, especially for lab safety, clinical topics, and other safety-sensitive biology contexts.
