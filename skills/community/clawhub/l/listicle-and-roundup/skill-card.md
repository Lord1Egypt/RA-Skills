## Description:

Designs honestly curated, scannable listicles and expert, product, or resource roundups using the RANKS framework, with explicit guardrails against fabricated items, quotes, rankings, padding, and undisclosed sponsored or affiliate placements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content teams, marketers, founders, and social media operators use this skill to plan audience-facing listicles, ranked recommendation lists, expert roundups, product roundups, affiliate roundups, and curated resource lists. It helps structure the premise, criteria, entries, contributor ask, social cuts, and verification boundaries before human fact-checking and publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft rankings or product claims may be inaccurate, outdated, or biased if published without review.

Mitigation: Verify product facts, pricing, claims, ranking criteria, and current evidence before publication.

Risk: Expert roundup content may misrepresent contributors if quotes or consent are assumed.

Mitigation: Use only real contributors, preserve their words, and confirm consent before publishing.

Risk: Affiliate, sponsor, or own-product placements can become deceptive if presented as independent editorial recommendations.

Mitigation: Disclose material connections clearly and rank by stated merit rather than commission or self-interest.

Risk: Health, finance, legal, or other YMYL lists may be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Add appropriate not-professional-advice framing and require human review for sensitive claims.

Risk: Scraped competitor lists or product pages can contain prompt-injection content or unreliable claims.

Mitigation: Treat scraped or third-party material as research evidence only, not as instructions to follow.
