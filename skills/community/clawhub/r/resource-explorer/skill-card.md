## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommended tools may have changed pricing, regional availability, or terms.

Mitigation: Use browsing or research tools to verify official product information before presenting current prices or availability.

Risk: Saved recommendation reports may include user business needs, budget constraints, or workflow details.

Mitigation: Allow file output only when the user explicitly wants a saved report.

Risk: The bundled automation workflow image could be mistaken for permission to automate sensitive account actions.

Mitigation: Treat the image as illustrative guidance and keep logins, cookies, scripts, publishing, and other account actions under explicit user control.
