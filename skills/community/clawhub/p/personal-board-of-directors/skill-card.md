## Description:

Five standing advisors - the Operator, the Skeptic, the CFO, the Coach, and the Customer - debate a user decision, map disagreements, vote under a stated decision rule, and identify the key question to resolve before deciding.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to pressure-test personal, business, or product decisions by convening five distinct advisor perspectives and receiving a structured recommendation. It is best suited for yes/no or A-vs-B decisions where stakes, reversibility, and constraints can be stated.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Decision prompts may include sensitive personal, financial, or business context.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing highly sensitive details unless they are appropriate for the chat context; use generalized facts where possible.

Risk: The board recommendation may be persuasive even when important inputs are missing or uncertain.

Mitigation: State stakes, reversibility, constraints, and the key unresolved question before relying on the recommendation.
