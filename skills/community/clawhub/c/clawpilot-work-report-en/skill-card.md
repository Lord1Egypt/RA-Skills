## Description:

Automatically extracts daily or weekly workplace progress, communication points, to-dos, and risks from work-related notifications and generates a concise work report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vivalavida-say-hi](https://clawhub.ai/user/vivalavida-say-hi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees use this skill to turn work-related notifications from chat, email, calendar, approval, project management, and code collaboration apps into daily or weekly reports for managers or team workbenches.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read broad local workplace notification files containing chats, emails, approvals, calendar items, and project updates.

Mitigation: Configure followed apps and groups narrowly, and avoid use where company policy restricts summarizing or redistributing notification content.

Risk: Generated reports can preserve exact names, project details, deadlines, and other sensitive workplace context.

Mitigation: Review each generated report before sharing it with a manager or posting it to a workbench, and redact sensitive details when needed.
