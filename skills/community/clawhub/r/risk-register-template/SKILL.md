# Risk Register Template

## Overview

Projects rarely die from the risk nobody imagined; they die from the risk everybody knew and nobody owned. A register forces the naming, scoring, and owning of risks while they are still cheap.

## When to use

At the start of any project with money, deadlines, or other people's assets involved; reviewed monthly.

## Workflow

1. **Brainstorm by category** — technical, financial, counterparty, regulatory, operational, security, key-person
2. **Write each risk as an event** — 'X happens, causing Y', not a vague worry
3. Score likelihood and impact 1-5 with the reasoning in one line; expected exposure is the product, and it is a ranking tool, not a prophecy
4. **Assign one owner per risk** — the person who watches it and acts first
5. **Define the mitigation and the tripwire** — what reduces it, and what signal means it is materializing
6. **Review monthly** — retire dead risks, add new ones, re-score the movers

## Guardrails

- Never let the register become a compliance artifact — if nothing ever changes in it, it is not being used
- The risk nobody wants to write down is usually the important one
- Mitigations need owners and dates too

## Output format

A register table: risk event, category, likelihood, impact, exposure, owner, mitigation, tripwire, status.
