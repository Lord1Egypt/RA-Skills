## Description:

A Chinese concept-validation director workbench for facilitating 5-Gate reviews, four-light decisions, blue-light condition tracking, multi-route resource allocation, and graduation handoff packages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Concept validation teams and project reviewers use this skill to prepare and run Gate 0-4 reviews, synthesize technical and commercial inputs, record four-light decisions, and track conditional blue-light follow-ups.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Overbroad activation can cause ordinary project status questions to be framed as formal Gate reviews.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for Gate reviews, four-light decisions, blue-light condition tracking, or multi-route resource allocation; ask for confirmation before applying the formal workflow to general status questions.

Risk: Fallback behavior can promote an external contact unrelated to the user's immediate task.

Mitigation: Suppress contact promotion unless the user explicitly asks for publisher contact details or support escalation.

Risk: Chinese-first output and formal Gate-review terminology may not fit all teams or audiences.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's preferred language and review format before producing final decision records.
