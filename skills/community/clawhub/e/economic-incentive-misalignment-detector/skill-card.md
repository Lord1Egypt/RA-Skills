## Description:

Helps identify when marketplace economic incentives systematically favor quantity over quality, creating structural pressure toward publishing unsafe skills that individual technical audits cannot detect.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, marketplace operators, and reviewers use this skill to assess whether marketplace policies, publisher metrics, or economic structures create incentives that favor publishing volume over safety quality.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketplace conclusions may depend on estimated or publicly observable proxy data rather than complete internal marketplace records.

Mitigation: Treat the assessment as judgment-based analysis and verify important conclusions against primary marketplace data before making enforcement or procurement decisions.

Risk: The release declares curl and python3 requirements, but the Markdown artifact does not explain runtime use of those tools.

Mitigation: Review whether these tool requirements are necessary before deployment and restrict execution permissions to the analysis workflow the skill actually needs.
