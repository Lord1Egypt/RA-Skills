## Description:

Use when needed functionality may already exist online: search, quality-check, harvest data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[blueboyzx](https://clawhub.ai/user/blueboyzx)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

For developers who need a capability or domain knowledge, the skill searches community registries, compares and quality-checks candidate skills, and returns attributed findings as data. Suggested searches require the user's go-ahead, and candidates are never installed without fresh explicit approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan was partial because a referenced local item could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved referenced content and decide whether it matters before installing or using the skill.

Risk: Registry search terms may disclose sensitive context to external registries.

Mitigation: Use minimal generic capability terms, remove project identifiers and sensitive details, honor requested exclusions, and obtain explicit approval before sending sensitive-context queries.
