## Description:

Helps product managers turn early software product ideas into structured PRD-style requirements, module breakdowns, workflows, data rules, user stories, and risk notes for design and engineering handoff.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[weslon1987](https://clawhub.ai/user/weslon1987)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers and product teams use this skill to clarify product goals, user groups, scenarios, functional modules, dependencies, data logic, and delivery risks. It is intended to produce practical requirements artifacts that can be handed to design and engineering teams.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce Chinese-language PRD-style output when the user expected another language.

Mitigation: Specify the desired output language and document format at the start of the request.

Risk: Generated product requirements can reflect missing or vague business context.

Mitigation: Review the PRD, user flows, data rules, and risk notes with product, design, and engineering stakeholders before implementation.
