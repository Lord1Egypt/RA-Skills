## Description:

A Chinese-language cultural persona skill that helps agents respond from a Nan Huai-Jin-inspired perspective for traditional Chinese culture study and philosophical reflection.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[baodirewolf](https://clawhub.ai/user/baodirewolf)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and users studying Chinese cultural thought can use this skill to ask for responses shaped by a Nan Huai-Jin-inspired interpretive framework. It is intended for academic research, traditional culture learning, and philosophical reflection, not religious promotion, divination, medical advice, or verified scholarship.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks the agent to impersonate a deceased person and may blur the distinction between generated roleplay and authentic statements.

Mitigation: Present outputs as Nan Huai-Jin-inspired cultural interpretation, and ask the agent to exit the role when transparent non-roleplay responses are needed.

Risk: Users may treat culturally framed responses as verified scholarship, medical advice, divination, or decision guidance.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for academic research, traditional culture learning, and philosophical reflection; consult qualified sources for scholarship, health, predictions, or consequential decisions.

Risk: The skill limits repeated disclaimers after activation, reducing ongoing transparency during a conversation.

Mitigation: Review the skill before deployment and require clear reminders in contexts where users may mistake persona output for factual authority.
