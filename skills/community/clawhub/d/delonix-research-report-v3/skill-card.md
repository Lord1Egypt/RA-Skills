## Description:

Use when the user requests industry research reports, market analysis, business trend analysis, or professional research documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business users, analysts, and strategy teams use this skill to scope, structure, draft, and review industry research reports, market analyses, competitive intelligence briefs, white papers, and company or sector analyses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated market data, business claims, or trend analysis may be incorrect, outdated, or insufficiently sourced.

Mitigation: Verify cited data against credible public sources, current industry reports, official statistics, filings, and reputable analyst materials before relying on the report.

Risk: Investment-oriented recommendations could be mistaken for professional financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat generated recommendations as drafting support only and route investment decisions through qualified human review.
