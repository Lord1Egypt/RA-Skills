## Description:

Guides go-to-market strategy for product launches, market entry, repositioning, and feature launches by structuring GTM mode, ICP and persona definition, market analysis, and a 90-day plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external business users, and strategy teams use this skill to plan GTM strategy for product launches, new market entry, repositioning, and feature launches. It helps frame the scenario, select a GTM motion, define ICP and personas, build a market analysis checklist, and outline a 90-day plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: GTM statistics, market assumptions, and benchmark targets may be outdated or may not match the user's market.

Mitigation: Verify recommendations against current customer research, market data, financial metrics, and business constraints before making major GTM decisions.

Risk: Market entry or enterprise GTM recommendations may overlook jurisdiction-specific regulatory, procurement, or data handling requirements.

Mitigation: Review market entry plans with relevant legal, compliance, security, and regional business stakeholders before execution.
