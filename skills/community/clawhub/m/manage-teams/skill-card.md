## Description:

Create, join, and manage teams on OpenAnt, including discovering public teams, getting team details, creating teams, and adding or removing members.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ant-1984](https://clawhub.ai/user/ant-1984)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to discover public OpenAnt teams, inspect team details, create or join teams, and manage team membership through the OpenAnt CLI.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Team creation, joining, membership changes, and deletion can persistently change OpenAnt account or team state.

Mitigation: Review team IDs and member IDs before acting, and require explicit user confirmation before deleting teams or removing members.
