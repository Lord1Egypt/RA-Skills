## Description:

Provides Chinese-language strategic decision support based on Clausewitz's On War and AI-era adaptations, focusing on trinity, multidimensional centers of gravity, friction, escalation, and culminating-point analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ruiyongwang](https://clawhub.ai/user/ruiyongwang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and strategy practitioners use this skill to frame competitive, organizational, AI adoption, and information-risk decisions through Clausewitzian mental models and decision heuristics. It is best suited for reflective strategic guidance, not automated operational command or legal, military, or safety-critical decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is written entirely in Chinese, which may limit review quality for teams without Chinese-language expertise.

Mitigation: Use a qualified Chinese-language reviewer before deployment or adaptation.

Risk: The skill frames business and AI strategy through military concepts that may be unsuitable for some organizational contexts.

Mitigation: Review outputs for context fit, ethical implications, and domain-specific constraints before acting on them.

Risk: Clausewitzian analysis is reflective strategic guidance and may be inappropriate for safety-critical or real-world conflict decisions.

Mitigation: Keep human expert review in the loop and avoid using the skill as an automated decision authority.
