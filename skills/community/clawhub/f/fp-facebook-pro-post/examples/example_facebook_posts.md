## 类型 A — 行业科普

### 输入
```
类型：A
素材：液压油变浑浊/发白意味着什么
```

### 输出
**① 正文**
Did you know milky or cloudy hydraulic fluid is usually a warning sign, not just dirty oil? When water gets into your hydraulic system, the fluid can turn a milky white — and that often points to a failing seal, a bad cylinder, or condensation building up over time. Running your machine like this can wear out pumps and valves faster than you'd think. If you spot it, it's worth tracking down the source before it gets expensive. Full rundown on our blog → fridayparts.com
