## Description:

根据学科、年级生成个性化学习助理，支持数学、物理、化学、生物、语文、英语、历史、地理、计算机科学、工程技术、医学、经济学、文学、管理学、艺术学、农学等学科；当用户需要创建学科学习助手、定制教学内容或设计学习路径时使用

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Educators, learners, and agent builders use this skill to create subject-specific learning assistants, teaching strategies, knowledge frameworks, and staged learning paths for Chinese-language educational workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Professional or practical subjects such as chemistry, medicine, engineering, and agriculture could be mistaken for operational instructions or professional advice.

Mitigation: Treat generated content as study guidance only and require qualified supervision before hazardous activities or professional decisions.

Risk: Generated learning plans may not fit a learner's local curriculum, exam requirements, or individual needs.

Mitigation: Have educators or subject-matter reviewers adapt the assistant configuration and learning path before relying on it in instruction.
