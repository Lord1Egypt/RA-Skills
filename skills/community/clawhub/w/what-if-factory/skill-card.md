## Description:

Generates playful multi-format parallel-universe content for what-if, alternate-reality, and wild-scenario prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and creative teams use this skill to turn hypothetical prompts into entertaining alternate-universe stories, image prompts, and social-share captions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases such as "what if" or "suppose" may activate the skill during serious hypotheticals.

Mitigation: Use stricter intent checks or ask a clarifying question before applying the playful alternate-universe format.

Risk: Creative news-style output could be mistaken for factual reporting if presented without context.

Mitigation: Keep outputs clearly framed as fictional parallel-universe content and review sensitive scenarios before sharing.
