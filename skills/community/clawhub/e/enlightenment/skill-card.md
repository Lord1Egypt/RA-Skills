## Description:

Provides grounded knowledge, distinctions, and reflective support around enlightenment-related topics such as 开悟, 觉醒, 见性, 无我, 空性, spiritual insight, and inner transformation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to ask for careful Chinese-language explanations of enlightenment-related concepts, reflect on awakening-like experiences, correct common misconceptions, and express subtle inner states without over-certifying spiritual attainment. It is intended for grounded discussion and reflection, not as a mental-health or spiritual-authority substitute.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat reflective spiritual guidance as confirmation of enlightenment or as a substitute for mental-health or spiritual-authority support.

Mitigation: Keep responses cautious, avoid certifying attainment, encourage grounding in routine and trusted relationships, and direct severe distress or safety concerns toward appropriate professional or emergency support.

Risk: The skill has scope and language-quality caveats, including one lifestyle-coaching example that can drift away from enlightenment-specific support.

Mitigation: Review outputs for topic fit, preserve the skill's grounded Chinese-language posture, and avoid expanding into general lifestyle advice unless it directly supports the user's spiritual-reflection question.
