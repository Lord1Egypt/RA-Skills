## Description:

Location strategy and site selection pack for geo-economic analysis, real estate intelligence, site scoring, tax incentive optimization, and TCO simulation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[romainsantoli-web](https://clawhub.ai/user/romainsantoli-web)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Business stakeholders and consultants use this skill to structure location strategy and compare candidate sites across market, workforce, incentives, scoring, and total cost factors.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real-estate, tax incentive, and cost outputs may be inaccurate or outdated for a specific site decision.

Mitigation: Validate outputs against current market data, tax authority guidance, and qualified legal or finance review before business commitments.

Risk: The skill supports location decisions that affect finance, legal, HR, and operations teams.

Mitigation: Route recommendations through the relevant departmental owners before using them for final site selection.
