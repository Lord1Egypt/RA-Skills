## Description:

A Chinese-language finance operations assistant that helps analyze financial statements, cash flow, costs, pricing, financing options, investment returns, budgets, and profitability questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and business operators use this skill to turn financial data and operating questions into plain-language Chinese financial diagnosis, calculations, and action recommendations. It is intended for practical business finance scenarios such as cash-flow review, cost optimization, pricing, financing, investment decisions, and report interpretation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad money-related mentions and provide finance analysis when the user did not intend a detailed review.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's finance-analysis intent before applying detailed diagnosis or recommendations to ambiguous money-related requests.

Risk: Users may share sensitive company or personal financial details while requesting analysis.

Mitigation: Ask users to omit or anonymize sensitive financial details unless they intentionally need those details analyzed.

Risk: Financial calculations and recommendations can be wrong or incomplete if the user provides partial data or unstated assumptions.

Mitigation: State assumptions, ask for missing figures when needed, and present calculations as decision support rather than certified financial advice.
