## Description:

Guides agents in answering questions about RSI, stochastic/KD, Williams %R, MACD, ROC, momentum, divergences, and contrarian sentiment extremes while keeping oscillator signals subordinate to trend context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to explain oscillator overbought/oversold signals, divergence warnings, and contrarian sentiment extremes in technical-analysis conversations. It helps distinguish range-bound use from trending-market use and warns against treating oscillator readings as standalone trading instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake educational oscillator and sentiment analysis for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: State that outputs are educational, keep users in control of market-data lookups and trading decisions, and avoid presenting any signal as an instruction to trade.

Risk: Oscillator overbought or oversold readings can be misleading when used without trend context.

Mitigation: Require trend context before interpreting signals and treat divergences or extremes as warnings that need confirmation.
