## Description:

Coaches B2B senders on cold-email copy, sequencing, targeting, deliverability, and infrastructure so they can improve opens, replies, and booked meetings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External B2B founders, SDRs, agencies, and freelancers use this skill to diagnose cold-outbound funnel problems, rewrite emails, design follow-up sequences, plan domain setup, and troubleshoot deliverability.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cold outbound guidance can be misused for unlawful or non-consensual outreach, misleading sender identity, missing opt-out handling, or behavior that violates email provider policies.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for lawful, consent-aware outreach with accurate sender identity, clear opt-out handling, and respect for email provider rules.
