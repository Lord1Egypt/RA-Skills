## Description:

Guides users through Porter's Five Forces industry structure analysis, collecting user-provided facts for each competitive force and producing industry attractiveness and competitive-position reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business strategists, product leaders, analysts, and consultants use this skill to evaluate industry structure for market-entry decisions, competitive strategy, and investment assessment. It is especially oriented toward manufacturing, supply-chain, and quality-management scenarios while remaining applicable across industries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may write local Markdown and HTML report files that collide with existing filenames.

Mitigation: Choose the output location before generation and confirm whether same-named files may be overwritten.

Risk: Users may provide sensitive strategy, market, or investment information during the analysis.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in an appropriate workspace for that information and avoid adding unnecessary sensitive details.

Risk: Industry attractiveness and competitive-position judgments can be misleading if the supplied facts are incomplete or unverified.

Mitigation: Validate source facts, keep skipped or uncertain force assessments marked for confirmation, and have the final strategic conclusion reviewed by the user.
