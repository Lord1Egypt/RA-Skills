## Description:

Generates engaging social media posts, targeted ads, follow-ups, and sales scripts to convert course viewers into paying customers for education providers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Training institutes, online course sellers, coaches, mentors, and education companies use this skill to draft social media posts, lead follow-ups, nurturing emails, ad targeting ideas, and sales scripts for course promotion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing copy could overstate course benefits, pricing, availability, or sales claims.

Mitigation: Review generated messages against current course details and approved marketing claims before publication or outreach.

Risk: Lead follow-up and email suggestions could be used without appropriate recipient consent or platform compliance review.

Mitigation: Confirm outreach consent and follow applicable email, advertising, and platform policies before sending messages.
