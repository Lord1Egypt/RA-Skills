## Description:

Generates a personalized message, converts to natural speech, and sends as an SMS notification with audio link.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

[tobeyrebecca](https://clawhub.ai/user/tobeyrebecca)

## Use Case:

Business owners and operations managers use this skill to create and send voice notifications for customer communication, such as appointment reminders or operational updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Voice and SMS notifications can expose customer, appointment, or other private details outside the organization.

Mitigation: Confirm the recipient phone number, message content, and authorization to contact the recipient before sending.

Risk: Automated or externally delivered messages can be sent with incorrect or misleading content.

Mitigation: Review generated messages for accuracy, readability, and actionability before external delivery.
