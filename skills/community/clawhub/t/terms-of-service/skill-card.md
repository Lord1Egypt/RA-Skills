## Description:

Terms Of Service helps generate Markdown terms of service drafts for services and applications.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bytesagain3](https://clawhub.ai/user/bytesagain3)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and business operators use this skill to draft terms of service text from the command line for SaaS, ecommerce, app, or general service workflows. Generated terms are reference material and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated terms of service may be legally incomplete, jurisdictionally inappropriate, or misleading if used without review.

Mitigation: Treat generated terms as draft reference material, set the governing law explicitly, and have qualified counsel review before publication or customer use.

Risk: The artifact includes a utility command script that persists arbitrary user-provided text and command history under the user's home data directory, which does not match the advertised terms-generation purpose.

Mitigation: Avoid entering secrets or sensitive business data into add, search, or export commands; inspect or override TERMS_OF_SERVICE_DIR before use and delete stored data when it is no longer needed.

Risk: Automation may call commands whose behavior differs from the public terms-of-service generator description.

Mitigation: Review the installed command list and script behavior before integrating the skill into unattended workflows.
