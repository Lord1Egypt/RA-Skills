## Description:

Generates a Chinese planetary psychology personality report from birth date, approximate birth time, and birth city while avoiding astrology jargon in the final report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users or agents use this skill to produce Chinese astrology-style personality reports for entertainment or self-reflection from birth date, approximate birth time, and city inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Birth date, approximate birth time, and city are personal inputs that can be sensitive when combined.

Mitigation: Ask only for the fields needed to generate the report, avoid retaining them beyond the session, and make clear that approximate birth time is acceptable.

Risk: The generated astrology-style report may be mistaken for professional psychological or medical advice.

Mitigation: Present the output as entertainment or self-reflection content and avoid using it for diagnosis, treatment, employment, financial, or other consequential decisions.

Risk: Generated reports include a fixed promotional attribution footer.

Mitigation: Review the footer before publication or customer-facing use so users understand the attribution and promotional language.
