## Description:

Provides Chinese-language, stage-based IPD consulting support for manufacturing and technology teams preparing for DCP decision reviews, TR technical reviews, cross-functional alignment, and portfolio decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external consultants, and product-development teams use this skill to evaluate IPD stage readiness and prepare advisory next steps for DCP, TR, and cross-functional review situations. It supports preparation and guidance only; final investment, launch, and redirection decisions remain with the responsible IPMT or business owners.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share more confidential project, financial, supply-chain, or technical detail than the advisory task requires.

Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the current IPD stage, DCP, or TR question, and redact unnecessary confidential details.

Risk: Advisory output could be mistaken for final approval or an investment decision.

Mitigation: Use the output as preparation guidance and keep final Go, No-Go, conditional, launch, or redirection decisions with the responsible IPMT or business owners.
