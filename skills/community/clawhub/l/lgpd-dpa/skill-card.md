## Description:

Structure and review Data Processing Agreements (DPA) with operators under LGPD Arts. 39, 42, 46, and 48, distinguishing operators from independent or joint controllers. Use when user mentions 'DPA', 'contrato com operador', 'cláusulas LGPD', 'data processing agreement', or onboarding a third-party service (cloud, analytics, payment, email, observability). Outputs DPA best-practice checklist + per-vendor sheets in `.lgpd/vendors/`.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Privacy, procurement, and engineering teams use this skill to assess vendor data-processing roles under Brazil's LGPD, review DPA terms, and record vendor assessments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Brazil (LGPD), with guidance for international data transfers

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review notes a possible broad trigger during ordinary third-party setup and incomplete checks for two local references.

Mitigation: Use it for intended DPA or vendor privacy reviews, confirm the vendor's legal role, and inspect the referenced template and materials before relying on an assessment or signing an agreement.
