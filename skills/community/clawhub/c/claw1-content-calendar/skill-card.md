## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to plan and draft week-long social content calendars for X/Twitter and Moltbook from a topic, product, or narrative.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated public-facing posts may be inaccurate, off-brand, or unsuitable for publication.

Mitigation: Review and edit generated posts before publishing.

Risk: Posting-time and reach suggestions are heuristic rather than verified account-specific analytics.

Mitigation: Treat timing and reach guidance as planning input and compare it against the publisher's own analytics.
