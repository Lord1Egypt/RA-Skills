## Description:

Make true scalable vector art such as logos, icons, app glyphs, and scalable illustrations, or convert a raster image into clean editable SVG.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Designers, developers, and agents use this skill to generate new SVG logos, icons, glyphs, and flat illustrations or to convert clean raster artwork into editable SVG vectors.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Design prompts and supplied raster images may be processed by third-party image/vector providers.

Mitigation: Avoid uploading confidential images or third-party artwork unless the user has permission and accepts provider handling.

Risk: Vector models may produce incorrect or illegible text in logos or wordmarks.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated text-in-image workflow for exact wordmarks and verify spelling before delivering final SVG assets.

Risk: Busy photos, gradients, or noisy source images can produce bloated or low-quality SVG traces.

Mitigation: Prefer clean, high-contrast, flat-color sources and inspect the SVG to confirm it uses editable paths with a reasonable path count.
