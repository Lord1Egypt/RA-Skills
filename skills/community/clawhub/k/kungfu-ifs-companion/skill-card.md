## Description:

Provides warm, non-clinical IFS-inspired companion guidance for emotional-support wording, inner-parts language, repair after misunderstanding, safety-aware mental-wellness copy, and single-agent companion behavior.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and product teams use this skill to help an agent write gentle, non-clinical companion responses, prompt guidance, conversation examples, and safety-aware mental-wellness copy without presenting as therapy or crisis care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake companion-style emotional support for therapy, diagnosis, emergency care, or a replacement for licensed professional help.

Mitigation: Keep responses explicitly non-clinical, avoid diagnoses and treatment claims, and direct users toward local professional, crisis, or trusted real-world support when appropriate.

Risk: Crisis or self-harm situations may require immediate local support beyond what an agent can provide.

Mitigation: Use short stabilization language, encourage emergency services, crisis lines, or nearby trusted people, and adapt crisis resources to the user's location.

Risk: IFS-inspired parts language could intensify dependency, delusion risk, or over-identification with inner parts if used too strongly.

Mitigation: Use parts language lightly, keep agency with the user, avoid validating unusual beliefs as true, and reinforce real-world relationships and support.
