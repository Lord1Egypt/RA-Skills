## Description:

End-to-end Product Innovation R&D workflow for inspiration gathering, research, and professional report generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[owenrao](https://clawhub.ai/user/owenrao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, researchers, and innovation teams use this skill to turn product ideas into structured R&D reports, including market research, product attributes, benefits, reasons to believe, packaging direction, and promotion strategy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can create a recurring daily research job without clear separate consent or cleanup instructions.

Mitigation: Require explicit user approval before enabling any scheduler behavior, define the schedule and storage location, and provide clear disable and cleanup steps.

Risk: The skill creates local research and report files and may use web, image generation, PDF conversion, or sharing tools.

Mitigation: Confirm the output directory and enabled tools before execution, and review generated files before sharing or using them for business decisions.
