## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for daily depression support, including mood logs, behavioral activation prompts, self-care check-ins, small-win tracking, and mood pattern reflection. It is supportive guidance only and is not a substitute for professional care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may involve sensitive mood notes and mental-health check-ins stored locally on the user's device.

Mitigation: Install only when local storage of this information is acceptable, and avoid entering information that should not remain on the device.

Risk: Users may treat supportive depression guidance as professional mental-health care.

Mitigation: Make clear that the skill is not a replacement for professional care and direct urgent self-harm concerns to crisis resources.
