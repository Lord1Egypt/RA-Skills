## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language framework for single-company moat, challenger, and long-term investment research, with source-labeled facts, assumptions, and validation gaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liufx13](https://clawhub.ai/user/liufx13)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and investment researchers use this skill to analyze one public or pre-public company through a hypothetical new-entrant challenge, testing business boundaries, competitive barriers, financial evidence, and long-term thesis durability. It is research support only and does not provide buy, sell, or timing advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce incorrect investment research if the target company identity or financial data is stale, incomplete, or mismatched.

Mitigation: Require company identity verification, source-labeled figures, and explicit labels for confirmed facts, reasonable inferences, and unverified hypotheses.

Risk: Users may mistake research framing for investment advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as research support, avoid buy or sell recommendations, and surface data gaps or assumptions before drawing conclusions.
