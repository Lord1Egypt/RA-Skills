## Description:

Public Opinion Scanner searches public web sources for recent information about a company or person, labels items as positive, negative, or neutral, preserves source links, and produces a structured Markdown public-opinion analysis report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hugogu](https://clawhub.ai/user/hugogu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to scan public Chinese-language web sources for brand, company, or personal reputation signals and produce a Markdown report with sentiment labels, source links, summaries, source distribution, keywords, and findings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports about individuals can include public personal information or reputational judgments.

Mitigation: Confirm a legitimate purpose before scanning individuals, avoid collecting unnecessary personal details, and restrict or delete saved reports when they are no longer needed.

Risk: Automated sentiment labels and summaries can be misleading or incomplete.

Mitigation: Manually review source links, excerpts, and sentiment labels before relying on the report.
