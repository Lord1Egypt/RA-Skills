## Use Case:

Marketing teams, growth operators, and agencies use this skill to score and revise ad creatives before launch across Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Google Ads, and Amazon Ads. It produces channel-aware creative objectives, hooks, asset specifications, test plans, and QA notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad advertising or growth strategy requests may select this skill when a narrower creative-review workflow is intended.

Mitigation: Confirm that the task is pre-launch ad creative scoring or revision planning before applying the workflow.

Risk: Platform policy, performance, and spend guidance can be misleading if treated as authoritative compliance or financial advice.

Mitigation: Verify platform policy constraints, performance claims, tracking status, and spend decisions against current channel documentation, observed campaign data, and business approval before launch.
