## Use Case:

External users, marketers, and SEO practitioners use this skill to perform SEO audits, diagnose ranking issues, plan topic clusters, build content calendars, and create prioritized organic growth roadmaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: SEO audits and content plans may rely on site reports, Search Console data, analytics data, or keyword metrics supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Use user-approved data sources and review any account or report access before sharing metrics with the agent.

Risk: SEO recommendations can be misleading if the business context, target markets, or scope are missing.

Mitigation: Provide the site type, goals, target markets, SEO focus, and available data; state assumptions when context is incomplete.
