## Description:

Remove AI writing patterns based on Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" research, using 24 detection and rewriting rules to make AI-generated text sound more natural.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lmanchu](https://clawhub.ai/user/lmanchu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and developers use this skill to revise AI-generated or AI-sounding prose by identifying common AI writing patterns and rewriting text while preserving meaning and tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may rewrite files or user-provided documents in ways the user did not intend.

Mitigation: Review proposed or applied edits before accepting them and use the skill only on documents intended for revision.

Risk: Rewrites may introduce unsupported specificity or change factual claims while making prose sound more natural.

Mitigation: Check revised text for accuracy, preserved meaning, and appropriate tone before publication.
