## Description:

Roadshow Planning helps founders and project teams prepare fundraising pitches with structured timing, talking points, market, competition, financial, team, funding-use, investor Q&A, rehearsal, and slide-planning guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, project teams, and fundraising advisors use this skill to turn company and financing details into a practical roadshow plan, including pitch flow, scripts, investor questions, and presentation structure. It is primarily intended for Chinese-language fundraising preparation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may request sensitive company, team, fundraising, and financial details while preparing roadshow materials.

Mitigation: Provide only business-plan files, financial data, and internal details that are appropriate to use for fundraising preparation.

Risk: Generated market, competition, financial, and investor-answer content may be incomplete or misleading if source assumptions are weak or outdated.

Mitigation: Review factual claims, financial assumptions, market figures, and investor-facing language before using the output in a live roadshow.
