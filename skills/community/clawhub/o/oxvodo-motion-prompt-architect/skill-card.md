## Description:

Turns rough creative ideas into structured English or Chinese motion prompts, reference bindings, shot sequences, and focused debugging loops for text-to-video, image-to-video, reference-to-video, video-to-video, product motion, cinematic scenes, transitions, and loops.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to turn short creative briefs into production-ready motion prompts, reference bindings, shot plans, continuity constraints, and focused revision guidance. It is useful for text-to-video, image-to-video, reference-to-video, video-to-video, product motion, cinematic scenes, transitions, and loops.
