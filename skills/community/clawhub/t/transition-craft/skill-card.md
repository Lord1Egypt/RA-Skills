## Description:

Provides six video transition templates with beat-timing guidance and presets for common editing styles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Video editors, motion designers, and developers can use the skill to plan and build beat-timed transitions such as recursive zooms, bokeh dissolves, tile flips, and shake cuts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
