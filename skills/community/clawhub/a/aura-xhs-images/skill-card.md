## Description:

Xiaohongshu infographic card style presets and prompt templates for astrology, tarot, and lifestyle content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[luoboask](https://clawhub.ai/user/luoboask)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and agents use this skill to plan Xiaohongshu-style image card series for astrology, tarot, lifestyle, rankings, and knowledge posts. The skill helps select visual styles, layouts, language handling, outlines, and image-generation prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is specialized for Xiaohongshu-style visual card prompts and uses Chinese defaults for that platform.

Mitigation: Choose the English, bilingual, or alternate-platform options described by the skill when creating non-Xiaohongshu or non-Chinese content.

Risk: The skill assembles prompts and style guidance but does not generate images by itself.

Mitigation: Use an external image-generation tool and review generated images before publication.
