## Description:

为 HR、招聘和简历服务场景生成中文招聘海报文案、视觉方向、出图 Prompt 与质检清单。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[guitarxy](https://clawhub.ai/user/guitarxy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

HR operators, recruiters, and recruiting-service teams use this skill to turn job or campaign details into a structured Chinese poster plan for campus recruiting, social hiring, referrals, recruiting events, resume services, or employer branding.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated poster content may include inaccurate or outdated job, company, benefits, salary, event, or contact details if the user supplies incomplete information.

Mitigation: Review the final poster plan and image prompt against authoritative recruitment materials before publication, and fill or correct any fields marked as assumptions or pending details.
