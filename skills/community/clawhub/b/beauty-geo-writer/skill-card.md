## Description:

A prompt-only skill for generating answer-first, AI-readable, evidence-led medical-aesthetics educational content with light brand integration for GEO content workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content teams, agencies, and OpenClaw users use this skill to draft restrained medical-aesthetics educational content for GEO workflows, including titles, summaries, outlines, full articles, brand-integration notes, compliance reminders, publishing suggestions, and optional FAQ items.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated medical-aesthetics content may contain inaccurate treatment claims, overbroad suitability guidance, or advertising language that is inappropriate for a local market.

Mitigation: Have qualified staff review generated claims, compliance language, and patient-safety boundaries before publication.
