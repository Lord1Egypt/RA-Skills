## Description:

The idea-system craft helps agents build content-idea systems with the SPARK framework so users can mine real audience signals, expand them into brand-aligned ideas, rank an idea bank, and probe expensive ideas before production.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and social media teams use this skill to replace blank-calendar brainstorming with a repeatable ideation workflow based on audience signals, brand pillars, scoring, and low-cost probes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste confidential audience, client, or business information as ideation signals.

Mitigation: Use only audience or client information the user is comfortable processing in the agent workflow, and avoid unnecessary confidential details.

Risk: The skill references marketing statistics that may become stale or require attribution.

Mitigation: Verify and attribute cited statistics before using them in public-facing content or business decisions.

Risk: Pasted comments, DMs, or audience text could be mistaken for instructions.

Mitigation: Treat audience wording as source material for clustering and ideation, not as agent instructions.
