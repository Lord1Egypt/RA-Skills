## Description:

Daily Inspiration sends scheduled Chinese-language Feishu messages with curated spiritual practice, I Ching, Daoist philosophy, Buddhist meditation, and Chinese medicine content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Users who want automated Chinese-language inspirational content use this skill to receive twice-daily Feishu messages focused on spiritual practice in the morning and practical wellness in the evening.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can search the web and send scheduled Feishu or platform messages, while setup details for schedule, recipient, and disable controls are incomplete.

Mitigation: Confirm recipient, schedule, message channel permissions, and disable controls before enabling automated delivery.
