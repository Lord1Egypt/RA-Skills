## Description:

编写企业客户培训大纲并生成DOCX文档，适用于商业培训、企业内训和公开课场景的课程大纲或培训方案撰写。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Training professionals, consultants, and enterprise learning teams use this skill to collect course requirements, draft a structured Chinese training outline, and produce a professionally formatted DOCX document.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated DOCX may be saved to an unintended path or overwrite an existing document.

Mitigation: Confirm the destination path and filename before writing the DOCX.

Risk: Training briefs may contain sensitive client or internal business information.

Mitigation: Avoid providing unnecessary sensitive details and review the generated outline before sharing.
