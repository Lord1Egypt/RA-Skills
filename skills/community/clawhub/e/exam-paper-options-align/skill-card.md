## Description:

Aligns multiple-choice options in Word exam papers and standardizes typography and spacing for printing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lianhaibin](https://clawhub.ai/user/lianhaibin)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Teachers and exam preparers use this skill to align multiple-choice options and standardize a Word exam paper's formatting before printing. It can inspect a document first, then save a formatted copy or update the original with a backup.
