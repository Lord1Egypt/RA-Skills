## Description:

Design distinctive user-facing interfaces and avoid generic AI-generated visual patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill when designing user-facing frontend interfaces that need stronger visual identity, distinctive typography, intentional color palettes, asymmetric layouts, and micro-interactions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer frontend aesthetic decisions toward opinionated typography, color, layout, and animation choices that do not fit a product's brand or accessibility requirements.

Mitigation: Review generated frontend designs against brand, accessibility, and product UX requirements before release.
