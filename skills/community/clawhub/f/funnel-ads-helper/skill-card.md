## Use Case:

External marketing teams and ecommerce operators use this skill to diagnose paid traffic conversion funnels, identify stage-level bottlenecks, and plan prioritized optimization experiments tied to CPA or ROAS goals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce weak recommendations when funnel stages are undefined, inconsistent, or missing stage-level evidence.

Mitigation: Provide funnel stage metrics, traffic source breakdown, conversion goal, and observation window; map stages canonically before diagnosis when definitions differ.

Risk: Funnel diagnosis may involve sensitive customer-level or business performance data.

Mitigation: Use aggregated funnel metrics and avoid sharing unnecessary customer-level or sensitive business data.

Risk: Running several high-impact funnel changes at once can make attribution unclear.

Mitigation: Prioritize experiments by expected impact and effort, then sequence major changes so results remain attributable.
