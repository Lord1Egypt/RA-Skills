## Description:

Edu Private Operations helps education and training organizations generate Chinese-language enrollment plans, community operations playbooks, parent communication scripts, content calendars, event plans, and operating metric analyses for private-domain marketing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaronnee520](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaronnee520)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Education operators, enrollment teams, and training institution managers use this skill to plan student acquisition, package courses, run parent communities, prepare compliant outreach copy, analyze conversion and renewal metrics, and organize promotional events.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Declared dependencies may be unsuitable for a deployment if left unpinned or unnecessary.

Mitigation: Remove unused dependencies or pin approved versions before deploying the skill.

Risk: Prompts or generated analyses may involve parent or student sensitive data.

Mitigation: Use anonymized inputs unless the hosting environment has appropriate privacy controls for real education records.

Risk: Marketing and parent communication guidance could be misapplied as exaggerated claims or over-frequent outreach.

Mitigation: Review generated campaigns for local education rules, truthfulness, pacing, and privacy before use.
