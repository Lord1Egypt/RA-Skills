## Description:

Estimate first-year value for one major-US credit card from an optional spending breakdown, including welcome bonus, estimated earn, credits, and annual fee.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users use this skill to compare the estimated first-year value of a specific major U.S. credit card using current offer terms and an optional spending profile.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Credit-card offers, eligibility rules, credits, earning rates, and annual fees can change after the estimate is produced.

Mitigation: Verify the final terms on the issuer page before relying on the estimate.

Risk: A value estimate could be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Present the result as comparison and estimation guidance, with confidence notes and assumptions clearly stated.
