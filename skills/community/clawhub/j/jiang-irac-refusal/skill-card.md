## Use Case:

External trademark professionals and legal teams use this skill to analyze CNIPA refusal notices, map refusal grounds to evidence, and draft examiner-readable refusal-review structures with SJ-6 evidence checks and A-E risk gates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global for PRC/CNIPA trademark refusal-review matters

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Document Mode output may be mistaken for a final filing-ready legal submission.

Mitigation: Require review by a qualified trademark professional before filing, with specific checks for deadlines, standing, evidence admissibility, and CNIPA strategy.

Risk: Incomplete refusal notices, citation-mark data, or evidence packets can produce conservative or incomplete route recommendations.

Mitigation: Collect the refusal notice, application details, citation marks, timeline, and evidence list before relying on route selection or risk grades.

Risk: Weak or unverifiable evidence can undermine the proposed IRAC arguments and trigger stop-loss recommendations.

Mitigation: Validate each exhibit against SJ-6 criteria and prioritize third-party, transactional, and public-record evidence over unsupported self-produced materials.
