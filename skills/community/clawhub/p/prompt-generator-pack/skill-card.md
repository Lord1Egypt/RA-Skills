## Use Case:

External users use this skill to request ready-to-copy Chinese prompt templates and brief improvement guidance for common AI writing, marketing, business, learning, productivity, and technical workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad invocation examples may cause an auto-router to select the skill for ordinary prompt-writing requests.

Mitigation: Route or invoke it for Chinese prompt-template requests, and confirm intent before applying templates to unrelated writing tasks.

Risk: Non-Chinese users may find the templates less useful because the pack is primarily Chinese-language.

Mitigation: Translate or adapt templates before use when the target user, audience, or output language is not Chinese.
