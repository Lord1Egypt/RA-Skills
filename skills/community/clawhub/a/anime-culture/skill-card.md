## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to plan niche anime, guochao, cosplay, and photo-friendly outings by filtering venues or projects by style, price, services, booking rules, and social-media popularity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Venue addresses, hours, prices, booking rules, and event details may become outdated or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Verify current details against official venue channels or other current public sources before relying on the recommendations.

Risk: Some locations may have queues, photography limits, try-on rules, or minimum-spend requirements that affect the visit.

Mitigation: Confirm site-specific rules in advance and plan reservations or off-peak visits where practical.
