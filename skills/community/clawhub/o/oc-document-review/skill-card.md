## Description:

Review messy specs, policies, and handoff notes with source-linked findings instead of vague rewrite advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiepu110](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiepu110)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external reviewers, and developers use this skill to review important specs, policies, and handoff notes against explicit criteria, producing source-linked findings, suggested fixes, and open questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive or confidential documents may be exposed to the current agent environment during review.

Mitigation: Review only documents that are appropriate for the active agent environment, and remove or redact sensitive content before use when required.

Risk: Readers may mistake document-review feedback for legal, tax, medical, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Keep findings tied to the supplied source material and route professional advice questions to qualified reviewers.
