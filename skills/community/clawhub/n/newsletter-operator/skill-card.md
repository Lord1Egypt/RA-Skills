## Use Case:

Newsletter operators, editors, and growth teams use this skill to manage recurring newsletter work including research, issue drafting, preflight review, promotion, growth, sponsorship, monetization, ROI review, and workspace handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Newsletter publishing, subscriber changes, sponsor outreach, payment collection, or workspace persistence could affect external audiences or business relationships if performed without approval.

Mitigation: Require explicit user approval before sending, scheduling, publishing, changing subscribers, contacting sponsors, committing spend, collecting payments, or saving to a connected workspace.

Risk: Newsletter drafts or promotion copy could include unverified facts, quotes, event details, metrics, sponsor claims, or deadlines.

Mitigation: Separate verified facts from assumptions, preserve source notes for factual claims, flag approval blockers, and recommend hold or revise when core links, facts, sponsor obligations, or approvals are missing.
