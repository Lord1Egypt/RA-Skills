## Description:

Create persuasive, SEO-friendly product descriptions for e-commerce platforms such as Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, eBay, and WooCommerce, with platform-specific formats, keywords, bullet points, copywriting scores, and alternative versions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers, marketers, and content teams use this skill to draft or optimize product listings across marketplaces and storefronts, including multi-language and platform-specific copy. It helps turn product details into customer-facing descriptions, SEO keyword sections, benefit bullets, calls to action, and copy variants for testing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated product copy may include inaccurate SEO, warranty, social-proof, or compliance claims if the input is incomplete or unverified.

Mitigation: Review claims against product, brand, marketplace, and regulatory requirements before publishing a live listing.

Risk: Input may include product or brand information that should not be shared with an agent runtime.

Mitigation: Provide only product and brand information that is approved for use in the target workflow.
