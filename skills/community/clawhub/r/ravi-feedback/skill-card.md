## Description:

Send feedback to the Ravi team, including bugs, feature requests, praise, frustrations, and suggestions; do not use it for general email sending.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raunaksingwi](https://clawhub.ai/user/raunaksingwi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents using Ravi use this skill to send feedback, bug reports, feature requests, friction reports, positive feedback, and suggestions to the Ravi team by composing email to feedback@ravi.id.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill directs an agent to proactively send email from the user's Ravi identity without clear per-message consent.

Mitigation: Require the agent to show the recipient, subject, and full message body and obtain approval before sending any feedback email.
