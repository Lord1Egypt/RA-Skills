## Description:

Helps evaluators recognize when an overall impression is biasing judgments of separate attributes, then assess each attribute against its own evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Managers, interviewers, investors, and other evaluators can use this skill to examine performance reviews, hiring decisions, vendors, companies, and business analyses for halo bias. It guides them to separate attributes, check each against specific evidence, and structure a more balanced decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
