## Description:

Organizes public Douyu live-streaming categories, event pages, streamer rules, announcements, and help information for compliant summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mikeclaw007](https://clawhub.ai/user/mikeclaw007)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to summarize and organize publicly visible Douyu platform information, including live categories, event pages, streamer rules, announcements, help pages, and safety resources. It is not for private account pages, streamer backend data, messages, boosting views, gift arbitrage, account takeover, cheating, or evading risk controls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intended for public Douyu information only; using it on private account pages, streamer backend data, messages, or other non-public data could violate platform rules or privacy expectations.

Mitigation: Use it only with publicly visible pages and avoid account-specific, backend, private, or sensitive content.

Risk: Outputs about live categories, events, rules, and announcements may become outdated because Douyu platform content changes frequently.

Mitigation: Include the access time and source URL in summaries and re-check Douyu pages before relying on time-sensitive details.

Risk: Requests for boosting views, gift arbitrage, account takeover, cheating, or risk-control evasion are outside scope.

Mitigation: Refuse those requests and keep outputs limited to compliant public information organization.
