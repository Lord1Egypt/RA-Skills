## Description:

Multi-platform content generator for Chinese social media that produces publish-ready posts for 小红书, 知乎, 公众号, and 抖音 with platform-native formatting, hooks, and CTAs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to draft Chinese social media posts for 小红书, 知乎, 公众号, and 抖音. It helps collect topic, platform, length, style, and image-suggestion preferences before producing platform-native copy with hooks, CTAs, and tags.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on generic writing prompts outside the intended Chinese social media workflow.

Mitigation: Invoke or configure it specifically for Chinese social media content tasks, especially when multiple writing skills are installed.

Risk: Generated content may include claims, data, reviews, or experiences that require verification before publication.

Mitigation: Review drafts for verifiable citations, avoid fake reviews or experiences, and remove absolute claims before publishing.
