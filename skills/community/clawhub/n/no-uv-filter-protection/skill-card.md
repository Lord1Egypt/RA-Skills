## Description:

Advises photographers when UV or skylight filters should be removed to avoid image degradation and lens flare, and when harsh conditions may justify a protective filter.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External photographers and photo-advice agents use this skill to answer UV-filter and skylight-filter lens protection questions, troubleshoot lens flare, and recommend lower-risk lens handling alternatives. It is not intended for creative filter use or pure gear-shopping recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked for broader lens-care or product-recommendation questions beyond UV filters and flare troubleshooting.

Mitigation: Use it for UV filters, skylight filters, lens flare, and harsh-environment protection decisions; route creative filter use and shopping advice elsewhere.

Risk: Overgeneralized advice could discourage protective filter use in harsh environments where a filter may reduce lens damage.

Mitigation: Check whether the user is shooting around sand, dust, salt spray, or similar hazards before recommending that the filter be removed.
