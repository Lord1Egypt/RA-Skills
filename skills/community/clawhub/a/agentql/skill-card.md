### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Page content or extracted data may be processed by AgentQL during scraping or browser automation.

Mitigation: Do not use the skill on sensitive, internal, regulated, or authenticated pages unless AgentQL data handling has been reviewed and approved.

Risk: The AgentQL API key is required for use and could be exposed if copied into scripts, logs, or shared prompts.

Mitigation: Store AGENTQL_API_KEY in the environment or a secret manager, and avoid hard-coding it in generated scripts or shared files.
