## Description:

Generates crisis communication playbooks for business teams, including holding statements, stakeholder updates, media Q&A, internal briefs, social media responses, timelines, and post-crisis review templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Business communications, legal, security, and leadership teams use this skill to draft crisis response materials for incidents such as breaches, outages, recalls, regulatory events, and public-relations issues. Generated messaging should be reviewed by responsible human owners before external use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft crisis messaging may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for legal, regulatory, security, or public-disclosure obligations.

Mitigation: Have legal, security, communications, and business owners verify facts, obligations, audience, timing, and wording before external use.

Risk: Generated statements may disclose sensitive details or make commitments before the organization has confirmed its response.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts and tailor them to confirmed facts, approved disclosure boundaries, and the organization's incident response policy.
