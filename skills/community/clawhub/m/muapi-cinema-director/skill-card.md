## Description:

Direct high-fidelity cinematic video with AI by translating creative intent into technical cinematographic directives for Veo3, Kling, and Luma video models via muapi.ai.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to turn a creative video brief into cinematography-aware prompts and script invocations for muapi.ai video generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Video briefs and generated cinematic prompts may be sent to muapi.ai and downstream video providers.

Mitigation: Avoid including secrets, private personal data, or proprietary material unless those services are approved for that content.

Risk: Generated cinematic directives can conflict with model strengths, temporal consistency, or physically realistic camera movement.

Mitigation: Review prompts before execution and prefer the skill's stated guardrails for realistic movement, temporal consistency, and model selection.
