## Description:

Helps beginners and early-stage game teams estimate rough game development budget ranges by analyzing scope, target milestone, team coverage, work model, geography, cost drivers, hidden costs, and budget-reduction options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, beginners, and early-stage game teams use this skill to estimate likely budget ranges for prototypes, vertical slices, releases, or live F2P projects. It helps identify main cost drivers, covered team capabilities, missing cost buckets, assumptions, and practical ways to reduce spend.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Budget ranges may be mistaken for precise quotes or financial commitments.

Mitigation: Present estimates as rough ranges with explicit scope, team, geography, work-model, and timeline assumptions, and recommend professional review before committing spend.
