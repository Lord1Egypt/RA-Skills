## Description:

Search products, services and shops listed on GenesisPay and read their verified-purchase reviews. Use when the user wants to find, compare or look up something to buy (a product, a paid API, a shop) and the read-only GenesisPay Catalog MCP tools (genesispay_catalog_search, genesispay_catalog_shops, genesispay_catalog_trending, genesispay_catalog_reviews) are available. The catalog never pays.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Shoppers and agents use this skill to find products, paid services, and shops in the GenesisPay catalog, compare listing details, and review verified-purchase feedback. It explains purchase options without buying, reserving, or paying.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shopping search terms may reveal sensitive interests when sent to GenesisPay.

Mitigation: Use broad, non-sensitive search terms and let the user know their query is sent to GenesisPay when that may affect their choice.
