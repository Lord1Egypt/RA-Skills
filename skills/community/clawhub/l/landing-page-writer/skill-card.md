## Description:

Plan, write or fill out a product, feature or service landing page so it is complete and true to the product, building from the site's own proof, pricing and screenshots first, with every claim sourced.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Content strategists, marketers, and product teams use this skill to plan or improve product, feature, and service landing pages using verified site material. It produces page copy alongside a completeness check, asset inventory, and sourced claims sheet.
