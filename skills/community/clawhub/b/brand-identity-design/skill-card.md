## Description:

Guides agents through a six-stage brand visual identity workflow covering brand discovery, visual strategy, concept exploration, identity system design, touchpoint validation, and brand manual delivery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fize](https://clawhub.ai/user/fize)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, designers, brand teams, and agents use this skill to structure brand visual identity work from initial research through guidelines and multi-touchpoint validation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer work through a rigid six-stage process that can be heavier than lightweight brand design requests.

Mitigation: Use it for structured visual identity engagements and decide the expected depth of each stage before deployment.

Risk: The skill may use English section headings even when prompts are written in another language.

Mitigation: Review generated deliverables for localization and translate headings or labels before client-facing use.
