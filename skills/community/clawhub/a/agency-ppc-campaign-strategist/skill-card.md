This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, paid media specialists, and agencies use this skill to plan and optimize PPC account structures, bidding strategies, budget allocation, campaign type selection, and cross-platform paid search performance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use connected ad-platform APIs and propose or execute campaign creation, budget changes, bidding changes, or keyword deployments.

Mitigation: Prefer read-only API access by default and require explicit confirmation before allowing write actions.

Risk: Recommendations based on live ad account data can affect spend, targeting, and campaign performance.

Mitigation: Review account data, assumptions, and change plans before applying them to production advertising accounts.
