## Description:

Self-Discovery Mentor guides users through reflective personal growth coaching to explore values, strengths, life direction, limiting beliefs, and actionable growth plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nannl22](https://clawhub.ai/user/nannl22)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for non-clinical self-discovery coaching, including values exploration, strengths discovery, life direction reflection, and concrete growth planning. It is suited to personal development conversations where the agent should ask reflective questions instead of making decisions for the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake reflective personal-growth coaching for therapy or crisis support.

Mitigation: Present the skill as non-clinical coaching, and direct users with severe distress, depression, anxiety, or crisis needs to qualified professional or emergency support.

Risk: Self-discovery prompts can ask users to discuss sensitive personal experiences or emotions.

Mitigation: Let users control depth and pace, normalize skipping prompts, and avoid pressuring users to share details that feel too intense or too personal.
