## Description:

Assists Spanish taxpayers with reviewing AEAT or foral draft returns, identifying candidate deductions, and preparing an estimated 2025 IRPF return from source documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[joseconti](https://clawhub.ai/user/joseconti)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and tax-support agents use this skill to collect Spanish IRPF facts, review draft returns, identify candidate deductions, and prepare an estimated Renta WEB input package for user or professional verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Spain

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for sensitive tax, family, banking, employment, health, and third-party information.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum required fields, redact unnecessary identifiers and account numbers, and avoid uploading full medical or third-party records.

Risk: Tax calculations and deduction eligibility can be wrong or incomplete for complex Spanish, autonomous-community, or foral cases.

Mitigation: Use results as a checklist or estimate and verify final figures against official AEAT or foral sources or a qualified tax professional before filing.
