## Description:

Analyze resumes for target roles, identify weak bullets, missing keywords, ATS gaps, and provide actionable rewrite suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[andrewgufx](https://clawhub.ai/user/andrewgufx)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users, especially students and early-career candidates, use this skill to evaluate resumes against target roles and improve weak bullets, keyword coverage, ATS readiness, and role fit. It is especially oriented toward analytics, data science, product analytics, business analyst, and related applications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume reviews may involve personal contact details, employment history, education records, or other sensitive user-provided information.

Mitigation: Users should omit or redact unnecessary personal details before sharing resume text.

Risk: Resume coaching can produce inaccurate, overstated, or poorly matched recommendations if the supplied resume or target-role context is incomplete.

Mitigation: Users should review suggested edits for accuracy and keep claims truthful, specific, and supportable.
