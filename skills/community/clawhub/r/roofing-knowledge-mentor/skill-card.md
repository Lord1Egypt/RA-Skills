## Use Case:

External roofing contractors, adjusters, inspectors, and sales teams use this skill to reason through roofing measurements, proposals, insurance narratives, workflows, performance metrics, and fundamentals. It supports practical decision-making and professional communication rather than automated pricing, coverage, or compliance determinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat roofing guidance as legal, insurance coverage, pricing, or compliance authority.

Mitigation: Use outputs as professional guidance only and verify legal, insurance, pricing, and code-compliance decisions with qualified sources.

Risk: Advice based on incomplete measurements, photos, or job details may lead to incorrect material decisions or unclear proposals.

Mitigation: State assumptions, verify roof geometry and site conditions, and re-check measurements before ordering materials or finalizing scopes.

Risk: The package includes an ancillary local validator script that is not required for normal roofing-advice use.

Mitigation: Use the skill through normal agent guidance flows; review local scripts before running them in a separate validation workflow.
