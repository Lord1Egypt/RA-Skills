## Description:

Meeting Minutes AI helps organize meeting recordings or notes, extract key decisions and action items, generate structured meeting minutes, and track follow-up items across common meeting types.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[awdasfdada](https://clawhub.ai/user/awdasfdada)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external collaborators use this skill to turn meeting transcripts or notes into structured minutes with decisions, owners, deadlines, priorities, discussion points, and next-meeting planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting transcripts can contain confidential or sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Only provide meeting content that is appropriate for the active agent environment and redact sensitive details before use.

Risk: Generated decisions, owners, or deadlines may be incomplete or inaccurate if source notes are ambiguous.

Mitigation: Review generated minutes against the original transcript before sharing or assigning follow-up work.
