## Description:

Triage, categorize, and draft responses to emails. Sorts by urgency, flags action items, and generates context-aware reply drafts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Employees and external users use this skill to sort pasted or forwarded emails by urgency, identify next actions, extract deadlines, and draft concise replies for actionable messages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill handles sensitive email content and may process confidential inbox material.

Mitigation: Use it only with emails intentionally provided to the agent, redact credentials and regulated data, and review all outputs before acting on them.

Risk: Drafted replies may contain incorrect wording, commitments, or context-sensitive decisions.

Mitigation: Review every draft before sending and confirm deadlines, owners, and next actions against the original email thread.

Risk: The skill points to external context packs without clear privacy boundaries.

Mitigation: Avoid using external context packs unless the source is explicitly trusted for the email domain and use case.
