## Description:

Build boolean and X-ray search strings to source candidates, including ready-to-paste strings with synonyms, must-haves, exclusions, X-ray variants, and a refinement plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Recruiters, sourcers, and hiring teams use this skill to turn a role brief into job-related boolean and X-ray candidate search strings. It helps balance precision and recall with title synonyms, skill terms, exclusions, platform variants, and refinement guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated recruiting search criteria could include unlawful or discriminatory filters if the user's brief asks for protected or proxy characteristics.

Mitigation: Review generated search strings before use and keep criteria job-related, lawful, and non-discriminatory.

Risk: Boolean syntax or platform-specific query behavior may produce noisy or incomplete candidate results.

Mitigation: Check syntax, note platform quirks, and use the refinement plan to broaden or narrow the candidate pool.
