## Description: <br>
Helps agents coach teams through OKR goal setting, turning qualitative objectives into measurable key results and a quarterly review cadence. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, founders, managers, and operating teams use this skill to draft, calibrate, align, and score quarterly OKRs. It is best suited for teams that need measurable outcomes, cross-team alignment, and a cadence for transparent review. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated OKRs may be poorly suited to the organization or misused for performance management and compensation decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated goals for organizational fit before use and keep OKR scoring separate from performance reviews and compensation. <br>
