## Description:

tico-travel helps agents generate research-backed, PDF-ready travel guides with verified entry, health, safety, logistics, budget, and modular planning guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jovicuz](https://clawhub.ai/user/jovicuz)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to build destination-specific planning guides tailored to nationality, travel style, duration, and year. It supports practical trip decisions across entry requirements, health, transport, safety, culture, lodging, activities, and realistic budgets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Travel guidance can become outdated for visa, health, price, safety, transport, and emergency details.

Mitigation: Verify critical trip details against current official government, health, airline, airport, border-control, and local sources before relying on them.

Risk: The skill may ask for travel-planning details such as passport nationality, destination, duration, travel style, and year.

Mitigation: Provide only the details needed for the trip-planning task and avoid sharing unrelated sensitive personal information.
