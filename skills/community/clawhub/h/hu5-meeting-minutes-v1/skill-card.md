## Description:

A four-column method for turning multi-party chats, long conversations, and meeting notes into decisions, action items, in-progress items, and disagreements, with source checks and echo filtering to keep summaries grounded.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openmsg88](https://clawhub.ai/user/openmsg88)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Employees and external collaborators can use the method to turn meeting transcripts, group chats, and long-running conversations into concise records of decisions, owners and deadlines, open items, and unresolved disagreements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A summary may omit or misstate a decision when key messages are missed or a later update supersedes an earlier conclusion.

Mitigation: Check each key fact against the original conversation, order updates by timestamp, mark superseded conclusions, and review the omission and disagreement checks.
