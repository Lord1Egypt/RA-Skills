## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and operators use this skill to audit an X account, content plan, or week of activity and identify positioning, content, reply, profile, and analytics improvements.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Growth recommendations could be incomplete or misleading if the user provides narrow examples or stale analytics.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current account goals, platform policy, and recent performance data before changing strategy.

Risk: Users may include sensitive account analytics or strategy details while requesting a review.

Mitigation: Share only the account context and aggregated metrics needed for the review, and avoid private credentials or account access tokens.
