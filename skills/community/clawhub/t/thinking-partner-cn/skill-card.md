## Description:

Collaborative thinking partner for exploring complex problems through questioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jirboy](https://clawhub.ai/user/jirboy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to explore complex topics through guided questioning, assumption testing, insight tracking, and periodic summaries before moving toward solutions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask the agent to look for existing notes or context, which could expose private notes if the conversation scope is too broad.

Mitigation: Keep private notes out of scope unless they are intended to be used in the discussion.

Risk: Exploratory questioning and summaries can shape decisions even when the skill is not providing final answers.

Mitigation: Review important conclusions before acting on them, especially for sensitive personal, business, legal, medical, or financial topics.
