## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Queries and stack details sent to the hosted AgentDeals service may be logged and publicly exposed.

Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential architecture, business plans, customer names, internal vendor lists, or secrets until request-log exposure is removed or access-controlled and privacy documentation is corrected.

Risk: The deal index and pricing-change summaries can become stale as vendors change plans and eligibility rules.

Mitigation: Verify important offers against the vendor's current pricing or program page before making purchasing or architecture decisions.
