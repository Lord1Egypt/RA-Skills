## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers and marketers use this skill to rewrite Korean product-page, ad, banner, live-commerce, and notification copy so it sounds natural, preserves selling points, and avoids high-risk promotional claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated ecommerce copy can still contain incorrect legal or platform-compliance claims.

Mitigation: Independently verify advertising, product, and platform-compliance claims before publishing ads or product pages.
