## Description:

Perform comprehensive SEO audits including technical SEO, content analysis, backlink profiling, and competitive benchmarking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[btizzy](https://clawhub.ai/user/btizzy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and marketing teams use this skill to audit a website's technical SEO, on-page SEO, off-page SEO, and competitive search opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The trigger language is broad and could be invoked when a full SEO audit is not intended.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when explicitly requesting an SEO audit.

Risk: Website data submitted for analysis may include information the user does not want reviewed.

Mitigation: Provide only website data the user is comfortable having analyzed.

Risk: SEO recommendations may be incomplete or misleading for a specific business context.

Mitigation: Review prioritized findings before implementing website changes.
