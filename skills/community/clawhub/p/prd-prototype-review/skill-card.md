## Description:

Reviews PRD and product prototype documents across requirements, interaction design, risk, and implementation dimensions, then produces a structured review report and optional document patches after user confirmation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[woai36d](https://clawhub.ai/user/woai36d)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, designers, and engineering teams use this skill to review PRDs and prototypes for completeness, clarity, consistency, edge cases, and delivery risk before implementation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can propose and, after confirmation, apply changes to PRD or prototype files.

Mitigation: Review the generated report and each proposed patch before approving edits, and keep document version history or changelogs available for rollback.

Risk: Review findings may affect product scope, priorities, or implementation plans.

Mitigation: Treat findings as review guidance and confirm material requirement or prototype changes with the responsible product and engineering owners.
