## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Political, legal, and weapons-adjacent commentary may be mistaken for operational guidance or legal advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as source commentary, avoid procedural assistance for harm, and direct legal questions to qualified counsel.

Risk: Ideological material may be presented as endorsed or one-sided analysis.

Mitigation: Keep responses neutral, preserve the volume's internal disagreements, and identify controversial claims as claims from the source material.
