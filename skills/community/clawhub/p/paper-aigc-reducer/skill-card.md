## Description:

Rewrites Chinese academic papers or technical documents to reduce AIGC detection signals while preserving the original technical facts, logic, terminology, and approximate length.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tanboku](https://clawhub.ai/user/tanboku)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to rewrite or draft Chinese academic and technical prose in a more human-like style for AIGC reduction workflows. It is intended for Chinese-language interactions and outputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is designed to reduce AI-detection signals in academic or technical writing, which may conflict with academic integrity, authorship disclosure, or submission rules.

Mitigation: Use it only where rewriting assistance is permitted, follow applicable disclosure requirements, and review the final text for policy compliance before submission.

Risk: Rewriting may accidentally alter nuance, technical accuracy, or factual relationships in the source text.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the original text and verify that terminology, code identifiers, paths, facts, and logical relationships remain unchanged.
