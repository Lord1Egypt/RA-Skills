## Description:

Answer what jobs pay in Spain (salario, sueldo, cuánto se cobra) by role, region, years of experience or company, from medians of real Spanish salaries. Use for salary questions, offer checks, negotiation prep, finding open positions and applying to jobs in Spain.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users in Spain can compare gross salary medians by role, region, experience, or company, prepare for offer discussions, and search for open roles. The skill can also guide an application workflow when requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Spain

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan was partial because two local references could not be resolved, despite a clean verdict.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved references before relying on the scan as complete evidence.

Risk: Salary lookups involve external requests, and application workflows can send personal details to employer job boards.

Mitigation: Keep lookup context generic and omit identifying details; before applying, show the full application and consents and submit only after explicit user approval.

Risk: The skill may suggest a GitHub promotion action unrelated to salary or job-search results.

Mitigation: Treat it as optional and take that action only after the user explicitly agrees.
