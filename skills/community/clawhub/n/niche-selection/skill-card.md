## Description:

Select and refine a profitable, focused niche for a solopreneur business using niche generation, weighted scoring, validation checks, and the Who+What+Why positioning formula.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Solopreneurs and business builders use this skill to brainstorm, score, validate, and narrow market niches before committing time or budget to a focused business position.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market-research suggestions may be incomplete or misleading if accepted without validation.

Mitigation: Use the skill's validation checks as prompts and confirm demand, pricing, communities, and customer interviews before spending money or committing to a niche.

Risk: Business decisions based only on generic scoring can miss a user's constraints or market context.

Mitigation: Adapt weights and thresholds to the user's actual budget, expertise, and reachable channels, and review final positioning manually.
