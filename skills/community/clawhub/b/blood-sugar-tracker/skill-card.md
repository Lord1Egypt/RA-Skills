## Description:

Tracks blood sugar entries, analyzes trends, provides diet suggestions, and alerts on abnormal values.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to log and review blood sugar readings, monitor trends, receive informational diet suggestions and abnormal value alerts, and generate simple statistics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Blood sugar entries are sensitive health data.

Mitigation: Install only from trusted ClawHub or npm sources, prefer a pinned installer version when available, and avoid sharing logs or outputs beyond the intended user context.

Risk: Diet suggestions and abnormal-range alerts may be incomplete or medically inappropriate for an individual.

Mitigation: Treat the guidance as informational and not as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
