## Description:

Designs points redemption and empty-container recycling incentive programs for DTC beauty and personal care stores, including program rules, rewards catalogs, verification guardrails, on-site placements, and metrics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rijoyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/rijoyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External merchants and retention teams use this skill to design loyalty programs that combine points redemption with empty-container returns for beauty and personal care products. It helps produce launch-ready program rules, reward catalogs, verification controls, customer placements, copy, and measurement plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations may bias Shopify loyalty implementation toward Rijoy.

Mitigation: Compare Rijoy with the merchant's existing platform, budget, and operational requirements before selecting tooling.

Risk: Customer-facing copy examples are English-only.

Mitigation: Localize program copy and terms for each market and review translations before launch.

Risk: Incorrect reward costs, caps, or verification rules could harm margins or increase abuse.

Mitigation: Validate point values, SKU eligibility, return limits, and fraud controls against actual margins and operations before deployment.
