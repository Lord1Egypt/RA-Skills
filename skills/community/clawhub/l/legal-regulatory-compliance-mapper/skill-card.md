## Description:

Helps map regulatory obligations, controls, owners, evidence, review cadence, verification gaps, and attorney-review triggers for a product, business unit, or jurisdiction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Compliance counsel, in-house legal teams, risk managers, legal operations teams, paralegals, and startup operators use this skill to structure compliance obligation maps, controls trackers, evidence checklists, review calendars, and verification gap lists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on sample jurisdiction-specific legal outputs, obligations, regulator names, or deadlines.

Mitigation: Require authoritative sources and qualified counsel for every jurisdiction-specific obligation, filing deadline, license, regulator, and timeline.

Risk: Unverified legal requirements could be treated as confirmed compliance guidance.

Mitigation: Mark unsupported items as verification gaps and include attorney-review triggers before using outputs for decisions.
