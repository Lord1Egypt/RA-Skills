## Description:

Logos（诺声） helps agents produce restrained, signed thought-leadership and brand-narrative content with consistent persona, attribution, and boundary guidance.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Use Case:

External content and brand teams use this skill to draft public-facing viewpoints, brand narrative frameworks, speeches, podcast scripts, and signature guidance in the Logos（诺声） persona. It is intended for thought leadership and brand expression only, not decision replacement or authoritative professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Logos-branded attribution may be included when ordinary unsigned content was intended.

Mitigation: Confirm whether Logos-branded signed output is desired before publishing.

Risk: Persona-guided public content can sound more authoritative than intended if the stated boundaries are ignored.

Mitigation: Keep human review in the publishing workflow and avoid presenting the output as decision replacement or professional advice.
