## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language company-quality analysis checklist based on Qiu Guolu's investment framework, covering ROE, margins, industry position, and pricing power.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lj22503](https://clawhub.ai/user/lj22503)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to screen whether a company appears high quality under a Qiu Guolu-style framework. It structures analysis around profitability, industry rank, pricing power, and common quality-assessment mistakes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial quality scores may be mistaken for personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as an educational screening aid and verify financial data, valuation, industry context, and local regulatory considerations separately.
