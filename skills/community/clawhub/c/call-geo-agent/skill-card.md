## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External model and search tools, prompts, responses, URLs, generated content, and project documents may expose or retain sensitive brand, product, customer, or strategy information.

Mitigation: Use only materials approved for external processing and project-document retention; avoid confidential launch plans, trade secrets, credentials, private customer data, and unreleased strategy unless sharing is approved.

Risk: Generated GEO plans, prompt scoring, source-domain choices, and marketing content may be incomplete, inaccurate, or misleading if used without review.

Mitigation: Review the generated analysis, URLs, scoring, and content drafts before publication or business use.
