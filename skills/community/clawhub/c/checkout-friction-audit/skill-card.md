## Description:

Audit checkout friction points and prioritize fixes that improve completed purchases without increasing risk.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Store operators, growth teams, and ecommerce developers use this skill to audit checkout abandonment signals, inspect accessible checkout flows, and prioritize fixes that improve completed purchases without increasing policy or payment risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Checkout audits may involve sensitive funnel data, screenshots, or browser access.

Mitigation: Share only checkout data, screenshots, or browser access that is appropriate for the audit.

Risk: Suggested changes may affect payment, shipping, compliance, or platform policy behavior.

Mitigation: Review recommended sprint-board or implementation actions before applying them.

Risk: Partial checkout observations can lead to overconfident recommendations.

Mitigation: State when the observed flow is incomplete and tie each recommendation to observed evidence.
