## Description:

Scan for bounties, gigs, freelance opportunities, and revenue lanes across platforms like ClawHub, GitHub, X, and web. Track and prioritize high-value opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaronmda](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaronmda)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to find, prioritize, and maintain a working list of revenue opportunities such as bounties, gigs, freelance work, and publishable ClawHub skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search queries to external platforms could expose sensitive project, business, or personal context.

Mitigation: Provide explicit platform scope and exclude confidential details from search terms or opportunity notes.

Risk: Autonomous scouting could produce stale, low-quality, or unsuitable revenue opportunities.

Mitigation: Review opportunities for value, effort, fit, legitimacy, and current availability before acting.

Risk: Publishing or outreach suggestions could lead to external commitments without adequate review.

Mitigation: Require user approval before publishing skills, contacting third parties, or applying for gigs.
