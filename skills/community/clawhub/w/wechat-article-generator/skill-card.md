## Description:

交互式生成公众号文章。当用户需要创作公众号推文时调用，通过步骤化引导收集文章要素，生成符合公众号风格的文章。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sondro110](https://clawhub.ai/user/sondro110)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and teams use this skill to draft WeChat public-account articles through a step-by-step workflow for topic intake, title options, outlines, article drafts, polishing, image-prompt recommendations, and optional document export.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated public-account drafts may contain inaccurate, overstated, or unsuitable marketing claims.

Mitigation: Review article drafts for accuracy, tone, and platform suitability before publishing.

Risk: Drafts, image-plan files, and exported Word documents may be saved locally when the user chooses those options.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive private information unless the workspace is trusted, and review saved outputs before sharing.
