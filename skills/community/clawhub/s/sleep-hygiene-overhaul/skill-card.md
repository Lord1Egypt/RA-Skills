## Description:

Evidence-based sleep improvement protocol for users who have trouble falling asleep, staying asleep, waking unrefreshed, or want to optimize sleep quality without medication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to run a two-week behavioral sleep improvement protocol with a sleep audit, consistent wake anchor, wind-down ritual, caffeine cutoff, environment checklist, nap guidance, and follow-up check-in.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks the agent to retain sleep schedule, caffeine, alcohol, screen-use, and possible health-flag information across sessions.

Mitigation: Confirm user consent before persisting sleep state and explain how to inspect or delete stored sleep information.

Risk: Sleep problems can reflect medical conditions that behavioral sleep hygiene cannot address.

Mitigation: Escalate waking unrefreshed, snoring, gasping, breathing pauses, restless legs, or lack of improvement after 14 days to medical consultation, and avoid prescription medication recommendations.

## Reference(s):

- [Edinger et al., Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Adults with Insomnia](https://doi.org/10.5664/jsl.8558)
- [Chang et al., Evening Use of Light-Emitting eReaders Negatively Affects Sleep](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1418490112)
