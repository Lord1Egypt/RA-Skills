## Description:

Guides reliability engineers, maintenance planners, asset integrity leads, and RCM facilitators through SAE JA1011- and MSG-3-aligned Reliability-Centered Maintenance analysis for industrial equipment or process systems, producing a draft worksheet for engineering and maintenance-manager review before maintenance program changes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Reliability engineers, maintenance planners, asset integrity leads, plant engineers, CMMS administrators, and RCM facilitators use this skill to structure RCM analyses and draft review-ready maintenance worksheets for industrial equipment and process systems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft RCM worksheets may include incorrect safety-critical intervals, MSI classifications, or redesign decisions when inputs or failure history are incomplete.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning aids only and require qualified engineering and maintenance management approval before any safety-critical interval, redesign decision, or maintenance program change is used.

Risk: Users could treat draft task lists as implementation-ready CMMS changes.

Mitigation: Preserve the draft review block, require Reliability Engineer of Record validation, and keep the skill without CMMS system access.
