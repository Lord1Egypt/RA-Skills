## Description:

Helps social science, education, and music education researchers synthesize user-provided corpora into literature reviews, theoretical frameworks, conceptual models, and research gap analyses with strict citation integrity and evidence hierarchy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[afanmusic](https://clawhub.ai/user/afanmusic)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, educators, and academic writers use this skill to turn user-provided music education and social science materials into structured literature reviews, theory models, evidence-chain tables, research gap matrices, opening-report arguments, and classroom-practice research designs. It is especially oriented toward music education, AI plus music education, Chinese core-journal framing, and corpus-based writing workflows that preserve citation integrity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Academic source metadata or citation status may be incomplete in user-provided corpora.

Mitigation: Require evidence classes, source IDs, and verification labels; mark uncertain items as unverified and tell the user what literature is still needed.

Risk: Users may request complete paper generation without a traceable evidence base.

Mitigation: Use a staged workflow that begins with literature review, theory framing, methods, data, and analysis before drafting prose.

Risk: Corpus access may exceed the intended scope if the user provides broad or ambiguous folders.

Mitigation: Read only explicitly provided research folders or exported materials, avoid credentials and unrelated directories, and ask for narrower inputs when scope is unclear.
