## Description:

Writes and edits Japanese across chat, business, formal documents, social posts, speech, and fiction, with attention to register, keigo, mechanics, names, punctuation, slang, and dialect.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers use this skill to draft, revise, and proofread Japanese text for messages, email, business documents, social posts, speeches, and fiction. It is intended for original Japanese writing and register repair, not source-bound translation, other languages, or Japan travel guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill automatically maintains local writing memory plus shared contact and project records.

Mitigation: Review or back up ~/Clawic/data before installation and use a stateless or explicit-confirmation workflow for sensitive communications.

Risk: Durable memory can capture business, personal, recipient, or project context from writing tasks.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting secrets or confidential details unless they are appropriate for local memory; use credential pointers instead of credential values.

Risk: The artifact describes deleting and migrating entries in local/shared memory locations.

Mitigation: Monitor named writes and deletions during use and keep recoverable backups of the configured Clawic data paths.
