## Description:

Build abandoned cart, abandoned checkout and browse abandonment reminder emails for an online store, plus post-purchase and win-back emails, using Shopify store activity or events an online store sends through the Events API. Use for ecommerce email marketing and cart recovery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce marketers and store operators use this skill to draft cart, checkout, and browse abandonment reminders, along with post-purchase and win-back emails, using the Shopify activity or store events available to their account.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A reminder flow may rely on a trigger the connected store does not provide.

Mitigation: Check the store connection and build triggers only from events available to the account.

Risk: A saved flow or campaign could be launched before its audience is approved.

Mitigation: Review the saved target and launch or schedule only after the user explicitly approves the audience.
