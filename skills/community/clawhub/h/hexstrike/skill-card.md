## Use Case:

Developers, security engineers, and authorized security practitioners use this skill to select security tools, plan CTF and pentest workflows, and generate command-oriented guidance for assessment tasks within approved scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated commands may enable live scanning, exploitation, brute force, or credential-cracking activity outside authorized scope.

Mitigation: Use only for owned, lab, CTF, or explicitly authorized targets, and confirm authorization before executing any command.

Risk: Full-port scans, vulnerability scanners, brute force tools, and credential-cracking workflows can affect networks, accounts, or services.

Mitigation: Confirm target scope, rate limits, and expected impact before execution; prefer non-invasive checks first and add limits or timeouts where appropriate.
