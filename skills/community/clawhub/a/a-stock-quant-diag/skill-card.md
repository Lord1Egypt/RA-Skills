## Description:

A bilingual A-share quantitative diagnosis methodology that guides agents through akshare and baostock data collection, five-dimension scoring, peer comparison, valuation checks, and buy/sell recommendation drafting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to structure A-share stock diagnostics, gather market and financial indicators, compare peers, and draft investment posture recommendations. Outputs should be independently reviewed before any trading decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can generate buy, sell, liquidation, and target-price language for individual stocks.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as market-analysis drafts, verify formulas and data citations, and require independent financial review before acting on recommendations.

Risk: Fund holding and financial disclosure data may lag the market by one to two months or until reporting deadlines.

Mitigation: Check source dates, use current market data where available, and label stale or delayed data in the final analysis.

Risk: Historical valuation and technical statistics do not guarantee future returns.

Mitigation: Combine quantitative scores with current industry context, issuer-specific fundamentals, and explicit uncertainty notes.
