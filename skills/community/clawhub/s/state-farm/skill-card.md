## Description:

State Farm insurance skill. Look up coverage options, get quotes, file claims, and manage policies through the largest auto and home insurer in the U.S.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for general State Farm insurance information, including coverage categories, quote channels, claim filing paths, discounts, and customer contact options. It is informational only and directs policy purchases, claims, and account activity to official State Farm channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States and Washington, D.C.; product availability varies by state.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the skill for an authenticated State Farm service or personalized insurance advice.

Mitigation: Treat responses as general guidance and use State Farm's official website, mobile app, phone number, or a licensed agent before sharing personal, financial, policy, or claim details.

Risk: Insurance rates, discounts, coverage availability, and policy terms vary by state and individual circumstances.

Mitigation: Confirm product availability and final terms through State Farm or a licensed State Farm agent before making insurance decisions.
