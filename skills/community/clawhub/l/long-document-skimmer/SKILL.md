# Long Document Skimmer

## Overview

Deep-reading everything is impossible; skimming badly is how key clauses get missed. This workflow produces a reliable map of a long document first — its claims, its evidence, its obligations — so deep reading goes where it matters.

## When to use

When facing any document over ~10 pages: research papers, contracts, protocol docs, regulatory texts.

## Workflow

1. **Map the structure** — headings, length per section, where the definitions and the money/risk clauses live
2. **Extract the claims** — the document's five load-bearing assertions, in its own terms
3. **Check the evidence** — for each claim: data, citation, assertion, or anecdote?
4. **Hunt the obligations** — in contracts and ToS: what you must do, what they may do, what changes without notice, how you exit
5. **Flag the unknowns** — terms used but never defined, numbers without sources, sections that contradict each other
6. **Decide the deep read** — which sections earn full attention and why

## Guardrails

- Never summarize a contract's risk away — quote the operative sentence for anything binding
- A confident tone in the document is not evidence
- Mark clearly what you skimmed vs read fully in any downstream use

## Output format

A document map: structure, top claims with evidence grades, obligations verbatim, unknowns, and the deep-read list.
