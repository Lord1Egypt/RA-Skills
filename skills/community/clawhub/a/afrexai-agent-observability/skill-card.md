Ops teams, platform engineers, and CTOs use this skill to assess AI agent fleet health, identify monitoring gaps, attribute costs, and plan remediation for production agent deployments.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Benchmark and savings claims may not match a user's environment.

Mitigation: Validate benchmark, waste, and savings estimates against internal telemetry before using them for operational or purchasing decisions.

Risk: Assessment prompts can elicit sensitive operational details such as spend, failures, monitoring gaps, and escalation paths.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive operational details unless the agent and environment are approved for that information.
