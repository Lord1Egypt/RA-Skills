## Description:

Real-time crypto market sentiment analysis that aggregates Twitter/X, Reddit, Google Trends, and exchange data to detect FOMO/FUD cycles and whale movements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and external users use this skill to monitor cryptocurrency sentiment signals across social media, search trends, exchange metrics, and whale transaction alerts. Outputs should be treated as market sentiment analysis rather than financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sentiment analysis may be mistaken for financial advice or over-relied on during volatile market conditions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as market sentiment analysis only and independently verify source data before making investment or trading decisions.

Risk: The skill may use external market or social-media data sources when invoked.

Mitigation: Review data-source access, terms, and credentials before deployment, and run the skill only in permitted environments.
