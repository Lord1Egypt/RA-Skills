## Description:

One-stop AI courier claim assistant for lost, damaged, or disputed packages: legal analysis, compensation calculation, escalation paths, and document generation for all major China express companies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users with China courier disputes use this skill to turn a lost, damaged, delayed, falsely delivered, or customs-held package incident into liability analysis, compensation estimates, escalation steps, evidence checklists, communication scripts, and draft complaint documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Courier dispute details can include sensitive tracking numbers, contact information, addresses, purchase records, and local claim records.

Mitigation: Collect only information needed for the claim, confirm whether local case storage can be disabled or cleared, avoid unnecessary personal details, and delete generated reports or case records after use when retention controls are unavailable.

Risk: The skill produces legal analysis, compensation estimates, and escalation guidance that may be wrong, incomplete, or outdated for a user's specific dispute.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as reference material, verify current rules and platform requirements before filing, and consult a licensed lawyer for high-value disputes, personal injury, litigation, or complex business matters.
