## Description:

Use under time pressure when the situation is still changing and you must act before certainty - cycle Observe, Orient, Decide, Act on about 70% confidence, then re-observe.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, operators, and incident responders use this skill to structure rapid decision cycles during live incidents, intermittent failures, and other time-bounded situations where waiting for certainty would cost more than a reversible action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-apply rapid OODA cycling to irreversible or high-impact actions.

Mitigation: Raise the evidence bar, gather more evidence, or escalate when the next step lacks a rollback path or has high blast radius.

Risk: The loop can become ineffective if action is not followed by immediate re-observation.

Mitigation: Name the next observation and time box before acting, then compare the result to the prediction before starting another loop.
