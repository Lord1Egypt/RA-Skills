## Description:

Guides users through the Theory of Constraints five focusing steps to identify a system constraint, structure improvement actions, and produce a TOC constraint analysis report without making decisions for the user.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Operations, quality improvement, and process leaders use this skill to run an interactive TOC analysis when improvement efforts lack focus or system throughput is constrained. The skill gathers user judgments step by step and formats them into a five-step constraint report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is written in Chinese and may not fit teams that require English-language guidance.

Mitigation: Confirm language fit with intended users before deployment or provide a reviewed translation.

Risk: The skill guides operational decisions but intentionally does not validate business data or choose the constraint for the user.

Mitigation: Require users to supply and review their own operational evidence before acting on the generated TOC report.
