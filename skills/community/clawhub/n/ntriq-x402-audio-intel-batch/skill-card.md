## Description:

Batch audio transcription for up to 500 files. Flat $9.00 USDC via x402.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ntriq-gh](https://clawhub.ai/user/ntriq-gh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External developers and agents use this skill to submit batches of audio URLs to ntriq's hosted transcription endpoint, pay via x402, and receive per-file transcript text, language, status, and duration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive or regulated audio URLs and resulting transcripts may be processed by ntriq's hosted service without detailed privacy or retention terms in the artifact.

Mitigation: Submit only authorized audio, and avoid confidential or regulated content unless the provider's privacy, retention, and access controls have been reviewed.
