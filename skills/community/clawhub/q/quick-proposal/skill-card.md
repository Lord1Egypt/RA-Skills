## Description:

Quick Proposal helps draft project proposals with requirement analysis, timeline and cost estimates, and risk assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, consultants, and project teams use this skill to produce first-draft customer project proposals from a project brief or requirements document. The draft can include scope, solution outline, schedule, budget, payment terms, team roles, risks, differentiators, and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated budgets, timelines, and contractual language may be template-based and inaccurate for a specific customer engagement.

Mitigation: Review and adjust estimates, payment terms, and commitments before sending proposals to customers.

Risk: The proposal draft may omit customer-specific constraints or overstate delivery assumptions.

Mitigation: Validate requirements, dependencies, and risk statements against the source brief before using the output.
