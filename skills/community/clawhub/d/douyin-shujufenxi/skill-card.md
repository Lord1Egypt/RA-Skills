## Description:

Collects public Douyin video, creator, comment, detail, and trending data as structured JSON for content, competitor, sentiment, and topic analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[engheng-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/engheng-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, analysts, and growth teams can use the skill to retrieve public Douyin videos, creator profiles, comments, and trending topics. It returns structured data for content planning, competitor and sentiment analysis, demand research, and public lead discovery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Links or video IDs provided for retrieval may be sent to a third-party data service.

Mitigation: Use public links and IDs that you are comfortable sharing with that service.

Risk: The security scan was benign with medium confidence, but only 31 of 53 components were fully analyzed.

Mitigation: Review the source and components that were not fully analyzed before installing or upgrading.
