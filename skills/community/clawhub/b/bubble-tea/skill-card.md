## Use Case:

External users use this skill to find popular bubble tea options, compare visit criteria such as location, budget, flavor, queue time, and accessibility, and plan efficient shop-hopping routes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Venue hours, popularity, hygiene ratings, and queue times may be stale.

Mitigation: Verify current venue details with reliable local sources before relying on travel plans.

Risk: Some venues may have on-site restrictions, minimum spend, or photography rules that affect the visit.

Mitigation: Check venue rules before arrival and adjust the route or recommendations when restrictions apply.
