## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Automated publishing and customer messaging could send content to the wrong account, audience, or channel. <br>
Mitigation: Require explicit account and audience selection, preview generated content, and confirm every post or message before execution. <br>
Risk: Payment, refund, and reconciliation workflows can affect funds or financial records. <br>
Mitigation: Use least-privilege credentials, require manual approval for every payment or refund action, and independently review reconciliation outputs. <br>
Risk: Customer profiles, conversation archives, tags, and marketing workflows may involve personal or sensitive customer data. <br>
Mitigation: Limit data access to the minimum needed, review outputs before use, and apply relevant privacy, platform, and internal compliance controls. <br>
