## Description:

Build or revise reader-first interactive data stories and visual essays in the spirit of The Pudding, covering editorial framing, data receipts, visual form, meaningful interaction, motion, implementation architecture, accessibility, mobile behavior, and QA.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tseng71](https://clawhub.ai/user/tseng71)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and editorial teams use this skill to plan, build, revise, or audit interactive data journalism and scrollytelling projects. It helps turn sourced datasets into reader-first visual essays with claim ledgers, storyboards, accessibility checks, implementation guidance, and QA notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be selected automatically for broad data-visualization or Chinese-language data-story requests where a Pudding-style editorial workflow is not desired.

Mitigation: Explicitly name another workflow or skill when the work should avoid this editorial framing.

Risk: The skill can shape published claims and visual explanations, so weak or stale source data could become persuasive reader-facing output.

Mitigation: Use the required claim ledger, data notes, source caveats, and QA checks before publishing or deploying the story.
