Risk: Mathematical answers may contain arithmetic mistakes or invalid proof steps, particularly in multi-step work.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to show steps, sanity-check results, and verify proofs against trusted course material or a qualified reviewer.

Risk: The skill may be unreliable for recent mathematical research or open problems.

Mitigation: Treat current or frontier research claims as tentative and verify them against authoritative publications or domain experts.
