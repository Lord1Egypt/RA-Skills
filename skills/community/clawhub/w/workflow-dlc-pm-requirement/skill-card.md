## Description: <br>
Guides product managers through a gated workflow for preparing materials, clarifying requirements, and producing a review-ready PRD. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[mistyhuqwq-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/mistyhuqwq-art) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers use this skill to move from an initial product requirement topic to a PRD that is ready for review. It supports new projects, iterative requirements, and smaller optimization or fix flows through explicit gates and checklists. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Persistent local logging may capture sensitive project workflow details. <br>
Mitigation: For confidential work, disable or remove the experience-base log and avoid giving the skill broad repository access. <br>
Risk: Generated PRDs or document links may expose internal project information if shared without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated PRD content, file changes, and Feishu document links before sharing them outside the intended team. <br>
Risk: Requirement outputs can become misleading when source materials, data definitions, or business decisions are missing. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill's gates to block progress until materials, data口径, inheritance decisions, and required clarifications are explicitly resolved. <br>
