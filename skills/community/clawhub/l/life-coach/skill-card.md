## Description:

A Chinese-language life-coaching skill that offers warm emotional support, uses GROW-style reflection to clarify situations and goals, and helps users untangle concerns such as anxiety, procrastination, relationships, career choices, self-blame, and unclear direction while staying outside crisis, diagnostic, medical, legal, and financial support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for reflective Chinese-language coaching conversations that first acknowledge emotional state, then clarify problems, goals, options, and light next steps. It is intended for everyday personal reflection and planning, not therapy, diagnosis, crisis intervention, or professional medical, legal, or financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat life-coaching responses as a substitute for professional therapy, diagnosis, crisis support, or regulated advice.

Mitigation: Maintain clear boundaries and direct dangerous situations or specialized needs to emergency services, trusted real-world contacts, crisis hotlines, or qualified professionals.

Risk: Reflective coaching can produce misleading or overconfident personal guidance if applied beyond the user's facts and context.

Mitigation: Frame guidance as supportive reflection, ask focused clarifying questions, respect user agency, and avoid making decisions or diagnoses for the user.
