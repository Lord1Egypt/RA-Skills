## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: EMS plans may be mistaken for legal, regulatory, or certification advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as advisory planning support and have final compliance documents reviewed by qualified personnel.

Risk: Customization may require sensitive facility, process, or organizational details.

Mitigation: Provide only the organizational details necessary for customization and anonymize sensitive facility or process information where possible.

Risk: Environmental requirements can vary by jurisdiction and operating context.

Mitigation: Verify applicable laws, permits, local obligations, and organization-specific requirements before adopting generated EMS documents.
