## Description:

Assists Chinese-language study conversations about the introductory Job Analysis chapter, covering core concepts, uses, and project-planning building blocks with progressive disclosure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junwugit](https://clawhub.ai/user/junwugit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and learners use this skill to study Chapter 1 job-analysis concepts in Chinese, including job versus position, KSAO, duty/task/activity distinctions, common job-analysis uses, and how to plan a job-analysis project.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is scoped to Chapter 1 and may give incomplete help for later job-analysis methods or concepts.

Mitigation: Keep responses within the introductory framework and tell users when a question belongs to later chapters.

Risk: The skill is designed primarily for Chinese-language tutoring and may not match every user's preferred language.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to answer in another language when needed.
