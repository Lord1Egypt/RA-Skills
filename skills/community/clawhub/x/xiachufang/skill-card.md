## Description:

Xiachufang helps agents find reliable Chinese home-cooking recipes using community signals, convert recipes into shopping lists and prep timelines, explain cooking terms, and diagnose cooking failures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhangifonly](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhangifonly)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and cooking-focused agents use this skill to select reliable Xiachufang recipes, plan meals, build grocery lists, translate vague recipe quantities into practical ranges, and troubleshoot ordinary home-cooking failures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides ordinary cooking guidance and is not specialized dietary or medical advice.

Mitigation: Use professional advice for infant, pregnancy, chronic-disease, allergy, or other specialized dietary needs.

Risk: Recipe adaptation can create food-safety risk if meat, poultry, eggs, leftovers, or reheating guidance is relaxed.

Mitigation: Apply conservative food-safety handling and doneness standards, and avoid compromise guidance for high-risk foods.
