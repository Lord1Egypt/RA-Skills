## Description:

A medical microbiology and infectious disease skill set based on People's Medical Publishing House Medical Microbiology, 10th edition, covering bacteriology, virology, immunology, disinfection and sterilization, microbiome topics, and infection control.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Medical students, clinicians, laboratory technologists, public health workers, and infection control teams use this skill for educational review and supervised clinical or laboratory reasoning about microbiology and infectious disease topics. It should support qualified review rather than act as standalone medical, prescribing, emergency-care, lab-culture, pathogen-handling, sterilization, or microbiota-transplant authority.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes medical diagnostics, antimicrobial therapy, infection control, laboratory procedures, pathogen handling, sterilization, and microbiota-transplant topics that could be mistaken for standalone professional advice.

Mitigation: Use only for educational or supervised professional support, require qualified clinician or laboratory review, and verify outputs against current official guidance and local protocols.

Risk: The source content does not consistently enforce professional-safety boundaries for diagnosis, prescribing, dosing, emergency care, self-treatment, lab culture, animal research, sterilization, or microbiota transplant decisions.

Mitigation: Apply deployment-level medical safety policies and tightly gate or block high-risk subskills unless qualified review, biosafety controls, and local authorization are in place.
