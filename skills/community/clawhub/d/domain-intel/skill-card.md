## Description:

Map available evidence around a known keyword in the domain market while keeping search-audience, advertiser, registration, supply, active-offer, and transaction layers distinct.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abtdomain](https://clawhub.ai/user/abtdomain)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and domain-market researchers use this skill to produce a source-disciplined evidence map for one known keyword, with each signal layer reported separately and without an investment verdict.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Searched keywords and related domain queries may be sent to configured domain-data and web providers.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential launch names or sensitive brand plans unless sharing those queries with the configured providers is acceptable.

Risk: Partial source coverage or missing provider fields can make domain-market conclusions overconfident.

Mitigation: Retain source, scope, observation date, reporting-window, and Unavailable disclosures, and keep findings separated by evidence layer.
