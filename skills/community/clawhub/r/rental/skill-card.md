## Use Case:

External renters, landlords, vacation hosts or guests, and equipment renters use this skill to compare options, estimate costs, prepare communications, identify scams or red flags, and review rental terms before acting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rental, lease, fair-housing, credit, background-check, insurance, and deposit rules vary by jurisdiction and contract.

Mitigation: Verify legal and financial guidance against local rules, policy documents, and qualified professionals before relying on it.

Risk: Rental workflows may involve sensitive applicant documents, addresses, Wi-Fi passwords, lockbox codes, payment details, or dispute evidence.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum necessary information, remove unnecessary personal data, and use secure channels for documents and access credentials.
