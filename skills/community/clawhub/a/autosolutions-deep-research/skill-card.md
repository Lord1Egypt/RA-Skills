## Description:

Conduct deep multi-phase research using parallel subagents and iterative search. Use for deep research requests, comprehensive analysis, competitive intelligence, market research, or thorough investigation of complex topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, analysts, and research teams use this skill to plan and conduct comprehensive multi-angle research, then synthesize findings into a cited Markdown report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can run many web searches, fetch external pages, and spawn multiple subagents, which may consume more time and tool budget than a simple research prompt.

Mitigation: Use it for genuinely deep research tasks, set scope and time expectations up front, and review the final citations and claims before relying on the report.

Risk: Research synthesis can include incorrect, outdated, or conflicting source claims.

Mitigation: Prefer primary sources, triangulate important claims across multiple independent sources, and explicitly flag uncertainty or disagreement.
