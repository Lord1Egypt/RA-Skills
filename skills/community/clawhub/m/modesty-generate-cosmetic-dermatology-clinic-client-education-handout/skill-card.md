Dermatologists and clinic growth teams use this skill to draft cosmetic dermatology client education handouts with explainer copy, FAQs, visual elements, and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft client education or marketing content could include inaccurate, procedure-specific, medical, or regulatory claims.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as draft material and require qualified clinician review before patient-facing use.

Risk: Generated handouts may be read as individualized medical advice if they are not clearly tailored and reviewed.

Mitigation: Use the skill for general education drafts and adapt final content to clinic policy, applicable rules, and the intended audience.
