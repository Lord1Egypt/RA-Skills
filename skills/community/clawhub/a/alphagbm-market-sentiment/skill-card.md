## Description:

Market-wide sentiment dashboard with VIX, Put/Call ratio, Fear & Greed Index, market breadth, and sector rotation analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[clementgu](https://clawhub.ai/user/clementgu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and traders use this skill to evaluate broad market sentiment, compare indicators against one-year context, classify risk-on, risk-off, or neutral regimes, and interpret what the regime may imply for options trading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market sentiment analysis and options-trading interpretation may be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational, verify market data independently, and apply separate financial judgment before trading.

Risk: Indicator readings or regime classifications may be stale, incomplete, or based on mock data during development and testing.

Mitigation: Confirm data freshness and source quality before relying on the dashboard for live market decisions.
