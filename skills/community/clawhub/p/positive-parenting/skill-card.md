## Description:

Positive Parenting is a Chinese-language parenting support skill for families with children ages 0-15, offering age-aware guidance for emotion regulation, behavior, learning, social challenges, adolescent communication, and parent support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gglinux](https://clawhub.ai/user/gglinux)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and families use this skill to receive concise parenting coaching in Simplified Chinese for common situations involving children and adolescents ages 0-15. It routes by age, scenario, timing, risk level, and parent state before producing practical scripts, actions, and longer-term guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could rely on the skill for medical, mental-health, legal, emergency, abuse, self-harm, violence, or immediate child-safety situations.

Mitigation: Use the skill only as parenting support; for immediate danger, suspected abuse, self-harm, violence, severe withdrawal, or urgent health concerns, seek local emergency services or qualified professionals.

Risk: Parenting guidance can be inappropriate if the child's age, situation timing, risk level, or parent state is misunderstood.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's routing approach before giving advice, ask only minimal clarifying questions when needed, and escalate red-risk situations to safety guidance.
