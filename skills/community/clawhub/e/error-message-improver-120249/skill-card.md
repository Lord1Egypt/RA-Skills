## Description:

Helps developers and support teams turn vague error messages into clearer guidance that explains what failed, why it may have failed, and what to do next.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users use this skill to rewrite, review, or plan clearer error messages and troubleshooting workflows. It can produce tailored guidance, reusable checklists, templates, analysis, code-change support, and verification notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may route general debugging or support requests to this skill.

Mitigation: Review and tighten trigger terms before deployment when precise skill routing matters.

Risk: Suggested error-message changes could misstate causes or remediation steps if applied without product-specific review.

Mitigation: Validate generated wording against product behavior, logs, and support policy before release.
