## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and other agent users apply this skill when they need structured reasoning for complex, ambiguous, multi-step, architectural, debugging, or higher-stakes tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for many complex or ambiguous tasks and make responses more deliberative or verbose.

Mitigation: Install it only when that reasoning posture is desired, and ask the agent for concise output when brevity matters.

Risk: Structured reasoning guidance can still lead to incorrect conclusions if the agent relies on assumptions instead of evidence.

Mitigation: Review important outputs and require evidence checks for high-impact recommendations.
