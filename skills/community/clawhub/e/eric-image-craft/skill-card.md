## Description:

Curated image generation assistant covering four categories: characters, scenes, products, and style transformation for generating high-quality stylized 3D images.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ericn26-star](https://clawhub.ai/user/ericn26-star)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to turn clear or guided requests into stylized 3D image-generation prompts and delivered image assets across character, scene, product, and style-transformation workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompts or outputs involving brand names, real people, or copyrighted movie scenes may require additional review.

Mitigation: Review generated prompts and image outputs before publication or commercial use.

Risk: Broad trigger words may activate the skill in ambiguous conversations.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intended image-generation workflow when the request could match multiple categories.

Risk: The skill may analyze user-provided images and occasionally search the web for visual reference details.

Mitigation: Avoid providing sensitive images unless image analysis and limited external lookup are appropriate for the deployment context.
