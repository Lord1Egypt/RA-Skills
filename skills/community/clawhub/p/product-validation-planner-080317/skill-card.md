## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, and practical analysis before building.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External builders, founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to decide whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It produces practical validation plans, reusable workflows, checklists, analysis, prototypes, positioning support, and follow-up risks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad product, SaaS, startup, or validation triggers may route unrelated prompts to this skill.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger metadata for strict routing, or invoke the skill explicitly only when product validation planning is desired.
