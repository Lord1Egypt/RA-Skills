## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Store metrics may be sent to Feishu or WeChat channels with broader visibility than intended.

Mitigation: Confirm what metrics are shared, who can read each channel, and whether the data is appropriate for those destinations before using notification workflows.

Risk: Competitor monitoring can violate platform rules or create load if collection frequency is too aggressive.

Mitigation: Follow Taobao platform rules, use reasonable rate limits, and avoid collecting sensitive data.

Risk: Future executable implementations could add behavior that is not visible in this documentation-only package.

Mitigation: Review and scan any future scripts, integrations, or automation code before deployment.
