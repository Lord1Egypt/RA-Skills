## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides jurisdiction-specific healthcare marketing guidance that may be mistaken for legal or regulatory advice.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as planning support, verify current DOH/DHA requirements before publishing campaigns, and obtain qualified compliance review for regulated advertising decisions.

Risk: Clinic marketing recommendations can be ineffective or noncompliant if clinic stage, specialty, location, or language needs are unclear.

Mitigation: Confirm the clinic stage, licensed specialty scope, UAE location, target patient demographic, and preferred languages before applying the guidance.

Risk: Marketing activity may misuse patient information or make prohibited clinical claims.

Mitigation: Avoid patient testimonials, before-and-after clinical result photos, guaranteed outcomes, prescription drug promotion to the public, and any marketing use of patient data without written consent.
