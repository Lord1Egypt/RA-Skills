## Description:

FDE Operator OS is an applied AI operator playbook for delivery leads qualifying AI opportunities, structuring messy operational reality, designing minimum viable loops, and defining delivery-ready expansion paths.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlxeva](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlxeva)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External delivery leads, FDEs, and applied AI operators use this skill to decide whether an AI opportunity is worth pursuing and to produce stage-gated artifacts for qualification, reality capture, system framing, pilot design, delivery architecture, and expansion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Several referenced templates and reference files are not present in the shipped artifact, so documented artifact paths may not resolve in every runtime.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as a playbook unless the referenced assets are available, and confirm required templates before relying on path-based outputs.

Risk: Non-Chinese users may not trigger the intended workflow from the Chinese example prompts alone.

Mitigation: Invoke the canonical slug fde-operator-os or describe the desired FDE workflow in the user's own language.
