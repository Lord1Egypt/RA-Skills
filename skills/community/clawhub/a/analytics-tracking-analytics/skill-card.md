## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: GA4 User ID tracking can create privacy risk if direct personal identifiers such as emails are sent to analytics systems.

Mitigation: Use only internal pseudonymous authenticated-user identifiers and verify consent, retention, access controls, and regional privacy requirements before implementation.

Risk: Incorrect or excessive event tracking can produce misleading attribution and fragmented reporting.

Mitigation: Prioritize a focused event taxonomy, validate events in GA4 Realtime or DebugView, and check for duplicate events or missing parameters before launch.
