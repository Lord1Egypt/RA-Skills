## Description:

Generates a Chinese-first daily business briefing from DXC, domestic company, customer, industry, tender, competitor, and partnership sources, with concise findings, opportunity notes, and source links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rozley](https://clawhub.ai/user/rozley)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Employees and business-development teams use this skill to produce daily Chinese-first briefings on DXC, domestic company updates, core customers, tenders, competitors, policy changes, and actionable commercial opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated briefings can surface outdated, inaccessible, or AI-generated public-web content.

Mitigation: Review cited original links before acting, skip inaccessible links, and mark content-limited sources in the briefing.

Risk: Business opportunity recommendations may be misleading if based on incomplete source material.

Mitigation: Use the skill's original-article reading and deduplication steps for high-value items, then have a human review opportunities before outreach or investment.
