## Description:

Research a user-provided topic across the web and current social conversation, then publish one X post in the user's voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dishant0406](https://clawhub.ai/user/dishant0406)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill to research a user-provided topic, draft a short post in their own voice, publish it on X, and verify the result.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can publish a public X post from the user's account without requiring final approval of the exact text.

Mitigation: Require draft-only behavior or explicit confirmation of the final tweet text and posting account before publishing.
