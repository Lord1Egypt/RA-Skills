## Description:

Guides agents to give available-light photography advice for indoor or dim scenes where the user wants authentic atmosphere without adding flash or studio lights.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and photography-focused agents use this skill to answer low-light, indoor, ambient-light, and no-flash photography questions. It provides metering, camera-setting, and boundary guidance for preserving scene atmosphere while accepting darker shadows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Photography advice may be educational rather than camera-specific, especially for exact exposure settings across different equipment.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against the user's camera, lens, stabilization, subject motion, and acceptable noise level before applying them.

Risk: The skill is Chinese-first, so English-only users may receive less complete nuance if the agent does not translate or adapt the guidance.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to provide localized English guidance or bilingual explanations when serving English-speaking users.
