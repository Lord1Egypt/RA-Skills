## Description: <br>
Guides agents through a PMF and Crossing-the-Chasm audit for startups with early traction, helping distinguish product-market fit gaps from whole-product and beachhead strategy gaps. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Founders, product leaders, startup operators, and strategy agents use this skill to evaluate whether early traction indicates real product-market fit and, if PMF holds, identify the beachhead and whole-product gaps needed to reach mainstream customers. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Business examples and PMF thresholds may be treated as guaranteed predictors of startup outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Use the audit as an advisory framework and validate recommendations against current customer, retention, segment, and market evidence. <br>
Risk: The skill can produce misleading strategy if users skip PMF measurement or provide weak cohort data. <br>
Mitigation: Require an active-user cohort, Ellis Test or equivalent retention signal, and a specific beachhead before acting on chasm recommendations. <br>
