## Description:

Structures credible before/after and transformation content using the SHIFT framework, with emphasis on real source material, honest journey framing, consent, typical-results substantiation, YMYL care, and required disclosures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketing and social-content teams use this skill to structure before/after posts, transformation reveals, progress stories, makeovers, and client-results content from real media and substantiated results. The skill helps an agent draft the arc, journey beats, visual specs, captions, and disclosures while leaving media creation, approval, and publishing to humans or downstream tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public before/after marketing content can mislead audiences if the result is staged, cherry-picked, fabricated, or presented without typical-results substantiation.

Mitigation: Require real un-staged source material, tracked results, clear typical-results disclosure when needed, and human review before publication.

Risk: Client images, stories, or lookalikes can create consent and likeness issues.

Mitigation: Confirm consent for the person's images and story, including separate ad-use consent where applicable, before drafting or publishing content.

Risk: Health, weight-loss, beauty, and other YMYL transformations can imply medical, cure, guarantee, or unsupported performance claims.

Mitigation: Require competent substantiation for health-related claims, disclose material aids, avoid cure or guarantee language, and include appropriate not-professional-advice framing.
