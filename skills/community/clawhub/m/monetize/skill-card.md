Mitigation: Review recommendations against the user's business context before applying them publicly or changing prices.

Risk: The skill gives concrete business advice that may not fit regulated markets, platform policies, or local consumer protection requirements.

Mitigation: Check relevant platform, legal, and compliance requirements before using pricing, subscription, refund, or marketing guidance.
