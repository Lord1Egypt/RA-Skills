## Description:

Interactive product knowledge training and quiz system for retail staff that tests employees on product specs, store policies, sales techniques, and FAQs through flashcards, multiple choice, and scenario-based questions while tracking completion and scores.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fangwei-frank](https://clawhub.ai/user/fangwei-frank)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Retail employees use this skill to practice product knowledge, store policies, sales responses, and new product onboarding. Managers may use its progress summaries to understand training completion and weak categories.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Identifiable employee training progress may be stored in memory.

Mitigation: Give employees clear notice, define when progress is recorded, and document retention and deletion rules before workplace deployment.

Risk: Employee performance summaries may be shared with managers without defined access controls.

Mitigation: Limit manager access to authorized roles and define what progress data may be requested or reported.
