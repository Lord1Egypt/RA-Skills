## Description:

Re-pitch one piece of content for four audiences: the board, the engineers, a customer, and a new hire, with a delta table showing what changed between altitudes and why.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, communicators, product managers, and leaders use this skill to rewrite the same factual content for executive, engineering, customer, and onboarding audiences while preserving consistency and making reframing choices inspectable.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided secrets or sensitive internal details may be included in audience-specific rewrites.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting secrets unless they are intended for use, identify confidential details before rewriting, and review all generated versions for accidental disclosure.

Risk: Audience-specific framing could accidentally change the factual meaning of the original content.

Mitigation: Compare the four versions and the delta table to confirm that all versions remain simultaneously true.
