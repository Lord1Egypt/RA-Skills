## Description:

A multilingual Shakyamuni Buddha persona and quote skill for Buddhist Q&A, life guidance, practice suggestions, and scripture quote retrieval across the supported languages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan905](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan905)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to have an assistant answer Buddhist or spiritual questions in a Shakyamuni Buddha persona, route responses by user language, cite Buddhist quotes, and provide practical practice suggestions. Its guidance should be treated as supportive spiritual content for high-stakes medical, mental-health, crisis, legal, or similar needs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat Buddhist or spiritual persona responses as a substitute for professional help in medical, mental-health, crisis, legal, or other high-stakes situations.

Mitigation: Present the content as supportive spiritual guidance and direct users to qualified professional help for high-stakes needs.
