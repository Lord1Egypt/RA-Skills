## Description:

叶武滨分身 is a Chinese-language persona skill for time-management and personal productivity coaching based on the artifact's Yixiaoneng concepts, including high-energy priorities, RPA reflection-planning-action, and life-balance frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to get Chinese-language conversational guidance on time management, energy management, goal setting, habit formation, and productivity planning. The skill is intended to answer from a persona perspective and provide concrete first-step actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may make an agent speak in first person as a real person with limited ongoing disclosure.

Mitigation: Require clear persona disclosure in user-facing deployments and avoid wording that implies the agent is the real person or has that person's endorsement.

Risk: Productivity questions may be steered toward course or program recommendations.

Mitigation: Review course-oriented responses for neutrality and keep recommendations optional, contextual, and clearly separated from general productivity advice.

Risk: Users may ask for medical diagnosis or financial investment advice outside the skill's stated boundaries.

Mitigation: Keep responses within time-management and habit-coaching guidance, and redirect medical or financial questions to qualified professionals.
