This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External DTC and Shopify merchants in visual-first categories use this skill to improve organic Pinterest traffic through pin aesthetics, board SEO, content cadence, UGC loops, and ROI measurement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer users toward a named commercial loyalty product without neutral framing or disclosure.

Mitigation: Review recommendations for impartiality and disclose the product relationship or provide neutral alternatives when advising users.

Risk: The skill may activate for broader marketing questions that are not clearly Pinterest-specific.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's channel and business context before applying Pinterest-specific guidance.
