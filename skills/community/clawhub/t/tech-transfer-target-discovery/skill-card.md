## Description:

Helps central SOE technology management teams identify evidence-backed internal or external targets for patent licensing, transfer, equity contribution, or pledge-value support using PatSnap MCP patent and web searches.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External technology management teams use this skill to collect patent-backed evidence, classify transfer paths, score candidate recipients, and produce shortlists and engagement recommendations for technology transfer decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential invention details or business plans may be entered into external patent and web search services.

Mitigation: Confirm PatSnap MCP authorization and internal data-sharing rules before use, and redact sensitive information when policy requires it.

Risk: Candidate shortlists and scores can be misleading if patent ownership, legal status, company identity, or public business signals are incomplete.

Mitigation: Require patent evidence, timestamps, source links, and manual review before outreach, valuation, licensing, transfer, equity contribution, or pledge decisions.
