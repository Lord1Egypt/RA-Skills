## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes anti-bot evasion guidance and can automate interactions with websites.

Mitigation: Only automate websites where permission is clear, avoid anti-bot evasion guidance, and review what will be submitted or saved before using it on sensitive accounts or pages.

Risk: The skill recommends system-wide dependency installation with --break-system-packages.

Mitigation: Use a virtual environment and pin Playwright to a reviewed version instead of installing into the system Python environment.
