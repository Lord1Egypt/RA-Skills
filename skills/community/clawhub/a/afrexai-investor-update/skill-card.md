## Description:

Generates structured monthly or quarterly investor updates from company metrics, milestones, challenges, cash position, and specific asks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Founders and startup operators use this skill to turn business metrics, milestones, challenges, cash and runway information, team updates, and investor asks into concise monthly or quarterly investor updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Investor updates may contain confidential company metrics, cash position, runway, customer details, or fundraising context.

Mitigation: Provide only information that is appropriate for the agent platform and review the draft before sharing it externally.

Risk: Generated business updates can be misleading if input metrics or milestone descriptions are incomplete, outdated, or overly promotional.

Mitigation: Verify all numbers, customer claims, timelines, and asks against current company records before sending.

Risk: The artifact includes unrelated promotional footer links and branding.

Mitigation: Remove unrequested promotional links or branding from generated investor communications.
