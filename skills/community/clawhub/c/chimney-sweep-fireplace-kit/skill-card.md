## Description:

Generates compliant marketing copy tailored for certified chimney sweep and fireplace service contractors meeting industry and safety standards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chimney sweep and fireplace service contractors, home-services marketers, and local SEO teams use this skill to draft seasonal campaigns, service pages, review-request sequences, referral letters, ads, and JSON-LD schema with chimney-industry compliance cues.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated copy may contain misleading credential, rating, license, insurance, or jurisdiction-specific claims.

Mitigation: Manually verify every credential, rating, license, insurance statement, and local regulatory claim before publishing.

Risk: Review-request flows can create endorsement-guidance risk if they steer only satisfied customers toward public reviews.

Mitigation: Change the review sequence so all customers receive the same neutral opportunity to leave an honest public review.
