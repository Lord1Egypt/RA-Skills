## Description:

Generates concise daily stock briefings that combine portfolio performance, market news, technical levels, capital-flow context, and informational trading suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[awdasfdada](https://clawhub.ai/user/awdasfdada)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors, traders, and finance-focused agents use this skill to create Chinese-language daily summaries for held stocks and on-demand single-stock analysis. The briefing covers market movement, holdings performance, relevant news, technical context, capital-flow notes, and informational buy/sell/hold suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may generate buy, sell, or hold suggestions that readers could mistake for professional financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat suggestions as informational support only and review decisions against current market data, risk tolerance, and qualified financial advice.

Risk: Portfolio inputs can include holdings details such as ticker, cost, and quantity.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive or unnecessary personal financial details, and handle any provided holdings data according to the user's privacy requirements.
