## Description:

Pilot Base Agent is a Chinese-language advisory agent for pilot-scale manufacturing planning, coordinating eight specialist roles across process scale-up, testing, quality compliance, safety, equipment scheduling, technology transfer, and industry consultation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External founders, product teams, and manufacturing operators use this skill to structure pilot-run planning for technologies moving from proof of concept toward production validation. It helps draft stage-gate plans, role responsibilities, quality and safety review points, customer validation materials, and investor-facing deliverables.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides manufacturing, quality, safety, regulatory, and investment-oriented advice that could be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific project.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning drafts and verify them with qualified manufacturing, safety, regulatory, legal, and investment professionals before operational use.

Risk: The skill is designed for Chinese-language advisory workflows and may be less reliable for users who need another language or jurisdiction-specific formal advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill in its intended language and localize any outputs through qualified domain reviewers for the relevant jurisdiction and industry.
