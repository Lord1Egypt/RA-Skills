## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect board pinouts, voltage levels, current limits, or wiring can damage electronics or create unsafe hardware behavior.

Mitigation: Verify board documentation, component operating voltages, current requirements, polarity, shared grounds, and wiring before powering physical devices.

Risk: Troubleshooting advice may be applied to a board or peripheral with different electrical characteristics.

Mitigation: Confirm the specific Arduino-compatible board, sensor, motor, LED load, and communication bus requirements before following hardware guidance.
