## Description:

Japan-focused social media agent for Pinterest JP, X (Twitter) JP, Instagram JP, and note.com. Handles content strategy, copywriting, and publishing for Japanese market.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[luoboask](https://clawhub.ai/user/luoboask)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External social media operators and content teams use this skill to plan, localize, draft, schedule, and optionally publish Japanese-market content across Pinterest JP, X (Twitter) JP, Instagram JP, note.com, and TikTok.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can support public posting automation to logged-in social accounts.

Mitigation: Require a visible preview, exact destination account and platform, and explicit user approval immediately before any live post.
