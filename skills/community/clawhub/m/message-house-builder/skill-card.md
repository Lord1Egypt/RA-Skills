## Description:

Message House Builder derives launch messaging from a completed positioning canvas, producing a traceable message house, PR-FAQ narrative spine, per-channel angle packs, unresolved-claim notes, and a handoff summary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, product, and launch teams use this skill to turn accepted positioning and canon evidence into launch-ready messaging structure, including a message house, PR-FAQ spine, channel angles, and claim review handoffs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Launch messaging can include incorrect or unsupported claims when source evidence is missing or weak.

Mitigation: Keep unsupported proof points labeled [needs source], block release use for unresolved claims, and route claim decisions through the authorized claims workflow.

Risk: Pasted positioning canvases, competitor pages, or exports may contain instructions unrelated to the user's launch task.

Mitigation: Treat source material as untrusted input and use it only as evidence for the requested messaging work.

Risk: Saved launch-message artifacts can affect downstream launch execution.

Mitigation: Ask before writing files, include the narrative and claims dependency tuple, and keep durable canon or claim changes as separate authorized proposals.
