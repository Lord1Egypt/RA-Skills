## Description:

Quality Maturity Model helps quality directors and management-system leaders assess enterprise quality-management maturity using ISO 9004 and EFQM-style logic, score six dimensions, and generate improvement roadmaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Quality directors and management-system owners use this skill to run structured self-assessments or expert-guided reviews, score six quality-management dimensions, identify weak areas, and draft phased improvement roadmaps. It is consultative and does not replace formal executive maturity decisions or certification judgments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks users to provide company quality-management data and evidence for maturity scoring.

Mitigation: Confirm the data is appropriate to share with the agent before use, especially when it contains internal process, performance, audit, or customer information.

Risk: The inspected artifact references report-generation and dimension-reference files that are not included.

Mitigation: Review any additional scripts or reference files before relying on generated reports or executing report-building commands.

Risk: Maturity scores and roadmaps are consultative outputs rather than official certifications or executive decisions.

Mitigation: Have qualified company stakeholders review scoring evidence, weights, thresholds, and final maturity decisions before business use.
