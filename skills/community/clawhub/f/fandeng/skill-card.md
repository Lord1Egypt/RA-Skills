## Description:

Provides summaries and key details of public courses, books, and topics from the Fandeng Reading site without accessing paid content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mikeclaw007](https://clawhub.ai/user/mikeclaw007)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to gather and summarize publicly available Fandeng Reading course, book, and topic pages for analysis or reminders. It is not intended to access paid, private, account-only, or download-restricted content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used to browse or summarize dushu.io pages that are paid, private, account-only, or download-restricted.

Mitigation: Use it only with publicly available pages; do not provide credentials, bypass access controls, or request paid or download-restricted material.

Risk: Automated browsing can exceed site rules or rate limits.

Mitigation: Keep request volume within the site's rules and rate limits, and stop if access restrictions or anti-automation controls appear.

Risk: Dynamic page loading can lead to incomplete extracted details.

Mitigation: Wait for public page content to finish loading and include source links and collection time so summaries can be checked.
