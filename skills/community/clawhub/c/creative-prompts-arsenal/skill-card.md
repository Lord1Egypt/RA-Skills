## Description:

Generate tailored creative prompt kits that diagnose creative blocks and provide domain-specific expansion, inversion, remix, refinement, and start-now prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creative workers, writers, designers, educators, parents, and business users use this skill to diagnose a creative block and get a compact prompt sequence that moves from ideation to refinement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompts may be unsuitable for sensitive professional, medical, legal, or other high-stakes contexts.

Mitigation: Review and adapt generated prompts before applying them in sensitive or high-stakes work.

Risk: Prompt generation alone may not solve a creative block caused by missing domain knowledge or excessive ideation.

Mitigation: Use the output as a starting sequence and limit follow-up prompting when a concrete next draft, sketch, or test is available.
