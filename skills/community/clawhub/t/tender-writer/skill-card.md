## Description:

Generates Word-format technical tender proposals for photovoltaic EPC, EMC, operations and maintenance, and intelligent weak-current projects from tender documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shendingyi](https://clawhub.ai/user/shendingyi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External proposal and business-development teams use this skill to draft technical bid documents for photovoltaic and weak-current engineering tenders. It analyzes tender requirements, scoring criteria, schedules, personnel requirements, and equipment parameters to produce a structured proposal document for user review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tender documents may contain sensitive procurement, customer, or project information.

Mitigation: Review input files and generated outputs before use, and share generated documents only through intended channels.

Risk: Generated bid content may contain inaccurate project details, non-compliant responses, or unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Validate proposal content against the tender document, scoring rubric, qualification requirements, and applicable industry standards before submission.

Risk: Feishu delivery can disclose the resulting document if enabled unintentionally.

Mitigation: Allow Feishu delivery only when the recipient and document are approved for sharing.
