## Description:

Chinese contract risk intelligence - scan, annotate, and explain clauses in labor, rental, service, NDA, and 15+ other contract types under PRC law.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and teams reviewing Chinese contracts use this skill to identify PRC-law risk issues, understand clause-level concerns, and prepare modification suggestions or negotiation priorities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contracts can contain personal, financial, signature, and confidential business information.

Mitigation: Redact unnecessary IDs, bank details, signatures, secrets, and unrelated confidential business information before use.

Risk: Contract review output can be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific PRC-law matter.

Mitigation: Treat the output as review assistance and have a qualified PRC lawyer review material decisions.
