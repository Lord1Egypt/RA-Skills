## Description:

Pre-meeting research, agenda generation, talking points, and post-meeting action item capture for client meetings, internal reviews, board presentations, vendor negotiations, and stakeholder briefings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samledger67-dotcom](https://clawhub.ai/user/samledger67-dotcom)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external business users, especially finance professionals, client advisors, and operations leads, use this skill to prepare for client, board, vendor, internal, or stakeholder meetings. It helps assemble meeting briefs, agendas, talking points, post-meeting summaries, action items, and follow-up drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can use sensitive meeting context from calendars, CRM records, notes, invoices, budget files, and relationship history.

Mitigation: Limit the agent's accessible sources to the calendars, CRM or notes folders, and financial files needed for the meeting.

Risk: Proactive mode can automatically read upcoming meeting context and persist generated briefs.

Mitigation: Keep proactive mode disabled unless source scope, storage location, and review expectations are defined.

Risk: Generated briefs or follow-up drafts may contain client-confidential information or unverified claims.

Mitigation: Review generated materials before sharing them outside the workspace and mark uncertain company, contact, or financial details for verification.
