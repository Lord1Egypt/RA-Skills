## Description:

A Chinese-language personal brand positioning skill that guides users through the Sanjiegun framework to analyze ability, public image, and credibility, then produce a structured IP positioning report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangzhongming2025-beep](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangzhongming2025-beep)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Entrepreneurs, consultants, team leads, and personal-brand builders use this skill to structure a Chinese-language positioning conversation and generate a one-page IP positioning report with a concise positioning statement, visual identity suggestions, content-topic directions, and persona risk checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses branded methodology, opening and closing language, and author-attribution wording that may be unsuitable for neutral consulting output.

Mitigation: Review and adapt branded phrases, attribution, and tone before using the output in a different consulting brand or public-facing workflow.

Risk: The skill is written for Chinese-language positioning conversations and may not meet expectations for another language or market without adaptation.

Mitigation: Translate and localize questions, examples, and final report wording before using it for non-Chinese audiences.
