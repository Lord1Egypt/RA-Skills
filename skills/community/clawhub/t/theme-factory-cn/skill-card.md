## Description:

为幻灯片、文档、报告和 HTML 页面提供专业主题配色与字体建议，可使用 10 套内置主题或按需求定制主题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to choose or generate coordinated color palettes, font pairings, CSS variables, and layout guidance for presentations, documents, reports, and HTML pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad requests such as asking to beautify content may trigger visual theme advice when the user wanted content editing.

Mitigation: Use explicit wording when requesting content edits instead of color, font, or theme recommendations.

Risk: Generated or selected themes may not satisfy a user's brand, accessibility, or readability requirements without review.

Mitigation: Review color contrast, font availability, and brand fit before applying recommendations to published materials.
