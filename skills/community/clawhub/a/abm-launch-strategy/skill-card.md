## Description:

This skill helps users plan product launches, feature announcements, release strategies, and Product Hunt launches using phased launch planning and owned, rented, and borrowed channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mariokarras](https://clawhub.ai/user/mariokarras)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, marketing, and founder teams use this skill to turn launch or announcement goals into practical channel plans, phased timelines, launch-day checklists, Product Hunt preparation, and post-launch recovery actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad launch or announcement phrasing and provide launch strategy when the user intended a different kind of marketing request.

Mitigation: Confirm the release context and redirect broader marketing brainstorming to the related marketing-ideas skill when appropriate.

Risk: The skill may read an optional local product-marketing context file if one exists in the workspace.

Mitigation: Review local product-marketing context files for sensitive or outdated information before using them to guide launch recommendations.

Risk: Launch plans and Product Hunt tactics can be mismatched to the product stage, audience, or available channels.

Mitigation: Ask for launch type, timeline, audience size, owned channels, prior launch results, and Product Hunt readiness before giving specific recommendations.
