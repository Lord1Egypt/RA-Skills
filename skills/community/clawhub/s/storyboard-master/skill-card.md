## Description:

Storyboard Master helps agents turn creative briefs for short films, commercials, and promotional videos into executable storyboard scripts with shot details, camera movement, dialogue or voiceover, timing, lighting, color, and sound cues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openlark](https://clawhub.ai/user/openlark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external creative teams use this skill during pre-production to convert creative briefs into structured, production-aware storyboard tables for video, advertising, and promotional projects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts may include confidential production details, client plans, or unreleased campaign information.

Mitigation: Avoid placing confidential production details in prompts unless sharing them with the agent environment is approved.

Risk: Storyboard guidance may be creatively useful but still require production review for feasibility, cost, safety, and client fit.

Mitigation: Have production leads review generated shot plans before using them for scheduling, budgeting, or on-set direction.
