## Description:

Generates ranked Zhihu-ready topic candidates from a user-specified theme by using discussion signals, platform fit, debate value, author writability, freshness, and long-tail value.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and content strategists use this skill to turn a theme into ranked Zhihu topic ideas, including title options, recommended structure, debate angles, freshness labels, scoring, and topics to avoid.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recent Zhihu or hot-topic claims may be stale or unsupported when current source access is unavailable.

Mitigation: Use current sources when browsing is available; otherwise ask for source material or label freshness as unverified.

Risk: The skill is optimized for Chinese Zhihu-style output and may be a poor fit for other publishing platforms.

Mitigation: Keep Zhihu platform fit separate from WeChat, Xiaohongshu, and short-video formats when interpreting or adapting the output.
