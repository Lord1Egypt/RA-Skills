## Description:

Use conference poster pitch for academic writing workflows that need structured execution, explicit assumptions, and clear output boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aipoch-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/aipoch-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Academic researchers, students, and conference presenters use this skill to draft 30-second, 60-second, or 3-minute poster pitch scripts from a poster title while keeping assumptions, limits, and review needs explicit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated poster pitches can be rough, generic, or academically inaccurate if the poster title lacks study details.

Mitigation: Review the pitch against the actual poster, methods, results, and conference audience before using it.

Risk: The packaged script executes locally and accepts user-provided poster titles.

Mitigation: Run it in the workspace or another controlled environment and avoid placing sensitive or unpublished details in prompts unless that handling is approved.
