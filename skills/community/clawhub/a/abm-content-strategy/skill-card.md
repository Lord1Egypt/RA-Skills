## Description:

Helps agents plan content strategies, choose topics, organize pillars and clusters, map content to buyer stages, and prioritize editorial work before drafting individual assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mariokarras](https://clawhub.ai/user/mariokarras)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external marketers, and content strategists use this skill to plan content pillars, topic clusters, keyword-stage mapping, and prioritized editorial roadmaps. It is intended for strategy and planning rather than writing final content assets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide public web or competitor research, which may produce incomplete or stale market signals.

Mitigation: Validate important keyword, competitor, and market claims against current authoritative sources before using the strategy for business decisions.

Risk: The skill may ask the agent to read local product-marketing context files if present.

Mitigation: Keep secrets and sensitive customer data out of product-marketing context files before installing or invoking the skill.

Risk: The skill is broad enough to touch strategy, SEO, copywriting, and social content workflows.

Mitigation: Use the related specialized skills for drafting, SEO auditing, programmatic SEO, or social content execution when the task moves beyond content strategy.
