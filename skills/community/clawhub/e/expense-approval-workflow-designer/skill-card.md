## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could treat workflow templates as financial, accounting, or policy advice.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as planning templates and review proposed workflows with finance, accounting, compliance, or legal stakeholders before adoption.

Risk: The artifact includes a local Python handler even though the documentation describes no real code execution.

Mitigation: Review the handler before deployment and install only where local execution of this JSON-generating skill is acceptable.
