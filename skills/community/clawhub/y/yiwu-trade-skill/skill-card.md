## Description:

义乌.skill helps agents provide practical Yiwu small-commodity export guidance covering 1039 market-procurement compliance, sourcing, logistics, customer acquisition, payment, finance, tax, and data decision workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External trade operators, sourcing teams, and sales or service agents use this skill to plan and check Yiwu small-commodity export workflows, including compliance, supplier selection, logistics choices, outreach, quotation, payment collection, cost analysis, and customer operations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill under-discloses third-party AI processing.

Mitigation: Confirm which external services receive data and disclose those services before use in customer or sales environments.

Risk: The skill supports broad persistent customer profiling.

Mitigation: Disable or tightly approve long-term memory and profiling unless users have provided appropriate consent.

Risk: The skill includes WhatsApp outreach and bulk customer-contact workflows.

Mitigation: Require human approval before sending messages and confirm that outreach follows applicable platform, privacy, and anti-spam requirements.

Risk: The skill provides payment, tax, customs, and foreign-exchange guidance.

Mitigation: Verify operational decisions with qualified professionals and current official sources before acting.
