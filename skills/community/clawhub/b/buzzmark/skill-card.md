## Description:

Write, plan and schedule social media posts in a business's own voice with Buzzmark's MCP server. Use when the user wants post ideas, posts for Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Threads or TikTok, a week of posts planned around their best times, a check on how much a post sounds like their brand, or what worked this month.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders and growing businesses use this skill to draft social posts in their brand voice, plan and schedule content for connected social accounts, and review post performance. It can also adapt drafts for different platforms and prepare a weekly content plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The CLI instructions use unpinned npx commands, so the package can change between runs; the CLI may access credentials or local data, and scan coverage was partial.

Mitigation: Use a reviewed, fixed CLI version and limit the CLI's credentials and access. Treat scan coverage as incomplete because a local path reference could not be resolved.
