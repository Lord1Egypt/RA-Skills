## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent to summarize technical indicators, screen stocks, and draft educational trading-signal analyses with risk-control context. The skill is suited to exploratory stock technical analysis rather than automated financial decision-making.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill gives direct trading-style guidance while the security review says its analysis engine may be incomplete or overstated.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational signals, review the implementation before use, and do not rely on buy/sell, position-size, stop-loss, or stock-screening recommendations without independent validation.

Risk: Technical indicators can lag market conditions and historical data may not predict future performance.

Mitigation: Pair any analysis with current market data, human judgment, and appropriate financial risk controls.
