## Description:

Personalized learning-pack builder for Chinese creators that turns vague learning goals into executable, reviewable Markdown learning packs with learner profiles, authority-first research, staged exercises, milestones, and cited sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cellinlab](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellinlab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, creators, and self-directed learners use this skill to convert a vague learning goal into a Chinese learning pack with a learner profile, staged path, exercises, milestones, source appendix, and review plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The default Chinese-first, creator-oriented workflow may not fit users who need another language or learning format.

Mitigation: State the preferred language, audience, and output format before invoking the skill.

Risk: A learning pack may include outdated or misleading guidance if cited sources are not checked.

Mitigation: Review the source appendix, dates, and credibility notes, especially for high-stakes topics.
