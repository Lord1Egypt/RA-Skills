## Description:

Coordinates isolated, read-only public-content research across user-selected EasyBR browser environments and consolidates account diagnostics, comment insights, and competitor research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xxjrq](https://clawhub.ai/user/xxjrq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

Content creators and teams use this skill to review public Douyin content across explicitly selected browser environments and compare findings by account. It produces per-account reports and script drafts while keeping browser sessions separate.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Hold installation until dependency versions and sources are reviewed; verify the CLI and dependency sources before running, especially for scripts that were not fully inspected.
