## Description:

How to talk to someone you are worried about, including concerns about depression, substance abuse, self-harm, or suicidal thoughts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to get structured conversation guidance when concerned about someone who may be depressed, misusing substances, self-harming, or suicidal. It helps them assess warning signs, choose supportive language, avoid harmful responses, and connect the person to crisis or professional support when needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mental-health guidance can be mistaken for professional diagnosis or treatment.

Mitigation: Use the skill as supportive conversation guidance only, avoid diagnosing, and connect the person to professional or crisis resources when warning signs are present.

Risk: Suicidal ideation or immediate self-harm risk requires urgent escalation.

Mitigation: Provide 988 and crisis resources immediately when suicidal ideation is mentioned, and contact emergency services or an emergency department when there is immediate danger.

Risk: The security review flags unnecessary filesystem access and an unpinned npx installer.

Mitigation: Install only when the publisher is trusted, limit host filesystem access, and prefer a pinned or integrity-verified installation workflow.
