## Description:

Turn messy service pricing notes into professional quotes, SOW line items, and invoice drafts with assumptions clearly surfaced.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[52yuanchangxing](https://clawhub.ai/user/52yuanchangxing)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External service providers, consultants, and business operators use this skill to convert unstructured scope and pricing notes into draft quotes, SOW line items, and invoice drafts with assumptions, exclusions, taxes, discounts, and payment terms surfaced for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated quotes or invoices may contain incorrect rates, taxes, discounts, payment terms, or assumptions if source inputs are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review all rates, tax and discount calculations, payment terms, and assumptions before sending any generated document externally.

Risk: The skill is a drafting aid, not an authoritative accounting or legal tool.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer approve final financial, contractual, and legal language before use.
