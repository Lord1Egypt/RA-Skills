## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Server security evidence reports a suspicious verdict and notes that a review helper defaults to a nested Codex review with sandbox bypass and full access. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when the publisher workflows are trusted; before using autoreview, run it with --no-yolo or review the exact command it will execute. <br>
Risk: The skill may handle sensitive child learning, health, and growth information. <br>
Mitigation: Keep child records local and encrypted as described by the skill, avoid entering unnecessary identifying data, and have a caregiver review outputs before use. <br>
Risk: Educational or parenting guidance may be incorrect, incomplete, or inappropriate for a specific child. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs as drafts for adult review and defer to qualified teachers, clinicians, or local policy requirements for high-stakes decisions. <br>
