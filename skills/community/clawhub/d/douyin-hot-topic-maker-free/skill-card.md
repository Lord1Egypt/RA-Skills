## Description:

Uses one authorized browser’s currently visible Douyin hot-page and optional search signals to create five account-tailored topic ideas with hooks, outlines, and source citations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xxjrq](https://clawhub.ai/user/xxjrq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

External content creators and planners use the skill to turn currently visible Douyin hot-page and optional keyword-search signals into five practical topic ideas tailored to an account’s niche and audience.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan reports that existing research and plan files may be deleted; some components were only partially inspected.

Mitigation: Review the deletion behavior before use, or use a fresh output directory that contains no work you need to keep.
