## Description:

Turn financial and operating spreadsheets into judgment-rich narratives for management — variance attribution (price vs. volume), trend interpretation, three-line risk warnings, and action recommendations with owners and verification metrics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chinabizskills](https://clawhub.ai/user/chinabizskills)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Finance and operations teams use this skill to turn P&L, sales, and operating data into management summaries and reports that explain performance changes, surface risks, and suggest actions with owners and verification metrics. It is intended to support review and decision-making, with recommendations treated as decision references.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The documented npx installation command is unpinned, and the scanner could not verify the remotely referenced skill's functionality.

Mitigation: Confirm the publisher and review the package source before running the install command; use a fixed reviewed release if the installer supports version pinning.

Risk: Management recommendations may be misleading when source data is incomplete or inconsistent.

Mitigation: Check calculations and assumptions against the source data, surface contradictions, and treat recommendations as decision references rather than professional advice.
