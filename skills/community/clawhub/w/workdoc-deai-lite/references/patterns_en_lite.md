# 15 common AI tells in English work writing

Decide from context. Cut when you can, state the specific thing when the source gives it, and never invent one. Technical terms, proper names, and quotes stay as written. The "Tell" column holds counterexamples.

| # | Tell | Try instead |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | utilize | use |
| 2 | In today's fast-paced world, ... | delete, start with the problem |
| 3 | It's worth noting that ... | delete the phrase, keep the point |
| 4 | Moreover, / Furthermore, / Additionally, | usually cut |
| 5 | crucial, paramount | say what breaks without it |
| 6 | foster, empower, elevate, unlock | use the concrete verb |
| 7 | underscore, highlight (as verbs) | state the fact alone |
| 8 | a testament to | keep the fact, drop the verdict |
| 9 | tapestry, landscape (figurative) | name the actual things |
| 10 | plays a vital role in | say what it does |
| 11 | significantly improves | give the sourced measurement, or `[NEEDED: before/after]` |
| 12 | It's not just X, it's Y. | state the point you mean, once |
| 13 | Not only does it X, but it also Y. | It does X and Y. |
| 14 | serves as, boasts | is, has |
| 15 | In conclusion, / Ultimately, | delete, or give the decision |

## Demonstration (fictional)

**Before**

> In today's fast-paced world, caching plays a vital role in our API. It's worth noting that we utilized a new cache layer, and p95 latency dropped from approx. 900 ms to approx. 350 ms in staging. Moreover, production rollout is tentatively planned for June 12, pending load testing. Ultimately, this is a testament to our team.

**After**

> With a new cache layer in our API, p95 latency in staging dropped from approx. 900 ms to approx. 350 ms. Production rollout is tentatively planned for June 12, pending load testing.

Both "approx." markers, "in staging", "tentatively", and the load testing condition stayed.
