## Description:

Audit a brand's AI search visibility, identify unanswered buyer questions, write targeted pages that AI will cite, and measure ranking improvements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gsmmediaro](https://clawhub.ai/user/gsmmediaro)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, content, and growth teams use this skill to audit AI search visibility for a brand, find commercial questions where competitors are named instead, draft a targeted page to close the gap, and re-measure results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing pages or competitor comparisons could contain incorrect, misleading, or unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Review generated claims, comparisons, citations, and factual support before publishing content or changing a live site.

Risk: A single-pass search audit can be mistaken for continuous AI answer tracking.

Mitigation: Treat one-pass retrieval checks as a proxy, and re-measure the same question set after publishing before claiming ranking improvement.
