## Description:

A Chinese Nonviolent Communication toolbox based on Marshall Rosenberg's NVC method for conflict handling, feeling expression, requests, self-coaching, empathic listening, and gratitude.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[susstoiclab](https://clawhub.ai/user/susstoiclab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to draft or transform Chinese communication into NVC-style observations, feelings, needs, and requests. It supports conflict de-escalation, anger reflection, self-empathy, empathic listening, gratitude, and Marshall Rosenberg-inspired teaching or writing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is strongly tailored to Chinese-language NVC-style communication coaching and may not match users seeking another language or a neutral tone.

Mitigation: Ask for the desired language and tone explicitly when neutral wording, localization, or non-Chinese output is needed.

Risk: Marshall Rosenberg-inspired wording can sound stylistically specific or directive in sensitive interpersonal situations.

Mitigation: Review generated wording before use and adapt it to the relationship, context, and consent of the people involved.
