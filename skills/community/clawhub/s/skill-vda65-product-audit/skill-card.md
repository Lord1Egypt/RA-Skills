## Description:

Provides Chinese-language VDA 6.5 product-audit guidance for planning audits, preparing checklists, analyzing findings, rating risk, and producing improvement recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Quality engineers, supplier quality teams, and auditors use this skill to conduct automotive product audits aligned with VDA 6.5, including audit preparation, on-site checks, nonconformance classification, reporting, and corrective-action follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audit photos or records could include people, unrelated proprietary information, or customer data.

Mitigation: Confirm photography is authorized, avoid capturing people or unrelated sensitive data, and store audit photos only through approved company channels.

Risk: Generic audit guidance may not reflect site-specific customer requirements, drawings, control plans, or regulatory obligations.

Mitigation: Have a qualified auditor review outputs against approved product specifications, customer special requirements, and internal quality procedures before acting on them.
