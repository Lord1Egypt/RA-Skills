### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat TCM diagnostic, formula, dosing, toxic-herb, acupuncture, tuina, cupping, moxibustion, or topical-treatment content as independent medical advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill only as an educational reference or as material for qualified clinician review, and verify recommendations against current official guidance and local rules.

Risk: Actionable clinical or procedural guidance may be unsafe for self-diagnosis, emergencies, pregnancy, pediatric issues, cancer care, chest pain, hemorrhage, respiratory conditions, toxic herbs, or invasive procedures.

Mitigation: Route emergency, high-risk, procedural, pregnancy, pediatric, oncology, cardiac, toxic-herb, and other sensitive scenarios to qualified medical supervision instead of using the skill as a treatment authority.
