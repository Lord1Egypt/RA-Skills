## Description:

Ruitian Html helps agents generate polished Chinese enterprise HTML documents with a purple gradient theme, card layouts, responsive sections, tables, flow diagrams, and Bootstrap-based components.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lcviva](https://clawhub.ai/user/lcviva)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, consultants, and business teams use this skill to turn project outlines, technical plans, internal training material, meeting notes, or product documentation into complete enterprise-style HTML pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated HTML can depend on external jsDelivr Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons resources.

Mitigation: For sensitive documents or offline workflows, replace CDN links with reviewed local copies or SRI-protected resources before use.
