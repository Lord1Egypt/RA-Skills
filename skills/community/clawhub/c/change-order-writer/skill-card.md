## Description:

Drafts construction change order requests with entitlement basis, scope delta, itemised pricing, schedule impact, and reservation of rights.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External construction project teams, contractors, and contract administrators use this skill to draft ready-to-send COR/PCO correspondence for changed work, extra work pricing, directives, and field changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafted change order language can affect payment, notice rights, schedule relief, and contract positions if clauses, dates, pricing, or impacts are wrong.

Mitigation: Verify contract clauses, notice deadlines, pricing records, markups, dates, and schedule impacts, and route final language through qualified contracts or legal counsel before sending.
