## Description:

Academic Results Writer helps agents draft, revise, and audit academic Results sections from statistics, figures, tables, captions, and drafts, with Chinese as the default output language and optional English or journal-specific styles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bin77-chris](https://clawhub.ai/user/bin77-chris)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic writers use this skill to turn provided statistical outputs, figures, tables, captions, target-paper structure notes, or draft Results text into publication-oriented Results prose. It is most relevant to psychology, behavioral science, cognitive neuroscience, sleep and memory, VR experiments, EEG/fMRI, psychometrics, intervention studies, questionnaires, meta-analysis, and qualitative research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Long outputs may be saved as local Markdown files on the user's Desktop.

Mitigation: Review where generated files are written before sharing or retaining academic material.

Risk: Academic Results prose can become misleading if generated statistics, inferred values, or revisions are not checked against the source data.

Mitigation: Manually verify all statistics, p-values, effect sizes, sample sizes, and interpretations against the user's actual analysis outputs before use.

Risk: The skill defaults to Chinese output, which may not match every publication or reviewer requirement.

Mitigation: Request English output or a specific journal style when the manuscript or audience requires it.
