# Example: "Paper Lanterns"

A worked example for a 45-second part of a song. The user asked for a "sing + story" video in 16:9 and will upload a photo of themselves. You cannot see the photo, so the appearance avoids gender, age, ethnicity and hair.

## Inputs

- Song: "Paper Lanterns", slow acoustic pop, 0:00–0:45.
- Kind of video: sing + story.
- Lyrics in that part:

```text
[Intro]
[Verse]
Paper lanterns on the river
Every wish a little flame
I wrote it down and let it go
A year of carrying your name
[Chorus]
Float on, float on
Take it where I cannot go
Float on, float on
Carry it slow, carry it slow
```

- Segments: 45 seconds ÷ 15 = 3 segments of 15 seconds.

## Plan

**Title:** Paper Lanterns

**Logline:** On the night of the lantern festival, someone lets go of a name they have carried for a year.

**Story:** A year after a goodbye, the main character walks to the river on festival night with a paper lantern. They write a name on it, hesitate on the stone steps, and set it on the water. As the chorus starts, their lantern drifts off to join hundreds of others, and they watch it go, lighter than when they came.

**Visual style:** Warm night photography, deep blue and lantern-orange palette, 50mm lens, soft glow and light mist over the water.

**Main character**

- Appearance: calm, quietly sad expression; slow, deliberate movements; often looks down at the water.
- Wardrobe: cream cable-knit sweater, long dark green coat, simple silver pendant.

**Cover prompt:** The main character stands on stone river steps at night holding a glowing paper lantern, hundreds of lanterns on the water behind them, light mist, cinematic poster composition.
