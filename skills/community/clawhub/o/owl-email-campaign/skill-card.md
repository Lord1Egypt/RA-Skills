## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Campaign guidance may emphasize conversion tactics such as urgency, offers, and subject-line optimization in contexts where those tactics are inappropriate.

Mitigation: Use the skill for marketing campaign drafting and optimization, and review outputs carefully before applying them to sensitive or non-marketing email contexts.

Risk: Generated campaign copy or benchmark guidance may be misleading or unsuitable for a specific audience, brand, legal regime, or deliverability environment.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer validate claims, offers, timing, and compliance requirements before sending campaign emails.
