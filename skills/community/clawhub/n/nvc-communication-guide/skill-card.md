## Description:

Calculates left-side dollar-cost averaging triggers, add-position sizing, exchange-cost comparisons, and risk warnings from user-supplied index, holding-cost, budget, and currency data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to produce structured DCA decision, exchange-cost, and position diagnosis reports from their own investment inputs. It is decision support only and requires complete user-provided data before producing calculation-based guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake calculation outputs for financial advice or direct buy, add-position, or exchange instructions.

Mitigation: Present outputs as decision support, require complete user-provided data, and include market, currency, and liquidity risk warnings in reports.

Risk: Broad finance trigger terms may activate the skill during general finance discussion.

Mitigation: Confirm user intent and request missing required data before producing calculation-based guidance.
