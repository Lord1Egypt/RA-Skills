## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may generate legal or financial templates that affect ownership, revenue sharing, exits, and disputes.

Mitigation: Treat generated documents as drafts and have qualified counsel review them before signing or relying on them.

Risk: SPV agreements and dispute terms can vary by jurisdiction and project facts.

Mitigation: Specify the governing jurisdiction, parties, contributions, project scope, and commercial facts before adapting any template.

Risk: Contribution-weighted profit sharing can create ambiguity if inputs or adjustment rules are incomplete.

Mitigation: Document contribution valuation methods, review cadence, approval process, and dispute-resolution steps in the final agreement.
