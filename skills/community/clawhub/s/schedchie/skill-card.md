## Description:

Schedule, post, publish, cross-post and reschedule social media posts, read analytics and run approvals on TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, X, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky and Google Business Profile, plus Mastodon, Telegram, Discord, Slack, WordPress, Dev.to, Hashnode, Ghost, Pixelfed and Listmonk, through the Schedchie MCP server after one OAuth sign-in, on the user's own Schedchie accounts. Use when the user asks to schedule or post a video, photo or text to social media, cross-post one post to several networks, move or fix a scheduled post, check what failed, see how accounts performed, or approve a teammate's posts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
