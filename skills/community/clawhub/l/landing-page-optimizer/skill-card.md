## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce recommendations that affect real ad spend or platform settings.

Mitigation: Review plans before implementation, validate assumptions, and require explicit approval before changing budgets, bids, targeting, tracking, or campaign settings.

Risk: Missing or weak inputs can lead to low-confidence channel, budget, or test recommendations.

Mitigation: Collect the required objective, budget frame, and channel scope first; mark assumptions clearly and use minimum test budgets when evidence is weak.
