## Description:

Drafts Chinese civil complaint materials by extracting case facts, party details, claims, and evidence information from chat records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuleff](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuleff)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to collect civil case information, organize evidence, and draft Chinese civil complaint materials for review before filing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks users for names, addresses, contact details, case facts, and evidence information.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary information and redact nonessential third-party or identifier details.

Risk: Generated complaint materials may be incomplete, inaccurate, or misaligned with local court requirements.

Mitigation: Review generated filing materials against local court requirements or with a qualified legal professional before use.
