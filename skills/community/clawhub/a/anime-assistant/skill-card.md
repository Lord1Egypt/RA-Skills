## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is Chinese-first and may answer in Chinese for users expecting another language.

Mitigation: Tell the agent the preferred response language when invoking the skill.

Risk: Creative guidance may include subjective art, style, or workflow recommendations that need adaptation for a specific project.

Mitigation: Review outputs against project requirements, originality expectations, and applicable intellectual-property constraints before reuse.
