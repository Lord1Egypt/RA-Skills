## Description:

Transforms Chinese client narratives into structured legal-intake reports that identify facts, legal issues, evidence, information gaps, and lawyer-facing risk notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zaj7373](https://clawhub.ai/user/zaj7373)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Legal professionals use this skill to convert incomplete or emotional client statements into organized Chinese-language intake reports for consultation preparation and initial case triage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce preliminary legal issue classifications that are incomplete or incorrect for a specific jurisdiction or fact pattern.

Mitigation: A qualified lawyer should review the generated report, confirm the facts, and apply current local law before relying on the analysis.

Risk: Client narratives may omit dates, amounts, parties, or evidence needed for a reliable assessment.

Mitigation: Use the generated information-gap section to guide follow-up questions and evidence collection before making legal judgments.
