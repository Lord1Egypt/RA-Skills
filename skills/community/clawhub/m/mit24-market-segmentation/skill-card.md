## Description:

Guides founders through MIT 24 Steps phase one activities: market segmentation, beachhead selection, user personas, TAM/SAM/SOM sizing, and customer decision-process analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide confidential business details, customer interview notes, or market assumptions while using the skill.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential business information unless it is appropriate for the agent session.

Risk: Market sizing or customer analysis can be misleading if based on unsupported assumptions.

Mitigation: Validate outputs against real customer interviews, cited market data, and clearly stated calculation assumptions.
