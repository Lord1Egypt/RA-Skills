## Description:

MBA thesis writing and formatting advisor that helps users diagnose drafts, improve argument quality, strengthen theoretical contributions, and prepare LaTeX/thuthesis manuscripts for submission.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chao1208](https://clawhub.ai/user/chao1208)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, advisors, and writing-support agents use this skill to review MBA thesis drafts, identify weak originality or evidence, rewrite sections in a more critical analytical style, and handle Chinese MBA thesis formatting tasks. It is especially oriented toward LaTeX and thuthesis workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is tailored to Chinese-language MBA thesis work and LaTeX/thuthesis conventions, so guidance may not fit other institutions, degree types, or languages.

Mitigation: Provide the applicable university requirements, degree rules, thesis template, and formatting constraints before relying on its recommendations.

Risk: The skill may inspect local thesis source files or signature-image formatting when asked to perform review or submission-preparation tasks.

Mitigation: Review which thesis files and images are shared with the agent, and avoid providing private or sensitive material unless it is necessary for the task.

Risk: Writing and formatting advice can introduce inaccurate citations, unsupported claims, or institution-specific compliance errors.

Mitigation: Have the author or advisor verify factual claims, sources, rubric scores, and final submission formatting against official program requirements.
