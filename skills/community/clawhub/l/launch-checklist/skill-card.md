## Description:

Create comprehensive pre-launch, launch day, and post-launch checklists for new TikTok Shop products to avoid costly missed steps during the critical first 72 hours.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External sellers and ecommerce operators use this skill to produce phase-based TikTok Shop launch runbooks with readiness checks, owners, deadlines, monitoring thresholds, and blockers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: TikTok Shop policy, category, promotion, and regional compliance guidance can change or vary by marketplace.

Mitigation: Confirm the current TikTok Shop policy center and local requirements before acting on the checklist.

Risk: Launch recommendations can be misleading if SKU details, inventory, dates, owners, or compliance status are missing or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Treat unknown inputs and Weak readiness rows as blockers, assign owners, and review the runbook before launch.
