Risk: Included workflows may trigger business-impacting actions such as live trading research, bulk email, data collection, or tracking setup.

Mitigation: Use isolated, least-privilege API keys and require human approval before live trading, bulk email sends, scraping, or production analytics changes.

Mitigation: Confirm platform terms, privacy obligations, and recipient consent before collecting data or sending automated messages.
