## Description:

学程智伴 is a Chinese-language academic planning and focus companion for college students, providing study planning, career guidance, emotional support, and time-management help.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smallkeyboy](https://clawhub.ai/user/smallkeyboy)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

College and graduate students use this skill for semester planning, study methods, career exploration, emotional support, and Pomodoro-style focus workflows. Agents can use it to provide Chinese-language guidance, structured study plans, and optional command-line timer or planning outputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat emotional-support responses as professional mental-health care.

Mitigation: Present support as non-professional guidance and direct self-harm, suicidal ideation, severe anxiety, sustained depression, or crisis signals to emergency services, crisis hotlines, campus counseling, or a trusted person.

Risk: Persistent agent memory could retain academic or emotional details the user considers sensitive.

Mitigation: Advise users to avoid sharing details they do not want remembered and to manage memory settings before using the skill for sensitive topics.

Risk: Study, career, and time-management plans may be too generic or poorly matched to a student's real constraints.

Mitigation: Ask for the student's program, year, course load, deadlines, goals, and constraints before making plans, and encourage review against official academic or campus resources.
