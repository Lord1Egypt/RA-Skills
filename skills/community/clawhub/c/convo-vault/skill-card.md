## Description:

Export or archive ChatGPT, Grok and Gemini chats in local Markdown, PDF and JSONL while preserving original languages and available reasoning and tool records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

People who want local archives of their ChatGPT, Grok, or Gemini conversations can use the skill to export selected chats or a whole ChatGPT project, then review the resulting conversation files and available activity records.
