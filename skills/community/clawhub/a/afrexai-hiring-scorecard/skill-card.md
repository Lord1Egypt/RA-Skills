## Description:

Objectively score and compare job candidates using customizable weighted criteria to support data-driven hiring decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Recruiters, hiring managers, and interview teams use this skill to generate weighted candidate scorecards, compare candidates side by side, and produce concise hiring summaries. It is intended to support structured evaluation from interview or review evidence rather than replace organizational hiring review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can process sensitive candidate data and produce hire/no-hire recommendations without built-in privacy controls.

Mitigation: Keep candidate data minimal and access-limited, and follow organizational privacy and record-retention rules.

Risk: Default criteria include culture fit, which can introduce bias if not tied to job-related evidence.

Mitigation: Replace or remove culture fit with job-related behavioral criteria and require evidence for every score.

Risk: Weighted scores and summaries may appear more objective than the underlying interview evidence supports.

Mitigation: Use the scorecard as decision support only and require human review under the organization's hiring process.
