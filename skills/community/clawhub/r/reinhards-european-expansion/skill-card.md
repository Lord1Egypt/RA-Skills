Mitigation: Have the agent state when it is using Reinhard's framework and verify that the user wants a book-specific analysis.

Mitigation: Confirm final license and rights before commercial redistribution or public use beyond study and research contexts.

Risk: Historical summaries and frameworks can be incomplete or misleading if treated as comprehensive scholarship.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a research aid and cross-check important claims against the source book and other scholarly references.
