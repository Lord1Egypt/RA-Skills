## Description:

Guides agents through public-source due diligence on foreign companies, including company confirmation, adaptive web research, sanctions screening, contact collection, physical presence checks, and a structured Chinese background-check report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xiezhenlin-tom](https://clawhub.ai/user/xiezhenlin-tom)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business users and agents use this skill to investigate overseas companies from a company name or email domain, gather public-source evidence, assess compliance signals, and prepare a structured due-diligence report in Chinese.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may over-collect personnel contact details or infer individual email patterns, which could support unwanted targeting or outreach.

Mitigation: Use it only for legitimate business due diligence, prefer official company contact channels, avoid storing unnecessary person-level details, and ask for confirmation before collecting employee-specific information.
