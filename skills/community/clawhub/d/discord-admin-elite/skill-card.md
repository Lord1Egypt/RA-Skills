### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Administrative recommendations may weaken server safety or expose members if role, invite, AutoMod, moderation, or visibility changes are applied without review.

Mitigation: Review proposed changes with server owners and moderators before applying them in Discord, especially changes to moderator permissions, public invites, AutoMod, verification, and member visibility.
