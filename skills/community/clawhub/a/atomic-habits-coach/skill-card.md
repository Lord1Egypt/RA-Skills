## Description:

A Chinese-language habit coaching skill that creates structured, personalized action plans for building good habits, breaking bad habits, handling relapse, and optionally tailoring plans with a brief Big Five personality check.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangzhanchao883](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangzhanchao883)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill as a self-help habit coach to turn a stated habit goal or unwanted behavior into a concrete Markdown action plan. It is intended for habit formation and behavior-change guidance, not clinical diagnosis, treatment, or ongoing automated tracking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake habit, addiction, or mental-health screening guidance for medical advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as self-help coaching only; for severe symptoms, loss of control, or medical concerns, follow the skill's referral posture and seek professional care.

Risk: The skill is designed for Chinese-language coaching and may be unsuitable for users who cannot review that output accurately.

Mitigation: Deploy it only where Chinese-language habit coaching is appropriate, or have translated outputs reviewed before use.
