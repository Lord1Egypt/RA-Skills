## Description:

Provides French-language guidance for drafting and structuring social-center management documents covering CAF approval, SENACS reporting, governance, funding, and activity planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thibautnext](https://clawhub.ai/user/thibautnext)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External social-center managers, association staff, and administrative teams use this skill to draft project plans, activity reports, CAF approval files, SENACS preparation material, governance notes, indicators, job descriptions, and institutional correspondence for French centres sociaux.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

France

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes regulatory figures, funding amounts, and process details that may change over time.

Mitigation: Verify official submissions and time-sensitive values against current CAF/CNAF guidance before use.
