## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad savings-related phrases and return coupon or rebate suggestions outside the user's intended shopping workflow.

Mitigation: Confirm the product, merchant, and shopping context before relying on the suggested savings path.

Risk: Coupon availability, rebate rates, and final prices may be outdated or biased toward a rebate channel.

Mitigation: Review rebate links, coupon eligibility, expiration, and the final checkout price before purchasing.
