## Description:

Guides cautious, evidence-weighted persona and relationship analysis of social-media traces, with explicit confidence limits and privacy safeguards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People exploring social-media signals for personal relationship or collaboration questions can use the skill to organize observable facts, tentative behavioral inferences, and questions for reflection. Its guidance calls for consent where feasible and excludes diagnosis and consequential decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personality and relationship inferences from social-media traces are speculative and may be mistaken.

Mitigation: Present conclusions as hypotheses with confidence limits, seek consent where feasible, and do not use them for diagnosis or consequential decisions.

Risk: The security review was incomplete because a local reference did not resolve to a bundled artifact.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and confirm it is available and relevant before installing or relying on the skill.
