## Description:

SemiWatch Pro is a semiconductor equipment intelligence skill that uses PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP or user-provided data to produce source-traceable patent, supply-chain, technology-gap, policy-control, and competitor-analysis dashboards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and semiconductor intelligence teams use this skill to turn supplied targets, conditions, files, or search expressions into reviewable patent and market intelligence outputs. It supports briefs and single-file HTML dashboards with key findings, evidence, traceability, and suggested next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent, FTO, infringement, or validity discussion may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat those outputs as research support only and route legal conclusions to qualified counsel.

Risk: Patent, market, or technical findings may be misleading if source data is incomplete or unverified.

Mitigation: Use PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP or user-provided data, state source scope and time range, and clearly mark uncertainty.
