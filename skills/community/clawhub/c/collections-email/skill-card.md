## Description:

Write a polite-but-firm payment-reminder and collections email sequence for overdue invoices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and business teams use this skill to draft staged invoice reminder emails, from pre-due nudges through final notices, while preserving client relationships and keeping payment instructions clear.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated emails may include incorrect invoice details, payment instructions, late-fee terms, or escalation language.

Mitigation: Verify invoice numbers, amounts, payment links or bank details, and any late-fee or escalation wording before sending.

Risk: Collections language can create legal or compliance exposure if it overstates rights or threatens actions that are not lawful or intended.

Mitigation: Confirm interest, statutory rights, late fees, and escalation steps with an appropriate professional for the relevant jurisdiction.
