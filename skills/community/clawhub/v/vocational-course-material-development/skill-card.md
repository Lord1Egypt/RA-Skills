## Description:

Helps vocational educators, instructional designers, and course developers design, develop, and optimize integrated vocational courses and teaching materials using competency maps and AI-assisted drafting workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[erich1566](https://clawhub.ai/user/erich1566)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External vocational educators, instructional designers, and course developers use this skill to structure job competency maps, modular course content, curriculum maps, and teaching resources for vocational education programs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users expecting English output may receive Chinese by default when no language environment signal is set.

Mitigation: Review language behavior before deployment and set the language environment explicitly when English output is required.

Risk: AI-assisted course and teaching-material drafts may be incomplete, inaccurate, or misaligned with local vocational standards.

Mitigation: Require review by qualified vocational educators before using generated curriculum, teaching resources, or textbook guidance.
