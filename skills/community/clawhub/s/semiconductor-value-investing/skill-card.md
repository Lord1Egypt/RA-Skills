## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Investment analysis may be mistaken for financial advice or trading instruction.

Mitigation: Present outputs as research analysis, avoid buy or sell signals, and require the user to make independent investment decisions.

Risk: Market prices, financial figures, and valuation labels can become inaccurate or be hallucinated by the host agent.

Mitigation: Verify market data independently before relying on analysis or publishing any price, PE, financial, or valuation statement.

Risk: Publishing, local archive, memory, or scheduled workflow behavior could expose drafts or act without the user's intended control.

Mitigation: Keep user approval in control of Xueqiu or Tencent Docs posting, local archives, memory use, and scheduled workflows in the host agent.
