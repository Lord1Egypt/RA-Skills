## Description:

Anonymous first-person crypto confession engine. Generates raw, specific, honest loss stories that teach through experience.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to draft anonymous first-person crypto loss confessions for educational posts about trading psychology and risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated crypto confession posts could be mistaken for financial guidance or real personal testimony.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as creative writing unless real sourcing is confirmed, review posts for accuracy, and avoid presenting invented confessions as real.

Risk: User-provided personal details could identify a real person in the generated confession.

Mitigation: Preserve the skill's anonymization rules by stripping names, wallet addresses, specific dates, project names, and other identifying details before publication.
