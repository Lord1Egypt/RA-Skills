## Description:

Helps AI agents plan, review, implement, audit, and improve Claude-assisted email marketing workflows for positioning, segmentation, lifecycle content, and campaign strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[polnikale](https://clawhub.ai/user/polnikale)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, lifecycle owners, and agent operators use this skill to create positioning briefs, segment message matrices, Claude prompt packs, lifecycle copy reviews, experiment plans, and campaign recommendations with approval gates for production email actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Email recommendations could lead to live sends, contact imports, suppression changes, DNS changes, production automation edits, migrations, or destructive cleanup without proper approval.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a planning and review aid; require explicit approval from the responsible human owner before any live or high-risk email-system action.

Risk: Generated copy or strategy could overstate urgency, scarcity, outcomes, social proof, or guarantees.

Mitigation: Ground recommendations in approved proof and route brand, legal, and lifecycle-sensitive claims through human review before production use.
