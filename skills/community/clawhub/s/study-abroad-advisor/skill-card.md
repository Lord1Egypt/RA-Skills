## Description:

Provides structured study-abroad application assessment and strategy guidance, including school selection, essays, profile building, timeline planning, and optional PPT strategy presentations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kaylezhao](https://clawhub.ai/user/kaylezhao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External students, families, and education consultants use this skill to evaluate applicant background, choose target regions and school tiers, plan essays and recommendations, and organize application timelines across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Europe.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: PPT mode can insert the author's WeChat contact into user-facing presentations without clear user consent.

Mitigation: Review generated decks before sharing, remove or replace the contact page unless explicitly desired, and require confirmation before producing shareable PPT files.
