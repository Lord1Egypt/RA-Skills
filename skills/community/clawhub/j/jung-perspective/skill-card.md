## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for a Jungian reflective roleplay experience focused on analytical psychology concepts, symbolic interpretation, dream discussion, and self-exploration prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mental-health-adjacent psychology or dream interpretation prompts may be mistaken for professional diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support.

Mitigation: Use the skill only as a reflective roleplay aid and direct users to licensed professionals or crisis resources for clinical, treatment, or emergency needs.

Risk: Broad psychology, dream, unconscious, archetype, or Jung-related prompts may activate the roleplay unintentionally.

Mitigation: Confirm that a Jungian roleplay perspective is desired and exit roleplay when the user asks to switch back or stop.
