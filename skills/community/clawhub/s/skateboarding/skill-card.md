## Use Case:

Travel and city-planning users use this skill to get popular skateboard route recommendations, filtering routes by difficulty, distance, road surface, season, weather, access requirements, safety needs, and crowd conditions.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Route recommendations may become inaccurate when local venue rules, weather, crowding, access, reservations, or safety conditions change.

Mitigation: Check current local conditions, venue rules, weather alerts, and reservation requirements before following a route.

Risk: Skateboarding routes can involve physical safety risks, including unsuitable road surfaces, steep grades, limited supplies, weak signal coverage, or emergency exit constraints.

Mitigation: Review the skill's risk points, supply stops, signal coverage, emergency retreat options, and equipment guidance before starting.
