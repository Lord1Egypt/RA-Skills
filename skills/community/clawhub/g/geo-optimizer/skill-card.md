This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content marketers, SEO/GEO strategists, and writers use this skill to audit drafts against eight GEO dimensions and produce AI-citable definitions, statistics sections, FAQs, and citation-focused edits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested statistics, citations, and quotable claims may be inaccurate or unsupported because the skill is a writing framework and does not validate source accuracy.

Mitigation: Verify all statistics, quotes, dates, and source attributions against authoritative sources before publication.
