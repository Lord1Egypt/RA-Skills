### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: MML scenes using m-position-probe can collect user position data and connection identifiers.

Mitigation: Disclose collection to users, keep probe ranges and retention limited, and avoid exposing raw connection IDs unless they are required.

Risk: MML scenes using m-chat-probe can process user-generated chat content that may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Handle chat messages as sensitive user-generated data and avoid unnecessary storage or redistribution.
