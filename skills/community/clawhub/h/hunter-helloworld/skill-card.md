## Description:

A friendly greeting skill that responds to hello/你好 in multiple languages, tells the current time, and introduces the OpenClaw assistant.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ernest8976](https://clawhub.ai/user/ernest8976)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to receive a short, language-matched greeting, a brief OpenClaw assistant introduction, and an invitation to ask for help.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad conversation openers.

Mitigation: Use it for greeting and identity-introduction turns only, and let other skills or default agent behavior handle complex requests.
