## Description:

Help a user recognize real growth with evidence, proportionate celebration, and a next step that keeps momentum alive.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to recognize personal growth milestones in effort, courage, consistency, recovery, or learning. It helps turn concrete evidence into a grounded celebration and a next step that sustains momentum.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Malformed structured input with an unsupported category may cause the handler to fail.

Mitigation: Use one of the supported milestone categories or omit the category so the skill can infer a supported default.

Risk: The skill may overstate progress if tiny actions are inflated beyond their evidence.

Mitigation: Keep celebrations proportionate and tie each milestone to concrete evidence before choosing the next step.
