## Description:

Builds a 13-week rolling cash flow forecast from a business's current balance, expected income, and expenses, with runway and cash-buffer risk alerts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders, agencies, and business operators use this skill to turn current balances, expected income, expenses, and a minimum cash buffer into a week-by-week cash forecast with risk weeks and recommended actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial forecasts can be misleading if inputs are incomplete, stale, or based on unreviewed assumptions.

Mitigation: Review balances, income timing, expenses, and buffer assumptions before relying on the forecast for business decisions.

Risk: Users may share more sensitive financial information than the forecast requires.

Mitigation: Provide only the financial figures needed for forecasting and avoid sharing bank logins or unnecessary account details.

Risk: The artifact includes external promotional links that are not required to use the skill.

Mitigation: Treat those links as optional references and review them separately before following or purchasing anything.
