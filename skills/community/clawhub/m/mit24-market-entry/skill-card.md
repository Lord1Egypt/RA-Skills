## Description:

Guides users through market entry planning for MIT's 24-step Disciplined Entrepreneurship method, including CAC analysis, sales process design, critical assumption prioritization, and hypothesis testing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[earthwalking](https://clawhub.ai/user/earthwalking)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Entrepreneurs, founders, and business teams use this skill to prepare a market entry strategy after defining a product and pricing. It helps calculate CAC, design repeatable sales workflows, identify critical business assumptions, and plan validation experiments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market-entry calculations and recommendations may be misleading if based on incomplete or unrealistic business data.

Mitigation: Review CAC, CLV, conversion, and assumption-test inputs against current operating data before making business decisions.
