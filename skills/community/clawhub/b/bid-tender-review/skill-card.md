## Description:

Analyse a construction bid or tender package for scope gaps, risk-shifting exclusions, unit-rate red flags, and front-loading in the schedule of values.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Construction estimators, owner representatives, project managers, and procurement reviewers use this skill to level bids, identify scope gaps and shifted risks, test pricing and schedule-of-values assumptions, and prepare pre-award clarifications before selecting a bidder.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bid award recommendations can affect financial and legal outcomes if assumptions, exclusions, or clarification questions are wrong or incomplete.

Mitigation: Have a qualified estimator, owner representative, or counsel verify assumptions, risk classifications, cost exposure, and the clarification list before relying on the recommendation.
