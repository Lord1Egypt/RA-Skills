## Description:

Analyzes changes in a main requirements document against related documents and reports which documents are already synchronized, need updates, or are unaffected.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kaycchang025-droid](https://clawhub.ai/user/kaycchang025-droid)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, product managers, and documentation owners use this skill to compare requirement document changes against related documents and identify precise synchronization work with priorities and suggested edits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill needs to read requirement and related documents that may contain sensitive project information.

Mitigation: Use it only with documents the operator is comfortable having the agent analyze.

Risk: Impact analysis may still miss or misclassify synchronization needs in complex document sets.

Mitigation: Review the cited affected sections and proposed edits before changing source documents.
