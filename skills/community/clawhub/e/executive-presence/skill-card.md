## Description:

Executive Presence provides scenario-specific coaching for high-stakes meetings and leadership presentations, including answer-first structure, concise messaging, hard-question handling, composure cues, and stronger language choices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and external professionals use this skill to prepare for executive-facing presentations, tense meetings, board Q&A, crisis calls, or other high-stakes moments where concise, composed communication matters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share confidential business, personnel, or meeting details to get useful coaching.

Mitigation: Share only the context needed for the coaching task and avoid unnecessary sensitive details.

Risk: Advice may be too generic or mismatched if the user's moment, audience, goal, or concern is incomplete.

Mitigation: Provide the specific meeting context requested by the skill and review the output against the actual room before using it.
