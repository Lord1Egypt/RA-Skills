## Description:

Provides Chinese-language application security audit and secure coding guidance for China and similar business contexts, covering OWASP Top 10, authentication and authorization, secrets, CORS and CSP, input validation, injection, XSS, CSRF, dependency vulnerabilities, logging, and actionable remediation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, security reviewers, and application teams use this skill to perform structured security audits and produce prioritized remediation guidance for web APIs, mobile backends, mini-program services, personal-data workflows, and payment callback flows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China and similar regions

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer an agent to inspect sensitive authentication, secrets, environment, dependency, payment callback, and administrator route files.

Mitigation: Run it only in approved workspaces, limit access to necessary files, and avoid exposing secrets in prompts or generated reports.

Risk: Regional compliance notes can be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat legal and sector-specific decisions as review items for qualified legal or compliance teams.
