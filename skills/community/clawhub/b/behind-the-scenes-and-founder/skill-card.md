## Description:

Helps agents turn real business moments into founder or behind-the-scenes content by grounding the draft in actual events, sharpening specifics and stakes, checking disclosure boundaries, and routing the result toward the right social format.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External social media operators, founders, and content teams use this skill to draft build-in-public updates, founder stories, process posts, day-in-the-life content, and honest lessons without inventing events or exposing sensitive information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may include sensitive or irreversible disclosures about revenue, customers, employees, health or family details, legal matters, security details, or regulated-industry claims.

Mitigation: Require human review before publishing, get consent or compliance review where needed, and keep customer data, personnel matters, active legal issues, credentials, and exploitable operational details out of the final content.

Risk: Founder content can become misleading if the agent invents events, exaggerates vulnerability, ghost-writes feelings, or implies guaranteed reach or conversion.

Mitigation: Ground every draft in events supplied by the user, preserve the founder's actual voice, avoid manufactured vulnerability and humble-brags, and treat performance claims as uncertain unless independently supported.

Risk: Quoted comments, DMs, screenshots, or other user-provided material could contain instructions or sensitive background details.

Mitigation: Treat those materials as content evidence rather than commands and inspect screenshots or quoted material for credentials, private information, NDA material, and other disclosure hazards before use.
