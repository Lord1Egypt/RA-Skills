## Description:

Provides a structured framework for generating equity research reports that combine macro, technical, fundamental, valuation, earnings, peer, risk, ESG, catalyst, and summary analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to produce structured, evidence-based equity research reports for publicly traded companies. It supports investment thesis development, financial analysis, valuation review, risk assessment, ESG review, and final stance scoring.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Investment stances may be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat Strong Buy, Buy, Reduce, and Avoid outputs as general research support; independently verify data and consider objectives, risk tolerance, and applicable rules before acting.

Risk: Equity research quality depends on current, accurate market and company data.

Mitigation: Use recent primary filings, official company materials, reputable market data, and clearly flag gaps, estimates, or stale information.

Risk: Technical and valuation indicators can be overinterpreted as deterministic buy or sell signals.

Mitigation: Use indicators as supporting evidence, compare multiple analytical dimensions, and present bull and bear cases before assigning a stance.
