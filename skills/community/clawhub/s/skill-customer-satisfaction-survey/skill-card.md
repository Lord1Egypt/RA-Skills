## Description:

Customer Satisfaction Survey guides quality teams through Chinese-language CSAT, NPS, and CES survey design and evidence-based customer satisfaction analysis reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees in customer quality or market quality roles use this skill to design CSAT/NPS/CES surveys, structure real response data, calculate satisfaction metrics, and draft action-oriented survey reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Customer survey responses may include confidential customer or business data.

Mitigation: Use only approved environments for sensitive data and avoid providing confidential customer data unless the environment is cleared for it.

Risk: Reports can be misleading if satisfaction metrics are generated without real response data.

Mitigation: Require real survey response data for analysis and mark sample size, NPS, and dimension scores as pending when evidence is missing.

Risk: Generated report files may be written locally.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended output directory before generating reports.
