## Description:

Writes clear, helpful error messages that explain what happened, what users can do next, and how variants should adapt across inline, toast, modal, and full-page surfaces.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product teams, designers, support writers, and developers use this skill to draft blame-free user-facing error copy with clear recovery steps and surface-specific variants. It also helps separate technical logging details from what users should see.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated copy can include inferred causes or support codes that are not product-accurate when the brief is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review final user-facing error copy against product behavior, recovery paths, and support-code policy before publishing.
