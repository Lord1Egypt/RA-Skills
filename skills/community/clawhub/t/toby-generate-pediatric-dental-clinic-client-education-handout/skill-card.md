## Description:

Create a polished explainer handout with visuals, FAQs, and clear next steps for a pediatric dental clinic.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tobeyrebecca](https://clawhub.ai/user/tobeyrebecca)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pediatric dentists and dental office managers use this skill to draft patient education handouts with explanatory copy, FAQs, visual guidance, and next steps. Patient-facing dental or health claims should be reviewed by a qualified human before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broad Bash and Read access that is not explained by its handout-generation purpose.

Mitigation: Review before installing, and prefer a version that removes shell access and limits file reads to explicit templates or user-provided materials.

Risk: Patient-facing pediatric dental content can contain inaccurate, incomplete, or overbroad health claims.

Mitigation: Have a qualified dental professional review the handout before sharing it with patients, caregivers, or clinic clients.
