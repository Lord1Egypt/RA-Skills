## Description:

Provides methods and rules for assisting financial audits across insurance, securities, and banking, including document review, image parsing, and draft audit reports for human review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Financial auditors and compliance teams use this skill to organize rule-based reviews of insurance, securities, and banking records and prepare checklists or report drafts. It is limited to explicitly requested audit and verification tasks; qualified staff must confirm findings and make final decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal or account data in audit materials could be exposed if submitted without minimization.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested audit module first, mask identifiers, and use placeholders where possible before sharing records.

Risk: Rule guidance or draft findings may be inaccurate or out of date.

Mitigation: Check cited rules and effective dates against official sources, and have qualified audit or compliance staff review all findings and reports.
