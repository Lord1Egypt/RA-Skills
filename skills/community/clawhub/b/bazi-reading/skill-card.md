## Description:

Provides Chinese Four Pillars (BaZi) chart interpretation from birth data, including pillars, stems, branches, five elements, day master themes, timing notes, and cultural caveats.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[codenova58](https://clawhub.ai/user/codenova58)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to structure reflective BaZi readings, request accurate birth inputs, explain assumptions, and avoid deterministic or professional-advice claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive birth details for a metaphysical reading.

Mitigation: Install and use only when comfortable sharing birth details; keep outputs framed as cultural or reflective interpretation.

Risk: Outputs could be mistaken for medical, legal, financial, psychological, or life-determining advice.

Mitigation: State that BaZi is not empirical science or professional advice, avoid absolute predictions, and direct users to qualified professionals for high-stakes decisions.

Risk: Exact pillars, true solar time, and luck cycles can be inaccurate without a reliable calendar engine.

Mitigation: Ask users to verify computed chart data with a reputable BaZi calculator or qualified practitioner when exact stems, branches, or timing cycles are needed.
