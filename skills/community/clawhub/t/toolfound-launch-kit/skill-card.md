## Description:

Research, prepare, submit, and track a maker product launch through the Toolfound MCP. Use when asked to list a product, compare directory alternatives, choose a launch date, verify ownership, check launch status, or get a listing badge.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[melmayssonowen](https://clawhub.ai/user/melmayssonowen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Makers use this skill to research directory categories and alternatives, prepare a factual product listing, select a launch date, and manage submission and follow-up through Toolfound. It asks for confirmation of the final listing details before submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
