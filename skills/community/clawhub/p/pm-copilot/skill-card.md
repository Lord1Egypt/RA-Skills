## Description:

General product-management copilot that acts as the default entry point for PM work.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, and product teams use this skill to turn broad or mixed product requests into the highest-confidence immediate PM artifact, such as context, requirements, synthesized feedback, meeting follow-up, competitive analysis, brainstorming output, critique, or workflow routing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is broad and may produce a less specific PM artifact when the desired output is already known.

Mitigation: Use this as the default entry point for vague or mixed PM work, and choose a narrower PM skill when the user clearly needs a specific artifact.

Risk: PM recommendations or synthesized artifacts may contain assumptions or miss important product context.

Mitigation: Surface assumptions, state remaining uncertainty, and review the immediate output before using it for decisions or downstream PM work.
