## Description:

Generates a 0-100 Supplier Risk Index score across financial, dependency, compliance, performance, and geographic risks with tiered action plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[flynndavid](https://clawhub.ai/user/flynndavid)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Procurement, operations, legal, compliance, and vendor-management teams use this skill to score supplier health during onboarding, annual reviews, and material vendor changes. It helps classify vendors into Green, Yellow, or Red tiers and choose follow-up actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Supplier scores may influence vendor selection, purchase-order holds, contract renewals, or other business decisions.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as decision-support guidance and require procurement, legal, compliance, and privacy review before scores affect operational or contract decisions.

Risk: The framework relies on data sources and geography-based scoring that may raise compliance, privacy, retention, or regional policy concerns.

Mitigation: Approve data sources, retention practices, and geography-based rules with the relevant internal stakeholders before use.
