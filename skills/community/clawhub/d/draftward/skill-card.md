## Description:

Draft, review, and verify formal academic, technical, and professional reports. Use for report outlines and first drafts, prose review, evidence and standards checks, and final PDF verification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[athndev](https://clawhub.ai/user/athndev)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT No Attribution

## Use Case:

Researchers, technical writers, and professional report authors use Draftward to plan and draft reports, review prose and evidence, check applicable reporting requirements, and verify final PDF exports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts or standards checks may include unsupported claims or apply requirements that do not govern the report.

Mitigation: Verify material claims against the cited evidence and confirm the applicable venue, standard edition, and submission requirements before release.
