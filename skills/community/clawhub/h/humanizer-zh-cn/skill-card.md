## Description:

Edits Chinese text to remove common AI writing traces and make the writing sound more natural while preserving meaning and tone.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[v585](https://clawhub.ai/user/v585)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Editors, reviewers, and writers use this skill to revise Chinese AI-generated or AI-styled drafts so they read more naturally while retaining the original meaning and intended tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rewriting can unintentionally change meaning, attribution, or tone.

Mitigation: Review the edited text against the source draft before using it externally.

Risk: Installer commands can be risky when run from unreviewed sources or with elevated privileges.

Mitigation: Install from a reviewed commit or release where possible and avoid elevated privileges.
