## Description:

Helps agents match technology commercialization projects with A-share and Hong Kong listed-company M&A acquirers, shell-company opportunities, acquirer profiles, sector fit, and financial feasibility analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to generate Chinese M&A matching analysis for technology projects, including candidate listed-company acquirers, shell-resource screening, due-diligence prompts, and matching rationale.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shell-company outreach guidance may encourage obscuring buy-shell or control-transfer intent.

Mitigation: Do not use pretext-based outreach; disclose purpose truthfully and route contact strategy through legal and securities-compliance review.

Risk: M&A matching work can involve sensitive company, contact, and diligence information.

Mitigation: Verify contact data from official filings, avoid insider-information solicitation, and handle diligence materials only through authorized secure channels.
