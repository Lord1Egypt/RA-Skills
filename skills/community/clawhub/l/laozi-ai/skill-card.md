## Description:

A Laozi and Dao De Jing reference skill for chapter lookup, multilingual comparison, quotation retrieval, Taoist concept explanation, and Laozi-style writing support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan905](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan905)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to answer questions about Laozi and the Dao De Jing, compare Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and English passages, retrieve themed quotations, explain Taoist concepts, and draft Laozi-inspired text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill during casual mentions of Laozi, Taoism, or related phrases.

Mitigation: Narrow activation wording during installation if casual Taoism mentions should not trigger this skill.

Risk: Responses may quote or rely on the bundled Laozi reference database and its selected translations.

Mitigation: Review quoted passages and translations before using them in formal, educational, or published contexts.
