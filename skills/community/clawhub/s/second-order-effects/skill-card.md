## Description:

Analyze decisions by tracing consequences beyond immediate outcomes to second and third-order effects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to analyze consequential decisions by mapping immediate, second-order, and third-order effects, then stress-testing assumptions across stakeholders and time horizons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Decision analyses and preferences may contain sensitive or outdated information because the skill stores them locally under ~/second-order-effects/.

Mitigation: Review or delete memory.md, patterns.md, and archived decision files periodically, especially after analyzing sensitive personal, business, or technical decisions.
