## Description:

Find the core that runs through everything — the ideas that survive across all your sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leegitw](https://clawhub.ai/user/leegitw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to synthesize three or more related sources and identify ideas that appear consistently across them. It helps turn repeated principles into Golden Master candidates while leaving final judgment to the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided sources may be processed by the configured AI service and retained in chat history or logs.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting proprietary or confidential material unless that handling is acceptable in the user's agent environment.

Risk: Golden Master candidates identify consistency across sources, not verified truth.

Mitigation: Treat candidates as evidence for review and use user judgment before adopting them as authoritative.
