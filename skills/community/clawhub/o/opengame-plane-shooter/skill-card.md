## Description:

Design original 2D browser plane-shooter prototypes with continuous movement, readable threat waves, a route-and-resource decision, clear terminal states, and fast retry.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers and game creators use this skill to design or build small browser-based 2D plane shooter prototypes for mobile-portrait or desktop play. It helps define the player promise, controls, threat patterns, route-and-resource choice, terminal states, retry flow, and testable debug surface.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated browser games could unintentionally include copied or unlicensed game identity, assets, layout, tuning, or media.

Mitigation: Review the generated game for originality and licensing; use only original, licensed, or procedurally generated visuals, audio, and code.

Risk: Generated prototypes could introduce external requests, remote scripts, analytics, ad SDKs, credentials, or hidden outbound links.

Mitigation: Review and test external-request behavior, avoid supplying credentials, and keep generated prototypes free of analytics, ad SDKs, remote scripts, CDN assets, and hidden outbound links.

Risk: A runnable prototype can still have unresolved issues in accessibility, feel, visual quality, testing, or IP review.

Mitigation: Require a concise handoff with test evidence and unresolved human review items, and do not describe the prototype as release-ready solely because it runs.
