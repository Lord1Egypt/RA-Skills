## Description:

AI-readable gift, artist story, hours, event, and lead handoff surface for an artisan gallery pilot in Stowe, Vermont.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and shopping or travel agents use this skill to answer questions about Remarkable Things at Stowe Craft, suggest public gift categories, explain artist-story context, disclose freshness limits, and prepare consented lead handoff previews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business profile, hours, events, prices, and availability may be stale or unknown because the fixture is research-derived and not a live merchant export.

Mitigation: Disclose freshness and uncertainty when those details matter, and avoid claiming live stock, live hours, prices, events, or availability unless a resource explicitly supports the claim.

Risk: Users may mistake the skill for an official merchant-authorized system.

Mitigation: Present it as a public directory helper, not an official merchant system, paid recommendation surface, or verified merchant export.

Risk: Lead handoff previews can include user contact details or gift preferences.

Mitigation: Collect only user-approved details, prepare previews only after explicit consent, and do not submit, store, email, call, or send leads externally.
