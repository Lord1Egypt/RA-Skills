## Description:

Fore VIP Find Customers is a B2B lead-generation assistant that turns a product or service description into sourced downstream-market analysis, prospect lists, outreach drafts, and compliant channel recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[onsoul](https://clawhub.ai/user/onsoul)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales, growth, and business-development users use this skill to identify likely B2B customer segments, build sourced lead lists, draft compliant outreach, and choose alternative publishing channels when direct leads are scarce.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can send one-to-one marketing emails from the user's connected mailbox without a clear per-message approval step.

Mitigation: Review and confirm every recipient, source, subject, body, attachment, and embedded contact detail before any email is sent; use draft-only output when approval is not explicit.

Risk: Lead information and public contact details may be stale or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify each high-priority lead through the cited source, company website, or phone before outreach.

Risk: Lead generation and publishing workflows can create privacy, platform-policy, or advertising-compliance issues.

Mitigation: Use only public business information, avoid private personal data, follow platform placement rules for contact details, and avoid exaggerated or absolute advertising claims.
