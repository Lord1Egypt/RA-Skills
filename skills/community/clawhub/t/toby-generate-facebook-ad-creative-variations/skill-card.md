## Use Case:

Marketers, agencies, and growth teams use this skill to generate Facebook ad copy and image creative variations for campaign setup and A/B testing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broad shell and file-reading access that is not needed for ad creative generation.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation, run it in a constrained environment, or remove unnecessary Bash and file-read permissions.

Risk: Generated ad copy or creative directions may be inaccurate, misleading, or unsuitable for publication.

Mitigation: Have a human review and approve generated ad variations before external publication or automated campaign execution.
