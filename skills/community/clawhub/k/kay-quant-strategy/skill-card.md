## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated strategy code or backtest analysis could be mistaken for financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational support and require independent financial, data, and implementation review before real trading use.

Risk: Backtest results may overstate live performance because of overfitting, data quality issues, transaction costs, or slippage.

Mitigation: Validate assumptions, include realistic fees and slippage, test out of sample, and use simulated trading before considering live deployment.
