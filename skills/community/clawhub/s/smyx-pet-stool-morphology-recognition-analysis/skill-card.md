## Description:

Analyzes images or video of pet stool and returns structured observations of color, consistency, blood, or mucus, without diagnosing disease.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Pet owners and caretakers can use the skill to summarize visible stool characteristics from images or video and review observation reports. Results are for health reference and do not provide a diagnosis or treatment advice.
