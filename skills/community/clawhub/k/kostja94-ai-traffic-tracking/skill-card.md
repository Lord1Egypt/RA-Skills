## Description:

Guides tracking of AI-driven search traffic in Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and marketing analysts use this skill to separate AI-driven referrals and AI Overview activity from other GA4 and GSC traffic. It helps them create reports, channel groups, filters, and regex patterns for AI search analytics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested GA4 channel group, report, filter, or regex changes may affect analytics classification and reporting accuracy.

Mitigation: Review and test proposed changes in GA4 or GSC before applying them to production reporting.

Risk: Broad AI-source regex patterns may over-match or under-match referral sources as platforms change.

Mitigation: Compare the regex against observed session sources and adjust it before saving dashboards or channel groups.
