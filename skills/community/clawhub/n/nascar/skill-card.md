## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Predictions, rumors, contract speculation, and race-week commentary can become outdated or be mistaken for confirmed facts.

Mitigation: Treat these outputs as opinionated, time-sensitive analysis and verify current racing news, entry lists, penalties, contracts, and standings against authoritative current sources before relying on them.
