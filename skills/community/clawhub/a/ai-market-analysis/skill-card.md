## Description:

Provides macroeconomic, market strategy, fixed income, and sector analysis frameworks for agents answering market or industry-analysis questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[footballqq](https://clawhub.ai/user/footballqq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to route macro, market, sector, or listed-company questions to the relevant framework, then produce structured investment-research style analysis with assumptions, evidence, KPIs, valuation framing, catalysts, and risks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference files may be mislabeled or contain unrelated sector content, which can mislead market or investment analysis.

Mitigation: Review the selected reference file before relying on its framework, and verify sector-specific assumptions, KPIs, and examples against authoritative current sources.

Risk: The skill provides analysis frameworks rather than validated financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat generated outputs as research support and require human review before making investment or business decisions.
