## Description:

Checks academic paper titles for grammar correctness and clarity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[3492027682](https://clawhub.ai/user/3492027682)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and researchers use this skill to review academic paper titles for grammar, clarity, word density, sentence structure, subject-verb agreement, passive voice, abbreviation use, and redundant wording.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for general title wording questions where an academic paper title review was not intended.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants an academic paper title grammar and clarity review before applying the checklist.

Risk: Grammar and clarity suggestions may be subjective or may not match a target journal style.

Mitigation: Have the user review suggested title changes against the intended publication venue and disciplinary conventions.
