## Description:

Company research using Exa search. Finds company info, competitors, news, tweets, financials, LinkedIn profiles, builds company lists. Use when researching companies, doing competitor analysis, market research, or building company lists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and research teams use this skill to find company information, competitors, market signals, news, public social commentary, financial context, and LinkedIn profiles for company research and list building.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may move from Exa search into browser-based access when results are insufficient or content is gated, including logged-in LinkedIn or other authenticated access.

Mitigation: Prefer public-source research and require explicit user approval before any authenticated browser access.
