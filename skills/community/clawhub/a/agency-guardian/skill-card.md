## Description:

Gentle reminders to stay human while using AI. Reflection, not restriction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aranej](https://clawhub.ai/user/aranej)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to add occasional reflective prompts, optional boundary-awareness nudges, and short reminders that encourage independent judgment while using an AI assistant.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reflective prompts and sensitive-topic nudges may distract users who want direct assistance.

Mitigation: Disable boundary nudges when they are unhelpful, as recommended by the security guidance and artifact configuration.

Risk: Guidance around health, finances, or relationships could be mistaken for a restriction or substitute for the user's own judgment.

Mitigation: Keep nudges gentle and non-blocking, and use them to support information gathering for the user's decision.
