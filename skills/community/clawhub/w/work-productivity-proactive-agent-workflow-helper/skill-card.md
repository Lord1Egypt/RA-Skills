## Description:

Helps agent users and skill teams improve proactive workflows with practical plans, checklists, analysis, and implementation support for bugs, setup, safety, and reliability.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn a workflow or reliability need into a concise plan, reusable checklist, analysis, code change, or decision aid. It can help troubleshoot bugs, harden setup, or adapt an established workflow to an adjacent need.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad everyday trigger terms may activate the skill for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for work-productivity or proactive-agent workflows, or narrow its trigger terms to those contexts.
