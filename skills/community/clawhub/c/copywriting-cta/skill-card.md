## Description:

Design end-of-article CTAs for blog posts, newsletters, essays, articles, and other long-form content, including copy, structure, mechanism choice, A/B testing guidance, and accessibility review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samber](https://clawhub.ai/user/samber)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External writers, marketers, newsletter operators, and content teams use this skill to design, review, and improve end-of-article calls to action that fit the article context, reader relationship, funnel stage, and primary conversion goal.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests editing and agent tools even though the observed content is advisory.

Mitigation: Review proposed file changes and limit execution to drafting, critique, and recommendation tasks unless broader editing is intentionally needed.

Risk: Generated CTA recommendations may overstate conversion impact or select a mechanism that does not fit the reader relationship.

Mitigation: Validate the CTA against the article context, audience, and primary objective before publication, and use the included A/B testing and accessibility checks as review gates.
