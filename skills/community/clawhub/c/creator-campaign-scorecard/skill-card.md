## Description:

Evaluate creator campaign plans pre-launch to identify risks, gaps, and readiness across goals, offer, creator fit, operations, tracking, and compliance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Campaign operators use this skill to pressure-test creator, affiliate, and TikTok Shop campaign plans before launch. It turns campaign notes into a readiness scorecard with prioritized blockers, medium-risk gaps, fastest fixes, and a launch, revise, or hold recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat readiness scoring as legal, financial, or compliance advice.

Mitigation: Use the scorecard as operational guidance and route legal, financial, or compliance questions to qualified reviewers.

Risk: Incomplete campaign details can produce overconfident launch recommendations.

Mitigation: Collect the requested campaign goals, offer, creator fit, deliverables, tracking, budget, timeline, and approval constraints before relying on the final recommendation.
