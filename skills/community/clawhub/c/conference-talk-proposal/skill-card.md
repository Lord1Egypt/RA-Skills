## Description:

Write a conference talk proposal / CFP submission for a tech or developer conference. Use when asked to submit to a CFP, propose a talk, or write a session abstract. Produces a compelling title, abstract, audience takeaways, an outline, and the speaker pitch - tuned to what selection committees actually look for.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, technical speakers, and conference submitters use this skill to turn a talk idea into a complete CFP submission with title options, a public abstract, audience takeaways, a timed outline, organizer notes, and a speaker bio.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process professional background, project details, or unpublished talk ideas supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential or sensitive details unless appropriate for the target CFP, and review the generated proposal before submission.

Risk: A generated proposal can overstate evidence, speaker credibility, or fit for the target audience if the inputs are incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify the abstract, takeaways, outline, and organizer notes against the actual talk scope and speaker experience.
