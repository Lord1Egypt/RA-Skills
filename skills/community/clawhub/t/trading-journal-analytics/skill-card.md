## Use Case:

External traders and trading teams use this skill to record trades, review P&L and win-rate metrics, analyze strategy performance, and capture emotion and discipline patterns for systematic improvement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trading journals may contain sensitive trading history, P&L, screenshots, and emotional notes, especially when cloud backup or collaboration features are used.

Mitigation: Keep records local unless cloud features are intentionally enabled; verify the storage provider, authentication, sharing permissions, encryption, and deletion controls before syncing or collaborating.
