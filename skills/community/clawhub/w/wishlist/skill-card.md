## Description:

Build a personal wishlist system for capturing wants, tracking prices, and smart purchasing decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to maintain a personal wishlist, track purchase priorities and budgets, and review price changes before buying.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Wishlist files may contain sensitive personal preferences, budgets, purchase intent, or store links.

Mitigation: Review the contents of ~/wishlist before sharing, syncing, or backing up the folder.

Risk: Price checking may send wishlist item details to external stores or search sources.

Mitigation: Use price checking only for items appropriate to disclose to those services and review configured preferred stores.
