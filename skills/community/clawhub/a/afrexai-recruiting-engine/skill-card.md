## Use Case:

Recruiting teams and hiring managers use this skill to create role blueprints, sourcing plans, outreach drafts, resume screening rubrics, interview scorecards, offer materials, and pipeline reports. It is intended to structure recruiting work while keeping final hiring decisions and sensitive communications under human approval.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can process and score resumes and candidate notes, which may involve sensitive personal data.

Mitigation: Use only approved recruiting data, keep candidate records in authorized HR or ATS systems, and define deletion or retention rules for resumes and notes.

Risk: Automated screening and scoring can influence candidate advancement or rejection without sufficient human oversight.

Mitigation: Require human review before rejecting or advancing candidates and document evidence-based reasons for decisions.

Risk: Candidate research and evaluation may introduce protected-characteristic inferences or unfair criteria.

Mitigation: Avoid protected-characteristic inferences, use structured scorecards, and review rejection patterns for bias.
