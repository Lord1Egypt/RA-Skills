## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Valuation ranges, market conditions, and comparable-company benchmarks can become outdated or misleading.

Mitigation: Verify current market data, transaction comparables, and company-specific assumptions before relying on the analysis.

Risk: Term-sheet, legal, financial, or investment guidance may be incomplete for a specific deal context.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as a general framework and obtain qualified legal and financial review before acting on investment decisions.
