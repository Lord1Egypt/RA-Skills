# System Instance: The Center Where All Parts Act Together

Every OpenXSys run works around a runnable system instance. Prompts, methods, and conversations are resources the instance uses. An instance may be a company, technology, project, codebase, or a person's work; it may also be a capability, a relation, or a higher-level system temporarily formed from several systems.

## Minimum semantics

When needed, a system instance can answer:

- **Identity and purpose**: what it is, whom it exists for, and what this run wants to change;
- **Boundary and scale**: what is inside now, what remains outside for now, and how long the observation spans;
- **State and understanding**: what reality is doing, what has been observed, how the model explains it, and what remains unknown;
- **Composition and relations**: which parts, flows, constraints, and feedback loops determine behavior;
- **Capabilities and resources**: what it can do now, through whom, with which tools, and under which conditions;
- **Ownership and authority**: who owns state, works, and decisions, and who may observe, act, correct, or exit;
- **Works and actions**: which understanding must reach a person and which changes must enter reality;
- **Feedback and continuity**: how effects are confirmed, where results return, and where the next run begins.

These are semantics for AI judgment. A simple system may live entirely in one natural-language entry. As complexity grows, works, data, practices, and subsystems can differentiate naturally.

## One instance can take many forms

The same object becomes a different system for different people and purposes. A codebase is a system of dependencies, tests, and changes to a maintainer, and a system of understanding and successful execution to a new user. A company does not have the same useful boundary for an operator and a job candidate. See [how different systems can be](shapes.md).

## Active picture and persistent entry

Stable instance semantics are defined here and in [state](state.md), [ownership](ownership.md), [composition](composition.md), and [continuity](continuity.md). Each run activates the relevant parts through [runtime composition](../runtime/compose.md). `SYSTEM.md` is a natural-language entry that people and AI can understand, correct, and carry.
