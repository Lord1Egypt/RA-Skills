## Description:

Builds a repeatable narrative enablement kit from an approved canon, including an elevator ladder, spokesperson Q&A, boilerplate/bio pack, and do/don't language sheet for sales, support, founders, and partners.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, sales, support, founder, and partner teams use this skill to turn an existing narrative canon into repeatable spokesperson enablement materials. It helps keep elevator pitches, Q&A answers, boilerplate, bios, and language guidance aligned with approved claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may propose or save persistent narrative and claim records even though its manifest advertises read-oriented capabilities.

Mitigation: Install only if persistent narrative and claim writes are acceptable, and require explicit confirmation before each durable write.

Risk: Unapproved claims could be repeated in spokesperson materials if the canon or claims ledger is incomplete.

Mitigation: Keep unapproved claims labeled [needs source] and route them for claim review before the kit is used by spokespeople.
