## Use Case:

External users, marketers, event organizers, hospitality teams, and developers use this skill to choose QR payload types, sizing, contrast, error correction, and deployment patterns before creating or publishing QR codes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: WiFi QR codes can disclose network passwords to anyone who scans or sees the code.

Mitigation: Use WiFi QR patterns only for intended guest or public networks, avoid sensitive internal networks, and rotate the password when access should end.

Risk: Incorrect sizing, contrast, or over-customization can make printed QR codes fail to scan.

Mitigation: Follow the sizing, contrast, and error-correction guidance, then test each QR code with multiple phones in the target environment before deployment.
