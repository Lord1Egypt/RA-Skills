## Description:

Research and write a fair, evidence-backed alternatives page for readers considering replacements for a named product. Use when a user asks for "X alternatives," "alternatives to X," "best X competitors," a replacement guide, or a migration-focused commercial page. The skill verifies switching reasons, product fit, current pricing, limitations, and publisher relationships instead of producing a generic affiliate list. Rankizz keyword and SERP data is optional; current product research is required.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External writers, marketers, and product teams use this skill to research and write alternatives pages that help readers decide whether to switch products and which qualified replacement fits their needs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outdated or unverified product details and pricing could lead readers to make a poor switching decision.

Mitigation: Check current official product sources, normalize pricing assumptions, date the research, and label inaccessible or unverified terms clearly.

Risk: Commercial relationships or uneven comparison criteria could bias recommendations.

Mitigation: Disclose ownership and affiliate relationships near affected recommendations, use the same criteria for every candidate, and explain when readers should stay with the incumbent.
