## 4. Risk Advisory

### 4.1 Sensitive Topics (Handle with Care)

| Topic | Risk | Recommended Approach |
|------|------|-------------|
| Competitor Comparison | May be interpreted as malicious disparagement | State facts objectively, cite third-party evaluations |
| Price Negotiation | Revealing bottom line too early | Confirm scope first, then discuss price |
| Technical Weaknesses | Loss of trust | Honest admission + alternative solution |
| Past Failed Projects | Capability questioned | Prepare "lessons learned and improvements" narrative |
