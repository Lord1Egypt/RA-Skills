## Description:

Create a calm seasonal closet swap checklist for rotating clothes, shoes, accessories, storage bins, repairs, weather readiness, and reset tasks without donation pressure or valuation advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this prompt-only skill to create a practical, low-pressure checklist for rotating seasonal clothing, storing off-season items, checking condition, labeling bins, and planning small follow-up tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include personal closet or clothing details they do not want reflected in the generated checklist.

Mitigation: Ask only for practical planning details and avoid including personal details the user does not want reflected in the output.

Risk: The checklist could drift into pressure to donate, discard, sell, assign value, or judge clothing choices if the skill boundary is not followed.

Mitigation: Keep choices user-led and reversible, using neutral holding options such as repair, fit check, sentimental, or decide later.
