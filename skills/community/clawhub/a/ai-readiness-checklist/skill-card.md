## Description:

Conducts a 15-question AI readiness assessment, scores key adoption dimensions, and delivers a prioritized, actionable report for enterprise AI deployment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[filipbl4gojevic](https://clawhub.ai/user/filipbl4gojevic)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business leaders, AI program owners, and governance teams use this skill to assess organizational AI readiness, identify adoption blockers, and prioritize concrete actions before scaling AI deployment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may include Resomnium branding and a consulting contact link when the bundled report template is used.

Mitigation: Review generated reports before sharing them and remove or replace branding and contact links when they are not appropriate for the audience.

Risk: Assessment answers may contain confidential organizational details in the chat context.

Mitigation: Use sanitized or non-sensitive organizational information unless the user is comfortable sharing the details in the agent session.
