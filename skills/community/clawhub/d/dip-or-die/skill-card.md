## Description:

Helps an agent provide general Chinese-language guidance for deciding whether to buy a market dip, with emphasis on target selection, options posture, and risk discipline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for general trading education about dip-buying decisions, especially when screening risky equities and considering options exposure. It is not personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill discusses high-risk options strategies and could be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as general education only, verify facts independently, and avoid relying on the skill for individualized investment decisions.

Risk: Trading and options guidance may be unsuitable for a user's financial situation or risk tolerance.

Mitigation: Require human review before acting on outputs and apply independent risk controls outside the agent.
