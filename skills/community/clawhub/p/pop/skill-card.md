## Description:

查询公司、供应商、POP商家及对接商的基础信息、董监高，及订单量级、收入、成本、毛利、利润、毛保、返利、净毛利、损益及排名等经营财务数据；注意：该SOP侧重查询毛利/利润情况，非销售数据。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business analysts and authorized employees use this skill to query company, supplier, POP merchant, and partner business records, then summarize gross profit, net margin, rebates, income, costs, order volume, profit/loss, and operating rankings for a named company.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may surface confidential company, supplier, POP merchant, or partner financial data.

Mitigation: Install and use it only in workspaces where the user is authorized to query and handle those internal financial tables.

Risk: Business decisions could be affected by incomplete or misread financial query results.

Mitigation: Review the returned metrics, table coverage, and ranking logic before using the output for operational decisions.
