## Description:

Review, verify, undo, redo, and safely recover Subclip video edits using rendered frame inspection, project state, native history, and saved revisions. Use after multi-step edits or when the user reports a visual, timing, caption, asset, undo, or project-state problem.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Video editors and agents use this skill to inspect Subclip projects, verify timeline and rendered-frame state, correct visible defects, and safely recover edits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Restoring a saved revision replaces the current saved project state.

Mitigation: Review project history and confirm the exact revision before restoring; the displaced state is preserved in history.
