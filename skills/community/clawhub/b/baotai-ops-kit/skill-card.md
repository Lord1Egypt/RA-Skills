## Description:

Generates Chinese-language marketing and operations drafts for the Liu Guozhong Pregnancy Preservation Center, including brand copy, social posts, public-account articles, short video scripts, customer service scripts, activity plans, poster copy, doctor IP content, community operations content, and press releases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External healthcare marketing and operations staff for Tianjin Kunru Mary Women's and Children's Hospital's Liu Guozhong Pregnancy Preservation Center use this skill to draft channel-specific Chinese content from a center profile, content templates, and brand voice guidance. Generated material is intended as review-ready draft copy, not medical advice or a substitute for clinical, legal, or advertising-compliance review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated healthcare marketing or operations copy may contain statements that need medical, legal, or advertising-compliance review before publication or patient use.

Mitigation: Treat all generated material as draft copy and require human medical, legal, and advertising-compliance review before publishing or using it with patients.

Risk: Patient case content could expose sensitive information or imply guaranteed treatment outcomes if reviewed carelessly.

Mitigation: Use the skill's case anonymization, no-guarantee, no-specific-dose, and no-absolute-claims guardrails, then have a human reviewer confirm privacy and claims compliance.
