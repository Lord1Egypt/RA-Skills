## Description:

Tech Tutor helps users build deep understanding of computer science and AI topics through Socratic guidance, project deconstruction, and learning records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leogoat2004](https://clawhub.ai/user/leogoat2004)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, students, and technical learners use Tech Tutor to explore CS and AI concepts or deconstruct codebases through guided questions, authoritative sources, and practice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning records may contain confidential code context or sensitive personal notes if those topics are discussed.

Mitigation: Choose the record location deliberately, avoid sensitive inputs, and delete or exclude .tech-tutor records when needed.

Risk: Installations from a mutable command can drift from the reviewed release.

Mitigation: Use a pinned installer or reviewed commit before production or commercial use.
