## Description:

Route a technology-intelligence request to a technology landscape, invention-mining workflow, or current-intelligence briefing, and produce an evidence-backed English HTML report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and technology, R&D, IP, and strategy teams use this skill to route emerging-technology requests into landscape analysis, invention mining, or current-intelligence briefing. It supports evidence-backed reporting for AI, semiconductors, displays, electronics, materials, and related engineering domains.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may retain PatSnap-branded wording that could imply PatSnap authorship or endorsement in neutral or client-facing deliverables.

Mitigation: Review and explicitly approve, remove, or adapt PatSnap branding before delivery.

Risk: Technology-intelligence reports can be misleading if factual claims, patent counts, charts, or opportunity labels are not grounded in current reviewed evidence.

Mitigation: Require source IDs, exact query details, counting units, evidence cutoff dates, and visible limitations for every material finding.

Risk: Patentability, infringement, freedom-to-operate, validity, claim scope, or legal-status conclusions may be mistaken if treated as agent determinations.

Mitigation: Keep legal topics as evidence-backed questions and route material patent decisions to qualified patent professionals and official registers.
