## Description:

Generates Nevada-focused marketing assets for business bankruptcy attorneys, including client acquisition campaigns, digital ads, website content, schema, review responses, and referral materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitflopez](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitflopez)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External legal marketers and Nevada business bankruptcy law firms use this skill to draft attorney advertising, intake campaigns, website copy, structured data, reputation responses, and referral program materials for Chapter 11, Subchapter V, Chapter 7 business liquidation, and creditor-rights services. Outputs should be reviewed by a Nevada attorney or qualified compliance reviewer before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Nevada, United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill generates regulated attorney advertising and public review responses that may create legal advertising or review-management risk.

Mitigation: Have a Nevada attorney or qualified compliance reviewer approve all outputs before publication.

Risk: Some templates may contradict the skill's compliance promises, including review-gating language or public responses that imply client status or matter details.

Mitigation: Correct review request language, remove client-status and matter-specific public response content, and keep responses generic.

Risk: Bankruptcy thresholds, disclosures, and attorney-advertising rules can change.

Mitigation: Verify current legal thresholds, required bankruptcy disclosures, and Nevada attorney advertising rules before use.
