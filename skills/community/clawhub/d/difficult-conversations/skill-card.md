## Description:

Frameworks and scripts for having hard conversations, including confronting a boss, asking for a raise, setting boundaries, discussing money with a partner, giving difficult feedback, or addressing avoided situations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to prepare for difficult interpersonal conversations by identifying the scenario, planning facts and tone, drafting opening lines, and rehearsing responses to defensiveness, deflection, anger, or silence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The release security summary says the skill requests filesystem access without explaining why.

Mitigation: Install and run the skill in a restricted environment, and avoid granting filesystem access unless the publisher documents the required paths and purpose.

Risk: The release security summary flags an unpinned npx installer.

Mitigation: Use a pinned, trusted installer version or otherwise verify the installer package before installation.

Risk: Conversation coaching can be inappropriate when a situation involves threats, abuse, retaliation, legal exposure, or medical concerns.

Mitigation: Prioritize safety planning and refer the user to appropriate professional, legal, workplace, medical, or emergency resources when the conversation context indicates elevated risk.
