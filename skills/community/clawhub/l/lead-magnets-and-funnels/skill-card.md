## Description:

Designs lead magnets and social-to-capture-to-nurture funnels that turn social traffic into subscribers, qualified leads, and customers using the MAGNET framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketers, creators, and business operators use this skill to plan offer-tied lead magnets, landing copy, short forms, delivery paths, nurture sequences, and social promo posts. It helps define the funnel while external landing, form, quiz, ESP, GA4, and WoopSocial tools handle implementation, publishing, and measurement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Funnel recommendations may include inaccurate benchmarks, fabricated performance metrics, or unsupported social proof if not reviewed.

Mitigation: Use real metrics from external tools and GA4, label benchmarks as directional where appropriate, and verify claims before publishing.

Risk: Email capture and nurture flows can create consent, unsubscribe, or advertising compliance issues.

Mitigation: Use clear opt-in consent, visible unsubscribe paths, no bought lists, and review GDPR, CAN-SPAM, FTC, and local requirements before launch.

Risk: The skill designs funnel assets but does not implement or secure external ESP, quiz, landing-page, analytics, or WoopSocial integrations.

Mitigation: Review each external integration separately before connecting accounts, publishing posts, or collecting lead data.
