This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Video editors and creators use this skill to create readable text overlays, correct and style captions, and manage transcription and speaker assignments in Subclip.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cloud transcription may send sensitive audio off-device without an upfront warning or confirmation.

Mitigation: Before choosing cloud transcription, explain that audio may be processed off-device and confirm the creator is comfortable with it.
