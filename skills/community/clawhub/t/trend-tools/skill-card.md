## Description:

Trend Tools helps agents explain support and resistance, trendlines, channels, retracement zones, speed lines, price gaps, and reversal days for technical analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to get Chinese-first educational technical-analysis guidance for chart levels, trendline breaks, retracement zones, gaps, and reversal-day signals. It is not intended to provide automatic trading instructions or financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could treat educational chart analysis as financial advice or automatic trading instructions.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as educational technical-analysis guidance and avoid directing trades, position sizes, or automated execution.

Risk: Trendlines, support and resistance, reversal days, and gap classifications can be subjective or premature.

Mitigation: Require confirmation signals, note false-break filters such as percentage or time filters where relevant, and explain uncertainty in the analysis.
