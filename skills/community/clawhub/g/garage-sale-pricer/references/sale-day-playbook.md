# Sale-Day Playbook

## The week before
- **Pricing night:** tag EVERYTHING (mystery-priced tables get skipped). Tags carry the day ladder: `$12 / after Sat noon $6`.
- **Schedule the donation pickup for Saturday 4–6 PM NOW** — the #1 post-sale failure mode is leftovers living in the garage for six more months. Salvation Army/VVA/GreenDrop pick up; otherwise load the car and book the drop-off slot.
- **Check the rules:** city sign ordinances (many ban signs on utility poles; permits required in some towns), HOA notice requirements, multi-family rules.
- **Friday night:** list the 3–5 hot items (tools, bikes, gaming, brand kids' gear) on marketplace with "garage sale tomorrow 7 AM, first come" — pulls serious traffic, and pre-sale offers set a floor on your pricing.

## Saturday timeline
| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 6:00 | Tables out (they're heavier than you remember) |
| 6:30 | Everything displayed; float in fanny pack; signs UP (balloons on each) |
| 7:00 | Open. **7:00–8:30 is 40% of revenue** — pros and neighbors; don't still be carrying boxes |
| 9:30–11:30 | Family traffic peak; this is when kids' stuff and toys move |
| 12:30 | Day-ladder discounts start (they're on the tags) |
| 2:00 | Close. Signs down IMMEDIATELY (fines + afternoon no-shows hammering your door) |
| 3:30 | Curb-alert the free pile; 4–6 PM donation pickup |

## Logistics details that pay for themselves
- **Float** (from the plan command): ~$130 for a mid-size sale — ones are king; get it at the bank FRIDAY.
- **Payment:** cash box is a fanny pack (never leave the box on a table); large bills walked into the house hourly; **Venmo/Zele QR code taped to every table** — 20–30% of buyers carry no cash at all.
- **Display:** waist-height tables, everything visible, nothing in bins to dig; clothes on RACKS not piles (2× price power); power strip + outlet available to demo electronics;Extension cord run before 6:30.
- **Free pile at the curb** — it's bait and it self-serves.
- **Price psychology:** round numbers only ($2, $5, $10 — never $3.50); colored-dot "everything red-dot is $1" tables run fast at big sales; big-ticket items get a typed card with details (works, dimensions, from-pet-free-home).
- **Helpers:** one person on the money, one floating to answer and wrap; solo sales get robbed by chaos, not people.

## Safety
- High-value small items (jewelry, games, tools) near the money station, in sight.
- Don't let anyone "test" electronics inside your house; run the cord outside.
- Garage door open, house locked; no buyers indoors — period.
- $100-bill at 7:10 AM is a counterfeit favorite — test the strip or decline politely.

## After
- Leftover routing (the `leftovers` command): consign furniture/power/tools worth ≥$50 band, donate clothes/books/housewares, curb-alert the sub-$10s, marketplace the sleepers.
- Cancel the marketplace listings that didn't sell in the parking lot, or the afternoon is messages.
- Count & record the take per table category once — next sale's pricing gets 2× better.
