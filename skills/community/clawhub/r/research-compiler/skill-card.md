## Description:

Guides agents through a Chinese-language, five-phase workflow for compiling scientific research project outcomes into structured reports and a final Word document.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[innocencexy](https://clawhub.ai/user/innocencexy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, academic staff, and agents assisting them use this skill to organize project materials, define an outcome framework, draft individual sections with sourced support, merge the work, and prepare a Word deliverable.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Web-supported drafting can introduce outdated, weakly sourced, or misattributed material into research outputs.

Mitigation: Require source links or citations for externally supported claims and review references before merging sections.

Risk: The workflow can compile user-provided project materials into a final Word document, including sensitive unpublished research content.

Mitigation: Confirm sharing permissions and remove confidential content before exporting or distributing the document.

Risk: The skill is optimized for Chinese academic research-output compilation and may not match other languages or simpler one-off summary tasks.

Mitigation: State the desired language, scope, and web-research limits before invoking the workflow.
