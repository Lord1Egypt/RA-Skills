## Description:

Categorises UK business expenses and invoices against HMRC Self Assessment categories, producing quarterly P&L summaries, VAT-ready reports, and MTD-compatible breakdowns from raw transactions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users such as UK sole traders, freelancers, self-employed contractors, small business owners, and accountants use this skill to categorise expenses for Self Assessment, VAT preparation, MTD quarterly reporting, and bookkeeping review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United Kingdom

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste bank statements or financial records containing unnecessary personal or account data.

Mitigation: Redact bank details, account identifiers, and irrelevant personal data before using the skill.

Risk: Expense categorisation may be incorrect or incomplete for mixed-use, capital, VAT, or borderline tax items.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as bookkeeping support and verify classifications with an accountant before filing or submitting tax information.
