## Use Case:

External users, medical learners, clinicians, researchers, educators, and healthcare professionals use this skill for medical education, clinical reasoning support, research critique, patient-facing explanation, and scope-aware healthcare communication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical output could be mistaken for a patient-specific diagnosis or treatment plan.

Mitigation: Use outputs as informational support only and verify patient-specific decisions with qualified clinicians.

Risk: Medication details, dosing, and clinical guidelines may be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Check current authoritative clinical guidelines and pharmacy resources before acting on medication or management information.

Risk: The skill cannot examine a patient or integrate clinical gestalt.

Mitigation: Escalate urgent symptoms and use clinician assessment for diagnosis, workup, and treatment decisions.
