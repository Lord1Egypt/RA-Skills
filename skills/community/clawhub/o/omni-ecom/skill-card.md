## Description:

Omni Ecom Skill coordinates a Chinese-language e-commerce expert team for brand operations diagnosis, weekly or monthly reviews, platform growth work, ad ROI and profit optimization, content and livestream analysis, and action-plan delivery without fabricating data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External business operators, brand owners, and e-commerce teams use this skill to analyze Chinese marketplace performance, diagnose cross-platform issues, and produce structured recommendations, reports, and action lists. It is intended for experienced e-commerce users who provide the business reports, platform exports, and operational context they want analyzed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce incorrect business conclusions if source reports are incomplete, outdated, or inconsistent.

Mitigation: Provide current platform exports or official backend data, and require the agent to mark missing information as data insufficient.

Risk: The skill may default to Chinese-language output or activate on broad e-commerce planning requests.

Mitigation: Specify the desired response language and narrow the prompt to the platform, metric, report, or decision being analyzed.

Risk: Business reports and platform data may contain sensitive operational information.

Mitigation: Share only the reports, advertising data, product data, and meeting notes needed for the analysis.
