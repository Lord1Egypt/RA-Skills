## Description:

Chinese-language creative assistant for brainstorming, naming suggestions, and concept design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiangtaoid](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiangtaoid)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creative practitioners use this skill to generate structured Chinese-language brainstorming directions, naming options, slogans, and campaign or product concept plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad Chinese activation phrases may trigger the skill in conversations where a narrower creative workflow was intended.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation wording and confirm the requested creative mode before relying on the output.

Risk: The skill is Chinese-first and may not suit English-first or bilingual workflows.

Mitigation: Set language expectations before use or pair it with review by a Chinese-speaking user.

Risk: Generated naming and campaign ideas may be unsuitable, unavailable, or misleading for real-world brand use.

Mitigation: Review outputs for audience fit, factual claims, trademarks, and local business requirements before deployment.
