## Description:

B2B LinkedIn ghostwriting skill that guides agents through a strategic interview, hook engineering, and post-body drafting for founder, executive, and B2B social content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samber](https://clawhub.ai/user/samber)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, marketers, founders, and executives use this skill to turn business stories, results, and insights into structured LinkedIn content. The skill asks for metrics and context first, then produces hook options and concise post copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated LinkedIn copy may include inaccurate, exaggerated, or non-public business claims if the user provides weak or unverified source material.

Mitigation: Review generated copy before posting and use only metrics, customer claims, and outcomes that are accurate and publicly claimable.

Risk: The workflow asks for business results, audiences, CTAs, and specific metrics that may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Provide only information approved for marketing use and remove confidential customer, revenue, or strategy details before sharing.

Risk: The skill may route a draft through a separate humanizer-style skill if one is available.

Mitigation: Review any follow-on rewrite for factual preservation, approved tone, and unchanged hook intent before publishing.
