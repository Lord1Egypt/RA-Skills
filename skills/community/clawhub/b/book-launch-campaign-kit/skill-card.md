## Description:

Create a reusable launch system for an English book or digital guide, including positioning, posters, promo videos, Amazon/Kindle conversion copy, people-holding-book ad concepts, polished campaign videos, and ready-to-publish launch packs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jinhuadeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/jinhuadeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors, marketers, and agent operators use this skill to turn a book cover and core hook into a repeatable English launch campaign with posters, promo videos, Amazon metadata, and folderized launch packs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The scaffold script creates folders and starter Markdown files in the target directory.

Mitigation: Choose and review the target directory before running the scaffold command.

Risk: The workflow is English- and Amazon-focused by default.

Mitigation: Tell the agent when a different language, marketplace, or publishing platform is required.

Risk: People-holding-book ad concepts could imply an unauthorized celebrity endorsement if exact real people are requested.

Mitigation: Use celebrity-level energy without depicting exact real celebrity endorsement unless legal authorization is confirmed.
