## Description:

Rental Management provides an end-to-end Taiwan property rental management SOP covering tenant sourcing, screening, lease signing, inspections, maintenance, rent collection, disputes, and renewal or termination for self-managed and agency-managed rentals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Landlords, property managers, and rental operations teams in Taiwan use this skill to run rental workflows, prepare checklists and guidance, and calculate rental-property return reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Taiwan

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tenant-screening guidance may create privacy, credit-reporting, or anti-discrimination risk when collecting identity, income, credit, or applicant background information.

Mitigation: Verify applicable Taiwan privacy, rental, credit-reporting, and anti-discrimination requirements; avoid nationality-based screening; collect only necessary documents; prefer visual verification over retaining ID copies or ID photos; secure records; and define deletion timelines.

Risk: Operational rental guidance may be applied as legal or compliance advice without local review.

Mitigation: Review workflows, notices, screening criteria, lease steps, and dispute actions with qualified local counsel or compliance staff before use in real tenant decisions.
