## Description:

Coordinates a reusable content workflow from topic or draft through research, review, platform adaptation, image planning and generation, publish checks, and reusable asset capture for WeChat, Zhihu, Xiaohongshu, Zhihu Idea, blog, and multi-platform content projects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, developers, and content operators use this skill to coordinate multi-platform Chinese content production, review, image generation, handoff, URL preparation, and final publication gates across several Wenchang skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated publish assets can affect public content or external accounts if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Review generated publish assets before approving uploads, URL rewrites, Computer Use handoff, or final publication.

Risk: The workflow may reach stages involving image plans, paid generation, sensitive supplied assets, or external prompt submission.

Mitigation: Pause for explicit human confirmation before paid or sensitive generation, Computer Use prompt submission, reference upload, or platform publishing.
