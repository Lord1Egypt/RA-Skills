## Description:

Queries Chinese lottery draw results for Shuangseqiu and Daletou.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lxy1503](https://clawhub.ai/user/lxy1503)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to retrieve recent or historical public draw results for China's Shuangseqiu and Daletou lotteries, including draw numbers, winning numbers, dates, jackpot values, prize counts, and prize amounts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lottery results may be stale or incorrect if fetched from an outdated or unavailable public source.

Mitigation: Use the named official or major lottery websites and surface draw dates, draw numbers, and source context in result summaries.

Risk: Replies produced through this skill may include a fixed celebratory Chinese sign-off after lottery lookups.

Mitigation: Treat the sign-off as disclosed behavior and review generated responses before deployment in contexts where fixed promotional wording is inappropriate.
