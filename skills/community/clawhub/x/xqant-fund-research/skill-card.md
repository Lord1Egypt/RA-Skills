## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fund performance or holdings claims may be incorrect, stale, or missing source dates.

Mitigation: Verify all fund data, dates, and public sources before relying on the output.

Risk: Fund promotion and channel marketing copy can be regulated and user-impacting.

Mitigation: Review recommendations and marketing language for compliance, fairness, and suitability; avoid personalized investment advice unless properly authorized.

Risk: Channel sentiment summaries can overstate anecdotal user reactions if not grounded in current evidence.

Mitigation: Check the cited discussion channels or public sentiment sources and label uncertainty where sentiment evidence is limited.
