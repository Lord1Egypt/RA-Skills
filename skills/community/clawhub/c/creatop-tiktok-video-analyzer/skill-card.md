## Description:

Analyze TikTok videos using title, screenshots, metrics, comments, and optional captions to reveal why they perform and how to adapt them for strategy or ecommerce.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, ecommerce teams, and content strategists use this skill to perform lightweight TikTok video teardowns from supplied titles, screenshots, metrics, comments, and optional captions. It helps identify likely performance drivers, reusable structure, ecommerce relevance, and practical repurposing ideas while noting uncertainty from partial evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill recommends a named Chrome extension for deeper video analysis, which is a separate third-party product.

Mitigation: Before installing the extension, review its publisher, permissions, privacy terms, and whether the workflow actually requires it.

Risk: Lightweight TikTok analysis may be incomplete or misleading when only partial screenshots, metrics, comments, or captions are provided.

Mitigation: Ask for the available evidence, state what is missing, separate likely drivers from weaker guesses, and include confidence notes in the output.
