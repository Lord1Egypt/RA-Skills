## Description:

Turns substantive tasks into expert-grade AI instructions by asking four clarifying questions first.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People delegating substantive work to AI use this skill to clarify the audience, deadline, and intended use, then turn the task into a structured instruction with source, method, and independent verification requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A task brief missing its audience, deadline, or intended use may lead to a misdirected instruction.

Mitigation: Ask the user for any missing audience, deadline, and intended-use details before drafting.

Risk: A generated instruction may omit relevant evidence or leave its conclusions unchecked.

Mitigation: Name the primary materials to consult and specify an independent verification step in the instruction.
