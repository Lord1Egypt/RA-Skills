## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the skill for generic reply-writing or emotional-support prompts.

Mitigation: Ask for missing context before drafting sensitive replies and make clear when the response is conversation coaching rather than authoritative advice.

Risk: Users may share sensitive personal details while seeking companionship or relationship advice.

Mitigation: Avoid requesting or retaining private information, and encourage users to provide only the context needed for the conversation task.

Risk: Emotional-support use could be mistaken for mental-health, legal, medical, or other professional advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses within communication support, avoid diagnosis or professional guidance, and direct serious emotional or professional issues to qualified help.

Risk: Role-play and social-script generation could be used to pressure, deceive, or harm others.

Mitigation: Follow the artifact's boundaries by refusing harmful or illegal advice and keeping suggestions respectful of healthy relationship boundaries.
