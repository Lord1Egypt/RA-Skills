## Description:

Analyze and optimize product or service pricing using value-based, cost-plus, competitive, and tiered models with actionable recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SaaS founders, agency owners, product managers, and other teams use this skill to evaluate pricing, model tiers, compare competitors, estimate willingness to pay, and generate advisory revenue-impact recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business-sensitive pricing, cost, volume, or competitor data may be included in prompts or outputs.

Mitigation: Provide only information suitable for AI-assisted analysis and handle generated outputs according to the user's internal data policies.

Risk: Generated pricing recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable as final business decisions.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as advisory and review them with relevant business, finance, legal, or market stakeholders before adoption.
