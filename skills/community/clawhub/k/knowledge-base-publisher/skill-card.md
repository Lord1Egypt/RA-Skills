## Description:

Organize, format, and publish raw notes, meeting transcripts, and scattered content into structured, publication-ready knowledge-base entries with metadata, cross-references, and version tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and documentation teams use this skill to turn raw Markdown, text, or transcript content into organized knowledge-base articles with consistent metadata, tags, cross-references, catalogs, and changelog tracking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can create or update multiple files in a chosen output directory.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated target folder and review generated articles, metadata, and changelog entries before publishing.

Risk: Generated summaries, tags, and cross-references may be incorrect or misleading.

Mitigation: Review generated content against the source material and taxonomy before publishing it elsewhere.
