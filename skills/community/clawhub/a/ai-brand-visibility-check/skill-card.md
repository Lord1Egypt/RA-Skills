## Description:

Check whether AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini recommend a brand, product, or company for category queries such as "best X".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketing, GEO, and competitive-research users use this skill to check whether a brand appears in live AI-assistant recommendations and to gather competitor context for reporting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Agents may contact an external service with brand or company names and may request paid x402 USDC calls.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when a live AI-brand-visibility check is desired, review disclosed pricing before payment, and avoid automatic broad marketing runs.
