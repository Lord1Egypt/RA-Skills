## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Example component values, RF dimensions, and engineering targets may not match a specific device, PCB stackup, frequency band, regulatory requirement, or current datasheet.

Mitigation: Verify values against current component datasheets, board-fabrication constraints, safety requirements, simulations, and lab measurements before using them in real hardware.

Risk: Applying RF design or debugging guidance directly to hardware can affect reliability, EMI/EMC compliance, thermal behavior, or test safety.

Mitigation: Have qualified RF engineers review the design, use calibrated instruments and controlled lab procedures, and run full validation before production or field deployment.
