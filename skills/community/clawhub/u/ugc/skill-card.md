## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Review generated campaigns for brand fit, advertising disclosures, platform rules, and legal compliance before publishing.

Risk: UGC trends and platform norms can change quickly, so trend-specific recommendations may become stale.

Mitigation: Validate trend, sound, and platform-specific recommendations against current campaign context before use.
