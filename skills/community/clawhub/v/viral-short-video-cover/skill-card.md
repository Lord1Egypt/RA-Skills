## Description:

根据用户提供的封面文案生成高点击率的中文竖版短视频封面。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and social media operators use this skill to turn Chinese short-video cover copy into a vertical, high-contrast cover image suitable for mobile feeds. It is intended for cover generation, not general image editing or title rewriting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private photos or sensitive text may be processed during image generation when supplied as cover inputs.

Mitigation: Avoid providing private photos or sensitive text unless the user is comfortable having that content processed for image generation.

Risk: Generated cover text may contain incorrect Chinese characters, low contrast, unwanted extra text, or composition issues.

Mitigation: Review the generated image for text accuracy, readability, cropping, and unwanted additions; use targeted regeneration for concrete defects.
