## Description:

A critical reading skill that helps users separate signal from hype in crypto news headlines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to review crypto headlines or pasted article text, separate facts from interpretation, and identify hype, missing context, and conflicts of interest before reacting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the analysis for investment advice or a live market/news check.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as critical reading guidance only and verify current market, regulatory, and source context before making decisions.

Risk: The skill depends on user-provided headlines or pasted text, so incomplete or biased inputs can produce incomplete analysis.

Mitigation: Provide the full article text or multiple sources when possible and review the verdict against the original source material.
