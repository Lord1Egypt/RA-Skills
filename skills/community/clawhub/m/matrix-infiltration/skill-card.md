## Description:

Analyzes community conversations to map emotional climate, audience archetypes, resistance patterns, and likely skepticism vectors around $NEURONS.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, community managers, and risk reviewers use this skill to analyze authorized social posts, threads, or chat logs for sentiment dynamics, resistance points, and how skepticism may spread so they can improve defensive communication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can identify social weaknesses and exploitation points in real communities.

Mitigation: Use it only for authorized, defensive discourse-risk analysis on appropriately redacted data; do not use outputs to manipulate, pressure, or target people or communities.

Risk: Reports may contain sensitive assessments of communities, participants, or adoption barriers.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as sensitive, limit access to reviewers with a legitimate need, and remove personal data before analysis.

Risk: The artifact includes safety limits against deception, impersonation, fraud, and harassment.

Mitigation: Keep those limits active during use and review generated strategies for compliance before taking action.
