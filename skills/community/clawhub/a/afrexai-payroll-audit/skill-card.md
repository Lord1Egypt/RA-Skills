## Description:

Payroll Compliance Audit helps an agent run preliminary payroll compliance reviews covering worker classification, overtime, withholding accuracy, checklists, and audit-ready report drafting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

HR teams, growing companies, and contractor-heavy businesses use this skill to guide an agent through payroll compliance audits across worker classification, overtime rules, tax withholding, and recurring compliance tasks. It supports preliminary review and report drafting, not final legal or tax determinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Payroll, tax, and employment requirements can vary by jurisdiction and change over time.

Mitigation: Verify current federal, state, and local requirements and consult qualified legal, tax, or payroll professionals before taking action.

Risk: Payroll audits may involve employee identifiers, pay records, and other sensitive workforce data.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum necessary information, redact employee identifiers where possible, and keep outputs within approved systems.

Risk: External promotional links are optional third-party sites outside the self-contained skill.

Mitigation: Review those sites separately before sharing information, purchasing services, or relying on their claims.
