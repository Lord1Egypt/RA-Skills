## Description: <br>
Psychiatry-PMPH-9edition provides 47 psychiatry clinical reference skills based on PMPH Psychiatry 9th Edition, covering assessment, diagnostic classification, treatment planning, emergency intervention, forensic evaluation, and community rehabilitation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Psychiatrists, psychotherapists, medical students, community mental-health workers, and consultation-liaison teams use this skill as supervised reference material for psychiatry assessment, diagnostic reasoning, treatment planning, crisis and forensic workflows, and rehabilitation planning. It should support clinician-reviewed workflows rather than independent diagnosis, prescribing, emergency care, involuntary treatment, legal capacity, or guardianship decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: High-stakes psychiatry guidance could be mistaken for independent diagnosis, prescribing, emergency care, involuntary-treatment, legal-capacity, or guardianship advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use outputs only as supervised reference material for qualified mental-health, medical, or legal professionals, and verify recommendations against current guidelines, local protocols, and licensed review. <br>
Risk: The skill includes clinical and forensic topics that may require jurisdiction-specific rules and patient-specific context. <br>
Mitigation: Apply local law, institutional policy, and privacy controls for patient data before using any output in clinical, emergency, or legal workflows. <br>
