## Description:

Helps internal auditors, SOX analysts, and co-sourced audit teams turn audit objectives, scope, and process documentation into an IIA/COSO 2013-aligned draft Risk-Control Matrix with risk ratings, key-control mapping, design gaps, and an audit-program outline for auditor and CAE review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and external audit professionals use this skill to draft an engagement-ready risk-control matrix and audit-program outline from confirmed scope, process, and control information before licensed auditor and CAE review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process confidential audit scope, process, and control information in the active session.

Mitigation: Use an approved environment, share only necessary engagement information, and keep generated drafts within the audit team's normal confidentiality controls.

Risk: Draft risk ratings, control mappings, sample sizes, and audit-program steps may be incomplete or unsuitable for the engagement.

Mitigation: Require a licensed internal auditor and CAE or equivalent reviewer to edit and approve the RCM before walkthroughs, test work, or reliance.

Risk: The skill does not validate evidence, perform fieldwork, or determine whether controls are operating effectively.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning material and perform Test of Design, Test of Operating Effectiveness, and evidence review under the audit methodology.
