## Description:

Distills public thinkers' mental models to stress-test projects, companies, and investment targets from multiple perspectives, producing a checklist with explicit failure conditions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openmsg88](https://clawhub.ai/user/openmsg88)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this agent to stress-test a project, company, investment target, or decision through public thinkers' frameworks. It surfaces evidence gaps and returns model-by-model judgments, failure conditions, and optimistic, neutral, and cautious summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Model-based analysis of projects, companies, or investments can be mistaken for verified facts or investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat the report as a thinking exercise, verify claims against source materials, and conduct due diligence before making real-world decisions.
