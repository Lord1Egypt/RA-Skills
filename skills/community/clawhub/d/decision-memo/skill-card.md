## Description:

Write a crisp decision memo that drives a clear decision, not a discussion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, leaders, and operators use this skill to turn decision context, options, trade-offs, risks, and deadlines into a recommendation-first decision memo for a named decider.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated recommendations may contain incorrect, incomplete, or misleading business judgment.

Mitigation: Have the decider or memo owner review the recommendation, options, assumptions, risks, and deadline before relying on the memo.

Risk: Decision context may include confidential business information.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive context unless the user is comfortable sharing it with the active agent environment.
