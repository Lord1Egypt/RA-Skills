## Description:

Use when planning a funeral or comparing funeral home prices: itemizes General Price List quotes against the FTC Funeral Rule, compares burial, cremation, and green burial options, flags possible package padding and casket markups, and drafts negotiation scripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People arranging a funeral or planning ahead can use this skill to compare funeral-home quotes, understand common cost drivers, and prepare questions and negotiation scripts. It is intended for U.S. funeral planning and price comparison.
