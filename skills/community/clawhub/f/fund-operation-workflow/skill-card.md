## Description:

Helps fund operations teams build compliance-aware AI workflows for internet fund e-commerce by mapping six workflow nodes to MBTI-aligned roles and STAR+ prompt templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External fund operations teams use this skill to structure fund e-commerce workflows, assign workflow responsibilities, and draft compliance-first prompts for collection, archiving, strategy, implementation, testing, and monitoring tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The release recommends an unverified remote installer.

Mitigation: Review installation steps before execution and prefer direct Markdown prompt templates or a verified OpenClaw installer.

Risk: The artifact includes WeChat and Feishu funnel material that can collect user identity and activity beyond the fund-workflow purpose.

Mitigation: Treat registration, referral, screenshot, GitHub issue, WeChat, Feishu, and community signup flows as optional; do not submit client data, regulated financial information, credentials, internal business details, or personal contact information without a clear privacy notice and deletion process.

Risk: Fund-operation outputs may still be unsuitable for regulated use without review.

Mitigation: Have qualified compliance personnel review AI-generated fund sales, marketing, and operations content before deployment.
