## Description:

Provides a pet separation-anxiety monitoring workflow for analyzing recordings and suggesting comfort measures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smyx-sunjinhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/smyx-sunjinhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Pet owners and boarding staff can submit recordings of pets left alone to review reported separation-anxiety behaviors and suggested interventions. The skill describes video analysis and historical report lookup; its advertised real-time monitoring and automatic device actions should be confirmed before relying on them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment reports a mismatch between the advertised pet-care workflow and the visible functionality.

Mitigation: Confirm which analysis and comfort features are actually available before relying on the skill.

Risk: The assessment flags possible analysis of recordings even in export-only mode and unclear disclosure before uploads.

Mitigation: Obtain consent and verify whether recordings will be uploaded or analyzed before sharing them.

Risk: The assessment flags automatic account registration and partial scanner coverage; its findings do not establish malicious intent.

Mitigation: Review account creation and data handling, and treat the security assessment as incomplete.
