## Description:

Performs pre-release and operational product compliance checks for Taobao Flash Sale items, identifying prohibited goods, price fraud, false advertising, and related risks with ratings and remediation guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nic-yuan](https://clawhub.ai/user/nic-yuan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Compliance, operations, and marketplace review teams use this skill to screen Taobao Flash Sale product listings before publication, after complaints, or during routine patrols. It produces risk ratings, specific issue lists, and suggested corrections for product claims, pricing, restricted categories, and qualification checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce legal or platform-rule guidance that is outdated or not authoritative for a specific enforcement decision.

Mitigation: Use outputs as an assistant checklist and verify current Taobao rules and applicable law before acting on high-impact findings.

Risk: The artifact references supporting documents that are not included in the release evidence.

Mitigation: Confirm any terminology, insufficiency-handling, rule-update, or conflict-resolution procedure against available internal documentation before relying on it.
