## Use Case:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste intimate or third-party chat content into prompts.

Mitigation: Redact names, contact details, and sensitive information before using the skill for chat analysis.

Risk: Dating advice or wording could be used for coercion, harassment, stalking, or continued pressure after a rejection.

Mitigation: Use outputs only for respectful communication that honors consent and the other person's stated boundaries.

Risk: Generated relationship guidance can be incomplete or poorly matched to the real interpersonal context.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as suggestions to review and adapt, not as authoritative instructions.
