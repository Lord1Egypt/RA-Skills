## Description:

Analyze rental property investment deals by calculating key metrics including cap rate, cash-on-cash return, DSCR, GRM, and the 1% rule.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[adamsjb](https://clawhub.ai/user/adamsjb)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, real-estate operators, and landlords use this skill to evaluate rental property purchases, compare deals, run price or financing scenarios, and produce a clear investment verdict with supporting metrics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Completed analyses may include property addresses, prices, financing assumptions, and cash-flow estimates saved locally.

Mitigation: Store reports only in appropriate workspaces and remove or anonymize sensitive property details before sharing.

Risk: Investment verdicts depend on user-provided assumptions, market rent estimates, and financing inputs.

Mitigation: Review calculations, assumptions, and comparable rent data independently before making financial decisions.
