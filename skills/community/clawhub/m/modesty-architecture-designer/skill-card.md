## Description:

Use when designing new system architecture, reviewing existing designs, or making architectural decisions, including system design, architecture review, design patterns, ADRs, and scalability planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and engineering teams use this skill to design or review software architecture, compare patterns, document ADRs, evaluate technology choices, and identify scalability, operational, security, and reliability trade-offs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Remote setup-guide content can change outside the reviewed package.

Mitigation: Review the external setup-guide link separately before relying on it.

Risk: Architecture proposals may be incorrect or incomplete when requirements, constraints, or operational context are missing.

Mitigation: Validate requirements, trade-offs, failure modes, and ADRs with stakeholders before using the design for implementation.
