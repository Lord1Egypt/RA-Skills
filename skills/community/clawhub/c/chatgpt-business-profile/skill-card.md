## Description:

Get a business ready to claim its profile in ChatGPT (OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT, managed in Business Hub). Finds what customers search about the brand, checks what ChatGPT tells them today against the website, then writes the profile to paste in, the site fixes to make first, the questions to test a Business Agent with, and the steps to claim and verify. Works best with the RomeoSEO MCP connected and still works by hand without it. Use for "claim our business on ChatGPT", "set up Business Hub", "make a Business Agent" or "what does ChatGPT say about us".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business owners and their authorized representatives use this skill to compare a business's public website with customer searches and ChatGPT answers, prepare accurate profile text, and plan claim and verification steps. It also helps prepare Business Agent test questions where that feature is available.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; Business Agent eligibility and some claiming flows are limited to the United States at launch.
