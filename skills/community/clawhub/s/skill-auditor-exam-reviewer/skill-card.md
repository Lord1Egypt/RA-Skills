## Description:

注册审核员考试主观题复习工具，支持五个科目（认证通用基础、管理体系认证基础、质量/环境/职业健康安全管理体系基础）的历年真题浏览、模拟练习、答案批改与解析；当用户需要备考审核员考试、练习主观题、查询知识点或生成复习计划时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners and exam candidates use this skill to review Chinese registered-auditor subjective questions, practice simulated answers, receive answer critique, and look up subject knowledge points from local reference materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked for study tasks outside the named registered-auditor exam subjects.

Mitigation: Use it for the five stated auditor-exam subjects and confirm the requested subject before relying on its output.

Risk: Exam explanations and scoring feedback may be incomplete or out of date if the local reference materials are incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Review important answers against official exam materials or current course references before using them for final preparation.
