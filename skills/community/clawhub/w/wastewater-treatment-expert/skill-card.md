## Description:

A Chinese-oriented wastewater treatment expert agent for process design, equipment selection, commissioning, operations consulting, and engineering troubleshooting across industrial and municipal wastewater contexts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and engineering teams use this agent to draft wastewater treatment process schemes, sizing calculations, equipment recommendations, commissioning plans, and troubleshooting reports. Its guidance is intended as preliminary engineering reference material that must be checked against current standards, site permits, SDS information, and qualified process-safety review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Wastewater design parameters, chemical dosing ranges, and troubleshooting suggestions may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific facility.

Mitigation: Verify recommendations against current standards, site permits, SDS requirements, and qualified process-safety review before implementation.

Risk: The artifact provides professional reference guidance but does not prove site-specific regulatory compliance or operational safety.

Mitigation: Use outputs as preliminary engineering material and require review by responsible engineers before issuing operating instructions or design decisions.
