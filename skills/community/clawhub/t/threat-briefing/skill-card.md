## Description:

Generate a daily or weekly cybersecurity threat briefing from open sources. Covers new vulnerabilities, active exploits, ransomware campaigns, APT activity, and industry-relevant threats.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[solomonneas](https://clawhub.ai/user/solomonneas)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Security analysts, SOC operators, and IT teams use this skill to produce concise daily or weekly open-source threat briefings covering vulnerabilities, active exploitation, ransomware, APT activity, and prioritized recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases such as "cyber news" or "security briefing" may activate the skill more often than intended.

Mitigation: Invoke it for explicit threat-briefing tasks and review whether the generated briefing matches the requested scope.

Risk: Threat intelligence is time-sensitive and public-source summaries can become outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Review cited sources and confirm high-priority actions against authoritative vulnerability and advisory sources before acting.
