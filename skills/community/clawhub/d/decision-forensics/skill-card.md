## Description:

Reconstructs the decision actually made in a messy Slack, email, or meeting thread into a decision record with named commitments, surfaced assumptions, non-decisions, quoted evidence, dismissed options, and reconstruction confidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and team leads use this skill to turn Slack, email, or meeting threads into reconstructed decision records when agreement, ownership, assumptions, or non-decisions are disputed or unclear.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pasted Slack, email, or meeting content may contain sensitive, confidential, or regulated information.

Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive material before use, and invoke the skill explicitly only when a decision record is needed.

Risk: A reconstruction can overstate agreement or attribute positions beyond what the source thread supports.

Mitigation: Require quotes or nearby original wording for every attribution, label assumptions as reconstructed, and state clearly when no decision was reached.
