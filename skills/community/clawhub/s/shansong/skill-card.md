## Description:

Shansong is an intra-city courier guide for one-to-one direct delivery positioning, pricing logic, time planning, and high-value item precautions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to choose between same-city delivery options, plan order timing and cost expectations, and apply precautions for urgent or valuable Shansong deliveries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may provide stale or city-specific price, timing, insurance, or prohibited-item guidance.

Mitigation: Verify current prices, delivery rules, insurance terms, and prohibited-item policies in the official app before placing an order.

Risk: Urgent or high-value deliveries can result in loss, delay, damage, or insufficient reimbursement if order details are incomplete.

Mitigation: Use insured service when appropriate, document package condition during handoff, and keep app receipts and support records for disputes.
