## Description:

Create structured change management plans for organisational changes, including stakeholder analysis, impact assessment, communication strategy, training and support, resistance management, and adoption metrics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, change managers, project leads, and consultants use this skill to draft practical change management plans for organisational transformations, system rollouts, process changes, and adoption initiatives.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated change plans may include inaccurate assumptions about stakeholders, timelines, adoption risks, or operational impact.

Mitigation: Review each plan with accountable sponsors, managers, and affected teams before using it to guide an organisational change.

Risk: Change planning can involve sensitive stakeholder, personnel, or organisational details.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential information in the chat context and remove unnecessary personal or sensitive details from generated plans.
