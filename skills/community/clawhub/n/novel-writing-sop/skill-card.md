中文网文/爽文全流程写作引擎，用于选题、大纲、分章正文、续写、卡文处理、去 AI 味和双视角质检。

## Use Case:

External authors and agent users use this skill to plan and draft Chinese web novels through quick, standard, or batch-production workflows. It guides topic selection, structured outlining, chapter drafting, five-chapter review gates, and style cleanup.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-detection-evasion framing could be misused to bypass disclosure or platform rules.

Mitigation: Use the cleanup guidance for readability and style editing only, and follow applicable disclosure, provenance, and platform policies.

Risk: Close style-imitation guidance could encourage copying an identifiable author or work too closely.

Mitigation: Use broad craft patterns from original or authorized references, avoid imitating living or identifiable authors, and do not copy protected scenes, settings, or text.

Risk: Helper scripts create or inspect local project files.

Mitigation: Run scripts only in the intended project directory and review generated files before using them in a writing workflow.
