## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Football probability and betting-style guidance can be uncertain or misleading if treated as a guarantee.

Mitigation: Present probability estimates with uncertainty, avoid absolute claims, and review outputs critically before using them for decisions.

Risk: Public sports, lineup, injury, tactics, and odds data can be incomplete, stale, or inconsistent across sources.

Mitigation: Use reliable sources, cite source links and retrieval timestamps, cross-check important claims, and clearly mark degraded inference when data is missing.
