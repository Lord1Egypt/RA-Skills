## Description:

Designs structured new-user onboarding flows from journey maps, including first-win tasks, staged feature guidance, and activation metrics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zxw-wwwww](https://clawhub.ai/user/zxw-wwwww)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product teams, UX designers, and design learners use this skill to turn a product concept, Aha moment, and target user profile into a four-stage onboarding plan with activation success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad onboarding phrases may trigger the skill when a user intended a different onboarding-related task.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's product context and desired deliverable before relying on the generated onboarding plan.

Risk: The skill is oriented toward Chinese-language onboarding guidance, which may not match every user's preferred language.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for another output language when needed.

Risk: Generated activation targets and onboarding recommendations are generic baselines.

Mitigation: Review the plan against product analytics, user research, and compliance constraints before implementation.
