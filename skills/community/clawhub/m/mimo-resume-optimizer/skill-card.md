## Description:

Helps users diagnose and improve Chinese or English resumes by quantifying achievements, aligning keywords with target roles, improving ATS compatibility, and preparing related LinkedIn or cover-letter guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qqyougitcom](https://clawhub.ai/user/qqyougitcom)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers and career-support agents use this skill to review resumes against target roles, rewrite experience bullets with grounded metrics, improve ATS readability, and prepare supporting job-search materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume and job-description inputs may contain personal information.

Mitigation: Review inputs and outputs for unnecessary phone numbers, addresses, or other sensitive details before sharing or storing them.

Risk: Optimized wording may overstate or misrepresent a user's experience if not checked.

Mitigation: Validate every generated claim against the user's actual background and keep the artifact's no-fabrication boundary in force.
