## Description:

Turn a still image into a short video with directed subject, camera, atmospheric, or reference-video motion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[runware](https://clawhub.ai/user/runware)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and creative operators use this skill to turn user-provided still images into short animated clips, including cinematic image-to-video prompts or motion transfer from a reference video.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the skill normally sends images, videos, prompts, and generated outputs to external model services through Runware, and outputs may be available as hosted URLs.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential, regulated, private, or non-consensual portraits or videos unless the provider handling and retention terms are acceptable.
