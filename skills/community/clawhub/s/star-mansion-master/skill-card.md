## Description:

Star Mansion Master helps agents produce Chinese 28 Star Mansions personality readings, relationship compatibility analyses, social media content, and short comment replies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to look up a Chinese 28 Star Mansion from a lunar birthday, generate personality and compatibility readings, and draft social media posts or comment replies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Relationship or personality readings could be mistaken for deterministic advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as cultural or entertainment content and preserve the skill's guidance that star mansions describe tendencies rather than determine outcomes.

Risk: Generated social posts or comment replies may include promotional calls to action that are not appropriate for every account.

Mitigation: Review content before posting and adjust calls to action, monetization language, and platform tone for the intended audience.

Risk: Local preference storage may create account-specific configuration that changes generated content.

Mitigation: Review saved EXTEND.md preferences before deployment and keep only non-sensitive content strategy settings.
