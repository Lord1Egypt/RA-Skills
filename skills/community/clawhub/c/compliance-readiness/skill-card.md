## Description:

AI Compliance Readiness Assessment - evaluate how prepared an organization is for AI governance regulations, score readiness across eight dimensions, and generate an action plan for compliance gaps, audits, and governance roadmaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Employees, compliance teams, legal teams, healthcare organizations, financial services teams, and other AI governance stakeholders use this skill to assess AI compliance readiness before audits, identify gaps across governance dimensions, and build an action plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Organizational AI inventories, vendors, audit dates, and compliance certifications may contain sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Limit shared inputs to information appropriate for the agent environment and avoid including secrets or unnecessary proprietary details.

Risk: Regulatory deadlines and obligations can change, so generated readiness guidance may become outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify compliance timelines and requirements against current legal or regulatory sources before relying on the report for audit planning or legal decisions.
