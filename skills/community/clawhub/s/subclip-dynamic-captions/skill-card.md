## Description:

Turn an existing or newly generated transcript into editable, semantic, high-retention Dynamic Captions using Subclip presets, sparse word-priority tiers, intentional phrase pages, and visual verification. Use when the creator asks for dynamic, viral, animated, emphasized, word-by-word, scroll-stopping, or preset-based captions. Do not use for ordinary subtitle correction or unrelated TextPanel overlays.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Video creators use this skill to turn an existing or newly generated transcript into editable, semantically emphasized Dynamic Captions in Subclip. It also records the caption strategy and visual verification in the director brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Caption preset or style changes can affect captions beyond the currently selected phrase.

Mitigation: Confirm the active project and caption scope before applying changes, then inspect the opening, representative middle pages, boundaries, longest page, and ending.
