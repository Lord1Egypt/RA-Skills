## Description:

Time-blind friendly planning, executive function support, and daily structure for ADHD brains.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for ADHD-friendly daily planning, time estimation, transition buffers, and practical executive-function support. It provides wellness and planning guidance, not medical care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat wellness planning guidance as medical, medication, crisis, or major life decision support.

Mitigation: Present the skill as planning support only and route diagnosis, medication, crisis, and major life decisions to qualified professionals.

Risk: Planning advice may be ineffective or overwhelming if applied rigidly to users with varying executive-function needs.

Mitigation: Encourage flexible use, small next actions, transition buffers, and user review of whether a strategy fits their current capacity.
