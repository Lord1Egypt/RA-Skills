## Description:

Generates Chinese-language company research, due diligence, and competitive analysis reports covering business models, competitive landscape, SWOT analysis, market size, management assessment, and risk factors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Investment managers, strategy analysts, business development teams, financial advisors, and industry researchers use this skill to prepare company background research, investment due diligence summaries, competitor analysis, and business visit briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated market, financial, or company facts may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for investment, vendor, or strategy decisions without review.

Mitigation: Verify generated business research against current reliable sources before relying on it for commercial decisions.
