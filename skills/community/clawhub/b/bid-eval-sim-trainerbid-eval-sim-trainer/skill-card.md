## Description:

评标/政采评审专家的安全模拟训练陪练，覆盖《招标投标法》体系与《政府采购法》体系双线场景，并输出合规判定、法条依据、案例对照与后果分层。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External bid-evaluation and government-procurement reviewers use this skill to practice Chinese-language review scenarios, route cases between procurement-law systems, explain their decisions, and receive structured coaching on compliance, responsibility type, legal basis, and likely consequences. It is a training aid and does not replace legal advice, official current texts, or required review procedures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake simulated training responses for current legal advice or a binding review decision.

Mitigation: Use the skill for practice only; verify cited laws, dates, cases, and procedures against official current sources and seek qualified legal advice for formal matters.

Risk: Optional external knowledge bases may contain stale, incomplete, or noisy procurement-law and case materials.

Mitigation: Treat retrieved material as context to be checked, prefer official current sources, and require explicit uncertainty when a law, date, or case cannot be verified.

Risk: Bid-evaluation scenarios can be misused to ask for circumvention, collusion, concealment, biased scoring, or confidentiality breaches.

Mitigation: Keep the skill in training and compliance posture: refuse evasion guidance and redirect to lawful review steps, documentation, and escalation paths.
