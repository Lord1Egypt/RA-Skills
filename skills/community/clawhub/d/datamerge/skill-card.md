## Description:

DataMerge enriches companies and finds B2B contacts through the DataMerge MCP server, supporting firmographic data, validated contact emails or phone numbers, lookalike company discovery, corporate hierarchy data, and target-account list management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, sales operations teams, and agent builders use this skill to enrich target accounts, find validated B2B contacts, discover lookalike companies, retrieve corporate hierarchy data, and manage company or contact lists with DataMerge.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can help look up and use B2B contact data, including mobile phone numbers.

Mitigation: Confirm a lawful and appropriate basis for contact-data use, follow applicable privacy, retention, and outreach rules, and request phone fields only when they are truly needed.

Risk: Large searches or phone-number enrichment can consume DataMerge credits quickly.

Mitigation: Check the credits balance before large searches and prefer email-only enrichment unless phone data is explicitly required.
