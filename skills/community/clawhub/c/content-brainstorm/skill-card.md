## Description:

Social media content ideation engine. Generates a 7-day topic plan with titles, angles, SEO keywords, and trend scores for any niche and platform.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[alexandrali1989-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/alexandrali1989-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to generate a structured 7-day topic calendar for Chinese social media platforms, including platform-native titles, hooks, SEO keywords, virality estimates, competitor references, and estimated creation time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad brainstorming or content-planning requests.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants a Chinese/social-platform content calendar before using account examples or past performance data.

Risk: Competitor examples and prior account performance can shape recommendations in ways the user may not intend.

Mitigation: Use provided examples only as planning references and avoid copying competitor content or exposing private account strategy.

Risk: Trend-driven topics can become outdated quickly.

Mitigation: Check current platform trends when generating time-sensitive topics and flag topics that should be published the same day.
