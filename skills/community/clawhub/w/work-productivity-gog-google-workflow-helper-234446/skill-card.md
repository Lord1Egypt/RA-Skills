## Description:

Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn Gog-style Google Workspace workflow needs into practical plans, artifacts, checklists, analysis, implementation support, and verification notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to clarify Google Workspace or Gog-style productivity requests, create local-friendly workflows or templates, and validate the resulting artifact against visible success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad auto-invocation triggers for ordinary Google, Gmail, Drive, CLI, and bug-fix requests.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or disable implicit invocation where supported, then review the skill's proposed workflow before acting on it.

Risk: Workflow or implementation guidance could be incomplete or unsuitable for a user's environment.

Mitigation: Validate outputs against the stated success criteria and keep assumptions, limits, and required inputs visible before use.
