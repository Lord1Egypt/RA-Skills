## Description: <br>
全能竞品情报官 helps agents monitor competitors across commerce, social, app-store, search, and industry sources and turn findings into market analysis and action recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, product, operations, and strategy teams use this skill to define competitor targets, monitor public market signals, and produce periodic intelligence reports with recommended responses. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad multi-platform competitive monitoring may collect data beyond the user's authorization or platform rules. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only for lawful, public, user-authorized competitive research and explicitly limit the target platforms before use. <br>
Risk: Continuous monitoring can run without clear limits on frequency, retention, report destinations, or stop conditions. <br>
Mitigation: Define the date range, monitoring frequency, report destinations, retention period, and stop conditions before asking an agent to run it. <br>
