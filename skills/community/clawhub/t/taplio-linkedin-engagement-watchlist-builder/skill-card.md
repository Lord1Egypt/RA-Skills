## Description:

Build a daily LinkedIn engagement watchlist by sampling Taplio feeds, ranking creators into reach targets and peers, and suggesting feeds and comment angles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

LinkedIn users and professionals use this skill to build a daily engagement routine from live Taplio feeds. It ranks creators into reach targets and peers, suggests comment angles, and recommends feeds and review cadence.
