## Description:

Adds a visible AI Usage Notice to AI-produced projects and documents by default, unless the user opts out.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mowenqwq](https://clawhub.ai/user/mowenqwq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers, writers, and agent operators use this skill to add concise AI-use disclosure notices to finished code projects, websites, apps, scripts, articles, tutorials, and documents. The notices communicate AI-generated or AI-assisted status, generation date, and a short review caution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Default AI-use notices may not match a user's jurisdiction, publisher policy, or desired disclosure wording.

Mitigation: Review and adjust the notice text before publication; opt out when a disclosure is not wanted or not applicable.

Risk: Incorrect disclosure wording could misrepresent whether a deliverable was AI-generated or AI-assisted.

Mitigation: Choose wording that accurately reflects the generation process and require human review before release.
