## Description:

Helps Chinese-speaking users assess one-person-company startup readiness with a 12-item checklist covering demand, repeatable delivery, employment boundaries, non-compete and resource conflicts, cash runway, positioning, acquisition, and delivery capability.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Chinese-speaking users use this skill to decide whether to start low-cost OPC validation, run a limited trial, or defer while resolving employment-boundary, compliance, cash-runway, acquisition, or delivery gaps. It can also produce concrete remediation actions, owners, due dates, and near-term milestones.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill discusses legal, tax, employment, contract, intellectual-property, and non-compete topics that may vary by jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Use its prompts as screening questions only and have binding decisions reviewed by a qualified local professional.

Risk: A short-term increase in side-business income could lead to an overconfident full-time entrepreneurship decision.

Mitigation: Review client stability, cash reserves, and family risk tolerance before using the readiness result to support resignation.

Risk: Users may be tempted to disclose employer names, customer names, contract text, or sensitive financial details during assessment.

Mitigation: Ask for ranges or summaries where possible and avoid collecting employer, customer, or contract-identifying information.
