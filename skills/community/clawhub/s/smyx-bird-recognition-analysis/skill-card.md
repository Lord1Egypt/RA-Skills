## Description:

Identifies bird species in images and videos, supports recognition of at least 500 common species, and allows customized model training.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Birdwatchers and ecological observers can identify species in photos or videos and review structured analysis results. The skill also supports looking up historical analysis reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan rates the release suspicious for undisclosed account and data handling, behavior beyond its stated bird-recognition purpose, and an export-only option that may still trigger remote analysis. The scanner assessment is partial because a local reference was missing.

Mitigation: Clarify account creation and credential storage, remote data transfers, and export-only behavior with the publisher before installing. Avoid uploading sensitive media until these details are clear; do not rely on export-only to prevent remote analysis.
