## Description:

Helps ML engineers, data scientists, MLOps teams, and responsible-AI leads draft a review-ready Model Card for a machine-learning or AI model covering intended use, data, evaluation, limitations, and ethical considerations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers, ML engineers, data scientists, MLOps practitioners, and responsible-AI leads use this skill to gather model details and assemble a draft Model Card for governance, publication, or regulatory documentation review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The drafted Model Card may contain incomplete or user-supplied claims about sensitive attributes, performance metrics, legal compliance, or governance sign-off.

Mitigation: Require ML engineering and governance review before publication, regulatory filing, or external use, and resolve every flagged documentation gap.

Risk: The skill does not train models, run evaluations, verify facts, or conduct bias audits.

Mitigation: Treat its output as documentation guidance and confirm evaluation data, disaggregated metrics, privacy risks, and fairness claims against authoritative project evidence.
