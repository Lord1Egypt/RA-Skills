## Description:

AfrexAI Lead Hunter Pro helps AI agents discover, enrich, score, segment, and prepare outreach for B2B leads.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales and growth teams use this skill to define ICPs, discover and enrich B2B prospects, score leads, draft outreach sequences, and organize CRM-ready pipeline records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Autonomous collection of business contact data and unsolicited outreach may violate platform rules, privacy obligations, or customer policies.

Mitigation: Use only lawful and permitted B2B data sources, confirm regional privacy requirements, and maintain opt-out and suppression lists before operating the workflow.

Risk: Autopilot outreach could send first-contact messages without adequate review.

Mitigation: Require human approval before any first contact or sequence send, especially for email, LinkedIn, CRM, or phone-channel actions.
