## Description:

Helps users assess how deeply they understand a company as an investment by using a four-question framework, targeted follow-up questions, and a Markdown diagnostic report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xbthh](https://clawhub.ai/user/xbthh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to test an investor's understanding of a company before or during ownership. The skill guides public research, interactive questioning, blind-spot detection, scoring, and focused learning suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The assessment can be mistaken for financial advice or a recommendation to change position size.

Mitigation: Treat the score and feedback as an educational self-assessment and require independent financial judgment before making investment decisions.

Risk: The workflow uses public web research and may involve sensitive portfolio or watchlist details shared in chat.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing private portfolio details unless the user is comfortable using them in the conversation and related web-research context.
