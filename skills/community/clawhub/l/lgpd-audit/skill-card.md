## Description:

Coordinates LGPD privacy engineering, code audits, implementation, and governance for software projects, routing active breaches to incident response and organizing work into reviewed governance artifacts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[goul4rt](https://clawhub.ai/user/goul4rt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and privacy teams use it to assess and improve LGPD controls in a software project, coordinate incident response, and prepare versioned technical and governance artifacts for human or legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Brazil

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan had medium confidence because one component was only partially inspected and some referenced paths were unavailable.

Mitigation: Review the skill source and relevant referenced files before installing or executing it.

Risk: Privacy and legal guidance may not match a project's facts or current requirements.

Mitigation: Verify current official requirements and obtain human or legal review for ROPA, RIPD, privacy policies, and incident communications.
