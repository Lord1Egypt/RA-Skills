## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Refusal-reframing guidance could be misused to bypass safety rules.

Mitigation: Review the refusal-related troubleshooting example critically and adapt it only for legitimate, policy-compliant use cases.

Risk: Prompt recommendations can produce incorrect, misleading, or inconsistent agent behavior if copied without testing.

Mitigation: Test recommendations against representative prompts, review outputs before deployment, and keep human judgment in critical applications.

Risk: Platform-specific examples such as local file paths may not match the installer's environment.

Mitigation: Adjust paths and platform-specific instructions before using the examples.
