## Description:

Identify, structure, screen, and route potentially patentable inventions from R&D projects, technical improvements, standards work, existing innovations or portfolios, competitor patents, design-around needs, cross-domain technology transfer, or emerging opportunity hypotheses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

R&D engineers, technical leaders, and IP professionals use this skill to mine invention candidates from technical work, compare them against patent and prior-art evidence, and route them toward validation, counsel review, filing, secrecy, or no-pursuit decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: External patent research tools may receive sensitive invention details.

Mitigation: Confirm organizational approval for the named PatSnap MCP services before sharing unpublished or confidential invention text.

Risk: Patentability, infringement, ownership, disclosure, and filing decisions may be legal conclusions.

Mitigation: Route legal conclusions and filing decisions to qualified patent counsel.

Risk: Incomplete searches or unsupported facts can produce misleading invention-mining outcomes.

Mitigation: Preserve evidence IDs, search scope, limitations, unresolved facts, and confidence levels; do not fabricate missing patent, product, experiment, or prior-art evidence.
