## Description:

Helps agents analyze a code repository or technical description, extract academic contribution candidates, and draft a Chinese journal paper with structured sections, experiment placeholders, and GB/T 7714 citation templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gaolegao2024](https://clawhub.ai/user/gaolegao2024)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, researchers, and technical authors use this skill to turn project code or technical plans into a Chinese academic paper draft for core or general journals. The output remains a draft and requires manual review of claims, experiments, plagiarism checks, journal formatting, and citation accuracy before submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated academic claims, experiment results, or citations may be incomplete or incorrect.

Mitigation: Manually verify all claims, run or document real experiments, and confirm every reference against source material before submission.

Risk: The skill analyzes project files to identify technical contributions, which may expose sensitive repository content to the active agent session.

Mitigation: Use only on repositories the user is authorized to share with the agent, and review generated summaries before distributing them.

Risk: A draft paper can still violate journal formatting, originality, or academic-integrity requirements.

Mitigation: Perform plagiarism checks, adapt the draft to the target journal template, and retain human responsibility for final submission decisions.
