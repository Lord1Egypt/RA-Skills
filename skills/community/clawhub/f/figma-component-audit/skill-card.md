## Description:

Audits a Figma component library for consistency, coverage gaps, naming issues, variant completeness, and prioritized fixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Design system teams, product designers, and developers use this skill to review Figma component libraries for naming consistency, component coverage, variant completeness, documentation health, and actionable remediation priorities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste more Figma or product context than needed for an audit.

Mitigation: Provide only the component names and design-system details needed to generate the checklist-style audit.

Risk: The audit report may produce incomplete or misleading recommendations if the provided component list is partial.

Mitigation: Review the generated findings against the actual Figma library before acting on naming, coverage, or variant recommendations.
