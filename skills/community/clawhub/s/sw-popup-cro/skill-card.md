## Description:

Design optimized popups with clear calls to action triggered by exit intent, scroll depth, time on page, or click behavior to support lead capture and conversions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Marketing teams, growth practitioners, and developers use this skill to design popup campaigns with trigger rules, concise copy, targeting guidance, and frequency caps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Popup implementations that use cookies, email capture, or behavioral triggers can create privacy, consent, or accessibility issues.

Mitigation: Review the implementation against applicable privacy rules, consent requirements, and accessibility expectations before deployment.

Risk: Intrusive popups can interrupt conversion flows or degrade the user experience.

Mitigation: Use visible close controls, mobile bottom bars instead of blocking overlays, sensible frequency caps, and avoid showing popups during active conversion flows.
