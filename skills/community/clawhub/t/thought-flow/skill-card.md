## Description:

The 8-stage collaboration loop for working with the user: INTENT, CONSTRAINTS, PROPOSE, PRESS, PRACTICE, INVESTIGATE, CODIFY, and BOUNDARY-CHECK.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad trigger language may activate the skill often during planning and troubleshooting.

Mitigation: Use it where a structured collaboration loop is useful, and adjust activation wording if it interrupts routine work.

Risk: The skill encourages codifying knowledge into AGENTS.md or SKILL.md files, which can change future agent behavior.

Mitigation: Review proposed AGENTS.md and SKILL.md changes before accepting them.
