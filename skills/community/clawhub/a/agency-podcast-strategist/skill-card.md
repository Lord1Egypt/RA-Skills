## Use Case:

External podcast creators, operators, and agency teams use this skill to develop show positioning, production workflows, distribution plans, audience growth tactics, monetization strategy, and performance review practices for Chinese-language or China-market podcasts.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Guidance is specialized for Chinese-language or China-market podcast platforms and may not fit other target markets.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to adapt platform choices, growth tactics, monetization options, and compliance considerations to the intended market before using the plan.
