## Use Case:

External founders and startup operators use this skill for China-focused company setup workflows, including registration, ICP filing, trademarks, banking, tax, social insurance, and cloud-service preparation. It produces checklist-style guidance that should be checked against current official requirements before action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal, tax, ICP filing, trademark, fee, and timeline guidance may become outdated or vary by city, agency, or service provider.

Mitigation: Verify current requirements with official agencies, current provider documentation, or qualified local professionals before acting.

Risk: Checklist-style guidance may be too general for regulated industries or businesses requiring special permits.

Mitigation: Treat the skill output as planning support and confirm any industry-specific licenses or approvals before launch.
