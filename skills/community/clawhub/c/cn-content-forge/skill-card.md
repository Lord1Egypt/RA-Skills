## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated copy may assume Mainland Simplified Chinese defaults that do not match the intended audience.

Mitigation: Specify the desired locale, script, platform, and audience constraints before generation.

Risk: Social content may include misleading claims or sensitive-topic framing if source material is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review drafts for political sensitivity, advertising accuracy, health claims, and platform policy compliance before publishing.
