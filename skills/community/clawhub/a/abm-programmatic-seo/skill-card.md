## Description:

Helps users plan SEO-driven pages at scale using templates, structured data, keyword patterns, internal linking, and indexation strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, marketers, SEO practitioners, and developers use this skill to design programmatic SEO strategies, page templates, content quality checks, and scaled page architectures for keyword, location, comparison, integration, glossary, directory, and related page patterns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on adjacent bulk page-generation requests that are not truly SEO-driven, at scale, or within this skill's intended strategy scope.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is planning programmatic SEO pages and route one-off page requests, general content strategy, or technical SEO audits to the more appropriate workflow.

Risk: Programmatic SEO plans can produce thin, duplicate, or low-value pages if templates are applied without page-specific data and quality checks.

Mitigation: Require unique value per page, validate search intent and data sources, use internal linking and indexation checks, and noindex or avoid weak page variants.
