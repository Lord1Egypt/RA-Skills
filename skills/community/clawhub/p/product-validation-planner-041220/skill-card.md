## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, analyses, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test product ideas before spending heavily. It structures validation work into practical plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, and verification notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad product, startup, validation, prototype, or SaaS wording may activate the skill in situations where the user only needs a narrow answer.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's outcome, constraints, available inputs, and success criteria before producing a validation plan or artifact.

Risk: Business planning output can be mistaken for proof of market demand.

Mitigation: Treat the output as planning support and independently validate target customers, demand evidence, willingness to pay, and build costs before major spending.

Risk: The included demand evidence is broad and may not directly validate a specific product idea.

Mitigation: Check the cited evidence against the user's target market and add direct customer interviews, landing-page tests, or prototype trials where appropriate.
