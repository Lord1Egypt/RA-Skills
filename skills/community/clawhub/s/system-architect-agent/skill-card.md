## Use Case:

Founders, product managers, and technical leads use this Chinese-language agent to turn early business requirements into architecture proposals, outsourcing specifications, acceptance checklists, risk matrices, and cost estimates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated architecture, outsourcing, cost, and risk documents may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific business or technical decision.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance and review them with qualified business, product, and technical stakeholders before acting.

Risk: The workflow is written for Chinese-language architecture advisory use and may be less effective for teams expecting another language or local terminology.

Mitigation: Confirm the working language and have translated or localized deliverables reviewed before sharing them with execution teams.
