## Description:

Data Analyst Agent helps track book performance, evaluate operating metrics and ROI, analyze reader feedback, generate trend reports and dashboards, and raise anomaly alerts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ouyang198000](https://clawhub.ai/user/ouyang198000)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, operators, and publishing workflow agents use this skill to monitor public book-platform metrics, analyze reader sentiment and revenue trends, produce periodic reports, and trigger alerts for significant performance, income, competitor, or policy changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be connected later to real platform accounts or non-public data sources.

Mitigation: Keep collection user-authorized, narrowly scoped, and limited to public or permitted data.

Risk: Reader comments and engagement data may contain personal or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Anonymize reader feedback and avoid storing personal reader data.

Risk: Analytics conclusions or revenue estimates may be stale or misleading if source timing is unclear.

Mitigation: Label data sources and freshness, and mark estimated income as an estimate.
