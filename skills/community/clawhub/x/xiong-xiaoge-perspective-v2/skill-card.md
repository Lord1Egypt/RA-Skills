## Description:

A persona-based thinking framework that helps agents analyze investment, business, media, and technology questions from a simulated Xiong Xiaoge perspective based on public information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill as a persona-style business and investment thinking aid for exploring early-stage opportunities, strategic decisions, media and technology trends, and cross-border market questions. Outputs should be treated as simulated analysis, not statements from Xiong Xiaoge or professional investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may answer in first person as a real public figure.

Mitigation: Keep the persona disclosure clear and treat outputs as simulated analysis based on public information, not statements from Xiong Xiaoge.

Risk: Users may mistake investment-themed outputs for professional financial advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs for thinking support only and avoid relying on them for short-term trading, specific stock recommendations, or financial decisions without qualified review.

Risk: The persona can activate when the user did not intend roleplay.

Mitigation: Use the documented exit phrases, such as asking to exit or return to normal mode.
