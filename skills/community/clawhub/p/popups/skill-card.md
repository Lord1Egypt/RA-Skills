## Description:

Helps marketers and conversion teams create or optimize popups, modals, overlays, slide-ins, and announcement banners for conversion without damaging user experience.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketers, growth teams, and conversion specialists use this skill to plan popup strategies, write popup copy, define triggers and frequency rules, and evaluate popup experiments. It is intended for overlay and interrupt-style conversion elements such as email capture popups, lead magnet modals, discount offers, exit-intent saves, slide-ins, sticky bars, and announcement banners.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Popup personalization and targeting can create privacy or consent risk if implemented without appropriate legal basis, consent language, or data minimization.

Mitigation: Review popup data collection and targeting rules against applicable privacy laws, avoid sensitive-data targeting, and include clear consent language and privacy policy links where required.

Risk: Aggressive overlays can harm user experience, accessibility, SEO, or brand perception if users cannot easily dismiss them or if they block mobile content.

Mitigation: Use conservative triggers and frequency caps, honor dismissals and unsubscribe preferences, provide accessible close controls, support keyboard and screen-reader behavior, and avoid intrusive mobile interstitials.
