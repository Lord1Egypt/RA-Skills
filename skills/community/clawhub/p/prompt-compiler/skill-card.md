## Description:

Converts vague user intent into a structured Brief, executable Prompt, and downstream Skill routing for websites, posters, presentations, videos, reports, product plans, and research tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[taogeo](https://clawhub.ai/user/taogeo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to turn rough ideas, incomplete requirements, or draft prompts into an actionable task brief, prompt, acceptance criteria, and handoff guidance for another AI agent or skill.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated briefs or prompts may contain inferred assumptions when user requirements are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review confirmed, inferred, and missing markers before using the prompt for downstream work.

Risk: Downstream Skill routing suggestions may be mistaken for completed external actions.

Mitigation: Treat routing as guidance only and explicitly confirm any downstream execution, installation, upload, or publication step.
