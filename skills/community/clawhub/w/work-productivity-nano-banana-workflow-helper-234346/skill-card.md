AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn demand for Nano Banana Pro-style workflows into local-friendly plans, checklists, artifacts, code changes, and validation notes.

Risk: Broad activation keywords may cause the skill to be invoked for unrelated requests.
