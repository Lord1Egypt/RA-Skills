## Description:

A monthly or quarterly review skill that helps users learn from their crypto behavior, update conviction, and tighten rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to run a structured monthly or quarterly reflection on crypto activity, including decisions, behavior, safety patterns, and lessons. It helps turn the review into a keep, change, and stop rule for the next period.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive portfolio notes or activity summaries during reflection.

Mitigation: Share only information you are comfortable providing, and do not provide wallet credentials, tax records, or account access.

Risk: Users may mistake the reflection output for a tax report or formal performance attribution.

Mitigation: Use the output as a personal review aid only, and rely on qualified professionals or dedicated tools for tax reporting and formal performance analysis.
