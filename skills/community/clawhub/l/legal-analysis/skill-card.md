## Use Case:

External users use this skill for Mainland China legal-information workflows, including issue spotting, legal basis organization, evidence review, procedural path analysis, and practical risk assessment. It provides structured analysis for reference and does not replace formal advice from a licensed legal professional.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat legal-information analysis as formal legal advice or apply it outside Mainland China legal workflows.

Mitigation: Present outputs as reference analysis only, limit use to Mainland China matters, and direct users with out-of-scope or specialized matters to an appropriate licensed professional.

Risk: Legal analysis may involve sensitive personal, company, medical, minor, financial, or case information.

Mitigation: Ask users to provide only necessary, dispute-relevant information and to redact sensitive identifiers before analysis.

Risk: Evidence guidance could be misused for unlawful collection, fabrication, alteration, harassment, or public exposure of private information.

Mitigation: Require lawful evidence preservation, original-source retention, and explicit avoidance of forgery, tampering, threats, harassment, and privacy violations.
