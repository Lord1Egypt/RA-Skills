## Description:

Helps DTC and e-commerce merchants evaluate, source, and price technically differentiated blue-ocean products through viability, supplier-vetting, margin, and loyalty strategy guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rijoyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/rijoyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External DTC and independent e-commerce merchants use this skill to decide whether a differentiated product is viable, how to vet suppliers, and what price and margin structure can support growth.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Vendor-specific promotion may make otherwise neutral sourcing guidance appear impartial.

Mitigation: Review generated recommendations for promotional bias and disclose or remove Rijoy-specific recommendations when impartial vendor comparison is required.

Risk: Pricing analysis can be misleading when merchant cost, shipping, fixed-cost, or marketing inputs are incomplete.

Mitigation: Require assumptions to be stated clearly and recalculate margins with verified merchant inputs before making sourcing or pricing decisions.

Risk: Supplier and product viability guidance does not replace legal, regulatory, or compliance review.

Mitigation: Escalate product safety, regulatory, import, intellectual-property, and contract questions to qualified professionals before committing to production.
