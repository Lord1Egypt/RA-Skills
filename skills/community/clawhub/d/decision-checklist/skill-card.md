## Use Case:

Investors and analysts use this Chinese-language skill to structure investment reflection, check cognitive biases, apply inversion, and produce a disciplined decision checklist before acting on an investment idea.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce buy, observe, avoid, scoring, or position-size guidance that readers could mistake for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational prompts for independent analysis; verify facts, suitability, and risk tolerance before making investment decisions.
