## Description:

Guides agents through Xiaoe-tech knowledge-commerce SaaS selection, store setup, funnel design, cost comparison, and compliance checks for courses, livestreams, memberships, and private-domain operations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, education operators, and agents advising them use this skill to decide whether Xiaoe-tech fits a knowledge-commerce business, map course, livestream, and community capabilities, compare platform economics, design sales funnels, and identify content-compliance risks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing, product packaging, and education compliance requirements may change after release.

Mitigation: Verify current Xiaoe-tech pricing, platform terms, and applicable education regulations with official sources before making business or legal decisions.

Risk: Platform-selection and marketing-funnel guidance may be too general for regulated education offerings or high-stakes commercial claims.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a planning checklist and have qualified reviewers confirm course qualifications, advertising language, and compliance-sensitive decisions.
