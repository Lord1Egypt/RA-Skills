## Description:

Guides founders through idea validation frameworks and discovery interviews for testing startup demand before building.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[c0ldsmi1e](https://clawhub.ai/user/c0ldsmi1e)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders and startup advisors use this skill to assess an idea, plan customer discovery, test demand signals, and decide whether to proceed, pivot, or stop before investing significant build effort.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outreach, community posts, landing pages, waitlists, or pre-sales suggested by the skill may raise legal, platform, or spam-compliance concerns.

Mitigation: Users should review and approve validation activities against applicable laws, platform rules, and outreach policies before running them.

Risk: Startup validation advice may be mistaken for a guarantee of market demand or business viability.

Mitigation: Treat the output as advisory planning support and base go/no-go decisions on independently gathered customer evidence.
