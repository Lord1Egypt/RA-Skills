## Description:

产品工坊（Product Workshop）is a Chinese-language product-management workflow skill for turning early product ideas into deliverable plans, PRDs, user stories, iteration guidance, and retrospectives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, and product teams use this skill to structure product planning, requirements analysis, PRD writing, user research, prioritization, agile execution, and post-launch review. The skill is most useful when an agent needs to choose the right product lifecycle stage and produce practical Chinese-language working documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may cause the skill to engage for product-adjacent requests where the user expected narrower guidance.

Mitigation: Confirm the product lifecycle stage and scope before applying a template, then use only the relevant framework, checklist, or document format.

Risk: Chinese-language source content may not fit users who need another language or localized product terminology.

Mitigation: Clarify the user's language preference and adapt or translate the generated guidance when needed.

Risk: Template-generated product documents can contain unsupported assumptions when the user's input lacks user evidence, data, or business constraints.

Mitigation: Flag assumptions explicitly and request user data, customer evidence, or acceptance criteria before treating the output as final.
