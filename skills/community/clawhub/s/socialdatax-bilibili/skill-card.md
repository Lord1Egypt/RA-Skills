## Description:

用于 B站数据助手、视频和专栏搜索、内容详情、评论分析、点赞转发观察、UP主资料及视频、专栏和动态列表，并支持按需下载视频到本地。覆盖 Bilibili / 哔哩哔哩，来自社媒数据助手 SocialDataX。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People researching Bilibili content can search videos and articles, review content and comments, inspect reactions and creator profiles or posts, and save a video locally when requested.
