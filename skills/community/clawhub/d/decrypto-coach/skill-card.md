## Description:

Strategic coach for the board game Decrypto (截码战), helping users practice clue-giving, analyze interceptions, learn strategy, and review past rounds.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[enihsago](https://clawhub.ai/user/enihsago)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Board-game players and teams use this skill to generate ranked Decrypto clue sets, reason about opponent clue histories, choose interception attempts, and review completed rounds in English or Chinese.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can recommend competitive misdirection and pre-agreed clue systems as part of normal Decrypto play.

Mitigation: Use the guidance within consensual board-game contexts and keep shared clue systems limited to the game session.

Risk: Advanced clue techniques such as character decomposition or hidden associations can confuse teammates if they are not aligned beforehand.

Mitigation: Pre-align special clue conventions with teammates before use and favor clearer clues when the team is at risk of miscommunication.
