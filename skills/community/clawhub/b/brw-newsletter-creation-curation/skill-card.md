## Description:

Industry-adaptive B2B newsletter creation with stage, role, and geography-aware workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Marketing operators, founders, and GTM teams use this skill to plan, draft, refine, and distribute B2B newsletters tailored to industry, company stage, role, and target geography. The skill supports issue blueprints, publish-ready markdown drafts, approval checkpoints, distribution notes, and KPI targets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with explicit India-first and US-first guidance.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Newsletter claims may be inaccurate, unsupported, or sensitive in regulated, HR, fintech, or employee-led contexts.

Mitigation: Verify claims and add manager, legal, or compliance review before publication when the topic or role requires it.

Risk: Users may expose confidential company metrics, customer details, or internal plans while drafting public content.

Mitigation: Use only authorized information, anonymize examples where appropriate, and confirm approval before sharing company-specific metrics.

Risk: Market and distribution guidance may be less reliable outside the India-first and US-first contexts described by the artifact.

Mitigation: Provide local market, channel, regulatory, and timing context before relying on geography-specific recommendations.
