## Description:

Maps current and target career competencies side by side and produces a prioritized 1-2 quarter development plan focused on evidence-generating projects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and professionals use this skill to compare their current evidence against a target career ladder level, identify promotion gaps, and prepare a sequenced plan for manager calibration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may involve sensitive career, performance, or promotion details.

Mitigation: Share only information appropriate for the agent context and treat the result as manager-calibration preparation, not official HR guidance.

Risk: An inaccurate or flattering self-assessment could produce a misleading development plan.

Mitigation: Use the actual career rubric, current evidence, and manager alignment checks to calibrate gaps before acting on the plan.
