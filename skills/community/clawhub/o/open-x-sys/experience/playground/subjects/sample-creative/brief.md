# Field Notes on Small Tools: the brief

I write one issue a week about small, well-made software tools and the people who make them. Each issue is one tool, looked at closely: what it does, a decision its maker got right, and what I would steal from it.

Who it is for: people who build things alone or in very small teams and care about craft.

What I want: 2,000 subscribers by the end of the year (I have about 575 after twelve issues), and for an issue to feel like a letter from someone who actually used the thing.

What I will not do: listicles, sponsored reviews, anything I have not used myself for at least a week.

Constraints: I have a day job. I can give this about eight hours a week, mostly Sunday. I would like it to be six.
