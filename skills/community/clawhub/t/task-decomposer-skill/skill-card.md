## Description:

Task Decomposer Skill helps agents break complex requirements into executable task lists with dependencies, estimates, milestones, priorities, and acceptance criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[whiskeyforsun](https://clawhub.ai/user/whiskeyforsun)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, project planners, and engineering teams use this skill to analyze requirements and turn them into small, trackable implementation tasks, estimates, dependency plans, milestones, and acceptance criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill's default workflow and templates are primarily in Chinese, which may reduce clarity for users who expect another language.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to produce task plans in the desired language and review the generated plan before use.

Risk: Task estimates, priorities, dependencies, and acceptance criteria are planning guidance and may be incomplete or inaccurate for a specific project.

Mitigation: Have a project owner or engineer review generated plans, adjust estimates and dependencies, and confirm acceptance criteria before execution.
