## Description:

Runs a structured PM design review against product requirements, producing requirements coverage, UX concerns, open questions, and an explicit approval status.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, designers, and engineering partners use this skill to review Figma designs against product requirements before handoff. It helps identify missing requirements coverage, incomplete user-flow states, PM concerns, open questions, and the approval decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Design screenshots, summaries, or requirements may contain sensitive product information.

Mitigation: Provide only Figma materials, summaries, and product requirements that are appropriate to share with the reviewing agent.

Risk: A review can be incomplete or misleading if the design is assessed without requirements, user-flow context, or review stage.

Mitigation: Supply the PRD, acceptance criteria, user flow, and review stage before relying on the approval decision.
