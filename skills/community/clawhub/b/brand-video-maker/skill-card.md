## Description:

Brand Video Maker is an AI tool for replacing logos, packaging, product shots, and brand elements inside existing videos without reshooting, helping marketing teams localize campaigns, swap branded assets, adapt visuals for different markets, and generate new brand videos from winning source footage.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketing teams and content creators use this skill to guide AI-assisted replacement of logos, packaging, product shots, text overlays, and brand colors in existing videos for localization, white-label content, product placement, and A/B testing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing through an unverified installer path could expose the user to unexpected installation behavior.

Mitigation: Use a pinned or verified ClawHub installer path when available and avoid running install commands with elevated privileges.

Risk: Video and brand assets may include sensitive or proprietary material.

Mitigation: Provide only the source videos and brand assets needed for the specific editing task.

Risk: AI-assisted brand replacement can create misleading or unauthorized brand depictions if used outside an approved campaign.

Mitigation: Confirm brand rights, placement approvals, and localization requirements before generating or publishing edited video assets.
