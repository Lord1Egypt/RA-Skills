## Description: <br>
商业分析超级工作台 is a Chinese-language business analysis workbench that helps users produce structured analysis, requirements, business cases, financial analysis, stakeholder analysis, process analysis, and related BA deliverables using established BA and consulting frameworks. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[yinjianheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/yinjianheng) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
Personal Use Only <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Business analysts, product teams, consultants, and learners use this skill to structure Chinese-language business analysis work across strategy, market research, competitive analysis, finance, requirements, solution evaluation, process improvement, data analysis, risk analysis, and change management. It is suited for drafting and organizing analysis artifacts, with generated business or financial recommendations reviewed before decisions. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated financial or business recommendations may be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable for a specific decision. <br>
Mitigation: Independently review generated recommendations, verify key data, and consult qualified professionals before using outputs for business, financial, or technical decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill may activate broadly on generic business-analysis terms and add repeated license or disclaimer text to outputs. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for Chinese-language BA template and methodology work, and review outputs for relevance, scope, and repeated notices before sharing. <br>
Risk: The artifact states personal learning, research, and non-commercial use restrictions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm intended use complies with the stated Personal Use Only terms before deployment or redistribution. <br>
