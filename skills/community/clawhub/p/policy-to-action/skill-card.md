## Description:

政策翻译官 —— 官话 → 人话 → 行动。把复杂政策条文转化为「能不能用、能省多少、该怎么做」的标准化报告，企业/个人通用，双输出 HTML + 可转发文本总览。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and business operators use this skill to turn Chinese policy or tax materials into an actionable assessment for a company or individual. It guides policy research, profile matching, benefit estimation, prioritized next steps, and report generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can auto-trigger broad Chinese policy or tax workflows and produce concrete tax-planning guidance.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation, treat outputs as draft informational material, and have filing, deduction, invoicing, or entity-structure decisions reviewed by a qualified tax professional.

Risk: The workflow may use web research and business or personal profile details to assess policy eligibility and benefits.

Mitigation: Provide only appropriate profile details and review generated reports before sharing or acting on them.
