## Description:

Write a board pre-read that's sent before the meeting so the meeting is about decisions, not status.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Company leaders, founders, and operators use this skill to prepare concise board pre-read materials before a board meeting. It structures the update around headline context, metrics versus plan, recent changes, decisions and asks, and risks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Board materials can include confidential financial, runway, strategy, and operating data.

Mitigation: Only provide information that is appropriate for the agent to process, and review the generated pre-read before sharing it with directors or other recipients.
