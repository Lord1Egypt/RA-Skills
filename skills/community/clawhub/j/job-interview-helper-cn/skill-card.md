## Description:

面向大学生的中文求职面试助手，支持简历优化、面试模拟、求职进度管理和行业信息速查。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smallkeyboy](https://clawhub.ai/user/smallkeyboy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External job seekers, especially university students, use this Chinese-language skill to improve resumes against job descriptions, practice interviews, track applications, and draft job-search communications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process resume details, job-search records, and other personal career information supplied by users.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive personal information and review generated resumes, tracking tables, and messages before reuse.

Risk: Salary ranges and industry trends are static guidance dated 2024-2025.

Mitigation: Verify salary, market, and industry trend claims against current sources before making career or compensation decisions.
