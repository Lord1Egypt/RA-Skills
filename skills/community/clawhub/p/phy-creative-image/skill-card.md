This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, brand, and design teams use this skill to create image-generation prompts for social visuals, banners, product mockups, pitch decks, event graphics, and campaign assets that stay within CanMarket brand constraints.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated image prompts may not match the intended brand style, platform choice, or audience language without review.

Mitigation: Review and adapt prompts against the included CanMarket examples and brand rules before using them in production creative workflows.
