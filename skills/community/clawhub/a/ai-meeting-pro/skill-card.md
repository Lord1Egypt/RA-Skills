## Description:

AI meeting assistant for agenda generation, minute taking, and action item tracking using Manager-Worker pattern.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[banxian87](https://clawhub.ai/user/banxian87)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, meeting facilitators, and team leads use this skill to draft agendas, capture minutes, record decisions, and track follow-up action items from meeting discussions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting transcripts may contain sensitive, confidential, regulated, customer, legal, personnel, or proprietary information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with appropriate participant consent and organizational approval, and avoid submitting protected content unless the environment and data-handling policy allow it.

Risk: Generated minutes, decisions, action items, owners, or deadlines may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Have a meeting owner review outputs before sharing them or using them to assign follow-up work.
