## Description:

Turn requirement materials into a clickable prototype that plays business workflows over time, with playback controls, visible actors and data changes, and deterministic mocks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[advnljs](https://clawhub.ai/user/advnljs)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and product teams use this skill to turn requirement documents and sample materials into an interactive prototype for reviewing screens, business rules, actors, data changes, and workflow timing. It is suited to discussion demos across approvals, integrations, data processing, dashboards, automation, and similar IT projects.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence reports a potential privacy risk involving spreadsheet data.

Mitigation: Review the spreadsheet data the workflow may handle and use non-sensitive examples until its handling is confirmed.

Risk: The security evidence reports a possibility of unrelated browser sessions being stopped.

Mitigation: Before installation, save work and confirm which browser sessions may be affected; evaluate in an isolated browser profile when practical.
