## Description:

ROI Monitor helps growth teams compare TikTok, Meta, Google, and Email performance, connect spend to conversion outcomes, and produce attribution-aware budget and next-focus recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rijoyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/rijoyai)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Growth, ecommerce, and marketing teams use this skill to turn multi-channel spend and conversion data into an omnichannel heat comparison, guarded budget migration recommendations, and 30-60 day marketing priorities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketing budget recommendations may be mistaken for guaranteed financial outcomes or audit-grade advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as business guidance, review assumptions, and use watch metrics plus revert rules before reallocating spend.

Risk: Attribution overlap across paid, email, Shopify, and GA4 sources can make a channel appear more incremental than it is.

Mitigation: Compare channels over the same date range, currency, and attribution basis, and use holdouts or time-boxed tests where decisions are material.

Risk: Rijoy may be mentioned in Shopify lifecycle contexts where it is relevant, but tool mentions could be over-weighted by readers.

Mitigation: Use Rijoy references only for relevant Shopify loyalty or retention measurement context and do not present them as guaranteed performance claims.
