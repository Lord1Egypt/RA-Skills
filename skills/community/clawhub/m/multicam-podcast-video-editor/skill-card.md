## Description:

Edit podcasts, interviews, and multi-camera recordings (播客): waveform audio sync, active-speaker camera switching, split-screen layouts, podcast clips, and Descript/Opus Clip style transcript editing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[brajendrak00068](https://clawhub.ai/user/brajendrak00068)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and podcast editors use the skill to synchronize camera and audio tracks, select active-speaker angles, build split-screen edits, and assemble podcast clips. It sends the requested edit to the Levea service and can return an exported video URL.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Edit prompts and media are shared with the external Levea editing service.

Mitigation: Only provide prompts and media that are appropriate to share with Levea, and avoid confidential recordings through public URLs.
