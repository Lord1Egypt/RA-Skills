## Description:

Plan and execute fair GitLab hackathon participation, including Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons, by analyzing rules, selecting qualifying issues/MRs, tracking scoring levers, and keeping an exploit watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to plan fair participation in GitLab Quarterly and Transcend Hackathons. It helps them verify current requirements, choose reviewable contributions, and track scoring while avoiding abusive or invalid activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hackathon rules, dates, scoring, and eligibility can change between events.

Mitigation: Check the linked official event pages and contribution guide before planning or acting.

Risk: Optimizing points through spam, padded commits, inaccurate labels, or invalid issue closures can undermine fair participation.

Mitigation: Keep contributions genuine and reviewable, and only comment, label, or close issues when it advances valid work.
