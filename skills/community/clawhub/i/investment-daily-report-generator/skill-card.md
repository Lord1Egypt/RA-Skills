Mitigation: Review each generated report against the cited source names and publication times before using it for outreach or decision-making.

Risk: The skill is designed for Chinese招商 reporting and may be inappropriate for users who do not need location-specific public policy and industry intelligence.

Mitigation: Install and use it only for Chinese investment-attraction workflows that can rely on public web sources.
