## Description:

Helps an agent decide whether a user should learn something now, defer it, or learn only the smallest useful subset by focusing on the concrete problem, existing confirmed knowledge, knowledge decay, ROI, and explore-versus-apply tradeoffs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[li-evan](https://clawhub.ai/user/li-evan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill during learning-planning conversations to turn vague learning interest into a concrete learn, defer, or minimum-useful-scope decision. It is especially suited to time, energy, and project tradeoffs where current knowledge may already be sufficient.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer learning-planning discussions toward concrete goals, minimal useful study, and deferring low-ROI topics.

Mitigation: Users should clarify their actual goal and language preference, then confirm whether a minimal or deferred learning plan fits their needs.
