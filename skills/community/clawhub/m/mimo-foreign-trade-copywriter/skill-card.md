## Description:

Generates localized foreign-trade marketing copy for B2B outreach, including cold emails, inquiry replies, product descriptions, exhibition invitations, social posts, and follow-up messages in English, Spanish, Arabic, French, Portuguese, Russian, and related target-market contexts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External trade, sales, and marketing users can use this skill to draft multilingual B2B export copy and adapt it for customer outreach, marketplace listings, product pages, social channels, and exhibition follow-up. Users should confirm target-market facts, product claims, certification requirements, and legal wording before sending or publishing the drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may contain incorrect market, certification, legal, or product-claim details if the user supplies incomplete or unverified inputs.

Mitigation: Confirm the target market, preferred language, product facts, certifications, and legal claims before using generated copy externally.

Risk: Localization guidance may not reflect current local laws, cultural norms, or buyer expectations for a specific transaction.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer check localized wording, compliance-sensitive claims, and trade terms for the target country or region.
