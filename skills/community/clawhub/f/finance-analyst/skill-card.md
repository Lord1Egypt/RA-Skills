## Description:

Professional Financial Advisor (FA) skill for primary market financing - replaces traditional investment advisory services with AI-powered project assessment, pitch deck generation, valuation analysis, and investor matching

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhenstaff](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhenstaff)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Startup founders and investors use this skill to assess fundraising readiness, build pitch deck and business plan outlines, estimate valuations, match investors, and prepare investment analysis materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide confidential company plans, financial statements, founder biographies, investor lists, or PDFs.

Mitigation: Use redacted or example data where possible and review generated reports before sharing them.

Risk: Valuation estimates, investor matches, and fundraising recommendations may be incomplete or misleading if inputs, market assumptions, or investor data are stale.

Mitigation: Validate outputs with professional advisors, current market research, and up-to-date investor information before acting on them.

Risk: Optional PDF/OCR setup includes system package installation commands.

Mitigation: Run optional package installation commands only after reviewing and approving the packages for the target environment.
