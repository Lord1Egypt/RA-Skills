## Description:

Self-Relationship helps an agent guide self-reflection conversations so users can understand themselves, separate facts from feelings and judgments, avoid identity labels, and identify grounded choices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to support reflective conversations about self-acceptance, self-understanding, personality-test framing, self-criticism, and life choices. It is intended to help clarify facts, feelings, interpretations, judgments, and choices without providing diagnosis, crisis support, or major life decisions on the user's behalf.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat reflective guidance as medical, psychological, or clinical diagnosis.

Mitigation: State clear boundaries, avoid diagnosis, and recommend qualified professional help for serious psychological distress, crisis, or medical concerns.

Risk: Broad self-reflection triggers may activate during sensitive personal topics.

Mitigation: Use a restrained, nonjudgmental posture that clarifies facts, feelings, interpretations, judgments, and choices without deciding for the user.

Risk: Personality tests or temporary emotional states may be overinterpreted as identity labels.

Mitigation: Frame tests and states as reflection inputs rather than definitions of who the user is or what they can become.
