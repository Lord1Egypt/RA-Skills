## Description:

Provides China domestic marketing copy compliance guidance covering advertising law red lines, cross-platform rules, and WeChat, Douyin, and Xiaohongshu prohibited terms, replacement wording, and risk logic.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, content, and operations teams use this skill to review and rewrite China-facing promotional copy for common domestic channels. It helps agents check platform-specific risk terms, avoid unsupported regulated claims, and suggest compliant alternatives.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security summary says the skill can lead an agent to create risky marketing copy or disguise regulated claims.

Mitigation: Use it for compliance review and safe rewrites only; remove unsupported claims instead of rephrasing them to bypass moderation.

Risk: The security guidance flags public skin-photo solicitation as a risk.

Mitigation: Avoid prompts that request public skin photos or other sensitive personal content; keep review inputs limited to the marketing copy being assessed.

Risk: The security guidance flags reliance on an unaudited companion skill.

Mitigation: Review any companion skill before use and do not treat companion output as compliance-approved without separate checking.
