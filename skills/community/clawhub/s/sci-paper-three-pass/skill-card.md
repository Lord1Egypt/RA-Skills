## Description:

SCI论文三轮滚动精修助手 uses a three-pass academic-editing method to reduce rhetorical density, label evidence strength, and surface caveats for SCI papers, core-journal manuscripts, and academic reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External researchers and academic authors use this skill to refine manuscripts by preserving meaning, tightening academic expression, adding evidence-level labels, and identifying caveats before journal or report submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Anti-AI-detection rewriting could be misused to hide AI assistance or misrepresent authorship in academic work.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for transparent editing and follow all journal, university, funder, and employer disclosure rules.

Risk: Unpublished manuscripts and document paths may contain sensitive research, authorship, or institutional information.

Mitigation: Handle manuscript text and file paths as sensitive input and avoid sharing content beyond the intended editing workflow.

Risk: Manuscript revisions may unintentionally overstate evidence or change the meaning of scientific claims.

Mitigation: Review every revision against the source manuscript, data, citations, and target journal requirements before submission.
