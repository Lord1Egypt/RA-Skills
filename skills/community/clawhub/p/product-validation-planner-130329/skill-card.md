## Description:

Helps solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It helps turn validation needs into practical workflows, checklists, lightweight analyses, implementation support, and verification notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad startup, SaaS, validation, and prototype triggers may route general product requests to this skill.

Mitigation: Use specific invocation wording or disable implicit invocation when tighter routing is needed.

Risk: The skill provides advisory planning output that may be incomplete or misleading if the user's constraints or evidence are weak.

Mitigation: Review assumptions, success criteria, and verification notes before using outputs for product or investment decisions.
