## Description:

Helps agents guide position sizing, stop-loss placement, reward-to-risk checks, capital limits, and pyramiding discipline for trading risk management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and trading-focused agents use this skill to apply disciplined money-management heuristics before or during trade planning. It is intended for educational risk-management support, not personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides trading risk-management heuristics that could be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as educational support and require users to make their own financial decisions or consult a qualified professional.

Risk: The skill content is largely Chinese-language, which may limit reviewability for some users.

Mitigation: Have bilingual reviewers or qualified translators review the guidance before relying on it in production workflows.
