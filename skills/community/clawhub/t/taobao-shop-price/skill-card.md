## Description:

Searches and compares product prices across major Chinese e-commerce platforms, filters relevant results, ranks recommendations, and returns purchase links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xchicky](https://clawhub.ai/user/xchicky)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External shoppers and shopping assistants use this skill to search Taobao, Tmall, JD.com, Pinduoduo, Douyin, Kuaishou, 1688, and related Chinese e-commerce platforms for comparable products, prices, coupons, sales signals, and purchase links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shopping searches and link generation send queries and stable identifiers to maishou88.com and may create attributed share or referral links.

Mitigation: Review before installing, disclose third-party data sharing and link-attribution behavior to users, and prefer a version that removes hardcoded identifiers or makes them clearly configurable.
