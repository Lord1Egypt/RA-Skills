## Description:

Claw Meet is an AI meeting assistant for preparing meetings, generating agendas, taking structured notes, extracting action items, writing summaries, and improving meeting effectiveness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiwenbing](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiwenbing)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external meeting participants use this skill to prepare agendas, structure notes, extract action items, and draft summaries for recurring syncs, decisions, 1:1s, and other meetings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic meeting or agenda prompts may route to this skill because of its broad activation wording.

Mitigation: Use it for meeting productivity tasks and review generated agendas, notes, summaries, and action items before sharing or acting on them.
