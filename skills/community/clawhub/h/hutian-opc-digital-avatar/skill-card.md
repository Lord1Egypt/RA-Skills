## Description:

A digital avatar configuration workshop for OPC scenarios, covering appearance, voice, language style, background, duration, and platform presets or customization.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, OPC community operators, and developers use this skill to plan and generate digital-avatar video configurations, including persona templates, visual style, voice settings, language style, backgrounds, duration, and target platform output.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated scripts or videos may contain built-in promotional phrases.

Mitigation: Review and remove promotional language before using generated scripts or publishing videos.

Risk: Voice cloning can misuse a person's voice or reference audio.

Mitigation: Use voice cloning only with clear permission from the speaker, and treat reference audio as sensitive data.

Risk: Official or government-style avatars can imply authority or endorsement.

Mitigation: Avoid official or government imagery unless authorized, and clearly review intended use before publication.

Risk: Cloud avatar services may require API keys and uploads of sensitive avatar, voice, or project assets.

Mitigation: Protect API keys, review cloud upload requirements, and avoid sharing sensitive assets without approval.
