## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It structures product validation work into repeatable planning, checklist, prototype, positioning, analysis, and follow-up outputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad startup, SaaS, prototype, or business prompts.

Mitigation: Use explicit prompts or disable implicit invocation when tighter routing is needed.

Risk: Product validation work can involve sensitive business context.

Mitigation: Share only the business details needed for the validation task.

Risk: Planning outputs may contain incorrect assumptions or misleading validation guidance.

Mitigation: Review the output against real customer evidence and the stated success criteria before acting on it.
