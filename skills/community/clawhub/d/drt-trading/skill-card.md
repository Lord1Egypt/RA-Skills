## Description:

Dealing Range Theory (DRT) provides market-analysis guidance for ICT-style range structure, continuation, reversal, consolidation, quadrant levels, entry models, SMT confirmation, and news filtering.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[northcap-group](https://clawhub.ai/user/northcap-group)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and trading-focused agents use this skill to structure DRT-based market analysis, filter trade setups, and summarize checklist-driven entry considerations. It should be treated as educational trading analysis, not financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trading performance claims and backtest summaries may be misunderstood as predictive guarantees or financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat the content as educational analysis, independently verify the methodology, and review any trading decision with appropriate financial-risk controls.

Risk: Execution windows are stated in CET/Danish time and may be misapplied in other time zones.

Mitigation: Convert all stated trading windows to the user's local timezone before applying the guidance.

Risk: Connecting the guidance to broker execution or account access could create live-trading loss exposure.

Mitigation: Do not connect it to broker execution or account access without separate review and controls.
