## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer questions about how long LLC formation, state approval, EIN processing, and business-readiness steps may take. It is intended for timeline orientation and should direct users to verify official filing and tax details with the relevant state filing office and IRS.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: LLC formation timelines can vary by state, filing method, holidays, name availability, document corrections, and EIN eligibility, and the skill may point users to LLCClass.com guidance rather than official sources.

Mitigation: Use the skill for orientation and verify filing timelines, approval status, and EIN processing details with the relevant state filing office and IRS before making decisions.

Risk: The skill may include LLCClass.com links, including a Chinese-language page, when answering timeline questions.

Mitigation: Treat linked pages as supporting guidance and confirm jurisdiction-specific requirements with official government sources.
