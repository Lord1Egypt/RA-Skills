## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Smart contract write examples can lead to irreversible loss if used against the wrong chain, contract, recipient, token decimals, or amount.

Mitigation: Verify transaction details, simulate first, prefer a testnet or local chain, and require explicit confirmation before broadcasting.

Risk: Wallet seed phrases, private keys, and approvals can expose funds if mishandled.

Mitigation: Keep seed phrases offline, avoid hardcoded keys, review approvals before signing, and revoke unused approvals.
