## Description:

Helps agents create and maintain a product marketing context document with offering, audience, persona, positioning, customer-language, proof-point, and goal details for downstream marketing tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mariokarras](https://clawhub.ai/user/mariokarras)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and marketing teams use this skill to create or update a reusable product marketing context file before running other marketing workflows. It supports products, services, and hybrid offerings across B2B and B2C contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Auto-drafting may read project materials and summarize private strategy, customer names, pricing, or positioning into a local marketing context document.

Mitigation: Review the generated context before relying on it or sharing it outside the project.
