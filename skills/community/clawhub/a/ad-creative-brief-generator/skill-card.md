## Description:

Turn campaign goals, product facts, audience context, and channel constraints into clearer ad creative briefs that teams or creators can actually execute.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Performance marketing, creative, creator, and growth teams use this skill to turn campaign inputs into execution-ready ad creative briefs with message hierarchy, proof direction, CTA logic, and guardrails.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Campaign plans, customer data, unreleased claims, or sensitive product details could be included in prompts.

Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential or unreleased information unless the agent environment is approved for that data.

Risk: Ad briefs can contain claims that are too broad, unsupported, or sensitive for the target market.

Mitigation: Review proof points, forbidden claims, compliance boundaries, and risk-safe wording before production use.
