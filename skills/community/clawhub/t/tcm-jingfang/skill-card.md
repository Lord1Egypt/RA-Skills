## Description:

tcm-jingfang helps agents provide Chinese-language traditional Chinese medicine study references for Jingfang and Han Tang formulas using pattern differentiation, formula lookup, dosage logic, herb notes, and safety disclaimers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ahfai1](https://clawhub.ai/user/ahfai1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill as a Chinese-language TCM study aid for symptom-to-formula discussion, formula lookup, herb lookup, and pattern-differentiation explanations. It should not be used as medical diagnosis, a prescription, or a substitute for licensed clinical care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can provide high-risk medical prescription guidance, including toxic-herb dosing and serious-disease claims.

Mitigation: Use outputs only as study/reference material, require licensed medical review for real health decisions, and avoid relying on the skill for diagnosis, prescriptions, cancer, bleeding, pregnancy, children, older adults, severe symptoms, or chronic disease management.

Risk: Users may self-administer formulas or toxic herbs based on generated output.

Mitigation: Keep explicit disclaimers visible, direct users to licensed medical professionals, and do not treat generated formulas as actionable prescriptions.
