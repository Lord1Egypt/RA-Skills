## Use Case:

External users use this skill to request trending bar recommendations, compare venues by city, budget, wait time, reservation availability, and preferences, and plan practical nightlife routes.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Nightlife recommendations can become stale or inaccurate for hours, pricing, reservation availability, and venue rules.

Mitigation: Verify current venue details with reliable local sources or the venue before relying on the recommendation.
