## Description:

Coach for people running multiple side hustles in parallel who need to decide what to keep, kill, or scale while planning a path away from day-job dependency without burning out.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[charlie-morrison](https://clawhub.ai/user/charlie-morrison)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users with 3-8 active income streams use this skill to audit portfolio health, prioritize which streams to kill or scale, calculate replacement-income targets, and create a focused 30/60/90 day plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake business coaching and tax or entity examples for professional legal, tax, financial, immigration, or healthcare advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as planning guidance and direct users to qualified professionals for legal, tax, financial, immigration, healthcare, and country-specific decisions.

Risk: Country-specific assumptions, especially US tax and entity guidance, may not apply to non-US users.

Mitigation: Ask for the user's country and adapt recommendations, or avoid jurisdiction-specific claims when the user has not provided a location.

Risk: Portfolio recommendations can be misleading if users provide incomplete revenue, cost, time, runway, or burnout information.

Mitigation: Run the diagnostic intake before recommendations and label uncertain recommendations when key numbers are missing.
