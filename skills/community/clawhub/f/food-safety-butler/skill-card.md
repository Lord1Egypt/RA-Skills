## Description:

Helps restaurant operators turn PRC food-safety requirements, HACCP, GB 31654-2021, licensing, complaint response, and group risk controls into practical checklists, audit reports, workflows, scripts, and directives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[monsterdt](https://clawhub.ai/user/monsterdt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Restaurant operators, food-safety managers, and group compliance teams use this skill to perform self-checks, license preparation, complaint response, HACCP and GB 31654 gap analysis, and multi-store risk control.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China / PRC-oriented use; verify local regulator requirements before operational reliance.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Regulatory, licensing, and compensation guidance may vary by local authority and may become outdated.

Mitigation: Verify requirements with the relevant local regulator or qualified advisor before making operational, licensing, or legal decisions.

Risk: Food-safety audits and complaint traces may involve personal data, customer details, supplier records, or sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary details, redact personal or commercially sensitive data where possible, and follow internal data-handling rules.

Risk: The skill can produce operational compliance guidance but cannot replace official inspections, legal advice, or management accountability.

Mitigation: Use outputs as review aids and have responsible staff validate findings, corrective actions, and communications before execution.
