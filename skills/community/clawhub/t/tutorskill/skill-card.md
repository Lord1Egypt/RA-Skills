## Description:

Helps students prepare for exams by extracting definitions, key points, keywords, diagram notes, exam-ready sentences, and practice questions from user-provided study material and a syllabus.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ayushsaklani-min](https://clawhub.ai/user/ayushsaklani-min)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use this skill to turn their notes or PDFs and syllabus into focused exam preparation material, including definitions, examiner-expected key points, topic priorities, MCQ tricks or written answer lines, and recall questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intentionally restrictive and may be less useful for open-ended tutoring or general knowledge explanations.

Mitigation: Use it for exam preparation from supplied notes or PDFs and a syllabus; use a general tutoring workflow when outside knowledge is required.

Risk: Missing or incomplete study material can lead to missing-topic responses instead of full explanations.

Mitigation: Provide both the study material and the syllabus before relying on the output.

Risk: If the notes and syllabus use different topic names or scopes, the answer may follow the notes rather than the expected syllabus wording.

Mitigation: Review any note that the material covers a topic under a different name and confirm it matches the exam scope.
