## Description:

Provides a structured investment research framework for analyzing stocks, commodities, ETFs, and themes across macro conditions, scarcity, company quality, timing, scoring, and risk rebuttals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[97fusky-droid](https://clawhub.ai/user/97fusky-droid)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and investment researchers use this skill to structure medium- to long-horizon research on stocks, commodities, ETFs, themes, and regional allocation. It is best used as a research prompt framework whose outputs should be checked against current primary sources before any real investment decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on position-style labels as personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research prompts only and require human review before using them for real investing.

Risk: Investment analysis may be based on stale or incomplete market data.

Mitigation: Verify current facts against primary sources such as regulatory filings, company reports, central bank data, exchange data, and official statistics.
