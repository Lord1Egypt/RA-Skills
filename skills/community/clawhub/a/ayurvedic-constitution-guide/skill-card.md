## Description:

Helps users reflect on Ayurvedic constitution patterns and get low-risk nutrition, lifestyle, and wellness tips with medical safety boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[slakov](https://clawhub.ai/user/slakov)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for a reflective, non-diagnostic Ayurvedic constitution conversation that estimates dosha patterns and suggests ordinary food, sleep, movement, stress, and seasonal-comfort practices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat Ayurvedic constitution scoring or wellness suggestions as medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

Mitigation: Present results as informal reflection, keep suggestions conservative and ordinary, and direct users with symptoms, diagnoses, medications, pregnancy, breastfeeding, restricted diets, allergies, or eating-disorder history to a qualified clinician.

Risk: Users may ask for herbs, supplements, detoxes, cleanses, fasting, panchakarma, medication changes, or disease-specific claims.

Mitigation: Do not recommend those interventions; keep responses educational and refer product, medication, urgent, or disease-management questions to appropriate medical care.
