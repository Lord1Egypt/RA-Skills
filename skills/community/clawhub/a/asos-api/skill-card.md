## Description:

Provides ASOS product search, details, reviews, market listings, and online prices and stock by country, currency, and size through Scavio.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[scavio-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/scavio-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and shopping agents use this skill to retrieve ASOS catalog and review information and compare country-specific prices and online size stock. They can poll readings over time to build a price history, which ASOS does not provide.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shopping requests, including queries and filters, may be sent to Scavio; the security scan found no explicit privacy notice.

Mitigation: Avoid including unrelated personal or sensitive information in shopping queries and filters.
