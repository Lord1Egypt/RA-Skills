## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to structure public-information research on Chinese ST/*ST stocks, focusing on financial reversal, restructuring progress, technical signals, capital flows, sentiment, and risk boundaries. It is intended to produce concise analytical reports with source cross-checking and general risk disclaimers.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill text asks the agent to save every completed report as a PDF to a fixed local downloads folder.

Mitigation: Review or modify the skill so reports are exported only after the user explicitly requests an export and confirms the destination.

Risk: ST/*ST stock analysis can be mistaken for investment advice even when based on public information.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as informational research, preserve the skill's risk disclaimer, cite data sources, and ask users to make independent decisions.
