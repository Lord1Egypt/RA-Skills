This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated fundraising answers may infer assumptions from a short brief and present them confidently.

Mitigation: Review each answer against source facts, replace marked assumptions, and keep risk framing specific and honest before using the output externally.
