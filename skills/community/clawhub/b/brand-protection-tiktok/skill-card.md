## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is presented as TikTok Shop brand protection, but server security evidence says the packaged scripts produce Amazon-focused enforcement guidance and complaint templates.

Mitigation: Do not rely on the generated materials for TikTok Shop enforcement without reviewing and replacing platform-specific workflow, portal, and complaint language.

Risk: The artifact suggests an unpinned global npx installation command.

Mitigation: Install in an isolated or project-local environment and pin or verify the installer version before use.

Risk: Generated infringement, takedown, and complaint materials may contain legal or platform-policy claims that require context-specific review.

Mitigation: Have qualified staff review evidence, platform fit, and legal language before sending reports or notices.
