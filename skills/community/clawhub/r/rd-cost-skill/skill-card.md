## Description:

Rd Cost helps Chinese-language business, finance, and project teams estimate R&D expenses, build budgets, analyze project costs and ROI, and produce compliance-oriented planning reports for China-context high-tech enterprise and R&D super-deduction workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, enterprise R&D teams, finance teams, and project managers use this skill to estimate R&D budgets, check project cost structure, analyze return on investment, and prepare China-context R&D expense planning reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce China-context compliance and tax planning guidance that may be mistaken for authoritative legal or tax advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a planning aid and confirm jurisdiction-specific conclusions with qualified legal, tax, or compliance reviewers before relying on it.

Risk: Budget estimates may be inaccurate if required project inputs are missing, stale, or based on unsuitable assumptions.

Mitigation: Collect the required project parameters, review formulas and coefficients against current internal standards, and validate estimates before budgeting decisions.
