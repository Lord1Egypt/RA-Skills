## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may encourage code changes during engineering work, and incorrect simplification could remove needed safeguards or produce misleading guidance.

Mitigation: Use it in repositories where normal agent-assisted edits are acceptable, review proposed changes, preserve critical safeguards, and run a targeted verification command for non-trivial logic.
