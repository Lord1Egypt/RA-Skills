## Description:

Generate a full week of social media content for any topic, with platform-optimized posts for Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and Instagram plus hashtags and posting times.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[claudiodrusus](https://clawhub.ai/user/claudiodrusus)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and small teams use this skill to draft a reusable weekly social media calendar for a topic and target audience. It creates a Markdown planning file with daily Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and Instagram draft prompts, posting times, hashtags, and content strategy notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generator creates or overwrites content-calendar.md in the current directory.

Mitigation: Run it from a working folder where that filename is safe to replace, or move an existing content-calendar.md before execution.

Risk: Generated calendar entries are reusable draft templates and may include generic or inaccurate marketing guidance.

Mitigation: Review and edit every post, hashtag, posting time, and platform recommendation before publishing.
