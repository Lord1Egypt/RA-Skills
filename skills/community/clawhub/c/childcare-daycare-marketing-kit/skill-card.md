## Description:

Generates compliance-aware marketing materials and parent communications for licensed childcare centers, daycares, preschools, and home daycare providers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitflopez](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitflopez)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Childcare providers, center directors, and marketers use this skill to draft enrollment campaigns, parent communications, seasonal promotions, local ads, SEO pages, and review responses with prompts that call out childcare-specific compliance checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing copy may include unverified claims about licensing, accreditation, ratios, pricing, testimonials, or photo and media consent.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as draft copy and verify the center's facts, consent records, and applicable childcare requirements before publishing.

Risk: Childcare marketing involves children and families, so inaccurate or overbroad copy can create regulatory or trust concerns.

Mitigation: Review all public-facing copy against current state childcare rules and relevant COPPA, FTC, NAEYC, CACFP, TCPA, and CAN-SPAM obligations.
