This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Strategy, partnerships, and business development teams use this skill to produce competitive analysis, market sizing, pricing intelligence, opportunity scoring, and go-to-market recommendations across target market verticals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Filesystem access could expose unrelated confidential files if the skill is run in a broad workspace.

Mitigation: Grant filesystem access only to workspaces that contain research inputs or report outputs intended for this skill.

Risk: Market research, pricing, and opportunity recommendations can be incorrect or misleading if citations are weak or stale.

Mitigation: Review generated citations and validate important findings against at least two independent sources before using them for business decisions.
