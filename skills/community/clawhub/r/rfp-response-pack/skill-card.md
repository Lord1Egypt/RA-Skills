## Description:

Bid/RFP response pack — extract requirements from hundreds of tender pages, reverse-engineer the scoring method, generate point-by-point response drafts, deviation tables, and a 28-item disqualification (bid-failure) self-check.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chinabizskills](https://clawhub.ai/user/chinabizskills)

## Use Case:

Bid and proposal teams use this skill to extract tender requirements, map scored criteria to response actions, draft point-by-point bid materials, and check for disqualification issues. Its outputs are assisted drafts that require review by the bid owner and legal before submission.
