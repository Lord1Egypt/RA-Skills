## Description:

Provides Chinese-language reliability design guidance for reliability analysis, FMEA, quantitative reliability metrics, design verification, and quality improvement during product or system design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Quality, reliability, product, and systems engineers use this skill to structure reliability design reviews, run FMEA analysis, calculate reliability indicators such as MTBF or MTTF, and identify design improvement actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some referenced resources are empty in the inspected artifact.

Mitigation: Confirm required templates and reliability metric references before relying on them in a review workflow.

Risk: FMEA scoring and reliability metric calculations can be misleading when based on limited samples or subjective ratings.

Mitigation: Review outputs with qualified reliability or quality engineers and validate conclusions against sufficient test or field data.
