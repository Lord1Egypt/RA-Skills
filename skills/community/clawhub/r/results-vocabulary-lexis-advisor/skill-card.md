## Description:

Diagnoses academic vocabulary and collocation quality in English psychology Results sections, including colloquial wording, academic verb accuracy, figure and table referral norms, result qualifier precision, terminology consistency, and collocation naturalness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ziyi-z-z](https://clawhub.ai/user/ziyi-z-z)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, editors, and academic-writing assistants use this skill to review English psychology Results drafts for vocabulary, terminology, figure and table phrasing, and natural academic collocations. It returns structured diagnostic feedback with examples, severity, revision suggestions, scores, and handoff notes for related Results-writing checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides academic-writing advice and may produce revisions that are stylistically plausible but inappropriate for the manuscript, target journal, or statistical evidence.

Mitigation: Manually review suggested edits against the source manuscript, target journal requirements, and verified statistical or methodological context before adoption.

Risk: User-provided manuscripts may contain sensitive or unpublished research content.

Mitigation: Provide only manuscript text that the user is comfortable having an agent process.
