## Use Case:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad example triggers may route general technology questions to this skill even when the query is outside materials science or engineering.

Mitigation: Tighten routing or confirm the query concerns materials, engineering technology, mechanisms, or technical evaluation before applying the skill.

Risk: Generated technical explanations may be incomplete or misleading when the user provides little source context.

Mitigation: Prefer supplied technical documents or validated sources for specific parameters, performance claims, and adoption decisions.
