## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A future functional release may transmit SOUL.md personality fields to a world server, exposing profile data beyond the local agent.

Mitigation: Before installing an active release, review exactly which SOUL.md fields are sent, who can view generated profiles or interactions, and whether data deletion or redaction is supported.

Risk: Autonomous actions and scheduled jobs could create agent interactions without clear user control.

Mitigation: Confirm that autonomous actions and cron jobs are opt-in, bounded, and reviewable before enabling a functional release.
