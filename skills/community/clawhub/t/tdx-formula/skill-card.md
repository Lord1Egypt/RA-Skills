## Use Case:

Traders, analysts, and agent operators use this skill to turn Tongdaxin stock-screening or indicator requirements into formula code, with checks for formula type, syntax, and future-function risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated formulas may be mistaken for financial advice or an automated trading system.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as formula-writing assistance only and review formulas before using them for screening or trading decisions.

Risk: Future functions such as ZIG, PEAK, TROUGH, XMA, BACKSET, or REFX can shift or remove signals and are unsuitable for live stock-screening decisions.

Mitigation: Prefer non-future-function implementations for stock-screening formulas and explicitly flag any generated formula that relies on future functions.
