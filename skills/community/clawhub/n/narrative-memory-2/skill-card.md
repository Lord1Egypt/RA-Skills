## Description:

Keep memory as layered files you tend yourself — curated long-term record, dated daily logs, entity pages — with capture at the moment of making and corrections kept visible.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[torchkeeper-simon](https://clawhub.ai/user/torchkeeper-simon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External agent users can maintain continuity across sessions by writing durable facts, decisions, and preferences to a curated memory file, with dated logs and entity pages preserving context and corrections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automatically recorded memory can retain personal details across sessions.

Mitigation: Limit capture to information needed for future work, omit sensitive personal details, and review or delete records that are no longer needed.
