## Description:

UX 启发式评估专家。基于 Nielsen Norman 10 条可用性原则检查用户体验问题。当用户说「review UX」「检查用户体验」「UX 评估」「可用性检查」「启发式评估」时触发。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Designers, product teams, and developers use this skill to review pages, flows, and interface concepts against Nielsen Norman usability heuristics and produce a prioritized UX evaluation report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: UX review output may be incomplete or subjective when the agent has limited product context.

Mitigation: Have a human reviewer confirm high-priority findings against the actual user flow, audience, and product requirements before implementation.

Risk: The skill is written primarily in Chinese, which may lead to misunderstandings for teams expecting another language.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to provide the final report in the team's preferred language when needed.
