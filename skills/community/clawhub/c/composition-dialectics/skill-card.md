## Description:

Helps users reason about when to learn, follow, or intentionally break photography composition rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners and photography-focused agents use this skill to answer questions about the value of composition rules and to guide beginners or experienced photographers toward learning rules as tools before breaking them intentionally.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be misapplied to concrete composition technique questions or direct photo evaluation requests.

Mitigation: Use it for questions about the role of composition rules; route technique questions to composition-eight-questions and photo evaluation requests to photo-three-principles.

Risk: Users who need English-only responses may be surprised by the skill's mostly Chinese source content.

Mitigation: Preserve the user's requested language and document language expectations for English-only deployments.

Risk: Rule-breaking advice may be misunderstood as advice to ignore fundamentals.

Mitigation: Frame rule breaking as intentional expression after the user understands the basic rule or technique.
