## Description:

Build a realistic, conflict-free study plan from a user-provided schedule, tasks, and available time.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[serrhua](https://clawhub.ai/user/serrhua)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students use the skill to fit prioritized study tasks into user-provided availability while preserving fixed commitments and transition time. It produces a practical proposal based only on the information supplied in the conversation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A plan may miss conflicts or misjudge feasibility when the supplied commitments, available windows, time zone, or task durations are incomplete.

Mitigation: Confirm the relevant schedule details and assumptions with the user; label estimates clearly and review the proposed plan before relying on it.
