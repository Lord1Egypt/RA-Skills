## Description:

Create academic literature review PPTX slide decks from research papers or articles, translating content into Chinese while preserving citations, figures, tables, and statistical details.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, students, researchers, and presenters use this skill to turn academic papers into Chinese-language PPTX literature review decks for journal clubs, group meetings, paper reports, and related presentations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The default workflow produces Chinese-language PPTX output, which may not match requests for English, bilingual, or non-PPT deliverables.

Mitigation: Users should explicitly request a different language mix or output format when they need one.

Risk: Slide content can become misleading if source-paper extraction, translation, figure capture, table recreation, or statistical transcription is wrong.

Mitigation: Review generated decks against the original paper before presentation, with special attention to citations, figures, tables, hypotheses, and statistical values.
