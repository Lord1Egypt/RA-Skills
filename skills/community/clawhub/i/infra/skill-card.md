## Description:

Infra helps engineers, DevOps professionals, and technical founders manage, design, and troubleshoot infrastructure across cloud architecture, server configuration, networking, security hardening, cost optimization, incident response, and infrastructure as code.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to reason through infrastructure design, cloud operations, incident response, security hardening, cost optimization, and infrastructure-as-code work. It is suited for planning changes, troubleshooting operational issues, and drafting reviewable infrastructure guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Infrastructure guidance could propose changes that affect availability, security, or cost.

Mitigation: Review proposed changes against environment-specific requirements and apply them through normal change control, infrastructure-as-code review, and approval processes.

Risk: Incident-response recommendations may be based on incomplete operational evidence.

Mitigation: Confirm current logs, metrics, blast radius, and recent changes before acting on remediation steps.

Risk: Security-hardening advice may be too broad for a specific environment.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against least-privilege requirements, compliance obligations, and existing network and access policies before deployment.
