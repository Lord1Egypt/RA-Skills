## Description:

Deep ETL/ELT design workflow for extract patterns, transforms, loading strategies, idempotency, validation, and reconciliation when designing batch data flows or hardening pipelines for correctness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and data engineers use this skill to design or harden batch ETL/ELT pipelines, including source contracts, extract strategy, deterministic transforms, idempotent loading, validation, reconciliation, and backfill planning.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pipeline guidance may be applied to data with sensitivity or operational constraints the skill cannot know.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against the organization's data classification, access constraints, SLA, and reconciliation requirements before implementation.

Risk: Batch ETL/ELT guidance may be incomplete for near-real-time or streaming workflows.

Mitigation: Document micro-batch or streaming semantics separately before applying this workflow to near-real-time pipelines.
