## Description:

Analyze user-provided fandom elements to create LOFTER-style fanfiction and fandom posts with clickable titles, emotional outlines, chaptered drafts, review, and revision.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Writers and fandom-content creators use this skill to turn an IP, pairing, trope, or rough idea into LOFTER-native Chinese fanfiction workflows, including concept options, titles, hooks, outlines, drafts, critique, and revisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated fandom content may include sensitive tropes, rating concerns, or material that does not fit a community's norms.

Mitigation: Review generated content for fandom appropriateness, rating boundaries, sensitive tropes, and platform expectations before posting.

Risk: Bundled fanfiction samples may influence tone, pacing, and structure.

Mitigation: Use samples as style and packaging references only; avoid copying wording, scenes, or ordering from the sample material.
