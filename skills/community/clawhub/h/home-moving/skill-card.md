## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance for cross-city moving, local hauling, bulky-item packing, and transparent pricing and booking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[clawkk](https://clawhub.ai/user/clawkk)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External consumers or service agents use this skill to guide Chinese-language home moving, local hauling, bulky-item packing, and booking conversations, including pricing, service range, urgency, labor estimates, staff credentials, insurance coverage, and follow-up support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is written for Chinese-language moving-service scenarios, which may not match every installer's intended users or region.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended language and market before deployment, and localize or narrow trigger examples where needed.

Risk: Moving-service recommendations can affect in-home visits and handling of valuable or bulky property.

Mitigation: Prompt users to verify provider identity, insurance coverage, and service scope, keep valuables secured, and have someone present during on-site service.
