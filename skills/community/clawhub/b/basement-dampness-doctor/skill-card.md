## Description:

Use when a basement or crawl space smells musty, shows mold, efflorescence, or wet spots — or when sizing a dehumidifier. Separates condensation from liquid-water intrusion using dew-point physics (deep-soil wall temperature model), ranks the 10 classic moisture sources, sizes dehumidifier capacity in pints/day, and analyzes humidity logs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners and property maintainers use this skill to investigate damp basements or crawl spaces, distinguish likely condensation from liquid-water intrusion, compare practical moisture fixes, size a dehumidifier, and review humidity logs.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A diagnosis may be inaccurate when wall-temperature or humidity inputs are inaccurate, or when the regional seasonal soil estimate does not fit local conditions.

Mitigation: Use measured wall temperatures and reliable humidity readings when available. Treat results as screening guidance, and consult a qualified professional for active flooding or structural warning signs.
