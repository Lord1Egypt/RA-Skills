## Description:

Designs onboarding, activation, and retention email sequences for SaaS products, including drip campaigns and email automation guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

SaaS founders, marketers, and growth teams use this skill to plan lifecycle email flows for onboarding, activation, trial conversion, retention, re-engagement, and feature announcements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is scoped to SaaS lifecycle email strategy and may be a poor fit for generic one-off email writing.

Mitigation: Use it for onboarding, activation, trial conversion, retention, re-engagement, and related SaaS lifecycle flows.

Risk: Granting direct email-platform sending or account access could turn advisory output into automated customer communication without separate review.

Mitigation: Keep the skill as a planning and drafting aid unless email-platform access is separately approved and governed.
