## Description:

Produce a commercial teardown of a post, creator profile, product page, landing page, or app page from URLs, screenshots, or pasted text.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[guoxinling](https://clawhub.ai/user/guoxinling)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, creators, and indie builders use this skill to analyze posts, profiles, product pages, landing pages, app pages, bundles, and competitor sets. It turns supplied evidence into commercial positioning, audience, growth, monetization, credibility, risk, and recommendation guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mixed-mode research can introduce outdated or unsupported public-source claims when current pricing, reviews, rankings, or other fresh facts are requested.

Mitigation: Use public sources only when allowed, cite factual claims, and keep observed facts, supported inferences, and unknowns separate.

Risk: Commercial teardown recommendations can overstate traction, revenue, conversion rates, or audience psychology when source evidence is sparse.

Mitigation: State confidence explicitly, keep low-evidence conclusions lightweight, and review recommendations before relying on them for business decisions.

Risk: The default output structure is largely Chinese, which may not fit every intended reader.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to localize headings or final prose when the target audience needs another language.
