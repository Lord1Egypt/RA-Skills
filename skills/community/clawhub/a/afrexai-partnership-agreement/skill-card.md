## Description:

Generate comprehensive partnership agreements, joint venture frameworks, and strategic alliance documents for B2B relationships.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders, agencies, legal operations teams, consultants, and other external users use this skill to draft first-pass partnership agreements, joint venture frameworks, and strategic alliance documents. It gathers partnership context, generates clause language, and flags common agreement issues for legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated partnership terms may be incomplete, unsuitable for the governing jurisdiction, or commercially risky if used as final contract language.

Mitigation: Use the output only as a first-pass draft and have a qualified lawyer review ownership, liability, IP, tax, valuation, exit, and dispute provisions before signing or sending.
