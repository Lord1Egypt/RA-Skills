## Description: <br>
Persistent learning for AI agents. Open engram format. Your agent learns from corrections, remembers across sessions, and transfers knowledge across domains. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[plur9](https://clawhub.ai/user/plur9) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External developers and agent users use PLUR Memory to persist corrections, preferences, patterns, decisions, and conventions as engrams so agents can reuse them across sessions, tools, and devices. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Persistent memories may contain sensitive context and can be reused later or shared across tools and devices. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid storing credentials, tokens, private personal data, or confidential business details unless storage, sync, review, and deletion behavior is understood. <br>
