## Description:

Analyzes a user-provided resume and returns a structured diagnosis with scores, key issues, optimization actions, and rewrite suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[51mee-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/51mee-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers, career advisors, and resume reviewers use this skill to assess resume quality across content, structure, formatting, keyword coverage, and language expression, then produce prioritized improvement guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume files may contain personal information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with resumes the user intentionally provides and handle resume contents according to the user's privacy requirements.
