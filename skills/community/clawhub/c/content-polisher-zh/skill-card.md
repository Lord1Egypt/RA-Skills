## Description:

中文内容精修助手 helps agents polish Chinese AI-generated drafts by reducing mechanical AI phrasing, improving layout, adapting text for common Chinese publishing platforms, refining titles, and using emoji sparingly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, content creators, operators, students, and workplace writers use this skill to convert Chinese AI-generated drafts into more natural Chinese copy for Xiaohongshu, WeChat public accounts, Zhihu, Moments, or a general style.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make AI-generated Chinese text appear more human-written, which may conflict with AI-use disclosure, academic honesty, workplace policy, or platform attribution rules.

Mitigation: Use it only where rewriting AI-assisted text is allowed, and preserve required authorship or AI-use disclosures.
