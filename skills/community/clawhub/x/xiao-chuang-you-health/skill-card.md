## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to receive Chinese-language general wellness guidance for diet, seasonal food therapy, tea drinks, sleep routines, daily schedules, acupoint care, and traditional movement practices. It is intended for supportive lifestyle guidance, not diagnosis, drug recommendations, or treatment planning.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general wellness suggestions, acupoint guidance, diet therapy, or body-constitution advice as medical guidance.

Mitigation: Present the skill as general wellness support and direct users to qualified clinicians for health conditions, pregnancy, medications, injuries, severe symptoms, or persistent symptoms.

Risk: Traditional wellness recommendations may be unsuitable for a user's individual health context.

Mitigation: Keep recommendations cautious, include contraindications and limits, and avoid diagnosis, prescription drugs, or treatment planning.
