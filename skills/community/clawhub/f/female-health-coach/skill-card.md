## Description:

Provides lightweight Chinese-language health coaching for office workers, including fitness, diet, menstrual-cycle-aware exercise adjustments, and basic stress-support guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for practical, Chinese-language wellness coaching around simple meals, short home workouts, fatigue-aware exercise adjustments, and basic stress management. It is bounded to ordinary health management and should not be used for medical or mental-health diagnosis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake general diet, fitness, menstrual-cycle, or stress-support advice for medical or mental-health diagnosis.

Mitigation: Keep recommendations within ordinary wellness coaching, avoid diagnosis or medication advice, and direct users to qualified professionals for serious symptoms or psychological risk.

Risk: Health coaching conversations can invite unnecessary disclosure of sensitive personal health details.

Mitigation: Ask only for details needed to tailor general guidance and remind users not to share sensitive information unless necessary.

Risk: Exercise or diet suggestions may be inappropriate for users with undisclosed conditions, injuries, pregnancy, eating disorders, or other clinical needs.

Mitigation: Prefer low-cost, low-complexity options, reduce intensity when fatigue or menstrual symptoms are present, and encourage professional review when users report risk factors.
