## Description:

Transforms business requirements, PRDs, or product descriptions into a structured requirements analysis covering functional modules, non-functional requirements, architecture constraints, and domain classification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, architects, and engineering teams use this skill to turn ambiguous PRDs or business descriptions into a structured requirements analysis report for architecture planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses a Chinese-language output template, which may not fit teams expecting another language.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to translate or adapt the report format before using the output in another language context.

Risk: The analysis may label unspecified non-functional requirements or constraints as assumptions or items to confirm.

Mitigation: Review the generated confirmation items with product, architecture, and compliance stakeholders before treating them as requirements.
