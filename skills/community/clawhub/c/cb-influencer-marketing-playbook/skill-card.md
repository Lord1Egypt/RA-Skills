## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creator contracts, advertising disclosures, payment terms, and tax obligations vary by jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Verify legal, tax, disclosure, and contract guidance with current local experts before operational use.

Risk: Influencer pricing, platform dynamics, and campaign benchmarks can become stale or differ by market.

Mitigation: Validate pricing and performance assumptions against current market data and recent campaign analytics.
