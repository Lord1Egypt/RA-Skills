## Description:

Organize an insurance claim: incident record, document checklist, timeline, and follow-up tracker. Use after any loss, damage, or medical event with a claim attached.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[manteclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/manteclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External insurance claimants use this workflow after a loss, damage, travel disruption, or medical event to assemble a factual incident record, organize claim documents and insurer contacts, and track deadlines and follow-ups.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Claim workflows may send sensitive medical or incident information without prompting a review of recipients and attachments or use of a secure submission channel.

Mitigation: Before sending claim materials, review every recipient and attachment and use a secure submission channel.
