## Description:

Run a close-out retrospective on a client engagement, capturing lessons, results, commercial reality, and the renewal or referral path.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External consultants and client-facing teams use this skill to close out engagements with an honest outcome review, reusable lessons, margin and scope reflection, and a concrete renewal, testimonial, or referral ask.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive client, pricing, margin, and relationship details provided by the user.

Mitigation: Use only engagement information appropriate for the agent context, and review generated testimonial, referral, renewal, and pricing recommendations before acting on them.
