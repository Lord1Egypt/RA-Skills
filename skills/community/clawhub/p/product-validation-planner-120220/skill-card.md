## Description:

Product Validation Planner helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It structures validation work into practical plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, lightweight analyses, and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad product, startup, validation, or SaaS requests.

Mitigation: Review routing policy before deployment and constrain implicit invocation where broad advisory planning is not acceptable.

Risk: Advisory product validation outputs may be incomplete or misleading if user assumptions, constraints, or success criteria are wrong.

Mitigation: Have users review assumptions, validate conclusions against real customer evidence, and treat generated plans as decision support rather than proof of demand.
