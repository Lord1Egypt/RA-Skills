## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and career-focused agent workflows use this skill to search employer-sourced jobs, improve or generate resumes, and request career planning or skill-gap guidance through Workopia's remote MCP service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career questions and resume text may be sent to Workopia's remote MCP service.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive personal details unless the user intends to use Workopia for that task and accepts the stated privacy handling.

Risk: Resume generation and tailoring may involve temporary generated files and session identifiers.

Mitigation: Review Workopia's privacy policy before sending resume content and delete or rotate any generated links that are no longer needed.

Risk: AI-generated job summaries, career advice, and resume edits may be incomplete or misleading.

Mitigation: Verify job details against the original employer posting and review career or resume recommendations before acting on them.
