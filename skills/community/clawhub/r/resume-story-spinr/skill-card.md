## Description:

Turn flat resume bullets ('Responsible for X') into quantified STAR-method achievement narratives, score resumes against job descriptions for ATS keyword coverage and weak-phrase hazards, and expand each bullet into interview-ready stories with likely follow-up questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, career coaches, and agents use this skill to rewrite resume bullets into defensible achievement statements, compare resumes with job descriptions, and prepare STAR interview stories from the same source material.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill reads the resume, bullet, and job-description files selected by the user, which may contain personal or employment history data.

Mitigation: Use it only with files intended for local processing and avoid adding unnecessary sensitive information to those inputs.

Risk: Generated resume claims, keywords, or metrics may be misleading if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Review every claim for accuracy, use only metrics the user can defend, and rehearse interview stories for final bullets.
