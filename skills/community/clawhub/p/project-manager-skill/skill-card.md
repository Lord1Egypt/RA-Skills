## Description:

Assists with objective product research by analyzing strengths, weaknesses, risks, user value, and actionable improvement suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External product teams, founders, and reviewers use this skill to produce balanced product analysis reports covering strengths, weaknesses, risks, user value, verdicts, and improvement priorities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for general product review requests and produce subjective product recommendations.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the specific product context and treat recommendations as analysis guidance rather than final approval.

Risk: A balanced report may still miss domain-specific privacy, safety, market, or technical constraints.

Mitigation: Have a knowledgeable reviewer check high-impact findings before using the analysis for product or business decisions.
