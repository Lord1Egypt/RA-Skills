## Description:

The Threads Growth skill helps an agent plan conversation-first Threads account growth strategies, draft original posts and suggested replies, and route publishing, replies, analytics, and related work to the appropriate human or companion-skill workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to create Threads account-growth plans grounded in a brand profile, goals, niche, cadence, format mix, reply strategy, and Threads-to-Instagram funnel. It is intended for strategy, post drafts, suggested replies, and measurement guidance while public replies, DMs, communities, dashboards, analytics, and final scheduled posts remain human-reviewed or native-platform controlled.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can draft or schedule public Threads content based on brand goals and performance metrics.

Mitigation: Review scheduled public posts before publication through WoopSocial and keep final posting approval with a human.

Risk: The skill gives growth strategy advice in a platform area that changes over time.

Mitigation: Re-verify Threads platform assumptions quarterly and rely on native analytics for current metrics instead of fabricated or stale numbers.

Risk: Users may expect the agent to run replies, DMs, communities, or analytics directly.

Mitigation: Keep replies, DMs, communities, dashboards, and analytics in human-controlled or native Threads workflows as described in the skill scope.
