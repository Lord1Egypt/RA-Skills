## Description:

Helps software maintainers, QA engineers, contributors, and product teams plan, write, and verify useful unit tests for existing codebases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, QA engineers, maintainers, and product teams use this skill to turn testing goals into practical unit-test plans, implementation guidance, checklists, and verification steps for existing codebases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad testing or quality requests and may propose tests that need project-specific review.

Mitigation: Use it for unit-test planning, writing, or verification tasks, and review generated tests against intended behavior before committing changes.
