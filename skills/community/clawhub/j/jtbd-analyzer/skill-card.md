## Description:

Uncover the real "job" customers hire your product to do by looking beyond features to functional, emotional, and social motivations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[artyomx33](https://clawhub.ai/user/artyomx33)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, researchers, and customer-strategy teams use this skill to analyze why customers choose a product, identify functional, emotional, and social jobs, compare alternatives, and prioritize underserved needs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad product or customer-strategy questions because its trigger phrases include general customer-motivation language.

Mitigation: Use it when a Jobs-To-Be-Done analysis is intended, and review whether the resulting framing matches the user's actual product-strategy question.

Risk: JTBD outputs can be speculative if they are not grounded in customer evidence.

Mitigation: Treat job statements, alternatives, underserved needs, and priorities as analysis drafts to validate through customer interviews or other product research.
