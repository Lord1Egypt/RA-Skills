## Description:

Plans camping and overlanding trips on U.S. national forest and BLM land using sourced American Adventurer camp records, while identifying gaps and avoiding unsupported claims about access, legality, road conditions, or safety.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[davelalande](https://clawhub.ai/user/davelalande)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users planning camping or overlanding trips on U.S. national forest and BLM land can use the skill to find sourced camp records, plan around recorded rules and conditions, identify information gaps, and prepare a printable trip sheet.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trip summaries may omit record links, source dates, or caveats.

Mitigation: Check place claims against their linked records and retain the source, date, and stated gaps.

Risk: Runtime tool permissions were not verified, and scanner coverage is partial.

Mitigation: Review the installed connector's permissions and the unresolved coverage before enabling use.
