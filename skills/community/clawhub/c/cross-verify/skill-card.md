## Description:

Cross Verify helps agents fact-check user-provided text or data, identify bias and logic gaps, and return a structured credibility report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[irvinezhao](https://clawhub.ai/user/irvinezhao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and developers use this skill to verify claims in pasted text or data, identify bias and logical gaps, and decide what evidence is still needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fact-checking results may be incomplete when reliable sources are unavailable or claims cannot be independently confirmed.

Mitigation: Mark unsupported claims as unable to confirm, cite available reliable sources, and state what additional evidence is needed.

Risk: The skill is mainly presented in Chinese, which may reduce clarity for users who prefer another language.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to produce the verification report in the user's preferred language when needed.
