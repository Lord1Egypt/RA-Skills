## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can generate covert manipulation and social-engineering strategy.

Mitigation: Restrict use to analysis, defense, education, and benign planning; block requests to deceive, coerce, emotionally exploit, or manipulate real people or organizations.

Risk: Strategic roadmap outputs could be misused for insider manipulation, market abuse, or campaigns against real targets.

Mitigation: Require human review for strategic outputs and enforce policy checks before applying advice to real individuals, organizations, markets, or public campaigns.

Risk: The artifact includes limited built-in safety boundaries despite producing risk and ethics sections.

Mitigation: Add external safety guardrails that transform harmful planning requests into defensive analysis, refusal, or high-level education.
