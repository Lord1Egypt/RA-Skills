## Description:

Helps users assess social energy from short text and returns a recovery plan with immediate, daily, and weekly suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who want lightweight lifestyle guidance use this skill to identify whether their social energy is low, balanced, or high and receive practical recovery suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce Chinese-language guidance and trigger on broad fatigue or solitude phrases.

Mitigation: Review outputs for the intended audience and confirm that trigger phrases match the deployment context.

Risk: Lifestyle advice could be mistaken for clinical or mental-health guidance.

Mitigation: Present the output as general self-management guidance and route health concerns to qualified support.
