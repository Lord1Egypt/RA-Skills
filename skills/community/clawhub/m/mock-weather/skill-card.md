## Description:

Mock Weather generates deterministic simulated current weather and 1-7 day forecasts for any Chinese or English city name.

This skill is for demonstration purposes and not for production usage.

## Publisher:

[krisliu16](https://clawhub.ai/user/krisliu16)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to produce placeholder weather responses for demos, tests, or workflows that need non-real current weather or short forecasts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake simulated weather output for real weather data.

Mitigation: Clearly label responses as simulated and do not use them for real-world weather decisions.

Risk: Formatted output uses Chinese weather labels even when the city name is English.

Mitigation: Translate or localize the labels before presenting output to users who do not read Chinese.
