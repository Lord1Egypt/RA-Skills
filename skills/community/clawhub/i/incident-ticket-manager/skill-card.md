## Description:

Create and route ad operations incident tickets for Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Ads, TikTok Ads, and multi-platform campaign failures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Ad operations and growth teams use this skill to capture advertising incidents, classify severity, route owners, and produce mitigation, escalation, and monitoring guidance for Meta, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, YouTube Ads, and related channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording could cause this skill to be used outside ad incident triage.

Mitigation: Use it only for actual ad operations incidents or audits, and confirm the operational scope before relying on its output.

Risk: Recommended containment actions, ticket payloads, or owner contact details could be incomplete or wrong.

Mitigation: Review proposed actions, escalation details, and owner routing before acting on them.
