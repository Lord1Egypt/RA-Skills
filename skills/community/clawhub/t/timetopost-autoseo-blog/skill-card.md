## Description:

Connect a site and run a human-reviewed AutoSEO blog workflow with TimeToPost MCP. Use when asked to set up an SEO blog pipeline, research article topics, generate a blog draft, review an AutoSEO post, or assess SEO and AI visibility.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External site owners and content teams can connect a publishing site, research article topics, and create and review SEO blog drafts. The skill keeps content as a draft until the user approves the exact content and destination.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review is partial because it could not resolve a local path-like reference in SKILL.md, leaving the setup reference and hosted MCP destination for review.

Mitigation: Review the setup instructions and hosted MCP destination before connecting, as the security guidance recommends.

Risk: Keyword research estimates could be mistaken for measured rankings or traffic.

Mitigation: Describe keyword estimates as estimates and do not present them as measured search performance.
