## Description:

A Chinese-language ADHD education and support assistant for women diagnosed in adulthood or midlife, with practical strategies, emotional support, and science-grounded explanations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[celia998](https://clawhub.ai/user/celia998)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, especially women ages 30-55 with late-diagnosed or suspected ADHD, use this skill for ADHD education, self-understanding, emotional support, and practical executive-function strategies. It is informational support and does not replace clinical diagnosis, prescribing, crisis care, or medical decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat ADHD education, hormone-related discussion, fNIRS information, or treatment explanations as medical advice.

Mitigation: Keep the skill framed as informational support and direct users to qualified clinicians for diagnosis, medication, crisis care, hormone decisions, and fNIRS interpretation.

Risk: Users may share sensitive personal, family, child, or medical records while seeking support.

Mitigation: Advise users not to enter identifiable records unless the AI service's privacy handling is acceptable for that information.
