## Description:

Review Arabic and bilingual localization for apps, websites, course notes, and UI screens with RTL, tone, terminology, and consistency checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, educators, and content reviewers use this skill to check Arabic and bilingual UI copy, website text, course notes, and public content for meaning match, tone, terminology consistency, RTL readability, and UI fit before release.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide sensitive source text, customer data, passwords, keys, tokens, or confidential documents for localization review.

Mitigation: Ask only for the minimum needed text and avoid collecting confidential documents, customer records, passwords, keys, tokens, OTPs, or private account data.

Risk: Localization changes may introduce unsupported religious, legal, medical, financial, security, or brand claims.

Mitigation: Preserve the user's intended meaning, avoid adding unsupported promises or claims, and mark sensitive wording as needing human or native review.

Risk: Arabic or bilingual output may be misleading if RTL layout, numbers, contact details, CTAs, or terminology are inconsistent.

Mitigation: Check meaning match, RTL readability, UI fit, repeated terminology, numbers, dates, email, website, phone details, and CTA consistency before classifying the content as ready.
