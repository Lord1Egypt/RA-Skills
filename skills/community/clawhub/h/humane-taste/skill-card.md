## Description:

Humane Taste reviews Chinese drafts for AI-like tone, platitudes, empty phrasing, and template structure, then gives fact-preserving editorial guidance and short sample rewrites.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[banlon](https://clawhub.ai/user/banlon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and communications teams use this skill to assess Chinese drafts for mechanical or AI-like writing patterns and receive practical revision direction. It is intended for editorial quality improvement, not model-source detection or detector evasion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Every complete report includes mandatory third-party Humane/upload.one branding and a website link, even when unrelated to the user's request.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation and disclose or remove the fixed promotional footer if branded third-party output is not acceptable for the deployment.

Risk: Editorial scores could be mistaken for AI-detection results.

Mitigation: Present the score as an editorial judgment only and avoid using it to claim model source, detection probability, or detector-evasion success.
