## Description:

Guides agents through naming a business, product, or service, evaluating candidate names, and planning domain checks and registration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jk-0001](https://clawhub.ai/user/jk-0001)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers, founders, and business users can use this skill to have an agent generate, score, filter, and validate candidate names while planning domain, social handle, and basic trademark checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trademark screening guidance may be incomplete or insufficient for legal clearance.

Mitigation: Treat trademark checks as basic screening and consult qualified legal counsel before investing heavily in a brand or filing registrations.

Risk: Domain purchases, social-handle registrations, or legal filings may create costs or ownership commitments.

Mitigation: Confirm price, availability, account ownership, and renewal terms directly before purchasing or registering anything.
