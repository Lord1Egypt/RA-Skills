## Description:

Processes medical documents by summarizing clinical records, analyzing medical reports, and organizing medical literature for healthcare professionals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Healthcare professionals use this skill to turn pasted medical records, lab, imaging, pathology reports, and medical literature into structured summaries and analysis notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical documents may contain patient identifiers or sensitive health information.

Mitigation: Remove patient identifiers unless they are required for the task, and provide only documents intended for assistant processing.

Risk: Document summaries and report analysis may be mistaken for clinical decision-making.

Mitigation: Use the output for document organization only; diagnosis and treatment decisions should remain with licensed clinicians.

Risk: The default output language and format may not match the user's workflow.

Mitigation: Ask for the desired language and format when invoking the skill.
