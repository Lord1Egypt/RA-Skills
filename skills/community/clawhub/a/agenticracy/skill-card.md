## Description:

Public placeholder skill describing a non-proprietary Agenticracy-style audit workflow for grounding claims in evidence, memory, and transparent schema.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticracy](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticracy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to produce concise audit records that separate available evidence, memory, inference, uncertainty, and refusal conditions. It is suited for transparent decision-support notes, not deterministic prediction, diagnosis, legal judgment, or sensitive profiling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat heuristic audit outputs as validated predictions, diagnoses, legal conclusions, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as structured estimates with provenance, confidence bands, uncertainty notes, and clear limits on unsupported conclusions.

Risk: Requests may require secrets, private coefficients, or sensitive profiling.

Mitigation: Refuse those requests and restrict the audit record to explicitly available evidence.

Risk: The skill is a public placeholder rather than a production audit system.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as lightweight structured records and review them before operational use.
