## Description:

Diagnoses grammar and syntax issues in psychology Method sections, checking tense, passive voice, sentence structure, and grammar conventions while returning scores and top-journal examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjinhongmy-pixel](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjinhongmy-pixel)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, graduate students, and academic writing reviewers use this skill to diagnose grammar and syntax issues in psychology empirical-paper Method sections. It returns a structured diagnostic report with dimension scores, severity-ranked findings, revision suggestions, and relevant examples.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some supporting resources referenced by the skill may be missing from the package, which can reduce diagnostic reliability.

Mitigation: Confirm that the checklist, rubric, example files, and any cited source materials needed for the intended review are available before relying on the diagnosis.

Risk: The skill provides diagnostic guidance and revision suggestions, but it is not designed to rewrite text automatically.

Mitigation: Have a human author or reviewer apply and verify any suggested revisions against the source manuscript and target journal requirements.
