## Description:

Tracks technical debt as actual financial debt with principal, interest rates, minimum payments, compound growth, maturity dates, and payoff strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jcools1977](https://clawhub.ai/user/jcools1977)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers and engineering teams use this skill to inventory technical debt, estimate its current and future cost, classify severity, and compare payoff strategies during planning and review cycles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Debt discovery may involve analyzing project files and producing estimates that influence engineering priorities.

Mitigation: Review the analyzed file scope and validate principal, interest, maturity, and payoff assumptions with project maintainers before using the ledger for roadmap decisions.
