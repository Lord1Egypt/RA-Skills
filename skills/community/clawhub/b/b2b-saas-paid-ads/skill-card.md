## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paid media recommendations may be unsuitable for a specific market, budget, or compliance environment.

Mitigation: Have a qualified marketer review channel recommendations, spend levels, creative claims, and targeting before launch.

Risk: Broad paid advertising trigger phrases can overlap with other marketing skills in the same workspace.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger phrasing when multiple marketing or demand generation skills are installed.
