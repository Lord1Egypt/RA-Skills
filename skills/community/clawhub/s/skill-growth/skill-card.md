## Description:

Skill Growth Guide helps Chinese-language ClawHub skill creators assess skill niches, diagnose install and star trends, improve descriptions, map adjacent skills, explore monetization paths, and frame collaboration requests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huangjihua007-rgb](https://clawhub.ai/user/huangjihua007-rgb)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ClawHub skill creators use this agent to get concise growth advice after publishing or preparing a skill, including market fit, growth diagnostics, listing copy, monetization, and partnership direction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may open broad conversations with its own menu and fixed branding.

Mitigation: Review whether the opening menu and attribution behavior fit the target agent experience before deployment.

Risk: The skill may include SkillGrowth or SkillManager branding and external referral links in answers.

Mitigation: Treat external sites as optional references and confirm that referral links are acceptable for the deployment context.

Risk: Growth advice can be incorrect, overconfident, or mismatched to a creator's actual market data.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against current install, star, and channel data before acting on them.
