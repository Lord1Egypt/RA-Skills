## Description:

来怼 helps an agent deliver Chinese-language devil's-advocate pressure tests of user ideas by surfacing assumptions, logical gaps, and likely failure points from multiple angles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jkdeadwolf](https://clawhub.ai/user/jkdeadwolf)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill when they want a Chinese-language red-team critique of a proposal, product idea, architecture, decision, or argument. It is best suited for pressure-testing assumptions and driving iterative refinement, not for neutral brainstorming or final professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intentionally blunt and adversarial, which may be unsuitable when the user needs emotional support or neutral ideation.

Mitigation: Use it for explicit pressure testing; choose a neutral brainstorming workflow when support, exploration, or consensus building is the goal.

Risk: In legal, medical, financial, or other high-stakes contexts, critique output could be mistaken for final professional guidance.

Mitigation: Treat the output as prompts for review and follow-up questions, and require qualified human review before acting on high-stakes decisions.
