## Description:

Extracts a leader's or mentor's guidance, work requirements, priorities, and action items for a specific person from Chinese or English meeting transcripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tengjie888](https://clawhub.ai/user/tengjie888)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and teams use this skill to turn meeting transcripts into structured summaries of guidance for a named participant, including action items, priorities, deadlines, context, and relevant quotes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting transcripts may contain sensitive or personal information.

Mitigation: Share only appropriate transcript content with the agent and handle generated summaries according to the meeting's confidentiality requirements.

Risk: ASR errors, indirect statements, or language mismatch can cause inaccurate extracted guidance, priorities, or deadlines.

Mitigation: Review the generated report against the original transcript before assigning work or making decisions from it.
