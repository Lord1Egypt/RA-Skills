## Description:

国企采购评审专家 provides Chinese-language guidance for PRC state-owned-enterprise procurement review, tendering compliance, bid rejection analysis, supplier collusion indicators, dispute handling, document review, and SOE governance risk checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chesaram](https://clawhub.ai/user/chesaram)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external users involved in PRC SOE procurement use this skill to assess procurement-law fit, review tender or response-file risks, identify bid rejection and collusion concerns, handle objections or complaints, and structure compliance recommendations. It is scoped to SOE procurement under the PRC Tendering and Bidding Law and SOE procurement management norms, not general government-procurement or private-company purchasing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat procurement compliance guidance as formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as decision support, require verification against current law and local rules, and route high-impact matters to counsel or enterprise legal review.

Risk: Using the skill outside PRC SOE procurement or tendering contexts may misapply legal frameworks.

Mitigation: Apply the skill only after confirming SOE procurement scope and use its boundary response for government-procurement, private-company, or otherwise out-of-scope matters.

Risk: Knowledge-base access may be unavailable or incomplete, reducing source traceability.

Mitigation: When ima knowledge bases are unavailable or do not return relevant results, disclose the limitation and verify conclusions against current statutes, local platform rules, and enterprise internal policies.

Risk: Procurement laws, local public-resource platform rules, and SOE internal policies can change.

Mitigation: Check current national rules, local requirements, and enterprise procurement policies before acting on recommendations.
