## Description:

Apply counterfactual thinking to explore "what if" alternative histories and understand causation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and agents use this skill to structure counterfactual analysis of past decisions, outcomes, mistakes, and causal claims. It guides the agent through factual history, pivotal moments, alternative scenarios, causation analysis, hindsight-bias checks, and lessons for future decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad activation wording and may steer general decision reviews or mistake analysis into a counterfactual framework when a narrower method would be enough.

Mitigation: Use it when structured counterfactual reasoning is desired, and choose a narrower reasoning approach for simple reviews or direct factual questions.

Risk: Counterfactual scenarios can become speculative or be mistaken for established facts.

Mitigation: Keep each alternative tied to known facts, state assumptions, check plausibility, and include confidence levels for projected outcomes.
