## Description:

Write concise, personalized backlink outreach emails for an editorial replacement, guest contribution, disclosed sponsored post, or narrow reciprocal-link partnership using verified page, product, audience, authority, and traffic evidence. Use when a user needs subject lines, body copy, a low-friction CTA, prospect-specific variants, or a backlink outreach campaign draft with clear do, do not, limitation, disclosure, and search-policy checks. Rankizz metrics are optional.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Security scanning reported no findings, but coverage is partial because a local path-like reference in SKILL.md could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved reference and its target before installation.

Risk: Outreach proposals could support ranking manipulation if users bypass the skill's editorial and sponsorship safeguards.

Mitigation: Keep paid placements disclosed and qualified, and propose reciprocal links only when each independently serves its audience.

Risk: A draft does not establish that commercial email is lawful or suitable to send in a particular jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Check applicable sending rules, contact eligibility, sender identification, and opt-out requirements before sending.
