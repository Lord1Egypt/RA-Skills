## Description:

Analyzes learning needs and performance gaps to recommend and blueprint the best-fit instructional strategy with human oversight for corporate training.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mrummler17](https://clawhub.ai/user/mrummler17)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Instructional designers, L&D leads, learning consultants, and capability teams use this skill to assess discovery inputs, decide whether training is appropriate, classify performance gaps, and produce structured strategy blueprints for human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Client, SME, or policy documents may contain sensitive business and training context.

Mitigation: Review source documents before sharing them with the agent and limit inputs to information appropriate for the intended review workflow.

Risk: Execution manifests could be mistaken for approved downstream automation plans.

Mitigation: Request execution manifests only when planning automation, and keep the human approval points called out by the skill before acting on generated steps.
