## Description:

Review a secondhand furniture listing before pickup with seller questions, photo requests, measurement checks, transport planning, hygiene and pest warnings, recall reminders, and safer meetup guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to evaluate online secondhand furniture listings before pickup. It produces practical seller questions, photo requests, fit and transport checks, hygiene and pest warnings, recall reminders, and safer pickup guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can organize recall, hygiene, pest, and pickup checks, but it cannot verify recalls or inspect furniture.

Mitigation: Use official recall sources where relevant and inspect the item in person before pickup or use.

Risk: The skill does not provide legal advice, authenticate items, appraise value, or guarantee that a listing is safe, sanitary, pest-free, authentic, or worth the price.

Mitigation: Treat its output as a shopping checklist and seek qualified professional advice for legal, valuation, safety certification, or pest inspection questions.
