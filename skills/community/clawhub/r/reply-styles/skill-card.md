## Description:

Draft high-EQ, style-controlled replies for chat, email, DMs, customer support, community operations, sales, and difficult conversations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tobewin](https://clawhub.ai/user/tobewin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to draft ready-to-send replies with preset communication styles, intensity levels, channels, personas, and reply modes while preserving the user's intent and boundaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The style presets are Chinese-focused, so replies in other languages or channels may miss the intended tone if the user does not specify context.

Mitigation: Ask for or state the desired language and channel before drafting when the conversation is not Chinese or not WeChat-oriented.

Risk: Tone rewriting for sales, support, or difficult conversations can make a message sound polished while still being unsuitable to send.

Mitigation: Review the final reply for accuracy, boundaries, and audience fit before sending it.
