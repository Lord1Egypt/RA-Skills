## Description:

Generate structured daily standup notes from a free-form brain dump into a clean three-section update for Yesterday, Today, and Blockers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external contributors, and developers use this skill to convert scattered notes, chat, bullets, or transcripts into a concise daily standup update with Yesterday, Today, and Blockers sections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Standup notes may include work details, ticket IDs, links, or names that are not appropriate to share or store.

Mitigation: Review the generated update before sharing, and only save or append notes when the included details are suitable for the destination.
