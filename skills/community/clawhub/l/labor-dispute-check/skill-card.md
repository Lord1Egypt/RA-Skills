## Use Case:

Employees, employers, and advisors use this skill to check common China labor dispute issues, estimate severance, overtime, and probation outcomes, and organize evidence for negotiation or arbitration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Labor-law guidance can be affected by local rules, changing requirements, and case-specific facts.

Mitigation: Treat the output as informational and verify important decisions with a qualified lawyer or local labor bureau.

Risk: Simplified severance, overtime, and probation calculations may be inaccurate when key facts are missing or assumptions are wrong.

Mitigation: Collect dates, wage base, contract terms, termination reason, overtime records, and local context before relying on estimates.

Risk: Evidence gaps can reduce the usefulness of an arbitration package.

Mitigation: Preserve contracts, payslips, attendance records, written communications, screenshots, and originals before negotiation or filing.
