## Description:

Validates and evaluates startup business ideas using proven frameworks like TAM/SAM/SOM, competitive analysis, and market feasibility assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huajianjiu000](https://clawhub.ai/user/huajianjiu000)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Entrepreneurs, founders, and business planners use this skill to assess startup ideas before significant investment. It produces structured viability analysis covering problem-solution fit, market sizing, competitive position, revenue model viability, go-to-market potential, and recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce market sizing, scores, or business recommendations that appear precise but are planning estimates.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as directional planning guidance and validate them with independent financial, market, customer, and competitive research before making investment decisions.
