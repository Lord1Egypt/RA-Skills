## Description:

Write a clear, reproducible bug report that gets fixed fast and turns vague issue reports into actionable tickets with a precise title, reproduction steps, expected and actual results, environment, severity, priority, and evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, testers, support teams, and external contributors use this skill to convert observed defects or vague failure reports into reproducible, developer-ready bug reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bug reports can include sensitive details from logs, screenshots, request IDs, account records, or environment data.

Mitigation: Review and redact tokens, credentials, customer data, and private account details before sharing the report publicly or with third parties.

Risk: Inferred reproduction steps, environments, or suspected causes may be mistaken for observed facts.

Mitigation: Keep inferred details labeled for confirmation and attach only evidence that was actually observed.
