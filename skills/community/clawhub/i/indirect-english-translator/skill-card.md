## Description:

Translate indirect English into direct meaning and safe replies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People reviewing workplace, social, academic, customer-service, diplomatic, hiring, negotiation, dating, or everyday English messages use this skill to identify likely subtext, rewrite the meaning in direct simple English, and draft safe clarifying replies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may auto-activate for indirect-English requests.

Mitigation: Review invocation behavior before deployment so users understand when the communication helper may be used.

Risk: Subtext readings can be mistaken or over-applied, especially in employment, legal, medical, financial, housing, safety, or contract situations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as language guidance, confirm important interpretations directly in writing, and use qualified professional advice for high-stakes decisions.
