## Description:

Analyzes the Chinese government procurement lifecycle, from tender discovery and bid decisions to document drafting, compliance checks, and award tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fyniujin](https://clawhub.ai/user/fyniujin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Suppliers, procurement agencies, and bid consultants use this skill to find public tenders, assess bid fit and compliance, draft bid materials, and track procurement outcomes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review reports broad, undeclared access and an unclear remote update process.

Mitigation: Review and limit permissions, and clarify how updates are delivered before installation or use.

Risk: OCR data handling and crawling behavior need clarification.

Mitigation: Clarify how OCR processes and stores documents and confirm crawling behavior before using confidential tender files or collection features.

Risk: Bid and compliance conclusions may be unreliable, and scanner coverage was incomplete.

Mitigation: Treat results as decision support and verify conclusions against current tender documents and qualified procurement or compliance reviewers.
