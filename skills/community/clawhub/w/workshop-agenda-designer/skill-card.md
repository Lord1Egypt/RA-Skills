## Description:

Turns workshop goals, participants, timing, and constraints into reviewable agendas, facilitation runbooks, expected outputs, and follow-up plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[52yuanchangxing](https://clawhub.ai/user/52yuanchangxing)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, facilitators, and team leads use this skill to convert meeting goals, participant context, available time, and known constraints into a structured workshop agenda or runbook. It is best suited for workshop design, customer co-creation sessions, and internal alignment meetings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags under-disclosed local audit and scanning modes in the bundled Python script that go beyond agenda drafting.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with explicit meeting-material inputs, avoid pointing it at sensitive repositories or broad directories, and review or remove the audit modes before general deployment.

Risk: Workshop inputs can contain personal or sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Provide only necessary context, redact sensitive details where possible, and keep outputs reviewable before sharing.
