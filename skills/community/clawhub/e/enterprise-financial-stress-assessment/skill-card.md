## Description:

生成中文企业资金健康度和资金压力评估报告，基于公开合规信息分析现金流、融资活动、债务负担、偿债能力、信用风险、经营承压信号和风控建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, risk teams, investment promotion teams, investors, and due diligence reviewers use this skill to assess a target company's financial stress, credit risk, repayment pressure, project delivery risk, and cooperation risk from public information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for legal, credit-rating, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Use the report as decision support and verify important financial, litigation, tax, and operating claims against primary public sources before making decisions.

Risk: Financial stress conclusions can be overstated when public data is incomplete, especially for private companies.

Mitigation: Keep uncertainty labels in the report, avoid unsupported definitive claims, and supplement public signals with primary documents or formal due diligence where available.
