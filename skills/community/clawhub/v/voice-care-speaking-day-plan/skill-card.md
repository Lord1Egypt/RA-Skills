## Description:

Builds a general, non-medical speaking-day routine for hydration, pacing, warmups, breaks, and recovery, with clear escalation for pain, voice loss, breathing issues, or persistent symptoms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this prompt-only skill to create a practical speaking-day plan for teaching, meetings, keynotes, workshops, streams, podcasts, or other heavy speaking schedules. The plan helps structure red-flag screening, hydration, gentle warmups, pacing, breaks, recovery, reminders, and escalation guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake general voice-care planning for medical diagnosis or treatment.

Mitigation: Keep the non-medical boundary visible and direct users to qualified professional or urgent help for pain, sudden or repeated voice loss, breathing difficulty, coughing blood, severe illness, or persistent or recurring symptoms.

Risk: A speaking-day routine could encourage continued voice use when warning signs are present.

Mitigation: Screen for red flags before routine-building, recommend pausing when pain or serious symptoms appear, and avoid advice to push through pain or force the voice.
