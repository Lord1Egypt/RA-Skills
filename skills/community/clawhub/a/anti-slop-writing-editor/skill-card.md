## Description:

Edit prose to remove AI-coded rhetoric, filler, and cadence tics while preserving voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to audit and revise human-facing prose so it removes AI-coded rhetoric, filler, repeated cadence patterns, and inflated phrasing while preserving the user's point and voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can influence prose style when invoked and may change emphasis or tone in user-provided writing.

Mitigation: Review revised prose against the user's intended meaning, facts, and voice before publishing or sending it.

Risk: A prose audit can incorrectly flag an intentional stylistic choice as AI-coded or generic.

Mitigation: Use the audit findings as editorial suggestions and preserve intentional quirks when they serve the piece.
