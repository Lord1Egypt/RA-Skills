## Description:

Produces a sermon outline from a scripture passage or theme, ready for preaching.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chenios](https://clawhub.ai/user/chenios)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External pastors, preachers, and church ministry users use this skill to turn a scripture passage or theme into a preach-ready sermon outline with cited main points, applications, and a conclusion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may receive sermon outlines framed around the skill's default English, ESV, and Evangelical assumptions when they need another language, Bible translation, or theological tradition.

Mitigation: State the desired language, Bible translation, and theological tradition explicitly in the request.
