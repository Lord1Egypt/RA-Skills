## Description:

Helps creators and communicators turn an Ask HN product-help request into a practical workflow, artifact, checklist, analysis, or implementation support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and communicators use this skill to clarify product-help requests, produce a concrete deliverable such as a workflow, checklist, analysis, or implementation plan, and check the result against stated success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may trigger on broad product-help wording because implicit invocation is enabled and trigger terms are general.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation for product validation tasks and consider narrowing trigger terms before deployment.
