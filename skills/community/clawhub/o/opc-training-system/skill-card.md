## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can generate Markdown files under ./output/ and could overwrite prior work if the agent is allowed to write without review.

Mitigation: Confirm generated file paths and require approval before overwriting existing files.

Risk: Training or mentor-evaluation content may be applied outside its OPC context or treated as professional certification criteria.

Mitigation: Review generated training plans and assessment tables with qualified program owners before operational use.
