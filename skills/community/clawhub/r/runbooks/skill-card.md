## Use Case:

Developers, operators, and technical teams use this skill to draft or improve operational runbooks with clear triggers, staged procedures, validation checks, rollback points, escalation paths, and post-run follow-through.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may be offered for review or communication tasks that are not truly runbook-related.

Mitigation: Use the workflow only when structured runbook stages, verification, rollback, and operational follow-through are useful for the task.

Risk: Runbook guidance can be incomplete or misleading if operational context, constraints, owners, or success criteria are missing.

Mitigation: Clarify scope, success criteria, constraints, validation checks, rollback points, and escalation paths before relying on the runbook.
