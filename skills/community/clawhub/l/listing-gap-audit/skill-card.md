## Description:

Audit listing competitiveness by comparing your PDP/listing against top competitors and surfacing the highest-impact copy/proof gaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers, ecommerce operators, and listing teams use this skill to compare a product detail page or listing against competitors and prioritize high-impact copy, proof, offer, and compliance gaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Listing recommendations could include inaccurate, unsupported, or compliance-sensitive product claims.

Mitigation: Review all suggested product, proof, and compliance claims before publishing.

Risk: Browser-based audits may expose private account pages or rely on incomplete page evidence.

Mitigation: Provide only listing text or URLs intended for review, use Browser Relay only intentionally, and label partial browser evidence clearly.
