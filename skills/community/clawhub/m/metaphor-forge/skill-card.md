## Description:

Turns abstract concepts, product benefits, complex problems, or emotions into clear, memorable, and checkable cross-domain metaphors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[weslon1987](https://clawhub.ai/user/weslon1987)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and writers use this skill to explain abstract concepts, product benefits, complex problems, and emotions through memorable metaphors tailored to an audience and setting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad trigger phrases may activate the skill when metaphor generation was not intended.

Mitigation: Use the listed trigger phrases when metaphor generation is desired; state explicitly when a response should use facts without metaphors.
