## Description:

Book Digest guides deep reading of nonfiction, business, or technical books by gathering summaries, unpacking chapters, testing understanding with Socratic questions, and mapping concepts to practical use.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xyzach](https://clawhub.ai/user/xyzach)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to structure active reading sessions for nonfiction, business, or technical books, from quick overviews through deeper chapter analysis, comprehension checks, and application planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may offer to create a persistent book-specific reference skill after a reading session.

Mitigation: Approve that follow-up only when reusable book-specific guidance should be saved, and review the generated skill before future use.
