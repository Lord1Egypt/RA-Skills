## Use Case:

External users, founders, and small teams use this skill to turn common business legal questions into plain-language risk summaries, action steps, negotiation language, and document drafting guidance. It is oriented to China mainland commercial scenarios and flags when a licensed lawyer should be consulted.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China mainland-oriented; reviewers should confirm local applicability for the user's city or regulator.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat generated legal guidance or templates as formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as general information, preserve the skill's advisory limits, and escalate disputes, major transactions, criminal exposure, cross-border matters, and formal filings to a licensed lawyer.

Risk: China mainland labor and compliance rules can vary by city or regulator.

Mitigation: Ask for the applicable city or regulator, avoid unsupported numeric conclusions when locality is unknown, and verify current local rules through official channels before acting.
