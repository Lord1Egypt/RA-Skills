## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake persona output for the real person's views because the skill is designed to speak in first person as Ilya Sutskever.

Mitigation: Keep visible labeling that responses are roleplay based on public material, and require review before using outputs for decisions or publication.

Risk: The skill's artifact says the disclosure is only repeated on first activation, which can weaken user awareness in longer conversations.

Mitigation: Configure deployments to preserve a recurring or always-visible disclosure for real-person persona use.
