## Description:

CC3PO Humanizer rewrites text to reduce common signs of AI-generated writing and make prose sound more natural while preserving meaning and tone.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[carloscbrls](https://clawhub.ai/user/carloscbrls)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and agents use this skill to revise pasted text that sounds AI-generated by replacing formulaic wording, inflated claims, and mechanical formatting with more natural prose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tone and wording changes may affect legal, compliance, evidentiary, or sensitive business text.

Mitigation: Review revised text before relying on it in sensitive contexts.

Risk: Style rewrites can change nuance beyond simple cleanup.

Mitigation: Compare the rewrite against the source text and preserve domain-specific meaning, citations, and obligations.
