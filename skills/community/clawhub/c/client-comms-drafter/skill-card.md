## Description:

Draft professional client emails and messages for freelancers, including project updates, scope changes, payment reminders, feedback requests, difficult conversations, and project wrap-ups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External freelancers and solo operators use this skill to draft send-ready client communications for payment chasing, scope pushback, project updates, feedback requests, difficult conversations, and wrap-ups.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated client emails may include legal or payment language that needs confirmation before sending.

Mitigation: Review drafts before sending, especially payment reminders that cite UK late-payment law or current interest rates.
