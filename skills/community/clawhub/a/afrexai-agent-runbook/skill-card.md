## Description:

Generates operations runbook drafts for deploying AI agents, including rollout stages, monitoring, rollback procedures, cost management, and incident response templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers, engineering leaders, and operations teams use this skill to draft production deployment runbooks for AI agent systems, including launch gates, monitoring thresholds, rollback paths, cost planning, and incident response workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated metric targets, compliance notes, and cost estimates may not match a user's production data or regulatory requirements.

Mitigation: Review the generated runbook against internal telemetry, financial assumptions, and applicable regulatory obligations before using it operationally.
