## Description:

Researches keyword demand, difficulty, trends, and competitors, then recommends whether to target a keyword or compare alternatives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[notegpt-official](https://clawhub.ai/user/notegpt-official)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO practitioners use this skill before content production to assess a target keyword or compare 3–5 candidates and choose where to focus.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Keywords may reveal confidential products or business plans when sent to external services.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential or sensitive terms; use public or sanitized keywords.

Risk: The security review is partial, so its evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the source and unresolved references before deployment.
