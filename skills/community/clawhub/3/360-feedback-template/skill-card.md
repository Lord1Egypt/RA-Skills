## Description:

Designs a 360-degree feedback survey or writes a structured 360 feedback report with rating scales, open-ended questions, themes, strengths, and development areas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, HR partners, and coaches use this skill to create 360-degree feedback surveys or synthesize reviewer notes into structured, development-oriented feedback reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Feedback notes may contain names, confidential HR details, or small-team quotes that identify reviewers or reveal sensitive performance information.

Mitigation: Use summarized or anonymized reviewer notes where possible, and remove names, identifiers, confidential HR details, and identifying quotes unless authorized to share them in the agent session.
