## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat conceptual Dow Theory output as financial advice or an automated trading signal.

Mitigation: Present the skill as educational technical-analysis guidance and require users to independently verify market data and investment decisions.

Risk: Dow Theory signals can be delayed or uncertain, especially when market indexes diverge.

Mitigation: Call out the documented limitations and encourage corroboration with additional analysis before acting.
