## Description:

Create a privacy-light gift-idea match board that maps recipients, occasions, interests, constraints, and thoughtful idea candidates without collecting sensitive personal data, giving financial advice, or using shopping links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize privacy-light gift planning for birthdays, holidays, thank-yous, graduations, milestones, and similar occasions. It helps compare generic gift candidates by fit, effort, timing, uncertainty, and next steps without shopping links or financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide sensitive personal details while describing recipients or occasions.

Mitigation: Ask for privacy-light labels, broad interests, and general constraints; omit or generalize sensitive details.

Risk: Budget discussion could be mistaken for financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat budget only as a user-supplied comfort range or non-monetary effort level and avoid debt, credit, investment, or overspending advice.

Risk: Gift ideas can rely on sensitive or uncertain preferences.

Mitigation: Flag health, religion, culture, allergies, age-restricted items, workplace, sizing, and relationship-sensitive ideas for confirmation instead of guessing.

Risk: Shopping or affiliate links could bias recommendations or create tracking exposure.

Mitigation: Keep ideas generic and link-free; provide only generic search phrases or local category suggestions if the user wants to shop later.
