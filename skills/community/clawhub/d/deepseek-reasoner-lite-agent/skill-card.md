## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad content-creator persona may be a poor fit for workflows that require a different assistant style or stricter task boundaries.

Mitigation: Review the skill behavior before deployment and enable it only in sessions where this writing posture is intended.

Risk: Template variables such as $DATE$ and $SESSION_GROUP_ID$ may need runtime substitution by the host environment.

Mitigation: Confirm the target agent runtime substitutes expected variables or leaves them harmless before publishing the skill.
