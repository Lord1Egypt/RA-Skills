## Use Case:

Developers and product teams use this Chinese-language skill to turn early creative ideas into validated design specifications before implementation. It guides collaborative brainstorming, compares alternatives, and records an approved design document when the design is ready.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may inspect project context and produce design guidance that does not match the user's intent if requirements are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review each proposed design section during the skill's validation steps before allowing implementation to begin.

Risk: The workflow may create and commit a design document after validation.

Mitigation: Set expectations before use if automatic commits are not desired, and review the generated design document before commit.
