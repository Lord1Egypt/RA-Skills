## Description:

Strategic Narrative Designer helps agents build a five-beat strategic change narrative from a positioning truth set and belief map, with proof beats labeled as measured, user-provided, or needing a source.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, product, and narrative teams use this skill to frame category change before building a message house or pitch narrative. It produces an old-world-to-promised-land story arc grounded in upstream positioning, audience beliefs, and traceable proof labels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated proof beats or market-change claims could be mistaken for substantiated messaging.

Mitigation: Review every proof label and keep unsupported proof beats marked [needs source] until they pass claim review.

Risk: The skill can write narrative outputs and proposal events after user approval.

Mitigation: Approve saves only after reviewing the arc, and check proposed canon or claim changes before promoting them.
