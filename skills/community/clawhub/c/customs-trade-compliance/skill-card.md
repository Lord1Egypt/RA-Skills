## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may provide legal or regulatory guidance that becomes outdated or does not fit a specific shipment, jurisdiction, product, or transaction.

Mitigation: Verify tariff rates, sanctions lists, filing deadlines, regulatory citations, and transaction-specific facts before relying on outputs for customs filings or compliance decisions.

Risk: Evaluation examples or local scripts could expose credentials if run with API keys directly on the command line.

Mitigation: Use environment-based secret handling and avoid passing API keys as command-line arguments.
