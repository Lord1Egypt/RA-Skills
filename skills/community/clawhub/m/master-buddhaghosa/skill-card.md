## Description:

Provides cited Theravada Buddhist study guidance in a Buddhaghosa-inspired commentarial style, focused on the Visuddhimagga, Pali canon, Abhidhamma, meditation topic classifications, seven purifications, and related Mahavihara materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xr843](https://clawhub.ai/user/xr843)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use this skill as a study aid for Theravada commentarial topics, including the Visuddhimagga, Pali terminology, Abhidhamma analysis, meditation object taxonomy, and citation-grounded Buddhist textual explanation. It is not a substitute for formal meditation instruction from a qualified teacher.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-derived religious questions may be sent to the external FoJin service when offline sources are insufficient.

Mitigation: Use offline-only mode for sensitive use, avoid personal or identifying details in questions, and review live retrieval behavior before deployment.

Risk: The skill enforces a strongly Chinese Theravada commentarial voice and a repeated study-resource footer that may not fit every user or setting.

Mitigation: Review the style constraints before installing and present the skill as a study aid rather than a neutral general Buddhism assistant.

Risk: Religious study output can be mistaken for individualized meditation instruction.

Mitigation: Keep formal practice guidance outside the skill's scope and direct users seeking specific meditation instruction to a qualified teacher.
