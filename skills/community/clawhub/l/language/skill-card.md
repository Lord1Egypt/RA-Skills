## Description:

A comprehensive AI agent skill for language learners at every level. Builds personalized study plans, teaches vocabulary in context, corrects your writing and speaking with explanation, prepares you for real conversations before they happen, tracks your progress, and keeps you moving forward when motivation fades.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External language learners use this skill to build personalized study plans, learn vocabulary in context, receive writing and speaking correction, rehearse real conversations, and track progress over time.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personalization may lead users to share unnecessary sensitive personal details for examples or practice material.

Mitigation: Share only the personal context needed for better examples, and avoid sensitive personal details unless intentionally using them in practice material.

Risk: Language-learning corrections or study plans may be incorrect, incomplete, or mismatched to the learner's level.

Mitigation: Review important corrections and plans before relying on them for exams, professional communication, travel, or other high-stakes use.
