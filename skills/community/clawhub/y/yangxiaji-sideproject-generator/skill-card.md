## Description:

Generates Chinese-language AI side-project recommendations and phased action plans from a short preference questionnaire.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[freedompixels](https://clawhub.ai/user/freedompixels)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking users exploring AI side projects use this skill to answer five preference questions and receive a recommended direction, a 100-day action plan, a self-introduction template, and a first-week checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated business recommendations may be generic and may not reflect every questionnaire answer.

Mitigation: Treat the plan as a starting point and review market fit, time commitment, and personal constraints before acting.

Risk: The bundled script can save a generated markdown plan in the local working directory.

Mitigation: Run it in an intended workspace and review the generated file before sharing it.
