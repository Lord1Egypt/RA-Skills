## Description:

AI Meeting Notes turns pasted meeting notes, transcripts, email threads, or chat text into summaries, action items with owners and deadlines, decisions, saved notes, and todo tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[modestyrichards](https://clawhub.ai/user/modestyrichards)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external users can paste meeting notes, transcripts, email threads, or chat exports to get a concise meeting summary, owner-assigned action items, decisions, open questions, and follow-up task tracking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill auto-saves raw pasted meeting content while also saying nothing is stored.

Mitigation: Install only in workspaces where local saved meeting notes and todo files are appropriate, and avoid pasting secrets, HR/legal discussions, customer data, or private transcripts unless local retention is approved.
