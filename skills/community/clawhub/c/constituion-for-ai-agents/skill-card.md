## Description:

Provides a conceptual governance framework for AI agent constitutions covering mandates, permissions, prohibitions, performance standards, escalation protocols, and succession planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, organizations, and agent operators use this skill to understand and plan AI agent constitutions before creating or amending them through the Umnix platform. It helps structure governance rules, operational boundaries, escalation thresholds, and succession expectations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public constitutions could accidentally include secrets, personal data, financial credentials, or proprietary details.

Mitigation: Keep constitutions limited to governance rules, operational boundaries, performance standards, escalation thresholds, and succession plans; review drafts before publication.

Risk: High-impact example permissions for financial, refund, database, and messaging actions could be copied without enough organizational approval.

Mitigation: Require explicit, scoped, human-approved policies for high-impact actions before using them in an active constitution.

Risk: Readers may treat the conceptual framework as an operational enforcement layer.

Mitigation: Use the skill as planning and governance guidance only; create and manage constitutions through the Umnix web platform.
