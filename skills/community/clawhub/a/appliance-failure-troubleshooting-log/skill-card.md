## Description:

Creates a repair-ready appliance failure log with symptoms, timing, model details, error codes, safe checks, photo evidence, warranty questions, and technician call notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users such as homeowners, renters, and household managers use this skill to organize appliance failure symptoms, timelines, safe checks, evidence, warranty questions, technician call notes, and follow-up records before repair conversations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may mistake the log for an appliance diagnosis or repair procedure.

Mitigation: Keep output focused on documentation, communication, safe user-level checks, and professional follow-up; do not claim a diagnosis or provide repair steps.

Risk: Hazard reports such as gas smell, smoke, sparks, flooding, shock risk, carbon monoxide alarms, or fire concerns need urgent handling.

Mitigation: Pause routine logging and direct the user to stop using the appliance and contact emergency services, the utility provider, a landlord, or a qualified professional as appropriate.

Risk: Warranty, landlord, refund, or repair-cost outcomes may be uncertain.

Mitigation: Frame warranty and service content as questions, notes, and records rather than guarantees about coverage, refunds, repair costs, or authorization.
