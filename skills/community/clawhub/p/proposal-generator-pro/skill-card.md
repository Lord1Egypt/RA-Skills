## Use Case:

Freelancers, agencies, consultants, and service providers use this skill to turn client briefs, discovery call notes, or RFP requirements into polished proposal drafts with scope, timeline, pricing tiers, terms, and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated legal, payment, or pricing terms may be unsuitable for a specific client, jurisdiction, or engagement.

Mitigation: Review proposal terms, pricing, and payment language before sending them to clients, using qualified legal or business review when needed.

Risk: Client briefs and discovery notes may contain confidential business or personal information.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting confidential client information unless the agent setup is approved for that data.

Risk: Proposal drafts may include persuasive claims, timelines, case studies, or expected outcomes that need verification.

Mitigation: Confirm all claims, examples, timelines, and deliverables against the actual service offering and client context.
