## Description:

Educational Content & How-To helps an agent plan accurate educational social content by choosing one teachable topic, structuring it with the TEACH framework, writing teaching copy and CTAs, and routing the result to format writers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Social media teams, marketers, creators, and their agents use this skill to turn brand expertise, audience research, and content pillars into useful how-to lessons, tutorials, explainers, myth-busters, and authority-building posts. It is intended for planning the lesson substance, copy, CTA, accuracy checks, and downstream routing rather than publishing or independently verifying facts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated how-to content can include incorrect claims, fabricated statistics, or advice beyond the publisher's expertise.

Mitigation: Require human review before publication, verify factual claims against cited sources, and only teach topics within real expertise.

Risk: Health, finance, legal, safety, or other high-impact how-to content can cause harm if presented as professional advice.

Mitigation: Use extra expert review, include a not-professional-advice disclaimer, and point readers to qualified professionals.

Risk: Platform metrics and algorithm claims may become stale.

Mitigation: Re-verify marketing and platform signal claims quarterly and attribute cited marketing research rather than presenting it as settled fact.
