This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for Chinese-language reflective I Ching readings, including divination-style prompts, hexagram interpretation, and symbolic decision reflection.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat reflective divination output as advice for financial, legal, medical, safety, or other high-stakes decisions.

Mitigation: Present readings as symbolic reflection and direct users to qualified professional sources for high-stakes decisions.

Risk: Oracle Voice responses may be mistaken for factual prediction or deterministic guidance.

Mitigation: Keep responses non-prescriptive and frame the output as an interpretive mirror rather than an answer.
