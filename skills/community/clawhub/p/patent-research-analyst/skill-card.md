## Description:

Guides patent research projects from user-confirmed technical inputs through multi-pass PatSnap/Zhihuiya patent search, five analysis views, and structured HTML plus Word report output.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Enterprise IPR teams, R&D engineers, and research project owners use this skill to collect patent-search requirements, query patent data, evaluate technical routes, assess novelty and FTO-style risks, track competitors, and prepare structured patent analysis reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: FTO, infringement, and legal-risk outputs may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill's conclusions as patent research support only and require qualified legal review before relying on them for business or legal decisions.

Risk: Patent searches can disclose technical details or competitor names to the configured patent data service.

Mitigation: Confirm user inputs before tool use and provide only technical details or competitor names that are appropriate to share with the configured service.

Risk: Patent reports can become misleading if live patent tools, patentId resolution, legal status, or claim text are unavailable or stale.

Mitigation: Verify MCP configuration, query current legal status and claim 1 text before FTO-style analysis, and avoid fabricating patent links, identifiers, or search data.
