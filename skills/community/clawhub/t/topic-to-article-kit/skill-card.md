## Description:

Turn a chosen AI topic into a public-account writing package by collecting evidence from X/Twitter and other public sources, extracting high-value comments, and producing title options plus an Obsidian-ready research packet and outline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rotbit](https://clawhub.ai/user/rotbit)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Writers and content researchers use this skill to prepare public-account articles about AI topics by gathering evidence, extracting useful comments, and organizing an Obsidian-ready research packet and outline.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally creates article folders and Markdown files in the user's real Obsidian vault.

Mitigation: Review the target vault path before use and keep backups if the vault contains important writing work.
