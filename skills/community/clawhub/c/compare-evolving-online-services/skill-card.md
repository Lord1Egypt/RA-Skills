## Description:

Compare fast-changing online services using current, scoped sources for features, pricing, access rules, and policy limits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nextaltair](https://clawhub.ai/user/nextaltair)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to compare online services whose features, pricing, access rules, models, or policies change frequently. It guides agents to build current source bundles, separate claim scopes, resolve conflicts, and recommend services by user priority.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill guides live research on fast-changing services, so stale, incomplete, or scope-mismatched sources can lead to misleading comparisons.

Mitigation: Use current first-party sources for decisive claims, record each claim's date and scope, and mark unresolved conflicts or unknown time-sensitive details explicitly.
