## Description:

A Sichuan cuisine reference skill that helps agents answer questions about classic recipes, flavor profiles, ingredients, substitutions, techniques, meal planning, and Sichuan food culture.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and culinary assistants use this skill to provide Sichuan-focused cooking help, including recipe steps, ingredient guidance, flavor-profile explanations, and technique advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recipes involving hot oil, deep frying, or smoking can create burn, fire, or ventilation hazards.

Mitigation: Use strong ventilation, stable cookware, close supervision, and appropriate grease-fire safety practices.

Risk: Ingredient advice may not fit every location, allergy profile, or dietary need.

Mitigation: Adjust ingredients and substitutions for local availability and the user's dietary requirements.
