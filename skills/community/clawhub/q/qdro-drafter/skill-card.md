## Description:

Drafts plan-typed U.S. retirement-division order packets for attorney review, including QDRO, COAP, TSP RBCO, MPDO, and governmental DRO drafts with survivor-benefit, award, and pre-approval cover-letter components.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External matrimonial attorneys, QDRO specialists, and divorce financial professionals use this skill to collect plan facts and draft attorney-review retirement-division order packets before plan-administrator submission or court entry.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft retirement-division orders may be incomplete, inconsistent with plan rules, or based on stale legal assumptions.

Mitigation: Verify plan rules, current law, model-order language, and all draft provisions with qualified matrimonial counsel before submitting anything to a plan administrator or court.

Risk: The workflow may involve sensitive party identifiers, including SSNs and plan account information.

Mitigation: Keep full SSNs and account numbers out of working drafts; use last-4 identifiers in draft text and place full identifiers only in sealed addenda or plan-approved channels.

Risk: Participant retirement, loans, disability, or death before order entry can reduce or extinguish alternate-payee rights.

Mitigation: Treat timing flags as attorney-review priorities and route drafts through plan-administrator pre-approval whenever the administrator accepts pre-entry review.
