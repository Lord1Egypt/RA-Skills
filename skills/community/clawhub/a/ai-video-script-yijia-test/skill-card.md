## Description:

Generates Chinese-oriented AI short-video script packages with positioning, shot breakdowns, visual prompts, narration, subtitles, and consolidated prompts for Yijia, Sora, or Grok video-generation tests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External short-video creators, AI video producers, and content marketers use this skill to turn a topic or keywords into a structured script, shot plan, copy layer, and test-ready AI video prompt.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional Yijia command example references a local path, API-key-dependent generation flow, target account, and language/category settings that may not match the user's environment.

Mitigation: Review and update the path, credentials, target, category, language, duration, resolution, and model settings before executing any generated or copied command.
