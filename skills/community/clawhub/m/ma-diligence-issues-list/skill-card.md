## Description:

Use this skill when a corporate-development associate, M&A attorney, or PE deal team needs to turn data-room artifacts into a structured diligence issues list with severity-rated findings, Top-10 critical issues, deal-killer flags, and reps and warranties recommendations for licensed-counsel and deal-partner review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Corporate development teams, private equity deal teams, M&A attorneys, investment-banking analysts, and integration leads use this skill to convert provided data-room materials into a draft, evidence-anchored diligence issues package for review by licensed counsel and the deal partner.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process confidential diligence materials, including contracts, financials, customer names, employee details, and regulatory information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in an approved workspace after confirming the user is authorized to provide the materials to the agent.

Risk: Draft issue lists and recommendations could be mistaken for legal, tax, accounting, valuation, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs labeled as draft work and route them to licensed counsel and the deal partner before transaction decisions are made.

Risk: Missing or incomplete data-room artifacts can lead to unconfirmed findings or understated diligence gaps.

Mitigation: Require every issue to cite provided source evidence or be logged as an unresolved question for management.
