## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated SEO content may contain inaccurate or unverified claims if AI-written copy is not grounded in structured evidence.

Mitigation: Require source data, evidence blocks, citations or freshness labels for volatile facts, and review high-impact pages before publishing.

Risk: Scaled page templates can create thin, duplicate, or low-value URLs that harm search quality.

Mitigation: Use selective indexation, noindex or prune weak URLs, deduplicate intents, and launch in monitored batches.

Risk: Third-party, public, scraped, UGC, or customer data can create permission, privacy, or consent issues.

Mitigation: Confirm source permissions, attribution requirements, aggregation or anonymization, moderation, and consent before using data at scale.
