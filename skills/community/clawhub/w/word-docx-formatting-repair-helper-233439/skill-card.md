## Description:

Helps users plan, automate, and validate Word/DOCX formatting repair, find-and-replace, style cleanup, and repeatable document editing workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Office workers, legal and operations teams, students, and documentation maintainers use this skill to turn Word/DOCX formatting cleanup needs into practical workflows, checklists, scripts, or implementation guidance. It is suited for style repair, find-and-replace planning, document automation, and validation notes that help users act without reviewing the original demand evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for general productivity or automation requests outside Word/DOCX formatting.

Mitigation: Use the skill explicitly for Word/DOCX formatting, style cleanup, find-and-replace, and related document repair tasks.

Risk: Generated scripts or automation guidance may modify sensitive documents incorrectly if run without review.

Mitigation: Review scripts before execution, test on document copies, and verify results against the user's success criteria.
