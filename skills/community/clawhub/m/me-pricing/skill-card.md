## Description:

Help figure out pricing for a product or service using minimalist entrepreneur principles. Use when someone is setting prices, considering price changes, or struggling with what to charge.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[carollili](https://clawhub.ai/user/carollili)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and business builders use this skill to set an initial price, evaluate price changes, plan tiered pricing, and estimate the customer count needed for financial independence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing guidance may be mistaken for validated financial or business advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as starting points and verify prices against costs, competitors, customer feedback, and applicable legal, tax, or accounting obligations before use.

Risk: Activation boundaries may be unclear in broad business discussions.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill for pricing decisions, price changes, tier design, and revenue-target calculations; confirm scope with the user when the request is ambiguous.
