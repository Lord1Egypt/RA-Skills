## Description:

Plans content creation, distribution, and lead magnets for startup content marketing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[c0ldsmi1e](https://clawhub.ai/user/c0ldsmi1e)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders and startup teams use this skill to turn product, audience, capacity, and growth goals into a practical content marketing plan with pillars, creation cadence, distribution tactics, lead magnets, PR guidance, and a 30-day action checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public distribution and media outreach tactics can affect brand reputation if applied without review.

Mitigation: Review recommended communities, posts, pitches, and amplification tactics before publishing or contacting third parties.
