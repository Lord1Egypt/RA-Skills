## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill as a quick reference when designing or reviewing lithium-ion battery management concepts, including balancing, SOC/SOH estimation, thermal controls, protection thresholds, communications, topologies, and safety considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Battery-management reference values could be treated as production-ready safety limits.

Mitigation: Verify all safety-critical battery designs against current component datasheets, applicable standards, and qualified engineering review before use.

Risk: The skill provides local help-style technical guidance rather than a validated design workflow.

Mitigation: Use the output as educational starting material and require independent validation for cell selection, protection thresholds, thermal design, and functional safety decisions.
