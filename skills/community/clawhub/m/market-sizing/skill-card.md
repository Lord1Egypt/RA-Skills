Developers, founders, analysts, and business planners use this skill to produce defensible market-size estimates with defined TAM/SAM/SOM boundaries, multiple calculation methods, triangulation, sensitivity analysis, and clear assumptions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market-size estimates may be misleading when external data, cited sources, or assumptions are weak or outdated.

Mitigation: Review cited sources and assumptions, document uncertainty ranges, and validate estimates before using them for business or investment decisions.
