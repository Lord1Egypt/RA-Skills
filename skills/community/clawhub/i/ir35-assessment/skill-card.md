## Description:

Assess IR35 employment status for UK contractors using HMRC criteria and case law factors. Generates risk ratings, recommended contract amendments, and Status Determination Statements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External UK contractors, personal service companies, recruitment agencies, end clients, accountants, and tax advisers use this skill to assess IR35 employment status from engagement details or contract text. It produces factor analysis, risk ratings, contract and working-practice recommendations, and Status Determination Statement drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United Kingdom

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the IR35 assessment as definitive legal or tax advice.

Mitigation: Present the output as an assessment aid, include the legal disclaimer, and recommend qualified professional advice for borderline or complex cases.

Risk: Contract text and working-practice details can contain commercially sensitive or personal information.

Mitigation: Handle inputs and outputs according to the user's data policy and avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive details.

Risk: The assessment can be incomplete or misleading when the user provides thin or one-sided engagement facts.

Mitigation: Ask targeted follow-up questions, state confidence and evidence gaps, and distinguish contract wording from actual working practices.
