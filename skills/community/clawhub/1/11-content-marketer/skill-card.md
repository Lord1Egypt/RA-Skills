## Description:

Helps create Chinese-language content marketing plans based on product positioning, including a content matrix, topic plan, distribution strategy, and performance tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and marketing teams use this skill to plan content strategy for products, including platform-specific topics, publishing cadence, distribution channels, and measurable success indicators.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow assumes Chinese-language content channels such as WeChat, Zhihu, Xiaohongshu, and Douyin.

Mitigation: Review the generated channel plan and adapt platforms, language, and metrics to the target market before use.
