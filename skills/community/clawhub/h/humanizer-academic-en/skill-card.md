## Description:

Humanizer Academic English edition. Trigger when the user asks to polish academic prose, remove AI tone, reduce AIGC traces, or says things like "make this sound less AI".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and academic writers use this skill to revise English academic prose so it reads naturally while preserving citations, factual claims, disciplinary register, and authorial voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill rewrites academic prose and may obscure the extent of editorial assistance if used outside institutional authorship rules.

Mitigation: Use it consistently with the relevant institution's authorship and editing policies, and request diagnostics when changes need to be reviewed.

Risk: Meaning, citations, or evidence logic could shift during rewriting.

Mitigation: Review revised passages against the original text, especially claims, citations, uncertainty, and discipline-specific terminology.
