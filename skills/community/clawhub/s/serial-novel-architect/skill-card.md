## Description:

当用户需要搭建、重构或撰写长篇小说、系列小说或网文连载项目时使用此技能。它适合把零散灵感整理成可持续推进的写作系统，包括世界观、时间线、人物弧线、分卷结构、章节卡、线索回收、平台化开篇优化和 Markdown 写作工程整理。

## Use Case:

External writers and writing-focused agents use this Chinese-language skill to turn scattered long-form or serial-fiction ideas into workable story architecture, including worlds, timelines, character arcs, volume outlines, chapter cards, continuity trackers, and publication-facing copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is specialized for Chinese long-form fiction and serial-fiction workflows, so outputs may be poorly matched to non-Chinese writing or unrelated Markdown project work.

Mitigation: Review outputs for language, genre, and project fit before adopting them outside Chinese fiction-planning workflows.

Risk: Story plans, timelines, character arcs, and continuity trackers can introduce misleading or inconsistent creative guidance if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Treat generated planning documents as drafts and check major changes against the project's timeline, glossary, character files, chapter cards, and clue-recovery table.
