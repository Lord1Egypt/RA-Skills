## Description:

Screen a pre-token DeFi protocol before farming or entering it. Runs the pre-entry checklist from the Token Launch Radar method — is it genuinely pre-token, does its category have live-token peers to price against, and are its size, momentum, age and reach real — and returns a pass / caution / fail verdict per check. Use when asked "is this pre-token protocol legit?", "should I farm this?", "check this protocol before I put time or money in", or before adding any no-token protocol to a watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

DeFi researchers and prospective farmers use this skill to screen a no-token protocol before adding it to a watchlist or deciding whether to farm it. It checks whether a token already trades, the availability of live peers, corroborated TVL, seven-day growth, protocol age, and chain reach, then gives a pass, caution, or fail verdict for each check.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A screening verdict could be mistaken for an audit or assurance that a protocol is safe.

Mitigation: Treat the checklist as preliminary screening only; it does not audit contracts, teams, or tokenomics, so review those independently before making a decision.

Risk: A passing screen could be read as a promise that a token, airdrop, or farming reward will follow.

Mitigation: Present the result as a watchlist screen only and state that passing does not predict a launch or reward.
