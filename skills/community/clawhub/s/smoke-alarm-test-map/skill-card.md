## Description:

Build a room-by-room smoke alarm map and recurring test log from user-provided home details, focusing on checklist tracking and manufacturer or local fire-safety guidance without electrical repair advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize home, rental, office, or other space details into a smoke alarm map, walkthrough test route, reusable test log, issue list, follow-up checklist, and reminder plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could mistake the checklist for code-compliance certification, repair guidance, or an assurance that a space is safe.

Mitigation: State that the output is only a smoke alarm map and test log, and direct failed, damaged, hardwired, missing, or emergency situations to manufacturer, landlord, local fire-safety, qualified professional, or emergency guidance as appropriate.

Risk: Unknown alarm types, expired devices, chirping alarms, failed tests, blocked devices, or damaged devices could be under-prioritized if they are buried in notes.

Mitigation: Mark those items clearly in the issue list and follow-up checklist for manual review.
