## Description:

Creates standardized product Feature List tables for product initiation or version planning, with multi-level module breakdowns, user stories, and software/hardware dependency notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kira2red](https://clawhub.ai/user/kira2red)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers and planning teams use this skill to convert business needs, workflows, or early product ideas into structured Feature List tables for roadmap planning, development scheduling, and project management.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated Feature Lists can omit project-specific constraints or misstate software, hardware, or upstream system dependencies.

Mitigation: Review the table with product, engineering, and hardware owners before using it for scheduling or implementation.

Risk: The bundled template and guidance are Chinese-language and schema-specific.

Mitigation: Adjust or translate the column schema before using it for teams that need another locale or format.
