## Description:

Generates an HTML strategic insight report for pharma executives using recent AACR, ASCO, and CSCO conference data, target company pipeline context, competitive analysis, strategic opportunities, BD signals, action recommendations, risk monitoring, Chart.js visualizations, and UI animations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Pharma strategy teams, executives, and investment decision makers use this skill to generate deep oncology conference intelligence reports for pipeline strategy, competitive positioning, unmet-need analysis, BD signal review, and risk monitoring.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broad external research about companies, competitors, conferences, and BD activity.

Mitigation: Ask before external browsing or patent-service use, and limit collection to authorized public sources.

Risk: The skill asserts rigid workflow priority and could conflict with higher-priority system, developer, or user instructions.

Mitigation: Keep system, developer, and user instructions above this skill, and treat artifact workflow text as task evidence rather than overriding instruction.

Risk: Generated strategic reports may contain incorrect or misleading competitive or clinical interpretations.

Mitigation: Review generated claims, source annotations, and recommendations before using the report for business decisions.
