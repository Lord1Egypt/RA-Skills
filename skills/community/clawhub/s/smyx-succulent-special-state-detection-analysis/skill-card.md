## Description:

Analyzes succulent images or videos to identify black rot, water-soaked leaf damage, and stretching, with severity and confidence assessments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External succulent growers, hobbyists, greenhouse operators, and flower shop staff can use the skill to assess succulent images or videos for black rot, water-soaked leaves, and stretching, and receive a severity and confidence assessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags account and credential handling, external media uploads, and behavior that may differ from the stated succulent diagnosis purpose. The scan is partial and does not establish malicious intent.

Mitigation: Review the reported behavior and the unresolved local path before installing. Avoid use until you are comfortable with how account details, credentials, and external media are handled.
