## Description:

Generates ideal customer profiles, lead acquisition strategies, LinkedIn and Google search formulas, structured lead lists, and cold outreach connection tips for B2B sales prospecting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders, SDRs, BDRs, account executives, growth marketers, consultants, and agencies use this skill to turn targeting inputs into B2B prospecting playbooks with ICPs, search formulas, lead-list templates, scoring guidance, and outreach angles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be triggered broadly for lead-generation-adjacent requests.

Mitigation: Review the generated prospecting plan before acting on it and provide only business targeting details appropriate for sales planning.

Risk: Generated prospecting outputs may include inferred lead criteria, example contacts, or contact-status assumptions that are not verified live data.

Mitigation: Independently verify company, role, contact, and trigger information before adding prospects to a CRM or outreach sequence.

Risk: Prospecting and outreach workflows can create privacy, anti-spam, or platform-terms compliance obligations.

Mitigation: Check applicable privacy laws, CAN-SPAM requirements, and platform terms before collecting prospect data or sending outreach.
