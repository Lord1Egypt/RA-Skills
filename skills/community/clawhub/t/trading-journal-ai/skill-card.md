## Description:

Log every trade with full context (thesis, entry, exit, PnL, emotion, lesson), generate weekly and monthly performance reports, and identify patterns in wins and losses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mars82311111](https://clawhub.ai/user/mars82311111)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External traders and trading-system operators use this skill to record trade context, review weekly or monthly performance, identify recurring patterns, and update trading rules from observed outcomes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The local journal can contain sensitive trade history, strategy notes, PnL, and emotional context.

Mitigation: Store the journal only in trusted local workspaces and avoid sharing it without reviewing or redacting sensitive financial details.

Risk: Generated strategy adjustments may be incorrect or inappropriate for live trading.

Mitigation: Review generated recommendations before applying them to any live trading workflow.
