## Description:

Basic programmatic SEO audit - analyze page templates, crawl budget issues, and indexing health. Free version covers template analysis, crawl budget checklist, and basic content quality scoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[apex-stack-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/apex-stack-ai)

## Use Case:

Developers, SEO operators, and growth teams use this skill to perform a basic programmatic SEO audit for a provided URL or domain, covering page templates, crawl budget issues, indexing health, content quality, and prioritized recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce SEO conclusions for domains the user does not control or is not authorized to analyze.

Mitigation: Use it only with domains the user is allowed to audit.

Risk: The artifact includes a promotional link to a paid full version.

Mitigation: Review promotional links before publishing or deploying in environments with content restrictions.
