## Description:

GymBuddy is a mostly Chinese-language fitness coaching skill for posture correction, body composition and training data analysis, periodized training planning, and TDEE estimation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[joeyxin-del](https://clawhub.ai/user/joeyxin-del)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use GymBuddy to add a fitness coaching assistant that answers training, posture, nutrition-energy, and planning questions using bundled knowledge notes and conservative safety guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fitness, posture, and nutrition advice can be mistaken for medical diagnosis or treatment.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational and seek qualified professional help for pain, injury, chronic disease, pregnancy, neurological symptoms, or cardiovascular concerns.

Risk: Bundled knowledge notes may not cover every user situation or contraindication.

Mitigation: Use the skill's conservative fallback behavior when sources are insufficient and prefer professional assessment for uncertain or higher-risk cases.
