## Use Case:

External content creators, local businesses, and marketers use this skill to draft Hong Kong and Chinese-language social-media posts, reply copy, hashtags, and content calendars. It provides reusable templates and guidance for Xiaohongshu, X, Douyin, WeChat, and related channels rather than executable publishing automation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may assume the skill can schedule, publish, analyze, or reply on social-media accounts even though the release evidence describes a template pack with no executable code.

Mitigation: Treat generated content as draft text only; require separate explicit approval, account permissions, and platform controls before any agent posts or replies publicly.
