## Description:

Generate draft articles, outlines, and editorial content matching a distinctive analytical, skeptical voice with sharp critical commentary, conversational tone, and strategic humor.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[abeltennyson](https://clawhub.ai/user/abeltennyson)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, strategists, and content teams use this skill to draft skeptical but fair technology and business articles, structured analysis outlines, social threads, quick takes, and research-backed opinion pieces. It is especially oriented toward AI, automation, market dynamics, and claims that need evidence-driven critique.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts, draft content, and user-provided context may be sent to SkillBoss API Hub, and the skill does not provide detailed privacy or data-handling disclosure.

Mitigation: Use a dedicated, revocable API key, avoid including secrets or confidential drafts unless policy permits it, and consider applying the markdown guidance locally with an approved model instead of the API example.

Risk: The skill is designed to produce persuasive critical commentary, which can mislead readers if generated claims are unsupported or insufficiently checked.

Mitigation: Require human editorial review, verify major claims against credible sources, preserve nuance and counterarguments, and do not publish generated analysis without fact-checking.
