## Description:

Map a decision into options, non-negotiables, criteria, best/base/worst cases, reversibility, and a review checkpoint.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to compare multiple life, work, or project options, separate facts from assumptions and fears, and produce a structured decision brief with a provisional choice or next test.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the decision brief as professional legal, medical, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Present the output as a thinking aid and direct high-stakes legal, medical, or financial decisions to qualified expert advice.

Risk: The skill may make uncertain outcomes feel more certain than the available information supports.

Mitigation: Keep facts, assumptions, and fears visibly separate, avoid claims of certainty, and prefer smaller reversible tests when uncertainty is high.
