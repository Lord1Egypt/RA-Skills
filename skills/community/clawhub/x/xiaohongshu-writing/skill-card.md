## Description:

Generates Xiaohongshu and RedNote post drafts with title options, conversational body copy, suggested tags, SEO guidance, and checks intended to make the writing sound less AI-generated.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and social-media operators use this skill to draft Xiaohongshu or RedNote posts, including title alternatives, short-form body copy, and hashtag suggestions. It is intended for content ideation and drafting, with human review before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-assisted social posts may mislead readers if edited to hide automation or if personal-experience claims are untrue.

Mitigation: Disclose AI assistance when required and use firsthand claims only when they accurately reflect the publisher's real experience.

Risk: Generated marketing copy may include exaggerated claims, clickbait framing, undisclosed promotion, or platform-policy issues.

Mitigation: Review each draft for accuracy, substantiation, sponsorship disclosure, and Xiaohongshu or RedNote policy compliance before publishing.
