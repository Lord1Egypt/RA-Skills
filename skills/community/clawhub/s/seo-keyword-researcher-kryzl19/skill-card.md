## Description:

Researches SEO keywords for a topic and produces a Markdown article brief with primary and secondary keywords, competition analysis, article structure, and SEO recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kryzl19](https://clawhub.ai/user/kryzl19)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content marketers, SEO writers, and developers use this skill to plan SEO articles, identify low-competition keyword opportunities, and create article briefs from public search results and competitor pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad content planning or keyword research prompts may invoke the skill and cause public web searches or competitor page fetches.

Mitigation: Use explicit wording when SEO research is intended, and state when web-based SEO research should not be performed.

Risk: Keyword and competitor recommendations can become stale or vary by search context.

Mitigation: Review generated briefs against current search results and authoritative SEO requirements before publishing content.
