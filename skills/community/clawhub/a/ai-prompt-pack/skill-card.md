## Description:

Provides Spanish-language prompts that help digital sellers generate product ideas, sales copy, launch emails, Etsy SEO, Gumroad descriptions, and promotional content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External digital sellers and creators use this skill to draft and adapt prompts for product validation, sales pages, launch email sequences, marketplace SEO, product descriptions, upsells, FAQs, and short promotional video scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The artifact references an external Gumroad page that may involve separate purchase terms or content outside this reviewed release.

Mitigation: Review the Gumroad page independently before purchasing or using any external materials.

Risk: The prompts are written for Spanish-language digital sales workflows and may be less useful when applied directly to other languages or markets.

Mitigation: Translate and adapt prompts for the target audience, then review generated copy before publication.
