## Description:

Best Minds reframes a user question by identifying who would understand the topic best and answering from that expert perspective using public ideas and quotations when possible.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this prompt-method skill to get concise answers framed through relevant expert viewpoints instead of generic assistant opinion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad phrases such as "best minds" or equivalent Chinese trigger terms may invoke the skill during ordinary comparison requests.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for ordinary comparison, or name the desired response language and framing when the skill should or should not be used.

Risk: Expert-perspective simulation can produce guidance that sounds authoritative even when public-source grounding is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review named experts, claims, and quotations against public sources before relying on the answer for consequential decisions.
