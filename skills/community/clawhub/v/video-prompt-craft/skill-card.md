## Description:

Guides users through structured Chinese-language prompts and style references to craft professional English AI video-generation prompts from vague or detailed video ideas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[showtimewalker](https://clawhub.ai/user/showtimewalker)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill to turn rough video concepts into structured AI video prompts with scene rationale, style choices, cinematography, lighting, narrative guidance, and improvement suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad video-prompt wording and may not match users who expect all output in another language.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired language and output style when user intent is ambiguous, especially for users who do not want Chinese-guided responses.

Risk: Generated video prompts can still include incorrect assumptions about missing scene details.

Mitigation: Review the decision table before using the prompt and adjust any inferred subject, environment, style, lighting, or narrative choices.
