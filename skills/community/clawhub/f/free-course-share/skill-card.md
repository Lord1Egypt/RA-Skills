## Description:

Helps Chinese-language creators verify free courses and prepare share-ready course drafts, collages, redacted certificate images, and vertical covers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Chinese-language content creators use this skill to check course costs, access limits, and credential types, then prepare local share drafts and visuals. It helps protect personal details in certificate images and leaves publishing for the user to review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Redaction verification files may persist and may temporarily contain sensitive text; scan coverage is incomplete because two referenced paths were missing.

Mitigation: Follow the scanner guidance: consider the privacy risk before installing, and if proceeding, check how verification files are handled and removed while accounting for the incomplete scan.
