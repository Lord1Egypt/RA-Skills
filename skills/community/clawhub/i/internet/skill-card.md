## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive account details, precise addresses, or router administration access while troubleshooting connectivity or provider issues.

Mitigation: Ask only for the minimum information needed, avoid collecting credentials, and redact account numbers or exact addresses from retained notes.

Risk: Provider or eSIM recommendations can lead to avoidable cost, throttling, incompatibility, or contract penalties if local details are not verified.

Mitigation: Verify coverage, device compatibility, data caps, contract terms, post-promotion pricing, and early termination fees before recommending a purchase or switch.
