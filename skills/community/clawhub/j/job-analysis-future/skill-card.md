## Description:

A Chinese-language study assistant for Chapter 10, "The Future of Job Analysis," covering future work analysis concepts, changing social, technological, and business conditions, flexible work, virtual teams, AI and automation, electronic performance monitoring, job crafting, role definition, analysis units, and storage or retrieval of job-analysis data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students, instructors, and job-analysis learners use this skill for Chinese-language explanations, review outlines, discussion support, and case-based application of Chapter 10 of Brannick, Levine, and Morgeson's job and work analysis material. The skill helps map external changes in work to job-analysis descriptors, sources, methods, units of analysis, role definition, and information management.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is scoped to one textbook chapter and may be incomplete for broader job-analysis topics.

Mitigation: Use it for Chapter 10 learning and route other chapters or broader topics to the appropriate source or skill.

Risk: Textbook discussion of workforce, technology, and labor-market trends may be mistaken for current real-world data.

Mitigation: When users ask for current facts or latest statistics, verify them with up-to-date sources instead of relying on textbook predictions.

Risk: Educational guidance could oversimplify privacy, consent, and trust issues around electronic performance monitoring.

Mitigation: Frame monitoring examples as textbook concepts and remind users to consider legal, ethical, privacy, consent, and organizational trust constraints.
