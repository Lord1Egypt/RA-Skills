## Description:

Compares two or more materials about the same topic or event for consistency across numbers, product names, facts, terminology, source attributions, structural promises, and platform-specific wording, then recommends unified wording without modifying originals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

PR, product, and editorial teams use this skill to compare related drafts and published materials before release, including press releases, articles, presentations, web pages, and social posts. It flags inconsistencies and provides suggested wording while leaving the original materials unchanged.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
