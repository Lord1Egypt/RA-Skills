## Description:

Guides agents through proposal qualification, discovery extraction, value-based pricing, proposal drafting, follow-up, and objection handling for sales opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External sales teams, consultants, agencies, and business developers use this skill to qualify opportunities and create structured, value-focused proposals, pricing tiers, follow-up plans, and proposal reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Proposal, pricing, legal-term, or client-specific claims may be inaccurate or unsuitable for external delivery.

Mitigation: Review generated proposals for factual accuracy, legal terms, pricing assumptions, scope, and client-specific claims before sending them externally.
