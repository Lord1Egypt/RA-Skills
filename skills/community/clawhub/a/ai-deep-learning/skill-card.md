## Description:

Guides users through a structured research workflow for deeply learning a domain using citation networks, source-grounded knowledge bases, and multi-AI cross-examination.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[facadefish](https://clawhub.ai/user/facadefish)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to plan a focused, multi-round study of a new domain, identify foundational literature, build a document-grounded knowledge base, and produce structured knowledge cards and literature lists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may upload private, copyrighted, or sensitive documents to third-party knowledge-base tools while following the workflow.

Mitigation: Review document sensitivity and the selected service's data-handling terms before uploading materials.

Risk: The skill may activate broadly for learning requests where a full deep-research workflow is unnecessary.

Mitigation: Confirm whether the user wants the full structured workflow or a faster conceptual answer before proceeding.

Risk: AI-generated literature suggestions and critiques can be incomplete or misleading when they are not checked against source material.

Mitigation: Use the cited documents and source-grounded knowledge base to verify claims before treating outputs as reliable.
