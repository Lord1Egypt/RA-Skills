## Description:

Guides an AI assistant to redesign SaaS onboarding flows using the Activation Path Method, helping users define their aha moment, map time-to-value, eliminate friction, and produce a concrete minimum viable activation path.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

SaaS founders, growth product managers, and onboarding teams use this skill to diagnose activation friction and produce a specific onboarding redesign plan that shortens the path from signup to first value.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can recommend letting new users enter a product before email verification, which may be unsafe for flows that trust the claimed email identity.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before applying them to authentication flows and require verified email before tenant access, invitations, billing, integrations, exports, API credentials, recovery changes, or other identity-sensitive actions.
