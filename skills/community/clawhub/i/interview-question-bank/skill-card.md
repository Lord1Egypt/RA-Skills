This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Hiring managers, recruiters, and HR professionals use this skill to generate structured interview question banks for specific roles, experience levels, responsibilities, and company cultures.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated interview questions or scoring guidance may be unfair, insufficiently role-specific, or unsuitable for real hiring decisions.

Mitigation: Review generated questions for fairness, role relevance, and legal or HR compliance before use.
