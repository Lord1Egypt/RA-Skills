## Description:

Provides organized summaries of LINE's public platform features, content formats, operational rules, and help resources without accessing private data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[codenova58](https://clawhub.ai/user/codenova58)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to summarize publicly available LINE product pages, help resources, operational rules, creator information, and commercial options while avoiding account operations and private data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be misused to plan account automation, private-message handling, group administration, or access to non-public LINE content.

Mitigation: Use it only for researching and summarizing publicly available LINE information, and do not provide account credentials or request private or administrative actions.

Risk: LINE public pages, rules, and help resources can vary by region and change over time.

Mitigation: Retain source links, dates, and region notes in outputs so readers can verify the current applicable guidance.
