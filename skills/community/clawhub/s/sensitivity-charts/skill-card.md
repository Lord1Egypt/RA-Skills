## Description:

Visualize how changes in variables affect outcomes. Use for risk analysis and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linuszz](https://clawhub.ai/user/linuszz)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and decision-support users can use this skill to structure sensitivity analysis, tornado charts, scenario tables, variable impact rankings, and mitigation notes for user-provided inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad what-if or variable-impact wording when sensitivity analysis was not intended.

Mitigation: Confirm that a sensitivity-analysis workflow is appropriate before applying the generated structure.

Risk: Financial or decision assumptions supplied by the user may be incomplete, unrealistic, or misleading.

Mitigation: Validate input assumptions, ranges, probabilities, and outputs with domain experts before using the analysis for decisions.
