# The moving bottleneck

> When the constraint that limits a system is relieved, the limit moves somewhere else. Improvement is a sequence of constraints, and the habits built around the old one stay behind.

## The structure

A system's output is set by its constraint. Relieve that constraint and output rises until the next-weakest point becomes the constraint. The rules, measures and routines that were built around the old constraint remain in place and now work against the new situation. Improvement therefore has no end state: it is a repeating cycle of finding where the limit is now.

## How to recognize it

- An improvement that **worked, and then stopped paying**: output rose, then stalled at a new level.
- A **new queue in a new place**: work now piles up somewhere it did not before.
- Effort continues at the old constraint out of habit, with no effect.
- **Policies that made sense before** now cause harm: batch sizes, approval steps, staffing ratios and performance measures designed for the old limit.
- After a tool or an AI makes one step fast, the step after it is overwhelmed: drafts are produced in minutes and review takes a week.

## Where it usually breaks

- **Inertia becomes the constraint.** The physical limit has moved and the organization still runs by the old rules.
- **The constraint moves outside**: capacity now exceeds demand, and the limit is the market, which needs entirely different work.
- **The constraint moves to a person**, typically whoever must decide or approve.
- **Nobody looks again**, because the problem was declared solved.

## Where to push

- **Before relieving a constraint, predict where it will go next**, and decide whether the system is ready for that.
- **After relieving it, start again from the first question**: what limits the whole now?
- **Review the rules** made for the old constraint and remove those that no longer serve.
- **Choose where the constraint should be.** A constraint cannot be eliminated, and it can sometimes be placed where it is easiest to manage.

## What to check before naming it

Show the earlier constraint, the improvement, and the new place where work waits. If output never rose, the earlier diagnosis was wrong and the constraint was elsewhere all along.

## Source

- **Eliyahu Goldratt**, *The Goal* (1984) and his later statements of the five focusing steps, the fifth of which is to return to the first step once a constraint is broken, with the warning not to let inertia become the system's constraint.
