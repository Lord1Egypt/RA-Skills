## Use Case:

External users ask about Seoul for visiting, moving, working, studying, or starting a business. The skill helps an agent provide practical, role-aware guidance across neighborhoods, transport, costs, visas, food, safety, healthcare, education, business, and local culture.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Visa, tax, employment, healthcare, legal, and emergency information may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a user's specific situation.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as a practical starting point and confirm important decisions with official Korean government sources, medical professionals, or qualified legal and tax advisors before acting.

Risk: Local social, parking, dining, or workplace customs described by the artifact could conflict with consent, health, safety, or local law in specific contexts.

Mitigation: Prioritize consent, health, safety, and current local law over custom-based suggestions.
