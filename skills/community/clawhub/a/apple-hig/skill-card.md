## Description:

Provides Chinese-language practical guidance for applying Apple Human Interface Guidelines to macOS and iOS UI design decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and designers use this skill to apply Apple-style color, typography, control, layout, Dark Mode, accessibility, and platform-difference guidance when designing or auditing macOS and iOS interfaces.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-language guidance may be limiting for users or reviewers who need English or bilingual material.

Mitigation: Use with Chinese-capable reviewers or pair it with an English Apple HIG reference when review language matters.

Risk: Platform-specific Apple design guidance can be misapplied to Android, Windows, or web-only interfaces.

Mitigation: Use this skill for macOS and iOS design work, and use platform-specific guidance for other environments.
