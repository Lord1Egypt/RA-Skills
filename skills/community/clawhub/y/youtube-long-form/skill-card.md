## Description:

Youtube Long Form helps agents plan YouTube long-form growth strategies, packaging concepts, retention structures, scripts, publishing handoffs, and native Studio diagnostics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, channel operators, and marketing teams use this skill to turn a long-form YouTube idea into a packaging-first plan, retention structure, shootable script, and publishing or analytics handoff. It is intended for long-form video strategy, not Shorts-first growth or direct publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connected publishing tools can affect scheduled YouTube posts.

Mitigation: Review WoopSocial and YouTube Studio permissions before connecting tools, and validate scheduled posts before publication.

Risk: Using the skill outside YouTube long-form strategy can produce unsuitable recommendations.

Mitigation: Use it for long-form planning and route Shorts-first strategy or unrelated growth tasks to the appropriate skill.

Risk: Invented analytics can lead to misleading strategy decisions.

Mitigation: Read CTR, average view duration, retention, traffic sources, and session metrics natively in YouTube Studio instead of fabricating numbers.

Risk: AI-generated footage may require platform or regulatory disclosure.

Mitigation: Apply YouTube Altered Content disclosure when AI-generated footage is used.
