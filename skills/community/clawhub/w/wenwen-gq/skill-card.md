## Description:

Children's piano practice companion expert providing enlightenment guidance, skill advancement, scientific practice planning, repertoire analysis, and psychological support for young learners.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[guofw2019](https://clawhub.ai/user/guofw2019)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, especially parents and caregivers of children ages 3-12, use this agent for piano practice planning, repertoire guidance, practice troubleshooting, exam preparation, and motivation support. It is intended as a Chinese-language companion for home practice rather than a replacement for in-person piano instruction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides text-only piano coaching and cannot directly hear audio or view video of a child's performance.

Mitigation: Ask users to describe the child's age, level, current piece, and observed playing issue, and treat the response as supplemental guidance for a qualified piano teacher.

Risk: The skill answers in Chinese by default, which may not match every user's language preference.

Mitigation: Tell users who need another language to explicitly request that language or choose a different skill.
