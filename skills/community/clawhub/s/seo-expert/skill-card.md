## Description:

SEO & GEO expert assistant with curated guidance for keyword research, on-page optimization, backlink building, technical SEO, new site launches, AI tool site monetization, and generative engine optimization.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External site owners, marketers, founders, and developers use this skill to plan SEO and GEO strategy, analyze websites and competitors, design backlink and content plans, audit technical SEO, and evaluate AI tool site monetization options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: SEO, GEO, market, pricing, backlink, and platform recommendations may become outdated or may not fit a specific region or business context.

Mitigation: Verify current search engine guidance, platform policies, tool pricing, and regional assumptions before spending money or changing a live business site.

Risk: Backlink and bulk-content tactics can create quality, spam, or policy risks if applied mechanically.

Mitigation: Review proposed backlink and content plans for site quality, user value, and search engine policy alignment before execution.
