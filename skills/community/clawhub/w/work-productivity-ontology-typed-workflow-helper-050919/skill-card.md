## Description:

Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams plan, implement, and validate ontology-style productivity workflows, including bug fixes, setup hardening, reliability improvements, and adjacent skill creation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn demand for ontology-style workflows into practical plans, checklists, artifacts, code changes, and validation notes. It is intended for local-friendly productivity work around typed knowledge graphs, structured memory, workflow hardening, and adjacent skill creation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic keywords and broad activation wording may route unrelated work-productivity, ontology, or graph requests to this skill.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or tighten triggers when precise routing is important.

Risk: The skill can produce plans, code, shell commands, configuration snippets, or checklists that may be incomplete or mismatched to the user's environment.

Mitigation: Review generated artifacts before use and validate them against the stated success criteria.
