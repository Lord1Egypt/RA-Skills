## Description:

Family Nutrition Planner generates weekly meal plans, shopping lists, and meal allocation plans based on family health needs, dietary preferences, and nutrition goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and families use this skill to estimate nutrition needs, generate a weekly household meal plan, and create a categorized shopping list with budget estimates. The generated plan should be reviewed by a person before it is used for allergy-sensitive or medical dietary decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Allergy-safety claims are not enforced and generated plans could include unsafe meals.

Mitigation: Require human review for allergy-sensitive plans and do not rely on the skill for allergy-safe meal planning until allergen filtering is fixed and verified.

Risk: Menus, nutrition targets, and budget estimates are rough planning outputs rather than medical or guaranteed cost guidance.

Mitigation: Treat generated plans as examples and review them against household needs, dietary restrictions, and current prices before use.
