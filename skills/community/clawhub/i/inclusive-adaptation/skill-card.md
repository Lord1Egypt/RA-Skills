## Description:

This skill guides agents in profiling household needs and planning inclusive smart-home adaptations, from environmental assessment and modeling guidance to tailored implementation plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjiaocheng](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjiaocheng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Households and adaptation planners can use the skill to understand residents' functional needs, assess home environments, compare suitable devices, and prepare tailored improvement and implementation guidance. It supports planning across older adults, children, disabled people, pregnant people, people recovering from illness, and other household members.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Household profiling may involve sensitive health, disability, or family details.

Mitigation: Explain why information is needed, let users skip questions, focus on functional needs rather than diagnoses, avoid identifying details, and take care before saving or sharing profiles.

Risk: Adaptation recommendations can affect physical safety or touch on health needs.

Mitigation: Use profiles as an initial planning aid; leave medical diagnosis and formal ability assessment to qualified professionals, and have safety-critical work reviewed and installed by professionals.
