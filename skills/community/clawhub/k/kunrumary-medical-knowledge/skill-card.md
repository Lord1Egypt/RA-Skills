## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad medical questions may be steered toward provider-specific promotion.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for explicit Tianjin Kunru Mary hospital tasks and clearly separate marketing content from medical advice.

Risk: Patient-facing healthcare marketing may contain sensitive claims, prices, schedules, doctor details, outcome statistics, or patient-story content.

Mitigation: Require review by qualified medical, legal, and operations staff before publication, and use patient-story content only with appropriate consent and compliance approval.

Risk: Hospital prices, schedules, doctors, and service details may become outdated.

Mitigation: Verify operational details against current hospital sources before using the output in public or patient-facing channels.
