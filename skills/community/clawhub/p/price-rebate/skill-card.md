## Description:

跨平台商品比价与返利综合工具，计算各平台真实到手价（售价-券-返利），推荐最优购买渠道。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External shoppers use this skill to compare matching products across marketplaces, calculate real final prices after discounts, coupons, rebates, and shipping, and choose the best purchase channel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad shopping trigger phrases such as lowest price may invoke the skill during general shopping discussions.

Mitigation: Use the skill when the user explicitly wants product price comparison or rebate-aware purchase guidance.

Risk: Marketplace prices, coupons, rebates, shipping costs, and historical lows can change quickly and may be inaccurate if not checked against current sources.

Mitigation: Confirm final prices and rebate terms on the marketplace or rebate provider before purchase.
