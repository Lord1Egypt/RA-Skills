## Description:

阿德影视制作大师 helps creators adapt novels, stories, articles, or outlines into film scripts, generate AI image asset prompts for scenes and characters, and create AI video storyboard prompts with optional de-identification and PDF export.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sjqweb3-oss](https://clawhub.ai/user/sjqweb3-oss)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External creators, video producers, and AI media teams use this skill to turn source text into production-ready screenplay drafts, visual asset prompts, and timed storyboard prompts for short drama, trailer, and AI video workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: PDF exports are automatically watermarked with the skill branding.

Mitigation: Review generated PDFs before sharing, publishing, or delivering them to clients, and confirm the watermark is acceptable for the intended use.

Risk: The skill can activate from broad film, script, storyboard, and AI video trigger terms.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for film-production workflows and review outputs before relying on them in unrelated writing or planning tasks.
