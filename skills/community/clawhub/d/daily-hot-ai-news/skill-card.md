## Description:

Provides on-demand Chinese-language briefings on recent AI news, industry developments, technical breakthroughs, viewpoints, and source links.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chenhao11235813](https://clawhub.ai/user/chenhao11235813)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to request concise Chinese AI news briefings with source links, covering current industry trends, technical breakthroughs, major events, and community or expert viewpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Briefings may depend on current web results that can be incomplete, fast-changing, or source-dependent.

Mitigation: Require source links, publication dates, and clear separation between reported facts, analysis, and commentary.

Risk: The skill may answer in Chinese by default, which may not fit every deployment audience.

Mitigation: Specify the desired response language when invoking the skill or route output through translation for non-Chinese readers.
