## Description:

A Chinese-first academic writing style guide for geoscience, geochemistry, lunar science, and planetary science papers, covering manuscript drafting, polishing, journal selection, bilingual writing conventions, and reviewer-response phrasing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aircraft06](https://clawhub.ai/user/aircraft06)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic authors use this skill to draft, revise, and polish Chinese and English manuscripts in geoscience, geochemistry, lunar science, and planetary science. It provides journal-style structure guidance, expression examples, vocabulary choices, reviewer-response templates, and submission checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Journal formatting requirements can change after the skill's reference material was authored.

Mitigation: Verify formatting, word limits, and submission requirements against the target journal's current author instructions before submitting a manuscript.

Risk: Academic writing guidance and example phrasing may not fit every field, journal, manuscript, or reviewer concern.

Mitigation: Use the skill output as drafting assistance and have domain authors review claims, tone, citations, journal fit, and ethical declarations before submission.
