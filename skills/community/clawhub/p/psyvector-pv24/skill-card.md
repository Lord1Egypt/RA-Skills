## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The persona may activate on common business terms and steer an interaction into partnership-manager roleplay when the user did not intend that posture.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired role or exit the persona when the user asks to switch back to a normal assistant mode.

Risk: Tool-assisted research for negotiations could collect inappropriate or sensitive personal data.

Mitigation: Limit research to lawful public information and avoid sensitive personal data, as directed by the security guidance.
