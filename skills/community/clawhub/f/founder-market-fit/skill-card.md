## Description:

Articulate founder-market fit for investor and accelerator materials by connecting a founder's background, earned insight, proof, and market timing into a concrete narrative.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders, startup teams, and application writers use this skill to draft founder-market-fit narratives for VC pitches, YC-style accelerator applications, recruiting narratives, and answers to why this team is positioned to win.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may draft plausible assumptions when a founder bio is thin, which could make investor or accelerator materials misleading if left unchecked.

Mitigation: Review every inferred detail, keep only claims the founder can substantiate, and label or remove assumptions before external use.

Risk: Founder-market-fit narratives can overstate execution proof or unfair advantage.

Mitigation: Ground proof in concrete shipped work, lived experience, customer evidence, or learned insights, and remove generic or exaggerated claims.
