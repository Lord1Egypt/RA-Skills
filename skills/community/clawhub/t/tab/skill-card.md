## Use Case:

Developers and designers use this skill when implementing or reviewing tabbed interfaces, including accessible ARIA tab patterns, keyboard behavior, responsive overflow strategies, and state handling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Copied examples or recommendations could be applied without validating accessibility, keyboard navigation, focus handling, or state persistence in the target application.

Mitigation: Review adapted tab implementations against the skill's accessibility and checklist guidance, and test with keyboard, screen reader, responsive viewport, and form-state scenarios before production use.
