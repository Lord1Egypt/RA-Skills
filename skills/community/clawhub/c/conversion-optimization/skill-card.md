## Description:

Helps agents improve conversion rates by planning CRO work, A/B tests, funnel optimization, checkout optimization, form optimization, and friction reduction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, growth, product, and ecommerce teams use this skill to structure conversion-rate optimization work, prioritize experiments, and produce funnel maps, hypotheses, test plans, and implementation checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask the agent to read local project context files to tailor CRO recommendations.

Mitigation: Review which project context files are available before use and avoid including sensitive customer, credential, or confidential business data unless the agent is authorized to process it.

Risk: CRO recommendations and experiment plans may be incomplete or misleading if analytics baselines, sample sizes, or funnel data are inaccurate.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against current analytics, experiment design requirements, and business constraints before launch.
