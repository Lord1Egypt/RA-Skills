## Description:

Helps agents plan, evaluate, and scope free marketing tools for lead generation, SEO value, and brand awareness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, founders, and product teams use this skill to decide whether a free tool is worth building, compare tool concepts, choose a lead-capture posture, and define an MVP scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The trigger wording may activate on broad tool-building or marketing-tool prompts.

Mitigation: Review and narrow the trigger description if this skill should only handle explicit free-tool or engineering-as-marketing requests.

Risk: The skill may read local product-marketing context files when present.

Mitigation: Use it in workspaces where product-marketing context is appropriate for marketing strategy assistance, and review local context files before sharing sensitive information.

Risk: The skill produces strategic recommendations that can affect lead capture, SEO investment, and product scope.

Mitigation: Have a human owner validate assumptions, scoring, and implementation tradeoffs before committing budget or roadmap work.
