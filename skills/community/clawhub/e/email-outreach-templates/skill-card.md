## Description:

Provides Chinese-language business outreach email templates for first contact, follow-up, negotiation, and closing, with A/B variants and industry-specific examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gdp6539](https://clawhub.ai/user/gdp6539)

## Use Case:

Sales and marketing users use this skill to draft and adapt Chinese-language partnership outreach emails across outreach stages and target industries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outreach templates include performance and case-study claims that may not apply to the sender or recipient.

Mitigation: Review and personalize generated emails before sending, and verify any claim, statistic, or case study included in the message.

Risk: Generated sales outreach can be inappropriate if sent without recipient, industry, or compliance review.

Mitigation: Adapt templates to the target audience and apply the sender's legal, brand, and consent requirements before use.
