## Description:

Researches ancestry-based citizenship eligibility, cross-checks family lineage against nationality-law references, and guides general immigration planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[eosbot](https://clawhub.ai/user/eosbot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users researching citizenship by descent or migration options use this skill to structure family-history intake, compare country pathways, and produce document checklists and next steps. Outputs are informational and should be verified against official sources or qualified immigration counsel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive family-history, identity, or details about living relatives during genealogy intake.

Mitigation: Collect only the minimum information needed, avoid unnecessary details about living relatives, and handle identity documents carefully.

Risk: Citizenship and immigration guidance can become outdated or be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Verify conclusions against official consulates or government sources and consult a qualified immigration lawyer before relying on the result.

Risk: External genealogy, government, or search tools may receive queries that reveal ancestry or immigration intent.

Mitigation: Review external-source queries before execution and avoid sending private names, dates, or document identifiers unless necessary.
