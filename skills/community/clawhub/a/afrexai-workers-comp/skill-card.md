## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Workers' compensation requirements vary by state and can change over time.

Mitigation: Verify state-specific requirements with current official or professional sources before acting.

Risk: Claims analysis may involve sensitive employee, medical, legal, or insurance information.

Mitigation: Redact names, dates of birth, claim numbers, attorney details, medical record identifiers, and other unnecessary personal information before use.

Risk: The skill provides business guidance and should not be treated as legal, medical, or insurance advice.

Mitigation: Use qualified legal, medical, insurance, or compliance professionals for decisions with regulatory, coverage, or claims consequences.
