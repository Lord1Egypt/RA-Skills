## Description:

Helps create a realistic daily hydration plan with personalized intake ranges, simple tracking anchors, and alerts for under- and overhydration risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn hydration goals into a realistic daily routine with intake ranges, anchors around meals or workouts, simple tracking, and watch-outs for drinking too little or too much.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general hydration guidance as medical advice.

Mitigation: State that the output is general wellness guidance and that medical conditions, fluid restrictions, pregnancy, or clinician-provided limits require clinician guidance.

Risk: Untrusted structured input may appear directly in the generated Markdown plan.

Mitigation: Review user-provided input before reuse and avoid treating generated Markdown as trusted executable or authoritative clinical content.
