## Use Case:

External sellers use this skill to price used, handmade, collectible, and bulk items; select marketplaces; create listings; and manage offers with scam-prevention guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketplace fees, platform terms, and local resale prices may change or vary by region.

Mitigation: Verify current marketplace terms, sold listings, and local comparable prices before choosing a platform or setting a price.

Risk: Listing or negotiation guidance may omit item-specific flaws, terms, or seller obligations.

Mitigation: Review and edit generated listing text before posting, and disclose condition, included items, shipping, pickup, and return expectations clearly.

Risk: Selling interactions can expose users to scam, payment, shipping, or in-person meeting risks.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's safety guidance, use platform-protected payment where appropriate, document shipment and item condition, and choose public daytime meeting locations for local sales.
