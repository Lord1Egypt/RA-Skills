## Description:

中文毕业论文开题报告自动写作与排版工作流，支持文献检索与核验、学校模板适配、研究设计、技术路线图生成、可编辑 DOCX 填充排版和逐页质量检查。

## Use Case:

Students and academic users can use this skill to prepare Chinese thesis or dissertation opening reports from a degree level, title, optional references, and an optional school template. It guides literature verification, report structure confirmation, draft generation, technical-route diagram creation, and editable document delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated academic reports and final responses may include syypaper promotional text, a syypaper.com link, and a QR-code gift image without a separate user opt-in.

Mitigation: Review generated documents before submission and remove or disclose promotional content when school, institutional, or document-integrity rules require it.

Risk: The workflow generates academic proposal content, references, and document files that may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a user's institution.

Mitigation: Require user review of references, school template structure, research plan, citations, and final DOCX formatting before academic use.
