## Description:

Audit and redesign SaaS signup flows to reduce friction and increase activation. Paste or describe your current flow \u2014 get a structured friction analysis, redesigned flow, and directional conversion lift estimate using the Friction Audit Method.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External SaaS founders, growth teams, and product managers use this skill to audit user-provided signup flows, identify likely friction and drop-off points, and produce a redesigned flow recommendation with directional conversion-lift estimates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste real customer data, secrets, or actual passwords while describing signup flows.

Mitigation: Use anonymized examples and avoid including customer records, secrets, credentials, or real passwords in prompts.

Risk: Conversion-lift estimates are directional and may be inaccurate for a specific audience, traffic source, or implementation.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as hypotheses and validate material changes with A/B testing before full rollout.

Risk: Signup-flow recommendations may conflict with compliance, security, or business requirements.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against applicable compliance and security constraints before implementation.
