## Description:

Customer-acquisition desk for indie tools that collects public pain-point leads, drafts channel-native outreach, and builds a human approval queue without posting until the user confirms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, indie tool builders, and operators use this skill to collect public lead candidates, draft channel-appropriate responses, and review an approval queue before any outreach is sent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outreach drafts could become spam or violate platform rules if sent in bulk or without review.

Mitigation: Keep publishing human-gated, answer specific public posts, follow platform and community rules, use only official APIs when publishing, and set volume limits before running.

Risk: Lead collection could capture private or unsupported contact data.

Mitigation: Use public pages only, store only handles and public URLs, avoid scraped emails, direct messages, and phone numbers, and drop leads without live source URLs.

Risk: Generated copy could include unsupported claims, invented prices, fake testimonials, or misleading offers.

Mitigation: Require user review of every draft, cite the source URL, omit prices unless supplied in the session, and avoid fake metrics, ratings, gifts, or reviews.
