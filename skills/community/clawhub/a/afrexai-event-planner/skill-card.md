## Description:

Plan, execute, and measure business events - conferences, webinars, workshops, product launches, networking events, trade shows, and corporate gatherings, covering the event lifecycle from concept to post-event ROI analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external event teams, and business operators use this skill to plan, budget, market, execute, and evaluate business events such as conferences, webinars, workshops, product launches, networking events, trade shows, and corporate gatherings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Event workflows may involve attendee, lead, or sponsor data.

Mitigation: Capture explicit consent, limit exported attendee data, and follow applicable privacy and email-marketing rules before using generated plans in real operations.

Risk: Generated budgets, timelines, and operational plans may not match local venue, accessibility, safety, legal, or vendor requirements.

Mitigation: Have an event owner review generated plans against current contracts, venue policies, and applicable local requirements before execution.
