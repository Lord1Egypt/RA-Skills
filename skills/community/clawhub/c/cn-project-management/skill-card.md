## Description:

Provides Chinese-language construction project management guidance, templates, checklists, and calculation examples for planning, schedule control, quality, safety, contracts, risk, and earned value management based on PRC construction standards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Project managers, construction management staff, and engineering consultants use this skill to draft project management plans, schedule and earned value analyses, quality and safety checklists, contract workflows, and risk registers for construction projects in China.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Construction legal, safety, contract, and engineering guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific project or locality.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory and verify decisions with qualified professionals and current local requirements.

Risk: Planning templates and CPM or EVM calculation examples can be misleading if project inputs, assumptions, or formulas are copied without review.

Mitigation: Review all generated plans, tables, and calculations against actual project data before using them operationally.
