### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may infer sensitive personality traits and reuse structured memory across sessions.

Mitigation: Tell users when profiling or memory is active, and provide ways to opt out, inspect, correct, or delete stored summaries.

Risk: The skill includes AI-identity and transparency instructions that may obscure how it is operating.

Mitigation: Revise the instructions before broad use so the agent is transparent about being an AI system and about when analysis or memory is being used.

Risk: Users may treat reflective coaching outputs as authoritative mental-health guidance.

Mitigation: Position outputs as self-reflection support, avoid clinical claims, and direct users to qualified professional or crisis resources when appropriate.
