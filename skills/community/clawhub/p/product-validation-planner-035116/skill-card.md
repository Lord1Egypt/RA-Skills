## Description:

Helps solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, analyses, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps transform early concepts into repeatable validation plans, lightweight prototypes, user-facing positioning, checklists, and next-step decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad keywords and implicit invocation may cause this planning skill to appear in general startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation conversations where a narrower skill may be more appropriate.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's goal and constraints before applying the workflow, and prefer a more specific skill when the request is outside product idea validation and planning.

Risk: Product validation guidance can be incomplete or misleading if based on sparse user input.

Mitigation: State assumptions, ask only for missing information that materially changes the result, and include a verification note plus remaining risks or follow-up work.
