## Description:

Generates Chinese-language project landing risk assessment reports that evaluate construction, funding, operations, market, policy, compliance, credit, and regional support risks with risk grades and mitigation advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Government investment attraction teams, industrial park operators, and project governance reviewers use this skill to screen landing risk, decide whether a project should proceed, and prepare mitigation actions before signing, construction, production, or operations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce legal, financial, policy, and enterprise-credit conclusions that may be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Verify important conclusions against authoritative project, company, policy, and credit sources before using the report for decisions.

Risk: Project or company information supplied to the skill may contain sensitive commercial details.

Mitigation: Provide only the project and company information needed for the assessment, consistent with the security guidance.

Risk: Insufficient project evidence can lead to overconfident risk ratings or unsupported go-forward recommendations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support and require reviewers to confirm key inputs such as funding, approvals, land, environmental, safety, and operating conditions.
