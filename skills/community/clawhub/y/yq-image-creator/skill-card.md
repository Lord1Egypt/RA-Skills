## Use Case:

External users use this skill to generate stylized 3D images from short prompts, uploaded reference images, or product and brand descriptions across character, scene, product, and style-transform categories.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process uploaded personal photos or product images through image understanding and image generation tools.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive personal, confidential, or proprietary images unless that processing is acceptable for the intended use.

Risk: The skill can activate on broad image-adjacent terms and may route ambiguous requests into image-generation workflows.

Mitigation: Review the selected style and requested inputs before generation, especially when the prompt is incomplete or ambiguous.

Risk: Generated product, brand, city, landmark, or movie-scene imagery may be stylized or inferred rather than factually exact.

Mitigation: Use generated images as creative assets and review them before commercial publication or brand-sensitive use.
