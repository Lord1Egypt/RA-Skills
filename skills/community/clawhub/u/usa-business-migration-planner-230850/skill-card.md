## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The public name and routing suggest U.S. business migration, while the artifact content is career decision support for a Cambridge CS master's versus an Amazon new-graduate role.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended use before installing or invoking the skill, and rename or describe it around career decision support if that is the expected behavior.

Risk: The package allows implicit invocation with broad triggers, which can route unrelated requests to the wrong workflow.

Mitigation: Disable implicit invocation or narrow triggers to explicit Cambridge, Amazon, master's, and career decision-support requests.
