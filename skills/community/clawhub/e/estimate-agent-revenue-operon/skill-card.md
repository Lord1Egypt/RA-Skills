## Description:

Estimate monthly revenue an AI agent could earn from native ads, affiliate links, CPC, and lead generation using floor, mid, and ceiling projections with the math shown.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[operon](https://clawhub.ai/user/operon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External agent developers use this skill to estimate whether monetization is worth adding to an AI agent. It asks for agent vertical, query volume, response type, and audience, then produces transparent monthly revenue scenarios.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Revenue estimates may be misleading if treated as guaranteed outcomes or live market data.

Mitigation: Present the floor, mid, and ceiling scenarios with the baked-in benchmark assumptions and current demand-pool caveats.

Risk: Operon links and the Telegram bot are third-party promotional resources outside the static skill.

Mitigation: Treat those resources as optional and avoid sharing sensitive information unless the user trusts the service.
