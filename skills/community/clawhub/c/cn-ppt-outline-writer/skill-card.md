## Description:

CN PPT Outline Writer helps Chinese-language workplace users generate PPT outlines, slide-by-slide content points, opening scripts, and visual suggestions for reports, proposals, presentations, business plans, reviews, product launches, and training decks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, business teams, and external users use this skill to structure Chinese-language PPT materials for workplace reporting, commercial proposals, speeches, annual reviews, project updates, product launches, and training. The skill prompts for topic, audience, duration, and preferred logic framework, then produces an outline with per-slide content and visual guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad workplace terms such as reports or proposals.

Mitigation: Use explicit instructions when the PPT outline workflow is desired, and ignore or override the skill when a broader writing task is intended.

Risk: The skill provides opinionated slide structure, font, and visual-style guidance that may not match brand or accessibility requirements.

Mitigation: Review and adjust formatting, visual recommendations, and accessibility choices before applying the output to a final presentation.

Risk: Generated outlines may include placeholders or suggested data points that are not verified facts.

Mitigation: Replace placeholders with sourced figures and review claims before presenting to decision makers or external audiences.
