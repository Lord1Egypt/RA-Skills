## Description:

Perform structured audits on code, workflows, prompts, and products. Use when: (1) Something is not working as expected, (2) User asks for review or feedback, (3) A system behaves inconsistently, (4) Before deploying or shipping, (5) When optimizing performance or UX, (6) When debugging recurring issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, operators, and product teams use this skill to structure reviews of provided code, workflows, prompts, logs, or product details and receive prioritized findings with evidence and fixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be selected for a wide range of review or debugging requests and could receive sensitive code, logs, prompts, or workflow details.

Mitigation: Provide only the materials intended for analysis and review audit findings before acting on them.
