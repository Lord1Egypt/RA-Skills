## Description:

Provides guidance for booking door-to-door recycling of used home appliances, cardboard, and plastic items, and for earning or redeeming recycling points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to ask for guidance on recycling appointments for used appliances, cardboard, and plastic items, including workflow summaries, key entry points, status tracking, privacy cautions, and points redemption.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A real recycling appointment flow may involve address, phone, or authorization details.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing personal or authorization details unless the third-party provider being used is trusted.

Risk: Some guidance may depend on third-party recycling service interfaces whose availability can change.

Mitigation: Confirm current availability and appointment status in the relevant provider experience before relying on the guidance.
