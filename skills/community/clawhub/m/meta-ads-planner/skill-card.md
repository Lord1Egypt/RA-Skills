## Description:

Plan profitable Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns for ecommerce businesses with structured audience segmentation, creative strategy, placement optimization, bidding configuration, and budget allocation across funnel stages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators, media buyers, and marketing managers use this skill to plan Meta ad campaigns across audience segmentation, creative strategy, placements, bidding, budget allocation, and measurement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business recommendations could conflict with current Meta policies, consent requirements, or internal budget limits.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current Meta policies, customer-list consent requirements, and approved budget limits before applying them in a live ad account.

Risk: The skill provides campaign planning guidance but does not validate live account state, tracking quality, or policy compliance.

Mitigation: Use the included launch checklist and verify Business Manager status, payment method, tracking events, landing pages, privacy policy, and ad compliance before launch.
