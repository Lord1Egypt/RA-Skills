## Description:

Read your marketing claims, landing page, or ad copy the way a consumer-protection investigator would (FTC/ASA framing) and draft the inquiry letter they could send.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, compliance, and product teams use this skill to review pasted marketing materials, ads, landing pages, emails, or app store copy for consumer-protection claim risk. It produces a claim inventory, substantiation demands, a simulated regulator inquiry letter, and per-claim fix-or-drop guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the simulated regulatory reading or inquiry letter as legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as a compliance-review aid and have an advertising-law attorney review final copy, especially for regulated claims.

Risk: Health, finance, children's products, or other regulated verticals may require a higher substantiation standard than the default review posture captures.

Mitigation: Collect jurisdiction and vertical information before review, flag high-risk categories, and confirm load-bearing claims with qualified counsel.
