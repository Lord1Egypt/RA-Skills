## Use Case:

External creators, founders, and social media writers use this skill to turn rough ideas into clarified insights and draft X or LinkedIn posts in a casual, specific voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may search private notes, journal entries, or prior sessions without sufficiently narrow user-directed scoping.

Mitigation: Set explicit source limits before use and approve the sources the agent may consult.

Risk: Private personal or business details could be transformed into public social posts.

Mitigation: Review and approve all details before publication, and remove sensitive information from drafts.
