## Description:

Check a ClawHub publisher's trust score before installing their skill and return TRUSTED, ESTABLISHED, NEW, or FLAGGED based on public signals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ordo-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/ordo-tech)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and operators use this skill before installing unfamiliar ClawHub skills to review public publisher signals and decide whether to install, install with caution, or avoid the skill.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may include promotional third-party links in verification results.

Mitigation: Review generated recommendations for unrelated commercial links before relying on or sharing the output.

Risk: Trust scores are advisory and depend on public ClawHub profile data and web search results that may be incomplete or unavailable.

Mitigation: Confirm important installation decisions against the cited public evidence and treat incomplete checks as cautionary.
