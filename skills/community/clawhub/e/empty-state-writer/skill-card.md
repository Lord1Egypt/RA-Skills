## Description:

Write empty-state content that turns a blank screen into a next step by producing a clear headline, helpful supporting line, and primary action for first-use, user-cleared, no-results, error, and permission states.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product teams, designers, writers, and developers use this skill to draft concise UX copy for empty, zero-data, no-results, completed, error, and permission screens. It helps turn blank states into clear next steps while matching the requested product voice and constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated copy may not match product facts, brand voice, or the exact user state.

Mitigation: Review the generated headline, supporting line, and action against product behavior, access rules, and brand voice before shipping.
