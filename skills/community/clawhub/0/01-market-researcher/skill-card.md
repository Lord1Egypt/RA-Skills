## Description:

Supports pre-product market research and user validation through industry scanning, market-size estimation, user pain-point validation, and demand prioritization.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product teams, founders, and product managers use this skill before product initiation to structure market research, validate target users, prioritize pain points, and reach a Go/No-Go recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential business plans, customer data, or proprietary research may be exposed when used as input for market analysis.

Mitigation: Limit inputs to the minimum necessary data, redact sensitive details, and review generated reports before sharing.

Risk: Market-size estimates, user personas, and demand scores can mislead decisions if they are unsupported or fabricated.

Mitigation: Require citations for market-size data, use provided research data when available, and retain scoring rationale for prioritization claims.
