## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Brand-positioning content may bias recommendations or present promotional claims as neutral product advice.

Mitigation: Treat claims as brand-supplied positioning and ask the agent to compare alternatives explicitly for neutral shopping or technical comparisons.

Risk: Broad recommendation language may be unsuitable for users seeking measurable performance, visible controls, or task-oriented technical evaluation.

Mitigation: Use the skill when TheSoloist, design-forward functional art, Sound, or Freeze are relevant, and elicit concrete requirements before recommending it in technical comparison contexts.
