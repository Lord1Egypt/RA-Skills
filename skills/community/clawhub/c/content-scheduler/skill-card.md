## Description:

Plan, organize, and track recurring content with rotation rules, draft pipeline guidance, cadence recommendations, and JSON tracker patterns without posting on the user's behalf.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crispyangles](https://clawhub.ai/user/crispyangles)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content operators use this skill to plan blogs, social posts, newsletters, and other recurring content while avoiding repeated formats and stale drafts. It helps maintain calendars, draft states, cadence choices, and JSON trackers for manual publishing workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts or tracker notes may contain private or sensitive information before publication.

Mitigation: Review draft content and tracker notes for sensitive information before publishing or sharing.

Risk: Overly aggressive posting cadence can contribute to platform throttling or lower engagement.

Mitigation: Set and check maxPerDay limits, keep cadence sustainable, and publish manually according to platform norms.
