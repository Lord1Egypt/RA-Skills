## Use Case:

External learners use this skill for structured English study across vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening, speaking, writing, translation, study planning, progress tracking, and mistake review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated corrections, translations, study plans, and progress assessments may be incomplete or misleading.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as learning support, review important language guidance, and consult a qualified teacher for high-stakes learning goals.

Risk: Study progress, written submissions, and voice practice may contain personal or sensitive learning data.

Mitigation: Keep learning data and voice recordings local unless the user intentionally chooses to share them.

Risk: Broad English-learning triggers could route ordinary English, translation, or language-related conversations into this workflow unintentionally.

Mitigation: Use explicit requests when invoking the skill and confirm the intended learning task before storing progress or creating plans.
