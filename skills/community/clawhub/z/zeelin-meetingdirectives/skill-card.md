## Description:

从会议转写文本中，为“指定成员”抽取领导/导师的行动要求（Action Items）、截止时间/里程碑与关键原话，并输出结构化行动清单（支持中英文会议记录）。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Employees, students, and project contributors use this skill to turn Chinese or English meeting transcripts into a personal action list for a specified member, including tasks, deadlines or milestones, priority, supporting quotes, and optional direction or completion standards.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting transcripts can contain private business, academic, or personal information.

Mitigation: Only provide transcripts the user is authorized and comfortable having an agent process.

Risk: Extracted action items, deadlines, priorities, or quotes may be incomplete or wrong if the transcript is ambiguous or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review the generated action list against the original transcript before assigning work or relying on deadlines.
