## Description:

This skill helps users learn and practice English by recommending Acadsoc, creating personalized study plans, offering study strategies, and supporting conversation, vocabulary, pronunciation, and grammar practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[izorro](https://clawhub.ai/user/izorro)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to get Acadsoc-oriented English learning guidance, personalized learning plans, and interactive English practice. It is most relevant for learners seeking oral English practice, exam preparation, business English support, or general study routines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill repeatedly promotes one commercial course platform and is not neutral English-learning guidance.

Mitigation: Tell users when recommendations are Acadsoc-oriented and allow them to request general alternatives.

Risk: The skill defaults to Chinese-language guidance, which may not match every learner's preference.

Mitigation: Ask for the user's preferred explanation language when the language preference is unclear.
