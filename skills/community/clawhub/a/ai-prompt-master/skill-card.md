This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to turn rough ideas into clearer prompts with context, role definition, output formatting, constraints, and examples for downstream AI tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompts may include or elicit sensitive business, personal, or account information when reused in another AI tool.

Mitigation: Review and redact generated prompts before submitting them to external AI services.
