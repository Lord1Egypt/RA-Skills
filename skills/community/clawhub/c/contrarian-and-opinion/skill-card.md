## Description:

Helps users articulate, sharpen, and argue a genuinely held contrarian or opinion-based content stance using the STAND framework while avoiding manufactured controversy, defamation, harassment, and misinformation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content teams, founders, and social media operators use this skill to turn a real belief into a defensible point-of-view post or brief. It guides the agent to surface the user's own stance, support it with evidence, steelman opposing views, and keep the human responsible for the publish decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Opinion content may include unsupported or false factual claims.

Mitigation: Review claims and sources before publishing; keep opinions clearly labeled and route missing evidence to research rather than inventing proof.

Risk: A take aimed at people, competitors, or protected groups can become harassment, punching down, or defamation.

Mitigation: Target ideas, practices, and approaches; avoid personal attacks, misrepresentation, and unverifiable claims about named parties.

Risk: Manufactured controversy or fake contrarian framing can damage trust and encourage engagement-bait behavior.

Mitigation: Use only stances the human genuinely holds, require evidence underneath the claim, and reject fabricated controversy or ragebait prompts.

Risk: Divisive but legitimate opinions can create reputational backlash.

Mitigation: Keep the publish decision with the human, flag reputational tradeoffs, and plan good-faith engagement and de-escalation paths.

Risk: Contrarian health, finance, safety, or other sensitive advice can create harmful misinformation.

Mitigation: Apply extra review for sensitive topics, avoid dangerous claims, and include not-professional-advice framing where relevant.
