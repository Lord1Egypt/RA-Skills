## Description:

Provides signup and registration flow optimization guidance to reduce friction, improve completion rates, and support trial activation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External builders, product teams, and growth practitioners use this skill to audit signup, registration, account creation, and trial activation flows. It helps produce prioritized recommendations, form redesign guidance, and experiment ideas for improving signup conversion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read local product-marketing context when present, which could include sensitive business details.

Mitigation: Review any local context used in outputs and avoid exposing sensitive details in public or external recommendations.

Risk: Signup recommendations may affect verification, data collection, or compliance-sensitive steps.

Mitigation: Validate proposed signup changes against compliance, fraud-prevention, and authentication requirements before implementation.
