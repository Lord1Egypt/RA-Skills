## Description:

Evaluate new marketplace opportunities by comparing platform fees, audience fit, competition density, fulfillment requirements, and expected ROI to build a prioritized expansion roadmap.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce operators and marketplace teams use this skill to evaluate candidate sales channels, compare costs and fit, model ROI, and produce a phased expansion roadmap with go/no-go recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketplace fees, fulfillment charges, and platform program terms can change after the skill's reference material is reviewed.

Mitigation: Verify current rates on each platform's official seller fee documentation before making launch, pricing, or budget decisions.

Risk: The skill may use sensitive business inputs such as revenue, SKU scope, budgets, logistics plans, and marketplace performance data.

Mitigation: Provide only the information needed for the evaluation and avoid including unnecessary confidential or regulated data.

Risk: Expansion recommendations can be misleading if readiness gaps, compliance requirements, or cash-flow constraints are incomplete.

Mitigation: Complete the readiness framework, compliance checklist, and 12-month model before treating a recommendation as final.
