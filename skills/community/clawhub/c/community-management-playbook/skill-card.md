## Description:

Build a community management playbook for a brand's social media channels, including guidelines for comments, DMs, moderation policy, response frameworks, escalation paths, response templates, and tone guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Social media managers, community managers, marketing teams, and agencies use this skill to create operational playbooks for managing comments, DMs, moderation decisions, crisis escalation, tone of voice, and community health metrics across active brand channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: DM response templates may prompt teams to request customer details in social messages.

Mitigation: Adapt DM language to the organization's privacy policy, ask only for the minimum needed, avoid payment or sensitive identity details, and route account verification or complex support cases to approved support channels.

Risk: Suggested response-time SLAs and escalation contacts may be unrealistic or incomplete for a specific team.

Mitigation: Review the playbook against actual staffing, support coverage, and escalation ownership before operational use.
