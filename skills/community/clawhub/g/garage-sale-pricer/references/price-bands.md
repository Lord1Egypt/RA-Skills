# Price Bands — what garage-sale items actually sell for

Garage-sale prices run **30–50% below online marketplace** for the same item: buyers price in no-returns, cash-only, today-only, and your desire to not carry it back inside. Tag at garage-sale reality or route the item to marketplace/consignment instead.

## Negotiation doctrine
- Every tag implies a **floor at ~60% of tag** ("$12 → walk-away $7"). Decide floors the night before; at 7 AM you will cave.
- Bundling is your friend late: "both for $8."
- The pros arriving at 7:05 with a flashlight will offer 50% of tag on everything — counter at 85% and let them pick volume; they buy 10+ items.
- Day 2 or Saturday-after-noon: −50% across the board; final hour = fill-a-bag $5.
- Anything you'd pay to dispose of (old TVs in some towns, mattresses) → free pile/curb-alert, never "negotiable."

## Never-sell list
Drop-side cribs (federally banned), car seats past expiration (~6–10 yrs) or after ANY crash, crashed helmets, recalled items (recalls.gov lookup — especially kids' products), expired medicine, non-GFCI bathroom-space-heater class items. Selling banned items transfers liability to you.
