## Description:

Review supplied US patent application claims and relevant specification, drawings, Office-action, and prosecution context for claim quality, prosecution readiness, 35 U.S.C. 101/102/103/112 risk, BRI, 112(f), dependency/form, restriction/election, continuation/divisional strategy, infringement observability, and amendment options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Patent practitioners, inventors, portfolio teams, and product counsel use this skill to review US patent claim sets and related materials for prosecution readiness, statutory risks, claim architecture, amendment options, and qualified-counsel review gates.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent claim review can be mistaken for legal advice or a filing, validity, infringement, or allowance opinion.

Mitigation: Present outputs as drafting and review assistance and route filing decisions, legal conclusions, inventorship, deadlines, new matter, prosecution responses, and infringement questions to qualified US patent counsel.

Risk: Optional PatSnap connectors may send patent identifiers, search queries, or application context to external services.

Mitigation: Use connectors only when external lookup or search is authorized, keep API keys secret, and avoid sharing confidential application materials with services the user is not permitted to use.

Risk: A claims-only or incomplete record can lead to unsupported conclusions about written description, enablement, 112(f) structure, amendment basis, prior art, legal status, or deadlines.

Mitigation: State missing materials and search status, use support locators, label preliminary findings, and do not fabricate prior art, claim text, status, support, deadlines, or search conclusions.
