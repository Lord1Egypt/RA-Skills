## Description:

Reads chart or price data to produce structured market structure analysis across trend, swing points, BOS/CHoCH, liquidity zones, order blocks, fair value gaps, and directional bias.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dromlakhani](https://clawhub.ai/user/dromlakhani)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and trading analysts use this skill to turn supplied charts or price data into a structured technical market narrative with bias, key zones, triggers, invalidation, and targets. Outputs should be treated as educational chart analysis rather than personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trading outputs may be mistaken for personalized investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat buy/sell, target, and invalidation language as educational technical analysis and verify decisions with personal risk controls or a qualified financial professional before trading.

Risk: Market structure reads can be wrong when the supplied chart, timeframe, or price data is incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Confirm the instrument, timeframe, current price data, and stated assumptions before relying on the narrative.
