## Description:

Content-factor toolkit for short-video e-commerce that helps decompose viral material into reusable factors, rank candidate focus topics by expected value, and build a reusable factor library.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External e-commerce content teams use this skill to analyze short-video creative, prioritize content-factor experiments, and set up reusable factor, material, and focus-tracking tables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill presents a first-run branded message with optional off-platform contact promotion.

Mitigation: Review onboarding text before installation and remove or disclose promotional content when it is not appropriate for the deployment.

Risk: The skill may store onboarding state, brand profile notes, conclusions, and delivered analysis files locally.

Mitigation: Use it only in approved workspaces and review or disable archive behavior before handling proprietary product, advertising, ROI, or business strategy details.
