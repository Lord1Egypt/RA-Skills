## Description:

Analyzes Chinese-language sleep habit descriptions to assess sleep quality and return prioritized lifestyle recommendations, basic sleep hygiene guidance, and medical-care prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to turn short descriptions of sleep timing, fatigue, insomnia, and bedtime habits into a structured sleep-quality assessment and practical improvement plan. It is informational lifestyle guidance and does not replace professional medical diagnosis or treatment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat sleep-quality scores or suggestions as medical diagnosis or treatment.

Mitigation: Present the skill as lifestyle guidance only and direct users with persistent, severe, or concerning sleep symptoms to qualified medical care.

Risk: Rule-based parsing may miss important sleep context or oversimplify symptoms.

Mitigation: Ask users to provide sleep schedule, symptoms, duration, and relevant habits, and avoid relying on the score as a clinical measure.
