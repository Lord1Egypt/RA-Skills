## Description:

Breaks a vague or overwhelming goal into milestones, tiny next actions, visible dependencies, and a review checkpoint.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers can use this skill to turn broad personal or work goals into milestone ladders, next actions, dependencies, risks, and a review checkpoint.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated plans may be too broad or unrealistic if the original goal is unclear or the timeline is compressed.

Mitigation: Review the first milestone, dependencies, and checkpoint before acting, and clarify the goal or narrow the timeline when needed.
