## Description:

Plan ethical leadership influence using Baker's push/pull and logical/emotional strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Leaders, managers, and workplace communicators use this skill to choose an ethical influence mode, plan a difficult conversation, draft practical language, and stress-test what could backfire.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Influence planning can become manipulative, coercive, deceptive, or unfair in workplace settings.

Mitigation: Review generated plans and scripts for truthful information, meaningful agency, consent, power dynamics, and workplace appropriateness before use.

Risk: The skill may be selected automatically for leadership or persuasion-related prompts.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory guidance and confirm that the selected strategy fits the actual context before acting on it.
