## Description:

Feishu All In One Pro helps agents automate Feishu office workflows across documents, messages, calendars, spreadsheets, drive files, approvals, attendance, tasks, and meeting notes.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broad Feishu office automation access without clearly documented scope, safeguards, or confirmation controls.

Mitigation: Review carefully before installing, use a Feishu app with the minimum scopes needed, and avoid tenant-wide access by default.

Risk: Bulk exports, file changes, mass messages, attendance or approval access, and task or status updates could affect sensitive enterprise data or workflows.

Mitigation: Require explicit confirmation before these actions and review proposed changes before execution.
