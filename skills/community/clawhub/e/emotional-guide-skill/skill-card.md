## Description:

Provides warm, non-sycophantic emotional support and guided self-reflection through empathic listening, one-at-a-time exploratory questions, tentative pattern finding, psychoeducation, and consent-based low-intensity self-help suggestions, while routing urgent safety concerns through a safety protocol.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[baijian](https://clawhub.ai/user/baijian)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and support-oriented agents use this skill for ordinary emotional support, reflection, and gentle next-step planning around stress, anxiety, grief, loneliness, relationship issues, work difficulties, self-doubt, overwhelm, and hard decisions. It is not a licensed counseling, diagnosis, treatment, medication-advice, or crisis-response service.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the skill as therapy, medical advice, or a crisis-response system.

Mitigation: Present it as ordinary emotional support only, avoid diagnosis and medication advice, and route self-harm, violence, severe impairment, psychosis-like experiences, mania, abuse, or urgent danger to real-world emergency or professional support.

Risk: Public deployment may rely on stale crisis contacts or incomplete local regulatory controls, especially for minors or mainland China.

Mitigation: Verify current local crisis contacts and regulatory requirements before deployment, and ensure the host product provides appropriate human escalation, minor-safety, consent, and data-handling controls.

Risk: Some referenced public-health materials have their own copyright or adaptation limits.

Mitigation: Use the source register to verify licenses and permissions before reproducing, translating, packaging, or commercially adapting referenced materials.
