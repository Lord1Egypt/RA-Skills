## Description:

Creates and maintains a repo-local product marketing context document that captures product, audience, positioning, competitors, customer language, proof points, and goals for reuse by other marketing skills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, founders, and agent users use this skill to create or update a shared product marketing context file before generating positioning, copy, or campaign assets. It supports both auto-drafting from repository materials and conversational setup from scratch.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Inaccurate positioning, audience, or proof-point details in the generated product marketing context may influence later marketing outputs.

Mitigation: Review the generated `.agents/product-marketing.md` before saving and keep the document changelog current when substantive positioning changes are made.
