## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Assessment data, goals, session identifiers, timestamps, and optional user identifiers may be sent to the provider's API.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal details unless the provider's privacy practices are acceptable; use anonymous requests where possible by setting userId to null.

Risk: Generated career roadmaps and certification recommendations may be incomplete, outdated, or mismatched to a user's jurisdiction or employer requirements.

Mitigation: Review roadmap recommendations against current certification, regulatory, employer, and training requirements before acting on them.
