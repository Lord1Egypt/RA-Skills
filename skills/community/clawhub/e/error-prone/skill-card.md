## Description:

Makes AI-generated text sound more human by intentionally introducing realistic imperfections.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dylanfeltus](https://clawhub.ai/user/dylanfeltus)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and writers use this skill to rewrite emails, messages, social posts, and other prose with deliberate imperfections that make the output sound more informal or human while preserving meaning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make AI-generated text appear intentionally less detectable as AI-written, creating authenticity and disclosure concerns.

Mitigation: Use it only for transparent stylistic editing and follow applicable recipient, platform, school, employer, or disclosure requirements.

Risk: Intentional typos, informal phrasing, and punctuation changes can reduce clarity or professionalism in formal contexts.

Mitigation: Review final copy before use, choose lower imperfection levels for professional settings, and confirm the rewritten text preserves the user's meaning.
