## Description:

Write a firm, effective complaint letter that gets a resolution.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to draft structured complaint letters and short email versions for product, service, refund, replacement, or escalation issues. It helps turn a brief into a firm professional message with facts, impact, requested resolution, deadline, and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated complaint letters may include bracketed or inferred facts that are inaccurate if sent without review.

Mitigation: Review and fill in dates, reference numbers, amounts, deadlines, recipient details, and prior-contact history before sending.

Risk: An escalation path may be inappropriate or unsupported for the user's jurisdiction or situation.

Mitigation: Confirm any claimed escalation route before including it in the final letter or email.
