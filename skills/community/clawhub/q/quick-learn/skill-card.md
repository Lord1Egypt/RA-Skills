## Description:

A Feynman-based learning coach for systematic multi-day learning plans and quick article/book breakdowns. Supports EN and ZH.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wjyhahaha](https://clawhub.ai/user/wjyhahaha)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Learners, employees, and external users use Quick Learn to turn topics, articles, books, and links into structured learning plans, concise breakdowns, Feynman prompts, review cards, knowledge maps, and progress reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Learning plans, breakdowns, daily logs, review state, and mood or progress notes may be stored locally under learning-data.

Mitigation: Avoid using sensitive study material unless local retention is acceptable, and review or remove learning-data records when they are no longer needed.

Risk: The skill may set up recurring learning or review pushes.

Mitigation: Confirm the schedule before enabling reminders, and pause or disable recurring pushes when the plan changes.
