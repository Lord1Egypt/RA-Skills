## Description:

Turn a business into reels that all look the same, in plain words, with any AI agent. Your AI agent drafts a Playbook.md with your colors, fonts, logo, and tone, picks one of three reel formats, Explainer, Challenge, or Stage Talk, then drafts the script, caption, and edit brief for owner review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Small business owners use the skill to create a consistent brand playbook and draft one reel's script, caption, hashtags, and edit brief for review before recording or posting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A draft script or caption may contain brand details or claims that need correction before publication.

Mitigation: Check drafts against the approved playbook and pasted evidence, then have the owner review them before recording or posting.
