## Use Case:

Marketing practitioners, founders, growth teams, and developers use this skill to select a relevant marketing module and produce draft copy, checklists, analysis, campaign plans, CRO recommendations, SEO guidance, tracking plans, and implementation notes.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some modules describe ad-account and social-publishing workflows that could affect budgets or public channels.

Mitigation: Use outputs as drafts only, and require explicit human approval before changing budgets, launching campaigns, uploading audience lists, scheduling posts, or publishing posts.

Risk: Marketing workflows may involve customer behavioral data, audience lists, or scraped platform data.

Mitigation: Use only approved data sources and complete privacy and legal review before using customer data, audience uploads, or scraped platform data.

Risk: Generated recommendations may include unsupported metrics, keyword volumes, or marketing claims if source data is missing.

Mitigation: Label assumptions and replace them with verified first-party or approved third-party data before publication or execution.
