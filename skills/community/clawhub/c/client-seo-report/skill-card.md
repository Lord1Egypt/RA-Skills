## Description:

Produce a monthly (or custom-period) SEO report for a client or boss: what changed in clicks, impressions and positions versus the previous period and last year, which pages and queries drove the change, rank tracker movement, conversions from organic search when Google Analytics is connected, work done this period, and the next priorities, written for a non-SEO reader and published as a shareable report link.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
