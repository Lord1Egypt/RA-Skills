## Description:

Reviews Japanese patent application claims or Japan-bound PCT/Paris-route claims across JPO compliance, novelty and inventive-step pre-screening, claim architecture, examination risk, translation and foreign-origin risk, and strategic protection.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and patent professionals use this skill to produce an evidence-bound, English HTML review of Japanese patent claims, including JPO compliance issues, prior-art search planning or results, amendment options, and counsel-review gates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent claims, priority documents, translations, and commercial objectives may contain sensitive unpublished information.

Mitigation: Treat all supplied patent materials as confidential and only use external searches or PatSnap/JPO-connected workflows with explicit user approval.

Risk: The skill may surface legal, procedural, prior-art, or translation issues that require professional judgment.

Mitigation: Present findings as evidence-bound review outputs and route proposed Japanese wording, amendments, priority positions, and procedural actions to qualified Japanese patent counsel or translators.
