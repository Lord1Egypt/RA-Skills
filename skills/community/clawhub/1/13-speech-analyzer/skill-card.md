## Description:

Analyzes oral expression quality across 20 dimensions for livestreams, podcasts, and speeches, then produces a structured Markdown analysis report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, creators, coaches, and developers use this skill to evaluate recorded speech from livestreams, podcasts, or presentations and receive quantified feedback on habits, pacing, acoustic qualities, topic structure, interaction, cognitive load, and improv performance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audio recordings may contain private or sensitive speech.

Mitigation: Analyze only recordings the user has permission to use, avoid uploading private conversations unless necessary, and confirm where selected analysis tools process or store files.

Risk: Speech-quality scores can mislead users if they are not grounded in the supplied recording and clear formulas.

Mitigation: Base analysis on actual audio data, use explicit calculation formulas for scores, and keep improvement recommendations concrete and actionable.
