## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to get short, pattern-based judgments for low-stakes code, UI, writing, and conversation feedback, with analytical reasoning reserved for novel, low-confidence, or high-stakes situations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pattern-based judgments can be confidently wrong in high-stakes, rare, or low-validity domains.

Mitigation: Use the skill for low-stakes quick reads and require analytical reasoning plus independent verification for medical, legal, financial, security, business, or sensitive interpersonal decisions.

Risk: Fast interpersonal or demographic pattern matching can reflect bias rather than reliable signal.

Mitigation: Ask what specifically triggered the read, check whether the pattern is substantive, and defer to human judgment when lived experience or domain expertise conflicts with the response.
