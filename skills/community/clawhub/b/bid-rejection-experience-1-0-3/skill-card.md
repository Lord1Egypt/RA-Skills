## Description:

投标否决/废标经验问答技能。已关联公开知识库「招标投标专家否决汇集」，即装即用。当用户询问投标否决原因、废标案例分析、否决风险预警、"什么情况会被废标"、"否决投标常见问题"、"投标被拒怎么办"时触发。不适用于一般性招标文件解读、投标文件编制、数据采集。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and procurement practitioners use this skill to ask Chinese-language questions about bid rejection and bid invalidation causes, examples, and risk warnings. It helps users review common rejection patterns and practical safeguards using a public bid-rejection knowledge base plus web supplementation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bid rejection and legal-risk guidance can be wrong or incomplete for a specific tender.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as reference material and verify them against the specific tender documents and applicable regulations before acting.

Risk: The skill relies on a named public knowledge base and web search, so source availability and freshness may affect answers.

Mitigation: Review the answer's data source notes, prefer cited knowledge-base cases or current public sources, and disclose when relevant cases or web supplements are unavailable.

Risk: Internet material could be confused with knowledge-base cases.

Mitigation: Keep knowledge-base cases and web supplementation clearly labeled, and do not present web content as a retrieved case.
