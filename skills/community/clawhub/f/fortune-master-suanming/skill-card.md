Provides BaZi/Four Pillars fortune-analysis guidance with structured reasoning, classic-source framing, and tiered outputs based on the completeness of the user's birth information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for cultural or entertainment-oriented BaZi/Four Pillars readings, including daily, weekly, monthly, annual, relationship, career, finance, health-pattern, and life-stage interpretations. Agents should frame outputs as reflective guidance rather than medical, legal, financial, investment, safety, or life-planning advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for sensitive personal details such as birth time, birth location, sex, relationship context, or household details.

Mitigation: Collect only the information needed for the requested reading, avoid sharing third-party data without consent, and keep identifying details out of any web searches unless the user explicitly approves.

Risk: Fortune-analysis outputs can be mistaken for medical, legal, financial, investment, safety, or life-planning advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as entertainment or cultural interpretation and redirect high-stakes decisions to qualified professionals or appropriate support resources.

Risk: The skill can produce deterministic-sounding predictions about health, finances, relationships, legal issues, or life events.

Mitigation: Avoid absolute, fear-based, or coercive claims and use cautious language that emphasizes uncertainty, practical reflection, and user agency.
