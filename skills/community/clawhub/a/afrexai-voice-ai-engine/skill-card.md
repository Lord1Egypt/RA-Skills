## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Voice-agent disclosure, call recording, outbound calling, privacy, payment, and healthcare requirements vary by company policy and jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Before production use, update the disclosure and compliance templates to match applicable AI disclosure, call recording, TCPA, GDPR, PCI, HIPAA, and state-law requirements.

Risk: Payment or sensitive personal data could be mishandled if templates are adapted without compliance review.

Mitigation: Use compliant payment and data-handling flows, keep card data out of the STT-to-LLM pipeline, and review logging, retention, and redaction policies before deployment.
