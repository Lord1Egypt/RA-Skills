## Description:

Generate optimized ad copy for Google, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and retargeting campaigns from product details and audience targeting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketers, founders, agencies, and advertisers use this skill to draft platform-ready ad copy, retargeting messages, and campaign recommendations from campaign inputs. It is intended for human review before publication on ad platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated ad copy can include unsupported claims, urgency, social proof, privacy notices, or targeting language that may be inaccurate or noncompliant with platform policies.

Mitigation: Review all generated copy against the advertiser's evidence, privacy commitments, and the relevant ad platform policies before publication.

Risk: Retargeting and buyer-psychology prompts can create persuasive messages for sensitive audiences or campaign stages.

Mitigation: Use only lawful audience segments and remove language that could be misleading, discriminatory, or inconsistent with consent and targeting requirements.
