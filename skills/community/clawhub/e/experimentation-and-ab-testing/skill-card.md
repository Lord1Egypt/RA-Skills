## Description:

Designs disciplined social-media A/B tests by isolating one content variable, setting a decision rule before publishing, controlling context, and treating organic results as directional evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media strategists, content teams, and agents use this skill to turn content debates into controlled organic experiments. It produces test briefs, variant guidance, decision rules, guardrails, repetition plans, and readout criteria tied to brand goals and KPIs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may route generic experiment-planning requests to social-media A/B testing.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants social-content experimentation before applying the skill to generic experiment requests.

Risk: Organic social-media tests can be mistaken for statistically conclusive A/B tests.

Mitigation: Use pre-set decision rules, guardrail metrics, meaningful effect thresholds, and repeated paired tests; describe conclusions as directional learning.
