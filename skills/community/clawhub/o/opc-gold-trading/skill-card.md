## Description:

跨境黄金交易全流程咨询技能，覆盖合规分析、供货方尽调、交易流程、分账税务、账本管理、风险识别、银行验证、交易审核八大模块。

## Use Case:

OPC mentors and trade consultants use this skill to draft cross-border gold-trading compliance reviews, supplier due diligence, transaction workflow guidance, ledger templates, risk screening, bank verification steps, and transaction audit checklists. It is intended as a checklist and drafting aid for qualified review, not as legal, tax, banking, or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China, Hong Kong, Laos, and Southeast Asia

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat generated material as legal, tax, banking, or investment advice for high-risk cross-border gold transactions.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a checklist and drafting aid only, and require qualified legal, tax, banking, compliance, and investment review before acting.

Risk: Regulations, tax rates, bank details, SWIFT messages, counterparties, and transaction documents may be outdated, false, or fraudulent.

Mitigation: Verify current rules, bank instructions, counterparties, funds, and documents through official channels and trusted professionals before entering or relying on transaction details.
