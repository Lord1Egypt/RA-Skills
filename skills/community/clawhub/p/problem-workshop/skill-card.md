## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer agent responses toward Chinese NOI/CSP/OI contest-problem authoring even when a user expects general coding help or another language.

Mitigation: Use it only for Chinese OI-style problem generation, or disable it for unrelated coding and general assistance workflows.

Risk: Generated contest problems, examples, constraints, or solution hints may be incorrect or may not match the intended difficulty.

Mitigation: Have a contest author review each generated problem, validate samples and constraints, and test reference solutions before publication.
