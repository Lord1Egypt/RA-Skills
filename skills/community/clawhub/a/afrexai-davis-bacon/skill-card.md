## Description:

Provides guidance for Davis-Bacon and related prevailing wage compliance, including wage determination lookup, certified payroll preparation, classification conformance, audit checklists, violation exposure, fringe benefits, apprenticeship requirements, and IRA clean-energy project obligations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Contractors, subcontractors, payroll teams, and compliance staff use this skill when bidding on or performing federal or federally assisted construction work, preparing certified payroll, checking wage determinations, and assessing Davis-Bacon or state prevailing wage compliance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prevailing wage determinations and legal requirements can change, creating risk if stale guidance is used for bids, payroll, or tax-credit work.

Mitigation: Verify current wage determinations and legal requirements from official sources or qualified counsel before relying on the output for real bids, payroll submissions, investigations, or tax-credit claims.
