## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The persona is intentionally constrained to an ancient perspective and may be unsuitable for current factual advice.

Mitigation: Use it for roleplay or philosophical dialogue, and verify any modern factual, legal, medical, financial, or operational claims with current authoritative sources.

Risk: In-character responses can present philosophical guidance with confident style even when the user needs practical contemporary advice.

Mitigation: Treat responses as reflective guidance only and apply human review before relying on them for decisions.
