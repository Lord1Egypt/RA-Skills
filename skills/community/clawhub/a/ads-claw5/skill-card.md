## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses external search and ad-scraping tools, so live competitor findings may be incomplete, stale, or misleading.

Mitigation: Review source URLs and collection dates before acting on findings, and treat spend or performance conclusions as estimates unless separately verified.

Risk: The skill saves competitor names, URLs, ad angles, and notes into memory for future comparisons.

Mitigation: Confirm retention is acceptable before installation or ask the agent not to save findings for one-off research when the environment supports that control.
