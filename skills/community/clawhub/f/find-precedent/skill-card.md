## Description:

Find Precedent helps an agent find and compare relevant historical cases such as contracts, proposals, projects, incidents, decisions, designs, or exceptions without treating similarity as authority.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raguets](https://clawhub.ai/user/raguets)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers use this skill when a current decision may benefit from prior comparable work. It guides the agent to find relevant precedents, explain comparability, surface material differences, and separate reusable lessons from stale or case-specific details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may surface sensitive historical contracts, proposals, incidents, or decisions.

Mitigation: Install it only where the agent is authorized to search those repositories and keep access scoped to appropriate knowledge sources.

Risk: A prior contract, proposal, or incident may be stale or materially different from the current case.

Mitigation: Require outputs to show material differences, limits of analogy, and what must not be copied blindly.
