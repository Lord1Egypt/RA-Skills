This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Marketing, growth, and media-buying teams use this skill to plan CTV and OTT ad campaigns, choose buying channels, define creative requirements, set metrics, and prepare launch checklists.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may apply the skill to traditional broadcast or cable TV buys outside its intended CTV/OTT scope.

Mitigation: Clarify whether the campaign is for connected TV, OTT, or streaming TV before relying on the guidance.
