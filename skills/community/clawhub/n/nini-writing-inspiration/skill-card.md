## Description:

Helps users start or continue travel writing, TIL notes, or general articles through stepwise inspirational questions rather than diary writing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[niracler](https://clawhub.ai/user/niracler)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and writing-focused agents use this skill to guide ideation and drafting for travel writing, TIL notes, and general articles. The skill helps gather user intent one question at a time, confirm answers, and organize the result into a coherent draft.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad writing requests and could overlap with diary-style writing because the reference material includes week and month journaling prompts.

Mitigation: Route explicit diary writing to a diary-specific skill and use this skill only for travel writing, TIL notes, and general article ideation.
