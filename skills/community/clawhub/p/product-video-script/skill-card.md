## Description:

Write engaging product video scripts for short-form platforms like TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, ecommerce marketers, UGC partners, and in-house editors use this skill to create platform-optimized short-form product video scripts. It turns a product brief into hooks, beat-by-beat spoken lines, on-screen text, shot direction, captions, hashtags, and production notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated persuasive sales scripts may include unsupported product claims, fabricated reviews, fake urgency, or regulated health or earnings claims.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims, reviews, scarcity, and regulated claims before publishing, and check the relevant platform advertising policies.
