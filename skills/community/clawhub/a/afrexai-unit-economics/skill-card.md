## Use Case:

Business operators, finance teams, and founders use this skill to calculate unit economics, compare metrics against industry benchmarks, identify unprofitable customer segments, and plan scenarios around CAC, churn, retention, and pricing.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the skill may require sharing sensitive business metrics such as revenue, churn, margins, acquisition costs, and segment performance with an agent.

Mitigation: Review inputs before use and avoid sharing confidential figures that are not needed for the analysis.

Risk: Estimated inputs and benchmark comparisons may produce incorrect or misleading business conclusions if assumptions are wrong.

Mitigation: Validate formulas, assumptions, and estimated values against source financial data before acting on recommendations.

Risk: The artifact includes external promotional links that are not required for runtime behavior.

Mitigation: Treat external links as optional resources and review them independently before relying on them.
