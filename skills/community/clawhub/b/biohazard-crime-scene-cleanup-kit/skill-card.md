## Description:

Generates compliance-focused marketing materials for licensed biohazard and crime scene cleanup companies, enforcing federal and state legal requirements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitflopez](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitflopez)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Biohazard cleanup operators, restoration companies, and agencies use this skill to draft marketing campaigns, service pages, review responses, referral outreach, ads, local SEO content, and structured data for regulated cleanup services.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Nevada and Clark County; other locations require local legal and regulatory revalidation before publication.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated legal, regulatory, insurance, review, or advertising claims may be inaccurate outside the Nevada and Clark County context.

Mitigation: Use the content as draft copy and have local counsel or regulators revalidate legal references before publication.

Risk: Trauma, hoarding, crime-victim, insurance, and coroner-related communications can be privacy-sensitive or emotionally sensitive.

Mitigation: Review public responses, ads, and outreach for privacy, tone, consent, and factual accuracy before use.
