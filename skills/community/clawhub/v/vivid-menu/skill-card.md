## Description:

Turn restaurant menu text, structured JSON, or dish lists into print-ready single-page SVG or PNG designs with hand-drawn style illustrations, themed palettes, and automated layout, contrast, and legibility checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Restaurant owners and agents use this skill to turn dish lists or existing menu text into clear, print-ready menu designs and editable menu data. It also supports interactive editing and menu layout checks.
