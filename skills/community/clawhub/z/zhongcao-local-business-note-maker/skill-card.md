## Description:

Creates a coordinated vertical 3:4 Xiaohongshu or REDnote local-business post from supplied photos or a merchant brief, with title ideas, caption angles, tags, and optional Xiaohongshu research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Local business owners, marketers, and content creators use the skill to prepare a coordinated Xiaohongshu or REDnote promotional post for a physical venue. It turns supplied business facts and visual references into an ordered image story and accompanying post copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
