## Description:

A bundle of 432 internal medicine clinical skills based on People's Medical Publishing House's Internal Medicine, 10th Edition, covering diagnosis criteria, risk assessment, treatment strategy, laboratory and imaging interpretation, emergency handling, and chronic disease management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xllgreen](https://clawhub.ai/user/xllgreen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Clinicians, supervised medical learners, and medical educators use this skill bundle to route internal-medicine questions to focused modules for structured clinical reasoning, textbook-based review, and clinician-reviewed decision support. It is not appropriate as a standalone source for diagnosis, prescribing, emergency care, invasive procedures, or patient self-care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: High-risk medical protocols may be unsafe if used outside clinician-supervised care.

Mitigation: Install and use the skill only in qualified clinical or supervised medical-education environments.

Risk: Outputs may be incorrect, outdated, or inconsistent with local guidelines, institutional protocols, or patient-specific contraindications.

Mitigation: Require review against current local guidance, institutional policy, and licensed clinician judgment before acting on any output.

Risk: Diagnosis, prescribing, emergency procedure, invasive procedure, and patient self-care use could cause patient harm if treated as standalone advice.

Mitigation: Do not use the skill as a standalone clinical authority; direct emergencies to acute care and keep outputs within clinician-reviewed decision support or supervised education.

Risk: Identifiable patient or genomic data may create privacy or compliance risk.

Mitigation: Avoid entering identifiable patient or genomic data unless the operating environment is approved for that use.
