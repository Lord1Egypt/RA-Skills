## Use Case:

External users and smart-home agents use this skill to adjust thermostat settings, troubleshoot comfort or HVAC issues, estimate energy savings, and configure schedules, away modes, and smart-home automations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An agent could guide or perform real thermostat setpoint, schedule, vacation, or automation changes that affect comfort, pets, plants, guests, pipes, humidity, or rental properties.

Mitigation: Keep confirmation enabled for actual thermostat and smart-home changes, and review proposed settings before applying them.

Risk: Home climate troubleshooting or savings estimates may be incomplete for the user's HVAC system or local conditions.

Mitigation: Use the skill's guidance as decision support, verify system behavior and safety constraints, and involve an HVAC professional for persistent faults, error codes, leaks, smells, or abnormal operation.
