## Description:

The Q12 engagement survey assistant helps team managers, HRBPs, and organizational development specialists design Gallup Q12-style employee engagement surveys, interpret aggregate results, and create 90-day improvement plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[muippt](https://clawhub.ai/user/muippt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Managers, HRBPs, and organizational development specialists use this skill to draft Q12 questionnaires, analyze aggregate engagement scores, identify weak areas and high-risk score combinations, and plan concrete engagement improvements. It is not intended for employee-initiated surveys without management authorization, large-scale statistical modeling, or replacing a professional survey platform.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Employee engagement survey data can include confidential or identifiable HR information.

Mitigation: Use aggregated scores, avoid names and free-text comments, and check the AI platform's data retention settings before sharing business or HR data.

Risk: Very small team breakouts can make anonymous survey responses re-identifiable.

Mitigation: Avoid reporting small groups separately; use larger aggregates or manager-led 1:1 interviews when anonymity cannot be protected.
