## Description:

Generates structured Chinese daily or weekly work reports from described tasks, with general, simple, detailed, and military-style formats.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yongjie666888](https://clawhub.ai/user/yongjie666888)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and project teams use this skill to turn Chinese work descriptions or task lists into standardized daily or weekly status reports. It supports general workplace reporting and military-style report structures that include technical status, quality record, review, and standards-reference fields.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is designed for Chinese-language daily and weekly report templates and does not evidence alternate output languages.

Mitigation: Confirm that Chinese-language output is acceptable for the intended users before deployment.

Risk: Military-style report fields and standards references may be unsuitable if treated as verified compliance documentation.

Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers check generated military-style reports against organizational reporting and compliance requirements before use.

Risk: The advertised detailed format may not provide materially more detail than the general format.

Mitigation: Review sample outputs for the selected format and adjust prompts or templates if more detail is required.
