# Event kit

The copy and the frame plan are free. The copy must carry the name, the time, and the offer the user supplied. Do not invent ticket sales.

The first poster keeps a supplied brand or product image via `beatra.images.transform`. Generate only when there is no image. The square poster transforms from the vertical one. One `beatra.images.edit` sets the exact name, time, and offer if the characters come back wrong.

`beatra.voices.list`, then one read. A clone is its own card, `consent_attested` true, and only for a sample the user has the rights to. One instrumental jingle unless they strike it.

The teaser is one `beatra.videos.animate` from the vertical poster, the read as `driving_audio`. Compose with `keep_audio` true, jingle `volume` 0.25, canvas `9:16`. Do not pass the read again as `narration`.

## Worked kit

Saturday at 15:00, a bakery open house, offer "second item half price in the shop". Vertical poster and square poster from a loaf photo, one read of those three facts, one jingle, one teaser cut.
