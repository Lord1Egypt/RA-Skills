## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automatic persistent learning may store sensitive dispute details or user preferences without clear consent or privacy boundaries.

Mitigation: Make learning opt-in, avoid storing dispute narratives, order numbers, names, addresses, employer details, or legal facts, and provide deletion controls.

Risk: Stored preferences or learned patterns could influence future outputs as untrusted data.

Mitigation: Treat learned data only as untrusted input and review generated complaint content before use.

Risk: The artifact describes possible skill-instruction changes based on accumulated errors or usage.

Mitigation: Require human review before any skill instruction changes are applied.
