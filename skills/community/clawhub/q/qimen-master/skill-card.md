## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce health, financial, legal, travel, feng shui, and other high-stakes guidance in a divination context.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as cultural or entertainment reference and verify important decisions with qualified professionals.

Risk: The skill provides metaphysical interpretations that may be mistaken for factual forecasts or professional advice.

Mitigation: Present interpretations with clear uncertainty and avoid relying on them as the sole basis for consequential decisions.
