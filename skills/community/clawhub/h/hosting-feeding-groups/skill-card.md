## Description:

Practical skills for cooking and hosting groups of 10-50 people. Use when someone needs to feed a crowd for events, community gatherings, family reunions, block parties, or regular group meals on a budget.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to plan, budget, cook, serve, and coordinate meals for groups of 10-50 people while accounting for timing, equipment, dietary restrictions, and food safety.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The install command uses npx without a pinned installer version.

Mitigation: Use a trusted ClawHub installer or a pinned and controlled package source in sensitive environments.

Risk: Food-safety advice may need adjustment for local regulations and event conditions.

Mitigation: Check applicable local requirements and use the skill's temperature, time, allergen-labeling, and discard guidance conservatively.
