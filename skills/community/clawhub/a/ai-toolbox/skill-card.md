## Description:

电商工具箱 is a Chinese e-commerce assistant for competitor research, marketplace copywriting, customer-service scripts, daily monitoring, and product-selection guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howerlin0329](https://clawhub.ai/user/howerlin0329)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers and operators use this skill to research public marketplace signals, generate Chinese product copy and customer-service responses, configure recurring competitor monitoring, and evaluate product opportunities across Chinese commerce platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recurring monitoring can continue after setup and may create LLM or search costs.

Mitigation: Confirm monitored keywords, report destinations, schedule cadence, expected costs, and how to stop the schedule before enabling daily or weekly monitoring.

Risk: Competitor and trend reports depend on public search results, which may be incomplete, stale, or influenceable.

Mitigation: Treat generated market reports as advisory and review important pricing, sourcing, and product decisions before acting on them.

Risk: Generated marketplace copy may include claims or urgency language that requires platform-compliance review.

Mitigation: Review titles, live scripts, and product descriptions against platform rules and the included prohibited-word guidance before publishing.
