## Description:

HiLegal supports bilingual cross-border business compliance research and Q&A covering trade, data, labor, tax, foreign law research, corporate credit checks, and global price lookups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[netllc](https://clawhub.ai/user/netllc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business users and advisors use this skill for preliminary cross-border compliance research, structured compliance Q&A, foreign law research, corporate credit checks, and global pricing lookups in Chinese or English.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Compliance, legal, tax, credit, and pricing guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for final decisions.

Mitigation: Use the skill for preliminary research only and confirm important results with current official sources or a qualified professional.

Risk: Broad multi-jurisdiction reference content may not reflect the exact country, industry, transaction, or company facts in a user query.

Mitigation: Provide specific jurisdiction and business context, then treat the output as a starting point for targeted review.
