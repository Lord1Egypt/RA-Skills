## Use Case:

Livestream commerce operators, content reviewers, and agent users can use this skill to draft and review China-market AI avatar livestream materials, including AI disclosure language, prohibited-word checks, compliant sales scripts, monitoring workflows, and recap templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill combines compliance checks with sales scripts that may encourage urgency, scarcity, social pressure, or unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Verify price, inventory, scarcity, social-proof, health, and performance claims before use, and treat the stricter compliance checklist as authoritative.

Risk: A user could rely on the templates as complete legal or platform compliance advice.

Mitigation: Review outputs against current platform rules, product substantiation, and qualified legal or compliance guidance before publishing livestream content.
