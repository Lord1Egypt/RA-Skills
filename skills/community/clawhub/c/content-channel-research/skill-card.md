## Description:

Structured GO/NO-GO framework for validating content topics before scripting or recording by running audience segmentation, adoption research, saturation checks, and competitive differentiation analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pingukim225](https://clawhub.ai/user/pingukim225)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and channel strategists use this skill to decide whether a content idea is worth producing and to identify the audience segment, market gap, angle, positioning, and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations may depend on incomplete or time-sensitive market and audience data.

Mitigation: Review cited searches, competitive examples, and final recommendations before acting on them.

Risk: The skill encourages external searches and competitive review that may surface outdated or low-quality sources.

Mitigation: Prefer current, relevant sources and validate saturation or adoption claims against multiple results.
