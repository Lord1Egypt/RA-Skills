## Description:

Generates Chinese-format enterprise IT integration solution proposal documents in Word .docx format, including cover, table of contents, project overview, requirements analysis, technical design, architecture, implementation, project management, training, support, and acceptance sections.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[a-lhliang](https://clawhub.ai/user/a-lhliang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and business proposal teams use this skill to create enterprise IT integration solution proposals with Chinese Word document formatting conventions. It is intended for customer-facing technical proposal drafts that need structured sections and consistent .docx styling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated table of contents behavior may not match the documentation.

Mitigation: Review the generated table of contents in Word before sharing or relying on the document.
