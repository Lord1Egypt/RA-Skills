## Description:

Generates compliance-focused marketing content for NADCA-certified air duct cleaning and indoor air quality contractors, including campaigns, service pages, review templates, ads, SEO assets, schema markup, and outreach copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Air duct cleaning contractors, HVAC contractors, IAQ firms, and marketing agencies use this skill to draft contractor-specific marketing materials with built-in prompts for NADCA, EPA, FIFRA, mold-claim, Nevada contractor-license, FTC, and TCPA review points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States, with Nevada-specific examples and license checks

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing, review, SMS, or service-page copy may be inaccurate for a contractor's location, credentials, equipment, or current legal obligations.

Mitigation: Verify local licensing, advertising, TCPA, FTC, NADCA, EPA, FIFRA, and mold-related requirements before publishing or sending any output.

Risk: Credential, health, mold, biocide, HEPA, or HVAC modification claims can create compliance exposure if the contractor lacks substantiating documentation.

Mitigation: Confirm NADCA membership, ASCS technician credentials, equipment specifications, EPA registration numbers, customer consent, IAQ testing data, and applicable contractor licenses before retaining those claims.
