## Description:

Turn a product and an audience into one full-campaign marketing pack: topics taken from public posts, one poster, one product clip, and one jingle. Full-campaign marketing writes the plan, then makes the pictures, clip, and music. Use it when one offer should leave as a set of publishable pieces the same day.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
