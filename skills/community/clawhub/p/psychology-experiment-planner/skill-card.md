## Description:

心理学实验规划与数据收集预检查助手，用于在实验开始前帮助用户厘清研究设计、规划测量变量、推荐数据收集方式、设计预实验或前测、预估样本量并生成数据收集清单。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External researchers, students, and research planners use this Chinese-language skill before collecting psychology study data to plan study design, variables, measurement tools, sample size, analysis alignment, ethics checks, and data collection checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation phrases may cause the skill to engage for general research-planning requests.

Mitigation: Use it when Chinese-language psychology study planning is intended, and verify the generated plan fits the actual research context.

Risk: Statistical, ethics, privacy, and scale-copyright guidance may be incomplete for a specific institution or study.

Mitigation: Confirm sample-size calculations, ethics requirements, privacy handling, and scale authorization with institutional policies and qualified reviewers before data collection.
