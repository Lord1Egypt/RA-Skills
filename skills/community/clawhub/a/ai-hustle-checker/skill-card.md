## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to assess Chinese-language AI side-hustle, course, or opportunity claims and receive a credibility score, red flags, green flags, and practical cautionary guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill produces heuristic credibility scores for AI side-hustle and course claims, which could be mistaken for financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat scores and recommendations as screening guidance only, and verify business, legal, and financial decisions through independent due diligence.

Risk: The evaluator is designed around Chinese-language side-hustle phrasing and may miss or misclassify non-Chinese workflows.

Mitigation: Use it primarily for Chinese-language claims and manually review outputs when applying it outside that language or market context.
