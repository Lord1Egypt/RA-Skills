## Description:

Gathers sources, key facts, contrarian evidence, quote candidates, contradictions, and confidence notes before content is written or published.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators, editors, and agents use this skill to gather reliable sources, key facts, contrarian points, quote candidates, contradictions, and confidence signals before drafting or publishing content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unchecked or unavailable sources can lead to weak confidence or stale factual claims in research notes.

Mitigation: Require source checking for live facts, record missing evidence explicitly, and lower confidence when sources cannot be verified.

Risk: Research notes may include incomplete or one-sided evidence if contrarian sources are skipped.

Mitigation: Use the skill's required contrarian evidence, limits, contradictions, and confidence sections before drafting content.

Risk: Publisher identity or language context may matter for some installation workflows.

Mitigation: Review the server-resolved publisher profile and linked homepage before deployment.
