## Description:

Pilot Base helps agents provide Chinese-language planning and operating guidance for pilot-scale industrial validation platforms across positioning, funding, staffing, compliance, project management, ecosystem building, and stage review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External institutions, industrial parks, manufacturers, government platform teams, and research organizations use this skill to plan, build, and operate TRL 4-7 pilot-scale validation platforms. It supports platform-level decisions around policy funding, talent, governance, IP, operating models, compliance, project management, partnerships, annual goals, and staged assessment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Safety, legal, policy, IP, and financing guidance may become outdated or may not fit a specific jurisdiction, facility, or project.

Mitigation: Use the skill as planning support only and require current official rules plus qualified safety, legal, IP, finance, and engineering review before operational decisions.

Risk: Pilot-scale industrial validation platforms, especially chemical, pharmaceutical, biological, and special-equipment contexts, can create high safety and environmental exposure.

Mitigation: Require formal safety assessment, environmental review, emergency planning, and qualified professional sign-off before implementing platform recommendations.

Risk: The skill is Chinese-language advisory material for a specialized industrial-policy and operations context.

Mitigation: Confirm local applicability and obtain domain review when using the guidance outside its intended policy, language, or industrial context.
