## Description:

Provides Chinese-language operational guidance for subscription-platform creators, covering positioning, pricing, content calendars, fan CRM, traffic acquisition, DM workflows, and revenue optimization.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators and creator-business operators use this skill to draft subscription-platform operating plans, including pricing, content scheduling, fan segmentation, retention workflows, and growth metrics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creator-business guidance may be applied without checking platform rules, age restrictions, legal obligations, or tax requirements.

Mitigation: Review the current platform terms, age and local legal requirements, and tax obligations before using the generated operating plan.

Risk: Third-party automation tools and messaging templates may not fit a creator's platform rules or audience expectations.

Mitigation: Evaluate any third-party tool and adapt DM or automation workflows before deployment.

Risk: Pricing and revenue benchmarks may be mistaken for guaranteed outcomes.

Mitigation: Treat benchmarks as planning inputs and validate them against the creator's own revenue, retention, ARPU, and conversion data.
