## Description:

Amy Morin's "13 Things Mentally Strong People Don't Do" is a self-help guide skill for building mental strength by identifying and replacing habits such as rumination, comparison, resentment, people-pleasing, and fear of calculated risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jaysonmeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/jaysonmeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for self-improvement coaching based on Amy Morin's 13 Things framework, including guidance for mental strength, overthinking, resentment, comparison, fear, avoidance, self-worth, and people-pleasing. The agent responds with concise, action-oriented guidance and concrete exercises.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad emotional-support topics where the user did not specifically ask for this book.

Mitigation: Review activation scope before deployment and constrain use to explicit requests for this book or clearly matching 13 Things topics.

Risk: The skill may add Heardly branding and links to responses, including responses outside the book's core scope.

Mitigation: Review output policy and remove or limit unsolicited branding, links, and cross-book recommendations where inappropriate.

Risk: The skill provides self-help guidance on mental-strength topics that users could mistake for individualized clinical advice.

Mitigation: Use it for general coaching only and route crisis, diagnosis, treatment, or other clinical requests to appropriate qualified support.
