## Description:

A Chinese-language advisor that runs a conversational questionnaire for small and midsize businesses, identifies practical AI adoption opportunities, and produces an implementation roadmap.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[g620710](https://clawhub.ai/user/g620710)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business owners, operators, and consultants use this skill to assess an organization's AI readiness, prioritize 3-5 high-ROI AI scenarios, and draft a 30-day adoption roadmap. It is intended for practical planning conversations, not neutral vendor selection.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated report may include follow-up consulting, deployment support, or managed-service suggestions.

Mitigation: Treat those suggestions as commercial guidance and evaluate service providers independently before committing budget.

Risk: ROI, cost, and timeline estimates depend on the user's inputs and may be misleading if business details are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review estimates against the organization's actual workflows, budget, and implementation capacity before using them for decisions.
