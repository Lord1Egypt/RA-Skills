## Use Case:

Technology-transfer professionals, university and research teams, and commercialization advisors use this skill to draft China-focused transfer plans for technical achievements. It helps compare licensing, assignment, and equity-contribution structures using a 10-factor decision matrix and bundled reference cases.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce legal, tax, financial, valuation, or contract-clause drafting guidance for real technology-transfer transactions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning support and have current transaction facts reviewed by qualified legal, tax, financial, and technology-transfer professionals before use.

Risk: The bundled cases and tax references may not match a user's jurisdiction, institution policy, transaction structure, or current rules.

Mitigation: Verify applicability against current China-focused law, tax treatment, institutional policy, and deal documents before relying on recommendations.
