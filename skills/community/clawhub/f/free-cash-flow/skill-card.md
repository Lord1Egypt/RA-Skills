## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs may be misleading if the model uses weak or unreviewed assumptions for revenue, costs, discount rates, or terminal value.

Mitigation: Review all assumptions and sensitivity results before relying on the analysis for investment decisions.

Risk: Broad finance trigger terms may activate the skill for general finance questions outside cash-flow modeling.

Mitigation: Use the skill for cash-flow modeling and valuation tasks, and route unrelated finance questions to a more appropriate workflow.
