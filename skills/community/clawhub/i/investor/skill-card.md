## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to structure investment opportunity evaluation, due diligence, term-sheet review, portfolio strategy, and exit-planning discussions. It provides general investment-analysis guidance and should not be treated as personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides general investment-analysis guidance that could be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Users should independently verify facts and consult qualified professionals before making investment decisions.

Risk: Investment evaluations can rely on incomplete or optimistic claims from founders or deal materials.

Mitigation: Use the skill's diligence posture to verify claims independently, reconcile metrics, and seek direct customer and reference evidence.
