## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides generic exercise text rather than validated medical or coaching advice.

Mitigation: Users should treat outputs as general planning support and consult qualified professionals for medical, injury, or individualized training decisions.

Risk: The skill creates a local data directory in the user's home directory.

Mitigation: Review the disclosed path before deployment and ensure local storage is acceptable for the target environment.
