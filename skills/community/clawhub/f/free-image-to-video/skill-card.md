## Description:

Generate a short video clip with sound on Free Image to Video (freeimagetovideo.ai, MiniMax H3) with the user's own API key, from a text prompt or by animating a photo (image to video). Use when the user asks for a video from a prompt, wants to animate a still or photo, asks to check a run, or asks how many credits are left, and FREEIMAGETOVIDEO_API_KEY is set.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
