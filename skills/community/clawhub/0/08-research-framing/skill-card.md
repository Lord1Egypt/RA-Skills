## Description:

Use when one research source chain is stable enough to turn into paper framing or a provisional framing sketch by comparing closest related works, checking claim-level citation support, clarifying positioning and differentiation, and producing a writing blueprint.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[snake-fan](https://clawhub.ai/user/snake-fan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, authors, and developer agents use this skill to turn a single stable research source chain into a paper-facing framing package or provisional sketch. It supports close-work comparison, claim support checking, positioning, differentiation, and writing blueprint creation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill guides an agent to inspect selected local research artifacts and create or update Markdown framing files.

Mitigation: Point it only at research material intended for paper positioning and review generated framing files before relying on them.

Risk: Generated claim-safety and citation-support outputs may still be incomplete or misleading if source materials are unstable or close works are poorly selected.

Mitigation: Review the claim-support bank, close-work set, and citation boundaries before using the framing package in paper writing.
