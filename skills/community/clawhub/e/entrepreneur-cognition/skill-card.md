## Description:

Entrepreneur Cognition helps agents structure startup decisions with market analysis, business model validation, pivot memory, and human review checkpoints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kingofzhao](https://clawhub.ai/user/kingofzhao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent developers use this skill to guide startup and entrepreneur workflows, including market sizing questions, business model stress tests, competitive checks, cash-flow red lines, and pivot memory.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested business memory files may contain sensitive startup, financial, customer, or market information if users choose to create them.

Mitigation: Review the documentation before installing, limit sensitive entries to appropriate workspaces, and apply normal access controls for any generated decision logs, market analysis, pivot history, or financial model files.

Risk: The skill supports business decision framing but does not automatically verify market, financial, or competitive claims.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision-support guidance and require human review, source checking, and current business data before acting on strategic or financial recommendations.
