## Description:

Rebuild dense ideas into authoritative long-form writing for Medium, optimizing clarity, intellectual structure, reader trust, and deep-read momentum for essays, explainers, and expert articles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External writers, editors, founders, operators, and subject-matter experts use this skill to turn dense or academic drafts into clearer Medium-style long-form articles with stronger structure, authority, readability, and reader trust.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be selected for ambiguous long-form writing requests where the user only wants a simpler rewrite.

Mitigation: Clarify whether the user wants Medium-oriented deep-read optimization or a simpler rewrite before applying the full assessment workflow.

Risk: Article drafts may rely on facts, citations, legal claims, compliance requirements, or domain expertise that the supplied material does not establish.

Mitigation: State missing context clearly, avoid inventing certainty, and request the needed source material or review before strengthening claims.
