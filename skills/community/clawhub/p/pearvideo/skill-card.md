## Description:

Pearvideo helps agents summarize public Pear Video short-video and topic pages without downloading content or reverse engineering APIs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mike47512](https://clawhub.ai/user/mike47512)

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and operators use this skill to inspect public Pear Video pages and summarize topic, account, and ranking performance for internal analysis and alerts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could misuse the workflow for downloading, bypassing platform controls, or bulk collection.

Mitigation: Keep requests limited to public content, respect platform rules, and do not use the skill for downloading, bypassing controls, or bulk collection.

Risk: Dynamic rendering and human verification can make page observations incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Manually open pages when needed, review extracted summaries before relying on them, and keep request frequency limited.
