## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated FastAPI endpoints may omit authentication, authorization, validation, or rate limiting for sensitive operations.

Mitigation: Review generated routes, schemas, and update or delete APIs before deployment to confirm access controls, input validation, and rate limiting are appropriate.
