## Description:

Professional entry-level satellite communication engineering career roadmap platform that generates personalized learning paths based on skills assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, students, and engineers use this skill to call a third-party API that generates personalized satellite communication engineering learning roadmaps from experience, skills, goals, and session metadata.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The third-party service may process personal career history, goals, skills, session ID, timestamp, and optional user ID.

Mitigation: Submit only information needed for the roadmap request and review the provider's privacy practices before sharing personal identifiers.

Risk: Generated career roadmaps and certification suggestions may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a user's situation.

Mitigation: Treat the roadmap as planning guidance and review recommendations against current employer, school, certification, and industry requirements.
