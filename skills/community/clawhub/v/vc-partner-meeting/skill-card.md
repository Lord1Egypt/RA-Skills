## Description:

Simulates a VC partner meeting after a pitch, with four partner archetypes debating the deal and producing an internal verdict memo plus a fixability debrief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, fundraising teams, and advisors use this skill to stress-test how investors may discuss a pitch after the founder leaves the room. It produces adversarial fundraising feedback, an internal fund/pass/track memo, and a debrief on objections that may be fixable before a real meeting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pitch decks and fundraising details may contain confidential business information.

Mitigation: Share only business information the user is comfortable providing to the agent.

Risk: The simulated partner discussion could be mistaken for legal, financial, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Use the output as adversarial fundraising feedback and have qualified reviewers assess real fundraising decisions.
