## Description:

Sustoiclab Think Framework is a Chinese-language structured thinking toolbox that routes user problems to root-cause, strategy, decision, multi-angle, or efficiency frameworks and guides step-by-step analysis toward structured conclusions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[susstoiclab](https://clawhub.ai/user/susstoiclab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this skill to structure ambiguous thinking tasks such as finding root causes, evaluating strategy, comparing decisions, exploring multiple perspectives, and prioritizing effort. The skill provides guided prompts and structured Markdown-style reports while leaving final decisions to the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger words may activate the skill more often than intended.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected module when a request is broad, generic, or could match multiple thinking frameworks.

Risk: Strategic, decision, and productivity outputs could be mistaken for professional advice in high-stakes situations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as structured guidance and seek qualified review before acting on high-stakes business, legal, financial, medical, or safety decisions.
