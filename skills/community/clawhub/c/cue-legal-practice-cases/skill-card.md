## Description:

Searches public Chinese court decisions, regulatory guidance, and practice cases to summarize comparable rulings and practical legal issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals and legal professionals use it to find public Chinese cases and regulatory guidance, compare how similar disputes were handled, and gather case references for further review. It provides research support rather than legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account checks and document conversion may be unrelated to legal research, and the available security review is partial.

Mitigation: Before installing, ask the publisher to explain these behaviors, review the complete skill, and make the unrelated promotional workflow optional.
