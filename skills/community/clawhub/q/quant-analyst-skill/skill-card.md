## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated financial models or trading strategies may be mistaken for financial advice or autonomous trading decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as analytical drafts; review assumptions, calculations, and strategy behavior before using them with real money.

Risk: Backtests may overstate performance if market data quality, transaction costs, slippage, or out-of-sample validation are weak.

Mitigation: Validate data, include costs and slippage, and require out-of-sample testing before relying on results.
