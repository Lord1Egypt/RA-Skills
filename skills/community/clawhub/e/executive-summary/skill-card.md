## Description:

Write an executive summary for any document, report, or proposal.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, consultants, and other business writers use this skill to turn a source document, report, proposal, or topic into a concise executive summary for senior stakeholders. It emphasizes a clear bottom line, audience-specific framing, a recommendation, immediate next steps, and risks of inaction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be applied to an ordinary summary request where the user did not want an executive brief.

Mitigation: Use it when the user asks for an executive summary, management summary, board briefing, senior-stakeholder one-pager, or similar decision-focused output; otherwise ask for the intended audience and format first.
