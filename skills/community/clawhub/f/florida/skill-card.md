## Use Case:

External users use this skill for Florida-specific decisions about visiting, moving, living, running a local business, or managing seasonal residence. It helps compare regions, sequence resident logistics, and account for insurance, storms, healthcare, transportation, and local verification needs.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional local memory may save sensitive personal context in a plain Markdown file under ~/florida/.

Mitigation: Review the memory behavior before installing, keep saved notes coarse, and do not store passwords, account numbers, SSNs, Medicare IDs, payment details, or full street addresses.

Risk: Florida rules and workflows can be county-specific or change over time.

Mitigation: Verify precise compliance steps through official state, county, district, insurance, emergency-management, health, or education sources before acting.
