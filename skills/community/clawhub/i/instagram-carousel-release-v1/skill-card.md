## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated statistics, case studies, or cited sources may be outdated or incorrect before publication.

Mitigation: Review every statistic and source against current primary sources before publishing the carousel.

Risk: Generated carousel copy and visuals may not fully match the intended brand, language, or audience.

Mitigation: Provide explicit brand details, language, palette, and tone, then review the final HTML slides before export.
