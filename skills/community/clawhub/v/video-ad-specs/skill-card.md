## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Quick Start asks users to pipe a mutable remote installer directly into a shell.

Mitigation: Inspect the installer first or use a pinned, checksum-verified manual CLI installation before running infsh commands.

Risk: Platform ad specifications and safe-zone guidance may become outdated as social platforms change requirements.

Mitigation: Confirm current platform ad requirements before publishing or buying media with generated assets.
