## Description:

Audits game feature, system concept, prototype plan, or preproduction proposal intent and scope fit by distinguishing selling demos, learning prototypes, and confused hybrids.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, producers, and development teams use this skill to audit whether a prototype should sell an idea, reveal unknowns, or be split into clearer prototype efforts. It helps teams align prototype scope with the decision the prototype is meant to unlock.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may provide direct, opinionated critique that influences prototype scope or team priorities.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as review input and confirm scope changes with the project team and prototype decision owner.

Risk: Incomplete prototype context can lead to an overconfident diagnosis of intent, scope fit, or unknowns coverage.

Mitigation: Use the skill's clarification questions to capture the prototype idea, audience, decision, and success criteria before acting on the audit.
