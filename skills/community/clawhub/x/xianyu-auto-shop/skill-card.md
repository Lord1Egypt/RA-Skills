## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad UI, notification, and background-task permissions can affect marketplace listings and private messages.

Mitigation: Install only in a constrained environment and grant the minimum permissions needed for the intended workflow.

Risk: Unattended marketplace automation and anti-detection behavior may affect platform-policy compliance and account standing.

Mitigation: Confirm the workflow is permitted by the marketplace, keep human oversight enabled, and disable unattended automation when compliance is uncertain.

Risk: Automated private-message replies can send unintended or inaccurate responses.

Mitigation: Review reply templates before use and monitor conversations when auto-reply mode is active.
