## Description:

Designs, organizes, and improves tracking systems for work, goals, follow-ups, pipelines, projects, habits, issues, and ongoing operations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and operations teams use this skill to design tracker schemas, status logic, ownership fields, review cadences, and cleanup rules for ongoing work and follow-ups.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tracker outputs may be applied as professional judgment or compliance assurance in regulated, HR, accounting, legal, or audit-critical workflows.

Mitigation: Review and adapt tracker designs to the user's jurisdiction, system requirements, and internal controls before relying on them in sensitive workflows.

Risk: A tracker can become misleading if fields, statuses, ownership, stale rules, or review cadence are unclear or not maintained.

Mitigation: Use the skill's quality checklist to confirm the tracked object, decision-supporting fields, distinct statuses, visible ownership, review cadence, stale rules, cleanup logic, and next step.
