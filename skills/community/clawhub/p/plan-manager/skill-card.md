## Description:

Unified plan management system for coordinating OpenClaw Agent and Human plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wx528](https://clawhub.ai/user/wx528)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, agents, and human collaborators use this skill to create, organize, and maintain coordinated markdown plans for agent execution work and human tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Plan files may contain sensitive personal or business planning details stored in the local workspace.

Mitigation: Avoid adding sensitive details unless the workspace storage and access model are acceptable for that information.

Risk: Outdated or inaccurate plan status can mislead human or agent coordination.

Mitigation: Review and update agent and human plan files regularly, especially before using them to guide work.
