## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Strategic recommendations could be mistaken for automatic financial or media-buying instructions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support, require human review before capital deployment, and keep governance limits and approval thresholds explicit.

Risk: Poor or stale market signals could produce misleading allocation guidance.

Mitigation: Provide current cost, demand, competition, and conversion data; when signal quality is inconsistent, use confidence bands and data remediation steps.

Risk: High-stakes decisions with low confidence could exceed organizational risk tolerance.

Mitigation: Use the skill's executive review gate, downside thresholds, and defensive allocation mode when governance limits are breached.
