## Description:

Cn Email Template generates Chinese business email drafts for common scenarios from user-provided fields.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[freedompixels](https://clawhub.ai/user/freedompixels)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and developers can use this skill to generate Chinese business email subjects and bodies for outreach, follow-up, thank-you notes, quotes, refusals, apologies, meeting invitations, weekly reports, farewell notes, and holiday greetings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill states that it is automatically generated without human review.

Mitigation: Review generated email drafts for tone, accuracy, recipient details, and brand fit before sending.

Risk: Output and help text are Chinese-only.

Mitigation: Use this skill for Chinese business email workflows or pair it with translation review when serving multilingual users.

Risk: Required template fields may be omitted by the user.

Mitigation: Provide all fields referenced by the selected scenario, or run the help command to inspect available scenarios before generation.
