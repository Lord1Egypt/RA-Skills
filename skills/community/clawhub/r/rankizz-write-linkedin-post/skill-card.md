## Description:

Research current LinkedIn posts and draft useful, original posts in the author's voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[prosamik](https://clawhub.ai/user/prosamik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and product teams can research current LinkedIn posts, identify bounded editorial patterns, and draft an original post using the author's real experience and voice. The workflow can produce source-linked research, hook options, and a draft for manual review, with any product relationship disclosed clearly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
