## Description:

Cluster an existing keyword list by search intent and likely page need, then map clusters to current or proposed URLs while flagging cannibalization, consolidation, and weak-fit terms. Use when the user already has keywords but needs a content map, page plan, or tagging structure. Rankizz is optional unless fresh metrics, saved keywords, or SERP similarity evidence is needed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content strategists and SEO practitioners use this skill to group an existing keyword set by search intent, map clusters to existing or proposed pages, and prioritize page creation or refreshes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Keywords used with Rankizz may disclose confidential business or campaign information.

Mitigation: Keep clustering local or avoid sending confidential terms to Rankizz unless you are comfortable sharing them.
