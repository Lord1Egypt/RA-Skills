## Description:

诊断英文心理学论文结论或讨论部分的衔接与连贯问题，并输出结构化诊断报告。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jeoyee](https://clawhub.ai/user/jeoyee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and academic writers use this skill to review uploaded English psychology conclusion or discussion sections for topic sentences, information flow, referential clarity, and connective or hedging use. It returns a structured diagnostic report with risk levels, examples, and revision suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the fixed rubric and example-based thresholds as authoritative academic validation.

Mitigation: Present results as writing guidance and review them against the target journal, advisor, or course expectations before submission.

Risk: The default report language is Chinese, which may not match all review workflows.

Mitigation: Ask the agent for the desired output language when invoking the skill.
