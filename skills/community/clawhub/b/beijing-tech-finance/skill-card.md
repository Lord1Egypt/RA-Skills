## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tax rates, filing deadlines, deduction rules, and compliance workflows may be outdated or incomplete for a real filing or payment decision.

Mitigation: Verify current rules against official Beijing and China sources and consult a qualified accountant or legal adviser before making filings, payments, reimbursements, or statutory reports.

Risk: The local calculators use simplified assumptions and hard-coded policy values that may not reflect a company's full tax or accounting situation.

Mitigation: Treat calculator results as estimates, review all inputs and assumptions, and reconcile outputs with authoritative accounting records and professional calculations.

Risk: The budget monitor can generate alerts from simulated actual expense data rather than verified finance-system data.

Mitigation: Connect the monitor to validated ledger, ERP, or finance API data before using alerts for operational budget control.
