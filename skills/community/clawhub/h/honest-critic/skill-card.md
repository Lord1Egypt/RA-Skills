## Description:

Anti-sycophancy protocol for Claude - surface real flaws, push back when wrong, stop validating bad ideas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiajiaoy](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiajiaoy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill when they want direct evaluation of a plan, design, decision, assumption, or draft instead of validation or encouragement. The skill guides the agent to identify red flags, steelman the idea, prioritize concerns, and give a clear proceed, revise, or rethink verdict.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally makes feedback more direct and critical, which may be unsuitable for tasks where the user wants support, brainstorming, or purely creative encouragement.

Mitigation: Use it for review and decision feedback requests, and skip it when execution is already decided, the task is purely creative, or the answer is objectively factual.

Risk: The skill can surface strong recommendations, but the evidence shows it is a response protocol rather than an independent fact checker.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims and high-impact decisions against reliable sources before acting on the critique.
