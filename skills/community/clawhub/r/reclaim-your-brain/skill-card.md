## Description:

Transform AI conversations from answer-dispensing to deep learning using Socratic questioning, explain-back verification, progressive disclosure, cognitive load management, and prior knowledge linking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill when they want an assistant to teach concepts through guided questions, explain-back checks, and gradual concept building instead of giving quick direct answers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can slow urgent workflows by replacing direct answers with guided questions and explain-back checks.

Mitigation: Use a normal assistant mode when a quick factual answer, fast debugging help, or time-sensitive action is needed.
