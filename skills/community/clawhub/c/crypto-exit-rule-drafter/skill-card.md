## Description:

A rule-drafting skill that helps users define exit conditions in advance for their crypto positions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn user-provided crypto position details, thesis, and risk triggers into written exit rules before emotion-driven decisions arise.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask users to share crypto position details.

Mitigation: Share only the position information needed to draft exit rules and avoid unnecessary account, wallet, or identifying details.

Risk: Drafted exit rules could be mistaken for automated trading or financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as written planning guidance; independently review rules before acting and do not assume the skill can execute trades or fetch live market data.
