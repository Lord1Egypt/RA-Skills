## Description:

Case Brief Drafter turns a single judicial opinion into a structured IRAC-format case brief with facts, procedural posture, issues, rules, holding, separate-opinion summaries, and unresolved-information flags.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[archlab-space](https://clawhub.ai/user/archlab-space)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External law students, paralegals, and junior associates use this skill to transform a supplied judicial opinion into a draft IRAC case brief for class preparation, research memos, moot court preparation, or matter-specific reading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on a draft brief as legal advice or as a substitute for the official opinion.

Mitigation: Label outputs as drafts and require users to verify quotations, citations, and legal conclusions against the official opinion before relying on them.

Risk: Matter-specific use may expose confidential client identifiers or sensitive facts.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting confidential client identifiers unless appropriate, and redact names or identifiers before supplying matter-related material.

Risk: Incorrect quotations, citations, or holdings could mislead study or legal research work.

Mitigation: Use the supplied opinion as the source, require source confirmation before drafting, quote only verifiable text, and flag unresolved citation or opinion gaps.
