## Description:

Creates a Chinese-language symbolic destiny report that combines Chinese metaphysics, European esotericism, and New Age frameworks when the user explicitly invokes the skill.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users invoke this skill for entertainment-oriented, symbolic Chinese destiny readings that synthesize Chinese metaphysics, European esotericism, tarot, numerology, and New Age framing. The skill is intended for explicitly requested symbolic reports, not factual advice, psychological diagnosis, or professional decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide sensitive personal details such as family, relationship, photo, health, legal, or financial information for the reading.

Mitigation: Ask users to share only the minimum details needed for the symbolic report and avoid unnecessary sensitive information.

Risk: The skill may produce confident symbolic interpretations about health, legal, investment, crisis, safety, or other high-stakes topics.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as symbolic analysis and direct users not to treat them as professional advice or a substitute for qualified judgment.
