## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill relies on web results for market analysis, so business or investment conclusions may be incorrect, stale, or based on weak sources.

Mitigation: Verify important conclusions against primary sources, current reports, and authoritative market data before using them for decisions.

Risk: Broad market-analysis triggers may activate the skill when a user asks general industry research questions.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested industry, analysis purpose, and focus areas before producing the full research output.
