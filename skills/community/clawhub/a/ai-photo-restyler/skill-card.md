## Description:

Turn a photo into anime, manga, comic, cartoon, watercolor, clay, or 3D character art while the person, pet, or product stays recognisable. This AI photo restyler works as a photo-to-anime converter and AI cartoonizer you can steer: apply a style like a filter over your own picture, or add style samples to steer the palette, line weight, and shading. A selfie, portrait, pet photo, product shot, or travel picture becomes illustration-style art for social avatars, profile pictures, sticker sets, posters, merchandise, and content series, with one chosen look repeated across a whole batch.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
