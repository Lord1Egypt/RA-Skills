## Description:

Build 13-week cash flow forecasts, identify liquidity gaps, and design capital allocation plans for ecommerce businesses navigating inventory cycles and seasonal demand.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce founders, operators, and finance teams use this skill to build 13-week rolling cash flow forecasts, stress-test base, optimistic, and pessimistic scenarios, and plan inventory financing and capital allocation decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used with confidential ecommerce financial data, including revenue, bank balances, supplier terms, payroll, advertising spend, and inventory plans.

Mitigation: Limit inputs to the data needed for the forecast, keep completed forecasts in approved secure systems, and share outputs only with authorized reviewers.

Risk: Forecasts and capital recommendations can be misleading when source data, settlement timelines, inventory assumptions, or scenario inputs are incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Reconcile assumptions against current bank, payment processor, accounting, advertising, and supplier records, then review the forecast before using it for financing or capital allocation decisions.
