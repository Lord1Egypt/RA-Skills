## Description:

A Chinese-language guide that helps university startup teams plan resource matching across institutional policies, platform programs, investors, industry partners, and government support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[earthwalking](https://clawhub.ai/user/earthwalking)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, university teams, and advisors use this skill to produce a resource connection plan for university entrepreneurship support, platform programs, investment channels, industry partners, and government resources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Policy, funding, tax, or institutional details may be outdated, jurisdiction-specific, or different across universities.

Mitigation: Verify those details with official school or government sources before acting on the generated resource plan.
