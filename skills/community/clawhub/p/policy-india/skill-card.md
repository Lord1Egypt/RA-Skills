## Use Case:

External trade teams, cross-border ecommerce operators, and policy researchers use this skill to query India market policy, regulations, tariff context, import restrictions, and compliance considerations from product names or HS codes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trade policy, tariff, import restriction, and compliance answers may be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Treat responses as informational and verify important decisions against official Indian government sources before acting.

Risk: Broad tariff queries and unclear calibration or language-selection behavior can produce answers that are too general for a specific product or HS code.

Mitigation: Provide a precise product name or HS code and review the returned policy basis, license requirements, special provisions, and risk notes.
