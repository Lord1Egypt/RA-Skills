## Description:

Turns topic briefs and research materials into evidence-backed deep-dive articles for technology and enterprise audiences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Industry researchers, analysts, and technology writers use it to turn a topic brief, event, vendor case, policy change, or research corpus into a single deep-dive article. It supports source verification, editorial planning, drafting, review, and final evidence packaging.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search queries may disclose confidential or unpublished material.

Mitigation: Authorize external searches before use, and remove or generalize sensitive details in queries.

Risk: The security review is incomplete because some references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the skill and its referenced materials before use, especially when handling sensitive research inputs.
