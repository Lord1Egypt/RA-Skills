Plan creator partnerships across TikTok, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Instagram, YouTube, or similar channels. Use when a team needs creator mix recommendations, outreach logic, brief structure, compensation options, collaboration timeline, or risk controls without live creator marketplace or attribution platform access.

## Use Case:

External brand, ecommerce, and creator marketing operators use this skill to turn campaign goals into actionable creator collaboration plans. It helps structure creator mix, outreach, compensation, briefs, timelines, measurement, and risk controls without claiming live creator data or attribution access.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides heuristic planning and does not access live creator rosters, audience analytics, attribution systems, or legal systems.

Mitigation: Users should validate creator fit, performance assumptions, compensation, disclosure, contracts, and attribution decisions with current business data and human review.

Risk: Creator plans can overstate expected reach, sales, or ROAS when campaign context, audience fit, or product differentiation is weak.

Mitigation: Treat the plan as an operating draft, keep outcome claims conservative, and review measurement logic before committing spend.
