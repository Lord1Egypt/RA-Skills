## Description:

Draft, schedule and publish social media posts with FoPost across X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, Bluesky, Pinterest, Reddit, Mastodon, Discord, Slack, Telegram, Google Business and more, upload media, run the posting queue, and read analytics. Use when asked to post, schedule, queue, cross-post, check a social account or report on social performance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and social media managers use FoPost to draft, schedule, publish, and monitor content across connected social networks. The agent prepares drafts for review, checks them against target networks, and reports fetched analytics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes account-following and Google Business profile-editing capabilities beyond its stated posting and analytics purpose.

Mitigation: Use those commands only when explicitly intended, and review profile changes before applying them; obtain approval before making public changes.
