## Description:

Organizes public Zhangyue/iReader book library, membership, help, and activity page information into compliant summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to summarize public Zhangyue/iReader website content, including catalog entries, rankings, membership benefits, FAQ/help pages, activity pages, and copyright notices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Requests may try to use the skill for piracy, membership sharing, account sharing, access bypass, or non-public account information.

Mitigation: Decline those requests and limit outputs to publicly visible Zhangyue/iReader pages, help information, and lawful summaries.

Risk: Membership benefits, download rules, regional availability, and activity terms may change over time or vary by region.

Mitigation: State the page context and timing used for the summary, and advise verification against the current public Zhangyue/iReader page before acting on terms.

Risk: Using the skill on personal account pages could expose private bookshelf, purchase, reading-history, or account data.

Mitigation: Do not use the skill with logged-in personal pages or private account records; restrict collection and summaries to public pages.
