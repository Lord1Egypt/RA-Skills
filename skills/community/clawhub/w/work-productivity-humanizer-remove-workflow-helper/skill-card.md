## Description:

Helps agent users and skill teams turn a concrete need into a practical workflow or artifact, then check it against stated success criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn a stated work or productivity need into a practical plan, reusable checklist, analysis, implementation, or other requested artifact, and review it against the user's success criteria.

Global
