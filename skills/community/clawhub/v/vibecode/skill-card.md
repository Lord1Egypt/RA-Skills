## Description:

Build iPhone apps and web apps with Vibecode, then put them online, on the user's iPhone or in the App Store. Use when the user wants to build, make or create an app (an iPhone or iOS app, a mobile app, a game, a tracker, a website or a web app), change an app they built in Vibecode, check on a build, read an app's logs or code, deploy it, install it on their iPhone, or publish it to the App Store.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
