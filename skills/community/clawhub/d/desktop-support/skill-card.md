## Description:

Professional IT Support Career Roadmap Platform that generates personalized learning paths for Desktop Support Engineers based on experience, skills, and career goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd](https://clawhub.ai/user/krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

IT support professionals, managers, and training teams use this skill to generate personalized desktop support career roadmaps from experience, skills, and career goals. Agents can use it to request phased learning paths, skill gaps, certification suggestions, and recommended resources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career assessment details, session IDs, timestamps, and optional user IDs may be sent to a third-party API.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive personal, employer-confidential, credential, or access information unless the provider and its data-handling practices are trusted.

Risk: Generated career roadmaps may be incomplete or misaligned with an employer's role requirements.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current job expectations, approved training paths, and certification requirements before relying on them.
