## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated persona-style skills can speak as real people in first person without enough disclosure or consent.

Mitigation: Review generated skills before enabling them, especially for living people; require clear AI disclosure, third-person analytical framing, and consent-aware boundaries where appropriate.

Risk: Persistent persona-style skills can embed incorrect or misleading representations into future agent behavior.

Mitigation: Scan and manually review generated SKILL.md files before deployment, with particular attention to source quality, boundaries, and claims about living people.
