This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Designers, developers, and product teams use this skill to turn websites, screenshots, and existing documentation into structured style guides and design system documentation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive websites, authenticated URLs, intranet pages, or documents may be inspected when provided as source material.

Mitigation: Provide only materials intended for analysis, or confirm that inspection of sensitive content is acceptable before using the skill.

Risk: Sparse or incomplete source material can lead to inferred style-guide details or placeholders.

Mitigation: Review the generated guide before treating it as a source of truth for design or development teams.
