## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated or modified skills may contain incorrect, misleading, or unsafe instructions.

Mitigation: Review and scan generated skills before installing, publishing, or sharing them.

Risk: The workflow can write workspace files, spawn evaluation runs, and launch a local review viewer.

Mitigation: Run it only in workspaces where file changes and evaluation runs are expected, inspect generated artifacts, and stop any launched viewer process after use.
