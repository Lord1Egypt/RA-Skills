## Description:

Build a sourced Flashscore match report from covered team and player statistics, lineups, absences, momentum, box scores, point-by-point or darts feeds, and editorial coverage. Use for detailed event analysis; use multi-sport-match-research for basic fixture and score checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crawlora-org](https://clawhub.ai/user/crawlora-org)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sports analysts and fans can use the skill to assemble a sourced report for one Flashscore event, selecting covered match, player, lineup, absence, momentum, or editorial data to answer a detailed question.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Match queries are sent to the external Crawlora API.

Mitigation: Review the provider's data handling and use only queries you are comfortable sharing.
