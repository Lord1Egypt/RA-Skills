## Description:

Converts Anthropic's AI-native startup playbook into reusable agent guidance for validating, building, launching, and scaling a startup across four stages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lloyd-c137](https://clawhub.ai/user/lloyd-c137)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders and startup operators use this skill with an AI agent to identify their current startup stage and receive stage-specific validation, MVP, launch, or scale guidance. The skill provides questions, exit criteria, exercises, and transition prompts for moving through the startup lifecycle.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Third-party package installation can carry ordinary supply-chain risk.

Mitigation: Review the package source before installing and prefer a pinned or trusted installer version.

Risk: The stage-aware guidance may be applied when the user's startup stage is unclear or the conversation is unrelated.

Mitigation: Confirm the startup stage when ambiguous and keep use focused on startup-stage guidance.
