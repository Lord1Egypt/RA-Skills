## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive text or audio may be exposed if a cloud TTS provider is used.

Mitigation: Use local or offline TTS for sensitive material and review provider data-handling terms before sending content.

Risk: Incorrect sample-rate conversion, loudness targets, or timing edits can create out-of-spec dubbed audio.

Mitigation: Confirm the native sample rate, measure loudness after edits, re-normalize when timing changes, and check sync drift against the task requirements.
