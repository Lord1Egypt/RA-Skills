## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and agent users use this skill to produce Xiaohongshu note titles, cover copy, structured drafts, tag sets, topic matrices, and performance diagnostics. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing claims or platform advice may be inaccurate, overbroad, or unsuitable for a specific product category.

Mitigation: Review drafted claims before publication, especially medical, efficacy, financial, and promotional language.

Risk: The local compliance checker is a limited heuristic and does not guarantee platform approval or legal compliance.

Mitigation: Use checker warnings as a screening step, then verify final content with official platform review tools and human judgment.

Risk: Xiaohongshu policies, commercial tooling, and ranking behavior can change over time.

Mitigation: Confirm current platform rules and commercial workflows before relying on operational recommendations.
