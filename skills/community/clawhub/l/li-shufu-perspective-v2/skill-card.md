## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks the agent to speak as Li Shufu in first person, which could make generated responses appear to be actual statements from a real person.

Mitigation: Treat responses as a public-information-based simulation and make that limitation clear where user context could create confusion.

Risk: The artifact states that its research was current as of 2026-04-06, so later business conditions or facts may not be reflected.

Mitigation: Verify current facts and market conditions before relying on generated strategy guidance.
