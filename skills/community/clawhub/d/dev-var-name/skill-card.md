This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to turn Chinese or English feature and domain terms into variable, function, API, and path naming candidates across common programming naming conventions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill returns naming results in a fixed Chinese two-line format, which may not fit teams that require English labels or flexible formatting.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired language and formatting expectations before relying on the output in shared code or documentation.
