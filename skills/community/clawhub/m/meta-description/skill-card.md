## Description:

Guides optimization of meta description tags for search engines and SERP display.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, marketers, and SEO practitioners use this skill to draft and refine page-level meta descriptions with appropriate length, intent alignment, keywords, and calls to action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use local project-context files for brand voice and keywords.

Mitigation: Do not place secrets or sensitive internal information in project-context files used for generated copy.

Risk: Generated meta descriptions can be inaccurate, misleading, too long for a target locale, or misaligned with page content.

Mitigation: Review proposed copy against the actual page, target search intent, locale-specific length guidance, and brand requirements before publishing.
