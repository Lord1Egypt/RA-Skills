## Description:

Generates Chinese-language weekly ADC patent intelligence reports by reviewing WO patent publications for a specified week, filtering for relevance, extracting P025 triad details, and summarizing development signals, opportunities, and risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent intelligence, BD, and R&D teams use this skill to produce weekly ADC patent briefings, identify relevant new publications, and decide which high-relevance patents need claim comparison or follow-up review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent coverage, date ranges, or source availability may be incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Verify source coverage and publication dates before relying on the report for business decisions.

Risk: FTO, infringement, validity, and claim-comparison outputs could be mistaken for legal conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat legal-adjacent findings as research support and route consequential decisions to qualified counsel.
