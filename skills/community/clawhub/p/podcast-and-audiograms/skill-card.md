## Description:

The audio-to-social pipeline skill turns a podcast episode into short, branded, captioned clips and audiograms that drive people back to the full episode.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and social media teams use this skill to plan podcast repurposing: select retention-led moments, choose clip or audiogram formats, write hooks and CTAs, and plan a publishing cadence. The skill guides the agent's planning and briefing work while separate tools and creators produce final media assets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may handle episode materials, transcripts, retention analytics, and brand guidance that can contain sensitive or proprietary information.

Mitigation: Install and use it only when users are comfortable providing those materials to the agent, and limit inputs to content needed for the repurposing plan.

Risk: Scheduled posts can misstate content, overpromise virality, omit required captions, or publish before review.

Mitigation: Review planned posts, hooks, CTAs, captions, and schedules before publishing; treat retention-based selection as guidance, not a guarantee.

Risk: Repurposing copyrighted podcasts without ownership or permission can create infringement risk.

Mitigation: Use only episodes the user owns or has permission to repurpose, and avoid clipping third-party podcasts for a brand account.
