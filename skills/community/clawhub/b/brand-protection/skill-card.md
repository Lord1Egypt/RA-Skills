## Description:

Guides agents through collecting evidence, choosing reporting channels, and planning prevention steps for brand impersonation, fake websites, phishing sites, trademark infringement, and domain squatting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this skill to organize brand-abuse evidence, draft reports to registrars, hosts, search providers, and platforms, and prioritize prevention steps for official-site verification and user education.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mistaken abuse reports can harm unrelated sites or services.

Mitigation: Review all evidence, legal claims, URLs, and recipients before submitting any report.

Risk: Trademark and DMCA paths may require confirmed legal rights or ownership.

Mitigation: Confirm registered trademark status, copyright ownership, and the correct reporting channel before making legal claims.

Risk: Registrar, hosting, or CDN attribution can be wrong if evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Collect current URLs, screenshots, WHOIS data, hosting information, and origin evidence before reporting.
