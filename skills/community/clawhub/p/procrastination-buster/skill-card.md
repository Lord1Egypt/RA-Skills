## Description:

Beat procrastination with task breakdown, 2-minute starts, and accountability tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to break avoided tasks into startable steps, identify blockers, create short commitments, and track progress locally.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can help record commitments, blockers, and progress locally, which may include personal productivity details.

Mitigation: Installers should be comfortable with local tracking and review what task history or memory the agent keeps.

Risk: Broad procrastination-related trigger phrases may activate the skill during unrelated work and distract the user.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger phrases if accidental activation would be disruptive.
