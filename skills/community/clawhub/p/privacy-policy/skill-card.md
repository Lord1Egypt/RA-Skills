## Description:

Privacy Policy helps agents generate privacy policy drafts for websites and apps, including GDPR- and CCPA-oriented versions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and site or app operators use this skill to draft privacy policy text and GDPR/CCPA-oriented variants for review before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated privacy policies may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific jurisdiction or business model.

Mitigation: Treat generated policy text as a draft and have qualified legal counsel review it before publication.

Risk: The bundled security utility persistently logs command inputs and includes weak security helper behavior.

Mitigation: Review the package before installation, avoid passing sensitive values to its commands, and do not rely on it for scanning, encryption, hashing sensitive values, or password generation.
