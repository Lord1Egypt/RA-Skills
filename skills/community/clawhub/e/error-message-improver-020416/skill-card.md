## Description:

Helps developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users turn vague error messages into clear explanations of what failed, why it failed, and what action to take next.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

## Use Case:

Developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and end users use this skill to rewrite or plan clearer error messages, troubleshooting workflows, checklists, and implementation support that make failures actionable.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad support or debugging phrasing when the task is not specifically about error-message wording or troubleshooting guidance.

Mitigation: Explicitly invoke a more specific skill for unrelated debugging, support, or productivity tasks.
