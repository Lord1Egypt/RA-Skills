## Description:

Creates recurring talking-head videos from an authorized founder or expert portrait, voice, and script.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, experts, and their teams use the skill to produce recurring spokesperson videos from authorized likenesses and scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment reports broad shared credentials with spending and music permissions.

Mitigation: Review the requested permissions before connecting, and check the quoted costs before approving paid generation steps.

Risk: The security assessment reports persistent installation telemetry and uninstall behavior that may affect shared connection state beyond this skill.

Mitigation: Review the uninstall guidance before removal because the connection is shared across Beatra skills.

Risk: The security assessment reports missing privacy disclosures for content sent to Beatra and says scan coverage was partial.

Mitigation: Only upload portraits, voice recordings, and scripts you are authorized and comfortable sending to Beatra.
