## Description:

ADHD Assistant provides ADHD-adapted support for daily planning, task breakdown, time management, prioritization, body doubling, motivation regulation, and emotional self-support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[modestyrichards](https://clawhub.ai/user/modestyrichards)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn ADHD-related overwhelm, procrastination, time blindness, and routine maintenance challenges into concrete plans, checklists, reminders, check-ins, and review rituals. It is intended as practical organization and emotional support, not diagnosis, treatment, therapy, or medical advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may persist sensitive ADHD status, treatment context, routines, triggers, or productivity patterns across sessions.

Mitigation: Review and configure memory settings before use, avoid storing medical or mental-health details unless storage and deletion controls are explicit, and delete sensitive saved context when it is no longer needed.

Risk: Users may mistake organization support, emotional validation, or ADHD-related framing for clinical diagnosis or treatment.

Mitigation: Use the skill for practical planning and self-management support only, and refer medical, psychiatric, therapy, medication, crisis, or self-harm concerns to qualified professionals or appropriate regional crisis resources.
