## Description:

Guides agents in identifying continuation chart patterns such as triangles, flags, pennants, wedges, rectangles, broadening formations, and diamonds, with boundaries for when to defer to reversal-pattern or trend-tool skills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill as educational support for technical-analysis questions about whether observed chart consolidations are likely continuation patterns or possible reversal warnings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake chart-pattern interpretation for financial advice or an automated trading signal.

Mitigation: Treat the skill's output as educational support and review any market decision independently before acting.

Risk: Continuation and reversal patterns can be confused when the broader trend stage is unclear.

Mitigation: Use the skill's boundary guidance to check trend stage and defer reversal-pattern or trend-tool questions to the appropriate neighboring skill.
