## Use Case:

QA leads, developers, and delivery teams use this skill to design risk-based validation plans across unit, HTTP, E2E, WLS, and documentation checks before acceptance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill expects Weline project guidance files and collaboration conventions that may not exist in unrelated workspaces.

Mitigation: Use it only in workspaces where the referenced Weline files and role handoff conventions are expected.

Risk: A validation strategy can be incomplete or disproportionate when task scope, changed surfaces, runtime sensitivity, or release confidence are underspecified.

Mitigation: Provide the changed components, risk areas, runtime sensitivity, expected confidence level, and available specialist outputs before using the plan.
