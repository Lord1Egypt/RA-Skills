## Description:

Restructure pacing and story with Subclip UnifiedTimeline using cuts, splits, timing, ordering, B-roll, text beats, and audio synchronization. Use for shortening, removing sections, rearranging scenes, pacing, hooks, highlights, montage construction, or timeline cleanup.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Video editors and creators use this skill to reshape a video's pacing by cutting, ordering, and layering footage, text, captions, and audio. It supports montage construction and timeline cleanup while preserving source ranges and checking important edit points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project-library cleanup removes every timeline placement linked to a selected file.

Mitigation: Use project-library cleanup only for explicitly selected file IDs after checking all linked placements.

Risk: Timeline edits can leave gaps or an unintended blank tail.

Mitigation: Re-list edited items and inspect key cut points and the final frame after changes.
