## Description:

Eric's Engineering Wisdom routes hardware engineering questions across motor, gear, NVH, electronics, and innovation domains, then produces integrated diagnostic guidance with Eric-style engineering judgment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shun1989](https://clawhub.ai/user/shun1989)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External developers and engineering teams use this skill as a first-stop advisor for hardware product development, technical diagnosis, failure analysis, and cross-domain engineering questions. It classifies the problem domain and returns structured Markdown guidance grounded in the skill's engineering reference notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide confidential engineering history or project details for diagnosis.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential engineering information unless that disclosure is intentional and approved.

Risk: Broad routing across domains can select an incomplete or incorrect engineering lens when the prompt is ambiguous.

Mitigation: Confirm assumptions, request missing operating context, and review recommendations against project-specific data before acting.

Risk: Personal-experience framing may be mistaken for universally validated engineering guidance.

Mitigation: Treat Eric-style examples as advisory context and validate conclusions with measurements, standards, or qualified engineering review.
