## Description:

Provides Chinese labor law compliance and employment risk analysis for issues such as labor contracts, termination, arbitration, non-competes, overtime, cross-border employment, and severance calculations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

HR, legal, and operations teams use this skill as a PRC labor-law reference aid for preliminary compliance checks, employment-risk assessments, dispute response planning, and structured guidance before consulting qualified counsel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with substantive guidance focused on PRC labor law and jurisdiction-specific local rules.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Employment law is time-sensitive and jurisdiction-specific, so generated guidance may be outdated or incomplete for a local PRC rule or current figure.

Mitigation: Verify current statutes, local rules, compensation figures, and official notices before making major HR decisions.

Risk: Users may treat preliminary labor-law analysis as formal legal advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an internal reference aid and consult a licensed labor lawyer or local labor authority for major employment decisions, complex disputes, or high-value claims.
