## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes business case claims and ROI-like examples that could be over-applied to new commercial decisions.

Mitigation: Independently verify case evidence, market fit, trademark and domain availability, and financial assumptions before acting on recommendations.

Risk: The framework may be applied broadly to brand-analysis prompts even when a user needs platform-specific, legal, pricing, or contract advice.

Mitigation: Use the output as strategy guidance and route legal, pricing, contract, and other binding commercial decisions to qualified reviewers.
