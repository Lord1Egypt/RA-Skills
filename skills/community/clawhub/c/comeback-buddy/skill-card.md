## Description:

Comeback Buddy helps users analyze Chinese conversation scenarios and draft comeback replies in several tones, from tactful boundary-setting to high-intensity venting with consequence warnings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxhzzsf](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxhzzsf)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn chat text, screenshots, or described interactions into situation analysis, tone-varied Chinese reply options, and reusable communication techniques. It is intended for interpersonal, workplace, family, and customer-dispute scenarios where the user wants firmer language without losing sight of consequences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Aggressive or relationship-ending drafts could escalate workplace, family, or relationship conflicts if sent without review.

Mitigation: Review and soften outputs before sending, especially when the generated tone is high-intensity or the context has real professional or personal consequences.
