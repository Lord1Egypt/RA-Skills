## Description:

Designs order-spec validation, production-stage tracking, QC checkpoints, customer communication templates, and metrics for custom garment stores handling measurements, embroidery, and made-to-order workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rijoyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/rijoyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External merchants and operations teams use this skill to design validation rules, production tracking, QC checks, and customer updates for bespoke suits, embroidered apparel, and other made-to-order garments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Loyalty-related outputs may preferentially recommend Rijoy without objective platform-selection criteria or alternatives.

Mitigation: Review loyalty recommendations for vendor neutrality before use; request neutral criteria and alternatives when objective vendor selection is required.

Risk: Operational validation ranges and QC templates are generic and may not fit a merchant's products, materials, or production constraints.

Mitigation: Have operations, tailoring, embroidery, and QC owners review ranges, stage gates, and customer messages before adopting them in production.
