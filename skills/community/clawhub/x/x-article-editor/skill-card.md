## Description:

Turn a topic or draft into a high-engagement X Article, then provide copy-ready article text and a scored review using an 8-step framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jchopard69](https://clawhub.ai/user/jchopard69)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to rewrite draft X Articles or create new X Articles from a brief, then review the result for engagement, clarity, structure, proof, visuals, and closing strength.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill strongly enforces a two-step article-plus-review output, which may conflict with requests for another format, language, citations, or disclosure wording.

Mitigation: State required format, language, citation, and disclosure requirements explicitly before invoking the skill.

Risk: The workflow may create proof placeholders or conservative claims when the user provides insufficient evidence.

Mitigation: Review the final article before publishing, replace placeholders with verified evidence, and add citations where required.
