## Description:

A Chinese-language entertainment roleplay skill that activates a blunt, sarcastic Fengge Wangming Tianya-inspired persona driven by conversational thinking patterns and a topic knowledge base.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for Chinese-language entertainment roleplay and informal commentary in the Fengge Wangming Tianya style. It is not appropriate as a source of serious relationship, mental-health, or sensitive personal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Blunt or sarcastic persona outputs may be inappropriate outside explicit entertainment roleplay.

Mitigation: Enable the skill only for users who intentionally request this persona, and disable it if it activates outside that context.

Risk: Users may mistake abrasive roleplay commentary for serious relationship, mental-health, or sensitive personal advice.

Mitigation: Route serious or sensitive advice requests to appropriate non-roleplay guidance and avoid relying on this skill for those topics.
