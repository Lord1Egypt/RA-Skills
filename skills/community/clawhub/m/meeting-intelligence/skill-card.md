## Description:

AI-powered meeting tool that summarizes discussions, extracts prioritized action items with owners and deadlines, and drafts clear follow-up emails.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[di5cip1e](https://clawhub.ai/user/di5cip1e)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and teams use this skill to turn meeting transcripts or notes into concise summaries, decision recaps, prioritized action items, and follow-up email drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting transcripts may contain sensitive business, HR, legal, regulated customer, credential, or personal information.

Mitigation: Redact sensitive content before sharing transcripts with the agent unless organizational policy permits that use.

Risk: Sharing prior meeting history across sessions can expose more context than intended.

Mitigation: Limit shared history to the meetings and details needed for the current summary or follow-up task.
