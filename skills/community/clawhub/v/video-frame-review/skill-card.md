## Description:

Use when reviewing a local video visually with systematic timestamped frame sampling, contact sheets, selected full-dimension frames, or dense inspection of a short interval.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[theangrypit](https://clawhub.ai/user/theangrypit)

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and reviewers use this skill to inspect local video through timestamped frame samples, contact sheets, and selected full-dimension decoded frames. It documents the sampled coverage and the limits of visual inspection.
