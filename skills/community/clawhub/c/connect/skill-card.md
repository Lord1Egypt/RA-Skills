## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Memory-based cross-user matching can expose sensitive memory-derived signals if matchability, consent, revocation, or visibility are unclear.

Mitigation: Confirm which memories are matchable and require explicit consent, revocation, and cross-user visibility controls before use with real Echo or Supabase accounts.

Risk: The reviewed artifact is descriptive and does not implement or enforce the claimed privacy controls.

Mitigation: Review and test any future implementation separately before deployment, including data-access boundaries and consent behavior.
