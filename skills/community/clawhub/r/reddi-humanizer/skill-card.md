## Description:

Humanizer rewrites AI-sounding prose into more natural text by identifying patterns such as inflated significance, promotional language, vague attribution, em dash overuse, rule-of-three phrasing, and common AI vocabulary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nissan](https://clawhub.ai/user/nissan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and developers use this skill to revise generated or overly polished text while preserving meaning, facts, and the intended voice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may alter factual wording, tone, typography, or regional style while rewriting prose.

Mitigation: Review proposed diffs before accepting changes, especially where exact wording, factual precision, or house style matters.
