## Description:

道德经智慧 is a bilingual Chinese-English agent skill that applies Daodejing principles such as wu-wei, softness, dialectical thinking, non-contention, and humility to reflection, strategy, leadership, conflict, and mindset scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and developers use this skill for Daodejing-based reflection and strategy framing in leadership, management, negotiation, personal cultivation, conflict handling, and long-term planning. It is intended to provide philosophical perspective rather than professional legal, medical, safety, financial, or technical guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake philosophical framing for professional legal, medical, safety, financial, or technical advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for reflective perspective only and route professional or high-stakes questions to qualified domain guidance.

Risk: Daodejing concepts such as wu-wei, softness, or non-contention may be misapplied as passivity during urgent or high-stakes situations.

Mitigation: Follow the artifact's out-of-scope guidance and avoid using the skill for emergencies, technical incidents, legal compliance, or other situations requiring decisive expert action.

Risk: Broad philosophical analogies may overgeneralize complex workplace, leadership, or negotiation contexts.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as one interpretive lens, validate recommendations against concrete facts, and combine them with situation-specific judgment.
