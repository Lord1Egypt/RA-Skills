## Description:

A Chinese-language personal office assistant skill for drafting, polishing, summarizing, planning, and structuring everyday workplace materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Employees and individual office users use this skill to create reusable workplace text, organize meeting notes and fragmented information, draft reports and plans, and prepare structured communication materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated workplace text may be unsuitable for legal, financial, contract, performance, or external communications without review.

Mitigation: Have a qualified person review outputs before using them in sensitive or externally shared contexts.

Risk: Short Chinese office-writing requests may invoke the skill even when the user provides limited context.

Mitigation: Check that the response matches the intended task, audience, and facts before reuse.
