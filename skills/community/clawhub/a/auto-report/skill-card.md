## Description:

Automates daily, weekly, monthly, and ad-hoc report generation with scheduled delivery to chat channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Employees, operators, and teams use this skill to generate structured work, market, health, standup, and KPI reports on a schedule or on demand, then deliver them to approved chat or document destinations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scheduled reports can read local data and send summaries to chat channels, which may expose sensitive information to unintended audiences.

Mitigation: Confirm each report's data sources, target chat or document, timezone, and retention expectations before scheduling; avoid sensitive files and broad channels unless recipients are authorized.
