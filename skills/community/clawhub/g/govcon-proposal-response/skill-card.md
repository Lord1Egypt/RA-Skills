## Description:

Helps capture managers, proposal writers, business development leads, and contracts professionals draft compliant U.S. federal proposal responses with compliance matrices, technical approach sections, past performance narratives, management sections, and final review gates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users at U.S. federal contractors use this skill to turn solicitation requirements and company-provided capabilities into a draft proposal package. It supports compliance matrix construction, proposal section drafting, action-item surfacing, and proposal-manager, contracts, pricing, and legal review before submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Proposal drafts may contain inaccurate, incomplete, noncompliant, or legally sensitive content if user-provided solicitation or company data is missing or wrong.

Mitigation: Require proposal manager, contracts, pricing, and legal review before submission, and keep generated content marked as draft until all action items and placeholders are resolved.

Risk: Classified material, CUI, export-controlled information, or other sensitive procurement data may be inappropriate for the agent environment.

Mitigation: Use only information suitable for the environment, do not paste classified material or CUI into unsuitable systems, and handle controlled or classified solicitations through approved systems and procedures.
