## Description:

Generates content ideas from RSS feeds, Reddit, Hacker News, X/Twitter, and web search, with hooks, angles, formats, and content-calendar support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, founders, and operators use this skill to turn trend signals into draft content ideas for posts, articles, and weekly content calendars.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recurring daily or weekly workflows can continue running and writing files after initial setup without clear opt-in, expiry, or removal instructions.

Mitigation: Before enabling scheduled runs, confirm the schedule, timezone, output directory, data sources, expiry or retention expectations, and how to list and remove the scheduled job.

Risk: Generated ideas can reflect fast-changing or low-quality trend signals from public feeds and searches.

Mitigation: Review source relevance, engagement claims, hooks, and publication timing before using generated ideas in public content.
