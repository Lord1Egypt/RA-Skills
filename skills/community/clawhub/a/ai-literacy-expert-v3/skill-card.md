## Description:

AI Literacy Expert V3 helps teachers and students design Chinese-language AI literacy lessons, answer course questions, and generate p5.js interactive courseware for AI concepts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gqliang2026](https://clawhub.ai/user/gqliang2026)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers, students, and curriculum developers use this skill to explain AI literacy concepts, prepare lessons, create assessments, adapt examples across disciplines, and produce browser-based p5.js courseware.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated courseware may contain executable HTML and JavaScript.

Mitigation: Review generated HTML before sharing with students and validate it with browser-console checks or pinned local tools.

Risk: External IMA or CDN links and generated demos involving cameras, microphones, uploads, localStorage, rules, hooks, or automation may create privacy or operational risk.

Mitigation: Prefer synthetic data and local or pinned validation paths; avoid sensitive student data and disclose any browser features or external dependencies before classroom use.
