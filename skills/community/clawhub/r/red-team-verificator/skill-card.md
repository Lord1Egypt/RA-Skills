## Description:

Red Team Verificator audits startup strategy and red-team analyses, focusing on factual errors, hallucinations, contradictions, and distorted evidence in Russian-market assumptions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[reactlab23](https://clawhub.ai/user/reactlab23)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, product strategists, and startup reviewers use this skill to pressure-test proposed startup analyses before committing engineering effort. It produces a skeptical verification report that checks claims, unit economics, timelines, legal constraints, market assumptions, and the reliability of red-team critiques.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intentionally hard-edged and may produce blunt strategic judgments that overstate uncertainty or reject viable ideas.

Mitigation: Review outputs as decision support, verify cited facts independently, and compare the result with primary market, legal, and financial evidence before acting.

Risk: The artifact focuses on Russian-market startup assumptions and may not transfer cleanly to other jurisdictions or market contexts.

Mitigation: Use it for Russian-market analysis unless reviewers adapt the legal, payment, competitive, and compliance checks for the target market.
