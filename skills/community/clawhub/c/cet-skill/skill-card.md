## Description:

CET Skill helps Chinese learners prepare for CET-4 and CET-6 with exam-style writing, translation, reading, listening, feedback, and study planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liuxiangjian-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/liuxiangjian-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners preparing for China's CET-4 or CET-6 use this skill to generate realistic practice tasks, receive diagnostic feedback, and plan targeted study across writing, translation, reading, and listening.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for exam level, current score, target score, exam date, weak section, and study availability to personalize coaching.

Mitigation: Share only the exam-preparation details needed for the session and avoid providing unnecessary personal information.

Risk: The skill is Chinese-first by default, which may not match every user's preferred language.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for English when English-language interaction or feedback is preferred.
