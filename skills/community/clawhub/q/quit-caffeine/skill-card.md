## Description:

Reduce or quit caffeine with withdrawal tracking, tapering plans, and energy milestones.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jhillin8](https://clawhub.ai/user/jhillin8)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to reduce or quit caffeine by logging intake, creating tapering plans, tracking withdrawal symptoms, and finding non-caffeine energy and sleep strategies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Caffeine intake, symptoms, sleep quality, mood, and energy logs can contain personal wellness data.

Mitigation: Keep logs local where possible, avoid sharing sensitive details unnecessarily, and remove personal data before exporting or posting progress records.

Risk: Withdrawal and tapering guidance may be mistaken for medical advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as wellness support only and rely on a qualified professional for health concerns or severe withdrawal symptoms.

Risk: Generated tapering schedules and withdrawal timelines may not fit every user's health situation.

Mitigation: Review plans before following them and adjust based on individual tolerance, health needs, and professional guidance.
