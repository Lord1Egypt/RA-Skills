## Description:

Compare India's new and old income tax regimes for Tax Year 2026–27 and estimate tax, rebates, surcharge, cess, HRA exemption, monthly TDS, and break-even deductions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sanketbhatia](https://clawhub.ai/user/sanketbhatia)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals in India can use the skill to estimate salary and other ordinary income tax under both regimes, compare monthly TDS, and see how deductions affect the comparison. It is intended for resident individuals and provides estimates to confirm with the official tax portal or a CA.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

India

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tax estimates may be outdated or omit circumstances outside the calculator's supported scope.

Mitigation: Treat results as estimates, confirm them with the Income Tax Department portal or a CA, and consult a CA for unsupported income types or taxpayer categories.

Risk: Incorrect income or deduction inputs can produce misleading regime comparisons.

Mitigation: Check yearly rupee amounts and assumptions, and review the calculator's supported inputs before relying on its comparison.
