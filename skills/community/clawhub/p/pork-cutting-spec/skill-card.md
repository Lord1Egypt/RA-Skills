## Use Case:

External users, slaughterhouse operators, meat companies, and meat-processing teams use this skill for Chinese-language guidance on standardized pork cutting, including hot and cold cutting workflows, product separation, quality checks, labeling, packaging, storage, and transport. The guidance is training support and should be applied with local SOPs, legal requirements, PPE, sanitation controls, machine guarding, lockout/tagout, and qualified onsite supervision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat pork-cutting procedures as standalone workplace safety instructions.

Mitigation: Use the skill as training support only and follow employer SOPs, required PPE, machine guarding, sanitation, lockout/tagout, legal requirements, and qualified onsite supervision before cutting, sawing, packaging, storage, or transport work.

Risk: Guidance is static Chinese-language standards content and may not cover every site-specific or jurisdiction-specific requirement.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable local standards, inspection requirements, hygiene controls, and facility procedures before applying the guidance operationally.
