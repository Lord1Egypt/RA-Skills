## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance for finding official courier hotlines, choosing complaint or escalation paths, and preparing next steps for common delivery issues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jvy](https://clawhub.ai/user/jvy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and support agents use this skill to answer Chinese courier service questions, find official contact channels, and prepare practical action checklists for delays, missing deliveries, damage, loss, returns, or suspected fraud.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Courier phone numbers, service hours, complaint paths, and platform rules can change over time.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive details on the carrier's official site, app, or hotline before relying on them.

Risk: Compensation amounts and responsibility for delivery disputes can depend on current carrier terms, platform rules, and local law.

Mitigation: Use the skill for practical preparation and evidence collection, then confirm formal claims against official terms or qualified advice.

Risk: Suspected courier fraud can involve links, verification codes, payment requests, or remote-control apps.

Mitigation: Use only official carrier channels for verification and avoid sharing codes, payment credentials, or screen access.
