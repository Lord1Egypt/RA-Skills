## Description:

Helps users turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning through repeatable workflows, checklists, templates, and lightweight analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It helps convert product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, and decision-support artifacts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger terms and implicit invocation may activate the skill for general product or startup discussions.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation or narrow the trigger phrases when precise routing is required.

Risk: Planning outputs can be incomplete if the user provides limited market, audience, or constraint information.

Mitigation: Review assumptions, confirm success criteria, and validate recommendations with real user evidence before committing significant resources.
