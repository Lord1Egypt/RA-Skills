## Use Case:

External accounting and finance users use this Chinese-language skill to generate bookkeeping entries, review vouchers, manage invoices, reconcile bank records, calculate tax, prepare financial reports, and automate spreadsheet-based accounting workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Accounting workflows can involve sensitive tax IDs, bank statements, invoices, ledgers, and production financial records.

Mitigation: Redact or mask unnecessary sensitive data before use and confirm proposed changes before applying them to production financial records.

Risk: Generated accounting entries, tax calculations, reconciliations, and spreadsheet formulas may be incorrect or incomplete for a specific organization or jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer verify outputs against source records, applicable accounting policies, and current tax requirements before filing or posting.
