## Description:

Guide for reading, interpreting, and applying statutes, regulations, and rules in legal and compliance contexts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, legal operations teams, compliance teams, and developers use this skill to structure statutory and regulatory interpretation, extract requirements from legal text, and compare obligations across jurisdictions. It supports analysis workflows but does not replace review by qualified legal counsel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat statutory interpretation output as authoritative legal advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an aid for understanding statutes, verify current law, and consult qualified counsel for decisions with legal consequences.

Risk: Applicability conclusions can be wrong when user facts, jurisdiction, effective dates, amendments, or implementing regulations are incomplete.

Mitigation: State factual assumptions, identify missing information, check effective dates and amendments, and review implementing regulations before drawing conclusions.

Risk: Cross-jurisdictional requirement extraction can miss exemptions, preemption, enforcement differences, or unusual thresholds.

Mitigation: Separate applicability, exemptions, enforcement mechanisms, and requirement types, and compare jurisdictions explicitly instead of relying on a single baseline.
