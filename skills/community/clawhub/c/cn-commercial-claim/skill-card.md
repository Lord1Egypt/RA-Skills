## Description:

Provides Mainland China commercial auto-insurance claim guidance based on the China Insurance Association's 2020 trial claims-practice guidance, covering reporting, inspection, damage assessment, human-injury handling, payout calculation, claim review, required materials, and refusal-risk awareness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External vehicle owners in Mainland China use this skill to understand commercial auto-insurance claim workflows, required claim materials, payout formulas, claim timing, and common refusal risks. It is informational guidance and not a substitute for an insurer's decision, policy terms, or professional legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mainland China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake informational claims guidance for legal advice or an insurer's official coverage decision.

Mitigation: State that outputs are informational, and direct users to verify policy terms, coverage decisions, and disputes with the insurer or a qualified legal professional.

Risk: Claim-material checklists include sensitive identity, banking, and medical documents.

Mitigation: Advise users not to paste full documents or unnecessary personal data into chat and to submit required documents through official, secure insurer channels.

Risk: Insurance limits, procedures, and regulatory expectations may change after the referenced 2020 trial guidance.

Mitigation: Encourage users to confirm current limits, forms, and claim procedures against their policy, insurer notices, and current official sources.
