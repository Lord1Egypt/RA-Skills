## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking users through a multi-round screening of early-stage OPC (one-person company) projects, covering team fit, market validation, technical feasibility, business model, financial health, incubation recommendations, and priority ranking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External founders, startup advisors, and incubator reviewers use this skill to facilitate structured conversations about whether an early-stage one-person-company project is viable and ready for incubation. It is intended for advisory project screening, not as financial or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-read the startup screening conclusions as financial or investment advice.

Mitigation: Frame conclusions as advisory project-screening guidance and encourage independent validation before funding, investment, or launch decisions.

Risk: The skill may activate for broader startup feasibility requests beyond OPC project screening.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants an OPC or early-startup screening conversation before applying the full five-round workflow.
