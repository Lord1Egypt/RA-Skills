## Description:

Guides a TRIZ-based patent avoidance workflow that reconstructs patent function models, performs trimming and function-oriented search, generates non-equivalent alternatives, and supports infringement analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Patent, product, and engineering teams use this skill to analyze uploaded patents or patent numbers, define application requirements, and generate alternative technical designs intended to avoid claim coverage. The skill also structures infringement-support analysis, but final legal conclusions should be reviewed by a patent professional.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may rely on PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP access for patent-data retrieval.

Mitigation: Confirm the MCP authorization and data-access scope before installing or using the skill.

Risk: Generated infringement conclusions may be mistaken or incomplete if treated as final legal advice.

Mitigation: Use the conclusions as drafting support and have a qualified patent professional review final judgments.
