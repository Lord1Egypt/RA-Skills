## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use Name Analyzer to generate entertainment-oriented name meaning, origin, numerology, compatibility, and scoring reports for Chinese and English names.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Numerology, personality, compatibility, and scoring outputs can be mistaken for professional advice or deterministic assessment.

Mitigation: Present results as entertainment and cultural context only, and avoid using them for legal, hiring, identity, medical, financial, or other consequential decisions.

Risk: The skill is oriented toward Chinese name analysis and may provide incomplete or culturally narrow coverage for some names.

Mitigation: State the cultural scope of the analysis and treat unsupported or low-confidence names as limited coverage rather than authoritative results.
