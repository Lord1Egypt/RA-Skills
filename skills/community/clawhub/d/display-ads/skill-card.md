## Description:

Guides agents in planning display, banner, ad network, programmatic display, native ad, and retargeting display campaigns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Marketing practitioners and agents use this skill to structure display advertising plans, choose formats and buying options, define common metrics, prepare creative requirements, and set attribution conventions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may suggest campaign tactics or attribution conventions that are unsuitable for a specific business, jurisdiction, audience, or ad platform policy.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current advertising policies, privacy obligations, budget limits, and brand standards before launching campaigns.

Risk: The skill provides planning guidance but does not operate ad platforms or verify live campaign setup.

Mitigation: Use authorized ad platform workflows and human approval to configure, launch, and monitor campaigns.
