## Description:

Guides agents in creating article body content such as blog post drafts, long-form guides, listicles, introductions, body sections, conclusions, and CTA copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and content teams use this skill to draft or optimize article body copy for blog posts, how-to guides, listicles, and other long-form content. It helps structure hooks, sections, takeaways, citations, product connections, and conclusions for SEO-oriented publishing workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated statistics, SEO claims, or citations may be inaccurate or unsupported.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims, statistics, and citations against authoritative sources before publishing.

Risk: Article drafts may be generic or lack original information gain.

Mitigation: Add subject-matter expertise, fresh evidence, proprietary data, or a concrete counter-narrative during editorial review.
