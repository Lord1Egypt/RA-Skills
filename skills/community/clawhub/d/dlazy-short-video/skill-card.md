## Description:

Creates a 15–25 second vertical social video from a hook-first storyboard, with generated shots, voiceover, and subtitles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and social media teams use this skill to turn a prompt and optional reference files into short vertical videos for TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Douyin.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached files are sent to dLazy's hosted service and media storage; the security review is partial with medium confidence.

Mitigation: Review dLazy's service terms and the CLI install and package details, and avoid sending sensitive prompts or files unless your organization approves that use.
