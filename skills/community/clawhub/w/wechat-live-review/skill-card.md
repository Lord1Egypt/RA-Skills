## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language review framework for WeChat Video Account live commerce that diagnoses private-domain traffic, social sharing, product cadence, audience fit, and seven-day follow-up trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External live-commerce operators and analysts use this skill to produce structured post-live reviews for WeChat Video Account sessions, with emphasis on private-domain import, social amplification, product pacing, audience matching, and seven-day retention or repurchase signals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include personal contact lists, individual chat records, raw customer identifiers, or other personally identifiable data when asking for live-commerce review.

Mitigation: Use aggregated, authorized business metrics and avoid pasting personal or customer-identifying information.

Risk: The review framework may be misapplied to unsupported live-room formats such as pure paid-traffic rooms, very new accounts, Douyin-style simulcasts, or entertainment livestreams.

Mitigation: Check the documented applicability boundaries before relying on the recommendations.
