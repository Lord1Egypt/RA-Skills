## Description:

Turn product specifications, manuals, catalogs, or ecommerce listings into conversion-focused shopping videos with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers and marketing teams use this skill to turn product information or marketplace listings into shopping and advertising videos for cross-border sales.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the CLI involves signing in and may list projects in the user's dLazy organization; the security review says some access details were not fully reviewed.

Mitigation: Before installation or use, confirm this access fits the task and review the full skill and CLI installation details.

Risk: Files attached to a chat are uploaded to dLazy media storage for processing.

Mitigation: Attach only files that are appropriate to send to the hosted service.
