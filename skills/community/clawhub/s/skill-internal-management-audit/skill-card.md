## Description:

Guides quality-management internal audits through four structured stages: audit program, implementation plan, departmental checklist, and audit report, with ISO 9001/IATF 16949-oriented prompts and printable txt plus Markdown outputs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Quality managers, internal auditors, and operational teams use this skill to plan and document internal management-system audits. It is intended to help prepare audit programs, schedules, departmental checklists, and reports while leaving audit judgments, approvals, and signatures to qualified company personnel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated audit reports or checklists may be incomplete, misleading, or unsuitable for formal use if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Require qualified company personnel to review generated documents and retain responsibility for audit judgments, approvals, and signatures.

Risk: The artifact references helper files that are absent from the package, which may reduce the reliability of staged prompts or generated document structure.

Mitigation: Confirm required reference material is available before use, or supply missing audit criteria, checklist guidance, and nonconformance classification rules manually.

Risk: Uploaded quality manuals, procedures, and audit records may contain sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Provide only files appropriate for the agent environment and follow the organization's document-handling and retention requirements.
