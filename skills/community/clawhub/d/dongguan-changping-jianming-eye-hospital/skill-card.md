## Description:

A Chinese-language reference pack for Dongguan Changping Jianming Eye Hospital that helps agents answer grounded questions about hospital facts, doctors, specialties, outpatient schedule patterns, and FAQs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, content teams, and developers can use this skill as a grounded Chinese-language source for retrieval, question answering, knowledge-base assembly, and factual summaries about Dongguan Changping Jianming Eye Hospital. It is not intended to provide individualized medical advice or replace official hospital communications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with content focused on Dongguan, Guangdong, China.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Doctor rosters, practice-validity dates, and clinic schedules may become stale.

Mitigation: Verify current details through the hospital website, official WeChat account, or direct hospital contact before patient-facing or operational use.

Risk: Hospital reference material may be mistaken for individualized medical advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for public-information retrieval and factual summaries only, and refer medical decisions to qualified clinicians and official hospital channels.
