## Description:

PLC代码分析与审查工具包。12个维度系统化分析InoProShop/CoDeSys ST语言PLC代码，输出结构化报告和问题清单。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

PLC developers, controls engineers, and reviewers use this skill to analyze InoProShop or CoDeSys Structured Text PLC files, identify safety, logic, signal-mapping, state-machine, and maintainability issues, and produce prioritized review reports with concrete modification suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: PLC safety-interlock findings or proposed code changes may be incomplete or unsuitable for production machinery.

Mitigation: Have a qualified controls or safety engineer validate safety-interlock findings and proposed changes before relying on the output for production safety.
