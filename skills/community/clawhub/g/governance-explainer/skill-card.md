## Description:

Explain DAO governance rules and implications clearly; translate constraints into allowed/forbidden actions with consequences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mzfshark](https://clawhub.ai/user/mzfshark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

DAO contributors, operators, and reviewers use this skill to translate governance rules and proposed actions into plain-language constraints, allowed actions, forbidden actions, prerequisites, and consequences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked for broad policy or permission questions outside DAO governance because some trigger phrases are generic.

Mitigation: Use it for DAO governance interpretation and narrow prompts to the provided governance rule and situation; do not rely on it for general legal, compliance, or permission advice.

Risk: Ambiguous governance text can lead to overconfident interpretation.

Mitigation: Treat ambiguous cases conservatively, distinguish explicit rule text from interpretation, and request the minimum clarifying information before advising on allowed or forbidden actions.
