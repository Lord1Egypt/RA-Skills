## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and agents use this skill to draft common professional emails, including cold outreach, follow-ups, apologies, introductions, polite declines, and subject-line ideas.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated emails may include generic placeholders or claims that are not tailored to the recipient.

Mitigation: Review and personalize templates before sending, and verify any factual claims about the recipient, company, dates, remedies, or offers.

Risk: Estimated open rates are generic writing guidance rather than verified marketing performance data.

Mitigation: Do not use estimated open rates for forecasting, guarantees, or compliance decisions without separate campaign data and review.
