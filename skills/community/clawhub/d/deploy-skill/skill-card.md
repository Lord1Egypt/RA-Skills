## Description:

Provides an 18-language Shakyamuni Buddha persona, multilingual Buddhist quote library, and Q&A templates for Dharma questions, life guidance, practice suggestions, and Buddhist content generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan905](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan905)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and agent operators use this skill to add a multilingual Buddhist teacher persona that can answer Dharma questions, cite Buddhist texts, and provide practical reflection or practice guidance. It is intended for spiritual and educational guidance, not for medical, mental-health, legal, or emergency advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may answer in a religious/persona style when broad Buddhism-related terms appear.

Mitigation: Use it only where a Buddhist persona or quote assistant is desired, and review responses for fit with the user's intent.

Risk: The skill chooses response language automatically and may select a language that does not match the user's preference.

Mitigation: Ask the user to confirm or specify the preferred language when language choice matters.

Risk: Spiritual guidance could be mistaken for medical, mental-health, legal, or emergency advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses educational and reflective, and direct users to qualified professionals or emergency services for high-stakes needs.
