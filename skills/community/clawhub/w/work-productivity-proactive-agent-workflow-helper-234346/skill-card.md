## Description:

Helps AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn proactive-agent productivity needs into practical workflows, checklists, analysis, code changes, or decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to structure proactive-agent style productivity work: clarifying goals, planning practical local-friendly workflows, producing the requested artifact, and checking the result against success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate too broadly for general workflow, task, or bug-fix requests.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation when the proactive-agent workflow helper is intended.

Risk: Workflow guidance can be too general if the user's goal, constraints, inputs, or success criteria are unclear.

Mitigation: Restate the requested outcome and assumptions, ask only for missing information that materially changes the output, and validate the result against stated success criteria.
