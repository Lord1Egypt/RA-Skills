## Use Case:

Developers, operators, and professional users use this skill to guide agents through domain-specific workflows for engineering, marketing, sales, finance, legal operations, product management, customer operations, research, and content creation. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide broad professional automation involving command execution, external searches, file writes, persistent memory, archive moves, and report delivery.

Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before those actions and constrain allowed paths, recipients, retention, and review steps.

Risk: The skill covers finance, customer, legal, and personal-data workflows where incorrect handling can create material business or privacy risk.

Mitigation: Use only reviewed inputs and require human review before acting on sensitive data, sending reports, or making operational decisions.
