## Description:

Cn Smart Email is a Chinese-language helper that classifies pasted email text and generates simple reply templates using only the Python standard library.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[freedompixels](https://clawhub.ai/user/freedompixels)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers can use this skill to classify Chinese email content as work, personal, notification, advertising, or other, and to draft formal, casual, or brief reply templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat generated reply templates as ready-to-send responses for sensitive or context-heavy email.

Mitigation: Review and edit replies before sending, and avoid pasting sensitive content unless local handling is acceptable.

Risk: Users may expect the skill to connect to mail accounts or perform full email-client workflows.

Mitigation: Use it only as a local classifier and canned-reply helper for text supplied by the user.
