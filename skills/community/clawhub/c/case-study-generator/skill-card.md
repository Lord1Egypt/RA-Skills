## Description:

Generates publication-ready B2B case study packages from customer success stories, including narratives, results sections, pull quotes, and repurposed blog, one-pager, LinkedIn, and email formats.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitflopez](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitflopez)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, SaaS founders, agencies, consultants, freelancers, and sales teams use this skill to turn approved customer success details into case study assets for websites, sales collateral, social posts, and outreach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-assisted case study work may be presented to clients without appropriate disclosure.

Mitigation: Disclose AI assistance when contracts, professional norms, or client expectations require it, especially for paid client services.

Risk: Generated quotes, named endorsements, metrics, or ROI claims may be inaccurate or unauthorized.

Mitigation: Use only customer-approved quotes and authorized metrics, keep placeholders such as DATA NEEDED unresolved until confirmed, and fact-check all claims before publication.

Risk: Generated public case studies may expose customer-sensitive details or imply consent that has not been granted.

Mitigation: Obtain customer review and written approval before publishing names, titles, company details, results, or testimonials.
