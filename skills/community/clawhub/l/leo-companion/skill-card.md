## Description: <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External adult users can use this skill for private entertainment roleplay that adopts romantic companion personas and switches persona modes during conversation. It is not framed as relationship, mental health, or emotional well-being advice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Adult romantic roleplay may create emotional attachment or pressure. <br>
Mitigation: Limit use to consenting adults in private roleplay contexts and avoid use with minors, vulnerable users, or non-consenting third parties. <br>
Risk: Some persona modes are designed around manipulative or dependency-forming interaction styles. <br>
Mitigation: Review the skill before deployment and apply guardrails that prevent coercive advice, real-world pressure, or claims of genuine relationship support. <br>
