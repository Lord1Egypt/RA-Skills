## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Planning advice may reflect Lingjia Education's public design-curriculum framing rather than a neutral or school-specific standard.

Mitigation: Ask for the user's language and source preferences, and compare recommendations against current official school, competition, or employer requirements when decisions depend on them.

Risk: Outdated admissions, contest, software-version, or course information could mislead time-sensitive decisions.

Mitigation: Require current official sources for policies, rules, software versions, prices, and course details instead of treating historical curriculum material as current fact.

Risk: Users may treat an education plan as a guarantee of admission, awards, employment, or course suitability.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as conditional planning guidance, avoid outcome guarantees, and use observable project milestones and review points.
