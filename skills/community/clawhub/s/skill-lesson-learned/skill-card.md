## Description:

Helps quality and project engineers capture, classify, and index lessons learned into reusable text and Markdown knowledge-base records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Quality and project engineers use this skill to turn project outcomes, incidents, audit findings, or closed issues into structured Lesson Learned records. It guides users through source context, classification, root-cause linkage, corrective and preventive actions, ownership, status, tags, and searchable indexes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lesson-learned entries may contain sensitive internal project, supplier, personnel, incident, or quality information.

Mitigation: Review and redact generated records before sharing them outside the intended organization or system.

Risk: Incomplete source evidence can lead to weak reuse value or misleading conclusions if gaps are treated as facts.

Mitigation: Keep missing root causes, actions, owners, status, and data explicitly marked for enterprise completion.
