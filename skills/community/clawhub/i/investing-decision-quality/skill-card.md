## Description:

Apply a research-grade decision pipeline to investment ideas using news-aware bull/bear synthesis, calibrated probability estimation, process audit, Kelly-based position sizing, execution checks, and concentration discipline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dpneko](https://clawhub.ai/user/dpneko)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Investors and analysts use this skill to turn an investment thesis into a structured research-to-trade decision, including probability ranges, process checks, position sizing, execution guidance, and concentration review. It is intended for decision support, not personalized financial advice or automated trading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce actionable investment and trading guidance.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support only; users should verify market data, assumptions, and suitability before acting.

Risk: Users could over-rely on generated position sizing or execution recommendations.

Mitigation: Do not connect brokerage authority or place trades solely from the skill's recommendation.
