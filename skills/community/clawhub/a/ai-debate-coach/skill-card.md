AI吵架助手。帮你分析对方逻辑漏洞，组织有力反驳，找到真正问题。不是为了赢，而是为了把事情说清楚。

## Use Case:

External users use this Chinese-language debate coaching skill to analyze another person's argument, identify logic gaps or fallacies, organize a rebuttal, and choose an appropriate tone for conflict-sensitive communication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Debate or rebuttal wording could be used to harass, threaten, or escalate a personal dispute.

Mitigation: Review outputs before use, avoid threatening or abusive phrasing, and prefer the skill's calm-advice framing in sensitive conflicts.

Risk: Argument analysis may be incomplete or may frame a relationship issue too adversarially.

Mitigation: Treat the output as drafting support, verify the situation-specific facts, and adjust the tone for the relationship and context.
