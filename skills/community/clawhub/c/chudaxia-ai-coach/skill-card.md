## Description:

Chudaxia Ai Coach helps enterprise teams design and deliver AI transformation training, workshops, digital employee definition, business scenario interviews, scene selection cards, and PoC planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[charleschor](https://clawhub.ai/user/charleschor)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Enterprise AI coaches, consultants, and training facilitators use this skill to plan and run Chinese-language B2B AI transformation programs. It supports training agendas, client discovery, business scenario interviews, scene selection cards, digital employee definition, and AI PoC planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used with client business information, interview transcripts, or training participant data.

Mitigation: Use only public or approved client information and obtain participant consent before recording or transcribing interviews.

Risk: The skill can invoke related skills for digital employee prompt and skill-package generation.

Mitigation: Review the related skills separately before using or deploying outputs that depend on them.

Risk: Training or PoC recommendations can affect enterprise AI adoption decisions.

Mitigation: Have qualified business, technical, and compliance reviewers validate plans before client delivery or implementation.
