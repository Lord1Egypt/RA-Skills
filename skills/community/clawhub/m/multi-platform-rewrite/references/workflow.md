# Multi-platform rewrite

The rewritten set is free. Label every version with its platform, its ratio, and the day it should go out. The source offer stays. The hook, the length, and one new sentence change.

## Ratios

Build each cover at its ratio. Do not crop one master.

- Douyin, WeChat Channels, TikTok, Reels: 9:16 cover and a clip. Hook in the first two seconds. Claim and face stay out of the lower caption band.
- Xiaohongshu: 3:4 cover and the note. A clip only when asked, also 3:4.

WeChat copy and pictures do not come with metrics. Do not read WeChat, Channels, or Bilibili.

## Lookup

One execute. Copy `schema_hash` from `tools.get`.

- `social.douyin.video.search`
- `social.douyin.video.comments.list`
- `social.xiaohongshu.note.search`
- `social.xiaohongshu.note.comments.list`
- `social.tiktok.video.search`
- `social.twitter.tweet.search.recent`

## Covers, lines, clips

Transform from the source picture or the first accepted cover. Generate only with no picture. One edit repairs an unreadable claim.

One ready voice for every line. One shared instrumental bed when any clip is in the set.

Each clip is one `beatra.videos.animate` from its own cover, hook as `driving_audio`, duration the next whole second. Then one `beatra.videos.compose` for that clip: `keep_audio` true, bed `volume` 0.25, the on-screen line as the subtitle, canvas `9:16` or `3:4` to match the cover.

## Worked set

Source: a 400-word note about a thermos that does not leak sideways. Douyin gets a 9:16 challenge hook and a clip. Xiaohongshu gets a 3:4 cover and a note that adds where the seal sits. Channels gets a 9:16 cover, a shorter spoken hook, and a clip. Same offer, three labels.
