## Description:

把征稿启事、通知、比赛规则、制度文件、招标要求等转成可执行检查清单与时间线。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn policy documents, calls for papers, contest rules, application guidance, bid requirements, and similar notices into actionable checklists, material lists, and timelines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can read and print the full system clipboard, which may include passwords, keys, personal data, or confidential work.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installing; invoke it only after intentionally copying the document to process, or paste the text directly when clipboard access is not desired.
