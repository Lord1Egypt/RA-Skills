## Description:

A gentle personal coach that helps people identify the 1–3 priorities that matter now and turn one into a small action they can start today.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

For individuals who feel busy but unfocused and want help choosing what matters, planning a small next step, reflecting on the day, or resuming a longer-term priority.

### Deployment Geography for Use:
