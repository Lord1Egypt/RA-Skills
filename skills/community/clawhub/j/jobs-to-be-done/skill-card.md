## Description: <br>
Guides agents through Jobs to Be Done analysis for product strategy, customer switching behavior, churn diagnosis, competitor discovery, and roadmap implications. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product managers, founders, marketers, and customer research teams use this skill to identify the real job customers hire a product to do, reconstruct switching moments, diagnose churn, and translate customer progress into product and positioning choices. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-provided customer interview notes, churn details, or business strategy examples may contain confidential or personally identifiable information. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid pasting confidential or personally identifiable information unless the agent is intended to use it in the analysis. <br>
Risk: JTBD outputs can overstate conclusions if they are based on assumptions rather than recent switch interviews. <br>
Mitigation: Validate job statements, forces of progress, and competitor sets with 5-10 switch interviews before making high-impact product or positioning decisions. <br>
