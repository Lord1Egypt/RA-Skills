## Description:

高考报名+志愿填报全能辅助智能体，面向高三考生、家长、复读生和社会考生，提供高考报名政策解读、流程材料指导、志愿填报规划、院校推荐、位次换算、冲稳保方案和专业就业分析。

## Use Case:

External students, parents, repeat candidates, and social candidates use this skill to plan China gaokao registration steps and college application choices. It helps produce official-source-oriented policy explanations, material checklists, volunteer application tables, school recommendations, major comparisons, and planning guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for education-planning details that are relevant to gaokao registration and volunteer application guidance.

Mitigation: Collect only the necessary planning details and avoid full ID numbers, admission-ticket numbers, or other unnecessary private identifiers.

Risk: Gaokao policies, deadlines, score bands, and admissions data are time-sensitive and province-specific.

Mitigation: Verify deadlines, policies, and admissions data with the official provincial examination authority before acting.

Risk: Admissions planning can be misunderstood as a guarantee of admission.

Mitigation: Present recommendations as planning references only and do not make guaranteed-admission or unofficial-entry claims.
