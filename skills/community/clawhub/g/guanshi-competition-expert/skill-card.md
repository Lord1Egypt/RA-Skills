## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitive analysis may mix public facts, assumptions, and rumors when internal competitor data is unavailable.

Mitigation: Verify sources before relying on recommendations and clearly label each material claim as public information, inference, or rumor.

Risk: Competitive intelligence workflows can be misused for improper competitor targeting or attempts to gather non-public information.

Mitigation: Limit use to lawful, appropriate analysis of public or authorized information and reject requests for non-public competitor data.
