## Description:

Generates customized interview question sets from a job description and candidate resume, including technical, behavioral, and situational questions with difficulty levels, follow-ups, and scoring criteria.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[51mee-com](https://clawhub.ai/user/51mee-com)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Recruiters, hiring managers, and interviewers use this skill to turn a role description and candidate resume into a structured, role-specific interview plan. It helps prepare question banks with evaluation criteria and follow-up prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill processes candidate resumes, which may contain sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Provide only the candidate details needed for interview preparation and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal data.

Risk: The artifact's prompts and output templates are written in Chinese and may not fit every recruiting workflow.

Mitigation: Confirm that the language, schema, and interview format match the intended workflow before deployment.
