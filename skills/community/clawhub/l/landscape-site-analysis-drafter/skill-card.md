## Description:

Use this skill when a landscape architect, RLA, site designer, or planning consultant needs to draft a site analysis report before beginning design development, covering physical inventory, microclimate, visual and contextual analysis, regulatory constraints, and a draft opportunities-and-constraints synthesis for licensed LA review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External landscape architects, site designers, planning consultants, and landscape architecture interns use this skill to turn project intake, field observations, and desktop research into draft pre-design site analysis reports. The output supports schematic design and feasibility work but requires registered landscape architect review before client presentation or submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft site analysis output could be mistaken for a final professional determination or permit-ready submission.

Mitigation: Keep the report clearly labeled as draft-only and require registered landscape architect review, field verification, AHJ confirmation, and professional seal before client presentation or submission.

Risk: Missing or uncertain site data could lead to incorrect design assumptions.

Mitigation: Flag missing inputs as data gaps and require confirmation of field conditions, soils, wetlands, floodplains, utilities, easements, and regulatory constraints before design decisions are finalized.

Risk: The skill could overstep into regulated geotechnical, wetland, environmental, biological, cultural-resource, or legal determinations.

Mitigation: Refer slope stability, soil bearing, wetland delineation, protected habitat, cultural-resource, and similar specialty issues to appropriately licensed professionals.
