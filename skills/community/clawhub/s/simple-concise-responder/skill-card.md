## Description:

Generate simple, easy-to-understand, and concise responses when the user asks for brief, clear, to-the-point answers without unnecessary elaboration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sunba666](https://clawhub.ai/user/sunba666)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and employees use this skill when they want an agent to answer in short, direct, easy-to-understand prose. It is especially suited to requests for concise summaries, direct choices, and brief operational steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may make answers very short when common brevity phrases are used, especially Chinese brevity phrases.

Mitigation: Use it when terse responses are desired, and ask explicitly for detail, caveats, or examples when a fuller answer is needed.

Risk: Concise answers can omit nuance that may matter for high-impact decisions.

Mitigation: Review critical answers and request expanded reasoning before relying on them for consequential work.
