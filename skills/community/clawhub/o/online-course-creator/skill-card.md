## Description:

Generates structured online course packages, including course outlines, video scripts, quizzes, assignments, and marketing materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lvjunjie-byte](https://clawhub.ai/user/lvjunjie-byte)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Educators, content creators, training teams, and course builders use this skill to turn a topic and options into a draft course package. It is most useful for quickly creating first-pass curricula, lesson scripts, assessments, and promotional copy that a human can review and adapt.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Running the example script can create or overwrite a JSON export under the skill directory.

Mitigation: Review the example script and output path before execution, and inspect generated files before committing or publishing them.

Risk: Generated course, quiz, and marketing copy may be generic or unsuitable for the intended audience without review.

Mitigation: Have a subject-matter expert validate learning objectives, assessment answers, pricing claims, and promotional language before release.

Risk: The publisher is a third-party ClawHub user rather than NVIDIA.

Mitigation: Verify the publisher profile and release details before purchase, installation, or use in a commercial workflow.
