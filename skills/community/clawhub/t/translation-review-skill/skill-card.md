## Use Case:

External users and language learners use this skill to structure translation review requests, produce reusable checklists, and improve draft wording without sharing secrets or unnecessary personal data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include secrets or unnecessary personal data in translation examples.

Mitigation: Ask users to avoid passwords, OTPs, private keys, wallets, seeds, session secrets, and unnecessary PII before reviewing text.

Risk: Translation review guidance may be incomplete or misleading for high-stakes content.

Mitigation: Keep outputs factual and reversible, and have a qualified reviewer check important translations before use.
