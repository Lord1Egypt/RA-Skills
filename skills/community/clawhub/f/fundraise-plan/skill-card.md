## Description:

Create a structured fundraising plan including goal setting, target audience, channel strategy, timeline, and success metrics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users such as nonprofit, community, school, or individual campaign organizers use this skill to turn a fundraising idea into a campaign plan with donor segments, channels, timeline, budget, contingency actions, and KPIs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fundraising plans may include incorrect or incomplete fundraising, tax, legal, or platform-specific guidance.

Mitigation: Review the generated plan against applicable fundraising rules, tax obligations, legal requirements, and platform policies before acting on it.
