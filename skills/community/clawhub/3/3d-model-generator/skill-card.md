## Description:

Automates a Meshy.ai workflow for turning text prompts or reference images into textured 3D model files such as STL or OBJ.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rioo-maker](https://clawhub.ai/user/rioo-maker)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, designers, and 3D content creators use this skill as a French-language workflow guide for generating, texturing, and exporting Meshy.ai models for 3D printing, games, or architectural visualization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts or reference images may be sent to Meshy.ai during model generation.

Mitigation: Review prompts and images for sensitive or proprietary content before using them with Meshy.ai.
