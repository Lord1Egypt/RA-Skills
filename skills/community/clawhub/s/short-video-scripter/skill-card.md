## Description:

Short Video Scripter creates ready-to-shoot short-form vertical video scripts with timestamped retention beats, muted-viewing text, asset keywords, platform format notes, and synthetic-media disclosure guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to turn a short-video idea, draft, or transcript into a platform-aware vertical video script package for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Douyin, and WeChat Channels. It is scoped to script specifications and handoff guidance, not rendering, TTS, editing, scheduling, or upload automation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved script results or proposed claim and channel facts could be incorrect if approved without review.

Mitigation: Review saved outputs and proposed memory events before approval, and keep unmatched product or offer claims marked as needing a source.

Risk: Users may mistake the skill for a rendering, TTS, publishing, scheduling, or social-platform automation tool.

Mitigation: Keep the workflow limited to script specifications and use separate user-controlled tools for production and publishing.
