## Description:

Track the latest Iran conflict situation with concise headline summaries, source links, and a three-column comparison of US claims, Iranian claims, and third-party verifiable facts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linxkon](https://clawhub.ai/user/linxkon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to request fast Chinese-language updates on Iran-related conflict events, battle damage claims, casualty reports, escalation signals, and source-backed verification status.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Conflict updates can include disputed casualty, damage, or escalation claims.

Mitigation: Require source links, distinguish claims from verified facts, and label third-party verification confidence for each issue.
