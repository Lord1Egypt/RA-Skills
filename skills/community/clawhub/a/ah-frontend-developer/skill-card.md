## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can steer an agent toward comprehensive frontend workflow steps that may be heavier than needed for very small edits.

Mitigation: Scope small requests explicitly and ask the agent to limit context discovery, tests, documentation, and handoff details to what the change justifies.
