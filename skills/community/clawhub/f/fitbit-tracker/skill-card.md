## Description:

Personal Fitbit (by Google) integration for daily health tracking with adaptive sleep and activity reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to fetch, normalize, and summarize their own Fitbit sleep, activity, heart-rate, and related health data in chat after completing Fitbit OAuth setup.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill handles sensitive Fitbit health data and OAuth tokens.

Mitigation: Install only when comfortable granting Fitbit health-data access, keep local token files private, and revoke the Fitbit app when no longer needed.

Risk: Default OAuth scopes may grant broader access than a user needs.

Mitigation: Reduce FITBIT_SCOPES to the minimum required for the requested reporting, such as activity-only or sleep-only access.

Risk: Generated health-data outputs may be written to shared temporary paths.

Mitigation: Use private output locations and avoid shared /tmp paths for raw or normalized Fitbit data.

Risk: Health-data requests in broad chats could expose sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Use explicit Fitbit-specific prompts in private chats.
