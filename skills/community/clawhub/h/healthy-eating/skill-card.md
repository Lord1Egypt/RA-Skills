## Use Case:

External users use this skill to log meals, check nutrition context, get healthy meal ideas or swaps, plan meals, and track eating habits while keeping data local.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on ordinary food-related phrasing.

Mitigation: Install it only where broad healthy-eating and nutrition assistance is expected, and review trigger scope before deployment.

Risk: Meal logs can contain personal information.

Mitigation: Keep meal data local, avoid entering unnecessary sensitive details, and review any logs before sharing them outside the local environment.

Risk: Nutrition suggestions may be incomplete or unsuitable for specialized medical or dietary needs.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as general guidance and require qualified review for medical diets, allergies, eating-disorder concerns, pregnancy, or chronic conditions.
