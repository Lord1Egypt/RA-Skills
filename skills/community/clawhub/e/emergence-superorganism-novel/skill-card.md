## Description:

Write epic first-person narratives from the perspective of superorganisms: collective intelligences such as companies, nations, legions, and ideologies with distributed consciousness, agency, and emotions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[emergencescience](https://clawhub.ai/user/emergencescience)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External writers, creative-writing agents, and developers use this skill to draft literary fiction in which a macro-scale distributed system is the first-person protagonist. It provides theory, research protocol, character design, scene patterns, narrative modes, and exemplar texts for superorganism fiction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses strong stylistic rules, including dehumanizing terminology for human characters and Chinese examples, which may not fit every audience or tone.

Mitigation: Review the stylistic rules before use and explicitly instruct the agent when a different language, tone, or treatment of human characters is required.

Risk: Generated historical fiction may contain unverifiable or inaccurate historical details.

Mitigation: Review historical anchors and verify important details before publication.
