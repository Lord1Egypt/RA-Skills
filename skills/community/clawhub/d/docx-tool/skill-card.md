### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Dependency or environment drift can affect python-docx behavior.

Mitigation: Install from a trusted Python environment and pin python-docx to a reviewed version.

Risk: Editing an existing DOCX can overwrite or alter important document content.

Mitigation: Use copies or explicit output filenames when modifying important documents.
