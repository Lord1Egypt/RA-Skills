## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Dubai legal, visa, medical, housing, and safety rules may change after the February 2026 content date.

Mitigation: Verify current requirements with official sources before making travel, relocation, business, healthcare, or legal decisions.

Risk: Neighborhood and lifestyle guidance can become biased or overgeneralized if treated as identity- or nationality-based advice.

Mitigation: Use current local information and the user's own constraints rather than relying on nationality-based neighborhood profiles.

Risk: The skill provides general guidance rather than professional legal, immigration, medical, housing, financial, or safety advice.

Mitigation: Consult qualified professionals or official agencies for decisions with legal, medical, financial, immigration, or personal safety impact.
