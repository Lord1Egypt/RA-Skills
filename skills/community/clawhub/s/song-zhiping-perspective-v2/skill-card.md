## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake generated persona output for statements, advice, or endorsement from Song Zhiping.

Mitigation: Present the skill as a simulation based on public information and treat outputs as generated analysis, not statements from Song Zhiping.

Risk: Broad business trigger phrases may activate the persona unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when a simulated Song Zhiping-style business perspective is desired, and allow users to exit the persona when it is not needed.
