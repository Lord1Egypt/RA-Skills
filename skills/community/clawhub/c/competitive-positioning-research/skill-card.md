## Description:

Strategic competitive analysis skill for positioning research. Defines comparison dimensions, selects structural analogues, researches each comp, scores your approach 1-5, and produces ranked recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nissan](https://clawhub.ai/user/nissan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, marketing, and strategy teams use this skill before writing public-facing pages or making positioning decisions. It structures competitive and analogue research, scores the current approach, and produces ranked recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitive research may incorporate public web pages or user-provided product materials that are incomplete, outdated, or selectively framed.

Mitigation: Review important claims against source materials before using recommendations in public messaging or strategy decisions.

Risk: The workflow may process confidential internal strategy if users provide it during research.

Mitigation: Use public or approved materials unless confidential strategy has been cleared for the research workflow.
