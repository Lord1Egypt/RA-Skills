## Description:

Startup idea validation skill. Helps indie developers validate product ideas, analyze competitive landscapes, and assess market saturation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openlark](https://clawhub.ai/user/openlark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Indie developers and product builders use this skill to evaluate startup ideas, analyze competitive landscapes, assess user needs, and create MVP roadmaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated market and competitor reports can include generic or placeholder analysis rather than verified market research.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as directional guidance and verify market size, competitors, pricing, and customer demand with independent research before making business decisions.

Risk: The optional requests dependency is present for future web-research behavior but is not required for the current local report generator.

Mitigation: Install and run the skill in an isolated environment, and avoid enabling optional web behavior unless a future release clearly requires it.
