## Description:

Applies psychological principles, mental models, and behavioral science to marketing strategy, copy, pricing, conversion, onboarding, and buyer decision-making tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, founders, growth practitioners, and agents use this skill to select and apply mental models such as social proof, scarcity, loss aversion, anchoring, and choice architecture to practical marketing decisions. It emphasizes ethical implementation and asks the agent to tailor recommendations with available product-marketing context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketing psychology guidance can be misused to create fake scarcity, misleading social proof, coercive defaults, or manipulative pricing and subscription patterns.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before publication and allow only truthful scarcity, substantiated social proof, transparent defaults, and clear pricing or cancellation terms.

Risk: Mental-model recommendations may be applied without enough product or audience context.

Mitigation: Provide product-marketing context where available and require human review for claims, targeting assumptions, and customer-impact tradeoffs.
