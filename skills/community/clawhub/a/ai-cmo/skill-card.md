## Description:

Find the best social media promotion spots for any product and generate platform-tailored engagement copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xjouska](https://clawhub.ai/user/xjouska)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to identify ranked social media posts and threads for product promotion, then draft platform-tailored engagement copy for review before posting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Submitted product names or URLs are shared with AI-CMO's external service.

Mitigation: Do not submit confidential, internal, or pre-launch product information unless that sharing has been approved.

Risk: Generated comments may be inaccurate, too promotional, or unsuitable for a community or brand account.

Mitigation: Review and adapt every suggested comment before posting publicly.

Risk: High-volume engagement can conflict with platform norms or community rules.

Mitigation: Use the platform guidance and scheduling recommendations to pace engagement and respect each community's rules.
