## Description:

Prevent bad strategic decisions by surfacing assumptions, testing feasibility, and exposing critical flaws before execution.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mzfshark](https://clawhub.ai/user/mzfshark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and teams use this skill to test plans before commitment by making assumptions explicit, checking feasibility, and identifying critical flaws. It is suited for decisions with meaningful downside risk or weak supporting evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make an assistant more critical when discussing plans, which may be unsuitable for casual brainstorming.

Mitigation: Invoke it when a feasibility review or assumptions check is desired, and avoid invoking it for open-ended ideation where critical review is not wanted.

Risk: Decision reviews may be over-trusted if users treat the viability score or recommendation as a guarantee.

Mitigation: Use the score and recommendation as decision-support guidance, and verify high-impact assumptions with the proposed tests before acting.
