## Description:

Ru Pack provides six Russian-language helper skills for understanding contracts, utility bills, medical lab results, complaints, government documents, and sole-proprietor tax questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Russian-speaking external users use this skill pack to get plain-language explanations, simple calculations, and draft responses for everyday documents, housing utilities, medical results, complaints, government correspondence, and taxes in Russia and the CIS.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Russia and Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal, medical, tax, and government-document guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or inappropriate for a specific situation.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a first-pass explanation aid and verify important decisions with a qualified doctor, lawyer, accountant, or official source.

Risk: The skill may produce plausible but incorrect interpretations or calculations for Russian-language documents.

Mitigation: Review source documents and calculations before acting on the output, especially for contracts, medical results, complaints, and taxes.
