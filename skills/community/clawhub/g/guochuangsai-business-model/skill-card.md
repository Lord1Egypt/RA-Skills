## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance for designing, evaluating, and presenting the business-model portion of China International College Students' Innovation Competition projects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[earthwalking](https://clawhub.ai/user/earthwalking)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External students, mentors, and competition coaches use this skill to diagnose or design business models for Guochuangsai projects, align pitch deck content to judging criteria, and prepare business-model Q&A.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the skill effectively may involve sharing project, market, revenue, and operating details with the agent.

Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential or unnecessary sensitive business details, and review the agent's data handling environment before use.

Risk: Business-model, market-sizing, and financial-planning guidance can be inaccurate or incomplete for a specific project.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against current competition rules, project evidence, and qualified business or academic reviewers before submission.
