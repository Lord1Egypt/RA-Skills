## Description:

Build private knowledge bases for AI-powered document Q&A with multi-format uploads, smart chunking, vector retrieval, and source citations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, support teams, and developers use this skill to upload internal or product documents, build a searchable knowledge base, and answer questions with source citations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded documents may become retained and searchable knowledge-base content.

Mitigation: Before adding sensitive files, confirm who can query the knowledge base, how indexed content is isolated, and how deletion or recovery works.

Risk: Poorly formatted or newly uploaded documents can produce incomplete retrieval results.

Mitigation: Use clear source documents and allow indexing to complete before relying on answers.
