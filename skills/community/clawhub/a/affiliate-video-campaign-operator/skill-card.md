## Description:

Review affiliate video campaign plans, disclosures, claims, creative notes, and platform captions before a human decides what to create or publish.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zack-dev-cm](https://clawhub.ai/user/zack-dev-cm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, affiliate marketers, and campaign reviewers use this skill to check draft video or carousel campaigns for disclosure placement, claim support, testimonial honesty, creative rights notes, platform fit, and publish-readiness before a human makes final decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide private account credentials, payment details, confidential analytics exports, or unpublished third-party materials during campaign review.

Mitigation: Avoid providing those materials; the skill asks only for review-critical campaign details and explicitly excludes credentials, payment details, private analytics exports, session cookies, private customer data, and unpublished prompt packs.

Risk: A review result may be mistaken for permission to publish or for a legal, financial, health, safety, or income outcome guarantee.

Mitigation: Keep the output as planning guidance with a human-review checklist; the skill does not publish, schedule, upload, operate accounts, approve purchases, bypass platform review, or promise outcomes.
