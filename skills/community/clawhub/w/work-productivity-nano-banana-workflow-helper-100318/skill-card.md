## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may cause accidental invocation for generic requests involving terms such as generate, edit, pro, or bug fix.

Mitigation: Before installation or deployment, narrow or disable implicit invocation so the skill activates only for relevant Nano Banana Pro-style productivity workflow tasks.

Risk: Workflow, checklist, code, or configuration guidance may be incomplete or incorrect for a user's local environment.

Mitigation: Review generated artifacts against the stated success criteria, scan any code or configuration before deployment, and keep assumptions and remaining risks visible.
