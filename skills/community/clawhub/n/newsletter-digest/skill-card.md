## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn newsletter text and article links into concise summaries, key insights, topic digests, and prioritized reading lists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Newsletter or email text and article links may include sensitive personal or business information.

Mitigation: Share only content you are comfortable processing with the assistant, and avoid forwarding sensitive newsletters or private email threads unless your environment permits it.

Risk: Reading-list and preference-learning behavior may retain information about user interests.

Mitigation: Confirm how your environment reviews, updates, or deletes saved reading-list items and preferences before relying on long-term personalization.
