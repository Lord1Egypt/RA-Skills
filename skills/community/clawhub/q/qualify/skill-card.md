## Description:

Qualify leads, prospects, clients, candidates, projects, opportunities, and requests before deeper time, money, or effort is committed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agimodel](https://clawhub.ai/user/agimodel)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, operators, sales teams, recruiters, and project leads use this skill to define qualification criteria, screen opportunities, and decide whether to pursue, pause, reject, nurture, or escalate a lead, client, candidate, project, or request.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Qualification outputs may be mistaken for final authority in legal, HR, procurement, medical, financial, compliance, or other regulated decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision frameworks and adapt them with jurisdiction, industry, and internal approval review before relying on them.

Risk: Poorly chosen qualification criteria can filter out valid opportunities or preserve weak ones.

Mitigation: Review criteria against real outcomes and update disqualifiers, readiness signals, and decision paths before operational use.
