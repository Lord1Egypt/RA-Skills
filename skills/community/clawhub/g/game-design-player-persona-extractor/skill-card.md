## Description:

Extracts the ideal player persona and anti-persona for a game, feature, loop, or progression structure from the design's mechanics, motivations, tolerances, and audience-fit signals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and publishing stakeholders use this skill to identify who a game design is most likely to delight, who it may alienate, and how that audience fit should shape design decisions. It is useful for early concept definition, feature targeting, difficulty tuning, prototype review, and pitch or market-audience framing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persona, demographic, or market-fit conclusions may be misleading if the input design details are incomplete or if optional market validation is not requested.

Mitigation: Review the analysis against the actual game design, keep demographic claims labeled as hypotheses, and request current external evidence when using the output for publishing or market decisions.

Risk: Optional web research for demographic or market validation may rely on broad external sources rather than title-specific evidence.

Mitigation: Prefer recent platform, publisher, genre, or comparable-title sources and separate evidence-backed findings from inference.
