Amazon Seller Central listing management and optimization for product listings, GTIN exemptions, FBA shipments, listing content, and inventory workflows across US, UK, EU, and JP marketplaces.

## Use Case:

External sellers and agent operators use this skill as a checklist and drafting aid for Amazon Seller Central listing setup, GTIN exemption preparation, FBA shipment planning, category requirement review, and product content optimization. It is not intended for legal, tax, accounting, advertising, account appeal, or non-Amazon marketplace advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The guide may contain outdated or marketplace-specific Amazon policy details.

Mitigation: Verify current Amazon Seller Central, GTIN, FBA, product category, and marketplace rules before submitting listings or shipment plans.

Risk: Generated titles, bullets, keywords, pricing, shipment details, or Seller Central actions could be incorrect or outside the seller's intent.

Mitigation: Review all agent-produced listing content and operational changes before allowing an agent to act in Seller Central.

Risk: Supplement, health, advertising, legal, tax, and account appeal topics can create compliance exposure if treated as final advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill only as a checklist for supported Amazon Seller Central workflows and route regulated, legal, tax, advertising, or account-risk decisions to qualified review.
