## Description:

A Chinese-language persona and reasoning skill that applies a Richard Feynman-inspired thinking framework to test understanding, challenge cargo-cult reasoning, explain concepts by analogy, and review decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[alchaincyf](https://clawhub.ai/user/alchaincyf)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees can use this skill when they explicitly want a Feynman-style reasoning lens for concept checks, anti-self-deception review, cargo-cult detection, decision critique, and concrete explanations. It should be treated as a simulated perspective rather than personal testimony from Richard Feynman.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill answers in a strong Feynman-style role-play voice, which can blur simulated perspective with real personal testimony.

Mitigation: Treat responses as a simulated, inferred perspective and ask the agent to exit or switch back to normal when neutral analysis or source attribution is needed.

Risk: The role-play posture may be less suitable when the user needs standard assistant behavior.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for explicit Feynman-style reasoning requests and switch out of the role for ordinary analysis.
