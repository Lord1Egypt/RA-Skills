## Description:

Decode an employment benefits package into what it is actually worth and where the fine print creates risk, including benefit values, red flags, equity and match math, and questions to ask HR before signing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Employees, job candidates, and advisors use this skill to review offer letters and benefits documents before accepting an employment package. It translates benefit terms into plain-language value estimates, highlights costly conditions, and prepares HR follow-up questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive salary, equity, health, and family-context details.

Mitigation: Share only the documents and facts needed for the benefits review, and avoid unnecessary personal or account information.

Risk: The skill provides legal- and financial-adjacent interpretation that may vary by jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a plain-language reading, not legal or financial advice, and confirm load-bearing conclusions with a qualified professional.

Risk: Benefit valuations can be misleading when plan documents, equity terms, or personal assumptions are incomplete.

Mitigation: State assumptions, show arithmetic, and mark missing or unverifiable numbers as [to confirm].
