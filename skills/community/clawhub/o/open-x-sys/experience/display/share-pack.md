# Sharing: Let a Real Discovery Reach More People

> **Read when**: the user wants to share a work; a finding has clear public power; a result needs to fit a platform, audience, or collaboration setting; or one system is preparing to give its capability to another.

Sharing is a work capability OpenXSys can call. It begins with a discovery that already stands, chooses the medium with the greatest force for a real audience, and lets the work remain accurate, understandable, and traceable after leaving its original setting.

## First decide what the sharing should accomplish

A shared work can let someone:

- see a discovery that changes understanding in seconds;
- enter the complete work and keep exploring;
- apply a method to a subject of their own;
- receive a capability they can adapt, validate, and evolve;
- join a higher-level system and produce new results together.

Choose one primary medium from the goal, platform, audience, and user's existing voice, then add only what materially improves the arrival. A powerful publication may be one sentence, one image, a short video, an explorable page, a deck, a letter, or a connected family of works.

## Discovery and form grow together

A shared artifact preserves four things:

- **Central discovery**: the same judgment as the original work;
- **Necessary mechanism**: enough to understand why it holds;
- **Source and time**: enough for the receiver to check it;
- **Continuation point**: the complete work, system, method, or next action.

Visual language, rhythm, proportion, duration, and platform format grow from the real communication setting. A mobile feed may call for vertical composition. A talk may reveal the structure over time. Team collaboration may need an annotatable document. Another system needs a natural-language entry and operating conditions. Platform requirements provide a boundary; the work keeps a shape of its own.

## Common forms

| Form | What it carries best |
|---|---|
| **Discovery card** | One-sentence finding, one decisive image, source, and complete entry |
| **Short video or animation** | Structural change, an unfolding mechanism, before-and-after, and an invitation into the complete work |
| **Copy** | Why this deserves attention in the user's own voice, and how the finding changes the old picture |
| **Deck or long visual** | A sequence of structures, evidence, and choices that need to unfold in order |
| **Replay** | How a real run traveled from ordinary language to discovery and work |
| **System or capability package** | Purpose, operating conditions, inputs and outputs, validation record, lineage, and continuation |

Together these forms create a sharing capability library. When the user already has a mature communication process, let OpenXSys discoveries and works enter that process directly.

## Publication and ownership

Sharing capability prepares material to publication quality and checks the exact text, images, data, identity, and connections that will become public. The user, team, or external system holding publication rights decides the platform, account, timing, and final version. When OpenXSys has explicit publication authority and an execution path, it can publish and confirm the result.

For material about the user's own system, the user chooses the public range. The system can minimize private information, create a preview, and preserve evidence and operating conditions; the real owner holds the final right to share. See [Rights, Authority, and Sharing](../../system/consent.md).

## Truth gives sharing its life

Numbers, quotations, people, images, and replays in a shared work lead to real sources. Compression preserves the conditions of the original judgment. Drama comes from the structure itself. The audience can travel from short form into complete grounds. Feedback after publication can return to the work, understanding, and sharing capability, making the next expression more accurate and more powerful.

## Where it lives

Shared artifacts keep a clear relation to the original work and live where the user or project naturally uses them. The system entry records the audience served, location, source work, and publication state. Versions for several platforms share one central discovery and each speak the language of their setting.
