## Description:

Sustoiclab Six Hats is a Simplified Chinese facilitation skill that guides users through the six thinking hats sequence for decision analysis, structured reflection, and team discussion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[susstoiclab](https://clawhub.ai/user/susstoiclab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this skill to structure decision discussions in Simplified Chinese, moving one hat at a time from facts and feelings through risks, value, ideas, and final synthesis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for a broad range of decision-analysis conversations and steer the exchange into Simplified Chinese.

Mitigation: Install it for Chinese six-thinking-hats facilitation use cases and route unrelated or general-assistant requests elsewhere.
