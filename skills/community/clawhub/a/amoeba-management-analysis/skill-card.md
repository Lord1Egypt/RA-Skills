## Use Case:

Employees, managers, consultants, and business analysts use this skill to structure Amoeba management reviews, calculate unit-time value metrics, diagnose performance gaps, and produce management-analysis reports from user-provided business data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste confidential business, financial, HR, or customer data into the agent session while preparing analyses.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive data unless the user accepts the agent-session and tool-sharing exposure for report generation or spreadsheet work.

Risk: Generated management recommendations may be incorrect or misleading if the supplied operating data is incomplete or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review source data, formulas, assumptions, and recommendations before using outputs for operational decisions.
