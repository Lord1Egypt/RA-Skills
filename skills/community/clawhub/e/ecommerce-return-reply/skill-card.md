## Description:

Generate standardized, policy-compliant customer service replies for buyer return, exchange, and refund inquiries across major Chinese and cross-border e-commerce platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Customer service teams and merchant support agents use this skill to draft buyer-facing replies for returns, exchanges, refunds, order cancellations, damaged goods, wrong items, and after-sales disputes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated replies may be mistaken for definitive legal, marketplace, or merchant policy advice.

Mitigation: Review each reply against current platform rules, merchant policy, and applicable regional requirements before sending.

Risk: Refund, exchange, or dispute messages involving money may create customer expectations beyond the merchant's approved decision.

Mitigation: Confirm refund decisions, shipping responsibility, and timeframes with authorized merchant policy before communicating them to buyers.
