## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Static city facts such as prices, schedules, immigration or student requirements, and business information can become outdated.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive facts against current official sources before relying on them for bookings, applications, budgets, or legal/compliance decisions.

Risk: Neighborhood and safety recommendations may include subjective or overgeneralized wording.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as orientation, compare them with current local sources, and adapt advice to the user's circumstances and risk tolerance.
