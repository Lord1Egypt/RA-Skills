## Description:

Create professional App Store and Google Play screenshots with automatic sizing, device frames, marketing copy, and iterative visual learning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, designers, and app publishers use this skill to turn raw mobile app captures, brand inputs, and store requirements into App Store and Google Play screenshot sets with marketing copy, sizing, review, iteration, and organized exports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may persist reusable memories, learnings, and per-user or verbatim feedback in ~/screenshots without clear consent, retention, or deletion controls.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive screenshots where possible, ask the agent to disable persistent learning for confidential work, and delete ~/screenshots/memory.md and ~/screenshots/learnings.md when finished.
