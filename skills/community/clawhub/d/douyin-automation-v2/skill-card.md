## Description:

Automates Douyin community interaction workflows, including comment replies, private messages, likes, and topic search, with predefined non-promotional response patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linbo405](https://clawhub.ai/user/linbo405)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and operators can use this skill to draft short Douyin replies and follow browser automation workflows for account interaction tasks. It is intended for accounts and recipients where the user has permission to interact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automated comments, direct messages, and likes can affect other users and may violate platform rules or trigger account enforcement.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with accounts and recipients where interaction is permitted, verify current Douyin rules before deployment, and keep sending actions manual or confirmation-gated.

Risk: The artifact provides weak recipient scoping and limited safety controls for outbound interactions.

Mitigation: Require explicit recipient selection, review generated messages before sending, and configure strict rate limits for any repeated interaction workflow.
