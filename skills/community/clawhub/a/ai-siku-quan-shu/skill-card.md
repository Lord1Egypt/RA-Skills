## Description:

An offline Chinese AI encyclopedia and retrieval skill for finding concepts, events, techniques, quotations, and chapter locations across 48 volumes and two outlines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harryucl](https://clawhub.ai/user/harryucl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Readers and researchers can search the Chinese AI encyclopedia, locate passages by volume and chapter, and synthesize material across its four divisions. The skill returns relevant excerpts and citations for grounded answers or offline reading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reader's optional online AI mode may send questions, conversation history, and retrieved book excerpts to external services; package inspection was partial.

Mitigation: Use the offline search and reading features when online AI is unnecessary. Before enabling online AI, review the provider configuration and the content that may be sent.
