# Decision Log Keeper

## Overview

Decisions get re-litigated when their reasons are forgotten. A decision log is cheap insurance: one short entry per decision, written at decision time, capturing what was known then — not rewritten later to look smarter.

## When to use

For projects, investments process, product choices, architecture calls — anywhere 'why did we do this?' will be asked in six months.

## Workflow

1. **Write the entry at decision time** — date, decider, and the decision in one sentence
2. **Record the context** — the facts and constraints as they were known then, including the numbers used
3. **Record the alternatives** — what else was seriously considered and why it lost
4. **Record the bet** — what you expect to happen, and by when you will know
5. **Set the revisit trigger** — a date or a measurable condition that reopens the decision
6. **Review on schedule** — at each revisit: still right, overtaken by events, or wrong? Update the status, never the history

## Guardrails

- Never edit a past entry's reasoning — append a new entry instead
- Never log a decision nobody actually made; hallway consensus is not a decision
- Keep entries under 150 words or nobody will write them

## Output format

Log entries in a fixed format: date, decision, context, alternatives, expected outcome, revisit trigger, status.
