## Description:

Guides scenario-based bank card strategy analysis across debit and credit positioning, customer profiles, benefits packaging, competitor comparison, and one-page strategy summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[scubiry-glitch](https://clawhub.ai/user/scubiry-glitch)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Bank card product, strategy, and business development teams use this skill to evaluate whether a scenario is worth pursuing and to shape debit or credit card positioning, customer targeting, benefits design, and competitive comparisons. Agents use it to produce concise scenario recommendations and one-page strategy summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, subject to local banking regulations and market context.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Strategy recommendations may not reflect local banking regulations, market conditions, or institution-specific risk policy.

Mitigation: Have qualified banking, compliance, and risk reviewers validate assumptions before using recommendations in product decisions.

Risk: The skill is mainly Chinese-language guidance, which may be misapplied if translated or used outside its intended business context.

Mitigation: Confirm terminology, market references, and card-benefit assumptions with local subject-matter experts before publication or execution.
