## Description:

Diagnoses cohesion and narrative flow in the Discussion section of English psychology research papers without generating or rewriting prose.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[laninga](https://clawhub.ai/user/laninga)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External academic writers, editors, and reviewers use this skill to assess whether a psychology paper's Discussion section has clear local cohesion, forward motion, and a persistent take-home message.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Feedback may be less suitable outside English psychology research-paper Discussion sections.

Mitigation: Use the skill for its documented academic-writing context and review diagnostic comments before applying them to other genres, languages, or disciplines.
