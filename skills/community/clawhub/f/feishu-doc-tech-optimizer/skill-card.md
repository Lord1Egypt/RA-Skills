## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated edits may change tone, terminology, or original wording in ways that are unsuitable for a specific document.

Mitigation: Review the final document before relying on it, especially for documents where exact wording or terminology must be preserved.

Risk: The prompt enforces a strongly opinionated technical-document style that may be inappropriate for non-technical or collaborative Feishu documents.

Mitigation: Install and use it only for documents that require strict technical-document polishing.
