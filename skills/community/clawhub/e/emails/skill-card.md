## Description:

Helps agents create and optimize email sequences, drip campaigns, automated email flows, and lifecycle email programs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[coreyhaines31](https://clawhub.ai/user/coreyhaines31)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, lifecycle, and product teams use this skill to plan welcome, nurture, onboarding, retention, win-back, and campaign email sequences. It helps an agent ask for relevant context, propose timing and segmentation, draft email bodies, define calls to action, and outline metrics for optimization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat advisory email sequence guidance as ready-to-send legal or compliance approval.

Mitigation: Review privacy disclosures, consent basis, unsubscribe handling, and email platform permissions before using proposed behavior-triggered or usage-summary emails.

Risk: Drafted email content or timing recommendations may not match a brand's audience, lifecycle stage, or customer expectations.

Mitigation: Validate sequence goals, segmentation, claims, calls to action, and performance benchmarks before launch.
