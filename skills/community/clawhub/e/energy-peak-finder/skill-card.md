## Description:

Turn several days of observation into a simple energy pattern review with likely peak windows, low windows, disruptors, task matching, and a one-week experiment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn self-observed energy notes into a practical scheduling review for deep work, administrative tasks, recovery, and social activity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The energy review could be mistaken for health advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as planning guidance only, consistent with the security guidance and the skill's descriptive-only scope.

Risk: Users may overfit a schedule to one unusually good or bad day.

Mitigation: Use repeated observations where possible and validate the pattern with the suggested one-week experiment.

Risk: User observations may include sensitive health or personal details.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive medical details unless the user is comfortable sharing them with the host agent session.
