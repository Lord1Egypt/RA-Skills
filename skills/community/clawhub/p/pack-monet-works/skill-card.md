External financial content creators, newsletter writers, and marketing teams use this pack to prepare investment-adjacent writing for publication by combining compliance-oriented QA remediation with human-quality editing.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The pack delegates behavior to two referenced skills that are not embedded in this artifact.

Mitigation: Review monet-works-content-qa-dv and ogilvy-humanizer-dv separately before installing or using them.

Risk: Automated edits to financial content could remove nuance or introduce incorrect compliance wording.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer check the revised content, disclaimers, CTAs, and change-report output before publication.
