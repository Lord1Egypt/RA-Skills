## Description:

Provides Chinese-language Xiaoliu Ren divination support for agents, including number, lunar-time, and direct-palace charting with structured interpretation guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tianmingwangluo](https://clawhub.ai/user/tianmingwangluo)

## Use Case:

External users and agent builders use this skill to answer Xiaoliu Ren divination requests, produce a transparent chart, and explain the reading in practical Chinese prose. It is scoped to Xiaoliu Ren concepts and excludes other divination or astrology systems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat divination readings as a substitute for medical, legal, financial, or other important decisions.

Mitigation: Present readings as entertainment or cultural reference and direct important decisions to appropriate real-world expertise.
