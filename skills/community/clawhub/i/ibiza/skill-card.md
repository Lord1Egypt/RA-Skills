## Description:

Navigate Ibiza as visitor, resident, remote worker, or founder with neighborhoods, transport, costs, visas, and local operational guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and travel or relocation assistants use this skill to answer Ibiza questions for holidays, relocation, seasonal work, remote work, and business setup. It supports zone selection, transport planning, cost framing, visa context, safety basics, lodging decisions, food, nightlife, healthcare, and local services.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal, visa, tax, pricing, mobility, and healthcare details can change after the March 2026 data snapshot.

Mitigation: Verify official cited sources before users spend money, relocate, apply for visas, make healthcare decisions, or book transport and lodging.

Risk: Travel and relocation guidance may be mistaken for authoritative legal or medical advice.

Mitigation: Present the skill as practical destination guidance and direct users to official agencies or qualified professionals for legal, tax, immigration, and medical decisions.
