## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill collection includes biomedical, clinical, gene therapy, CRISPR, recombinant DNA, and emergency-care topics that may be incomplete for real-world decision-making.

Mitigation: Use it as educational or reference support only, and require qualified clinical, institutional, regulatory, or biosafety review before applying outputs to patients, prescribing, emergency management, or laboratory work.

Risk: Textbook-derived guidance may be outdated, generalized, or insufficient for patient-specific diagnosis and treatment.

Mitigation: Cross-check outputs against current clinical guidelines, institutional policy, and qualified professional judgment before relying on them.
