## Description:

Guides branch-office administrative staff through opening, expanding, and removing meeting-room administrator permissions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Branch-office administrative employees use this skill as a process reference for requesting new meeting-room administrator permissions, adding workplace data scope, or removing permissions when an administrator leaves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The OA path or named contacts may be outdated, which could cause users to follow an obsolete access-request process.

Mitigation: Confirm the OA path and contacts are current before relying on the skill for permission-request guidance.

Risk: The process guide could be mistaken for authorization to grant or retain meeting-room administrator permissions.

Mitigation: Use the skill only as a reference and continue to follow normal approval channels for permission changes.
