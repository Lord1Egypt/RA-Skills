# Destination Validity Rules ( Passport Entry Requirements )

The single most common non-expiry boarding denial: **the passport is still valid but not valid *long enough* for the destination.** Airlines are fined for carrying inadmissible passengers, so check-in agents enforce the letter of the rule — the immigration officer's discretion never gets a chance to save you.

## The three rule families

| Family | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **6-month-beyond-arrival** | Passport must be valid ≥ 6 months after the date you arrive | Thailand, China, Egypt, Indonesia, UAE, Vietnam, India, Turkey (5 mo effectively enforced as 6) |
| **Schengen 3-month-beyond-exit** | Valid ≥ 3 months after the day you *leave* Schengen, AND issued within the previous 10 years | All 29 Schengen states (France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Greece, Nordic…) |
| **Valid-for-stay** | Just unexpired for the duration of the visit | USA, Mexico, Canada, UK, Japan, Brazil, Argentina, Australia, NZ |

## Rule table for popular destinations (US-passport perspective, 2026)

Verify before booking: IATA Travel Centre (airline source of truth) or the destination's embassy page. Rules do change (Thailand briefly softened, then re-hardened).

- **Thailand** — 6 months beyond arrival, 1 blank page.
- **China** — 6 months beyond arrival, 2 blank pages (visa required for US).
- **Schengen (ETIAS era)** — 3 months beyond exit, issued < 10 years ago. Some carriers still enforce a flat "6 months" as a blunt instrument — if the passport has 4 months past exit, expect *occasional* check-in friction even though the rule is satisfied.
- **Mexico / Canada (land/air)** — valid for stay; a passport CARD works for land/sea Canada-Mexico but never for international air.
- **South Africa** — 2 facing blank pages (dates aside, this is the classic SA denial).
- **Japan** — valid for stay, but onward/return ticket required.
- **Costa Rica** — valid for stay (day count must cover stay).
- **India e-Visa** — passport valid 6 months from application date, 2 blank pages.
- **Singapore** — 6 months beyond arrival, 1½ blank pages in practice.

## Kid passports — the family trip-killer
- US under-16 passports: **5 years** validity (adult 10).
- UK child passport: 5 years. EU child: commonly shorter in practice.
- Children's passports expire on an offset clock from the parents' — one parent renews, books the family trip, and forgets the kid's clock. Rule: **check the child's passport first, always.**
- Documentation beyond the passport: unilateral-travel consent letters when one parent is absent, birth-certificate name matching for differently-surnamed families.

## Secondary checks the engine flags
- Blank pages (South Africa 2, China/India 2, most others 1).
- Issue-date age (Schengen 10-year issuance rule).
- Physical condition: water damage, torn picture page → carrier can refuse.
- Name mismatch vs ticket (marriage, hyphenation) → flag in registry notes.

## How to verify any destination in 60 seconds
1. IATA Travel Centre → destination → "Passport validity".
2. Cross-check the destination embassy in your country.
3. If the two disagree → assume the stricter one; that is what the airline desk will use.
