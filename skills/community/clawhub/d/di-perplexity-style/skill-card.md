## Use Case:

External users, developers, and analysts use this skill to guide an agent toward source-heavy search analysis with quick and full modes for current events, markets, technology, and geopolitics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad topics such as markets or geopolitics can lead to long responses in the default full-analysis mode.

Mitigation: Ask for quick mode when a short answer is preferred.

Risk: Search analysis can become misleading when time-sensitive claims are not clearly sourced or dated.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's freshness, confirmed-versus-unconfirmed, and source-linking requirements for key information.
