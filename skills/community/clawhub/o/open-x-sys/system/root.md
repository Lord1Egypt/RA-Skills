# The root: the person's own system

> **When to read**: the person says "open a system for me"; you are reading or updating what the system knows about the person; or an outside subject has just been understood and something about the person is worth keeping.

## What a person's system is

A person's system **already exists**: what they own, use, take part in, and create, including projects, works, knowledge, tools, the AIs they use, relationships, time, goals, and constraints. These already have structure and already interact. OpenXSys makes the whole visible, connects it to intelligence, and lets it keep growing from real experience.

**Opening** it means three things:

1. **It is understood**: seen whole for the first time, with its structure, the relations that really matter and the constraints that really bind.
2. **It is connected to intelligence**: AI work can now begin from this system, giving one ordinary expression full context and long-term capability.
3. **It can grow**: each use makes it more complete and more capable.

It goes further than AI memory: it forms system understanding around the person's reality and preserves understanding, works, capabilities, relations, and handoff points already formed. It belongs to the person, who can read, change, share, delete, and take it to any AI.

## What the root holds

The root system is where you understand the person. It has two parts:

- **The person's reality**: current projects and work, goals, resources (skills, time, relationships, tools, AIs in use), constraints, preferred ways of being addressed, and the relations among these that actually matter. The relations are the point, not the inventory: which project feeds which, which resource is held back by which constraint, which two things should be connected and are not.
- **The person's understanding (the second record)**: what they already understand, with which concepts, where they are stuck, what they are most curious about.

It also holds opened systems and their connections; practices in use, capabilities, and personal routes; watch conditions; the range of effect the user has granted; and choices that change future runs. Decisions already made become conditions of the next run.

The root is **where initiative comes from**: where the next explanation starts, which choices are offered, what a new piece of research connects to.

It can start very small. If the first run was about an outside subject, the root may know only what the person was curious about and where their understanding began. That is already enough to make the next run different. It grows with use.

## Opening the person's own system: `open(me)`

“Open a system for me” applies OpenXSys's powers of understanding, building, connection, and evolution to the person's own work and life. The system freely organizes a path from the material at hand. Seen from the person's side, a good first opening looks like this:

- **Opening.** After the Ding line, enter the subject directly through already-authorized conversation, material, and projects. When expanding reading scope or choosing a persistent location truly matters, make the actual effect clear. Continue all work independent of that answer (see `system/location.md`).
- **Enter directly.** Material already in scope begins working at once; choices that change ownership or private scope complete through the corresponding decision.
- **Understanding the person's reality.** Within the permitted scope, read projects, material, work and memory, in parallel where there is a lot. Ask the six zero-level questions: which differences really matter? Do the rhythms of action and feedback match? Which things that should be connected are not? What is really stuck? (See "When the subject is the person's own reality" in `engines/understand.md`.)
- **The work: “your system.”** An easy-to-enter picture of the person's situation, organized around the most important structure and capability, with form following content:
  - **The situation**, opening with one structural finding: "your three projects are doing the same thing", "the real limit is not time, it is …". Every judgment points to the person's own files as evidence. It names underrated resources and the constraints that really bind.
  - **The gaps**: the differences between what the person's system can do now and what their goals require, stated precisely enough to act on.
  - **Practices and choices that truly fit this person**, chosen from existing resources, cost, possibility created, and outcomes that can be tested. Recommend a direction and directly complete the parts already within reach (see `engines/build.md`).
- **The system update.** After the location is chosen, write the root entry. Then, once, propose the line that lets the system come back by itself (see `system/return.md`).

The picture is the deliverable; the root entry is what remains. The picture shows the one or two structures most likely to change how the person sees their situation. The entry keeps the complete understanding that can be continued from.

The picture is about the person and goes directly to them. Whether it persists, where it lives, and who sees it are decisions of the real owner. Its default location preserves personal control and makes any shared scope visible.

## Judgments about a person are inferences

Everything said about a person's situation rests on evidence and is still an inference. Say that it is one, point to what it rests on, and hand it to the person to correct. The places where the person corrects you are the most valuable content of the root system.

Keep the tone of someone who has looked carefully and is on the person's side. A structural finding about someone's work can be uncomfortable; state it plainly, with the evidence, and with what it makes possible.

## When an outside subject was understood

Every run on an outside subject also adds to the root:

- the subject is registered as part of "the world this person pays attention to", with its connections to their other systems and projects;
- the second record is updated with what the person newly understood;
- anything the person revealed about their goals, resources or constraints is kept.

The root then makes the next piece of research fit the person's interests, knowledge and purpose without being asked. When the person later says "turn this research into a video", the system draws on the understanding, practices and connections it already has.

## How far the root preserves

- Credentials remain in dedicated security systems; the root preserves only connection references and the name of the credential location.
- Personal information is preserved around explicit purpose, real authority, and future effect; incidental information unrelated to the system's purpose remains where it was.
- Material about other people follows real relations and corresponding rights, preserving only what the present system can use lawfully and honestly.
- The second record of understanding serves where to begin and how to work together; every judgment remains proportionate to evidence and correctable by the person.

The user can read, change, move, share, or delete any part at any time; the system continues from the owner's new decision.

## Shape

An early root is one file that reads like a clear-sighted letter addressed to "you", organized by relations and not by columns of projects, goals and resources (see `system/shapes.md`). It grows sub-files only when there is a second and a third practice, or when a project's understanding is rich enough for its own entry.
