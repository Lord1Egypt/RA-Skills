## Description:

自动发布内容到多个平台，支持微信公众号、微博、知乎等平台定时发布。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content teams and operators use this skill to publish or schedule supplied articles and announcements across platforms such as WeChat, Weibo, Zhihu, and Jianshu, while adapting formatting and reporting publication results.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public or third-party platform posting can disclose sensitive, private, or confidential content if the supplied content or schedule is wrong.

Mitigation: Review target platforms, content, schedule, and credentials before posting; do not provide secrets, private personal data, or confidential business content unless it is intended for publication.
