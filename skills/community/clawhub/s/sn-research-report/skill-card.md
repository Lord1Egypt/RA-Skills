## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance for organizing research reports by reader task, such as overview, comparison, investigation, or timeline, and by domain conventions for academic, medical, legal, and policy reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sensenova-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/sensenova-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to choose report structures and formatting rules for research outputs, including industry overviews, option comparisons, entity investigations, event timelines, academic reviews, medical evidence summaries, legal memos, and policy briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Report structures for medical, legal, and policy topics may be mistaken for domain advice if the generated report is not reviewed.

Mitigation: Require qualified review for regulated or high-impact domains, and preserve evidence levels, jurisdiction, uncertainty, and source reliability labels in the final report.

Risk: The skill is written in Chinese, so non-Chinese users may misread or lose nuance through translation.

Mitigation: Use a fluent reviewer or validated translation before applying the guidance in production workflows.
