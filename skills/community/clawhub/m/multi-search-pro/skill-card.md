## Description:

Enhanced multi-engine search guidance for general, news, finance, and stock-research queries across Chinese and international search providers, with no API key requirement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to choose search engines, construct search queries, and cross-check general, news, financial, and stock-market information across multiple providers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms may be routed to third-party search and financial information providers.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive personal, financial, or confidential business terms unless those providers are approved for the query.

Risk: Search results and financial news can be stale, incomplete, or inconsistent across providers.

Mitigation: Cross-check important claims with multiple engines and authoritative sources such as official issuers, exchanges, regulators, or primary documents.
