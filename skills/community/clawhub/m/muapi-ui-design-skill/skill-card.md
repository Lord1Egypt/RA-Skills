## Description:

Generate high-fidelity UI/UX mockups for mobile and web apps using Atomic Design principles — creates wireframes and design systems via muapi.ai

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[anil-matcha](https://clawhub.ai/user/anil-matcha)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and product teams use this skill to turn product briefs into high-fidelity mobile or web UI mockup prompts with platform layout, design token, accessibility, and style guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts may contain confidential product or roadmap details that are sent to the configured image-generation backend.

Mitigation: Use placeholders or sanitized briefs unless the backend and data-handling terms are approved for the information being provided.

Risk: Generated output is a UI image mockup prompt workflow, not a complete design-system or wireframe automation pipeline.

Mitigation: Review generated mockups and prompts for accessibility, brand fit, text accuracy, and implementation feasibility before use.
