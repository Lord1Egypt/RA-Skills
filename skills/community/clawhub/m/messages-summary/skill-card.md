## Use Case:

Employees and team members use this skill to summarize Feishu group or direct-message conversations, extract key decisions and action items, and prepare structured discussion records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Feishu conversations may include confidential, personal, or regulated content.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for conversations you are allowed to access, and avoid submitting sensitive chat content unless you have permission.

Risk: Summaries and extracted action items may be incomplete or unsuitable for direct sharing.

Mitigation: Review summaries before sharing them or turning extracted items into documents or tasks.
