## Description:

辅助实验室内部审核员进行ISO17025管理体系审核，支持体系文件符合性与适用性审核、基于实际文件的审核清单生成、不符合项判定与分级、审核报告生成；当用户需要进行实验室内部审核、文件审核、不符合项分析或生成审核报告时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External laboratory quality managers, internal auditors, and quality engineers use this skill to review ISO17025/CNAS management-system documents, generate audit checklists, classify nonconformities, and draft audit reports with cited standard clauses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide confidential laboratory, customer, sample, equipment, or personnel information while requesting audit assistance.

Mitigation: Provide excerpts or redacted copies where possible, and remove customer names, sample identifiers, signatures, contact details, equipment serial numbers, and other confidential records unless sharing is authorized.

Risk: Audit guidance or drafted findings may be incomplete or unsuitable as a formal certification decision.

Mitigation: Use the output as audit assistance and have qualified personnel verify conclusions, cited clauses, and applicability before relying on the result.
