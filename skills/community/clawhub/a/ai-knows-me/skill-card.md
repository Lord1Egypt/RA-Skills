## Use Case:

External users use this skill to help an agent learn their identity, work context, communication preferences, and AI enablement goals through a guided conversation. It produces personalized AI onboarding, diagnosis, action-plan, memory, and behavior-rule documents that users can review and adapt as future agent context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can generate persistent documents describing a user's identity, work, preferences, memories, and confidential information.

Mitigation: Review generated files before reuse, sharing, syncing, committing, or adding them to future agent context.

Risk: The artifact includes strong privacy assurances that may depend on the user's runtime environment and storage choices.

Mitigation: Treat privacy claims as conditional; avoid entering passwords, API keys, tokens, regulated data, financial identifiers, or private personal details.
