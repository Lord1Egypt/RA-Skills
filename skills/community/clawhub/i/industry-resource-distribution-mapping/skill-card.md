## Description:

Generates Chinese-language industry resource distribution mapping reports that analyze enterprise, innovation, platform, talent, and carrier resources for a target industry.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and industry strategy analysts use this skill to map where a target industry's core resources are concentrated, identify regional strengths and gaps, and draft resource introduction or investment attraction recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated resource maps and business recommendations may be incomplete or inaccurate because they depend on public web information and current search results.

Mitigation: Use the report as decision support, verify important claims against authoritative sources, and review recommendations before acting on investment, policy, or strategy decisions.

Risk: The skill is written for Chinese-language industry research and may require adaptation for another language or non-China-focused framing.

Mitigation: Confirm the target language, geography, and framing with the agent before relying on the generated report.
