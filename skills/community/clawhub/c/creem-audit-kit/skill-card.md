## Description:

Generate a Creem audit-ready compliance pack with legal policies, a preflight checklist, and site surface punchlists for Merchant of Record onboarding or review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, founders, and operators use this skill to draft Creem-focused compliance pages, a preflight checklist, and site placement punchlists before Merchant of Record onboarding or after a review rejection.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated legal and compliance pages may be incomplete or inaccurate for a specific product, jurisdiction, or operating model.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a draft, replace all TODOs and placeholders, and have final legal language reviewed before publishing.

Risk: Refund policy templates can conflict if multiple template options or unused wording remain in the final page.

Mitigation: Choose one refund model, remove unused template text, and run the consistency checks before release.

Risk: Inconsistent Merchant of Record wording can misrepresent the relationship between the product owner and Creem.

Mitigation: Keep checkout, pricing, and legal pages consistent with Creem as the payment or Merchant of Record platform.
