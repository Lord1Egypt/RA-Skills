## Description:

Find viral outlier posts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, pull creator stats, transcribe and analyse videos, build competitor watchlists, and crawl profiles on demand. Prepaid credits, MCP or REST.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Marketers, creators, and social media researchers use this skill to find unusually high-performing posts, compare creators, track competitors, and review video content across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.
