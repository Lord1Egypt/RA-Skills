## Description:

Use this skill when a team physician, athletic trainer, or sports clinician needs to draft a graduated Return-to-Sport and Return-to-Learn plan for a concussed athlete for qualified healthcare professional review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[archlab-space](https://clawhub.ai/user/archlab-space)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sports medicine clinicians and qualified healthcare professionals use this skill to draft individualized Return-to-Sport and Return-to-Learn documentation after concussion intake, current-status review, governing-body policy review, and stage mapping. The output is draft clinical documentation for supervising healthcare professional review and signature, not athlete clearance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The draft plan could be mistaken for medical clearance or authority to return an athlete to contact practice or play.

Mitigation: Use only under qualified concussion-management clinician oversight; keep outputs as draft documentation and complete Stage 5/6 clearance decisions and signatures outside the agent.

Risk: Incomplete clinical intake or governing-body policy details could produce an unsuitable staged plan.

Mitigation: Confirm the skill's assumption summary, governing-body rules, red-flag screen, stage minimums, and unresolved questions before relying on the draft.
