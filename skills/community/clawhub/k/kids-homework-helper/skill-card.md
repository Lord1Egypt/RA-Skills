## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review reports persistent learning and self-modification behavior that is broader than the tutoring purpose and lacks clear consent or review controls.

Mitigation: Review before installing; remove or disable the learner script and self-evolution section unless persistent notes and instruction changes are explicitly desired and reviewed.

Risk: Stored preferences, usage notes, and error patterns could retain sensitive information about a student's homework or learning needs.

Mitigation: Avoid recording personal student data, periodically inspect learned_patterns.json, and clear stored notes when they are no longer needed.
