## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automation examples may require connecting business accounts or moving customer, financial, CRM, or campaign data between services.

Mitigation: Connect only necessary accounts, share the minimum fields needed, and review workflows that post publicly, send campaigns, or change accounting or CRM records before enabling them.

Risk: Broken or poorly tested automations can create incorrect records, duplicate actions, missed notifications, or silent workflow failures.

Mitigation: Test each trigger and action with realistic edge cases, enable error notifications, review workflow logs, and document troubleshooting steps.
