## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to frame market-analysis conversations, risk-management checks, and trading-discipline reminders as educational guidance rather than personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could mistake general trading education for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as educational guidance, avoid account-specific recommendations, and direct users to consult a licensed advisor before risking money.

Risk: Trading decisions can expose users to losses from leverage, liquidity, fees, slippage, or weak risk controls.

Mitigation: Keep risk-management reminders visible, including predefined stops, position sizing, transaction-cost awareness, and only using capital the user can afford to lose.
