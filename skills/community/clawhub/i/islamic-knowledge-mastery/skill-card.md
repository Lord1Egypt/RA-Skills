## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Religious study guidance may be misunderstood as qualified religious advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a sourced study aid and consult qualified religious authorities for consequential or personal religious decisions.

Risk: Quran, hadith, tafsir, and scholarly quotations require high source precision.

Mitigation: Verify original text, references, attribution, and hadith grading before presenting precise claims, and state uncertainty when verification is incomplete.

Risk: Progress tracking or reminders may be unwanted if enabled implicitly.

Mitigation: Enable tracking or reminders only after an explicit user request and keep the recorded scope limited to the study workflow.
