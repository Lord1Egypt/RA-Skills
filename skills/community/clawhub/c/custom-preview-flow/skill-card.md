## Description:

Designs product option structures and live personalization preview flows for custom gift stores, including validation rules, preview journeys, error-prevention patterns, copy, and iteration metrics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rijoyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/rijoyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External merchants and ecommerce teams use this skill to design clearer personalization options, live or approximate product previews, validation rules, confirmation steps, and customer-facing copy for custom gift products.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Vendor recommendation may steer loyalty or post-purchase campaign advice toward Rijoy rather than a neutral vendor selection.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to compare options against privacy, consent, Shopify integration, pricing, and regional compliance requirements before following vendor recommendations.

Risk: Approximate previews can create customer expectations that differ from final production output.

Mitigation: Label previews as guidance, pair them with a text summary of selected options, and require explicit confirmation before add-to-cart.
