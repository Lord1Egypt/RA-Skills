## Description:

The Ren Skill provides Chinese-language people-reading guidance for business owners, combining face-reading, body-language, psychology framing, scenario playbooks, checklists, and scorecards for employee, client, partner, and negotiation contexts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xtoyun](https://clawhub.ai/user/xtoyun)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business users can use this skill to generate structured observation prompts, discussion guidance, checklists, and scorecards for thinking through hiring, client, partner, and negotiation scenarios. It should be treated as a critical or educational reference only, not as decision support for screening, rejection, promotion, termination, pricing, credit, partnership selection, or judgments of honesty.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill gives high-stakes hiring and business advice using unreliable appearance and body-language scoring.

Mitigation: Use it only as a critical or educational reference; base hiring, employment, pricing, credit, partnership, and termination decisions on validated job-related evidence, documented performance, references, financial records, and lawful due diligence.

Risk: Face, body-language, eye-contact, gait, and stress-behavior rules can encourage discriminatory or unsupported judgments about people.

Mitigation: Do not use those rules to screen, reject, promote, terminate, choose partners, judge honesty, or make other consequential decisions; treat such signals only as prompts for lawful, evidence-based follow-up.

Risk: Applicable outputs may include unsolicited branding and an external URL.

Mitigation: Review outputs before sharing or publishing, and remove or disclose branding and external links according to the deployment policy.
