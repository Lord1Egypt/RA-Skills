## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ambiguous ecommerce requests may trigger external public-news browsing.

Mitigation: Narrow the trigger behavior or ask a clarifying question in deployments where automatic external news fetching is not desired.

Risk: Platform policy summaries can become incomplete or stale because cross-border ecommerce rules change quickly.

Mitigation: Include original source links and direct users to verify important policy changes against official platform notices.
