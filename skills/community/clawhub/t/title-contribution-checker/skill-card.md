## Description:

Evaluates whether an academic paper title clearly communicates the study's core idea, research focus, and contribution.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[3492027682](https://clawhub.ai/user/3492027682)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and academic writers use this skill to review whether a paper title states what was studied, includes a concrete research focus, and avoids broad or low-contribution wording.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The title-contribution assessment is rubric-based and may miss discipline-specific title conventions.

Mitigation: Use the output as editorial guidance and have the author or reviewer confirm final title choices against field expectations.

Risk: The skill can suggest title improvements, but evidence supports only text-only guidance and not validation of research quality.

Mitigation: Treat suggestions as writing feedback rather than proof that the underlying paper contribution is novel or sufficient.
