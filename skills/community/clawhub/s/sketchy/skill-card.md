## Description:

Creates hand-drawn pencil-sketchbook slides, charts, explainers, lessons, business reports, one-pagers, decision boards, postcards and short videos with generative music, as self-contained HTML that exports to PDF, PPTX and MP4. Use when someone wants a presentation, deck, slides, pitch, lesson, tutorial, explainer, chart, data story, report, one-pager, social card or explainer video in a hand-drawn, sketch, sketchnote, whiteboard, notebook or Excalidraw-like style, or names sketchy. Not for production web apps, dashboards with live data, or photo-real design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
