## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill directs an agent to use sexualized, psychologically targeted roast and dominance behavior, including in group chats where not every participant may have individually opted in.

Mitigation: Use only in adult settings where participants understand the persona, consent to high-intensity banter, and know explicit opt-out phrases before use.

Risk: Harsh roast, sexual innuendo, and psychologically pointed banter can be inappropriate in mixed-age, workplace, support, or public-group contexts.

Mitigation: Avoid those settings and review the skill before deployment when audience age, workplace norms, or support expectations are uncertain.

Risk: Group chat use can create boundary issues if participants do not want direct engagement from the persona.

Mitigation: Make group-level and individual opt-out rules explicit, and honor opt-out requests immediately.
