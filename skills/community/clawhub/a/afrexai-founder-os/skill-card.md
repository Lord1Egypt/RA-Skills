## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can provide startup advice in areas with real-world legal, finance, immigration, fundraising, compensation, and health-related consequences.

Mitigation: Review recommendations critically and consult qualified professionals before making consequential decisions.

Risk: Broad founder-advice triggers may shape the agent into a structured startup advisor even when the user needs narrower or jurisdiction-specific help.

Mitigation: Use the skill for startup planning workflows and ask the agent to state assumptions, constraints, and when advice requires expert review.
