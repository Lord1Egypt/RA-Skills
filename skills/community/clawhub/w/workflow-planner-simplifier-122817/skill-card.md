## Description:

Workflow Planner Simplifier helps maintainers turn a specific adaptive planner enhancement request into concise plans, checklists, implementation support, and validation notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Maintainers and developers use this skill to structure work on an adaptive planner enhancement, including clarifying constraints, producing implementation-oriented artifacts, and checking outputs against review criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad planning and enhancement triggers may invoke the skill for unrelated tasks.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly for this workflow requirement or narrow its trigger terms before deployment when tighter routing is needed.
