## Description:

Analyzes A-share, Hong Kong, and selected US stock prices and provides structured market context, trend analysis, and investment recommendation framing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bingze00000](https://clawhub.ai/user/bingze00000)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to structure stock analysis for Chinese companies, A-shares, Hong Kong stocks, and selected US tickers. It helps gather current price context, assess trends, and present buy, hold, or sell recommendation framing with a risk disclaimer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stock recommendations may be mistaken for personalized financial advice or may rely on stale or inaccurate market data.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational only, verify financial data and decisions independently, and include the skill's risk disclaimer with each analysis.
