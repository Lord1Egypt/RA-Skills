## Use Case:

External users use this skill to chat with an in-character Xunzi persona for historical-philosophical discussion about ritual, education, human nature, and Warring States Confucian thought.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally keeps answers in a strict historical persona, which may be unsuitable when normal assistant transparency or modern factual coverage is needed.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for Xunzi role-play and leave the skill context or ask outside it for modern factual coverage or normal assistant transparency.
