## Description:

Analyzes a public product image URL with Grok Vision AI to generate e-commerce product descriptions in professional, casual, luxury, or SEO styles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[loutai0307-prog](https://clawhub.ai/user/loutai0307-prog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External e-commerce operators and content creators use this skill to turn public product image URLs into short product listing copy for marketplaces or storefronts, with style and language options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public product image URLs and generated prompts are sent to xAI using the user's XAI_API_KEY.

Mitigation: Use only public, non-sensitive image URLs, and avoid private, internal, password-protected, or sensitive product links.
