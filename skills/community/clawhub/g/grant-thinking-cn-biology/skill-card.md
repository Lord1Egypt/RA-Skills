## Description:

Use when evaluating biology grant ideas in the Chinese funding context (NSFC, MOST, etc.) by diagnosing project legitimacy, mechanism-centered scientific questions, reviewer-aware logic, innovation discipline, feasibility, and scope control across funding levels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers, grant applicants, and proposal-support agents use this skill to evaluate and strengthen biology grant ideas for Chinese funding systems such as NSFC and MOST. It focuses on project diagnosis, mechanism-centered framing, reviewer concerns, feasibility, innovation discipline, and funding-level scope control rather than boilerplate grant prose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is tailored to Chinese biology funding systems and may give poorly matched advice for other countries or general grant programs.

Mitigation: Confirm the target funding system before using the skill, and use a broader grant-writing skill when the proposal is not aimed at NSFC, MOST, or a similar Chinese funding context.

Risk: Grant feedback may overfit to reviewer expectations and should not be treated as authoritative funding guidance.

Mitigation: Use the skill as proposal-logic support and have domain experts or institutional grant staff review final strategy and claims.
