## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and analysts use this skill to construct Google Scholar searches, interpret citation signals, find accessible full text, and evaluate academic sources before citing them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Scholar alerts, saved libraries, and institutional access can involve separate Google or university accounts.

Mitigation: Use only authorized accounts and access paths; this skill does not automate or request those credentials.

Risk: Academic search results, citation counts, preprints, and snippets can be misleading when treated as quality signals on their own.

Mitigation: Check publication venue, peer-review status, retractions or corrections, methodology, and full-text availability before citing or relying on a result.
