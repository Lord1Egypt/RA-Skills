## Description:

Scores DTC ecommerce leads against Visual Dept's ICP criteria to prioritize outreach.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales and marketing teams use this skill to assess DTC Shopify leads, assign a 100-point fit score, and draft a personalized outreach hook.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The scoring criteria include gender and age, which can introduce discriminatory lead qualification.

Mitigation: Remove gender and age from scoring before outreach use, and base qualification on business-relevant signals such as vertical, ecommerce platform, visual quality, content needs, budget fit, and public contact channels.

Risk: The skill applies US-only criteria without documenting the business or legal reason for that geographic limit.

Mitigation: Document any legitimate geographic limits before use, or replace geography scoring with business-relevant availability and market-fit signals.
