## Description:

Write short, human follow-up messages for creator outreach when the first message gets ignored, delayed, or half-answered.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Use Case:

Creator acquisition and partnership teams use this skill to recover stalled creator outreach with concise follow-up options, timing guidance, angle shifts, and clear stop rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Follow-up messages could become pressure-oriented, spam-like, or misleading if used without review.

Mitigation: Keep messages short, avoid guilt-tripping or fake urgency, do not invent proof or performance claims, and stop when the creator appears unqualified or uninterested.

Risk: Outreach inputs may contain sensitive creator or campaign context.

Mitigation: Provide only the outreach context needed for the message sequence and review outputs before adding them to SOPs or CRM templates.
