## Description:

Generates testimonial request emails and formats collected testimonials for marketing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, founders, and customer-facing employees use this skill to request customer testimonials, structure interview questions, format received quotes for marketing channels, and audit existing testimonials for specificity and attribution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated testimonial content may include customer quotes, names, company details, logos, or results claims without confirmed permission.

Mitigation: Confirm documented customer permission for the quote, attribution, company name, logo, and results claims before publishing; anonymize details where permission is unclear.

Risk: Formatted testimonials or case study summaries may overstate outcomes if raw customer input is vague or unverified.

Mitigation: Review claims against source responses and supporting evidence, especially numbers, timeframes, and before/after comparisons.
