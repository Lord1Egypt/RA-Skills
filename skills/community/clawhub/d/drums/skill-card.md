## Use Case:

Drummers and music learners use this skill to get practice advice, troubleshoot technique, choose rudiment priorities, and maintain local progress notes for songs, goals, grooves, and tempos.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may store practice sessions, songs, goals, rudiment tempos, and related notes in local files under ~/drums/.

Mitigation: Use progress logging only when local habit tracking is acceptable, avoid sensitive entries, and remove the ~/drums/ folder if those notes should not be retained.
