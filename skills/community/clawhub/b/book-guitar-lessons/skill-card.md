## Description:

Book guitar-lesson services through Lokuli MCP for users who need to find availability and create lesson bookings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to search for guitar-lesson providers, check available appointment times, and create bookings through Lokuli MCP.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Booking calls may disclose customer contact details to Lokuli's external MCP service.

Mitigation: Confirm customer consent and review the name, email, and phone before allowing a booking call.

Risk: Incorrect provider, service, date, or time details could create an unwanted lesson booking.

Mitigation: Confirm the provider, service, date, and time slot with the user before creating a booking.
