## Description:

End-to-end GEO content publishing and distribution orchestrator for turning optimized content into channel-specific variants and AI-crawler-visible publishing plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content, marketing, growth, and developer teams use this skill to plan GEO-conscious content releases across websites, blogs, docs, social channels, newsletters, structured data, sitemaps, and llms.txt updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated public-facing copy, schema, sitemap, llms.txt, or social recommendations may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a production channel.

Mitigation: Review all generated copy, structured data, sitemap, llms.txt, and social recommendations before applying them to production channels.

Risk: Users may mistake the skill for an automated publishing connector.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as a planning and copy-generation workflow unless a separate reviewed publishing integration is provided.
