## Description:

Help the user close the loop after effort by turning a completed task into a loot table, a fair reward plan, a visible record of the win, and a sensible next unlock condition.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill after completing a habit streak, project milestone, exam, or demanding task to choose a proportional celebration, record the win, and set a sensible next unlock condition.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reward suggestions could encourage overspending or choices that undermine health or recovery needs.

Mitigation: Keep rewards proportional, budget-aligned, and restorative; avoid celebrations that create money stress or health backlash.

Risk: Users may mistake the skill for financial planning, addiction support, or a tool that can complete purchases.

Mitigation: Use it only for reward-planning guidance; it does not make purchases, handle payments, store records, or access private accounts.
