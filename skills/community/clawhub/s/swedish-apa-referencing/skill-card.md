## Description:

Swedish APA 7 referencing with Sophiahemmet (SHH) deviations: reference lists, in-text citations, laws (SFS), ICN codes, secondary sources. Use for any assignment requiring referenslista enligt SHH APA / APA 7 på svenska.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to format Swedish APA 7 reference lists and in-text citations for Sophiahemmet Högskola coursework, including SHH-specific handling for laws, public documents, ICN codes, web pages, and secondary sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The SHH-specific citation rules may be inappropriate for generic APA, another institution, or non-Swedish citation requirements.

Mitigation: Confirm that the assignment requires Sophiahemmet Högskola APA conventions before applying the skill's output.

Risk: Citation assistance can introduce incorrect or unsupported references if source details are incomplete or fabricated.

Mitigation: Check every generated citation against the source material and verify that each in-text citation matches a reference-list entry.

Risk: Course AI policies may restrict use to language checking or prohibit AI-generated drafting.

Mitigation: Review the applicable course policy before using the skill for coursework beyond citation formatting.
