## Use Case:

Industrial water-treatment engineers, operators, and project teams use this skill to design, select, commission, troubleshoot, and maintain pure water and ultra-pure water systems for industrial, electronics, and pharmaceutical applications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Technical recommendations could be incomplete or unsuitable for real pure-water and ultra-pure-water systems, especially in pharmaceutical, semiconductor, electrical, or compliance-sensitive projects.

Mitigation: Have qualified engineering and compliance reviewers validate recommendations against source water analysis, target quality requirements, applicable standards, vendor specifications, site constraints, and commissioning data before implementation.

Risk: Troubleshooting or operating changes may affect water quality, equipment reliability, or regulatory compliance if applied directly.

Mitigation: Test changes under controlled conditions, monitor critical parameters, keep operating records, and follow established change-control and maintenance procedures.
