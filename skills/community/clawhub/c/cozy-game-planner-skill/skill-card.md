## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include secrets or sensitive personal data in planning prompts.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive goals and constraints only; omit passwords, private keys, wallets, seeds, OTPs, session tokens, and unnecessary personal data.

Risk: Generated plans or checklists may contain unsuitable or misleading guidance if applied without review.

Mitigation: Review outputs before acting and prefer reversible checklist-based steps.
