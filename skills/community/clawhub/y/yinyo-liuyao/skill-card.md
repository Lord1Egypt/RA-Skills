## Description:

Yinyo Liuyao helps an agent interpret Liuyao hexagrams using Yehe-style rules distilled from Zengshan Bu Yi, including input completion, yongshen selection, line relationships, transformations, clashes, void or broken conditions, and timing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to complete Liuyao hexagram inputs and generate structured, uncertainty-aware divination interpretations for a single stated question. It is also useful for Liuyao technical questions such as yongshen selection, monthly breaks, void branches, transformations, and timing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on divination output for medical, financial, legal, or other consequential decisions.

Mitigation: Present interpretations as uncertain reference guidance and direct users to qualified professional advice for consequential decisions.

Risk: Incomplete or mixed-question inputs can produce misleading Liuyao interpretations.

Mitigation: Require one clear question and confirm the casting date, hexagram names, line details, moving lines, and changed hexagram before interpreting.
