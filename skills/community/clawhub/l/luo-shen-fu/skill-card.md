## Description:

Researches and drafts comprehensive Simplified Chinese analysis of "Ode to the Goddess of Luo River" (洛神赋), including literary significance, cultural context, influence on Dream of the Red Chamber, Zhi Yanzhai commentary, Chan Buddhist dimensions, feminine imagery, citations, and textual annotations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External users, students, and researchers use this skill to plan and draft sourced Chinese-language literary analysis of 《洛神赋》 and its relationship to 《红楼梦》, including scholarly disputes and interpretive boundaries.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Web research and citation requirements can still produce unsupported, weakly sourced, or outdated literary claims.

Mitigation: Review cited sources, prefer academic or authoritative editions, and cross-check claims about authorship, versions, Red Chamber parallels, and Chan interpretation.

Risk: The skill asks for interpretive connections between classical texts that may be speculative.

Mitigation: Label interpretations as consensus, disputed, or conjectural, and avoid presenting unsupported parallels as established fact.
