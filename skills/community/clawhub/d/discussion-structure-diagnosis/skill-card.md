## Description:

Diagnose the structural completeness and move ordering of the Discussion section in an English psychology research paper.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[laninga](https://clawhub.ai/user/laninga)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic-writing reviewers use this skill to diagnose whether a psychology paper's Discussion section covers expected moves, follows a coherent order, and reflects the Introduction. It provides structural feedback and suggestions without generating or rewriting prose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Academic-writing feedback may not match a user's advisor, journal, or course requirements.

Mitigation: Treat the diagnosis as guidance and review recommendations against the applicable advisor, journal, or course expectations.

Risk: The skill uses a mixed Chinese-English teaching style that may not match the user's preferred response language.

Mitigation: Specify the desired response language before using the skill.
