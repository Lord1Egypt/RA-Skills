## Description:

Translate a user's felt state into a color-based mood map with body cues, possible emotion words, likely needs, and one gentle next step under 10 minutes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for a color-first emotional check-in when naming feelings directly is difficult. It maps body cues, color metaphors, possible emotion words, likely needs, and one small next step without presenting the result as diagnosis, therapy, or crisis support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake reflective mood mapping for therapy, diagnosis, emergency support, or professional mental health care.

Mitigation: Present the output as descriptive reflection only, avoid diagnostic claims, and direct users in serious distress toward trusted human or professional support.

Risk: A single color-to-emotion mapping can oversimplify mixed or uncertain emotional states.

Mitigation: Offer possible emotion words, support secondary colors for mixed states, and separate body sensations from self-judgment.
