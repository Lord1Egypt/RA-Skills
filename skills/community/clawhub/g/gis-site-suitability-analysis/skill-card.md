## Description:

Drafts a multi-criteria site suitability analysis report for GIS analysts and planners, documenting spatial data layers, scoring criteria, weighting rationale, weighted overlay methodology, results, and limitations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

GIS analysts, urban planners, environmental consultants, and site selection professionals use this skill to draft defensible planning and decision-support reports for weighted overlay, Boolean screening, and multi-criteria GIS evaluations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft reports could be mistaken for regulatory, permitting, environmental, legal, or field-verified determinations.

Mitigation: Keep outputs labeled as draft planning and decision-support material, and require qualified review before use in site commitments or formal decisions.

Risk: Report conclusions can be misleading if user-provided spatial layers, criteria weights, or results are incomplete, outdated, or unverified.

Mitigation: Document every data source, flag user-provided layers for independent verification, confirm weights before scoring, and include sensitivity analysis for subjective weight assignments.
