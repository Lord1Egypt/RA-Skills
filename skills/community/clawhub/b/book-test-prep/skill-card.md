## Use Case:

External users use this skill to search for test-prep services through Lokuli, check availability, and prepare booking requests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Booking requests can send personal contact details to Lokuli.

Mitigation: Before creating a booking, confirm the provider, time slot, customer name, email, and phone number with the user.
