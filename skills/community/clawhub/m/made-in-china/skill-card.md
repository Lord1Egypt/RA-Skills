## Description:

Analyze China's export volumes, product categories, and trade destinations using official customs data to reveal trends and summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and business users use this skill to ask for summaries of public China customs trade statistics by product category, destination, timeframe, or region.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Important trade figures may be incomplete, stale, or misread from summaries, especially because the referenced official portal is provided over HTTP.

Mitigation: Verify material figures directly against the General Administration of Customs of China statistics portal before relying on them.

Risk: Users may mistake aggregated public customs statistics for e-commerce platform data or company-specific data.

Mitigation: Keep outputs scoped to public aggregate trade statistics and state when a requested platform, company, or private dataset is outside the skill's evidence.
