## Description:

Generate a persistent talking-avatar video with Subclip AI from a saved avatar, a Subclip AI Actor, or a project portrait, speaking a typed script or supplied speech audio. Also saves custom avatars. Use for presenter, spokesperson, UGC or character avatar requests that should appear in AI Assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers can generate a presenter, spokesperson, UGC, or character video from a saved avatar, an AI Actor, or a project portrait, using a script or supplied speech audio. They can also save a custom avatar for later use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Portraits, scripts, or speech audio may be sent for external processing, and disclosure about that sharing may be unclear.

Mitigation: Explain what will be shared and confirm the user is comfortable sending the selected project material before generation.
