## Description:

Generates a short-video production package covering topic structure, storyboard beats, AI visual prompts, platform strategy, and compliance reminders for Chinese short-video workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[boboy-j](https://clawhub.ai/user/boboy-j)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, MCNs, and brand marketing teams use this skill to turn a topic, product, or campaign into a ready-to-review short-video content package. The package includes storyboard planning, AI-generation prompt parameters, platform-specific posting guidance, and compliance/copyright reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated compliance, copyright, advertising, or platform-policy reminders may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review marketing claims, rights clearance, and platform-policy guidance with appropriate human reviewers before publishing.

Risk: AI model and platform parameters can change after the skill's examples were written.

Mitigation: Verify generated model parameters and posting recommendations against current model and platform documentation before production use.
