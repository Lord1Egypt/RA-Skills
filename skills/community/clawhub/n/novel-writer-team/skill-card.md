## Description:

A Chinese-language multi-agent novel-writing workflow that helps writers turn an idea into a structured story plan, platform-specific style guide, chapter drafts, editor reviews, and a final manuscript.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External writers and creators use this skill to plan, draft, review, and package Chinese web novels or short fiction for platforms such as 起点中文网, 番茄小说, 晋江文学城, 知乎盐选, and 豆瓣阅读. It is useful when a user has only a rough story idea and wants guided development from concept through final manuscript.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automatic mode may proceed through the full writing workflow after a short idea, reducing user control over creative direction.

Mitigation: Use the step-by-step mode when the writer wants checkpoints for story concept, planning, and chapter progress.

Risk: Long-form generated drafts may drift from the outline, character definitions, or platform style target.

Mitigation: Use the built-in editor review loops and pause points to check continuity, style fit, and chapter quality before publication.
