## Description:

Helps agents research books and produce Douyin book-promotion video plans with verified facts, audience positioning, scripts, storyboards, audio direction, product handoff, and post-publish review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mxx1995](https://clawhub.ai/user/mxx1995)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, book marketers, and commerce teams use this skill to turn book titles, covers, pages, product screenshots, or reading notes into shootable short-video plans for Douyin. It supports fact checking, audience and purchase-task definition, creator stance, hooks, voiceover, shot direction, product handoff, compliance checks, and performance review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided screenshots, unpublished pages, notes, or sales data may contain sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use public book metadata or an offline workflow when possible, and avoid sharing sensitive customer, sales, or unpublished source material with external services.

Risk: Book-promotion scripts can become misleading if market observations, creator interpretations, or unverified claims are presented as book facts.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's source-layer labels visible: separate user-provided material, verified public facts, market observations, creative inferences, and items still requiring verification.

Risk: Showing too much interior book content or invented pages could create copyright or authenticity concerns.

Mitigation: Use only limited excerpts from user-provided materials or official public previews, avoid scan-like substitutions for the book, and ask for real product shots when needed.
