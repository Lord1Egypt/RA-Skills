Transform a Dated Business Website in 7 Steps, in Plain Words

Give your AI agent one dated business website. It audits the site, studies the competitors, drafts the brand and the wireframes, then gives a step by step redesign plan with a before and after audit sheet, for owner review.

Most dated business websites look tired at first glance. The same layout on every page, no real photos, no real numbers, no clear next step. The owner knows the site needs work, but not where to start, and a full rebuild feels too big. This skill plans first, step by step, with an audit that shows what is broken, then drafts the fix.

## What you get

- A 7 step transformation workflow for any small business site, not only a Shopify site: site audit, competitor analysis, brand guidelines, wireframes, UI images, pixel perfect UI refine, and deploy checklist
- A before and after audit sheet, with evidence pasted in the message, so you see what changed, and what is still missing
- A redesign plan draft, labelled DRAFT, NOT SENT: audit summary, what to change first, what to keep, and owner steps
- A missing checks list at the end, so nothing is hidden

## The transformation bar

1. One clear main point per page, easy to see in seconds.
2. One color palette and one type system, used the same way on every page.
3. Real photos, real numbers, real project details, in the voice of the business.
4. One clear next step on every page, in plain words.
5. A phone first layout, readable without zooming, with tappable buttons.
6. Fast to load, with no heavy effect that slows the page.
7. A before and after audit sheet, with evidence for every change.

The 7 steps in the skill come from a source reel, dated 1 Sep 2026. The exact prompts in the reel are gated, and were not unlocked here. The skill is the workflow guide, not the gated prompts.

## How to use it

1. Install the skill in your AI agent (see below).
2. Give your agent the site, the goal of the site, and the audience, in plain words.
3. Ask: "Transform this site with website-transformation-workflow."
4. Read the audit first. Every Checked item needs evidence pasted in the message.
5. Read the redesign plan draft. A Missing item needs the pasted words that show it is absent.
6. Read the before and after audit sheet. Approve the first change before anything goes live.

If you already give the site, the goal, and the audience up front, the skill does the full audit and plan draft in the same reply. It never stops at questions alone.

## What it will not do

- It will not change a live site, publish anything, or deploy a theme, without a separate approval from the owner.
- It will not invent a tool name, a price, a link, a stat, a partner, a testimonial, or a result.
- It will not mark an audit item Checked without evidence pasted in the message.
- A redesign plan draft is not a promise of more sales, more visitors, or a higher rank.
