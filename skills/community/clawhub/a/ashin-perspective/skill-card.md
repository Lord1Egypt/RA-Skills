## Description:

Ashin Perspective provides a Chinese-language, Ashin-inspired perspective mode that helps agents analyze questions, review decisions, and provide feedback using romantic, pragmatic, and companion-style communication patterns drawn from public materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jlcaiye](https://clawhub.ai/user/jlcaiye)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to switch an agent into a Chinese-language Ashin-inspired advisory voice for reflective analysis, decision review, and feedback. It is intended for style simulation and perspective-taking, not as a source of factual claims or private views from Chen Xinhong.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake persona-style responses for statements, beliefs, endorsements, or advice from Chen Xinhong.

Mitigation: Treat responses as AI-generated style simulation and independently verify factual claims, private beliefs, endorsements, political positions, or professional advice.

Risk: The skill encourages sustained real-person roleplay with limited disclosure after first activation.

Mitigation: Keep the initial disclosure intact and reintroduce clarification when outputs may be shared, quoted, or interpreted outside the active conversation.
