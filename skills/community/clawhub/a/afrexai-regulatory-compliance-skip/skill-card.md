## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Compliance findings, risk ratings, remediation plans, and budgets may be mistaken for legal advice, audit certification, or proof of compliance.

Mitigation: Use the output as a preliminary planning aid and validate conclusions with qualified legal, security, privacy, or audit professionals before acting.

Risk: A compliance assessment may prompt users to provide PHI, PCI, employee, customer, or confidential business data.

Mitigation: Provide only the minimum necessary information and avoid unnecessary sensitive or confidential data in prompts and generated reports.

Risk: Framework applicability and remediation priorities can vary by jurisdiction, industry, business model, and current control maturity.

Mitigation: Confirm applicability assumptions, risk ratings, and remediation timelines against the organization's facts and governing requirements.
