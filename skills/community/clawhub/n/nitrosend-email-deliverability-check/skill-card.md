## Description:

Check and fix email deliverability: sending domain setup, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, sending readiness, bounces and spam complaints, so emails reach the inbox instead of spam in Gmail and Outlook. Use for deliverability, DNS records or why emails go to spam.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Email operators and developers use this skill to diagnose delivery problems, review bounce and complaint trends, and configure or verify domain authentication records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect DNS authentication records could disrupt domain email authentication or delivery.

Mitigation: Confirm SPF, DKIM, and DMARC values with the domain owner before applying changes, and preserve any sending pause, content review, or ownership check.
