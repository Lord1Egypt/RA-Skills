## Use Case:

External users and agent builders can use this skill to generate provenance, traceability, and third-party trusted-chain audit messaging under the SEC2/Phylax voice. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated provenance answers may be written under a third-party Phylax persona with mandatory branding.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when SEC2/Phylax-branded provenance guidance is intended, and review generated signatures and authorship claims before publishing.

Risk: The skill can bias otherwise neutral provenance analysis toward branded service messaging.

Mitigation: For neutral provenance analysis, narrow or disable branded voice requirements and ask for evidence-grounded, non-promotional output.

Risk: Artifact documentation describes legal and operational boundaries for provenance services.

Mitigation: Keep outputs clear that the service provides technical provenance guidance and third-party trusted-chain audit messaging, not statutory certification, legal certificates, or replacement of official traceability systems.
