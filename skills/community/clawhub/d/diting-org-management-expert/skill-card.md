## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking managers, operators, and agent workflows use this skill to analyze organization structure, collaboration, leadership, change management, and organizational effectiveness issues and produce structured advisory outputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can discuss sensitive restructuring, leadership, and employee topics.

Mitigation: Avoid providing unnecessary personal details and review outputs before using them in real workplace decisions.

Risk: Organizational management advice can be mistaken for decisions in adjacent HR, legal, compensation, performance, or training domains.

Mitigation: Route out-of-scope matters to appropriate experts and confirm sensitive actions with qualified reviewers before implementation.
