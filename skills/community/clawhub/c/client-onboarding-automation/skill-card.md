## Description:

Automates client onboarding workflows for intake processing, document collection, welcome emails, CRM setup, task assignment, and follow-up reminders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Service providers, SaaS teams, agencies, healthcare offices, and real estate teams use this skill to coordinate new-client onboarding workflows, including intake forms, required documents, CRM records, email sequences, staff tasks, and follow-up reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The onboarding workflow may collect sensitive identity, document, healthcare, insurance, or payment information.

Mitigation: Use scoped client data, explicit consent, secure upload and storage systems, retention and deletion rules, and role-based access controls.

Risk: Ordinary email or spreadsheet workflows may be inappropriate for IDs, payment information, or regulated client records.

Mitigation: Use PCI-compliant payment handling and appropriate regulated-data safeguards before placing sensitive records in email, spreadsheets, or CRM systems.
