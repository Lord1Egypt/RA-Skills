## Description:

Blog Writer helps draft blog posts and articles in a direct, conversational style, using provided research and example posts to calibrate voice through review, Notion publication, and final-example archiving.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tobeyrebecca](https://clawhub.ai/user/tobeyrebecca)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to draft long-form posts, thought leadership, and articles that match a specific personal writing style. The workflow supports research review, draft iteration, Notion publication, and optional retention of finalized posts as future examples.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may be uploaded to a fixed Notion database before they are final.

Mitigation: Require explicit user confirmation before each Notion upload and verify that the destination database belongs to the intended workspace.

Risk: Private notes, client work, business plans, or unpublished research could be exposed through publication or retained examples.

Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive material before use, and save finalized examples only when the user confirms that retention is acceptable.
