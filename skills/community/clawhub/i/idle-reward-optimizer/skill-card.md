## Description:

Designs low-friction idle, light-interaction, and micro-progress actions for fragmented or low-energy time while protecting recovery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn waiting windows, transitions, commuting gaps, family interruptions, or tired periods into light action plans that preserve rest and recovery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad low-energy or waiting-time productivity requests where rest is more appropriate.

Mitigation: Keep recommendations gentle, prioritize leave-blank and restorative options, and avoid pressuring users to optimize every spare minute.

Risk: Users may mistake the guidance for reminders, schedules, or automation.

Mitigation: State that outputs are descriptive text only and do not create reminders, automate schedules, access accounts, or modify local data.
