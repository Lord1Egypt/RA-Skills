## Description:

Provides Portuguese-language guidance on Brazilian public-sector accounting, including MCASP, NBC TSP, SICONFI, SINC, LRF, public-accountability filings, fiscal reports, budget revenue and expenditure, and public-sector financial statements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External users, public-sector accounting professionals, and agents serving Brazilian municipal finance workflows use this skill to answer questions, prepare structured guidance, and check obligations around public accounting, fiscal reports, transparency, and accountability submissions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill gives regulatory and fiscal-deadline guidance that can become outdated or vary by TCE, STN, CFC, entity type, or current filing calendar.

Mitigation: Verify current deadlines, legal requirements, and filing instructions with the applicable TCE, STN, CFC, or other official source before relying on outputs for filings or formal decisions.
