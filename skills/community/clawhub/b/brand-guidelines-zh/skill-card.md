## Description:

Generates complete Chinese brand guideline documents covering logo usage, color systems, typography, image style, and brand voice for brand teams and creative agencies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[olina1ye](https://clawhub.ai/user/olina1ye)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand managers, marketing directors, design leads, and creative agencies use this skill to draft complete brand guideline manuals that align logo usage, color systems, typography, imagery, and brand voice. It supports both new brand identity creation and documentation of existing brand standards.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated brand claims or positioning statements may be inaccurate or misleading if used without review.

Mitigation: Review the generated brand manual before external sharing and confirm all factual claims, audience descriptions, and brand promises.

Risk: Recommended fonts, logo rules, or color usage may conflict with existing brand or licensing requirements.

Mitigation: Confirm font licensing, logo usage permissions, and color specifications against the organization's approved brand assets.
