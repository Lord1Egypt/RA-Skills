## Use Case:

Parents or authorized guardians use this skill to set up and operate parental controls on Windows child devices, including app and website restrictions, time limits, lock-screen enforcement, and usage reporting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Monitoring and reports may expose child activity data or be used without appropriate consent.

Mitigation: Use only on devices you are legally authorized to manage, disclose monitoring where required, and confirm where reports are stored or sent.

Risk: Application blocking, site filtering, and lock-screen enforcement can prevent legitimate device use or recovery.

Mitigation: Test restrictions before relying on them, document recovery steps, and confirm how to disable limits before deployment.

Risk: Web-based remote controls may allow unauthorized changes if access is poorly protected.

Mitigation: Protect the management console with strong authentication, restrict network exposure, and review access regularly.
