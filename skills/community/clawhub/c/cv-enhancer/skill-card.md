## Description:

Optimizes Chinese resumes for ATS screening with STAR-style achievement bullets, quantified results, keyword alignment, and HR-friendly phrasing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huajianjiu000](https://clawhub.ai/user/huajianjiu000)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External job seekers use this skill to improve Chinese resume content for a target role or industry. It helps rewrite resume sections with clearer structure, ATS-aware keywords, STAR framing, quantified achievements, and market-appropriate professional wording.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume improvements may introduce inaccurate or overstated achievements, especially quantified outcomes.

Mitigation: Review all generated resume content for factual accuracy before using it in applications.

Risk: Resume optimization requires personal career information that may include unnecessary sensitive details.

Mitigation: Share only the resume details needed for the target role and omit unnecessary personal information.
