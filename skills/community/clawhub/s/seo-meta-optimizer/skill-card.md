## Description:

Generate and optimize meta tags, Open Graph tags, Twitter Cards, and JSON-LD structured data for web pages to improve SEO and social sharing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[noahcraft-open](https://clawhub.ai/user/noahcraft-open)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, site owners, and content teams use this skill to generate or audit copy-paste SEO metadata, social sharing tags, and JSON-LD structured data for web pages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated SEO tags or structured data may contain inaccurate URLs, images, locale, prices, dates, or claims if the provided page details are wrong or incomplete.

Mitigation: Review generated tags and JSON-LD before publishing, especially canonical URLs, image metadata, locale, product details, dates, and structured-data claims.
