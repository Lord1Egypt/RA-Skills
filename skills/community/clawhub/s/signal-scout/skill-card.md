## Description:

Turn a startup URL or product description into an evidence-backed, source-verified shortlist of first customers (people to message), market segments (audiences to target with content), and companies (partners to pitch) — using public signals only.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orensegal](https://clawhub.ai/user/orensegal)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Founders and product or growth teams use Signal Scout to identify public, evidence-backed potential early customers, audience segments, and partnership targets, then plan respectful manual outreach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags network-bypass guidance and a private-address option, which may conflict with host network controls.

Mitigation: Review these options before use; leave them disabled unless an authorized administrator approves the access and destination scope.

Risk: The scan is suspicious with medium confidence and was partial because one script could not be fully analyzed.

Mitigation: Inspect the unanalyzed script and review generated outputs before relying on them; independently verify the cited public sources.

Risk: The scanner reports capabilities or output boundaries that do not fully match the stated research purpose.

Mitigation: Keep research to public, intentionally shared professional or business information and check generated files against that scope.
