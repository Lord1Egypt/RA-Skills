## Description: <br>
Career Counselor provides Chinese-language career planning guidance for university graduates, including major-to-role analysis, job direction selection, city choice, job-search tactics, multi-year contingency planning, and support for career uncertainty. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill to help Chinese university graduates explore career directions, compare offers, validate planned paths, choose cities, and turn uncertain goals into practical next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The release security summary flags instructions that may rewrite skill reference files from web search results without clear user approval or review. <br>
Mitigation: Run the skill where installed skill files cannot be modified automatically, or require explicit review before any reference-file updates are accepted. <br>
Risk: The skill may ask about family finances, education background, and career constraints to tailor advice. <br>
Mitigation: Collect only information needed for the current counseling task and avoid storing personal details unless the user explicitly requests it. <br>
Risk: Career, salary, policy, and job-market guidance can become stale or vary by city, school, and user background. <br>
Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive claims with current sources and present recommendations as decision support rather than guaranteed outcomes. <br>
