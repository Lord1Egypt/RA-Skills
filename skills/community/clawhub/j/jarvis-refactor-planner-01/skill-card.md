## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to plan refactoring work with measurable success criteria, quality gates, rollback points, and implementation-ready next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad trigger language and may activate for ordinary planning tasks.

Mitigation: Use narrower routing or invoke it explicitly when a refactor-focused plan is needed.

Risk: Planning guidance can be incomplete or mismatched to the actual codebase if the available artifacts are thin.

Mitigation: Review the generated plan against the current code, tests, logs, and constraints before implementation.
