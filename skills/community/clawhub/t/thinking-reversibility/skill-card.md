## Description:

Before heavy deliberation, classify the decision as cheap or costly to undo; decide two-way doors fast and stage one-way doors to preserve options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tjboudreaux](https://clawhub.ai/user/tjboudreaux)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, product leads, and other decision owners use this skill to classify choices by undo cost, choose an appropriate deliberation depth, and redesign high-lock-in moves as staged experiments when possible.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may treat an irreversible, security-sensitive, data-destructive, financial, or public commitment as a low-cost reversible decision.

Mitigation: Require explicit confirmation and normal review gates for irreversible changes, security decisions, data deletion, financial commitments, and public commitments.
