## Description:

Extract evidence-backed design methods from an architect's writings, interviews, drawings, and built works, then apply those methods critically to a current architectural project.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[caninlew](https://clawhub.ai/user/caninlew)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Architects, designers, researchers, and agents use this skill to build evidence-backed architect method profiles, compare approaches, apply selected methods to a current project, or audit weak methodological claims without copying signature style.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Architectural research or critique may be mistaken for code, compliance, buildability, or engineering approval.

Mitigation: Review outputs as professional design support and verify decisions with the project architect, consultants, client, or authority having jurisdiction.

Risk: Project folders or documents provided to the agent may contain sensitive design, client, or site information.

Mitigation: Provide only documents intended for the research task and review generated profiles or critiques before sharing.
