## Description:

Systematically identifies, scores, and prioritizes technical debt across codebases with impact analysis and remediation roadmaps for engineering teams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Engineering managers, CTOs, tech leads, and agencies use this skill to turn system descriptions and known pain points into a prioritized technical-debt audit with cost estimates and a remediation roadmap.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive repository, architecture, staffing, or business-cost details while requesting an audit.

Mitigation: Limit prompts to information approved for the target agent environment, and redact secrets, customer data, private incidents, and confidential financial figures.

Risk: The artifact includes outbound AfrexAI links and a paid context-pack mention.

Mitigation: Review those links as ordinary marketing before publication or deployment.
