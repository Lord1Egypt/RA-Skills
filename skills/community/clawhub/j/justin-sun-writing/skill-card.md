## Description:

Create or revise restrained Chinese first-person narratives using credible concrete details, narrator blind spots, recurring objects, and relationship-driven tension for original fiction or supplied personal material, while avoiding impersonation or claims about real people.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External writers and agents use this skill to draft, revise, or outline restrained Chinese first-person fiction from original prompts or user-supplied personal material while avoiding impersonation and unsupported claims about real people.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mutable main-branch installation URLs can change after review.

Mitigation: Prefer a reviewed registry release or a pinned commit before installation.

Risk: A writing skill named after a living person can invite impersonation or unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Use the skill for original fiction or supplied material only; fictionalize identifiable people and avoid unverified claims.

Risk: Narrative prose can introduce false precision when used with nonfiction material.

Mitigation: Use exact facts and numbers only when supplied or verifiable, and avoid inventing nonfiction details.
