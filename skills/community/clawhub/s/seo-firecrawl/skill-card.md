Developers and SEO practitioners use this skill to crawl, map, scrape, and search websites through Firecrawl MCP for audits, content inventory, broken-link checks, and site structure analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crawled URLs, page content, metadata, screenshots, and search results may be processed by Firecrawl.

Mitigation: Crawl only authorized public or approved sites, and avoid internal, staging, authenticated, or secrets-bearing pages unless authorization and Firecrawl data handling terms have been reviewed.

Risk: Large crawls can consume Firecrawl credits or encounter service rate limits.

Mitigation: Estimate page counts and credit use before broad crawls, set conservative limits, and reduce crawl scope or retry later when rate limited.
