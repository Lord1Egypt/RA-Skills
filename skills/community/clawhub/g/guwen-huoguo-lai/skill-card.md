## Description:

Creates Chinese-language AI short-video scripts that turn junior-middle-school classical Chinese texts and historical figures into publishable scripts using hooks, four-act structure, debate-style framing, shot planning, and publishing notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Educators, video creators, and Chinese-language learning teams use this skill to convert classical Chinese curriculum texts into short-video scripts with titles, hooks, narrative structure, original-text study cards, storyboard directions, AI visual prompts, and publishing guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs may include Chinese-only wording and promotional publishing elements such as campaign hashtags, an account mention, or a branded follow line.

Mitigation: Review generated materials before reuse and remove or adapt branded publishing text when neutral educational materials are required.

Risk: Generated educational scripts could distort classical text meanings or add unsupported historical details.

Mitigation: Check translations, study cards, and historical claims against trusted curriculum or primary-source references before publication.
