## Use Case:

Short-form video operators, marketers, and analysts use this skill to search public Douyin videos and creators, inspect posts and comments, track trending topics, and organize findings for content planning, competitor analysis, sentiment review, or lead research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and Douyin links are sent to the third-party API at guaikei.com; the security evidence also notes that scan coverage was partial.

Mitigation: Submit only queries and public links appropriate to share with that provider, and account for the limited scan coverage when deciding whether to install.
