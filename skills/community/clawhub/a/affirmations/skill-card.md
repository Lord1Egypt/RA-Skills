## Use Case:

External users use this skill to receive, create, personalize, and track affirmations for daily practice and situational mindset support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local affirmation files may contain sensitive personal notes, preferences, focus areas, favorites, and practice history.

Mitigation: Review and delete files under ~/affirmations/ as needed, and avoid storing details there that should not persist locally.

Risk: Affirmation guidance may not fit the user's needs or style.

Mitigation: Configure focus areas and delivery style, and revise or discard affirmations that do not resonate.
