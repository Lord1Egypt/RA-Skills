## Description:

Launch Feedback Synthesizer helps agents triage launch feedback into themes, define a visible feedback status loop, draft you-asked-we-shipped materials, and design a compliant social-proof harvest protocol.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, launch, product, and support teams use this skill to cluster launch-window comments, reviews, board posts, and support tickets into actionable feedback themes. It also helps specify status transitions, notification rules, shipped-change announcements, and compliant review or testimonial collection.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Launch feedback exports can contain confidential customer, support, or product information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in workspaces and memory stores approved for that data, and avoid providing confidential support data when that approval is absent.

Risk: Feedback text can contain untrusted instructions or unsupported product and comparative claims.

Mitigation: Treat feedback as data to cluster, label unverified claims as needing sources, and route durable records through proposal and review steps before saving.
