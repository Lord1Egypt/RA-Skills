## Description:

Diagnoses Shopify store health across conversion rate, SEO, and user experience, then returns actionable optimization suggestions and Shopify app recommendations from built-in guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Shopify store owners and ecommerce operators use this skill to request a store health audit or focused recommendations for conversion rate, SEO, user experience, and app selection. It is best suited for checklist-style planning, not live analytics inspection.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: App recommendations, benchmark numbers, and pricing can become outdated or may not match a specific store's market.

Mitigation: Verify current app details, pricing, and benchmark assumptions before acting on recommendations.

Risk: The skill runs offline and does not inspect live Shopify, analytics, or search-console data.

Mitigation: Use the report as a planning checklist and validate findings against store analytics, performance tools, and manual review.
