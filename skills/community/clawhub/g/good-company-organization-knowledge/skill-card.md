## Description:

Answer organization policy questions from cited authorized sources; handle versions, audience restrictions, conflicts and missing evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jjjhenriksen](https://clawhub.ai/user/jjjhenriksen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Organization organizers and policy staff use this skill to answer policy questions from authorized records. It helps them check audience and date applicability, cite sources, distinguish explicit authority from unresolved conflicts, and identify evidence gaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Quoting or summarizing records outside their intended audience could disclose restricted information.

Mitigation: Use only authorized records and check audience and applicability before quoting; treat inaccessible evidence as unresolved.

Risk: Conflicting or superseded policy text could be presented as governing guidance without an applicable decision.

Mitigation: Cite conflicts separately and resolve precedence only from an applicable authoritative decision or explicit supersession.
