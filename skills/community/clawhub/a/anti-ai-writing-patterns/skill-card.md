## Description:

Rules to avoid identifiable AI writing patterns. Apply to all text output regardless of model or context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[juanbastias](https://clawhub.ai/user/juanbastias)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to make agent-written text follow explicit prose-style rules that reduce common AI writing tells across messages, reports, summaries, emails, and analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill claims control over all agent output and may suppress useful limitation disclosures.

Mitigation: Use it only for sessions where a strong prose-style filter is wanted, and preserve required safety, citation, currentness, legal, compliance, and template language.
