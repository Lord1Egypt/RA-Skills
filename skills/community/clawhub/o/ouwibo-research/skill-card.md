## Description:

Conducts web research for factual or time-sensitive questions, summarizes findings from reliable sources, and includes cited URLs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ouwibo](https://clawhub.ai/user/ouwibo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agent users use this skill when they need concise, source-backed answers to factual questions that may require current web information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Web research can surface stale, low-quality, or conflicting public sources.

Mitigation: Prefer high-quality sources, cite URLs for referenced claims, and briefly note disagreements between sources.

Risk: Browsing for factual questions may disclose query text to external websites or search services.

Mitigation: Avoid including private, confidential, or credential-like information in research queries.
