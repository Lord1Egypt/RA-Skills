## Description:

Creates a talking-avatar video from a portrait and short script or supplied speech track, with narration preparation and lip-sync review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and teams can turn an authorized portrait and script or recording into a presenter video for explainers, lessons, announcements, onboarding, and social content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
