## Description:

Guides mining, beneficiation, and smelting technical-service partners through contract model selection, five red-line checks, and a contract outline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fethan56](https://clawhub.ai/user/fethan56)

### License/Terms of Use:

Proprietary License

## Use Case:

Mining and mineral-processing technical-service providers use the skill to choose among standard, buyout, equity, and staged-consulting arrangements, review draft contracts for five common risk areas, and prepare a clause outline and attachment checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake contract review guidance for legal advice or rely on it as sufficient for signing.

Mitigation: Use the output as a negotiation reference and have a licensed lawyer and relevant specialists review the contract before signing.
