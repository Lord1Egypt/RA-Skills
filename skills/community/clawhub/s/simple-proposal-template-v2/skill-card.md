## Description:

Simple Proposal Template helps freelancers create basic, professional client proposals with essential sections such as a cover letter, project summary, timeline, investment, and next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Freelancers and independent service providers use this skill to draft quick, simple client proposals with a single price point and standard proposal sections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated proposal wording, pricing, payment terms, or upsell language may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a client.

Mitigation: Review and tailor all proposal content, pricing, payment terms, and upgrade mentions before sending anything to a client.
