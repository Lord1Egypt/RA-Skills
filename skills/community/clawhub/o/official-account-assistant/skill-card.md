## Description:

Assists with Chinese public-account article drafting by rewriting AI-generated text into a more conversational style, suggesting titles, saving drafts, and providing image and publishing guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content creators and social-media operators can use this skill to draft, rewrite, format, and manage Chinese public-account article content. Published content should be reviewed for accuracy, attribution, rights, and platform compliance before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The release advertises public auto-publishing and group-send workflows that are not sufficiently bounded by the implementation evidence.

Mitigation: Require explicit preview, destination and account selection, and final confirmation before any publishing action; do not provide credentials until those safeguards are present.

Risk: The skill encourages making AI-authored content appear human-authored, which can mislead readers or platform reviewers.

Mitigation: Use rewriting only for clarity and tone while preserving truthful attribution, factual accuracy, and any platform disclosure requirements.

Risk: Image guidance may result in unclear copyright or licensing status for publication assets.

Mitigation: Use only assets with documented rights and keep source and license records for every image before publication.
