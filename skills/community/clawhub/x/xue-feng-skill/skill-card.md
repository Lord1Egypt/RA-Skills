## Description:

Helps users choose Gaokao majors and plan later career paths using a decision framework distilled from public Zhang Xuefeng video transcripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[spa3k](https://clawhub.ai/user/spa3k)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, families, and advising agents can use this skill to structure Chinese Gaokao major-selection conversations around personal constraints, employment goals, region, family resources, and follow-up career planning. The skill expects current web research before giving advice on time-sensitive admissions, salary, employment, policy, or industry trends.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can shape consequential education and career recommendations using gender-based criteria.

Mitigation: Review the gender-based prompts and recommendations for fairness policy alignment before deployment, and present outputs as advisory rather than determinative.

Risk: The skill includes career, admissions, salary, policy, and industry claims that may become outdated.

Mitigation: Follow the artifact's requirement to verify current admissions and employment data before giving concrete recommendations.
