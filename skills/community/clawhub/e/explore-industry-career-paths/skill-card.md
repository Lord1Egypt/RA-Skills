## Description:

面向中文零基础用户，把转行、学技能、求职、接单或创业的模糊目标转化为行业研究、能力训练、7天低风险实验和持续复盘。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Chinese-language users use this skill to evaluate career changes, learn practical skills, prepare job-entry evidence, validate freelance or small-business ideas, and continue from prior progress cards. It emphasizes low-risk validation, current-source verification when needed, and clear boundaries for investing, regulated professions, health or safety services, and local business setup.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Career, business, or local-resource guidance can become misleading if applied without the user's country or region, budget, risk tolerance, and current market context.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's location, budget, time constraints, and risk tolerance; verify current courses, prices, platforms, policies, recruiting signals, and local institutions before relying on named resources.

Risk: Investing-related content could be mistaken for personalized advice to buy or sell securities.

Mitigation: Treat investing material as education only, use simulated practice and public information review, and do not provide specific buy or sell instructions or return promises.

Risk: Regulated professions, beauty or health-adjacent services, and local business setup may involve safety, licensing, compliance, or professional-advice requirements.

Mitigation: Use official regulatory or professional channels for confirmation, avoid presenting training certificates as legal qualifications, and stop or redirect when safety, credential, privacy, copyright, or budget limits cannot be controlled.
