## Description:

Content Goldmine helps agents deconstruct high-performing Chinese-language articles into reusable writing building blocks, including structure, hooks, quotable lines, transformation arcs, paradoxes, persuasion patterns, and rhythm notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators, marketers, and writing teams use this skill to analyze a supplied article and extract reusable writing patterns for future drafts and content libraries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Article breakdowns may include private or sensitive source material if saved into a personal content library.

Mitigation: Confirm the destination folder, filename, and whether source content should be retained before writing to an Obsidian vault or other library.
