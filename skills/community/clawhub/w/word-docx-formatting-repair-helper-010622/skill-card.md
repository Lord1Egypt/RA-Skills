## Description:

Helps knowledge workers repair Word/DOCX formatting by turning document cleanup requests into practical workflows, checklists, templates, scripts, or verification guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external users such as office workers, legal and operations teams, students, and documentation maintainers use this skill to clean up Word/DOCX formatting, styles, and find-and-replace tasks and to create repeatable local workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad implicit triggers may activate the skill for general productivity requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the request is about Word/DOCX formatting, styles, find-and-replace, or related document cleanup before relying on the skill output.

Risk: Generated find-and-replace or formatting automation could change more of an important document than intended.

Mitigation: Apply changes to a copy first, preview the affected scope, and keep version history or backups available.
