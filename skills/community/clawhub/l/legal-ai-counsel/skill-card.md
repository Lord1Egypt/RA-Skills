## Description:

Legal Ai Counsel provides China-oriented legal information, scenario analysis, contract risk scanning, labor compensation estimates, litigation fee estimates, and legal document skeletons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for preliminary China-oriented legal information, contract and labor dispute triage, litigation cost estimation, and preparation for attorney consultation. It is intended for general guidance and document skeletons, not formal legal advice or representation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on preliminary legal information as formal legal advice for urgent, high-value, criminal, family, immigration, or jurisdiction-specific matters.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as informational, include the legal disclaimer, and direct users to consult a qualified attorney before decisions or filings.

Risk: China-oriented guidance may be incomplete or inappropriate for other jurisdictions or recent legal changes.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's jurisdiction and facts, avoid definitive legal opinions, and recommend local professional review for specific matters.

Risk: Legal questions can include sensitive personal or case details.

Mitigation: Ask users to avoid unnecessary personal identifiers and limit inputs to facts needed for preliminary guidance.
