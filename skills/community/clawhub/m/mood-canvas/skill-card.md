## Description:

Turn a vague emotion, theme, or aesthetic idea into a structured visual mood board blueprint. Use when the user wants a visual brief for design, photography, illustration, film, branding, set design, or AI image generation; needs mood, palette, lighting, texture, composition, and scene elements translated from an abstract concept into concrete creative direction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Designers, creative directors, photographers, illustrators, filmmakers, branding teams, and AI image generation users use this skill to turn abstract moods into concrete visual direction. It helps produce reusable mood board blueprints covering mood, palette, scene elements, textures, lighting, composition, avoid lists, and a summary brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expect the skill to generate finished images.

Mitigation: Set expectations that the skill produces written visual briefs and prompts, not image files.

Risk: Creative outputs could imitate copyrighted works too closely when users ask for direct stylistic copying.

Mitigation: Use the skill's avoid-list guidance to steer toward original direction and avoid overly close imitation of protected works.
