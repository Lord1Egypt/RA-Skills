## Use Case:

Developers, content creators, product managers, and students can use this skill as a lightweight Chinese-language assistant for routing requests to query templates, ideation prompts, quick text or encoding tools, and quality-check suggestions.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Query and quality-check outputs may look authoritative even though server evidence describes the analysis and scoring behavior as lightweight templates or demos.

Mitigation: Use outputs as drafts or prompts for review; verify facts, code review conclusions, and quality scores with trusted sources or human review before relying on them.

Risk: The skill is a Chinese-language local utility bundle, so users who cannot review Chinese output may misread guidance or limitations.

Mitigation: Use it in workflows where Chinese output can be reviewed accurately, or translate and re-check important results before action.
