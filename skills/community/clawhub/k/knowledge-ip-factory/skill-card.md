## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may request commercially sensitive business, audience, pricing, or monetization context.

Mitigation: Provide only information suitable for advisory planning and avoid sharing confidential details unless the deployment environment is approved for that data.

Risk: Competitor analysis and monetization estimates can be inaccurate, outdated, or dependent on assumptions.

Mitigation: Verify market data, pricing, conversion assumptions, and revenue estimates independently before acting on the plan.

Risk: Generated positioning, course outlines, and content plans may overstate a user's expertise or product readiness.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the user's real experience, evidence, and delivery capacity before publication or sale.
