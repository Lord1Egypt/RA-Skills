## Description:

Designs a public-safe 7-day Skool challenge for inviting member action, collecting visible proof, testing demand, and turning repeated tasks into free templates or course modules without promising results.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External community operators, course creators, and product builders use this skill to plan a 7-day Skool challenge that invites public-safe participation, visible proof, reminder comments, trust CTAs, success metrics, and follow-up product signals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Member proof examples could expose personal, account, or customer data if screenshots or text are not redacted.

Mitigation: Review proof examples before use and require redaction of names, handles, emails, phone numbers, private URLs, order IDs, account IDs, and sensitive UI.

Risk: Challenge copy could imply guaranteed income, growth, conversion, ranking, time-saved, health, financial, legal, or education outcomes.

Mitigation: Review generated posts and metrics so outcomes are framed as participation signals or baselines, not promises.

Risk: Community engagement could drift into private scraping or consentless outreach.

Mitigation: Keep the workflow based on visible member posts or explicit replies, and do not request private communities, DMs, paid lessons, hidden pages, credentials, or private exports.
