## Use Case:

Product managers, developers, and operations teams use this skill to plan and document authorized video translation, subtitle, and multi-platform distribution SaaS workflows. It helps structure PRDs, review agendas, development task breakdowns, testing acceptance criteria, and launch retrospectives with EARS-style requirements.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may be used to plan automated acquisition or republication of third-party video content without authorization.

Mitigation: Require documented content rights, official platform API authorization, and Terms of Service review before implementation work begins.

Risk: Automated posting could publish non-compliant or copyrighted material before review.

Mitigation: Place human or policy gates before public posting and make copyright, platform-rule, and sensitive-content checks blocking acceptance criteria.

Risk: Implementation choices such as watermark removal or IP-pool behavior could bypass platform or copyright controls.

Mitigation: Remove bypass-oriented requirements and document rate-limit compliance, audit logging, and authorized API use in the PRD.
