## Description:

Autonomous 6-agent pipeline that researches industries, finds businesses, and generates personalized outreach - one API call.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External sales, marketing, consulting, and agent-orchestration users can call this skill to research businesses, qualify leads, identify pain points, and draft personalized outreach for a selected industry and location.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated contacts and cold outreach may raise privacy, anti-spam, suppression, opt-out, or CRM-import compliance issues.

Mitigation: Verify lawful basis and anti-spam compliance, enforce suppression and opt-out handling, and require human review before importing leads into a CRM or sending generated outreach.
