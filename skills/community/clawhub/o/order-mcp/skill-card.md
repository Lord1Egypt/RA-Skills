识别、整理和处理项目中的废弃内容，帮助用户审查废弃、下线、历史逻辑、旧接口、旧文档、legacy、deprecated 和清理无用内容相关工作。

## Use Case:

Developers and maintainers use this Chinese-language skill to review deprecated project content, assess references and impact, and produce scoped recommendations before deletion, migration, compatibility retention, or deprecation marking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may recommend deletion or replacement of deprecated content when the scope is unclear.

Mitigation: Define the deprecated content scope before use and confirm any deletion, replacement, or migration step before allowing edits.
