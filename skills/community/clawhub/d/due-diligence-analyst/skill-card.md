## Use Case:

External investors, M&A advisors, and corporate development teams use this skill to generate preliminary company profiles, quick due diligence reports, risk assessments, and due diligence guidance for investment decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may overstate high-stakes due diligence capabilities.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary draft guidance only and require professional review before investment, legal, or compliance decisions.

Risk: Due diligence prompts may include confidential business information or personal background-check data.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive data unless the user's OpenClaw logging and LLM data-handling controls are acceptable for that data.

Risk: AI-generated company facts, risk ratings, and recommendations may be incomplete or out of date.

Mitigation: Verify material facts through official registries, company disclosures, professional due diligence providers, or other authoritative sources.
