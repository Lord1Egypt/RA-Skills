## Description:

Logic Inversion Reflector challenges decision assumptions by extracting core anchors, inverting one assumption into a counter-model, and returning meta-level probes for deeper reflection.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tuobadaidai](https://clawhub.ai/user/tuobadaidai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and strategy reviewers use this skill before non-urgent decisions to challenge implicit assumptions, generate counter-axioms, and prompt deeper reflection. It is most useful when a plan needs adversarial review rather than fast execution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill deliberately challenges assumptions, so responses may feel confrontational to users who do not want adversarial reflection.

Mitigation: Use it only when the user has opted into critical review or when the workflow clearly calls for structured challenge.

Risk: Using the skill during urgent decisions may slow action or encourage over-analysis.

Mitigation: Reserve it for non-urgent planning, strategy, design, diagnosis, or risk-review sessions.
