## Description:

Mortgage process guidance that supports affordability estimates, mortgage type explanations, application document preparation, lender offer comparison notes, and approval milestone tracking without providing mortgage advice or specific lender recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agenticio](https://clawhub.ai/user/agenticio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to organize mortgage preparation, estimate affordability, compare mortgage concepts or lender offers, prepare document checklists, and track application milestones. It is intended as educational support and organization, not as mortgage, lender, rate, or approval advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat educational affordability estimates, lender comparisons, or milestone tracking as mortgage or lender advice.

Mitigation: Present calculations and comparisons as rough, user-supplied summaries and verify decisions with a licensed mortgage professional.

Risk: The skill may involve sensitive personal and financial details during mortgage preparation.

Mitigation: Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive financial details and keep mortgage records limited to the local workspace files described by the skill.

Risk: Broad activation around home buying, rates, affordability, applications, and lender comparisons may trigger the skill in high-stakes financial contexts.

Mitigation: Keep responses within educational organization, estimates, checklists, and tracking; do not recommend lenders, guarantee rates or approvals, or replace a licensed mortgage broker.
