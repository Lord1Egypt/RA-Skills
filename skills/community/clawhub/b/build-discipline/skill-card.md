## Use Case:

External users use this skill to define daily commitments, stack habits onto existing routines, check streaks, and review discipline patterns across sleep, exercise, nutrition, focus, and recovery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may keep local notes about habits, commitments, streaks, and check-ins.

Mitigation: Use it only when discipline tracking is intended, and review or remove local notes that contain sensitive personal routine data.

Risk: Broad trigger phrases could activate the skill when the user did not intend habit tracking.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger phrases or invoke the skill explicitly for discipline tracking workflows.
