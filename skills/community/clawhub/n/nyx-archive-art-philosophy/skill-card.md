## Description:

Auto-learns your visual language by adapting to how you see, what you value, and why you create, from color theory and composition to the meaning behind your choices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and creative practitioners use this skill for adaptive art critique, visual-language profiling, art-philosophy discussion, palette reflection, and exercises that respond to their stated artistic preferences and goals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may build an ongoing profile of a user's art preferences, creative motivations, critique style, and decision patterns.

Mitigation: Use explicit commands for art-related tasks and ask the agent not to retain or reuse inferred preferences when that profiling is not desired.
