## Description:

A comprehensive AI agent skill for retirement planning at any stage of life. Whether you are twenty-five and just starting to think about it or fifty-five and suddenly realizing it is closer than you expected, this skill helps you understand how retirement accounts work, calculate what you actually need, build a savings strategy that fits your real life, optimize your contributions for tax efficiency, plan withdrawal sequences, and make decisions today that your future self will thank you for.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to understand retirement account options, estimate retirement savings needs, compare contribution scenarios, and prepare for informed conversations with licensed financial or tax professionals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat educational retirement guidance as personalized financial or tax advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for education and scenario modeling, and consult a licensed financial or tax professional before acting on personalized recommendations.

Risk: Retirement planning inputs can include sensitive financial or identity information.

Mitigation: Keep inputs minimized and generalized; do not provide account numbers, tax IDs, login credentials, or full sensitive documents.
