## Description:

Miaoji Scene Studio Pro helps Amazon and cross-border ecommerce sellers plan visual strategy with A/B test concepts, platform image specifications, seasonal scene ideas, and competitor visual-gap analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangm-a3](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangm-a3)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers and marketing teams use this skill to create product visual strategy reports for Amazon and related commerce channels. It supports scene direction, A/B test planning, platform adaptation, seasonal content planning, and competitor visual-gap analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Benchmark, CTR, and competitor-analysis outputs may be mistaken for measured marketplace evidence.

Mitigation: Treat these outputs as planning templates unless real marketplace, campaign, or competitor evidence is supplied and reviewed.

Risk: Generated creative briefs may include demographic or model defaults that do not fit the product, market, or brand.

Mitigation: Review target-audience, model, and brand-positioning assumptions before using the plan in production.

Risk: The skill provides visual strategy guidance but does not generate images or verify copyright, platform compliance, or campaign suitability.

Mitigation: Have marketing, platform, and rights reviewers approve final creative assets before publication.
