## Description:

Freelancer Proposal Engine helps users draft client proposals, project quotes, freelance contracts, scopes of work, invoices, cold pitch emails, LinkedIn outreach, rate increase letters, and other client-facing freelance business documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Freelancers and independent professionals use this skill to draft client-facing business documents for proposals, outreach, scopes of work, invoices, onboarding, and rate changes. It is intended as a drafting aid that should be reviewed before documents are sent or relied on.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated contracts, invoices, tax language, and rate benchmarks may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a specific client, jurisdiction, or engagement.

Mitigation: Review and adapt all generated business documents before use, and obtain legal, tax, or market-rate verification where the document creates obligations or financial reliance.

Risk: Client-facing drafts may overstate capability, pricing certainty, timelines, or contractual protections if user-provided details are incomplete.

Mitigation: Confirm project scope, deliverables, assumptions, fees, dates, and client responsibilities before sending any proposal, SOW, invoice, or outreach message.
