## Description:

Free simple invoice generator. Creates clean, professional invoices with your branding. Use when you need to bill a client quickly without complex tracking or automation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and small business operators use this skill to draft clean, professional invoices quickly from basic client, service, amount, due date, and payment details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Invoices may include business, client, and payment details that users did not intend to share.

Mitigation: Review the generated invoice before sending it and include only the details that should appear on the invoice.

Risk: The skill includes an upsell reference to a separate Invoice Tracker skill.

Mitigation: Treat the upsell as optional product guidance and verify any separate skill before installation or use.
