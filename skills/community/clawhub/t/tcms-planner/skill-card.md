## Description:

Generates structured content topic briefs from knowledge-base updates, competitor signals, the content calendar, and performance data, providing topic judgment without drafting content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand-side content marketing teams use the skill to turn product knowledge, competitor activity, content schedules, and performance signals into one to three prioritized topic briefs. Each brief is advisory and needs human confirmation before drafting begins.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
