## Description:

Xungen generates a Chinese-language, playful Shang-dynasty ancestry-style report from a user's surname or hometown, mapping it to clans, states, totems, offices, and lands.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sumleo](https://clawhub.ai/user/sumleo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use the skill for cultural-history entertainment, surname-origin exploration, social icebreakers, and family-gathering discussion. It is intended to frame results as playful cultural interpretation rather than verified genealogy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the generated ancestry-style report for verified genealogy or historical proof.

Mitigation: Present outputs as cultural entertainment and preserve confidence labels for evidence-based, inferred, and purely playful claims.

Risk: Automatic activation from ordinary surname or hometown mentions may surprise users.

Mitigation: Use the explicit /xungen command when intentional activation is important.
