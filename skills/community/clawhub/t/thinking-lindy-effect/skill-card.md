## Description:

Use when longevity of a non-perishable option matters. Treat survival duration as a remaining-life prior, then check domain drift before favoring the proven.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and decision makers use this skill to evaluate long-lived technology, architecture, dependency, protocol, or practice choices by applying the Lindy effect only after confirming the target is non-perishable and checking for domain drift.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Lindy heuristic can over-favor older options when the target is perishable, the domain has shifted, or a newer option satisfies a necessary gap.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's non-perishable scope check, domain-drift check, and burden-of-proof step; treat the result as heuristic guidance rather than a rule.
