## Description:

Sanity-check a greenhouse gas inventory before it goes into a report or gets audited, producing boundary review, data-quality assessment, emission-factor sensitivity, year-over-year bridge, and fix-prioritization outputs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sustainability, finance, and compliance teams use this skill to review greenhouse gas inventories before publication, audit, or assurance. It helps identify boundary gaps, weak data quality, factor sensitivity, double-counting risks, and year-over-year changes that need explanation or remediation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft inventory data, entity boundary decisions, contractual instruments, and assurance materials may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Share only authorized or redacted data with the agent and follow internal access controls for sustainability, finance, legal, and assurance materials.

Risk: The skill reviews carbon-accounting rigor but does not certify compliance or replace legal, regulatory, or assurance sign-off.

Mitigation: Verify boundary, methodology, disclosure choices, and claims against applicable standards and regulations with the compliance team or assurer before publication.
