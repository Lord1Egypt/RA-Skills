## Description:

Evaluate side gigs, product ideas, business value, AI projects, passive-income ideas, open-source monetization, and mini-games with evidence, first-transaction paths, Phase 0 experiments, portfolio fit, and stop rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to evaluate indie business opportunities before building, with emphasis on real payment evidence, ethical boundaries, first-transaction design, Phase 0 validation, and a clear commit/test/research/watch/park/avoid decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Referenced rubric and Phase 0 pattern files are absent from the artifact, so detailed scoring and experiment patterns may be incomplete unless supplied separately.

Mitigation: Ask the user for the missing reference material when exact scoring criteria matter, or state assumptions and lower confidence when applying the framework.

Risk: The skill may surface legal, tax, licensing, medical, financial, data-compliance, employer/IP, or platform-policy concerns during opportunity evaluation.

Mitigation: Use the skill's boundary review posture for those cases and require professional or authoritative review before treating an opportunity as compliant.
