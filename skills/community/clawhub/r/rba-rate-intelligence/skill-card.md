This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to review Reserve Bank of Australia cash rate information, meeting dates, CPI context, and estimated mortgage repayment impacts. The skill is general information only and should be checked against current official or lender sources before financial decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Australia

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial figures, cash rate data, meeting schedules, CPI data, and lender rates can become stale.

Mitigation: Verify current RBA, ABS, and lender data before relying on the skill for financial decisions.

Risk: Mortgage repayment examples are general estimates and may not reflect an individual borrower's loan terms.

Mitigation: Consult a qualified mortgage broker or financial adviser before making borrowing or refinancing decisions.
