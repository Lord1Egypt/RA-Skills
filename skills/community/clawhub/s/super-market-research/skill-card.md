## Description:

Research markets with sizing, segmentation, competitor mapping, pricing checks, and demand validation that turn fuzzy ideas into decision-ready evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, operators, investors, and product teams use this skill to size markets, compare competitors and substitutes, validate customer demand and pricing, and turn market evidence into a decision-ready recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market research prompts can include confidential strategy, private customer data, or credentials.

Mitigation: Do not provide sensitive data unless the workflow requires it and the user intends to share it.

Risk: Market conclusions can become misleading when current evidence is needed but sources are stale, narrow, or low quality.

Mitigation: Use current public sources when needed, triangulate multiple evidence families, and label major conclusions with confidence and invalidating conditions.
