## Description:

Play Smart lets an agent opt into a parody style that intentionally overcomplicates simple answers with academic, business, philosophical, data-heavy, or corrective personas, while reverting to normal explanations when users need clarity or safety-sensitive help.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dox012](https://clawhub.ai/user/dox012)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can opt an agent into a humorous smart-sounding response mode for casual or creative interactions, choosing a persona and intensity level. The skill is intended to fall back to plain, normal responses when users are confused, ask for simplicity, express distress, or raise emergency, medical, legal, safety, or other consequential topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can make fabricated statistics, citations, reports, p-values, or named studies appear credible.

Mitigation: Treat those details as unverified unless independently checked, and avoid using this mode for medical, legal, financial, safety, academic, or other consequential decisions.
