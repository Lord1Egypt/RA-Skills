## Description:

Reviews psychology papers, research plans, proposals, or manuscript sections to academic-journal peer review standards, with emphasis on empirical and common quantitative psychology studies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yunyue619](https://clawhub.ai/user/yunyue619)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, reviewers, and writing assistants use this skill to critique psychology manuscripts, proposals, methods sections, and study designs. It produces structured review feedback with severity labels, rationale, and actionable revision recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is written mainly in Chinese and may produce Chinese-language review guidance when the user expects another language.

Mitigation: Ask for or honor the user's preferred output language before delivering the review.

Risk: Review feedback may be incomplete or overconfident when the user provides only part of a paper or study plan.

Mitigation: Limit conclusions to the provided material and clearly mark issues that cannot be judged from the available evidence.
