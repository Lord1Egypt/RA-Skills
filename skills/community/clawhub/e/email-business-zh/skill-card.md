## Description:

Generates clear, formal Chinese business emails from user-provided key points for client communication, internal notices, meeting invitations, project updates, and complaint resolution.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[olina1ye](https://clawhub.ai/user/olina1ye)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external business users use this skill to draft professional Chinese business emails from concise notes across common workplace scenarios.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated email drafts may contain incorrect recipient names, dates, commitments, or tone.

Mitigation: Review and edit the final message before sending.

Risk: The skill may be less appropriate for non-Chinese or non-business email tasks.

Mitigation: Use it intentionally for Chinese business email drafting unless another use is explicitly desired.
