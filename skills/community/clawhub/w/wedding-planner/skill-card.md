## Description:

Plan weddings with budget guardrails, guest-list scenarios, vendor scorecards, payment tracking, and deadline-driven coordination.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users planning weddings use this skill to organize budget commitments, guest-list scenarios, vendor comparisons, RSVP status, milestone timelines, and day-of coordination notes. It is suited for couples, family organizers, planners, and coordinators who want structured trade-offs and a local planning record.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persistent wedding-planning notes may contain budgets, guest-list scenarios, vendor notes, deadlines, and family context.

Mitigation: Use persistent memory only after user approval, keep notes local under ~/wedding-planner/, and avoid storing payment credentials, full contracts, IDs, or deeply personal details unless explicitly necessary.

Risk: Budget, vendor, RSVP, or timeline suggestions could affect real commitments if treated as final decisions without review.

Mitigation: Confirm before changing notes tied to live contracts, deposits, final guest communication, or day-of coordination, and keep decisions logged with trade-offs and unresolved risks.
