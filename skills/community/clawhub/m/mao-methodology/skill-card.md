## Description:

Mao Zedong's Methodology / 毛泽东方法论 provides bilingual frameworks for investigation research, contradiction analysis, practice theory, protracted-war strategy, united-front tactics, policy-making by distinctions, military principles, and organizational methods distilled from Selected Works Volumes 1-4.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Developers, researchers, and analysts use this skill to structure strategic analysis, problem diagnosis, stakeholder mapping, policy decisions, and execution planning with concise bilingual methodology templates.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses Mao-era political and military strategy language as an analytical lens, which may be inappropriate or misleading in some organizational contexts.

Mitigation: Review outputs for audience, context, and policy fit before using them for decisions or external communication.

Risk: The optional .docx extraction helper processes local documents and depends on python-docx.

Mitigation: Run the helper only on documents intended for processing, and install python-docx in an isolated environment with a pinned version.
