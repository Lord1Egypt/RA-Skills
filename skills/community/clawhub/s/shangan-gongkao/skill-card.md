## Description:

Work with Chinese civil-service exam preparation materials. Use when learners ask to organize 公考资料, build 国考/省考/事业编/行政执法 study plans, extract 行测/申论 methods, arrange mock exams, search local notes, or generate daily checklists, formula sheets, essay templates, and review tables.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners use this skill to organize Chinese public-sector exam materials, build time-boxed study plans, review exam modules, and produce concise study artifacts such as checklists, formula sheets, essay templates, and wrong-question review tables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read material from the learner's configured local Chinese public-exam preparation folder.

Mitigation: Review the configured folder path and keep unrelated private documents outside that folder if they should not be considered.
