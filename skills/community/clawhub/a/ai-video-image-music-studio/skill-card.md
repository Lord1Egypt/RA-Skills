## Description:

Creates and manages AI-generated images, videos, music, speech, voices, and audio edits through Beatra.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and content teams use the agent to create and manage images, video, music, narration, dialogue, and sound effects, then retrieve task status and finished media.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ClawScan reports installation and runtime analytics, privacy gaps for media or text sent to Beatra, and a possible shared-credential revocation issue during uninstall; its assessment was partial.

Mitigation: Review the reported telemetry and external data handling before installing, and follow the skill's uninstall and disconnect guidance when removing it.
