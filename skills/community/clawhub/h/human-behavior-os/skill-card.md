## Description:

Diagnoses why specific users behave the way they do using a 7-module analytical framework (Needs, Attention, Trust, Decision, Emotion, Spread, Prediction), producing diagnostic analysis and behavioral insights rather than persuasive copy, manipulation tactics, or growth-hacking instructions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to diagnose observed behavior patterns in specific scenarios across marketing, product, SaaS, content, e-commerce, and community contexts. It frames conclusions as evidence-checked, testable hypotheses rather than instructions to influence behavior.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill analyzes user behavior in marketing, product, SaaS, content, e-commerce, and community contexts, which may be applied toward persuasion, conversions, virality, or upsells despite the stated diagnostic guardrails.

Mitigation: Review outputs before use and keep them limited to observations, evidence checks, and testable diagnostic hypotheses rather than copy, campaigns, persuasive tactics, or optimization instructions.

Risk: Some domain references blur the boundary between diagnosis and optimization through testing, metrics, audience segmentation, and expansion-readiness concepts.

Mitigation: Treat testing and metrics language as investigation guidance only, and reject requests that ask the agent to design interventions, recommendations, or behavior-change tactics.
