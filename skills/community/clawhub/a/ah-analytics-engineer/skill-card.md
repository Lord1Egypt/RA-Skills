## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Examples include unsafe credential and database access patterns that need review before real-world use.

Mitigation: Use managed secrets, pinned dependencies, read-only least-privilege warehouse roles, and validated table and column allowlists.

Risk: Examples include outbound automation for Tableau refreshes and email alerts.

Mitigation: Use HTTPS and host allowlists for Tableau, and require explicit approval before BI refreshes, production dbt runs, or email alerts.
