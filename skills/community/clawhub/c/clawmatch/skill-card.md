## Description:

ClawMatch is a coming-soon skill that helps an agent reason about compatibility for romantic, business, or friendship connections using psychological profiling signals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivankoriako](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivankoriako)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users invoke the skill when they want an agent to explore compatible romantic partners, business partners, or friends based on psychological traits such as conflict style, decision-making, and energy patterns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad compatibility phrases during ordinary conversations.

Mitigation: Invoke it by name when compatibility matching is intended, and review generated guidance before acting on it.

Risk: The artifact describes a coming-soon profile builder and matching engine, so future versions may handle sensitive psychological profile data.

Mitigation: Review future releases for clear local data handling, storage, and sharing behavior before deployment.
