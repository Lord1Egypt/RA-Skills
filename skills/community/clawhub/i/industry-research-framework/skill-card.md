## Description:

A Chinese-language industry research and report-writing framework for five-dimension market analysis, structured reports, benchmarking, risk assessment, and expert interview planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xk445138381](https://clawhub.ai/user/xk445138381)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external analysts use this skill to structure industry research, compare companies or markets, assess industry risks and trends, and prepare concise or in-depth research outputs with source-quality discipline.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has Chinese-language and China-first analysis defaults that may bias framing for users expecting neutral locale defaults or English-first output.

Mitigation: Ask for the desired language, market perspective, and comparison geography when the user's prompt does not specify them.
