## Description:

Helps agents write, revise, and review Chinese technical blog posts by applying style, structure, SEO, and AI-tone checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leapya](https://clawhub.ai/user/leapya)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to draft Chinese technical blog posts, revise drafts for a less artificial tone, adjust voice, and plan follow-up article topics from an existing blog style and SEO profile.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for a blog URL or sample writing, which may expose private drafts, unpublished plans, or sensitive pages if the user provides them.

Mitigation: Use a public blog URL or sanitized sample posts unless private material is intentionally being used to shape future writing.

Risk: Style and SEO analysis can overfit future drafts to the provided blog material.

Mitigation: Review generated drafts before publishing and adjust tone, claims, links, and SEO suggestions for the intended audience.
