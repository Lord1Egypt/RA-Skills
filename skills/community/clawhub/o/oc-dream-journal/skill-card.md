## Description:

Records, learns from, and analyzes dream narratives by extracting dream imagery, improving titles, storing dated entries, expanding a personal imagery library, and supporting search and statistics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[michealxie001](https://clawhub.ai/user/michealxie001)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to keep a dated dream journal, extract recurring imagery, expand a personal dream-symbol vocabulary, and search or summarize past dream records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Dream narratives, tags, learned imagery, and statistics may contain sensitive personal content that is stored in local memory files.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in workspaces where local dream records are acceptable, and review or delete those files on shared or backed-up systems.
