## Description:

Analyze ecommerce ad spend notes across Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, Amazon Sponsored, and Xiaohongshu promotional feeds, then recommend budget reallocation across channels, campaign types, and audience segments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators and media buyers use this skill to turn user-provided ad spend notes into a practical budget review, channel efficiency diagnosis, and rebalancing brief without live ad platform access.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use directional assumptions when complete spend and revenue data is missing.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as planning guidance and have a human verify proposed spend changes before acting.

Risk: The generated brief does not reflect live ad platform state.

Mitigation: Confirm current campaign, analytics, and budget data in the relevant ad platforms before implementation.
