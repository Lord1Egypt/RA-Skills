## Description:

Ad Copy Gen helps marketers, business owners, and ad buyers generate platform-specific ad copy, hooks, CTAs, and targeting suggestions for Facebook, Google, TikTok, and LinkedIn.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketers, business owners, and ad buyers use this skill to ask an agent for ad headlines, hooks, CTAs, platform-specific copy, and audience targeting suggestions for paid campaigns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill embeds off-platform payment, contact, and crypto wallet prompts while users may expect ad-copy assistance.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation, remove or disable payment prompts where inappropriate, and do not send money or request follow-up files through those channels unless independently verified outside the skill.

Risk: Generated ad copy and targeting suggestions may be inaccurate, non-compliant, or unsuitable for a specific product, platform, or regulated industry.

Mitigation: Have a qualified human review generated claims, audience targeting, and calls to action against applicable advertising policies, laws, and brand requirements before publishing.
