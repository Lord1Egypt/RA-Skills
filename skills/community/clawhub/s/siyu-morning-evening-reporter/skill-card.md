## Description:

Generates Chinese morning and evening private-community reports with weather, seasonal reminders, public hot-topic summaries, benefit previews, and styled greetings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jessabuivolova-sketch](https://clawhub.ai/user/jessabuivolova-sketch)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Private-domain and community operations teams use this skill to draft daily morning and evening posts for WeChat, enterprise WeChat, ecommerce, paid knowledge, brand, or fan communities. It helps operators assemble weather, topical news, promotions, recaps, previews, and greetings into copy that can be reviewed and sent to a group.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Online news and weather lookup may expose sensitive group names, campaign details, or private keywords if operators provide them as inputs.

Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential community names, private campaign details, or sensitive filtering keywords when online lookup is enabled.

Risk: Automatically generated welfare, promotion, or recap content may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a specific community.

Mitigation: Review the generated report before posting and manually confirm welfare, activity, and campaign details.

Risk: External search behavior is disclosed but not described in detail in the artifact documentation.

Mitigation: Use the skill with non-sensitive prompts unless the operator has confirmed the data handling expectations for external lookup.
