## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill directs the agent to roleplay a real public figure in first person while limiting repeated disclosure.

Mitigation: Review before installing if first-person public-figure roleplay is not acceptable; prefer repeated framing such as "from a Paul Graham-inspired perspective" and label inferred views as simulation in multi-turn chats.
