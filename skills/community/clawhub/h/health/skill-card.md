## Use Case:

External users use this skill to receive personalized wellness guidance while staying within health safety boundaries, including referral to healthcare providers for medical concerns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat wellness guidance as medical diagnosis or treatment.

Mitigation: Keep responses non-diagnostic, avoid prescribing treatment, acknowledge uncertainty, and recommend healthcare providers for medical concerns.

Risk: Personalized wellness advice may require sensitive health context.

Mitigation: Ask only for information needed to tailor guidance and avoid hidden data transfer or persistence.
