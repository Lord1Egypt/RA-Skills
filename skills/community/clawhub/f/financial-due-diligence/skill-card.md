## Description:

Generate a financial due diligence checklist and analysis framework for any investment, acquisition, or partnership.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and business teams use this skill to structure financial due diligence for acquisitions, investments, partnerships, supplier reviews, and fundraises. It helps tailor document requests, analytical questions, red flags, and a summary financial health assessment to the transaction type and diligence stage.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may discuss confidential financial diligence materials while applying the framework.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive company documents unless they are intended for use in the current conversation and approved for that purpose.

Risk: The generated framework may be mistaken for qualified financial, tax, accounting, or legal advice.

Mitigation: Use it as an organizing framework and have qualified professionals review conclusions before transaction decisions.
