## Use Case:

External users, developers, and research-focused teams use this skill to plan and deliver structured multi-source investigations with explicit scoping, source evaluation, synthesis, caveats, gaps, and methodology notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can encourage broader web searching and more detailed source documentation than a quick lookup requires.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired research depth and effort budget before starting to avoid unnecessary time and scope expansion.

Risk: Research outputs may include incorrect, outdated, biased, or weakly supported claims if sources are not evaluated carefully.

Mitigation: Apply the artifact's source-evaluation checklist, prefer primary and expert sources, document confidence, and call out caveats and gaps.
