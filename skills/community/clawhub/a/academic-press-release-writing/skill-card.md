## Description:

Academic Press Release Writing helps agents draft Chinese academic press releases from research papers or scientific achievements using a structured writing workflow, quality checklist, and reusable template.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and communications teams use this skill to turn academic papers, research results, figures, and team information into Chinese public-facing press releases with accurate technical details and publication-ready structure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research papers, figures, or team details may be confidential, embargoed, or not approved for public release.

Mitigation: Confirm that the source paper, figures, output folder, and release permissions are approved before drafting or generating files.

Risk: Local PDF image extraction and DOCX generation may read source documents and write derived files to disk.

Mitigation: Use trusted input PDFs, choose an intended output location, and review extracted figures and generated documents before sharing.

Risk: A press release can overstate claims or introduce inaccurate technical details.

Mitigation: Validate terminology, author information, publication details, figure captions, metrics, and claims against the original research materials.
