## Description:

Industry Research Analyst 行业研究分析师 produces structured bilingual industry research reports covering market sizing, value chain analysis, competitive landscape, growth drivers, risk assessment, and investment thesis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ybridge](https://clawhub.ai/user/ybridge)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to turn an industry, value-chain segment, or research topic into a concise investment-oriented industry report. It supports market overview, value-chain mapping, competitive analysis, trend assessment, risk review, and investment thesis development.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Investment ratings, stock comparisons, and suggested positions may be inaccurate, incomplete, stale, or unsuitable if treated as financial advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs as research assistance only; verify cited sources and obtain qualified financial review before acting on recommendations.

Risk: The workflow may use web searches and public market data, so source availability, timestamps, and data quality can affect the report.

Mitigation: Check data dates, cross-validate core figures with at least two authoritative sources, and mark unverifiable data as pending validation.
