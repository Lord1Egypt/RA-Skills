## Description:

Designs referral and affiliate program structure for startups that want help setting up referral, affiliate, or word-of-mouth growth programs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[c0ldsmi1e](https://clawhub.ai/user/c0ldsmi1e)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Startup founders and SaaS operators use this skill to choose a referral, affiliate, or combined program model and receive a tailored launch plan. It helps structure incentives, tracking, reward triggers, promotion, affiliate tactics, and practical next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broader word-of-mouth marketing requests outside referral or affiliate program planning.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is seeking referral, affiliate, or word-of-mouth program design before applying the full framework.

Risk: The skill includes external further-reading resources that may change or reflect third-party viewpoints.

Mitigation: Evaluate linked resources normally before relying on them for program policy, budget, or vendor decisions.
