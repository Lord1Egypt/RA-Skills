## Description:

Provides financial ratio benchmarks with structured analysis, recommendations, and next steps without external calls or transactions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business operators, analysts, founders, and investors use this skill to compare company financial ratios and unit economics against benchmark ranges and produce structured follow-up actions. It is informational only and should not be treated as current or personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could mistake static benchmark examples or stock-related guidance for current or personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational only, verify figures against current authoritative sources, and consult qualified financial professionals before making investment or business decisions.
