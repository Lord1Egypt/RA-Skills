## Description:

Run structured SWOT analyses for businesses, products, or strategic decisions with scored factors and actionable recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and business operators use this skill to evaluate a business, product, launch, market entry, or competitive position. It gathers context, optionally uses current market research, scores SWOT factors, and turns them into prioritized SO, WO, ST, and WT strategic moves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business-strategy prompts and optional web research may expose confidential business details if users provide sensitive context.

Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential business details unless they are approved for use in the agent's analysis context.

Risk: Strategic recommendations can be incomplete or misleading when the input context is vague or market assumptions are stale.

Mitigation: Provide specific business context and review current-market claims, priorities, and recommendations before acting on the analysis.
