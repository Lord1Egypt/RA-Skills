## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is Chinese-language and specific to housekeeping-service guidance.

Mitigation: Confirm that the language and service category match the intended users before installation or deployment.

Risk: Users may treat generated service comparisons as final booking or safety decisions.

Mitigation: Verify provider identity, pricing, service scope, qualifications, insurance coverage, and availability before booking.

Risk: In-home service arrangements can involve personal safety and property exposure.

Mitigation: Keep someone present during service, secure valuables in advance, and disclose fragile or restricted areas to the provider.
