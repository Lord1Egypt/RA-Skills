## Description:

List Growth Designer helps agents plan email list growth with acquisition channels, lead magnets, compliant opt-in capture flows, referral mechanics, and labeled growth targets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, newsletter operators, and growth practitioners use this skill to design owned email-list acquisition plans, lead-magnet offers, consent-aware opt-in capture flows, referral loops, and measurable growth targets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Jurisdiction-specific marketing consent rules may be incomplete or misapplied.

Mitigation: Review consent and email-marketing requirements with qualified counsel before launch.

Risk: Pasted exports or reports may contain untrusted instructions or misleading records.

Mitigation: Treat imported records as evidence only and review the resulting plan before using it.

Risk: Saved growth plans can persist outdated strategy or consent assumptions.

Mitigation: Save plans only after user approval and revisit assumptions before reuse.
