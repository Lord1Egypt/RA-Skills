## Description:

Supports commercial due diligence for cultural and creative companies before investment, acquisition, or incubation, with a focus on identifying inflated assets and assessing cultural IP value.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External diligence analysts, investors, and business teams use this skill to structure pre-investment, M&A, or incubation diligence for cultural and creative companies. It helps produce comparable evaluations of content assets, traffic quality, monetization, IP, compliance, finance, and team risks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The diligence workflow can involve sensitive company records, financial data, contracts, brand outreach, customer information, and personal or banking details.

Mitigation: Use only for authorized diligence work, define the mandate in writing, redact unrelated personal or banking details, use secure transfer and storage, and limit access to people with a need to know.

Risk: Investment or acquisition decisions may be affected if source company data is incomplete, unaudited, or presented without cross-checking.

Mitigation: Mark missing data as unavailable, cross-check platform statements, contracts, and bank flows where authorized, and require human review before relying on conclusions.
