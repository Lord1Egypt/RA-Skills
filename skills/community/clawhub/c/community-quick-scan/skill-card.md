## Description:

Scans a Telegram or Discord community and returns a single actionable fix.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Community managers, project teams, and external users use this skill to get a quick review of a Telegram or Discord community and identify one specific improvement to make next.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may offer a paid audit after the quick feedback, which users could mistake for an independent platform endorsement.

Mitigation: Treat the paid audit as the author's optional service and review the free scan result independently before purchasing deeper analysis.
