## Description:

Professional tier of Seedream 5.0, stronger on fine detail, typography and complex composition. Suited to commercial key visuals and demanding brand assets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

## Use Case:

Developers and creative teams use this skill to generate commercial key visuals and brand assets from text prompts, optionally using up to 10 reference images.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review was partial because referenced material was missing and noted a possible mismatch with the stated image-generation purpose.

Mitigation: Review the CLI and referenced material before installation, and confirm its command and service match the intended image-generation task.

Risk: Prompts and local reference files are sent to dLazy's hosted service, and requests require a dLazy API key.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting sensitive content and review the CLI's authentication flow and the service's data terms before use.
