## Description:

Drafts field-by-field Nutrient Management Plans aligned to NRCS CPS 590 and the 4R framework for qualified planner review, including nutrient budgets, state P-index checks, 4R application planning, records, contingencies, and unresolved-information flags.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External CCAs, NRCS TSPs, state-approved nutrient-management planners, and agronomy consultants use this skill to draft field-by-field nutrient-management packets for CSP, EQIP, CAFO, state regulation, voluntary 4R, or agronomic-only planning before qualified review and sign-off.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may guide high-impact agricultural and regulatory planning.

Mitigation: Keep outputs labeled as drafts and require qualified planner review and sign-off before any operational or compliance use.

Risk: Users could enter identifying farm, producer, parcel, GPS, address, or permit information during intake.

Mitigation: Use operation and field codenames and avoid entering identifying location, producer, parcel, address, GPS, or permit data.
