## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste private or sensitive story material, character notes, or personal data into prompts.

Mitigation: Use non-secret goals and constraints, avoid credentials or private identifiers, and keep any personal information minimal.

Risk: Generated dialogue plans or draft copy may not match the intended tone, audience, or safety constraints without review.

Mitigation: Review outputs before reuse and revise them against the stated audience, tone, length, and project constraints.
