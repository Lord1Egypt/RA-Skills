## Description:

Turn a user goal into a bounded, verifiable mission plan with tasks, dependencies, budgets, risks, and approval points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mdjahid11978-design](https://clawhub.ai/user/mdjahid11978-design)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People coordinating multi-step work use this skill to turn goals into verifiable mission plans with task ownership, dependencies, risks, limits, and approval points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad activation condition may cause the skill to trigger for routine multi-step tasks.

Mitigation: Use it when a request needs coordinated steps, agents, tools, or verifiable deliverables.

Risk: A plan could be mistaken for authorization to carry out its proposed actions.

Mitigation: Confirm required permissions and approval gates with the JAHIDS Governor before execution.
