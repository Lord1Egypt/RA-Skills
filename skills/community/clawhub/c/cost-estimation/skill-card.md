## Description:

Helps an agent estimate architecture costs across compute, storage, network, AI services, middleware, operations, labor, and third-party services, then produce cost breakdowns, multi-option comparisons, forecasts, and optimization guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, architects, and engineering teams use this skill during architecture review to estimate monthly and annual costs, compare deployment options, identify cost drivers, and prepare optimization recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cost estimates can be inaccurate when provider prices, regions, currencies, discounts, or usage assumptions are missing or outdated.

Mitigation: Ask the user for preferred providers, region, currency, and workload assumptions, and cite current pricing sources and estimate dates in the report.
