## Description:

Registers the Dongchedi skill for organizing public automotive platform pages, vehicle information, buying-guide content, and service entry points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mikeclaw007](https://clawhub.ai/user/mikeclaw007)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to summarize public Dongchedi vehicle pages, buying guides, event pages, help content, and service entry points while preserving official links and avoiding private or backend data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be misapplied to private lead-management data, purchase records, dealer backend systems, or logged-in account data.

Mitigation: Use it only for public automotive information unless a separate authorized workflow has been reviewed.

Risk: Vehicle prices and incentives can vary by region and date.

Mitigation: Avoid absolute pricing claims and preserve official Dongchedi links for verification.
