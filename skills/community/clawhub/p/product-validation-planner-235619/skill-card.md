## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, workflows, checklists, and user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to decide whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It guides them through demand validation, lightweight planning, prototype framing, positioning, and practical next-step artifacts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad product, startup, prototype, or validation wording.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly with $product-validation-planner when tighter routing is needed.
