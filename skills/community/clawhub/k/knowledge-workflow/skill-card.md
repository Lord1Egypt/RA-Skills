## Description:

A Chinese-language knowledge-management workflow that helps users collect notes, add tags, store them, evolve selected notes through five reflection modes, and produce markdown outputs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lj22503](https://clawhub.ai/user/lj22503)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Knowledge workers, teams, and content creators use this skill to move notes through collection, tagging, storage, optional knowledge evolution, and draft output workflows. It is especially oriented toward markdown-based personal knowledge bases and Chinese-language content production.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill reads markdown notes from the configured knowledge-base directory, which may contain private or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Configure base_path narrowly and review selected notes and generated outputs before sharing them.

Risk: Generated reflections, habits, or content drafts may be incomplete or misleading if treated as final analysis.

Mitigation: Use the generated markdown as a draft and apply human review before publishing or making decisions from it.
