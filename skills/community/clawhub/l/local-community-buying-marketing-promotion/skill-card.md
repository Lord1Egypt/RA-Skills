## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Promotion copy or campaign recommendations may create legal, platform-policy, or consumer-protection exposure if used without review.

Mitigation: Review promotional claims, prices, limits, coupon terms, and platform-specific rules before publishing or sending materials to customers.

Risk: The included calculator snippets can produce misleading ROI or campaign results if adapted directly with unvalidated inputs or unsupported assumptions.

Mitigation: Validate numeric inputs, document business assumptions, and test calculator behavior before using the snippets in production workflows.

Risk: High-subsidy coupon and low-price promotion tactics can increase fraud, overspend, or customer complaints if operational controls are missing.

Mitigation: Apply budget caps, anti-abuse checks, inventory controls, and human approval for high-risk campaigns.
