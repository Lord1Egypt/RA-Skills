## Description:

Complete startup workflow from 0 to 1, integrating founder, business-model-canvas, market-research-reports, competitive-analysis, business-plan, pitch-deck-visuals and other skills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, founders, and startup operators use this skill to structure a 0-to-1 startup process from idea validation through market research, business planning, MVP development, product-market-fit validation, and fundraising preparation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad startup-related trigger words may invoke the skill during general startup discussions.

Mitigation: Review trigger configuration and scope deployment to startup planning workflows where this behavior is expected.

Risk: The workflow is mostly Chinese-language, which may limit reviewability for teams that do not operate in Chinese.

Mitigation: Use reviewers who can assess the Chinese guidance or localize the workflow before broader deployment.
