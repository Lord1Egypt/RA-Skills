## Description:

Obsidian Master Assistant provides Obsidian setup guidance, plugin recommendations, note templates, AI workflow configuration, and knowledge management best practices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and knowledge workers use this skill to configure Obsidian vault structure, templates, plugins, AI-assisted workflows, and setup checklists for personal knowledge management.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chat imports may copy private Telegram, WeChat, or Feishu content into Obsidian and memory files, and AI tools may process that content depending on the user's setup.

Mitigation: Review the AI workflow before enabling it, limit synced chats, avoid regulated or confidential data unless authorized, and confirm where memory files are stored and how to delete them.
