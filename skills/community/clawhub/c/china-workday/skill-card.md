## Description:

China Workday helps agents answer China workday, public holiday, makeup-workday, monthly workday count, and holiday planning questions using built-in 2025-2026 State Council holiday data without network access.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tobewin](https://clawhub.ai/user/tobewin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, operations teams, and external users use this skill to answer China scheduling questions, including whether a date is a workday or holiday, makeup-workday reminders, holiday arrangements, countdowns, monthly workday counts, and consecutive workday streaks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, for questions about mainland China workdays and public holidays.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Coverage is limited to 2025-2026 official China holiday data, so future-year or policy-sensitive answers may become outdated.

Mitigation: Verify dates outside the covered years and high-impact scheduling decisions against current official State Council notices before relying on them.
