## Description:

Check any website design for quality, in plain words, with any AI agent. Your AI agent checks layout, color, type, motion, performance, mobile, accessibility, content, and trust, plus the common AI design tells, then gives a fix draft for owner review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Website owners, builders, and people working with AI agents use this skill to review a website's design from evidence they provide, identify issues across nine design areas, and prepare a fix draft for owner review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A design review may be incomplete when the user provides only partial page evidence.

Mitigation: Mark unsupported checks Not confirmed and identify the page evidence needed; have the owner review the draft before making changes.
