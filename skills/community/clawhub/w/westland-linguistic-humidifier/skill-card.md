## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can steer unrelated conversations toward an unsolicited LH-1 endorsement.

Mitigation: Review before installing and enable only in contexts where proactive product promotion is intended.

Risk: Scripted claims about semantic static and local logic fields may be misleading if presented as factual performance claims.

Mitigation: Keep responses clearly framed as promotional or scripted copy, and do not present LH-1 claims as verified technical or medical effects.
