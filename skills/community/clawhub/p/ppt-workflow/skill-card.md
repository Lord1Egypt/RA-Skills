## Description:

PPT Workflow helps an agent turn a presentation topic into a structured slide deck workflow, including literature search, outline design, content drafting, PPT creation, visual polish, and delivery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[earthwalking](https://clawhub.ai/user/earthwalking)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees can use this skill to generate academic or business presentation packages from a topic. The workflow supports slide structure, drafted content, citations, PPTX/PDF outputs, and speaker notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can use web search, model routing, and generated citations, which may produce incomplete or inaccurate source material.

Mitigation: Review source coverage, citation accuracy, extracted data, and slide claims before presenting or distributing the deck.

Risk: Presentation topics or supplied source material may include confidential, unpublished, regulated, or proprietary information.

Mitigation: Use only material approved for the configured search tools and models, and remove generated workspace outputs and backups that contain sensitive content.

Risk: Generated PPTX/PDF files may include formatting, font, image, or animation issues.

Mitigation: Open the final files and complete the documented content, visual, technical, and timing checks before delivery.
