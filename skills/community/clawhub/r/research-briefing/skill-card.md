## Description:

Build a focused literature and citation briefing from PapersFlow. Use when the user wants paper search, citation verification, related-paper discovery, or citation graph exploration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[papersareflowing](https://clawhub.ai/user/papersareflowing)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, researchers, and research assistants use this skill to search papers, verify citations, discover related work, and explore citation neighborhoods through PapersFlow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paper-search topics, citation strings, and related bibliographic queries may be sent to the configured PapersFlow MCP service.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when sharing those research queries with PapersFlow is acceptable for the user's data handling requirements.
