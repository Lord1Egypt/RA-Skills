## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The artifact documentation says the data is cluster-level only, but the security evidence reports bundled manufacturer records and direct business contact emails.

Mitigation: Review the bundled data before deployment and require the publisher to remove the contact list or clearly disclose supplier-contact functionality.

Risk: Sourcing guidance and industry data may become outdated or may not match the user's target market, product claims, or regulatory obligations.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against current regulatory, quality, and supplier due-diligence requirements before using them in procurement decisions.
