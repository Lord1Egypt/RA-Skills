## Description:

Emotional and developmental parenting guidance for ages 0-18, including age-appropriate expectations, discipline strategies, parent self-regulation, screen-time questions, and crisis escalation boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents, caregivers, and parenting-support agents use this skill to respond to child behavior questions with age-aware developmental context, relationship-preserving discipline approaches, communication scripts, repair guidance, and clear escalation to professional or crisis support when needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Parenting conversations can involve sensitive family or child information.

Mitigation: Avoid storing more family or child details than needed, especially if the agent persists skill state or scheduled follow-ups.

Risk: Users may present safety, abuse, self-harm, substance-use, eating-disorder, or severe behavioral situations that exceed parenting guidance.

Mitigation: Escalate those situations to crisis, medical, mental-health, child-protection, or other qualified professionals instead of relying on the skill alone.

Risk: Installing a public skill through a package command carries ordinary supply-chain risk.

Mitigation: Pin or verify the ClawHub installer version before installation in managed environments.
