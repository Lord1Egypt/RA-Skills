## Description:

Generates polished executive stakeholder reports with status, risks, milestones, budget, decisions, and outlook from raw project data, tailored by audience.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Project leads and business teams use this skill to turn raw notes, metrics, and blockers into stakeholder updates for boards, investors, clients, or internal leadership.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports may include sensitive budget, client, investor, or internal project details.

Mitigation: Use only information you are comfortable giving to your agent, and review generated reports before sharing them externally.
