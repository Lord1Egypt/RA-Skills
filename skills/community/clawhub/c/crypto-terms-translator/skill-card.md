## Description:

A plain-language crypto glossary that translates technical crypto terms into everyday meaning, context, and examples.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to turn crypto terms, pasted text, or confusing crypto sentences into simple definitions, explanations of why the terms matter, everyday examples, and common misunderstandings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake plain-language crypto terminology help for financial advice, wallet/account inspection, or live market research.

Mitigation: Keep use limited to user-provided terms or text, avoid wallet/account inspection, and state when a term's meaning depends on protocol or market context.

Risk: Crypto terms can vary by chain, protocol, meme usage, or local language, which can make a single definition misleading.

Mitigation: Explain uncertainty when context is vague and prefer concise definitions that call out common misunderstandings.
