## Description:

Use this skill when a licensed forensic engineer, expert witness, or engineering consultant needs to draft a failure analysis report for an insurance claim, litigation matter, or safety investigation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Licensed forensic engineers, structural/mechanical/electrical engineers, expert witnesses, and engineering consultants use this skill to turn investigation findings into a structured draft failure analysis report for insurance, litigation, safety, product liability, construction defect, or casualty loss matters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft reports may contain incorrect conclusions, outdated standards, or incomplete evidence if users rely on generated text without professional review.

Mitigation: Require a licensed Professional Engineer to review, revise, stamp, and approve any report before delivery or reliance.

Risk: Historical code or standards editions may be misapplied when the applicable construction, manufacture, or installation date is unknown.

Mitigation: Verify applicable standards independently and flag unknown editions for PE confirmation instead of substituting current editions.

Risk: Unnecessary personal information could be included in investigation notes or draft reports.

Mitigation: Avoid entering unnecessary personal information and limit location details unless the reviewing PE specifically provides them.
