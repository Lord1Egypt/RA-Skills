## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students and readers use this skill to review, explain, and apply Chapter 4 social cognition concepts from Intimate Relationships in Chinese-language study conversations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat chapter-scoped relationship explanations as personal counseling.

Mitigation: Keep responses framed as study support for Chapter 4 concepts and avoid presenting them as a substitute for professional counseling.

Risk: The skill may be pushed beyond its evidence base into unsupported chapters, citations, dates, or research claims.

Mitigation: Stay within the Chapter 4 scope and state uncertainty instead of inventing research details.

Risk: Users needing English-first or multilingual tutoring may not get the expected experience.

Mitigation: Set expectations that the skill is Chinese-language, with English terms introduced for chapter concepts when relevant.
