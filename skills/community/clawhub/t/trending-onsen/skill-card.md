## Description:

提供关于网红温泉的推荐清单、打卡路线与指南。用户搜索网红温泉或规划相关出行时调用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users planning hot-spring trips use this skill to compare trending onsen venues, filter by city, theme, budget, or time, and draft visit routes with practical notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Venue popularity, prices, hours, queues, and rules may change after the skill responds.

Mitigation: Verify current venue information before booking or travel.

Risk: Route and timing suggestions may be incomplete for local conditions, crowding, or traveler-specific constraints.

Mitigation: Treat routes as planning drafts and confirm transportation, reservations, accessibility, and onsite requirements.
