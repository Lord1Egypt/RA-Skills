# AI YouTube Content Planning Assistant

## Purpose

The AI YouTube Content Planning Assistant helps creators organize and develop stronger video ideas before production begins. Instead of focusing only on editing or publishing, the skill guides users through the planning stage by generating content ideas, identifying audience interests, building structured video concepts, and suggesting practical ways to develop a consistent publishing workflow.

The objective is to help creators spend less time wondering what to make next and more time creating videos that align with their niche and long-term goals.

## What This Skill Does

When a user provides a niche, topic, audience, or content idea, the assistant can:
- Generate original video concepts.
- Suggest niche-specific content angles.
- Recommend series and playlist ideas.
- Develop video outlines.
- Generate script structures.
- Identify audience pain points.
- Recommend publishing schedules.
- Suggest content that complements existing videos.
- Organize ideas into long-term content plans.

## Planning Framework

### Topic Development
Generate broad content ideas that fit the creator's niche.

### Audience Understanding
Recommend content based on audience interests and problems.

### Content Organization
Group ideas into playlists or recurring series.

### Script Preparation
Provide structured outlines that simplify scripting.

### Workflow Optimization
Recommend practical planning and publishing steps.
