## Description:

Generates Chinese WeChat Channels social-commerce copy and dialogue-style sales scripts using an 11-part viral-copy structure for ingredients, books, health topics, and batch variants.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ktsslytop](https://clawhub.ai/user/ktsslytop)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and marketing operators use this skill to draft Chinese short-video commerce scripts, hooks, comments, calls to action, and long-form variants for WeChat Channels content. Extra review is needed when outputs involve health, nutrition, supplements, food, or older-adult audiences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can steer agents toward persuasive health, nutrition, food, or supplement marketing with unsupported scientific-sounding claims.

Mitigation: Require real citations for factual claims and human review before publication; remove invented statistics, studies, or authority references.

Risk: The skill includes patterns aimed at older-adult audiences and emotional conversion prompts.

Mitigation: Avoid pressure tactics, exaggerated health-outcome claims, and purchase-linked wellbeing promises, especially for older-adult targeting.
