# Job Posting Writer

## Overview

Postings that list fifteen 'requirements' hire whoever applies anyway. This workflow writes postings around the actual work — the first 90 days, the real must-haves vs nice-to-haves, and a small work-sample task that does the filtering for you.

## When to use

When opening any role, freelance engagement, or contributor position.

## Workflow

1. **Define the first 90 days** — the outcomes the hire must produce, in order; this becomes the posting's core
2. **Split requirements honestly** — must-have (the work fails without it), trainable (can be learned in month one), and fantasy (delete it)
3. **Describe the real day** — tools, team, meeting load, and the hard parts; honesty here is the filter
4. **State the deal plainly** — range, location/timezone, contract type; vagueness costs you the best candidates
5. **Add a small application task** — 30-60 minutes, directly sampled from the real work, with the evaluation criteria implied by its design
6. **Close with the process** — steps, timeline, and when applicants will hear back either way

## Guardrails

- Never list a requirement the current team does not itself meet
- Never use the posting to advertise the company instead of describing the job
- Every applicant gets an answer by the stated date — put it in the process and keep it

## Output format

A posting: role summary, 90-day outcomes, must-haves vs trainables, the real day, the deal, application task, process timeline.
