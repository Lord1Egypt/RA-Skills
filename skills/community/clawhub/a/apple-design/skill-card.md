## Description:

Apple Design provides Apple-inspired UI, motion, typography, accessibility, and interaction-review guidance for web interfaces.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Designers, developers, and UI reviewers use this skill to apply Apple-style interaction principles to web interfaces, especially gesture-driven components, fluid motion, translucent materials, typography, and accessibility behavior.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked for broad UI, typography, accessibility, or interaction-review requests where Apple-style guidance is not the user's intended design direction.

Mitigation: Confirm that Apple-inspired interaction guidance is appropriate before applying the skill broadly, or narrow trigger wording during deployment.
