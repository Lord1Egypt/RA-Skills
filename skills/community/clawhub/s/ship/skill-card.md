## Use Case:

External users, ecommerce sellers, importers, exporters, and logistics operators use this skill to compare carriers, estimate shipping costs, handle customs documentation, plan fulfillment, track exceptions, file claims, and optimize shipping decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Carrier rates, customs duties, restrictions, claims deadlines, and legal requirements may change or vary by carrier and jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Verify current rates, deadlines, restrictions, and legal requirements with carriers or official sources before making shipping decisions.

Risk: The skill may activate for broad physical-goods movement questions where shipping advice is only part of the user's actual need.

Mitigation: Confirm the shipping scenario, destination, item type, value, timing, and regulatory context before relying on the guidance.
