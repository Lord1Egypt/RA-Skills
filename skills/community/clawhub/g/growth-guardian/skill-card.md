## Description:

Growth Guardian helps parents maintain structured child growth journals across eight development dimensions, analyze conversations for developmental signals, and generate weekly, monthly, and quarterly reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duzhilei951](https://clawhub.ai/user/duzhilei951)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, especially parents or caregivers, use this skill to create child growth records, organize observations by development dimension, analyze child conversations, and produce concise growth reports. It is intended as reflective parenting support, not diagnostic medical or psychological advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may store highly sensitive information about children in local growth records.

Mitigation: Use aliases instead of real child names, choose a private folder, avoid unnecessary raw recordings or full transcripts, and periodically delete records that are no longer needed.

Risk: Conversation and psychological observations could be mistaken for diagnostic conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat observations as non-diagnostic parenting reflections and seek qualified professional help for medical, psychological, or safety concerns.

Risk: The skill can create or update child journal and report files.

Mitigation: Confirm the child, date, destination folder, and intended file changes before creating or updating records.
