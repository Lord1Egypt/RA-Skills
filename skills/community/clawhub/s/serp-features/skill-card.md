## Description:

Helps agents explain and optimize for SERP feature types such as People Also Ask, sitelinks, rich results, knowledge panels, local packs, zero-click results, and AI search summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External SEO practitioners, content strategists, and agents use this skill to understand SERP feature types, assess zero-click and obtainability implications, and prioritize schema, content, and structure optimization.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: SERP statistics and search-engine feature availability can change over time, which may make guidance stale or misleading.

Mitigation: Treat metrics as advisory and verify current SERP behavior before using the guidance for SEO decisions.

Risk: Live SERP checks may involve external search services.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential keywords, unreleased campaigns, or sensitive business plans to external search services unless approved.
