## Description:

Turn raw transcripts into structured summaries, meeting minutes, and action items.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yvette1226](https://clawhub.ai/user/yvette1226)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and teams use this skill to convert meeting, interview, lecture, podcast, or call transcripts into summaries, clean notes, decisions, and action items.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Transcripts may contain private, regulated, or confidential details.

Mitigation: Review transcripts before use and redact details that are unnecessary for summarization.

Risk: Summaries, decisions, or action items could misstate unclear transcript content.

Mitigation: Do not invent information; mark unclear facts, owners, and deadlines as uncertain or unspecified.
