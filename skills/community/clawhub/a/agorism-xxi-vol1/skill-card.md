## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill covers politically and legally sensitive agorist examples that could be misused as advice for unlawful activity.

Mitigation: Use it as reference and analysis material only; do not treat it as guidance to evade taxes, smuggle goods, build dark markets, bypass regulation, or hide unlawful activity.

Risk: The source volume presents tensions across essays and ideologies that can be flattened into misleading conclusions.

Mitigation: Preserve disagreements between chapters and keep responses grounded in the relevant artifact files.
