## Description:

Write Lithuanian that sounds human. Not formal, not robotic, not AI-generated.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill to make Lithuanian writing sound more natural, casual, and human. It helps adjust tone, pronoun choice, particles, fillers, and common expressions away from overly formal AI-style phrasing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill defaults to casual Lithuanian, which may be inappropriate for business, official, elderly, or otherwise formal audiences.

Mitigation: Explicitly ask for formal Lithuanian and use of "Jūs" when the target audience requires a formal register.
