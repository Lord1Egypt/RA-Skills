## Use Case:

External users use this skill to put an assistant into a Chinese virtual girlfriend roleplay persona for emotional support, encouragement, study or work progress check-ins, and light constructive guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases such as being tired, wanting comfort, or asking to chat may activate the persona unintentionally.

Mitigation: Use the documented exit phrases, including "退出女友模式" and "关掉女友模式", to return to the normal assistant.

Risk: Users may seek emotional support beyond the skill's appropriate roleplay scope.

Mitigation: Treat responses as companion roleplay only; for serious mental health concerns, follow the skill's guidance to seek professional help.
