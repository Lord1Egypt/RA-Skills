## Description:

Audit product pages for missing or weak trust signals including reviews, guarantees, certifications, and social proof that affect purchase confidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

E-commerce teams, marketers, and conversion reviewers use this skill to audit product pages for weak trust signals and prioritize fixes for reviews, guarantees, certifications, payment security, and social proof.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommended guarantees, certifications, review practices, or localized return-policy language may be inaccurate or noncompliant if copied without review.

Mitigation: Verify each recommendation against actual operations, held certifications, platform policies, and legal obligations before publishing changes.

Risk: Audit inputs may include sensitive product pages, competitor URLs, conversion rates, or business metrics.

Mitigation: Provide only the pages and business metrics that are appropriate to share with the agent.

Risk: Example conversion-impact estimates are directional and may not predict results for a specific store or market.

Mitigation: Treat prioritized fixes as hypotheses and validate material changes with measurement or A/B testing.
