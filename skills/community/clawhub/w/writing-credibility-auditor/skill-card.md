## Description:

Audits user-provided writing for missing citations, unsupported claims, logical fallacies, weasel words, and misleading statistics, then produces a structured credibility report with flagged excerpts, severity ratings, and suggested fixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[arbazex](https://clawhub.ai/user/arbazex)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, journalists, students, editors, and other external users use this skill to review articles, essays, reports, research summaries, and arguments for credibility issues before citing, publishing, or acting on them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the audit for live fact-checking or source verification.

Mitigation: State that findings assess reasoning structure and language only, and use separate source verification for current or domain-specific factual claims.

Risk: Credibility findings may be less reliable for non-English or highly specialized technical text.

Mitigation: Use the skill primarily for English-language text and route specialized factual review to a subject-matter expert or live research workflow.
