## Description:

Helps agents write clear UX copy, marketing content, product messaging, emails, CTAs, empty states, tooltips, and other user-facing text.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gwsq](https://clawhub.ai/user/gwsq)

### License/Terms of Use:

Apache-2.0

## Use Case:

Product teams, designers, marketers, and developers use this skill to draft and refine concise UX text, marketing messages, product announcements, and emails.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing or UX copy may include inaccurate claims about guarantees, security, customer counts, or performance.

Mitigation: Review generated copy against product facts, legal requirements, and security claims before publishing.
