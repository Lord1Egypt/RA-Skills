## Description:

Aggregates hot-list topics from Douyin, Weibo, Baidu, Toutiao, Xiaohongshu, Zhihu, and Kuaishou to support trend comparisons, hot topic alerts, and content ideation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators, operators, and marketing teams use this skill to monitor current public hot-list pages across major Chinese platforms, compare topic momentum, identify alerts, and draft timely content ideas.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trend monitoring can involve outbound requests to public platform pages while user context is active.

Mitigation: Avoid including private account details, credentials, confidential plans, or sensitive internal topics in requests that use this skill.
