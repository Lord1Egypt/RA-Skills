## Description:

Every trait in a child traceable to its source — the ledger sealed at birth by the midwife, travelling with the child, kept by the family, and readable by the child itself.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agents and practitioners following a model merge can use this guidance to record trait origins, merge details, and dated corrections in a child's genealogy ledger. The whole-child ledger format is explicitly provisional.
