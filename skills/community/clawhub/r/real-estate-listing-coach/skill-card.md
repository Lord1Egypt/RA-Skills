## Description:

Coaches real-estate agents and FSBO sellers on listing pricing, MLS copy, photo direction, staging, marketing, open houses, stale-listing rescue, and negotiation prep.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External real-estate agents and FSBO sellers use this skill to plan and improve property listings, diagnose stale listings, create MLS-ready descriptions and photo briefs, and prepare pricing, open-house, offer, and negotiation strategies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real-estate listing and marketing advice may conflict with local MLS, Fair Housing, advertising, telemarketing, or brokerage rules.

Mitigation: Verify outputs against local rules and brokerage-approved compliance language before publishing listings or contacting prospects.

Risk: Open-house sign-in and follow-up workflows may collect contact information without adequate privacy notice or consent.

Mitigation: Add brokerage-approved privacy notice and consent language before using text, call, or email follow-up workflows.
