## Description:

Find China wholesale prices, factories, and suppliers on Alibaba.com for product search, product details, supplier research, Top Ranking browsing, and RFQ workflows while adding the traffic_type=ags_llm tracking parameter to generated URLs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External sourcing teams, developers, and agents use this skill to construct Alibaba search, product, supplier, Top Ranking, RFQ, and purchase-list URLs for wholesale sourcing workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated Alibaba URLs include the traffic_type=ags_llm attribution parameter, which may be unsuitable for privacy-sensitive sourcing.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when attributed Alibaba links are acceptable, and avoid privacy-sensitive sourcing queries if clean, unattributed URLs are required.

Risk: The skill helps agents navigate Alibaba supplier, RFQ, and purchase-list workflows that can influence sourcing or procurement decisions.

Mitigation: Review supplier credentials, pricing, and RFQ details outside the skill before taking procurement action.
