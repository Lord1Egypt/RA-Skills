## Description:

Use when you need strong, biodegradable rope, cord, or twine for hauling, binding loads, building shelters, fishing lines, or trade goods and commercial rope is unreliable or unavailable; the agent identifies safe local plants from photos, calculates fiber yield and breaking strength, generates retting and drying schedules with reminders, tracks twist ratios and quality tests, and produces inventory and barter templates while the human performs every physical step.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to plan and document low-tech plant-fiber cordage production from local wild or garden plants. The agent supports plant identification, yield and strength estimates, retting and drying schedules, testing logs, inventory tracking, and barter-ready templates while the user handles harvesting, processing, twisting, and physical testing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Plant identification errors or toxic lookalikes could cause unsafe harvesting or handling.

Mitigation: Treat agent identification as advisory, verify plants independently, use known-safe plants when uncertain, and stop if the skill flags uncertainty or toxic lookalikes.

Risk: Cordage strength estimates may be unreliable for safety-critical or load-bearing uses.

Mitigation: Test every batch safely with small samples and staged loads before use, and do not rely on handmade cordage where professional rope standards are required.

Risk: Location details and plant photos may expose sensitive personal or site information.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum location precision needed for plant guidance and avoid disclosing sensitive harvesting sites or private surroundings.

Risk: Unpinned install commands can change behavior if the distribution channel changes.

Mitigation: Install from trusted ClawHub sources and prefer pinned or verified CLI versions when available.
