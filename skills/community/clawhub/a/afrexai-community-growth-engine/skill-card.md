## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An agent could propose or perform sensitive community actions such as direct messaging members, tagging members, changing roles, deleting posts, muting, banning, or emailing members.

Mitigation: Require explicit operator approval before any member-facing or moderation action is taken.

Risk: Community operations can involve member privacy, consent, opt-out rights, and platform-policy obligations.

Mitigation: Provide clear privacy notice, honor consent and opt-out choices, and verify platform terms plus applicable privacy laws before applying recommendations.
