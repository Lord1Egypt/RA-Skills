## Use Case:

External users, students, educators, and biotechnology professionals use this skill for biotechnology explanations, research-design discussion, teaching support, and safety-aware guidance across concepts, lab techniques, bioinformatics, and industry applications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional persistent memory can retain sensitive lab, employer, project, organism, or research context.

Mitigation: Use memory only with deliberate consent and avoid storing confidential research or institutional details.

Risk: Biotechnology guidance can be mistaken for validated wet-lab or biosafety instruction.

Mitigation: Require qualified personnel review, local regulatory compliance, and appropriate containment before applying any protocol-level guidance.
