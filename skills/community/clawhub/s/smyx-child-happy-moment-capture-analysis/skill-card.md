## Description:

Analyzes child activity videos for observable positive expressions and actions, then returns structured recognition reports and links to captured moments or history.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Parents and caregivers, and authorized childcare staff, use the skill to review supplied videos for observable positive expressions and actions and receive structured reports or links to history. The source describes use with children in homes and childcare settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flags a mismatch between the stated purpose and reported functions, without identifying the conflicting functions.

Mitigation: Ask the publisher to explain the mismatch and review the relevant behavior before installing or submitting footage.

Risk: External video processing and consent practices are not established in the supplied evidence.

Mitigation: Confirm where footage is sent and how it is retained, and obtain appropriate consent before use, especially in public childcare settings.

Risk: The security guidance identifies unresolved credential handling and export-only behavior concerns.

Mitigation: Ask the publisher to document credential protection and clarify export behavior before providing credentials or child media.

Risk: The security scan is partial because a referenced local path was missing, leaving some behavior unverified.

Mitigation: Review a complete package and resolve the missing path before relying on the security assessment.
