## Description:

Plans camping and overlanding trips on U.S. national forest and BLM land using sourced camp records, while identifying unknowns and directing users to land managers for confirmation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[davelalande](https://clawhub.ai/user/davelalande)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users planning camping and overlanding trips use this skill to find recorded camps, organize trip details, estimate fuel and water needs, and identify what they should confirm with land managers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review is benign with medium confidence, but inspection was partial; the manifest was malformed or unsupported and one referenced path was unresolved.
