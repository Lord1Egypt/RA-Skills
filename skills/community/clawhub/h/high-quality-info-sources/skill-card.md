## Description:

Build, curate, score, and maintain high-quality information source lists for AI, technology, business, or any topic.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to create compact, high-signal source lists, watchlists, and monitoring workflows for AI, technology, business, or other topics. It helps organize sources by role, apply quality filters, and maintain reusable source systems over time.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad requests about trusted sources or watchlists and produce opinionated recommendations.

Mitigation: Review the output as an editorial source list, confirm that recommended sources fit the user's goal, and adjust or prune entries before relying on it.

Risk: Recommended source lists can become stale as publishers, accounts, and information quality change.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's maintenance rules by reviewing lists periodically, removing redundant or low-signal sources, and prioritizing current primary sources.
