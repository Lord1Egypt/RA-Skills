## Description:

Converts textbooks or PDFs into personalized, multimodal interactive learning materials including handwritten notes, quiz webpages, slides, audio courses, and mind maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ericn26-star](https://clawhub.ai/user/ericn26-star)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners, educators, and agents use this skill to transform textbooks, PDFs, or topic descriptions into grade-adapted study assets such as notes, quizzes, slides, mind maps, and audio lessons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated quiz HTML may render study content unsafely.

Mitigation: Review quiz-generation behavior before installing, use trusted source materials, avoid hosting generated quizzes on authenticated or sensitive origins, and prefer safe text escaping for answers and explanations.
