## Description:

Khazix Writer 卡兹克写作 helps agents draft Chinese WeChat-style long-form articles in the Khazix voice using style rules, content methodology, and a four-layer self-check process.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kkkkhazix](https://clawhub.ai/user/kkkkhazix)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill when they provide source material and want an agent to draft, continue, restructure, or polish a Chinese public-account long-form article in the Khazix style.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is tuned for a specific Chinese WeChat-style Khazix voice and may be unsuitable for general writing help, non-Chinese output, or a different tone.

Mitigation: Use it only when that voice is desired; otherwise instruct the agent not to use this style or choose a narrower writing skill.

Risk: The skill asks the agent to self-check factual assertions, but generated drafts can still include unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Review factual statements against the user's source material before publishing.
