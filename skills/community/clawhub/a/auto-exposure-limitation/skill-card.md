## Description:

Helps users understand why auto and program exposure modes can make photos look similar, explains their creative limitations, and guides transition toward aperture priority, shutter priority, or manual control.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Photography learners and camera users use this skill when auto-mode images feel repetitive, when they want to understand program exposure limits, or when they are moving toward semi-automatic or manual exposure control.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expect camera automation or exact exposure settings from a conceptual photography skill.

Mitigation: Keep responses framed as educational guidance about exposure modes and route exact parameter selection or metering questions to more specific skills.

Risk: The bundled test-prompts.json is malformed, which can reduce reliability of automated testing.

Mitigation: Publisher should repair the test prompt JSON before relying on automated test results for release validation.
