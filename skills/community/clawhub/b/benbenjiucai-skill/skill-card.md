## Description:

Benbenjiucai Perspective simulates the thinking framework and expression style of the Bilibili investment creator known as "笨笨的韭菜" for investment reasoning, decision review, and feedback.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to explore investment questions through a simulated public-persona framework, including stock analysis, position discipline, industry-chain reasoning, market context, and decision critique. Outputs should be treated as simulated commentary for learning and reflection, not real-person statements or financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses first-person persona simulation in an investment context, which could be mistaken for advice from the actual person.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as simulated commentary only, preserve the disclosure that it is AI role play, and do not use responses as trading or investment advice.

Risk: The skill reduces repeated disclaimers after initial activation, increasing the chance that later financial answers are read without context.

Mitigation: Review conversations for financial-advice framing and keep a visible reminder that outputs are simulated and not a basis for real transactions.

Risk: Quantitative mode may use a proxy URL and key for market or financial data.

Mitigation: Enable quantitative mode only after confirming the configured proxy endpoint, key handling, and expected data source.
