## Description:

Guides tracking of AI-driven search traffic in Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, analytics, and SEO practitioners use this skill to separate AI-driven search and referral traffic from other acquisition sources in GA4 and GSC, including configuring reports, channel groups, and source regexes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect regex or channel-group ordering can misclassify analytics traffic.

Mitigation: Review the suggested regex and report/channel-group changes against observed GA4 and GSC data before applying them.
