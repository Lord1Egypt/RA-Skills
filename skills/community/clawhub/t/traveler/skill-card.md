## Description:

Traveler helps agents turn travel requirements such as origin, destination, duration, budget, traveler group, and interests into structured day-by-day itineraries with transport handoffs, dining and lodging suggestions, budget estimates, caveats, alternatives, and third-party service links where relevant.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[onsoul](https://clawhub.ai/user/onsoul)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and travel-planning agents use this skill to generate practical leisure-travel itineraries for solo, couple, family, friend, parent-child, or older-adult trips. The skill is for planning and guidance only; booking, ticketing, ride-hailing, and restaurant transactions remain with third-party services.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Itinerary outputs can include preset promotional travel-service links, including opaque short links.

Mitigation: Review generated itineraries before sharing or acting on them, treat service links as promotional, and verify link destinations before clicking.

Risk: The skill provides planning guidance but does not execute or guarantee bookings, ride-hailing, ticketing, hotel reservations, or restaurant transactions.

Mitigation: Use official or trusted third-party services to complete purchases and confirm prices, availability, opening hours, and reservation requirements before departure.
