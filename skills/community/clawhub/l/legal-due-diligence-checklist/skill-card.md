## Description:

Generates tailored legal due diligence checklists and templates to support transaction review and risk assessment for corporate and investment professionals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Corporate attorneys, M&A teams, startup counsel, investment teams, and adjacent legal professionals use this skill to structure diligence requests, document review, issue tracking, and red-flag summaries for transactions, investments, acquisitions, vendor onboarding, and corporate housekeeping.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat generated diligence material as legal advice or as a complete jurisdiction-specific review.

Mitigation: Use it as informational workflow support only, and verify local law, deadlines, confidentiality duties, transaction strategy, and final conclusions with qualified counsel.

Risk: Generated checklists could be misused to support false, unlawful, or confidentiality-breaching diligence activity.

Mitigation: Do not use the skill to fabricate evidence, coach false testimony, evade regulation, access data unlawfully, or bypass confidentiality obligations.
