## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can guide Create, Update, Modify, or Set operations against Alibaba Cloud Contact Center AI resources.

Mitigation: Use least-privilege Alibaba Cloud credentials and confirm the region, resource identifiers, and desired action before any mutating operation.

Risk: Saved outputs may include operational details from API responses or troubleshooting sessions.

Mitigation: Review artifacts under output/aliyun-ccai-manage/ before sharing or retaining them.
