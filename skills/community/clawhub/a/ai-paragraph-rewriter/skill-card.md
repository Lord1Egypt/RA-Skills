## Description:

Rewrite paragraphs to sound natural and human-written, with guidance that includes detector-evasion behavior.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to rewrite AI-generated or mechanical-sounding paragraphs into more natural prose while preserving meaning. The artifact includes light, medium, and aggressive rewrite modes and a response template for rewritten text plus a short change summary.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be used to hide AI authorship or bypass academic, workplace, or platform integrity checks.

Mitigation: Use only in contexts where rewriting assistance is permitted, disclose AI involvement when required, and do not use it to misrepresent originality.

Risk: Aggressive rewriting may alter nuance, emphasis, or factual meaning.

Mitigation: Review rewritten text against the original claims before publication or submission.
