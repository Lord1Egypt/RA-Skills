## Description:

Provides stage-by-stage Buddhist study curricula by tradition and learner level, including recommended texts, teachers, key concepts, blind spots, and follow-up paths.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xr843](https://clawhub.ai/user/xr843)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users studying Buddhism use this skill to receive a sequenced learning path for a selected tradition and current level. The skill routes curriculum-planning requests to tradition-specific study paths and asks for missing learner context before producing guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat a generated Buddhist curriculum as personal religious authority or formal practice instruction.

Mitigation: Keep the study-only disclaimer visible and direct users seeking formal practice guidance to qualified teachers.

Risk: The skill is mainly Chinese and may be misrouted in multilingual or multi-skill environments.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking for Buddhist curriculum planning, then collect tradition, level, and constraints before producing a path.
