## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trading analysis and trade-planning output may be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a planning aid only, and require users to make independent suitability, risk, and execution decisions.

Risk: Market data, news, and theme strength can become stale or be inaccurate.

Mitigation: Verify prices, sector fund flows, and news against authoritative live market sources before acting.

Risk: Ultra-short equity strategies can amplify losses when discipline rules are ignored.

Mitigation: Apply the documented position limits, stop-loss rules, drawdown controls, and post-trade review process.
