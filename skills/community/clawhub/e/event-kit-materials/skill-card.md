## Description:

Create an event kit with promotional posters, a teaser clip, a spoken read, and an optional jingle from the event's name, date, and offer.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External event organizers and small business teams use it to prepare coordinated promotional materials for a store event, launch, or promotion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review marks this release suspicious because reported credential and account-management capabilities and automatic telemetry extend beyond event-material creation; the scan was partial and some referenced paths could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Before installation, independently review and narrow shared credential permissions, telemetry, and uninstall behavior, and resolve the missing paths as part of a complete review.
