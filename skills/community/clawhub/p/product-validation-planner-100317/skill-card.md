## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, reusable workflows, checklists, analysis, and implementation support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It helps structure demand validation, MVP planning, prototype decisions, positioning, checklists, and follow-up review criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for adjacent business tasks outside explicit product idea validation, MVP testing, customer discovery, or positioning planning.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger metadata and confirm the user's validation goal, constraints, inputs, and success criteria before producing recommendations.

Risk: Planning outputs can overstate confidence if assumptions, evidence quality, or missing customer input are not visible.

Mitigation: Expose assumptions, limits, required inputs, validation criteria, and remaining risks in the final artifact.
