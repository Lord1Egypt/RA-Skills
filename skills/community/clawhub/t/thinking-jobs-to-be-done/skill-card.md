## Description:

Deciding what to build or why adoption fails. Recover the progress users hire a solution for under a circumstance, then rank by outcome and competing workarounds.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tjboudreaux](https://clawhub.ai/user/tjboudreaux)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, designers, researchers, and builders use this skill to decide what to build, cut, reposition, or investigate when user need or adoption is unclear. It produces an evidence-backed Jobs-to-Be-Done decision artifact that names the job, performers, forces, competition, outcomes, priority implication, countercase, and evidence gaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-provided PRDs, tickets, support notes, analytics, logs, or current product behavior may contain sensitive product or customer information.

Mitigation: Use only materials appropriate for the agent session and avoid supplying sensitive support notes, analytics, or logs unless authorized.

Risk: The skill can produce misleading product strategy if the available evidence is insufficient or if user quotes and personas are invented.

Mitigation: Require at least one evidence source, identify evidence gaps, and stop with a research recommendation when the artifacts cannot support a decision.
