## Description:

Provides a daily sequence of Reddit post titles and bodies for promoting the Awesome OpenClaw Resources repository across selected subreddits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sebconejo](https://clawhub.ai/user/sebconejo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, maintainers, or community managers can use this skill as draft promotional copy for manually reviewed Reddit posts about Awesome OpenClaw Resources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could post or schedule promotional Reddit content from a user's account without enough per-post control.

Mitigation: Require explicit user approval for each target community, final post text, linked repository, posting account, and schedule before publishing.

Risk: Promotional posts may conflict with subreddit self-promotion rules or community norms.

Mitigation: Review each target subreddit's rules before use, revise posts for fit, and skip communities where promotion is not allowed.
