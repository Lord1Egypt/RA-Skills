## Description:

Helps agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams turn ontology-style workflow demand into practical plans, checklists, analyses, code changes, or implementation guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI-agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to adapt popular ontology-style workflow patterns into reliable local workflows, reusable checklists, implementation support, and validation notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked for loosely related tasks because its trigger keywords are broad and implicit invocation is enabled.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation when ontology-style workflow support is intended, and review the response for fit before acting on it.

Risk: Workflow guidance can be incomplete or mismatched if the user's constraints, inputs, or success criteria are unclear.

Mitigation: Restate assumptions, ask only for materially missing information, and validate the final output against the stated success criteria.
