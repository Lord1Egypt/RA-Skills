Retail investors, financial advisors, portfolio managers, and fintech developers use this skill to request AI-assisted fundamental analysis for NSE/BSE stocks across stated investment horizons.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can return BUY/SELL-style financial analysis that may be incorrect, incomplete, or unsuitable for a user's circumstances.

Mitigation: Verify recommendations with independent financial judgment before making investment decisions.

Risk: Requests to the third-party API may disclose stock research interests or portfolio-related details to the provider.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive portfolio information unless the provider is trusted and the data sharing is acceptable.
