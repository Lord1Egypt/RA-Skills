## Description:

Chinese short-video script workflow for benchmark-led script creation, turning one to five benchmark videos plus product or topic context into a structured script pack with hook analysis, formula extraction, script and storyboard generation, pacing, CTA, and compliance checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and agent users use this skill to create Chinese short-video script packs for Douyin-style seeding, ad creative, tutorials, reviews, and comparisons. It helps analyze benchmark videos, extract reusable structures, draft scripts and storyboards, and include CTA and compliance checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated ad or product scripts may include overstated claims, absolute language, or promises that are not supported by the video content.

Mitigation: Apply the included compliance checklist and require human review before publication or paid distribution.

Risk: Benchmark-led scripting can drift into copying source videos too closely.

Mitigation: Use benchmark content only to extract transferable structure, pacing, and hook patterns; avoid reusing exact copy, brand assets, or non-transferable creator context.

Risk: When no benchmark is supplied, category fit and winning-pattern confidence are lower.

Mitigation: Label formula-first drafts as baseline scripts and validate them with category-specific examples or performance testing.
