## Description:

Reads public Douyin data and returns structured results for video discovery, creator research, post and comment analysis, hot-list tracking, and content planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[guaikei](https://clawhub.ai/user/guaikei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Content creators, social media operators, and market analysts use this skill to review public Douyin videos, creators, comments, and trends. It returns structured data for content breakdowns, hook analysis, audience feedback, topic planning, and reusable content checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends supplied search keywords and Douyin links to the third-party Guaikei API.

Mitigation: Use public content only, avoid confidential or sensitive query details, and review the provider's data handling terms before calling the API.

Risk: The security record is benign with medium confidence and notes an unresolved disclosure notice.

Mitigation: Review the publisher's disclosure and provider information before installation, and proceed only if the terms fit your use.
