## Description:

Design a support/incident escalation tree - who handles what, when it escalates, and to whom.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Support, incident-management, and operations teams use this skill to design escalation paths, ownership tiers, severity criteria, time-based triggers, and customer communication cadences for support or incident workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Escalation planning can involve personal contact details or sensitive incident information.

Mitigation: Avoid including unnecessary personal contact details or sensitive incident details in prompts unless they are required for the policy being drafted.
