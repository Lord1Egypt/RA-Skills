## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Requests to block apps or change notifications may imply device-level behavior that this artifact does not implement directly.

Mitigation: Treat blocking and notification changes as guidance unless a separate user-approved device or app permission flow is configured.

Risk: Urge logs may contain sensitive behavior patterns, platform use, times of day, and emotional-state details.

Mitigation: Keep detox logs local and avoid sharing them unless the user explicitly chooses to export or disclose them.
