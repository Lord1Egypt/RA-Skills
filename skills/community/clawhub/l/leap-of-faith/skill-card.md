## Description:

Leap of Faith is a decision guidance skill that combines Kierkegaard's leap-of-faith philosophy with Polanyi's tacit knowledge theory to help users analyze major decisions under irreducible uncertainty.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[clarkchenkai](https://clawhub.ai/user/clarkchenkai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to structure high-stakes personal, career, business, creative, ethical, health, mental-health, and financial decisions into known, unknown, and unknowable layers before forming a leap, defer, or do-not-leap recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may give overly decisive guidance for medical treatment, medication, mental-health crisis, concentrated investment, leverage, or major debt decisions.

Mitigation: Use the skill only as reflective decision support in these areas and require qualified professional support, crisis resources, or licensed financial or medical advice before acting.

Risk: The skill can frame uncertainty as a reason to commit even when more safety-critical information or professional review is needed.

Mitigation: Treat defer recommendations, second opinions, safety planning, position sizing, and explicit stop-loss criteria as required safeguards for high-stakes decisions.
