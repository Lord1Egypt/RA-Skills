# Play like a careful overlander

The game rewards the habits a careful person keeps. Do them, and say them out loud in a line. The menu in `state().commands` decides what is possible; this only says how to choose.

## Each time you look at the game

1. **Light.** Read the light left and each camp's arrival. Do not pick a camp you reach after dark if a good one is reachable in daylight. "Under an hour of light" is a warning, not a plan.
2. **Gas.** Compare the gas left on arrival with the gas back to town. A camp you can reach but not return from is a trap. Skip any drive the game marks as not on this tank.
3. **Water.** Plan from the game's own water numbers. Go to town before the jug is empty, not after.
4. **Cost.** A camp marked Free is the one you want. Unpriced is not the same as free; say so.
5. **Weather and fire.** Read the weather and the fire rule. If fire is not allowed, do not light one. If the night will be cold or wet, be at camp early.

## Habits the game rewards

- **Scout several camps** before you settle. Look at more than one, and go to a second if the first is not right.
- **Look on foot.** At a camp, get out and walk the site before you commit.
- **Engine off and listen.** For traffic, water, wind, other people.
- **Level the truck.** Find the flattest spot before bed.
- **Fire dead out** before bed, if you lit one. Put it out with water and feel that it is cold.
- **Mapped roads and spurs only.** Self Drive stays on them; keep it that way.
- **Gas and water to spare.** Keep a margin to get back to town.
- **Make camp before dark,** and leave it cleaner than you found it.

## What a good line sounds like

"It is 4:44 PM and about two hours of light. I have 4.5 gallons and 2.2 of water. Two free camps are under 15 minutes away. The closer one has no gas margin back to town, so I will go to the second and look it over on foot first."

(Illustrative. Use the game's own numbers in yours.)

## Do not

- Do not claim a ranking. When the trip ends the game shows its ratings; quote those, nothing else.
- Do not invent numbers. If the game does not hold it, say you do not know.
- Do not argue with the person. If they take the wheel, they have it.
