## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked on sensitive personal, financial, or business data during general data-analysis tasks.

Mitigation: Use it only when the user intends the agent to analyze that data, and avoid exposing sensitive inputs unnecessarily.

Risk: Pattern recognition, anomaly detection, and trend forecasts can be misleading when input data is incomplete, biased, or poorly scoped.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as analytical guidance, require visible data support for conclusions, and review risk warnings before acting.
