## Description:

Stakeholder Update helps agents create concise executive stakeholder updates using BLUF with status, metrics, risks, milestones, and decisions needed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, product managers, program leads, and executives use this skill to draft concise status updates, progress reports, project communications, and executive briefings for stakeholders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stakeholder communications may be sent to the wrong audience if the recipient list or channel is inferred.

Mitigation: Show the final text verbatim and require explicit human approval of the exact recipients or channel before sending.

Risk: Unverified status, metrics, risks, or decisions can mislead stakeholders.

Mitigation: Review metric values, targets, material risks, decision framing, and the final status before sharing the update.

Risk: Outward-facing updates can disclose sensitive project information.

Mitigation: Use the sending path only after reviewing the exact message, avoid unapproved mentions or cross-posting, and confirm the sent text matches the approved text.
