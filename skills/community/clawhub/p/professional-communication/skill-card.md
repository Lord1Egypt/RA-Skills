## Description:

Write effective professional messages for software teams.

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, managers, and software-team stakeholders use this skill to draft clear emails, Slack or Teams messages, meeting agendas, status updates, and non-technical explanations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated professional messages may include secrets, customer data, internal-only links, or sensitive incident details if the user provides them in context.

Mitigation: Review each message before sending and redact secrets, customer data, internal-only links, and sensitive incident details.

Risk: Meeting recording or distribution language may conflict with organizational policy or applicable consent rules.

Mitigation: Confirm meeting recording and disclosure practices are allowed by policy and applicable consent requirements before use.

Risk: Install commands carry normal supply-chain risk if installed from an unreviewed source or moving target.

Mitigation: Install only from a trusted source and pin to a reviewed release or commit when possible.
