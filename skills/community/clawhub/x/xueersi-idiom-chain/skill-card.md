## Description:

Xueersi Idiom Chain Game helps agents run an interactive Chinese chengyu chain game with explanations, difficulty levels, and vocabulary-learning support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners and educators use this skill to play a Chinese chengyu chain game, validate idiom links, and receive meanings, examples, and practice prompts during vocabulary learning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Chinese-language prompts and examples may be unexpected for users who do not intend to practice chengyu.

Mitigation: Install and use this skill only for Chinese idiom-chain play and vocabulary learning.

Risk: The agent may incorrectly validate an idiom, meaning, origin, tone, or sentiment during gameplay.

Mitigation: Review uncertain idiom explanations against a trusted Chinese-language reference before relying on them for instruction or assessment.
