This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External operators and growth teams use this skill to choose affiliate commission models, recruitment channels, tracking tools, launch checklists, and program risk controls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read local project-context files when present to tailor affiliate-marketing advice.

Mitigation: Review project-context content before use and avoid including confidential business details unless they are needed for the task.

Risk: Affiliate-program recommendations can affect commission terms, platform choices, disclosures, and partner policies.

Mitigation: Review proposed terms, platform choices, disclosures, and anti-fraud controls with the appropriate business or legal owner before launch.
