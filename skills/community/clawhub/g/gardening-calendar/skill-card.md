## Description:

Precise horticultural advice for UK and international gardening.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and gardening assistants use this skill to answer month-by-month sowing, planting, and harvesting questions for UK and selected international growing contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Non-UK gardening advice may be inaccurate if the user does not provide enough local context.

Mitigation: Ask for the user's country or climate zone and preferred units before giving regional planting guidance.
