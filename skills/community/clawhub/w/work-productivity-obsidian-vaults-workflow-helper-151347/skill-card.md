## Use Case:

External agent users, skill authors, maintainers, and teams use this skill to turn Obsidian-style productivity needs into concrete workflows, checklists, templates, implementation plans, or decision support. It is intended for practical local-hardware-friendly work where assumptions, success criteria, and remaining risks need to be visible.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad productivity and markdown-related activation wording, which may cause accidental invocation outside Obsidian or vault workflow tasks.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for Obsidian or vault workflow work, and narrow trigger wording before relying on implicit invocation.

Risk: Workflow, checklist, or implementation guidance may be applied without enough context for a specific vault setup or team process.

Mitigation: Review assumptions, success criteria, and validation notes before applying outputs to an existing workflow.
