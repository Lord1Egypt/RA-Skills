## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to drive exhaustive, source-backed research on complex questions that require clarification, iterative why-chain investigation, multi-source verification, and a comprehensive final report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can drive very broad, time-intensive research that may use many searches and produce long reports.

Mitigation: Define scope, language and source preferences, budget expectations, and stopping criteria before invoking it.

Risk: The workflow may inspect relevant local context during research, which can be inappropriate for sensitive or narrowly bounded tasks.

Mitigation: Limit the provided workspace context and state any confidentiality or source-boundary constraints before use.
