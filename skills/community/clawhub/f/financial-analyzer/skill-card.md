## Description:

AI-powered financial analysis assistant for financial statement analysis, ratio analysis, cash flow analysis, investment evaluation, and financial health assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Investors, analysts, and developers use this skill to analyze financial statements, calculate ratios, estimate valuation, assess business risk, and generate financial health summaries from user-supplied company data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial scores and valuations may rely on simplified or under-disclosed assumptions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as rough analytical aids, verify calculations and assumptions against source statements, and do not treat generated scores as investment advice.

Risk: The documented install command adds unnecessary dependency exposure.

Mitigation: Review dependencies before installation and pin or remove unused packages in sensitive environments.
