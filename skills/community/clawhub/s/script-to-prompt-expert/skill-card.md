## Description:

Converts scripts into detailed AI storyboard prompts for scenes, animation, manhua-style videos, and trailer-style cuts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[303008132-collab](https://clawhub.ai/user/303008132-collab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and production teams use this skill to turn scripts into copy-ready storyboard prompts with segment timing, character placement, action beats, visual direction, and sound cues. It is especially oriented toward Chinese-language workflows for AI video tools such as Seedance and 即梦.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated storyboard prompts may retain source dialogue or other user-provided script content.

Mitigation: Review rights, confidentiality, and intended disclosure of source scripts before using generated prompts in downstream AI video or image tools.
