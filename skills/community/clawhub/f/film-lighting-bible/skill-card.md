## Description:

Film Lighting Bible is a Chinese-language reference skill that helps agents build cinematic lighting, color-temperature, shadow, and prompt guidance for AI image and video generation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beermanzz](https://clawhub.ai/user/beermanzz)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, prompt writers, and agents use this skill to translate cinematic lighting concepts into AI image or video prompts, including key-light direction, color temperature, shadow control, volumetric light, and bilingual lighting terms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Non-Chinese users may misunderstand or mistranslate the primarily Chinese lighting guidance.

Mitigation: Translate and review key terminology, color-temperature guidance, and prompt snippets before relying on the output in production creative workflows.
