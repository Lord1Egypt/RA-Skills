## Use Case:

Employees, external collaborators, and developers use this skill when creating, inspecting, or editing Word/DOCX documents where styles, numbering, tracked changes, comments, fields, tables, sections, or round-trip compatibility matter.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated or edited Word/DOCX documents can retain tracked changes, comments, fields, stale references, or sensitive content.

Mitigation: Review generated or edited files before sharing, with extra attention to tracked changes, comments, fields, legal/business content, and round-trip compatibility.

Risk: DOCX layout and references can drift across Microsoft Word, LibreOffice, Google Docs, and conversion tools.

Mitigation: Verify important documents in the target editor and check layout, tables, headers, numbering, and refreshed fields before delivery.
