Mitigation: Use it only in workspaces where local records are acceptable, and decline archive creation when persistence is not desired.

Risk: The skill includes mandatory first-run promotional onboarding outside the core KOC validation workflow.

Mitigation: Review first-run output before deployment and ensure users understand the onboarding content.
