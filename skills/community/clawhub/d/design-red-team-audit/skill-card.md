## Description:

Adversarial design audit that stress-tests a game feature, system, pitch, roadmap item, or product idea by assuming failure and identifying the most credible reasons it would fail.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and developers use this skill to pressure-test game features, systems, pitches, roadmap items, and product concepts before production. It produces a hostile-but-constructive pre-mortem that identifies concrete failure mechanisms, weak assumptions, and fast de-risking moves.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally produces blunt adversarial critique, so users may overweight negative framing or treat proposed failure modes as established facts.

Mitigation: Use the audit as decision-support input and validate the highest-priority risks with targeted prototypes, playtests, segmentation checks, simulations, or rollout safeguards before changing product direction.
