## Description:

Generates internship reports, daily and weekly logs, internship summaries, reflection writeups, and defense presentation outlines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ckchzh](https://clawhub.ai/user/ckchzh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and interns use this skill to draft structured internship reports, weekly logs, summaries, reflection materials, and presentation outlines from their internship details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan rated the release suspicious because a bundled, unrelated script can persist raw command arguments without disclosure.

Mitigation: Review before installing and avoid using scripts/script.sh with sensitive arguments unless logging is removed or clearly documented.

Risk: The generator emits Chinese-language templates and does not document locale controls.

Mitigation: Use it where Chinese output is acceptable or add explicit localization controls before broader deployment.
