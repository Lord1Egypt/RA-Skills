## Description:

Practical physical childcare skills for ages 0-5. Use when someone is a new parent, babysitter, grandparent, or anyone suddenly responsible for a young child and needs immediate practical guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External caregivers use this skill for practical guidance on caring for children ages 0-5, including safe sleep, feeding, bathing, fever response, choking response, and home safety. It is intended for new parents, babysitters, grandparents, and others who suddenly become responsible for a young child.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides medical and emergency childcare guidance that can be misapplied or vary by country.

Mitigation: Treat the guidance as general support and direct urgent symptoms, poisoning, breathing problems, seizures, or infant fever to local emergency services, poison control, or a pediatric clinician.

Risk: The release requests filesystem access even though the evidence describes mostly static childcare guidance.

Mitigation: Review permissions before installation and prefer a release without filesystem access unless a specific workflow requires it.

Risk: The installer command is unpinned.

Mitigation: Pin installation to a reviewed version or verified release fingerprint before deployment.
