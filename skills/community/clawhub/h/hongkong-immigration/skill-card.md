## Description:

Answers and plans Hong Kong visa, entry-permit, residency, and immigration-pathway questions with official-source verification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[djanngau](https://clawhub.ai/user/djanngau)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and advisors use this skill to identify the relevant Hong Kong entry permit, visa, residency, or immigration pathway and produce action checklists grounded in official sources. It covers mainland residents, overseas visitors, and work, study, dependent, talent, and right-of-abode planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing from an unpinned GitHub branch could pull unreviewed changes.

Mitigation: Install through ClawHub or use a pinned, reviewed commit.

Risk: Hong Kong immigration thresholds, quotas, fees, and visa-free rules can change and may make stale guidance misleading.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive facts against official immigration sources and include the information date.

Risk: Users may treat general immigration information as individualized legal advice.

Mitigation: State that outputs are general information, distinguish legal authority from policy and practical experience, and direct complex cases to relevant authorities or qualified professionals.
