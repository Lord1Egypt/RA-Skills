Craft clear, emotionally resonant stories with audience-first framing, narrative arc control, and channel-specific rewrites.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, employees, and content teams use this skill to shape product stories, founder narratives, case studies, speeches, long-form writing, and short-form adaptations around a clear audience outcome.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local storytelling notes may retain audience assumptions, reusable anecdotes, voice preferences, edit history, or source material under ~/storytelling/.

Mitigation: Use data minimization, avoid storing sensitive personal details, confidential business facts, credentials, or private source material, and review local notes periodically.

Risk: Narrative drafts could overstate claims, fabricate facts or testimonials, or imply outcomes that evidence does not support.

Mitigation: Keep claims tied to supplied evidence and use domain, factual, or legal review when accuracy requirements are material.
