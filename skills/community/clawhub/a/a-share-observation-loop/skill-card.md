## Description:

把结论落成每日检查清单、观察时点、通过失败动作和场景切换条件。适用于用户要求把观察项落下来、每天去看、或希望把研究结论转成重复执行的盘中与收盘检查框架。

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to turn A-share market research, portfolio context, and market views into a repeatable daily observation checklist. It helps structure pre-market, intraday, end-of-day, and post-market checks with clear pass and fail actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the checklist as broker automation or final investment advice.

Mitigation: Present it as a planning and observation aid, verify market data independently, and keep final investment decisions user-directed.

Risk: Observation items can become stale when current A-share market data is missing or the market scenario changes.

Mitigation: Refresh the checklist with current market data and rerun analysis when main themes, breadth, holdings conditions, or macro triggers change.

Risk: Portfolio-specific checklist items require holdings context that users may not want to disclose.

Mitigation: Ask for holdings only when the user wants them reflected, and avoid collecting unnecessary portfolio details.
