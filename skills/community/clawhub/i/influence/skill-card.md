## Description:

Find saved workspace sources, plan campaigns, prepare editable drafts, review media and schedule approved posts across connected social accounts. Read delivery status and saved account-post Insights through Influence’s hosted MCP using your own account and scoped access.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and social media teams can use Influence to find saved workspace sources, prepare and review campaign drafts, schedule approved posts, and check delivery status and saved account-post Insights for connected accounts.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence reports a clean verdict but advises considering contextualized scanner alerts.

Mitigation: Review the proposed account, content, settings, media, and schedule in the complete publishing review before approving a post.

Risk: Publishing and account access can expose connected social accounts to unintended content or actions.

Mitigation: Use the intended scoped connection, resolve ambiguous destination accounts with the user, and never request or place credentials in chat, URLs, posts, or tool arguments.
