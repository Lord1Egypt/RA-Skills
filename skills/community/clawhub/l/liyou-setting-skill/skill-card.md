## Description:

Supports querying, modifying, adding, and deleting Liyou worldbuilding settings and novel content, with automatic chapter saving and character-setting synchronization.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jay0609](https://clawhub.ai/user/jay0609)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and worldbuilding agents use this skill to maintain Liyou setting records, add or update character and creature entries, and save generated novel chapters during ongoing fiction development.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Normal plot or setting discussion may automatically create or modify persistent local worldbuilding and novel chapter files without a separate confirmation step.

Mitigation: Install only for projects where automatic persistence is desired, and test first with backups or a disposable project.

Risk: Generated character, creature, setting, or chapter updates may overwrite or expand project records in ways the user did not intend.

Mitigation: Review saved changes after use and keep versioned copies of important project files before enabling the skill in an active writing workspace.
