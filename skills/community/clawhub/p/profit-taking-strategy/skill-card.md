## Description:

Provides tiered profit-taking guidance for A-share stock positions by selling portions at 10% and 20% gains while retaining a trend-following remainder.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haohanyang92](https://clawhub.ai/user/haohanyang92)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for educational A-share profit-taking guidance, including when to sell portions of a profitable stock position and when to keep a smaller trend-following position.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could treat fixed sell percentages as personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Present the strategy as educational guidance and require users to evaluate it against their own objectives, risk tolerance, and applicable rules before trading.

Risk: The guidance could be connected to automated trading without adequate controls.

Mitigation: Do not connect the skill to automatic trading unless separate safeguards and explicit confirmation steps are added.
