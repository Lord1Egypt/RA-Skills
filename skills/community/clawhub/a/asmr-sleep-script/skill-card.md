## Description:

Generates structured ASMR sleep and relaxation scripts, including guided meditation, roleplay, trigger-sound descriptions, bedtime stories, whisper comfort, and environmental soundscapes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[widoxm](https://clawhub.ai/user/widoxm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and agents use this skill to draft ready-to-record ASMR sleep scripts with timelines, spoken cues, pauses, trigger sounds, and environmental audio notes. It is oriented toward Chinese output by default and can produce English scripts when requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad sleep-story or sleep-script requests and defaults to Chinese when language is unspecified.

Mitigation: Confirm language, script type, duration, trigger preferences, and tone when those details materially affect the output.

Risk: Generated relaxation or sleep content may be mistaken for medical or therapeutic advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses framed as creative ASMR scriptwriting and avoid diagnosis, treatment claims, or clinical sleep advice.
