## Description:

全类型视频分镜生成指引。基于17本经典电影/动画分镜专著整合，覆盖短视频/营销视频/长视频。从内容到分镜、从叙事到镜头语言、从角色设计到图像生成参数，一站式输出。当用户需要将文案/脚本转化为视频分镜时加载。

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and video production teams use this skill to turn copy, scripts, or articles into structured storyboard guidance for short videos, marketing videos, and longer narrative videos. It helps plan story arcs, shot language, character and scene assets, image-generation prompts, audio cues, transitions, and quality checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is written as Chinese-language storyboard guidance and may surprise users who expect another locale.

Mitigation: Publish with an explicit Chinese-language or locale note so users understand the expected output language before installation.

Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause the skill to activate for general video-script requests where detailed storyboard production guidance is not desired.

Mitigation: Narrow the trigger description or document that the skill is intended for converting copy, scripts, or articles into storyboard plans.

Risk: Generated storyboard or marketing guidance can become misleading if source claims, product capabilities, or rights-cleared assets are not checked.

Mitigation: Review final storyboards for factual claims, intellectual-property constraints, and the skill's own prohibited-content guidance before production use.
