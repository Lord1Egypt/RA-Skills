## Description:

Add an emotional safety layer to reminiscence, life review, grief, trauma-adjacent, or otherwise emotionally loaded conversations by detecting distress signals, pausing depth work, grounding the person, and returning control to them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[oiioai](https://clawhub.ai/user/oiioai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent developers use this skill to add a conversational guardrail for emotionally loaded discussions, including reminiscence, life review, grief, trauma-adjacent, and sensitive personal disclosure contexts. It helps agents notice distress, stop deepening questions, validate the moment, offer a simple grounding prompt, and return control to the person.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the skill's conversational de-escalation guidance for therapy, crisis intervention, emergency support, or professional care.

Mitigation: Use it only as a conversational guardrail and switch to the host system's emergency or professional-help protocols for immediate danger, self-harm risk, medical emergency, urgent abuse concerns, or severe disorientation.
