## Description:

Run a silent 21-day diagnostic to determine if a specific role is being phased out or automated in the next 6 to 18 months, then build a financial and skill buffer while still employed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees use this skill to assess workplace automation or layoff risk, record a role-specific risk score, and plan financial, skills, documentation, and networking preparation while still employed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks an agent to handle sensitive financial and workplace planning with weak consent and scoping.

Mitigation: Use the skill as advisory planning only; require separate explicit approval before opening accounts, moving money, contacting coworkers, modifying calendars, publishing content, collecting workplace emails, or storing plans on employer-managed or shared devices.
