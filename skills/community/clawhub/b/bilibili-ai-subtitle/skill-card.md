## Description:

Download Bilibili AI-generated subtitles (auto-subtitles) for videos. Use when you need to quickly get subtitles from Bilibili videos that have AI-generated captions. Supports 9 languages: Chinese, English, Japanese, Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese, Korean, German, French. Language priority can be customized.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to download available Bilibili AI subtitles for a specified video, optionally choosing language priority for Chinese, English, Japanese, Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese, Korean, German, or French captions.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may automatically use cookies from local Chromium or Windows Edge browser profiles to access Bilibili.

Mitigation: Run it only in an environment where that cookie access is acceptable, or modify the script to require an explicit cookie flag or user-supplied cookie file.

Risk: Downloaded subtitles are AI-generated by Bilibili and may be inaccurate or unavailable for some videos.

Mitigation: Review generated transcript text before relying on it, and expect failures for videos without the requested AI subtitle language.
