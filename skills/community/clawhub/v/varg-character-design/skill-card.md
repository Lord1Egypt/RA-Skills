## Description:

Guides users through defining memorable characters and creating reusable references that keep their appearance consistent across images and videos.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use this skill to design fictional characters, mascots, and personas, then create character cards and reference prompts for consistent image and video generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference images may be sent to external services during generation or identity training.

Mitigation: Use only reference images you are authorized to share, and review the service's data-handling terms before uploading identifiable material.

Risk: Style-request guidance may encourage changing wording or routing after a model refusal.

Mitigation: Follow the model's content policies and do not rephrase or route prompts to evade a refusal.
