## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Waterfall analysis can produce incorrect or misleading financial conclusions when assumptions, baselines, or input values are incomplete or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review the source inputs, calculations, and material drivers before relying on the analysis for financial or strategic decisions.
