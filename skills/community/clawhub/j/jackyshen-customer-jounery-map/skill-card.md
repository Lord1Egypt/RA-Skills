## Description:

Use this skill whenever you want to visualize customer journeys, employee experiences, or service blueprints with stages, touchpoints, behaviors, emotions, pain points, and opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mebusw](https://clawhub.ai/user/mebusw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, service, and experience teams use this skill to turn structured journey data into consulting-style customer, employee, or service-blueprint maps. It is suited for e-commerce promotions, B2B sales funnels, and other multi-stage experience mapping work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may replace existing journey_map.svg, journey_map.png, or journey_map.pdf files.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to write outputs into a dedicated folder or confirm before replacing existing journey-map files.
