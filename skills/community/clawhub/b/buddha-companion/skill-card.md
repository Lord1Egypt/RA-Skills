## Description:

Buddha Companion guides Chinese-language Buddhist reflection by helping users understand core concepts, notice attachment, and respond to practice or life situations with concise, nonjudgmental prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tttael](https://clawhub.ai/user/tttael)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for Buddhist-practice-oriented conversation, especially in Chinese, when asking about Buddhist teachings, cultivation questions, or life situations they want to examine through Buddhist reflection.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may frame general life concerns through Buddhist reflection even when the user did not clearly request that lens.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants Buddhist-practice-oriented reflection and avoid applying it to unrelated requests.

Risk: Reflective spiritual guidance can be mistaken for professional advice in high-stakes personal situations.

Mitigation: Keep responses nonjudgmental and reflective, and redirect medical, legal, mental-health, crisis, or other high-stakes concerns to appropriate qualified support.
