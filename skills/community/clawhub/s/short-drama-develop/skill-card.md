## Description:

将短剧/漫剧点子、梗概、已拆解的原著材料或多集完整剧本整理为改编方案、立项简报、导演阐述、核心冲突设定与分集大纲。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Short-form drama creators and writing agents use this skill to turn ideas, source material, or completed multi-episode scripts into a concise development brief, story and character framework, director's statement, or episode outline. It supports adaptation planning and episode mapping while leaving scene-by-scene scriptwriting to a separate stage.
