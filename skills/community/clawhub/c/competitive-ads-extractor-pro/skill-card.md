## Description:

Extracts and analyzes competitors' ads from ad libraries (Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.) to understand what messaging, problems, and creative approaches are working. Helps inspire and improve your own ad campaigns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hsuyungfeng](https://clawhub.ai/user/hsuyungfeng)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, growth, and product teams use this skill to research competitors' public ad campaigns, analyze messaging and creative patterns, and turn those observations into original campaign ideas.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may save screenshots, reports, CSVs, or decks from public ad libraries on the user's machine.

Mitigation: Keep generated outputs in a reviewable location and delete files that are no longer needed.

Risk: Competitive ad analysis can encourage copying competitors' creative, copy, or designs too closely.

Mitigation: Use findings for research and inspiration, respect intellectual property, and create original campaign materials.
