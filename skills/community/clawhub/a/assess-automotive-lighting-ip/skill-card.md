## Description:

Assess automotive-lighting component architecture, sourcing strategy, and preliminary patent and design-right risk for lamps and related components, producing evidence-backed recommendations and an accessible tabbed HTML report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Automotive lighting engineers, sourcing teams, and IP analysts use this skill to decompose lamp designs, compare make/buy/hybrid sourcing paths, and run preliminary patent and design-right screening. It supports evidence-backed decision preparation and routes final legal clearance to qualified counsel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential product images, CAD exports, customer materials, NDA-covered content, or export-controlled content may be sent to third-party PatSnap processing during patent or design screening.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when third-party PatSnap processing is authorized; confirm which images will be sent, whether multiple views are needed, and whether redaction or metadata removal is required.

Risk: Preliminary patent and design-right screening may be mistaken for legal clearance.

Mitigation: Keep conclusions framed as preliminary and route claim construction, infringement, validity, enforceability, and clearance decisions to qualified counsel in each relevant jurisdiction.

Risk: Unavailable MCP tools or missing product, market, image, or legal-status inputs can make the screening incomplete.

Mitigation: State assumptions, label unexecuted searches clearly, document search limits, and verify candidate rights, claims, registered design views, and legal status before relying on risk ratings.
