## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ISO 45001, legal, and safety guidance may be incomplete or stale for a specific workplace, jurisdiction, or certification audit.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a planning aid and verify current regulatory requirements, audit expectations, and safety decisions with a qualified compliance or occupational health and safety professional.

Risk: Templates and risk matrices can be misapplied if organization-specific hazards, contractors, work sites, or operational changes are not fully assessed.

Mitigation: Adapt every checklist, risk assessment, and control plan to the organization's actual activities, workforce, legal obligations, and documented evidence.
