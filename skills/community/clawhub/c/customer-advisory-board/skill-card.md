## Description:

Plan and run a customer advisory board (CAB), including objectives, member selection criteria, agenda, discussion guides, roles, logistics, and follow-up/value-capture planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product, customer success, marketing, and executive teams use this skill to design CAB programs and meetings that gather candid strategic customer input while delivering value to members.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: CAB planning may involve confidential customer rosters, competitor overlap, budget information, or other sensitive relationship details.

Mitigation: Avoid entering unnecessary confidential customer details and handle rosters, competitors, budgets, and related inputs according to organizational privacy and confidentiality policies.
