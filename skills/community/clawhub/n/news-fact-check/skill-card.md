## Description:

新闻事实核查与真伪辨别助手。当用户需要验证新闻真实性、查证消息来源、核实事件真伪时使用。包括：(1) 给定新闻内容进行真伪判断 (2) 提供的事实核查方法和步骤 (3) 帮助用户建立批判性思维习惯

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this Chinese-language skill to evaluate news claims, compare sources, assess source reliability, and present fact-checking conclusions with evidence and caveats.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may direct users to external websites and tools during verification.

Mitigation: Review the source quality and privacy practices of external services before sharing sensitive claims, media, or personal information.

Risk: Fact-checking conclusions can be affected by incomplete, outdated, or low-quality source material.

Mitigation: Use multiple independent authoritative sources and preserve caveats when evidence is insufficient or mixed.
