## Description:

Humanizer Clark helps agents identify and rewrite AI-sounding writing patterns so text reads more natural and human-written.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[clark516lian](https://clawhub.ai/user/clark516lian)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to have an agent edit drafted text, detect common AI writing patterns, and return a more natural rewrite while preserving meaning and tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Aggressive tone and style rewriting may change nuance or make edits unsuitable for formal, legal, academic, or brand-sensitive writing.

Mitigation: Use the skill on selected text where stylistic edits are acceptable, and review the rewrite before accepting it.
