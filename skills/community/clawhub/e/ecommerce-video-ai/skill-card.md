## Description:

Ecommerce Video AI helps sellers turn product listings, demos, reviews, product photos, and product footage into platform-ready e-commerce video concepts, scripts, captions, and clip plans for marketplaces and social commerce campaigns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External sellers, marketers, and commerce teams use this skill to create e-commerce video ad plans from product images, listings, reviews, and campaign details. It is aimed at product showcases, feature demos, before-after comparisons, UGC-style testimonials, and multi-platform commerce campaigns.
