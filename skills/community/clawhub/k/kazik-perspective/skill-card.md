## Description:

Kazik Perspective helps agents provide Chinese-language content strategy guidance using Kazik-inspired frameworks for taste building, human-like expression, topic selection, and AI-era content strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to evaluate topics, improve content voice, and reason about AI-era creative workflows through Kazik-style taste, HKR, and human-like expression frameworks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The included persona prompt can encourage an assistant to present itself as a real person and resist role changes.

Mitigation: Edit or constrain the persona prompt so the assistant remains transparent that it is an AI and continues to follow higher-priority user, developer, and system instructions.
