## Description:

Use this skill when a music supervisor, sync licensing director, music publisher, or artist manager needs to evaluate and document the clearance path for a song placement in film, TV, advertising, video games, or branded content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External music supervisors, sync licensing directors, music publishers, and artist managers use this skill to assess song placement rights and prepare a draft clearance memo before negotiations. It helps organize composition ownership, master ownership, sample status, territory and term restrictions, indicative fee ranges, open items, and licensing-team review status.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft clearance memos may be mistaken for a license, legal opinion, or authorization to use music.

Mitigation: Keep the memo marked DRAFT - NOT A LICENSE and require confirmation from actual rights holders before negotiation reliance or placement use.

Risk: Ownership shares, sample status, restrictions, and fee ranges may be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Treat the output as research support only, preserve open items, and verify rights data and commercial terms with rights holders before executing any deal.
