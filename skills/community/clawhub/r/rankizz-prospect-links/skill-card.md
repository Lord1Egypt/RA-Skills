## Description:

Find, qualify, and prioritize relevant backlink prospects for an existing useful page, study, tool, template, dataset, guide, or other linkable asset. Use when the user wants link prospecting, resource-page discovery, competitor backlink opportunities, or evidence-based outreach angles. Rankizz is optional until live backlink, SERP, or authority data is needed.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SEO and content teams use this skill to find and qualify editorially relevant backlink prospects for an existing useful asset. It helps produce an evidence-based prospect list and, when requested, respectful outreach drafts without sending them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prospect recommendations could be inaccurate or lack a genuine editorial fit.

Mitigation: Verify that each page is live, relevant to the asset's audience, and offers a concrete placement; record supporting evidence and reject spam or link schemes.

Risk: Outreach could rely on invented contact details or be sent without authorization.

Mitigation: Label unverified contact details, draft messages only when requested, and do not send outreach or take related actions without explicit authorization.

Risk: The security scan is not a guarantee of safety because behavioral code analyzers did not apply to the inspected files.

Mitigation: Review whether the skill's stated purpose fits the intended use before installing, consistent with the supplied security guidance.
