## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to create reproducible Multisim circuit experiments from explicit requirements, including design option review, netlist preparation, simulation planning, result inspection, and PASS/FAIL or unverified conclusions supported by evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Circuit designs, component choices, netlists, and simulation plans can be incorrect or incomplete if requirements or approvals are missing.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended Multisim MCP environment and review generated specifications, component choices, netlists, and simulation plans before approval.

Risk: A simulation result may be mistaken for a verified engineering conclusion when supporting measurements or acceptance criteria are absent.

Mitigation: Compare theoretical values, simulation measurements, tolerances, and verification conclusions, and mark unsupported indicators as unverified.
