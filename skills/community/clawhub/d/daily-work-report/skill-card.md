## Description:

根据今日完成事项、进行中任务和卡点，整理成可直接提交的工作日报。用户说写日报、整理今日工作、把进度汇总给领导时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangaiyang2013](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangaiyang2013)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and team contributors use this skill to turn user-provided daily work updates, in-progress tasks, blockers, and next-day plans into a concise Chinese daily report suitable for managers, project groups, or personal records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated daily reports may include inaccurate or sensitive workplace information if the source notes are incomplete or unsuitable for sharing.

Mitigation: Review the report for factual accuracy, workplace sensitivity, and audience fit before sending.

Risk: The skill could be invoked when the user did not intend daily-report formatting.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for 日报-style reporting tasks and provide the completed work, ongoing work, blockers, next plan, and intended audience.
