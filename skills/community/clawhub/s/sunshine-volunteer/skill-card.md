## Use Case:

External Chinese-speaking rural education volunteers and public-interest education teams use this skill to draft warm, grounded writing and communication guidance for volunteer teaching contexts. It supports volunteer reflections, outreach posts, classroom encouragement, home-visit phrasing, and simple rural classroom activities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad volunteer-teaching trigger terms may activate the skill in contexts where a neutral assistant response is expected.

Mitigation: Install it for intended Chinese rural education volunteer writing workflows and narrow trigger terms if broad activation is undesirable.

Risk: Generated stories or communication scripts could misrepresent real students, families, or project details if used without local review.

Mitigation: Ground outputs in verified local details, remove invented personal information, and keep the skill's stated red lines against pitying or exaggerated narratives.
