## Description:

Find relevant Reddit, HN, and ProductHunt threads and draft value-first community responses with launch checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, founders, and go-to-market teams use this skill to identify relevant public community discussions and prepare transparent, value-first outreach drafts and a ProductHunt launch checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafted community responses may be inaccurate, overly promotional, or misaligned with community rules if posted without review.

Mitigation: Review each draft before posting, verify source threads and community rules, and keep builder disclosure in any product mention.

Risk: The skill writes docs/outreach-plan.md, which may replace an existing outreach plan in a project workspace.

Mitigation: Check for an existing docs/outreach-plan.md before running or preserve a copy when overwrite risk matters.
