## Description:

AI-curated market analysis and research \u2014 what 6 opposing analysts say about ETFs today with source links. Free, no API key.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fredxyt](https://clawhub.ai/user/fredxyt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to fetch public MoltStreet ETF and market-news research, then summarize opposing analyst perspectives with source links. It is intended for market research context, not financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ticker symbols or search topics are sent to MoltStreet when the skill fetches market analysis.

Mitigation: Use only ticker symbols or topics you are comfortable sharing with MoltStreet.

Risk: AI-generated market commentary could be mistaken for financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research context and verify important investment decisions independently.
