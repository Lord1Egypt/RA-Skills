## Description:

Write the follow-up messages that keep a candidate on the radar without being annoying, including post-interview thank-yous, no-reply nudges, stalled-application follow-ups, and offer-timeline check-ins.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers and career-support agents use this skill to draft timed follow-up sequences after interviews, unanswered outreach, stalled applications, or offer-timeline discussions. It helps produce concise messages that reference real conversation details, add value, and stop before becoming intrusive.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use personal job-search context supplied in chat, such as interviewer names, company details, conversation specifics, timelines, or competing offers.

Mitigation: Share only the details needed to draft the follow-up, avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information, and review each message before sending.
