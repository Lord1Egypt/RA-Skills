## Description:

Instagram Growth diagnoses where Instagram account growth is stuck and routes the agent toward strategy, content, profile, cadence, and measurement guidance based on native Instagram Insights.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and marketing operators use this skill to diagnose blocked Instagram growth loops, decide whether reach, follow-conversion, retention, sends, or sustainability is the constraint, and route the agent to companion skills for concrete fixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may route work to companion marketing, publishing, or scheduling skills.

Mitigation: Review companion skills separately before allowing them to post, schedule, or otherwise act on an Instagram account.

Risk: The skill relies on user-provided brand, audience, strategy, and Instagram Insights data.

Mitigation: Provide only the account and performance data needed for the diagnosis, and review recommendations before applying them.

Risk: Growth recommendations can be mistaken for guaranteed outcomes.

Mitigation: Treat the output as strategy guidance; the artifact explicitly avoids growth hacks, fake engagement, and viral-growth guarantees.
