## Description:

Provides daily A-share market review reports covering market movers, sector limit-up activity, capital flows, sentiment, and next-day strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cnxufei-tech](https://clawhub.ai/user/cnxufei-tech)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers and analysts use this skill to generate daily A-share market recap reports after market close or for a specified date. The report is intended for market commentary workflows and can be pushed to Feishu when the runtime environment supports that integration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can generate market commentary and next-day strategy from public finance sources.

Mitigation: Treat the report as informational analysis, not professional financial advice, and review it before distribution.

Risk: If Feishu posting is enabled by the runtime environment, generated reports may be shared externally or with a broader audience than intended.

Mitigation: Confirm Feishu posting targets and approval expectations before enabling automated distribution.
