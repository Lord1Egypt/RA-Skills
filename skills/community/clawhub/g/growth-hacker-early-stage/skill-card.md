## Description:

Growth Hacker (Early Stage) provides rapid user acquisition, viral-loop, conversion-optimization, and growth-experiment guidance for early-stage products seeking first users, better activation rates, referral mechanics, A/B tests, and distribution strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External founders, indie builders, and growth teams use this skill to diagnose acquisition, activation, retention, referral, and revenue bottlenecks, then plan low-cost channel experiments and conversion improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Email, LinkedIn, and social outreach tactics can create legal, consent, unsubscribe, or platform-rule risk if used without review.

Mitigation: Use outreach only with appropriate consent, unsubscribe handling, platform-rule compliance, and human review before sending or posting.

Risk: Growth experiments can lead to misleading conclusions when traffic or conversion volume is too low.

Mitigation: Use adequate sample sizes, track source and medium for signups, and review results against activation and retention data before acting.
