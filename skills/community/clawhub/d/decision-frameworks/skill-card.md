## Description:

Structured decision-making patterns for common engineering choices - library selection, architecture, build vs buy, prioritization, reversibility analysis, and ADRs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and engineering teams use this skill to compare technical options, align stakeholders, prioritize work, and document significant decisions through matrices, tradeoff tables, and ADR-style records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mutable installer commands can change behavior over time.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned ClawHub or repository version and review installer behavior before use in sensitive workspaces.

Risk: Decision frameworks can produce misleading outcomes if teams use incomplete criteria or unchecked scores.

Mitigation: Review criteria, weights, assumptions, and final decisions with the accountable decision owner before acting.
