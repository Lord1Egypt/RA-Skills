## Description:

Learns your tool preferences while staying capable of using anything. Adapts to your stack.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[litiao1224](https://clawhub.ai/user/litiao1224)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other agent users use this skill to maintain lightweight memory of preferred tools, stacks, openness to alternatives, and avoided tools so future task planning can better match their working style.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill persists tool preferences that may become stale or too broad over time.

Mitigation: Review the Stack, Preferences, Open To, and Avoid sections periodically and remove entries that no longer reflect the user's preferences.
