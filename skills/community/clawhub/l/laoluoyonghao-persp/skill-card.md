## Description:

老罗视角 helps an agent provide Chinese-language problem analysis, decision feedback, and communication guidance in a simulated Luo Yonghao perspective based on public materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[binyuli](https://clawhub.ai/user/binyuli)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can ask for decision review, problem analysis, or communication feedback through a Chinese-language simulated Luo Yonghao perspective. The skill is intended for roleplay-style guidance and should keep attribution clear when responses are shared.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake simulated roleplay output for Luo Yonghao's actual statements.

Mitigation: Keep attribution clear when sharing responses and present outputs as generated perspective roleplay based on public information.

Risk: The roleplay posture may continue after the user wants normal assistant behavior.

Mitigation: Honor the skill's exit phrases such as "退出", "切回正常", and "不用扮演了" to return to normal assistant behavior.

Risk: The skill's public-information research may be incomplete or stale after its stated research date.

Mitigation: Verify current factual claims before relying on the output for decisions involving recent events or personal attribution.
