## Description:

Generates character portraits for TRPG character sheets and embeds them into Excel files.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ice26985850](https://clawhub.ai/user/ice26985850)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External TRPG creators and game facilitators use this skill to create consistent portraits for sample player characters, important NPCs, and key enemies, then place those portraits into Excel character sheets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Image generation can consume credits and create more images than expected.

Mitigation: Confirm the character count and image budget before generation begins.

Risk: The skill may modify Excel character sheets to reserve space and embed portraits.

Mitigation: Keep backups of important spreadsheets and review affected sheets after portrait insertion.

Risk: Sparse character descriptions can lead to generated portraits with unsupported details.

Mitigation: Review style prompts and generated portraits against the available character descriptions before embedding them.
