## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste secrets, sensitive personal data, or private source notes into prompt-building requests.

Mitigation: Ask for goals and constraints without secrets, keep PII minimal, and decline passwords, OTPs, private keys, wallets, seeds, and session secrets.

Risk: Generated prompt guidance could be mistaken for executable automation or irreversible instructions.

Mitigation: Keep outputs as plans, checklists, and editable copy; avoid remote installers, chained download-and-run snippets, and destructive actions.

Risk: The artifact includes a donation link that could be confused with operational setup.

Mitigation: Treat the PayPal link as optional support only and not as part of installing or using the skill.
