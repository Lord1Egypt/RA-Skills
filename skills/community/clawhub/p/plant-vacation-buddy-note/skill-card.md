## Description:

Builds a room-by-room vacation plant sitter handoff note with water amounts, timing, photo slots, a visit checklist, and skip-if-unsure instructions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Homeowners or renters preparing for travel use this skill to turn owner-provided plant locations, water amounts, timing, and notes into a practical handoff note for a sitter.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated note may include travel dates, plant locations, and optional contact details.

Mitigation: Include only details the owner is comfortable sharing with the sitter, and omit contact information unless it is needed.

Risk: Invented plant-care instructions could lead to incorrect watering or care.

Mitigation: Use only owner-provided water amounts, timing, and notes; leave blanks or use skip-if-unsure instructions when details are unclear.

Risk: Toxicity, pet safety, or urgent home-safety claims may be outside the skill's intended scope.

Mitigation: Avoid toxicity and safety claims, and direct urgent leaks, electrical issues, mold, breakage, or unsafe access problems to the owner or appropriate local help.
