## Description:

Simulate the acquirer's diligence team hunting for reasons to cut your price — their internal red-flags memo with a price-chip estimate per finding.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Founders, operators, and deal teams use this skill to stress-test a company before a sale by simulating the acquirer's internal diligence critique, including red flags, expected evidence requests, retrade language, and fix-versus-disclose guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may prompt users to share sensitive company, customer, revenue, legal, or deal-process details.

Mitigation: Use only information appropriate for the agent environment and avoid sharing confidential details that are not needed for the stress test.

Risk: The output could be mistaken for legal, financial, or M&A advice.

Mitigation: Treat the result as planning analysis and route real transaction decisions through qualified counsel, bankers, and internal reviewers.
