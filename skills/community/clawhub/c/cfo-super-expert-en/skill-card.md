## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Financial, investment, legal, audit, tax, or compliance guidance could be mistaken for professional advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as research support, retain human review, and consult licensed professionals before making investment, legal, audit, tax, or compliance decisions. <br>
Risk: The skill depends on other installed finance-related sub-skills whose behavior and evidence may differ from this release. <br>
Mitigation: Review the underlying sub-skills before relying on routed outputs, especially for investment analysis, legal risk, audit, compliance, and capital markets workflows. <br>
