## Description:

MktClaw is a demand-to-delivery marketing agency skill that clarifies briefs and produces first-draft brand plans, campaigns, media strategies, livestream scripts, data analyses, and crisis-response materials across 10 agency roles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qomob](https://clawhub.ai/user/qomob)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketing teams, founders, creators, and agency practitioners use this skill to convert marketing needs into structured briefs and draft deliverables such as brand positioning, campaign concepts, media plans, KOL plans, livestream scripts, MMM analysis, and crisis PR responses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can read user-specified data files and may process sensitive business or campaign data.

Mitigation: Run it only where Read access is limited to the skill bundle and host-verified current-session uploads; do not provide PII, credentials, arbitrary local paths, or sensitive raw exports unless host privacy and retention terms cover that use.

Risk: Privacy, deletion, no-third-party-sharing, and Vault retention statements are documentation claims rather than enforceable guarantees from this package.

Mitigation: Confirm the host platform's privacy, retention, deletion, and sharing controls before relying on those statements.

Risk: Outputs for regulated domains such as medical aesthetics, finance, education, food, and crisis PR may create legal or compliance exposure.

Mitigation: Require human legal, compliance, or domain-expert review before using regulated or crisis-sensitive outputs.
