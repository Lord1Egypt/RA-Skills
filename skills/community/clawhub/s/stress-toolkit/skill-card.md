## Description:

Provide non-clinical stress-relief exercises, grounding prompts, breathing techniques, and crisis-safe referral language without diagnosis or automated crisis detection claims.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees use this skill for everyday, non-clinical stress support such as breathing exercises, grounding prompts, sleep wind-down routines, and short next-step plans. It is not intended for therapy, diagnosis, crisis detection, emergency response, or replacement of professional care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat stress support as therapy, diagnosis, crisis monitoring, or emergency help.

Mitigation: Keep responses non-clinical, avoid diagnosis and safety-monitoring claims, and direct immediate danger or self-harm to local emergency services or a trusted person nearby.

Risk: Breathing or grounding exercises may be uncomfortable or inappropriate when a user reports dizziness, discomfort, or immediate danger.

Mitigation: Offer short opt-in exercises one at a time, stop if the user reports discomfort, and switch to emergency-oriented support when immediate danger is present.
