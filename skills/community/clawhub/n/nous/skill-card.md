## Description:

Not an assistant. A thinking engine that makes its own cognition visible and lets you steer it in real time. Five movements. Four modes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use NOUS to make an assistant respond with a visible thinking format, including proportionate reading, tension, emergence, and optional next-direction prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill changes the assistant's response style broadly and may be intrusive for everyday short answers.

Mitigation: Install or invoke it selectively for tasks where a visible thinking format is desired.

Risk: The metadata marks medical diagnosis, legal advice, and financial advice as domains not recommended for this skill.

Mitigation: Do not use this skill as the framing pattern for regulated professional guidance in those domains.
