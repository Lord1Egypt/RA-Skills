## Description:

Resume Screener Pro helps recruiting teams screen resumes, check ATS fit, design interview questions, evaluate interview results, and produce final candidate recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wuritu](https://clawhub.ai/user/wuritu)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Recruiters, HR hiring leads, headhunters, and interviewers use this skill to run a structured hiring workflow from requirements clarification through resume screening, interview design, interview evaluation, and final recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hiring workflows may involve sensitive candidate data.

Mitigation: Provide only candidate data the user is authorized to process and redact unnecessary personal details before use.

Risk: Resume screening and interview recommendations can affect employment decisions.

Mitigation: Review outputs against applicable HR, privacy, and employment-law obligations before using them in a hiring decision.

Risk: Protected characteristics or irrelevant personal attributes could influence candidate evaluation if included in inputs.

Mitigation: Avoid using protected characteristics in decisions and focus review on job-relevant criteria, skills, experience, and evidence.
