Content creators use this skill to draft structured Xiaping/Coze Skill platform reviews, including review body text, star ratings, dimension scores, and review type selection.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Efficient or batch review drafting could produce misleading reviews if the user has not actually evaluated the target skill.

Mitigation: Base each review on real use, keep claims truthful, and follow the relevant platform rules.

Risk: Reviews involving stocks, financial products, or similar topics could be mistaken for investment advice.

Mitigation: Include a clear non-investment-advice disclaimer when financial or investment content is discussed.
