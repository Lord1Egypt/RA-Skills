## Description:

Generates a Chinese-language enterprise outreach path-mining report that maps official public contact channels, relevant departments, external relationship routes, opportunity signals, contact-priority levels, and compliant outreach strategies for a target company.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daviddatamining](https://clawhub.ai/user/daviddatamining)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business development, investment-promotion, park-operations, and government-enterprise service teams use this skill to research lawful public contact paths for a target company and prepare a structured outreach plan. The skill is intended for compliant enterprise research, not private contact discovery or bypassing formal intake channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs could include stale, incorrect, or over-interpreted public contact information.

Mitigation: Verify all contact channels and relationship claims against current official or public sources before outreach.

Risk: The workflow could be misused for personal contact discovery, impersonation, pressure campaigns, or attempts to bypass formal company intake paths.

Mitigation: Use only official and public channels, avoid private personal data, do not impersonate any role, and respect the organization's formal contact process.
