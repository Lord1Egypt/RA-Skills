## Description:

A comprehensive AI agent skill for anyone facing criminal charges, civil lawsuits, or regulatory investigations. Helps you understand what you are facing, protect your rights from the first moment of contact, work effectively with defense counsel, evaluate your options at every stage, and make informed decisions when the stakes are highest.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users facing criminal charges, civil lawsuits, or regulatory investigations use this skill to understand defense processes, protect rights, preserve evidence, work with counsel, and frame plea or settlement decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake general legal-defense information for legal advice tailored to a specific criminal, civil, or regulatory matter.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as informational decision support and confirm deadlines, rights, and strategy with a licensed attorney in the relevant jurisdiction.

Risk: Incorrect timing or strategy in active legal matters can affect rights, evidence obligations, plea decisions, settlements, and collateral consequences.

Mitigation: Use the skill to organize questions and context for counsel, preserve documents, avoid unsupervised statements, and verify matter-specific obligations before acting.
