## Description:

Schedules the full training operations workflow, including dates, lecturer invitations, course design, materials, venue and hotel booking, and preparation timelines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Training organizers and operations staff use this skill to turn training notices, historical training records, and planning constraints into a training plan, course schedule, lecturer follow-up plan, venue and hotel checklist, and countdown preparation schedule.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Training records can include participant lists, payment status, cost data, lecturer details, and historical operational records.

Mitigation: Share only the event data needed for the planning task and keep sensitive participant, payment, and cost details under the user's explicit control.

Risk: Generated schedules, reminders, venue plans, and lecturer follow-ups may be incomplete or unsuitable for a real event.

Mitigation: Have the event organizer confirm the final schedule, constraints, venue and hotel bookings, lecturer availability, and reminder cadence before execution.
