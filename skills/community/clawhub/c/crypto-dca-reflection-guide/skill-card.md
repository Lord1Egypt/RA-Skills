## Description:

A reflection skill that helps decide whether a recurring buy habit fits the user's goals, budget, and temperament.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to reflect on whether a crypto dollar-cost averaging habit fits their goals, budget, cash flow, and response to volatility. It helps compare DCA with waiting, learning more, or avoiding allocation for now, then frames simple guardrails when appropriate.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake reflective crypto DCA planning guidance for financial advice or a recommendation to buy.

Mitigation: Keep responses framed as reflection, avoid guaranteed outcomes, compare DCA with waiting or avoiding allocation, and encourage separate financial review when decisions have material impact.

Risk: Users may try to connect the skill to wallets, exchanges, accounts, or automated trading workflows outside its reviewed scope.

Mitigation: Use it only as a prompt-only guide and do not connect it to wallets, exchanges, accounts, or trading automation without separate review.
