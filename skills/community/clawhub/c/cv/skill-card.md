## Use Case:

External users and career-support agents use this skill to draft and review resumes or CVs for target roles, with ATS-friendly structure, quantified experience bullets, and industry-specific content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resume and CV conventions vary by role, employer, and country.

Mitigation: Confirm the guidance against the target role, employer expectations, and local hiring conventions before relying on final application materials.

Risk: Users may provide personal information while drafting resume or CV content.

Mitigation: Review and limit personal information before sharing, exporting, or publishing generated application materials.
