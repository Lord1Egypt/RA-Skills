## Description:

Create all assets needed for a Product Hunt launch for SaaS founders, product-led growth teams, and startup founders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External founders and product-led growth teams use this skill to draft and refine Product Hunt launch copy, supporting assets, and image-generation prompts before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated launch copy or images may be inaccurate, overstate product claims, or be unsuitable for public Product Hunt publication.

Mitigation: Review and edit generated copy and images for accuracy, brand fit, and compliance before publishing.

Risk: The skill declares Bash and Read access even though the visible workflow does not clearly need shell execution.

Mitigation: Grant only the permissions needed for the launch-drafting task and avoid shell access unless a reviewed workflow requires it.
