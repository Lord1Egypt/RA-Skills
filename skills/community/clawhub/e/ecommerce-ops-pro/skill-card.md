## Description:

Provides prompt templates for cross-border ecommerce operations, including listing optimization, competitor analysis, customer support replies, product research, advertising copy, and sales-data analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leiyuriy-byte](https://clawhub.ai/user/leiyuriy-byte)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketplace sellers and ecommerce operators use this skill to draft and optimize listings, analyze competitors and product opportunities, prepare customer replies, generate ad copy, and review sales data for platforms such as Amazon, Shopee, Temu, TikTok Shop, Lazada, and independent stores.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The review-request workflow asks for positive reviews in exchange for gifts, coupons, discounts, or benefits.

Mitigation: Revise review requests before use so they ask for neutral, honest feedback and do not offer incentives tied to leaving a review.
