## Use Case:

External users use this skill for reflective resilience planning during adversity, chronic stress, setbacks, burnout, or major life transitions. It helps structure self-assessment, practice plans, and recovery protocols without replacing professional medical, mental-health, legal, or other qualified support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users experiencing serious distress, self-harm thoughts, or feelings that life has no meaning may need urgent support beyond reflective resilience guidance.

Mitigation: Direct users to local emergency services, a crisis hotline, or a qualified mental-health professional instead of relying on the skill alone.

Risk: Resilience plans could be mistaken for medical, psychological, legal, or professional advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as self-reflection and personal development support, and remind users to consult qualified professionals for health, mental-health, legal, or serious personal concerns.
