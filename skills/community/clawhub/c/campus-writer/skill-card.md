## Description:

专属校园文案写作助手，帮助大学生撰写活动策划书、演讲稿、团日活动心得、实习周记、短视频脚本、朋友圈文案、公众号推文、论文大纲等，灵活调整语气风格，每次提供2-3个不同风格初稿供选择。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students use this skill to draft and polish Chinese-language campus writing such as event plans, speeches, activity reflections, internship journals, social posts, public-account articles, and paper outlines. It provides 2-3 style variants and adapts tone for formal, rigorous, lively, humorous, or comforting contexts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is focused on Chinese-language campus writing and is not explicit about multilingual support.

Mitigation: Users needing another language should request that language directly and review the output for tone, accuracy, and local context.
