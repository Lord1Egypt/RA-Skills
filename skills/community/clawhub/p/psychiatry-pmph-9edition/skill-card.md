## Description:

A Chinese-language psychiatry clinical reference skill set based on People's Medical Publishing House's Psychiatry, 9th edition, covering 47 skills for assessment, diagnosis, pharmacotherapy, psychotherapy, emergency intervention, forensic evaluation, and community rehabilitation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Psychiatrists, psychotherapists, medical students, community mental health workers, and consultation-liaison teams use this skill as educational or clinician-reviewed support for structured psychiatric assessment, treatment planning, crisis workflows, forensic-ethics topics, and rehabilitation planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes psychiatry diagnosis, medication, emergency, procedural, involuntary-treatment, and legal-capacity guidance that could be misused as independent professional advice.

Mitigation: Use it only as educational or clinician-reviewed reference material and require qualified professional review before applying outputs to diagnosis, prescribing, emergency care, involuntary treatment, or legal decisions.

Risk: The security review notes that the source content does not consistently enforce runtime safeguards limiting use to qualified clinicians.

Mitigation: Deploy system-level medical safety policies and verify recommendations against current official guidelines, local protocols, and applicable law.
