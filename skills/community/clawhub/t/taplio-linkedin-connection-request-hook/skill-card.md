## Description:

Drafts three ranked LinkedIn connection request notes under 300 characters, tailored to cold or warm outreach and informed by Taplio data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[porenes](https://clawhub.ai/user/porenes)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use the Taplio MCP to draft three concise LinkedIn connection notes for cold outreach or contacts from posts, events, and mutual introductions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outreach notes may use an inaccurate reference or an unexpected language, and a queued invitation may reach the wrong person.

Mitigation: Check the target, source reference, and language before using a note; require explicit approval for an invitation to that person and check for an existing request first.
