## Description:

Pick the right emoji or sticker for any message context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[enihsago](https://clawhub.ai/user/enihsago)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and users composing replies, reactions, or social text use this skill to select 1-3 emoji or stickers that fit the message tone, topic, and platform.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause the skill to activate during general reply composition.

Mitigation: Installers should decide whether broad emoji-assistance activation is desirable for their workflow before deployment.

Risk: Emoji choices can be inappropriate if the agent mismatches the message tone or context.

Mitigation: Review suggestions for tone fit and keep usage restrained, especially in sensitive, formal, or negative contexts.
