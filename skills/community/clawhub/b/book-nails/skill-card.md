## Use Case:

External users use this skill to search nail service providers, check appointment availability, and create bookings through Lokuli.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creating a booking sends customer name, email, phone number, provider, service, and time slot details to Lokuli.

Mitigation: Confirm the provider, service, time slot, and customer contact details with the user before creating a booking.
