## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill guides users through publishing public articles, where inaccurate, plagiarized, commercial, or noncompliant content can affect account standing or publication quality.

Mitigation: Review article content, originality, commercial intent, references, and platform rules before publishing.

Risk: The skill describes manual login and publication workflows, but users remain responsible for account access and final publication decisions.

Mitigation: Use the OSCHINA interface directly for authentication and publication, and verify each step before submitting content.
