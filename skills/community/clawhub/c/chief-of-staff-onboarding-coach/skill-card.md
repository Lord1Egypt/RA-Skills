## Description:

Coach a newly hired Chief of Staff through the first 90 days of role definition, principal alignment, stakeholder navigation, operating cadence setup, and selection of high-leverage initiatives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[charlie-morrison](https://clawhub.ai/user/charlie-morrison)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees use this skill when onboarding into a Chief of Staff role and need structured coaching for clarifying the role with a CEO or founder, interviewing stakeholders, reviewing operating materials, setting cadence, and choosing early initiatives.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may reference company-sensitive onboarding documents or executive materials while seeking coaching.

Mitigation: Keep sensitive materials under the user's control and share only the minimum summary needed for advice.

Risk: The skill may activate on generic workplace phrases that are not actually Chief of Staff onboarding scenarios.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is in a Chief of Staff context before applying the onboarding guidance.

Risk: Role and operating-cadence advice can be misleading if applied without the principal's expectations or company context.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations with the CEO or founder and relevant executive stakeholders before changing process or scope.
