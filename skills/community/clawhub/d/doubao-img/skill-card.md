## Description:

Generate high-quality images using Doubao AI image generation for artwork, illustrations, and other visual content, with support for custom prompts, multiple styles, and high-resolution output.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to guide an agent through generating images in a logged-in Doubao browser session, downloading generated image assets, and returning them to the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and generated content are sent to Doubao through a logged-in account.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive prompts or confidential image content, and use only accounts approved for the intended work.

Risk: Temporary generated image files may remain in the workspace after use.

Mitigation: Periodically remove temporary image files from the workspace when privacy matters.
