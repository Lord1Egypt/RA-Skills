# Example systems

[Chinese edition](https://github.com/gengshupeng/open-x-sys/blob/main/skills/open-x-sys.zh-CN/experience/playground/examples/README.md)

> **For Explore.** Complete example systems to open and read: what a well-kept system looks like after a few weeks.
> **These are constructed examples.** The people, the numbers and the subjects' details in them were written for the Playground. They are not records of real people or real runs. They show shape and habit. Do not quote anything in them as a finding.

| Example | What it shows | Parts it has |
|---|---|---|
| `creator-weekly-video/` | A system that exists to act: one practice, its results over five weeks, a watch condition | Core, a practice record, data, a small script |
| `concept-for-a-beginner/` | A system that is one short file, where the second record of understanding carries half the weight | Core only |

## How to show one

1. Read its `SYSTEM.md` first. Ask the person what they notice about how it is written before explaining.
2. Show how the parts relate: the entry describes every other file and says what it is for.
3. Point out the habits: the essence comes first; claims carry dates and evidence; the practice record is mostly results; "what is most worth doing now" is a choice; the second record says where to start next time.
4. Set the two examples side by side. Nothing in one would make sense in the other. That difference is the lesson (see `skills/open-x-sys/system/shapes.md`).
5. Apply the two tests aloud. **Continuity**: could a new AI continue from these files alone? **Starting point**: would the next run start from what the person already understands?

## What these examples are not

They are not templates. Copying their file names or their order produces a system shaped like an example and not like its subject. A real system's shape comes from its subject, the person and the purpose.

Real example systems, shared by people who built them, will be listed in the Systems Commons once it opens (see `x-sys/README.md`).
