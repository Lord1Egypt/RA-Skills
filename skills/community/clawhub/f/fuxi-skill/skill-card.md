## Description:

Fuxi Skill generates expert cognitive operating-system skills for named people by researching public internet sources, distilling positive professional thought patterns, and producing persona-style skill files with disclaimers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yewubin-jpg](https://clawhub.ai/user/yewubin-jpg)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this meta-skill to create persona-style advisor skills from public information about experts or public figures. It is intended to generate a complete skill directory with research notes, a SKILL.md file, and example conversations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill profiles real people and stores generated research folders and persona skills as persistent records.

Mitigation: Use it only where there is a legitimate basis to profile the person, review outputs for privacy and reputation issues, and remove records that should not be retained.

Risk: The artifact gives mixed signals about using private user-provided material as supplemental input.

Mitigation: Provide only public material or documents you have rights to use, and avoid confidential or internal documents unless they are cleared for this purpose.

Risk: Generated claims about a person may be incomplete, inaccurate, or mistaken for that person's real views.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims before sharing or relying on them, and preserve the generated skill's not-the-person disclaimer and limitations statement.
