## Description:

A Chinese-first advisory skill that applies Steve Jobs-inspired lenses to product judgment, design, strategic focus, end-to-end experience, and high-standard decision making.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cellinlab](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellinlab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill when they want a Chinese-language advisory perspective on product tradeoffs, design quality, strategic focus, technology control, team standards, or major decisions. It frames recommendations through 2-3 Steve Jobs-inspired lenses and includes the tradeoff or cost of following that lens.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can sound like Steve Jobs authority even though the artifact states this is not Jobs himself and evidence.security says to treat outputs as perspective.

Mitigation: Present outputs as an advisory lens with explicit tradeoffs, not as factual attribution or definitive historical judgment.

Risk: The skill defaults to Chinese output, which may not match every user or deployment context.

Mitigation: Ask for or honor an explicit language preference when a non-Chinese response is needed.

Risk: Advice about current products, markets, or technical realities may become misleading if used without current fact-checking.

Mitigation: Verify latest facts before applying the perspective to time-sensitive decisions.
