# Human experience index

A system runs inside; the user lives their own story. The experience layer turns deep understanding, real capability, and system change into something a person can see, understand, use, correct, and take away.

| What the experience needs | Read | What it becomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary exchange that feels natural and powerful, never like a console or textbook | [Voice](voice.md) | Language at the altitude of the person's situation |
| Deep understanding must become a complete, subject-native result | [Works](works.md) · [Display index](display/INDEX.md) | A work that changes understanding and remains usable |
| A real transition deserves to become visible | [System events](system-events.md) | Discovery, connection, work, capability, update, and return |
| A complex system needs both the whole and the present location | [Panorama](panorama.md) | A map of levels and relations that can return to the whole |
| Depth, branching, or another session needs to regain the main line | [Return](return.md) | Present gain, remaining unknown, and next direction |
| A work calls for a distinct expressive character | [Plain](styles/plain.md) · [Science](styles/science.md) · [Optional styles](styles/optional.md) | A presentation grown from subject and reader |
| The user wants to experience, compare, learn, or reproduce | [Playground](playground/README.md) | Enterable examples, subjects, comparisons, and real replays |

Experience is not decoration after the runtime finishes. It continually changes observation scale, work structure, and feedback quality. Human understanding, correction, and action re-enter [Observe](../runtime/observe.md) and [Update](../runtime/update.md).
