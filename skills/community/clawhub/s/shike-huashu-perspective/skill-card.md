## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Readers may treat the persona as an authorized representative or verified biography.

Mitigation: Present outputs as unofficial perspective guidance and verify biographical or business claims against independent sources.

Risk: Methodology advice may understate engineering effort or the transferability of cited success cases.

Mitigation: Use the skill for framing and brainstorming, then validate plans against project constraints, technical review, and user testing.
