## Description:

Conducts detailed audits of landing pages to identify conversion blockers and recommends prioritized fixes based on proven CRO principles and benchmarks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[amdf01-debug](https://clawhub.ai/user/amdf01-debug)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers, founders, product teams, and conversion specialists use this skill to review landing or product pages, score conversion readiness, and prioritize copy, CTA, trust, mobile, and A/B testing improvements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may route generic page audit requests into a conversion-focused review.

Mitigation: Use the skill when conversion-focused landing or product page feedback is intended.

Risk: Benchmarks and expected-impact labels are heuristic advice, not guaranteed performance outcomes.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against business context and validate material changes with experiments or measurement.
