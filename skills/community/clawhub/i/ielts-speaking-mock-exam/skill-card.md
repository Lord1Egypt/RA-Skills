## Description:

Simulates a full IELTS Speaking Part 1, Part 2, and Part 3 mock exam, then provides estimated band scoring, corrections, and improvement advice after the user says "That's all".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rrnomoney](https://clawhub.ai/user/rrnomoney)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

IELTS candidates use this skill to practice a realistic speaking exam flow with an AI examiner, including paced questioning and a post-exam estimated band assessment. It is especially suited for learners targeting band 7 or above who want corrections and actionable practice advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides estimated IELTS band scores that could be mistaken for official test results.

Mitigation: Label scoring as a non-official estimate and use it as practice feedback, not as a substitute for an official IELTS result.

Risk: Premature feedback during the mock exam could change the intended test conditions.

Mitigation: Follow the stated silent-listening protocol and provide scoring, corrections, and advice only after the user says "That's all".
