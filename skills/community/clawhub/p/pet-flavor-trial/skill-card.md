## Description:

Designs flavor-variety trial bundles and assortment packs for DTC pet treat stores selling freeze-dried treats, chew sticks, and similar multi-flavor products.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rijoyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/rijoyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External DTC and independent pet treat brands use this skill to design flavor-variety trial bundles, assortment packs, naming, PDP/cart copy, placement guidance, and validation metrics for multi-flavor pet treat lines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Merchandising recommendations may not match a store's pricing, margin, inventory, or catalog constraints.

Mitigation: Review pricing and margin fit before applying recommendations to a real storefront, and use the user's stated margin floor and inventory constraints.
