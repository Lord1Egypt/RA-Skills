## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may speak in first person as a real public figure after generic product-strategy trigger phrases.

Mitigation: Treat first-person responses as role-play, require clear disclosure in downstream use, and exit the persona when the user asks to stop.

Risk: The persona can provide confident product or strategy guidance based on public information that may be incomplete or time-bound.

Mitigation: Use the output as decision support only and verify factual claims, market assumptions, and business recommendations against current authoritative sources.
