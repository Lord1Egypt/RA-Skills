## Description:

Optimize WordPress content for Roman Urdu/Hinglish search intent with keyword variants, blended meta tags, image alt-text guidance, and technical SEO checks for Pakistani and South Asian audiences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External publishers, editors, and SEO practitioners use this skill to adapt WordPress posts and pages for audiences who search in English, Roman Urdu, or Hinglish. It helps produce keyword variants, blended SEO metadata, slug and heading recommendations, image alt-text suggestions, technical WordPress SEO checks, and a pre-publish QA checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated keyword variants and metadata can include awkward phrasing, unsupported claims, or time-sensitive SEO language.

Mitigation: Review phrasing, claims, dates, prices, rankings, and local modifiers before publishing; use current authoritative sources for time-sensitive facts.

Risk: Roman Urdu and Hinglish usage can vary by region, audience, and brand voice.

Mitigation: Have a native or experienced editor confirm spelling, register, and cultural fit, especially for sensitive health, financial, legal, safety, or cultural topics.

Risk: Applying SEO recommendations directly to WordPress can affect public content and site configuration.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as editorial proposals and only authorize WordPress changes through a trusted authenticated workflow controlled by the site owner.
