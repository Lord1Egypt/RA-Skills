## Description:

Trademark Quick Check helps small businesses and individual entrepreneurs perform lightweight trademark pre-checks, similarity and risk assessments, Nice Classification recommendations, and application-process navigation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and business operators use this skill to quickly screen proposed trademarks, compare similarity signals, choose likely Nice Classification classes, and understand China-focused filing, renewal, transfer, and non-use cancellation workflows. It is preliminary guidance and is not a substitute for official database checks or professional trademark advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China-focused; global public trademark database lookups where applicable

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trademark availability, filing status, fees, and legal deadlines can be incomplete, stale, or jurisdiction-specific.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary guidance and verify important decisions through official trademark databases or a qualified trademark professional.

Risk: The skill uses heuristic similarity scoring for sound, shape, and meaning, so risk ratings may miss legal nuance or examiner practice.

Mitigation: Use the scores as screening signals only and perform human review before filing or rejecting a proposed mark.

Risk: China trademark searches can miss applications that are still in the blind search period.

Mitigation: Repeat official searches shortly before filing and preserve the skill's blind-period warning in user-facing reports.
