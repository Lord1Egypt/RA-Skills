## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated medical or obstetrics content could be inaccurate, outdated, or unsuitable for a specific patient situation.

Mitigation: Have qualified medical and compliance reviewers verify medical claims, service descriptions, and patient-facing advice before publication.

Risk: Marketing copy could violate medical advertising rules through absolute claims, unsupported outcomes, or unverified pricing and contact details.

Mitigation: Review advertising language, pricing, contact information, QR-code references, and platform-specific requirements before publishing.

Risk: Patient case content could expose personal information or imply consent that has not been obtained.

Mitigation: Use anonymized cases only and confirm consent, privacy disclosures, and any patient-image permissions before use.
