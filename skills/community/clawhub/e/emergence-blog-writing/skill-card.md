## Description:

Instructions for creating engaging, viral-optimized, and SEO/GEO-friendly blog content for general human audiences across platforms like Medium, Zhihu, and LinkedIn.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[emergencescience](https://clawhub.ai/user/emergencescience)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External writers, content teams, and agent users use this skill to turn source material such as academic papers, logs, interviews, or practitioner notes into high-utility blog drafts. It emphasizes information gain, SEO/GEO structure, localization, citations, and publishing-ready calls to action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may include unsupported first-person practitioner claims.

Mitigation: Review each first-person or tested-experience claim against supplied source material before publishing.

Risk: Generated content may include publisher-promotional SEO or tracking links.

Mitigation: Remove or approve every Emergence Science, tracking, and SEO link during editorial review.

Risk: The workflow may persist project files in the workspace.

Mitigation: Run the skill only in a chosen workspace and review generated files before using or publishing them.
