## Description:

A Chinese writing assistant for generating titles, marketing copy, and professional email drafts from user-provided context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiangtaoid](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiangtaoid)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking writers, marketers, and business users use this skill to draft platform-ready titles, promotional copy, and email text from supplied goals, audience, platform, and tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on common Chinese writing requests such as writing titles, copy, or emails.

Mitigation: Install it where that activation scope is expected, and review generated drafts before sending or publishing.

Risk: Generated marketing copy or email drafts can contain inaccurate claims, unsuitable tone, or missing recipient-specific details.

Mitigation: Have a human review factual claims, brand fit, compliance-sensitive language, and recipient details before use.
