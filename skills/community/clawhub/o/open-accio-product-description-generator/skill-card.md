## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers, marketplace operators, and content teams use this skill to draft product listing copy, SEO metadata, platform-specific descriptions, and bulk product descriptions from structured product inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated sales copy may include inaccurate product claims, unverifiable social proof, artificial urgency, or marketplace-noncompliant benefit language.

Mitigation: Review outputs against product facts, substantiation requirements, and the target marketplace's listing policies before publishing.

Risk: SEO and platform formatting guidance may be applied too broadly across products or marketplaces.

Mitigation: Confirm platform-specific limits and required listing fields for each marketplace and product category before bulk publication.
