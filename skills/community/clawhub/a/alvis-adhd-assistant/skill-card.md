## Description:

ADHD-friendly life management assistant that helps with planning, task breakdown, time management, prioritization, body doubling, dopamine regulation, routines, and emotional support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[alvisdunlop](https://clawhub.ai/user/alvisdunlop)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and assistants use this skill to create ADHD-friendly plans, task breakdowns, check-ins, reminders, and routine supports. It is intended for productivity scaffolding and emotional support, not diagnosis, medical advice, therapy, or medication guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may store sensitive productivity and health-adjacent patterns through memory features.

Mitigation: Ask the agent not to save diagnosis, medication, therapy, triggers, or sensitivities unless explicitly approved for each memory; prefer general preferences such as reminder style or focus times.

Risk: The skill can activate on ordinary productivity requests without explicit consent or retention limits.

Mitigation: Confirm before enabling ADHD-framed support or saving recurring patterns, and keep memory retention scoped to practical preferences.

Risk: The skill discusses ADHD-related emotional support and crisis scenarios but is not a medical or mental health service.

Mitigation: Use it for planning and self-management support only; for crisis, diagnosis, treatment, medication, or persistent distress, contact appropriate professionals or regional crisis resources.
