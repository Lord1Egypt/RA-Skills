## Description:

Psych Paper Deconstruction helps users turn psychology research papers into structured close-reading reports covering hypotheses, research design, variables, statistics, limitations, practical implications, and critical evaluation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qingjiu061](https://clawhub.ai/user/qingjiu061)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, instructors, and applied psychology readers use this skill to deconstruct psychology research papers into assignment-ready analysis reports. It supports close reading of study background, hypotheses, design, variables, statistical methods, results, limitations, implications, and APA-style citation details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Paper analyses can become misleading if the source document is incomplete, OCR extraction is poor, or statistics are not traceable to the original paper.

Mitigation: Confirm bibliographic details before analysis, preserve traceability to the paper for statistics and findings, and clearly label inferences or reconstructed hypotheses.

Risk: The skill defaults to a Chinese coursework-oriented report format, which may not match every user's language or formatting needs.

Mitigation: Ask for a different language or output format when needed before generating the final report.
