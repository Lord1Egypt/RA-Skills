## Description:

Tarot Spreads helps an agent provide tarot-style readings with common spreads, symbolic Major and Minor Arcana interpretation, upright or reversed meanings where appropriate, and entertainment-only boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mike47512](https://clawhub.ai/user/mike47512)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent for structured tarot-style reflection using one-card, three-card, or Celtic Cross spreads. The skill is for symbolic storytelling and reflective prompts, not predictions or medical, legal, financial, safety, or crisis advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat tarot-style outputs as predictions or professional advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses framed as entertainment and reflection, include a clear disclaimer, and redirect medical, legal, financial, safety, or crisis decisions to appropriate professionals or local resources.

Risk: The skill may trigger on broad card, spread, or reflection wording when tarot was not intended.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants a tarot-style or symbolic reading before proceeding when intent is ambiguous.

Risk: Card draws may be presented as random even when the product cannot draw cards fairly.

Mitigation: State that cards are illustrative or ask the user to draw physical cards and report them.
