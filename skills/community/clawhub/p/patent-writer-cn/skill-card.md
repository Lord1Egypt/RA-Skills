## Description:

Patent Writer Cn helps agents draft Chinese invention patent disclosure materials and prepare software copyright application documents from user-provided technical details.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jessy-huang](https://clawhub.ai/user/jessy-huang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and product teams use this skill to turn technical descriptions, screenshots, and software metadata into Chinese patent disclosure drafts, claims outlines, figure descriptions, software design materials, and application checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expose portal passwords, identity document images, secrets, API keys, or unrelated proprietary code while preparing filing materials.

Mitigation: Provide only the technical and filing details needed for the draft, and redact sensitive identifiers or unrelated proprietary information where possible.

Risk: Patent and software copyright drafts may be inaccurate or incomplete if the provided technical and filing facts are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review generated drafts, checklists, and filing details with the applicant or qualified intellectual property reviewer before submission.
