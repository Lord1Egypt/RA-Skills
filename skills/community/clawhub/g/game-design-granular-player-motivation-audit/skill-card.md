## Description:

Audit a game, feature, progression system, social system, live-ops loop, monetization surface, or onboarding flow through a granular player motivation taxonomy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and researchers use this skill to evaluate how a game design, feature, progression system, social system, live-ops loop, monetization surface, or onboarding flow serves or frustrates granular player motivation segments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The taxonomy can be over-applied as a definitive model of players rather than an advisory design lens.

Mitigation: Use the audit as guidance and validate conclusions with product context, player research, and design review.

Risk: Audit findings may involve confidential product plans or player research.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive design, monetization, or research details with systems that are not approved for that information.
