## Description:

Scans GitHub and Hacker News live to surface trending repositories, hiring posts, launches, and funding signals for developers and investors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, investors, freelancers, and job seekers use this skill to scan public GitHub and Hacker News signals for emerging repositories, hiring threads, launches, and funding-related opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

Mitigation: Review the provider and endpoint before enabling the skill, and use the returned public-source signals as leads that still require verification.
