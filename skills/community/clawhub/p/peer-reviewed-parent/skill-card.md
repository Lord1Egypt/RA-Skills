## Description:

Evidence-based parenting assistant for children 0-24 months. Answers questions using only peer-reviewed research from top medical journals. Covers cognitive, language, motor, social-emotional, sleep, nutrition, screen time, and discipline development.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[riverventures](https://clawhub.ai/user/riverventures)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Parents and caregivers use this skill to ask infant and toddler development questions for children ages 0-24 months. The skill provides age-aware, citation-focused guidance across milestones, sleep, feeding, screen time, and related parenting topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Infant-care guidance may sound more medically vetted than the underlying evidence supports.

Mitigation: Require users or reviewers to verify claims against pediatricians or authoritative clinical guidance, especially for feeding, supplements, allergies, sleep safety, symptoms, and developmental concerns.

Risk: The skill covers health-adjacent topics for children ages 0-24 months, where incorrect advice can affect safety.

Mitigation: Keep doctor-escalation guidance prominent and avoid using the skill as a substitute for professional pediatric evaluation.
