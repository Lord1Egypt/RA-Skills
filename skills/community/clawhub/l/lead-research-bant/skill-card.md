## Description:

Guides an agent through public B2B company research, decision-maker identification, BANT qualification, lead scoring, and sales follow-up recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

B2B sales, SDR, and customer success teams use this skill to qualify prospects and prepare outreach. It helps an agent gather public company signals, rate Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline, and produce an actionable lead research report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public web research may be incomplete, stale, or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review outputs for accuracy, prefer current public sources, date older information, and preserve labels for unavailable or inferred facts.

Risk: Company and decision-maker research can drift into inappropriate personal-data collection.

Mitigation: Use only public business information and avoid private emails, phone numbers, or non-public personal data.

Risk: BANT scores and sales recommendations may be treated as final decisions rather than qualification support.

Mitigation: Have sales staff confirm budget, authority, need, and timeline before outreach or prioritization decisions.

Risk: Broad or batch searches can run beyond the user's intended scope.

Mitigation: Confirm user intent before running broad or batch company and people searches.
