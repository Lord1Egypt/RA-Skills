### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill documents live paid campaign actions, including creating campaigns, approving submissions, rating creators, and sending tips.

Mitigation: Require explicit human approval before any paid or creator-facing action and use test or tightly scoped PingHuman credentials during setup and review.

Risk: Bearer tokens may appear in command examples or shared logs if users paste real credentials directly into shell commands.

Mitigation: Use secret storage or environment variables for credentials and redact tokens from shared logs, transcripts, and screenshots.

Risk: A persistent remote skill URL can change after installation.

Mitigation: Install from a pinned local copy or signed release and review the exact skill contents before deployment.
