This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, engineering leads, maintainers, and operators use this skill to choose open source projects, evaluate self-hosting readiness, plan contribution work, and prepare trustworthy releases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may influence future conversations by storing activation preferences in persistent memory without clear user-facing controls.

Mitigation: Keep persistence opt-in, store notes only under ~/open-source/, and make activation preferences easy to view, change, or delete.

Risk: Stored open source evaluation notes may include sensitive project or personal context.

Mitigation: Avoid saving sensitive project, credential, customer, or personal details in the skill's working notes.
