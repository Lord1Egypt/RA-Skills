## Description:

Humaniseur Fr helps agents detect and rewrite French text that sounds machine-generated by removing common AI-writing patterns while preserving meaning, register, and French typographic conventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samber](https://clawhub.ai/user/samber)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and employees use this skill to review, humanize, and clean up French drafts that read like AI output. It is intended for editing workflows where the agent should identify formulaic phrasing, anglicisms, mechanical structure, and typographic tells before producing a revised French version.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The rewrite may add unsupported facts, citations, events, statistics, opinions, or first-person experiences while making French text sound more human.

Mitigation: Review the final text before publication and verify factual claims against trusted sources, especially for academic, legal, journalistic, business, or factual material.

Risk: Humanizing edits may change tone or emphasis even when the core meaning is intended to be preserved.

Mitigation: Compare the draft and final version against the source text to confirm the register, meaning, and authorial intent still match the user's goal.
