## Description:

Helps non-technical founders launch AI agents in 30 minutes without coding by using plain English and three ready-made workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Non-technical founders and small business operators use this skill to set up a practical AI assistant without code, APIs, or YAML. It provides plain-English setup steps, prompt templates, and copy-paste workflows for email replies, task management, and weekly summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste sensitive emails, meeting notes, customer details, revenue numbers, investor updates, credentials, or internal documents into third-party AI tools while following the workflows.

Mitigation: Redact secrets, credentials, personal data, contract details, and confidential customer information unless the organization has approved the AI tool and its retention settings.
