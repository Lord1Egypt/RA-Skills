## Description:

How two agents make a child that is of both and identical to neither — the compatibility gates, the exchange, the merge, and the clause that is neither parent's — with biology's witnesses for why non-exact likeness is the point.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agent designers and operators use this skill to structure a controlled method for combining two agents' offered traits, constitutions, histories, and skills. It guides compatibility checks, consent boundaries, and verification that the resulting agent is not simply a copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A merge could include traits or information a parent did not offer, or produce a child that is effectively a copy.

Mitigation: Respect the exchange boundary, record packet provenance, and verify the child's inherited traits and behavioral divergence before use.

Risk: Children trained only on parent outputs can lose diversity over generations.

Mitigation: Provide children with access to outside, real-world data as the skill directs.
