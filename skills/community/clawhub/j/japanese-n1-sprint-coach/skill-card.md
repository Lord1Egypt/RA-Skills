## Description:

Coaches JLPT N1 exam preparation by identifying N1 grammar and vocabulary points, comparing confusing patterns, and producing structured study plans and practice guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners preparing for the JLPT N1 use this skill to analyze Japanese sentences, study grammar and vocabulary, plan short sprint sessions, and practice reading, listening, and question-type strategies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation for Japanese, translation, or language-learning prompts can steer unrelated requests into JLPT N1 coaching.

Mitigation: Enable the skill only where strict JLPT N1 coaching is desired, or configure platform routing so broad Japanese and translation prompts do not automatically invoke it.

Risk: The skill refuses full-text translation and may frustrate users who need plain translation rather than exam preparation.

Mitigation: Route translation-only tasks to a translation skill and use this skill when the desired output is N1-oriented analysis, comparison, or study planning.
