## Description:

Draft and manage vendor outreach emails for quotes and availability, follow-up cadence, and response summarization into structured decision tables.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[austineyapp](https://clawhub.ai/user/austineyapp)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Travel, events, and procurement operators use this skill to contact hotels, transport vendors, and activity providers for quotes or availability, send consistent follow-ups, and compare vendor replies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outbound vendor emails can expose unnecessary personal, itinerary, pricing, or business-sensitive details.

Mitigation: Review recipient lists and email contents before sending, and include only decision-critical details.

Risk: Generated RFQs, follow-ups, or comparison summaries may contain incorrect requirements or misstate vendor responses.

Mitigation: Check generated messages and summaries against the user's constraints and actual vendor replies before acting on them.
