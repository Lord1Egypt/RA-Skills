## Description:

Audit spending to find leaks, including recurring subscriptions, creep, and cuttable costs, ranked by impact.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals, households, and financial wellness advisors use this skill to review spending data, identify recurring charges and cost creep, and prioritize realistic cuts with monthly and annual savings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide sensitive transaction or subscription details while asking for an audit.

Mitigation: Redact account numbers, identifiers, and any details the user is not comfortable sharing before using the skill.

Risk: Budgeting suggestions could be mistaken for regulated financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat the output as educational budgeting guidance and verify important decisions against statements or a qualified advisor.
