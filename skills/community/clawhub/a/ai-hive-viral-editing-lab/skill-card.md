## Description:

Chinese-language video editing planning helper that breaks down reference videos and turns a user's own footage into original short-form edits, ad variants, and multi-platform cut plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, editors, marketers, and agents use this skill to analyze a reference video's reusable structure and produce original editing plans, shot decisions, captions, audio notes, platform variants, and AI-generated filler-shot prompts when authorized.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference-video analysis could drift into copying protected people, dialogue, music, trademarks, footage, or distinctive shot combinations.

Mitigation: Confirm rights to source and reference materials, reuse only general structure and production techniques, and recreate scripts, captions, music choices, visuals, and shot sequences as original work.

Risk: Paid AI-HIVE image or video generation can create cost, rights, or duplicate-task issues.

Mitigation: Confirm user authorization, pricing, model settings, source-material rights, and task status before submitting or retrying any generation job.

Risk: Advertising or conversion edits can introduce unsupported claims about price, sales, endorsements, or product effects.

Mitigation: Keep platform variants tied to brand facts and evidence, and remove claims that are not supported by the user's materials.
