## Description:

A VC diligence and investment decision skill that helps produce five-dimension diligence checklists, three-scenario valuation models, and IC memos for venture deals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhuiwensm99](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhuiwensm99)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

VC investors, analysts, and deal teams use this skill to standardize diligence and investment-decision work for a target company. It produces referenced Markdown DD checklists, scenario valuation tables, and IC memo sections while leaving final investment decisions to human reviewers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Potentially sensitive deal analysis may be published to an external ima knowledge channel without an explicit final approval step.

Mitigation: Require explicit human approval of the destination, permissions, redactions, and final Markdown before any ima publishing action.

Risk: Valuation ranges and investment recommendations may rely on assumptions or incomplete source data.

Mitigation: Label assumptions and data sources clearly, and require human investment committee review before treating the output as an investment decision.
