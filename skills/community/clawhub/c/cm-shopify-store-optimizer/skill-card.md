## Description:

Audits Shopify stores end-to-end and produces a prioritized fix list to improve conversion rate, average order value, SEO traffic, store speed, product pages, collections, cart and checkout flow, retention, and app spend.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Shopify merchants, ecommerce operators, and consultants use this skill to diagnose conversion, AOV, SEO, speed, product page, collection, cart, checkout, retention, and app-stack issues. It returns ranked, Shopify-specific recommendations with expected lift, effort estimates, and implementation notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide customer PII, credentials, private exports, or sensitive internal screenshots while requesting a store audit.

Mitigation: Share sanitized aggregate metrics and public store details where possible, and include sensitive materials only when explicitly intended for the audit.

Risk: Recommendations may affect storefront conversion, checkout behavior, app costs, or theme configuration.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before applying them, prioritize low-risk changes first, and validate impact with store analytics.
