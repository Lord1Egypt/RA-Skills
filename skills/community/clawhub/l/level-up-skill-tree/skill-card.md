## Description:

Organize learning goals into a skill tree with dependencies, current level, upgrade conditions, and the next best path.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, students, self-learners, career shifters, and long-term builders use this skill to turn scattered learning goals into a dependency-aware growth roadmap with next-step practice actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the output as a formal career, academic, or certification assessment.

Mitigation: Present the output as conversational planning guidance and verify important education or career decisions with qualified sources.

Risk: The handler may summarize the skill template rather than produce a complete personalized skill tree by itself.

Mitigation: Use the generated guidance as a planning scaffold and have the agent collect the user's goals, baseline, constraints, and blockers before finalizing a roadmap.
