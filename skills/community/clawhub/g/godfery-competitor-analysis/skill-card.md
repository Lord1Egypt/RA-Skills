## Description:

Analyzes competitor SEO and GEO strategies by examining keywords, content, backlinks, technical signals, and AI citation patterns to identify opportunities to outperform competitors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[godferylindsay](https://clawhub.ai/user/godferylindsay)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, SEO, content, and growth teams use this skill to benchmark competitors, compare search and AI visibility signals, and turn observed gaps into content, backlink, positioning, and action-plan recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connected SEO, analytics, search-console, or AI-monitor tools may expose business metrics or competitor research data to the agent.

Mitigation: Connect only the tools and accounts needed for the intended analysis, and review outputs for sensitive data before sharing.

Risk: The optional unpinned npx install-all command may retrieve package code that has changed since release review.

Mitigation: Prefer the normal OpenClaw installation path, or verify and pin the package source before running the npx command.

Risk: Competitor recommendations can be misleading if based on stale, estimated, or user-provided metrics.

Mitigation: Label each data source in the report and require recommendations to cite specific supporting data points.
