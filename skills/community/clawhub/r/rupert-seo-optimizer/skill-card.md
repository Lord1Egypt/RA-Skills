## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private drafts, unpublished strategy, or sensitive website analytics may be shared while requesting SEO advice.

Mitigation: Share only content and analytics that are intended to be used by the agent for SEO recommendations.

Risk: Keyword and optimization suggestions may need validation against current search data and site-specific analytics.

Mitigation: Review recommendations with trusted SEO tools and internal analytics before publishing or making production changes.
