## Description:

A plain-language explanation helper for talking to family members about crypto involvement. Use when discussing crypto with non-crypto people. Prompt-only.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and families use this prompt-only skill to frame calm conversations with non-crypto family members about crypto involvement, focusing on concerns such as safety, legitimacy, environmental impact, financial risk, and scam history.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake conversational framing for specialized financial, legal, or scam-forensics advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as plain-language conversation support and refer high-stakes financial, legal, or suspected scam situations to qualified professionals.

Risk: Conversations with elderly relatives, people with limited financial buffer, or prior scam victims could become overly persuasive.

Mitigation: Use conservative framing, validate skepticism, avoid hype, and focus on risks and uncertainties before any personal position.

Risk: Requests may try to turn the skill into local file, wallet, or account inspection.

Mitigation: Keep file, wallet, and account inspection out of scope; the release evidence describes the skill as prompt-only with no hidden access, persistence, or data collection.
