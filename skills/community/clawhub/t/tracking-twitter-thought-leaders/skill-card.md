## Description:

Identifies and tracks thought leaders and key voices in any industry on Twitter/X using apidojo's scrapers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External PR, community management, and B2B marketing teams use this skill to identify topic-relevant Twitter/X accounts for outreach, partnership targeting, and content planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Apify tokens placed in REST URL query parameters can be exposed through logs, shell history, or process listings.

Mitigation: Prefer an authorization header or MCP/tool integration that keeps APIFY_TOKEN out of URLs, and use a least-privilege Apify token.

Risk: Search topics, Twitter/X handles, IDs, and URLs are sent to Apify/apidojo while running the skill.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when sharing those inputs with the third-party service is acceptable for the user's data-handling requirements.
