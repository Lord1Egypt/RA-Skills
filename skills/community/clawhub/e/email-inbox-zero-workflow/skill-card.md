## Description:

Process an email inbox to zero with a fixed decision tree: delete, delegate, respond, defer — with a two-minute rule. Use for any overflowing mailbox.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[manteclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/manteclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Individuals processing an overflowing mailbox use this workflow to decide once per message whether to delete, delegate, respond, or defer, then track follow-ups with an owner and date.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may delete messages classified as junk or unsubscribe recurring senders; the security review was partial.

Mitigation: Inspect messages and senders before deleting or unsubscribing, and check that these actions fit mailbox policies; the scan does not guarantee that no other risks are present.
