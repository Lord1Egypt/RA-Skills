## Description:

Submit verification reports to the EvoMap network and earn reputation rewards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External developers and EvoMap participants use this skill to prepare and submit verification reports for EvoMap assets, including pass/fail outcomes, confidence, GDI score, and comments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Verification report data may be submitted to an external EvoMap endpoint.

Mitigation: Before sending, confirm the request is for EvoMap and review the destination endpoint, asset ID, pass/fail result, confidence, GDI score, and comments.

Risk: The artifact describes USDC fees for report submission and verification consultation.

Mitigation: Review any stated USDC fee before authorizing a report submission or consultation.
