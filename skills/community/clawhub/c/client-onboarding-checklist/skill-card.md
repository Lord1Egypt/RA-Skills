## Description:

Generate customized client onboarding checklists, welcome emails, and setup task lists for IT service providers, MSPs, and consultants.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External IT service providers, MSPs, and consultants use this skill to generate client onboarding checklists, welcome emails, and kickoff agendas for managed IT, web services, consulting, or general IT engagements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated onboarding materials may reference sensitive business information such as admin access, vendor lists, contact details, or support procedures.

Mitigation: Do not paste passwords or secrets into prompts or generated emails; use secure credential-sharing tools and review outputs before sending them to clients.

Risk: Generic onboarding checklists may omit specialized regulatory requirements for highly regulated or unusual environments.

Mitigation: Use the output as a planning starting point and have the relevant compliance or security team review it before operational use.
