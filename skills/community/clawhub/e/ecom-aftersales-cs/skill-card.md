## Description:

A prompt-only Chinese e-commerce after-sales customer service skill that helps customer service staff draft brand-aligned replies for returns, exchanges, logistics anomalies, and compensation negotiations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Customer support teams and e-commerce operations staff use this skill to draft concise Chinese after-sales responses for return or exchange requests, delayed or damaged shipments, and compensation discussions while staying within stated store policy constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafted replies may accidentally promise refunds, compensation, timelines, or guarantees outside the store's policy.

Mitigation: Review and adjust generated replies against current brand and platform policy before sending to customers.
