## Description:

Part of the Overpowered skill suite, this skill helps design or review where human approval, judgment, or accountability must remain in an automated workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raguets](https://clawhub.ai/user/raguets)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, workflow designers, and operations teams use this skill to decide which approval steps should remain human-owned and which legacy manual steps can be automated with adequate controls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Approval-gate recommendations may conflict with an organization's legal, financial, safety, or policy requirements.

Mitigation: Validate retained and removed approval rules with accountable owners before relying on the workflow design.
