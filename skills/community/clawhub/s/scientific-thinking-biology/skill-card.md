## Description:

Helps agents interpret biology and life science evidence by framing biological level, separating evidence from interpretation, evaluating experimental systems, and calibrating claim strength.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agents365-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/agents365-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, researchers, and agent users use this skill to produce structured, cautious reasoning for biology and life science questions. It is especially relevant for interpreting experimental results, omics findings, model-system evidence, genotype-phenotype claims, and competing biological hypotheses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Biology-related prompts may involve clinical, biosafety-sensitive, or procedural laboratory advice.

Mitigation: Use normal platform safeguards and require qualified human review for clinical decisions, biosafety-sensitive work, or wet-lab procedures.

Risk: Structured scientific reasoning can still overstate conclusions when source evidence is weak, indirect, or context-limited.

Mitigation: Keep claims tied to provided data, background knowledge, or inference, and review conclusions before relying on them for research or operational decisions.
