## Description:

Generate and create UGC-style product review and testimonial videos using SJinn Seedance 2.0, with 9-layer prompt architecture for authentic selfie-style content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jax-explorer](https://clawhub.ai/user/jax-explorer)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to collect product campaign details, compose authentic UGC-style review or testimonial video prompts, and submit them to SJinn Seedance 2.0 when available.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product assets, unreleased campaign material, or personal images may be shared with SJinn during generation.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential assets or personal images unless the user is comfortable sending them to SJinn.

Risk: The fallback install path uses an unpinned npx command.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned and reviewed installer or source before using fallback installation.
