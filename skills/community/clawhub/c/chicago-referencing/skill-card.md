## Description:

Chicago style both systems: notes-bibliography (footnotes) and author-date. Covers system selection, note forms, bibliography, shortened citations. Use for papers requiring Chicago.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vhsgreed](https://clawhub.ai/user/vhsgreed)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, writers, editors, and research-support agents use this skill to choose and apply Chicago notes-bibliography or author-date citation forms for papers requiring Chicago style.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill summarizes Chicago style and may not capture every edition-specific or instructor-specific requirement.

Mitigation: Verify final citations against the required Chicago edition, assignment rubric, or instructor guidance before submission.
