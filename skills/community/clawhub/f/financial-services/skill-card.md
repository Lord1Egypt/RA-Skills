## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial analysis outputs could be mistaken for final lending, investment, insurance, or regulatory decisions.

Mitigation: Require qualified human review before relying on any output for financial or regulatory decisions.

Risk: Configured data connectors could expose more financial documents or provider data than intended.

Mitigation: Scope MCP data connectors to the specific document repositories and financial data providers needed for the task.

Risk: Unverified figures or stale data could make generated financial deliverables misleading.

Mitigation: Apply the source-attribution workflow so every material number has a source, date, and cross-check where available.
