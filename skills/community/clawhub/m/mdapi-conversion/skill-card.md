## Description:

Use mdapi.io to transform files and webpages into AI-ready Markdown or structured data, with prompt-driven transformation, streaming, x402 payments, token activation, and REST/MCP/ACP/A2A/OpenAI-compatible access.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mdapiio](https://clawhub.ai/user/mdapiio)

## Use Case:

Developers and AI agents use the skill to convert files, webpages, or raw text into Markdown or prompt-shaped structured data through mdapi.io's REST and agent-protocol interfaces.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Content sent for conversion may include confidential or sensitive information processed by a third-party service.

Mitigation: Send only content authorized for external processing; remove secrets and review compliance before submitting regulated or proprietary material.

Risk: Paid conversion and token activation can involve irreversible payments.

Mitigation: Require user consent or prior authorization, and verify payment details against the service's official payment challenge before paying.
