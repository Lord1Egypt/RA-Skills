## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to explain, set up, analyze, and compare common options strategies across directional, neutral, volatility, income, and multi-leg trading scenarios.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Options strategies can involve total loss, assignment, margin exposure, and in some cases unlimited loss.

Mitigation: Present strategy output as educational guidance, require users to apply their own risk tolerance and account limits, and encourage professional guidance before acting.

Risk: Strategy explanations may be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses framed as educational options content and avoid personalized trade recommendations unless the user provides appropriate context and accepts the limitations.
