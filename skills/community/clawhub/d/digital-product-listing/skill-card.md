## Description:

Generates optimised product listings for Etsy, Gumroad, and Payhip from a simple product description, including platform-specific titles, descriptions, tags, and pricing suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketplace sellers, and digital product makers use this skill to draft platform-ready product listings for Etsy, Gumroad, and Payhip from one product description.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated pricing, currency, marketplace claims, or SEO guidance may be inaccurate for a specific product or market.

Mitigation: Review all generated prices, tags, currency assumptions, and product claims before publishing listings.

Risk: Installation through an unpinned package command can expose users to installer-version drift.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned or trusted ClawHub installer version when installing the skill.

Risk: The skill drafts listing content but is not intended to manage marketplace accounts or publish products automatically.

Mitigation: Do not provide marketplace credentials or grant account access; copy reviewed listing text manually into the intended platform.
