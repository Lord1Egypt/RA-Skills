## Description:

Cn Social Reply helps content creators generate Chinese social-media comment replies across platforms and manage reusable reply-style templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[freedompixels](https://clawhub.ai/user/freedompixels)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and social-media operators use this skill to draft one-off or batch Chinese comment replies, adapt tone for platforms such as Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Weibo, Bilibili, and Zhihu, and preserve reusable persona or reply-style preferences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Saved reply-style templates may contain sensitive persona details or brand preferences that influence future replies.

Mitigation: Review templates stored under ~/.qclaw/workspace/cn-social-reply/styles.json before sharing or reusing them in sensitive contexts.
