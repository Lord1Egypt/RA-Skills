## Description: <br>
Generates qualified B2B leads through systematic research, data collection, and scoring. Delivers structured lead lists with contact information and qualification scores. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External business users and sales operators use this skill to define an ICP, research potential B2B prospects, score fit, and package lead lists for outreach. The skill is intended for lawful business-contact research where privacy, platform, and outreach rules can be followed. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can scrape and export business contact data for outreach. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only for lawful business lead research, respect site and platform terms, avoid prohibited scraping, and collect only data needed for the stated outreach purpose. <br>
Risk: Generated lead files may contain sensitive contact information. <br>
Mitigation: Store exported JSON, CSV, and Markdown reports securely, limit access, and delete stale lead files when they are no longer needed. <br>
Risk: Placeholder or unsourced contacts can be mislabeled as decision makers. <br>
Mitigation: Verify every contact name, title, email, source, and qualification score before outreach, and do not treat placeholder fields as real people. <br>
Risk: Outreach based on generated leads can create privacy or compliance exposure. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm GDPR, CAN-SPAM, consent, opt-out, and business-purpose requirements for each target geography before sending messages. <br>
