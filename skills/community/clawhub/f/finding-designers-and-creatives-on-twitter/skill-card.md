## Use Case:

External recruiting teams, design agencies, product design leads, and creative directors use this skill to discover and prioritize designers or creative professionals on Twitter/X for talent sourcing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill handles recruiting profile data from Twitter/X and can store candidate exports locally.

Mitigation: Apply recruiting, privacy, retention, and consent requirements before bulk profiling candidates, and keep exports out of shared repositories.

Risk: The skill requires an Apify token and includes a query-string token pattern in the REST fallback.

Mitigation: Use a least-privilege Apify token and prefer an Authorization header where supported.
