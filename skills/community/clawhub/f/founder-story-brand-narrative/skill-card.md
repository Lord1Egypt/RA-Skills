## Description:

Creates and refines Founder Story and brand narrative content for DTC/independent stores selling founder-driven products (e.g. handcrafted leather, artisan sauces, small-batch goods).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rijoyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/rijoyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, operators, and marketers for founder-led DTC or independent stores use this skill to turn brand facts into founder story frameworks, placement guidance, and ready-to-use narrative copy for About pages, PDP brand blocks, landing page heroes, email/SMS, packaging, and social channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated founder-story copy may be inaccurate if the brand facts, proof points, or claims supplied by the user are incomplete or unverified.

Mitigation: Review all generated copy against confirmed founder, product, process, and compliance facts before publication.

Risk: The skill includes optional local Python helper scripts that may be run on user-provided copy or brief files.

Mitigation: Review the scripts before execution and run them only on intended local input files.

Risk: Broad brand-copy trigger terms could invoke the skill for a task without a meaningful founder or maker narrative.

Mitigation: Use the skill's own scope guidance to switch to value-proposition or feature-led messaging when founder story is not relevant.
