## Description:

Search for products on Chinese e-commerce platforms (Taobao, JD, Vipshop, PDD, Meituan, Ele.me, etc.) using web search.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and shopping assistants use this skill to search China-market e-commerce platforms, compare product prices, filter by platform or item attributes, and summarize candidate purchase options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shopping results may include unverified prices, seller claims, affiliate links, or outdated availability.

Mitigation: Confirm price, seller, stock, delivery, and return details on the official platform before purchasing.

Risk: The skill focuses on China-market shopping platforms and may be less useful outside that marketplace.

Mitigation: Use it when China-market product search is intended, and choose other shopping sources for different regions.
