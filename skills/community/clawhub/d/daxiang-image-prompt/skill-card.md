## Description:

Daxiang Image Prompt helps agents produce two-part Chinese image-generation prompts with a detailed scene description and a fixed hand-drawn illustration style.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daxiangnaoyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/daxiangnaoyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, creators, and agents use this skill to turn requests for article illustrations, covers, concept diagrams, flowcharts, comparisons, and summaries into consistent Chinese image-generation prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The fixed prompt style may weaken safety refusals for sensitive, protected, copyrighted, or brand-like image requests.

Mitigation: Review and edit the skill before installation so platform safety policy and user instructions take priority over the fixed prompt text.

Risk: The prompt asks for substitute likenesses when sensitive or protected people are involved.

Mitigation: Apply image-generation policy review for public figures, sensitive subjects, copyrighted characters, and brand-like imagery before using generated prompts.
