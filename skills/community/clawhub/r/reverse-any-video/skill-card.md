## Description:

Rebuild any video file as an editable HyperFrames project - motion graphics, talking heads, live action or AI-generated footage - checked against the original frame by frame. Use to reverse, recreate, remake, replicate or copy a video, or 「复刻这个视频」「照着这个视频做一个」.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and video editors use this skill to rebuild a video they have the right to use as an editable HyperFrames project, recreating its shots, graphics, timing, and sound.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The video workflow may send video, face, or voice data to third parties without clear approval, and processing may involve possible credit use.

Mitigation: Use only media you have the right to process; check privacy and consent requirements, explain third-party processing and possible credit use, and get approval before submitting media.

Risk: The scanner marked the skill suspicious in part because it calls an unpinned command-line package and reports extra regeneration and reporting; some references were unresolved during scanning.

Mitigation: Review the package and commands before execution, confirm expected regeneration and reporting actions, and inspect unresolved references before relying on the workflow.
