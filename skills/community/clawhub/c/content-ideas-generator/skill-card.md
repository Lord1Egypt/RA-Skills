## Description:

Generates structured post outlines from reference materials for wisdom-style social posts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vincentchan](https://clawhub.ai/user/vincentchan)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to turn newsletters, scripts, notes, journal entries, raw ideas, or fetched URL content into five structured outlines for wisdom-style social posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated outline files may retain excerpts or derivative ideas from user-provided materials.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting highly sensitive journal entries or private documents unless saving derived local output is acceptable.

Risk: The skill may analyze user-provided URLs.

Mitigation: Provide only URLs whose contents should be fetched and analyzed.
