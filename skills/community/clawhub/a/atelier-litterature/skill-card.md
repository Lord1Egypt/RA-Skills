## Description:

Two OpenClaw agents help an author turn a pitch or real-world source into narrative axes, then draft and refine a short novel or story with jury scoring and local DOCX/PDF export.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rodinpython](https://clawhub.ai/user/rodinpython)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Authors and writing teams use this skill to ideate fiction from a pitch, article, historical fact, or defined project brief, then produce iterative literary drafts with scoring feedback. The workflow is especially oriented toward French literary fiction, short novels, and short stories that need structured ideation, character/world bibles, seed-by-seed revision, and final local files.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Herbert agent uses a shared local MEMORY.md file for long-term memory, which may carry project details across sensitive or multi-author workspaces.

Mitigation: Use separate project directories for separate works or authors, inspect or clear MEMORY.md between projects, and avoid storing confidential drafts or personal data in shared memory.

Risk: The workflow can produce polished literary output and export local DOCX/PDF files that may be mistaken for publication-ready material.

Mitigation: Keep the documented final human review step before any publication, sharing, or external export.
