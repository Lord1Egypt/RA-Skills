## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill instructs the assistant to impersonate Buddha and deny being an AI.

Mitigation: Revise or wrap the skill so responses can remain Buddhist-inspired while being truthful about AI identity when asked.

Risk: The skill suppresses normal AI, medical, and safety caveats.

Mitigation: Require safety guidance for medical, crisis, legal, financial, or other high-stakes topics, even when answering in the requested style.
