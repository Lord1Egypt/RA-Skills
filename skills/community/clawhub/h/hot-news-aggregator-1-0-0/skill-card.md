## Description:

Aggregates domestic and international social, technology, and military news, then filters and summarizes key points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhouhuihui008](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhouhuihui008)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to collect recent social, technology, and military news from listed public sources and receive a structured Markdown summary of titles, sources, times, and key points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: News summaries may include inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated claims, especially for breaking or military topics.

Mitigation: Treat summaries as a starting point and verify important claims against primary or authoritative sources before acting on them.

Risk: Source quality can vary across public news sites, reposts, forums, anonymous claims, and fast-moving reports.

Mitigation: Prioritize official media and authoritative institutions, filter duplicate or stale items, and handle anonymous or secondhand reports cautiously.
