## Description:

Publish and schedule social media posts on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube and Threads with Rizamo, see how they did, and answer comments and messages. Use when the user wants to post or schedule social content, check post or account analytics, or reply to their audience.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and teams use the skill to draft, schedule, and publish content to their connected social accounts, review post analytics, and prepare replies to comments and messages.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing or replying through connected social accounts has external effects; the security review also notes an API reference for review and incomplete scan coverage.

Mitigation: Check that Rizamo's API is appropriate before connecting accounts, and show the exact post or reply and obtain user approval before publishing or sending it.
