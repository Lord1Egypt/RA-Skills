## Description:

Capture Arabic voice notes, transcribe them safely, clean dialect/MSA text, preserve meaning, and prepare bilingual summaries or action items.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and productivity-focused agents use this skill to process authorized Arabic or mixed Arabic/English voice notes into clean transcripts, summaries, action lists, bilingual handoffs, and message drafts while preserving meaning and right-to-left formatting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process personal, client, or business information contained in audio or transcripts.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is authorized to process the content and preserve only details needed for the requested transcript, summary, or draft.

Risk: Dictated audio can be unclear or incomplete, which may lead to incorrect names, numbers, dates, or commitments.

Mitigation: Mark uncertain words clearly, avoid inventing missing facts, and verify names, numbers, dates, punctuation, and line breaks before final output.

Risk: Prepared text could be sent, uploaded, or published before the user has reviewed it.

Mitigation: Prepare draft text only and require explicit approval before any external sending, uploading, or publishing action.
