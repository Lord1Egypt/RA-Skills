## Description:

Audits game randomness systems for player-perceived fairness, streak pain, hidden pattern assumptions, agency damage, and practical design mitigations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, economy designers, and gameplay developers use this skill to review loot, combat, reward, procedural, and chance systems for perceived unfairness and agency loss before tuning mechanics or UX.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Game-design recommendations may be over-applied to sensitive reward, loot, or gacha systems.

Mitigation: Review outputs as design advice and validate changes with the appropriate game design, economy, product, and compliance stakeholders before release.

Risk: The skill can produce persuasive perceived-fairness analysis without direct access to player telemetry or production tuning data.

Mitigation: Use its findings as an audit prompt and compare recommendations against playtest results, telemetry, and the system's intended design goals.
