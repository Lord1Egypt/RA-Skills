## Description:

Generates Chinese A-share daily closing recaps and short-term trading scenario analysis in a fixed six-section Markdown structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[albert-mk](https://clawhub.ai/user/albert-mk)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External A-share market participants use this skill after market close to draft concise Chinese recaps, identify short-term sentiment leaders, and outline next-day scenario branches. Outputs are informational market analysis and should not be treated as financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can discuss watchlists, position levels, and trading conditions in market-recap outputs.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational analysis, keep the included financial disclaimer, and have users verify market data and decisions independently.

Risk: The skill may trigger from broad market-recap phrasing.

Mitigation: Use it only when Chinese A-share closing recaps or short-term trading scenario analysis are intended.
