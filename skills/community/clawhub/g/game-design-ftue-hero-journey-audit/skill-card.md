## Description:

Audit a game's FTUE (First Time User Experience) through the lens of the Hero's Journey / monomyth.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and design reviewers use this skill to audit onboarding, tutorial flow, early-session retention risks, and whether the FTUE gives players an emotionally meaningful first heroic arc.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce qualitative product and design advice that may be incomplete or subjective.

Mitigation: Use the audit as design review input and validate recommendations with playtest data, product goals, and human review.
