## Description:

Guide users buying an air purifier through room size, pollutant type, CADR, and filter questions to find the exact purifier specs they need for their space and health situation - region-aware, brand-neutral.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill when choosing an air purifier for a specific room, pollutant concern, health situation, and region. It guides the agent through intake questions, CADR and filter reasoning, buyer-risk checks, and concise product-fit suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product availability, prices, replacement filters, and specifications can become outdated.

Mitigation: Verify current product pages, local filter availability, regional certifications, and pricing before purchasing.

Risk: Incomplete room, pollutant, health, or regional details can lead to unsuitable CADR, filter, voltage, or certification recommendations.

Mitigation: Complete the consultation inputs before final recommendations and recalculate CADR, ACH, and non-negotiable specs when any input changes.

Risk: Users with allergies, asthma, smoke exposure, or chemical sensitivity may be harmed by marketing claims such as HEPA-type filters or weak carbon layers.

Mitigation: Distinguish True HEPA from HEPA-type claims, require appropriate activated carbon for odors or VOCs, and frame product suggestions as starting points for verification rather than endorsements.
