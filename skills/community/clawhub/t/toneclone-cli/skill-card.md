## Description:

Write in the user's authentic voice using ToneClone trained personas, knowledge cards, and CLI commands for emails, messages, social posts, and other content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to draft user-facing writing through the ToneClone CLI in a selected trained persona, optionally adding knowledge cards and prompt context for more accurate content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts may include private conversation history, relationship context, or other sensitive details sent to the ToneClone service.

Mitigation: Confirm trust in ToneClone before installing, avoid secrets and regulated data, and provide only the minimum context needed for the writing task.
