## Description:

A Chinese-language skill for analyzing English long sentences and reading passages with sentence segmentation, core structure identification, modifier analysis, translation, and synthesis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zwli123](https://clawhub.ai/user/zwli123)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, educators, and reading-support agents use this skill to break down English exam-reading sentences or passages into structure, modifiers, logic cues, vocabulary meanings, and a synthesized Chinese translation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases may activate the skill for general sentence-analysis or reading-comprehension requests where the user expects a different language or tutoring style.

Mitigation: Ask the user to confirm that they want Chinese-language English reading analysis, or have them specify the desired language and tutoring style.

Risk: Educational grammar and translation analysis can be incomplete or misleading for ambiguous, idiomatic, or context-poor passages.

Mitigation: Encourage users to provide surrounding context and review the final explanation against the source passage or course material.
