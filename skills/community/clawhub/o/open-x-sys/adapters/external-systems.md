# External Systems: Connect Without Absorbing

An external system may be a database, enterprise application, device, organization, platform, another AI, or an OpenXSys system owned by another person. A connection is real when the two sides can produce a usable effect.

## Begin with independence

Every system has its own purpose, state, language, permissions, clock, owner, and ways of failing. The external system continues to hold its authoritative state. OpenXSys preserves understanding of its meaning, relation, and continuation path.

## A runnable relation

An important connection makes clear:

- who sends what to whom;
- how the receiver interprets and transforms the sender's meaning;
- the conditions, authority, and time window in which it is valid;
- who executes and who owns the result and decision;
- how request, execution, state change, and final effect are confirmed;
- where failure, conflict, and delay return;
- how the relation can pause, change, withdraw, or end.

When the sides have a gap in language, capability, time, or power, build a bridge system with observation, transformation, action, confirmation, and feedback. It may contain adapters, review, human decisions, state synchronization, receipts, and feedback.

## Form a world of systems

Independent systems connected by real relations around a shared purpose can create a whole-system capability none has alone. Higher-level results feed back into the affected subsystems, while each party retains ownership, internal freedom, and the right to exit.
