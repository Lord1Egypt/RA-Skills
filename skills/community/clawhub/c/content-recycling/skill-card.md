## Description:

Content Recycling helps an agent resurface proven evergreen social posts, refresh the insight with current facts and a new hook or format, and prepare a human-reviewed recycling queue.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External social media teams and content operators use this skill to identify evergreen winners from native analytics, refresh them without duplicate reposting, and place them into a deliberate recycling cadence for human review and scheduling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Refreshed social content may carry stale facts, statistics, dates, prices, links, or compliance claims from the original post.

Mitigation: Verify current facts and compliance language before reuse, and keep the human-review step before scheduling or publishing.

Risk: Identical or high-frequency reposting can create duplicate-content, audience-fatigue, or platform originality risks.

Mitigation: Refresh the insight with a new hook, format, opening line, or updated data, and space recycled assets on a deliberate cadence.
