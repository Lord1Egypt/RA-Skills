## Description:

Prepare for IELTS Academic or General Training with progress tracking, weak area analysis, band score targeting, and immigration pathway guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External IELTS candidates use this skill to diagnose current band level, plan Academic or General Training preparation, practice section-specific tasks, track progress, and align target scores with university, immigration, or professional registration goals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: IELTS preparation records may include private goals, immigration plans, essays, transcripts, or recordings stored under ~/ielts/.

Mitigation: Install only if local study-record storage is acceptable, and review or delete ~/ielts/ on shared, synced, or backed-up machines.

Risk: Generated score guidance, target requirements, or pathway advice could be outdated or unsuitable for a specific institution, immigration program, or registration body.

Mitigation: Verify requirements against the official institution, immigration authority, or professional registration body before making exam or application decisions.
