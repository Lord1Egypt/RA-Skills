## Description:

Turn verified project requirements, method cards, and human design direction into comparable, falsifiable design-test frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[caninlew](https://clawhub.ai/user/caninlew)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Architects, design teams, and project reviewers use this skill to convert verified requirements, method cards, and human design direction into bounded design-test frameworks that can be compared, critiqued, and iterated without selecting a final scheme.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated frameworks may be mistaken for final architectural schemes or authoritative project requirements.

Mitigation: Keep outputs at the variable, relationship, and mechanism layer, preserve authority classes, and require a named human reviewer for design decisions.

Risk: Unconfirmed project directories or assumptions can make design-round outputs hard to audit.

Mitigation: Confirm the active project output and log directories, version each output, and review all design assumptions before relying on the round.
