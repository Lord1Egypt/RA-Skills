## Description:

Ai Engineering Legal helps agents analyze Chinese construction cost and legal workflows, including contract risk review, variation claims, settlement disputes, evidence-chain analysis, and draft forensic appraisal materials using the referenced Tencent IMA knowledge base.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Construction cost engineers, legal counsel, project managers, auditors, and appraisal professionals use this skill to structure issue spotting, evidence review, prompt-assisted drafting, and risk screening for construction contracts, claims, settlements, and forensic appraisal workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with content focused on Chinese construction cost and legal practice.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste sensitive contracts, identity data, privileged communications, sealed case materials, or trade secrets into an unapproved environment.

Mitigation: Redact sensitive and privileged material unless the deployment environment is approved for that data.

Risk: Outputs are drafting and risk-screening assistance and may be unsuitable as legal advice or filing-ready analysis.

Mitigation: Have a qualified lawyer or cost-appraisal professional review major disputes and filing-ready documents.
