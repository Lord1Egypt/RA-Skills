## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to get concise Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.x gameplay answers, including mining levels, mob behavior, crafting, enchanting, progression, and troubleshooting guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill defaults to Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.x, so answers may not apply to Bedrock Edition or materially different versions.

Mitigation: Ask users to specify edition and version when their question depends on platform-specific or version-specific mechanics.

Risk: Offline reference answers may not reflect a user's current in-game state.

Mitigation: Use this skill for general Minecraft knowledge and require an appropriate live-game integration when answering questions about current inventory, location, or nearby entities.
