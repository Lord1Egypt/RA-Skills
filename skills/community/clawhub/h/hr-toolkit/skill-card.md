## Description:

Hr Toolkit helps HR users generate recruitment, onboarding, offboarding, policy, handbook, and HR template materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bytesagain3](https://clawhub.ai/user/bytesagain3)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

HR employees and people-operations teams use this skill to draft recruitment process materials, onboarding and offboarding checklists, company policies, employee handbooks, and reusable HR templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive employee or candidate details may be retained in plaintext by the bundled local data logger/export utility.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive HR data unless the publisher documents storage, deletion, permissions, and retention behavior or removes the generic persistence feature.
