## Description:

Reviews Chinese government procurement solicitation documents, negotiation or consultation files, inquiry notices, procurement announcements, and contract drafts for compliance risks under the Government Procurement Law framework, then outputs a cited modification checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External procurement staff, procurement agencies, compliance reviewers, and legal support teams use this skill to review Chinese government procurement documents before release or remediation. It helps identify qualification, evaluation, policy-function, fair-competition, procedure, timing, and contract risks with legal citations and practical edits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal conclusions may be incomplete or stale if the cited knowledge bases do not cover newer procurement rules.

Mitigation: Verify important conclusions against current official sources or a qualified professional, and disclose any missing or unavailable source coverage.

Risk: The skill may be inappropriate for procurement matters outside China's Government Procurement Law framework.

Mitigation: Use it only for the stated government procurement goods, services, and applicable engineering scenarios; redirect state-owned enterprise purchasing, pure bidding-law projects, formal legal opinions, and administrative decisions to qualified tools or professionals.

Risk: Users could seek guidance for unlawful procurement conduct such as collusion, forged qualifications, bid-splitting, fabricated urgency, or false complaints.

Mitigation: Refuse those requests, explain the legal concern, and do not provide workaround instructions.
