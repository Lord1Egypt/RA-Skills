## Description:

Maps QuickBooks Online chart of accounts and transaction categories to U.S. IRS schedules, workpaper formats, reclassification checks, and crypto cost basis reporting guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Accounting and tax practitioners use this skill to convert authorized QuickBooks Online exports into U.S. federal tax schedule mappings, workpaper structures, reclassification checks, and crypto cost basis summaries for professional review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unauthorized or sensitive QBO exports could be used outside an appropriate client or business authorization context.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with QBO exports the user is authorized to handle.

Risk: Tax workpapers, schedule mappings, or reclassification entries may be incomplete or inappropriate for a taxpayer's facts.

Mitigation: Have workpapers and reclassification entries reviewed by a qualified CPA, EA, or attorney before filing or making accounting changes.

Risk: Users may mistake the skill's mapping guidance for direct tax filing support or legal tax advice.

Mitigation: Use the output as workpaper preparation support only; filing and tax advice require licensed tax software and qualified professional review.
