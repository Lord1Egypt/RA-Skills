This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Amazon sellers and ecommerce operators use this skill to plan Amazon Ads campaign structures, bid ceilings, keyword match strategies, negative keyword workflows, budget allocation, and optimization calendars based on product economics and campaign data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can influence advertising spend decisions.

Mitigation: Use it as planning support, verify margins and campaign data independently, and apply bid or budget changes manually in Amazon Ads.

Risk: Incorrect or stale product economics can lead to unprofitable bid and budget recommendations.

Mitigation: Confirm average selling price, gross margin, conversion rate assumptions, and campaign reports before using the generated plan.
