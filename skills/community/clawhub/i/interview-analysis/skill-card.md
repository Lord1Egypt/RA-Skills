## Description:

Deep interview analysis using dynamic expert routing. Automatically selects top domain thinkers based on role type to distinguish genuine capability from performance, identifying Battle Scars over Methodology Recitation. Applicable to any professional position including product management, engineering, design, operations, sales, and data science.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Hiring teams, interviewers, recruiters, and people managers use this skill to turn interview transcripts into structured candidate assessments, role-specific capability insights, interviewer meta-analysis, and decision notes for professional hiring decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Interview transcripts and generated candidate cards may contain confidential hiring records.

Mitigation: Store transcripts and generated analysis only in access-controlled locations with clear retention and deletion practices.

Risk: Candidate assessments may be misleading if generated conclusions are treated as final hiring decisions without review.

Mitigation: Review generated profiles, insights, red flags, and recommendations against the source interview evidence before using them in a hiring process.
