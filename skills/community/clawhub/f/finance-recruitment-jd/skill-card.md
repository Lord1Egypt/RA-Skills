## Description:

Generates structured finance recruitment job descriptions from a role name, core capability requirements, capability weights, and optional organization background.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[7679457979](https://clawhub.ai/user/7679457979)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Recruiters, HR teams, and finance hiring managers use this skill to draft Markdown job descriptions for finance roles with responsibilities, qualifications, and KPI sections aligned to selected capability requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated job descriptions may include inaccurate, biased, or policy-misaligned hiring language.

Mitigation: Review generated JD content for accuracy, bias, and alignment with organizational hiring policies before use.

Risk: Generic HR phrases may activate this skill when a broader recruiting response was intended.

Mitigation: Confirm that the request is for a finance recruitment job description before relying on the generated output.
