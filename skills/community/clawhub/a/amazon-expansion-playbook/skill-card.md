## Description:

Amazon Expansion Playbook helps Amazon sellers evaluate and plan expansion from the United States into Canada, Mexico, Europe, Japan, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia with market comparisons, localization, logistics, tax, and compliance checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Amazon sellers and ecommerce operators use this skill to decide whether to expand beyond their primary market, compare target regions, and draft market-specific launch plans, localization work, compliance checklists, and timelines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with market-specific guidance for North America, Europe, Japan, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may apply tax, legal, fee, certification, or marketplace policy guidance that is outdated or not valid for their country, product category, or current Amazon policy version.

Mitigation: Verify country-specific requirements, product certifications, marketplace fees, and Amazon policies with current official sources or qualified advisors before acting.

Risk: Chinese-language guidance may be misunderstood by readers who are not fluent in Chinese.

Mitigation: Use qualified translation or bilingual review before relying on the guidance for commercial decisions.
