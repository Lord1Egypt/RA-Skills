## Description:

Cleans up videos by removing watermarks, logos, and burnt-in subtitles, upscaling to 2K or 4K, and adding or translating subtitles while keeping the footage content unchanged.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[adsturbo](https://clawhub.ai/user/adsturbo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Video creators and editors use this skill to remove watermarks, logos, or burnt-in subtitles, upscale footage, and add or translate subtitles. It is suited to cleanup and subtitle localization when the picture content should stay unchanged.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local media may be uploaded to AdsTurbo and shared through a publicly accessible link.

Mitigation: Before uploading, confirm you have permission to share the media with the service and through a public link; avoid sensitive footage.
