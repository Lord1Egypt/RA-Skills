## Use Case:

External creators and developers use this Chinese-language skill to turn video ideas into structured story beats, shot-by-shot scripts, dialogue and audio cues, subtitles, and Seedance-oriented prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic video-related triggers may activate the skill in broad AI-video or storyboard conversations.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants the Chinese-language storyboard and Seedance prompt workflow before applying the skill.

Risk: The workflow may ask for or incorporate reference media intended for image or video tools and external platforms.

Mitigation: Use only reference media that the user is authorized and willing to provide to those tools or platforms.
