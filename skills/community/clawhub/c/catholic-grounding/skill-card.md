## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be mistaken for an authoritative or exhaustive theology source.

Mitigation: Review the bundled references for accuracy and use the skill as a Catechism-first grounding aid, with pastoral support recommended for complex personal questions.

Risk: The prayer helper script may not work exactly as documented.

Mitigation: Test the local helper scripts before relying on them in an agent workflow and correct the prayer helper if needed.
