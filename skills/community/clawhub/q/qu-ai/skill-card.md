## Description:

A Chinese-language editing skill that diagnoses AI-like text patterns and rewrites creative or informal writing with role-specific human-sounding style, detail, rhythm, and voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kongzichixiangjiao](https://clawhub.ai/user/kongzichixiangjiao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External Chinese-language writers and editors use this skill to transform AI-generated drafts into more natural creative writing, social posts, speeches, essays, and other informal text. It is not suitable for academic, legal, news, or other fact-sensitive documents unless every generated detail is reviewed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is designed to make AI-generated text appear human-written.

Mitigation: Use only where humanized rewriting is appropriate and avoid authorship-sensitive contexts that require disclosure or traceability.

Risk: The skill can add unsupported personal, narrative, or factual details during rewriting.

Mitigation: Require review of every generated detail and remove unsupported additions before use, especially outside creative or informal writing.

Risk: Broad activation triggers could cause accidental rewrites.

Mitigation: Narrow triggers or require explicit user confirmation before applying the skill.
