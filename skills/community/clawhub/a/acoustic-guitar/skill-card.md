## Use Case:

External users and music learners use this skill to get acoustic guitar practice advice, troubleshoot technique, and track repertoire, sessions, techniques, and goals in local Markdown notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create a local practice folder and store personal practice notes such as goals, repertoire, sessions, and technique struggles.

Mitigation: Use progress logging only when desired, review notes before saving sensitive details, and delete or edit local files under ~/acoustic-guitar/ as needed.

Risk: Guitar technique and instrument-care guidance may not fit every player, instrument, or physical condition.

Mitigation: Treat advice as practice guidance, stop techniques that cause pain or instrument damage concerns, and consult a qualified teacher or repair professional for persistent issues.
