## Description:

Plans annual training schedules, arranges session dates, detects venue, instructor, and resource conflicts, supports quarterly or monthly adjustments, and compares historical training data for planning decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Training and operations teams use this Chinese-language skill to draft annual training plans, schedule sessions, identify resource conflicts, maintain change records, and summarize progress against historical training data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated schedules may be incomplete or unsuitable if training needs, resource availability, budgets, or historical training data are inaccurate.

Mitigation: Provide current planning inputs and have the responsible training owner review and approve schedules before execution.

Risk: Training schedules, resource availability, budgets, and historical training data may contain sensitive organizational information.

Mitigation: Share only data appropriate for the agent environment and avoid including unnecessary sensitive or personal information.
