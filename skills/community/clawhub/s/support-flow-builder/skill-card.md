## Description:

Turn ecommerce support policies, ticket patterns, and channel context into reusable support flows, agent playbooks, macros, bot handoff logic, and governance notes for CX leaders, support managers, and operations teams.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

CX leaders, support managers, and ecommerce operations teams use this skill to turn policies, recurring ticket patterns, and channel goals into reviewable support workflows, agent playbooks, macros, bot handoff fields, and governance notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated macros and escalation rules could be incorrect or incomplete for a team's actual policy.

Mitigation: Review generated macros, escalation boundaries, legal wording, and compensation promises before operational rollout.

Risk: Support inputs can include customer-sensitive details that are not necessary for workflow drafting.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting unnecessary customer-sensitive data and use summaries or redacted examples when possible.

Risk: The output could be mistaken for an automatic production change.

Mitigation: Treat the output as policy guidance for human review; the skill does not connect to live helpdesks, bots, or production systems.
