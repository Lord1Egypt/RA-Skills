## Description:

Professional counseling assistant for life challenges, career decisions, relationship issues, and personal growth. Provides guidance, perspective, and practical advice in a supportive counseling style. Use when users face difficult decisions or need structured guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for structured reflection on personal decisions, career choices, relationships, life changes, and personal growth. It helps users clarify values and options while directing serious mental health needs to professional counseling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general counseling-style guidance as a substitute for licensed counseling or emergency support.

Mitigation: Frame responses as reflection and decision-support, and refer users to qualified professionals or emergency resources for serious mental health, legal, medical, financial, or crisis needs.

Risk: Broad personal-advice triggering may produce guidance for situations outside the skill's intended support role.

Mitigation: Ask clarifying questions, keep advice non-prescriptive, and narrow the response to values clarification, options, and next-step planning.
