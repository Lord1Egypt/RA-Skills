## Description:

Run engineering simulations on your own machine and read each result with its provenance, across aerodynamics, vehicle dynamics, structures, thermal, acoustics, crash and electromagnetics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use Arxyne to run local engineering simulations, inspect recorded results and provenance, and manage solver and product data on their machine.
