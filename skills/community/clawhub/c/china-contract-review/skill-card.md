## Description:

Reviews Chinese-language contracts in a China-law context to identify clause-level risks, compliance issues, legal references, and suggested revisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tobewin](https://clawhub.ai/user/tobewin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to perform preliminary reviews of labor, sales, rental, service, cooperation, confidentiality, and related Chinese contracts. It helps surface potential legal risks, compliance concerns, supporting legal references, and suggested wording changes before human legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; designed for Chinese-language contracts under China-law context.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contract review output may be incomplete, model-dependent, or unsuitable as final legal advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for preliminary review only and consult a qualified lawyer for important contracts.

Risk: The skill discloses an unpinned python-docx document-processing dependency.

Mitigation: Install dependencies in an isolated environment and prefer a pinned, reviewed python-docx version.
