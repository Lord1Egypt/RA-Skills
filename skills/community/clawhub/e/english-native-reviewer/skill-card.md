## Description:

English Native Reviewer reviews and rewrites English text for grammar, Chinglish and translationese, word choice, and style, with Chinese explanations when useful.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tuobadaidai](https://clawhub.ai/user/tuobadaidai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to polish English drafts, detect Chinglish or translationese, and produce clearer native-like wording for emails, reports, academic paragraphs, marketing copy, and similar English writing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rewrites of sensitive documents may shift meaning, tone, or locale-specific wording.

Mitigation: Review the rewritten text for meaning, tone, and required English variant before sending or publishing it.

Risk: The skill does not verify factual accuracy and does not replace legal, medical, financial, or other professional review.

Mitigation: Use subject-matter review for factual claims, regulated content, or professional terminology before relying on the final text.
