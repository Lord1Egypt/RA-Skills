## Description:

This skill rewrites emotionally charged or blunt Chinese workplace messages into professional, goal-oriented drafts that clarify risks, align stakeholders, and protect responsibility boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ssssshaoa](https://clawhub.ai/user/ssssshaoa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and managers use this skill to turn rough Chinese workplace drafts into IM, email, escalation, or client-response wording with risk diagnosis and next-step guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is optimized for Chinese workplace messaging and may keep using its Chinese template when another language is preferred.

Mitigation: Tell the agent the desired language explicitly or ask it to translate the final draft after polishing.

Risk: Workplace drafts can contain sensitive names, client details, incidents, or internal project context.

Mitigation: Remove or generalize confidential details before sharing them in the agent session.

Risk: Escalation or pressure-oriented drafts can affect workplace relationships if sent without local context.

Mitigation: Review recipients, tone, deadlines, and responsibility statements before sending.
