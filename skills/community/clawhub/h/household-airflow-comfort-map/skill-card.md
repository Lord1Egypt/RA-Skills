## Description:

Creates a comfort-focused room airflow map, timing routine, and weekly review checklist for stuffy rooms without HVAC diagnosis, repair advice, allergy guidance, or unsafe window use.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to create a household comfort routine for rooms that feel stale, stuffy, or uneven. It produces a room-by-room airflow map, daily timing routine, observation log, weekly review checklist, and safety notes limited to comfort habits using existing airflow options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could receive unsafe or overconfident home ventilation, HVAC, air-quality, window-use, or product advice because some examples exceed the stated comfort-only boundary.

Mitigation: Review examples and generated responses before deployment, keeping advice limited to household comfort habits, existing airflow options, safe window use, and referrals to qualified professionals or official guidance for hazards, equipment concerns, health issues, or outdoor air warnings.
