## Description:

Helps plan, organize, and guide household cleaning tasks room by room, including quick tidy-ups, deep cleaning, and recurring routines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[know-hub](https://clawhub.ai/user/know-hub)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to plan household cleaning work, prioritize tasks by time and energy, and generate quick resets, deep-clean checklists, guest-prep plans, or recurring maintenance routines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cleaning-product or mobility-related suggestions may not fit every home, surface, product label, or physical constraint.

Mitigation: Review advice before acting, follow product labels and local safety guidance, and adapt tasks to the user's available time, energy, and mobility.
