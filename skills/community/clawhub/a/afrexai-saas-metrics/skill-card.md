## Description:

Generates a SaaS metrics analysis from user-provided business numbers, including core B2B SaaS KPI calculations, benchmark comparisons, red/yellow/green flags, and prioritized action items.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Founders, CFOs, operators, and business teams use this skill to calculate and benchmark SaaS health metrics from raw operating data, then turn the results into a board-ready summary and prioritized action list.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: SaaS metrics can be confidential and business-sensitive.

Mitigation: Provide only numbers you are comfortable sharing with your agent and follow your organization's data-handling rules.

Risk: Benchmark and action advice may be incomplete for a specific company context.

Mitigation: Use the output as decision support and have finance or business leadership review important decisions before acting.
