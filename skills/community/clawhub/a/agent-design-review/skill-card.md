## Description:

Reviews LLM agent designs for reliability, cost, and safety, producing structured findings and prioritized fixes across control flow, tools, memory, failure handling, and safety.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill to critique and harden LLM agent architectures before launch, especially multi-step or tool-using agents that need reliability, cost, and safety review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad invocation wording may overlap with adjacent agent-design skills.

Mitigation: Installers can narrow activation wording or document precedence when multiple related design-review skills are installed.

Risk: Reviews based on incomplete briefs may include incorrect inferred assumptions.

Mitigation: Treat inferred assumptions as review prompts and confirm them before applying high-impact recommendations.
