## Description:

Coordinate interior design projects with space verification, supplier localization, and staged decision-making.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to plan interior design, renovation, staging, rental optimization, and furniture decisions from confirmed room dimensions, budget, style preferences, supplier availability, and project constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may store room details, budgets, suppliers, and preferences in local files under ~/interior-design.

Mitigation: Keep sensitive personal or financial details out of those notes, and review or remove the files before sharing a machine or workspace.

Risk: Specific furniture, material, or layout recommendations can be wrong if measurements, entry paths, supplier region, or availability are not confirmed.

Mitigation: Confirm dimensions, door and stair clearances, fixed elements, user location, supplier access, and current availability before acting on recommendations.

Risk: Rental and staging ROI estimates are planning aids and may not match actual market outcomes.

Mitigation: Validate costs, nightly-rate assumptions, comparable listings, and local market conditions before purchasing or staging.
