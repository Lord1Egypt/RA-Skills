## Description:

Turns a period's financials into the finance section of a board deck.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[skillsandagentsco](https://clawhub.ai/user/skillsandagentsco)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

CFOs and finance teams use this skill to draft the finance section of a board deck from current-period P&L data, optional comparisons, plan figures, CFO notes, and a prior deck format. It produces editable structured text while surfacing missing, inconsistent, or unsupported inputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intended to process confidential financial documents such as P&L exports, plans, CFO notes, and prior board-deck finance sections.

Mitigation: Provide only financial inputs that are appropriate for the agent to process in the user's environment.

Risk: Incomplete, unreadable, or unreconciled financial inputs could lead to misleading board-deck language if not reviewed.

Mitigation: Use the skill's required subtotal checks and always-present unresolved-gaps section, then review the draft against source financials before sharing.
