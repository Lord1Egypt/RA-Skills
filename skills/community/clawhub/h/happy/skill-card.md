## Description:

Pick 2-3 random happy moment stories from the HappyDB dataset and retell them as short stand-up comedy bits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[goog](https://clawhub.ai/user/goog)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to get light, funny retellings of randomly sampled happy moments from the bundled HappyDB CSV when they want humor, cheering up, or short comedy content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The bundled HappyDB CSV contains raw user-written entries that may be awkward, profane, linked, or otherwise unsuitable for some audiences.

Mitigation: Moderate or review sampled entries before presenting them, and resample any entry that is unsuitable for the audience.
