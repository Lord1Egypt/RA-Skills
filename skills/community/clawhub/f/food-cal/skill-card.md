## Description:

Food expiration calculator. Use when user wants to check if a food product has expired based on production/purchase date and shelf life. Calculates expiration date and tells user if food is still good, expired, or close to expiration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to estimate whether a food item is fresh, expiring soon, or expired from a product date and shelf-life duration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The calculator is an estimate and may not reflect food safety for a specific item or storage condition.

Mitigation: Treat results as planning guidance and follow package labels, storage instructions, recalls, and food-safety judgment before consumption.

Risk: Month and year shelf-life values are approximated as 30-day months and 365-day years.

Mitigation: For date-sensitive decisions, verify expiration dates against manufacturer guidance or calculate with exact calendar rules.
