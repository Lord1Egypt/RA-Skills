## Description:

Reviews video scripts and PPT drafts for pacing, timing allocation, visual-narration sync, information density, hooks, transitions, platform fit, and rewrite opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mayf3](https://clawhub.ai/user/mayf3)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and presentation reviewers use this skill to assess the pacing of video scripts or PPT narration before production. It identifies timing, visual-narration sync, information-density, hook, transition, retention, and platform-fit issues and returns prioritized rewrite suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include private scripts or unreleased presentation content in prompts.

Mitigation: Provide only the script or presentation sections that need review and remove sensitive material when possible.

Risk: Pacing scores and retention predictions may be mistaken for guaranteed publishing outcomes.

Mitigation: Treat scores as editorial guidance and validate decisions with human review, platform analytics, or audience testing.

Risk: The skill does not evaluate legal, compliance, or creator monetization risks.

Mitigation: Run a separate compliance or monetization review before publishing when regulated claims, ads, or sponsorships are involved.
