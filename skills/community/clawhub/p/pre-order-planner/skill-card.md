## Description:

Plan and execute pre-order campaigns with waitlist sequencing, early-bird incentive design, and launch communication timing for new product drops.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators and marketing teams use this skill to plan pre-order campaigns, waitlist sequencing, incentive tiers, customer communications, and fulfillment coordination for new product drops.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketplace pre-order rules, refund obligations, and payment-capture policies can vary by platform and may change over time.

Mitigation: Verify current platform rules and legal obligations before launching a campaign based on this guidance.

Risk: Pre-order planning may involve customer fulfillment files and supplier or 3PL coordination.

Mitigation: Share customer fulfillment data only through approved supplier, warehouse, or 3PL workflows.

Risk: Campaign recommendations may be unsuitable if supplier timelines, refund policies, or fulfillment buffers are not confirmed.

Mitigation: Confirm production lead times, shipping dates, refund terms, and delay communications before accepting pre-orders.
