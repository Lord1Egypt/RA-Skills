## Description:

ARS Academic Paper is a 12-agent academic writing workflow for planning, drafting, reviewing, citing, formatting, revising, and disclosing AI use in scholarly manuscripts.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[sedey999](https://clawhub.ai/user/sedey999)

### License/Terms of Use:

CC BY-NC 4.0

## Use Case:

External users, researchers, students, and academic writers use this skill to plan and draft scholarly papers, check citations, generate bilingual abstracts, prepare revisions, format manuscripts, and produce AI-use disclosure language.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process private drafts, reviewer comments, bibliographies, and optional prior writing samples.

Mitigation: Avoid uploading confidential, unpublished, co-authored, or institutionally restricted material unless the user has permission and an appropriate workspace policy.

Risk: Citation-checking and auto-correction workflows can introduce incorrect citations, DOI errors, or misleading source claims.

Mitigation: Require human review of all citations, reference entries, DOIs, and source-dependent claims before submission.

Risk: Working drafts may contain hidden audit comments or intermediate review markers.

Mitigation: Run a final formatting and disclosure review before sharing or submitting any generated manuscript.
