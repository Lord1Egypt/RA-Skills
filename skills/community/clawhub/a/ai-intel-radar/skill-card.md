## Description:

Build a high-signal AI intelligence brief from fresh web sources, with separate daily and weekly formats.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yikailucas](https://clawhub.ai/user/yikailucas)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Employees, external users, developers, and business decision makers use this skill to generate daily or weekly AI intelligence briefs from current web sources, with concise news, technical depth, commercial judgment, risks, opportunities, and action items.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad requests for latest AI news or market updates may activate the skill when the user intended a different style of response.

Mitigation: Ask the user to specify language, timeframe, and source preferences when those details are ambiguous.

Risk: Chinese-by-default output may not match every user's preferred language.

Mitigation: Set the response language explicitly when installing or invoking the skill for non-Chinese workflows.

Risk: Fresh news and market intelligence can include weak, duplicated, or uncertain source material.

Mitigation: Prefer official and primary sources, include source links, remove duplicate reports, and mark uncertain claims as "待验证".
