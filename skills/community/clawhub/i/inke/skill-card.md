## Description:

Provides lightweight summaries and performance analysis for public Inke short-video and topic pages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[codekungfu](https://clawhub.ai/user/codekungfu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to summarize public Inke short-video or topic-page results, compare recent account performance, and describe ranking or topic distributions for internal analysis and reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public-page analysis could drift into bulk scraping, downloads, or attempts to bypass human verification.

Mitigation: Keep use limited to public pages, avoid bulk collection and downloads, and do not bypass platform verification or access controls.

Risk: Summaries of visible engagement metrics may be incomplete or misleading if dynamic pages fail to load fully.

Mitigation: Have a human open or verify the public page state before relying on extracted metrics.
