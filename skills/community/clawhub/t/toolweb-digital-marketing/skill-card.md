## Description:

Professional career roadmap platform that generates personalized digital marketing learning paths based on user experience, skills, and goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd](https://clawhub.ai/user/krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, career coaches, educational platforms, and HR teams use this skill to generate personalized digital marketing learning roadmaps, identify skill gaps, and review specialization paths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The external API may receive career history, skill inventory, goals, session identifiers, timestamps, and optional user IDs.

Mitigation: Submit only the personal or career details needed to generate the roadmap and avoid unnecessary sensitive information.
