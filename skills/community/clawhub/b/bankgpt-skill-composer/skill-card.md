## Description:

Compose multi-step workflows using the 24 business skills in this repository. Use when a financial or operations task needs multiple skills with explicit inputs, outputs, and handoffs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bankgpt](https://clawhub.ai/user/bankgpt)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Finance and operations users use this skill to design workflows across available business skills, with clear inputs, outputs, dependencies, transformations, handoffs, and validation gates. For execution requests, it can guide use of available tools within the authorized scope and report outcomes or blockers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A composed workflow could carry incorrect or misleading guidance or handoffs into another skill.

Mitigation: Confirm each selected skill's instructions and review its inputs, outputs, and validation gates before execution.
