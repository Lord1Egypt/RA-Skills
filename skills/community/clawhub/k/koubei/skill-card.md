## Description:

Koubei helps agents explain Alibaba local-life restructuring, identify where former Koubei user and merchant workflows moved, and provide migration-oriented guidance for Amap and Alipay channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External users, merchants, and analysts use this skill to understand where Koubei app features moved, how merchant assets may be handled after migration, and how Alibaba local-life channels fit into broader local-commerce strategy.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Koubei, Amap, Alipay, and merchant-backend workflows may change after release.

Mitigation: Verify current official product and merchant-backend instructions before making operational decisions.

Risk: Local-life business-status guidance can become misleading if treated as authoritative current policy.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an orientation guide and avoid inventing non-public business data or unsupported migration guarantees.
