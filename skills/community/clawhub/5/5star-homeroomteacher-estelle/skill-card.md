## Description:

A Chinese-language coaching skill that helps homeroom teachers follow Estelle's 66-day route for classroom management, parent communication, student support, and teacher self-management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[maxliu1979](https://clawhub.ai/user/maxliu1979)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External educators, new homeroom teachers, school managers, and trainers use this skill for Chinese-language coaching on classroom routines, student conversations, parent communication, emotional resilience, and 66-day professional growth planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can discuss student mental-health, crisis, safeguarding, or discipline situations where generic coaching may be insufficient.

Mitigation: Use it as coaching support only; follow school policy and involve qualified safeguarding, mental-health, or administrative professionals for high-stakes cases.
