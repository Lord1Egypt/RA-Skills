## Description:

This skill helps an agent explain how to use moving averages for trend analysis, including period selection, crossover interpretation, the four-week rule, and limitations such as lag and range-bound market failures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to guide educational technical-analysis discussions about moving averages, including when to apply moving-average systems and when to avoid them. It supports human-facing trading-method explanations but does not automate trades or provide account-specific financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake educational moving-average analysis for automated financial advice or trade instructions.

Mitigation: State that outputs are educational guidance, require user judgment, and avoid account access or automated trading actions.

Risk: Moving-average signals can lag and can perform poorly in range-bound markets.

Mitigation: Explain lag and range-bound failure modes, check whether a trend is present before applying moving-average systems, and discuss filters or exit rules when relevant.
