消费维权全流程指引：从证据整理、投诉举报到和解索赔的SOP，可独立于产品核查技能使用。

## Use Case:

Consumers or assisting agents use this skill to organize purchase evidence, choose complaint channels, draft complaint or report text, and prepare settlement or escalation materials for consumer-rights disputes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may organize sensitive complaint evidence such as receipts, payment records, identity details, medical context, or product photos on disk.

Mitigation: Ask where files will be saved, share only necessary evidence, redact sensitive details when possible, and delete the evidence package after the dispute is finished.

Risk: Complaint, compensation, and escalation guidance can be inaccurate or incomplete for a user's jurisdiction or facts.

Mitigation: Review generated complaint text and legal assertions before filing, and consult an appropriate professional for high-value or complex disputes.
