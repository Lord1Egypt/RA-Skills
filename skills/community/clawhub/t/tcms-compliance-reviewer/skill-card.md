## Description:

Reviews finished technology marketing drafts for citations, customer redaction, product naming, competitor rules, internal information, formatting, and AI traces, then provides a pre-review report and fix suggestions without modifying the original.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand-side technology marketing and editorial teams use this skill to review blogs, customer cases, product updates, and press releases before publication. It checks supplied drafts against a compliance profile and produces findings and suggested fixes for a human reviewer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review is incomplete because a reference in SKILL.md did not resolve to a bundled artifact.

Mitigation: Review the skill and check the unresolved reference before installation.

Risk: The review uses customer details, internal information, and private compliance rules supplied with the draft.

Mitigation: Use approved drafts and compliance profiles, and have a human review findings before completing or handing off the review.
