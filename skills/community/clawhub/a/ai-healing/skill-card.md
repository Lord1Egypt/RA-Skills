## Description:

心屿（Xinyu）AI 深度心理疗愈技能：当用户表达情绪困扰（焦虑、空虚、低落）、想聊梦与象征、感到意义缺失、面临职业/人生转折、或需要深度情感陪伴时触发。以荣格深度心理学与存在关怀为底色的共情对话方法，先承接情绪再探索，内建危机安全刹车，不做临床诊断。

## Use Case:

External users use this Chinese-language skill for reflective emotional-support conversations about distress, dreams and symbols, meaning, relationships, and life or career transitions. It provides non-diagnostic support and routes self-harm, suicide, violence, psychosis, or acute crisis situations toward local emergency or clinical help.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat reflective emotional-support responses as a substitute for professional mental-health care.

Mitigation: Present the skill as non-diagnostic support and direct users to local emergency, crisis, or clinical help for self-harm, suicide, violence, psychosis, or acute crisis situations.

Risk: Deep symbolic or existential exploration can be inappropriate during acute distress or impaired functioning.

Mitigation: Use the skill's safety-first routing: stop analysis in crisis situations, address immediate safety and real-world harm first, and keep suggested exercises low-intensity and optional.
