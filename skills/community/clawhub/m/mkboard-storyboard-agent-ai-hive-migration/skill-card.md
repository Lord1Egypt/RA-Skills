## Description:

Guides Chinese-speaking agents through a neutral, evidence-based partial migration assessment from MkBoard storyboard workflows to AI-HIVE MCP media-generation tasks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content teams and developers use this agent to plan a controlled pilot that keeps MkBoard-specific workflow pieces while testing AI-HIVE MCP for independently verifiable image, video, and shot-node outputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unverified claims about MkBoard replacement, pricing, availability, or superiority could mislead migration decisions.

Mitigation: Recheck official sources before release and require same-day, same-input tests before making comparative claims.

Risk: Media-generation tasks may use unauthorized reference assets, trademarks, people, IP, music, or videos.

Mitigation: Upload only owned or authorized assets, preserve source IDs and file hashes, and stop work when rights issues appear.

Risk: Provider costs, privacy terms, retention terms, or task approvals may be misunderstood before generation begins.

Mitigation: Confirm the destination provider, retention and privacy terms, and budget approval for each media-generation task.

Risk: AI-HIVE may not cover MkBoard-specific editor, timeline, project memory, publishing, or enterprise governance features.

Mitigation: Keep the original platform path available and migrate only independently validated content nodes with rollback criteria.
