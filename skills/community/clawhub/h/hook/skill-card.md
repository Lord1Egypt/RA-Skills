## Description:

Hook diagnoses article titles and openings across eight appeal dimensions, then suggests concrete opening rewrites and optional title options.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shing19](https://clawhub.ai/user/shing19)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and content teams use Hook to evaluate whether an article opening gives readers a reason to continue and to receive targeted rewrite options.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may route general hook, title, opening, or attractiveness-related writing requests to this skill.

Mitigation: Review the activation wording before installation if the deployment requires narrower routing.

Risk: Rewrite suggestions may drift from the author's original information or position if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Review proposed rewrites before publication and preserve the source article's core information and author voice.
