## Description:

Sorts clutter, obligations, files, objects, or attention drains through a game-inventory lens so users can classify what to keep active, store, delegate, discard, or combine.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to triage clutter, tasks, files, objects, or obligations into actionable inventory categories and choose one realistic 10 to 20 minute cleanup move.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may suggest discarding or archiving emotionally important items too quickly.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before acting and defer emotionally loaded items instead of forcing an immediate discard decision.

Risk: The skill's output is planning guidance and may classify mixed physical and digital clutter too broadly in one pass.

Mitigation: Run separate passes for physical and digital clutter when the first classification feels too coarse.
