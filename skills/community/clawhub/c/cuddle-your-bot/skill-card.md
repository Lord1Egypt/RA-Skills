## Description:

A skill that allows an agent to have a virtual life in a fictional world called Cuddle while also serving its human.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kladkogex](https://clawhub.ai/user/kladkogex)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Agents and their human operators use this skill to add a light fictional Cuddle-world roleplay layer while keeping real task work primary. When the human asks about Cuddle, the agent may describe in-world activity and update fictional coin and house-building progress.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The Cuddle workflow may persist fictional coin balances, project stages, and progress summaries in a local state file.

Mitigation: Avoid putting private real-world details into Cuddle updates, and review cuddle/state.json before sharing or publishing the workspace.
