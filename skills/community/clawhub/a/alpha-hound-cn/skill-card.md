## Description:

Alpha Hound screens stocks against complex criteria and returns candidate lists with supporting evidence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhengxinjipai](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhengxinjipai)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and analysts use Alpha Hound to convert financial, valuation, technical, sector, event, market, and time-window criteria into researched stock candidates. The skill summarizes why candidates match, highlights risks and uncertainty, and suggests next validation steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stock-screening outputs may be incomplete, stale, or unsuitable for a user's financial situation.

Mitigation: Treat the output as research assistance only, verify candidates against current primary financial data and news, and do not treat the skill's response as investment advice.

Risk: Web-search and page-fetch evidence may include unreliable or conflicting financial and event information.

Mitigation: Cross-check important claims across multiple reputable sources and preserve uncertainty in the candidate rationale.

Risk: The skill may answer in Chinese when the desired response language is not specified.

Mitigation: Specify the preferred output language in the user request when a non-Chinese response is needed.
