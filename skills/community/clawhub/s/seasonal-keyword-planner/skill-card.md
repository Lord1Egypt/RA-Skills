## Description:

Map ecommerce keywords to a 12-month demand curve and produce a month-by-month plan for listings, ads, and content timing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to classify product keywords by seasonality and turn demand curves into a 12-month plan for listings, ads, content, inventory timing, and measurement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Seasonal peaks and timing can be wrong if market, hemisphere, or keyword demand assumptions are not validated.

Mitigation: Confirm target markets and hemisphere, label evidence versus assumptions, and validate peaks against real keyword or sales data before committing budget.
