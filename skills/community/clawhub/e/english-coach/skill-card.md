## Description:

English Coach helps users practice conversational English and review submitted English material using a chunk-based learning method, with structured post-session feedback guided by the Zone of Proximal Development.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kennethg128](https://clawhub.ai/user/kennethg128)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill as an English practice coach for natural conversation, chunk extraction from English materials, correction review, and progress tracking. The skill is intended for manual invocation during language-learning sessions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Practice text, corrections, learner profile notes, and progress history may be retained locally in the skill state files.

Mitigation: Do not provide private chats, credentials, confidential business text, or other sensitive material unless local retention is acceptable.
