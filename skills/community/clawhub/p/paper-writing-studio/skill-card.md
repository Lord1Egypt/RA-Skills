## Description:

Paper Writing Studio is a Chinese-language workflow skill for journalism and communication papers that helps classify the paper type and writing stage, then produce structured research topics, literature review material, research design, empirical analysis text, conclusions, and revision prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to guide Chinese-language journalism and communication paper writing through topic selection, literature review, research design, empirical or case analysis, and conclusion drafting. It is best suited to staged academic writing support where sources, samples, methods, and claims remain subject to user review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad paper-writing requests may invoke this skill even when the user needs another discipline-specific workflow.

Mitigation: Confirm the discipline, paper type, and current writing stage before applying the workflow.

Risk: Academic citations, research claims, or literature summaries may be unreliable if the user has not provided verified sources.

Mitigation: Require user-provided sources or independent source verification before treating citations, authors, dates, journals, or claims as final.

Risk: Empirical or analysis sections can become misleading without real samples, data, coding rules, or method choices.

Mitigation: Base analysis drafts on explicit user-provided data, samples, methods, and boundaries, and label assumptions that still need confirmation.
