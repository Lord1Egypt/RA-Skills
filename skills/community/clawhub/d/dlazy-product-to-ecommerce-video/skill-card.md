## Description:

Turn a product specification, manual, catalog, or marketplace listing into a conversion-focused shopping video with multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Online sellers and marketing teams use this skill to turn product information or marketplace listings into shopping videos for product advertising and cross-border commerce.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
