## Description:

Build a personal restaurant system for tracking places to try, favorites, and dining memories.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to maintain a local Markdown restaurant journal for places to try, favorites, visited meals, ratings, occasions, and dietary preferences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local restaurant history and dining preferences may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Review the Markdown notes saved under ~/restaurants/ and avoid storing details you do not want retained locally.
