## Description:

Meeting Agenda helps agents create Chinese-language meeting agendas, standup templates, retrospectives, one-on-ones, decision meetings, meeting minutes, and simple local task lists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bytesagain-lab](https://clawhub.ai/user/bytesagain-lab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, team leads, and agents use this skill to draft structured meeting agendas and minutes for standups, retrospectives, one-on-ones, and decision meetings. It can also keep lightweight task entries in a local meeting-agenda data directory.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Task text added through the helper can be stored locally in plaintext under the meeting-agenda data directory.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive meeting notes, credentials, customer secrets, or confidential action items unless the local storage path and permissions have been reviewed.
