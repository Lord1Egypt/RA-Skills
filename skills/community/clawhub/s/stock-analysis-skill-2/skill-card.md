## Description:

Stock Analysis Expert guides an agent through Chinese A-share stock analysis across policy, macro, industry, fundamentals, technical indicators, capital flows, valuation, ESG, behavioral finance, and risk assessment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External investors, analysts, and research users can use this skill to structure Chinese A-share research reports and investment-support analysis. Its outputs should be treated as informational research rather than professional financial advice or automated trading guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce ratings, target prices, stop-loss levels, and buy/sell signals that may be mistaken for professional financial advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as informational analysis only, verify market data independently, and consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.

Risk: The skill requires web access for market data, so stale, unavailable, or incorrect data can affect analysis quality.

Mitigation: Confirm the freshness and source of market data before relying on the generated report.

Risk: The artifact includes performance and accuracy claims for stock analysis.

Mitigation: Treat those claims as unvalidated release content unless supported by independent evaluation evidence.
