## Description:

Provides Chinese-language requirements analysis, complexity assessment, critical-thinking prompts, and structured action recommendations for product, technical, project, and personal decision scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[guilinleolee](https://clawhub.ai/user/guilinleolee)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn a requirements or decision prompt into a structured Chinese-language analysis report covering the underlying need, user context, complexity, risks, and next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat product, career, or investment decision support as professional financial, strategic, or career advice.

Mitigation: Use the output as advisory analysis only and have qualified stakeholders review high-impact decisions before acting.
