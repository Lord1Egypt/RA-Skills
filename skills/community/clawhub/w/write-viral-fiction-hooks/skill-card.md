## Description:

Generates five selectable Chinese short-fiction opening hooks from a scene idea and, after selection, a final micro-fiction copy under 200 Chinese characters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jinghong137](https://clawhub.ai/user/jinghong137)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External authors, editors, and content creators use this skill to turn a brief scene idea into selectable Chinese short-fiction opening directions and a polished short copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill defaults to dramatic Chinese suspense or romance-style openings, which may not match neutral or non-fiction writing tasks.

Mitigation: Use it for Chinese short-fiction ideation and review outputs for tone, genre fit, and factual suitability before reuse.
