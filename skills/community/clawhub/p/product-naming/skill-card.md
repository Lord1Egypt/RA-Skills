## Description:

Test product names and listing titles for TikTok Shop conversion potential before going live by scoring hook strength, search fit, and compliance risk.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External sellers, brand operators, and marketers use this skill to evaluate and improve product names and marketplace titles before launch. It scores candidate names for hook strength, search fit, compliance risk, character efficiency, memorability, and brand fit, then recommends platform-specific title adaptations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommended names may conflict with trademarks or protected terms in the target market.

Mitigation: Run independent trademark checks for each launch market before adopting a recommended name.

Risk: Names for regulated categories may imply health, performance, or compliance claims that require substantiation.

Mitigation: Review finalist names against applicable marketplace, advertising, and regulatory requirements before publication.

Risk: A name that works in one language or market may have negative connotations or poor search fit elsewhere.

Mitigation: Provide the target language and country for multi-market launches and use native-speaker or local-market review for finalists.
