## Use Case:

Writers, analysts, and agents use this skill to rewrite Chinese text so it reads more naturally, with less formulaic AI phrasing and better professional report style. It is suited for refining drafts, reports, summaries, and user-facing prose.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Rewrite output may skew toward Chinese report prose even when another language, tone, or format is desired.

Mitigation: Specify the target language, tone, and format before rewriting, and review the final text for audience fit.
