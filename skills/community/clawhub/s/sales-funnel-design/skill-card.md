## Description:

Design and optimize a sales funnel for a solopreneur business, covering funnel architecture, stage-by-stage tactics, conversion benchmarks, self-serve and high-touch models, and funnel analytics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and business operators use this skill to design, diagnose, and improve solopreneur sales funnels from awareness through retention. It helps map funnel stages, choose self-serve or high-touch motions, identify conversion leaks, and select practical optimization tactics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Conversion benchmarks and marketing tactics are generic and may not match a user's market, pricing, traffic source, or product maturity.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against the user's own funnel analytics, customer research, and current business constraints before changing campaigns or onboarding flows.

Risk: Outbound, retargeting, email automation, and paid advertising can create legal, privacy, or platform-compliance exposure.

Mitigation: Confirm applicable privacy, consent, advertising, and platform rules before launching campaigns or automations.
