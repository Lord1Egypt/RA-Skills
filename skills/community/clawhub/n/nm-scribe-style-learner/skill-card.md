## Description:

Extracts writing style patterns from exemplar text into a reusable profile.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[athola](https://clawhub.ai/user/athola)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and writers can analyze representative text to create a reusable style profile for guiding writing and editing. The profile combines measurable style features with selected exemplars and can be checked against new content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A profile based on too little or unrepresentative source text may misstate the intended style.

Mitigation: Use at least 1,000 words of representative samples, preferably multiple samples from the same genre, and validate the profile against new content.
