## Description:

实验室资质合规护栏。在实验室资质认定申请、CNAS认可文件、实验室管理体系文件、对外能力声明发布前，实时检测是否符合《检验检测机构资质认定管理办法》、CNAS-CL01及RB/T 214等规范要求，拦截不合规内容并给出修改建议。

Laboratory quality, accreditation, and compliance staff use this skill to review CMA qualification applications, CNAS accreditation documents, management-system files, personnel and equipment records, capability statements, and related public claims before submission or publication.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Compliance guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or mismatched to a specific laboratory's accreditation scope.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory and have qualified laboratory compliance staff verify current CMA, CNAS, RB/T 214, and applicable legal requirements before submission or publication.

Risk: The skill can recommend blocking or revising public capability statements, applications, or management-system content based on supplied text.

Mitigation: Review the cited issue, violated clause, and proposed correction before using the recommendation in official materials.
