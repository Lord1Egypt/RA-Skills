External content marketers, social-media operators, and creators use this skill to turn current or specified topics into Chinese social-media articles, variant titles, SEO and distribution suggestions, quality checks, and optional cover guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fresh hotspot output can be misleading when web research is unavailable, stale, or out of scope.

Mitigation: Confirm platform, article count, date range, and whether web research is allowed; state unavailable data and avoid invented rankings, metrics, or citations.

Risk: Generated social-media copy can include prohibited, sensitive, personal, or platform-restricted language.

Mitigation: Use the documented prohibited-word checker and perform human review for medium- or high-risk topics before publication.

Risk: Optional cover-image generation can add cost or produce platform- or copyright-sensitive visual guidance.

Mitigation: Confirm whether cover images should be generated, follow the bundled cover specifications, and use copyright-safe source material.
