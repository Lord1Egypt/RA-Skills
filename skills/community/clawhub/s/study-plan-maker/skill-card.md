## Description:

Creates personalized study plans, learning roadmaps, and skill acquisition schedules for learner goals such as exams, certifications, new skills, and deadline-driven preparation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[goldath](https://clawhub.ai/user/goldath)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners, educators, and advisors use this skill to turn a learning goal, deadline, current level, available study time, resources, and constraints into an achievable study plan. It supports curriculum scoping, phased schedules, SMART milestones, spaced review, and progress checkpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Study plans can be unrealistic or too generic if learner constraints are missing or example templates are copied directly.

Mitigation: Collect the learner's goal, deadline, current level, daily time budget, resources, and constraints; adapt templates and flag unrealistic timelines with alternatives.

Risk: Recommended resources or topic coverage may be incomplete for a specific exam, certification, or subject.

Mitigation: Prefer official syllabi, established learning paths, and user-provided materials when available, and ask the user to confirm required resources.
