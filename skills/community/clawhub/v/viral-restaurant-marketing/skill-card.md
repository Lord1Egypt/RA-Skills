## Description:

Helps restaurant owners and marketers create TikTok and Instagram content strategies, content calendars, viral hooks, Google Reviews growth plans, and website conversion recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Restaurant owners, marketers, and agencies use this skill to plan short-form food content, generate hooks and posting calendars, improve review collection workflows, and audit restaurant websites for booking or ordering conversion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Review-incentive guidance could lead users to offer rewards for Google reviews.

Mitigation: Do not offer rewards in exchange for Google reviews; use consent-based, policy-compliant review requests.

Risk: Customer reaction videos and review screenshots can expose identifiable customers or reviewer names.

Mitigation: Get explicit consent before filming identifiable customers or using reviewer names, and redact review screenshots unless permission is explicit.

Risk: Postiz or social posting automation could publish content before a human approves it.

Mitigation: Require manual approval before any Postiz or social posting automation publishes content.
