## Description:

Generate FOSMVVM Fields protocols with validation rules, FormField definitions, and localized messages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[foscomputerservices](https://clawhub.ai/user/foscomputerservices)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Developers use this skill to generate reusable FOSMVVM form contracts for Swift projects, including field protocols, validation methods, localized message structs, and YAML localization files.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated Swift and YAML templates may not fully match the receiving project's form model, validation conventions, or supported localization languages.

Mitigation: Review generated files before committing them and adjust field requirements, validation rules, and localization blocks for the target project.
