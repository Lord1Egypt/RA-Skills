## Description:

Draft Twitter and LinkedIn posts in one go. Use when user wants to create social media content for announcements, achievements, or updates. Combines tweet-writer and linkedin-writer skills for platform-optimized content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, creators, marketers, and professionals use this skill to turn announcements, achievements, or updates into platform-optimized Twitter/X and LinkedIn draft posts in one workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad trigger phrase "draft a post" may be applied when the user intended a non-social-media post.

Mitigation: Clarify whether the requested post is for Twitter/X and LinkedIn before drafting when the target platform is ambiguous.
