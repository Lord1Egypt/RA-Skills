## Description:

Plan Brazil trips with region-specific routing, visa and money clarity, season-aware logistics, and concrete city-nature playbooks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to build Brazil itineraries that account for passport-specific entry checks, regional routing tradeoffs, payments, accommodation, safety, weather, and practical logistics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill stores passport or nationality, trip dates, budget, dietary, mobility, and preference context locally in ~/brazil/memory.md.

Mitigation: Review, edit, or delete ~/brazil/memory.md when retained trip context is no longer desired.

Risk: Brazil entry, health, payment, weather, and destination logistics can vary by passport, route, season, and current official rules.

Mitigation: Confirm official sources before non-refundable bookings and treat the skill's travel plan as planning guidance rather than a substitute for live requirements.
