## Description:

Novel Writer helps agents create fast-paced Tomato Platform-style urban sci-fi fiction, including plot outlines, characters, chapters, humor, and logic checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[feiquan123](https://clawhub.ai/user/feiquan123)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External writers and creative-writing agents use this skill to plan, draft, and revise Chinese urban sci-fi web novels in a Tomato Platform style, including plot arcs, character profiles, chapter pacing, humor, and consistency checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad creative-writing trigger words may activate the skill for adjacent writing tasks.

Mitigation: Use it for urban sci-fi or Tomato-style fiction tasks, or explicitly ask the agent to use a different writing style for other work.

Risk: Strong style rules may bias outputs away from neutral, multilingual, or non-urban-sci-fi fiction.

Mitigation: State the desired genre, language, tone, pacing, and style exclusions when requesting other kinds of fiction.

Risk: Generated fiction may contain repetitive phrasing, weak causal links, or character behavior that needs editorial review.

Mitigation: Review and edit generated prose, then check plot logic, character consistency, and chapter pacing before publication.
