## Description:

Classifies product features into Kano categories so product teams can prune low-value scope, choose an MDP feature, and produce a prioritized backlog.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[monikazapisek](https://clawhub.ai/user/monikazapisek)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, designers, and product engineers use this skill to triage concrete feature lists, separate user-facing features from market-access prerequisites, and decide what to keep, kill, defer, or research before committing roadmap capacity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intentionally biased toward pruning features and may be too forceful for general project management or non-feature design discussions.

Mitigation: Use it for concrete feature backlog triage, MVP-vs-MDP scoping, and scope-creep audits; use a different planning approach for schedules, Gantt charts, visual polish, copy editing, or compliance prerequisites.

Risk: Low-confidence Kano classifications can become overconfident roadmap decisions when user research is missing.

Mitigation: Mark weak classifications as low confidence or Questionable and run the cheapest validation, such as five user calls or a short fake-door test, before spec lock.
