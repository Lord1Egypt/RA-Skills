## Description:

A six-stage pipeline that turns a one-line assignment into a submission-ready Chinese policy brief, from source retrieval through framing, drafting, official-format Word rendering, and page-by-page visual verification.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Government and public institution writing staff, policy analysts, and policy or general-affairs teams in state-owned enterprises and financial institutions use it to turn an assignment into a source-backed Chinese policy brief and submission-ready Word document.
