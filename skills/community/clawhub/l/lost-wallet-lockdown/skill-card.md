## Description:

Create a first-hour lost wallet lockdown checklist when a user has lost a wallet, purse, bag, cards, IDs, transit passes, keys, access badges, or cash and needs to quickly list contents, freeze cards, report IDs, monitor accounts, and plan replacements using official channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize the first hour after losing a wallet, purse, bag, cards, IDs, access items, keys, cash, or related documents. It produces a calm official-channel checklist for safety triage, last-known-place reconstruction, item inventory, freezes and reports, monitoring, replacement tracking, scripts, and confirmation logging.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The user may expose sensitive wallet or identity details while seeking help.

Mitigation: Use placeholders and redactions; do not request or share full card numbers, PINs, CVV codes, passwords, one-time codes, full SSNs, security answers, credentials, or unredacted ID images.

Risk: The user may rely on unverified contact paths during an urgent lost-wallet situation.

Mitigation: Direct the user to official apps, statements, issuer or agency websites, employer or school portals, in-person offices, or other verified institutional channels.

Risk: Lost keys plus address information, theft, coercion, or robbery may create immediate personal or home-security danger.

Mitigation: Prioritize emergency services, local authorities, building security, or trusted support when there is immediate danger or home-security risk.
