## Description:

Generates the leave-behind briefing packet for whoever watches your home, pets, and plants, with a completeness audit for critical gaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[voronindenis5](https://clawhub.ai/user/voronindenis5)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Homeowners and pet owners use the skill to prepare a sitter for household, pet, and plant routines. It audits a home registry and creates a practical handoff packet and emergency card.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: An alarm code may be included in a handoff packet when the registry opts into printing it, exposing the code if the document is photographed or shared.

Mitigation: Keep alarm codes out of generated documents by leaving the opt-in disabled; hand them over verbally or through a separate secure channel, and review the generated files before sharing.
