## Use Case:

Social media teams and account owners use this skill to plan, draft, schedule, publish, and manage posts across connected social accounts through SocialCannon.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill makes authenticated requests to SocialCannon and includes account and feed management capabilities; the security review was partial.

Mitigation: Review requested scopes and target accounts. Require human approval before live publishing, replies, deletions, disconnects, or enabling/resuming automated feeds and series; prefer draft, preview, and review modes when available.
