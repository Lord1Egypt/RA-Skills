This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and content-focused researchers use this skill to discover recent health, peptide, longevity, and biohacking information, organize findings, and generate content angles from current sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill researches health, peptide, protocol, and dosing topics where generated findings can be incomplete, outdated, or misleading.

Mitigation: Review generated findings critically and do not treat protocol or dosing content as professional medical guidance.

Risk: Scheduled heartbeat use can repeatedly perform web research and create markdown files in the workspace.

Mitigation: Enable scheduled runs only where recurring research notes are expected and periodically review the configured output locations.
