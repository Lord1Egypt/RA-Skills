## Description:

Chinese Bazi (Four Pillars) fortune calculation and basic interpretation, enabling agents to compute and read a birth chart using traditional Zi-Ping methodology.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[judyzj](https://clawhub.ai/user/judyzj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to receive a Chinese Bazi reading after providing birth date, birth time, gender, and birth city. Agents use the bundled lookup tables and arithmetic steps to produce the Four Pillars chart, Five Elements analysis, Day Master interpretation, favorable elements, and major fortune cycles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users provide birth date, birth time, gender, and birth city for an astrology-style reading.

Mitigation: Ask only for the minimum birth details needed for the chart, avoid collecting extra personal information, and add a short privacy notice.

Risk: Fortune-reading interpretations may be mistaken for factual or professional advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as traditional astrology interpretation and avoid medical, legal, financial, or other high-stakes recommendations.
