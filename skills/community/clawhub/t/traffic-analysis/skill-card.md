## Description:

Guides website traffic analysis across organic, paid, social, referral, direct, and email channels, with support for source attribution, dark traffic identification, and multi-channel reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and marketing analysts use this skill to evaluate website traffic sources, estimate dark traffic, improve UTM tagging, and segment acquisition reports for better attribution decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
