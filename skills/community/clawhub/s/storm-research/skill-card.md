## Description:

STORM Research structures a loose topic into perspectives, contradiction maps, research briefs, confidence reviews, and evidence plans before drafting content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, researchers, and developers use this skill to prepare source-backed writing by mapping perspectives, conflicts, blind spots, confidence levels, and follow-up evidence questions before drafting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Perspective maps and research briefs may be mistaken for verified facts.

Mitigation: Treat the output as planning material and use source-backed research before relying on claims, especially for high-stakes topics.

Risk: Topics with high-stakes advice, too many equally strong angles, or unverifiable core claims can exceed the skill's intended scope.

Mitigation: Pause the workflow and seek primary sources, expert review, or a better-scoped research question before continuing.
