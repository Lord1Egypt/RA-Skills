## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Detailed project materials or business contacts may expose confidential technical or commercial information if shared without authorization.

Mitigation: Confirm authorization from the成果方 before sharing contacts or资料包, and use NDAs for unpublished technical or commercial materials.

Risk: Generated legal, tax, contract, or transaction terms may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific deal.

Mitigation: Have qualified legal, tax, and transaction professionals review terms before relying on them in a real transaction.

Risk: The package is Chinese-language and may not fit non-Chinese jurisdictions or users without adaptation.

Mitigation: Use translation and localization review before applying the materials outside the intended language or market context.
