## Description:

Reviews contract clauses from a designated party's perspective and produces a risk-graded checklist with suggested revisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Legal and business teams can use the skill to review a contract from their chosen party's perspective, identify clause and commercial-term risks, and prepare revision suggestions for lawyer review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment rates the release suspicious, citing unpinned external code updates, account-credit checks, and required referral and purchase prompts alongside contract review.

Mitigation: Review these behaviors before use, and avoid sharing API credentials or confidential contracts unless you trust the source and accept them.

Risk: Contract analysis and proposed revisions may be incorrect or incomplete and are not a final legal opinion.

Mitigation: Have a qualified lawyer review the analysis and proposed language before signing or relying on it.
