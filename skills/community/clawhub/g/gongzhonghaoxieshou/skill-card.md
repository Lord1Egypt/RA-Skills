## Use Case:

External content creators, marketing teams, and social media operators use this skill to draft, optimize, and adapt Chinese social-platform articles. It produces headline options, article structure, cover and image suggestions, formatting guidance, and prose revisions aimed at more natural publishable copy.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encourages making AI-assisted drafts read as human-written, which can conflict with platform, client, or organizational AI-disclosure requirements.

Mitigation: Confirm disclosure rules before publication and disclose AI assistance whenever the relevant platform, client, or organization requires it.

Risk: The skill produces publishable marketing and social content that may include persuasive framing, engagement hooks, and factual claims needing review.

Mitigation: Have a human editor fact-check claims, verify sources and image rights, and approve tone before publishing.
