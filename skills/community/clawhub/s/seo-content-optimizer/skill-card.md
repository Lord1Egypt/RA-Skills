## Description:

Helps content creators analyze keyword density, headings, metadata, structure, internal links, readability, and technical SEO, then produce prioritized optimization guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content creators, marketers, and site operators use this skill to review articles, product pages, landing pages, knowledge-base content, press releases, and platform-specific posts for SEO improvement opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A public-post example may encourage invented personal experience, credentials, testimonials, or lived experience to increase credibility.

Mitigation: Use only truthful, verifiable claims; replace example persona or experience text with the author's actual background, sourced evidence, or omit it.

Risk: SEO reports may include projected ranking, traffic, click-through, or engagement improvements that readers could treat as guarantees.

Mitigation: Frame projected metrics as estimates, validate results with analytics data, and avoid publishing unsupported performance claims.
