## Use Case:

External users use this skill to generate Chinese liberal-arts and social-science paper titles, outlines, chapter drafts, abstracts, and keywords from user-provided references and research background. It is intended as an academic writing aid that supports iterative review, not as a substitute for citation checking or academic integrity review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated academic text may include inaccurate claims, weak citations, or content that does not meet institutional integrity standards.

Mitigation: Review all generated sections for accuracy, verify citations against the provided references, and revise the final paper before submission.

Risk: The artifact claims local-only processing, but privacy depends on the actual OpenClaw and model runtime configuration.

Mitigation: Confirm the runtime is configured for local processing before using sensitive research data or unpublished materials.
