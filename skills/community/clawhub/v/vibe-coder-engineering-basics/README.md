Learn 107 Software Engineering Basics in Plain Words, Then Check What Your App Needs

Give your AI agent one app. It teaches you the engineering basics behind it, in plain words, then checks which ones your app needs now, which ones later, and which ones not yet, with a fix draft for owner review.

Many apps are built fast with an AI agent, and they work on day one. Then a password leaks, a backup does not exist, a slow page drives users away, or a small change breaks the whole app. This skill teaches the basics first, in plain words, then checks what your app actually needs, so you fix the right things first.

## What you get

- A plain words lesson for 107 software engineering basics, grouped by theme: security, auth and identity, scaling and performance, reliability, and shipping and teamwork
- A deeper teach for the core terms in each group: what it is, when an app needs it, what happens if it is missing, and how to check it
- A need check for your app: Now, Later, or Not yet, with evidence only from what you paste in, never a guess
- A fix draft, labelled DRAFT, NOT SENT: terms taught, need check summary, what to learn first, what to check first, and what to keep
- A missing checks list at the end, so nothing is hidden

## Who this is for

People who build apps with an AI agent, and want to understand what is behind the app, in simple words. People who run a small business app, and want to know what is missing, and what to fix first. People who are not technical, and want to ask their builder the right questions, with the right words.

## Why this saves money

A taught owner stops paying for fixes they do not need yet, like scaling for 5,000 users when the app has 40. A need check stops a missing backup, or a leaked secret, from turning into a lost app, or lost customers. A fix draft, reviewed first, stops a live app from changing in a way the owner did not approve.

## Technical, for coders

Structured teach and check guide for 107 software engineering basics, grouped by theme, ready to brief any AI agent, or a human builder. Each term has a one line plain words explanation, and core terms in each group have a deeper teach and check: what it is, when an app needs it, what happens if it is missing, and how to check it with pasted evidence. No guarantee of more users, more sales, or a higher rank, and no claim that learning a term alone makes an app safe, fast, or ready. The fix draft is for owner review, not a live change.

## How to use it

1. Install the skill in your AI agent (see below).
2. Give your agent the app details, in plain words: what it does, who uses it, how many users, how people log in, where the data lives, and what is slow or broken.
3. Ask: "Teach and check this app with vibe-coder-engineering-basics."
4. Read the terms taught first. Every need needs evidence from what you pasted in.
5. Read the need check. A term with no evidence is Not confirmed, never a guess.
6. Read the fix draft. Approve the first fix before anything goes live.

If you already give the app details up front, the skill teaches, checks, and drafts in the same reply. It never stops at questions alone.

## What it will not do

- It will not change a live app, publish anything, or change live code, without a separate approval from the owner.
- It will not invent a term, a definition, a price, a link, a stat, a partner, a testimonial, or a result.
- It will not mark a need as confirmed without evidence pasted in the message.
- It will not ask you to paste a real password, a real key, or real customer data.
- A fix draft is not a promise of more users, more sales, or a higher rank.
