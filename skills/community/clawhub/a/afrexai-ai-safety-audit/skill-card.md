## Description:

Structured AI safety and alignment audit for businesses deploying AI agents, covering risk classification, 30 safety controls, gap analysis, and a 90-day remediation roadmap.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business, compliance, security, and engineering teams use this skill to assess AI deployments against safety, governance, security, monitoring, and organizational readiness controls. It produces a practical scorecard, prioritized gap analysis, and remediation roadmap for production AI systems.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide sensitive business details, secrets, credentials, customer data, or regulated personal information during an audit.

Mitigation: Provide only the business and AI-system details needed for the audit, and avoid sharing secrets, credentials, raw customer data, or regulated personal information.

Risk: The artifact links to optional third-party paid resources outside the skill.

Mitigation: Vet external paid resource links separately before relying on them or purchasing related materials.

Risk: Audit outputs may be mistaken for definitive legal, regulatory, or security determinations.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a structured self-assessment and review findings with qualified legal, compliance, and security stakeholders before making deployment decisions.
