## Description:

Set up a chart of accounts and rules for categorizing transactions, including examples, edge cases, and a clean-books routine, while avoiding tax or accounting advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and business operators use this skill to create a practical chart of accounts, categorize recurring transactions consistently, and prepare cleaner bookkeeping records for accountant review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bookkeeping categorization output could be mistaken for professional tax or accounting advice.

Mitigation: Confirm tax-sensitive categories, deductibility, capitalization, and jurisdiction-specific treatment with a qualified accountant.

Risk: Transaction examples or bank-export data may include sensitive financial information.

Mitigation: Share only the transaction details needed for categorization, or intentionally provide fuller data only when the agent should use it.
