## Description:

Generates a customized 90-day Amazon product launch plan covering category selection, listing optimization, inventory strategy, advertising, KPI tracking, and launch readiness scoring for Amazon sellers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking Amazon sellers and ecommerce operators use this skill to turn a product category and short product description into a practical launch plan, checklist, advertising framework, and KPI review workflow. The included planner script can score launch readiness, produce a schedule, and surface launch risks from provided product data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business recommendations or operational thresholds may be outdated because Amazon policies, category rules, and advertising limits can change.

Mitigation: Verify current Amazon official policies, ad limits, category requirements, and seller account constraints before using the plan operationally.

Risk: The playbook provides planning templates and scoring heuristics rather than guaranteed launch outcomes.

Mitigation: Adapt recommendations to actual market research, inventory capacity, margin targets, and product performance data.

Risk: The release evidence notes that the skill is primarily written for Chinese-speaking Amazon sellers.

Mitigation: Localize language, assumptions, and marketplace examples before using it with other seller audiences.
