## Description:

Drafts an AIHA-aligned occupational exposure assessment strategy for a workplace, process, or Similar Exposure Group, including basic characterization, SEG construction, qualitative exposure rating, sampling planning, statistical analysis, and an unsigned CIH/ROH/CSP review block.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

CIHs, ROHs, CSPs, industrial hygienists in training, and EHS managers use this skill to draft occupational exposure assessment strategies that organize site characterization, agent inventories, SEGs, sampling plans, statistical decision rules, corrective-action recommendations, and review blocks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated occupational-health strategy text could be mistaken for an approved exposure judgement or compliance decision.

Mitigation: Keep outputs marked as draft and require review and approval by the named qualified CIH, ROH, or CSP before decisions are made.

Risk: User-provided workplace data could include worker identifiers, medical records, SDS excerpts, or fit-test details.

Mitigation: Summarize sensitive material, avoid worker identifiers and raw medical records, and keep fit-test and respiratory-protection decisions outside the skill's authority.

Risk: A plan could be incomplete if it lacks analytical methods, laboratory accreditation, QA/QC blanks, chain of custody, or statistical evidence.

Mitigation: Require those items in the draft workflow and leave unresolved gaps open for qualified reviewer resolution.
