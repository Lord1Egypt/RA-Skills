## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The included scripts handle AI-HIVE API credentials and generated or downloaded files.

Mitigation: Use low-privilege revocable API keys, keep AI_HIVE_BASE_URL or --base-url on the official AI-HIVE endpoint, avoid sensitive production keys, and choose output directories where accidental overwrite would not be damaging.

Risk: Generated AI-search and media content can be inaccurate, unlicensed, or mistaken for guaranteed model ranking or citation outcomes.

Mitigation: Use user-confirmed facts and sources, verify rights for likenesses and protected media, review outputs for factual accuracy and copyright safety, and do not promise third-party search inclusion, ranking, or citation.
