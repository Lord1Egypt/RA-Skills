## Description:

Turns Word, Markdown, and other documents into explainer, report, or training video projects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users provide Word, Markdown, or other documents to create explainer, report, courseware, or training video projects through the dLazy file-to-video workflow.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and attached documents are sent to dLazy services, and attached files are uploaded for processing.

Mitigation: Only submit documents you are comfortable sharing with dLazy; exclude confidential information and review the service terms.

Risk: The security review is partial and notes that some reported CLI actions are not clearly connected to the skill's stated purpose.

Mitigation: Review the pinned CLI package and its behavior before installing or using it.
