## Description:

Helps agents conduct Chinese-language consumer research, including target audience profiling, needs analysis, questionnaire design, and data processing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cloudyxuq](https://clawhub.ai/user/cloudyxuq)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, marketing, and user research teams use this skill to structure consumer insight work, profile target audiences, identify user needs, design questionnaires, and prepare consumer research reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Survey responses and consumer research notes may contain personal or sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use anonymized or aggregated data where possible, add a consent and privacy notice before collecting responses, and avoid storing direct identifiers.

Risk: Generated reports and local data files may be stored outside the intended project area if outputs are redirected carelessly.

Mitigation: Keep generated reports and data files inside the intended project directory and review output paths before running the helper script.
