## Description:

Turn comments under the user's LinkedIn posts into a ranked warm-lead list with suggested replies and next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[porenes](https://clawhub.ai/user/porenes)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales professionals, founders, and creators use this skill to identify promising prospects among commenters on their LinkedIn posts. It scores public comments for fit and intent, then drafts a tailored reply and recommends a follow-up step; approved replies can be saved and scheduled through Taplio.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Approval to schedule replies may be interpreted as approval to like a lead’s public LinkedIn comment, creating a public-facing action without clear consent.

Mitigation: Require a separate, explicit confirmation for each like; do not treat approval to schedule replies as permission to like comments.

Risk: An unresolved local reference left the security scan incomplete.

Mitigation: Resolve and review the referenced file, then complete a security scan before installation.
