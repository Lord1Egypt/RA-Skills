## Description:

This Chinese-language skill uses a Three Ledgers diagnostic framework to help builders identify attention traps, detect category swaps, and assess whether work creates lasting value.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[2023network](https://clawhub.ai/user/2023network)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Builders, creators, and product teams use this skill to examine whether a project is optimizing for the work itself, the public story around it, or commercial outcomes. It provides self-inspection questions, signal-calibration checks, and evaluation matrices for redirecting effort toward durable value.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated outputs may include Chinese-language guidance and author or brand attribution.

Mitigation: Use the skill where Chinese-language reflective guidance is appropriate, and review outputs before using them in neutral-format or attribution-free contexts.

Risk: Reflective diagnostic advice may be mistaken for proof that a project is commercially viable or product-ready.

Mitigation: Treat the output as a decision aid and validate conclusions against real user, reliability, retention, and business signals.
