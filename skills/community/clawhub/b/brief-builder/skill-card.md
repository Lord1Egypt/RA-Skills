## Description:

Brief Builder interviews users with focused, one-at-a-time questions to turn vague requests into structured briefs with goals, audience, constraints, success criteria, and handoff guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, developers, and agent operators use this skill to clarify under-specified requests before producing work or handing a brief to another skill. It is most useful when the initial ask lacks concrete goals, audience, scope, voice, constraints, or success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad planning language when the user only wants casual brainstorming.

Mitigation: Ask the user whether they want a structured brief or a lighter brainstorming exchange, and accept redirection when they decline the brief-building flow.

Risk: The final brief can preserve incorrect assumptions if vague answers are accepted too early.

Mitigation: Follow up on vague answers, label defaults as assumptions, and confirm the brief before handing it to another skill.
