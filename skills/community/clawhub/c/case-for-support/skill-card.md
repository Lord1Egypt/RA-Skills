## Description:

Case For Support helps fundraising teams draft donor-centred cases for support, campaign case statements, major-gift appeals, and donation arguments with need, solution, credibility, impact, giving levels, urgency, and a clear ask.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External fundraising, development, and communications teams use this skill to create donor-facing cases for support, campaign case statements, major-gift appeals, and donation arguments. It structures the case around donor-centred need, solution, credibility, impact, urgency, giving levels, and a clear ask.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated statistics, gift amounts, or impact claims may be inaccurate if example data is left unchanged.

Mitigation: Review and replace all marked example figures and claims with verified organizational data before using the case externally.

Risk: Fundraising copy could overstate urgency, credibility, or outcomes if the brief lacks evidence.

Mitigation: Review need, proof, giving levels, and impact statements against approved organizational sources before publication.
