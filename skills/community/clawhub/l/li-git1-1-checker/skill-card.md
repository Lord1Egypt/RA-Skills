## Description:

Li Git1.1 Checker reviews standards drafts against GB/T 1.1-2020 using a two-phase checklist and source-standard review, then produces a structured findings report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[43622283](https://clawhub.ai/user/43622283)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Standards authors, reviewers, quality teams, and training users use this skill to check draft standards for GB/T 1.1-2020 structure, elements, wording, numbering, and reportable issues. It can also support approximate crosswalk reviews for ANSI, CEN/CENELEC, JIS, DIN, BS, AFNOR, and ISO/IEC structures when used as comparative guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft standards may contain confidential content, and the skill may analyze submitted documents visually.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential drafts unless the user is comfortable with visual document analysis in the agent environment.

Risk: International standards comparisons are approximate and may not provide authoritative compliance decisions outside GB/T 1.1-2020.

Mitigation: Use the international-standards sections as comparative guidance and confirm final decisions against the applicable standards body rules.
