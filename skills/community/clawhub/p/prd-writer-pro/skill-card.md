## Description:

PRD文档撰写助手Pro helps product managers turn product requirements and user stories into structured Chinese PRD documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ryan-wuxl](https://clawhub.ai/user/ryan-wuxl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers and product teams use this skill to analyze feature requirements, collect missing context, and draft complete PRDs in Markdown. It is especially suited to new feature PRDs, feature optimization requests, user-story-to-PRD conversion, and first-draft generation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated PRDs may omit privacy, consent, or third-party data-flow details for products involving notifications, APIs, financial data, or user data.

Mitigation: Review each generated PRD for privacy, consent, and third-party data-flow requirements before sharing or implementation.

Risk: Draft requirements may be incomplete if the initial feature context is sparse.

Mitigation: Use the skill's requirement-analysis step to collect background, target users, core scenarios, business goals, constraints, and success metrics before drafting.
