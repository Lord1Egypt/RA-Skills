## Description:

Provides reusable AI writing and revision guidance for Chinese C-journal-style academic papers, especially mechanism-focused work across journalism, education governance, and public administration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiiyyo](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiiyyo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, researchers, and academic writers use this skill to structure, draft, revise, and quality-check Chinese C-journal-style papers with stronger research questions, literature reviews, mechanism frameworks, typified empirical material, citation discipline, and restrained academic prose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated citations, factual claims, or empirical descriptions may be inaccurate or fabricated if used without review.

Mitigation: Check every citation and factual claim against real sources and keep references aligned with the paper text.

Risk: AI-generated structure or prose may substitute for the author's own research judgment and academic boundaries.

Mitigation: Use the skill for drafting and revision support only after the paper's research positioning, evidence base, and theoretical claims are independently established.
