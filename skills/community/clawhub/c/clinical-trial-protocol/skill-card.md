## Description:

Drafts a structured clinical trial protocol synopsis for interventional studies, covering objectives, design, eligibility, interventions, endpoints, statistics, and safety/ethics for expert review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External clinical research teams and protocol authors use this skill to draft protocol synopses that align objectives, endpoints, eligibility, statistical planning, and safety/ethics sections before expert review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A drafted protocol synopsis could be mistaken for medical, statistical, regulatory, or ethics sign-off.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary drafts and require review by qualified investigators, statisticians, IRBs or ethics committees, and applicable regulators before use.

Risk: Missing prior evidence, assumptions, or effect sizes could lead to unsupported protocol details.

Mitigation: Keep uncertain details as clearly labeled placeholders or draft assumptions and require the study team and statistician to confirm them.

Risk: A protocol draft that omits safety monitoring, stopping rules, informed consent, IRB or ethics approval, or GCP conduct could be unsafe or noncompliant.

Mitigation: Check that safety, ethics, consent, data integrity, and GCP elements are present before expert review and approval.
