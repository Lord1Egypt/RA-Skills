## Description:

Psych312 Scenario Analysis maps user-provided scenarios, stories, behavior fragments, case dialogue, media plots, interpersonal events, and student cases to core 312 psychology exam concepts for structured study analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tianzhen777](https://clawhub.ai/user/tianzhen777)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners and exam-preparation users use this skill to turn Chinese-language scenario text into structured 312 psychology exam review material. The output is intended for exam-oriented case analysis and knowledge reinforcement, not diagnosis, counseling, crisis support, or real-person mental-health judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may over-apply psychology terminology to casual or sensitive personal stories.

Mitigation: Use the output as exam-study material and explicitly steer it away from diagnosis, counseling, crisis support, or real-person mental-health judgments.
