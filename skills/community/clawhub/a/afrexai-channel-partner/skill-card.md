## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Advisory channel-program guidance may be connected to live CRM, billing, partner portal, or approval workflows.

Mitigation: Keep human approval on account blocking, deal approvals, registration restrictions, compensation changes, and other business-impacting actions.

Risk: Partner scoring, compensation, and conflict-resolution recommendations can affect revenue and partner relationships if applied automatically.

Mitigation: Use the skill output as decision support and require business owner review before changing partner status, payouts, registration access, or account ownership.
