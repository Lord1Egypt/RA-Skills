## Description:

Helps agents build bidding document frameworks and draft technical proposals for competitive negotiation and tender projects in Chinese.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[powerzzjohn](https://clawhub.ai/user/powerzzjohn)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Proposal teams and business users use this skill to analyze user-supplied .docx tender documents, summarize scoring criteria, build bidding document outlines, and draft technical proposal sections in structured Markdown.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-supplied tender documents may contain personal identifiers, tax or social-security details, or financial data.

Mitigation: Provide only the sections needed for the task and redact unnecessary sensitive information before use.

Risk: Generated pricing, legal, or compliance content may be inaccurate or unsuitable for submission.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer check pricing, legal, and compliance content before submitting a bid.
