## Description:

分析药物组合机制、协同证据与联合开发策略，用于研究两个或多个药物的互补机制、协同证据、耐药克服逻辑和组合开发策略，并支持联合用药假设形成和实验优先级排序。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External researchers, analysts, and drug-development teams use this skill to compare drug combinations, summarize synergy evidence, evaluate ADMET complementarity, generate testable combination hypotheses, and draft staged experimental plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated ADMET, synergy, and combination-development conclusions may be incorrect, incomplete, or only hypothesis-level.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research hypotheses and require expert review plus experimental validation before scientific, clinical, or commercial use.

Risk: The workflow may query external pharma, literature, patent, and chemical-analysis tools with drug names, indications, and related research context.

Mitigation: Install and run only where those external-tool queries are acceptable for the user's confidentiality and data-handling requirements.
