## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to produce structured traditional BaZi readings or explain BaZi concepts such as Heavenly Stems, Earthly Branches, Five Elements, Ten Gods, day-master strength, useful gods, luck cycles, and annual influences. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on fortune-telling outputs for medical, legal, financial, relationship, or other major life decisions.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as traditional cultural reference and reflective commentary, and explicitly avoid presenting them as professional advice.

Risk: Personalized readings may require sensitive birth details such as birth time, place, and gender.

Mitigation: Request only the details needed for the requested reading and remind users to share them only when they intentionally want a personalized interpretation.

Risk: Incorrect or incomplete birth time, location, calendar type, or boundary-hour handling can produce inaccurate charting.

Mitigation: Confirm calendar type, time, place, and assumptions before charting; for complex calendar or true-solar-time cases, advise checking against a professional charting tool.

Risk: Negative fortune-telling language can create anxiety or misleading certainty.

Mitigation: Use qualified, non-absolute language and avoid fear-inducing terms; present concerns as areas to watch rather than fixed outcomes.
