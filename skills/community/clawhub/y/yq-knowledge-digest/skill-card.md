## Description:

Converts PDFs, textbooks, and topics into personalized study assets including handwritten-style notes, quizzes, slides, mind maps, and audio lessons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tianheihei002](https://clawhub.ai/user/tianheihei002)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, educators, and learning-support agents use this skill to turn source materials or a learning topic into level-appropriate study materials. It supports notes, interactive quizzes, slides, mind maps, and audio lessons tailored to the learner's grade level and interests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated quiz HTML built from untrusted documents or web material may contain unsafe or unsanitized content if hosted or reused outside the local workflow.

Mitigation: Treat generated HTML as untrusted, review or sanitize it before hosting, and avoid serving it on authenticated sites without escaping checks.
