## Description:

A Chinese-language evaluation framework for judging whether a knowledge-product niche is suitable for overseas monetization across seven dimensions and producing a viability report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators, freelancers, and small teams use this skill to assess overseas knowledge-product opportunities before building courses, templates, communities, or other digital products.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market demand, pricing, traffic, and platform benchmark data may become stale or differ by region.

Mitigation: Refresh public market data and platform metrics before making launch, pricing, or investment decisions.

Risk: The skill may answer in Chinese by default.

Mitigation: Ask the agent for the desired output language when the report is intended for non-Chinese readers.

Risk: Some evaluated niches, such as medical, legal, investment, health, or earnings-related topics, may carry compliance or platform-policy risk.

Mitigation: Review claims, add appropriate disclaimers, and avoid promising outcomes before publishing content or products in regulated categories.
