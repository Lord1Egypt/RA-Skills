## Description:

Plans museum and exhibition visits, explains works on site, and helps visitors create sourced visit notes and share content within venue and copyright rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External museum visitors use this skill to plan a visit, understand works or exhibitions, and organize their own observations into a sourced memory. It also gives practical guidance for sharing visit content within venue and copyright rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
