## Description:

Itinerary-first flight support for babies, children, elderly passengers, reduced-mobility travelers, and people carrying medication or medical devices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External travelers and travel-support assistants use this skill to plan flights involving infants, children, elderly passengers, reduced-mobility travelers, medications, or medical devices. It produces timeline-based actions, request scripts, documentation checklists, and confirmation guidance in Simplified Chinese.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medication, medical-device, accessibility, airline, airport-security, customs, and health requirements may vary by carrier, airport, destination, and traveler condition.

Mitigation: Use the skill output as planning guidance and confirm current requirements with the airline, airport security authority, destination customs or health authority, and a clinician or pharmacist for medical decisions.

Risk: Travel plans involving infants, elderly passengers, reduced mobility, or medical devices can fail if required services or documentation are requested too late.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's timeline and request-card outputs, then verify deadlines, forms, equipment acceptance, and day-of-travel confirmation steps with the relevant carrier or airport.
