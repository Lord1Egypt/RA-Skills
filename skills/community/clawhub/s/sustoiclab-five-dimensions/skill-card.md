## Description:

Guides Chinese-language agents to analyze complex events, social issues, historical questions, and life decisions through five sequential dimensions, with a nested five-layer method when the user is stuck.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[susstoiclab](https://clawhub.ai/user/susstoiclab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to structure broad analysis of complex situations through history, interests, power, confrontation, and essence. It is most useful as an organizing framework and should not replace qualified professional advice for medical, legal, financial, mental-health, or crisis decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad analysis triggers can be applied to high-stakes medical, legal, mental-health, financial, or crisis decisions.

Mitigation: Use the skill only as an organizing framework for those topics and rely on qualified professional advice where appropriate.

Risk: The final structured analysis may reflect incomplete, uncertain, or user-supplied assumptions.

Mitigation: Keep information gaps visible in the report and treat conclusions as provisional until key facts are verified.
