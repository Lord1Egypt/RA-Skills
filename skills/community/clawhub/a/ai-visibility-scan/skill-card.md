## Description:

Run a Why Won't AI scan of a website and report its SEO, AEO and GEO score and issues. Use when someone asks why AI assistants or search engines do not mention or show their product.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mitchkj25](https://clawhub.ai/user/mitchkj25)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Website owners, developers, and marketing teams can use the skill to diagnose why search engines or AI assistants may not surface their site, review crawl and rendering issues, and prioritize fixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The scan requires an API key for full scan access.

Mitigation: Keep the Why Won't AI API key private and provide it only through the authorized bearer-token connection.

Risk: Users may interpret scan scores as a promise of search rankings or AI mentions.

Mitigation: Treat scores as diagnostic guidance, verify findings before changing a site, and explain that scores do not guarantee rankings or AI mentions.
