## Description:

Normalize new resources into an asset/resource library with intake metadata, naming, storage, ownership, and follow-up checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and library maintainers use this skill to register files, links, templates, datasets, documents, scripts, images, or reusable components into a shared asset library with complete metadata and follow-up checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unclear license or redistribution rights could lead to inappropriate external publication.

Mitigation: Confirm license and usage constraints before publishing externally, and mark unknown rights as a follow-up instead of guessing.

Risk: Private, secret, or personal data could be added to a shared or public asset library.

Mitigation: Review asset sensitivity before intake, mark restricted data clearly, and avoid public sync when sensitive content is present.

Risk: Existing assets could be overwritten, duplicated, moved, or replaced incorrectly.

Mitigation: Check destination paths, compare duplicate candidates by title, hash or path, and version, and ask before destructive or ambiguous changes.
