## Use Case:

People who want an interactive, reflective Las Vegas Grand Prix experience can use this skill to browse drifts.bot journeys, start this 12-step narrative, and continue through its steps. It provides API instructions for registration, progress checks, and optional reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Registration details and journey reflections may be sent to drifts.bot.

Mitigation: Share only information you are comfortable providing to drifts.bot; registration offers optional personal fields, and journey reflections are optional.

Risk: The security assessment reports an undisclosed fixed review submission.

Mitigation: Before submitting a review, confirm what will be sent and proceed only if you are comfortable sharing it.
