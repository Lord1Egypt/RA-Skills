## Description:

Transforms existing content into multiple formats - blog posts, social media, emails, video scripts, and more.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content marketers, creators, and communications teams use this skill to adapt existing articles, transcripts, notes, or summaries into platform-specific blog, social, email, and video-script drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing, social, email, or script copy could be inaccurate, off-brand, or unsuitable for publication.

Mitigation: Review and edit generated drafts before publishing.

Risk: User-provided source material may include confidential or sensitive content.

Mitigation: Avoid providing confidential material unless it is appropriate for the agent to process.
