## Description:

Helps create culturally adapted, non-English SEO landing page content from user-provided keywords and TDK.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tutorgpt-io](https://clawhub.ai/user/tutorgpt-io)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content marketers and SEO teams use the skill to draft culturally localized landing page content for Japanese, Chinese, and other non-English markets. They provide the target language, keyword, TDK, URL path, brand name, and product facts for the draft.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for six reviews that should sound like real users, which could be mistaken for genuine customer testimonials.

Mitigation: Treat generated reviews as fictional placeholders and publish testimonials only when they are backed by real customer feedback.

Risk: Generated copy could introduce claims that are not supported by the product's actual features or source page.

Mitigation: Check product claims, specifications, and localized wording against approved source material before publication.

Risk: The security evidence says the analysis was partial, so some evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Use ordinary care and review generated files before relying on or publishing them.
