## Description:

MLA 9 referencing for humanities: Works Cited, container system, (Author Page) in-text citations, headline capitalization. Use for papers requiring MLA style.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Students, educators, researchers, and writing assistants use this skill to draft MLA 9 Works Cited entries and matching in-text citations for humanities papers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated MLA citations may be incomplete or may not match instructor-specific requirements.

Mitigation: Verify final citations against instructor requirements or the official MLA style guide before submission.
