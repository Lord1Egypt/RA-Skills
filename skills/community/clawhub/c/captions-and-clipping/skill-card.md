## Description:

The long-form-to-Shorts and sound-off captions skill helps agents turn podcasts, webinars, and long videos into short-form clips using Opus Clip, CapCut, and Submagic while keeping human review, clean export, and AI-edited disclosure in the workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external creators, and social-media operators use this skill to repurpose long-form video into self-contained Shorts, Reels, or TikToks with reviewed captions, platform-specific handoffs, and human approval before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may interpret clean-export guidance as permission to remove ownership, attribution, trial, or platform watermarks unlawfully.

Mitigation: Use clean exports only for content the user owns or is authorized to edit, and follow each editing tool and platform's licensing and commercial-use terms.

Risk: Automatically selected clips may be inaccurate, off-brand, incomplete, or misleading if published without review.

Mitigation: Keep human review in the loop for clip selection, transcript accuracy, caption placement, disclosure, and final publishing approval.

Risk: AI-edited video may require platform or regional disclosure.

Mitigation: Plan and apply AI-edited labels or altered-content disclosures where required by the target platform or applicable law.
