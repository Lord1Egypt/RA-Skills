# Country module: Germany

Use this file when the intake label language is German. It sets **display
labels** only. Keys, option values (`transportJob`, `collectionReport`,
`deliveryReport`, `artwork`, `crate`, `pass`, `defect`, …) and tool names stay
as in the rest of the skill. This skill does not give legal advice. What a
job must record, and for whom, are intake answers from the business.

## Notes

The lists are the book the manager works from. Keep any loan agreement,
consignment note or insurer form the business already uses, in the form their
advisor has set. Declared-value fields store the figure the business provides;
they are not an insurance review.
