## Description:

Guides monthly or quarterly reviews of portfolio allocation, drift, concentration, and changes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[manteclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/manteclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Portfolio owners use this workflow monthly, quarterly, or after a major market or life change to compare allocation with targets, review concentration and period changes, record income, and document decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Portfolio review results could be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Use the workflow to organize and document a review; the portfolio owner makes the decisions, as stated in the skill.

Risk: The available scanner review is incomplete because the manifest could not be parsed and component coverage is reported as 0%.

Mitigation: Review the skill before use; the scanner evidence does not establish that it is risk-free.
