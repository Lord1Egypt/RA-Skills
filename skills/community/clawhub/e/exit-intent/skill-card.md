## Description:

Design exit-intent popup strategies for ecommerce sites including trigger rules, offer types, copy variants, and A/B testing plans that recover leaving visitors without degrading UX or brand perception.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Ecommerce marketers, conversion-rate specialists, and store operators use this skill to design exit-intent popup strategies, offer tiers, copy variants, creative requirements, A/B tests, and monitoring plans for cart and browsing abandonment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Exit-intent recommendations can affect customer consent, email or SMS capture, accessibility, mobile interstitial compliance, and discount economics.

Mitigation: Review privacy, accessibility, mobile interstitial guidance, and margin impact before implementing recommendations on a live store.

Risk: Poorly timed or aggressive popups can degrade user experience, brand trust, Core Web Vitals, and conversion measurement quality.

Mitigation: Use conservative trigger rules, session frequency caps, non-intrusive mobile formats, performance checks, and holdout-control A/B tests before scaling.
