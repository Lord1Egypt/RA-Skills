## Description:

Prepare for the CPA exam with section-order strategy, 18-month window tracking, score analysis, and state eligibility guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

CPA exam candidates, tutors, and supporters use this skill to plan section order, track study progress and deadlines, analyze scores, manage NTS timing, and reason about state eligibility requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may store CPA study plans, score reports, NTS dates, education details, and student records locally under ~/cpa/.

Mitigation: Keep sensitive records out of the tracker unless local storage is acceptable, and review the files before sharing or syncing them.

Risk: CPA eligibility, NTS timing, and state board requirements can change.

Mitigation: Verify requirements and deadlines against official state board or NASBA sources before making scheduling, application, or eligibility decisions.
