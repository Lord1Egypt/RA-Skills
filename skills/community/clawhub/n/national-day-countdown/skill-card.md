## Description:

Calculate and display the number of days remaining until China's National Day on October 1, including today's date and a readable countdown message.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gysmax](https://clawhub.ai/user/gysmax)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer questions about how many days remain until China's National Day or October 1. It is scoped to this holiday and returns a non-negative countdown for the current or next applicable year.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expect broader holiday or date functionality than the skill provides.

Mitigation: Use it only for China's National Day on October 1 and route other holiday or historical-date requests elsewhere.
