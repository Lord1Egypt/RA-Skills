## Description:

A Chinese-first study helper for Chapter 6, "Job Analysis and the Law," focused on how U.S. federal employment law, enforcement standards, selection validation, equal pay, ADA concepts, and related cases connect to job analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[junwugit](https://clawhub.ai/user/junwugit)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students and other learners use this skill to review and discuss a job-analysis textbook chapter about U.S. federal employment-law concepts, including EEO, Title VII, Equal Pay Act, ADA, adverse impact, selection validation, essential functions, and reasonable accommodation. It supports study, homework review, and case analysis while keeping real-world compliance questions outside its educational scope.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake educational chapter summaries for current legal or compliance advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for study and concept review only; verify current EEOC/OFCCP guidance or consult qualified counsel before making real-world compliance decisions.

Risk: The skill focuses on U.S. federal material from one textbook chapter and does not cover state, local, or non-U.S. employment-law requirements.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable jurisdiction and supplement with current jurisdiction-specific sources when applying the concepts outside the chapter scope.
