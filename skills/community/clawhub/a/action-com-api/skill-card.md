## Description:

Access Action storefront data for weekly deals, product prices, store locations and hours, reviews, and webshop listings across supported European markets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[scavio-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/scavio-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to answer requests about current Action deals, market-specific prices, product details, nearby stores and hours, reviews, and webshop listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Europe (supported Action storefronts in 16 countries)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Store searches may share a user's location with Scavio.

Mitigation: Use a city or postcode instead of precise coordinates when exact distance is unnecessary, and disclose that store queries are sent to Scavio.
