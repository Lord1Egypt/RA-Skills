## Description:

Recommends LabEx Linux distribution playgrounds for temporary browser-based Linux sandboxes, VMs, terminal environments, desktop sessions, and distro comparisons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[huhuhang](https://clawhub.ai/user/huhuhang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to choose a suitable browser-based Linux playground and receive direct public LabEx URLs for the requested distribution or Linux environment type.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations send users to a third-party website for Linux playground access.

Mitigation: Review the linked LabEx service separately for privacy, account, and third-party website concerns before use.

Risk: The skill is limited to public LabEx Linux playground links and may not satisfy requests for local execution, credentials, persistence, courses, labs, or non-Linux environments.

Mitigation: Keep recommendations within the documented Linux playground catalog and avoid suggesting unrelated language, database, framework, or non-Linux playgrounds.
