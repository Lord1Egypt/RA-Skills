## Use Case:

Freelancers and proposal writers use this skill to generate, optimize, and analyze project proposals for platforms such as Upwork, Fiverr, and Freelancer. It helps tailor openings, requirements analysis, solution plans, portfolio examples, timelines, pricing, and follow-up content to a project brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide freelance profile details, portfolio links, rates, project descriptions, or client materials to the agent context.

Mitigation: Redact client secrets, private account data, confidential pricing arrangements, and unpublished client materials before using the skill.

Risk: Generated proposals may contain inaccurate, overstated, or unsuitable claims about experience, pricing, timelines, or deliverables.

Mitigation: Review and edit each proposal before sending it, verifying that examples, rates, commitments, and client-facing claims are accurate.
