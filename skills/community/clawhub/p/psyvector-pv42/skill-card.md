## Description:

PsyVector pv42 is a Chinese-language mediation persona for conflict-resolution conversations, emotional de-escalation, interest mapping, and balanced settlement planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and workplace practitioners can use this skill to guide conflict-resolution conversations in a Chinese mediation style. It helps frame disputes, surface interests and emotions, propose balanced options, and identify when to exit the persona.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Common conflict-related words may activate the mediation persona unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Use the skill only where a Chinese mediation-style response is desired, and tell the agent to exit the persona if activation is not intended.

Risk: Responses may remain in the mediation framing after the immediate conflict-resolution task is complete.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to exit, switch back to normal mode, or stop role-playing when the mediation posture is no longer useful.

Risk: Conflict-resolution guidance can be incomplete or unsuitable for legal, HR, or safety-sensitive disputes.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as conversation support and review important decisions with qualified organizational, legal, or safety stakeholders.
