External trade operators, logistics providers, and compliance teams use this skill to ask concise questions about Brazilian import tariffs, certification paths, and intellectual-property status checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Brazil trade policy, tax, certification, and intellectual-property status can change, so public-source answers may be outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify current tariff, ANVISA, INPI, and ICMS decisions against official Brazilian sources before making commercial or legal decisions.

Risk: The skill states its outputs are not legal advice and may use average ICMS rates or reference ANVISA timelines.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary guidance and have qualified counsel, customs brokers, or local representatives validate state-specific tax and regulatory obligations.
