## Description:

Use this skill when a CFP professional, financial advisor, or paraplanner needs a SECURE 2.0-aware draft retirement readiness report for a single client household, including funded ratio, income gap, readiness tier, Roth-conversion flags, and a prioritized action checklist for licensed-advisor review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Financial planning professionals use this skill to collect one household's retirement-planning facts and draft a structured readiness analysis for professional review. It supports advisor preparation for discovery, annual, mid-year, or pre-retirement planning meetings without making investment, tax, or legal recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Retirement-planning intake can include confidential financial details and personal identifiers.

Mitigation: Use anonymized case IDs where possible, avoid SSNs and account numbers, and keep client data confidential.

Risk: A draft readiness report could be mistaken for investment, tax, or legal advice.

Mitigation: Keep the licensed-advisor-review notice on outputs and require advisor, tax professional, or attorney review before a client acts.
