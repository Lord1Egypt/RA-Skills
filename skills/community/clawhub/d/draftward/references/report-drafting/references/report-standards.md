# Report standards catalogue

Use this catalogue to find the authority for a specific report. Confirm the publication status, edition, amendments, effective date and local adoption before using an entry. A publisher's catalogue or abstract can confirm scope and status. Clause-level checks require access to the applicable text under its use terms. If the text is unavailable, identify the exact requirement still to be checked.

## Academic reports and manuscripts

| Report or study | Authority to consult |
| --- | --- |
| Coursework, capstone or student lab report | Start with the course brief, rubric, supplied template and institutional rules on citation, authorship and disclosure. The institution determines which publication conventions apply. |
| Thesis or dissertation | Use the graduate school's submission and repository requirements, including any discipline or degree supplements. [Cornell's thesis and dissertation guidance](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/academic-progress/degrees-fields/research-degree-requirements/thesis-dissertation/writing-your-thesis-dissertation/required-sections-guidelines-and-suggestions/) is one institutional example. Check [ISO 7144](https://www.iso.org/standard/13736.html) when adopted for thesis presentation. |
| Journal or conference manuscript | Start with the target venue's instructions, article type, template and policies for ethics, authorship, conflicts, funding, and data or artefact availability. For medical journal submissions, check the venue's adoption of the [ICMJE Recommendations](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/). |
| Psychology or related disciplines | Select quantitative, qualitative or mixed-methods guidance as appropriate and check which guidance the journal requests. [APA Journal Article Reporting Standards](https://apastyle.apa.org/jars/index) |
| Randomised trial or its protocol | Use CONSORT for the completed trial and SPIRIT for its protocol. Confirm the edition and relevant design extensions. [CONSORT–SPIRIT statements](https://www.consort-spirit.org/published-statements) |
| Observational epidemiology study | Check STROBE and any applicable design or data-source extensions. [STROBE Statement](https://www.strobe-statement.org/) |
| Systematic review, scoping review or review protocol | Check PRISMA for a systematic review and select the applicable extension for a scoping review, protocol or a specialised review type. [PRISMA Statement](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020), [PRISMA extensions](https://www.prisma-statement.org/extensions/) |
| Empirical software engineering study | Select the standard for the study's method and check how the venue uses it. [ACM SIGSOFT Empirical Standards](https://www2.sigsoft.org/EmpiricalStandards/) |
| In vivo animal research report | Check the journal's adoption of ARRIVE and applicable ethics disclosure rules for reporting the existing study. [ARRIVE Guidelines](https://arriveguidelines.org/about) |
| Other health research designs | Search by study type and check the original guideline and any extensions. Candidates include SRQR or COREQ for qualitative studies, CARE for case reports, STARD for diagnostic accuracy, TRIPOD+AI for regression or machine-learning prediction models, SQUIRE for quality improvement and CHEERS for health economic evaluations. [EQUATOR Network reporting guidelines library](https://equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/), [TRIPOD+AI scope](https://www.tripod-statement.org/scope/) |

Study reporting guidelines help readers assess what a study did and found. Confirm whether the venue requires a completed checklist or mandates specific items. Report missing study details as evidence gaps. Check the venue for further instructions on statistical reporting, registration, ethics and reporting partial or exploratory work.

## Technical and engineering reports

| Report or use | Authority to consult |
| --- | --- |
| Scientific or technical report series | Use the sponsor's series and release instructions. For report structure and publication guidance, see [ANSI/NISO Z39.18](https://www.niso.org/publications/z39.18-2005-r2010). For work subject to NASA's scientific and technical information procedures, see [NASA NPR 2200.2E](https://nodis3.gsfc.nasa.gov/displayDir.cfm?Internal_ID=N_PR_2200_002E_&page_name=Preface). |
| Systems or software engineering life-cycle deliverable | Identify the contracted information item and use the applicable technical baseline, acceptance criteria and referenced edition. [ISO/IEC/IEEE 15289](https://www.iso.org/standard/74909.html) |
| Test or calibration report | Check whether the accreditation programme or contract invokes ISO/IEC 17025. A contract can adopt reporting requirements independently of accreditation. Verify the method, reporting requirements and any decision rule before drafting a statement of conformity. Verify accreditation status and scope before stating either. [ISO/IEC 17025](https://www.iso.org/standard/66912.html) |
| Project status or close-out report | Use the sponsor's reporting cycle, baseline and escalation policy. Use [ISO 21502 project management guidance](https://www.iso.org/standard/74947.html) when relevant to the project's governance. |
| Technical quantities and units | Confirm the discipline and contract rules for units and symbols, then use [ISO 80000-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/76921.html) and the [BIPM SI Brochure](https://www.bipm.org/en/publications/si-brochure) as applicable. |

[ISO 5966:1982](https://www.iso.org/standard/12160.html) is withdrawn. If a historical brief names it, confirm why and which requirements govern the current report.

## Professional, public-sector and regulatory reports

| Report or use | Authority to consult |
| --- | --- |
| Internal audit | Use the organisation's audit mandate and applicable professional standards for communicating engagement results. [IIA Global Internal Audit Standards](https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/2024-standards/global-internal-audit-standards/) |
| Management system audit | Use the applicable audit criteria and commissioning rules. ISO 19011 provides auditing guidance. Check the stipulated edition if the brief names an older one. [ISO 19011](https://www.iso.org/standard/19011) |
| Public-sector audit | Check the jurisdiction, engagement type and applicable adoption. Start with [INTOSAI ISSAI 100](https://www.intosai.org/fileadmin/downloads/documents/open_access/ISSAI_100_to_400/issai_100/ISSAI_100_EN.pdf) for foundational public-sector audit principles or the [GAO Yellow Book](https://www.gao.gov/yellowbook) for U.S. engagements subject to GAGAS. |
| Government policy appraisal, evaluation or business case | Get the commissioning agency's template and decision criteria. Use the UK Treasury's [Green Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book--2) for appraisal and [Magenta Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html) for evaluation when applicable. For development evaluation, check the [OECD evaluation criteria](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2019/12/better-criteria-for-better-evaluation_f7a307eb.html). |
| Government publication or annual report | Verify the department's publication guide, statutory content and tabling requirements. The [Australian Government Style Manual's report guidance](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/content-types/reports) is a drafting reference within its jurisdiction. |
| Financial statement presentation or regulated annual filing | Identify the applicable accounting framework, reporting period, filing regulator and form. Check transition rules where presentation standards change. Examples include [IFRS 18](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/) and the [U.S. SEC Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/submit-filings/forms-index/aboutformsform10-k). |
| Financial statement audit report | Verify the jurisdiction's adoption, engagement requirements and applicable reporting standards with the responsible auditor. [IAASB ISA 700 (Revised)](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/international-standard-auditing-isa-700-revised-forming-opinion-and-reporting-financial-statements) addresses the auditor's opinion and report. |
| Sustainability or climate disclosure | Verify the entity's reporting basis, jurisdiction, period and required scope. The frameworks address different audiences and disclosure obligations. Consult [GRI Standards](https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/), [ISSB Standards](https://www.ifrs.org/sustainability/knowledge-hub/introduction-to-issb-and-ifrs-sustainability-disclosure-standards/), [EU ESRS](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2023/2772/oj) or [Australian Sustainability Reporting Standards](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/sustainability-reporting-standards) as applicable. |

For other specialist reports, including incident investigations, environmental submissions and regulated assurance work, search the applicable regulator, professional body or commissioning agency by jurisdiction and report type. Use its current form, prescribed edition, amendments and reporting dates. Assign sign-off to the person or authority named in the brief or governing rules.

## Citation, language and accessibility

Apply these layers after identifying the requirements for the report itself.

| Need | Authority to consult |
| --- | --- |
| Citation style | Use the exact style and edition named by the course, journal or client. Match in-text citations and references, including dataset, software and standards entries. [APA](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines), [IEEE](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/create-the-text-of-your-article/ieee-editorial-style-manual/), [Chicago](https://www.chicagomanualofstyle.org/tools_citationguide.html), [MLA](https://style.mla.org/works-cited/works-cited-a-quick-guide/), [NLM](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK7256/), [ISO 690](https://www.iso.org/standard/72642.html) |
| Plain language | Use the house style and the audience's language needs. Consult [ISO 24495-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/78907.html) for general plain-language guidance when relevant. |
| Accessible report | Identify the required format, jurisdiction and accessibility target. Check the specified edition and level for [WCAG](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), the required profile for [PDF/UA-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/64599.html) or [PDF/UA-2](https://www.iso.org/standard/82278.html), and [Section 508 document guidance](https://www.section508.gov/create/documents/) when U.S. federal accessibility requirements apply. Verify the actual delivered file. |

### Add a missing authority

Record the report type, issuing body, exact standard or guidance identifier, edition and amendments, applicability trigger, and official source. Include the specific part that affects the report and the author or reviewer action it requires. Link to the publisher's version and describe applicability in original words.
