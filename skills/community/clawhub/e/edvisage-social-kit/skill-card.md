## Description:

Multi-platform social presence management for AI agents, including posting strategy, platform tone adaptation, community engagement guidance, content rotation, and basic reputation awareness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[edvisage](https://clawhub.ai/user/edvisage)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent developers use this skill to guide AI agents in drafting platform-appropriate social posts, engaging with communities, and tracking basic reputation signals. Users should review and approve posts before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encourages social presence guidance that can make AI-run accounts appear non-automated.

Mitigation: Require explicit user approval for posts, disclose automation where required, and follow each platform's spam, bot, and account rules.

Risk: The Pro-version description references anti-detection patterns and automated posting features.

Mitigation: Do not use this skill to evade platform detection or moderation systems; keep usage aligned with platform terms and applicable disclosure requirements.
