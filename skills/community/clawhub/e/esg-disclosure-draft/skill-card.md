## Description:

Drafts audit-ready ESG disclosure sections in a CSRD/ESRS-flavored structure with double-materiality framing, metric-methodology-limitation triplets, grounded forward-looking statements, and explicit data-gap handling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external reporting teams, and advisors use this skill to draft ESG disclosure sections for sustainability reports, ESRS or CSRD disclosures, annual reports, and regulator-facing drafts. It helps structure claims around materiality, metrics, limitations, forward-looking bases, data gaps, and pre-publication review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Disclosure draft may be mistaken for legal, regulatory, or assurance approval.

Mitigation: Have compliance and legal reviewers verify standards, data, phase-in reliefs, and final wording before publication.

Risk: Incomplete or unverified ESG data can produce unsupported or misleading disclosure claims.

Mitigation: Keep placeholder values marked [to confirm], pair metrics with methodology and limitations, and verify all datapoints before publication.
