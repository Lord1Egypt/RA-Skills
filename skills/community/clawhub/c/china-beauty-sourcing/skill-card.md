## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Industry, regulatory, supplier, and certification details may be stale or incomplete because the skill uses bundled reference data rather than live verification.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary sourcing guidance and independently verify suppliers, regulations, certifications, and market claims before business decisions.

Risk: The skill provides cluster-level sourcing intelligence rather than vetted individual factory contacts.

Mitigation: Use it to shortlist regions and subsectors, then apply normal supplier due diligence before engagement.
