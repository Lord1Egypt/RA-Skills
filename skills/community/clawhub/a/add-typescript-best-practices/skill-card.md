## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may add or update project-level CLAUDE.md instructions that affect future agent behavior in the repository.

Mitigation: Review the proposed CLAUDE.md content before installation and keep project-specific instructions that should take precedence.

Risk: The TypeScript preferences may conflict with existing team conventions for enums, interfaces, exported types, libraries, or type assertions.

Mitigation: Compare the guidance against local engineering standards and adjust or reject conflicting rules before use.
