## Description:

Compresses responses into Classical Chinese to save tokens, with Extreme (/e), Medium (/m), and Light (/l) modes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[u5bf](https://clawhub.ai/user/u5bf)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill when they want shorter Classical Chinese replies that preserve technical literals such as code, paths, URLs, error messages, numbers, and markup.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Classical Chinese compression can reduce readability or omit nuance in detail-sensitive tasks.

Mitigation: Disable the skill or request normal-language clarity for debugging, legal, medical, safety, or other detail-sensitive work.

Risk: Technical content can become incorrect if literals are translated or compressed.

Mitigation: Keep code, variable names, paths, URLs, ports, error messages, formulas, numbers, versions, and markup unchanged.
