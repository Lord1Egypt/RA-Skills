## Description:

Speak through the free Oscova Avatar Engine, a realistic 3D avatar on your own computer.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users can have an AI agent speak brief replies through their Oscova avatar and adjust its expression or gesture when appropriate. If the avatar is disconnected, the skill provides setup guidance.
