## Use Case:

External creators, filmmakers, and production teams use this skill to plan AI-assisted films, generate shot prompts, route work across video tools, assemble edits, and prepare commercial or experimental deliverables.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-generated likenesses, face swaps, voices, music, brand assets, or platform disclosures may raise rights or consent issues.

Mitigation: Use the workflow only with fictional characters, owned assets, or clearly consenting people, and verify rights and disclosure requirements before publishing.

Risk: Generated film guidance can produce continuity, factual, legal, or brand-compliance mistakes if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Use the skill's shot logs, approval gates, continuity checks, and rights review steps before release.
