## Description:

Matches a user's current mood, available time, platform habits, and energy level to a fitting game experience, then suggests a low-friction way to start.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to reduce game-choice paralysis by turning mood, time, budget, platform, and recent play patterns into practical play-now, weekend, and avoid-for-now guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated recommendations may be mistaken for live purchasing, pricing, review, or release guidance.

Mitigation: Present the output as fit-based leisure advice and verify current prices, releases, and reviews before making purchase decisions.

Risk: Generated Markdown based on untrusted user input could contain misleading advice.

Mitigation: Treat generated Markdown as advice rather than trusted platform instructions, especially when the input came from another person or an untrusted source.
