## Description:

Assists agents with identifying permit requirements, preparing application checklists, navigating plan review, sequencing inspections, and estimating permit fees for construction projects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Contractors, developers, architects, real estate investors, homeowners, and their agents use this skill to reason through construction permit requirements, submittal checklists, plan review issues, inspection sequencing, and general permit fee estimates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: General permitting guidance may not match the exact jurisdiction, project type, or current local code requirements.

Mitigation: Provide the exact jurisdiction and project type, then confirm requirements with the local building department or a licensed professional before making real permitting decisions.

Risk: Permit fee estimates and compliance guidance may be incomplete for a specific project.

Mitigation: Treat estimates as reference material and verify fees, approvals, inspections, and compliance obligations with the authority having jurisdiction.
