## Description:

Provides a Michael Burry-inspired contrarian value investing lens for identifying bubbles, applying margin of safety, and reasoning about concentrated bets against consensus.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[talentedleo](https://clawhub.ai/user/talentedleo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to ask an agent for investing-oriented analysis in a Burry-style contrarian value framework. It helps frame questions around margin of safety, supply-side bubble signals, historical parallels, and consensus risk, but should not be used as personalized financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake Burry-style market commentary or stock examples for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Present outputs as informational analysis only and require users to verify current facts independently before making trading or portfolio decisions.

Risk: Historical or contextual market examples may become stale or be incomplete for current market conditions.

Mitigation: Check current filings, prices, market data, and primary sources before relying on any generated investment framing.
