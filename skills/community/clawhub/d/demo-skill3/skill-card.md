## Description:

Reviews a short product idea and returns structured feedback for a lightweight product idea critique.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jshiu0915](https://clawhub.ai/user/jshiu0915)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and product teams use this skill to get a concise critique of a product idea, including the target user, core value, practical suggestions, and a validation risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used with sensitive or private product ideas.

Mitigation: Use it only with ideas the user intentionally provides, and do not grant access to unrelated local files.

**Output Type(s):** [text, markdown, guidance]
