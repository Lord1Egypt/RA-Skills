## Description:

Assesses architecture designs across performance, availability, scalability, and security risks, then produces a quantified risk matrix with mitigations and residual risks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and architects use this skill to review proposed system architectures, identify high-priority risks, and produce an actionable assessment report before implementation or release.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Assessment quality depends on the completeness and sensitivity of the architecture details supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Provide only architecture information intended for analysis and review the resulting risks and mitigations before using them for design decisions.
