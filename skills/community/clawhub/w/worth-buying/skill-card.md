## Description:

Shopping decision skill for finding the lowest real price, the best value option, or the strongest discount across products and categories.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to compare products or categories across shopping channels and receive concise Chinese-language guidance on the lowest real price, best value option, strongest real discount, and whether to buy, switch, or wait.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shopping recommendations may be mistaken for proof of exact live cart prices.

Mitigation: Verify final cart price, coupons, shipping fees, seller identity, return terms, and after-sales coverage before purchase.

Risk: The skill can advise on public shopping information but has no authority to log in, claim coupons, check out, or pay.

Mitigation: Keep account access, coupon actions, checkout, and payment under direct user control.
