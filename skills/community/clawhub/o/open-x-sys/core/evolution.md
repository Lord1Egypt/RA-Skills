# Feedback and Evolution

A system evolves when its future effects change. Evolution may begin with new evidence, an actual result, a changed constraint, a person's correction, or a newly discovered connection. First locate the scale at which change occurred, then decide which part it should change.

## Move upward to the structure behind repeated problems

The first instance of a problem may be handled at the level of state or action. When the same kind returns, move upward through the levels below, find the structure that keeps generating lower-level events, and let the higher-level change re-enter real action:

```text
chance event or current state
→ current way of acting
→ missing, obsolete, or inapplicable capability
→ flawed relation, feedback, interface, or allocation of authority
→ higher-level resource structure, boundary, or goal that keeps generating the problem
```

“Higher level” means a relation that changes more slowly, remains more stable, and continues to generate lower-level events. Confirm repetition across times, instances, or counterfactuals. Update the system at the highest level the evidence supports.

## How a higher-level change acts downward

A new goal, boundary, or structure becomes evolution when it changes real capability and action:

```text
new purpose, model, or structure
→ changed capability requirements
→ changed components, relations, and authority
→ changed real action
→ observation of new state and effects
```

A review enters the system when its conclusion changes what will be observed next, how work is divided, which capability is used, or when action occurs.

## Dependency propagation: change what is truly affected

Important understanding, capabilities, and relations can state what they depend on. When new evidence arrives, find direct dependencies, then the works, decisions, tools, and downstream systems that actually use them. Mark what needs review, what remains valid, and what is currently unknown. Preserve user edits and unaffected work.

When the goal changes, recalculate success conditions and capability requirements. When a capability fails, update the effects that depend on it. When a relation is withdrawn, stop later calls and state who is responsible for copies already sent, actions already taken, and results still in flight.

| What arrived | What it can change | How the next run shows the change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A new observation or counterexample | Current judgment, the applicable range of a model, the observation still needed | The next explanation uses the new evidence or preserves the necessary uncertainty |
| A successful action | A reusable tool, its use, and the way its result is confirmed | Similar work can invoke it again and inspect the effect |
| A deviation sustained over time | Observation point, coordination timing, responsibility, or work structure | A class of signal is missed less often, or feedback returns sooner |
| A changed goal, right, or environment | System boundary, connection conditions, and decision owner | The system can adopt the new arrangement and knows where the old capability ceases to apply |
| The same relation found across systems | A reusable way to observe, practice, or connect | Other maintainers produce real results under their own conditions and can improve it |

## Return feedback to the place that can change

First distinguish an execution receipt, real state, effect in use, and a person's understanding. They may have different receivers. A work that helps someone understand a mechanism changes understanding and later choices. A tool becomes a continuing practice when it runs and its result is used. When an error comes from higher-level authority or input design, let that level change rather than assigning every repair to the immediate task.

Leave what real decisions need: the basis of a conclusion, its object, conditions of use, existing version, and responsible owner. Natural language connects it to parts that can actually be used. After work repeats, the system can say whether the practice still has value and where AI or another tool can keep improving it. When feedback conflicts, check sources, scales, delays, and interests; multiple explanations may remain alive.

## Exit and recovery are system capabilities

When observations age, authority is withdrawn, analysis proves wrong, or a tool fails, identify the downstream understanding and capabilities affected. Keep state unknown until reality is confirmed. Recover to still-reliable understanding, material, or tool versions. People with the relevant rights decide long-term changes and external commitments. Recurring runs have a real trigger, a clear stop path, and appropriate authority.
