## Description:

将学术文献文本按常见论文结构拆分，标注各板块的核心用途、作者意图和简要内容。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[11xinshou](https://clawhub.ai/user/11xinshou)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and knowledge workers use this skill to turn pasted abstracts or full academic paper text into a structured section-by-section outline for review and long-term study.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Academic text submitted to the active agent may include confidential or unpublished material.

Mitigation: Only provide material the user is comfortable having the active agent process; remove sensitive or unpublished details before analysis.

Risk: The structural breakdown may be incomplete or less precise for non-standard papers, book chapters, or partial source text.

Mitigation: Review the output against the original paper and keep missing sections marked as absent rather than forcing a standard structure.
