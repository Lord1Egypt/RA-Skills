## Description:

Analyzes equipment operating data to calculate OEE metrics, diagnose the six major loss categories, check data credibility, and produce prioritized improvement and ROI guidance with assumptions marked.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Manufacturing operations teams and engineers use this skill to turn equipment runtime, throughput, quality, and loss data into OEE diagnostics, improvement priorities, and decision-support reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Manufacturing performance data and ROI estimates can be misleading when company-specific parameters are missing and defaults are used.

Mitigation: Review and replace default assumptions such as standard cycle time, hourly value, annual operating hours, and target OEE before using the output for business decisions.

Risk: Improvement priorities and ROI values are decision-support estimates, not commitments of operational or financial results.

Mitigation: Require business owner review before using recommendations to approve process changes, maintenance plans, or investment decisions.
