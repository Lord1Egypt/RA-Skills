## Description:

Guides SQE and procurement quality engineers through two supplier assessment modes: building a structured assessment plan or organizing an existing plan and supplier data into reference-only scoring outputs for human confirmation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees in supplier quality and procurement quality roles use this skill to standardize supplier assessment workflows, create assessment plans, and prepare reference-only supplier scoring dashboards from user-provided criteria and data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference scores and supplier evaluations could be mistaken for final approval decisions.

Mitigation: Keep the required reference-only banner in scoring dashboards and require review by the assessor or quality manager before adoption.

Risk: The inspected package references a report-building script that is not included in the artifact.

Mitigation: Verify the supporting script or use the skill's structured guidance manually before relying on generated file outputs.

Risk: The workflow is primarily written for Chinese-language supplier quality and procurement contexts.

Mitigation: Review terminology, assessment thresholds, and local compliance expectations before using it in other languages or regions.
