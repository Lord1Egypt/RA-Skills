## Description:

Creates structured guidance for competitor comparison and alternative pages that support SEO, evaluation, and sales positioning across singular alternative, plural alternatives, you-vs-competitor, and competitor-vs-competitor formats.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, growth, and content teams use this skill to plan and draft competitor comparison, alternatives, and vs pages that support search visibility, buyer evaluation, and sales positioning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitor claims, pricing, and feature comparisons can become outdated or misleading.

Mitigation: Verify pricing, features, and competitor positioning against current sources before publication and schedule periodic refreshes.

Risk: Generated pages may use internal product-marketing context that is not approved for public release.

Mitigation: Review drafts for confidential positioning, customer details, and unsupported claims before publishing or sharing externally.
