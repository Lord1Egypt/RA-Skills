## Description:

Reviews contract clauses from a specified party’s perspective and produces a risk-graded checklist with suggested revisions and cited legal authorities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[panting09266-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/panting09266-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Legal teams, contract managers, and business users can review a contract from their side’s perspective, identify clause and transaction risks, and prepare suggested edits for lawyer review before signing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:
