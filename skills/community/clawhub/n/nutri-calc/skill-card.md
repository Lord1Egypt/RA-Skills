## Description:

Precise nutrition calculator - Chinese food database, goal-oriented macro tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to estimate nutrition for Chinese-language meal descriptions, calculate daily energy and macro targets from body-profile inputs, and compare intake against goal-oriented targets. It is useful for meal tracking, macro gap analysis, and practical Chinese-diet food suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses diet and body-profile details such as weight, height, age, gender, activity level, goals, and meal history.

Mitigation: Use only when sharing those details with the agent is acceptable; avoid adding unnecessary sensitive health context.

Risk: Nutrition values and portion estimates may be inaccurate, especially when exact foods are missing and similar dishes are used as fallback estimates.

Mitigation: Verify nutrition estimates before using them for medical decisions, strict dietary plans, or other high-stakes health needs.

Risk: The skill is optimized for Chinese foods and Chinese-language meal descriptions, so results may be weaker outside that food context.

Mitigation: Prefer Chinese food names, common Chinese serving units, and clear portion descriptions when using the skill.
