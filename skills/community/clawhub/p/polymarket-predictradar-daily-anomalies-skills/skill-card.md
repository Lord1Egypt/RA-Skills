## Description:

Generates daily Polymarket anomaly reports covering rapid probability shifts, opposing large bets, and suspicious new-wallet activity, with market links and real news context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cnica](https://clawhub.ai/user/cnica)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to scan Polymarket activity for daily anomaly leads across Black Swan, Whale Wars, and Insider Watch categories. The output is intended for review and market investigation, not as proof of wrongdoing or financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reports can expose full wallet-level Polymarket activity.

Mitigation: Use only where wallet-level reporting is acceptable, and avoid broad redistribution of full-address reports unless it is necessary for verification.

Risk: Insider Watch labels may be mistaken for evidence of misconduct.

Mitigation: Present Insider Watch results as investigation leads and require human review before making claims about intent or wrongdoing.

Risk: Dynamic SQL list construction may need hardening for production or adversarial data environments.

Mitigation: Harden query construction and validate inputs before relying on the workflow in production.
