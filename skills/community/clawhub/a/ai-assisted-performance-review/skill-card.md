## Description:

Evaluates AI-assisted work fairly by separating human contribution from tool output and producing review guidance, revised criteria, calibration rules, and conversation scripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Managers, HR partners, and review committees use this skill to evaluate performance when employee output is heavily AI-assisted. It helps audit criteria, identify evidence of human judgment and verification, calibrate mixed-adoption teams, and prepare difficult review conversations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Performance reviews can involve sensitive employee or organizational information.

Mitigation: Share only the personal or confidential details needed for the review task, and follow the organization's privacy and HR policies.

Risk: AI-assisted review guidance may over-credit tool output or under-value human verification if the inputs are incomplete.

Mitigation: Ground the review in role criteria, shipped-work evidence, error and rework history, and the organization's stated AI policy.
