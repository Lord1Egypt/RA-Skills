## Description:

Help patients find suitable doctors based on symptoms, specialty, location, and doctor ratings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to identify suitable medical specialties and compare doctor options based on symptoms, location, hospital preferences, experience, ratings, and availability. It supports provider search and triage-style routing, not diagnosis or treatment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical routing and emergency-number guidance may be inaccurate outside the intended locality or become outdated.

Mitigation: Ask users to verify specialty routing, doctor availability, ratings, booking details, and emergency instructions with local providers or emergency services.

Risk: Users may mistake provider-search guidance for diagnosis, treatment, or medication advice.

Mitigation: State that the skill helps find doctors only and direct users to licensed clinicians for diagnosis and treatment.
