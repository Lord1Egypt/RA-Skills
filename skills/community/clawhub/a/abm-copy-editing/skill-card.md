## Description:

Helps agents review and improve existing marketing copy through focused editing passes while preserving the original message and voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mariokarras](https://clawhub.ai/user/mariokarras)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, marketers, and copywriters use this skill to review existing marketing or conversion copy, identify clarity, tone, proof, specificity, emotional impact, and CTA-risk issues, and produce focused edits or tighter rewrites.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask an agent to read local brand or product-marketing context files when they exist.

Mitigation: Use it only in workspaces where those files are appropriate context for the requested copy edit.

Risk: Suggested edits to marketing copy could accidentally strengthen claims beyond the evidence provided.

Mitigation: Review rewritten claims, proof points, numbers, guarantees, and CTAs before publishing.
