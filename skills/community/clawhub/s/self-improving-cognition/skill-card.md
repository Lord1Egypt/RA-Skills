## Description:

Systematic improvement of thinking patterns, decision quality, problem-solving abilities, and mental performance. Use when noticing cognitive biases, making important decisions, solving complex problems, or wanting to enhance mental clarity and effectiveness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to help an agent structure cognitive self-assessments, decision analysis, problem-solving logs, bias corrections, and ongoing cognition improvement records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encourages persistent plaintext logging of sensitive personal thinking, lifestyle, and decision data.

Mitigation: Keep generated cognition files out of shared repositories, synced folders, and public workspaces; periodically delete records that are no longer needed.

Risk: Local exercise records may disclose personal cognitive performance or reflection details if committed or shared.

Mitigation: Review generated markdown files before sharing any workspace and add local cognition record paths to ignore rules where appropriate.
