## Description:

Chinese-language creator coaching skill that helps social media creators diagnose their stage, choose positioning, plan content production, improve hooks and retention, and design monetization paths.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yushuo-aicoder](https://clawhub.ai/user/yushuo-aicoder)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creator-focused agents use this skill to coach social media creators through account stage diagnosis, niche positioning, script and hook development, publishing cadence, energy management, and monetization planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is optimized for Chinese-language creator coaching, so non-Chinese users may receive less useful routing or responses.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for the preferred language and target platform before applying the templates.

Risk: Creator coaching and monetization templates may be too generic if applied without account-specific context.

Mitigation: Have the agent request the creator's current stage, platform, audience, recent content performance, and business goals before giving a plan.
