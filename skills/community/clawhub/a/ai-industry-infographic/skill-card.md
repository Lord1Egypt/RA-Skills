## Description:

Generates verified, ChatGPT-ready prompt packages for AI industry infographics, including topic design, data research, image prompts, and social media copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content creators, content teams, and industry analysts use this skill to create data-rich AI industry infographic material packs with source-checked facts, structured image prompts, and social post copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Published infographics can mislead readers if facts, metrics, or superlative claims are not verified before use.

Mitigation: Apply the skill's source grading, red-flag screening, and multi-round verification steps before publishing or selling the generated material.

Risk: The workflow performs many web searches and may create or update local project files during package generation and corrections.

Mitigation: Review tool permissions and output paths before running the workflow, and inspect generated or modified files before sharing them.

Risk: Default outputs are tailored toward Chinese AI-industry and Xiaohongshu-style content.

Mitigation: Adapt language, channel conventions, sourcing expectations, and copy tone for the target audience and publication venue.
