## Description:

为GESP等级考试生成作业卷子，包含选择/判断/编程题。知识点定制，答案随机分布防猜测。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Educators and training staff use this skill to generate GESP practice homework from specified class, date, and knowledge-point inputs, including multiple-choice, true/false, programming questions, and an answer file.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic homework or practice-paper requests may activate the skill with ambiguous scope.

Mitigation: Confirm the class, date, knowledge points, and output path before creating files.

Risk: Generated assignments and answers may not match the intended knowledge points or may contain incorrect educational content.

Mitigation: Review the generated questions, programming tasks, and answer file before assigning them to students.
