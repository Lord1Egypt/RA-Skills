## Description:

Humanize English ecommerce copy to remove obvious AI tone while preserving selling intent, channel fit, and safer claim boundaries for product pages, ads, captions, and customer-facing copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, ecommerce operators, and copywriters use this skill to make English product pages, ads, captions, and customer-facing selling copy sound more natural while preserving the offer, proof points, CTA intent, and channel fit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat rewritten ecommerce copy as regulated legal, medical, or compliance-approved language.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for copy humanization and have qualified reviewers approve regulated claims before publication.

Risk: The rewrite may make unsupported product claims sound more polished or persuasive.

Mitigation: Preserve only supplied proof points and replace risky wording with safer alternatives when evidence is missing.

Risk: Users may expect translation or bilingual localization even though the skill is English-only.

Mitigation: Provide primarily English input and use a dedicated localization workflow for translation.
