## Description:

The YouTube publish-metadata skill writes titles, descriptions with chapters, tags, category, privacy, and madeForKids settings for videos published via WoopSocial.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and publishing teams use this skill to prepare YouTube upload metadata and publish settings from a finished script or video. It helps produce search-oriented metadata while keeping legal and platform-sensitive settings such as madeForKids truthful.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated privacy, category, and madeForKids settings can affect public posting behavior and legal compliance.

Mitigation: Review these settings before publishing, especially child-directed content and any disclosure requirements.
