## Description:

Find and prioritize keywords to target: discover ideas from seed topics or Search Console, check search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, intent and live SERPs, and save the best terms to the HarborRank project.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[winsley-jeune](https://clawhub.ai/user/winsley-jeune)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO practitioners and website owners use this skill to find and prioritize keyword opportunities for content and local search. It produces a shortlist from HarborRank metrics when connected, or from available web evidence and Search Console exports when those data are available.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search Console queries may be sent to HarborRank for keyword-metric processing.

Mitigation: Before connecting Search Console, confirm that sharing its queries with HarborRank is acceptable; avoid sending query data that should remain private.
