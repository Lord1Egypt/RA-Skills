## Use Case:

External students and academic writers use this skill to plan paper structure, choose citation formats, and improve academic writing from proposal through final draft. It is intended as writing guidance, not thesis or paper ghostwriting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat writing or citation guidance as a replacement for school rules, advisor feedback, or original citation manuals.

Mitigation: Verify final structure, citation format, and academic requirements against institutional guidance, advisor feedback, and original style manuals.

Risk: The skill could be misused to outsource academic work instead of supporting original writing.

Mitigation: Use it for templates, planning, and revision guidance while preserving the user's original research, analysis, and writing responsibility.
