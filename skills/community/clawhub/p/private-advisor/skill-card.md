## Description:

用人类经典哲学思想解答任何现实困惑。不仅限于个人问题，还能分析国际事件、社会现象、科技伦理、历史争议等任何需要深度思考的话题。调取哲学、文学、政治学、经济学经典智慧进行多元解读。

## Use Case:

External users use this Chinese-language advisor to frame personal, social, ethical, historical, political, and economic questions through classic philosophy, literature, psychology, and social-science perspectives. It is intended for reflective guidance, not therapy, crisis support, legal advice, financial advice, or current geopolitical guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill covers depression and crisis-like distress without clear clinical safety boundaries.

Mitigation: Add explicit crisis escalation language, professional-care boundaries, and a statement that the skill is not therapy or crisis support.

Risk: The skill covers legal, financial, and geopolitical topics that can require current facts or professional judgment.

Mitigation: Use it only for reflective framing, verify current facts from reliable sources, and consult qualified professionals for high-stakes decisions.

Risk: Classic quotations and interpretations may be incomplete, disputed, or presented without enough source context.

Mitigation: Review cited passages against authoritative editions and present interpretations as perspectives rather than definitive answers.
