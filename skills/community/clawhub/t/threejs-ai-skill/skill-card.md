## Description:

Helps agents produce coherent Three.js, WebGL, and react-three-fiber scenes by applying guidance for model normalization, grounded placement, camera framing, lighting, controls, collisions, and animation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and engineers use this skill when creating, reviewing, or refactoring Three.js, WebGL, or react-three-fiber scenes and games. It guides agents toward visually coherent, playable 3D output with correctly scaled models, framed cameras, grounded layouts, responsive controls, and animation checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked broadly for 3D web work and can be slightly over-inclusive.

Mitigation: Confirm the task involves Three.js, WebGL, react-three-fiber, or related 3D scene work before relying on the detailed guidance.

Risk: Generated 3D scenes can still have visual or interaction issues even when the code compiles.

Mitigation: Use the skill's pre-flight checklist and visually verify scale, grounding, camera framing, collisions, controls, and character movement before release.
