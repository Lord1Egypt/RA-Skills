## Description:

Codified expertise for managing carrier portfolios, negotiating freight rates, tracking carrier performance, allocating freight, and maintaining strategic carrier relationships.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nocodemf](https://clawhub.ai/user/nocodemf)

### License/Terms of Use:

Apache-2.0

## Use Case:

Transportation, procurement, logistics, and supply chain teams use this skill to draft and review carrier scorecards, rate negotiation strategies, RFP evaluations, routing guide decisions, compliance checks, and carrier relationship communications.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Carrier awards, suspensions, exits, rate commitments, and external messages can affect freight cost, service continuity, compliance exposure, and carrier relationships.

Mitigation: Require approval from a transportation or procurement owner before acting on recommendations or sending generated communications.

Risk: Running evaluations with command-line API key arguments can expose secrets through shell history or process listings.

Mitigation: Provide API credentials through environment-based secret handling when running the eval script.
