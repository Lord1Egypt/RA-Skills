## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crypto/Web3 queries, keywords, and requested article IDs are sent to MarsBit's hosted MCP endpoint.

Mitigation: Do not include wallet seed phrases, private keys, credentials, proprietary trading plans, or other sensitive material in prompts that may become search parameters.
