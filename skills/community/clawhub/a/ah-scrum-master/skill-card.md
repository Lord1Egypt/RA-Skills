## Use Case:

Employees, external delivery teams, and agile practitioners use this skill to assess team maturity, facilitate Scrum ceremonies, remove impediments, track delivery metrics, and guide continuous improvement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Proposed process changes, reports, or metrics may be incorrect, misleading, or unsuitable for a specific team context.

Mitigation: Review recommendations with the team and relevant stakeholders before adopting changes or sharing reports.

Risk: Example delivery numbers and agile metrics could be mistaken for validated team performance data.

Mitigation: Treat generated metrics as examples unless they are explicitly grounded in the team's verified project data.
