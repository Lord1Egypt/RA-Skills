External users, SEO practitioners, marketers, and founders use this skill to plan backlink acquisition, outreach, linkable assets, and off-page SEO metrics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local project-context files may contain secrets or unrelated private information that could be used while tailoring SEO advice.

Mitigation: Keep `.claude/project-context.md` and `.cursor/project-context.md` limited to non-sensitive product, audience, and proof-point information before invoking the skill.

Risk: SEO outreach guidance can encourage low-quality or spammy link acquisition if applied without review.

Mitigation: Review outreach plans for relevance, authority, natural anchor text, and contextual placement before acting on them.
