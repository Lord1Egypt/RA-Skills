## Use Case:

Developers, prompt authors, and AI teams use this skill as a practical reference for designing prompts, building reusable prompt libraries, testing outputs, and adapting prompts across model families.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompt templates may be copied into production without adapting safety boundaries, domain constraints, or escalation rules.

Mitigation: Review and tailor prompts before production use, especially prompts involving tool use, customer actions, or system-level instructions.

Risk: Prompt examples can produce inaccurate or unsuitable outputs if used without evaluation.

Mitigation: Use the included CRAFT checks, test cases, scoring rubrics, and human review before deployment.
