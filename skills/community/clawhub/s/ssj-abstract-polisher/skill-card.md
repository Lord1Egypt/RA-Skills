## Description:

Refines social science academic abstracts to align with peer-reviewed journal expectations, including APA 7th edition style, clear structure, formal tone, and concision.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External academic authors, editors, and researchers use this skill to polish one English-language social science manuscript abstract while preserving the core research content. It helps revise structure, tone, APA-style conventions, and concision before journal submission.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill rewrites abstract text and could unintentionally alter the author's intended meaning or overstate findings.

Mitigation: Review the polished abstract against the source study before submission, especially claims about methods, findings, and implications.

Risk: The skill is scoped to English social science abstracts and is not designed for full manuscript editing or non-English abstracts unless modified.

Mitigation: Use it only for the stated abstract-polishing workflow, or adapt and re-review the skill before applying it to other manuscript sections or languages.
