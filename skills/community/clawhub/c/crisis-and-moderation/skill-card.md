## Description:

Crisis & Moderation helps agents triage social media crises, draft human-approved responses, advise fair moderation, and pause or reschedule queued posts when appropriate.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social media teams and brand operators use this skill when a post, claim, deepfake, pile-on, or moderation issue needs crisis triage, response drafting, escalation guidance, and human-approved next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crisis guidance or drafted public wording could be incorrect, premature, or beyond approved language.

Mitigation: Confirm facts first, keep outputs advisory, and require human approval with legal, leadership, or PR review for high-severity situations.

Risk: Moderation recommendations could be mistaken for direct platform moderation capability.

Mitigation: State that WoopSocial has no comment, inbox, moderation, listening, or analytics surface; humans perform moderation in the native platform tools.

Risk: Pausing, deleting, or rescheduling queued posts can change the publisher's planned social calendar.

Mitigation: Review confirmations carefully and have a human decide which pending posts to hold, delete, reschedule, or resume.
