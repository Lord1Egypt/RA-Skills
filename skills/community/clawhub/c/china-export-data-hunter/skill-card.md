## Description:

Proactively hunt and discover China's export trade data to identify competitors, track market movements, and uncover new business opportunities.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External market researchers, competitive intelligence professionals, sales strategists, business development teams, and traders use this skill to plan analysis of China's export activity, competitor movement, market entry signals, pricing trends, and port-level trade patterns.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automation against trade-data sources may exceed published rate limits, subscription terms, or applicable rules for scraping and competitive research.

Mitigation: Confirm that China-focused export intelligence is the intended use case, respect source rate limits and subscription terms, and add delays or caching for repeated data collection.

Risk: Trade-data interpretation can produce misleading business conclusions when HS code revisions, re-exports, trade modes, or seasonal effects are ignored.

Mitigation: Validate HS codes and source assumptions before acting on findings, and review output with domain expertise before using it for commercial decisions.
