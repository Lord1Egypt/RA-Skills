## Description:

Turns a topic, product, or audience note into a shootable short-video script and storyboard, with optional narration, music, and a finished vertical clip.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and small businesses use this skill to turn a topic, product, or audience note into a short-video brief, script, and storyboard. They can also use it to produce narration, music, and a finished vertical clip.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
