## Description:

Evaluates and ranks regional industry competitiveness using seven dimensions: scale strength, innovation capability, enterprise competitiveness, resource aggregation, capital attraction, ecosystem maturity, and development potential.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Industry strategy teams, regional economic researchers, investment promotion teams, and policy analysts use this skill to benchmark a target industry's competitiveness across Chinese regions, identify leading and lagging areas, and develop improvement or招商 strategies.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public industry and financing data may be incomplete, stale, or inconsistent across regions, which can affect rankings and scores.

Mitigation: Require citations for source data, disclose data gaps, and verify key figures before using the report for business or policy decisions.

Risk: Competitiveness rankings may influence investment promotion, benchmarking, or policy choices beyond the reliability of available public evidence.

Mitigation: Treat the report as decision support, review scoring assumptions with domain experts, and compare conclusions against authoritative government, company, and market sources.
