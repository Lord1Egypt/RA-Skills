## Description:

Grant Writer helps an agent draft tailored grant proposals and funding applications for government, foundation, and corporate funding programs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and organizations use this skill to turn project, budget, timeline, and funder details into structured grant application drafts, including narratives, objectives, budget justification, evaluation plans, sustainability plans, and support-letter templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Grant drafting may involve confidential business, financial, or partner details.

Mitigation: Use only information the user is comfortable having the agent process, redact sensitive details when possible, and review drafts before sharing them externally.
