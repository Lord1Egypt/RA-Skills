## Description:

Turns POC run evidence into an adoption plan, value measurement, and next-investment recommendation covering target users, behavior change, value metrics, baselines, data sources, risks, and scale conditions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xukun0821](https://clawhub.ai/user/xukun0821)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and FDE/customer-facing teams use this skill after a POC run to turn validation evidence into an adoption and value review package, including adoption behavior, value metrics, baselines, cost boundaries, risks, and next-investment recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process usage logs, support issues, costs, ownership, and recovery-readiness evidence supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Provide only data you are authorized to share and remove unnecessary sensitive or confidential details before use.

Risk: Scale or investment recommendations can affect commercial and operational decisions.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as decision inputs and require human review before investment, scale promotion, or personnel-impact decisions.
