## Description:

Guides agents in creating, optimizing, or auditing blog index and listing page structure, content strategy, SEO, and internal linking guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, marketers, content strategists, and developers use this skill to plan or audit blog index pages, content hubs, blog SEO structure, metadata, schema, and internal linking.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Project context files may contain sensitive audience, keyword, or business strategy details that can influence the generated guidance.

Mitigation: Review project context before use and avoid including confidential details that are not needed for blog page planning.

Risk: SEO and content guidance can become stale or may not match a site's market, jurisdiction, or analytics data.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against current SEO requirements, site performance data, and editorial goals before publishing changes.
