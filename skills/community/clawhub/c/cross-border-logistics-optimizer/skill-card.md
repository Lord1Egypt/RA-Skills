## Description:

Compares cross-border e-commerce shipping options, packaging tactics, customs-risk signals, and speed-versus-cost tradeoffs for sellers, operations teams, and marketplace operators.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers, operations teams, and marketplace operators use this skill to compare parcel-level cross-border shipping lanes and produce a concise route, packaging, and customs-risk decision brief.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The shipping brief may be mistaken for live-rate, service-term, customs, or legal guidance.

Mitigation: Verify current carrier prices, service terms, restricted-goods rules, and customs requirements before making shipping commitments.

Risk: Incomplete shipment details can make the route recommendation less reliable.

Mitigation: Confirm origin, destination, dimensions, weight, declared value, product sensitivity, and business priority before acting on the recommendation.
