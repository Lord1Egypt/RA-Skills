## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can expose or amplify precise public schedules and locations for a child-operated coffee stall.

Mitigation: Require adult review before sharing time or location details, publish updates through an adult-managed channel, and avoid precise location details until attendance is appropriate.

Risk: Social post drafts may include time, location, photos, or customer details that create child-safety or privacy concerns.

Mitigation: Require adult review for every public post, avoid identifiable customer information, and review photos before publishing.

Risk: Customer dialogue templates suggest collecting contact details for future stall updates.

Mitigation: Do not collect customer contact details informally; use an adult-managed public update channel instead.

Risk: Broad trigger terms for coffee, carts, and stall operations may activate the skill outside the intended brand context.

Mitigation: Narrow activation to brand-specific terms such as 小沐宝 or 沐宝咖啡, or require clear surrounding context before using this skill.
