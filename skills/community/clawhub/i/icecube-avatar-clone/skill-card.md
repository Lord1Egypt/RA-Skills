## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may upload photos, video, or voice data without proper consent or review of vendor retention terms.

Mitigation: Confirm consent from every depicted or recorded person and review each vendor's privacy and retention terms before using third-party avatar or voice services.

Risk: Avatar and voice-cloning workflows can be used for impersonation, deceptive content, or unlabeled synthetic media.

Mitigation: Avoid impersonation or deceptive use, clone only authorized identities, and label generated media as AI-created.
