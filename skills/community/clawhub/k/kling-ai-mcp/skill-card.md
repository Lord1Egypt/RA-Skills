## Use Case:

External users and developers connect an MCP-compatible agent to Kling AI's Global service to generate images and videos, manage reusable Elements, and check account credits and task status.

Risk: Prompts and media used with connected Kling tools may be sent to Kling.

Mitigation: Avoid sending sensitive content unless you are comfortable sharing it with Kling.
