## Description:

Analyzes pet ear images or videos for visible redness, discharge, and wax buildup, then provides observational findings and suggests veterinary follow-up without diagnosing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smyx-sunjinhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/smyx-sunjinhui)

## Use Case:

Pet owners, boarding centers, and veterinary clinics can use the skill to review pet ear images or videos, receive a structured summary of visible findings, and identify when veterinary follow-up may be appropriate.
