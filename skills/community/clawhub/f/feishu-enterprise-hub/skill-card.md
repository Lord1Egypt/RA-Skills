## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad automation across sensitive Feishu workspace data may expose or process information beyond the intended scope. <br>
Mitigation: Use only with Feishu administrator approval, documented data-source boundaries, role-based access controls, and audit logging. <br>
Risk: Recurring monitoring, reporting, and third-party integrations may act without clear consent or operational limits. <br>
Mitigation: Approve each scheduled workflow and integration before use, define opt-in and opt-out expectations, and review generated outputs before acting on them. <br>
