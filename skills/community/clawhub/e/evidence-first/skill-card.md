## Description:

Evidence First guides agents to require evidence proportional to material claims and to separate evidence, inference, assumptions, and uncertainty in research, analysis, recommendations, reports, audits, and decisions where unsupported confidence would be harmful.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[raguets](https://clawhub.ai/user/raguets)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and external users use this skill when an agent must make material claims traceable to evidence, inference, assumptions, or uncertainty. It is not intended for purely creative or stylistic tasks that do not need evidence scaffolding.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may make research or decision-support responses more structured than usual.

Mitigation: Use it for material claims where evidence and uncertainty improve reviewability, and avoid applying it to purely stylistic or creative tasks.

Risk: Users may overread a precedent or partial source as current authority.

Mitigation: Require the agent to distinguish direct evidence, derived inference, assumptions, and unknowns, and to seek an authoritative source for normative claims.
