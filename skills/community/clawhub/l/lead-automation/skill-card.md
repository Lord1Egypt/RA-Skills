## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lead scraping and outbound outreach can violate laws, platform terms, consent expectations, or rate limits.

Mitigation: Use only lawful data sources, follow each platform's terms and rate limits, and confirm consent, suppression lists, and opt-out handling before collecting data or sending messages.

Risk: Generated websites or personalized outreach may publish inaccurate claims or contact recipients without adequate review.

Mitigation: Require human approval before publishing generated sites, launching campaigns, or scheduling follow-up sequences.
