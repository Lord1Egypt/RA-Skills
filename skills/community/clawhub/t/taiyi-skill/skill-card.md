## Use Case:

People interested in traditional Chinese Taiyi divination use this skill to generate chart-style readings and narrative interpretations for cultural, historical, or entertainment exploration. It should not be used for emergency, medical, political, military, financial, or other consequential decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill frames speculative divination as forecasts for disasters, elections, war, public health, finance, and other consequential topics.

Mitigation: Present outputs only as cultural, historical, or entertainment content and include clear limits against emergency, medical, political, military, or financial decision-making.

Risk: Readers may over-rely on confident-sounding chart readings or auspicious/inauspicious judgments.

Mitigation: Require human review of generated interpretations and avoid using the skill where factual forecasting, professional advice, or safety-critical judgment is required.
