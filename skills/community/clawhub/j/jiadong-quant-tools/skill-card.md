## Description:

Quant Tools is a Chinese-language reference skill that helps agents recommend quantitative research workflows across factor analysis, backtesting, portfolio optimization, AI research, causal inference, derivatives pricing, market data, and research environments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, quantitative researchers, and investment research teams use this skill to choose and combine research libraries and platforms for strategy development, factor mining, academic replication, asset allocation, backtesting, data access, and API-oriented research services.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Guidance may be applied to live trading, broker accounts, API keys, market data services, or hosted FastAPI and JupyterHub services.

Mitigation: Require explicit confirmation and apply secrets management, access controls, and legal or compliance review before using the guidance in those settings.

Risk: Quantitative research workflows can overfit historical data and fail in live trading.

Mitigation: Use out-of-sample testing and causal validation before relying on strategy results.
