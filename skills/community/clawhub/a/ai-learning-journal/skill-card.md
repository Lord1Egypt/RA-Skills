## Description:

AI learning journal assistant for recording AI and LLM study notes, tool experiences, prompt techniques, learning plans, and summaries in Markdown.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pengpeng9527](https://clawhub.ai/user/pengpeng9527)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users use this skill to keep a bilingual AI learning journal, review prior AI study history, receive study guidance, and generate personalized learning plans or summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on casual AI-related comments and organize them into local journal records.

Mitigation: Use it only when local file-backed learning records are desired, and ask the agent not to save content when the conversation should remain transient.
