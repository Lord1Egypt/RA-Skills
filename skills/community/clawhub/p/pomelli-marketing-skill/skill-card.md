## Description:

Brand marketing companion for turning product context into campaign briefs, brand-book inputs, photoshoot directions, and AI image or video generation prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketing, brand, and product teams use this skill to turn product context into campaign concepts, brand-book inputs, photoshoot directions, and AI image or video prompt packs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for general brand-marketing or AI-media requests, not only explicit Pomelli requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the broad trigger posture is acceptable before installation and reserve the skill for brand-marketing planning and prompt-generation workflows.

Risk: Generated campaign concepts or creative prompts could include weak positioning, mismatched claims, off-brand visuals, or missing calls to action.

Mitigation: Use the skill's QA checklist and review outputs for brand consistency, claim safety, and channel fit before publishing.
