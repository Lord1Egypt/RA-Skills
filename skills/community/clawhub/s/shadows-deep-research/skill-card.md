## Description:

4-round deep research protocol - Explorer, Devil's Advocate, Multi-Perspective Synthesis, Crystallize. Use for major architectural decisions, technology choices, strategic questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and decision makers use this skill to structure deep research for architectural decisions, technology evaluations, and strategic planning questions. It guides the agent through landscape mapping, stress testing, multi-perspective analysis, and an actionable recommendation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording can route requests that only need a quick answer into a longer research workflow.

Mitigation: Use explicit wording when a quick answer is wanted and reserve the skill for major research or decision questions.
