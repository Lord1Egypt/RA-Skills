## Description:

Writes and optimizes Substack newsletter issues and web posts, including ghostwriting voice matching, formatting, SEO, growth tactics, social distribution, and monetization planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, newsletter operators, and content teams use this skill to plan, ghostwrite, format, optimize, and distribute Substack newsletter issues or web-first posts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ghostwriting workflows may use transcripts, recordings, and private communications without clear consent or data-retention safeguards.

Mitigation: Confirm consent from recorded participants and the person being ghostwritten, minimize confidential or unnecessary personal data, and delete voice guides and source transcripts when no longer needed.

Risk: Voice matching can misrepresent the person being ghostwritten if the generated voice guide is not validated.

Mitigation: Validate the voice guide with the user or represented person before drafting and revise outputs when they identify inaccurate tone, claims, or phrasing.
