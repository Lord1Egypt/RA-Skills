## Description:

Physiology-PMPH-10edition is a multilingual collection of 165 physiology reference skills based on PMPH Physiology, 10th Edition, covering core mechanisms, calculations, and clinical-assessment study scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xllgreen](https://clawhub.ai/user/xllgreen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Medical students, physiology educators, clinicians, and researchers use this skill collection for structured physiology study support, mechanism explanations, physiological calculations, and clinician-reviewed clinical-assessment references.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat educational physiology or clinical-assessment content as standalone medical advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs only as study material or clinician-reviewed support, and verify clinical claims against current professional guidance before patient-care decisions.
