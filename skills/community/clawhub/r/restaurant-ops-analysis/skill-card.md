## Description:

A Chinese-language restaurant operations analysis skill for interpreting platform, campaign, product, complaint, and cross-channel promotion data for food-service storefronts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Restaurant operators, marketers, and analysts use this skill to evaluate Douyin influencer videos, local promotion spend, product packages, platform complaints, and multi-platform launch plans. It produces structured conclusions, markdown tables, and action recommendations for restaurant storefront operations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Platform appeal drafts or operational recommendations may be inaccurate if the user's supporting facts or evidence are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review generated appeal language and business recommendations against transaction records, platform notices, and available store evidence before using them.

Risk: The skill is tailored to Chinese restaurant platform operations and may not fit other industries, regions, or platform policy contexts.

Mitigation: Confirm that the referenced platform rules, benchmarks, and templates apply to the restaurant, market, and current platform policy before acting.
