## Description:

首届上海'人工智能+消费'创新大赛报名助手。基于用户项目信息，从第一性原理出发通过对抗式审查提供赛道选择与报名填写专业建议。当用户参加AI+消费创新大赛、选择赛道或填写报名表时调用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External applicants, founders, student teams, and project owners use this skill to evaluate fit for the Shanghai AI+Consumption Innovation Competition, choose one competition track, pressure-test risks, and draft application-form guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with guidance tailored to a Shanghai, China competition.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask users for sensitive project, team, business, and compliance details to generate competition guidance.

Mitigation: Share only information appropriate for application drafting, remove unnecessary confidential details, and review generated text before using it in submission materials.

Risk: Competition contact details and deadlines can be stale or mistaken if users rely on the skill without checking official channels.

Mitigation: Verify current contest rules, dates, submission email, and phone contact through official competition sources before sending materials.
