## Description:

Data-driven writing and polishing for professional workplace documents, including performance reviews, retrospectives, resumes, weekly or monthly reports, OKRs, PRDs, bilingual drafts, tone alignment, structured templates, bullet rewriting, and iterative first-draft refinement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and professionals use this skill to turn raw workplace notes into concise, data-driven drafts and polished documents for reviews, resumes, reports, OKRs, and lightweight PRDs. It supports Chinese, English, and bilingual writing based on the user's requested language.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafts may include confidential business metrics, customer names, internal system names, or personal resume details.

Mitigation: Review generated documents before sharing and redact or replace sensitive details with approved placeholders.

Risk: Generated workplace claims can overstate impact if source metrics, baselines, or contribution boundaries are incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify metrics, data definitions, comparison baselines, and ownership claims before using the final document.
