## Description:

Generates Chinese-language film and TV commentary packages from a title, target duration, and style, including scripts, segmented subtitles, BGM recommendations, and editing cues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chenlizhen0031-ui](https://clawhub.ai/user/chenlizhen0031-ui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and media-production agents use this skill to draft short-video film or TV commentary packages with narration copy, subtitle timing, BGM suggestions, and shot-editing guidance. It is aimed at Chinese-language commentary workflows for platforms such as Douyin, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu, and YouTube.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated commentary, film-clip plans, or music suggestions may create copyright risk if users publish long quoted dialogue, unlicensed clips, or unlicensed BGM.

Mitigation: Review the generated script, clip plan, and BGM choices before publication; use indirect paraphrase, licensed clips, and platform-approved or commercially licensed music.

Risk: The skill may activate on broad media-production requests where the user wanted a different kind of subtitle, BGM, or editing workflow.

Mitigation: Ask the user to confirm the target work, duration, style, and platform when the request is ambiguous.
