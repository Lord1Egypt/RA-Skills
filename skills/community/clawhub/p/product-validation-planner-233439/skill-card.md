## Description:

Helps founders and small product teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, and repeatable validation workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to validate product ideas before heavy investment. It produces practical planning support such as workflows, checklists, templates, analysis, and next-step guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation wording may activate the skill for unrelated business advice.

Mitigation: Invoke a different skill explicitly when the request is not about product validation planning.

Risk: Validation plans and positioning guidance may be incomplete or misleading if based on weak user inputs.

Mitigation: Review assumptions, constraints, and success criteria before acting on the generated plan.
