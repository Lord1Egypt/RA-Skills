## Description:

Turn a short-drama idea into a workshop pack: a beat sheet, vertical storyboard stills, one spoken line per beat, and an original music bed. This short drama creation workshop writes the episode spine, generates stills, records dialogue, and creates a scene score you can review before a still is animated into a shot. Use it for romance, suspense, comedy, and tearjerker vertical dramas, with character, costume, and location held across the episode.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
