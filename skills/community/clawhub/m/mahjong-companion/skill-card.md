## Description:

A beginner-friendly mahjong companion that explains hand structure and rules in plain language, renders tile layouts, and pauses whenever asked.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bonniegeng-max](https://clawhub.ai/user/bonniegeng-max)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Adult beginners use this skill to practice a casual mahjong round, understand hand structure and common rules, and see their tiles as an image. It is for entertainment and practice only; it does not provide gambling advice or adjudicate wins and losses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review flagged a Chromium sandbox-disabled setting in the hand renderer, and scanner coverage was partial.

Mitigation: Review the Chromium sandbox setting before installing, as the security guidance recommends; the scan does not establish that the skill is safe.
