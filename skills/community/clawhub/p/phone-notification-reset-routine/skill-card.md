## Description:

Create a 20-minute phone notification audit card for attention hygiene, without account, credential, surveillance, or monitoring setup.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this prompt-only skill to reduce distracting phone notifications while preserving alerts they consider important. It guides a 20-minute audit that sorts apps into keep, mute, batch, badge-only, quiet delivery, or review-later decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user could over-quiet critical safety, medical, caregiver, school, work, or emergency alerts while reducing notification volume.

Mitigation: Keep critical alerts enabled by default and treat any changes to those alerts as outside the routine unless the phone owner independently chooses otherwise.

Risk: A user could share private message contents, credentials, or account details while describing notification problems.

Mitigation: Ask only for app names, notification categories, timing, and preferences; do not request private message content, credentials, account names, codes, screenshots with sensitive content, or device identifiers.
