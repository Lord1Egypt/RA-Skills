## Use Case:

Clinic operations staff, healthcare administrators, and supporting agents use this skill to manage scheduling, records, prescriptions, labs, staffing, billing, compliance, and patient communications under human or provider review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive healthcare workflows can expose or misuse patient information if the deployment grants broad record access.

Mitigation: Install only where the agent is authorized for clinic operations and has privacy controls, audit logging, and minimum-necessary access.

Risk: Patient communications, record releases, prescriptions, billing changes, and emergency escalation can affect patient safety or legal obligations.

Mitigation: Keep these actions under appropriate human or provider review and avoid autonomous execution for clinical, billing, or disclosure decisions.
