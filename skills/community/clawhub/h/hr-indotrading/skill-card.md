## Use Case:

HR teams and authorized IndoTrading stakeholders use this skill to request Indonesian-language reports on headcount, employee status, attendance, performance reviews, payroll data, and HR risk flags.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill discusses sensitive employee and payroll topics.

Mitigation: Use only with authorized HR data and apply local privacy, labor-law, retention, and access-control requirements.

Risk: Generated HR red flags could influence personnel decisions.

Mitigation: Treat red flags as decision support and require qualified human review before taking employment, compensation, or compliance action.

Risk: Reference files include placeholder data sources and configurable HR schedules.

Mitigation: Confirm source systems, reporting periods, payroll schedules, and approval chains before relying on generated reports.
