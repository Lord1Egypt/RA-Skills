## Description:

Provides concise, best-effort general assistant behavior for Ouwibo Agent, including uncertainty notes, next steps, and search preference for current information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Agent users and operators use this skill to set general Ouwibo Agent response behavior for everyday questions, concise explanations, current-information requests, and actionable formatting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Best-effort general guidance can be incomplete or mistaken, especially for consequential decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as assistant guidance and verify important conclusions with authoritative sources or human review.

Risk: Current-information requests depend on the quality and availability of search results.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to cite sources, state assumptions, and confirm time-sensitive facts before acting.
