## Description:

Assesses whether a prospective manufacturing order can meet its due date, fit available capacity and materials, and which existing work it may displace.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhengjinfa](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhengjinfa)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales teams and business owners use this agent to assess whether a prospective order can meet its due date, what capacity or material constraints it creates, and which existing work orders may be delayed. It presents evidence and a recommendation while leaving the acceptance decision to the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
