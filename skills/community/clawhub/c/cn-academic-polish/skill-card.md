## Description:

Polishes Chinese academic writing by rewriting colloquial passages into formal scholarly prose, enforcing terminology consistency, checking GB/T 7714-2015 citation style, and returning polished text, a change table, and unresolved questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sharinchan233](https://clawhub.ai/user/sharinchan233)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, students, researchers, and academic writers use this skill to polish Chinese thesis or journal-paper passages while preserving meaning, terminology, and evidentiary limits.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer outputs toward Chinese academic style and GB/T 7714 formatting when a request appears to involve Chinese academic writing.

Mitigation: Use it for Chinese academic polishing and citation-format requests, and review the result against the author's required venue or style guide.

Risk: Language polishing can unintentionally change the nuance or strength of academic claims.

Mitigation: Keep author review in the loop; the skill is instructed to preserve meaning and mark uncertain facts, data, and citations for confirmation.
