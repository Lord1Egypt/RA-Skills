## Description:

Drafts construction delay notices and claim letters with contract-clause citation, delay-cause classification, critical-path impact narrative, quantum reservation, and records-preservation guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Project managers, contractors, schedulers, and contracts teams use this skill to draft factual construction delay notices or claim letters that preserve contractual rights while routing the draft for counsel review before sending.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A generated notice or claim letter may cite incorrect notice requirements, deadlines, recipients, or legal positions.

Mitigation: Verify contract notice clauses, deadline calculations, recipients, and legal position with qualified contracts counsel before sending.

Risk: Delay classification or critical-path impact statements may overstate entitlement or weaken a later claim if based on incomplete project facts.

Mitigation: Confirm cause classification, schedule facts, critical-path activities, and supporting records before relying on the draft.
