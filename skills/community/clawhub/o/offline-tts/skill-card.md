## Description:

Converts text into an audio file using voices available on macOS, Linux, or Windows computers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People using a command-capable assistant can turn text, articles, files, or chat answers into audio to listen to later.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The chat-sandbox fallback may process text or audio in an internet-enabled environment, outside the stated local-processing boundary.

Mitigation: Review the fallback before use; use a local command-capable environment when content should remain on the user's computer.

Risk: Installing a speech engine or supporting tools may require elevated privileges.

Mitigation: Review the installation steps and requested permissions before installing packages.
