## Description:

Compare and synthesize findings across multiple completed DeepScan reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[papersareflowing](https://clawhub.ai/user/papersareflowing)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to compare completed DeepScan research runs, identify common findings and divergences, and produce concise cross-report synthesis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The agent may use existing DeepScan history and reports that contain sensitive or unpublished research context.

Mitigation: Install and use the skill only where agent access to those existing reports is appropriate, and review generated summaries or plots before sharing them.

Risk: Cross-report synthesis can overstate common themes or trends when the available DeepScan data is sparse.

Mitigation: Ground summaries in specific papers by title and year, and state when the available data is too sparse for a meaningful comparison or plot.
