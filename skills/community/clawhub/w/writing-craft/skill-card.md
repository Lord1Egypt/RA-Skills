## Description:

A Chinese-language writing-coaching skill that applies Liu Junqiang’s “Writing Is a Craft” methodology through time-scale selection, tree-structured outlining, three-step questioning, angle selection, drafting, review, quote extraction, and training exercises.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xtoyun](https://clawhub.ai/user/xtoyun)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, writers, creators, and writing coaches use this skill to turn a topic or draft into structured Chinese-language writing guidance, including outlines, full articles, diagnostic review, quote extraction, angle exploration, and daily practice prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for general writing requests and may answer in Chinese unless the host agent preserves the user’s preferred language.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended language and activation scope when installing or using the skill in multilingual or general-purpose writing workflows.
