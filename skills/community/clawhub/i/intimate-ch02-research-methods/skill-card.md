## Use Case:

Learners and instructors use this skill to review and apply research-methods concepts from Chapter 2 of Intimate Relationships in Chinese-language study conversations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is scoped to one textbook chapter and may be over-applied to unrelated relationship topics.

Mitigation: Use it for Chapter 2 research-methods concepts and consult other sources for later chapters or substantive relationship theories.

Risk: Educational explanations and study examples may be mistaken for authoritative citations.

Mitigation: Verify research names, years, and details against the source textbook before academic or formal use.

Risk: The default response style is Chinese-language instruction.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to change language when a different study language is needed.
