## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision-support artifacts before investing heavily.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test product ideas, shape validation plans, outline prototypes, and prepare user-facing positioning before committing significant resources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may select the skill for loosely related product, startup, or business-planning prompts.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's product-validation objective, constraints, inputs, and success criteria before producing plans or checklists.

Risk: Product validation outputs can be misleading when based on sparse or unverified market inputs.

Mitigation: Make assumptions visible, ask for missing information that materially changes the output, and include a verification note with remaining risks or follow-up work.
