## Description:

Extract and risk-rate hidden assumptions in product briefs or PRDs, producing a prioritised assumption map with confidence and impact scores, validation methods, and critical assumption flags.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, and product teams use this skill to review product briefs, PRDs, or concepts for hidden assumptions before development. It ranks assumptions across desirability, feasibility, viability, and usability and recommends concrete validation methods.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad product-plan review prompts may invoke the skill and produce recommendations based only on the user's supplied brief or PRD.

Mitigation: Review the assumption map against source product evidence and treat the validation recommendations as planning guidance, not proof that assumptions are true.

Risk: Untestable assumptions can be converted into misleading validation work if they are not explicitly flagged.

Mitigation: Flag untestable assumptions as owned risks and require each assumption in the map to have a plausible validation method.
