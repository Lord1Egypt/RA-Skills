## Description:

Generates ATS-friendly, professionally formatted resumes tailored for US, UK, EU, and APAC job markets in tech, finance, and corporate sectors.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jochoonhwan](https://clawhub.ai/user/jochoonhwan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External applicants and career-support agents use this skill to draft ATS-friendly resumes and CVs for international job applications. It supports regional formatting choices for US, Canada, UK, EU, and APAC markets across tech, finance, consulting, marketing, and corporate roles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated resumes may be inaccurate or poorly targeted if the target country, language, role, or job posting requirements are unclear.

Mitigation: Specify the target market, language, role, and job posting requirements before generation, then review the result against the actual application instructions.

Risk: Resume drafts may expose personal contact details, photos, or work history if shared without review.

Mitigation: Review and redact personal details, photos, and work history before submitting or sharing the resume.
