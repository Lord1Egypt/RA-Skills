## Description:

Generates evidence-based HTML initiation research reports for drugs, targets, or diseases, covering pipelines, competitors, patents/FTO, business development, clinical and safety data, markets, China regulatory paths, ROI, risk, and GO/NO-GO decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Pharma and life sciences teams use this skill to scope drug, target, or disease opportunities and produce traceable project-initiation reports for portfolio, BD, clinical, patent/FTO, regulatory, and market decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated patent/FTO, regulatory, safety, ROI, and GO/NO-GO conclusions may influence high-impact business decisions.

Mitigation: Require expert manual review of these sections before using the report for investment, regulatory, patent, clinical, or portfolio decisions.

Risk: The skill performs broad biomedical and business research queries, including web checks, so source quality and freshness can affect conclusions.

Mitigation: Validate material claims against cited primary or authoritative sources and confirm time-sensitive disclosures before acting on the report.
