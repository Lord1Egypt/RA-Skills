## Description:

Identify the single highest-leverage thing to remove from a game design, feature, system, UX flow, pitch, roadmap item, or prototype in order to improve it significantly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and developers use this skill to identify the one design element whose removal would most improve clarity, pacing, focus, usability, production scope, or player experience. It is suited for critiquing game designs, features, systems, UX flows, pitches, roadmap items, and prototypes that feel bloated or diluted.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill produces design judgment that may be incorrect, overconfident, or misaligned with player and business goals.

Mitigation: Review its recommendation as critique, then validate important removals with design review, prototype comparison, or player testing before committing.
