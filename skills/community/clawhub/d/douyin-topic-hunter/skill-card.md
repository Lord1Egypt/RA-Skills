## Description:

Read-only access to public Douyin data for keyword and creator searches, posts, comments, details, and trends, returning structured JSON for content research and monitoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[guaikei](https://clawhub.ai/user/guaikei)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content teams, marketers, and analysts use the skill to retrieve public Douyin videos, creator profiles, posts, comments, and trends for topic research, competitor monitoring, and audience analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms, links, or content IDs are sent to the third-party Guaikei API, and the security review notes that code assessment was partial.

Mitigation: Use only queries and identifiers appropriate to share with that provider, avoid confidential terms, and review changes before running the skill with a token.
