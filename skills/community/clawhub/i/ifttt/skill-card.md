## Description:

Connect OpenClaw to IFTTT's hosted MCP server and automate hundreds of services: discover triggers and actions, build and manage Applets, and run actions and queries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to connect OpenClaw to IFTTT, discover supported services and steps, and create or manage Applets for cross-service automations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: OAuth and service-connection prompts can grant access to connected IFTTT services.

Mitigation: Review authorization and connection prompts carefully before approving access.

Risk: Created Applets, removals, and one-off actions can affect connected services, including sending messages or changing device state.

Mitigation: Confirm that proposed Applets, removals, and direct actions match the intended behavior before execution.
