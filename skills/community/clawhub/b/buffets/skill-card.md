## Use Case:

External users and travel planners use this skill to request popular buffet recommendations, compare options by budget and location, and plan restaurant visit routes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Restaurant popularity, hours, ratings, wait times, and booking details may become outdated before a visit.

Mitigation: Verify current details with live sources or the restaurant before traveling or making a reservation.

Risk: Venue rules such as filming restrictions, minimum spend, accessibility, or reservation requirements may vary by location.

Mitigation: Check current venue policies and follow on-site rules before relying on recommendations.
