## Description:

Helps founders, builders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, workflows, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It helps structure validation work into practical plans, templates, prototypes, positioning, assumptions, risks, and next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The activation language may match broad startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation requests.

Mitigation: Review whether the skill is appropriate for the specific user request before relying on its planning guidance.

Risk: Product validation guidance can be incomplete or misleading if the user's market, customer segment, constraints, or success criteria are under-specified.

Mitigation: Make assumptions explicit, ask for missing information when it materially changes the output, and validate recommendations against the stated success criteria.
