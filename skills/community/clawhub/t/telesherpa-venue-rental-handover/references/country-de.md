# Country module: Germany

Use this file when the intake label language is German. It sets **display
labels** only. Keys, option values (`venueBooking`, `handoverProtocol`,
`returnProtocol`, `defect`, `pass`, …) and tool names stay as in the rest of
the skill. This skill does not give legal advice. Hire terms, deposits and
who may sign are intake answers from the club, parish or municipality.

## Notes

The lists are the book the chair or caretaker works from. Keep any hire
agreement the organisation already uses, in the form their advisor has set.
Rental and deposit fields store the figures the organisation provides; payment
stays in the account they already use.
