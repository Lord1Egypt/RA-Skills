## Description:

Video Prompting provides Chinese-language guidance, vocabulary, templates, and platform notes for composing detailed AI video generation prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[heyun9123](https://clawhub.ai/user/heyun9123)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, creators, and agent workflows use this skill to draft and refine prompts for AI video generation, including subject, action, scene, camera motion, lighting, style, and technical details. It is most useful when adapting prompts across video models such as Sora, Runway, Kling, Seedance, Veo, Hailuo, and Pika.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Platform-specific prompting advice can become outdated as video generation models change.

Mitigation: Check current official model documentation before relying on platform-specific recommendations.

Risk: The artifact is primarily a Chinese-language guide, which may not fit English-only agent workflows.

Mitigation: Use it in workflows that can read Chinese or review translated prompt guidance before deployment.
