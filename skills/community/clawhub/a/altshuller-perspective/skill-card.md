## Description:

A TRIZ perspective skill that helps agents analyze engineering, product, innovation, and management problems through Genrich Altshuller's contradiction, ideal final result, invention-level, and technology-evolution frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to have agents apply TRIZ-style contradiction analysis, ideal final result reasoning, invention-level framing, and technology-evolution heuristics to technical, product, innovation, and management questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can steer answers toward TRIZ-style contradiction analysis even when a problem is outside that framework's useful scope.

Mitigation: Review outputs for fit to the user's domain and preserve the skill's stated uncertainty and boundary checks.

Risk: Optional Zhihuiya/PatSnap MCP authorization could send patent or innovation research queries to an external service.

Mitigation: Authorize the integration only when needed, review the service permissions, and limit use to intended patent or innovation research tasks.
