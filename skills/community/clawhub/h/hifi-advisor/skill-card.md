## Description:

Evaluate hi-fi and audio gear options, build system recommendations, guide installation and tuning, and analyze used-market pricing/resale value.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to choose and match hi-fi equipment, improve room or desk audio setup, and assess second-hand audio listings. It is intended for practical recommendations, trade-off analysis, setup checklists, and fair-price bands.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for purchase context, room details, existing gear, and listing-price data.

Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the recommendation, and use the CSV helper only with listing data you are comfortable processing locally.

Risk: Audio gear pricing and fit can vary by region, condition, room, accessories, and shipping risk.

Mitigation: Use the skill's fair-price bands, checklist items, and caveats as decision support, then confirm final buying decisions against current local listings and inspection results.
