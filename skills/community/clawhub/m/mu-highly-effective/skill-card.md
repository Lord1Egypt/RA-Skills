## Description:

Seven Habits workplace coaching skill. Diagnoses habit gaps, customizes check-in plans, guides mission statement drafting, audits emotional bank accounts, and organizes time matrices. Part of the Mu Book Series.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[muippt](https://clawhub.ai/user/muippt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and knowledge workers use this skill to turn Seven Habits concepts into workplace-specific coaching, habit diagnosis, mission-statement reflection, relationship audits, time-matrix planning, and verifiable check-in plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Relationship audits, mission statements, and check-in logs may contain sensitive workplace or personal reflections.

Mitigation: Treat these outputs as private notes and avoid sharing identifiable workplace details unless the user has a clear need and permission.

Risk: Relationship audit workflows can involve information about colleagues, managers, or reports.

Mitigation: Do not record or identify other people without consent and appropriate workplace or legal permission.

Risk: The skill may receive workplace stress or personal-development requests that exceed Seven Habits coaching.

Mitigation: Keep guidance within the skill's coaching scope and direct severe psychological or health concerns to qualified professionals.
