## Use Case:

External users, founders, operators, and engineers use this skill to guide agents through data profiling, SQL and spreadsheet analysis, cleaning decisions, and action-oriented reports for business decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive, financial, customer, personal, or regulated data may be exposed while using an agent for analysis.

Mitigation: Use only data the user is authorized to access, prefer read-only sources, and redact or minimize sensitive fields before sharing data with an agent.

Risk: Business recommendations may be misleading if data quality, sample size, confounders, or limitations are not checked.

Mitigation: Profile datasets before analysis, document assumptions and limitations, and use the skill's statistical checks and scoring rubric before acting on findings.
