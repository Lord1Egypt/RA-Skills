## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The security evidence reports a suspicious verdict because a bundled autoreview helper may default to running a nested reviewer with full sandbox bypass. <br>
Mitigation: Review the skill before installation, install only from a trusted maintenance workflow, and disable YOLO-style execution by setting AUTOREVIEW_YOLO=0 or passing --no-yolo when using that helper. <br>
Risk: The skill can produce market, compliance, and investment-oriented analysis that may be inaccurate, outdated, or unsuitable as professional advice. <br>
Mitigation: Validate important outputs against authoritative current sources and treat investment, regulatory, veterinary, and brand-selection guidance as research support rather than final advice. <br>
