## Description:

Coach for B2B SaaS founders on pricing, including value metrics, tier structure, headline pricing, free trial or freemium choices, packaging, discounting policy, contract periods, repricing, and enterprise pricing transitions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[charlie-morrison](https://clawhub.ai/user/charlie-morrison)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders and startup operators use this skill to structure and adjust B2B SaaS pricing. It helps with choosing value metrics, packaging tiers and add-ons, setting headline prices, managing discounts, and planning repricing or enterprise transitions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing and contract recommendations could affect customer commitments if applied without review.

Mitigation: Have legal and finance review recommendations before applying them to overages, surcharges, renewals, price increases, or customer communications.
