## Description:

Create text-based art, including classic ASCII art and emoji pixel art, in various styles and themes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[roryyu](https://clawhub.ai/user/roryyu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to produce copyable ASCII art, emoji pixel illustrations, and simple pixel-style decorative art for messages, comments, and social media. It also helps choose between ASCII, emoji grid, and hybrid text-art styles.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Emoji pixel art may render with uneven alignment across clients, fonts, or copy-paste targets.

Mitigation: Keep output in fenced code blocks, maintain equal row widths, and use U+3000 full-width spaces for blank cells.

Risk: Much of the artifact guidance is written in Chinese, which may reduce accessibility for reviewers or users who do not read Chinese.

Mitigation: Summarize or translate the usage guidance when needed before relying on it.
