## Description:

Plans paid ad campaigns with platform selection and budget guidance for startups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[c0ldsmi1e](https://clawhub.ai/user/c0ldsmi1e)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders and startup teams use this skill to plan initial paid acquisition campaigns, choose suitable ad platforms, allocate budget, define creative direction, and set up measurement before launch.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive business context such as ad budget, CAC, LTV, target audience, and campaign performance.

Mitigation: Use approximate values when possible and avoid sharing confidential customer, financial, or account-level data unless the execution environment is approved for that information.

Risk: The skill provides advisory campaign planning and does not operate ad accounts or verify live platform conditions.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against current ad platform policies, tracking requirements, and internal budget controls before launching campaigns.
