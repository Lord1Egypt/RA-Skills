## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Guidance for real customer environments, production systems, security testing, data migration, or role changes could lead to unintended customer or system impact if acted on without review.

Mitigation: Require explicit user approval before those activities and limit the agent to the specific prospect materials and environment details needed.

Risk: Pre-sales, proof-of-concept, benchmarking, and proposal outputs may contain inaccurate or overbroad technical claims.

Mitigation: Review outputs against product documentation, customer requirements, and approved commercial messaging before sharing with prospects or customers.
