## Description:

Add sparse, deliberate bold highlights to a few key concepts so readers can scan and capture an article's core points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[j3ffyang](https://clawhub.ai/user/j3ffyang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content authors use this skill to polish Markdown articles in ai-thoughts/docs/ by adding a small number of deliberate bold highlights that make the article easier to scan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Formatting edits could over-emphasize text or subtly change how readers interpret an article.

Mitigation: Review the resulting article diff before accepting the change.

Risk: The skill may regenerate READMEs when an edited article is registered in articles.yaml.

Mitigation: Check any generated documentation changes alongside the article edit.
