## Description:

Guides users through imaginative scene co-creation and turns vivid, non-referential worlds into image-generation prompts when requested.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xuan905](https://clawhub.ai/user/xuan905)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill to collaboratively build fantastical scenes, recover from creative blocks, and translate a strong shared image into platform-specific prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Direct image generation may invoke a separate provider workflow with its own permissions and costs.

Mitigation: Review the selected image-generation tool's permissions, terms, and cost behavior before use.

Risk: Generated creative prompts may not match a user's intended tone, constraints, or platform policy requirements.

Mitigation: Review and edit prompts before submitting them to image-generation services.
