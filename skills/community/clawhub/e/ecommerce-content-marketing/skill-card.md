## Description:

Ecommerce Content Marketing helps e-commerce teams build or audit content strategies, including content calendars, topic ideas, competitor analysis, customer voice insights, SEO opportunities, and platform-specific recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External e-commerce operators and marketing teams use this skill to plan new content strategies or audit existing content across social, SEO, marketplace, and competitor channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The documented global install command is unpinned, so users may install a version they have not reviewed.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned or reviewed installer and source repository version, and consider project-local installation during evaluation.

Risk: Content audits can involve client strategy, private dashboards, account data, or personal mixed-use social accounts.

Mitigation: Provide public brand names, public handles, and public URLs where possible, and avoid sharing confidential strategy, credentials, private analytics, or personal account data unless intended for analysis.

Risk: The skill relies on public web research and cannot access private social analytics, exact engagement metrics, or paid trend data.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance and verify performance-sensitive decisions against owned analytics or paid research tools.
