## Description:

Helps agents organize and summarize public Douyin short-drama channel and episode page information.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill for lightweight summaries of public Douyin short-drama pages, including episode status, cast, popularity signals, comment highlights, rankings, and genre distribution.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill targets public Douyin pages that may include dynamic rendering or human verification.

Mitigation: Use manual page visits to trigger parsing and avoid bypassing verification or platform controls.

Risk: Bulk collection, downloading, interface reverse engineering, or high-frequency requests could violate platform limits.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for light summaries, keep request volume controlled, and do not download content or call private interfaces.
