## Description:

Analyzes higher-mathematics mistakes to identify error patterns, connect them to knowledge gaps, and recommend personalized review paths and intervention strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daigxok](https://clawhub.ai/user/daigxok)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, tutors, and education-support agents use this skill to turn calculus and higher-mathematics wrong-answer records into error taxonomies, personalized error books, review schedules, and teaching intervention plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process identifiable student records or student profiles.

Mitigation: Use anonymized or pseudonymous student data and avoid unnecessary names, identifiers, or sensitive profile details.

Risk: Generated student profiles and learning plans may create privacy or retention obligations in real educational settings.

Mitigation: Obtain appropriate consent, restrict access to generated profiles, and define retention and deletion practices before deployment.

Risk: Error analyses and intervention recommendations may be treated as authoritative educational assessments.

Mitigation: Have educators review recommendations before using them for grading, placement, or significant learning interventions.
