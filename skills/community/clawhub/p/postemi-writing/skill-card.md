## Description:

Write social media posts people stop for and read to the end, then schedule them with Postemi. Covers finding post ideas, hooks and headlines, opening lines, formatting and rhythm, threads, the writing rules of each network (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest and more) and a check before publishing. Writes in the user's language. Use when the user asks to write, rewrite or improve a post, thread, caption, hook or headline, asks what to post about, or wants a post to reach more people.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and creators use this skill to brainstorm ideas and write or improve social posts, captions, threads, hooks, and headlines in the user's language. It provides platform-specific writing guidance and helps prepare drafts for review before scheduling through Postemi.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A draft could include unsupported claims about the user's experience, results, or sources.

Mitigation: Use facts supplied by the user or an identified source, and ask for missing details instead of inventing them.

Risk: A scheduled post may be published publicly before the user is satisfied with it.

Mitigation: Show the draft for review and preview it before scheduling; wait for the user's confirmation.
