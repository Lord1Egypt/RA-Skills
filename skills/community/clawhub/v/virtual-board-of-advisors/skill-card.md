## Description:

Virtual Board of Advisors helps users consult structured perspectives from 10 business thinkers for strategic decisions across startups, decision science, AI, offers, performance, culture, marketing, audience building, values-led leadership, and fitness or nutrition product development.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[santaclaw8800](https://clawhub.ai/user/santaclaw8800)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External business users use this skill to get concise advisory guidance, compare multiple advisor perspectives, and generate long-form reports for business strategy, offers, pricing, positioning, marketing, AI adoption, leadership, culture, and health or fitness product decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Report or deep-dive requests may generate large responses and consume more tokens.

Mitigation: Use conversational mode or narrow the advisor set unless a full structured report is needed.

Risk: Strategic, AI, fitness, or nutrition outputs may be mistaken for licensed professional advice.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as planning input and obtain qualified review for medical, legal, compliance, or other regulated decisions.

Risk: Advisor-style recommendations may extrapolate from documented public frameworks.

Mitigation: Keep extrapolations labeled and verify material claims against primary sources before acting.
