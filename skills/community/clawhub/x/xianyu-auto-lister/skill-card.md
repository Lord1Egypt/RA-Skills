## Use Case:

External sellers and ecommerce operators use this skill to draft marketplace listing content and selling guidance for Xianyu items before manually posting them.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill's auto-publishing wording may lead users to expect automatic Xianyu posting.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as draft listing content and manually review and post them in Xianyu.

Risk: Listing drafts or proof-of-purchase images may expose personal information, order numbers, addresses, serial numbers, IMEI numbers, or account identifiers.

Mitigation: Redact sensitive identifiers before sharing item details or images with the agent.

Risk: Pricing, exposure, and conversion guidance may be inaccurate for a specific item or market condition.

Mitigation: Compare generated suggestions with current Xianyu listings and adjust before publishing.
