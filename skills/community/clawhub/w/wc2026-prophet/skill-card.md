## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad sports terms may route to this World Cup skill even when the user's intent is ambiguous.

Mitigation: Ask a clarifying question before using the skill when the request does not clearly match schedules, results, standings, brackets, predictions, team information, or fortunes.

Risk: Prediction and fortune outputs may be mistaken for factual forecasts.

Mitigation: Present predictions and fortunes as entertainment-only and avoid guarantees about match outcomes or champion likelihood.
