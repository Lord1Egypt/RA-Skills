## Description:

Generates a full marketing system for beauty salons and wellness businesses, covering client acquisition, retention, and consistent social media content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitflopez](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitflopez)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Salon owners, wellness operators, and agencies serving beauty clients use this skill to generate campaign copy, service-page content, review requests, retention sequences, referral assets, social calendars, and rebooking scripts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated medical-aesthetic claims, testimonials, before/after captions, and treatment-specific copy may need legal, compliance, or clinical review before publication.

Mitigation: Review claims and add treatment-specific safety language, disclosures, and local compliance requirements before publishing.

Risk: Generated SMS, email, and referral campaigns may be sent to contacts who have not consented or who have opted out.

Mitigation: Use only consented contact lists, honor opt-outs, and retain required opt-out language in SMS and email workflows.
