## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Nutrition guidance could be mistaken for medical advice in health-sensitive situations.

Mitigation: Use only for general meal-planning and macro guidance, and seek qualified medical nutrition advice for diabetes, kidney or liver disease, pregnancy, adolescence, eating-disorder history, underweight status, or other health-sensitive situations.
