## Description:

Transforms previously rewritten Chinese text with a second round of subtle style perturbation to reduce consistency in rhythm, phrasing, references, and local transitions while preserving academic tone and meaning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhengqizhuang](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhengqizhuang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to apply minor secondary stylistic edits to already rewritten Chinese academic text. It is intended to vary sentence rhythm, synonym choice, transitions, and references without adding new information or changing professional terminology.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Style perturbation may be inappropriate in contexts that require transparent authorship, a stable writing style, or minimal editorial intervention.

Mitigation: Use the skill only where stylistic rewriting is permitted, and review whether style perturbation is appropriate for the writing context before use.

Risk: Small synonym, reference, or transition changes may alter nuance even when the intended edit size is minor.

Mitigation: Review the output against the source text to confirm terminology, meaning, and academic tone are preserved.
