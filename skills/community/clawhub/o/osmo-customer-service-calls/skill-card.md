## Description:

OsmO's AI calls a company's customer service line, handles the phone menu and hold queue, and returns the answer with a summary, transcript, and recording link.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[getosmo](https://clawhub.ai/user/getosmo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People who need help with a refund, order, bill, booking, or account question can ask OsmO's AI to contact a company's customer care line and report the outcome. Each call is previewed and placed only after the user agrees.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States, Canada, United Kingdom, and Australia (the documented free-credit regions)

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Using the skill requires connecting to the hosted OsmO service, and the scan does not specify what information the service receives.

Mitigation: Review OsmO's privacy information before signing in, and provide only the details needed for the call. Do not include passwords, payment card numbers, PINs, one-time codes, or government ID numbers in call details.
