## Description:

Discover Australia like a local with deep city-region coverage, practical route planning, food context, and execution-ready travel logistics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to plan Australia trips with city-region guidance, realistic distance and seasonality tradeoffs, food and accommodation context, and practical safety logistics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can create long-lived plaintext trip memory in ~/australia/memory.md that may include booking, health, mobility, dietary, or travel-party details.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation, avoid storing passport numbers, payment data, full booking confirmation codes, or sensitive health details, and delete or edit ~/australia/memory.md when the trip no longer needs active memory.
