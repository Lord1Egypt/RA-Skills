## Description:

Teaching Plan Writer helps teachers generate structured Chinese compulsory-education lesson plans aligned with the 2022 curriculum standards and teaching-learning-assessment consistency.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hwx1971](https://clawhub.ai/user/hwx1971)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers use this skill to draft and revise lesson plans, teaching designs, and teaching plans for compulsory-education subjects including Chinese, mathematics, English, and science. It is intended for structured lesson-plan generation from a topic, grade, subject keyword, or teaching requirement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated curriculum citations, lesson examples, or web-search summaries may be inaccurate or outdated.

Mitigation: Verify curriculum citations, source materials, and lesson-plan claims before classroom use.

Risk: Lesson-planning prompts can accidentally include private student data.

Mitigation: Avoid entering personally identifiable student data and use generalized classroom context instead.

Risk: Document links or downloadable resources returned during lesson-plan generation may be untrusted.

Mitigation: Use trusted sources and scan downloaded files before opening or sharing them.
