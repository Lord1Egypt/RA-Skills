## Description:

鬼谷子智慧 is a Chinese/English strategy and persuasion coaching skill that applies Guiguzi concepts such as opening and closing, probing, rapport-building, and fissure analysis to negotiation, persuasion, strategic planning, and interpersonal dynamics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persuasion guidance could be misused for deception, coercion, political manipulation, or exploitation of vulnerable people.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for transparent, lawful negotiation and strategy planning, and enforce the artifact's refusal guidance for fraudulent, manipulative, harmful, legal, or medical-advice requests.

Risk: Strategic recommendations may be mistaken for final decisions or professional advice.

Mitigation: Keep a human decision-maker accountable for outcomes and route legal, medical, or other regulated matters to qualified professionals.

Risk: Guiguzi-style interpersonal analysis may be inappropriate in intimate or purely emotional contexts.

Mitigation: Prefer direct, transparent communication for close relationships and use the skill only for external problem-solving where all parties' lawful interests are considered.
