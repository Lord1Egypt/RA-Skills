## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tariff, VAT, customs, and logistics recommendations may be incomplete or outdated if product details, HS code, route, value, weight, shipping mode, trade terms, or destination country are missing.

Mitigation: Provide complete shipment details and verify outputs against current official customs rules or a qualified customs professional before operational use.
