## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests broad shell and local file-reading capabilities that are not needed for ordinary handout drafting.

Mitigation: Review and narrow tool permissions before installation or use, especially if the agent will handle school, family, student, or business materials.

Risk: Generated school-facing handouts may contain inaccurate or incomplete guidance if used without review.

Mitigation: Have a qualified reviewer check the handout for accuracy, readability, and audience fit before external publication.
