Developers, operators, and content creators use this skill to draft presentation outlines for business decks, TED-style talks, academic presentations, pitch decks, slide plans, and timing allocations.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated outlines may contain generic claims, placeholder structure, or timing assumptions that are not tailored to the final audience.

Mitigation: Review and adapt the outline, claims, citations, speaker notes, and timing before using it in a presentation.

Risk: Some helper commands may have reliability rough edges.

Mitigation: Run the command locally and verify the output before depending on it for a production deck or live presentation.
