## Description:

A decision capture and review system that records every significant choice with context, reasoning, and alternatives so users can detect blind spots and decision patterns over time.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[evanl1](https://clawhub.ai/user/evanl1)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to capture meaningful decisions, classify their context and reversibility, schedule follow-ups, and review patterns over weekly, monthly, and quarterly horizons.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may silently monitor conversations and store inferred behavioral profiles without clear user consent or visibility.

Mitigation: Use only with explicit opt-in, visible logging, and controls to inspect, correct, pause, export, and delete decision and profile entries.
