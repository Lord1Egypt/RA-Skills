## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill guides blockchain/game transactions that can spend or move assets.

Mitigation: Use interactive review, verify rates, denominations, addresses, provider terms, and transaction amounts before signing.

Risk: Provider agreements and staking or migration actions may lock value, incur penalties, or be difficult to reverse.

Mitigation: Compare provider terms before opening agreements, avoid committing short-term funds to reactor defusion cooldowns, and verify validator and destination addresses before migration or transfer.
