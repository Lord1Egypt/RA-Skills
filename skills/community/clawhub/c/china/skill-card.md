## Description:

Discover China like a local with deep city-region coverage, practical route planning, food context, and execution-ready travel logistics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External travelers and travel-planning agents use this skill to build China itineraries, choose regional bases, plan long-distance routes, and account for transport, payments, connectivity, seasonality, food, safety, and pacing constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may persist travel preferences and trip details in ~/china/memory.md.

Mitigation: Review or delete ~/china/memory.md if persistent local travel memory is not desired.

Risk: Entry rules, permit requirements, transport conditions, weather, and crowd patterns can change after the skill content was authored.

Mitigation: Confirm current official requirements and time-sensitive logistics before booking non-refundable travel.
