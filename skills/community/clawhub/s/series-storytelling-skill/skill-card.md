## Description:

Helps agents turn a premise, video notes, a one-episode idea, or a long novel into a multi-line long-form series or season outline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hshgogogo](https://clawhub.ai/user/hshgogogo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and creative agents use this skill to plan Chinese-language long-form fiction or screen series from sparse ideas, novels, video notes, or single-episode concepts. It supports loglines, story-line maps, character arcs, episode tables, season closure, and sequel hooks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users working outside Chinese-language storytelling may receive templates or terminology that need adaptation.

Mitigation: Confirm the agent can translate or adapt the templates before relying on outputs for another language or format.

Risk: Adaptations of existing novels or examples may unintentionally track source material too closely.

Mitigation: Use the skill for structural planning, review outputs for originality, and avoid verbatim reuse of protected source text.
