## Description:

Programmatic SEO planning and analysis for pages generated at scale from data sources, covering template engines, URL patterns, internal linking automation, thin content safeguards, and index bloat prevention.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External SEO practitioners, content strategists, and developers use this skill to plan or audit programmatic SEO pages generated from structured data sources, including templates, URL strategy, internal linking, quality gates, canonicalization, sitemaps, and index bloat controls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Publishing low-value generated pages at scale can create thin-content, scaled-content, or index-bloat risk.

Mitigation: Use the skill's quality gates, human review sampling, staged rollout, and explicit approval checkpoints before publishing large programmatic page sets.

Risk: The skill may analyze user-provided site or data-source information.

Mitigation: Provide only site and dataset details appropriate for agent review, and confirm recommendations before applying them to production SEO workflows.
