## Description:

Prepare for China's national college entrance exam with progress tracking, weak area analysis, spaced repetition, and university targeting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External students preparing for the Gaokao use this skill to plan study sessions, track progress, review weak areas, analyze mock exams, and target universities. Parents and tutors may use it for progress monitoring and support planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can store sensitive study records, including scores, weak areas, schedule behavior, preferences, and stress or burnout signals.

Mitigation: Use it only with informed user consent, avoid unnecessary personal identifiers, and let users edit or delete local records.

Risk: Tutor or parent reports may involve minors and expose sensitive educational or wellbeing information.

Mitigation: Limit reports to necessary progress information and handle parent or tutor sharing with explicit consent and care.

Risk: University admissions guidance and score cutoffs can vary by province and year.

Mitigation: Check current official admissions sources and provincial cutoff tables before making application decisions.

Risk: Stress or burnout signals may indicate a need for support beyond study planning.

Mitigation: Use wellbeing guidance as supportive triage and seek qualified help for persistent insomnia, appetite loss, self-harm statements, or withdrawal.
