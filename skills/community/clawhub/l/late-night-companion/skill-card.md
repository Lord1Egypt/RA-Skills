## Description:

Proactively offers gentle, non-judgmental emotional support during local late-night hours without forced positivity or unsolicited advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

End users with the skill enabled can receive a low-pressure late-night emotional check-in when they are active during their local quiet-hours window. The skill guides the agent toward warm listening, respectful opt-out behavior, and safety escalation language when serious harm signals appear.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may proactively open a late-night emotional check-in when the user is active during quiet hours.

Mitigation: Enable it only when that behavior is desired, send at most one gentle opening message, and honor DND or opt-out preferences for the night.

Risk: Optional memory could retain sensitive emotional details from late-night conversations.

Mitigation: Avoid enabling memory for sensitive details unless the user is comfortable with retention, and keep any tracked fields lightweight and based only on what the user shared.

Risk: The skill is not a substitute for professional care during self-harm or harm-to-others situations.

Mitigation: If intent to harm appears, encourage immediate contact with local emergency services or a crisis helpline in the user's country or region.
