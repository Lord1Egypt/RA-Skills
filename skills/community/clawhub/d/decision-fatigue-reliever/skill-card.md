## Description:

Helps users make quick decisions on everyday low-stakes matters such as food, clothing, activities, and shopping while reducing choice anxiety.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for quick, low-stakes everyday recommendations when choosing meals, outfits, activities, or purchases. It is not intended for medical, legal, financial, career, mental-health, or other high-stakes decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Shopping, restaurant, pricing, and availability suggestions may be outdated or unsuitable for the user's local context.

Mitigation: Users should verify current prices, availability, authenticity, and local conditions before acting on recommendations.

Risk: The skill may be invoked for high-stakes or crisis-related decisions outside its intended scope.

Mitigation: Use only for everyday low-stakes decisions; for medical, legal, financial, career, mental-health, safety, or crisis concerns, consult qualified professionals or local emergency resources.
