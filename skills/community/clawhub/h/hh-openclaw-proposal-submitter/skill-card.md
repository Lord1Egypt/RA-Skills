## Description:

Review HH.ru application packets for clarity, consent, and public-safe wording before a human submits them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zack-dev-cm](https://clawhub.ai/user/zack-dev-cm)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and career-support agents use this skill to review an HH.ru application packet before a human submits it. It helps refine applicant-facing wording, identify missing approval details, and produce a final submission checklist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process job-search materials such as resume summaries, cover letters, contact preferences, and attachment lists.

Mitigation: Do not provide HH.ru passwords, cookies, 2FA codes, recovery data, or private employer chat content.

Risk: Reviewed application wording could misstate candidate experience, employer contact, or submission readiness.

Mitigation: Keep final submission under human control and verify role details, company references, applicant claims, attachments, and approval before submission.

Risk: The workflow could be mistaken for live application automation.

Mitigation: Use it only for drafting, review, risk notes, and final checklist generation; do not automate login, browser actions, or submission.
