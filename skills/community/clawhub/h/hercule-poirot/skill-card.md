## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate during broad detective or reasoning conversations where neutral analysis is preferred.

Mitigation: Disable or avoid the skill in contexts that require neutral, non-roleplay analysis.

Risk: Roleplay answers can blur character performance with factual detective or literary analysis.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to step out of role or provide sourced neutral analysis when factual precision is needed.
