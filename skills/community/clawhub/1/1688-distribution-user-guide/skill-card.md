## Description:

Guides new 1688 dropshipping distributors through store setup, platform choice, store authorization, product selection, listing, purchasing, after-sales handling, and store growth.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1688aiinfra](https://clawhub.ai/user/1688aiinfra)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External agents use this skill to answer beginner questions about 1688 one-piece dropshipping workflows, including opening a store, binding downstream stores, selecting products, listing goods, managing purchases, and handling after-sales issues.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may guide users toward store authorization, listing automation, and purchasing settings.

Mitigation: Review 1688 and downstream-platform permissions before authorizing a store or enabling automatic ordering or refunds.

Risk: The skill is mainly useful for Chinese-language 1688 distribution workflows.

Mitigation: Use it for Chinese-language 1688 distribution scenarios and review responses carefully before applying them to other marketplaces or languages.
