## Description:

Use when traveling with prescription medications (especially controlled substances, insulin/GLP-1 cold chain, or daily-schedule meds across time zones). Screens a med list against TSA/airport security rules, destination bans, cold-chain storage physics, and supply math; builds timezone-shift dose schedules and a doctor letter.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Travelers carrying prescription medications use this skill to screen a trip against its medication travel rules, plan timing across time zones without changing prescribed doses, and prepare packing guidance and a doctor-letter request.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The offline travel rules may not reflect current destination or airport requirements.

Mitigation: Use the screening as a preliminary checklist and confirm destination import requirements with the relevant embassy and current airport rules with the carrier or authorities.

Risk: General timing or storage guidance may not fit a particular prescription or product.

Mitigation: Keep prescribed doses unchanged and confirm medication-specific timing and storage with a doctor or pharmacist, especially for narrow-window and cold-chain medicines.
