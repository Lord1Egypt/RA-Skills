## Description:

Interprets dreams through analytical psychology by structuring dream narratives, amplifying symbols, identifying archetypal patterns, and offering optional active-imagination prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to turn dream reports into structured Jungian interpretations, including four-phase narrative analysis, symbolic amplification, archetypal pattern detection, and cautious reflective exercises.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate too broadly and route casual dream mentions into deep psychological analysis.

Mitigation: Use explicit opt-in before deeper Jungian analysis or active-imagination exercises.

Risk: Active imagination and shadow work can be psychologically intense and may be inappropriate during acute distress.

Mitigation: Avoid these exercises during acute emotional crisis and present the skill as reflective and educational, not a substitute for professional mental-health support.

Risk: Dream interpretations can be speculative or misleading if presented as definitive meaning.

Mitigation: Frame interpretations as hypotheses and ask for the dreamer's personal associations before offering archetypal parallels.
