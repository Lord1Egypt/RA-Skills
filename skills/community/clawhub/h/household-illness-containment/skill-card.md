## Description:

When norovirus/flu/COVID/strep/RSV/pinkeye hits one member, stop it becoming the whole household — dated, illness-specific containment protocol with exposure-watch windows for everyone else, pathogen-matched disinfection (norovirus survives quats and hand sanitizer: bleach dilutions named in cups-per-gallon, contact times included), shared-bathroom rotation rules, laundry/dish handling, return-to-school/work and food-handler clocks, high-risk member routing (infant/pregnant/65+/immunocompromised), illness-specific red flags, and the one-store-run containment kit.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Households use this skill to build a dated containment plan after a household member develops a common contagious illness. It summarizes exposure-watch periods, hygiene and cleaning routines, return-to-school or work timing, high-risk member routing, and red flags for seeking care.
