## Use Case:

External users, developers, and analysts use this skill to plan and communicate decision-ready analysis across SQL, spreadsheets, notebooks, dashboards, exports, and ad hoc tables. It supports KPI debugging, experiment readouts, funnel and cohort analysis, anomaly reviews, metric quality checks, chart selection, and stakeholder briefs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User-directed SQL queries, scripts, spreadsheet automations, or related skills may access or transform sensitive data.

Mitigation: Review generated queries, scripts, and automations before execution, confirm the intended data scope, and apply normal data access controls.

Risk: Analytical outputs can become misleading when metric definitions, baselines, sample sizes, or confounders are unclear.

Mitigation: Use metric contracts, uncertainty ranges, caveats, and robustness checks before presenting conclusions or recommending action.
