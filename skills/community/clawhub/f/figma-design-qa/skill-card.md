## Description:

Runs a pre-handoff QA checklist on Figma designs and produces a structured report covering file hygiene, component usage, accessibility, and handoff readiness with pass/fail status per item.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers, PMs, and developers use this skill to check whether a Figma feature or screen is ready for engineering handoff and to identify blocking, pre-handoff, or follow-up fixes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Figma links or design details may expose product or customer information if shared unintentionally.

Mitigation: Share only design links and details intended for review, and follow the workspace's normal access and confidentiality controls.

Risk: Checklist-based QA output can miss issues that require human design, accessibility, or engineering judgment.

Mitigation: Use the report as a handoff aid and have the responsible designer, PM, or engineer review the findings before approval.
