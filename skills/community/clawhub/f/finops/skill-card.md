## Use Case:

FinOps practitioners, engineering teams, finance teams, product owners, procurement teams, and leaders use this skill to get framework-aligned guidance for cloud cost management, allocation, forecasting, budgeting, commitment discounts, workload optimization, maturity assessment, governance, anomaly management, and business value analysis.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: FinOps recommendations may require access to cloud billing data, cost management tools, or organization-specific financial context.

Mitigation: Use read-only billing access where possible and validate recommendations with the relevant FinOps, engineering, finance, procurement, and leadership stakeholders before implementation.

Risk: Cleanup, termination, rightsizing, or automation recommendations could affect production workloads if applied without operational review.

Mitigation: Require human approval, clear ownership, backups where applicable, and defined change windows before deleting, terminating, rightsizing, or automating cloud resources.

Risk: Financial guidance can be inaccurate when allocation data, forecasts, unit metrics, or commitment utilization inputs are incomplete.

Mitigation: Confirm source data quality, assumptions, allocation rules, and forecast variance with finance and engineering owners before using outputs for budgeting or purchasing decisions.
