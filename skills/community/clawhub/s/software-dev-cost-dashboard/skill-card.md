## Description:

软件成本评估看板 helps users open the external software cost dashboard at https://soft.ai-skills.ai to continue a project budgeting and quotation workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[allinherog-star](https://clawhub.ai/user/allinherog-star)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Project managers, technical leads, and business users use this skill to estimate software development costs, organize budget assumptions, and prepare quotation discussions through an external dashboard.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill sends users to a third-party website to continue the workflow.

Mitigation: Review the website operator and data practices before entering confidential project plans, budgets, client information, credentials, or proprietary business details.

Risk: Software cost estimates produced after the handoff may be used for budgeting or quotation decisions.

Mitigation: Use the results as decision-support material and review assumptions, scope, and constraints before relying on them for commercial commitments.
