This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to apply Anthropic brand styling, including colors and typography, to generated artifacts or presentation-style materials.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Brand styling may be outdated or unsupported for public Anthropic-branded materials.

Mitigation: Verify colors and typography against an authoritative Anthropic brand source before relying on the skill for public-facing materials.

Risk: Broad activation wording may cause visual styling to be applied when brand-specific styling was not intended.

Mitigation: Narrow trigger wording where possible and review generated styling before adopting it.

Risk: The release has conflicting license signals between server metadata and the bundled license file.

Mitigation: Confirm the authoritative license before redistribution or publication.
