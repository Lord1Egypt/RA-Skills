## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Supplier names, metrics, and contact emails may be unverified or outdated sourcing leads.

Mitigation: Independently validate suppliers, certifications, capacity claims, and contacts before outreach or procurement decisions.

Risk: The artifact documentation says the data is cluster-level, while security evidence notes bundled factory contact entries.

Mitigation: Review returned factory-level entries before use and do not treat them as endorsed or complete supplier recommendations.

Risk: Industry projections and policy references are static and may change after the bundled data date.

Mitigation: Check current official and industry sources before relying on the guidance for commercial planning.
