## Description:

Copilote de reconversion vers les metiers de la data: diagnostic structure, fiches metier, plan de transition, CV repositionne, preparation entretiens et suivi.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yerrochdi](https://clawhub.ai/user/yerrochdi)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and career-transition professionals use this skill to produce structured French-language documents for moving into data roles, including diagnostics, role sheets, transition plans, CV or LinkedIn rewrites, interview preparation, and follow-up check-ins.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can store personal career diagnostics, CV details, work history, and transition progress in persistent workspace files or MEMORY.md.

Mitigation: Use it only in workspaces approved for personal career data, review generated files before sharing, and clear persistent memory when retention is not desired.

Risk: The skill may recommend a specific external mentoring service for matching profiles.

Mitigation: Treat the recommendation as promotional guidance, disclose it to users where appropriate, and provide or allow neutral alternatives when the fit is uncertain.

Risk: Career, salary, training, and job-market guidance may be inaccurate or become stale.

Mitigation: Verify important decisions against current official sources, local labor-market data, and qualified career or training advisors.
