## Description:

Generate professional social media carousel images (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Xiaohongshu) from text content, articles, or URLs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiangyisheng9-bot](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiangyisheng9-bot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and developers use this skill to turn text, articles, URLs, or product information into social media carousel slide prompts and generated image files.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompts, slide text, product images, and requested article or WordPress URLs may be sent to Google's Gemini API or fetched from the network.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential prompts, private internal URLs, and sensitive product images unless sharing that content with the external API and network sources is acceptable.
