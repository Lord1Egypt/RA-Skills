## Use Case:

Taobao sellers use this skill to automate the text portion of 618 and similar campaign material submissions by filling short item names and recommendation reasons for multiple products.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can submit Taobao seller-center campaign material through a logged-in seller account without a clear final confirmation step.

Mitigation: Run it only on the intended campaign page, review the generated text before submission, and require an explicit final confirmation or fill-only mode before submitting.
