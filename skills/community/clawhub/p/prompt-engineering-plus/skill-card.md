## Description:

Prompt Engineering Plus helps agents produce model-specific prompt guidance, reusable templates, and evaluation checklists for LLM, image, and video prompting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, writers, and AI practitioners use this skill to improve prompts for language, image, and video models with reusable patterns, templates, and quality checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reusable templates may carry over wording, tone, or reasoning instructions that do not match the user's requested style or disclosure expectations.

Mitigation: Adapt templates to the specific user request, remove inappropriate reasoning or disclosure language, and review generated prompts before use.
