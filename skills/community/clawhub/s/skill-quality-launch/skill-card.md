## Description:

Helps quality leaders plan new production line or factory launches by structuring quality goals, APQP deliverables, quality gates, ramp-up controls, KPIs, and Markdown/HTML launch plan outputs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Quality managers and launch teams use this skill before production start to collect project, quality target, deliverable, quality gate, ramp-up, and KPI information and turn it into a structured launch quality plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill refers to scripts/build_report.py for report generation, but server security evidence says that helper is not present in the package.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a planning template unless the report helper is provided or verified before relying on generated Markdown or HTML files.
