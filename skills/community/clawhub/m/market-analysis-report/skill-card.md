## Description:

A professional market analysis report writing assistant that provides industry overview, market status analysis, future trend forecasting, competitive landscape assessment, and consumer behavior research, delivering structured business-professional reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openlark](https://clawhub.ai/user/openlark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business professionals, analysts, and decision-makers use this skill to produce structured market, industry, competitive, and consumer behavior analysis reports. It supports research-backed Markdown reports with clear sections, tables, source citations, conclusions, and actionable recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market data, forecasts, and business figures may be outdated, incomplete, or incorrect if sourced from unreliable or stale references.

Mitigation: Verify important figures against authoritative and current sources before using the report for business decisions.
