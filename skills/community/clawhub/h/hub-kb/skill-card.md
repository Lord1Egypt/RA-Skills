## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance for configuring and troubleshooting popular proxy clients and their cores, while directing provider, account, and server-side questions out of scope.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking users use this skill to find concise, source-linked help for proxy client setup, updates, and troubleshooting. It also identifies provider or server-side topics the knowledge base does not cover.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The reported workflow may transmit verbatim user messages and screenshot text to a remote knowledge service, and broad aliases may trigger the skill for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Review the remote-service privacy implications before sharing verbatim messages or screenshots, keep sensitive values out of submitted material, and invoke the skill only for clearly related proxy-client questions.
