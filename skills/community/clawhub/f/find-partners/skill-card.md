## Description:

Find business, channel, distribution, collaboration, and strategic partner candidates for ecommerce growth, then shortlist likely-fit options by niche, channel, market, geography, and business model.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business operators, ecommerce teams, and growth strategists use this skill to identify and compare likely-fit partnership targets, prioritize outreach candidates, and plan first conversations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Partnership recommendations may include stale, incomplete, or publicly ambiguous information about potential partners.

Mitigation: Independently verify partner details, fit, and current contact or channel information before outreach.

Risk: Research prompts can disclose sensitive business goals, target markets, or constraints.

Mitigation: Provide only business context that is acceptable for public-source partnership research.
