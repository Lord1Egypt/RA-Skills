## Description:

Structure portfolio and case-study pages around positioning, selected work, the user's role, actions, outcomes, and proof.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, job seekers, consultants, founders, and portfolio authors use this skill to turn project details into a focused portfolio page or case study. It helps present context, individual contribution, actions, outcomes, and supporting proof without exposing confidential details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Portfolio inputs may include confidential client, employer, or NDA-covered details.

Mitigation: Anonymize sensitive names, facts, and metrics before giving them to the agent; use ranges such as approximate percentage lift where exact figures are confidential.

Risk: A case study can mislead readers if it blurs the user's contribution with the team's work or omits outcomes.

Mitigation: State the user's specific role separately from team activity, include measurable or qualitative outcomes, and keep only strong projects with supporting proof.
