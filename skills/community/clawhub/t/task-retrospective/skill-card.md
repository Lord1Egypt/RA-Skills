## Description:

Conduct structured self-evaluations after tasks to analyze efficiency, accuracy, approach quality, and extract patterns for improved future performance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[charlie-morrison](https://clawhub.ai/user/charlie-morrison)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and AI agents use this skill after complex tasks, debugging sessions, or multi-step workflows to produce a structured retrospective with timelines, lessons learned, reusable patterns, and improvement actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated retrospectives may contain project details or lessons learned from prior work.

Mitigation: Decide where retrospectives are stored and who can access them before recording or sharing the output.
