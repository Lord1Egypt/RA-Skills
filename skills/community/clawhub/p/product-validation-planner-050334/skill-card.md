## Description:

Helps solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, analysis, or implementation support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users such as solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to evaluate whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It helps convert uncertain product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, reusable workflows, checklists, and decision support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may invoke the skill for general product or business requests where its validation workflow is not the best fit.

Mitigation: Review whether the user is asking for product validation, prototype planning, positioning, or related decision support before relying on the skill's workflow.

Risk: The skill can produce planning guidance from incomplete assumptions, which may lead to overconfident product decisions.

Mitigation: Keep assumptions visible and validate outputs against real customer evidence, constraints, and success criteria before committing resources.
