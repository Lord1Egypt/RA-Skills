## Description:

Generates customizable month-end and quarter-end close checklists for accounting firms and finance teams, including task ownership, status tracking, exception flags, reconciliations, adjusting entries, financial statement review, and close summary reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samledger67-dotcom](https://clawhub.ai/user/samledger67-dotcom)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External accounting teams, controllers, CFOs, and client-service accountants use this skill to run structured monthly or quarterly closes, assign close work, document reconciliations and exceptions, and produce a close summary package.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial-close guidance could be applied to the wrong client, entity, accounting period, or reviewer context.

Mitigation: Confirm the client or entity, period, and reviewer before using the checklist or distributing any close package.

Risk: Accounting actions such as posting entries, locking periods, or distributing financial reports can affect live books when paired with integrations.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before posting entries, locking accounting periods, or distributing financial reports through connected tools.
