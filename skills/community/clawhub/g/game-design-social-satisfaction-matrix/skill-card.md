## Description:

Evaluate a game's social features and multiplayer elements by their potential to create social satisfaction versus social dissatisfaction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and community-systems reviewers use this skill to audit multiplayer and social features, classify them as basic, critical, booster, or secondary, and prioritize changes that increase social satisfaction while reducing avoidable dissatisfaction.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for a range of multiplayer or community-system design questions.

Mitigation: Use it when a structured game-design analysis of social satisfaction, dissatisfaction risk, and improvement priority is appropriate.

Risk: Matrix classifications can guide product priorities and may be misleading if applied without game, audience, and safety context.

Mitigation: Review the generated rationale, risk view, and recommendations against the specific game mode, audience segment, and moderation constraints before acting on them.
