## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated outreach may be used with unlawfully obtained prospect data or in ways that violate anti-spam, privacy, unsubscribe, or opt-out requirements.

Mitigation: Use only lawfully obtained prospect data, avoid purchased or scraped lists, honor unsubscribe and opt-out requests, and check applicable anti-spam and privacy rules before sending.

Risk: Generated emails may contain inaccurate personalization, unsupported claims, or copy that is inappropriate for a recipient or campaign.

Mitigation: Review and edit every generated email before sending, including claims, personalization fields, compliance language, and links.

Risk: Poor sending practices can harm deliverability or increase spam complaints.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's deliverability guidance: use plain text, limit links and images, avoid spam-trigger wording, monitor bounces, and ramp sending volume gradually.
