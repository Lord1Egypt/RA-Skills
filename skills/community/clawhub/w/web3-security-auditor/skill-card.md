## Description:

Automated smart contract security auditing skill for identifying vulnerabilities in Solidity and Rust programs across Ethereum, Solana, and BSC and producing audit findings with severity ratings and remediation recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, DeFi teams, blockchain investors, and security reviewers use this skill to triage smart contract risks before deployment, investment, or deeper professional audit review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automated audit reports may miss vulnerabilities or present incomplete findings.

Mitigation: Use the skill for triage and require qualified human security review before deployment, investment, or other high-impact decisions.

Risk: The artifact is a short descriptive auditing prompt and does not provide detailed auditing procedures or tools.

Mitigation: Confirm the generated analysis against established smart contract audit practices and project-specific threat models.
