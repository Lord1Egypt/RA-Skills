## Description:

Manages vendor insurance compliance through collection, validation, scoring, and remediation of certificates of insurance with alerts and action steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[flynndavid](https://clawhub.ai/user/flynndavid)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Operations, procurement, vendor management, and compliance teams use this skill to build a COI registry, validate vendor insurance documents, score compliance, track expirations, and coordinate remediation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can influence vendor onboarding, purchase-order holds, work stoppage, and remediation decisions.

Mitigation: Tailor coverage thresholds and escalation decisions with legal, risk, procurement, or insurance stakeholders before operational use.

Risk: Coverage requirements and insurance practices may vary by jurisdiction, contract, vendor type, and risk tolerance.

Mitigation: Review the template against organization-specific policy, contract terms, and applicable insurance requirements before adopting it.
