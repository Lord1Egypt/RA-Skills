## Description:

Develop and screen engineering design-around concepts against two or more potentially relevant patent rights using an application-driven eight-step FTO workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and IP teams use this skill to structure a multi-patent freedom-to-operate pre-screen, generate engineering design-around concepts, cross-screen candidates against material claims, and prepare an attorney-review package. It does not provide legal clearance or a formal legal opinion.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may collect sensitive product, process, supplier, and patent information.

Mitigation: Use only authorized connectors and apply confidentiality handling, redaction, and access controls before sharing inputs or outputs.

Risk: A pre-screening output could be mistaken for legal clearance.

Mitigation: Route claim construction, current claim status, enforceability, equivalents, prosecution-history effects, and final freedom-to-operate decisions to qualified counsel in each relevant jurisdiction.

Risk: Live patent-search results may be incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Treat search results as evidence requiring verification against authoritative patent registers, current claims, legal status, and dated source records.
