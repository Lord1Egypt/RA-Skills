## Description:

Calculates and benchmarks fully loaded customer acquisition cost by channel and cohort, identifies savings opportunities, and builds data-driven CAC reduction plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Business operators, founders, growth teams, and finance teams use this skill to calculate true CAC, compare acquisition channels and cohorts, assess LTV:CAC and payback health, and prioritize CAC reduction actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Benchmark figures may not match a user's current industry, geography, business model, or reporting period.

Mitigation: Treat benchmarks as general business guidance and verify them against company data and trusted industry sources before making budget decisions.

Risk: CAC analysis can involve sensitive revenue, payroll, customer, and acquisition-spend data.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing confidential data unless the user is comfortable giving that information to the agent and its runtime environment.
