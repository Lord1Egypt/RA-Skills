## Description:

Create a comprehensive lead scoring model with separate Fit and Engagement scores using HubSpot's new Lead Scoring tool.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing operations and revenue teams use this skill to define HubSpot Fit and Engagement lead scores, calibrate scoring criteria, and verify score behavior before relying on it for sales prioritization or MQL handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Lead score changes can affect workflows, lists, reports, routing, alerts, and MQL handoff rules in a live HubSpot portal.

Mitigation: Review dependent business processes, test or stage changes where possible, document rollback steps, and confirm with the business owner before turning scores on.

Risk: Using the deprecated HubSpot Score property can leave teams with frozen or non-updating scoring behavior.

Mitigation: Use HubSpot's new Lead Scoring tool and review existing automation, lists, and reports for old HubSpot Score references before launch.
