## Description:

Build a low-effort room routine card for sneezing, irritation, or stuffiness that feels worse indoors during allergy season, with daily, weekly, and high-pollen-day actions plus clear medical safety boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn one-room indoor allergy-season discomfort into a practical cleaning, laundry, air, and entry routine. It produces a printable checklist with daily, weekly, and high-pollen-day actions while preserving clear medical safety boundaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake room-routine suggestions for medical advice or use them during dangerous symptoms.

Mitigation: Keep recommendations limited to low-risk room habits, avoid diagnosis and medication guidance, and direct users to urgent local care for breathing trouble, swelling, chest tightness, severe reactions, or symptoms that feel dangerous.
