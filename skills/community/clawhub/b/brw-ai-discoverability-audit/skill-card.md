## Description:

Audit how a brand appears in AI-powered search across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[brianrwagner](https://clawhub.ai/user/brianrwagner)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users, marketers, consultants, and brand operators use this skill to audit AI search visibility, document recognition and citation gaps, compare competitors, and produce a prioritized action plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audit findings may be incomplete or inconsistent because AI platform answers can vary by model, date, account, and browsing availability.

Mitigation: Record platform, date, account context, and browsing mode for each query, and review generated findings before using them for business decisions.

Risk: The skill may surface the author's strategy-call link in audit-style responses.

Mitigation: Review generated reports for promotional links and remove or replace them when they are not appropriate for the audience.
