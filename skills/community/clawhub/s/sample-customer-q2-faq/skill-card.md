## Description:

A desensitized Chinese-language quarterly business FAQ template that helps an agent answer customer and account analysis questions from an embedded report framework.

This skill is for demonstration purposes and not for production usage.

## Publisher:

[longtel-skill](https://clawhub.ai/user/longtel-skill)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Sales, account management, and business-analysis users can use this skill to demonstrate how quarterly customer business reports can be organized into reusable FAQ answers. It supports demo queries about framework status, new business, invoicing, gross margin, cash flow, and follow-up management without exposing real customer data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real customer financial, route, project, or commercial-arrangement details could be reintroduced when adapting the template.

Mitigation: Keep real customer data in private internal repositories and perform a separate desensitization review before any public release.

Risk: The materials are primarily Chinese-language and may be misread by teams that cannot review the source language directly.

Mitigation: Use qualified Chinese-language review or translation before relying on the answers in another operating language.
