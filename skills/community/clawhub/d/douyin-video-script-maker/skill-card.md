## Description:

Creates a filmable Douyin short-video script from a topic or product brief, with hook options, spoken lines, shot beats, subtitle cues, title ideas, hashtags, a comment prompt, and an optional matching vertical cover.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and small-business teams use the skill to turn a supplied topic or product brief into a filmable Douyin short, with a matching cover when requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
