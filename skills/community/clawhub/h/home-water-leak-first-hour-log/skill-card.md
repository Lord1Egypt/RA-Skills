## Description:

Create a first-hour action log for home water leaks with shutoff checklist, photo checklist, damage notes, contacts, and cleanup timeline.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External homeowners, tenants, landlords, and property managers use this skill to build a printable first-hour water leak action log for safe documentation, contact tracking, and professional handoff.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the log for plumbing, electrical, appliance, restoration, insurance, or legal advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs focused on documentation and safe handoff, and direct active flooding, electrical hazards, structural danger, contaminated water, or unsafe conditions to emergency help or qualified professionals.

Risk: Incomplete incident details may hide urgent safety hazards during the first hour.

Mitigation: Ask for safety-critical unknowns first, mark unresolved details clearly, and prioritize avoiding standing water near electricity or unstable areas before routine logging.
