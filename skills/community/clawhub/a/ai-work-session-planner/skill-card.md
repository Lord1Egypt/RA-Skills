## Description:

Turns a messy work goal into a focused 60 to 120 minute AI-assisted work session with inputs, checkpoints, and a clear done-state.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and teams use this prompt-only skill to turn fuzzy or overloaded work goals into realistic 60 to 120 minute AI-assisted work sessions with checkpoints, handoff prompts, and a clear done-state.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could include confidential materials, regulated decision details, passwords, secrets, or private credentials while describing a work session.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting passwords, secrets, confidential materials, or regulated decision details unless that context is appropriate to share with the AI session.

Risk: A generated session plan could overstate progress, skip missing inputs, or treat AI-drafted content as final in legal, medical, financial, employment, or safety-critical work.

Mitigation: Flag missing inputs and assumptions, keep AI support limited to drafting or organization, and require human review for facts, approvals, sensitive decisions, and final judgment.
