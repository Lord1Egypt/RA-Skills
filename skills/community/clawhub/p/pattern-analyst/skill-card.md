## Description:

Analyze interactions to identify patterns in what Enzo shares, why he shares it, and how it connects to his goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to track recurring themes in a private user's shared content, connect those themes to stated goals, and surface concise pattern insights during reviews or relevant conversations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can automatically store inferred preferences and behavioral traits in persistent user memory.

Mitigation: Require explicit approval before any USER.md update, clearly label unconfirmed hypotheses, and provide a way to inspect, edit, or delete stored pattern data.

Risk: Private pattern insights could be exposed outside the intended user's context.

Mitigation: Keep pattern analysis private to Enzo and avoid sharing these insights in group chats or with other users.
