## Description:

Check whether an ecommerce offer is commercially viable after discounts, costs, refunds, and traffic economics are included.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce, growth, and merchandising teams use this skill to pressure-test discounts, bundles, upsells, free shipping, and other offers before launch or scale. It clarifies assumptions, models baseline and scenario economics, surfaces break-even pressure, and recommends whether to test, revise, or avoid the offer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Profitability recommendations can be misleading if costs, refund rates, traffic economics, or evaluation thresholds are incomplete or unstated.

Mitigation: Confirm the business assumptions, preferred profitability metric, language, and decision guardrails before relying on generated analysis or scripts.
