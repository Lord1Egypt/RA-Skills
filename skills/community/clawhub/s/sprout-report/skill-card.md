## Description:

发芽报告 converts recordings, transcripts, or user-provided topics into multi-disciplinary analysis reports with core insights, story connections, creative angles, and reflection questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this Chinese-language analysis helper to turn recordings, transcripts, or topic descriptions into structured reports for deeper understanding, writing inspiration, and cross-disciplinary exploration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private, regulated, or third-party recordings may contain sensitive content that should not be transcribed or analyzed without permission.

Mitigation: Use only recordings, transcripts, or topic descriptions that the user is authorized to share and comfortable having analyzed.

Risk: Cross-disciplinary analysis and creative angles can be speculative or incomplete.

Mitigation: Review generated reports before using them for publication, decisions, or advice-sensitive contexts.
