## Description:

This skill helps agents plan and produce AI short dramas, comic dramas, animated comics, marketing videos, localization variants, and GEO/AEO support assets through an AI-HIVE MCP guided production workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and production teams use this skill to turn concepts, novels, products, character images, storyboards, or reference videos into a tracked production plan and deliverables for short dramas, comic dramas, dynamic comics, localized releases, and marketing clips.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may involve uploading source materials to AI-HIVE or related generation tools.

Mitigation: Confirm rights and privacy expectations for source videos, images, voices, music, products, and brand assets before upload.

Risk: The workflow may submit paid generation tasks through AI-HIVE MCP.

Mitigation: Require explicit user approval before any paid generation and record task IDs, pricing snapshots, and result links.

Risk: Default outputs may be Chinese-language because the skill is written for Chinese-language short-drama and marketing workflows.

Mitigation: Ask for the user's preferred output language when the desired language is unclear.

Risk: Marketing, product, or celebrity-style content can overstate rights, endorsements, prices, certifications, or platform relationships.

Mitigation: Keep unsupported claims as pending confirmation and avoid impersonation, false endorsements, and invented product facts.
