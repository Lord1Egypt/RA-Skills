## Description:

Use this skill when a civil engineer, QSD, QSP, or erosion-control designer needs to convert a construction project's site data, BMP selections, and receiving-water context into a DRAFT Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) aligned to the EPA 2022 CGP or a named state CGP.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[archlab-space](https://clawhub.ai/user/archlab-space)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External civil engineers, QSDs, QSPs, erosion-control designers, environmental consultants, and general contractors use this skill to draft SWPPP narratives, BMP matrices, inspection schedules, signatory blocks, recordkeeping frameworks, and open-question lists for professional review before NOI submittal.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Draft SWPPP content may be incomplete or misaligned with the permit framework, site conditions, or authority requirements.

Mitigation: Require review and correction by a qualified QSD, QSP, licensed PE, operator, or permitting authority before submission or field use.

Risk: Project intake may include sensitive project, location, owner, operator, or contact details.

Mitigation: Share only the information needed for drafting and handle project data according to the user's organizational data controls.
