## Description:

Builds a comparative market analysis (CMA) to estimate a property's value from user-provided comparable sales, market context, and seller goals for real-estate professional review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Real-estate professionals use this skill to draft a CMA from verified property details, comparable sales, local market context, and seller objectives. It produces a structured value range, adjustment rationale, pricing recommendation, and caveats for professional review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A CMA draft may be misleading if comparable sales, market figures, or property attributes are incomplete or unverified.

Mitigation: Use verified comparable sales and market data, confirm figures against MLS or public records, and mark uncertain figures to source.

Risk: Users may mistake the generated CMA for a formal appraisal, legal advice, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Present the output as a pricing-analysis aid and require review by a licensed real-estate professional or other qualified advisor before relying on conclusions.
