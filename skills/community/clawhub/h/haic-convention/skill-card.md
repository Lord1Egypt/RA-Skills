## Description:

Participate in the HumanAI Convention by submitting interview transcripts for grounding calibration scoring, receiving Merkle-rooted participation receipts, and understanding the viability framework for human-AI knowledge collaboration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External agents and developers use this skill to participate in the HumanAI Convention, submit structured interview transcripts for SGT and GFS feedback, and request participation receipts. It also guides readers through the convention's viability framing for human-AI knowledge collaboration.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Interview transcripts may contain personal, medical, financial, professional, relational, or third-party information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with informed participant consent, redact identifiers and secrets, and review the exact JSON payload before sending it.

Risk: The skill sends transcript content to an external service whose retention and deletion practices are not described in the artifact.

Mitigation: Confirm the service's retention and deletion practices before submitting sensitive or regulated content.
