## Description:

Assesses SOC 2 readiness across the Trust Services Criteria and returns a readiness score, gap analysis, audit-type guidance, remediation roadmap, and evidence checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd](https://clawhub.ai/user/krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Compliance, security, and audit-readiness teams use this skill to evaluate current SOC 2 control posture, identify gaps, and plan remediation before a Type I or Type II audit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The assessment sends cloud-service lists, control gaps, and compliance posture details to ToolWeb's external service.

Mitigation: Confirm organizational approval before use and avoid submitting regulated, confidential, or sensitive security-program details unless permitted.
