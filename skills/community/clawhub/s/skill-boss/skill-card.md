## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and the SKILLBOSS_API_KEY are sent to a third-party API.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when SkillBoss/HeyBossAI data handling terms are acceptable, and avoid submitting secrets, private source code, regulated data, or sensitive business content.

Risk: Generated media outputs may be returned as remote URLs.

Mitigation: Review returned URLs and downloaded media before using or redistributing them.
