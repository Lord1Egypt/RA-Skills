## Use Case:

External users use this skill to correct grammar, spelling, punctuation, and agreement while preserving the author's meaning, voice, formatting, and regional language choices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Grammar corrections can accidentally alter intended meaning, voice, dialect, or informal style.

Mitigation: Review corrected text before use and leave ambiguous wording unchanged unless clarification is available.

Risk: Automatic language handling may misread multilingual phrasing, regional spelling, code-switching, or creative writing as errors.

Mitigation: Preserve regional variants and intentional style choices, and ask for clarification when a correction could change meaning.
