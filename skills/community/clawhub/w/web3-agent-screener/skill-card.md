## Description:

Web3 AI Agent 项目专业筛选评估工具。输入项目名称或描述，通过11维度专业框架进行深度评估，输出可投资级别评级、核心逻辑、风险提示及综合建议。适用于三年以上价值投资视角的项目筛选。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to screen Web3 AI Agent projects with an 11-dimension investment research framework and produce a concise Chinese-language evaluation report.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce investment ratings and buy-window suggestions that may be wrong or become stale as market conditions change.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research prompts, not financial advice, and verify market data from current authoritative sources before making decisions.
