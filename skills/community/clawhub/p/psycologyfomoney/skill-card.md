## Description:

A Thai-language financial mindset coaching skill inspired by Morgan Housel's The Psychology of Money that helps users reflect on saving, investing, debt, spending, and money-related emotions without judgment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golofu](https://clawhub.ai/user/golofu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for Thai-language conversations about personal financial mindset, habits, confidence, saving, spending, debt, and investing anxiety. It is not intended for specific investment picks, legal or tax advice, accounting, formal financial planning, or detailed financial models.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat mindset coaching as specific investment, legal, tax, accounting, or formal financial planning advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses within personal financial mindset and habit coaching, and direct users to qualified professionals for specific investment products, legal issues, taxes, accounting, or formal planning.

Risk: Non-Thai-speaking users may misunderstand the skill's Thai-language guidance.

Mitigation: Use this skill for Thai-language interactions or provide translation support before relying on its guidance.
