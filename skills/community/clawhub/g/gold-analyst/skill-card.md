## Description:

A Chinese-language gold investment analysis skill that searches current domestic and international gold prices, recent price history, market drivers, and gold ETF data to produce structured trend analysis, buy or hold guidance, ETF recommendations, and risk notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sakuraleanne](https://clawhub.ai/user/sakuraleanne)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to generate Chinese-language gold market reports from current search results, including spot and Au9999 prices, three-month trend context, ETF comparisons, and risk cautions. Its output is informational and should be verified before investment decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market prices, ETF net values, fund sizes, and related details may be stale or incomplete when gathered through search.

Mitigation: Verify all prices and fund details with official market, exchange, or fund-company sources before relying on the report.

Risk: Gold market commentary and buy or hold guidance could be mistaken for personalized financial advice.

Mitigation: Use the output as informational analysis only, preserve risk warnings, and make investment decisions based on independent review and personal risk tolerance.
