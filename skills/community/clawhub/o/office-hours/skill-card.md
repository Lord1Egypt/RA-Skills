## Description:

Office Hours is a Chinese-first product strategy skill that uses YC-style first-principles questioning to validate product ideas, challenge assumptions, compare implementation paths, and produce a design document instead of code.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lawrencepage](https://clawhub.ai/user/lawrencepage)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product builders, founders, and developers use this skill before implementation to pressure-test new product or feature ideas, identify real user demand, challenge assumptions, and choose between multiple design directions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer pre-coding conversations into structured product questioning and design planning when the user expected immediate implementation.

Mitigation: Invoke it intentionally for product planning, or tell the agent when direct coding help is preferred.
