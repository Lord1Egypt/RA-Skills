## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated proposal claims, ROI figures, customer references, or timelines may be inaccurate or unsupported if the user does not supply validated source data.

Mitigation: Review all claims, calculations, examples, references, and delivery commitments against approved business records before sharing externally.

Risk: Drafted SOW terms, payment language, liability language, or follow-up messages may conflict with organizational policy or legal requirements.

Mitigation: Route legal, procurement, pricing, and compliance-sensitive sections through the organization's normal approval process before use.
