## Description:

Prioritize multiple goals like an achievement board by separating must-have achievements, side achievements, missable windows, low-value grinds, and a realistic graduation line.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to prioritize personal goals, learning plans, family tasks, or lightweight project milestones by ranking value, effort, timing, and missable risk. It helps convert a crowded milestone list into a practical route with stop-loss guidance for perfectionism.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the skill as a full progress tracker or automated goal system.

Mitigation: Present outputs as structured planning guidance and make clear that progress tracking remains the user's responsibility.

Risk: Users experiencing completion anxiety may over-prioritize low-value grinds.

Mitigation: Include stop-loss advice, a realistic done-enough threshold, and explicit tradeoff reasoning.
