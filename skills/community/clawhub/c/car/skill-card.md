## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general DIY repair and roadside steps as a substitute for vehicle-specific safety instructions or professional help.

Mitigation: Consult the owner's manual and call roadside assistance or a mechanic when conditions, tools, or experience are uncertain.

Risk: Roadside repair attempts can expose users to traffic, poor visibility, or unsafe work conditions.

Mitigation: Move away from traffic, stay out of active lanes, and prioritize emergency services or roadside assistance over self-repair when safety is uncertain.

Risk: Hybrid and EV high-voltage systems can create serious electrical hazards.

Mitigation: Avoid high-voltage hybrid and EV systems and use qualified service providers for those components.
