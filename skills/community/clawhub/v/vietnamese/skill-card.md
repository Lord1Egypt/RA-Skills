## Use Case:

Writers, translators, and agents use this skill to produce or revise Vietnamese text so it sounds more natural, casual, and native to Vietnamese readers while respecting pronouns, tone marks, particles, and regional consistency.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may make Vietnamese text too casual for formal, business, or official contexts.

Mitigation: Ask for the intended register and review pronouns, particles, and regional wording before using the output in formal settings.
