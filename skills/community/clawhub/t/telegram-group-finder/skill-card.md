## Description:

Find Telegram groups/channels by topic using web search and fetch. Use when you need to locate Telegram communities for specific subjects (e.g., payment channels, niche interests).

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, researchers, and community managers use this skill to find public Telegram groups or channels for a topic, then deduplicate Telegram usernames and t.me links into a concise result list.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search results may surface unverified Telegram communities, including harmful or unlawful communities.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for lawful, public community discovery; treat Telegram links as unverified and avoid requests involving scams, evasion, illegal markets, stolen data, or other harmful communities.
