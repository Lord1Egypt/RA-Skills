## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to save, retrieve, organize, update, and delete structured personal knowledge in an Ensue-backed second brain after user confirmation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may store sensitive notes, credentials, private paths, or confidential details in a remote Ensue-backed knowledge base.

Mitigation: Review proposed entries before confirming saves and avoid storing credentials, tokens, private file paths, confidential business data, or sensitive journal-style material unless explicitly intended.

Risk: Save, update, and delete operations can change remote knowledge entries.

Mitigation: Require user confirmation for writes and deletes, and show the draft or intended target before executing the operation.
