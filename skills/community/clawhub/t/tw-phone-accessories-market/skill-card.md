## Description:

Supports Taiwan and Taobao phone-accessory market research, price comparison, supplier selection, inventory and pricing planning, B2B or group-buy offers, and launch marketing while excluding device bodies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External sellers, sourcing teams, and ecommerce operators use this skill to research Taiwan and Taobao phone accessory launches, compare prices and specifications, evaluate supplier claims, plan inventory and pricing, and draft launch or B2B/group-buy content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; default research scope is Taiwan and Taobao.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market prices, launch status, and availability can change quickly.

Mitigation: Verify source pages on the requested date, record the page date and fetch date, and mark stale or incomplete observations for follow-up.

Risk: Supplier, material, MagSafe, Qi2, thickness, weight, and durability claims may be inaccurate or unsupported.

Mitigation: Treat titles and search terms as leads only; request samples, specifications, test data, certifications, and safety documents before making business decisions.

Risk: Taobao page prices can understate landed cost for Taiwan comparisons.

Mitigation: Separate page price from estimated landed cost and explicitly account for currency conversion, cross-border shipping, consolidation, taxes, platform fees, payment fees, returns, and missing assumptions.

Risk: Battery, wireless charging, and power accessories may carry safety and compliance concerns.

Mitigation: Independently verify rated capacity, input and output specifications, thermal behavior, cell information, Qi2 or MagSafe claims, and safety documentation before sourcing or marketing.
