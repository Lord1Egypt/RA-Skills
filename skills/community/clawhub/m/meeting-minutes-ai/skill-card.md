## Description:

Extract action items, decisions, and key insights from meeting transcripts or recordings, then transform the meeting content into structured, shareable minutes with owners and deadlines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wulaosiji](https://clawhub.ai/user/wulaosiji)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external collaborators, and team operators use this skill to convert meeting transcripts or notes into minutes that capture decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, open questions, risks, and next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting transcripts and notes can contain confidential personnel, customer, product, or financial details.

Mitigation: Use redacted transcripts when appropriate and choose an output path where storing meeting minutes is acceptable.

Risk: Generated minutes may miss context or produce placeholders when transcripts are fragmented or unclear.

Mitigation: Review decisions, owners, deadlines, and open questions before sharing or relying on the minutes.
