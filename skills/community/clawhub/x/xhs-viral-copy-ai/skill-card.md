## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The artifact includes pricing, contact details, and performance claims that may be promotional rather than verified.

Mitigation: Review those claims against the publisher's current offering before relying on them or republishing them.

Risk: Generated marketing copy can overstate product benefits or imply unsupported outcomes.

Mitigation: Human-review generated posts for accuracy, brand fit, platform policy compliance, and required disclosures before publication.
