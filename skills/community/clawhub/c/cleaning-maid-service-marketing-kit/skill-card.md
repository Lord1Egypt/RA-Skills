## Description:

Generates compliance-aware marketing campaigns, service pages, review and referral templates, local SEO content, ads, and outreach for cleaning and maid service businesses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Cleaning and maid service owners, local marketers, and agencies use this skill to draft marketing assets for residential, commercial, move-in/move-out, and vacation rental cleaning services. It helps produce channel-specific ad copy, service pages, review requests, referral materials, local SEO plans, and outreach templates with compliance checks for common marketing claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outreach emails, SMS messages, and referral language may create consent, unsubscribe, disclosure, or local marketing-law issues if used without review.

Mitigation: Review all outreach and referral materials before use; confirm lawful basis, consent, unsubscribe language, physical mailing address, platform rules, and local marketing requirements.

Risk: Claims about certifications, green products, disinfectants, insurance, bonding, licensing, reviews, and guarantees can be misleading if the business inputs are not verified.

Mitigation: Confirm product certifications, EPA registration numbers, insurance and bonding details, license requirements, review counts, and guarantee terms before publishing generated copy.

Risk: Generated marketing content may still need human judgment for factual accuracy and platform policy compliance.

Mitigation: Have a responsible reviewer check final ads, service pages, schema, review requests, and cold outreach against current platform policies and applicable regulations before deployment.
