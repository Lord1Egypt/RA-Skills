## Description:

Options Income Engine describes an options-income assistant intended to generate premium-collection strategies, backtests, and options-market signals for supported equities and ETFs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Options traders and agent users can use this skill as a prompt-level helper for income-focused options strategy ideas, including iron condors, cash-secured puts, covered calls, calendar spreads, and related market signals. Because the artifact contains only a skill description, outputs should be treated as informational and independently verified before trading decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Options strategy outputs may rely on stale, incomplete, or incorrect market assumptions.

Mitigation: Verify live prices, implied volatility, liquidity, expirations, and position risk with independent market data before acting.

Risk: The artifact contains only a description, so the skill may not perform all claimed analysis or backtesting.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as informational guidance unless its behavior is separately tested in the target agent environment.

Risk: Income-focused options strategies can produce losses that exceed collected premium.

Mitigation: Review max loss, assignment risk, concentration, margin requirements, and account suitability before placing trades.
