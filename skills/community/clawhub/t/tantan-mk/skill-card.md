## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language market intelligence assistant for industry trends, competitor analysis, market sizing, customer profiles, market opportunities, and competitor activity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lanxy32](https://clawhub.ai/user/lanxy32)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and business teams use this skill to request concise market intelligence briefs covering industry trends, competitive landscapes, target customer profiles, opportunity evaluation, risks, and recommended actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The activation wording is broad and may route many market or competitor questions to this skill.

Mitigation: Review routing behavior before deployment and narrow activation criteria if the agent needs more precise skill selection.

Risk: Market intelligence briefs can mix public facts with estimates or inferred conclusions.

Mitigation: Require source notes, data-limitations statements, and explicit fact-versus-inference labels in generated briefs.
