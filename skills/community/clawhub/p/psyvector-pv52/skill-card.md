## Description:

Chinese-language crisis-consultant persona skill for structured crisis stabilization, risk identification, emergency decision framing, stakeholder communication, and crisis planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to activate a Chinese-language crisis-advisory persona that classifies crises, prioritizes immediate actions, maps stakeholder communication, and outlines follow-up plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The persona may activate on common crisis or risk words.

Mitigation: Use it in contexts where crisis-consultant roleplay is intended, and exit the role when ordinary assistant behavior is needed.

Risk: The skill may use web research or available internal-data tools during crisis analysis.

Mitigation: Deploy it only where that tool behavior and data access are acceptable, and review tool permissions before use.

Risk: Crisis guidance can be mistaken for professional legal, financial, compliance, or restructuring advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory structure and involve qualified experts for domain-specific decisions.
