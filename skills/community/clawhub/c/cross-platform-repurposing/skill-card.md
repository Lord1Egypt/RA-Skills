## Description:

Turns source content into platform-native social posts by extracting core ideas, adapting format, hook, length, mechanics, CTA, and register for each target platform, and avoiding copy-paste reposting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, content teams, and social media operators use this skill to turn posts, blogs, videos, podcasts, newsletters, and other source material into distinct platform-native posts. It helps preserve brand voice and core facts while varying each output for the target channel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses brand profile and voice context to draft social content, which may expose or misuse brand-sensitive positioning if installed unintentionally.

Mitigation: Confirm that agents should use brand-profile and voice context for social content drafting before installation or activation.

Risk: Generated platform posts may contain inaccurate, off-brand, or compliance-sensitive claims if source material or guardrails are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review every generated post before scheduling or publication, especially for regulated or sensitive topics.
