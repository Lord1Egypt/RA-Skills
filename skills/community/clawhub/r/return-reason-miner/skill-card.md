## Description:

Analyze product return or refund notes and turn them into a structured reason taxonomy, root-cause view, and fix-priority brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce product, operations, CX, and merchandising teams use this skill to convert messy return or refund notes into a reason taxonomy, root-cause hypotheses, fix priorities, and a cross-functional action plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Heuristic root-cause suggestions could be mistaken for confirmed QA or operational findings.

Mitigation: Treat root causes as hypotheses and require human review before product, vendor, refund, inventory, or policy decisions.

Risk: Incomplete or unrepresentative return notes can skew the reason taxonomy and fix priorities.

Mitigation: Use representative return evidence and validate recurring patterns with operational review before prioritizing remediation.
