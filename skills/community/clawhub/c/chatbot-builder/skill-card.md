## Description:

Chatbot Builder helps agents create, train, and deploy AI chatbots using user-provided data across channels such as web, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, and Telegram.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and teams use this skill to plan and run chatbot creation workflows, including training on documents or websites and deploying the resulting assistant to customer or team communication channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Training data may contain confidential, sensitive, or unauthorized content.

Mitigation: Review and minimize documents or websites before use, and exclude material that should not be exposed to the chatbot or its downstream users.

Risk: Public web widgets and team messaging deployments can expose chatbot responses to unintended audiences if access controls are misconfigured.

Mitigation: Verify access controls, channel permissions, and deployment settings before releasing the chatbot to web, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, or Telegram channels.
