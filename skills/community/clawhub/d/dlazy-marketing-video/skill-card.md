## Description:

Creates marketing or promotional videos from a product, brand, or brief for social media or campaigns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External marketing and ecommerce teams can turn product information, listings, or a creative brief into promotional videos for social campaigns. The workflow supports multilingual voiceover and an optional virtual host.
