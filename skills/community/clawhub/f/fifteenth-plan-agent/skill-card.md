## Description:

Fifteenth Plan Agent helps collect, organize, compare, and answer questions about China's 12th through 15th Five-Year Plan documents and related economic, administrative, and livelihood data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Policy analysts, researchers, and other external users use this skill to gather Five-Year Plan materials, compare policy periods and regions, query structured economic and administrative indicators, and generate source-cited analysis reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Policy or economic data may be incomplete, stale, or not yet officially released, especially material related to the Fifteenth Five-Year Plan.

Mitigation: Verify cited policy data against official government sources or authoritative media and label unreleased or unconfirmed material clearly.

Risk: Users may mistake planning analysis or economic comparisons for investment advice or policy endorsement.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as informational analysis and preserve the artifact's stated limitation that the skill does not provide investment advice or policy judgments.
