## Description:

AdsTurbo AI Creative Suite helps agents create and refine advertising media by chaining product-link videos, AI images, spokesperson content, and video creation and editing workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[adsturbo](https://clawhub.ai/user/adsturbo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and creative teams can use the suite to create product advertising, e-commerce images, AI spokesperson media, and localized or edited videos. Its workflows can be chained to turn a product page or source media into finished creative assets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploads and external-service workflows may expose private media or product information.

Mitigation: Review each upload and external-service workflow before use; submit private images, videos, portraits, voice recordings, or unpublished product details only when approved for external processing.
