## Description:

Configure X growth skills for an account by defining the account plan, audience, content pillars, cadence, metrics, and voice rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and agent users use this skill when setting up an X account growth workflow or refreshing the account brief that guides related strategy, calendar, audit, and analytics skills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated growth brief can contain sensitive business positioning, audience, metrics, and voice rules.

Mitigation: Store the brief only in an appropriate repo or notes folder and review its contents before sharing or committing it.
