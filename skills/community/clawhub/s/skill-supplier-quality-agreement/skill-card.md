## Description:

面向SQE与法务/商务的供应商质量协议（及客户质量条款）起草与评审技能；覆盖五大核心条款并自动生成条款模板与差距评审文档（txt+md）。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

SQE, legal, and commercial teams use this skill to draft supplier quality agreements and review customer quality requirements. It structures core quality clauses, compares existing terms for gaps, and produces draft TXT and Markdown review documents for qualified business and legal review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process confidential supplier, customer, contract, or legal-review material.

Mitigation: Use only in an approved environment and avoid sharing sensitive agreement text beyond authorized reviewers.

Risk: Generated supplier quality clauses are drafts and are not final legal advice.

Mitigation: Route generated agreements, thresholds, penalties, and customer terms through qualified legal and business review before use.

Risk: The skill may create local TXT and Markdown report files containing agreement content.

Mitigation: Store generated files in approved locations and apply normal document retention and access controls.
