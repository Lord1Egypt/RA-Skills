## Description:

Helps PLSs, title underwriters, lender counsel, and real-estate attorneys draft a 2026 ALTA/NSPS Land Title Survey scope-of-services memo before fieldwork, including Table A selections, Item 15 written-agreement boilerplate, a pre-fieldwork checklist, and a compliance audit for PLS review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External land-survey and real-estate transaction teams use this skill to prepare a draft ALTA/NSPS survey scope memo before fieldwork. It organizes Table A selections, state-mandatory item checks, Item 15 agreement status, deliverables, certification parties, and follow-up questions for licensed PLS review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill handles potentially sensitive property, lender, title, and transaction details.

Mitigation: Use appropriate confidentiality controls for transaction materials and include only the details needed to draft the scope memo.

Risk: Draft scope language could be mistaken for final professional or legal determinations.

Mitigation: Keep the draft posture visible and require a licensed Professional Land Surveyor and appropriate counsel to verify current state-specific requirements and final wording.
