## Description:

Extract actionable product and experience insights from customer feedback across reviews, support tickets, surveys, and social mentions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

## Use Case:

Product, marketing, customer experience, support, and leadership teams use this skill to turn multi-channel customer feedback into categorized themes, aspect-level sentiment, prioritized insights, action roadmaps, and monitoring plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encourages analysis of support tickets, chat transcripts, surveys, reviews, and social mentions that may contain personal, account, order, payment, health, or other sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use only approved feedback sources and sanitized datasets; remove or anonymize sensitive content before analysis and avoid placing raw transcripts or verbatim quotes into unsecured tools or broad reports.

Risk: Voice of Customer outputs can influence product, marketing, customer experience, and operations decisions, so biased or low-quality feedback samples may lead to misleading priorities.

Mitigation: Document source coverage, channel bias, sample size, date range, and data quality notes before using the analysis for roadmap or messaging decisions.
