## Description:

Creates a printable office snack drawer map with zones, refill lines, stale checks, and work-context snack placement without nutrition advice or diet planning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Office workers, shared workspace organizers, and assistants use this skill to turn one snack drawer, shelf, basket, or small cabinet into a printable zone map with refill thresholds and stale-item checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may ask for nutrition, allergy, diet, medical, recall, workplace-policy, or buying advice outside the skill's organizing scope.

Mitigation: Keep responses limited to drawer organization, user-provided snack preferences, ownership labels, refill thresholds, stale checks, and neutral date or removal prompts.

Risk: Shared snack drawers can create ambiguity about ownership or workplace norms.

Mitigation: Recommend clear personal, shared, guest, and restock labels without making legal, policy, or compliance claims.
