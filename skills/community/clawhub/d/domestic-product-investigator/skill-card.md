## Description:

核查国内产品资质真实性，识别三无产品、虚假宣传和证照造假风险。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Consumers, reviewers, and support agents use this skill to investigate domestic product authenticity, production licenses, company registrations, category-specific approvals, and misleading claims using public lookup sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product details, company names, links, and image-derived information may be submitted to external regulatory or business lookup sites.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for information the user is comfortable querying externally, and avoid including unrelated personal details.

Risk: Receipts, product photos, screenshots, and generated evidence packages may contain personal or purchase information.

Mitigation: Redact receipts and images where possible, save only necessary evidence, and delete generated evidence packages when no longer needed.

Risk: Product-authenticity conclusions may affect purchase, safety, complaint, or refund decisions.

Mitigation: Review source links and screenshots before acting, and treat incomplete lookup results as uncertainty rather than proof.
