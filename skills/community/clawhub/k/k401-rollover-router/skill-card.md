## Description:

Old-401(k) decision engine for job changers — leave it, roll to an IRA, roll to the new plan, or convert to Roth. Compounds the fee-drag difference between plan and IRA over your real horizon, prices a Roth conversion at your actual marginal bracket, encodes the indirect-rollover 20%-withholding trap, the 60-day window, the age-55 separator rule vs 59½, and ERISA creditor-protection differences. Ends the "I have three old 401(k)s and no idea" freeze.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People changing jobs or consolidating old 401(k) accounts can compare leaving funds in a former employer plan, rolling them into a new plan or IRA, and considering a Roth conversion using their account details. The report is a decision aid, not tax advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The route comparison depends on user-provided fees, returns, tax brackets, and plan features, while tax and retirement rules can change.

Mitigation: Check the figures against current plan disclosures and IRS guidance, and review consequential rollover or conversion decisions with a qualified tax or financial professional.
