## Description:

Coordinates curator, researcher, reviewer, and scheduler roles to research, draft, edit, and schedule recurring newsletters across distribution channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[t3ratech](https://clawhub.ai/user/t3ratech)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Newsletter operators, content teams, and agent workflows use this bundle to monitor sources, synthesize research, review drafts, and schedule recurring newsletters. It is suited for recurring editorial operations that need coordinated research, review, and approval-gated scheduling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow uses web content, workspace files, and saved research context, so unrelated private files or low-quality sources could affect newsletter drafts.

Mitigation: Limit the bundle to relevant workspace folders and review sources and drafts before publishing.

Risk: The scheduler role can reason about calendar or distribution-channel changes.

Mitigation: Keep event or channel mutations approval-gated and inspect proposed schedule changes before execution.
