## Description:

Helps agents plan organic creator-to-creator collaborations by selecting aligned partners, drafting outreach and posts, coordinating cross-promotion, and setting honest measurement and disclosure boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, social media teams, and agents use this skill to plan mutual creator partnerships, choose collaboration formats, draft outreach, write each side's posts, and define per-source success metrics. It is advisory and planning-oriented; native platform collaboration actions, giveaway mechanics, DMs, agreements, disclosures, and analytics remain outside the skill output.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat advisory collaboration plans as executable automation for native platform features, giveaways, DMs, agreements, or analytics.

Mitigation: Review outputs before use and keep native collaboration posts, giveaway mechanics, outreach conversations, agreements, disclosures, and analytics collection in the appropriate human or platform-native workflow.

Risk: Requests involving payment or free products may be mistaken for unpaid mutual collaborations.

Mitigation: When money or free product changes hands, treat the work as a sponsorship, apply required disclosure, and route to the appropriate influencer or monetization workflow.

Risk: Collaboration recommendations can create misleading claims if engagement or metrics are bought, faked, or invented.

Mitigation: Use genuine partner fit, avoid bought or faked engagement, and report only per-source metrics from native analytics.
