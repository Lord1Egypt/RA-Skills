## Description:

Generate platform-optimized academic content from papers for ResearchGate, LinkedIn, and WeChat.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shaopanguo](https://clawhub.ai/user/shaopanguo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers and academic teams use this skill to turn paper details into platform-specific ResearchGate, LinkedIn, and WeChat posts, plus a reusable video introduction script. It supports research announcements and awareness campaigns by adapting academic material for different audiences.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated promotional posts or scripts may overstate, omit, or mischaracterize research details before publication.

Mitigation: Have the researcher or paper owner review all generated content against the paper before posting.

Risk: Optional video generation can send paper content to configured external providers.

Mitigation: Enable video generation only after approving the provider configuration and confirming the paper content may be shared with those services.
