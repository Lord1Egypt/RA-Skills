## Description:

Helps households, freelancers, family finance organizers, bookkeepers, and small business owners turn messy bank or card transaction exports into clearer budgets, cash-flow plans, categorization workflows, and subscription reviews.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and finance-support practitioners use this skill to clean up transaction exports, categorize spending, identify recurring subscriptions, and produce practical budget or cash-flow decision support. It is suited to local, document-driven workflows such as checklists, templates, lightweight analysis, scripts, and action plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked automatically in broad business or operations contexts that involve sensitive financial records.

Mitigation: Use explicit invocation or narrow triggers to bank export, CSV/OFX import, transaction categorization, subscription audit, or cash-flow cleanup requests.

Risk: Budgeting workflows may expose private bank or card transaction data to an agent.

Mitigation: Review the skill before installation, minimize shared financial data, and prefer local processing or redacted exports when practical.
