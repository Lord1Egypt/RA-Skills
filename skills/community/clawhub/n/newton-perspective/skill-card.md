## Description:

A Chinese-language Newton-style reasoning persona that applies first-principles thinking, inductive reasoning, scientific method, and six Newton-inspired mental models to answer framework, factual, and mixed analysis questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sjj2026](https://clawhub.ai/user/sjj2026)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent builders use this skill to have an assistant reason through problems in a Newton-inspired style, especially first-principles analysis, evidence gathering, scientific-method framing, and concise persona-based guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad scientific-method or first-principles prompts may be answered through a Newton persona even when the user expected a neutral assistant voice.

Mitigation: Use the skill when persona-framed reasoning is desired, and review outputs for unwanted roleplay framing before sharing them in formal contexts.

Risk: Fact-based questions may trigger web research and then produce a compact judgment rather than a full research report.

Mitigation: Ask for sources or a separate evidence summary when factual traceability is important.
