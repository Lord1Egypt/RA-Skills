## Description:

房车（RV/Motorhome/Touring Car）工业设计师技能。专注于旅居车、拖挂房车、商务房车、B型/C型房车的空间布局、外观造型、CMF设计、水电系统、人机舒适、智能控制、品牌体验设计。当用户需要房车设计方案、布局规划、设计规范、竞品分析、CMF材料工艺、人机工程、智能控制功能规划时使用此技能。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to generate RV industrial design documents covering concept direction, spatial layouts, CMF specifications, water and electrical system guidance, ergonomics, HMI planning, competitive analysis, and user journey mapping.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: RV design outputs may include engineering, safety, accessibility, or regulatory specifications that are not independently validated for a real vehicle build.

Mitigation: Review all engineering, safety, accessibility, and regulatory details with qualified domain experts before using the specifications in production vehicle design.
