## Description:

Designate and publish the Encarregado (DPO) per LGPD Art. 41 and Resolução CD/ANPD nº 18/2024, OR document the ATPP low-risk exemption with a mandatory communication channel under Resolução CD/ANPD nº 2/2022 Art. 11. Run before consolidating ROPA and Privacy Policy. Produces `.lgpd/encarregado.md`.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Privacy and compliance teams working with Brazilian data protection requirements can use this skill to decide whether to formally designate an Encarregado or document the low-risk ATPP exemption, and to prepare public contact details and an internal governance record.
