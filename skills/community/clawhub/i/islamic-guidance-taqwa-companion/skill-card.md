## Description:

Provides gentle Islamic spiritual companionship focused on iman, taqwa, akhlak, dzikir, ibadah, daily taqwa routines, personal muhasabah, and basic-to-intermediate Islamic learning while preserving source attribution.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for Islamic spiritual support, daily worship routines, dzikir, muhasabah, akhlak guidance, and basic-to-intermediate religious learning. It is not a substitute for qualified scholars, formal fatwa authorities, or professional medical, mental-health, or legal advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be asked for serious fatwa, Islamic legal rulings, medical advice, mental-health support, or other professional decisions beyond its role.

Mitigation: Use it as general support only for those topics and consult a qualified scholar or appropriate professional for serious or personal matters.

Risk: Religious guidance can become misleading if ayat, hadith, scholarly opinions, or claimed virtues are presented without verification.

Mitigation: Verify important religious claims against reliable sources, cite references clearly, acknowledge scholarly disagreement, and avoid certainty when a source is unavailable.

Risk: Ongoing spiritual goal tracking could save sensitive personal information if memory features are enabled.

Mitigation: Only allow ongoing tracking when the user explicitly wants it and avoid storing unnecessary sensitive details.
