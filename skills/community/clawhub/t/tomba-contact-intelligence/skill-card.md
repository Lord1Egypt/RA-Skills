## Use Case:

External developers and business users use this skill to guide Tomba-backed contact discovery, email verification, lead enrichment, company research, phone lookup, and competitor analysis workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contact intelligence workflows can submit personal or business identifiers such as emails, domains, LinkedIn URLs, phone numbers, or prospecting criteria to Tomba.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when authorized to submit the relevant data, and avoid sensitive, regulated, confidential, or unnecessary personal data unless data-handling obligations allow it.

Risk: Prospecting results may mix verified and inferred contact data.

Mitigation: Verify candidate emails before presenting them as final, and clearly distinguish confirmed data from inferred data in the response.

Risk: Users may ask for unsupported outreach or CRM actions.

Mitigation: Use the skill for Tomba-backed lookup and research workflows only; do not use it to send emails or perform CRM updates.
