## Description:

Make, design and print physical pieces for the user and have them shipped: business cards, flyers, postcards, posters, brochures, menus, invitations, stationery and tickets. Use when the user wants any of these made, designed, printed or ordered, or asks for a physical copy of a design from the conversation. Shows ready-made templates with pictures and opens the one the user picks in an editor with their own details already on it, makes two-sided business cards from a name and contact details, or prints a finished design (HTML or an image) at an exact size, and returns one link where the person pays as a guest. Works through the Designprint MCP tools when they are connected, and over plain HTTPS with curl when they are not, with no account and no API key. Not for on-screen graphics such as social posts, slides or web images. Ships within the United States only.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People who want physical printed materials can use this skill to choose a template, prepare business cards, or submit an approved print-ready design. The agent prepares the design or order link, and the customer reviews it and pays before printing or charging begins.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may send contact details and design assets to Designprint without a required privacy disclosure before transfer.

Mitigation: Tell the user what information and assets will be sent and get their approval before submitting them.

Risk: The security scan was partial because some local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Check the current service documentation and available tools before relying on a particular operation.
