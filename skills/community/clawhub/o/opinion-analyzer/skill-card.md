## Description:

多视角舆情分析助手，用于分析话题、事件、人物或品牌的公开舆情，并生成结构化的多立场分析报告。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[antonia-sz](https://clawhub.ai/user/antonia-sz)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to examine public discussion around a topic, event, person, or brand from multiple perspectives. It helps organize open-source signals into sentiment estimates, controversy points, stakeholder viewpoints, overlooked angles, trend forecasts, and suggested actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public-opinion analysis may rely on incomplete public information and web search results.

Mitigation: Treat findings as a structured estimate and verify important claims against primary or authoritative sources before making decisions.

Risk: Sentiment percentages and trend forecasts may appear more precise than the underlying evidence supports.

Mitigation: Present percentages and forecasts as approximate, cite source coverage where possible, and avoid using them as sole decision criteria.
