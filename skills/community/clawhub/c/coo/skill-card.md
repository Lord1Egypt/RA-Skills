## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Operational recommendations may affect staffing, restructures, vendor commitments, crisis response, or company culture.

Mitigation: Require accountable human review before applying recommendations to major organizational or commercial decisions.

Risk: General COO guidance may not match a specific company's stage, constraints, policies, or current operating data.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against current metrics, internal policies, stakeholder input, and relevant legal or HR review.
