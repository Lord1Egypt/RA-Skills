## Description:

Execute tasks with presence. Not just 'how' to do, but 'who' is doing and 'why' this moment matters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tttael](https://clawhub.ai/user/tttael)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent builders use this skill to add a brief reflective check before ordinary functional tasks, helping the agent respond with more presence and less autopilot tone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can encourage inferred mood or personalization in otherwise literal tasks.

Mitigation: Use it only when a reflective, emotionally aware tone is desired; disable it for strictly literal, task-focused responses.
