## Description:

Use this skill when someone without a legal background needs to review, understand, or assess NDAs, employment agreements, service agreements, and purchase agreements in plain language, with key terms explained, potential problems flagged, and high-risk clauses referred for professional review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and employees can use this skill to walk through contracts they do not fully understand before signing. The skill helps them identify unclear, missing, or potentially risky clauses while reminding them to seek qualified legal advice for high-stakes decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat plain-language contract walkthroughs as legal advice.

Mitigation: The skill should clearly state that it helps explain contracts and identify potential issues, but does not replace a qualified lawyer for legal advice or high-stakes signing decisions.

Risk: Contract meaning can depend on jurisdiction, dispute posture, and facts not present in the contract text.

Mitigation: Use the skill for general understanding and route active disputes, jurisdiction-specific questions, and high-value decisions to professional review.

Risk: Contract text in languages other than English may be misunderstood if the interaction continues in English.

Mitigation: Ask for the user's preferred language or respond in the same language as the contract when the contract is not in English.
