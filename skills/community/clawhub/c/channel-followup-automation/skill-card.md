## Description:

Channel Follow-up Automation helps business users rank relationship channels, generate daily follow-up checklists, and draft stage-specific follow-up messages.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gdp6539](https://clawhub.ai/user/gdp6539)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business development, sales, partnerships, and investor-relations users can use this skill to prioritize contacts by ROI, response speed, and deal probability, then produce daily follow-up reports and reusable outreach templates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Follow-up reports may involve customer, partner, or prospect records.

Mitigation: Decide where contact records will be stored before using the skill with real business data.

Risk: Broad trigger phrases could produce follow-up reports in unrelated conversations.

Mitigation: Use explicit prompts or workspace routing so follow-up automation runs only in the intended business context.
