## Description:

Management Review helps organizations conduct ISO 9001:2015 clause 9.3 management reviews through a three-phase question-and-answer workflow that produces review plans, meeting minutes, reports, and improvement tracking documents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Quality managers, management representatives, auditors, and business teams use this skill to collect management review inputs, structure ISO 9001 review meetings, document decisions, and track follow-up improvements through closure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated management review documents may contain sensitive audit, performance, customer, or corrective-action records.

Mitigation: Use the skill in a deliberate working folder, avoid entering confidential data unless local storage is acceptable, and review generated documents before sharing.

Risk: The skill provides review methods and document templates, but management decisions and approvals remain user responsibilities.

Mitigation: Have the highest management representative or authorized personnel validate conclusions, improvement decisions, signatures, and closure evidence before use.
