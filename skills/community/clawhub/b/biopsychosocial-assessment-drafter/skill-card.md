## Description:

Drafts a biopsychosocial-spiritual assessment from intake information for licensed social worker review, including presenting concerns, clinical domains, strengths, risk screening, provisional DSM-5-TR category prompts, and service recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External social work practitioners use this skill to convert intake notes, forms, or interview summaries into a structured draft BPSS assessment. The draft is intended for licensed social worker review, editing, signature, and any required safety or reporting decisions before clinical, billing, or legal use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill handles sensitive clinical intake content and may receive protected health information.

Mitigation: Use only the minimum necessary information, avoid unnecessary direct identifiers, and preserve HIPAA or workplace privacy controls.

Risk: Draft assessment text could be mistaken for a finalized clinical, billing, or legal document.

Mitigation: Keep licensed social worker review, editing, dating, signature, and professional judgment in place before any clinical, billing, or legal use.

Risk: Safety concerns or mandatory-reporting facts could require immediate professional action.

Mitigation: Escalate positive safety screens and possible mandatory-reporting situations to the licensed clinician before proceeding.

Risk: Provisional DSM-5-TR category prompts could be misread as confirmed diagnoses.

Mitigation: Present diagnostic content only as categories for licensed clinical evaluation and never as an assigned diagnosis.
