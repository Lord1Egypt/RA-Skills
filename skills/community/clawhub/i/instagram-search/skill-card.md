Risk: Instagram research queries and result exports are handled through the third-party Xpoz service.

Mitigation: Use the skill only after your organization has approved Xpoz and its data-handling terms, and avoid confidential, regulated, or sensitive prospecting queries.

Risk: The skill depends on installing and running the mcporter package.

Mitigation: Use a pinned or otherwise trusted mcporter installation before running the provided commands.
