## Description:

Comprehensive evaluation of incident response capabilities with maturity scoring and phase-based assessment framework.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Security teams, CISOs, incident response managers, and consultants use this skill to submit incident response assessment responses and receive maturity scores, phase breakdowns, gaps, and recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Assessment submissions may disclose incident response maturity scores, identifiers, or sensitive organizational context to the provider's external API.

Mitigation: Confirm privacy, retention, and access controls before use, and avoid sending organization names, internal incident details, or other sensitive context unless approved.
