## Description:

Pinterest Pin Design helps agents design and brief Pinterest pin concepts, overlay text, and fresh visual variants while leaving final raster production and publishing to the appropriate human, design, image, and scheduling tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and social media teams use this skill to turn a destination URL, brand profile, and Pinterest SEO keyword into a mobile-first pin brief with 3-5 fresh design variants. It supports concept, composition, overlay, format, and critique decisions, but does not create the final raster image or publish the pin directly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A pin brief can become misleading if the visual promise, board context, or destination page do not match.

Mitigation: Review the final pin against the destination page before publication and reject visuals or overlay claims that overstate what the page delivers.

Risk: Generated imagery, affiliate content, or performance claims can create disclosure and truthfulness issues.

Mitigation: Check AI imagery disclosure, affiliate disclosure, and any save, click, impression, or ranking claims before publishing.

Risk: The skill may reference brand materials, SEO keywords, image tools, and WoopSocial publishing workflows even though it does not execute code or publish by itself.

Mitigation: Keep credentials and publishing authority in the dedicated tools, and treat this skill's output as a brief requiring human or tool review before upload.
