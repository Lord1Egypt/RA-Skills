## Description:

Provides reflective astrology-style readings and emotional support using BaZi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, and Vedic astrology through feminist and modern psychological framing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for self-reflection, culturally framed astrology readings, life guidance, and supportive emotional check-ins. It is intended for reflective or entertainment-oriented support and does not replace medical, legal, or professional psychological advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive birth details or emotional context during conversation.

Mitigation: Install and use only when comfortable sharing birth date, birth time, birthplace, and emotional context with the agent.

Risk: Astrology and emotional-support responses could be mistaken for medical, legal, or professional psychological advice.

Mitigation: Treat responses as reflective or entertainment-oriented support, and seek qualified professional help for medical, legal, or mental health needs.

Risk: Users expressing self-harm or suicide risk require support beyond astrology-style interpretation.

Mitigation: The skill should stop divination-style responses for high-risk signals and provide crisis-support guidance and professional-help recommendations.
