## Description:

Discover keyword opportunities, evaluate metrics and SERPs, and save/tag promising terms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[roamseo](https://clawhub.ai/user/roamseo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO practitioners and content strategists use this skill to discover, evaluate, and prioritize keyword opportunities for a site or topic, then produce a research report to guide content decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Keyword research may update shared project context, including goals, competitors, key pages, and the research log.

Mitigation: Review shared project-context updates after research and keep them grounded in the findings.

**Output Type(s):** [Analysis, Markdown, Guidance]
