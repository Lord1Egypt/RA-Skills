## Description:

Localize product listings across English and Chinese ecommerce channels. Use when adapting Amazon, Shopify, Taobao, Pinduoduo, TK, or independent-site copy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bytesagain-lab](https://clawhub.ai/user/bytesagain-lab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators, localization teams, and developers use this skill to turn product facts into channel-ready listings, localized copy, SEO metadata, compliance checks, and launch bundles for English and Chinese ecommerce channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated SEO metadata can include the publisher brand in customer-facing output.

Mitigation: Review SEO titles and metadata before publishing, and remove or adapt publisher branding when it is not appropriate for the store.

Risk: Draft marketplace claims may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific channel policy.

Mitigation: Validate generated product claims and disclaimers against official marketplace policies before using them in live listings.
