## Description:

Hainan Cost Index helps agents query and compare Hainan Province construction cost indicators and draft planning-level investment estimates from bundled local reference data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ruiyongwang](https://clawhub.ai/user/ruiyongwang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Cost engineers, planners, and project teams use this skill to estimate and compare building and municipal project costs in Hainan using the bundled 2024 case indicators and adjustment coefficients.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Cost estimates may be mistaken for formal budget approval or professional cost consulting.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning estimates, verify project fit and inputs, and use a professional cost consultant for major investment decisions.

Risk: The bundled reference data is specific to Hainan construction-cost scenarios and may not apply to other domains or regions.

Mitigation: Confirm the project is in the Hainan construction-cost domain before relying on the generated estimates or comparisons.
