## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may copy an unsafe Graphviz/Python PATH setup example from the bundled drawing guide.

Mitigation: Use trusted local Python and Graphviz installs, verify the resolved Graphviz executable, prefer a virtual environment, and avoid copying the PATH example as-is.

Risk: Patent outputs may be mistaken for legal advice or final filing guidance.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting and research support, and have qualified patent counsel review disclosures, claims, prior-art conclusions, and infringement analysis before relying on them.
