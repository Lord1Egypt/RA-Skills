## Description:

Structure a blameless incident postmortem from rough notes: timeline, impact, root cause, and follow-ups with owners. Use after any outage, security event, failed automation or costly mistake.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[manteclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/manteclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Incident responders and engineering teams use this skill to turn notes about outages, security events, failed automation, or costly mistakes into a blameless postmortem with an evidence-based timeline, impact statement, root cause, contributing factors, and owned follow-ups.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan reports incomplete inspection because the manifest was unsupported and the Markdown was only partially inspected.

Mitigation: Review the complete skill source and its behavior before installation or use.

Risk: A postmortem based on incomplete or misremembered notes could present an inaccurate timeline or impact figures.

Mitigation: Verify timeline entries against sources, keep gaps and unknowns explicit, and check impact figures before sharing.
