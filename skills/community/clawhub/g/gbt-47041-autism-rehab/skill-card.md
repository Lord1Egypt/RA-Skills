## Description:

Helps agents interpret GB/T 47041-2026 and prepare autism rehabilitation institution compliance checklists, gap analyses, scoring sheets, and evaluation report outlines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and reviewers use this skill to apply GB/T 47041-2026 to autism rehabilitation institution service quality questions, evidence review, scoring, and improvement planning. It supports standards-reference workflows and should not be treated as official legal, medical, licensing, or accreditation advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill's standards-reference outputs could be mistaken for official legal, medical, licensing, accreditation, or government certification advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as assistance only and verify high-stakes conclusions against GB/T 47041-2026 and the relevant competent authority.

Risk: Compliance scoring or gap analysis can be misleading when institution facts or evidence are incomplete.

Mitigation: Mark missing evidence as pending verification and tie findings to specific clauses or scoring items before relying on them.
