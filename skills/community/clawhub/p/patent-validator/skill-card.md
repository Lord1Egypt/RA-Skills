## Description:

Turn your concept analysis into search queries - research the landscape before consulting an attorney. NOT legal advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leegitw](https://clawhub.ai/user/leegitw)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to turn patent-scanner findings or manual concept descriptions into search strategies, evidence maps, differentiation questions, and next steps for self-directed patent-related research planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent-related research strategies could be mistaken for legal advice or conclusions about patentability.

Mitigation: Review outputs before sharing, run the searches independently, and consult a registered patent attorney for intellectual property guidance.

Risk: Broad trigger examples and default branded, disclaimer, or next-step text may produce more output than the user expected.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested scope before use and edit generated reports for audience, branding, and length before distribution.
