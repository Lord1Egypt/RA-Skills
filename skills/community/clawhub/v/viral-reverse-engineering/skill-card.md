## Description:

Reverse-engineers why a social media post or video went viral or overperformed by sourcing observable inputs, identifying the load-bearing mechanism, checking replicability, and translating the principle to the user's niche.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, content strategists, and social media teams use this skill to analyze a single viral or overperforming post from visible inputs such as transcripts, screenshots, top comments, and public stats. The skill turns the teardown into a transferable content principle while avoiding exact copying and unsupported virality claims.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may request brand context, audience details, transcripts, screenshots, comments, and visible performance data.

Mitigation: Provide only information that is appropriate to use for analysis, and avoid private analytics or proprietary creative material unless it is approved for this workflow.

Risk: A bare social media link may not expose the video, frames, transcript, or comments needed for a reliable teardown.

Mitigation: Use the structured intake, transcripts, screenshots, top comments, and public stats; clearly name any layers that cannot be assessed.

Risk: Reverse-engineering a viral post can drift into copying a creator's specific expression or making unsupported repeatability claims.

Mitigation: Extract the underlying mechanism, apply it with original substance for the user's niche, check confounds such as account size and luck, and avoid promising virality.
