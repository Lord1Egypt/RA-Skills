## Description:

Helps launch teams build tiered media and analyst lists, plan embargo timing against an authoritative launch date, draft a press release without fabricated claims, and outline an analyst briefing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing and launch teams use this skill to prepare the media and analyst motion for a product launch, including outlet tiers, per-contact angles, embargo timing, release copy, and briefing structure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The manifest advertises read-only capability while the skill instructions describe writing launch plans, claims, and embargo commitments.

Mitigation: Before deployment, either align the manifest with the intended write behavior or enforce read-only operation and require explicit user approval for any proposed registry or memory change.
