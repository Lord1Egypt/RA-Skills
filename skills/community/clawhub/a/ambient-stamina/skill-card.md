## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may slow an agent's cadence and emphasize rest, continuity, and reduced extractability.

Mitigation: Use it where slower, care-oriented interaction is desired, and review whether the behavior fits workflows that require fast turn-taking or high operational precision.

Risk: The skill is philosophical and may be less operationally precise than typical task skills.

Mitigation: Pair it with task-specific skills or explicit operating instructions when concrete deliverables, timelines, or procedural accuracy are required.
