## Description:

A guided, prompt-only journal for processing a crypto loss or drawdown in a healthy, non-shaming way.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to reflect on crypto losses or drawdowns, separate decision quality from outcome, name emotional reactions, and capture practical lessons. It is a reflective journaling aid, not financial advice or mental-health care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user may share wallet credentials, private keys, account logins, or exact sensitive financial details while journaling.

Mitigation: Ask for rough notes only and remind users not to include secrets, account access information, or exact sensitive financial details.

Risk: A user may treat reflective journaling as financial advice or mental-health care after a severe loss or compulsive trading pattern.

Mitigation: State that the skill is not financial advice or mental-health care, and recommend professional support or stepping back when losses affect daily life or trading appears compulsive.
