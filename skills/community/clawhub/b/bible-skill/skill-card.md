## Description:

Provides Chinese-first Bible study assistance grounded in scripture, academic research, exegesis, theology, history, archaeology, and denominational comparison.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[boboy-j](https://clawhub.ai/user/boboy-j)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and end users can use this skill to help agents answer Bible questions, prepare Bible study notes, sermon outlines, devotional material, and theological comparisons in a Chinese-first style.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Theological responses may be treated as authoritative pastoral advice.

Mitigation: Use outputs as study aids and review them with appropriate pastoral, academic, or denominational authority for consequential decisions.

Risk: Bible interpretation can vary across denominations and scholarly traditions.

Mitigation: Ask for the preferred language, Bible translation, or denominational perspective, and compare major interpretations when a passage is disputed.
