## Description:

Reusable prompt for refining social science academic abstracts to align with peer-reviewed journal requirements including APA 7th edition compliance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, editors, and academic writing support teams use this skill to polish English-language social science manuscript abstracts for journal-style clarity, APA 7th edition alignment, logical flow, formal tone, and reduced redundancy without changing the study's core content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be applied outside its intended English-language social science abstract scope.

Mitigation: Use it for English-language social science abstracts, and adapt or review outputs carefully for other languages, disciplines, or journal formats.

Risk: The polished abstract may retain factual inaccuracies or miss journal-specific requirements if they are not supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Provide the relevant journal rules and verify factual accuracy, terminology, statistical notation, and claims before submission.

Risk: Over-polishing could unintentionally shift emphasis in the study's findings, methodology, or conclusions.

Mitigation: Compare the final abstract against the source manuscript and require that no new claims, data, references, or conclusions are introduced.
