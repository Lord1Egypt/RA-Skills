## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The pack installs seven unpinned external skills.

Mitigation: Review each included skill separately and prefer pinned versions from known publishers before installation.

Risk: Email automation and business-account workflows can send messages or change external services.

Mitigation: Grant only task-specific files and API keys, and require human approval before sending emails or changing business accounts.

Risk: Scraping, outreach, and trading-related workflows can create legal, compliance, or financial exposure.

Mitigation: Use scraping and outreach only with a lawful basis, honor site terms and opt-outs, and treat trading outputs as research requiring independent review.
