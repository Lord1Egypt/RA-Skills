## Description:

Turn one visual brief into a visual creation set: a poster and matching stills that keep the same subject, palette, and type. Visual creation lays out what each frame shows, then makes the poster and stills. Use it for a brand look, a poster series, and a set of pictures that have to belong together.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External creators and brand teams use the skill to plan and produce a poster with matching still images, plus an optional short motion piece with music, from one visual brief.
