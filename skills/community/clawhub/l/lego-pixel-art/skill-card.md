## Description:

Converts an uploaded image into LEGO brick-style pixel art and produces a pixelated preview, row-by-row building guide, and material purchase list.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openlark](https://clawhub.ai/user/openlark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, LEGO enthusiasts, educators, and craft builders use this skill to convert images into brick-style mosaic plans with a preview, row-by-row build guide, and materials list.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Oversized browser inputs may affect reliability while the input clamping issue remains open.

Mitigation: Stay within the documented 10-200 stud range and avoid oversized dimensions before exporting plans.

Risk: Generated color matching and material counts may need review before purchase or classroom use.

Mitigation: Check the preview, building guide, and material list against the intended image before buying bricks or distributing instructions.
