## Description:

Guides agents in building confidential whistleblower and ethics hotline workflows for anonymous intake, case triage, investigations, compliance mapping, retaliation prevention, and board reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Compliance, HR, legal, and governance teams use this skill to have an agent draft or review whistleblower hotline workflows, reporting policies, investigation steps, and board reporting templates across stated jurisdictions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with explicit artifact coverage for the United States, European Union, United Kingdom, Australia, and Canada.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Whistleblower obligations vary by jurisdiction, industry, and organization type.

Mitigation: Review generated policies and workflows with qualified legal or compliance counsel before use.

Risk: Real hotline reports can contain sensitive personal, employment, legal, or investigation data.

Mitigation: Treat reports as sensitive data and apply appropriate privacy, retention, access control, and confidentiality safeguards.

Risk: The artifact mentions third-party hotline vendors but does not evaluate their privacy, retention, access control, or contractual safeguards.

Mitigation: Evaluate any hotline vendor separately before routing real reports through that service.
