## Description:

Xueersi English Grammar Checker highlights, corrects, and explains grammar issues in pasted English sentences or paragraphs for K-12 writing practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhouzhi-tal](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhouzhi-tal)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, educators, and writing-support users can use this skill to review English text, identify grammar and usage errors, receive corrected wording, and get level-appropriate explanations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may choose Chinese explanations for inferred elementary-grade writing when the user has not specified a language preference.

Mitigation: Ask for English, Chinese, or bilingual explanations explicitly when language preference matters.
