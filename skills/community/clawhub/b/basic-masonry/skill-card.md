## Description:

Foundational masonry and concrete skills for home and property projects, including pouring concrete slabs, building retaining walls, laying bricks or blocks, repairing cracked concrete, and building outdoor structures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Homeowners, property owners, and agents assisting them use this skill to plan common masonry and concrete projects, choose appropriate materials, estimate quantities, follow safe construction steps, and identify when permits or professional help are needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installation commands can affect the user's agent environment.

Mitigation: Use a trusted or pinned ClawHub CLI when installing the skill.

Risk: Masonry and concrete work can create physical safety hazards, including silica dust, flying fragments, structural failure, and fall risks.

Mitigation: Use appropriate PPE, verify local permits and codes, and involve a structural engineer or licensed contractor for foundations, structural cracks, tall retaining walls, or work near buildings.
