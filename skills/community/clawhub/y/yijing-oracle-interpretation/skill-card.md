## Description:

Generates structured Chinese-language I Ching hexagram interpretation reports from a hexagram name, optional changing lines, user context, and a specific question.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tuipanda](https://clawhub.ai/user/tuipanda)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, Yijing beginners, traditional-culture creators, and I Ching enthusiasts use this skill to turn a hexagram and optional changing-line context into a concise and full Markdown interpretation with practical next steps. The output is reflective cultural guidance and should not be used as financial, legal, medical, mental-health, or other professional advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on readings for investment, relationship, career, health, legal, or other high-stakes decisions.

Mitigation: Present outputs as reflective cultural guidance, keep the required disclaimer, and advise users to consult qualified sources before making major decisions.

Risk: Unsupported, misspelled, or ambiguous hexagram and changing-line inputs may lead to misleading interpretations.

Mitigation: Ask the user to confirm the hexagram name, changing-line format, and question context when inputs are incomplete or outside the sixty-four standard hexagrams.
