## Description:

Design YouTube video thumbnails that earn the click: mobile-legible, one focal point, honest, planned as a brief and A/B test rather than a final raster image.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators, channel operators, and social media teams use this skill to turn a video, title, and brand profile into an honest YouTube thumbnail concept, concise text, handoff brief, and native A/B test plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill provides advisory creative guidance and may be mistaken for an automation skill that creates, uploads, or tests thumbnails directly.

Mitigation: Use it for the promise, concept, text, brief, critique, and A/B plan; keep final raster creation, YouTube Studio upload, and native testing under a human or separate approved tool.

Risk: Thumbnail claims can become misleading when they are not grounded in the actual video, title, and channel brand context.

Mitigation: Provide the brand profile, video content, and title before using the skill, then review the brief for promise accuracy before production.

Risk: Some YouTube feature and specification details are time-sensitive.

Mitigation: Verify current YouTube thumbnail specifications and Test & Compare availability before relying on dated operational details.
