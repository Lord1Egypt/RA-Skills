## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to understand what AI and technology developments mean, compare AI strategies, and judge where technical or market trends are heading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encourages confident judgments, which may lead to overconfident or misleading conclusions on high-stakes AI and technology topics.

Mitigation: Users should independently verify high-stakes claims and treat the output as analysis rather than a factual authority.

Risk: Recent technical details may change quickly, especially when the skill analyzes new AI releases or market moves.

Mitigation: Use web search to verify release dates, benchmarks, and specific technical claims before relying on them.
