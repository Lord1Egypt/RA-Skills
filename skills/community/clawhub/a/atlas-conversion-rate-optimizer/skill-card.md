## Description:

Atlas Conversion Rate Optimizer helps SaaS, agency, and product teams generate CRO copy variants, A/B test plans, CRO roadmaps, and growth experiment briefs for landing pages, signup flows, pricing pages, onboarding, and product conversion optimization.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, SaaS founders, agencies, product managers, and growth teams use this skill to draft conversion-focused copy, plan A/B tests, and structure CRO roadmaps for acquisition, signup, onboarding, and pricing flows.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Some security-audit and DeFi references appear copied from another product and may confuse the scope of the free CRO skill or paid upgrade claims.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as a lightweight CRO and copywriting template; verify any external paid Atlas offering separately before purchase or operational use.

Risk: Generated CRO plans and copy may imply conversion improvements that are not validated by evidence.

Mitigation: Run controlled tests, define primary and guardrail metrics, and measure results before relying on any conversion-lift recommendation.
