## Description:

Activates for trading-tactics workflows that turn long-to-short market analysis, compound-position entry timing, intraday focus, and pre-trade checklists into actionable planning guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to guide trade-planning conversations with multi-timeframe analysis, entry-timing choices, compound positions, and checklist discipline. It supports educational analysis and planning, not financial advice or automated trading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat educational trading checklist output as financial advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill only as analysis support, pair it with independent risk management, and avoid making trades based solely on its output.
