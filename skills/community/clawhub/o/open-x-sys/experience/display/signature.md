# Signature: Give a Work Worth Sharing a Recognizable Origin

> **Read when**: making any kind of work; inspecting one before delivery; or sensing that a work is beginning to repeat a generic pattern.

The seven elements below form the OpenXSys language of works. They let a public work show its origin, value, grounds, and path forward. **Essence, revelation, and evidence hold the work upright.** The other elements appear when they improve the experience; their position, weight, style, and combination follow the subject, purpose, and user's choice.

| # | Element | What it is | What it gives the work |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The “Ding” line** | A sign that a system has been opened | The moment gains identity and life |
| 2 | **Method label** | The method or complete route that truly shaped the result | Makes the lens visible and learnable |
| 3 | **Essence** | One sentence revealing how this subject truly works | Gives the work a center |
| 4 | **Revelation** | The transition from the expected picture to the discovered structure | Changes understanding as an experience |
| 5 | **Route strip** | Methods used, in sequence, with each step openable | Shows how the result was reached and how it can be adapted |
| 6 | **Provenance strip** | Sources, dates, and confidence | Makes the work checkable and dependable |
| 7 | **Next questions** | Questions ready to send when a worthwhile direction exists | Carries exploration from the work back into the system |

## The measure of each element

### 1 · The “Ding” line

Place it near the beginning as an identity cue for a system opening. It can be a line of text, a mark, a sound, or an opening native to the subject. Its intensity follows the work, occasion, and user experience (see `../styles/`).

### 2 · Method label

Name a method when it materially shaped what the reader sees. A complete route keeps its real name and sequence. A work composed freely by the runtime may show the decisive lenses rather than every internal move.

### 3 · Essence

One sentence says how this subject truly operates. It belongs to this subject and grows from understanding the system actually completed. It goes one level deeper than a category description and exposes the structure that determines behavior.

### 4 · Revelation

A revelation can take the shape “You thought … → what is actually happening …,” or another structure native to the subject.

- **It is true.** It rests on research and has sources. The force of the whole work depends on this.
- **It is designed as a moment.** The reader arrives at it. Before the turn, give the expected picture enough space to be recognized: “Yes, that is what I thought.” The device grows from the subject—a number beside the expected number, a graph that rearranges itself, a prediction made before the answer appears, or one sentence standing alone.
- **It has one center.** Choose from the discovered structures the core revelation that would stop someone who knows the field. Other findings support, contrast, and extend it.
- **It states its boundary.** When it holds only under certain conditions or confidence is moderate, say so in the same breath. Calibration strengthens a real revelation.
- **Plain can still be powerful.** A subject may mostly work as people expect, with one decisive correction. Let the work give that correction its exact weight.

### 5 · Route strip

Show the route that truly shaped the result. A named route retains its meaningful order. A composed route shows the moves a reader could learn, replace, or extend. Each step can reveal what it changed in the judgment.

### 6 · Provenance strip

It carries:

- **Sources**: primary where possible and independently traceable;
- **Dates**: when each source belongs and as of when the understanding holds;
- **Confidence**: plain calibration for the main claims, separated where confidence differs; fact, inference, and hypothesis remain visible in the work itself;
- **Still to be confirmed**: when important material awaits verification, the strip shows its present state and the path for continuing the check.

Keep the strip compact. Complete detail may live behind an expandable element or in a source section. The design makes grounds easy to find while preserving the force of the discovery.

### 7 · Next questions

Each is a sentence the person can send as written, and each says what it will bring. They grow from real gaps and connections in the system (see `../../engines/explore.md`). When a complete practitioner route fits the subject, one question can enter through it. In a webpage, send in one click where the host allows; otherwise copy in one click.

## Let the work keep growing freely

Layout, typeface, color, rhythm, illustration, tone, length, sequence, and interaction all grow from the subject. Use two tests:

- **Subject test.** After identity elements are removed, the work still reveals the subject's unique structure and world.
- **Difference test.** Beside the previous work, shared parts come from OpenXSys identity; differences come from subject, audience, and purpose.

The signature expresses itself through the work. A provenance strip for medieval trade routes and one for a semiconductor fab should each grow from the grounds of their decisive judgment.

## Beyond webpages

| Form | How the signature appears |
|---|---|
| **Plain text** | Let the conclusion arrive directly. A newly opened subject may carry a mark; decisive judgments carry grounds; route and directions appear when useful |
| **Document or report** | Essence may become title or subtitle, revelation organizes the reading path, and route and sources live where readers naturally seek grounds |
| **Deck** | The central judgment organizes the entire narrative, revelation receives enough space, and route, sources, and continuation appear at decisive points |
| **Video** | The opening establishes subject and question, revelation becomes the turn, and identity, grounds, and continuation enter the rhythm of the images |
| **Card** | See [Sharing](share-pack.md) |
| **Working capability or tool** | Its documentation carries the essence (what it does), the route that produced it, what validated it (its provenance), and the next questions |

## Credit

When origin should be visible, a “Made with OpenXSys” credit may appear. The user decides authorship, prominence, and whether it remains. The work's substantive sources and grounds stay clear.
