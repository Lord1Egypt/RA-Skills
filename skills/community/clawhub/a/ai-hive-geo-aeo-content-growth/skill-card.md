## Description:

A Chinese-language GEO/AEO content planning skill that turns brand or product source material into user question maps, direct answers, entity tables, evidence cards, dated source lists, multimedia prompts or assets, and AI visibility retest records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, content, and product teams use this skill to structure Chinese-language GEO/AEO content from verified brand or product sources. It helps plan answer-engine-ready pages, evidence cards, entity records, explanatory media, and multi-model visibility retesting without promising ranking or citation outcomes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Confidential brand, product, pricing, or legal materials may be exposed when supplied to the agent or external media-generation tools.

Mitigation: Review confidential sources before use and avoid sending restricted materials to external tools without authorization.

Risk: Generated answers, evidence cards, images, videos, sources, or dates may be inaccurate or misleading if published without review.

Mitigation: Manually verify claims, source links, media text, dates, and limitations before publication.

Risk: AI visibility retest results may be mistaken for guaranteed ranking, inclusion, recommendation, or citation outcomes.

Mitigation: Present retest results as dated observations and keep the stated limitation that third-party model ranking, inclusion, recommendation, or citation is not guaranteed.
