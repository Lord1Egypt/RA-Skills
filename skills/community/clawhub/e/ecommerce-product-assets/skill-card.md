## Description:

Turn one product into an ecommerce product asset set: a main image, detail stills, and one product clip.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Ecommerce sellers and product marketers use this skill to plan and create listing images and one short product clip before publishing a product listing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
