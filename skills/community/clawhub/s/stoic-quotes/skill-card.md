## Description:

Provides Stoic philosophy quotes from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus, Cato, and other Stoic philosophers on request.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bshandley](https://clawhub.ai/user/bshandley)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users can request attributed Stoic quotes for philosophical reflection, inspiration, or topic-specific guidance around themes such as resilience, control, virtue, and acceptance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may respond to broad inspiration or quote requests with Stoic quotes even when the user did not explicitly ask for Stoicism.

Mitigation: Deployers should ensure this behavior matches the intended assistant experience and adjust activation guidance if broader inspiration requests should remain general.
