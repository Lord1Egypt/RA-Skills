## Use Case:

Developers, website owners, and agents use this skill to make web presences easier for AI systems to find, parse, cite, and interact with. It guides the creation of agent-readable site files, structured service data, discoverability APIs, and claim-dense content.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated discoverability files, structured data, API descriptions, or citation-ready claims may publish inaccurate business details.

Mitigation: Review pricing, availability, contact details, service descriptions, and structured claims for accuracy before publication.

Risk: Agent-facing API endpoints or public machine-readable pages can expose stale or inconsistent information if they are not maintained.

Mitigation: Keep llms.txt, structured data, public API responses, and facts pages synchronized with current offerings and update them whenever services change.
