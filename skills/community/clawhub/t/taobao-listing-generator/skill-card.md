## Description:

Generates Chinese marketplace listing copy for Taobao, Tmall, Xianyu, and Pinduoduo from product details.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketplace sellers and ecommerce operators use this skill to turn product details into platform-specific Chinese listing titles, main-image copy, detail-page descriptions, and keyword tags.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketplace copy may include incorrect warranties, compliance statements, platform claims, or pricing language.

Mitigation: Review and edit all generated claims against the product facts and current marketplace rules before publishing.
