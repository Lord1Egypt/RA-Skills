## Description:

Finds business opportunities by mining Reddit, HackerNews, and ProductHunt for pain points, validating ideas, and surfacing emerging trends.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gunybuny](https://clawhub.ai/user/gunybuny)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and business builders use this skill to mine public market conversations for pain points, rate business ideas, spot emerging niches, and prepare opportunity briefings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad business-research prompts may cause an agent to search public market sources using private business context supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Do not provide private business data unless it is appropriate to use it for market analysis.
