## Description:

Helps launch teams design pricing tiers, launch offers, beta or early-adopter price paths, and guarantee or refund terms while treating drafted prices and offer terms as candidates for review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External launch, marketing, and product teams use this skill to plan commercial pricing and packaging for a product launch. It helps structure tiers, map value pillars to prices, define time-bounded launch offers, plan beta-to-GA price graduation, and draft refund or guarantee terms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prices, discounts, refund promises, or guarantee wording may be mistaken for approved business commitments.

Mitigation: Review all proposed pricing, discount, refund, and guarantee terms before publishing or committing them to customers.

Risk: The skill may save candidate terms into memory after user confirmation.

Mitigation: Confirm only terms that should be retained for future sessions, and review any optional analytics or launch-platform data before relying on it.
