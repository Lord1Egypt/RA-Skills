## Description:

Helps application developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users turn vague error-message problems into clearer explanations of what failed, why it failed, and what action to take next.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and end users use this skill to improve unclear errors, debugging notes, support responses, troubleshooting workflows, checklists, and implementation guidance so next steps are easier to act on.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad debugging and support triggers may activate this skill for general troubleshooting requests.

Mitigation: Install it where broad error-message assistance is intended, and review trigger scope during deployment.

Risk: Generated troubleshooting or error-message guidance may be incomplete for the user's specific system.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the stated success criteria and confirm assumptions, limits, and required inputs before applying changes.
