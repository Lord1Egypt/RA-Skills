## Use Case:

External staff, volunteers, and advisors for China-focused charitable organizations use this skill to ask operational management questions and receive structured reference guidance. It helps with charity-law interpretation, compliance workflows, governance practices, finance/tax basics, and HR scenarios while requiring human review for decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is China-focused and may provide guidance that is incomplete, outdated, or not specific to a local authority's current requirements.

Mitigation: Require human review before use and verify concrete deadlines, legal requirements, tax questions, and local procedures with the relevant civil-affairs, tax, legal, or accounting professional.

Risk: The skill may produce advisory language that users could mistake for professional legal, financial, or fundraising advice.

Mitigation: Keep the mandatory disclaimer in every answer and route legal disputes, tax filings, public fundraising, and high-impact decisions to qualified professionals or specialized tools.

Risk: Local audit or history logging may occur when the host provides storage.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal data, restrict access to stored logs, and align retention with the organization's privacy and compliance obligations.

Risk: Out-of-scope requests may be redirected to other Pozzzi-branded assistants.

Mitigation: Review any suggested handoff before relying on it, especially for document generation, applications, public fundraising, or legal work.
