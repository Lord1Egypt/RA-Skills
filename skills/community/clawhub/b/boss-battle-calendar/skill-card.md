## Description:

Transform upcoming high-stakes events into boss battles with countdown phases, preparation checkpoints, risk warnings, and recovery plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn exams, trips, deadlines, meetings, and other dated events into concrete preparation timelines with risk warnings, priority handling, and recovery checkpoints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the generated plan for an automated reminder, booking, registration, or calendar execution system.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning guidance and manually enter deadlines, reminders, bookings, and registrations into the appropriate external systems.

Risk: Preparation timelines and risk warnings may be incomplete if the user provides uncertain dates, missing requirements, or overlapping events.

Mitigation: Collect event dates or time windows, stakes, current preparation status, likely failure points, and recovery needs before relying on the plan.
