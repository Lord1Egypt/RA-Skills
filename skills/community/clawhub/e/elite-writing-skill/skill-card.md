## Use Case:

External users, employees, and developers use this skill to ask an agent for writing drafts, marketing copy, technical documentation, content strategy, creative storytelling, and structured edits. It is especially suited to Indonesian and English writing workflows that benefit from audience analysis, purpose-driven structure, hooks, templates, and layered editing.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Persuasive writing templates can lead to misleading claims, fake urgency, or unsupported social proof if used without review.

Mitigation: Review claims, statistics, testimonials, urgency, and calls to action before publishing.

Risk: Marketing or outreach copy may involve consent, privacy, or anti-spam obligations.

Mitigation: Confirm consent, audience data use, unsubscribe language, and applicable rules before sending.

Risk: Technical documentation or API examples may contain placeholder commands or snippets.

Mitigation: Treat generated commands and snippets as drafts; verify endpoints, credentials, parameters, and environment before use.

Risk: Broad writing and editing triggers may cause larger rewrites than intended.

Mitigation: Confirm scope before major rewrites and keep the work focused on one task per run.
