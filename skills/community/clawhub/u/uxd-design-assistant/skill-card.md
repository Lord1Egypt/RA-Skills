## Description:

UXD Design Assistant guides students, educators, and UX practitioners through user experience design workflows with methods, templates, and local generators for research, analysis, prototyping, testing, and reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kingsunzhang-openclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/kingsunzhang-openclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External learners, students, educators, and UX teams use this skill to follow a structured UXD process, generate research and testing templates, and plan UXDA competition deliverables without substituting for the user's own design work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Helper scripts can write generated Markdown or CSV to a user-provided output path.

Mitigation: Review the destination path before running scripts and avoid overwriting important files.

Risk: UX templates and generated prompts can be used without enough user research or usability evidence.

Mitigation: Require design decisions to be tied to research findings, competitive analysis, or usability test data before using outputs in final submissions.
