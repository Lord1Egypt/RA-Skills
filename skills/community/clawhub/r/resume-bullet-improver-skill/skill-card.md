## Use Case:

External users use this skill to improve resume bullets and career-writing drafts with concise plans, reusable checklists, next actions, and optional editable copy blocks while minimizing unnecessary personal data exposure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste more sensitive personal data than needed for resume editing.

Mitigation: Ask only for relevant resume content and constraints, and remind users to keep personally sensitive details minimal.

Risk: Broad trigger wording may invoke the skill outside focused resume bullet improvement tasks.

Mitigation: Use clearer invocation wording when a workflow should be limited to resume bullet drafting or critique.
