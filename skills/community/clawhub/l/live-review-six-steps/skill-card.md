## Description:

Guides agents through a six-step Douyin live-stream post-session review covering data, pacing, products, audience, short videos, traffic mix, and compliance checkpoints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Live-commerce operators, merchants, and agents use this skill to diagnose Douyin live-stream performance after a session and produce a prioritized review report with metrics, issues, ownership, and next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Operational guidance may rely on claimed 2026 Douyin platform rules that are not verified platform policy.

Mitigation: Check current Douyin policy, account status, and business dashboard data before using the review as the basis for business decisions.

Risk: The broad data-analysis trigger may cause the skill to appear for unrelated analysis requests.

Mitigation: Use this skill only for Douyin live-stream post-session review and select a domain-specific alternative for other analysis work.
