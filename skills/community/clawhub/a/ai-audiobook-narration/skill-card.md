## Description:

Turn final manuscript or course text into ordered chapter audio with one narrator, a representative sample, clear pricing, and focused refinements.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[beatra-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/beatra-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and audiobook producers use the skill to turn final manuscripts or course text into ordered, chapterized narrated audio. They can review a representative pilot before producing the remaining chapters.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Security review flags broad permissions, automatic installation tracking, and a shared credential that may grant more authority than narration needs; scanner coverage is partial.

Mitigation: Before proceeding, review what manuscript text and installation details are sent to Beatra, check the shared token's scope, and account for the unresolved references noted in the security guidance.
