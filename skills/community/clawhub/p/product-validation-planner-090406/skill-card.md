## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning before investing heavily.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to structure product validation work, produce practical checklists or workflows, and check whether an idea is worth building before heavy investment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad auto-invocation metadata may cause the skill to activate for loosely related planning or startup requests.

Mitigation: Narrow the activation wording or disable implicit invocation when explicit product validation planning requests are required.
