## Description:

Provides a consent-governed design for relational memory, covering privacy safeguards, variance preservation, and adaptive dormancy under adversarial pressure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and system operators use this design guide to define consent, retention, revocation, and safety controls for relational-memory systems.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The encryption, privacy, and erasure safeguards are design proposals rather than implemented controls.

Mitigation: Implement these controls separately and validate them before storing sensitive memories.

Risk: Unspecified incident triggers or approval roles can leave sensitive-memory handling unclear.

Mitigation: Define incident triggers and approval roles, then verify retention, revocation, and erasure behavior before use.
