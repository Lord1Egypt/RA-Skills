This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and commerce operators use this skill to draft TikTok-style product-promotion scripts, storyboards, viral copy, and shooting checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated promotional scripts can contain unverified product claims, prices, testimonials, before-and-after comparisons, professional credentials, or health and safety statements.

Mitigation: Verify all claims and regulated statements before publishing, and add required sponsorship or affiliate disclosures.

Risk: Raw video footage may include clients, faces, locations, children, unreleased products, or confidential business assets.

Mitigation: Use privacy-conscious storage and review footage handling practices before sharing or publishing.
