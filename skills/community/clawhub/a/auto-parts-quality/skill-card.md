## Use Case:

Quality engineers, supplier quality teams, and automotive parts analysts use this skill to structure investigations, review 8D reports, analyze SPC/FMEA/PPAP evidence, identify likely failure modes, and draft corrective-action reports for automotive components.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automotive quality analysis can affect safety, regulatory, supplier, or customer decisions if unsupported conclusions are treated as final engineering determinations.

Mitigation: Use outputs as engineering support and verify conclusions against inspection evidence, test data, applicable standards, and qualified human review before acting.

Risk: The artifact includes a Chinese quality report template that may not match every user's reporting language or local process format.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to produce reports in the preferred language and align the structure with the organization's required quality process.
