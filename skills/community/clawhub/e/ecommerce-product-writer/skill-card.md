## Description:

Write compelling product descriptions that convert browsers into buyers. Perfect for e-commerce sellers on Amazon, Etsy, Shopify, and other platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External e-commerce sellers and business users use this skill to turn product details into product titles, benefit copy, feature lists, social-proof prompts, and calls to action for online listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may include private supplier terms, unreleased product plans, or customer data while asking for listing copy.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive business or customer information unless it is approved for use in a writing prompt.

Risk: Generated product descriptions may overstate benefits, certifications, or differentiators if the prompt includes incomplete or unverified product details.

Mitigation: Review copy against accurate product specifications, approved claims, and platform listing rules before publishing.
