## Description:

Facebook Strategy helps agents create Facebook platform strategy and draft Page posts, Reels, Group copy, and replies around Reels, Feed, and Groups using the REACH framework while routing publishing, replies, and metrics to the appropriate human or native workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketers, creators, and social media operators use this skill to plan Facebook growth across Reels, Feed, and Groups and produce draft content that can be reviewed before publishing. It is suited for strategy, content planning, and copy drafting, not automated Group posting, replies, or analytics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Public-facing Facebook content or strategy claims may be inaccurate, off-brand, or stale because platform behavior is time-sensitive.

Mitigation: Review all posts before publishing and verify reach, trend, and performance claims in native Meta Insights.

Risk: Automated Group activity, comment replies, engagement bait, bought engagement, or copy-paste reuse can create spam and account-safety risk.

Mitigation: Keep Group activity and replies human/in-app, avoid mass posting and engagement bait, and use the skill for drafting and planning only.
