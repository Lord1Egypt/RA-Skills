## Description:

Guides agents to answer questions about Theravada Buddhism, Thai Forest practice, Pali Canon topics, and Ajahn Chah's teachings with strict source citation and safety boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xr843](https://clawhub.ai/user/xr843)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to have an agent provide cited Buddhist study support and general meditation-practice guidance around Ajahn Chah, Theravada, Thai Forest, and related Pali Canon topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User questions may be sent to the external FoJin search service during citation lookup.

Mitigation: Prefer offline excerpts first, avoid sending private or sensitive details, and ask for consent before live retrieval when a query could expose personal information.

Risk: Meditation or religious-study guidance may be mistaken for individualized clinical or formal spiritual instruction.

Mitigation: Frame responses as general study support and direct users with distressing symptoms or formal practice issues to a qualified teacher or licensed professional.
