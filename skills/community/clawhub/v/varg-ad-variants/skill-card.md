## Description:

Turns one winning video ad into a planned set of variants for Meta and TikTok using new concepts and single-variable iterations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sellers and performance marketers use the skill to plan and produce video ad variants from a winning ad, including new hooks, captions, consistent filenames, and a test plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment has medium confidence because the executable script was only partly inspected.

Mitigation: Review the hook-swap script before running it and confirm its behavior fits your workflow.

Risk: Ad media processed by the workflow may be shared with an external service.

Mitigation: Check the service's media handling terms and avoid submitting footage you are not comfortable sharing.
