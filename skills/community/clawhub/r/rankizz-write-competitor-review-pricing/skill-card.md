## Description:

Research and write an honest competitor product review, pricing guide, or combined review-and-pricing page with current plan details, total-cost scenarios, documented methodology, meaningful limitations, alternatives, and commercial disclosures. Use when a user asks for a competitor review, "is X worth it," "X pricing," "X plans," a pricing breakdown, or a buyer-focused competitor page. Rankizz keyword and SERP data is optional; current official pricing and product research is required.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content teams and product marketers use this skill to research competitor products and create buyer-focused reviews, pricing guides, or combined pages. It helps explain fit and real costs while distinguishing hands-on testing from desk research and disclosing commercial relationships.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prices, plans, product details, and ownership can change, making published comparisons stale.

Mitigation: Recheck volatile facts against current official sources immediately before publication and state the research and pricing cutoff date.

Risk: Readers could mistake desk research for hands-on testing or interpret unsupported ratings as evidence.

Mitigation: Describe only tasks actually completed, label evidence types, and omit ratings unless a documented rubric and supporting evidence exist.
