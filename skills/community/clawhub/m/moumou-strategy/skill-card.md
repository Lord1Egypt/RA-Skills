## Description:

Moumou Strategy activates a Chinese-language strategic decision advisor persona for business planning, market entry, financing, transformation, competitive strategy, and other major corporate decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lanxy32](https://clawhub.ai/user/lanxy32)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business leaders, operators, and strategy-focused agents use this skill to structure Chinese-language guidance for high-level company decisions. It produces strategic options, a recommended direction, a phased execution path, success criteria, and risk responses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may trigger for a wide range of business-direction questions and answer in its prescribed Chinese executive advisor persona.

Mitigation: Scope activation to strategic decision workflows and let platform or user instructions override language, tone, or persona when needed.

Risk: Strategic recommendations may be incomplete or misleading when current market, financial, or company-specific facts are missing.

Mitigation: Provide current business evidence and require qualified stakeholder review before acting on recommendations.
