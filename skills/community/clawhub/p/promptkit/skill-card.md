## Description:

Auto-select and apply the best of 56 prompting frameworks to generate clear, structured prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[khamalismadie](https://clawhub.ai/user/khamalismadie)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, developers, and content creators use PromptKit to turn task requests into structured prompts by selecting an appropriate prompting framework and filling in clear prompt components.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated prompts may contain incorrect, incomplete, or misleading instructions for the intended task.

Mitigation: Review and refine generated prompts before using them in consequential workflows.

Risk: Users may include private or sensitive information while asking the skill to build a prompt.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive information unless it is intended to be shared in the resulting prompt.
