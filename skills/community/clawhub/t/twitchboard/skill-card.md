## Description:

Answer streamer questions with TwitchBoard's free MCP server - exact image sizes for Twitch, YouTube, Discord, Kick and TikTok, username ideas that follow platform rules, Minecraft/Roblox name checks, OBS widget URLs and bitrate settings, Discord fancy text and timestamps, and Twitch/Kick/TikTok earnings estimates. Use when someone is setting up or growing a stream or gaming channel.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
