## Description: <br>
Transforms decomposed requirements into structured QA scenario trees covering happy paths, alternative paths, exception paths, and data-flow scenarios with requirement traceability. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
QA engineers and product teams use this skill after requirement decomposition to design traceable scenario trees for complex workflows, page transitions, state changes, and exception handling. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can activate on broad QA scenario-design requests and may shape test coverage for complex workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Use it when structured scenario trees are desired, then review the generated paths against the source requirements before adopting them. <br>
