## Description:

The Instagram SEO skill helps an agent research Instagram keyword clusters, write search-first captions, specify on-screen and spoken keyword signals, and advise profile fields and alt text while keeping native Instagram settings and metrics outside the skill's control.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External social media operators, creators, and marketing teams use this skill to make Instagram posts and Reels more searchable by building real-query keyword clusters, drafting natural captions, and preparing profile and visual-signal recommendations. It is intended for caption and guidance generation, with human review before public posting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated SEO guidance could overstate ranking, search volume, hashtag value, or expected discovery results.

Mitigation: Review captions and claims before posting, use observed query research, and do not publish fabricated volumes or guaranteed rankings.

Risk: Caption, alt text, on-screen text, or spoken keywords could misrepresent the actual media.

Mitigation: Confirm that all text and audio signals match the visible image or video before publication.

Risk: The skill can advise profile fields, alt text, category, auto-captions, and Instagram Search or Insights review, but those surfaces remain native to Instagram.

Mitigation: Have a human apply or verify native Instagram settings and analytics outside the skill workflow.
