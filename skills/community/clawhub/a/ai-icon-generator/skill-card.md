This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, designers, and product teams use this skill to generate icon assets from natural language prompts for web applications, mobile apps, design systems, marketing materials, prototypes, and automated icon creation workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Icon descriptions, generation parameters, and generated assets are sent to the third-party api.mkkpro.com service.

Mitigation: Do not include secrets, regulated data, private customer information, or proprietary unreleased product details in prompts unless the provider's privacy and retention terms have been reviewed.

Risk: Generated icons may not be appropriate for final brand, legal, or accessibility requirements without review.

Mitigation: Review generated assets before using them in production applications, design systems, or marketing materials.
