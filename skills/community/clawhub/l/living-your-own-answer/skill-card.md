## Description:

A Chinese-language reflective coaching skill that helps users record, review, and learn from real-life experiences to build personal judgment, boundaries, creativity, and self-directed life decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[web-seeker](https://clawhub.ai/user/web-seeker)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for structured self-reflection around life choices, personal growth, relationships, boundaries, emotions, and recurring patterns. It supports journaling, review, practice, and reflection, but should not be treated as professional mental health, legal, medical, financial, or relationship advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be invoked for sensitive personal, emotional, relationship, or life-decision topics.

Mitigation: Keep responses framed as reflective support and encourage users to consult qualified professionals for mental health, legal, medical, financial, or relationship decisions.

Risk: Broad personal-topic triggers may cause the skill to appear in many reflective conversations.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intent before applying the framework and allow the user to opt out or narrow the topic.
