## Description:

[Didoo AI] Conducts competitive intelligence for Meta Ads campaigns — analyzes competitor ads, landing pages, and market benchmarks. Use before launching a new campaign or entering a new market.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketers, agencies, and growth teams use this skill before launching Meta Ads campaigns or entering new markets to research competitors, ad creative, landing pages, funnel paths, benchmarks, and differentiated creative angles to test.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitive ad research may rely on third-party sites or paid intelligence tools suggested during the workflow.

Mitigation: Review each site or tool before use and avoid sharing credentials or sensitive local data unless independently approved.

Risk: Research outputs can include uncertain ad spend or benchmark estimates.

Mitigation: Report only observed or sourced data, label estimates clearly, and do not fabricate CPM, CPL, CPA, endurance, or spend signals.

Risk: Competitor analysis can drift into copying protected branding or ad creative.

Mitigation: Use findings to adapt general principles and differentiated positioning, not to reproduce competitor brands, trademarks, or ads.
