## Description:

Chinese-language lottery research helper for rules lookup, historical draw analysis, number-selection examples, and probability or expected-value calculations for major Chinese Welfare and Sports lottery games.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[361066029](https://clawhub.ai/user/361066029)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to research Chinese Welfare and Sports lottery rules, analyze historical draw data, generate sample number selections, and calculate lottery probabilities or expected value. The skill is for probability study and entertainment reference, not financial or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated numbers or historical statistics could be mistaken for financial advice or a way to improve winning odds.

Mitigation: Present results as probability study or entertainment reference and state that lottery draws are independent with negative long-term expected value.

Risk: Public lottery data endpoints may change, fail, or return incomplete data.

Mitigation: Prefer official sources or validated local normalized files when accuracy matters, and disclose data-source uncertainty in analysis.

Risk: Scripts can read from or write to user-provided file paths.

Mitigation: Review input and output paths before running scripts that process local lottery data.
