## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language advisory lens for applying Naval Ravikant-style leverage, specific knowledge, desire management, freedom, and system-design perspectives to user decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cellinlab](https://clawhub.ai/user/cellinlab)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to apply a Naval-style advisory frame to career choices, opportunity evaluation, wealth and freedom questions, desire management, and recurring system problems. It is intended to produce structured perspective and boundaries, not investment advice, therapy, or a claim to represent Naval Ravikant personally.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-apply a Naval-style lens to financial, investment, mental-health, or high-context personal situations.

Mitigation: Keep the artifact's stated boundaries visible: do not provide investment advice or therapy, and include a blind spot or limitation in responses.

Risk: The Chinese-language default may create localized guidance gaps for non-Chinese users.

Mitigation: Clarify or adapt the response language when the user's language needs differ from the default.

Risk: A personality-based advisory frame can oversimplify resource differences, emotional factors, or execution details.

Mitigation: Retain the skill's constraints around starting point, resources, environment differences, and problems that should not be solved through leverage framing alone.
