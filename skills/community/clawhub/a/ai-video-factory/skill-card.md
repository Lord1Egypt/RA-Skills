## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, and content teams use this skill to plan and generate short-form video packages for platforms such as Douyin, Kuaishou, TikTok, YouTube, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu, and WeChat Channels. It supports scripts, shot plans, asset guidance, voiceover settings, subtitle plans, music choices, quality checks, export manifests, and publishing guidance.
