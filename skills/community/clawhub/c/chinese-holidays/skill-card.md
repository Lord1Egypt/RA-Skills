## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer China holiday and working-day questions, such as checking a date, listing a year's statutory holidays, or finding the next holiday.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bundled holiday data may become stale or incomplete for legal, payroll, travel, or business scheduling decisions.

Mitigation: Verify results against current official State Council announcements before relying on them for consequential decisions.

Risk: The artifact only includes static holiday data for supported years.

Mitigation: Check whether the requested date falls within the bundled data range and update the data before using future years.
