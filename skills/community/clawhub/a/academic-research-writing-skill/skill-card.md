## Description:

Helps users develop clear, logically structured postgraduate academic research writing for proposals, literature reviews, hypotheses, methods, ethics sections, and bilingual Chinese-English academic polishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hoffnung131](https://clawhub.ai/user/hoffnung131)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and academic writers use this skill to structure and polish postgraduate research materials, including literature reviews, proposals, research questions, hypotheses, methods, statistical analysis plans, and ethics-related writing. It is also useful for translating or refining Chinese-English academic text while maintaining cautious academic tone and methodological consistency.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Academic writing guidance may require field-specific accuracy checks, citations, and validation against current literature or institutional requirements.

Mitigation: Review outputs for source support, domain accuracy, citation needs, and alignment with the user's proposal, ethics, or reporting requirements.

Risk: Users may provide sensitive research, participant, or patient information while requesting proposal or ethics-writing help.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive data and review any provided information under the user's applicable privacy, ethics, and institutional policies.
