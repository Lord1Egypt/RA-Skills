## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Review reports for cited sources, search scope, time range, and uncertainty before relying on recommendations.

Risk: PatSnap/Zhihuiya data may be unavailable or outside the user's account permissions.

Mitigation: Confirm the relevant data access rights and account permissions before installing or using the skill.

Risk: Outputs related to FTO, infringement, or patent validity may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat legal-sensitive outputs as R&D reference material and route legal judgments to qualified counsel.
