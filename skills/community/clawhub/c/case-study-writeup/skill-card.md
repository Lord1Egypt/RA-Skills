## Description:

Writes results-led client case studies for consulting or agency work, covering the client challenge, approach, quantified outcomes, client quote slot, and takeaway.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External consultants, agencies, and portfolio authors use this skill to turn project details into persuasive case studies that emphasize outcomes, proof, and relevance to future clients.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Case studies may expose confidential client details or commercially sensitive outcomes.

Mitigation: Anonymize sensitive details where needed, avoid sharing confidential inputs with the agent, and get client approval before publishing.

Risk: Unverified outcome claims or testimonials may mislead future clients.

Mitigation: Use sourced before-and-after metrics, ranges when exact figures are confidential, and publish client quotes only with permission.
