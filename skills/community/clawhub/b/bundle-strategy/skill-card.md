## Description:

Design product bundles that increase average order value by analyzing purchase affinity data, margin contribution, and perceived value to create offers that sell better together than apart.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Ecommerce operators, growth marketers, and merchandising teams use this skill to design and document product bundles using purchase affinity, margin contribution, perceived value, inventory, and cannibalization checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The agent may reason over sensitive ecommerce sales, margin, inventory, and customer-segment data supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Use only data approved for agent processing, remove unnecessary sensitive fields, and review generated recommendations before sharing or acting on them.

Risk: Bundle recommendations can be misleading if affinity, cost, inventory, or cannibalization assumptions are stale or incomplete.

Mitigation: Validate the input metrics against current business systems and rerun the margin and cannibalization checks before launch decisions.
