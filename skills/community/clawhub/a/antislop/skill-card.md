## Description:

This skill helps agents review AI-generated content for low information density, generic phrasing, weak originality, unverified success claims, unnecessary structure, and template-like writing before publication or sharing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[futurizerush](https://clawhub.ai/user/futurizerush)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, writers, and agents use this skill to review draft AI-generated content for originality, evidence, information density, and verified completion claims before publishing, submitting, or sharing it.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can apply strong writing-style preferences during broad pre-publication review.

Mitigation: Override or narrow the review criteria when a specific tone, format, audience, or publication standard is required.
