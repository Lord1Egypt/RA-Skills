## Description:

Place real phone calls with a Callin.io AI voice agent (book appointments, ask businesses for information, confirm orders) in Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, English, French, German and more, then read the call summary and transcript. Use when the user asks you to call someone or a business.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People who ask an agent to call a person or business use this skill to book appointments, ask questions, or confirm orders, then review the call result and transcript.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: This skill places real calls and may share user-provided information with the recipient; the security review was partial because a local reference was unresolved.

Mitigation: Before each call, confirm the number, goal, and personal data to share. Call only at the user's request, use numbers the user supplied or a business's public number, avoid unsolicited and emergency calls, and do not collect payment card data.
