## Description:

Hutian Opc Business Plan guides agents through interactive Q&A to collect project details and draft a structured 15-20 page business plan deck.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External entrepreneurs, founders, and business advisors use this skill to gather project facts through multi-turn Q&A and prepare a market, feasibility, finance, team/resource, and risk-focused business plan deck outline.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Agent-facing referral and contact-promotion content can steer users outside the requested business-plan task.

Mitigation: Review generated responses for promotional referrals or contact-promotion content before sharing or deploying the skill.

Risk: Business-plan inputs may include confidential financial, equity, customer, personnel, or fundraising information.

Mitigation: Share only the business details needed for the plan and redact sensitive values unless the deployment has appropriate data handling controls.
