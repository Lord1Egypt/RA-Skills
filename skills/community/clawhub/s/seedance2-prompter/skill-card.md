## Description:

Seedance2 Prompter helps agents create structured English prompts for Seedance 2.0 video generation, including asset mapping, time-coded scene beats, camera language, negative constraints, and generation settings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wild-river2016](https://clawhub.ai/user/wild-river2016)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use this skill to turn Seedance 2.0 video ideas and reference assets into structured prompts for text-only, first-frame, all-reference, extension, replacement, dialogue, product, and long-form segmented workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may guide agents to rework copyrighted or brand-like prompts after platform rejection.

Mitigation: Prefer clearly original concepts, remove recognizable brands, characters, logos, and likenesses, and have a human review prompts before generation or publication.

Risk: Generated video prompts can still create misleading, infringing, or policy-rejected outputs if reference assets contain protected IP or real faces.

Mitigation: Check reference asset rights and platform policy before use; replace restricted material with original descriptions and explicit negative constraints.
