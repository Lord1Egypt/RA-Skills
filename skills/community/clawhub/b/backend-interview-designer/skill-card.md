## Description:

Generates high-signal senior-level backend interview questions by topic, covering API design, distributed systems, databases, caching, messaging, reliability, security, and event-driven architecture.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cngvc](https://clawhub.ai/user/cngvc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, and interviewers use this skill to generate one senior-level backend interview question on a requested topic, with a concise note on what the question evaluates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated interview prompts may not fully match a specific role rubric or hiring process when the requested topic is broad.

Mitigation: Have a human interviewer review each generated question against the target level, competencies, and approved hiring criteria before use.

Risk: The skill intentionally produces one question without follow-ups, which may be insufficient for a complete interview plan.

Mitigation: Use the output as a focused prompt seed and pair it with human-authored scoring guidance and follow-up questions when needed.
