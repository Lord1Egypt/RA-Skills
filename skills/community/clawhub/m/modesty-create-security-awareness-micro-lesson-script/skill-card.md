## Description:

Writes short, audio-first security-awareness micro-lessons for recurring employee training.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[modestyrichards](https://clawhub.ai/user/modestyrichards)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Security teams, compliance leads, and IT managers use this skill to draft concise employee security-awareness lesson scripts, especially for audio-oriented training workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated lesson content may contain inaccurate security guidance or company-specific compliance claims if published without review.

Mitigation: Review and tailor each lesson before publication or automated delivery, especially where compliance obligations or internal policy claims are mentioned.

Risk: Audio-oriented material may be delivered to employees before the intended audience, business goal, and constraints are confirmed.

Mitigation: Confirm the audience, training goal, and delivery controls before using text-to-speech or recurring training automation.
