## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to search for personal-trainer providers, check appointment availability, and create bookings through Lokuli.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creating a booking sends the user's name, email, and phone number to Lokuli's external service.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user understands what contact details will be sent before creating the booking.

Risk: Incorrect provider, time slot, or contact details could create an unintended appointment.

Mitigation: Confirm the provider, selected time slot, and customer contact details immediately before booking.
