## Description:

Provides Chinese listed-company compliance guidance, checklists, and drafting templates for disclosures, independent directors, restructurings, shareholder reductions, audit oversight, internal controls, related-party transactions, fundraising proceeds, and ESG reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Corporate secretaries, securities affairs teams, compliance teams, and audit teams at Chinese listed companies use this skill to review common compliance obligations and draft reference materials. It is a compliance drafting aid and should be checked against current PRC law and professional legal advice before use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Compliance guidance may be outdated, incomplete, or unsuitable for a specific issuer, transaction, industry, or regulatory update.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a drafting aid only and validate outputs against current PRC law, exchange rules, and qualified legal or sponsor review.

Risk: Insider-information templates may involve identity numbers, trading records, relatives' information, or other sensitive personal and corporate data.

Mitigation: Apply strict access controls, data minimization, secure storage, and current PRC legal review when handling completed templates.
