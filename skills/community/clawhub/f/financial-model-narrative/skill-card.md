## Description:

Turn financial model outputs into a clear written narrative for board, investor, management, bank, or internal audiences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Finance, executive reporting, and management users can use this skill to convert financial model outputs, P&L results, variance analysis, margin movement, cash position, and forecast commentary into a board-ready narrative.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial narratives may be inaccurate or misleading if the provided model outputs are incomplete, stale, or misinterpreted.

Mitigation: Review the generated commentary against the source model, variance support, and approved finance assumptions before using it in board, investor, or lender materials.

Risk: Financial model inputs may include sensitive business information.

Mitigation: Provide only the financial data the agent should analyze and avoid unnecessary confidential details.
