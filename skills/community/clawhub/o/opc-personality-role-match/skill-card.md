## Description:

通过五大主流性格测试模型（MBTI/DISC/九型人格/霍兰德/大五人格）评估OPC创业者性格优势，匹配六大核心角色类型（技术/创意/销售/领袖/运营/连接），输出MVP组合方案。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External OPC founders, independent builders, and team organizers use this skill to collect personality-test answers, identify likely OPC role strengths, and receive individual or team MVP role-combination guidance.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with information the user is comfortable sharing with the executing agent, and avoid entering unnecessary sensitive details.

Risk: Generated assessment and team-role reports may be shared more broadly than intended.

Mitigation: Review reports before sharing them with teammates or employers, and delete or redact output files that should not be retained.
