## Description: <br>
Generates audience-specific Chinese QA test reports, including daily, weekly, iteration, and specialized reports with quantified test results, defect analysis, risk assessment, and recommendations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[kokxi](https://clawhub.ai/user/kokxi) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
QA testers, test leads, project managers, and management teams use this skill to turn test execution data, defect data, and optional quality metrics into reports tailored to the audience and decision point. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may activate on generic daily, weekly, progress, or report requests when the user does not mean a QA test report. <br>
Mitigation: Clarify that the requested report is for testing or quality reporting before applying the templates. <br>
Risk: Generated reports can include sensitive test results, defect details, or quality metrics supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Include only test and defect data the user is comfortable sharing in the report audience and distribution context. <br>
