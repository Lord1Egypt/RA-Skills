## Description:

Provide expert advice on boiler water treatment, covering feedwater pretreatment, softening, desalination, deoxygenation, and dosing to ensure steam quality and safe boiler operation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nickliu54](https://clawhub.ai/user/nickliu54)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External plant operators, water-treatment engineers, and maintenance teams use this agent to plan boiler feedwater treatment, troubleshoot scaling or corrosion, select treatment equipment, and review chemical dosing approaches.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Boiler water treatment calculations, chemical recommendations, or operating advice may be incomplete or incorrect for a specific site.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary and have site-specific designs, dosing decisions, and operating changes reviewed by qualified boiler or water-treatment professionals before implementation.

Risk: Advice based on missing or stale water-quality, equipment, or operating data can lead to inappropriate treatment recommendations.

Mitigation: Provide current water analysis, boiler parameters, treatment equipment details, and operating constraints before relying on recommendations.
