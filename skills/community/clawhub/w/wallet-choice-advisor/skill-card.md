## Description:

A decision helper that recommends a wallet setup pattern based on the user's goals, activity level, and risk tolerance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill when choosing a wallet setup pattern for holding, learning, spending, using apps, or light trading. It helps match wallet convenience, cost, backup discipline, and security tradeoffs to the user's goals and risk tolerance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general wallet setup guidance as financial advice.

Mitigation: Frame recommendations as setup guidance and encourage users to make financial decisions independently.

Risk: Users may disclose seed phrases, private keys, exact balances, account logins, or recovery material while asking for wallet advice.

Mitigation: Advise users not to share recovery material or sensitive account details with the agent.
