## Description:

This skill lets users screen calls forwarded from their existing number through OsmO, review caller details and messages, and manage screening settings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[getosmo](https://clawhub.ai/user/getosmo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People using OsmO use the skill to review screened and missed calls, see caller messages, and manage caller rules and screening preferences from their AI app.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security scan does not establish what information the external OsmO service receives.

Mitigation: Review OsmO's privacy practices and terms before enabling call forwarding.

**Output Type(s):** [Text, Markdown]
