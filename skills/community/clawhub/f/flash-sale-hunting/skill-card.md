## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may discuss third-party shopping services, sensitive account authorization, or purchase-related privileges.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing credentials or granting purchase/account permissions unless the user independently trusts the underlying service, and review any authorization step before acting.

Risk: Flash-sale and preorder availability can change quickly and may depend on third-party service stability.

Mitigation: Verify current offer terms, inventory, timing, and platform status before making purchase decisions.
