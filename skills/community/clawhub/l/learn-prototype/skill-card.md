## Description:

Guides Chinese-language coaching for learning by first creating a rough runnable prototype, identifying defects, testing improvement hypotheses, and turning iterations into reusable methods.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External users and learning-focused agents use this skill to coach prototype-first learning, improvement of existing work, and skill development through user-led defect discovery and iterative refinement.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad make, research, or improve requests where the user expected narrower routing.

Mitigation: Confirm that prototype-first coaching is desired before applying the workflow, or narrow the activation scope for deployments that need stricter routing.

Risk: The skill is written for Chinese-language coaching and may not match users who expect another language.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's preferred language when language expectations are unclear.

Risk: Coaching based on assumed prior knowledge can mislead the user or skip needed prerequisites.

Mitigation: Use only user-confirmed prior knowledge and ask directly when the user's background is uncertain.
