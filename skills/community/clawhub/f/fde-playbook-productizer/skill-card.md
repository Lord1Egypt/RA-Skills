## Description:

Turns validated customer-delivery learning into reusable delivery playbooks, product-capability candidates, or Agent Skill assets with explicit evidence, applicability boundaries, and maintenance ownership.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xukun0821](https://clawhub.ai/user/xukun0821)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

FDEs and delivery leads use this skill after adoption and value review to identify repeated delivery patterns, select the minimum effective reusable asset form, and create a bounded playbook or product feedback package.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Single-customer success may be overgeneralized into a reusable asset before there is enough evidence.

Mitigation: Require two independent deliveries across customers or teams, or one delivery plus a reproducible experiment, before promoting the output beyond a candidate observation.

Risk: Customer-sensitive data or proprietary workflows could be included in reusable playbooks or public case assets.

Mitigation: De-identify customer material, remove restricted details, and obtain customer or internal approval before publishing public case studies.

Risk: A playbook may depend on oral context from the original delivery team and fail when reused by another FDE.

Mitigation: Run a reuse test with an FDE who did not participate in the original engagement and iterate on gaps before promotion.
