## Description:

Guides paid promotions within directories and marketplaces such as Taaft, Shopify App Store, G2, and Capterra.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, marketers, and founders use this skill to plan paid directory or marketplace promotions after a product listing exists. It helps compare ad formats, budget considerations, UTM tracking, and pre-launch checks for sponsored listings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ad platform statistics, promotional credits, targeting limits, and prices may change and could lead to poor spend decisions if treated as current policy.

Mitigation: Confirm current terms, credits, targeting options, and prices directly with each platform before purchasing campaigns.
