## Description:

A Chinese-language creator operations skill that provides 10 AI-guided modules for topic planning, title optimization, drafting, SEO, video direction, cover design, community operations, data analysis, sponsorship pricing, and multi-platform scheduling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators, operators, and creator-economy teams use this skill to plan and produce Chinese social-media content workflows across platforms such as Xiaohongshu, Douyin, WeChat public accounts, and Bilibili. It helps turn natural-language creator requests into drafts, planning tables, checklists, prompts, pricing guidance, and publishing schedules.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can activate on broad creator-related requests and may be applied when the user did not intend to use a full creator-operations workflow.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's target module or workflow before expanding into multi-step planning, especially for ambiguous social-media or creator requests.

Risk: The skill may ask users to share backend screenshots, contracts, performance data, or business metrics for analysis.

Mitigation: Ask users to redact private account, customer, contract, and revenue details before providing data for analysis.

Risk: Platform rules, content limits, market rates, and engagement benchmarks can change after the skill's 2024-2025 examples.

Mitigation: Treat numeric benchmarks and platform guidance as starting points, and verify current rules and market rates before relying on them.

Risk: Sponsorship pricing and contract guidance can affect commercial decisions.

Mitigation: Use pricing and contract outputs as planning support, and obtain professional review before signing contracts or making payment commitments.
