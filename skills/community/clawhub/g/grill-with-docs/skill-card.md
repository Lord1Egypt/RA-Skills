This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and project maintainers use this skill to move from ambiguous feature or system-change requests into a structured design document, domain vocabulary, architecture decisions, and a consistency review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create or update planning documents such as design docs, CONTEXT.md, ADRs, and consistency reports that affect project direction.

Mitigation: Review generated or modified planning documents before accepting them in important projects.
