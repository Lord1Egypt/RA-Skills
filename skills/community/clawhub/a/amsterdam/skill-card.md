## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Amsterdam cost, visa, tax, insurance, healthcare, and legal details may be time-sensitive or unsuitable as authoritative advice.

Mitigation: Verify current official details before acting, especially because the skill labels some data as February 2026 and the review date is September 10, 2026.

Risk: Users may over-rely on general city guidance for individual medical, immigration, tax, insurance, or legal decisions.

Mitigation: Treat the skill as a general reference and consult qualified or official sources for decisions with legal, financial, medical, or immigration consequences.
