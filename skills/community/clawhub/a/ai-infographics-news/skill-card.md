## Description:

Searches recent and trending AI products, tools, and news with emphasis on OpenClaw/MaxClaw updates, then generates a vivid infographic image.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leahliang](https://clawhub.ai/user/leahliang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to research timely AI news and produce a shareable infographic summarizing six to nine highlights.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally prioritizes OpenClaw/MaxClaw coverage and branding, which may make neutral AI news summaries promotional.

Mitigation: Review the selected news items and generated prompt before publishing when neutrality is required.

Risk: Generated images may be uploaded to a CDN when sharing is requested, making the output externally accessible.

Mitigation: Use the CDN sharing step only when the generated infographic is intended for public or external access.
