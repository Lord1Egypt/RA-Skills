## Description:

Guide users through systematic Chinese baby naming: bazi analysis, classics search, wuxing verification, and multi-dimensional comparison.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[left-uestc](https://clawhub.ai/user/left-uestc)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to guide a structured Chinese baby-naming process, including requirements gathering, optional bazi and wuxing review, classical text sourcing, candidate verification, and comparison. It supports decision-making without making the final naming choice for the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may collect sensitive family details such as parent names, birth times, birthplaces, and relatives' names.

Mitigation: Provide only details needed for the naming task, skip optional bazi and family fields when unwanted, and keep completed templates out of public repositories, shared folders, and unnecessary cloud logs.

Risk: Optional bazi and wuxing analysis can involve disputed interpretive methods.

Mitigation: Present method differences and limitations clearly, let the user choose the preferred approach, and keep the final naming decision with the user.
