## Description:

Comprehensive guide to every major US government assistance program: SNAP, Medicaid, unemployment, LIHEAP, TANF, WIC, Section 8, Lifeline, SSDI, SSI, and more. Covers eligibility, application process, processing times, and approval tips for each program.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to identify US public assistance programs they may qualify for and organize application steps, documents, follow-up dates, denials, and appeals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may store sensitive financial, household, medical, and benefits-application details in local tracker files or persistent agent state.

Mitigation: Ask for clear user consent before saving trackers, minimize stored fields, avoid full identifiers and unnecessary medical details, and delete trackers when they are no longer needed.

Risk: Benefits eligibility and application rules vary by state and can change over time.

Mitigation: Use current official program sources and state-specific application pages before giving eligibility or deadline guidance.

Risk: The skill is US-specific and may not apply to users in other jurisdictions.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's country or region and substitute local programs with cited official sources when the user is outside the United States.
