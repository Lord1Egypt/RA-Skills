## Use Case:

External users and developers use Lotus Wisdom to work through complex problems, paradoxes, creative blocks, strategic decisions, philosophical questions, and system design questions through paced reflective dialogue.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is intentionally slow and may be a poor fit for simple factual queries or time-sensitive tasks.

Mitigation: Use it only when paced reflection is desired, and switch to a direct response style when speed or factual retrieval is the priority.

Risk: Skipping the required pause after each step can reduce the quality of the reflective process.

Mitigation: Present one step at a time and wait for the user to explicitly request continuation before proceeding.
