## Description:

Analyzes emotional journey and story structure in long-form content, mapping pacing, engagement, and narrative tension to help strengthen hooks, transitions, conclusions, and series cohesion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ncreighton](https://clawhub.ai/user/ncreighton)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content creators, editors, and marketers use this skill to audit long-form drafts, scripts, email sequences, and guides for narrative pacing, emotional arc, weak transitions, and conclusion payoff before publication.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pasted drafts or transcripts may contain confidential or unpublished business content that is shared with the configured AI provider.

Mitigation: Review content sensitivity before use, redact private material where practical, and follow the configured AI provider's data handling policy.

Risk: Generated rewrites and narrative advice may be unsuitable, inaccurate, or misaligned with the publisher's intent.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as editorial suggestions and require human review before publication.

Risk: The skill analyzes structure and pacing rather than factual accuracy.

Mitigation: Independently fact-check claims, statistics, and domain-specific assertions before relying on the edited content.
