External users and developers use this skill to create structured local GEO plans for local businesses, including location pages, map/listing alignment, review and Q&A programs, structured data, crawler signaling, and measurement loops.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill has broad triggering language and may apply a full local marketing workflow to business questions where the user did not intend local discovery, listings, reviews, or AI-search optimization.

Mitigation: Ask a clarifying question before applying the full workflow unless the user explicitly wants local discovery, map/listing, review, or AI-search optimization advice.

Risk: The skill can produce customer-facing local business recommendations such as hours, service areas, listing descriptions, structured data, and review response drafts that could be misleading if based on incomplete business facts.

Mitigation: Verify names, addresses, phone numbers, hours, service areas, prices, practitioner details, and regulated claims with the business before publishing changes to websites or listings.
