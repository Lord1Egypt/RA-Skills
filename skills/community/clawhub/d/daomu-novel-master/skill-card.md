## Description:

Chinese-language creative-writing orchestration skill that coordinates tomb-adventure novel outlining, drafting, review, revision, and final TXT formatting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kongzichixiangjiao](https://clawhub.ai/user/kongzichixiangjiao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External writers and content creators use this skill to generate or continue Chinese tomb-adventure fiction from concept through outline, manuscript drafts, editorial review, revision, and final TXT delivery.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can autonomously create manuscript, review, and final TXT files in the selected output directory.

Mitigation: Run it in a dedicated output directory and review generated files before publishing or sharing them.

Risk: Generated historical, folklore, feng-shui, or tomb-culture details may be inaccurate or fabricated.

Mitigation: Use editorial and factual review before treating manuscript details as reliable or publication-ready.

Risk: The workflow uses named author personas and genre-style guidance as creative scaffolding.

Mitigation: Review final text for attribution, rights, and undue similarity concerns before commercial publication.
