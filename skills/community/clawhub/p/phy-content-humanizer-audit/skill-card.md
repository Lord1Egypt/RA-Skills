## Description:

Phy Content Humanizer Audit audits social media drafts for eight linguistic signals associated with AI-generated content and reports platform-specific scores, flagged wording, and focused fixes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, social media, and writing teams use this skill to audit post drafts before publishing. It helps identify linguistic patterns, platform threshold risks, and concrete edits for LinkedIn, Reddit, Twitter/X, and Hacker News style content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can help reduce platform AI-detection signals before social posts are published, which may mislead audiences or bypass authenticity policies.

Mitigation: Use it as a writing audit with human review, follow platform rules, and disclose AI assistance when required.
