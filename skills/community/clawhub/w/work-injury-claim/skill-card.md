## Description: <br>
Work Injury Claim helps agents assess Chinese work-injury claim scenarios, build materials checklists, estimate benefits, explain regional rule differences, and draft claim documents. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[wangyue71](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangyue71) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Use for assisting workers or their representatives with work-injury recognition, disability assessment preparation, benefits calculation, evidence organization, and draft claim paperwork. It is not a substitute for legal advice or official determinations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
People's Republic of China, with regional rules varying by province and city. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may process sensitive identity, employment, salary, and medical information. <br>
Mitigation: Use a private output folder, provide only necessary records, and avoid uploading unrelated personal or medical documents. <br>
Risk: Legal deadlines, benefit calculations, and regional rules may be incorrect, outdated, or overstated. <br>
Mitigation: Verify deadlines and calculations with local authorities or a qualified lawyer, and treat generated documents as drafts before filing. <br>
