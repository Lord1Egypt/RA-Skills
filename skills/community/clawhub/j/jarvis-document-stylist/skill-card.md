## Description:

Provides a Russian-language formatting guide for analytical DOCX documents, including page setup, typography, sections, tables, symbols, and pre-submission fact-checking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[claudedamir-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/claudedamir-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and document authors use this skill to apply a consistent Russian-language style guide when drafting or reviewing analytical reference documents in DOCX format.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The formatting assumptions and required fact-checking checklist may not match every document workflow.

Mitigation: Review the style requirements and checklist before applying the skill to a production document.

Risk: Incorrect legal references, dates, figures, or source claims could be carried into an analytical document.

Mitigation: Verify legal citations, dates, numbers, source existence, and cited regulation identifiers before sending the document.
