## Description: <br>
Super Taizi Knowledge Base helps agents store, organize, and retrieve personal knowledge using vector search, entity relationships, and note management. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[subaru0573](https://clawhub.ai/user/subaru0573) <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and knowledge workers use this skill to add, search, and manage local personal knowledge, notes, and indexed files through semantic search and entity relationships. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User-provided notes, text, and selected files may be stored in local knowledge-base directories. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid saving sensitive material unless retention and removal expectations are clear. <br>
Risk: The command examples rely on a local vector_kb.py script. <br>
Mitigation: Verify the referenced local script exists and is trusted before running the examples. <br>
