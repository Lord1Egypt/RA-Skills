## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat textbook-oriented job analysis guidance as legal, compliance, or formal statistical advice for real HR decisions.

Mitigation: Frame responses as learning support and direct users to verify real compliance questions, legal requirements, and formal study designs against current law, professional standards, and qualified reviewers.

Risk: Summarized chapter guidance may omit details needed for a specific course assignment or workplace context.

Mitigation: Encourage users to compare outputs with assigned course materials and provide project-specific constraints before applying recommendations.
