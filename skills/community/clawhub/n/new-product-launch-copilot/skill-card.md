## Description:

Turns a product idea, new SKU, or collection launch into a channel-ready ecommerce launch brief, messaging matrix, readiness checklist, milestone timeline, KPI framework, and risk watchlist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators, founders, growth leads, agencies, and consultants use this skill to turn product or collection launch notes into practical launch planning documents. It supports channel planning, messaging, readiness checks, timelines, KPI framing, risk review, and post-launch learning loops without live commerce or advertising integrations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated launch plans may be mistaken for final go-live approval.

Mitigation: Keep launch readiness, pricing, stock, claims, compliance, and go-live decisions under human review.

Risk: The skill cannot verify live storefront, ad platform, inventory, analytics, or project-management state.

Mitigation: Validate operational readiness against authoritative systems before publishing listings, campaigns, or launch communications.

Risk: Messaging and offer recommendations may be incomplete when product proof, inventory, tracking, or asset details are missing.

Mitigation: Require clear assumptions, confidence notes, and review of proof assets before using generated copy or planning guidance.
