## Description:

Diagnose and score cohesion and flow writing quality in the Results section of English psychology/neuroscience papers across connectives and transitions, sentence-to-sentence cohesion, paragraph coherence, and information flow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bhlia](https://clawhub.ai/user/bhlia)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, students, and academic writing reviewers use this skill to evaluate cohesion and flow in English psychology/neuroscience Results sections. It produces a Chinese-language Markdown report with four dimension scores, quoted problem sentences, revision advice, model rewrites, referrals for out-of-scope issues, and a revision priority table.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expect English-language feedback because the skill reviews English Results sections, while the bundled report template is Chinese.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired report language before use or explicitly request English output when Chinese feedback is not appropriate.

Risk: Model rewrites could become misleading if they are not grounded in the bundled examples and rubric.

Mitigation: Use the bundled rubric and examples when writing revisions, retain provenance labels for real examples, and label constructed examples as constructed.
