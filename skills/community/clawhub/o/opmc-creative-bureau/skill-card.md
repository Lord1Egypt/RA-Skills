## Description:

Opmc Creative Bureau is a Chinese-language SOP agent for moving creative and product ideas from direction setting through MVP planning, business modeling, IP planning, UX design, and developer-ready technical specifications.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, founders, product teams, and developers use this skill to structure creative projects, product MVPs, and startup incubation work into staged planning deliverables. It guides market direction, product strategy, business model analysis, IP considerations, UX flows, technical specifications, review reports, and final project packaging.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce legal, IP, investment, and business-planning guidance that may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific jurisdiction or deal.

Mitigation: Treat those outputs as planning assistance and have qualified professionals verify legal, IP, investment, and commercial decisions before relying on them.

Risk: Generated project documents, technical specifications, and package files may contain errors or mismatches with the user's actual product constraints.

Mitigation: Review the generated Markdown files, API definitions, database schemas, prompts, and deployment plans before development or distribution.
