## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Companion install commands can execute mutable remote packages in sensitive legal and compliance environments.

Mitigation: Pin reviewed versions of `clawhub` and each companion skill, verify publisher identity and package integrity, and approve installs before use.

Risk: Legal-document workflows may expose confidential or regulated information.

Mitigation: Run workflows in a restricted environment with least-privilege access, audit logging, and approved data-handling controls.
