## Description:

Build a temporary access coordination plan for an elevator outage, including affected-person roster, alternative routes, contact assignments, delivery guidance, and resident update card.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Property managers, building staff, front desk teams, event coordinators, and residents use this skill to coordinate temporary access during an elevator outage while keeping emergency situations with official responders or building emergency contacts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat a logistics plan as sufficient for trapped people, injury, distress, fire, smoke, power hazards, or unsafe stair use.

Mitigation: Direct those situations to local emergency services or official building emergency contacts before continuing access coordination.

Risk: Access rosters and notices may collect or expose unnecessary resident-identifying or medical details.

Mitigation: Use the minimum logistics details needed and avoid unnecessary medical or resident-identifying information.

Risk: Temporary routes may conflict with official building instructions or local emergency guidance.

Mitigation: Follow official building instructions and local emergency guidance, and do not recommend unsafe stair use or elevator repair actions.
