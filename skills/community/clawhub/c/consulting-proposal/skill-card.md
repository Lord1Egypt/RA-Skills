## Description:

Writes outcome-focused consulting proposals for client engagements or RFP responses, including the client's problem, approach, deliverables, outcomes, timeline, tiered investment options, proof, and next step.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

External consultants, agencies, and business developers use this skill to draft value-based proposals for client engagements or RFP responses. It helps structure the client problem, outcomes, scope, milestones, investment options, proof, and next step.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Consulting proposals may include confidential client details, regulated data, credentials, or proprietary pricing.

Mitigation: Use only information the user is authorized to process in the agent environment, and omit or anonymize sensitive details when authorization is unclear.
