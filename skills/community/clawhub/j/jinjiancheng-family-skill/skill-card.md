## Description:

Provides concise family, parenting, couple-coordination, household-boundary, and long-term stewardship guidance through a system-first, stage-aware lens inspired by Jin Jiancheng.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to evaluate family relationship, parenting, partner-coordination, household-boundary, and long-term family stewardship questions. It helps turn those questions into concise, stage-aware guidance while staying outside medical, psychological, legal, investment, and asset-allocation advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat family or parenting guidance as medical, psychological, legal, financial, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Keep responses within the skill's family decision-framework scope and refer diagnostic, treatment, legal, financial, or crisis questions to qualified professionals.

Risk: Installation or source integrity may be uncertain because server-resolved provenance is unavailable.

Mitigation: Review the ClawHub release source and pin or otherwise verify the npx installer and repository source before deployment.

Risk: Reviewers or users may miss scope boundaries because the primary skill content is Chinese-language.

Mitigation: Review the Chinese-language README, SKILL.md, and research notes before enabling the skill in settings where professional advice boundaries are important.
