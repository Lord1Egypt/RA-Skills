## Description:

Supports psychology experiment paradigms across cognitive, social, developmental, educational, and clinical domains and generates APA 7th edition Method sections.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zxw-wwwww](https://clawhub.ai/user/zxw-wwwww)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and psychology writers use this skill to draft APA 7th edition Method sections from a research question, experiment paradigm, and variable design. It can optionally include OpenSesame or PsychoPy-style Python pseudocode when requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated sample sizes, ethics wording, materials, and analysis plans may not match the user's actual study or institutional requirements.

Mitigation: Treat generated Method sections as drafts and verify all study details, ethics statements, and analysis plans before use in coursework, theses, or publication.

Risk: The skill may trigger on broad Chinese-language academic-writing prompts.

Mitigation: Use explicit prompts that specify the research question, paradigm, and variable design, and review whether the selected paradigm is appropriate.
