## Description:

Helps founders and builders turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, user-facing positioning, checklists, and practical decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before heavy investment. It helps convert early product questions into repeatable workflows, validation plans, lightweight prototypes, positioning notes, and next-step checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad startup, SaaS, prototype, or validation requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's actual product-validation need before relying on the workflow or deliverable.

Risk: Demand evidence appears noisy and should not be treated as independent market proof.

Mitigation: Review outputs before using them for business decisions and validate conclusions against direct customer or market evidence.
