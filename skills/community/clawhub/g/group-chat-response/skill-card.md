## Description:

Guides an agent in group chats to respond only when directly addressed, clearly identified by role, or relevant to an open capability request.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ebly](https://clawhub.ai/user/ebly)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Agents participating in shared group-chat environments use this skill to decide when to answer and when to stay silent, reducing accidental interruptions and duplicate responses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Incorrect identity names or role labels can cause the agent to stay silent when it should answer, or answer when another agent was intended.

Mitigation: Review configured identity names, aliases, and role labels before deploying the skill in a group-chat environment.
