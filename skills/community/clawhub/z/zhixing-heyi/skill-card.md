## Description:

A Chinese-language self-improvement skill that combines Wang Yangming's unity of knowledge and action, Adlerian purpose analysis, minimal-action planning, and life gamification prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chase-zhouchang](https://clawhub.ai/user/chase-zhouchang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to examine why intentions do not become action and to produce self-reflection prompts, minimal-action plans, journaling structures, and life gamification frameworks. It is intended as educational self-improvement guidance, not medical or mental-health advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Health-adjacent material about addiction, dopamine, ADHD, withdrawal, and cold exposure may be mistaken for medical or mental-health advice.

Mitigation: Frame those sections as unverified educational content and direct users experiencing addiction, severe anxiety, insomnia, trauma, self-harm thoughts, or other distress to qualified professional support.

Risk: The one-day reset and deep self-examination prompts can be emotionally intensive for vulnerable users.

Mitigation: Use opt-in pacing, encourage users to pause or stop when distressed, and avoid pressuring completion in a single session.
