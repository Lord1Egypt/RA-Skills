## Description:

Helps teams amplify approved creator content through paid media or repurpose it into rights-tracked assets for ads, website, email, and organic channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, influencer, and growth teams use this skill to decide which approved creator posts to boost, plan whitelisting or Spark Ads, allocate paid budgets, and turn existing UGC into multi-channel assets. It assumes content is already cleared and routes publishability, disclosure, and claim-compliance checks to an auditor skill.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce paid ad spend recommendations based on incomplete or estimated campaign data.

Mitigation: Confirm budget, performance metrics, and platform assumptions before launching or scaling paid campaigns.

Risk: The skill may process sensitive marketing inputs such as creator metrics, rights terms, CRM audiences, or ad-platform performance when the user provides them.

Mitigation: Share only data approved for agent use and approve memory saves only for information that may be reused later.

Risk: Repurposing creator content without confirmed rights can create commercial, legal, or platform-policy exposure.

Mitigation: Confirm usage rights and expiration dates before recommending paid, website, email, or other reuse.
