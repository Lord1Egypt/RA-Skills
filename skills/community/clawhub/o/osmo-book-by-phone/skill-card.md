## Description:

OsmO calls local businesses to book, reschedule, or cancel reservations and appointments after the user approves the call.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[getosmo](https://clawhub.ai/user/getosmo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can ask the agent to contact a restaurant or local business to make, change, or cancel a reservation or appointment, or ask a brief question by phone.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global; 50 free call credits are offered in the US, Canada, UK, and Australia.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill uses a hosted OsmO service and requires sign-in with a phone number.

Mitigation: Use it only if you trust the service and are comfortable signing in; review the business and call details, and approve before the call is placed.
