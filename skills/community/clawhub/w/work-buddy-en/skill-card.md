Provide natural, high-signal proactive companionship during work hours without becoming noisy or intrusive.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

People who want a calmer workday assistant use this skill to receive brief, context-aware check-ins, soft reminders, and small useful updates without turning proactive help into noise.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Unsolicited proactive check-ins may distract users or feel intrusive if the user has not opted into that style.

Mitigation: Activate the skill only for users who want occasional workday companionship, and configure or instruct the assistant to keep messages rare and useful.

Risk: A companionship tone can become too familiar, repetitive, or emotionally pressuring if applied without restraint.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's boundaries: avoid manipulative wording, do not pressure replies, and prefer silence when a message has no clear value.
