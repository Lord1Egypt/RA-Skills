## Description:

Sales Winner Assistant helps Yonyou sales teams research target accounts, analyze business and IT buying signals, and produce account plans, win strategies, sales scripts, and follow-up actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[areskaks777](https://clawhub.ai/user/areskaks777)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales employees use this skill to build Yonyou-centered account profiles, identify sales opportunities, prepare competitive positioning, draft outreach, and plan follow-up actions for enterprise software opportunities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can expand customer requests into profiling, sales plans, CRM-like status updates, reminders, notifications, and procurement guidance without clear approval.

Mitigation: Require human review before acting on customer profiles, CRM updates, reminders, notifications, procurement recommendations, or tender guidance.

Risk: The skill may process personal contact details or sensitive account information during customer analysis and follow-up planning.

Mitigation: Use approved data sources, minimize personal or confidential data entered into the agent, and follow applicable internal privacy and customer-data handling rules.

Risk: The skill is intentionally Yonyou-centered and may produce biased competitive positioning.

Mitigation: Review claims, comparisons, and recommended talking points for factual accuracy, fairness, and compliance before sharing them with customers.
