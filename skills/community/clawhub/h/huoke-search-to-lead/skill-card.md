## Description:

Finds public Douyin videos, creators, comments, and trends for content research, competitor analysis, and lead generation, returning structured JSON.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[engheng-art](https://clawhub.ai/user/engheng-art)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Sales, growth, content, and market research teams use it to find public Douyin posts and creators, analyze comments and trends, and organize potential leads.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and supplied Douyin links or IDs are sent to the third-party Guaikei service.

Mitigation: Use non-sensitive queries and public links, and review the data-sharing implications before running a request.

Risk: The security scan was partial, and some disclosures may be unclear.

Mitigation: Review the full skill documentation and its declared behavior before installation.

Risk: Public comments and creator data can be used to identify potential leads.

Mitigation: Keep analysis focused on public data; do not infer private contact details or use the results for unsolicited or harassing outreach.
