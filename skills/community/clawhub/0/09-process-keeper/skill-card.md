## Description:

Records key decisions, bug fixes, turning points, and task context during product development so they can be reused as structured workflow logs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and product teams use this skill to capture task goals, current state, key files, technology context, decisions, fixes, lessons learned, and reusable process patterns during development work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated process logs may capture secrets, file paths, customer information, incident details, or sensitive debugging context.

Mitigation: Review generated logs and remove sensitive information before committing or sharing them.
