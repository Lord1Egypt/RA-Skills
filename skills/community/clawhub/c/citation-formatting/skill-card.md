## Description:

Formats literature metadata for books, journal articles, theses, webpages, conference papers, and newspaper articles into GB/T 7714-2015 or APA 7 reference entries and in-text citations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[realhhhelena-oss](https://clawhub.ai/user/realhhhelena-oss)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, editors, and writing assistants use this skill to normalize incomplete or inconsistent citation data into GB/T 7714-2015 or APA 7 reference entries and matching in-text citations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may apply the wrong citation style or edition when the user does not specify one.

Mitigation: Specify the exact citation style, edition, language, and any institution-specific rules before relying on the formatted output.

Risk: Incomplete citation inputs can produce placeholders or incomplete references.

Mitigation: Review placeholders such as missing author, year, page range, DOI, URL, or access date and supply authoritative bibliographic details before submission.
