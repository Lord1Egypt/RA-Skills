## Description:

When the user wants to create, optimize, or audit a feedback or roadmap page.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and site authors use this skill to plan or audit feedback and roadmap pages that collect feature requests, communicate product direction, and guide integrations with tools such as Canny, FeatureBase, or UserVoice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read local project-context files containing sensitive roadmap or product planning details.

Mitigation: Keep project-context files scoped to information appropriate for feedback-page planning before invoking the skill.

Risk: Broad roadmap or feedback wording may activate the skill in conversations where detailed page planning is not intended.

Mitigation: Confirm that feedback or roadmap page guidance is desired before applying its recommendations.
