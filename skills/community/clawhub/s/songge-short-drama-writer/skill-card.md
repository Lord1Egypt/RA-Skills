## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad creative-writing requests may be steered into the short-drama or AI manga workflow and may include ViFlow JSON exports by default.

Mitigation: Confirm the desired format before use and ask the agent to omit ViFlow JSON when a plain script, outline, or brainstorming response is sufficient.

Risk: Generated scripts may include intense conflict, violence, revenge, or other platform-sensitive story elements from the hook and genre templates.

Mitigation: Review and revise generated content against the target platform's content, advertising, and age-rating policies before publication.

Risk: Market statistics and platform-policy claims in the artifact may become outdated or may not match the user's distribution channel.

Mitigation: Validate commercial decisions against current platform analytics, publisher rules, and market research before relying on generated recommendations.
