## Description:

Provides Chinese-language guidance on the foundational assumptions, boundaries, and common objections of technical analysis, especially when users question whether technical analysis works or how it differs from fundamental analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to answer educational questions about the philosophy of technical analysis, including its three core assumptions, its distinction from fundamental analysis, and when chart-based reasoning may fail. It is not intended to produce specific trading signals or financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake the skill's educational discussion of technical-analysis assumptions for financial advice or specific trading signals.

Mitigation: Present the material as educational context, state that it does not provide buy or sell signals, and redirect signal requests to appropriate analysis workflows.

Risk: Users may overstate technical-analysis premises as certainty, especially the assumptions that prices reflect all information or that history repeats.

Mitigation: Emphasize the documented boundaries: the assumptions are empirical premises, can fail under distorted market conditions, and should not be treated as guaranteed predictions.
