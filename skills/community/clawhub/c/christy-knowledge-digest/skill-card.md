## Description:

Converts textbooks or PDFs into personalized, multimodal interactive learning materials including handwritten notes, quiz webpages, slides, audio courses, and mind maps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ericn26-star](https://clawhub.ai/user/ericn26-star)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Educators, students, and learning-content creators use Knowledge Digest to transform uploaded textbooks, PDFs, or topic descriptions into study assets tailored to grade level and learner interests.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Uploaded learning materials may be processed by the active agent and tools.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with documents you are comfortable sharing with the active agent and configured tools.

Risk: Search-based courseware may include externally sourced material that needs confirmation before use.

Mitigation: Review the compiled courseware and continue only after confirming it is usable for the learning task.

Risk: Generated notes, quizzes, slides, mind maps, or audio may be misleading if source parsing or concept extraction is wrong.

Mitigation: Check generated study assets against the original materials before relying on them for teaching or assessment.
