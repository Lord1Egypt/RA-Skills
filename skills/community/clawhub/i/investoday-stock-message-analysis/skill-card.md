## Description:

Analyzes recent A-share stock news, event sentiment, institutional validation, and market feedback using Investoday finance data to produce structured message analysis reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kenneth-bro](https://clawhub.ai/user/kenneth-bro)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and financial-analysis agents use this skill to identify recent A-share stock events, assess whether news is bullish, bearish, or neutral, and compare those events with institutional views and market response. The output is informational market analysis and not investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stock names or codes from user requests are sent to the Investoday finance-data integration.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when sharing those stock identifiers with the integration is acceptable.

Risk: Financial news and market-response analysis may be incomplete, delayed, or unsuitable as a basis for trading decisions.

Mitigation: Treat the report as informational analysis only and review primary disclosures, market data, and qualified financial advice before making decisions.
