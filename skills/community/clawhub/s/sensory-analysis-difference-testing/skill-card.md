## Use Case:

Food product developers, sensory analysts, and quality teams use this skill to plan and interpret sensory difference tests under GB/T 10220-2012/ISO 6658:2005, including paired comparison, triangle, duo-trio, two-out-of-five, and A-not-A methods.

Risk: Users may treat sensory difference testing guidance or significance-table lookups as final decisions without expert review.
