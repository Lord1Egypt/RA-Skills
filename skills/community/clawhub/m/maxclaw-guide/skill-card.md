## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Telegram bot tokens can grant control over the connected bot if exposed.

Mitigation: Paste the token only into the intended MaxClaw channel setup page, do not share it in chats, screenshots, logs, or support messages, and rotate it in BotFather if exposure is suspected.

Risk: MaxClaw is described as a persistent cloud agent with scheduled automation and tool access.

Mitigation: Review configured channels, scheduled tasks, and tool permissions before relying on recurring automation.
