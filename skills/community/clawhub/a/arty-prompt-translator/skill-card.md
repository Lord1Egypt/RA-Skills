## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Creative outputs may raise rights, trademark, likeness, or factual-claim issues before public use.

Mitigation: Review generated prompts and resulting assets for rights, approvals, and factual claims before publishing or using them commercially.

Risk: Prompts that request exact text inside generated images may produce unreliable typography.

Mitigation: Add exact text later in a design tool when typography, spelling, or brand treatment must be precise.
