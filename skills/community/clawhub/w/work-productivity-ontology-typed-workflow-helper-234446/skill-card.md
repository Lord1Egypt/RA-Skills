## Description:

Helps agent users and skill authors create ontology-style productivity workflows, fix bugs, harden setup and safety, improve reliability, and produce repeatable artifacts or checklists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, maintainers, skill authors, and teams use this skill to turn ontology-style work-productivity requests into concrete plans, artifacts, checklists, code changes, and validation notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad keywords and implicit invocation may make the skill activate for loosely related workflow, knowledge-graph, memory, or bug-fix requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's requested outcome and constraints before producing artifacts, and avoid applying the skill when the request is outside ontology-style productivity or typed workflow support.
