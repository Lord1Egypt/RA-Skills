## Description:

Write concise, implementation-oriented functional PRDs with structured interview, quality checklist, and common pitfalls guide.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jasonsongxd-kidd](https://clawhub.ai/user/jasonsongxd-kidd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, designers, and developers use this skill to produce implementation-ready functional PRDs for feature development. It guides requirement clarification, scope sizing, structured PRD writing, and quality checks for testable behavior, interfaces, data rules, permissions, and edge cases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated PRD may be incomplete or misleading when the user provides underspecified requirements.

Mitigation: Use the skill's requirement clarification step and final checklist before treating the PRD as implementation-ready.

Risk: Chinese-first output may not fit teams that require English documentation.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for English output when an English PRD is required.
