## Description:

Target Discovery guides reverse target discovery for compound libraries from SMILES inputs, combining ADMET screening, scaffold and similarity analysis, target validation, FTO patent review, and SAR extraction into prioritized target and compound-optimization recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External drug discovery teams, academic labs, CROs, and pharmaceutical researchers use this skill to assess compound libraries, form target hypotheses, identify patent and safety risks, and prioritize follow-up optimization paths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Compound libraries and SMILES inputs may contain sensitive business or research data when sent to referenced PatSnap/Zhihuiya services.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with approved accounts and data-handling expectations, and avoid submitting proprietary compounds unless that use is authorized.

Risk: Generated target hypotheses, FTO assessments, and SAR recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for research, legal, or investment decisions if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Verify conclusions against cited sources and have qualified scientific and legal reviewers confirm target, patent, and SAR findings before acting on them.
