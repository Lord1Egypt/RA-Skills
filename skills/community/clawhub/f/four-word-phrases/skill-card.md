## Description:

A Chinese-language English learning helper that presents categorized common four-word English phrases for daily conversation, emotional expression, social etiquette, and work or study practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aadebuger](https://clawhub.ai/user/aadebuger)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and language learners use this skill to browse, group, and practice common four-word English phrases with Chinese explanations across everyday, social, emotional, and study or work scenarios.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad English-learning requests.

Mitigation: Review trigger wording and phrase quality before deployment when precise activation behavior is important.

Risk: Phrase guidance could be incomplete or contextually imperfect for a learner's intended situation.

Mitigation: Base responses on the bundled phrase reference and review selected phrases for the user's requested scenario.
