## Use Case:

External e-commerce sellers and agents can use the skill to generate product profitability reports, cost breakdowns, break-even analysis, and pricing suggestions. Review outputs carefully because security evidence reports an Amazon/FBA-oriented implementation behind the TikTok Shop positioning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could receive misleading TikTok Shop pricing guidance because the shipped calculator is Amazon/FBA-oriented.

Mitigation: Do not rely on the skill for TikTok Shop pricing until the publisher fixes the implementation or clearly re-scopes it; pin and verify the installer package and avoid global installation when testing.
