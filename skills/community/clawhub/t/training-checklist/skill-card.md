## Description:

Training Checklist helps users review training preparation across venues, hotels, equipment, instructors, teaching materials, and logistics, then produce item-level status checks and weekly progress summaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Training organizers and operations teams use this skill to turn a training plan into a staged preparation checklist, track completion, flag missing work, and summarize weekly progress before an event.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generic reminder and checklist trigger phrases may route some unrelated planning conversations to this skill.

Mitigation: Use narrower trigger phrases or confirm that the user is asking about training preparation before applying the checklist.

Risk: Generated preparation status and weekly summaries may be incomplete or misleading if the organizer has not verified the underlying plan details.

Mitigation: Have the training owner review each checklist item, deadline, responsible role, and progress summary before acting on the output.
