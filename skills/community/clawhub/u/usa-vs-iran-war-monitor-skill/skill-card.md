## Use Case:

External users, analysts, and safety teams use this skill to monitor public USA-Iran conflict-risk developments, check rumors, and produce confidence-rated alerts or daily briefs focused on civilian safety, GCC/Kuwait implications, cyber defense, and business continuity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Conflict or attack claims may be false, outdated, propagandistic, or based on recycled media.

Mitigation: Cross-check public claims against official or primary sources and credible independent reporting; label unresolved claims as unconfirmed.

Risk: Briefs could be mistaken for certainty about military events or for trading or investment advice.

Mitigation: Use confidence labels, state unknowns, focus on civilian safety and business continuity, and avoid financial recommendations.

Risk: Cyber-tension updates could drift into offensive cyber guidance.

Mitigation: Limit cyber recommendations to defensive hygiene and incident readiness; do not provide retaliation, disruption, doxxing, or unauthorized testing steps.
