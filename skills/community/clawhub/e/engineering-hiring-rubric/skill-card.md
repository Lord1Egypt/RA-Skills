## Description:

Builds an engineering hiring rubric and technical interview scorecard for evaluating software engineers at a specific level, including a scorecard, behavioral question bank, technical question set with evaluation criteria, system design rubric, and debrief agenda.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Recruiting teams, hiring managers, and engineering interviewers use this skill to create calibrated software engineering interview loops, rubrics, scorecards, and debrief formats for a specified role, level, team context, and tech stack.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Hiring rubrics and scorecards can affect sensitive employment decisions if used without review for role fit, fairness, or policy compliance.

Mitigation: Treat generated rubrics and scorecards as drafts and have qualified humans review them against company policy, legal requirements, and fairness expectations before use.
