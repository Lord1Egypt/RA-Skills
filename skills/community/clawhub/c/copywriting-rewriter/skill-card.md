## Use Case:

External marketers, product teams, and writers use this skill to turn technical or sales-heavy drafts into clear Chinese-facing commercial copy for websites, product descriptions, and CTA text.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Non-Chinese copy may be rewritten with Chinese-oriented terminology, examples, or tone assumptions.

Mitigation: State the desired output language explicitly and review terminology choices for non-Chinese use.

Risk: Marketing rewrites may soften or overstate product claims, especially around security, privacy, and ease of use.

Mitigation: Review factual claims, safety claims, and CTA text against the source product before publication.
