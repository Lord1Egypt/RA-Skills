## Description:

Helps solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, and user-facing positioning before heavy investment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It turns rough product ideas into practical validation workflows, checklists, templates, lightweight analysis, prototypes, and positioning support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger terms may cause the skill to activate for general business or SaaS requests where a more specific skill would fit better.

Mitigation: Review trigger terms during installation and prefer a more specific skill when the request has a narrower domain.

Risk: Product validation outputs may be incomplete when the user provides limited context, constraints, or success criteria.

Mitigation: Restate assumptions, ask only for missing information that materially changes the output, and validate the result against visible success criteria.

Risk: Planning artifacts can be mistaken for confirmed market evidence.

Mitigation: Use the generated workflow, checklist, prototype, or positioning as decision support and verify demand with real user or customer signals before heavy investment.
