## Description:

Aggregates and summarizes the latest AI news from multiple sources, including AI news websites and web search, and provides concise news briefs with direct links to original articles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nicky1108](https://clawhub.ai/user/nicky1108)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to gather recent AI news, filter and categorize relevant stories, and receive a concise daily briefing with source links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill fetches and summarizes current AI news from public websites, so briefings can reflect inaccurate, stale, paywalled, or duplicated source material.

Mitigation: Review cited links and timestamps before relying on summaries; use source diversity and deduplication as described by the skill.

Risk: Broad requests such as AI updates may trigger web searches across public sources.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill for explicit daily AI briefings, or set a topic focus and timeframe when tighter control is needed.
