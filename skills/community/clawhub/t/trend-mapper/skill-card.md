## Description:

Identify trending audio, viral formats, and meme templates relevant to a product category and adapt them into ecommerce content briefs quickly.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[leooooooow](https://clawhub.ai/user/leooooooow)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Social commerce marketers, ecommerce founders, and content teams use this skill to assess short-form video trends, decide whether to participate, and produce product-specific adaptation briefs with scripts, shot lists, timing guidance, and performance benchmarks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad ecommerce trend-planning requests and influence public social content decisions.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against brand guidelines, product fit, trend origin, sensitive associations, and platform context before posting.

Risk: Trend timing and saturation guidance depends on current manual observations and live platform conditions.

Mitigation: Verify current use counts, niche saturation, creator examples, and competitor activity before committing production resources.
