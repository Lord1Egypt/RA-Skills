## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sponsored TikTok ads may be written to appear unscripted or accidental without clear disclosure.

Mitigation: Use the generated scripts only for transparent, policy-compliant sponsored content with required disclosures and truthful claims.

Risk: Generated advertising claims may overstate product outcomes or create misleading expectations.

Mitigation: Review all hooks, benefits, testimonials, and production notes against product evidence, platform policies, and applicable advertising rules before publishing.
