## Description:

Build and execute a content marketing strategy for a solopreneur business.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[marjoriebroad](https://clawhub.ai/user/marjoriebroad)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and solopreneurs use this skill to plan content goals, research audience needs, choose formats and channels, build content calendars, distribute and repurpose content, and measure performance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill recommends publishing, emailing, or direct messaging as part of content distribution.

Mitigation: Review and execute any public posting, email, or outreach recommendations manually.

Risk: Content strategy advice may not fit every audience, channel, or business context.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against the user's actual customer research, platform norms, and performance metrics before acting.
