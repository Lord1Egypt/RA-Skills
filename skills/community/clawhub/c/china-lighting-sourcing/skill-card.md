## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sourcing figures, supplier examples, certifications, prices, and regulations may become outdated or vary by market.

Mitigation: Use the skill as reference guidance and verify current supplier credentials, certificates, pricing, and import requirements before making business decisions.

Risk: Cluster-level guidance is not a substitute for due diligence on individual suppliers.

Mitigation: Perform supplier audits, sample testing, certificate checks, and third-party inspections before placing orders.
