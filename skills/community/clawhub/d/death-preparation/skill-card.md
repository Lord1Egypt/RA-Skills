## Description:

Practical end-of-life preparation and philosophical mortality awareness for wills, advance directives, digital legacy, funeral preferences, family conversations, and mortality reflection.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize end-of-life planning tasks, including advance directives, wills, digital legacy, funeral preferences, and conversations with family or trusted contacts. It also supports mortality-awareness reflection while directing users toward professional help for complex legal or mental-health needs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may involve highly sensitive end-of-life documents, account lists, passwords, or digital legacy information.

Mitigation: Keep control over where files are saved and do not paste master passwords, private keys, or complete account credentials into the agent.

Risk: Optional annual or proactive reminders could surface sensitive death-planning context unexpectedly.

Mitigation: Enable reminders only when the user explicitly wants them and understands what information may be retained or resurfaced.

Risk: End-of-life planning guidance can be jurisdiction-specific and may not replace professional legal, medical, or mental-health support.

Mitigation: Use the skill as planning guidance, verify local requirements, and seek professional help for complex estates, medical directives, persistent anxiety, depression, or crisis situations.
