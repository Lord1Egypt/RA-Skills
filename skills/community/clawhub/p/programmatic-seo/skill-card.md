## Use Case:

External users, developers, marketers, and SEO practitioners use this skill to plan programmatic SEO pages, choose page patterns, define data requirements, design templates, and reduce thin-content and indexation risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad SEO or landing-page requests may invoke the skill even when the user needs a different SEO workflow.

Mitigation: Confirm the request is about planning SEO-driven pages at scale; defer technical audits to the referenced SEO audit workflow when appropriate.

Risk: Programmatic page recommendations can lead to thin, duplicate, or low-value pages if templates only swap variables.

Mitigation: Require unique page value, search-intent fit, defensible data, internal linking, indexation checks, and noindex handling for thin variations.

Risk: The skill may read local product-marketing context when present.

Mitigation: Review local marketing context for sensitive business information before reusing or sharing generated outputs.
