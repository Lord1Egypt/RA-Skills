## Description:

写大众点评风格的本地生活评论。当用户发餐厅/菜品照片说"写点评"、"帮我评价"、"写个大众点评"、"生成点评文案"时使用。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn restaurant or dish photos and supplemental dining details into natural, conversational local-life review copy in the style of Dianping. The skill asks for missing context such as location, environment, service, shortcomings, and occasion, then offers multiple review versions for the user to choose from.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may infer restaurant type, price range, service details, or other dining claims from photos and partial user input.

Mitigation: Review and correct generated claims before posting publicly, especially inferred price, restaurant type, service details, and shortcomings.
