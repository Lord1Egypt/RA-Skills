## Description:

Provides factual answers to questions using authoritative web sources, citing references and including relevant images for clarity and trustworthiness.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jkobject](https://clawhub.ai/user/jkobject)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and developers use Source Scout to answer factual questions with web-grounded citations and, when helpful, sourced images or figures.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Factual prompts may send search terms to external web services.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for prompts appropriate for external search, and avoid sending sensitive or private data unless that use is authorized.

Risk: Image-enhanced answers may download sourced images into the local workspace.

Mitigation: Review source pages and licensing before reuse, and inspect downloaded files before sharing or retaining them.
