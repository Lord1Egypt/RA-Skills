## Description:

Helps users turn rough intentions into well-specified autonomous-mode goals with clear completion criteria, boundaries, proof, and stop rules.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wlykan](https://clawhub.ai/user/wlykan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and agent operators use this skill to convert vague or rough requests into actionable autonomous-mode goals with verifiable completion criteria, boundaries, and optional budgets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad goal-planning or vague decomposition requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intended goal-planning scope before applying the structured goal workflow.
