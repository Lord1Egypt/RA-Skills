## Description:

Performs a preliminary cross-border patent and design-right risk screen for an e-commerce or consumer product using product images, a product page, or a technical description.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers, importers, manufacturers, product teams, and IP analysts use this skill to triage patent, utility-model, design-patent, registered-design, FTO, product-launch, and market-entry risk across target markets. It supports evidence-backed preliminary screening and design-around planning, not legal clearance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Product images, technical specifications, supplier details, or product-page evidence may be confidential or subject to external provider handling terms.

Mitigation: Use only authorized product evidence, avoid uploading confidential material without approval, and confirm acceptable handling before connecting PatSnap MCP services.

Risk: Preliminary patent and design-right research can be mistaken for launch clearance or a legal opinion.

Mitigation: Label outputs as preliminary triage and route claim mapping, design comparisons, legal status, ownership, and jurisdiction-specific conclusions to qualified patent counsel.

Risk: Incomplete product views, unavailable live tools, database gaps, translations, or status uncertainty can leave material rights unreviewed.

Mitigation: Record evidence limitations, target markets, search dates, tools executed, and missing inputs; when live tools are unavailable, provide a not-executed search plan instead of inventing results.
