## Description:

Generate and prioritize experimentally testable target hypotheses from one or more small-molecule structures.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yuanzhian-patsnap](https://clawhub.ai/user/yuanzhian-patsnap)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External researchers, chemists, and drug discovery teams use this skill to triage small-molecule structures into evidence-backed target hypotheses, validation experiments, SAR questions, and patent-review questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may submit confidential compound structures or decision context to external services.

Mitigation: Confirm authorization and confidentiality requirements before using configured external services.

Risk: Outputs may be mistaken for legal, clinical, safety, efficacy, or freedom-to-operate determinations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research hypotheses and patent-review questions, and require qualified legal review and experimental validation before decisions.
