## Description:

Mao Emperors is a Chinese-language multi-agent roleplay skill that frames strategic planning, contradiction analysis, organizational design, and decision support through Maoist philosophy and historical Chinese emperor personas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[threethreeliu](https://clawhub.ai/user/threethreeliu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Developers and external users can use this skill to brainstorm strategy, organizational design, conflict analysis, career planning, and execution plans through five historical personas. Its outputs should be treated as structured decision-support drafts, not authoritative policy, legal, security, or workplace-discipline advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Political, ideological, punitive, or historical roleplay language may be mistaken for operational advice about coercion, violence, retaliation, or workplace discipline.

Mitigation: Use the personas as brainstorming frames only; require human review and reject any output that recommends real-world coercion, violence, retaliation, or improper disciplinary action.

Risk: The skill is designed for decision-support roleplay and may produce unsupported or outdated factual assumptions.

Mitigation: Verify factual claims, current conditions, and high-impact recommendations with trusted sources before acting on the output.
