## Description:

Helps agents verify content claims before publication by finding load-bearing claims, tracing them to primary sources, catching zombie stats and AI-hallucinated citations, and preparing attribution.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content teams, social media operators, and agents use this skill to make stat-heavy or AI-assisted drafts publish-ready by checking claims, source chains, citation honesty, freshness, and attribution before scheduling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: High-stakes health, legal, tax, or financial claims may be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Keep the skill's human-review expectations and disclaimers, use official or peer-reviewed primary sources, qualify claims carefully, and involve qualified professionals where publication risk warrants it.

Risk: The agent may be unable to fully verify claims when web search, paywalled sources, or source access is unavailable.

Mitigation: State verification limits clearly, provide a source-by-source checklist, and require a human to open and confirm unresolved links before scheduling.

Risk: Incorrect, stale, retracted, or fabricated citations could be published if the verification protocol is skipped.

Mitigation: Use the FACTS workflow before publication: inventory load-bearing claims, ascend to primary sources, check freshness and retractions, verify that each source says the claimed point, and maintain a source log.
