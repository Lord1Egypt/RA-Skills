External users and social media researchers use this skill to research TikTok content and creators through post search, comment analysis, and creator lookups.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment flags repeated use of an automatically installed, unpinned latest CLI package as a supply-chain risk involving credentials and local access; its analysis is partial because three local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Pin the CLI to a reviewed version and obtain it through a verified installation. If installed, review credential storage and limit the credentials and local access available to the process.
