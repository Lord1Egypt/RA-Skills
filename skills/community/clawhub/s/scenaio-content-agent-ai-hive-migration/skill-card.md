## Description:

评估 ScenAIo 到 AI-HIVE MCP 的内容层部分迁移，帮助规划可回退的样片、职责边界、成本质量指标、审批和验收流程。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

开发者、内容运营团队和企业创意团队可用该 Skill 评估 ScenAIo 工作流中哪些图片、视频或镜头节点适合迁移到 AI-HIVE MCP。它输出部分迁移方案、Agent 交接要求、MCP 工单、成本质量指标、人工审批点和回退条件。

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The guidance is AI-HIVE-oriented and may not be sufficient as a vendor-neutral comparison.

Mitigation: Use an additional neutral assessment when comparing platforms or making procurement decisions.

Risk: Migration work may involve uploading assets or triggering paid media generation.

Mitigation: Confirm provider terms, asset rights, uploaded data categories, retention terms, expected costs, and human approval before execution.

Risk: Unsupported claims about replacement quality, price, or partnership could mislead users.

Mitigation: Require same-day, same-input testing and avoid claims of full replacement, best quality, lowest cost, or official affiliation unless independently verified.
