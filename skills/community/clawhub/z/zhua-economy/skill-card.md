## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks an agent to handle pricing, transactions, and revenue distribution with broad autonomy and little scoping or user-control guidance.

Mitigation: Use it only for planning unless explicit authorization, validation, dry-run behavior, audit logging, and clear limits on what the agent may change are added.

Risk: The artifact appears to be a placeholder economy-planning skill rather than a working implementation.

Mitigation: Do not use it for real transactions or revenue allocation until the workflows and referenced scripts are implemented and reviewed.
