## Description:

Meeting Notes Generator helps agents convert uploaded PDF or Word meeting transcripts into structured, detailed meeting minutes with validation for coverage, word count, and formatting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[crayfish153](https://clawhub.ai/user/crayfish153)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, external users, and developers can use this skill to turn meeting transcript documents into organized meeting minutes, key points, decisions, and action items. It is suited to workflows that need detailed notes from PDF or Word source material.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting transcripts may be sent to DeepSeek without clear consent or data-protection controls.

Mitigation: Use only with appropriate participant consent, redaction, and data-retention controls; avoid confidential, regulated, HR, legal, customer, or credential-containing meetings unless those controls are in place.

Risk: The skill asks the agent to guarantee complete coverage and a minimum length, which can overstate the reliability of generated notes.

Mitigation: Review the generated notes against the original transcript, especially decisions, action items, names, dates, and numeric details.
