## Description:

Technical support communication: draft or revise customer and stakeholder messages, de-escalate tense interactions, coach empathic listening, or apply IT service people skills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and support teams use this skill to draft or revise technical support messages, de-escalate tense interactions, and turn confirmed incident facts into respectful, action-oriented replies. It can also coach empathic listening and communication self-assessment without treating coaching models as guarantees.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be asked to draft messages when facts, owners, causes, or timelines are incomplete.

Mitigation: Use the documented fact-boundary checkpoint: ask for material missing facts, preserve unknowns explicitly, and review any send-ready message before use.

Risk: The skill may read referenced local files or user-provided drafts when invoked.

Mitigation: Provide only relevant support context and avoid pointing the agent at unrelated private material.

Risk: Communication coaching frameworks may be overread as personality judgments or guaranteed outcomes.

Mitigation: Apply frameworks only to observable behavior and state that coaching models are not predictive guarantees.
