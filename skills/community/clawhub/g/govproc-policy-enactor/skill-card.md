## Description:

采购人/采购代理机构编制政府采购文件时，合法落实中小企业扶持、绿色采购、特殊群体视同等政策红利，输出可直接粘贴的条款模板与合规红线。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Government purchasers and procurement agencies use this skill to draft compliant Chinese government-procurement policy benefit clauses, including SME reservations or price preferences, green procurement, mandatory energy-saving procurement, special-group equivalence, and related red-line checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China / PRC government-procurement workflows

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated procurement text may include attribution, feedback text, or related-tool suggestions that are inappropriate for official documents.

Mitigation: Review generated content and remove attribution, feedback, and related-tool suggestion text before pasting into official procurement documents when those elements are not appropriate.

Risk: The skill is tailored to Chinese/PRC government-procurement workflows and may not fit other legal regimes.

Mitigation: Use it only for applicable PRC government-procurement contexts and route other jurisdictions or legal regimes to qualified local procurement or legal review.
