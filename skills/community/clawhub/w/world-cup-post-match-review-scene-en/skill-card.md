## Description:

Used to generate post-match reviews of World Cup football matches, and supports standardized scene input, post-match information retrieval and fan group notification extraction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to generate concise World Cup football match reviews, summaries, best-player notes, tactical observations, and post-match talking points for sharing in fan communities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can read local notifications and group-chat content when producing hot-topic summaries.

Mitigation: Use explicit application, group, and time limits, and avoid notification-based summaries when private banking, work, health, or personal messages may be present.

Risk: Post-match reviews can be misleading if live or uncertain match information is treated as final.

Mitigation: Confirm match status before review generation and label unfinished-match outputs as current or pre-game information rather than final conclusions.
