## Description:

Assists users in drafting or revising ISO 9001-aligned quality management system policies using strategic inputs, clause coverage checks, industry examples, KPI alignment, and review reminders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Quality, compliance, and operations teams use this skill to draft new QMS quality policies or revise existing policies for ISO 9001 and related management-system contexts. It helps align policy language to organization strategy, customer concerns, process risks, KPI paths, and management review needs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: ISO clause and template guidance may be incomplete if referenced supporting files are absent or not independently checked.

Mitigation: Verify applicable standard requirements and provide or review the referenced materials before relying on outputs for audits.

Risk: Generated policy drafts may be mistaken for approved quality policy or certification evidence.

Mitigation: Require review by responsible quality or compliance personnel and formal top-management approval before publication or audit use.
