# Insurance Claim Organizer

## Overview

Claims are won on documentation assembled early. This workflow creates the claim file in the first day: the incident record written while memory is fresh, every document gathered against a checklist, and a follow-up cadence so the claim never dies of neglect.

## When to use

After property damage, theft, travel disruption, or a medical event involving insurance.

## Workflow

1. **Write the incident record today** — what happened, when, where, who was involved, in factual language with no speculation about fault
2. **Photograph and preserve** — damage, documents, receipts; originals kept, copies submitted
3. **Open the claim and record the identifiers** — claim number, adjuster name and contact, and every deadline they state
4. **Build the document checklist** — policy, proof of loss, receipts/estimates, photos, reports (police/medical), correspondence
5. **Log every contact** — date, person, what was said, what was promised; verbal promises get a same-day email restating them
6. **Track the follow-ups** — a dated task for every open item, on both sides

## Guardrails

- Never speculate about fault or cause in the incident record — facts only
- Never discard damaged items or original receipts before the adjuster releases them
- Deadlines from the insurer go on a calendar the day they are stated

## Output format

A claim file: incident record, document checklist with status, contact log, deadline calendar, follow-up tasks.
