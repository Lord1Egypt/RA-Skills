## Description:

Create a practical charging roster for cordless tool batteries, chargers, labels, rotation status, storage locations, and basic fire-safe charging reminders without giving electrical repair guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this prompt-only skill to organize cordless tool batteries and chargers for a garage, workshop, shed, job kit, maker space, maintenance closet, or household tool shelf. It produces a printable roster with battery labels, charger assignments, rotation status, storage locations, compatibility holds, basic fire-safe reminders, and a session closeout routine.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat the roster as electrical repair, live testing, battery rebuilding, wiring, soldering, plug modification, or charger modification guidance.

Mitigation: Keep outputs to organization and basic safety reminders, direct users to manufacturer instructions, and route suspected electrical defects to manufacturer support or a qualified repair professional.

Risk: Users may charge batteries or chargers with unknown compatibility or visible damage.

Mitigation: Mark unknown combinations as hold for manual check, stop using visibly damaged or abnormal items, and follow manufacturer, retailer, recycler, or local disposal guidance.

Risk: Users may overread basic fire-safe reminders as a guarantee that a charging setup is fireproof or risk-free.

Mitigation: Avoid safety guarantees and present reminders as conservative habits that do not replace manufacturer instructions.
