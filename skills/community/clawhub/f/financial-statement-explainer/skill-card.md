## Description:

Explains P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow statements in plain English using user-provided figures, highlighting key line items, ratios, and watch-outs without giving financial advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, founders, managers, and operators use this skill to understand user-provided P&L, balance sheet, or cash flow statements. It turns accounting structure and selected ratios into plain-language educational guidance while flagging missing data and professional-review needs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake an educational explanation for financial, investment, tax, or accounting advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as educational explanations and direct important business decisions to a qualified professional.

Risk: Incomplete statements can lead to misleading ratios or interpretations if missing figures are filled in implicitly.

Mitigation: Use only figures supplied by the user, label missing data as unknown, and explain what to look for instead of inventing values.
