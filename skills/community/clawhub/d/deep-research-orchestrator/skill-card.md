## Description:

Plan and orchestrate deep research pipelines for multi-platform ads decision making across Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Ads, TikTok Ads, YouTube Ads, Amazon Ads, and DSP/programmatic.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing teams, growth operators, and agent users use this skill to plan ad research, evaluate hypotheses, synthesize evidence, and choose next experiments across major advertising platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad growth or revenue marketing prompts where the user wanted general business advice rather than ads-specific research orchestration.

Mitigation: Clarify whether the request is about advertising research before applying channel-specific recommendations.

Risk: Advertising recommendations can be misleading when evidence quality is weak or platform constraints conflict.

Mitigation: State evidence limits, separate facts from assumptions, rank conflicting hypotheses by evidence strength and recency, and include rollback or stop-loss conditions when spend risk exists.
