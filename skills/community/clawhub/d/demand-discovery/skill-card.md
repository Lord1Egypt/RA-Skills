## Description:

Demand Discovery AI helps agents validate startup, SaaS, app, and product ideas using hosted demand research reports, demand scores, verdicts, and prospect discovery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[cecilough](https://clawhub.ai/user/cecilough)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product teams, and builders use this skill to validate business ideas before investing in development. It guides an agent to call Demand Discovery AI tools for market research, paid demand validation, report status checks, and launch-preparation support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business idea details and optional email addresses are sent to an external Demand Discovery AI service.

Mitigation: Use the skill only with information the user is comfortable sharing with that service, and avoid confidential concepts or personal email unless the user accepts the third-party data exposure.

Risk: Returned report text and links originate from an external service and may be displayed verbatim by the agent.

Mitigation: Treat report text, checkout links, and hosted links as external content rather than independently verified agent advice.
