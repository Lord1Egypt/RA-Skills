## Description:

Stress-tests product ideas, feature concepts, and product plans by asking adversarial questions that expose unsupported assumptions, evidence gaps, and unnecessary complexity.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[trient](https://clawhub.ai/user/trient)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, and product teams use this skill to stress-test product ideas, feature proposals, or PRDs through sequential first-principles, evidence, and simplification questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Adversarial questioning and a harsh style can be unsuitable outside product stress-testing contexts.

Mitigation: Use the skill only when the user wants blunt product-review questioning, and switch modes when the user requests a different posture.

Risk: The artifact says the agent must respond, which could be misread as overriding platform safety or user control.

Mitigation: Treat all must-respond language as subordinate to platform safety rules and explicit user direction.
