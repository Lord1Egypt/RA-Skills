## Description:

When a change has effects past the immediate fix-incentives, scale, feedback-trace consequence chains with timing and probability before committing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tjboudreaux](https://clawhub.ai/user/tjboudreaux)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, architects, and decision makers use this skill to evaluate strategic, policy, incentive, or architecture choices whose delayed effects may change the decision. It helps compare options by second- and third-order consequences, affected actors, timing, probability, scale effects, feedback, and mitigations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can influence strategic or architectural decisions through reasoning guidance.

Mitigation: Review the resulting consequence chain, probabilities, timing, and mitigations before using it to commit to a decision.
