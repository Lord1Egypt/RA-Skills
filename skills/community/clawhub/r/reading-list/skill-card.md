## Description:

阅读清单 helps an agent manage reading lists, track progress, summarize notes, recommend related articles, and report reading statistics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yang1002378395-cmyk](https://clawhub.ai/user/yang1002378395-cmyk)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, researchers, content creators, and knowledge workers use this skill to organize articles, PDFs, and Markdown reading queues, track completion, capture notes, and share or export reading lists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Private research topics, internal URLs, PDFs, notes, or tags could be exposed through sharing or export workflows.

Mitigation: Confirm exactly what will be sent to Notion, Obsidian, or other destinations before sharing or exporting.

Risk: The reading list is disclosed as locally stored data and may contain sensitive links or notes.

Mitigation: Restrict access to the local workspace data and review retention expectations before using the skill with sensitive material.
