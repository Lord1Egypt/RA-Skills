Marketing operators, founders, and revenue teams use this skill to design and run affiliate programs, qualify partners, model commissions, validate tracking, review fraud signals, and manage lifecycle reporting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive partner names, payout details, fraud concerns, or compliance notes could be captured if the optional continuity files are used.

Mitigation: Use the skill statelessly for sensitive work, or keep local notes in ~/affiliate-marketing/ minimal and create them only after explicit user consent.

Risk: Affiliate guidance can affect real outreach, account changes, network access, or payout decisions if paired with separate tools or operational workflows.

Mitigation: Separately approve and review any real outreach, account changes, network access, or payout changes before execution.

Risk: Disclosure-sensitive endorsements, trademark use, regulated claims, or consent-sensitive email and SMS promotions can create legal or policy exposure.

Mitigation: Escalate these promotions for legal, brand, or policy review before launch and document the approved restrictions.
