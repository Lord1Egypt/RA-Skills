This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, product managers, and growth teams use this skill to draft structured A/B test plans with hypotheses, metrics, variant definitions, sample-size inputs, duration estimates, implementation notes, and decision criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases such as "experiment" or "variant" may activate the skill in unrelated discussions.

Mitigation: Use the skill when the user is explicitly planning an A/B or split test, and ignore or disable it for unrelated product or analytics conversations.
