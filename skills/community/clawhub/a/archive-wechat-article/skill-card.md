## Description:

Helps an agent archive WeChat public account articles into a knowledge base and document the workflow for reuse.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wcg-king](https://clawhub.ai/user/wcg-king)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and knowledge workers use this skill to preserve WeChat public account articles by manually retrieving article content in a browser, writing standardized archive records, and updating workflow or memory notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow can create durable knowledge-base and memory entries for an article-reading task.

Mitigation: Confirm the exact WeChat article source and target files before applying the skill, and review generated entries before retaining them.
