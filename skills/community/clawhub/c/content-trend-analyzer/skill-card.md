This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content strategists, marketers, writers, and developers use this skill to research public trend signals, identify audience intent and content gaps, and generate ranked article outlines for selected platforms, regions, languages, and timeframes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trend analysis can become too broad or weakly sourced when the user does not specify a target platform, region, language, or timeframe.

Mitigation: Clarify those constraints before research and require cited source URLs for data points used in recommendations.

Risk: Public trend signals can be noisy, short-lived, or misleading for content planning.

Mitigation: Separate short-term hype from sustained trends and compare signals across multiple platforms before ranking topics.
