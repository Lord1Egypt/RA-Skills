## Description:

检查学术论文标题中的声明是否有足够的证据支持，防止夸大研究结论。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, authors, and editors use this skill to check whether academic paper titles overstate causal, strength, novelty, or effectiveness claims relative to the available study evidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may flag or soften claims based on incomplete or incorrectly summarized study evidence.

Mitigation: Review the final title recommendation against the study design, methods, and results before publication.
