## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat generalized Midea-style management guidance as directly applicable business advice.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against the organization's context before adopting them, especially for strategy, talent, and M&A decisions.

Risk: Chinese-language content may be misunderstood by non-Chinese users.

Mitigation: Ask the agent for translation or bilingual clarification before operational use.
