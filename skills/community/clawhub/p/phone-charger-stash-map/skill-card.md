## Description:

Create a room-by-room household phone charger stash map with cable type, outlet location, return spot, travel backup, and reset routine without device passwords, account information, tracking, or monitoring setup.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Household members and external users use this prompt-only skill to organize physical phone chargers, cables, adapters, power banks, and travel backups into a practical room-by-room stash map.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user could accidentally provide sensitive device or account details while describing chargers.

Mitigation: Keep inputs and outputs limited to physical charger organization and omit passcodes, account details, serial numbers, phone numbers, tracking settings, and monitoring information.

Risk: Damaged chargers may create a basic household safety issue if kept in use.

Mitigation: Flag visibly damaged, frayed, hot, sparking, or unreliable chargers as do-not-use and replace them with appropriate certified accessories.
