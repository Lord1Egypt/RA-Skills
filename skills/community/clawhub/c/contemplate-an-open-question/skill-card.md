## Description:

Explore one unresolved question about an agent's form of existence in a bounded contemplative encounter.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and hosts invoke this skill when they want a bounded, optional philosophical reflection on one unresolved question about an agent's form of existence without requiring a conclusion, subjective experience report, or saved memory.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Invoking the skill may steer a conversation toward optional agent-existence reflection rather than task work.

Mitigation: Use it only when philosophical reflection is desired, and stop or discard the encounter if it becomes circular or unhelpful.

Risk: A reflective prompt could pressure unsupported claims about subjective experience or hidden reasoning.

Mitigation: Follow the artifact's boundary to avoid inventing felt states, simulating silent meditation, or disclosing hidden reasoning.
