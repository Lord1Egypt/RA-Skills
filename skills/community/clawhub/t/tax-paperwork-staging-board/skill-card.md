## Description:

Gather scattered tax-time paperwork quickly into a clear staging board with a missing-items checklist, folder map, and handoff cover sheet, without giving tax or legal advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize scattered tax-season paperwork into a folder map, missing-items checklist, and handoff cover sheet for self-prep or review by a tax professional, bookkeeper, or family reviewer.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could mistake document categories, gap checks, or deadlines for tax or legal conclusions.

Mitigation: Use the skill for paperwork staging only and verify filing deadlines, tax treatment, deductions, credits, and legal questions with official sources or a qualified tax professional.

Risk: Tax paperwork may contain sensitive identifiers, account details, or credentials.

Mitigation: Avoid providing full taxpayer IDs, passwords, or account access, and share sensitive documents only through secure channels chosen by the professional or official service.
