## Description:

Calculate Australian hospitality award pay rates, penalty rates, and labour costs under HIGA MA000009; optimise rosters to reduce wage spend while maintaining Fair Work compliance for hotels, pubs, bars, and accommodation venues.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Australian hospitality employers and payroll or rostering staff use this skill to estimate HIGA MA000009 wage costs, classify common venue roles, understand penalties and allowances, and identify roster changes that may reduce labour spend while preserving Fair Work compliance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Australia

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Embedded HIGA rates are dated 1 July 2025 and may become stale after annual wage reviews.

Mitigation: Verify current figures against Fair Work pay guides before setting or processing payroll, and update the embedded rate tables each July.

Risk: Users may treat general payroll guidance as legal, payroll, or employment advice.

Mitigation: Present calculations as guidance, direct users to current Fair Work sources, and recommend professional advice for complex or disputed cases.
