## Description:

Reviews product directions and business models through Chinese-language product, user psychology, business, and investment perspectives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product builders, founders, and strategy reviewers use this skill to critique a product idea, product direction, project, or business model across product value, user psychology, market strategy, investment, and founder-fit dimensions. It is intended to surface blind spots and produce direct, actionable recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad requests to evaluate a product idea and produce strategic critique from limited user context.

Mitigation: Ask for missing context when needed, state assumptions clearly, and treat recommendations as decision support rather than a final product or investment decision.

Risk: Outputs may be direct and critical, which can overstate confidence if the user's evidence is incomplete.

Mitigation: Tie critiques to the supplied facts, distinguish evidence from assumptions, and request additional data before making high-impact decisions.
