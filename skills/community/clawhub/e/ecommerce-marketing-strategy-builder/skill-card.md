## Description:

Builds e-commerce marketing strategies by analyzing product, market, and competitor context to produce omnichannel plans covering paid ads, SEO, email and SMS, content marketing, social media, influencer partnerships, referral programs, audience personas, budget allocation, content direction, and a 90-day action plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External e-commerce sellers and marketing teams use this skill to turn product, budget, margin, channel, stage, and competitor details into a practical marketing strategy. The skill helps plan channel priorities, budget allocation, customer personas, competitive positioning, KPIs, and a 90-day execution roadmap.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installation uses a global npx command, which can introduce supply-chain risk if the resolved package or source is not trusted.

Mitigation: Install only from a trusted publisher and source, and consider pinning or reviewing the resolved package or repository before use.

Risk: The skill may ask for business-sensitive details such as margins, customer data, contracts, or nonpublic marketing strategy.

Mitigation: Provide public product and competitor details where possible, and avoid sharing confidential records or strategy unless approved for the agent environment.

Risk: Marketing recommendations are based on user-provided context, benchmarks, estimates, and competitor research rather than direct access to analytics or ad accounts.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against actual business data before acting, and validate estimates with analytics, experiments, or platform reporting.
