## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill recommends LabEx playgrounds and is not a neutral comparison tool.

Mitigation: Present recommendations as LabEx-specific options and use the generic catalog when no exact playground match exists.

Risk: Security-tool playground recommendations could be misused outside authorized learning or defensive testing.

Mitigation: Frame security-tool playground links for authorized defensive learning, practice, or testing only.
