## Description:

Dark Horse Mindset provides a Chinese conversational framework for reflecting on career direction, life choices, personal motivation, and individualized success.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[0xcii](https://clawhub.ai/user/0xcii)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to structure reflective conversations about career uncertainty, personal direction, motivation, decision-making, action strategies, and short-term next steps. It is coaching material and should not be treated as mental-health, medical, legal, crisis, or professional career counseling.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat reflective coaching output as mental-health, medical, legal, crisis, or professional career counseling.

Mitigation: Frame the output as coaching material only and direct users in acute distress or regulated advice contexts to qualified support.

Risk: Career and life-direction guidance can be misleading if it is detached from the user's real constraints and context.

Mitigation: Use the framework as a diagnostic conversation aid, ask for the user's current situation, and keep recommendations concrete and context-bound.
