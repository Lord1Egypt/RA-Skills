## Description:

Fliggy is a travel-booking decision guide for comparing flights, hotels, package deals, voucher-style travel products, and refund or dispute paths.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and travel-planning agents use this skill to evaluate Fliggy travel purchases, compare booking options, assess voucher or prepaid package rules, and understand refund, change, and dispute escalation paths.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Travel prices, promotions, refund terms, and booking policies may change after the skill gives advice.

Mitigation: Verify current details on official or merchant pages before making a purchase.

Risk: Visa, entry, and travel-policy advice can be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Check consulate, immigration, airline, and other official sources before relying on policy guidance.

Risk: Users may disclose unnecessary payment or account details while seeking booking help.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing payment credentials or sensitive account information unless strictly required by a trusted official booking flow.
