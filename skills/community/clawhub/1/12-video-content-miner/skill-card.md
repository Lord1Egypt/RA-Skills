## Description:

video-content-miner helps agents batch extract spoken transcripts from Douyin and Kuaishou videos, analyze short-video content structure, and identify repeatable high-performing content patterns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content strategists, marketers, creators, and analysts can use this skill to turn public short-video examples into Markdown reports containing transcripts, structure analysis, hook and ending patterns, and reusable content formulas.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may lead users to rely on external transcript tooling or referenced projects that are not bundled or scanned with this release.

Mitigation: Review and scan any external tool, repository, or service separately before use.

Risk: Transcript extraction and content analysis can raise rights, platform-terms, or privacy concerns if applied to non-public or unauthorized videos.

Mitigation: Use only public data sources and keep extracted transcripts for permitted internal analysis, consistent with the skill's stated constraints.
