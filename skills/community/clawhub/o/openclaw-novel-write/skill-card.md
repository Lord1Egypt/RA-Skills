## Use Case:

Chinese-language fiction writers and agents use this skill to plan, draft, revise, and track long-form novel projects through a structured seven-step workflow. It helps create project memory, specifications, timelines, task lists, tracking files, Mermaid diagrams, feedback reports, and chapter drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Use explicit /novel commands for file-writing steps and review generated memory, tracking, task, and chapter files before relying on or publishing them.
