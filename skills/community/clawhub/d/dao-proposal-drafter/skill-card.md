## Description:

Draft a DAO governance proposal that can actually pass: problem, options, costs, and the exact on-chain action. Use when preparing a proposal for any Governor/Snapshot-style DAO.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[manteclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/manteclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

DAO contributors and delegates use this skill to draft governance proposals, temperature checks, and funding requests. It structures the proposal around evidence, options, an exact ask, budget math, objections, and measurable success criteria.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
