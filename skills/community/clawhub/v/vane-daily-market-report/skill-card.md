## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports may expose sensitive balances, P&L, account identifiers, or recommendations through Telegram, Substack, email, or other external channels.

Mitigation: Keep outputs draft-only by default and confirm that sensitive financial details are redacted or explicitly approved before external publishing.

Risk: Trading recommendations and signal scores may be incorrect, stale, or unsuitable for a reader's circumstances.

Mitigation: Review market data, assumptions, and recommendations before acting on or distributing the report.
