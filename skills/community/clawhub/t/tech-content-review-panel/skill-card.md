## Description:

Reviews finished technology, AI, and industry research or in-depth articles before publication through an eight-role expert panel and an evaluate-revise-recheck process.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers and editors use this skill to review completed technology, AI, and industry deep-dive articles for factual and originality concerns, style, depth, reader fit, and distribution. It is intended for long-form research and analysis, not news, marketing, documentation, tutorials, short opinion pieces, or unfinished drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Originality checks may send unpublished draft material to an external search provider.

Mitigation: Before using the originality check, consider whether the draft can be shared with an external provider; withhold sensitive or unpublished material when that sharing is not acceptable.

Risk: The security scan was partial because a local reference in SKILL.md could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Review the complete skill and resolve the missing local reference before relying on the scan as a complete security assessment.
