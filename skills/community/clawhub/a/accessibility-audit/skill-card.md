## Description:

Generate a WCAG 2.2 accessibility audit checklist and remediation suggestions for any UI or design.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers, developers, product teams, and accessibility reviewers use this skill to assess UIs, components, products, or design specs against WCAG 2.2 and produce prioritized remediation guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Accessibility audits may involve sharing UI screenshots, product details, or disability-related context.

Mitigation: Share only the information needed for the audit and avoid unnecessary sensitive user, product, or disability-related details.
