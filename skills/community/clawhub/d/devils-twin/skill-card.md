## Description:

Devils Twin helps writers stress-test a near-final document by drafting the strongest realistic opposing memo, a battle map of affected claims, and a pre-emption paragraph.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, product leaders, and reviewers use this skill before a document ships to see the strongest realistic opposition and decide whether to repair, pre-empt, or proceed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks for full document text, which may include confidential or sensitive material.

Mitigation: Use it only with material appropriate for the agent environment, and redact sensitive details when full fidelity is not required.
