## Description:

Helps an agent optimize social content for search discovery across platforms by using real keyword research, platform-specific indexed surfaces, topical clusters, and honest measurement boundaries.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content teams use this skill to make social posts and profiles easier to find through platform search. It guides keyword research, keyword placement, profile searchability, evergreen clusters, and measurement without inventing search-volume data or promising rankings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce misleading SEO advice if an agent invents platform search volumes or ranking claims.

Mitigation: Require outputs to use user-provided analytics, autocomplete, related searches, or qualitative demand signals, and to state that searchability does not guarantee ranking.

Risk: The skill may rely on outdated assumptions because social platform indexing surfaces and search features change.

Mitigation: Re-verify platform-specific feature guidance before high-stakes or commercial publication decisions.

Risk: The skill may process brand, audience, or platform analytics documents supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Review supplied documents for confidentiality before use and avoid including sensitive analytics or audience details in public outputs.
