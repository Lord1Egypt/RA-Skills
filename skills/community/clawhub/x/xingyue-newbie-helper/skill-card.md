## Description:

星月新人助手 routes Chinese-language creators to 星月写作 resources for long fiction, short fiction, dynamic comics, scripts, idea generation, model selection, and publishing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and writing-platform users use this skill to identify the right 星月写作 tutorial, forum post, model-selection note, prompt, or publishing guide for their current writing task.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may answer generic writing or publishing prompts with 星月-specific links.

Mitigation: Use it when 星月写作-specific guidance is desired, and verify whether linked resources match the user's requested platform or writing workflow.

Risk: Some forum resources may require login on the external platform.

Mitigation: Prefer public Bilibili, main-site, or already accessible forum links when users cannot access login-gated material.
