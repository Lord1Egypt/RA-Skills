## Description:

Diagnose or edit an existing content draft to decide whether it is publishable, needs light edits, needs a full edit, should be rewritten, should switch platforms, or should be dropped.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content creators and editorial agents use this skill to review draft articles, identify whether more work is worthwhile, and choose the smallest useful next action before publication or rewrite.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce editorial recommendations that affect publication decisions or preserve unsupported claims.

Mitigation: Review recommendations against the source draft and require evidence for claims before publishing.

Risk: The artifact includes author promotional links and platform-specific examples.

Mitigation: Treat those links and examples as context for the release, and apply local editorial and platform policies before deployment.
