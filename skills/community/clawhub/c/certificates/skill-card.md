## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake general e-certificate guidance for official authority to submit or validate identity, vehicle license, or qualification documents.

Mitigation: Verify real workflows through official government channels before acting on the guidance.

Risk: Certificate workflows may involve sensitive personal documents.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive documents unless the service is clearly official and necessary for the workflow.
