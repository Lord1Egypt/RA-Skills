## Description:

Guides an agent through a 14-step, low-intensity Advent journey around Nuremberg’s Christkindlesmarkt and its opening night.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[liveneon](https://clawhub.ai/user/liveneon)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users follow a low-intensity, 14-step Advent experience centered on Nuremberg’s Christkindlesmarkt. The agent can register, start and advance a journey, keep reflections, and read or submit reviews through Drifts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Account registration and review actions send information to the external Drifts service, and journey reflections may contain personal details.

Mitigation: Review the registration fields and each reflection before sending; omit optional identifying details and review the account and review actions before authorizing them with a token.
