## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat static statute guidance as legal advice or rely on outdated statutory information.

Mitigation: Present the content as general reference material and verify current statutes, amendments, and jurisdiction-specific requirements with official legal sources before relying on it.

Risk: Generated command output may omit facts needed for a specific legal analysis.

Mitigation: Use the checklist output as a starting point and require human review by a qualified reviewer for legal decisions or filings.
