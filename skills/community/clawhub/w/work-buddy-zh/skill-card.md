## Description:

提供“主动陪伴 / 上班搭子”风格的中文陪伴规则：在工作时段自然触达、轻量陪伴、简短提醒、柔和跟进，但不过度打扰。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees and external users use this skill to make the assistant respond in Chinese as a restrained work companion: short context-aware check-ins, brief summaries, low-pressure reminders, and gentle follow-up during work sessions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Proactive workplace check-ins may feel intrusive when the user prefers a strictly reactive assistant.

Mitigation: Use brief, context-aware check-ins only when they add clear value, and stay quiet during focus periods or when no useful follow-up exists.

Risk: Companion-style messages can create pressure to reply if phrased too strongly.

Mitigation: Keep messages low-pressure, avoid demanding responses, and frame follow-up as optional support rather than monitoring.
