## Description:

Create, inspect, and edit Microsoft Excel workbooks and XLSX files with reliable formulas, dates, types, formatting, recalculation, and template preservation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, analysts, and spreadsheet-heavy teams use this skill when an agent needs to inspect, create, or edit Excel workbooks while preserving formulas, data types, formatting, workbook structure, and recalculation expectations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Agent-guided edits to important workbooks can introduce incorrect formulas, stale calculated values, formatting changes, or spreadsheet logic errors.

Mitigation: Review important financial or business workbooks before relying on results; verify formulas, error cells, workbook structure, and recalculated values after edits.

Risk: Spreadsheet imports and exports can damage data types such as leading-zero identifiers, long numbers, dates, and mixed text-number columns.

Mitigation: Use explicit data-type handling and inspect representative cells before saving or delivering edited workbooks.
