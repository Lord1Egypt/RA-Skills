## Description:

AI Mood Journal helps users record daily emotions, analyze longer-term mood patterns, identify triggers, and generate coping suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chenyuan35](https://clawhub.ai/user/chenyuan35)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill as a reflective mood-journaling assistant that records emotions, triggers, body sensations, coping actions, and trends over time. It should support self-reflection and pattern tracking, not replace professional mental-health care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks an agent to collect and maintain sensitive emotional and mental-health profile information over time.

Mitigation: Use explicit consent, minimize sensitive detail, and only store or summarize entries when privacy, retention, and deletion controls are clear.

Risk: Users may treat the skill's mood analysis or suggestions as professional mental-health care.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as reflective journaling support and direct users to qualified professionals or crisis resources for clinical, urgent, or high-risk situations.
