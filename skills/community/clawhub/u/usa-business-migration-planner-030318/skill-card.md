## Description:

Helps users produce practical workflows, checklists, analysis, and decision support for requests involving WeChat data access planning on a protected iPhone when device and Apple account credentials are known.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and support agents use this skill to turn repeated online requests about a deceased spouse's protected iPhone and WeChat data into an actionable workflow, checklist, or decision-support answer. Reviewers should ensure outputs stay limited to lawful estate, backup, platform-support, and professional forensic channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is misbranded as a business migration planner while artifact content focuses on access to private communications on a protected phone.

Mitigation: Rename or rewrite the skill description and triggers so they accurately scope lawful estate, backup, Apple/Tencent support, or professional forensic referral workflows.

Risk: Broad implicit invocation could cause an agent to help with unauthorized private-data extraction.

Mitigation: Require clear authorization or estate authority before providing guidance, and refuse security-bypass or unauthorized extraction steps.

Risk: The skill may produce sensitive personal-data guidance without legal or consent guardrails.

Mitigation: Keep outputs to authority verification, non-bypass recovery options, backups, platform support channels, and professional legal or forensic escalation.
