## Description:

MediaWiki Wikitext markup language for Wikipedia and wiki-based sites. Use when creating or editing wiki articles, generating wikitext content, working with wiki tables/templates/references, or converting content to wikitext format. Triggers on requests mentioning Wikipedia, MediaWiki, wikitext, wiki markup, or wiki article creation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, editors, and agents use this skill to draft, edit, and convert content into MediaWiki wikitext for Wikipedia and wiki-based sites. It supports common markup tasks such as article structure, tables, links, templates, references, and reusable editor snippets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad Wikipedia-related requests may invoke this skill when the user needs factual research rather than markup formatting help.

Mitigation: Clarify whether the task is factual research, wikitext formatting, or article-structure drafting before producing final content.
