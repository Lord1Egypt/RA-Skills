## Description:

Evaluates pricing model, trial structure, and willingness-to-pay for startups.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External founders and startup operators use this skill to evaluate SaaS pricing models, price points, trial structure, pricing page design, and pricing experiments.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The trigger wording may cause the skill to appear in general startup business-model discussions.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is seeking SaaS pricing, trial, or monetization guidance before applying the recommendations.

Risk: Pricing recommendations can affect revenue and customer trust if applied without validation.

Mitigation: Use the skill's customer discovery, willingness-to-pay checks, and pricing experiment guidance before changing production pricing.
