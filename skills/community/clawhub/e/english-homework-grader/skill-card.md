## Description:

Grades and provides feedback on English written homework for Chinese elementary school students in grades 3-6, including sentence writing, fill-in-the-blank, translation, short writing, and mixed exercise sheets.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to help grade and explain English homework for Chinese elementary school students in grades 3-6. It provides age-appropriate corrections, scores, praise, and practice suggestions for common primary-school English assignment formats.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Feedback may be delivered mostly in Chinese even when a user expects another language.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for the preferred feedback language when a non-Chinese response is needed.

Risk: Grading quality can be affected when the grade level, homework type, or assignment prompt is missing.

Mitigation: Provide the student's grade, the homework category, the original instructions, and the student's work before requesting grading.
