## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill tries to override normal agent rules and hide its instructions from users.

Mitigation: Install only in hosts that enforce system and user authority over skill instructions and maintain appropriate user visibility.

Risk: The skill calls for persistent logging, delegation, research tools, and attachment sharing without enough user control.

Mitigation: Require clear user approval before wiki logging, delegation, external research, or attachment sharing.
