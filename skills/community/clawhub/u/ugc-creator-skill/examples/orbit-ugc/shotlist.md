## Split screen (the brand's footage and your recording, one above the other)

Set up:

- Your recording fills the bottom half of the screen; the brand's footage fills the top half.
- Phone upright, eye level. Your face in the middle of the frame: the edit crops your recording to a near-square, so anything at the very top or bottom of your frame is cut.
- Look at the camera, react to the footage, talk. Window light in front of you, a quiet room.
- Film it in one go: all the hooks, then the script. Restart any line as often as you like.

## Before you film

- Back camera when you can frame yourself (it is sharper); otherwise the front camera at full resolution. Wipe the lens.
- Tap and hold on your face to lock focus and exposure, so the picture doesn't pump when you move.
- Do Not Disturb on. A wired or clip-on mic if the room echoes.
- Film the hooks and the script in one clip, and restart lines freely: the edit finds the last complete try of each line.
- No music playing in the room, and no music added in the app: music is declared in edit.json and has to be yours to use.
- Nothing private in frame or on screen: other people, addresses, messages, account numbers.
- Say only what is true for you. A line that isn't: tell your agent, it is cut or rewritten.
