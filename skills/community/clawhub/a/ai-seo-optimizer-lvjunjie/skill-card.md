## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: SEO rankings, search volumes, competitor metrics, and business reports may be mock or randomly generated rather than measured from live providers.

Mitigation: Use reports as draft guidance only until the publisher documents live data sources or clearly labels mock mode; verify important SEO decisions against trusted analytics tools.

Risk: The skill interface and recommendations are primarily Chinese-language, which may cause review or interpretation issues for other locales.

Mitigation: Have a Chinese-speaking reviewer or localization owner review outputs before relying on them in non-Chinese workflows.
