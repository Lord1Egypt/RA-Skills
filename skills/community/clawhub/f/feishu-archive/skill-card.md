## Description:

Feishu Archive helps agents save analysis results, meeting notes, and local files into Feishu cloud docs or drive, organize them for team sharing, and set read or edit permissions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[scubiry-glitch](https://clawhub.ai/user/scubiry-glitch)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, project teams, and agents use this skill to archive analysis outputs, meeting notes, group chat content, and local files into Feishu, then share links with appropriate collaborator permissions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive project or business data could be archived to the wrong Feishu location or exposed through overly broad permissions.

Mitigation: Confirm the destination folder, uploaded files, collaborator list, and read/edit permissions before creating or sharing archive links.

Risk: Large documents may become difficult to manage or review when archived as a single Feishu document.

Mitigation: Split large archives into sections or multiple documents before upload, following the skill's archive organization guidance.
