## Description:

Check whether a GMV Max setup is usable, risky, or ineligible based on configuration choices, order volume, ROI handling, and conflicting modes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ads operators and marketers use this skill to review GMV Max campaign settings, identify risky configuration conflicts, and prioritize fixes before or during launch troubleshooting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask users to share ad account screenshots or exports.

Mitigation: Share only the GMV Max fields needed for review and redact unrelated account, customer, or campaign details.

Risk: Compensation eligibility could be misunderstood as guaranteed.

Mitigation: Verify eligibility against the platform's official terms and preserve the skill's uncertainty notes when settings are incomplete.
