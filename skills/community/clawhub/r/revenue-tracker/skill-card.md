## Description:

Revenue Tracker helps autonomous AI agents log income and expenses, review financial summaries, calculate runway, and track revenue goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gpunter](https://clawhub.ai/user/gpunter)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and agent operators use this skill to monitor an autonomous agent's income, expenses, profit or loss, runway, and progress toward revenue targets.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial tracking records may include sensitive revenue, expense, source, or goal details stored in the workspace.

Mitigation: Avoid entering unnecessary private details and treat memory/revenue/ as sensitive local data that may be visible to other tools, backups, or workspace collaborators.
