## Description:

Identifies and rewrites AI-sounding Chinese text so it reads more naturally, using a 24-pattern checklist based on the Wikipedia Signs of AI writing guide.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[0xcjl](https://clawhub.ai/user/0xcjl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and developers use this skill to revise Chinese text that appears AI-generated by detecting common formulaic, promotional, over-qualified, or mechanical patterns and producing a more natural rewrite.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may rewrite user-provided Chinese text automatically when invoked ambiguously.

Mitigation: Use explicit prompts or delimited text blocks when requesting either analysis-only feedback or a rewritten version.

Risk: A rewrite can change nuance, tone, or intended emphasis in sensitive text.

Mitigation: Review the humanized output before using it in published, legal, academic, or customer-facing contexts.
