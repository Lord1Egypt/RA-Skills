## Description:

Tech Alliance Leader Scout helps chain leader enterprises identify technology alliance partners and produce executive decision reports covering industry maturity, capability gaps, candidate fit, cooperation paths, leadership-control risks, HTML reporting, and a structured retrieval plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business strategy and industrial research teams use this skill to evaluate technology alliance opportunities for a specified chain leader enterprise, technology domain, and enterprise identity. It guides evidence gathering across policy, standards, market, patents, capability baselines, yearbooks, blue books, and candidate partner records, then turns the analysis into an executive HTML report and structured retrieval plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive company strategy, candidate partner, patent, and market information.

Mitigation: Avoid supplying confidential internal documents unless the user is comfortable with the agent using them in the report.

Risk: Unsupported market figures, deployment counts, financing data, patent counts, or candidate rankings could mislead executive decisions.

Mitigation: Require source citations for key KPIs, mark unverifiable figures as pending verification, and keep uncertain candidates out of the primary recommendation list.
