## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake educational explanations of shyness, loneliness, depression, or self-harm risk for mental health care.

Mitigation: Keep responses educational, provide safety and help-seeking boundaries for severe distress or self-harm risk, and avoid presenting the skill as therapy or crisis intervention.

Risk: The skill could overextend beyond Miller chapter 7 or add unsupported research details.

Mitigation: Anchor answers to the artifact references, state when a request belongs to another chapter or outside the provided material, and avoid inventing data, years, studies, or examples.
