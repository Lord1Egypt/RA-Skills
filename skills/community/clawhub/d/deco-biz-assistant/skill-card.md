## Description:

Deco Biz Assistant helps commercial renovation teams draft quotations, bid structures, proposals, project records, and material-price answers for office, industrial-park, retail, and exhibition-space projects.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Commercial renovation business teams use this skill to prepare human-reviewed quotation drafts, bid response outlines, proposal documents, project records, and local price-library answers for pre-sales workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated quotations, bid materials, proposals, or price answers could be mistaken for final business commitments.

Mitigation: Keep external sending, final approval, bid submission, price commitments, and price-library updates behind responsible human approval.

Risk: Local project archives, bid templates, price libraries, and audit logs may contain sensitive customer or commercial data.

Mitigation: Keep these workflows local, use redacted placeholders for displayed or stored customer and price details, and restrict administrator price updates.

Risk: Missing or stale price-library and template data could produce incomplete or incorrect business drafts.

Mitigation: Use the latest local price-library data, mark unavailable items as missing instead of inventing values, and route data gaps to an administrator or human owner.
