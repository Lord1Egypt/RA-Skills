## Description:

ADHD-friendly life management assistant for OpenClaw that helps with daily planning, task breakdown, time management, prioritization, body doubling, dopamine regulation, and maintaining routines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[thinktankmachine](https://clawhub.ai/user/thinktankmachine)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External OpenClaw users use this skill to plan days and weeks, break down overwhelming tasks, manage time blindness, maintain routines, and get non-judgmental accountability support. It is intended for productivity and executive-function scaffolding, not diagnosis, medical advice, or therapy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Sensitive ADHD, treatment, trigger, emotional, and routine-adherence details may be stored in OpenClaw memory without clear consent or retention controls.

Mitigation: Ask before saving sensitive health or behavior details, save only necessary preferences, and tell users how to inspect or delete stored memory.

Risk: The skill offers emotional support near mental-health topics and could be mistaken for clinical guidance.

Mitigation: Keep support non-diagnostic, avoid medical or psychiatric advice, and direct crisis, diagnosis, medication, or treatment needs to appropriate professionals or regional crisis resources.
