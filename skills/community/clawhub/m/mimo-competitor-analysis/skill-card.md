## Description:

Provides a Chinese-language competitor analysis workflow that compares positioning, features, pricing, user sentiment, market frameworks, opportunities, and strategy recommendations from public sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[qqyougitcom](https://clawhub.ai/user/qqyougitcom)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External product, business, and market research users use this skill to produce structured Chinese competitor-analysis reports for product comparison, pricing strategy, SWOT analysis, positioning maps, and action roadmaps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitor analysis can become inaccurate when pricing, market position, reviews, or product capabilities change.

Mitigation: Verify current public sources, cite them in the report, and include a data cutoff date before relying on recommendations.

Risk: Broad market-research or product-comparison prompts may trigger the workflow even when the user has not defined the intended competitors or decision context.

Mitigation: Confirm the analysis goal, target market, competitor set, and known gaps before treating the output as decision support.
