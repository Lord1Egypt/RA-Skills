## Description:

Plans the T+1 to T+30 window after a launch as a dated momentum calendar with announcement-tier routing, relaunch legitimacy guidance, spike-to-owned handoff briefs, and the next Tier-1 moment spacing check.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, product, and launch operators use this skill to keep post-launch attention from fading by scheduling milestone, shipped-loop, and badge moments. It helps route release announcements by tier, decide whether an update is a legitimate relaunch, and prepare handoffs for owned-channel follow-up.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can create or propose persistent launch-planning records after confirmation, while the package manifest declares only lite read-oriented capabilities.

Mitigation: Review the generated plan and approve any persistent memory or registry proposal before allowing writes.

Risk: Incorrect launch metrics, milestone claims, or release tiers could lead to misleading marketing guidance.

Mitigation: Require cited sources for announcement claims and keep estimated tiering clearly labeled until reviewed.
