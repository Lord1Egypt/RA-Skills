## Description:

Reviews and edits authorial prose for spelling, grammar, punctuation, clarity, flow, and style while preserving the author's meaning, voice, tone, and rhetorical choices.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tangentus](https://clawhub.ai/user/tangentus)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, and agents use this skill to proofread, copyedit, line edit, or apply approved edits to blog posts, essays, newsletters, and similar prose while preserving the author's voice and intent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Applied edits may modify user-provided text.

Mitigation: Use conservative editing, preserve the author's meaning and voice, and review material changes before relying on the result.

Risk: Uncertain factual, technical, naming, or quotation issues may require judgment beyond proofreading.

Mitigation: Flag uncertain issues as queries instead of silently changing them.
