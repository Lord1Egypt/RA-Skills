## Description:

Helps developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and users turn vague errors into clear messages that explain what failed, why it failed, and what action to take next.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, support teams, SaaS operators, and end users use this skill to rewrite or design error messages, troubleshooting copy, checklists, and workflows that make failures understandable and actionable.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad support or debugging wording may invoke the skill when the user only wanted general help.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation when requesting error-message rewriting or troubleshooting copy.

Risk: Suggested error wording or troubleshooting guidance could be inaccurate if the provided system context is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review outputs against the actual failure mode, user audience, and success criteria before publishing or sending them.
