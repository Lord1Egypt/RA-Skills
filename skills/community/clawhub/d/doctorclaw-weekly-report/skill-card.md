## Description:

Weekly report generator -- compile progress from tasks, emails, and calendar into one summary. Friday cron or on-demand.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ceobotson-bot](https://clawhub.ai/user/ceobotson-bot)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, consultants, and operators use this skill to compile weekly progress reports from selected tasks, calendar events, and email activity. It helps summarize wins, meetings, communications, blockers, metrics, and next-week priorities for personal, team, or client updates.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Weekly reports can combine sensitive task, calendar, email, recipient, and archive details into one output.

Mitigation: Before use, explicitly choose the task source, calendars, email account, delivery channel, recipients, and archive location; avoid recurring or shared delivery until those choices are confirmed.
