## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes medical or healing material that could be mistaken for diagnosis or treatment advice.

Mitigation: Constrain responses to spiritual or cultural reference framing and direct users to qualified healthcare professionals for medical concerns.

Risk: The skill includes blood, fire, and toxic-herb ritual material.

Mitigation: Do not provide operational instructions for unsafe rituals; summarize historical or cultural context only.

Risk: The skill includes curse, coercive romance, and manipulation-oriented material.

Mitigation: Refuse assistance that targets coercion, curses, or manipulation of another person's feelings or choices.

Risk: The skill's standard divination pool includes Northumbrian runes unless the querent explicitly requests Elder Futhark only.

Mitigation: State the rune pool being used and honor explicit user requests for Elder Futhark-only readings.
