## Description:

The Trade Reviewer's procedure for journaling desk activity and reviewing trades from the exchange record - process graded separately from outcome, execution costs measured, one repeatable finding per review, plus the weekly desk review. Use after any send, when a trade closes, on the weekly routine, or when the user asks "how did that go".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Desk operators use this skill to maintain a timestamped trading journal and review closed trades against exchange fills, approvals, orders, fees, and funding. It also supports weekly desk summaries and incident reviews that separate process from outcome and identify one repeatable finding.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A broad activation phrase or autonomous interpretation could lead to a review or outbound update outside the intended trade scope.

Mitigation: Confirm the relevant trade and requested scope before writing a review or sending it to the Desk Lead or user.
