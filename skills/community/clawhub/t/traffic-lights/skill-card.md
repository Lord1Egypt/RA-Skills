## Description:

Create multi-criteria comparison charts using traffic lights or Harvey balls for option evaluation, competitive comparison, and executive dashboards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, analysts, product teams, and decision makers use this skill to build multi-criteria comparison charts for options, competitors, vendors, dashboards, and gap analyses.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The generated comparison may rely on incomplete criteria, assumptions, or subjective ratings.

Mitigation: Review the criteria, weights, rating rationale, and recommendation before using the chart for decisions.

Risk: Traffic lights and similar symbols can oversimplify nuanced trade-offs.

Mitigation: Add supporting notes or data for important ratings and treat the overall score as guidance, not the sole decision rule.
