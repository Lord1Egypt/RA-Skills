## Description:

Creates a structured Figma design brief from product requirements, feature requests, or user stories.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, designers, and product teams use this skill to turn requirements into a design-ready Figma brief with goals, user flows, required screens, component needs, constraints, accessibility notes, success criteria, and open questions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated briefs may misstate product requirements or leave important assumptions unresolved.

Mitigation: Review the brief against the source PRD, ticket, or feature request before sharing it with designers or using it for implementation planning.

Risk: Broad trigger language may invoke the skill during general design-planning conversations.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly when a structured Figma design brief is needed and review the generated scope before acting on it.
