## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, educators, and developers use this skill to plan and run AI talking-head video workflows with prepared portraits, narration audio, lipsync models, captions, and clip stitching.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installer and related-skill commands can introduce supply-chain exposure.

Mitigation: Review installer contents, prefer the documented checksum-verification path, avoid elevated shells, and evaluate related-skill installs separately.

Risk: Talking-head workflows can misuse portraits or voices of real people.

Mitigation: Use only portraits and audio that the operator has permission to upload and process.
