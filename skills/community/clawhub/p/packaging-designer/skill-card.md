## Description:

Design product packaging and unboxing experiences that reinforce brand identity, encourage social sharing, reduce damage rates, and include insert cards that drive reviews and repeat purchases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, ecommerce operators, and brand teams use this skill to plan product packaging, unboxing sequences, vendor hand-offs, dunnage choices, insert-card copy, and transit-testing plans before production orders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated vendor briefs may include inaccurate cost, material, certification, logistics, or acceptance assumptions.

Mitigation: Review briefs against vendor quotes, product measurements, carrier constraints, target-market requirements, and signed production terms before placing orders.

Risk: Insert-card copy may create marketplace policy or consumer-law issues if it requests biased reviews or uses unclear incentives.

Mitigation: Use honest-feedback wording and have marketplace or compliance reviewers approve CTAs, discount terms, and QR destinations before print.

Risk: Sustainability claims may be misleading if recyclability, FSC, recycled-content, or compostability claims are not substantiated.

Mitigation: Verify each claim against vendor certification and disposal realities in the target markets before printing or publishing it.
