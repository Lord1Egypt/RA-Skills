## Description:

Helps software maintainers, QA engineers, open-source contributors, and product teams add useful unit tests and improve test coverage for existing codebases.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, software maintainers, QA engineers, open-source contributors, and product teams use this skill to plan, implement, and verify useful unit tests that raise coverage and reduce regression risk in existing codebases.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad words such as testing or quality.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for unit-test coverage work or disable implicit invocation when tighter routing is needed.

Risk: Suggested tests or coverage changes may be incomplete or misaligned with the target codebase.

Mitigation: Review proposed changes, run the relevant test suite and coverage command, and check remaining assumptions before relying on the result.
