## Description:

Guide users buying a smartwatch or fitness tracker through platform, health sensors, battery life, durability, and activity questions to get the exact specs they need -- brand-neutral.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External buyers use this skill to run a structured consultation before buying a smartwatch or fitness tracker. It helps them identify required specs, trade-offs, and matching product options without relying on brand-led sales advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The consultation may ask about health priorities, country, or carrier details that users could overshare.

Mitigation: Ask only for the minimum detail needed to shape a buying recommendation, and avoid collecting unnecessary personal health or location information.

Risk: Smartwatch health metrics and regional feature availability can be mistaken for medical advice or guaranteed product capability.

Mitigation: Frame health features as consumer screening or trend tools, and tell users to verify current product specifications, medical disclaimers, and regional activation before buying.
