# Report template

Write the report in the user's own language and register (match how they write to you). Keep hook examples in the language the target audience actually watches in. Aim for something the user can read in about five minutes; raw data lives in the CSV, not the report.

## Structure

**Setup line (top):** niche, platforms, region/language, window, date pulled, and any defaults you assumed.

**1. Snapshot.** Three to five bullets: the biggest finding, what is rising, what is saturated, and the one thing to do first.

**2. Top outliers.** Table of 8 to 12 rows:

| # | Video (linked title) | Channel | Views | Age (days) | outlier_x | Why it worked (one line) |

Mark the confidence label if any row is not API-verified.

**3. Patterns.** For each pattern (3 to 6 of them):
- the pattern in one sentence
- evidence: "k of n top videos", with 2 to 3 linked examples
- control-group note: is it more common among winners than average videos?
- how to use it

**4. Hook bank.** 10 to 20 hooks adapted to the user's niche, grouped by hook type, each one line. These are written fresh, not lifted from the videos.

**5. Content ideas.** 5 to 10 ideas as short cards: working title, hook, format and length, angle, evidence links, why it fits this user, effort (low/medium/high). Order them by expected payoff.

**6. Audience language.** Recurring questions, objections and phrases from comments and forums, paraphrased. Say which are best used as titles or openers.

**7. Timing.** For each major pattern: rising, peaking, saturated or evergreen, with the evidence for that call.

**8. Method and caveats.** Queries run, window, region, quota or coverage limits, what could not be observed (thumbnails, spoken hooks, TikTok/Instagram numbers), and what you would check next.

**Sources.** Linked list of pages and videos used.

## Files to hand over

- the report (document artifact or `.md`)
- `yt_*.csv` with the full ranked video data
- `yt_*.json` with the same data plus run metadata, if the user wants to reuse it

## Closing line

One sentence naming the single best next step (for example "start with idea 2, it is the only rising pattern from small channels").
