Risk: Budget, venue, and vendor guidance may not match current local prices or site-specific conditions.

Mitigation: Confirm recommendations against local market pricing, venue inspections, and supplier details before acting.

Risk: Contract-related guidance may be incomplete for binding wedding-service agreements.

Mitigation: Use qualified legal review for contract terms before signing or operationalizing them.
