## Description:

Produce, adapt, or review text for Bahasa Bali with an offline-first regional-language workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ramaaditya49](https://clawhub.ai/user/ramaaditya49)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to draft, adapt, and review Bahasa Bali wording for ordinary, tourism-facing, community, and culturally aware Indonesian-to-Balinese contexts. Sensitive ritual, temple, adat, legal, official, educational, or status-sensitive text should be treated as a draft for competent speaker or local expert review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bahasa Bali output may use the wrong speech level, dialect, or status-sensitive address for the audience.

Mitigation: Ask for the target audience, context, and speech level; preserve Indonesian terms or mark uncertainty when verified Balinese wording is unavailable.

Risk: Ritual, temple, adat, legal, official, educational, or status-sensitive text could be misleading if used without local review.

Mitigation: Treat such output as a draft and have a competent speaker or relevant local expert review it before use.
