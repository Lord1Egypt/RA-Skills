## Description:

Guides Chinese-language product requirement interviews with industry-specific option prompts and produces a structured requirements document.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ecparter](https://clawhub.ai/user/ecparter)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, and developers use this skill to clarify vague product-building requests in Chinese before implementation. It gathers scoped choices for sectors such as ecommerce, food, SaaS, content, and B2B, then turns answers into an actionable requirements document.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad product-building requests may be delayed by clarifying questions before implementation starts.

Mitigation: Redirect the agent explicitly when immediate coding is preferred, or answer the short option prompts to complete the requirements brief first.

Risk: The generated requirements document may omit details when the selected industry template does not cover the user's niche.

Mitigation: Use the skill's free-form supplement path for uncovered industries or requirements, then review the final document before implementation.
