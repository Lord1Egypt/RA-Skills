## Description:

Plan a public-safe offline message test checklist for owned Android devices, including consent boundaries, non-sensitive sample text, success criteria, and human verification steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to prepare a consent-based offline messaging test plan for owned or explicitly authorized Android devices, including sample non-sensitive messages and manual verification steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be misapplied to devices that are not owned or explicitly authorized.

Mitigation: Confirm device ownership or explicit authorization before planning any test.

Risk: Private messages, device identifiers, contact lists, or credentials could be exposed if used as examples.

Mitigation: Use only non-sensitive sample text and avoid collecting private logs, identifiers, contacts, or credential material.

Risk: A written checklist could be mistaken for operational device or network instructions.

Mitigation: Keep device and network operation outside the skill and limit outputs to written preparation and human verification.
