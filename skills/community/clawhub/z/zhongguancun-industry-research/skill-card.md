## Description:

Supports Chinese-language industry research for enterprise collaboration by producing company profiles, research-direction fit analysis, collaboration entry points, participant suggestions, and optional meeting agendas.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhangxiaopeng-zgci](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhangxiaopeng-zgci)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and collaboration teams use this skill to prepare enterprise partnership research for Zhongguancun College and the Zhongguancun Artificial Intelligence Research Institute. It helps assess company fit, identify collaboration models, and draft meeting agendas from public research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad company-research requests and produce outputs based on public-search findings.

Mitigation: Confirm the target company and research scope, and review public-source findings before relying on the report or agenda.

Risk: Generated meeting agendas or collaboration recommendations could be incomplete or misleading if source information is outdated.

Mitigation: Validate key facts, participants, and proposed collaboration modes against current authoritative sources before outreach.
