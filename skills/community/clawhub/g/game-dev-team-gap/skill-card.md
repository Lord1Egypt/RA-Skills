## Description:

Help a beginner or early-stage game team figure out which roles, skills, or disciplines they are missing for a given game concept and target scope.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game developers, small studios, and early-stage teams use this skill to assess whether their current people and skills match a game concept, target platform, and milestone. It identifies missing or weakly covered disciplines, risky role combinations, and the smallest realistic team shape.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on adjacent game-planning questions because its trigger wording is broad.

Mitigation: Confirm the user is asking for team-formation or role-gap guidance before applying the full diagnostic structure.

Risk: Team-gap recommendations may be incomplete when the user omits concept, milestone, platform, team composition, skills, or constraints.

Mitigation: Ask the minimum clarifying questions needed and clearly label assumptions when information is missing.
