## Description:

财政部财库〔2026〕2号 异常低价审查落地实务助手，覆盖采购人/评标专家/投标人三视角。

## Use Case:

External procurement practitioners, evaluation experts, and suppliers use this skill to structure abnormal low-price review workflows, prepare review records and notices, and draft supplier cost-rationale responses. It is intended to support human review, not replace procurement authorities or evaluation committee decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may force an author and WeChat footer into formal procurement outputs.

Mitigation: Review every generated formal document before official use and remove or revise attribution that is not appropriate for the user's organization or filing context.

Risk: Legal and procurement guidance or low-price calculations may be wrong for the current project, jurisdiction, or governing document version.

Mitigation: Verify generated guidance and calculations against current authoritative rules, the project procurement documents, and the responsible evaluation body's own judgment before relying on them.

Risk: Market-price references from web search may be incomplete or unsuitable as final evidence.

Mitigation: Treat web search results as reference anchors only, require source links and retrieval dates, and mark missing evidence as pending rather than fabricating prices or cost support.
