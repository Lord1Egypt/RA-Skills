## Description:

Helps software maintainers, QA engineers, open-source contributors, and product teams add useful unit tests, raise test coverage, and validate changes against regression risk.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, QA engineers, maintainers, and product teams use this skill to plan, implement, and verify unit-test coverage improvements in existing codebases. It produces practical workflows, checklists, code or test changes, and validation notes that help prevent regressions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may activate the skill for general testing or quality requests where unit-test coverage support was not intended.

Mitigation: Invoke the skill explicitly for unit-test coverage tasks and confirm the desired testing outcome, constraints, and success criteria before acting.
