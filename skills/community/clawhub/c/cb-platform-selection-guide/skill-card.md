## Description:

A descriptive decision guide for selecting overseas ecommerce, marketplace, DTC, social commerce, and wholesale channels based on market, category, margin, operating readiness, and policy risk.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce founders, marketplace operators, DTC brand teams, and cross-border expansion managers use this skill to compare overseas channel options, score platform fit, and plan staged pilots with explicit assumptions and validation tasks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketplace fees, eligibility rules, platform policies, tax duties, and local compliance requirements may change or vary by market.

Mitigation: Verify current requirements directly with each platform, qualified local professionals, and authoritative sources before launch.

Risk: The skill provides planning guidance and may produce assumptions that are incomplete for a specific product, region, or channel.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as a decision framework, document assumptions, and validate recommendations through small pilots before scaling spend or inventory.
