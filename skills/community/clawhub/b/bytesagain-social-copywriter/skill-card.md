## Description:

Generates platform-optimized social media copy for Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, and Weibo, including A/B hook variants, hashtag strategies, and engagement formatting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Creators, marketers, and agents use this skill to draft platform-specific social posts, hashtag strategies, and A/B hook variants for common social channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated social copy, hashtags, or posting advice may be inaccurate, off-brand, or unsuitable for the target audience if published unchanged.

Mitigation: Review and edit all generated copy and guidance before posting from an account.

Risk: The broad trigger wording can produce canned social media templates for many topics, and the Weibo mode intentionally produces Chinese-language output.

Mitigation: Apply topic, audience, platform, and language review before using the output in a campaign.
