## Description:

Removes signs of AI-generated writing from text and rewrites it to sound more natural while preserving meaning, tone, and voice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shing19](https://clawhub.ai/user/shing19)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, employees, writers, and editors use this skill to revise AI-sounding drafts into more natural English. It is suited for style editing when the user still reviews the final text for factual accuracy, preserved meaning, and appropriate authorship perspective.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The rewrite may change the user's meaning, tone, or authorship perspective if applied too broadly.

Mitigation: Review the final output against the source text and confirm that factual claims, intent, and point of view are preserved.

Risk: Aggressive style editing may be inappropriate for legal, academic, professional, or sensitive writing.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an editing aid and require human review before relying on the revised text in sensitive contexts.
