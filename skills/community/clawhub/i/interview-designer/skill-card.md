## Description:

Analyze resumes and role requirements to produce evidence-based interview strategies that define success standards, test past claims, and simulate future performance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mikonos](https://clawhub.ai/user/mikonos)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Hiring teams, interviewers, recruiters, and managers use this skill to turn a role description and candidate resume into a structured interview guide with a scorecard, balanced evidence review, pressure-test questions, future scenarios, and a decision matrix.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
