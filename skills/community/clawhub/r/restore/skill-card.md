## Use Case:

Employees, external users, developers, and operations teams use this skill to structure backup, restore, and disaster recovery planning conversations around RPO/RTO goals, validation steps, runbooks, ownership, and restore drills.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may be offered for adjacent backup or reliability discussions where the user did not ask for a structured process.

Mitigation: Ask whether to follow the four-stage workflow or continue freeform before applying it.

Risk: Backup and restore guidance can be incomplete or misleading when environment, scale, RPO/RTO targets, deadlines, or operational ownership are unknown.

Mitigation: Ask targeted context questions, state assumptions and trade-offs, and include restore drills or other verification steps matched to the change impact.
