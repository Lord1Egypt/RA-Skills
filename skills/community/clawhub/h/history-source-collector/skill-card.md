K12 history educators, students, and history researchers use this skill to collect primary historical sources and authoritative academic references for a historical topic, with citations organized by source type and chronology.

Risk: History research queries may include sensitive student, personal, confidential, or regulated information handled through the stated internal academic search tool.

Mitigation: Use only organization-approved queries and exclude sensitive information unless that use is permitted.

Risk: Collected source excerpts and citations may be incomplete or unsuitable for direct academic use without review.

Mitigation: Review cited primary and secondary sources before using the output for teaching, coursework, or research.
