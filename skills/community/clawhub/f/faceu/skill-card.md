## Description:

FaceU organizes public information about FaceU templates, editing features, export specifications, help documentation, and pricing.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[codekungfu](https://clawhub.ai/user/codekungfu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content teams use this skill to summarize public FaceU template, editing, export, help, pricing, and commercial-use information. It is limited to public sources and excludes account actions, private assets, paid-resource bypasses, and legal conclusions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: FaceU copyright, commercial-use, pricing, or plan details may be misunderstood if summarized without source context.

Mitigation: Use official public FaceU pages and preserve original links for copyright, commercial-use, pricing, plan, and help claims.

Risk: The skill could be misused for private account actions, unpublished assets, paid-resource bypasses, or legal conclusions.

Mitigation: Keep usage limited to public information organization and avoid account control, private assets, paid-resource access, or legal advice.
