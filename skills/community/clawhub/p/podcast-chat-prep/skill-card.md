## Description:

Helps an agent turn podcast or live-audio transcripts into episode notes, cross-episode guest stance tracking, persona profiles, chat material libraries, and content-draft material.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shiyan521](https://clawhub.ai/user/shiyan521)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

External users and content researchers use this skill to analyze provided podcast or live-audio transcripts, then generate structured notes, cross-episode summaries, guest profiles, conversational talking points, and content draft material. It is best suited to authorized transcript-based study and preparation rather than claiming firsthand listening.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can be used to misrepresent transcript-based preparation as firsthand listening.

Mitigation: Use outputs as study notes or conversation preparation, and disclose when knowledge comes from transcript analysis rather than listening.

Risk: Podcast or private recording transcripts may include copyrighted or unauthorized material.

Mitigation: Process only transcripts the user is authorized to use, especially for private recordings, paid feeds, or copyrighted podcast content.

Risk: Generated stance changes, persona profiles, or contradictions may overstate what the transcript evidence supports.

Mitigation: Require concrete episode references and source quotations for claims about changed views, contradictions, persona labels, and notable stories.
