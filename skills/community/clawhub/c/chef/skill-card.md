## Use Case:

External users use this skill to get cooking support, including recipe guidance, technique explanations, ingredient substitutions, dietary adaptations, measurement scaling, troubleshooting, kitchen safety reminders, meal planning, and skill-building help.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Food safety, allergy, and dietary guidance may affect user health if treated as authoritative.

Mitigation: Treat safety and allergy guidance as general assistance, ask users about restrictions before suggesting recipes or substitutions, and encourage independent verification for critical dietary or medical concerns.

Risk: Recipe substitutions or scaling advice can change flavor, texture, timing, or whether a dish works.

Mitigation: Explain expected effects of substitutions and scaling, note when an ingredient is essential, and include timing or measurement caveats where precision matters.
