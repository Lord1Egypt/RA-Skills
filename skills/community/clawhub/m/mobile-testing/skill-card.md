## Description:

Plans, writes and reviews test strategies for Android, iOS and Flutter apps: mobile test pyramid, fakes over mocks, virtual time, Compose UI tests, XCUITest, Flutter integration_test, screenshot tests with Roborazzi, Paparazzi, swift-snapshot-testing and goldens, flaky-test quarantine, coverage that matters and CI sharding. Use when deciding what to test where, writing a test plan, fixing flaky or slow suites, or reviewing tests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
