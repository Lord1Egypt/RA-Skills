## Description:

Psych Companion supports emotional journaling, stress self-checks, CBT and mindfulness coping suggestions, and professional referral prompts for crisis signals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this Chinese-language mental wellness skill for structured mood journaling, emotion analysis, stress-management prompts, and general coping guidance. It is not a diagnostic, counseling, treatment, or emergency intervention tool.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake general emotional support or screening-style prompts for diagnosis, counseling, or treatment.

Mitigation: Present the skill as a lightweight journaling and coping aid, and direct users to qualified professionals for clinical decisions.

Risk: Crisis guidance is limited and may not provide region-appropriate emergency resources outside the Chinese hotline guidance in the artifact.

Mitigation: For urgent danger or self-harm risk, instruct users to contact local emergency services or a qualified professional immediately.

Risk: Several planned functions, including deeper stress assessment, personalized plan generation, periodic review, and tracking, are documented as incomplete or placeholder features.

Mitigation: Treat those areas as limited guidance and review outputs before relying on them in user-facing workflows.
