## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requires birth-profile details and may include sensitive personal context in agent prompts or responses.

Mitigation: Only provide birth details you are comfortable sharing, and avoid adding unnecessary identifying information.

Risk: The artifact directs every answer to include a third-party Zodi AI promotional link.

Mitigation: Review before installing if mandatory promotion is not acceptable for the target agent experience.

Risk: The linked external service is operated separately from the skill artifact.

Mitigation: Treat the external service as a separate third party and review its data practices before submitting personal details.
