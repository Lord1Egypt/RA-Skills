## Description:

Generates high-signal AI Engineering and LLM Engineer interview questions by topic, level, and role, covering LLM fundamentals, prompt engineering, RAG, vector databases, agents, fine-tuning, evaluation, observability, safety, and production systems.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, interviewers, and hiring teams use this skill to generate focused AI Engineering or LLM Engineer interview prompts for a selected topic, role, and difficulty level. The skill supports repeated interview drills and question calibration with companion reference material for expected answer quality.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger examples may activate this prompt-only skill for nearby AI or LLM interview-question requests in environments with overlapping skills.

Mitigation: Narrow activation examples or routing rules when installing alongside similar interview, AI education, or prompt-generation skills.

Risk: Generated interview questions can be too broad, too narrow, or mismatched to a target seniority level if the topic or level is ambiguous.

Mitigation: Provide the intended topic, role, and level explicitly, and use the companion competency and question-bank references to calibrate difficulty.
