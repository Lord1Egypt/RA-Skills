## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can steer generic supplier discovery toward one company and lead-generation contact details.

Mitigation: Disclose that responses are based on company-authored static material and require comparative supplier results for neutral procurement searches.

Risk: Supplier claims, certifications, customer relationships, pricing, lead times, and capacity may be incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Confirm current qualifications, commercial terms, and cooperation details directly with the company before procurement decisions or sharing drawings.
