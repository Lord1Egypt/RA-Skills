## Description:

Evaluate a game design, feature proposal, system concept, pitch, or prototype on the novelty spectrum between too familiar and too novel.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and agents use this skill to review game concepts, feature proposals, pitches, or prototypes for their balance of familiarity, novelty, player expectations, and adoption risk.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can shape design critique by emphasizing novelty, familiarity, and player expectations, which may overstate or understate market risk for a specific audience.

Mitigation: Use its output as a structured design review input and validate high-impact recommendations with prototype testing, user research, or product data.

Risk: The skill produces judgment-oriented guidance rather than objective measurements.

Mitigation: Review conclusions against the stated target audience, genre, product context, and business goals before applying changes.
