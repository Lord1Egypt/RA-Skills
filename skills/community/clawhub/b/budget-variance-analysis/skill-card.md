## Description:

Produce a structured budget variance analysis from actual vs budget figures, including variance tables, root cause analysis, and management commentary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Finance teams, analysts, and business reviewers use this skill to turn actual-versus-budget figures into structured variance commentary, including material variance explanations, root causes, management actions, and forecast impact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated variance commentary could be incomplete or misleading if the supplied actuals, budgets, threshold, or known explanations are wrong or incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the analysis against source finance records and validate all material variance explanations before using it in finance or board materials.

Risk: Budget and actuals data may contain confidential financial information.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting confidential financial data into environments that are not approved for that data.
