### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Financial model outputs may be mistaken for accounting, tax, investment, or fundraising advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as planning support and have qualified finance, legal, tax, or investment professionals review them before decisions or external use.

Risk: Startup forecasts can be misleading when assumptions, growth rates, churn, hiring, or fundraising timing are inaccurate.

Mitigation: Document assumptions, test base/bull/bear scenarios, and update the model with actuals before relying on runway or funding-gap estimates.

Risk: Financial modeling may involve confidential company financial data.

Mitigation: Avoid pasting sensitive data unless the workspace, model provider, and retention settings are appropriate for that sensitivity.
