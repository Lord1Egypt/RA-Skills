## Description:

Builds customer service, sales, and internal assistant chatbots with knowledge bases, multi-turn conversation flows, and API integration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and business teams use this skill to plan and configure AI chatbots for customer service, sales, and internal support. It covers document and website knowledge ingestion, FAQ setup, multi-turn dialog management, channel integration, handoff behavior, and conversation analytics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used with customer conversations, internal documents, scraped websites, and analytics data.

Mitigation: Define consent, retention, and redaction practices before using the skill with sensitive or regulated data.

Risk: The skill requests read, write, web_fetch, and message permissions.

Mitigation: Confirm the requested permissions match the intended chatbot-building workflow before installation.

Risk: Generated chatbot configurations and dialog flows may affect customer-facing or internal support behavior.

Mitigation: Review chatbot flows, knowledge sources, handoff thresholds, and analytics settings before deployment.
