## Description:

Turns interview notes into an evidence-based candidate scorecard with competency ratings, supporting evidence, open questions, and a hire recommendation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external hiring teams use this skill to turn interview notes into structured, comparable candidate evaluations. It helps organize feedback by role competency, identify evidence gaps, and produce a clear recommendation with confidence.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Interview notes may contain sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Handle notes under the organization's privacy and retention rules, and include only job-related evidence needed for the scorecard.

Risk: A generated hire recommendation can be misleading if it overstates thin evidence or unassessed competencies.

Mitigation: Require human review against the hiring process; mark unassessed areas and use open questions for follow-up before making decisions.

Risk: Bias-prone criteria such as vague culture fit can distort evaluations.

Mitigation: Anchor every rating to role competencies and specific interview evidence, replacing non-job-related impressions with concrete concerns or not-assessed labels.
