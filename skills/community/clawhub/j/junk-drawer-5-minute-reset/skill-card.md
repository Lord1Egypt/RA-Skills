## Description:

Provides a timed five-minute plan to sort one junk drawer into keep, move, trash, and caution zones for fast visible progress.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Household users use this skill to generate a printable, timer-based reset card for one overflowing junk drawer without expanding the task into a broader decluttering project.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may encounter sharp, unknown, leaking, broken, battery, chemical, or fragile items while resetting a drawer.

Mitigation: Use the caution zone, handle those items carefully, and defer disposal or repair decisions to appropriate household safety guidance after the five-minute reset.

Risk: A quick reset can expand into donation planning, resale sorting, hazardous waste decisions, or a whole-home declutter.

Mitigation: Keep the output scoped to one drawer, obvious trash, move-out items, useful keep zones, and a stop rule when the timer ends.
