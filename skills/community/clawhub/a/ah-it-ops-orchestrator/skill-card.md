## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

IT operators, administrators, and developers use this skill to triage cross-domain Windows, Azure, M365, AD, .NET, and PowerShell requests, route work to specialist agents, and consolidate responses with safety and least-privilege review points.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Administrative plans may steer users toward PowerShell automation affecting AD, Azure, M365, or infrastructure systems.

Mitigation: Review generated plans before applying changes, use least privilege, and require change review for administrative actions.

Risk: Routing guidance can inherit errors from downstream specialist responses.

Mitigation: Treat consolidated outputs as proposals and validate them against the target environment before execution.
