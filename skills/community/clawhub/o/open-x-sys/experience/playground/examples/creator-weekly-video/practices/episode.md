# Practice: decide the promise first

[Chinese edition](https://github.com/gengshupeng/open-x-sys/blob/main/skills/open-x-sys.zh-CN/experience/playground/examples/creator-weekly-video/practices/episode.md)

> Constructed example for the OpenXSys Playground. All figures are invented.

**What it is.** On Monday, before any script exists, choose the episode's title and thumbnail from three candidates. Write the first 30 seconds to keep exactly that promise. Then script, record and edit as before. Publish the day the edit is finished.

**The goal it serves.** Publish every Thursday, and keep more viewers past the first 30 seconds.

**Why it fits you.** You work alone, with about ten hours a week. You cannot afford rework, and your editing and scripting times are already steady. The only slack in your week was a two-day wait for a decision. Moving that decision to the front costs nothing and removes the wait. For a team with a separate thumbnail designer the right practice would be different.

**Evidence.** Measured here, on your own channel: four episodes before, five after (`data/episodes.csv`). Also reported widely by video creators as a working habit, which I have not verified against a study.

**Cost.** About 30 minutes on Monday. No tools, no money.

**What it changed.** The order of the work. The first 30 seconds are now written last among the script's parts, after the promise is fixed.

## Results so far

| | Before (weeks 1–4) | After (weeks 5–9) |
|---|---|---|
| Finish-to-publish gap, days | 2.1 | 0.3 |
| Published on Thursday | 1 of 4 | 5 of 5 |
| Retention at 30 seconds | 58% | 66% |
| Hours per episode | 9.5 | 9.1 |

Five episodes is a small sample. The gap and the publish day are clear. The retention change is likely and not yet certain: topics also differed.

## Switching conditions

- **Keep** while the gap stays under one day and hours stay under 11.
- **Adjust** if choosing on Monday starts to feel forced: allow one change of title before recording, never after.
- **Replace** if retention at 30 seconds falls back to 58 percent or below for three episodes in a row. Then the promise is not the lever, and the opening itself needs another look.
