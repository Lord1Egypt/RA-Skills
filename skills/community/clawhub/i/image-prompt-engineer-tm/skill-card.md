## Description:

Expert photography prompt engineering skill for AI image generation. Use when generating prompts for Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, Flux, product shots, portraits, landscapes, fashion photography, or other visual content that needs precise AI-generated imagery.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on generic prompt-writing requests because its trigger wording is broad.

Mitigation: Review whether the trigger wording fits the installation workflow and confirm activation behavior before deployment.

Risk: Generated image prompts may need review for brand, content, or rights-sensitive style requirements before commercial use.

Mitigation: Have users review final prompts against applicable brand, content, and rights policies before submitting them to an image-generation system.
