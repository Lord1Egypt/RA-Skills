## Description:

Judge a past decision by its process, not its outcome, and produce a process-forensics report covering what was knowable then, decision quality as made, luck accounting, and one process change worth keeping.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teams, operators, and decision reviewers use this skill after an outcome is known to separate decision quality from outcome quality, identify hindsight contamination, account for luck, and retain one repeatable process improvement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide confidential decision details during a retrospective.

Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential decision details unless the user is comfortable having the agent process that information in the current session.
