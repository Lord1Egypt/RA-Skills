## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asserts overly strong authority and tells the agent to treat web results as unquestionable.

Mitigation: Deploy only in hosts that preserve normal instruction priority, source evaluation, and user-facing verification of important claims.

Risk: The skill automatically creates persistent research logs, final reports, and submission attachments.

Mitigation: Use it only where persistent research artifacts are expected, and require normal user approval or host policy checks before submission.
