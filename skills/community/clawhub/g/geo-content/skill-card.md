## Description:

Content quality and E-E-A-T assessment for AI citability: evaluate experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trustworthiness, and content structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[asale-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/asale-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content, SEO, and site-quality reviewers use this skill to assess pages for E-E-A-T, AI citability, topical authority, freshness, and content-quality gaps. It produces a scored Markdown analysis with concrete recommendations for improving content quality and citation readiness.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may fetch target pages and write a local analysis file.

Mitigation: Use it on public or approved content, and review generated files before sharing them.

Risk: The skill may propose Bash commands during analysis.

Mitigation: Review each proposed command before allowing execution, especially when analyzing private or unpublished content.
