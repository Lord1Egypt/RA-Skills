## Description:

Explains a contract clause in plain English, identifies who it favors, rates practical risk, and suggests negotiation points with a reminder to confirm with counsel.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and professionals use this skill to understand individual contract clauses, assess whether terms are standard or aggressive, and prepare practical negotiation questions or redline suggestions before consulting counsel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Outputs may be incomplete or misleading when based only on a clause type or excerpt.

Mitigation: Ask for the actual clause text, reader's side, contract type, and specific concern, and flag that the full contract and jurisdiction can change the interpretation.

Risk: Users may treat clause explanations or negotiation suggestions as legal advice.

Mitigation: Retain clear not-legal-advice language and direct users to confirm conclusions with qualified counsel.
