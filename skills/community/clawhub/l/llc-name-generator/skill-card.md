## Description:

Generates LLC name ideas by industry, keyword, and tone, with state naming reminders and guidance for domain, trademark, and filing checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Entrepreneurs, startup teams, business consultants, and agents use this skill to brainstorm LLC names and receive practical next-step guidance before checking official state, domain, trademark, and legal resources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may present legal, domain, trademark, or name-availability results as if real checks were performed.

Mitigation: Verify names with the relevant Secretary of State database, domain registrar, USPTO resources, and qualified counsel before filing or relying on results.

Risk: Generated LLC names may be unavailable, conflict with existing businesses, or create trademark risk.

Mitigation: Treat generated names as brainstorming candidates and complete official availability, domain, trademark, and legal review before use.
