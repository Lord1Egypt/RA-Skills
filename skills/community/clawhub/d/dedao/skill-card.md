## Description:

Access and summarize publicly available courses, columns, and topics from Dedao without bypassing paywalls or downloading content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[codenova58](https://clawhub.ai/user/codenova58)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to retrieve and summarize public Dedao course, column, and topic pages, including instructors, update details, key points, and source links.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill could be misused to request paid content, protected downloads, or attempts to bypass platform limits.

Mitigation: Use it only for public Dedao pages, do not request paywalled or downloadable content, and respect platform access limits.

Risk: Summaries of public pages may omit details or reflect dynamic page content at the time of access.

Mitigation: Include source links and review generated summaries against the public page before relying on them.
