This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

In-house legal and compliance teams use this skill to navigate privacy compliance workflows, review data processing agreements, handle data subject access or deletion requests, and assess cross-border transfer considerations. It supports workflow guidance and checklist-style analysis but does not replace review by qualified legal professionals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat compliance workflow guidance as legal advice or rely on stale regulatory assumptions.

Mitigation: Verify current requirements with authoritative sources and qualified counsel before making legal determinations.

Risk: Data subject deletion or cross-border transfer questions can involve sensitive, multi-jurisdiction legal decisions.

Mitigation: Escalate complex or high-impact matters to senior counsel and confirm retention obligations, legal holds, third-party rights, and transfer safeguards before acting.
