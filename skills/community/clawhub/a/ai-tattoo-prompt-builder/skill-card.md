## Description:

Turn vague tattoo ideas into stronger AI-ready prompts by combining motif, style, placement, scale, and mood into one clearer instruction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[alpha777778](https://clawhub.ai/user/alpha777778)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn rough tattoo concepts into renderable prompts for image generation, placement comparison, prompt iteration, or discussion with a professional tattoo artist.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Tattoo outputs may be mistaken for final design, placement, or sizing advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as concept drafts and consult a professional tattoo artist for placement, sizing, and suitability.
