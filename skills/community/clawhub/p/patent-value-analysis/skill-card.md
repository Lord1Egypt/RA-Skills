## Description:

Assists users in evaluating patent asset value by retrieving patent details, scoring technical, legal, market, economic, and strategic dimensions, and producing an advisory patent valuation report.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chase-zhouchang](https://clawhub.ai/user/chase-zhouchang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and IP analysts use this skill to request a patent valuation from a patent name, review public patent details, and receive an advisory report with multidimensional scores, value range estimates, and conversion or financing suggestions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Patent valuation output may be inaccurate if public patent details, legal status, market assumptions, or pricing inputs are incomplete or stale.

Mitigation: Treat the report as informational and verify legal status, assumptions, and pricing with patent professionals or authoritative patent databases before relying on it.

Risk: When patent information cannot be found, the skill may use typical industry data to provide a reference estimate.

Mitigation: Label low-information estimates as provisional and request patent numbers or authoritative records before making legal, investment, licensing, or financing decisions.
