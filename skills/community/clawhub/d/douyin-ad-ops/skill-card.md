## Description:

Plan, structure, and optimize Douyin advertising workflows for lead generation, ecommerce, local business, live-room traffic, and conversion-oriented campaigns.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketers, operators, and business owners use this skill to turn a Douyin ad idea into a practical paid acquisition plan with campaign structure, audience angles, short-video hooks, budget tests, KPI targets, and conversion handoff copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated advertising claims, targeting ideas, lead collection flows, or campaign copy may be inaccurate or unsuitable for platform, legal, or brand requirements.

Mitigation: Review generated claims, conversion flows, and public-facing copy against Douyin advertising policy, local advertising law, and internal brand standards before use.

Risk: The helper script produces a first-pass plan from limited business input and may miss pricing, compliance, audience, or sales-context constraints.

Mitigation: Use script output as a draft brief and refine it with business context, conversion data, and human review before budget allocation or campaign launch.
