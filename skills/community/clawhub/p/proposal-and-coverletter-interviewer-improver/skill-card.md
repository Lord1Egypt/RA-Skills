## Description:

Structured interview to discover personal facts and generate reusable, approved statements for proposals and cover letters.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[drafthead](https://clawhub.ai/user/drafthead)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and employees use this skill to run structured interviews that collect professional facts, generate proposal and cover letter statement variants, and build a reusable library of approved statements for platforms such as Upwork, LinkedIn, email, job portals, and grants.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill stores proposal-related personal and company facts in local markdown files under the skill folder.

Mitigation: Install only when local storage of these facts is acceptable, avoid adding unnecessary sensitive details, and periodically review stored files.

Risk: Recurring interview sessions can gradually collect more personal or company-specific information than intended.

Mitigation: Be deliberate before scheduling recurring sessions and review new facts or statements before retaining or reusing them.
