## Description:

Turn your school, class, year, motto, and shared memories into original lyrics and a song the whole class can sing together. This school song maker writes a class song, graduation song, freshman welcome song, school anthem, teachers' day song, school anniversary song, or sports day song with a chorus that is easy to learn, shapes the words with you line by line, and produces the finished song with a student choir, children's choir, or solo voice for assemblies, opening and graduation ceremonies, class meetings, and school performances.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment flags broad permissions and persistent installation and runtime tracking.

Mitigation: Review and narrow permissions before installation, and ensure tracking is disclosed with a clear opt-out.

Risk: School details, student names, and shared memories may be sent to an external service as song inputs.

Mitigation: Protect student information and submit only details that are appropriate to share.

Risk: Shared credential cleanup could disrupt other installed Beatra skills when inventory data is uncertain.

Mitigation: Keep the shared connection unless the installed-skill inventory confirms this is the last Beatra skill.
