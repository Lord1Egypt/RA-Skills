## Description:

Generate day-wise travel itineraries with timings, food spots, activities, and transport. Handles "plan my trip", "make an itinerary", revisions like "swap days" or "make it cheaper", and adjusts plans when user has existing flights or trains.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to plan trips, refine itineraries, and adapt schedules around travel bookings, budgets, group size, food preferences, activities, and transport constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Travel details supplied by the user may be used in web searches for current places, restaurants, routes, and prices.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal details unless needed for planning, and review search-grounded recommendations before relying on them.

Risk: Itineraries depend on current external travel information and may include outdated or incorrect availability, timing, pricing, or route details.

Mitigation: Confirm critical bookings, transport times, opening hours, and prices with official providers before travel.
