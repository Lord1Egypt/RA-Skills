## Description:

Create complete prompt-led videos in Subclip using native Media, Text, Captions, Audio, Elements, plus editable Remotion, HyperFrames, or compact HTML/CSS motion layers. Use for product/site launches, explainers, PR reveals, talking-head packages, music edits, presentation-style videos, and catalog motion recipes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
