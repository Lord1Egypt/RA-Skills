## Description:

Accesses and summarizes public SMZDM hot deals, rankings, and promotions for keyword-based price alerts and category statistics without ordering.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[codekungfu](https://clawhub.ai/user/codekungfu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and shopping researchers use this skill to summarize public SMZDM deal, discovery, ranking, coupon, and event pages for price alerts, discount extraction, and category analysis. It is limited to public page organization and reminders, not purchasing, account actions, API reverse engineering, or bulk scraping.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be misused for logged-in account actions, purchasing, CAPTCHA bypass, API reverse engineering, or bulk scraping.

Mitigation: Use it only to open and summarize public SMZDM shopping and deal pages; keep ordering, account operations, bypasses, reverse engineering, and bulk collection out of scope.

Risk: Deal prices, coupons, rankings, and event windows can become stale or misleading after collection.

Mitigation: Include collection time and source links in summaries, and re-check public pages before acting on time-sensitive promotions.
