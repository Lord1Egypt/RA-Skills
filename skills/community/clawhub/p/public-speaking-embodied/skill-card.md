## Description:

Physical presence and delivery skills for public speaking and presentations. Use when someone needs to present at work, speak at an event, lead a meeting, or wants to develop commanding physical presence in group settings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The release declares filesystem access and an unpinned npx installer.

Mitigation: Install from a pinned or verified path and run installation with least privilege.

Risk: The skill mentions medication for severe performance anxiety.

Mitigation: Treat medication discussion as a prompt to consult a licensed clinician, not as medical advice from the skill.
