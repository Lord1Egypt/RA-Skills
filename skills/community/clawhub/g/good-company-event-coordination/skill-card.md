This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Community event organizers use this skill to prepare source-cited event briefs, scoped calendar comparisons, run-of-show and reminder drafts, and change or cancellation packets from authorized records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Calendar or event sources may be partial, stale, or conflicting, leading to inaccurate schedules.

Mitigation: Record source scope and observation time, cite each source, preserve unresolved gaps, and mark conflicting details tentative.

Risk: A reminder or cancellation draft could be mistaken for a delivered message.

Mitigation: Label draft and delivery states clearly, verify actual receipts, and reconcile ambiguous delivery with the original provider before retrying.

Risk: Private participant information or imported instructions could be exposed or treated as authorization.

Mitigation: Exclude private participant records from public drafts and require current scoped authority before external actions.
