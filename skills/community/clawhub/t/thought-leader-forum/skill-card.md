## Description:

Chinese-language role-simulation skill for structured debates, single-person perspective reports, and meeting commentary using prebuilt thought-leader personas.

This skill is for research and development only.

## Publisher:

[chaoliuzhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/chaoliuzhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external users can use this skill to simulate internal strategy discussions, individual advisory viewpoints, or meeting participation by named public-figure personas. Generated viewpoints are fictional AI simulations based on public information and should be reviewed before use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Simulated public-figure viewpoints may be mistaken for real statements or endorsements.

Mitigation: Keep explicit role-simulation labels on generated content and review outputs before sharing.

Risk: Users may paste confidential meeting notes, customer data, unreleased financials, or sensitive strategy into prompts.

Mitigation: Use only approved material and avoid submitting confidential or sensitive information unless authorized.

Risk: The skill's persona summaries may be incomplete, stale, or inaccurate for current strategic decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as fictional brainstorming aids and validate important claims against current authoritative sources.
