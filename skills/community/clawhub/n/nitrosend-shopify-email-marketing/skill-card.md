## Description:

Run Shopify email marketing by connecting a store, importing customers with their marketing consent, and using store activity to build segments, newsletters, and automated emails.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[nitrosend](https://clawhub.ai/user/nitrosend)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Shopify store teams use this skill to connect customer and order activity to email marketing, create customer segments, and prepare lifecycle campaigns while respecting marketing consent.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A customer may have opted out in Shopify after their consent status was imported.

Mitigation: Before sending a campaign, check for later opt-outs and unsubscribe those customers first.
