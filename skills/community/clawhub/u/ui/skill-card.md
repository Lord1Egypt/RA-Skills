## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: UI guidance may not fully account for right-to-left languages or locale-specific interface conventions.

Mitigation: Adapt alignment, spacing, and interaction guidance for the target locale before applying it.

Risk: General design advice can be misapplied to specialized products or accessibility requirements.

Mitigation: Review the recommendations against product-specific design standards and accessibility criteria before release.
