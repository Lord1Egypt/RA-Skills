## Description:

逐句批改 CET-4、CET-6 和考研英语作文或翻译作答，标注语言、逻辑和题型问题，给出评分依据、修订稿、高分改写和提分建议。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External learners and exam-preparation users use this skill to receive Chinese-language feedback on English exam writing or translation answers. It supports sentence-level correction, scoring, faithful revision, higher-scoring rewrites, source-aware model-essay handling, and targeted improvement checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Feedback may be less useful when users provide an essay without the exam type, prompt, chart, source text, or word-count requirement.

Mitigation: State missing materials and assumptions, use conservative estimates, and avoid presenting uncertain scores as final examiner results.

Risk: External model essays or online samples could be misrepresented as verified official examples.

Mitigation: Require source verification before labeling an essay as a real excellent sample, and provide original same-topic demonstrations when reliable sources are unavailable.

Risk: Users expecting another language may receive Chinese labels and report structure by default.

Mitigation: Ask for or honor an explicit output-language preference when feedback should be delivered outside the default Chinese format.
