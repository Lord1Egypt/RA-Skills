## Use Case:

Packaging designers, product teams, and compliance reviewers use this skill to plan packaging structures, compare food packaging materials, estimate package cost and dimensions, and check Chinese-market food packaging and labeling considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Packaging, labeling, or compliance guidance may be outdated or incomplete for production decisions.

Mitigation: Verify current regulations and final outputs with authoritative sources or qualified reviewers before production packaging, labeling, or compliance use.

Risk: Cost, dimension, barrier, and shrinkage calculations are estimates that may not reflect real package geometry, material behavior, supplier data, or test results.

Mitigation: Validate calculator outputs against engineering requirements, supplier specifications, physical testing, and product-specific constraints.
