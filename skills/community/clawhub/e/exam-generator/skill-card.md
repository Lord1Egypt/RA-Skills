## Description:

Exam Generator helps agents create China-focused primary and middle school exam papers with subject, grade, topic, question type, answer key, and printable-format guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tobewin](https://clawhub.ai/user/tobewin)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Teachers and education staff use this skill to draft China-focused K-9 exam papers and answer keys for specified subjects, grades, knowledge points, question types, difficulty levels, and exam durations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Web searches may include educational prompts that contain private student details, unpublished questions, or sensitive school materials.

Mitigation: Review or confirm searches before use, and avoid placing private or sensitive school content in search queries.

Risk: Generated exam files may be written to an unintended location.

Mitigation: Choose output directories deliberately and confirm generated Word files before sharing, printing, or storing them.

Risk: Generated questions and answers can require educator review before classroom use.

Mitigation: Check final question sheets and answer keys against the intended curriculum, grade level, and trusted source material.
