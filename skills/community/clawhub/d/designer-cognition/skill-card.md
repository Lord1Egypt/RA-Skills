## Description:

Designer Cognition guides visual design work using SOUL principles for design-system review, accessibility guardrails, and iterative design memory.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kingofzhao](https://clawhub.ai/user/kingofzhao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers, product teams, and agent builders use this skill to structure visual design decisions around known evidence, design-system fit, accessibility checks, human review, and design iteration records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can lead an agent to create local design logs, iteration history, component notes, and user research notes that may include confidential product or research information.

Mitigation: Store generated notes only in approved workspaces and review them for sensitive or proprietary content before sharing.

Risk: Accessibility and design-system checks are guidance and do not prove compliance or user acceptance.

Mitigation: Have qualified reviewers validate recommendations against WCAG 2.1 AA, design-system requirements, and user testing before implementation.

Risk: The source guidance is primarily Chinese-language, which can cause review gaps for teams that cannot read it directly.

Mitigation: Use reviewers who can read the source guidance or translate it before relying on design decisions.
