## Description:

Manage residential rental properties: tenant screening, lease tracking, maintenance coordination, rent collection monitoring, vacancy optimization, and compliance reminders for small landlords and lightweight property managers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users such as small landlords, self-managing property owners, and lightweight property managers use this skill to organize residential rental operations, including leases, rent tracking, maintenance, vacancies, tenant screening, and compliance reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal, eviction, notice, deposit, tax, or compliance guidance may be incomplete, stale, or jurisdiction-specific.

Mitigation: Verify current official requirements or consult a qualified legal or tax professional before sending notices, filing cases, withholding deposits, or making tax decisions.

Risk: The skill may store sensitive rental, tenant, lease, and financial details in the local OpenClaw workspace.

Mitigation: Install only when local workspace storage is acceptable, restrict workspace access, and avoid adding unnecessary personal or financial details.
