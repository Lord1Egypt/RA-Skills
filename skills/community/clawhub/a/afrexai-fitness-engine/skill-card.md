## Description:

Complete periodized training system for program design, progressive overload, recovery optimization, body composition, and race preparation across strength, hypertrophy, endurance, hybrid, and general fitness goals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers and external users can use this skill to help an agent assess training programs, generate periodized strength or endurance plans, structure recovery and nutrition guidance, and produce workout logs or review templates. It is educational fitness guidance and should not be treated as medical advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake fitness, injury, nutrition, or supplement guidance for medical advice.

Mitigation: Present the skill as educational fitness programming support and direct users to qualified medical, nutrition, coaching, or physical therapy professionals for injuries, medical conditions, supplement suitability, severe symptoms, or return-to-sport decisions.
