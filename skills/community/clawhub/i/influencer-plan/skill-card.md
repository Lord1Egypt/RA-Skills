## Use Case:

Startup founders and go-to-market teams use this skill to plan influencer and creator partnerships, including creator selection, partnership models, outreach, campaign execution, and ROI measurement.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated outreach, pricing, contract, or ROI guidance could be inaccurate or unsuitable for a specific campaign.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before spending budget, contacting creators, or relying on contract-related guidance.

Risk: Influencer plans may overemphasize vanity metrics or misaligned creators if user inputs are incomplete.

Mitigation: Validate creator fit, audience overlap, attribution setup, and success metrics before launch.
