## Description:

Transform a single piece of content into platform-optimized versions for Xiaohongshu, WeChat Official Accounts, Douyin, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and related channels while preserving the core message.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Content marketers, founders, and communications teams use this skill to turn articles, transcripts, research summaries, or announcements into channel-specific posts and scripts. It helps adapt tone, structure, language, hashtags, and posting guidance for different social platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated posts may be misaligned with the intended audience, output language, or selected platform.

Mitigation: Treat generated posts as drafts and confirm the audience, language, and platform choices before publishing.
