## Use Case:

Community managers, founders, and marketing operators use this skill to plan engagement, onboarding, moderation, content calendars, and health tracking for Discord, Slack, Telegram, or forum communities.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local community notes may contain member details or moderation history if the user records them.

Mitigation: Keep member notes minimal and avoid storing PII, private contact details, credentials, or sensitive moderation records unless intentionally required.
