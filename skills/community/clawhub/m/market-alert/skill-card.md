This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to request market watchlist monitoring, movement alerts, stock research summaries, opening and closing commentary, and portfolio-oriented summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill expands from stock alerts into sensitive personal finance records, statements, tax details, bills, and allocation advice without clear data-handling limits.

Mitigation: Install only when personal finance assistance is intended, and require explicit user consent before processing account records, statements, tax details, bills, or allocation advice.

Risk: Financial summaries or recommendations may influence investment or transfer decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational guidance and require user review, independent verification, and strong identity checks before trades or large transfers.
