## Description:

副业/独立开发项目验证工具。当用户有了一个副业想法、产品 idea、或者想验证某个方向能不能做时使用。帮助分析需求真伪、竞品情况、市场规模、变现可能和实施难度。适用于副业规划、独立开发、产品验证等场景。附带真实案例库、数据调研脚本。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External builders, indie developers, and product planners use this skill to validate side-project or product ideas through demand checks, competitor analysis, market sizing, monetization review, implementation planning, and recommendation scoring.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Running the bundled research scripts can disclose search keywords or business ideas to third-party services.

Mitigation: Avoid sensitive or confidential terms when using the scripts, or perform equivalent research manually in approved tools.

Risk: Validation reports may influence business decisions with incomplete or stale market evidence.

Mitigation: Review cited search results, competitor data, and user evidence before acting on the recommendation score.
