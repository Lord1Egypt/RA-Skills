## Use Case:

External users and business teams use this Chinese-language skill to collect project details through guided Q&A and draft a 15-20 page PPT business plan for fundraising, technology transfer, market entry, or strategy planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may enter confidential business, financial, or strategy details during guided Q&A.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in a trusted agent environment and avoid entering sensitive details unless the environment is approved for that data.

Risk: Generated market sizing, financial projections, and risk analysis may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review outputs against source data and have qualified business, finance, or legal reviewers validate material assumptions before external use.
