## Description:

Converts bitmap physics, geometry, circuit, and annotated diagrams into precise editable vector code, with HTML Canvas as the default and SVG as an option.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wxdwqy](https://clawhub.ai/user/wxdwqy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, educators, and agents use this skill to inspect bitmap problem diagrams, verify their components with the user, and produce scalable Canvas or SVG representations for physics animation preparation or editable diagram reuse.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bitmap interpretation may omit, misplace, or mislabel physics diagram elements.

Mitigation: Use the skill's required problem-text cross-check and user verification steps before final vector code or export.

Risk: Chinese-first instructions may be less accessible for users who need fully bilingual documentation.

Mitigation: Confirm labels, workflow steps, and final output requirements with users who need English coverage.
