## Description:

Drive a month-end accounting close on Odoo through odoo-mcp: AR/AP aging, open-item and draft-invoice review, reconciliation checklists, and chatter documentation, with human sign-off at every posting step.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Finance operators and authorized business users use this skill to run an Odoo month-end close review, compare baseline and final accounting summaries, inspect AR/AP aging and draft invoices, and prepare close documentation with explicit approval before financial changes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can expose or help modify sensitive Odoo accounting records if used by someone outside the intended finance scope.

Mitigation: Install it only for users authorized to view and help manage Odoo accounting records, and confirm the Odoo company scope before use.

Risk: Posting, reconciliation, deletion, field correction, or chatter updates can have accounting impact if approved without review.

Mitigation: Review every previewed write carefully and approve only after the accounting impact is understood.
