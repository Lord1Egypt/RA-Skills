## Description:

Guides agents through GEO and AI search visibility work so a brand can improve its chances of being cited and recommended by AI answer engines.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketing teams, founders, and agents use this skill to audit AI answer-engine visibility, identify cited sources, improve retrievability, earn authentic mentions, structure extractable content, and clarify brand entity signals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may influence marketing strategy using changing AI-search behavior and public prompt-audit observations.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as advisory, re-run audits periodically, and verify current engine behavior and GEO tooling before acting on time-sensitive claims.

Risk: Users may ask for fabricated share-of-voice percentages or unmeasured citation frequency.

Mitigation: Report only measured audit, tool, or referral-analytics results; otherwise provide the measurement method and state the limitation clearly.

Risk: Users may request astroturfing, fake reviews, or paid community shilling to manipulate AI citations.

Mitigation: Refuse deceptive activity and redirect to authentic participation, earned mentions, real reviews, and transparent expert contributions.

Risk: Crawler, robots.txt, CDN, analytics, or third-party GEO-tool checks can affect site operations or expose operational assumptions.

Mitigation: Have qualified site owners review any crawler-access, CDN, analytics, or tooling changes before implementation.
