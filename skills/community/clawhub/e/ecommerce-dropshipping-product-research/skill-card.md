## Description:

Evaluates dropshipping products by scoring demand, competition, margin, creative fit, and risk to recommend go, test, or reject decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators, Shopify sellers, and product researchers use this skill to evaluate dropshipping product ideas with a heuristic scorecard and decide whether to go, test, or reject a candidate.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The product scores and recommendations are rough ecommerce heuristics and may be wrong for a specific market, product, or ad account.

Mitigation: Validate supplier costs, shipping times, compliance constraints, customer demand, and ad performance with real tests before spending meaningfully or scaling.

Risk: Broad trigger terms can activate the skill for general product-research requests where dropshipping-specific scoring was not intended.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants dropshipping-oriented scoring before relying on the go, test, or reject recommendation.

Risk: The skill does not provide financial, legal, or compliance clearance.

Mitigation: Review claims, intellectual-property issues, restricted-product rules, and marketplace or advertising policies before launch.
