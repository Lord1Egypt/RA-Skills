## Description:

Academic Search Zh helps users plan Chinese and English academic literature searches, choose suitable databases, analyze abstracts and research methods, and organize findings into structured literature-review guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[olina1ye](https://clawhub.ai/user/olina1ye)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, teachers, and R&D teams use this skill to formulate search strategies, identify relevant academic databases, interpret paper methods, and draft structured literature-review outlines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad research-writing prompts beyond literature-search support.

Mitigation: Review activation scope and use it for search strategy, paper analysis, and literature-review organization rather than unsupervised academic writing.

Risk: Users may ask for copyrighted paywalled full text.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing paywalled full text unless the user has rights; work from lawful access, abstracts, metadata, citations, or user-provided excerpts.

Risk: Search strategies and literature summaries can be incomplete without checking actual academic databases.

Mitigation: Verify suggested queries, databases, and literature claims in the relevant scholarly databases before relying on them.
