## Description:

Writing Assistant Pro is a Chinese-first writing assistant for drafting, rewriting, headline generation, and topic ideation using a three-layer identity, operations, and knowledge structure.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to draft Chinese-language copy, improve existing text, generate title options, and plan content topics for channels such as Xiaohongshu, WeChat public accounts, and sales copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill describes learning writing preferences across sessions.

Mitigation: Review or clear memory and shared-context files when retained style preferences are not desired.

Risk: Broad Chinese writing trigger words may activate the assistant aggressively.

Mitigation: Review generated output and provide explicit task boundaries when using general writing prompts.
