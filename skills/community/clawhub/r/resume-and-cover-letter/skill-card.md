## Description:

Generate ATS-optimized resumes and tailored cover letters matched to specific job descriptions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[seanwyngaard](https://clawhub.ai/user/seanwyngaard)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and job seekers use this skill to tailor resumes, cover letters, and keyword match reports for specific job descriptions using their career profile or existing resume.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated application materials may include personal career and contact details supplied by the user.

Mitigation: Provide only information intended for inclusion in resumes, cover letters, or reports, and review generated files before sharing them.

Risk: The skill writes resume, cover-letter, HTML, optional LaTeX, and report files under output/career-docs/.

Mitigation: Review existing files in output/career-docs/ before running the skill when preserving prior generated materials matters.
