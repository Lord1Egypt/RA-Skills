## Description:

Summarizes the current conversation into a clean, copy-ready Chinese problem summary for asking another AI platform.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jufangyuan](https://clawhub.ai/user/jufangyuan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn the active conversation into a factual, copy-ready Chinese summary for continuing the question on platforms such as Doubao or DeepSeek.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated summaries may include secrets, credentials, private messages, personal data, internal documents, or confidential business details from the active conversation.

Mitigation: Review the summary before sharing it with another AI service and remove sensitive or confidential details.
