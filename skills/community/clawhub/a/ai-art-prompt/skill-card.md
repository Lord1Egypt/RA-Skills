## Description:

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

External creators, designers, and developers use this skill to turn a supplied visual theme into structured AI art prompts and prompt variations for image-generation tools.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Mitigation: Avoid entering private, sensitive, credential-like, or confidential information in the theme field.

Risk: Use of the service is subject to documented pricing and request limits.

Mitigation: Review the applicable plan limits before relying on the skill for high-volume workflows.
