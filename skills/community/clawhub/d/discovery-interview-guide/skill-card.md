This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Product managers, founders, researchers, and customer-facing teams use this skill to plan discovery interviews, customer discovery sessions, Jobs-to-be-Done research, and problem validation. It helps produce behavior-focused interview guides with recruiting screeners and post-session synthesis structure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated interview guides may lead participants if questions validate a predetermined answer.

Mitigation: Review prompts to keep problem exploration open-ended, past-behavior focused, and separated from product or feature discussion until pain is confirmed.

Risk: Research conclusions may be overstated if synthesis is based on too few interviews.

Mitigation: Use the per-session synthesis template and wait for repeated patterns across the planned sample before treating findings as themes.
