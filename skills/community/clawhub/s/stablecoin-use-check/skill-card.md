## Description:

A decision guide that helps users evaluate whether a stablecoin use case makes sense for them.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to decide whether holding, sending, earning, converting, or buffering with a stablecoin fits their situation. It compares the use case with alternatives and surfaces depeg, platform, regulatory, and counterparty risks before the user commits funds.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat stablecoin decision support as financial advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as general decision support and independently verify stablecoin, platform, regulatory, and counterparty risks before committing funds.

Risk: Stablecoin use cases can hide depeg, platform, regulatory, and counterparty risks.

Mitigation: Use the skill's risk review and alternatives comparison as a checklist, then confirm details against current external sources before acting.
