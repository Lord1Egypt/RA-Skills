## Description:

将长篇小说或剧情片段转换为面向 Seedance 2.0 的 15 秒动态漫画分镜脚本，包含剧情容量分析、画风和画幅确认、旁白音色、6-8 个镜头、镜头运动、音频与输出检查。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

[fslong520](https://clawhub.ai/user/fslong520)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators, writers, and video-generation operators use this skill to turn Chinese novel chapters or plot excerpts into concise Seedance/VOMIC-style dynamic-comic storyboard scripts. It guides the user through story capacity analysis, style and aspect-ratio choices, narration voice selection, shot design, and final output checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill strongly steers responses toward a Chinese Seedance/VOMIC 15-second dynamic-comic workflow, which may be unsuitable for other languages, locales, platforms, or broader video-writing tasks.

Mitigation: Use explicit instructions or a different skill when the desired output is not Chinese Seedance/VOMIC-style dynamic-comic storyboarding.
