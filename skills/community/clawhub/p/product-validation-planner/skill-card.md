## Description:

Helps founders and small teams assess product ideas by turning goals into practical validation workflows, checklists, prototypes, and user-facing positioning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Solo founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to decide whether a product idea is worth building before investing heavily. It turns their goals and constraints into a tailored validation plan, reusable checklist, prototype, analysis, or positioning artifact.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad triggers such as “validation” or “prototype” may activate the skill for unrelated requests.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly or narrow trigger wording to product idea validation tasks.
