## Description:

Training Execution tracks on-site training delivery, including course status, attendance, material distribution, assessments, issue logs, and emergency handling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[golngod](https://clawhub.ai/user/golngod)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Training coordinators and operations teams use this skill to manage live training events from initialization through daily execution, issue handling, assessment tracking, and post-training summary reporting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Training rosters, attendance records, and issue logs may contain sensitive learner or participant details.

Mitigation: Provide only the learner details needed for attendance and training records, and omit unnecessary sensitive information.

Risk: Generated logs and summaries are reference suggestions and may not reflect the final on-site decision record.

Mitigation: Have the responsible organizer or site lead review and confirm execution records before they are used operationally.
