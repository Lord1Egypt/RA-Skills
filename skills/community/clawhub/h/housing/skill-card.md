## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Housing guidance can be mistaken for jurisdiction-specific legal, tax, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as general guidance and confirm local requirements with qualified professionals before acting.

Risk: Rental applications, tenant screening, and move-in documentation can involve sensitive personal or property data.

Mitigation: Collect only necessary information with consent, limit sharing, store it securely, and delete it when no longer needed.

Risk: Real estate market data and local housing rules can become stale quickly.

Mitigation: Verify current local prices, regulations, taxes, and tenant protections before relying on recommendations.
