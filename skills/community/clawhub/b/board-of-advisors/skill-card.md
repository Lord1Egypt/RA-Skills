## Description:

Access strategic advice from 15 fictional advisor personas for governance, fundraising, exits, risk, global expansion, scaling, talent, and other board-level company decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rmarji](https://clawhub.ai/user/rmarji)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Founders, executives, and business operators use this skill to frame company-defining strategic decisions through advisor-style questions, frameworks, and warning signs. It is intended for brainstorming and decision support, not as a replacement for legal, financial, board, or investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat fictional advisor output as legal, financial, board, or investment advice.

Mitigation: Use the responses as brainstorming support and have qualified professionals, executives, or board members review decisions before acting.

Risk: The broad strategic-business trigger may cause the advisor style to appear for general business questions.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants board-level strategic framing and choose only the advisor persona relevant to the stated challenge.
