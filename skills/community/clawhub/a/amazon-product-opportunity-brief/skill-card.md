## Description:

Create a decision-ready Amazon product opportunity brief from a product idea, keyword, ASIN, or URL while separating assumptions from evidence and defining a lean validation plan.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External sellers and product researchers use this skill to turn an Amazon product idea, keyword, ASIN, or URL into a structured opportunity brief, validation plan, and evidence boundary.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The brief could be mistaken for current marketplace validation or a product success prediction.

Mitigation: Treat the output as planning support and independently verify current marketplace data, fees, IP, platform policy, and legal or regulatory issues before acting.

Risk: User-supplied assumptions or dated evidence could lead to misleading product conclusions.

Mitigation: Keep known facts, assumptions, unknowns, and needs-verification items separate, and use falsifiable hypotheses with dated evidence collection.

Risk: Combining marketplaces can blur currencies, rankings, sales estimates, reviews, and policy constraints.

Mitigation: Prepare one brief per marketplace and state the marketplace, source date range, and missing evidence explicitly.
