## Description:

Practical guidance for navigating grief after loss. Use when someone has lost a person, relationship, job, or life chapter and needs help understanding what they're experiencing and how to move through it.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[howtousehumans](https://clawhub.ai/user/howtousehumans)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and their agents use this skill for practical, non-clinical support after the death of a person, the end of a relationship, job loss, or another major life transition. It helps users understand grief, maintain basic routines, plan rituals or check-ins, respond to unhelpful comments, and identify when professional or crisis support is needed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may retain sensitive grief and mental-health details across sessions without clear consent, retention, deletion, or storage safeguards.

Mitigation: Use it only where saved state can be reviewed and deleted, obtain explicit consent before retaining sensitive details, and minimize persisted information about loss, self-harm, depression, substance use, and check-ins.

Risk: The install command is unpinned and could be unsafe in privileged environments.

Mitigation: Review the release before deployment and install in a low-privilege environment with pinned or otherwise controlled package resolution.

Risk: Users may treat grief guidance as a substitute for professional support during traumatic loss, prolonged grief, depression, self-harm risk, or substance-use concerns.

Mitigation: Keep the skill framed as supportive guidance, escalate self-harm to crisis resources, and direct users with severe or persistent symptoms to qualified professional care.
