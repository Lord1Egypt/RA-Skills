## Description:

Generates standardized after-sales service procedures for returns, complaints, escalation, service recovery, compensation, quality monitoring, and team training.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Customer support leaders, operations teams, and service managers use this skill to turn business policies, team structure, common issues, and service standards into tailored after-sales workflows and guidelines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated refund, credit, escalation, or legal-complaint thresholds could be adopted without sufficient company policy review.

Mitigation: Review generated thresholds and escalation rules before using them in production customer-service operations.
