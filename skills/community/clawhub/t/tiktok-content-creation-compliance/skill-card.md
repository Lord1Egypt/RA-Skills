## Description:

Review TikTok Shop seller and creator promotional content compliance using official TikTok Shop Seller University guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[langyou](https://clawhub.ai/user/langyou)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External sellers, creators, and their AI agents use this skill to review TikTok Shop promotional videos, LIVEs, scripts, captions, listing images, and ads for policy risks and safer revisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

United States; verify market-specific TikTok Shop policy before use elsewhere.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: TikTok Shop policies can change after the bundled policy summary was retrieved.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to verify the live official TikTok Shop Seller University pages before relying on current-rule answers.

Risk: The skill is specialized for TikTok Shop promotional-content review and may not cover non-US market rules or edge cases.

Mitigation: Confirm the relevant market scope and request live official policy verification for non-US or ambiguous cases.

Risk: Compliance reviews can affect commercial content decisions.

Mitigation: Use the skill's verdicts and rewrites as review guidance, and escalate legal, regulatory, FDA, FTC, or unclear policy questions to qualified reviewers.
