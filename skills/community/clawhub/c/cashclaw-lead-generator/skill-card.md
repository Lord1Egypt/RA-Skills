## Description:

Generates qualified B2B leads through systematic research, data collection, and scoring. Delivers structured lead lists with contact information and qualification scores.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales, growth, and business development users use this skill to define an ideal customer profile, research public business sources, score prospects, and assemble qualified B2B lead lists for outreach.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security evidence flags broad contact harvesting with weak scoping.

Mitigation: Use narrowly defined business ICPs, avoid sensitive targeting queries, and confirm that collected contacts are business-only and legally usable before outreach.

Risk: The security evidence flags an unpinned external npm installer.

Mitigation: Prefer a pinned and reviewed package version or the inspected local script in a restricted environment.

Risk: Generated lead exports may contain contact data.

Mitigation: Protect exported files, limit retention, and delete lead files promptly when they are no longer needed.
