## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: India trade, certification, tax, and incentive rules can change quickly, so answers may become outdated or incomplete.

Mitigation: Verify important decisions against official BIS, DGFT, GST, and DPITT notices or a qualified advisor.

Risk: GST treatment, state-level execution, PLI eligibility, and BIS certification timing can vary by product, jurisdiction, or application window.

Mitigation: Treat the skill output as research assistance and confirm product-specific obligations, rates, deadlines, and certification timelines before shipment or filing.
