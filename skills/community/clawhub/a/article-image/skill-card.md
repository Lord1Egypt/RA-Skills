## Description:

Article Image helps agents recommend article image sources, search keywords, visual styles, sizes, and copyright reminders for covers and inline illustrations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[regexl](https://clawhub.ai/user/regexl)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, editors, marketers, and content agents use this skill to draft image search plans for articles, including candidate sources, bilingual keywords, visual direction, sizing guidance, and license reminders.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommended image sources or keywords may lead users to images with attribution, commercial-use, or paid-license requirements.

Mitigation: Verify each image license, attribution requirement, and usage scope before publishing, especially for commercial work.
