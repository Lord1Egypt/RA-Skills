## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Travel recommendations may become stale because route popularity, weather, appointments, permissions, crowding, costs, and venue rules can change.

Mitigation: Confirm current local conditions, official venue requirements, and weather alerts before relying on a recommended route.

Risk: The skill may activate for Chinese-language island travel-planning requests where the user did not intend to use a destination recommendation skill.

Mitigation: Review the skill description and trigger context before installation to ensure this behavior is desired.
