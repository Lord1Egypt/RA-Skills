## Description:

Chinese-first sales objection coaching skill that helps salespeople analyze customer objections and produce response talk tracks, emails, supporting material suggestions, warnings, and next actions for AI compute deals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dashiming](https://clawhub.ai/user/dashiming)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales teams use this skill to turn pricing, competitor, technical, process, and security objections into concise Chinese-first coaching guidance for AI compute deal conversations. It can support customer calls, email follow-up, and sales-stage planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated sales claims may include performance, certification, SLA, or pricing statements that do not match the seller's actual product or contract terms.

Mitigation: Review and adapt the generated claims before using them with customers.

Risk: Sales coaching templates may be too generic for regulated, government, or security-sensitive customers.

Mitigation: Have the responsible sales, legal, security, or product team approve customer-facing language for sensitive deals.
