## Description:

Audit test/calibration lab documents against ISO/IEC 17025:2017 before release. Flags nonconforming reports, certificates, and quality records with clause-level citations and fix suggestions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1003429073](https://clawhub.ai/user/1003429073)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Laboratory quality, accreditation, and technical staff use this skill to review test reports, calibration certificates, quality procedures, validation records, and related claims against ISO/IEC 17025:2017 before release. It highlights conformant items, observations, and nonconformities with clause-level citations and suggested corrections.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used as if it provides final accredited compliance, legal, or regulatory judgment.

Mitigation: Treat results as an ISO/IEC 17025 review aid and have qualified personnel validate findings before relying on them for accreditation, release, or legal decisions.

Risk: Incorrect or incomplete document inputs can lead to missed or misleading nonconformity findings.

Mitigation: Review the submitted laboratory documents and the generated clause-level findings item by item before release.
