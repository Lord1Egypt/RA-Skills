## Description:

Sign a business up for GhostEngine and run its paid ads on Google, Meta, TikTok, and Reddit through the GhostEngine MCP server, within limits the owner approves.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Business owners and marketing operators use the agent to connect a business to GhostEngine, configure and manage paid advertising campaigns, and review performance and attribution within owner-approved permissions and spending limits.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Before connecting accounts or installing the pixel, consider what data may be received and whether the site's disclosures and consent controls cover that tracking.

Risk: The security scan was partial because a local reference could not be resolved, leaving evidence about that reference incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the unresolved local reference before relying on the skill.
