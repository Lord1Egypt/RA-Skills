## Description:

超能去AI is a Chinese text rewriting skill that diagnoses AI-like wording and rewrites creative, social, and informal text through specialized editor roles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kongzichixiangjiao](https://clawhub.ai/user/kongzichixiangjiao)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to rewrite Chinese AI-generated text into more human-sounding creative, social, speech, and informal prose. It is not intended for academic, legal, news, technical, or other fact-sensitive documents.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may add or reshape details to make text feel more human, which can introduce inaccuracies in fact-sensitive writing.

Mitigation: Use it only for Chinese creative or informal rewriting, and review rewritten output against the original before publishing or relying on it.

Risk: Reducing AI-like style can obscure authorship or disclosure expectations in settings where transparency matters.

Mitigation: Avoid use for academic, legal, news, technical, or authorship-sensitive text unless the user has confirmed that rewriting and disclosure practices are permitted.
