## Description:

Generates a Chinese enterprise investment-attraction feasibility analysis report using public, lawful, verifiable information across expansion signals, industrial fit, regional capacity, local relationships, compliant outreach paths, scoring, and landing-risk controls.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External investment-promotion and economic-development teams use this skill to evaluate whether a target enterprise is worth recruiting, likely to expand or relocate, compatible with a target region, and reachable through compliant public channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The report could be mistaken for proof that a company intends to expand or relocate.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision support and verify expansion or relocation intent through formal, public, or direct company channels before action.

Risk: Unverifiable relationship claims or non-public personal contact details could create compliance and privacy issues.

Mitigation: Use only public, lawful, verifiable sources and exclude private contact details or unsupported relationship claims.
