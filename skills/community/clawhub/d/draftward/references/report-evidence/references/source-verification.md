# Source verification

Treat source existence, metadata accuracy, link accessibility, claim support and citation style as separate checks.

1. Inventory all citations and bibliography entries, including notes, captions, tables, appendices and listings. Reconcile cited keys, duplicates, unused entries and missing references using the actual final inventory.
2. Verify each work against the publisher, standards body, DOI registry, institution or official repository. Search snippets alone are insufficient. Record the authoritative page/document and verification outcome.
3. Match authors or corporate author, title, venue, year, edition/version, volume/issue, page range or article number, DOI and URL where applicable. Distinguish publication, revision and access dates. Do not invent absent metadata.
4. Open links and follow redirects. Verify the final destination is the intended work and version. Record working, moved, restricted, temporarily inaccessible, unresolved or incorrect. A paywall, network error or access block is not evidence that the work does not exist. Prefer a verified canonical page or official archive when repairing a link.
5. Check claim support in the actual source, including important quotations and page/section locators. A real bibliography entry does not validate a nearby claim. Handle mixed-source sentences so attribution remains clear. Investigate correction or retraction notices encountered.
6. Check the selected referencing style against its applicable primary guide and assignment rules. Do not convert a document to IEEE, impose journal layout, or upgrade a standards version merely because previous work used it.
7. After writing changes, recheck affected claims. After export, verify the final bibliography inventory and clickable external targets as well as internal citation destinations. Distinguish links structurally present in a PDF from destinations actually fetched successfully.

A compact per-entry record needs: citation key; intended work/version; authoritative source; metadata result; link target/status; claims checked; correction; remaining uncertainty. Scale claim checks to the requested scope, prioritising consequential claims while stating any unchecked coverage. If a full references audit was requested, account for every entry even when some cannot be verified.

Keep private report text and source data local unless uploading is authorised. Research public citations with bibliographic terms rather than transmitting confidential paragraphs.
