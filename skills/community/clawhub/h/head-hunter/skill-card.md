## Description:

AI-powered headhunter system for intelligent candidate-job matching that provides professional recruitment assistance with multi-dimensional matching algorithms, skill analysis, and candidate recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhenstaff](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhenstaff)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Recruiters, HR teams, and hiring managers use this skill to compare candidates against job requirements, rank candidate pools, and produce recruitment recommendations for human review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Candidate profiles may contain personal data.

Mitigation: Avoid providing unnecessary personal data, use appropriate privacy controls, and handle candidate information according to applicable hiring and data-protection policies.

Risk: Candidate rankings and recommendations may influence hiring outcomes.

Mitigation: Treat scores and recommendations as decision support only; require human review before interview, rejection, or hiring decisions.

Risk: The skill installs the email-validator package at runtime.

Mitigation: Install dependencies in a virtual environment and consider pinning email-validator before use.
