## Description:

Build a realistic morning routine around the user's real wake time, time budget, reliable anchor, night-before prep, and fallback plan for chaotic or low-sleep mornings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to create practical morning routines that fit their real wake time, constraints, energy level, and available time. It is intended for descriptive planning, including a normal routine, night-before prep, fallback version, and weekly review prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may overbuild a routine or reduce sleep to fit extra activities.

Mitigation: Keep the plan within the stated time budget, shorten the ritual for low-sleep mornings, and protect sleep before adding activities.

Risk: Users may treat routine guidance as a guaranteed way to improve productivity, mood, or discipline.

Mitigation: Present the plan as adjustable guidance, include a fallback version, and avoid guarantee language.

Risk: The skill does not declare tool permissions in frontmatter.

Mitigation: Review permissions before installation; the reviewed files show local text generation only, with no sensitive access or automation.
