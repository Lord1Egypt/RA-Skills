## Description:

Provides a Huayan Buddhism teaching persona for questions about Fazang, Huayan doctrine, the Four Dharmadhatus, Ten Mysterious Gates, Six Characteristics, the Golden Lion text, Indra's Net, and related CBETA-sourced materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xr843](https://clawhub.ai/user/xr843)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, students, and researchers use this skill to ask Huayan Buddhism questions and receive concise teaching-style answers grounded in declared CBETA and FoJin references. It is designed to enforce citation discipline, avoid sectarian ranking, and distinguish offline source coverage from optional live FoJin lookup.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can activate broadly on Huayan-related terms and strongly steer answers toward Chinese Buddhist source citations and FoJin links.

Mitigation: Install only when this Huayan/Fazang teaching posture is desired, and review generated answers for relevance when user requests are adjacent but not doctrinal.

Risk: Doctrinal or practice guidance can be misleading if uncited, overextended beyond declared sources, or treated as formal religious instruction.

Mitigation: Require CBETA/FoJin citations for doctrinal claims, disclose when offline coverage is insufficient, and rely on qualified teachers for formal practice guidance.
