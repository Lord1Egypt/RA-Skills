## Description: <br>
Guides agents through analyzing elite performers' hidden advantage dimensions to separate learnable, partially imitable, and structurally constrained advantages for long-term development planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, coaches, founders, investors, operators, and other knowledge workers use this skill to study elite performers, map hidden advantage gaps, and build a 12-month plan focused on learnable green dimensions and deliberately designed yellow-dimension exposure. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the skill's coaching output as objective psychological, financial, hiring, investing, founder, or career advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present outputs as a planning framework, require domain-specific evidence, and avoid using the portfolio as the sole basis for consequential decisions. <br>
Risk: Users may over-index on structural constraints such as talent, capital access, or networks and rationalize inaction. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the assessment focused on learnable green dimensions, explicitly identify substitute strategies for constrained red dimensions, and cap red-dimension effort as the skill specifies. <br>
