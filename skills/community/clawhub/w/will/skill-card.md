## Description:

A comprehensive AI agent skill for estate planning and will preparation. Helps you understand what a will covers, what happens without one, how to inventory your assets, what decisions you need to make, how to prepare for your attorney meeting, and how to keep your estate plan current as your life changes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to understand estate-planning decisions, organize asset and beneficiary information, prepare for an attorney meeting, and keep a will plan current as life circumstances change.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive estate, financial, or family details while using the skill.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum needed, redact account numbers and credentials, and avoid uploading documents with unnecessary personal identifiers.

Risk: Estate-planning guidance may be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Use a licensed estate-planning attorney for actual legal documents, jurisdiction-specific decisions, significant assets, complex family circumstances, or business interests.

Risk: Detailed inventories and legal documents can create privacy or storage exposure.

Mitigation: Store actual legal documents and detailed estate inventories only in secure locations.
