## Description:

Analyzes pasted tables or numeric data, validates their structure, recommends suitable charts, drafts Chinese chart titles, legends, and notes, and summarizes supported trends and comparisons.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[caroljiang150-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/caroljiang150-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users preparing reports, papers, or analysis notes use this skill to turn pasted experimental, survey, grade, or other structured numeric data into chart recommendations, Chinese chart copy, and data-grounded trend or comparison analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated chart recommendations or narrative conclusions may be inaccurate if pasted data has ambiguous labels, mixed units, missing context, or calculation mistakes.

Mitigation: Review the reconstructed data table, assumptions, calculations, chart labels, and generated outputs before using them in reports or papers.
