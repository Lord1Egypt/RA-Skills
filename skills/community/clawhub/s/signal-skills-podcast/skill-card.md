## Description:

Pull podcast transcripts on demand and extract ideas, trends, and mechanics into findings without placing raw transcripts in the main context.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[orensegal](https://clawhub.ai/user/orensegal)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, researchers, and analysts use this skill to resolve podcast sources and extract specific claims, workflows, and predictions into reusable findings. It can organize those findings in a local ledger and produce Markdown or HTML reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
