## Description:

Builds and maintains rolling 13-week cash flow forecasts with weekly receipts and disbursements, variance tracking, borrowing base calculations, covenant monitoring, and executive liquidity dashboards.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[samledger67-dotcom](https://clawhub.ai/user/samledger67-dotcom)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Finance operators, CFOs, turnaround advisors, and lender-facing teams use this skill to model near-term liquidity, track actual cash variance, monitor covenants, and plan management actions over a 13-week horizon.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may process sensitive company financial information, including cash balances, AR/AP aging, payroll, debt, and covenant data.

Mitigation: Use it only in approved finance workflows and provide the minimum confidential detail needed to build the forecast.

Risk: Forecast outputs can influence liquidity, covenant, vendor-payment, or revolver decisions.

Mitigation: Review model assumptions, reconcile opening bank balances, and validate covenant calculations before relying on the output for management or lender decisions.
