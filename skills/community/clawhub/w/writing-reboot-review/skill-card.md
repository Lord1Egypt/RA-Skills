## Description:

Guides Chinese-language writing retrospectives that identify why writing stopped, clarify writing goals and audience, and produce practical positioning and restart advice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External writers and content creators use this skill to run a structured Chinese-language retrospective on writing setbacks, clarify why writing stopped, and define sustainable content positioning and next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The guided retrospective may invite users to share sensitive personal experiences or motivations.

Mitigation: Advise users to avoid private details they do not want included in the conversation, and keep recommendations grounded only in information they choose to provide.

Risk: A user in emotional distress could receive a mechanical workflow instead of an appropriate pause.

Mitigation: Follow the artifact behavior to stabilize the conversation first and avoid mechanically advancing the review flow during an obvious emotional crisis.
