## Description:

Assesses third-party vendor risk for AI and SaaS products by evaluating security posture, data handling, compliance, financial stability, operational resilience, and contractual terms, then producing a scored risk report with mitigation recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Procurement, security, compliance, and vendor management teams use this skill to assess AI and SaaS vendors during onboarding, annual reviews, due diligence, and vendor management program development.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated reports include visible AfrexAI assessor and footer branding that may be inaccurate for the actual assessor.

Mitigation: Before sharing reports, verify the assessor field and footer, and replace or remove AfrexAI attribution unless that branding is intended.

Risk: Vendor risk scores and recommendations depend on the completeness and freshness of user-provided details and researched sources.

Mitigation: Review cited sources, request vendor security documentation when needed, and have procurement, security, or legal stakeholders validate decisions before relying on the report.
