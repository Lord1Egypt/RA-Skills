## Description:

Extracts text from video and audio links across 20+ platforms, using subtitle tracks first and speech recognition with AI correction when needed, with support for batch and multilingual content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, content creators, researchers, and developers use this skill to turn supported video and audio links into transcripts, timelines, and background music details for review, reuse, and analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Transcript extraction can send media links or extracted content to external platform, ASR, or AI services.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for content the user is allowed to process, and avoid private, sensitive, or copyrighted material unless permission exists.
