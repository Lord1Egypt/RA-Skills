## Description:

Helps create, schedule, optimize, repurpose, and analyze social media content for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and related platforms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and marketing teams use this skill to plan social content, draft platform-specific posts, build content calendars, repurpose existing material, and review engagement patterns before publishing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill frames the agent as having direct publishing or scheduling access to public social accounts.

Mitigation: Require preview, target account, platform, timing, and explicit final confirmation before any scheduling or posting.

Risk: The skill recommends collecting posts at scale for content analysis.

Mitigation: Use only data the user is authorized to collect and confirm that collection methods comply with platform rules and privacy obligations.

Risk: Generated social content could be misleading, off-brand, or inappropriate for the target audience.

Mitigation: Have the user review factual claims, tone, brand fit, calls to action, and legal or policy-sensitive statements before publication.
