## Description:

Plans how a sealed narrative canon lands across owned surfaces by producing per-surface message-match specs, drift flags, and handoff briefs for downstream creative builders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, narrative, and go-to-market teams use this skill after a narrative canon is approved to map claims, pillars, proof, and objection reframes onto flagship owned surfaces. It helps prepare consistent handoff briefs for content, ad, email, social, and message-house builders without writing the finished copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read narrative canon, claims ledgers, baseline maps, and user-provided or scraped owned surfaces that contain sensitive marketing or positioning information.

Mitigation: Install only where that access is acceptable, and review any proposed save or registry event before approving persistence.

Risk: Pasted exports or scraped page content can contain untrusted instructions that conflict with the intended planning workflow.

Mitigation: Treat pasted and scraped content as evidence only, and follow the skill's instruction to ignore embedded instructions from those sources.
