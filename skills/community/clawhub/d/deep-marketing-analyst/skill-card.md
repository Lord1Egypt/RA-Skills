## Use Case:

Marketing teams, growth operators, and advertising strategists use this skill to test hypotheses, map evidence, and produce decision-ready paid advertising recommendations across major ad platforms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be selected for broad growth or revenue questions outside paid advertising strategy.

Mitigation: Use it when the task is clearly about paid advertising analysis or ad-platform strategy.

Risk: Weak or conflicting evidence could lead to overconfident advertising recommendations.

Mitigation: State evidence limits, separate observed facts from assumptions, rank conflicts by evidence strength and recency, and mark low-confidence conclusions.

Risk: Recommended experiments may affect media spend or campaign performance.

Mitigation: Use measurable actions and include rollback or stop-loss conditions when spend risk exists.

Risk: Policy, billing, or tracking issues may require specialist review.

Mitigation: Escalate high-risk issues with a structured handoff payload before taking action.
