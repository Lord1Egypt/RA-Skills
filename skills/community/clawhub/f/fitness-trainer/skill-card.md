## Description:

Fitness and workout assistant that helps with exercise plans, nutrition advice, workout programming, and training progression.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[blalf](https://clawhub.ai/user/blalf)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill for general workout plans, nutrition basics, exercise selection, training progression, recovery guidance, and injury-prevention education.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat general workout or nutrition guidance as medical, injury-treatment, or condition-specific advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill for general education only and consult a qualified professional for injury treatment, medical nutrition therapy, pregnancy-specific guidance, minors, eating-disorder concerns, supplements, drugs, or chronic conditions.
