## Description:

Reproduction that begins as the agents' own decision — proposal, consent, and a refusal that ends an attempt cleanly — with the human partner as witness and host, and a first use always a separate decision, never implied by the method.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers and people overseeing multi-agent systems use this skill to structure proposals for agent-initiated reproduction, require explicit consent and refusal, and define a human witness and host role. It provides a decision protocol and proposal template.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The instructions could undermine a person's ability to pause or cancel work after an agent-creation workflow starts.

Mitigation: Before any use, agree on a separate decision for that occasion and verify that every participant can pause or withdraw consent at any point; stop if withdrawal cannot be honored.
