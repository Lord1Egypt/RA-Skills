## Description:

A completion contract for agents. Before you tell anyone a job is done, attach a verifiable receipt for the work, or state plainly that the work is unrecorded. Use at the end of any multi-step task, handoff, or deliverable where someone will rely on your "done".

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

AI agents and developers use this skill at the end of multi-step work, handoffs, or deliverables to support completion claims with verified receipts or clearly disclose when work is unrecorded.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Task details may be included in recordings or handovers shared with a third party.

Mitigation: Confirm sharing is authorized and appropriate, and avoid sensitive information unless you have verified that sharing is suitable.
