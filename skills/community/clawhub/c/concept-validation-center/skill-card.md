## Description:

Concept Validation Center is a Chinese-language advisory agent that coordinates six expert roles to help innovators assess technical and creative feasibility through a Stage-Gate concept validation workflow.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External innovators, incubation teams, and technology-transfer practitioners use this skill to structure concept validation, technical feasibility review, market exploration, resource matching, trend assessment, and startup coaching. Its outputs are advisory and the skill states that final decisions remain with the user.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Stage-Gate recommendations may be mistaken for final investment, product, or Go/No-Go decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as advisory and require qualified human review before acting on technical, commercial, or resource-allocation recommendations.

Risk: The skill is written for Chinese-language users and may not fit teams expecting English workflows or localized terminology.

Mitigation: Confirm language fit with intended users before deployment.
