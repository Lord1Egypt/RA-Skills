## Description:

Generates original short-drama plot outlines from a user's one-line idea, combining inspirational struggle, emotional resonance, and satisfying narrative twists.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[hexidyg](https://clawhub.ai/user/hexidyg)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Writers, creators, and short-video teams use this skill to expand one-line concepts into roughly 500-word, dialogue-free short drama outlines with structured twists and uplifting resolutions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad creative-writing trigger may activate for general short-drama brainstorming requests.

Mitigation: Install it only in agent profiles where short-drama plot ideation is an intended workflow.

Risk: Generated tropes may include mild peril, humiliation, crime, rescue, or coercive romance dynamics.

Mitigation: Review outputs for audience, brand, and content-policy fit before using them in scripts or production planning.
