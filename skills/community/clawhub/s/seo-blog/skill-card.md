## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes detailed UEESHOP TinyMCE editor-injection guidance that could change content in a live draft.

Mitigation: Use the skill for local file generation only, or remove the editor-injection section unless live CMS draft changes are explicitly approved and reviewed by a human.

Risk: SEO articles could include unsupported cosmetic, regulatory, or product-performance claims if source-page evidence is weak.

Mitigation: Keep every claim traceable to the live product page or cited authoritative sources, and omit certifications, test results, quantities, lead times, or efficacy claims that are not directly supported.
