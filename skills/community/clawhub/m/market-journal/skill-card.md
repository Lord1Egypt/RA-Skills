## Description:

Forecasts open Polymarket yes/no questions and BTC direction, then records and scores the estimates against market outcomes and price changes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[speteai](https://clawhub.ai/user/speteai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People using a compatible agent can record paper forecasts for open Polymarket questions or short-term BTC direction, then review how those estimates scored against market outcomes or price changes. The journal is for testing forecast quality and provides no trading advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security review is partial and notes network capability and request-routing concerns; declared permissions do not establish the host runtime's actual limits.

Mitigation: Review the skill's requested data sources and runtime permissions before use, and treat forecasts as paper estimates rather than advice.
