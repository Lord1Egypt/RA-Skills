## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: AI-HIVE usage can involve network calls, local API key storage, media uploads, and generation tasks that may incur cost.

Mitigation: Review final parameters, route, and pricing snapshot before generation; keep API keys out of prompts, logs, screenshots, and version control; start with a small sample before batch generation.

Risk: Uploaded reference media, logos, product claims, testimonials, or brand assets may be unauthorized or misleading.

Mitigation: Use only media and claims the user is authorized to provide, mark unverifiable facts for review, and avoid copying protected expression, impersonating brands, or fabricating testimonials.

Risk: Ad-fatigue diagnoses and refreshed creative proposals may be unreliable when historical performance data is missing or incomplete.

Mitigation: Treat data-light diagnoses as heuristic, do not promise traffic, conversion, approval, ROI, or ranking outcomes, and require human review before publishing.
