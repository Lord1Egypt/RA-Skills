## Description:

Generate comprehensive keyword maps for any niche, including clusters, search intent, difficulty estimates, content briefs, and internal linking strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tktk-ai](https://clawhub.ai/user/tktk-ai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External marketers, SEO practitioners, agencies, content teams, SaaS companies, and e-commerce teams use this skill to turn seed keywords into keyword clusters, content briefs, internal linking plans, competitor gap ideas, and publishing calendars.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Keyword difficulty, ranking timelines, search volume, and competitor gap outputs may be strategic estimates rather than live SEO measurements.

Mitigation: Validate high-stakes SEO decisions with current SEO data or web research tools before committing budget, publication schedules, or client recommendations.
