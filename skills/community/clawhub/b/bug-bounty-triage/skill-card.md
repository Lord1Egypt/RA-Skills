## Description:

Exact-match bug bounty triage workflow for Code4rena, Sherlock, HackenProof, Cantina-style security reviews, Solidity/EVM targets, and vulnerability report prep.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External security researchers, bounty hunters, smart contract auditors, DeFi teams, and agent operators use this skill to rank bounty targets, triage candidate findings, prepare submission checklists, and draft credible finding reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Triage guidance may be incorrect for a specific program scope, rule set, or severity model.

Mitigation: Verify bounty scope, program rules, severity, exploitability, and disclosure requirements before acting on or submitting findings.

Risk: Finding drafts could expose secrets, private keys, or undisclosed exploit details if copied into public examples.

Mitigation: Remove secrets and private exploit details, and share only information approved for responsible disclosure.

Risk: The skill includes a disclosed commercial upsell link.

Mitigation: Treat the link as optional commercial material and evaluate it separately from the free workflow guidance.
