## Description:

A professional job description (JD) writing assistant that uses a job title and recruitment requirements to draft precise, professional job descriptions covering responsibilities, qualifications, and key skills.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openlark](https://clawhub.ai/user/openlark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Recruiters, hiring managers, and HR teams use this skill to generate structured job descriptions from a job title and recruitment requirements. It is intended to produce JD content with responsibilities, qualifications, and key skills for hiring workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad recruiting or job-description phrases when the user did not intend to request a full JD.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants JD-formatted output before applying the skill in ambiguous recruiting discussions.

Risk: Generated job descriptions can include misleading requirements or inappropriate wording if the input is incomplete or sensitive.

Mitigation: Review the JD before use for accuracy, inclusive language, and alignment with hiring policy.
