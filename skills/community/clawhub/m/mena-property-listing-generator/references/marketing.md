## Privacy & Data Handling

**What is sent to the LLM:** property specs (location, type, price, area, bedrooms, bathrooms, features), agent name and phone. **No photos are transmitted** — all photo processing is local-only.

**Where data is stored:** listing files are saved locally at `~/.openclaw/mena-property-listing-generator/`. No data is sent to third parties other than the minimax LLM API.

**Before uploading property photos:** remove any EXIF metadata that may contain GPS coordinates, device info, or personal details.
