## Use Case:

Career and portfolio users use this skill to turn project goals, constraints, and owned notes into structured portfolio-writing plans, checklists, next actions, and editable draft copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste secrets or unnecessary personal information into portfolio drafts.

Mitigation: Ask for goals, constraints, and owned notes only; avoid passwords, OTPs, private keys, wallets, seeds, session secrets, and unnecessary PII.

Risk: Draft portfolio text may include unclear, overstated, or hard-to-reverse claims.

Mitigation: Keep outputs factual, editable, and checklist-oriented so users can review and revise before publishing.

Risk: The artifact includes a PayPal support link that could be mistaken for an operational dependency.

Mitigation: Treat the PayPal link as a disclosed donation link only; it is not required to use the skill.
