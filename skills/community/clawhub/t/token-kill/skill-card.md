## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Slash commands such as /new, /compress, /stop, and /restart can affect conversation context or current work.

Mitigation: Treat these commands as deliberate user actions and confirm their expected effect before running them.

Risk: Script integrations and model tiering can route data through providers or automations with different regional, privacy, availability, and data-sensitivity constraints.

Mitigation: Choose models, providers, and scripts that match the user's region, privacy requirements, provider availability, and data sensitivity.
