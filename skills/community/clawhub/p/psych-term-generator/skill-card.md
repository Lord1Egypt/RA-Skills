## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may produce uncited or incomplete psychology explanations for study use.

Mitigation: Use the output as a memorization draft and verify important terms against the assigned textbook or course materials.

Risk: The skill is narrowly scoped to Chinese-language exam memorization and may not support clinical, multilingual, or broader academic psychology needs.

Mitigation: Apply it only to the stated study use case unless the skill is modified and reviewed for a broader scope.
