## Description:

Applies Wittgenstein-style language analysis to identify pseudo-problems, clarify language games, and expose conceptual confusion in philosophical and practical disputes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to examine disputed terms, communication failures, and unclear questions through meaning-as-use, language-game analysis, and philosophical therapy. It is especially framed for contract interpretation, cross-disciplinary engineering communication, and identifying whether a disagreement is factual or rule-based.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may steer unrelated requests into a Wittgenstein-style Chinese philosophical analysis if triggered too broadly.

Mitigation: Use narrow activation wording and add an explicit language-preference rule when deploying it in mixed-language or general-purpose agent environments.
