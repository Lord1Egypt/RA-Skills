## Description:

Helps agents generate Chinese-language prompts and guidance for anime or manga-style images, including characters, scenes, storyboards, and consistent visual variants.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[epstion518](https://clawhub.ai/user/epstion518)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and agents use this skill to turn visual ideas into anime or manga-style image prompts and image-generation requests for character designs, scene illustrations, avatars, wallpapers, emoticons, and multi-panel storyboards.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for a broad range of anime-styled image requests.

Mitigation: Use it only when the user is asking for anime or manga-style image prompting or generation.

Risk: The skill is primarily written in Chinese, which may reduce clarity for non-Chinese users.

Mitigation: Translate or adapt the guidance before relying on it in non-Chinese workflows.

Risk: Image requests can drift toward real-person portraits or prohibited content.

Mitigation: Apply the platform content policy and keep generation scoped to anime or manga-style fictional material.
