## Description:

Generates platform-specific social media posts from articles for Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Threads, and Bluesky.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[fly3094](https://clawhub.ai/user/fly3094)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External content marketers, solopreneurs, and agencies use this skill to convert articles or URLs into platform-specific social posts, short-form video scripts, image prompts, and scheduling-ready content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated social posts can contain incorrect claims, prices, links, hashtags, or brand tone mismatches.

Mitigation: Review and edit all generated content before scheduling or publishing, especially for brand or client accounts.

Risk: The release metadata may install third-party npm packages even though the inspected artifact mainly provides drafting instructions.

Mitigation: Review the package dependencies and install them only in an environment where third-party npm packages are acceptable.
