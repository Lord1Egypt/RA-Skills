## Description:

China-focused car-buying assistant for budget planning, new and used car selection, fuel versus new-energy comparisons, loan options, vehicle inspection, negotiation, registration, and local cost calculations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users considering a vehicle purchase in China use this skill to compare purchase options, estimate budgets and loan payments, prepare inspection checklists, and understand common dealer or used-car risks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prices, tax exemptions, registration rules, loan rates, and vendor recommendations may become outdated or vary by locality.

Mitigation: Treat guidance and calculations as reference material and verify current requirements, rates, and offers with official sources, lenders, insurers, dealers, and local vehicle-registration offices before acting.

Risk: Calculator outputs are simplified estimates based on user inputs and fixed assumptions.

Mitigation: Use estimates for planning only and compare them against written dealer quotes, loan contracts, insurance quotations, and local fees.
