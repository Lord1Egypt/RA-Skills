## Description:

Redesign seat-based pricing for the agent era, when agents erode seat counts and products need usage- or outcome-based pricing that preserves revenue during migration.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product, pricing, and go-to-market teams use this skill to redesign seat-based SaaS pricing for human-and-agent usage. It produces a value metric decision, agent/API tier design, revenue cannibalisation bridge, migration plan, and pricing tripwires.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing migration guidance may affect revenue, customer contracts, and customer trust if assumptions or cohort math are wrong.

Mitigation: Validate the value metric, cohort calculations, customer impact, legal constraints, and commercial assumptions before using the output for pricing decisions.
