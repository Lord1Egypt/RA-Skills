## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to structure stock-picking analysis around company categorization, Lynch-style checklist review, PEG valuation, and life-observation validation. The skill is an educational analysis framework and should not be treated as personalized investment advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can produce direct buy, sell, observe, or strong-buy style investment recommendations without enough financial-advice guardrails.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as an educational framework only; add clear disclaimers, collect time horizon and risk tolerance, and require independent verification before any recommendation is shown or acted on.
