## Description:

Write session-based exploratory testing charters to find what scripted tests miss. Use when asked to plan exploratory testing, write a test charter, design a testing session, or do risk-based exploration of a feature. Produces focused charters -- a mission, areas/risks to explore, tactics and oracles, and timeboxed sessions -- so exploration is purposeful and accountable, not random clicking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, QA engineers, and product teams use this skill to plan risk-prioritized exploratory testing sessions for a feature or product area, especially when scripted tests may miss unknown risks.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may infer assumptions from an incomplete testing brief, which can misstate product risk or testing scope.

Mitigation: Review and adjust inferred assumptions, risk priorities, and scope before using the charters to guide testing.

Risk: Generated charters may contain incomplete or misleading test guidance if they are not checked against product requirements and user expectations.

Mitigation: Validate the charters against the relevant specification, comparable behavior, and known quality concerns before execution.
