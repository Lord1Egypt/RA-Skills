## Description:

Generates Chinese-language daily crypto market morning briefs using public web searches to summarize market conditions, BTC and ETH trends, macro and regulatory news, risks, and sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[terrycarter1985](https://clawhub.ai/user/terrycarter1985)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and analysts use this skill to gather public crypto market information and produce a concise morning brief for situational awareness. The output is informational only and should be independently verified before any financial decision.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Crypto market data and news can be stale, incomplete, or inconsistent across public sources.

Mitigation: Cross-check prices, market context, and cited news against multiple current sources before relying on the brief.

Risk: The brief may be mistaken for investment advice.

Mitigation: Use the output for informational market awareness only and make financial decisions through independent analysis and appropriate professional review.
