## Description:

Social Worker helps users navigate social services, welfare resources, community support programs, and advocacy options for vulnerable individuals and families.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[jiwenbing](https://clawhub.ai/user/jiwenbing)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and assistants use this skill to identify relevant social services, welfare benefits, housing or food support, mental health resources, domestic support, elder care, child welfare resources, and advocacy steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may rely on outdated or location-mismatched social service eligibility, application, or crisis contact information.

Mitigation: Ask for the user's location when needed and advise verification against current local program rules and crisis contacts before acting.

Risk: Users seeking social service help may disclose more personal information than necessary.

Mitigation: Request only the minimum context needed to identify resources and remind users not to share unnecessary personal details.
