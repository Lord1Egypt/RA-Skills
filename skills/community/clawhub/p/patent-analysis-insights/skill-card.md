## Description:

MCP-driven patent retrieval, tagging, and analysis workflow that turns patent data into strategic guidance for competitive intelligence.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

## Use Case:

Patent analysts, R&D teams, and competitive intelligence teams use this skill to retrieve and tag patent evidence, analyze competitor strategies, identify technology and market intent, and generate patent layout recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Relevant patent questions, user-provided materials, and patent-search context may be sent to configured PatSnap/Zhihuiya MCP tools.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended data-sharing posture before installation or use, especially for confidential patent or R&D materials.

Risk: FTO, infringement, validity, or claim-comparison outputs could be mistaken for legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat these outputs as decision support and route legal conclusions through qualified patent counsel.

Risk: Patent, market, or technology evidence could be overstated if the source data is incomplete or unsupported.

Mitigation: Require generated analysis to identify data sources, search scope, time range, and uncertainty, and avoid unsupported patent or market claims.
