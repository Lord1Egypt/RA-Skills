## Description:

Professional AI + IoT Engineering Career Roadmap Platform that generates personalized learning paths and specialization guidance for aspiring and current engineers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd](https://clawhub.ai/user/krishnakumarmahadevan-cmd)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, engineers, career planners, and technical managers use this skill to generate AIoT career roadmaps, review specialization options, and retrieve structured learning paths based on experience, skills, and goals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Roadmap generation sends user-provided assessment data, including career goals, skills, session IDs, timestamps, and optional user ID, to the provider's external API.

Mitigation: Do not submit sensitive personal, employer-confidential, credential, or proprietary career-planning information unless sharing it with the provider is intended and approved.
