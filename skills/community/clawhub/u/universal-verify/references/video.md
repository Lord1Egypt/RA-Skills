# Video pack
Layer 1 (ffprobe/ffmpeg): duration, resolution, fps, audio present and in sync, black or frozen frame detection, subtitle timing file valid.
Layer 2: VBench-style dimensions — subject consistency, background consistency, motion smoothness, temporal flicker, spatial relationships.
Judge: sample keyframes per shot (first/middle/last) and check character identity across shots, continuity, story beats vs script, subtitle accuracy, aesthetic per image pack.
