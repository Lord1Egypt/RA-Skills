## Description:

Ask helps agents respond to problems, decisions, and uncertain situations with clarifying, reframing, assumption-surfacing, decision-forcing, and root-cause questions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[agimodel](https://clawhub.ai/user/agimodel)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Users and agents use Ask as a thinking-partner skill to clarify vague problems, surface assumptions, structure difficult decisions, and identify when action or acceptance is more useful than further analysis.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The broad activation scope may cause an agent to ask coaching-style questions more often than desired.

Mitigation: Install it only where reflective questioning is wanted, and adjust activation guidance or disable the skill if direct answers are preferred.
