External users and developers use this skill to analyze crypto markets and on-chain activity, review wallet and contract risks, compare DeFi strategies, and draft or audit Solidity code. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Market, DeFi, wallet, or contract outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate if source data is stale, unavailable, or misread.

Mitigation: Verify prices, balances, transactions, approvals, and contract findings against trusted explorers, protocol documentation, and independent audits before acting.

Risk: Users may expose sensitive wallet credentials or grant unsafe signing permissions while investigating assets or contracts.

Mitigation: Never provide private keys, seed phrases, wallet passwords, keystore files, or signing permissions to the agent or untrusted contracts.

Risk: Generated Solidity code or audit findings may miss vulnerabilities or deployment-specific constraints.

Mitigation: Treat generated code as test or study material until it passes independent review, automated tests, and a deployment-specific security audit.
