## Description:

Generate comprehensive step-by-step tutorials for tools, workflows, and technical topics.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, technical writers, educators, and support teams use this skill to produce structured tutorials for technical topics, tools, and workflows. It helps organize prerequisites, setup steps, examples, troubleshooting, and next steps into reader-facing instructional content.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad tutorial or guide-writing requests and shape user-facing instructions.

Mitigation: Review generated tutorials for factual accuracy, environment-specific assumptions, and appropriate safety notes before publishing or operational use.

Risk: Tutorials may include commands, configuration examples, or setup steps that need validation in the target environment.

Mitigation: Test commands and examples before reuse, include version constraints when relevant, and mark unverified steps clearly.
