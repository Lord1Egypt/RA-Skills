## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Divination-style output can be mistaken for reliable decision support.

Mitigation: Present results as reflective guidance only and do not rely on them for important medical, legal, financial, safety, or life decisions.

Risk: AI interpretation may be limited because the artifact contains local calculation and data lookup logic, while reasoning depends on the host agent.

Mitigation: Make uncertainty explicit in responses and keep interpretations concise and non-authoritative.
