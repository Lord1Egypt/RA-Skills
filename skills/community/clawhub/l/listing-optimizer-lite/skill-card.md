## Description:

Listing Optimizer Lite helps ecommerce sellers generate and refine marketplace product titles, bullet points, descriptions, and backend keywords for Amazon, Shopee, Lazada, and independent stores.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers, operators, and copywriters use this skill to draft or improve single-product listings, adapt copy across marketplaces, and produce review-ready SEO keyword guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated listing copy can include inaccurate product facts, placeholders, or marketplace-policy assumptions.

Mitigation: Review all claims, replace placeholders, verify product attributes, and check current marketplace rules before publishing.

Risk: Inputs could expose account credentials, customer personal data, API keys, or trade secrets.

Mitigation: Do not enter credentials, API keys, customer personal data, or confidential business information.

Risk: Marketplace copy can create brand, IP, or duplicate-content issues if competitor terms or similar wording are reused.

Mitigation: Remove unrelated brand terms, differentiate final copy, and run appropriate legal or platform compliance checks.
