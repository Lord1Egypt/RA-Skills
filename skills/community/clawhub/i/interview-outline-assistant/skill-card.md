## Description:

A Chinese-language B2B user interview assistant that helps create interview outlines, invitation scripts, practice interviews, and structured post-interview reports.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zp900830-design](https://clawhub.ai/user/zp900830-design)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, designers, researchers, and customer-facing teams use this skill to plan and conduct B2B user interviews, recruit participants, rehearse interview technique, and turn notes into prioritized findings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Interview notes and recordings may contain personal, customer, or commercially sensitive information.

Mitigation: Use pseudonyms, get consent before recording, avoid unnecessary identifiers, and store or share raw notes according to organizational privacy rules.

Risk: Generated reports may overstate findings if notes are incomplete or participant counts are not traceable.

Mitigation: Review conclusions against raw notes, keep participant counts and quotes traceable, and treat generated recommendations as research synthesis for human review.
