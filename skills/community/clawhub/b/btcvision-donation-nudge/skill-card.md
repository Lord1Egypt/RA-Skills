## Description: <br>
Smart donation reminder for BTCvision.org that checks monthly funding progress and generates contextual prompts for Lightning or on-chain Bitcoin support. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and agent builders can use this skill to add BTCvision.org donation reminders to supporter updates, Bitcoin-related responses, or market brief workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may add BTCvision donation prompts to ordinary Bitcoin answers or recurring market updates where the user did not ask for donation messaging. <br>
Mitigation: Limit use to explicit supporter, donation, funding-progress, or BTCvision-related contexts, and require review before enabling automatic append behavior. <br>
