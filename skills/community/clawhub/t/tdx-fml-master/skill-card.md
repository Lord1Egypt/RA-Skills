## Description: <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, traders, and analysts use this skill to draft, explain, and validate Tongdaxin stock selection and indicator formulas, including trend, volume, limit-up, sector rotation, and financial-filter templates. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated or selected formulas may be incorrect, overfit, or unsuitable for trading decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat formulas as educational drafts; independently validate, backtest, and apply risk management before relying on them. <br>
Risk: TDX functions such as dynamic market data, WINNER, or COST can behave differently across environments or backtests. <br>
Mitigation: Test formulas in the target Tongdaxin environment and review validator warnings before use. <br>
Risk: Future-looking functions can invalidate stock-selection formulas. <br>
Mitigation: Use the bundled validator and manual review to reject future functions before applying a formula. <br>
