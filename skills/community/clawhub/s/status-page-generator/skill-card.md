## Description:

Helps agents create, optimize, or structure status pages for service health, uptime, incident updates, and system status communication.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kostja94](https://clawhub.ai/user/kostja94)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product teams, developers, support teams, and incident responders use this skill to draft customer-facing status page structure, status definitions, incident templates, subscription options, and hosting recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad uptime or system-status requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the user wants status page planning or incident communication before applying the guidance.

Risk: Drafted status page language can misstate service health or incident details if project context is incomplete.

Mitigation: Review generated structure, statuses, timelines, and subscriber messaging against current operational facts before publishing.
