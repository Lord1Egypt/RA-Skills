## Use Case:

Developers, designers, and product teams use this skill to plan or scale a design system across products. It guides strategy, tokens, component APIs, accessibility, documentation, governance, and adoption work.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Applying broad design-system changes across shared repositories or product libraries can introduce disruptive or inconsistent UI behavior.

Mitigation: Review proposed implementation steps with design-system owners before applying changes across shared libraries or products.

Risk: Component guidance may miss product-specific accessibility issues if it is adopted without validation.

Mitigation: Verify keyboard behavior, focus handling, labels, error states, and screen-reader behavior before shipping components.
