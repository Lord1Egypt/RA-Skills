## Description:

Mira transcribes and summarizes videos and social posts, extracts on-screen text and structured details, and can provide downloads through a paid connector.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[narekchang](https://clawhub.ai/user/narekchang)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

People using an agent can share a supported video, social post, carousel, or web page to get a transcript, translation, summary, on-screen text, or structured details such as recipes and places. Download links are available on the eligible Mira plan.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A supported link may be sent to Mira for processing even when the user did not clearly ask for transcription or extraction.

Mitigation: Only share links intended for processing; avoid private or credential-bearing URLs unless comfortable sending them to an external service and potentially consuming quota.
