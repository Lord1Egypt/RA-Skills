## Description: <br>
Guides agents in diagnosing and sequencing planned organizational change using Kotter's eight-step change model. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deciqai](https://clawhub.ai/user/deciqai) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees, consultants, and organizational-change practitioners use this skill to assess stalled or planned transformations, identify skipped Kotter steps, and produce a readiness diagnostic, roadmap, and milestone timeline. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Organizational-change recommendations may be incomplete or inappropriate for sensitive legal, HR, labor, or personnel decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as a structured business-framework aid and have qualified legal, HR, or change-management professionals review decisions before implementation. <br>
