## Description:

Guides agents creating, editing, or verifying skills before deployment.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivansslo](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivansslo)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and agent-skill authors use this skill to write process-oriented skills, improve trigger descriptions, and validate skill behavior with pressure scenarios before release.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mandatory deletion guidance could cause loss of uncommitted skill work.

Mitigation: Require the agent to show the exact affected changes, confirm they are recoverable, and receive explicit approval before following deletion instructions.
