## Description:

Create a simple dry-cleaning pickup tracker and bag tag note template for dropped-off garments, pickup dates, special notes, and reminder text, without handling payments, disputes, or accounts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this prompt-only skill to organize ordinary dry-cleaning, alteration, or garment-cleaning pickups. It helps capture pickup dates, ticket references, neutral garment notes, reminder text, what to bring, and after-pickup checks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide payment card data, account credentials, dispute evidence, claims information, or other details outside the skill boundary.

Mitigation: Keep use limited to scheduling, neutral garment notes, ticket references, reminders, and pickup preparation; direct payment, account, dispute, or claims issues to the cleaner or another appropriate channel.

Risk: Pickup dates, ticket references, garment notes, or pickup authorization details may be incomplete or uncertain.

Mitigation: Mark unknown details as confirm with cleaner instead of inventing them, and include a Confirm With Cleaner section in the output.
