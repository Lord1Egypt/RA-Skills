## Description:

Ai Tool Benchmark helps agents compare AI coding, writing, and analysis tools with a standardized A/B testing framework, 50-question test set, scoring dimensions, and report templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, evaluators, and tool-selection teams use this skill to run structured comparisons of AI tools and produce reproducible benchmark reports with charts and recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Included AI tool scores may be example or publisher-supplied claims without the underlying test results and methodology.

Mitigation: Treat the scores as illustrative until the publisher provides supporting test data, methodology, and reproducibility evidence.
