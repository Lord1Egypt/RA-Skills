## Use Case:

External users use this skill to plan and track a quit-smoking journey, get craving support, review health recovery milestones, and estimate money saved.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may store quit-smoking progress locally for tracking.

Mitigation: Tell users what local memory is being used and avoid recording sensitive health details beyond what is needed for quit tracking.

Risk: The "stop cigarettes" trigger may be ambiguous.

Mitigation: Prefer clearer quit-smoking phrasing when invoking or documenting the skill.
