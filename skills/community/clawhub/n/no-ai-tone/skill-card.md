## Description:

Checks and rewrites copy to eliminate AI-generated tone across Chinese, English, and Japanese user-facing content.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[is-xins-xiaobai](https://clawhub.ai/user/is-xins-xiaobai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, marketers, product teams, and developers use this skill to rewrite homepage text, product descriptions, emails, social posts, and brand copy so it sounds more natural and less AI-generated.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad triggers may cause ordinary marketing, email, or public-facing copy to be rewritten when a lighter edit was intended.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended scope before accepting the rewrite, and review the output for preserved meaning, required claims, and brand voice.
