## Use Case:

External users, analysts, and agent developers use this skill to research China market, industry, company disclosure, procurement, regulation, and public-sector data from free official sources. ## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Search terms and requests may be visible to the public official sites being queried.

Mitigation: Avoid entering secrets, cookies, login-only data, or personal sensitive information.

Risk: Some official pages may require login, CAPTCHA, authorization codes, digital signatures, paid access, or anti-abuse checks.

Mitigation: Stop using blocked or protected resources and switch to another listed official source instead of attempting bypasses.

Risk: Market conclusions can be misleading if source scope, metric definitions, or dates are omitted.

Mitigation: Keep official URLs, metric definitions, and time ranges with findings and prefer source pages or official files.
