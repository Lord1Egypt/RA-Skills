## Description:

Plan an e-commerce category or collection page that ranks and merchandises well, including search intent, keywords, intro copy, merchandising logic, filters, internal links, and SEO notes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, marketers, merchandisers, and e-commerce teams use this skill to plan category, collection, and product listing pages that balance shopper experience, merchandising priorities, and organic search visibility.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated SEO and merchandising recommendations may not match real keyword demand, platform constraints, or available inventory.

Mitigation: Review briefs against actual keyword data, platform behavior, and current inventory before publishing page changes.
