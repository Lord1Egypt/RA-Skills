## Description:

Provides bilingual B2B enterprise software sales coaching prompts for discovery, POC validation, negotiation, renewal, objection handling, follow-up messaging, and proposal refinement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales teams, founders, and go-to-market operators use this skill to generate structured, actionable coaching and templates for enterprise software sales scenarios. It is designed for B2B software, SaaS, cybersecurity, and infrastructure sales cycles rather than consumer sales, self-serve PLG, channel-only management, or legal contract advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill intentionally limits detailed help after two substantive questions and may add upgrade prompts.

Mitigation: Review the preview behavior before installation and set user expectations that later responses may be shortened or include an upgrade call to action.

Risk: The package references a QR-code payment asset, but the release evidence says the payment destination cannot be verified from this package.

Mitigation: Do not rely on any payment prompt unless the publisher independently verifies the payment destination and provides the missing payment asset through a trusted channel.
