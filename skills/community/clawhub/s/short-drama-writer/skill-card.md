## Description:

Short Drama Writer helps agents draft Chinese vertical short-drama outlines, character sheets, episode scene breakdowns, hooks, twists, and related writing prompts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ckchzh](https://clawhub.ai/user/ckchzh)

## Use Case:

External creators, writers, and developers use this skill to generate Chinese short-form drama planning materials for vertical video scripts, including plot outlines, character profiles, scenes, hooks, twists, and monetization prompts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A bundled command script may retain entered topics or prompts in a persistent local plaintext history file.

Mitigation: Avoid confidential campaign names, unpublished scripts, personal data, or proprietary ideas unless logging is removed, disabled, or redirected to a controlled location.
