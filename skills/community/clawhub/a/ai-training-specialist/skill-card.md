## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide private platform credentials or confidential task materials while seeking evaluation or qualifier-prep help.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing credentials or confidential materials unless the agent is intended to process them, and redact sensitive task data where possible.

Risk: Platform-specific onboarding, pay, and qualification guidance may affect commercial AI training work decisions.

Mitigation: Confirm current platform requirements, policies, and compensation terms directly with the relevant platform before acting on the guidance.
