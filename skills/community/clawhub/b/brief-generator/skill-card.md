## Description:

Produces structured influencer campaign briefs with deliverables, key messages, creative direction, timeline, disclosure requirements, and compensation terms.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing, influencer, and partnership teams use this skill to turn campaign, product, platform, timeline, compensation, and compliance inputs into creator-ready briefs. It is useful for standardizing brief formats, onboarding ambassador partners, and adapting brief guidance for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and other creator workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated briefs may contain campaign, compensation, contact, and creator details that are sensitive in a shared workspace.

Mitigation: Provide only intended campaign information, allow file saves only in an appropriate workspace, and review saved briefs before sharing.

Risk: Marketing or product claims may be unsupported, outdated, or not approved for the audience, market, media, or offer window.

Mitigation: Use accepted narrative and claim sources, keep required disclaimers attached, and block or mark unresolved claims until they are approved.

Risk: Sponsored content briefs can create compliance issues if disclosure requirements or usage rights remain vague.

Mitigation: State disclosure placement, acceptable disclosure language, usage rights, duration, and territory before treating a brief as creator-ready.
