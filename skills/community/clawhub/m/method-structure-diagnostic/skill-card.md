## Description:

Diagnoses the structural completeness and discourse logic of Method sections in psychology research papers, including module coverage, required details, scoring, and journal-style exemplar references.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangjinhongmy-pixel](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangjinhongmy-pixel)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Researchers, editors, and academic-writing assistants use this skill to review psychology Method sections for reproducible structure, required submodules, and prioritized revision guidance. It is focused on structure and organization, not scientific validity or language proofreading.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may overstate completeness when the submitted text is not a full Method section.

Mitigation: Use it only on visible Method-section content and require the report to identify missing or partial modules instead of filling gaps by inference.

Risk: The skill text refers to positive examples under a path that differs from the packaged artifact layout.

Mitigation: When the stated positive-example path is absent, use the packaged artifact/references/examples directory and verify exemplar availability before relying on a citation.

Risk: Example excerpts can distract from the diagnostic finding or raise reuse concerns if copied too extensively.

Mitigation: Keep exemplar excerpts brief, problem-specific, and subordinate to the revision guidance.
