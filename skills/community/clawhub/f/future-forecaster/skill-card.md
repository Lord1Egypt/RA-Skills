## Description:

Helps agents analyze long-term industry, technology, market, social, and civilization trends using Kevin Kelly-inspired forecasting models and structured scoring.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lj22503](https://clawhub.ai/user/lj22503)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to structure long-horizon trend analysis, strategic planning, and investment-opportunity screening around stated signals, time horizons, and change layers. Outputs should be treated as probability-oriented analysis rather than financial advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Investment-related trend outputs could be mistaken for professional financial advice or current market research.

Mitigation: Use outputs as general scenario and trend analysis, and validate investment decisions with qualified financial advice and current market data.

Risk: Chinese-language documentation may be misunderstood by installers or reviewers who cannot read it fluently.

Mitigation: Have a Chinese-fluent reviewer inspect the skill instructions and generated outputs before deployment.

Risk: Long-range forecasting can overstate certainty when source signals are incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Require generated analyses to preserve uncertainty, state assumptions, and refresh supporting signals before relying on recommendations.
