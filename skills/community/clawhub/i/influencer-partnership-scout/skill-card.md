## Description:

Generates influencer shortlist tiers, analyzes creator fit by campaign goal, recommends partnership mode, and drafts outreach briefs for e-commerce brands.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketing teams and e-commerce operators use this skill to plan influencer outreach for SMB e-commerce campaigns, including creator tiering, partnership mode selection, contact briefs, and brand-safety risk notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can activate on broad marketing terms such as "creator" or "outreach".

Mitigation: Use it only when influencer or creator partnership planning is intended, and review the generated brief before acting on it.

Risk: Recommendations are heuristic and do not use live creator, audience, pricing, or performance data.

Mitigation: Manually validate creator fit, brand safety, rates, availability, and expected campaign performance before contacting creators or making spend decisions.
