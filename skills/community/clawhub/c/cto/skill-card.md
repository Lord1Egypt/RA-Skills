## Description:

Acts as a chief technology officer: architecture calls, build vs buy, hiring and team scaling, tech debt, engineering metrics. Use when making technical strategy decisions or advising founders as CTO.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ivangdavila](https://clawhub.ai/user/ivangdavila)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, CTOs, and engineering leaders use this skill to frame architecture, build-vs-buy, hiring, technical debt, operations, and stakeholder-risk decisions. It produces decision guidance rather than hands-on implementation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Local company-stage, team-size, and stack notes may contain sensitive company context.

Mitigation: Review files under ~/Clawic/data/cto/ before sharing the workspace or logs.

Risk: The skill provides strategic recommendations that can affect architecture, hiring, incidents, vendor choices, or security response.

Mitigation: Keep final decisions with an accountable human reviewer, especially for one-way technology choices, people decisions, incident response, and vendor commitments.
