## Use Case:

External users use this prompt-only skill to plan a door-side pet walk station with a hook map, trip-type gear groupings, refill cues, chew-hazard controls, and a walk-ready checklist. It is for household organization and routine planning, not veterinary advice, behavior diagnosis, training prescriptions, or product safety certification.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat pet gear organization guidance as veterinary, behavioral, fit, or product safety advice.

Mitigation: Keep recommendations within household organization; direct users to a veterinarian or qualified professional for medical, behavioral, fit, or suitability concerns.

Risk: Hooks, leashes, harnesses, clips, lights, bags, or small parts may be unsafe if overloaded, worn, poorly mounted, within pet reach, or treated as escape-proof.

Mitigation: Advise users to verify mounting and weight limits, inspect gear condition, follow product instructions, keep chewable or swallowable items secured, and avoid escape-proof claims.
