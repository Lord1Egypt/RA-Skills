## Description:

Conducts freedom-to-operate patent searches and infringement risk analysis from technical descriptions or images, producing search strategies, relevant patent lists, feature comparisons, and risk conclusions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[chaochaosz](https://clawhub.ai/user/chaochaosz)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and patent or product teams use this skill to structure FTO searches before product launch or technology implementation. It helps collect search scope, decompose technical features, query granted and pending patents, compare claim elements, and draft a non-legal risk report for attorney review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The workflow may involve confidential product, invention, or launch details.

Mitigation: Use the skill only for intentional FTO or patent-risk work, confirm jurisdiction and search platform first, and limit confidential details to approved systems and accounts.

Risk: The report template includes vendor promotional links and disclaimer language.

Mitigation: Review generated reports and remove or edit promotional language when a neutral report is required.

Risk: Patent search and infringement conclusions can be incomplete or misleading if treated as legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as preliminary research and have a qualified patent attorney review conclusions before business or product decisions.
