## Description:

Recommend skills from this repository for financial documents, bookkeeping, planning, BI, reporting, and business operations. Use when the user asks which available skill fits a task or how to get started.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bankgpt](https://clawhub.ai/user/bankgpt)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and business teams can use the navigator to match a financial or business task to one of the repository's skills. It returns the best match, why it fits, required inputs, and a reusable prompt.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A clean scanner verdict does not guarantee zero risk, and a recommended skill may not suit the user's task.

Mitigation: Check that the selected skill's stated purpose fits the task and read its instructions before installing or invoking it.
