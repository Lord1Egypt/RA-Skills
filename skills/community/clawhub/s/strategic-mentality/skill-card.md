## Description:

Apply battle-tested business mentality frameworks from Sun Tzu (Art of War), Alex Hormozi ($100M Leads), The 12 Week Year, and Dan Kennedy (No BS Direct Response) to real business decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External business operators and advisors use this skill to evaluate opportunities, plan 12-week execution sprints, shape offers, score leads, handle sales objections, and make strategic decisions with structured business frameworks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes recurring Telegram check-ins and stored accountability history without clearly documented opt-in, disable, or privacy boundaries.

Mitigation: Enable scheduled reminders only after confirming the user wants them, and document how score history is stored, reviewed, and disabled.

Risk: The skill may process business performance metrics such as revenue, leads, demos, and closed deals through Telegram prompts.

Mitigation: Avoid sharing sensitive business metrics unless Telegram and the operating environment meet the user's privacy expectations.

Risk: Prospect research and cold outreach can implicate privacy, anti-spam, and local compliance requirements.

Mitigation: Review applicable outreach, data protection, and consent rules before using prospect data or sending cold email sequences.
