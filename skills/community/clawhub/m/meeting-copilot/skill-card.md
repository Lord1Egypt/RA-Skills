## Description:

Meeting Copilot helps Chinese-speaking workplace users prepare meeting briefings, structure meeting minutes, track action items, and draft follow-up emails or messages in boss or executor modes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and workplace operators use this skill to turn user-provided meeting details into Chinese-language meeting briefings, structured minutes, action-item tracking lists, and follow-up drafts.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Meeting summaries, action items, and follow-up drafts may be incomplete or inaccurate because the skill relies entirely on user-provided meeting details and does not verify facts.

Mitigation: Review generated briefings, minutes, action lists, and draft messages before using them for business decisions or sending them.

Risk: Users may expect operational automation such as transcription, calendar integration, notifications, or email sending that the skill does not provide.

Mitigation: Use the skill as a drafting and formatting helper only, and handle transcription, scheduling, reminders, and message delivery through separate reviewed workflows.
