## Description:

Transcribes, translates, voices, captions, and renders video dubs, with optional social posting plans.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[social-media-skills](https://clawhub.ai/user/social-media-skills)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Creators and their agents use this skill to dub videos they own or have permission to use into other languages, create matching captions and rendered videos, and optionally prepare social posting plans.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Audio may be sent to a paid cloud transcription provider when credentials are available, without per-job approval.

Mitigation: For privacy-sensitive footage, use a local transcription route; before enabling cloud-provider credentials, confirm the data handling and any charges with the user.

Risk: Security scan coverage is partial because some local references could not be resolved.

Mitigation: Resolve and review the referenced files before relying on the scan's findings.
