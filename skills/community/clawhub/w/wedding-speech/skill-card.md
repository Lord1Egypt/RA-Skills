## Description:

A writing aid for drafting a short wedding toast that balances humor, warmth, audience fit, delivery notes, and a cut list for unsafe or distracting jokes.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to turn relationship details, suitable stories, and audience constraints into a best-man, maid-of-honour, parent, or friend wedding speech. It is intended to produce a concise 2-4 minute toast with delivery guidance and explicit cuts for inappropriate material.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share private, embarrassing, or sensitive personal details while seeking speech material.

Mitigation: Provide only details they are comfortable having reflected in the final wedding speech draft.

Risk: Humor about exes, arrests, hazing, or other sensitive history can embarrass the couple or alienate the audience.

Mitigation: Use the skill's cut-list behavior to remove material that punches down, depends on inside context, mentions past relationships, or conflicts with stated audience constraints.
