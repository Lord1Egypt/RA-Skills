## Description:

Helps create B2B sales enablement collateral such as pitch decks, one-pagers, objection handling docs, ROI calculators, demo scripts, playbooks, persona cards, and proposals.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mariokarras](https://clawhub.ai/user/mariokarras)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Sales, product marketing, and go-to-market teams use this skill to draft and structure sales collateral that supports prospect conversations, internal champion enablement, objection handling, demos, ROI discussions, and proposals.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated collateral may include inaccurate pricing, ROI, security, or competitive claims if users provide incomplete or unverified inputs.

Mitigation: Review and approve those claims against authoritative internal sources before sharing materials with prospects.

Risk: Broad sales-related trigger phrases may activate the skill when a narrower sales, marketing, or competitive-positioning skill would be more appropriate.

Mitigation: Confirm the intended asset type and route public comparison pages, marketing website copy, and cold outreach to the specialized skills named by the artifact.
