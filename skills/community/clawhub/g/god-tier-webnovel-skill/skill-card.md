## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is tuned for intense fictional violence and a rigid Chinese xianxia action style.

Mitigation: Review generated scenes for audience suitability and explicitly override the style when flexible, restrained, or non-violent writing is required.

Risk: The skill may over-apply its fixed protagonist and reversal conventions to requests that need a different narrative structure.

Mitigation: Provide explicit plot, tone, character, and genre constraints when the desired output should depart from the default formula.
