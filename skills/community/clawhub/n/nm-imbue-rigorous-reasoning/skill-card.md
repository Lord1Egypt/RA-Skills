## Description:

Applies anti-sycophancy checklist to override agreement bias.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[athola](https://clawhub.ai/user/athola)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and other agent users use the skill to assess contested claims, interpersonal conflicts, and ethical questions with explicit evidence and harm or rights checks. It helps agents resist agreement based on social pressure and state supported conclusions clearly.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Guidance applied broadly across discussions may produce conclusions that miss important context.

Mitigation: Apply the relevant checklist to the specific claim or situation, and review facts and context before stating a conclusion.
