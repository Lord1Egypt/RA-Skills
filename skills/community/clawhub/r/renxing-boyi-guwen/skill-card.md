## Description:

A Chinese-language interpersonal analysis agent that uses classic strategy texts, psychology, and game-theory-style frameworks to interpret motives, relationship dynamics, negotiation posture, and possible responses.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[daisy0506](https://clawhub.ai/user/daisy0506)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users or employees use this Chinese-language skill to analyze interpersonal conversations, workplace interactions, intimate relationships, business negotiations, and social or family situations. It produces structured perspective on surface meaning, deeper incentives, risks, classic-framework analogies, response options, and likely next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill's interpersonal advice can use adversarial framing around leverage, escalation, and pressure.

Mitigation: Treat leverage and escalation guidance as perspective rather than instructions to pressure or manipulate people, and adapt any response to legal, ethical, workplace, and cultural boundaries.

Risk: Sparse user descriptions can lead to over-interpretation of motives or relationship dynamics.

Mitigation: Ask for missing context, distinguish observed facts from interpretations, state uncertainty clearly, and avoid reducing people to fixed character judgments.

Risk: Suggested wording or action plans could intensify sensitive interpersonal conflicts.

Mitigation: Review proposed wording before use, prefer proportionate and de-escalating options where possible, and use formal channels for legal, compliance, workplace, or safety issues.
