## Description:

Guides agents in using point-and-figure charts to identify support and resistance, apply three-box reversal signals, and estimate price targets with horizontal counts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for educational technical-analysis guidance on point-and-figure chart construction, signal discipline, support and resistance review, and target estimation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users could treat educational point-and-figure chart guidance as personalized investment advice or automatic trading instructions.

Mitigation: Present outputs as informational support for the user's own analysis and risk controls, not as individualized financial advice.

Risk: The skill uses firm language about chart signals, which could encourage overconfident trading decisions.

Mitigation: Frame signals as outputs of the selected charting method and pair them with explicit risk review, stop-loss planning, and user judgment.
