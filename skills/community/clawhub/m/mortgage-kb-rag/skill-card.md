## Description:

Instant answers to mortgage, credit, and home-buying questions from a curated knowledge base, with sourced responses and gap flags when the knowledge base does not support an answer.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External mortgage loan officers, real estate agents, buyers, investors, and content creators use this skill to ask plain-language mortgage, credit, home-buying, and loan-program questions and receive concise sourced guidance from the skill's knowledge base.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Mortgage, credit, loan-program, and legal details may change or vary by borrower, lender, jurisdiction, and date.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as informational and verify current details with qualified mortgage, legal, tax, or financial professionals before relying on them.

Risk: The reviewed artifact does not include the underlying knowledge base implementation or source corpus.

Mitigation: Evaluate the deployed knowledge base content, citation quality, update process, and gap handling before using the skill in a client-facing workflow.
