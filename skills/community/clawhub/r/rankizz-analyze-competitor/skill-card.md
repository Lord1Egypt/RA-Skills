## Description:

Analyze a competitor’s organic search footprint, strongest pages, authority signals, positioning, and strategic gaps, with optional Rankizz research.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[prosamik](https://clawhub.ai/user/prosamik)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Marketers, SEO practitioners, and site owners use the skill to compare a named competitor’s search visibility and identify evidence-backed opportunities for their own site.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Organic-search and AI visibility data may be estimates, incomplete, unavailable, or time-sensitive.

Mitigation: Label estimates, preserve source dates and provider status, and describe unavailable results as missing evidence rather than zero visibility.

Risk: Hosted Rankizz research uses credits and may be unavailable when the account has too few credits.

Mitigation: Inspect available exports and public sources first, make selective research calls, and stop when a low-credit response appears.

Risk: A benign security scan does not guarantee zero risk.

Mitigation: Review the skill contents before installation if additional assurance is needed.
