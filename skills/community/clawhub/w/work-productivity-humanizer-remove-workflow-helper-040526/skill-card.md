## Description:

Revise AI-looking or over-polished text into clear, audience-appropriate writing while preserving truth, author intent, citations, and compliance constraints.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Writers, marketers, students, support teams, and skill authors use this skill to revise generic or AI-looking drafts into clearer, more natural text while preserving facts, citations, author intent, and compliance constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad implicit triggers may cause the skill to be selected for general writing or workflow requests outside its core revision purpose.

Mitigation: Invoke it explicitly for humanizing, revision, clarity, tone, or copy review tasks, especially when automatic routing could be ambiguous.

Risk: Rewriting can accidentally change facts, citations, commitments, tone, or legal meaning.

Mitigation: Review final text against the original facts, required claims, cited sources, compliance constraints, and intended audience before use.
