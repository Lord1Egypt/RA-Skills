## Use Case:

Product builders, founders, and go-to-market teams use this skill to select marketplace and app-store channels, optimize listings, and reduce procurement friction for apps, plugins, integrations, and SaaS products.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketplace and app-store requirements, fees, and listing policies may change after the skill content was published.

Mitigation: Verify recommendations against current marketplace documentation before pricing, listing, or launch decisions.

Risk: Distribution recommendations may not fit the product stage, legal obligations, pricing model, or enterprise procurement constraints.

Mitigation: Review channel choices with product, legal, and go-to-market owners before acting.
