## Description:

Creates vocational education exam blueprint tables from a supplied syllabus, mapping knowledge points to taxonomy levels, item types, scores, difficulty, workplace scenarios, validation checks, and scoring guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[flyboat403](https://clawhub.ai/user/flyboat403)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External educators, assessment designers, and agents supporting them use this skill to turn vocational education syllabi into structured exam blueprints with taxonomy-aligned objectives, item distribution, scoring guides, validation checks, and design rationale.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad exam-creation requests beyond its vocational education exam-blueprint focus.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants a vocational education exam blueprint before applying the workflow.

Risk: The artifact references supporting taxonomy and example materials that are not present in the supplied artifact files.

Mitigation: Ask the user to provide the missing reference materials or proceed only with explicitly available syllabus content and visible workflow guidance.
