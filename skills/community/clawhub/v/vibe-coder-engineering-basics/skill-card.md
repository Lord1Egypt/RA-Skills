## Description:

Learn 107 software engineering basics in plain words, then check if your app needs each one now, later, or not yet, with any app, any stack, and any AI agent. Your AI agent teaches each term in simple words, what it is, when an app needs it, and what happens if it is missing, then checks only the evidence you paste in, and gives a fix draft for owner review.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

App builders, small business app operators, and nontechnical owners use the skill to learn relevant engineering concepts, assess an app using evidence they provide, and prepare fix drafts for owner review.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may inspect a live app when the user requests it, which can expose information visible in that app to the agent.

Mitigation: Limit inspection to content the user is authorized to share and that is visible in the app; do not provide real passwords, keys, or customer data.
