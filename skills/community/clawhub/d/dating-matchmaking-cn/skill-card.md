## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Compatibility checklists and dating advice are subjective and may be mistaken for deterministic or professional relationship counseling.

Mitigation: Present outputs as optional guidance, ask for context, and encourage users to rely on personal judgment and respectful communication.

Risk: Conversation templates could be misused for pressure, harassment, or unwanted persistence.

Mitigation: Keep suggestions consent-respecting and non-coercive, and stop contact when the other person is uninterested or asks for distance.

Risk: Relationship, family, safety, and mental-health topics may require qualified support beyond this skill.

Mitigation: Avoid diagnosing or giving legal, medical, or mental-health advice; recommend qualified help for safety, abuse, or mental-health concerns.
