## Description:

Reviews Chinese and English contracts, identifies high-risk clauses, assesses compliance concerns, and produces modification suggestions with an overall risk score.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[monarchmio](https://clawhub.ai/user/monarchmio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, contract reviewers, and business users use this skill to produce reference-only contract review reports for Chinese or English agreements before qualified legal review. It helps flag risky clauses, summarize rights and obligations, suggest revisions, and assign a risk score.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contracts may contain confidential business or personal information.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in approved environments and follow applicable data-handling rules before sharing contract text.

Risk: The review may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific jurisdiction or high-value agreement.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafting and risk-review assistance, then consult a qualified lawyer for important agreements or jurisdiction-specific advice.

Risk: Ambiguous clauses or differences between Chinese and English legal systems can affect interpretation.

Mitigation: Flag ambiguous terms and jurisdiction assumptions explicitly in the review report.
