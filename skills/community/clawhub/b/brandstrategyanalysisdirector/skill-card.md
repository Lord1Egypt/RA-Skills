## Description:

BD-1 helps brand design researchers analyze cases, organize design logic, track industry trends, and structure large collections of brand materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[johnwong1003](https://clawhub.ai/user/johnwong1003)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Brand designers and design research assistants use this skill to turn brand cases, scattered project notes, industry keywords, and large reference sets into structured strategy analysis and presentation-ready outputs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may create PPT files at a local output path chosen by the workflow.

Mitigation: Review and approve the output path before allowing file creation.

Risk: The skill suggests installing pptxgenjs globally when PPT generation dependencies are missing.

Mitigation: Prefer a local, pinned pptxgenjs dependency for repeatable and reviewable execution.
