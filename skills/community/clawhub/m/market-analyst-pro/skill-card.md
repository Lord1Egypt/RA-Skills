## Description:

Provides Chinese-language global automotive market screening, competitive analysis, field research planning, and risk warnings for Chinese vehicle brands evaluating overseas expansion.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Business users and market strategy teams use this skill to structure overseas automotive market selection, compare target countries, plan field validation, and prepare market-entry recommendations.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for commercially sensitive market, inventory, supply-chain, or channel information.

Mitigation: Share only business details appropriate for the active chat and avoid entering confidential information unless the environment is approved for that use.

Risk: Market-entry recommendations may rely on user-supplied or time-sensitive market data.

Mitigation: Validate key market, policy, pricing, and infrastructure data against current authoritative sources before making investment or expansion decisions.
