## Description:

Enterprise Risk Management Engine helps organizations identify, assess, mitigate, monitor, and report enterprise risks across operational, financial, strategic, compliance, cyber, reputational, people, and external categories.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Employees, developers, and risk practitioners use this skill to structure enterprise risk management work, including risk registers, likelihood-impact scoring, controls, KRIs, scenario analysis, continuity planning, and board or management reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated risk assessments or reports may be mistaken for qualified legal, compliance, security, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Use the output as a business risk analysis aid and have qualified professionals review material conclusions before relying on them.

Risk: Risk workshops and reports may involve confidential business, financial, security, or personnel data.

Mitigation: Share only the minimum necessary information, redact sensitive details where practical, and use an approved environment for confidential work.

Risk: Generated reports may contain incomplete or inaccurate findings if used directly with executives, regulators, customers, or staff.

Mitigation: Validate reports with risk owners and leadership before external or formal internal distribution.
