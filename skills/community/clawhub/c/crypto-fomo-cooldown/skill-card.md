## Description:

A prompt-only mindset skill that interrupts hype-driven crypto investment decisions and guides the user toward a calmer, more deliberate next step.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill when they feel pressure to make a crypto investment because of price spikes, social hype, friend success stories, or countdown-driven urgency. It helps them name assumptions, run a cooldown checklist, choose a pause window, and identify a safer alternative action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat reflective cooldown guidance as financial advice or market analysis.

Mitigation: Present outputs as decision support only and remind users to make independent financial decisions.

Risk: Users experiencing compulsive trading or severe distress may need support beyond a prompt-only pause plan.

Mitigation: Recommend stepping away from trading activity and seeking real support when distress or compulsive behavior appears.

Risk: The skill cannot enforce a trading pause.

Mitigation: Convert the moment into a user-controlled pause rule, budget check, or alternative action without claiming enforcement.
