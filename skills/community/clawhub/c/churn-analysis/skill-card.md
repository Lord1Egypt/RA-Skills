## Description:

Produces structured churn analysis reports that separate avoidable from unavoidable churn, calculate customer and revenue churn metrics, identify at-risk segments, and prioritize retention interventions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Customer success, product, and revenue teams use this skill to analyze customer churn, distinguish avoidable from unavoidable causes, calculate churn and NRR metrics, and plan targeted retention interventions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used with sensitive customer and revenue data.

Mitigation: Use only customer and revenue data you are permitted to analyze, and avoid including unnecessary confidential details.

Risk: Incorrect or incomplete churn inputs can produce misleading retention priorities.

Mitigation: Validate source metrics, churn reasons, and segment definitions before acting on the recommendations.
