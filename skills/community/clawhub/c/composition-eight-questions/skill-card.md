## Description:

Helps photographers systematically check composition before shooting so the subject stands out and the image has stronger visual impact.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bianchunhui](https://clawhub.ai/user/bianchunhui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External photographers and photography learners use this skill before shooting or revising a composition to make the subject more prominent, increase visual impact, and avoid common composition pitfalls.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is scoped to pre-shot composition and visual emphasis, so users may over-apply it to full photo evaluation, lighting-direction choice, or pure camera operation questions.

Mitigation: Use it for composition planning and subject emphasis; route full photo evaluation, dedicated lighting-direction, and camera-operation requests to more appropriate skills or guidance.

Risk: Bilingual source content may leave response language expectations unclear.

Mitigation: Respond in the user's language, or ask for the preferred language when it is unclear.
