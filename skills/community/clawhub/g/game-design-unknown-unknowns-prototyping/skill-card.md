## Description:

Detect unknown unknowns in game design and decide what to prototype before committing to production.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[stanestane](https://clawhub.ai/user/stanestane)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Game designers, product teams, and developers use this skill to turn underdefined game feature concepts into uncertainty maps, prototype questions, sequenced prototype plans, stop criteria, and decision records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prototype recommendations may shape production decisions despite being advisory design guidance.

Mitigation: Review recommendations with the design team and validate them through actual prototype results before committing production work.
