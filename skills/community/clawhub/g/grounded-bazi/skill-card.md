## Description:

BaZi-style reflective readings for personality, money, career, relationships, life themes, annual themes, and current luck-cycle interpretation using date/time/place of birth.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for reflective BaZi-style readings about personality, career, money, relationships, timing themes, and practical life patterns. It can also guide a combined BaZi and feng shui-style reading when the user provides space details or asks for environmental alignment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may ask for birth details, birthplace, focus areas such as money or relationships, and optional room photos or layout details.

Mitigation: Share only details appropriate for a reflective reading, and avoid unnecessary personal or location-specific information.

Risk: BaZi and feng shui-style outputs could be mistaken for objective predictions or professional advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as reflective entertainment or self-exploration, not as a basis for medical, legal, financial, or major life decisions.

Risk: Broad routing language could activate the skill for ordinary advice-seeking prompts that only loosely resemble BaZi or feng shui requests.

Mitigation: Confirm explicit BaZi, Four Pillars, or feng shui intent before using the skill for general advice.
