## Description:

Builds the storyline and slide-by-slide structure for a board presentation, including narrative beats, talking points, and content guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Company leaders, founders, and operators use this skill to shape board meeting materials into a concise narrative with slide-by-slide guidance, discussion points, and clear board asks.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide confidential board materials, financial metrics, or strategic decisions to tailor the deck.

Mitigation: Use only information appropriate for the active agent environment and redact sensitive details when needed.

Risk: Generated board narratives may be incomplete or misleading if inputs omit important context, risks, or performance gaps.

Mitigation: Review each slide message against source metrics, plan, financial context, and board decisions before use.
