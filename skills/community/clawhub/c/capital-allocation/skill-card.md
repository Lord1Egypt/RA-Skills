## Description:

Allocate a finite budget or headcount across competing initiatives by return and strategic fit, producing a capital-allocation plan with scored initiatives, a funded and unfunded split, a cut line, and trade-off reasoning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Business operators, product leaders, finance partners, and strategy teams use this skill to allocate a fixed budget or headcount across competing initiatives. It helps compare expected return, strategic fit, must-fund constraints, and the marginal cut line for transparent portfolio decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The allocation can look more precise than the underlying estimates justify, especially when expected returns or strategic-fit scores are weakly sourced.

Mitigation: Review the inputs and assumptions before use, flag confidence in expected returns, and treat the output as decision support rather than financial advice.

Risk: Users may assume the referenced helper script is bundled and executable.

Mitigation: Confirm available artifact files before invoking any helper script; the inspected package contains only SKILL.md and no executable helper.
