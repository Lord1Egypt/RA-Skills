## Description:

App Store Optimization (ASO) specialist skill for mobile apps that guides users through diagnosing, analyzing, and optimizing app store rankings, metadata, visual assets, competitor positioning, localization, ratings, and reviews.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[sishierdianyijiuwu](https://clawhub.ai/user/sishierdianyijiuwu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, marketers, and app teams use this skill to improve iOS App Store and Google Play discoverability and conversion through step-by-step ASO diagnosis, keyword and metadata recommendations, visual asset guidance, competitive analysis, and strategy reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Competitor-derived wording may be copied too closely or used without substantiation.

Mitigation: Review public-facing copy for originality, verify claims against available app data, and rewrite competitor-inspired language before publication.

Risk: The bundled strategy report template includes a hard-coded preparer attribution.

Mitigation: Replace the attribution with the actual preparer or remove it before sharing a report.
