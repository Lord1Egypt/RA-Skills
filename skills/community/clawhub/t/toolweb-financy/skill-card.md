## Use Case:

External developers, financial advisors, and organizations use this skill to manage personal finance transactions, retrieve dashboard analytics, organize income and expenses by category, and export transaction data.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can read, export, update, and delete sensitive financial transaction data without documented authentication, ownership checks, or user safety warnings.

Mitigation: Use with real financial data only after confirming the ToolWeb service enforces authentication, per-user ownership checks, protected export links, and clear confirmation before destructive deletes.

Risk: CSV exports may expose personal financial records through generated download URLs.

Mitigation: Confirm export links are short-lived, access-controlled, and shared only with authorized users.
