## Description:

Creates marketing, promotional, or advertising videos for social media or campaigns from a product, brand, or brief.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External sellers, marketers, and brand teams use the skill to turn product information or a campaign brief into ecommerce and promotional videos, with optional multilingual voiceover or a virtual host.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill relies on the dLazy CLI and hosted service, and local files attached to a request are uploaded to dLazy media storage.

Mitigation: Review and trust the CLI and service before installing; attach only files approved for upload.

Risk: The security assessment reports incomplete coverage and recommends reviewing the skill text for content that a whitespace finding may obscure.

Mitigation: Review the skill text before use, especially around whitespace findings and external CLI or API references.
