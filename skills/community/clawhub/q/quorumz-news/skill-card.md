## Description:

Current geo-tagged world news for agents, split into viewpoints with a count of the media, social and on-the-ground posts behind each. Use when a user asks what people are saying about a country, conflict or public story, wants the distinct positions on a story, wants an early signal ahead of the wider press, or wants something followed over time by a research agent of its own. Pay per call with an API key (free 7-day trial) or with x402 in USDC on Base, Polygon or Arbitrum.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill to get current, location-aware news organized by viewpoint and supporting source counts, or to propose and manage a research agent that follows a topic over time.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The research workflow sends the user's brief and feedback to Quorumz before explaining that disclosure.

Mitigation: Review the information before submitting, avoid confidential or personal details unless comfortable sharing them, and make the disclosure clear before sending.
