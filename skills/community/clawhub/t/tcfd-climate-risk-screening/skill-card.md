## Use Case:

CFOs, sustainability leads, enterprise risk managers, ESG reporting officers, climate risk advisors, counsel, and boards use this skill to structure a preliminary TCFD-aligned climate risk assessment and draft internal disclosure materials for review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill produces preliminary climate risk and disclosure drafting support that may be incomplete or unsuitable for public reporting without review.

Mitigation: Have sustainability, finance, board, audit, and qualified compliance advisors review outputs before public reporting, regulatory filing, or investor disclosure.

Risk: The skill does not run asset-level quantitative climate modelling and relies on business context provided by the user.

Mitigation: Treat physical risk exposure ratings and financial impact estimates as preliminary and validate them with asset-level modelling or qualified specialists where needed.

Risk: Unsubstantiated climate targets or claims can create greenwashing and compliance risk.

Mitigation: Flag unsupported claims as data gaps and substantiate targets, emissions data, scenario analysis, and disclosure statements before use.
