## Description:

Create a first-week new-job admin board for access requests, forms, equipment, meetings, questions, deadlines, blockers, and follow-ups while avoiding passwords, identity numbers, payroll details, and sensitive HR data.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, contractors, interns, and people changing roles use this skill to create a safe first-week onboarding board for access requests, forms, equipment, meetings, questions, deadlines, blockers, and follow-ups. It keeps sensitive HR, identity, banking, credential, medical, and immigration details out of the board.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may be tempted to record credentials, identity numbers, payroll values, medical details, immigration details, or other sensitive onboarding data in the board.

Mitigation: Use the skill's privacy boundary and safe placeholders; track only owners, deadlines, statuses, next actions, support paths, and official secure locations.

Risk: Payroll, tax, identity, medical, immigration, and credential tasks can require official company systems and careful handling.

Mitigation: Complete those tasks only in approved company portals or in person with the appropriate HR, payroll, IT, or security contact.
