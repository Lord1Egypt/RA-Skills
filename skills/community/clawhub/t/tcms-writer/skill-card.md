## Description:

For tech-product marketing teams, this skill turns a product knowledge base into brand-side long-form drafts (blogs, case studies, and product write-ups), rather than third-party analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[haiyangchenbj](https://clawhub.ai/user/haiyangchenbj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Tech-product marketing teams use this agent to turn a content brief, product knowledge base, and brand guidelines into a long-form technical blog, customer case study, or product write-up, with citation traceability and a pre-review checklist. A human reviews each draft; the skill does not publish it, write short social content, adapt channels, or produce neutral industry research.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:
