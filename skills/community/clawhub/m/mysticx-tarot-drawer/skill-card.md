## Description:

Draw tarot cards from MysticX.ai: one card, daily card, or any of 13 spreads, and browse the full 78-card deck without an API key.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[shrekuu](https://clawhub.ai/user/shrekuu)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users can ask an agent to draw tarot cards, request daily cards or named spreads, and browse tarot card or spread information from the MysticX public API.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal tarot questions can be sent to MysticX in URL parameters.

Mitigation: Use only prompts suitable for sharing with MysticX and avoid names, sensitive details, or private context.

Risk: Responses are expected to include MysticX promotional links.

Mitigation: Review generated responses before use and make the third-party promotional link expectation clear to users.
