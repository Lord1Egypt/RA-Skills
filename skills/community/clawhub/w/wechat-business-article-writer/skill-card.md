## Description:

Create WeChat public account style business and technology articles with professional yet approachable tone.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[openlark](https://clawhub.ai/user/openlark)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and content teams use this skill to draft WeChat public account style business and technology articles, product reviews, and analysis with professional but approachable language.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill's output style is platform- and China-audience-oriented, which may not fit every publication context.

Mitigation: Review drafts for audience, platform, and cultural fit before publishing.

Risk: The skill produces writing guidance and article drafts, so factual claims in generated content may need source review.

Mitigation: Fact-check business, technology, and product claims before publication.
