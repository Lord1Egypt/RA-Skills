## Description:

Scores user-provided resumes against a target role and produces a prioritized diagnosis plus a revised plain-text resume.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yangmanqi2104201431-ship-it](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangmanqi2104201431-ship-it)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Job seekers and resume reviewers use this skill to evaluate resume quality, role fit, wording, structure, and readability before producing an improved resume draft.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Resumes and job descriptions may contain personal or employer-sensitive information.

Mitigation: Provide only documents intended for review and remove unnecessary identifiers such as phone numbers, addresses, IDs, or private employer details before use.

Risk: Generated resume edits or suggested metrics could misrepresent a candidate's actual experience if accepted without review.

Mitigation: Review the revised resume before use, keep only accurate claims, and supply missing metrics instead of allowing unsupported additions.
