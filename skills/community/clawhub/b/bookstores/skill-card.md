## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bookstore requests may be answered with ticketed event or exhibition logistics that do not apply to ordinary bookstores.

Mitigation: Clarify whether the request is for a bookstore, exhibition, performance, or ticketed attraction before relying on booking, entry, or venue guidance.

Risk: Schedules, prices, reservation rules, photography limits, and minimum spending requirements can change.

Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive venue details with current official venue or booking channels before travel or purchase.
