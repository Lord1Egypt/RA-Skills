## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill maintains a persistent personal/ folder that may contain sensitive reflections or personal knowledge.

Mitigation: Use it only when persistent personal note organization is desired, and review the personal/ files before sharing the workspace.

Risk: Inbox processing may clean up personal/inbox.md after routing its contents.

Mitigation: Review personal/inbox.md or keep a backup before running inbox processing when preserving the raw text matters.
