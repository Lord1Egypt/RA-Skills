## Description:

Career Coach AI helps users with career planning, resume optimization, mock interviews, salary negotiation, promotion planning, job changes, workplace relationships, and probation-period anxiety using structured coaching frameworks such as GROW, Ikigai, SWOT, and STAR.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lzh1998-ui](https://clawhub.ai/user/lzh1998-ui)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees and external individual users use this skill as a career coach for planning next steps, improving resumes, practicing interviews, and preparing workplace communication or negotiation scripts. It is designed to ask clarifying questions, diagnose the main issue, and provide concrete next actions without making major decisions for the user.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may persist personal career or workplace details across sessions without explicit consent or retention controls.

Mitigation: Review persistence behavior before installing, avoid entering identifiable employer, coworker, salary, or crisis details, and prefer anonymized examples.

Risk: Career, salary, workplace, legal, medical, or psychological guidance could be mistaken for authoritative professional advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as coaching support, verify compensation or employment data against official current sources, and consult qualified professionals for legal, medical, psychological, or crisis situations.
