## Description:

Queries curated brand DESIGN.md files and returns design-system guidance such as color palettes, typography, component styles, layout principles, and do's and don'ts for users or AI agents.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mebusw](https://clawhub.ai/user/mebusw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, designers, and AI agents use this skill to look up a supported brand's design language and apply it when generating UI, pages, or reusable design references.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The bundled brand design docs are unofficial approximations and may not exactly match authoritative brand systems.

Mitigation: Review generated UI against the relevant brand's official guidance before using it in public or commercial work.

Risk: Installing an unpinned skill release can make future behavior depend on upstream changes.

Mitigation: Install from a pinned source revision or use a sandboxed environment for installation and review.
