## Description:

Generates personalized B2B cold outreach messages for WeChat, email, SMS, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Salespeople, recruiters, small-business sellers, and financial advisors use this skill to draft channel-specific cold outreach copy, follow-up sequences, subject lines, calls to action, and reply-rate tips from prospect and offer details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated outreach workflows may save prospect details, sales preferences, and outreach records in local memory.

Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal, financial, health, or confidential customer details unless the platform provides a way to inspect and delete saved memory files.

Risk: Cold outreach messages may become misleading, overly aggressive, or unsuitable if user-provided claims or prospect assumptions are inaccurate.

Mitigation: Review generated messages for accuracy, tone, consent expectations, and applicable sales or marketing rules before sending.
