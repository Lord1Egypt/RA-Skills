## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Authenticated or destructive Halo CLI operations could affect the wrong Halo site or profile.

Mitigation: Review commands that use --profile or --force and confirm the intended Halo site/profile before changing posts, plugins, themes, backups, comments, or notifications.
