## Description:

Activates the Yingying sales negotiation coach to provide sales strategy, negotiation tactics, customer pursuit planning, pricing guidance, objection handling, deal scripts, and customer relationship support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lanxy32](https://clawhub.ai/user/lanxy32)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and sales teams use this skill to ask an assistant for sales negotiation plans, pricing and concession strategy, objection-handling scripts, and customer engagement tactics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger wording may cause the assistant to apply assertive sales-coach guidance to adjacent sales, pricing, or negotiation questions.

Mitigation: Install only for intended sales and negotiation workflows, and review outputs before using them in customer-facing work.

Risk: Psychological persuasion tactics may be inappropriate or unethical if applied without context.

Mitigation: Review generated tactics for accuracy, ethics, and fit with customer, legal, and commercial constraints before use.
