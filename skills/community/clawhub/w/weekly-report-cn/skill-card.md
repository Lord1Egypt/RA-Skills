## Description:

Automatically generate professional Chinese weekly/daily work reports from bullet-point inputs, transforming raw task notes into polished 周报/日报 with structured sections, highlighted achievements, next steps, and team-style customization.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[goldath](https://clawhub.ai/user/goldath)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and individual contributors use this skill to turn short Chinese work notes into concise daily or weekly reports with completed work, highlights, risks, next plans, and coordination needs.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may provide confidential workplace chats, screenshots, credentials, customer data, or internal metrics while asking for report drafting.

Mitigation: Review and redact sensitive workplace, customer, credential, and internal metric data before using the skill in an agent session.

Risk: Generated reports may overstate impact or add unsupported metrics when raw notes are incomplete.

Mitigation: Check the draft against source notes and replace any inferred or placeholder metrics with verified figures before sharing.
