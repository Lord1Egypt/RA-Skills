## Description:

Plan consistent ecommerce product image sets from photos or a brief, with complete prompts, product identity checks, and a handoff for the user's preferred reference-image tool.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[visualgptio](https://clawhub.ai/user/visualgptio)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External ecommerce sellers and content teams use the skill to plan coordinated product image sets from product photos or briefs. It provides product-specific prompts and review guidance for the image tool they choose.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The optional VisualGPT workflow involves sharing product images and briefs with an external provider.

Mitigation: Use it only after confirming you are authorized to share the material and accept the provider's data-handling terms; avoid confidential or sensitive material otherwise.

Risk: The contents of the three bundled WebP examples were not fully assessed by the security review.

Mitigation: Review the example images before reusing or distributing them.
