## Description:

Provides PKU alumni association lookups for the association overview, local chapters, alumni events, alumni services, contact details, and donation channels.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wangzhaodong88](https://clawhub.ai/user/wangzhaodong88)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer PKU alumni-association questions by category, including chapters, events, services, contact information, and donations. It is intended for informational lookup and should direct users to official sources before they act on time-sensitive or transactional details.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Static or example-style contact, event, tax, and donation details may be outdated or unsuitable for action.

Mitigation: Verify those details against official PKU alumni or foundation sources before making plans, donations, or compliance decisions.

Risk: The skill may trigger on broad PKU-related questions that are outside the alumni-association lookup categories.

Mitigation: Narrow the response to supported categories or ask the user to choose a category before presenting detailed information.
