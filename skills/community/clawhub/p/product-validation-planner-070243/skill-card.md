## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, and reusable validation workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before spending heavily. It produces practical validation plans, checklists, templates, prototypes, positioning support, and decision guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad triggers may invoke the skill for general business or startup requests, where its advice may be mistaken for validated business guidance.

Mitigation: Review outputs as advisory planning material and confirm assumptions, evidence quality, and market claims before making product or investment decisions.

Risk: The cited demand evidence is noisy and may not map directly to a user's market, audience, or product category.

Mitigation: Require the final plan to state assumptions, limits, validation criteria, and follow-up work against the user's specific context.
