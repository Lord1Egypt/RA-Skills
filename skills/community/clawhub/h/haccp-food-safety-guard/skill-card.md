## Description:

Audit food safety plans, HACCP records, and inspection reports against Codex HACCP principles and ISO 22000 before release, with findings for nonconforming hazard analyses, CCP limits, and verification records.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Food safety, quality, compliance, and audit teams use this skill to review HACCP plans, monitoring records, corrective actions, verification records, inspection reports, and food-safety claims against Codex HACCP principles and ISO 22000 expectations before release.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Food safety and regulatory compliance are high-impact domains, and checklist output may be incomplete for a specific jurisdiction or product category.

Mitigation: Confirm conclusions against current jurisdiction-specific rules and qualified food-safety expertise before relying on the output for release decisions.

Risk: The skill may flag HACCP or ISO 22000 issues from submitted documents, but it does not execute code, persist data, or access hidden resources.

Mitigation: Use it as a documentation review aid and keep release approval with the organization's food-safety and compliance process.
