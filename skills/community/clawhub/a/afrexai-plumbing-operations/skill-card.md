## Description:

AI operations assistant for plumbing businesses that helps with job estimates, schedule optimization, parts inventory, customer communications, profitability analysis, warranty tracking, and compliance reminders.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[afrexai-cto](https://clawhub.ai/user/afrexai-cto)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Owner-operators and small plumbing companies use this skill to prepare estimates, optimize technician schedules, track common parts, generate customer messages, and review job profitability.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated estimates or operational guidance may rely on outdated or non-local permit fees, supplier pricing, license requirements, or margin targets.

Mitigation: Verify local fees, supplier prices, licensing rules, and business margin targets before relying on outputs.

Risk: Customer, job, inventory, and business performance details may be sensitive.

Mitigation: Use the skill only in a trusted agent environment and avoid entering unnecessary sensitive data.
