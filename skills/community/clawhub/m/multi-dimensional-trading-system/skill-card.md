## Description:

Provides structured multi-timeframe technical analysis using EMA channels and Fibonacci levels for short-term trading decisions in A-shares, DSE Bangladesh, and crypto.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[rashed-mamoon](https://clawhub.ai/user/rashed-mamoon)

## Use Case:

External users and traders use this skill to request structured technical trading analysis across A-shares, DSE Bangladesh equities, and cryptocurrency markets. The skill is intended to organize market data into EMA, Fibonacci, multi-timeframe scoring, and risk-warning sections, not to replace independent financial judgment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trading outputs may include entry, stop-loss, or take-profit levels that are unreliable if not grounded in current market data.

Mitigation: Require a cited, current, verifiable market data source before accepting any concrete price level.

Risk: Users may mistake structured technical analysis for investment advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as technical reference only and apply independent review, risk controls, and financial judgment before trading.
