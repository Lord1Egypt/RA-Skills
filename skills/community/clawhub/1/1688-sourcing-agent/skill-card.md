## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Procurement guidance may be mistaken for a guarantee of supplier reliability, product quality, or transaction safety.

Mitigation: Independently verify supplier claims, certifications, payment terms, samples, and business credentials before placing orders.

Risk: Example exchange rates, freight estimates, and customs assumptions may be stale or inaccurate for a user's shipment.

Mitigation: Confirm current exchange rates, freight quotes, duties, customs requirements, and regulated-product obligations with authoritative sources before spending money.

Risk: Regulated or high-risk products may require specialized compliance review beyond the skill's general checklist.

Mitigation: Use qualified sourcing, legal, customs, or compliance experts for medical, food, children's products, complex OEM work, or high-value orders.
