## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The package includes lead-generation material that may handle regulated or sensitive contact data.

Mitigation: Add consent, lawful-use, retention, and export controls before using lead collection or CRM export workflows.

Risk: Templates mention trading integrations, external alerts, API keys, and CRM tokens without enough operational safety guidance.

Mitigation: Store credentials outside plaintext config files, restrict alert and CRM tokens, and review any trading or external notification setup before deployment.

Risk: Dependency installation guidance is not paired with dependency pinning or audit instructions.

Mitigation: Pin and audit dependencies before following package installation steps.
