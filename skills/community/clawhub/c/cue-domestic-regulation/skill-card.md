## Description:

Searches domestic Chinese laws and regulations through the Cue platform and produces sourced summaries of legislative background and compliance points.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Legal, compliance, and research users can ask questions about Chinese domestic regulations and receive structured reports with applicable provisions, compliance points, and source links. The reports are informational and the artifact says they are not legal advice.
