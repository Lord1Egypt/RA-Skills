## Description:

Vault discovery, comparison, and analytics for Paradex vaults, ranking vaults by risk-adjusted returns, analyzing operator track records, monitoring TVL changes, and recommending vaults based on user risk profile through Paradex MCP vault tools.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to analyze Paradex vault performance, compare risk-adjusted returns, assess operator and liquidity risks, and prepare vault screening or deep-dive reports.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Vault analysis or recommendations could be mistaken for investment advice.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an analytics aid only, and independently verify current vault data, lockups, operator risk, liquidity, and personal suitability before acting.

Risk: Past vault performance, low drawdown, or high ROI may not predict future returns.

Mitigation: State performance caveats clearly and compare current positions, leverage, liquidity, TVL trends, and withdrawal constraints before presenting conclusions.
