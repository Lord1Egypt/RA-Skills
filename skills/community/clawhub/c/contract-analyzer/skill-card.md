## Description:

Analyze and review contract documents. Use when extracting key clauses, detecting high-risk terms, comparing two contracts, generating a contract summary, or getting a contract review checklist.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[pressbytesagain](https://clawhub.ai/user/pressbytesagain)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and external users use this skill to inspect plain-text contract documents for key clauses, risk indicators, differences between versions, summaries, and review checklist items.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contract contents and excerpts may be displayed in the terminal or agent transcript.

Mitigation: Use only files intentionally provided for review, and avoid shared or logged environments for confidential agreements.

Risk: Pattern-based contract findings can miss context or produce misleading legal conclusions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as screening support and obtain qualified legal review before relying on findings or signing an agreement.
