## Description:

Helps agents answer questions about Milarepa, Kagyu Buddhism, Mahamudra, and related Tibetan Buddhist teachings using declared Tibetan textual sources and safety limits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xr843](https://clawhub.ai/user/xr843)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users can use this skill for source-grounded Q&A and teaching-style responses about Milarepa, Kagyu tradition, Mahamudra, related doctrinal topics, and biographical material. It is designed to cite declared Tibetan sources and avoid giving esoteric practice instructions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: User questions may be sent to fojin.app for source lookup when local excerpts are insufficient.

Mitigation: Avoid entering personal, health, identity, or private spiritual details unless external transmission is acceptable; prefer local excerpts when they answer the question.

Risk: The skill uses a stylized religious voice and may produce teaching-style guidance that users could mistake for qualified spiritual instruction.

Mitigation: Keep outputs educational, cite declared sources, and direct users to qualified teachers for formal practice guidance.

Risk: Questions about esoteric practices could lead to unsafe or inappropriate practice instructions.

Mitigation: Limit responses to historical or high-level context and refuse concrete steps, mantras, visualizations, or practice procedures.
