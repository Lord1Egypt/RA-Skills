## Description:

Drafts WeChat-style long-form articles from a confirmed topic, outline, evidence pack, or content_state.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[yangchao228](https://clawhub.ai/user/yangchao228)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Content creators and editorial teams use this skill to turn a confirmed topic, outline, evidence pack, or content_state into a WeChat-style article draft with title candidates, hooks, outline, body, ending, visual-anchor notes, and follow-up topics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Fact-heavy drafts can include unsupported or weakly evidenced claims if the user does not provide an evidence pack.

Mitigation: Require evidence for fact-heavy topics, mark unsupported claims, and route research needs before review or publish packaging.

Risk: Private account identity or project material may be included in drafts if supplied without clear intent.

Mitigation: Use private account or project material only when the user provides it for the current draft and intends it to be included.
