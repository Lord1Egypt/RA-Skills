## Description:

Reason from fundamentals with in-session claim ledgers, mechanism maps, assumption checks, Fermi estimates, evidence grounding, verification questions, red-team critique, and structured brainstorming.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, researchers, and business users use this skill to structure complex decisions, debugging, architecture, strategy, and brainstorming around fundamentals, assumptions, mechanisms, and verification.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad prompts may receive more analysis than a user needs for small mechanical tasks.

Mitigation: Use direct instructions or the documented skip phrases when direct implementation is preferred.

Risk: Reasoning output may be misleading if assumptions, reported claims, or external facts are not verified.

Mitigation: Review the claim ledger, confirm unknowns, and use evidence-gathering tools when the task requires current or source-grounded facts.
