## Description:

Growth Referral provides concise guidance for designing and launching referral programs with valued incentives, timely prompts, low-friction sharing, double-sided rewards, and funnel measurement.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wellyxy](https://clawhub.ai/user/wellyxy)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Growth, product, and marketing teams use this skill to plan referral programs that encourage word-of-mouth acquisition. It helps choose incentive models, time referral asks, reduce sharing friction, reward both referrers and referred users, and measure conversion through the referral funnel.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes a promotional third-party service mention that may not be appropriate for every deployment.

Mitigation: Treat the service as optional and evaluate it separately before sharing business, product, or contact information.

Risk: Referral-program guidance can be misapplied before product-market fit or without measuring downstream conversion quality.

Mitigation: Validate program readiness and track referral-to-paid or referral-to-activation conversion before scaling incentives.
