## Description:

Organize and structure evidence for legal proceedings.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and legal-support teams use this skill to organize evidence for hearings, discovery, exhibit preparation, and case review. It creates structured evidence lists, categories, custody notes, gap analysis, summaries, checklists, and next steps for attorney review.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake evidence organization guidance for legal advice or an admissibility decision.

Mitigation: Review organized evidence, admissibility issues, and filing requirements with a qualified attorney before court use.

Risk: Incomplete descriptions, dates, sources, claim assignments, or custody notes may weaken the evidence package.

Mitigation: Verify each evidence item has accurate metadata, source information, chain-of-custody details where needed, and attorney-reviewed relevance before submission.

Risk: Evidence materials may contain privileged, confidential, or sensitive personal information.

Mitigation: Handle evidence under the user's confidentiality and privilege-review process, and avoid sharing sensitive materials outside approved systems.
