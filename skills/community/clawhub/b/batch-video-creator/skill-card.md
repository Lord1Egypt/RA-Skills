## Description:

Batch Video Creator is an AI workflow for batch video production when you need dozens of consistent clips without manual editing one by one.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[imwyvern](https://clawhub.ai/user/imwyvern)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, agencies, brands, sellers, and content operations teams use this skill to ask an agent for batches of short-form video variants from source clips, including caption, hook, voiceover, color, format, and export guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Installing through a package-style CLI flow carries normal package-install risk.

Mitigation: Use a pinned or trusted ClawHub CLI source when available and install from a normal non-admin account.

Risk: The text-only skill may lead an agent to process user-provided media through downstream tools or services.

Mitigation: Review the selected tools, service terms, permissions, and media data handling before processing sensitive or proprietary content.
