## Description:

Build a paperwork checklist and submission packet for appliance rebate claims, including eligibility facts, required documents, deadline checks, proof copies, and follow-up tracking.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to organize appliance rebate paperwork before submitting claims themselves. It helps them verify rebate rules and deadlines, assemble proof documents, redact unnecessary payment data, and track follow-up steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Receipts or screenshots may contain full payment numbers or unrelated personal data.

Mitigation: Share only required rebate details and redact full payment card numbers, bank numbers, security codes, and unrelated personal data before using the skill.

Risk: Rebate eligibility, model requirements, and deadlines may be outdated or program-specific.

Mitigation: Verify deadlines, eligible models, proof requirements, and program terms against the official rebate source before submitting a claim.

Risk: A prepared packet could be mistaken for approval, legal, tax, warranty, consumer-credit, or financial advice.

Mitigation: Use the packet as organizational support only, and consult the official program administrator or a qualified professional for disputes, tax credits, benefits, or complex eligibility questions.

Risk: Users might be tempted to fill gaps with unsupported documents or claims.

Mitigation: Do not fabricate receipts, labels, installation proof, disposal proof, signatures, account status, purchase dates, or other evidence.
