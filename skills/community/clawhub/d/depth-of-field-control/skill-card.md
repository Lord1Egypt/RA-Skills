## Description:

Helps agents explain how aperture, focus distance, and focal length affect photographic depth of field for background blur or sharp foreground-to-infinity shots.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Developers and agents use this skill to answer photography questions about controlling background blur, achieving large depth of field, and choosing aperture, focal length, focus distance, or hyperfocal-distance guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
