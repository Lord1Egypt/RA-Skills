## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill asks the assistant to automatically store user preferences, decisions, and conversation details as long-term local memory without clear opt-in, retention, review, or deletion controls.

Mitigation: Install only when users understand and accept this behavior; avoid sensitive personal, business, credential, health, or financial information and add explicit consent, review/delete controls, and retention limits before broader deployment.
