# Platform playbook

Status: practitioner heuristics (not platform rules) unless a line says "limit". Limits come from a vendor help page (undated) and can change; verify in the platform's composer. Algorithms change; use the user's analytics to confirm what works for them.

| Platform | Limit | Strong formats | Structure | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | caption 2,200; stories 120 | Reels, carousels, single images, stories | Hook in the first line (only the start shows before "more"), value in short lines, one CTA (save, share, comment, DM) | Alt text for every image; carousels reward a clear promise on slide 1 and a payoff at the end; covers should read on a small grid |
| LinkedIn | post 3,000; first comment 1,250 | text posts, document carousels (PDF), polls, short video | Strong first two lines, one idea, short paragraphs, a lesson or opinion, a question or soft CTA | Professional tone does not mean stiff; stories with a specific detail work; put links in the first comment only if it fits your test results |
| X | 280 on free accounts (paid accounts can write longer) | short posts, threads, replies, images | One idea per post; thread: standalone hook post, one point per post, payoff, recap | Fewer hashtags; reply and quote culture matters; links can reduce reach for some accounts (unverified) |
| Facebook | 5,000 | short video, images, groups, events | Conversational, local, community-oriented | Groups and Pages behave differently; older audiences skew here for many niches (varies) |
| TikTok | caption 2,200 | short vertical video | Hook in 1 to 3 seconds, one idea, native sound and text, caption adds keywords and a question | Script with `short-form-video-scripts`; captions and on-screen text help muted viewing |
| YouTube Shorts / video | Shorts description 5,000; title about 100 | Shorts, long video, community posts | Title promise + keyword, first 2 lines of description for the hook, chapters on long video | Thumbnails: `thumbnail-design`; titles and descriptions carry search value |
| Threads | 500 (counted in UTF-8 bytes) | short text, conversational posts | Casual, question-led, one thought | Non-Latin scripts and emoji use more of the limit |
| Pinterest | 500 | vertical images, idea pins | Keyword-rich title and description, clear visual, link to useful page | Search-driven; evergreen content lasts longer |
| Bluesky | 300 | short text, threads | Like X, calmer tone | Custom feeds reward niche relevance |

## Cross-platform habits

- Lead with the point. Cut greetings, throat-clearing and "in today's world".
- Use mobile-friendly line breaks: 1 to 2 sentences per paragraph.
- One CTA, phrased as an action the reader can do in under 10 seconds.
- Keywords in natural language beat a hashtag wall; 3 to 5 relevant hashtags is a common rule of thumb, not a requirement.
- Alt text describes what the image shows and why it matters, in one sentence.
- Match the voice guide; vary sentence length; avoid stock phrases.
- Post for one reader ("you"), not "everyone".

## Language notes (Roman Urdu, Urdu, Hinglish)

- Pick the language the audience actually reads on that platform; LinkedIn audiences in Pakistan and India often expect English, Instagram and TikTok often accept a mix. Confirm with the user's own analytics and comments.
- Keep Roman Urdu spelling consistent (see the brand voice guide).
- Searchable terms (product names, topics) often work better in English even inside a Roman Urdu caption.
- Urdu script posts need a check of line breaks and punctuation on the actual platform.

## Quality bar before delivering

- The first line makes someone stop or stay. Could it be misread or boring?
- Does the post contain one concrete detail only the brand could say?
- Is the claim true, supported and allowed?
- Are limits, alt text and a CTA handled?
- Would a person on the team post this under their own name?
