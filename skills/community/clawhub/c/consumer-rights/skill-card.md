## Description:

Generate complaint letters, 12315 filings, and escalation paths for consumer disputes in China.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to draft China consumer-dispute materials, including merchant negotiation letters, platform complaints, 12315 complaint applications, evidence checklists, escalation paths, and litigation risk notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose unnecessary personal data while describing a consumer dispute.

Mitigation: Provide only the minimum facts needed and redact unrelated personal data, addresses, phone numbers, IDs, payment details, and account or order identifiers where possible.

Risk: Generated legal citations, filing routes, or complaint strategy may be incomplete or outdated.

Mitigation: Verify legal citations and official filing routes before submitting any complaint or other official material.

Risk: Outcome estimates and litigation risk notes may be over-relied on as legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat generated assessments as drafting support and review important disputes with a qualified professional or appropriate consumer authority.
