## Description:

Provides Chinese-language step-by-step decluttering plans with sorting rules, time estimates, and resale or donation suggestions for spaces such as wardrobes, bookshelves, and drawers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Users invoke this skill for Chinese-language decluttering guidance for a specific storage area or room. It returns a structured plan with sorting criteria, step-by-step actions, time estimates, tips, and resale or donation channels.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may trigger on broad storage or tidying phrases.

Mitigation: Review activations in context and confirm the user is asking for decluttering guidance before relying on the response.

Risk: Resale and donation suggestions may be China-oriented.

Mitigation: Adapt resale or donation channels to the user's location and local availability before acting on them.
