## Description:

Openclaw Design Consult helps agents act as product design system consultants that clarify requirements, propose coherent visual systems, and generate DESIGN.md plus CLAUDE.md design guidance.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[x-rayluan](https://clawhub.ai/user/x-rayluan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers, product teams, and design-focused agents use this skill to establish or update a product design system, preview typography and color choices, and persist agreed design guidance into project files.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may persistently modify project guidance files, including DESIGN.md and CLAUDE.md.

Mitigation: Ask the agent to show the exact planned changes and get explicit approval before write steps, especially for CLAUDE.md.
