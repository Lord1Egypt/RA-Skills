## Use Case:

External ecommerce operators and marketing teams use this skill to obtain paid ads optimization guidance for Meta, TikTok, Google, or multi-channel campaigns across targeting, creatives, bidding, landing pages, and analytics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can send TOKENAPI_KEY and business advertising data to a configurable API endpoint, including non-HTTPS URLs.

Mitigation: Install only when Yufluent is trusted, keep TOKENAPI_KEY secret, and configure TOKENAPI_BASE_URL only to a trusted HTTPS endpoint or trusted local development endpoint.

Risk: Optimization guidance may affect advertising budget allocation, targeting, creatives, attribution review, or landing page decisions.

Mitigation: Have a qualified human review recommendations before applying changes in an advertising platform, and check platform policies and target-market legal requirements.
