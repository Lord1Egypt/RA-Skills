## Description:

This skill should be considered when you need to answer reflow machine maintenance questions or provide detailed guidance based on thermocouple data, MES data or defect data and reflow technical handbooks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Manufacturing engineers, process engineers, and maintenance teams use this skill to answer reflow equipment maintenance questions, interpret thermocouple, MES, and defect data, and cross-check calculations against reflow technical handbook constraints.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can analyze manufacturing process data supplied by the user, which may include private production information.

Mitigation: Use it only with thermocouple, MES, defect, and handbook files intended for reflow maintenance analysis, and avoid pointing it at broader private directories.

Risk: Incorrect handbook extraction or calculation setup could lead to misleading maintenance guidance.

Mitigation: Confirm computed limits and threshold selections against the applicable reflow handbook and process requirements before acting on recommendations.
