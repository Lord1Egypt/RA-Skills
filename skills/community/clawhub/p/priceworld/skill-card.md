## Description:

Pricing intelligence across web hosting, email marketing, domain registrars, savings rates, and AI tools. Query real prices, compare providers by true cost (including renewal markup), find hidden fees, and check which banks silently cut APY. Use when users ask about software pricing, hosting costs, domain renewals, savings account rates, AI subscription comparisons, or budget recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

External users and agents use PriceWorld to look up, compare, and rank provider pricing across hosting, email marketing, domains, savings rates, and AI subscriptions. Its guidance is decision support for budgeting and purchasing, not a substitute for checking current official provider terms.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global, with US pricing as the default and documented regional pricing for selected hosting comparisons.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing, APY, fee, and product-limit information may be stale or incomplete when a user is ready to spend money or move funds.

Mitigation: Verify current prices, APYs, renewal terms, limits, and fees on official provider pages before making purchases or financial changes.

Risk: Users may share sensitive account material while asking for price comparisons or savings-rate analysis.

Mitigation: Do not provide invoices, screenshots, API keys, account details, or other sensitive personal or credential data to the skill.
