## Description:

Evaluate and revise an academic Results section using six evidence-based dimensions, then produce a prioritized results-quality summary report with scores, concrete problems, evidence, and actionable revisions while preserving the Results/Discussion boundary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linuoxu](https://clawhub.ai/user/linuoxu)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External researchers, students, reviewers, and academic writing assistants use this skill to evaluate supplied Results sections across six reporting dimensions, prioritize problems by scientific risk, and propose evidence-preserving revisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Suggested revisions could unintentionally change the user's reported data, statistics, or scientific claim strength.

Mitigation: Review all recommendations and rewrites against the original Results material before using them.

Risk: Incomplete source material can limit the reliability of scoring and diagnosis.

Mitigation: Use the skill's own limitation posture: assess only the supplied evidence and state missing information instead of filling gaps.
