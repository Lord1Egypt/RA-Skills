## Use Case:

External marketing teams, growth operators, and analysts use this skill to assemble daily or weekly advertising performance reports, summarize findings, and recommend channel-aware next actions across paid media platforms.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad business-performance questions outside a concrete ad-reporting workflow.

Mitigation: Use it for ad reporting or campaign analysis, and provide explicit platform, KPI, date range, and data-source context.

Risk: Recommendations can be misleading when metrics, attribution windows, or sample sizes are unclear.

Mitigation: Clarify metric definitions and time windows before analysis, mark low-sample results as directional, and include rollback or stop-loss conditions when spend risk exists.
