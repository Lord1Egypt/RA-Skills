## Description:

When the user wants to plan, implement, or optimize discount and promotional pricing strategy.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and business teams use this skill to plan discount structures, promotional campaigns, redeem code approaches, and financial guardrails for SaaS, e-commerce, and tool products.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may use local project context files containing product, pricing, or business goal details.

Mitigation: Review or redact project context files before invoking the skill when they contain sensitive information that should not be used in discount strategy work.

Risk: Discount recommendations can harm margins, brand perception, or customer quality if applied without economic guardrails.

Mitigation: Validate proposed discounts against LTV:CAC, margin, support capacity, campaign goals, and qualification criteria before launch.
