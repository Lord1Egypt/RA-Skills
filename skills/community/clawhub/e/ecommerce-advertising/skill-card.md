## Description:

Full-funnel e-commerce advertising planner for cross-channel campaigns covering keyword research, competitor ad analysis, audience insights, campaign architecture, budget allocation, ad copy generation, and creative direction for Amazon, Shopify, Walmart, eBay, and multi-channel sellers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Install instructions rely on an unpinned global third-party skill installation.

Mitigation: Review install commands before running them and prefer a pinned, verified, non-global installation path where possible.

Risk: Web research prompts can expose sensitive competitor lists, product economics, budgets, margins, or launch plans.

Mitigation: Remove or generalize commercially sensitive inputs before allowing external web searches.

Risk: Advertising benchmarks, keyword signals, and campaign recommendations can be estimates rather than account-specific operating data.

Mitigation: Validate recommendations against platform reporting, current campaign data, and controlled budget tests before making spend decisions.
