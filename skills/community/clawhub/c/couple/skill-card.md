### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Personal relationship notes may persist in local files under ~/couple/ and could be exposed on shared computers or systems with cloud backup enabled.

Mitigation: Avoid storing private partner information, credentials, health, financial, intimate, or non-consensual details; use the skill only on devices and storage locations appropriate for personal relationship notes.
