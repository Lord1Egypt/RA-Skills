## Use Case:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Trading outputs may be mistaken for financial advice or actionable recommendations.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational analysis only, verify every trading signal independently, and consult qualified financial professionals before making investment decisions.

Risk: Stop-loss, take-profit, and position-size calculations may be inaccurate or unsuitable for a user's risk profile.

Mitigation: Review all calculations manually, account for fees, slippage, liquidity, and volatility, and apply conservative position limits before acting.

Risk: Backtested strategy performance may not predict future market behavior.

Mitigation: Use forward testing, scenario analysis, and independent validation before relying on any strategy output.
