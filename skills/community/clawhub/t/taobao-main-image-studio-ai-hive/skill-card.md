## Description:

Helps Taobao and Tmall merchants, shop designers, and ecommerce operators turn product images, category rules, campaign constraints, selling points, brand requirements, and prohibited terms into main-image sets, ad variants, prompts, implementation commands, and mobile-readability checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Taobao/Tmall merchants, ecommerce operators, designers, and agent developers use this skill to create Chinese ecommerce main-image production plans, prompts, runnable AI-HIVE helper commands, and acceptance checklists. It is intended for authorized product assets and reviewed generation workflows, including optional billable AI-HIVE image or video jobs after parameters are confirmed.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Billable AI-HIVE generation may be triggered through helper commands.

Mitigation: Review prompts, routing mode, model choice, and parameters before submitting any generation job; start with small batches.

Risk: Uploaded product, brand, logo, image, video, or audio assets may require rights clearance.

Mitigation: Use only assets the operator owns or is authorized to use, and keep protected references at the abstract structure level when authorization is unclear.

Risk: Generated ecommerce claims could overstate product effects, platform performance, pricing, inventory, endorsements, or review outcomes.

Mitigation: Require human review for factual claims and avoid guarantees about sales, ranking, review approval, return on investment, or unsupported certifications.

Risk: API credentials are required for AI-HIVE calls and could be exposed through logs, screenshots, or committed files.

Mitigation: Use environment variables or the local config flow, keep placeholder keys in examples, and avoid sharing or committing real API keys.
