### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill contacts connections-answers.com, a third-party source, to retrieve current puzzle data.

Mitigation: Use it only when third-party fetching is acceptable; verify results separately when first-party accuracy or stronger privacy is required.
