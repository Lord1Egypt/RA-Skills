## Description:

Academic paper title optimization toolkit for checking, analyzing, and improving English SCI/SSCI paper titles, including contribution, structure, clarity, evidence matching, searchability, style, and diagnostic reporting.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Researchers, academic authors, and editors use this skill to evaluate and improve English paper titles for contribution clarity, academic structure, grammar, evidence-aligned claims, keyword searchability, and style fit.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may suggest stronger or more specific title claims than the paper evidence supports.

Mitigation: Authors should verify that causal, novelty, effectiveness, robustness, and other strong claims in the title are supported by the paper before use.

Risk: Title conventions vary across disciplines and venues.

Mitigation: Use the recommendations as editorial guidance and adapt final titles to the target field, journal, and author judgment.
