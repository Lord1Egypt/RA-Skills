## Description:

Generates complete pre-launch campaigns for SaaS, apps, courses, hardware, and communities, including waitlist landing pages, countdown emails, Product Hunt launch kits, and early-access nurture sequences.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gitflopez](https://clawhub.ai/user/gitflopez)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Founders, indie hackers, creators, community builders, and agencies use this skill to generate launch-ready marketing copy and campaign assets for building a waitlist, preparing a Product Hunt launch, and converting early-access users.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated testimonials, metrics, security claims, compliance statements, or social-proof claims may be inaccurate if placeholders are published without verification.

Mitigation: Replace placeholders with substantiated evidence and review all factual claims before publishing campaign assets.

Risk: Product Hunt, email, social, Slack, Discord, or community outreach copy may conflict with platform rules or audience expectations if used without adaptation.

Mitigation: Use only permitted outreach channels, follow platform and community rules, and tailor requests to audiences with permission or clear relevance.
