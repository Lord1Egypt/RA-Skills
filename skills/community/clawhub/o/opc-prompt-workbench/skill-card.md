## Description:

Selects and customizes 16 Chinese OPC prompt templates for requirements analysis, product design, project delivery, content operations, quotations, business reflection, emotional reflection, and contract screening.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and solo-company operators use this skill to choose, adapt, and quality-check prompt templates for common OPC workflows. It helps produce copy-ready prompts while preserving placeholders or asking focused follow-up questions when business facts are missing.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Contract-screening prompts may be mistaken for professional legal advice.

Mitigation: Treat contract outputs as preliminary risk screening and have important contracts reviewed by qualified local professionals.

Risk: Emotional-reflection prompts may be used beyond reflection and action planning.

Mitigation: Do not use the skill for diagnosis or therapy; seek professional support when self-harm risk or sustained impairment appears.

Risk: Sensitive contract, customer, employee, or health information may be sent to an external model.

Mitigation: Redact or generalize sensitive details before sharing content with any external model.

Risk: Generated prompts may include invented cases, data, prices, or legal facts when source context is missing.

Mitigation: Keep clear placeholders or ask focused follow-up questions instead of fabricating missing information.
