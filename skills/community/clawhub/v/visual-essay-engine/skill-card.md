## Description:

Transform AIGC images into a structured visual essay with narrative sequencing, bilingual captions, artist statements, and Instagram-ready packaging.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[milab-bit](https://clawhub.ai/user/milab-bit)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External creators, AI artists, founders, and brand storytellers use this skill to turn a set of AIGC images into a publishing-ready visual essay package with narrative order, bilingual captions, an artist statement, and hashtag structure.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated captions, artist statements, or narrative sequencing may misrepresent the supplied images or the creator's intent.

Mitigation: Review and edit the generated series package before publishing, especially where visual interpretation, identity framing, or brand positioning matters.

Risk: The default bilingual English/Chinese packaging may not fit every audience, platform, or release plan.

Mitigation: Tell the agent to adapt the language, caption structure, or platform packaging before generating final copy.
