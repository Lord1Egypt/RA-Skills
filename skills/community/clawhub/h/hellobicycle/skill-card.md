## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill for China bike-sharing guidance: comparing the major HelloBike, Meituan Bike, and Didi Qingju options, deciding whether a ride pass is economical, and preparing practical steps for HelloBike fee disputes or safety-sensitive hitchhiking decisions.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Pricing, ride-pass value, and bike availability vary by city and can change after release.

Mitigation: Use the skill for decision structure, then verify current prices, vehicle density, electronic-fence rules, and pass terms in the live app before acting.

Risk: Hitchhiking guidance can understate personal safety and timing uncertainty if treated as a guarantee.

Mitigation: Keep expectations conservative, share trip details, verify the vehicle and plate, and avoid deep-night long-distance solo trips when risk is elevated.
