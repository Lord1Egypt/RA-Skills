## Description:

Generate ideas fast. Adapt depth and structure to what the user actually needs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to help an agent generate brainstorming ideas, option lists, naming candidates, comparisons, and creative prompts with depth matched to the request.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad requests such as asking for options or help thinking something through.

Mitigation: Review the skill's trigger behavior and scope before deployment in contexts where broad brainstorming responses could be disruptive.

Risk: Brainstormed ideas may be incomplete, unsuitable, or misleading if the user's constraints are missing.

Mitigation: Have users or reviewers validate generated ideas against domain, policy, and business constraints before acting on them.
