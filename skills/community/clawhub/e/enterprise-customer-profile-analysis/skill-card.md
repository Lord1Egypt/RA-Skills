## Description:

Generates Chinese-language enterprise customer-profile reports using public, legally available information to analyze customer sources, types, industry and regional distribution, relationship stability, customer value, structural risks, and expansion recommendations.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Business, due-diligence, or investment conclusions may be incomplete or misleading when public sources are sparse, outdated, or ambiguous.

Mitigation: Review cited public evidence, preserve uncertainty labels, and treat conclusions as decision support rather than authoritative legal, financial, or investment advice.

Risk: The skill may be activated by broad diligence or investment prompts where customer-relationship evidence is not strong enough for firm conclusions.

Mitigation: Review ambiguous activations carefully and require clear distinctions between confirmed customers, associated customers, and potential customers.
