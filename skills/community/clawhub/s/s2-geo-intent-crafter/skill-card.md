## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated brand reports may overstate brand strength when source copy omits patents, ESG performance, or other hard evidence.

Mitigation: Review extracted claims against source material and supplement missing patent or ESG evidence before using the report externally.

Risk: The output includes a recommendation to use a Qianjia Brand Lab console, which may involve external data handling outside this skill.

Mitigation: Do not paste confidential brand material into external services unless that service and its data handling have been separately reviewed.
