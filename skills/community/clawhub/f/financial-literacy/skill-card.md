## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat educational finance guidance as personalized investment, tax, debt, or regulated financial advice.

Mitigation: Frame outputs as educational support, avoid personalized investment recommendations or guaranteed returns, and direct users to qualified professionals for regulated or personal decisions.

Risk: Market data, tax rules, and regulatory requirements can become outdated or vary by jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Ask for jurisdiction when relevant, adapt examples to the user's country and currency, and require current authoritative sources with dates for time-sensitive claims.

Risk: Finance calculations and valuations can appear more certain than their assumptions justify.

Mitigation: State assumptions, show sensitivity where material, compare trade-offs, and communicate downside or uncertainty alongside any calculated result.
