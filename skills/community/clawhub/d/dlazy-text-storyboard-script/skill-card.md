## Description:

Turns user-provided themes, structured copy, or outlines into detailed short-video storyboard scripts.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[dlazyai](https://clawhub.ai/user/dlazyai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators use the skill to turn themes, scripts, or outlines into shot-by-shot short-video plans, with scene direction and spoken copy.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The security assessment rates the release suspicious because its authentication and CLI image-generation steps conflict with the stated storyboard-writing purpose; the scan was partial.

Mitigation: Use the storyboard-writing guidance only when that is the intended task. Avoid installing the CLI or providing an API key unless its need and the service destinations have been independently reviewed.
