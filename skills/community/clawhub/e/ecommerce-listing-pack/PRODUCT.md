# ecommerce-listing-pack · Product & Monetization

> Monetization model: **free tier → Pro subscription → Enterprise**.
> Pricing anchored below the 48.3 USD/month psychological ceiling for AI subscriptions;
> the free quota wall is the paywall trigger.

## 1. Tier boundaries

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Free — 3 product titles/month |
| **Pro Monthly** | **$19/month — bulk 100-SKU + category keyword library** | Unlimited + full Pro pack + export + template library |
| Pro Annual | $190/year | Same Pro benefits, annual discount |
| Enterprise | $99/month (multi-store voice + competitor mapping) | Team unlimited + enterprise templates |
| Pay per use | $12 one-time (single listing) | Zero-friction entry for low-frequency users |

## 2. Pricing skeleton

Hybrid model (base subscription + overage) — the most common 2026 pattern:
predictable revenue, heavy-user value captured.

## 3. Conversion funnel

```text
Free tier (Free — 3 product titles/month)
  → quota exhausted -> paywall prompt
  → 7-day free trial (no card required)
  → Pro Monthly $19/month — bulk 100-SKU + category keyword library
  → Pro Annual $190/year
  → Enterprise $99/month (multi-store voice + competitor mapping)
```

## 4. Success metrics (3 months)

- Free → trial ≥ 15%
- Trial → paid ≥ 25%
- Monthly retention ≥ 60%
- NPS ≥ 45

## 5. Compliance guardrails

- Always attach "AI-assisted tool, not professional advice" disclaimer
- Unit price must stay above per-run API cost
- Generic capabilities have weak pricing power — sell the vertical specialist delta
