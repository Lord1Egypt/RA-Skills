## Description:

Assists with identifying workplace environmental factors and physical, chemical, and biological hazards, then provides classification analysis and prevention recommendations for safety assessment, risk identification, environmental monitoring, and EHS audit workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[duding-engicool](https://clawhub.ai/user/duding-engicool)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

EHS, safety, operations, and audit teams use this skill to structure workplace hazard identification across manufacturing areas, laboratories, warehouses, logistics spaces, and limited office settings. It helps collect site information, classify environmental factors and hazards, assess risk level, and draft advisory control recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat advisory EHS analysis as a substitute for qualified inspections, legal certification, workplace monitoring, or specialist assessments.

Mitigation: Use the skill as an initial identification and reporting aid, then require qualified EHS review, measurements, certification, or specialist assessment before operational decisions.

Risk: The workflow and source material are in Chinese, which can create review risk for teams that cannot validate Chinese safety terminology.

Mitigation: Have Chinese-proficient EHS personnel review inputs and outputs before adopting recommendations.
