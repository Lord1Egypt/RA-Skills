## Description:

八字算命AI大师 is a Chinese-language traditional metaphysics skill for Bazi charting, Mei Hua Yi Shu divination, and Feng Shui self-checks, using bundled local calculation scripts for cultural entertainment rather than scientific prediction.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External Chinese-speaking users use this skill to request culturally framed Bazi readings, one-question Mei Hua Yi Shu divination, and home or office Feng Shui self-check guidance. The skill asks for only the details needed for the selected workflow, such as birth date/time, gender, city, a specific question, or layout information.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may disclose sensitive birth, location, personal-question, or home/office layout details.

Mitigation: Collect only the minimum details required for the selected workflow and avoid unnecessary personal or third-party information.

Risk: Divination or Feng Shui output could be mistaken for professional advice.

Mitigation: Present results as cultural entertainment and direct medical, legal, financial, and safety matters to qualified professionals.

Risk: The workflow and outputs are Chinese-only.

Mitigation: Use this skill only where a Simplified Chinese interaction is appropriate for the intended users.
