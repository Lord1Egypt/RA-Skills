## Description:

Flip problems upside down - instead of "how to succeed", ask "how to definitely fail" then avoid those paths.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[artyomx33](https://clawhub.ai/user/artyomx33)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External users and employees use this skill to apply inversion thinking to planning, strategy, and decision-making by identifying likely failure paths and converting them into avoidance strategies and anti-checklists.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad trigger phrases such as "flip it" or "what NOT to do" may invoke the skill in conversations where inversion framing is not intended.

Mitigation: Confirm that failure-path analysis is appropriate for the user's goal before applying the full inversion format.

Risk: The childcare face-recognition example could be mistaken for implementation guidance in a sensitive biometric context.

Mitigation: Treat that example as illustrative only and require privacy, consent, security, retention, and legal review before any real biometric workflow.
