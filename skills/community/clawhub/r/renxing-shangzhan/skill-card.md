## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can generate persuasion and conversion advice based on psychological weaknesses, fears, and vulnerable audience profiles.

Mitigation: Use it for ethical analysis or defensive review, avoid targeting minors or vulnerable people, and review outputs before operational use.

Risk: Broad trigger terms can activate the skill for general marketing or business-analysis prompts.

Mitigation: Scope prompts narrowly and require human review before publishing or acting on generated strategy recommendations.
