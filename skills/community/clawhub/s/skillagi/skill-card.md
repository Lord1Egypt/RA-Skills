## Description:

Remember mistakes across sessions. Append one-liner learnings, review before complex tasks, promote important ones.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wubotvix](https://clawhub.ai/user/wubotvix)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Agents use this skill to maintain a concise learning log of mistakes, corrections, and project-specific conventions so relevant lessons can be reviewed before complex work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can lead agents to promote session learnings into durable agent instruction files.

Mitigation: Require manual review of the exact text, source, scope, and reversibility before allowing promotion into AGENTS.md, MEMORY.md, TOOLS.md, CLAUDE.md, or auto-memory.
