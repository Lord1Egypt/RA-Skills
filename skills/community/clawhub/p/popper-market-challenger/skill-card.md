## Description:

Falsification-oriented market thesis challenger for A-shares and U.S. markets that attacks an existing market thesis, morning brief, close review, regime call, positioning note, or catalyst narrative by decomposing it into falsifiable claims, auditing evidence, mapping fastest disconfirming signals, and deciding whether the base case still survives.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Portfolio managers, traders, and market analysts use this skill to challenge an existing A-share or U.S. market thesis by decomposing it into falsifiable claims, auditing news/data/price-action evidence, and converting the challenge into sizing and timing decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may overread the challenge output as financial advice or an automated trading decision.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as analytical support for human review; the PM or analyst remains responsible for final sizing, timing, and trade decisions.

Risk: The analysis may be stale or weak if current news, market data, or price action is unavailable.

Mitigation: Use current evidence when available, mark missing evidence buckets explicitly, and treat fully ungrounded claims as untested.
