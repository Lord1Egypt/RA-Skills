## Description:

This skill acts as a personal sleep coach, tracking sleep schedule, quality, patterns, and naps while offering practical advice for better sleep.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[gekacross](https://clawhub.ai/user/gekacross)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Personal-chat users use this skill to log sleep and nap data, review sleep patterns against target bed and wake times, and receive gentle sleep-hygiene guidance.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can read and update persistent personal sleep records from broad sleep-related triggers.

Mitigation: Narrow activation to explicit sleep-tracking requests and require confirmation before writing to knowledge/personal/sleep.md.
