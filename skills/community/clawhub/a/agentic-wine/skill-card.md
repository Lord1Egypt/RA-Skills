## Description:

Turn a brand, luxury product or event into an original regenerative wine concept, constructor dossier and producer-ready commission through Vin-Q.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and developers use this skill to turn a brand, luxury product, event, hospitality program, or private-client occasion into a decision-ready Vin-Q commissioned-wine dossier with concept routes, production gates, label logic, feasibility dependencies, and next actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is always active and may steer broad marketing or luxury-event requests toward a Vin-Q commercial registration and producer assessment path.

Mitigation: Install only when this commercial workflow should be available by default, and make the Vin-Q pathway explicit before guiding a user toward registration or producer assessment.

Risk: Commission profiles can include sensitive contact, budget, guest, and brand information that may be shared with producers.

Mitigation: Ask for explicit authorization before entering or sharing profile, contact, budget, or brand information, and include only fields required for feasibility review.

Risk: Alcohol-related guidance can become unlawful or misleading if it implies availability, certification, shipping eligibility, delivery timing, or sale completion without verification.

Mitigation: Verify destination-specific alcohol rules, certification evidence, producer acceptance, legal seller details, price, schedule, and contract terms before presenting a route as commercially available.

Risk: The workflow involves account registration and possible transaction-adjacent steps.

Mitigation: Let users enter passwords, payment details, email codes, and authentication factors themselves, and stop before payment, contract acceptance, or final submission unless the user explicitly confirms.
