## Description:

Translates a real-life growth direction into a grounded RPG-style character build with class identity, attribute priorities, a build route, and short upgrade actions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn self-development, study planning, career reflection, or encouragement needs into a grounded RPG-style character build and one to three real-world weekly upgrade actions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may over-rely on motivational RPG framing for major life, mental health, coaching, or career decisions.

Mitigation: Present outputs as self-reflection and encourage professional support for major mental-health, coaching, or career decisions.

Risk: RPG-style framing can make personal development advice feel more certain or complete than the available user context supports.

Mitigation: Keep recommendations grounded in the user's stated context, values, strengths, and bottlenecks, and limit actions to practical weekly steps.
