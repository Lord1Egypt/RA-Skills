## Description:

Generates, evaluates, and refines Chinese short-form oral video scripts using an eight-step structure for hooks, empathy, argument, counter-intuition, methods, emotional close, call to action, and title.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[muwerr-spec](https://clawhub.ai/user/muwerr-spec)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and content teams use this skill to plan topics, draft 500-800 word Chinese oral-video scripts, improve existing scripts, and apply a consistent no-AI-flavor editorial style across six defined content lanes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill is highly specialized and may over-constrain outputs outside Chinese short-form oral-video scripting.

Mitigation: Use it for the listed script-writing, topic-evaluation, script-optimization, and content-matrix workflows; choose another writing skill for general-purpose drafting.

Risk: Scripts touching sensitive social events or personality labels can produce unsupported factual judgments or harmful framing if used without review.

Mitigation: Keep sensitive-event handling to psychology or human-reaction analysis, avoid fact judgments and insinuation, and review claims before publication.
