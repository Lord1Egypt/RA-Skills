## Description:

Generates hyper-contextual microcopy, including CTAs, subject lines, push notifications, form labels, and error messages, for awareness, consideration, decision, and retention micromoments.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External marketers, product teams, and developers use this skill to draft conversion-focused microcopy variants for web, mobile, email, e-commerce, and SaaS touchpoints. It is useful when teams need audience-aware alternatives, A/B test candidates, or messaging rationale for a specific customer journey moment.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated marketing copy may include unsupported compliance, scarcity, urgency, social proof, or privacy-sensitive targeting claims.

Mitigation: Review claims, deadlines, inventory cues, social proof, targeting assumptions, and privacy implications before publishing.

Risk: The skill may use customer or analytics context supplied in the prompt.

Mitigation: Provide only the minimum customer or analytics data needed for the requested copy and avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Risk: Copy variants are theoretical until tested with the intended audience.

Mitigation: Treat variants as drafts, validate them through brand, legal, accessibility, and A/B testing review, and keep the current winner as a baseline.
