## Description:

Create a polished explainer handout with visuals, FAQs, and clear next steps for a physical therapy practice.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Physical therapists and rehab clinic operators use this skill to draft patient-facing orthopedic physical therapy education handouts with visuals, FAQs, and clear next steps.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill requests Bash access even though its stated purpose is drafting handouts.

Mitigation: Install only when shell access is acceptable, and prefer a reduced-tool version for drafting, reading user-provided material, and optional image generation.

Risk: Patient-facing medical education content may contain incorrect, incomplete, or regulated medical claims.

Mitigation: Have a qualified human reviewer approve the handout before patient distribution.
