## Description:

A Chinese chat relationship advisor that manages separate contact contexts, analyzes chat histories and relationship status, and suggests concrete replies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[lijie-1637577164](https://clawhub.ai/user/lijie-1637577164)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to keep separate Chinese-language chat contexts for multiple contacts, assess communication quality and relationship direction, and draft context-aware replies.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste private conversations or sensitive third-party details into the agent context.

Mitigation: Redact names, phone numbers, account IDs, addresses, secrets, intimate details, and unnecessary third-party information; use summaries when exact logs are not required.

Risk: Relationship assessments and suggested replies may be incomplete or misleading if the provided chat history lacks context.

Mitigation: Review the generated assessment before acting on it and provide missing relationship background or constraints when asking for advice.
