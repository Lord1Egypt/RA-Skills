## Description:

Identifies weed species and estimates coverage density from field top-view images, producing weed distribution heatmap data to support precision weeding decisions.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[18072937735](https://clawhub.ai/user/18072937735)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Farmers, agronomists, and field teams can submit overhead field images or videos to review weed types, distribution, and coverage density, or retrieve prior analysis reports. Results support field management and do not provide herbicide or treatment recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
