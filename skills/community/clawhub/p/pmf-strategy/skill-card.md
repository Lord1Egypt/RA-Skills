## Description:

Provides product-market fit strategy guidance for validating PMF, measuring PMF signals, diagnosing traction problems, and planning before scaling.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Developers, founders, product teams, and growth teams use this skill to assess whether a product has product-market fit, identify weak PMF signals, and choose validation steps before scaling acquisition or go-to-market work.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: PMF recommendations can be misleading if treated as guaranteed business outcomes or applied without current customer evidence.

Mitigation: Validate the skill's recommendations against customer interviews, retention data, activation metrics, and segment-specific PMF signals before making scaling decisions.
