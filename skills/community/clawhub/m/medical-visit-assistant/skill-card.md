## Description:

A tool to help users organize symptoms, records, medications, and questions before medical visits, while offering clear explanations of examination reports and cautious risk reminders without diagnosing, prescribing, or replacing professional care.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to prepare for medical appointments, organize symptoms and medical records, understand examination reports in plain language, draft clinician questions, and record visit outcomes. It supports communication with healthcare professionals and does not replace professional medical care.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may mistake organized medical information or plain-language report explanations for diagnosis, treatment, emergency triage, or medication advice.

Mitigation: The skill states that it does not diagnose, prescribe, replace professional care, or determine emergency status with certainty from chat alone.

Risk: Sensitive health information may be unnecessarily repeated or expanded during preparation and record-keeping.

Mitigation: The skill instructs the assistant to use only user-supplied or current-context information, avoid unnecessary personally identifying information, and avoid repeating sensitive information when it is not needed.

Risk: Potentially urgent symptoms could be delayed by routine information gathering.

Mitigation: The skill prioritizes urgent safety reminders for severe or suddenly worsening symptoms before routine questioning and directs users to local emergency medical help when appropriate.
