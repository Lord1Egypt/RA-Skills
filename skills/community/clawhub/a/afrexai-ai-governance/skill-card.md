## Description:

Builds practical internal AI governance policies, including acceptable use, vendor review, data handling, compliance mapping, incident response, and board reporting templates.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[1kalin](https://clawhub.ai/user/1kalin)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

CTOs, CISOs, compliance officers, operations leaders, and governance teams use this skill to draft and review AI governance programs for organizations adopting AI tools. It is especially relevant for regulated or enterprise-facing organizations that need policy templates, procurement checks, incident response procedures, and board-level reporting.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may paste confidential, personal, regulated, or proprietary business data into the assistant while adapting policy templates.

Mitigation: Use approved organizational workflows and avoid entering sensitive data unless legal, privacy, and security teams have approved that use.

Risk: Governance, monitoring, HR, legal, or compliance policies drafted by the skill may be incomplete or unsuitable for a specific jurisdiction or business context.

Mitigation: Have legal, privacy, security, and HR stakeholders review generated policies and monitoring practices before adoption.

Risk: External AfrexAI and Stripe links in the artifact may receive visit metadata if opened.

Mitigation: Treat linked commercial sites as external resources and follow organizational browsing and procurement review practices before using them.
