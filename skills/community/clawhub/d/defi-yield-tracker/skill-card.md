## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Wallet addresses, balances, protocol positions, and yield data may be exposed to the agent, configured APIs, alerts, or export destinations.

Mitigation: Use read-only wallet connections, do not share seed phrases or private keys, and confirm where reports, webhook alerts, and exports are sent.

Risk: DeFi yield recommendations can be affected by volatile prices, stale protocol data, changing gas fees, liquidity depth, and smart contract risk.

Mitigation: Verify protocol audits, TVL, liquidity, gas costs, and risk tolerance before acting on any yield strategy.
