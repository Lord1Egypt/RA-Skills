## Description:

Clinical nephrology support for kidney disease questions across AKI, CKD, proteinuria, hematuria, glomerular disease, acid-base and electrolytes, critical care nephrology, CRRT, dialysis, transplant, pregnancy kidney disease, and onconephrology.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

[juanbastias](https://clawhub.ai/user/juanbastias)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Clinicians, clinical educators, and medically supervised agents use this skill to structure nephrology differential diagnosis, identify urgent red flags, plan literature searches, and draft consult-style clinical reasoning. It is intended for education and decision support, not as a replacement for local clinical judgment, bedside assessment, or authoritative guidelines.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical guidance could be misapplied to urgent, high-risk, or patient-specific nephrology decisions.

Mitigation: Use as educational and clinical reasoning support only; for real patient care, urgent abnormalities, pregnancy, transplant, oncology, ICU, procedures, dialysis decisions, or medication dosing, rely on the treating clinical team, local protocols, and current authoritative guidelines.

Risk: Incorrect thresholds, doses, formulas, or guideline claims could affect clinical interpretation.

Mitigation: Confirm units and formulas, avoid inventing doses or thresholds, and verify current guideline or literature sources before applying recommendations.
