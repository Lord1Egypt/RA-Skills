This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Car owners and other external users use this skill to interpret vehicle symptoms, warning lights, OBD-II codes, maintenance intervals, repair estimates, and DIY-versus-mechanic decisions.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Safety-critical automotive advice can be incorrect or incomplete for issues such as brakes, steering, suspension, overheating, oil-pressure warnings, fuel leaks, electrical faults, or uncertain symptoms.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as educational guidance and consult a qualified mechanic before driving or attempting repairs in safety-critical or uncertain cases.

Risk: Repair cost estimates may vary by vehicle, region, parts choice, labor rate, and additional faults found during diagnosis.

Mitigation: Use estimates as planning ranges and verify diagnosis, parts, labor, and total cost with a mechanic or trusted repair source.
