## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validated plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and next steps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to evaluate whether a product idea is worth building before major investment. It helps structure assumptions, validation plans, prototypes, positioning, reusable checklists, and verification notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill when the user did not intend product validation planning.

Mitigation: Prefer explicit invocation or narrow the trigger text where accidental activation would disrupt the agent workflow.

Risk: Product validation guidance can be misleading when based on weak assumptions or incomplete market evidence.

Mitigation: Review assumptions, validate recommendations with customer or market evidence, and treat plans as decision support rather than proof of demand.
