## Description:

Auto Translate formats an agent's answer in Chinese, then appends English and Japanese translations for trilingual response scenarios.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vegetablesh](https://clawhub.ai/user/vegetablesh)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to answer in Chinese and append English and Japanese translations when trilingual output or translated follow-up text is requested.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may make answers longer or conflict with requests for a different response format.

Mitigation: Enable it when Chinese, English, and Japanese output is desired, and disable or override it for tasks that require a different format.

Risk: Translated professional terminology can affect meaning if the source answer is ambiguous or domain-specific.

Mitigation: Review high-impact translations for accuracy and terminology consistency before relying on them.
