## Use Case:

Food production quality, safety, and compliance teams use this skill to build, review, and maintain HACCP plans under GB 14881-2025. It supports hazard analysis, CCP determination, monitoring design, corrective action planning, verification records, and SC certification preparation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat HACCP guidance as a final regulatory or food-safety decision without checking current official standards or professional requirements.

Mitigation: Verify HACCP plans, limits, monitoring programs, and compliance decisions with qualified food-safety professionals and current official standards before operational use.

Risk: The guidance is focused on Chinese GB 14881-2025 food-production compliance and may not fully match another jurisdiction, product category, or facility context.

Mitigation: Confirm jurisdiction, product-specific standards, customer requirements, and facility prerequisites before applying the templates or decision trees.
