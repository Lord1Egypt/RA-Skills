## Description:

Draft a grant application that reviewers can fund: problem, milestones, budget, and proof of capability. Use for ecosystem grants, DAO grants, and public-goods funding rounds.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[manteclaw](https://clawhub.ai/user/manteclaw)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Grant applicants use this skill to draft applications for ecosystem grants, DAO grants, public-goods funding rounds, and other grant programs. It organizes the proposal around the funder's rubric, measurable milestones, a reconciled budget, evidence of capability, and sustainability.
