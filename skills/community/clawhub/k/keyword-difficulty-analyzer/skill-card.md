## Description:

Assesses SEO keyword difficulty and competitors to recommend which keywords to target.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[tutorgpt-io](https://clawhub.ai/user/tutorgpt-io)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

SEO practitioners and content marketers use this skill to compare keyword demand, difficulty, trends, and competitors, then decide whether to pursue a keyword or consider long-tail alternatives.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Keywords sent to external research services may reveal confidential plans.

Mitigation: Avoid submitting confidential or unreleased keywords, and consider the destination services before running an analysis.
