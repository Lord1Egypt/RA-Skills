## Use Case:

External marketing operators and agents use this skill to plan Meta Ads campaigns before launch by clarifying the offer, objective, audience, structure, budget, bidding approach, and decision timeline.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Optional benchmark web research could expose sensitive campaign context such as product URLs, budgets, customer-list details, or launch plans.

Mitigation: Avoid web search for confidential campaigns or provide benchmark data manually.

Risk: Campaign strategy recommendations may be unsuitable if success metrics, budget, audience, or offer details are incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the strategy brief before launch and confirm CPL or CPA targets, budget realism, audience scope, and decision points.
