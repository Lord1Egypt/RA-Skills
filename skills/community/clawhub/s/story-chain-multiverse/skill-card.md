## Description:

多元宇宙互動故事接龍系統。根據玩家輸入與當前劇情，續寫下一小段故事。支援科幻、未來、歷史、穿越、系統、仙俠、魔法、奇幻、影視動漫同人、武俠、重生、都市、異能等多題材自由混合。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users use this Traditional Chinese interactive fiction skill to start and continue branching multiverse story chains across genres such as science fiction, xianxia, fantasy, time travel, urban powers, and fan-fiction-inspired settings.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can suggest optional adult story branches, which may be unsuitable for users who want all-ages fiction.

Mitigation: Keep adult content disabled by default and only enter that branch after explicit user opt-in; users can request all-ages stories.
