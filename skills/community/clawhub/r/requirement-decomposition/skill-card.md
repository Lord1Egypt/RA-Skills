## Description:

Decompose business scenarios into Agent capability modules, tool chains, and implementation roadmaps.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[bettermen](https://clawhub.ai/user/bettermen)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Product managers, solution architects, and agent developers use this skill to turn vague requirements, concrete business scenarios, or PRDs into structured Agent capability modules, tool-chain plans, user stories, implementation roadmaps, and risk notes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Connected project-tracking or document-collaboration accounts may be used to create Jira/Linear issues or publish Notion/Feishu documents.

Mitigation: For confidential requirements, request a local Markdown draft and require explicit approval before any issue creation or document publication.

Risk: Generated requirement decompositions may contain incomplete capability mappings, inaccurate dependencies, or unrealistic roadmap estimates.

Mitigation: Have product and engineering reviewers check the generated modules, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and risks before implementation.
