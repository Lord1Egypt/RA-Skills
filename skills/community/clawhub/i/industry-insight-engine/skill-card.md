## Description:

Researches an industry, brand, or topic through hypothesis-led analysis and produces a decision report with market structure, opportunity judgments, forecasts, and action guidance, with quick, standard, deep, and refresh workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[xp1001](https://clawhub.ai/user/xp1001)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Decision makers using an agent workflow can use the skill to assess an industry, brand, or business idea, examine opportunities and risks, and plan practical next steps. Its research is oriented toward the China market.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

China

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad automatic activation triggers may match requests outside industry research.

Mitigation: Limit automatic activation to industry, brand, and business research requests, or invoke the skill explicitly when scope control matters.

Risk: The benign security verdict is not a guarantee, and some references were unresolved, leaving scan evidence incomplete.

Mitigation: Review the source material and report citations, then verify consequential claims with primary sources or direct market checks before acting.
