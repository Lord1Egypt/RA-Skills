## Description:

Helps assess social energy, evaluate upcoming events' impact, and create realistic plans with boundaries and recovery to manage social overload.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to assess social energy, classify upcoming social commitments, prepare boundary language, and reserve recovery time before overload.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat social-energy planning as mental-health care, crisis support, or medical advice.

Mitigation: Use it only as a lightweight planning aid for social energy, recovery time, and boundary wording; seek qualified help for severe distress, self-harm thoughts, depression, anxiety, or impaired daily functioning.

Risk: Generated plans may not fit unavoidable work, family, or social obligations.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before acting and adapt buffers, exits, and recharge plans to real commitments.
