### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Marketing outputs may include factual claims, metrics, or performance estimates that are incorrect or unsupported.

Mitigation: Review claims and metrics against approved sources before publishing or using them in sales, campaign, or executive materials.

Risk: Recommendations may not match the intended brand fit, audience language preference, or Frutero-specific voice.

Mitigation: Run outputs through brand and audience review, including Spanish/English localization where relevant, before launch.

Risk: Bold creative recommendations may exceed the user's brand-safety, legal, or stakeholder risk tolerance.

Mitigation: Require human approval for sensitive campaigns and validate high-impact messaging with tests before committing budget.
