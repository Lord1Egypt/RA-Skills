## Description:

GZH Operation Optimizer analyzes WeChat public-account articles from a URL or pasted text, scores viral potential across eight dimensions, explains performance drivers, and recommends concrete revisions and content growth strategies.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External content operators, marketers, editors, and creators use this skill to diagnose WeChat article performance, compare articles or titles, and turn the diagnosis into concrete article revisions and growth tactics.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A user-provided article URL may cause the page to be fetched for analysis.

Mitigation: Use pasted article text for sensitive sources, and only fetch URLs the user is authorized to analyze.

Risk: The skill evaluates communication effectiveness and does not certify factual accuracy in expert domains such as health or finance.

Mitigation: Verify domain claims with qualified sources or reviewers before publishing revised content.

Risk: Viral-content optimization can produce stronger hooks than the source article can support.

Mitigation: Keep titles and openings aligned with the article's actual claims, and reject suggestions that overpromise outcomes.
