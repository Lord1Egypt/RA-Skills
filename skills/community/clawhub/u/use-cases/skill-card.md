## Use Case:

Employees, developers, business owners, creatives, and new AI users use this skill to identify practical AI-agent use cases by role, task type, and skill level. It helps an agent tailor examples, prompts, limitations, and adoption advice to the user's context.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may share sensitive data after reading broad privacy reassurance.

Mitigation: Follow the AI product and workplace privacy policies, and avoid entering passwords, API keys, confidential business data, personal records, financial details, medical information, or legal secrets unless the data handling terms are understood.

Risk: AI use-case suggestions or generated examples may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for final decisions.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as drafts or ideas, verify important facts such as names, dates, and URLs, and keep human review as the final decision point.
