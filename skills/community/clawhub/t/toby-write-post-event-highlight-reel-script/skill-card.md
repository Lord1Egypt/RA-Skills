## Use Case:

External event marketers, field teams, and sponsorship managers use this skill to convert event notes into narration-ready recap scripts for post-event highlight reels and social videos.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Mitigation: Review scripts before external publication for accuracy, sponsor mentions, and brand tone.
