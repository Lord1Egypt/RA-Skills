Translate strategy into channel-specific execution plans for Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Ads, TikTok Ads, YouTube Ads, Amazon Ads, Shopify Ads, and DSP/programmatic.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Marketing, growth, and advertising operators use this skill to turn campaign objectives, budgets, and channel scope into actionable launch plans, test matrices, budget and bidding plans, and scale or stop rules.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Recommendations could affect live advertising spend, bidding, billing, tracking, or policy-sensitive campaign settings.

Mitigation: Review recommendations before applying them in live ad platforms, and require human approval for budget, bidding, billing, tracking, or policy-sensitive changes.

Risk: The skill provides planning guidance and does not execute ad-account actions or verify platform outcomes.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as proposals, validate assumptions against current account data and platform policies, and include rollback or stop-loss conditions before launch.
