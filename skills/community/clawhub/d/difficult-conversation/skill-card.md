## Description:

Prepare for and script a hard conversation: conflict, bad news, a boundary, or an apology.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[mohitagw15856](https://clawhub.ai/user/mohitagw15856)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Employees, managers, and individual contributors use this skill to prepare for hard workplace or interpersonal conversations. It helps clarify the real goal, represent the other person's likely perspective, draft an opening, identify key points, plan calm responses, and close with a concrete next step.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate for broad requests about conflict or hard conversations.

Mitigation: Use it as interpersonal preparation guidance and review whether the situation needs a qualified legal, HR, medical, safety, or crisis professional.

Risk: Conversation scripts can be incomplete or mismatched to the other person's actual perspective.

Mitigation: Check that the brief separates facts from interpretations, represents the other side fairly, and keeps responses calm and non-escalating.
