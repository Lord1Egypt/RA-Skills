Mitigation: Verify time-sensitive details with current sources or the venue before visiting.

Risk: High-demand venues may have long queues, reservation requirements, photo restrictions, or minimum-spend rules.

Mitigation: Reserve ahead when possible, visit outside peak periods, and follow posted venue rules.
