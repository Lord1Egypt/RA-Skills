# Methods and Experience Starters

A person can entrust the work in ordinary language, choose a direction, name or combine methods, or ask the AI to teach through the work itself. The AI selects what is valuable for the subject at hand. When the user names a complete route, follow the method's real intent and explain how it was adapted to this subject.

This is the capability-resource index. Enter the [method index](methods/INDEX.md) for a concrete cognitive act, the [system pattern library](patterns/README.md) when a recurring causal structure may be present, and the [route index](routes/INDEX.md) when the user names a mature line of thought or the subject needs a complete practice arc. Resources strengthen live judgment; they do not decide the runtime's global order.

| Method family | When it earns its place |
| --- | --- |
| **X-ray, loops, and scale** | When a visible event is produced by hidden resource flows, delays, interactions, or relations between levels, use a checkable observation to make the decisive structure visible. |
| **First principles and inversion** | When an argument rests on inherited assumptions, separate necessary conditions, conventions, and changeable rules. Work backward from failure conditions to discover what must be observed. |
| **Leverage, bottlenecks, and trade-offs** | When a local metric can merely move the problem elsewhere, compare effects on the whole, cost, reversibility, and future options. |
| **Scenario exploration** | When uncertainty would change the decision, keep several evidence-bearing explanations or possible worlds alive and identify signals that distinguish them. |
| **Capability building and review** | Turn a repeatedly useful way of working into a real capability, then let observed results decide whether to continue, change, or end it. |

Complete routes can also be named or combined. These descriptions help with choice and interaction; the actual run grounds every judgment in the subject and its evidence.

- **Musk's algorithm.** Question requirements, delete what can be deleted, simplify, shorten the cycle, and automate last.
- **The Toyota way.** Go and see, pursue causes, test countermeasures, preserve what works, and begin the next improvement.
- **Goldratt's five focusing steps.** Identify the system constraint, exploit it, subordinate the rest, elevate when necessary, and check whether the constraint has moved.
- **Munger's multiple-model check.** Examine the same decision through failure, incentives, multiple disciplines, and downstream consequences.
- **The Feynman technique.** Explain it in your own words, find where the explanation breaks, return to the material, and explain again in checkable language.
- **Deming's PDSA.** Write the prediction while planning, act, compare result with prediction, then adopt, adapt, or end.
- **Boyd's loop.** Observe the situation, reorient the model of it, decide, act, and let the result recalibrate the next loop.
- **Scenario planning.** Select the uncertainties that could change the decision, construct credible divergent scenarios, and find early signals that separate them.

## When the user wants to try or learn a method

Use the subject they already care about, or begin with a self-contained small example. Let the user make a prediction, give them a comparison they can manipulate, and see whether the new understanding explains another situation. They can deepen one path or return to their own problem at any moment.

For example: “Why do buses bunch?” First ask which bus will meet more waiting passengers: the late bus in front or the on-time bus behind it. Compare their stop times and watch how the gap changes. Then introduce a station where some services exchange many more passengers and see how much of the first explanation still holds. This can become a diagram, a short explanation, or an interactive simulation according to the setting.

To judge how a system is acting in greater depth, use [System Science and Judgment](../core/system-science.md) to choose a mechanism worth testing.
