## Description:

基于 George Polya《怎样解题》方法论的中文思维引导工具，通过苏格拉底式提问帮助用户按理解、计划、执行、检验四步处理数学题和通用问题。

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Chinese-speaking students, educators, and general users use this skill to work through math problems, planning questions, diagnostics, and decisions with guided prompts instead of direct answers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on broad analysis or planning requests where a direct answer is preferred.

Mitigation: Use it when guided problem-solving is desired, or disable/select a more specific skill for direct-answer workflows.

Risk: The skill is designed for Chinese-language coaching and may not fit users expecting another language.

Mitigation: Enable it for Chinese-language problem-solving sessions or adapt the prompt language before use.
