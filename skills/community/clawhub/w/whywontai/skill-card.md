## Description:

Check what GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and other AI crawlers get from a website, and run a Why Won't AI scan for its SEO, AEO and GEO score and issues. Use when someone asks why ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Google does not mention, cite or show their site or product.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.
