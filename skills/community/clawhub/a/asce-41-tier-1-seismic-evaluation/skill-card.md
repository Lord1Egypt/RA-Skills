## Description:

Guides a licensed structural engineer through an ASCE/SEI 41-23 Tier 1 deficiency-based seismic evaluation, including performance-objective selection, FEMA building-type classification, checklist execution, and a peer-reviewable memo with Tier 2 or Tier 3 recommendation.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Licensed structural engineers and reviewing engineers use this skill to draft ASCE/SEI 41-23 Tier 1 seismic evaluation materials for existing buildings, including project scope, seismicity, checklist results, deficiencies, and next-step recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Drafted evaluation materials may influence building-safety decisions.

Mitigation: Keep a licensed structural engineer in control, require independent verification of code references and checklist results, and treat the output as draft material only.

Risk: Incorrect or missing project inputs can make Tier 1 eligibility or recommendations unreliable.

Mitigation: Require the engineer to provide and verify performance objectives, risk category, site class, seismic parameters, building type, height, and checklist dispositions before relying on the memo.

Risk: Building records may contain owner, security, or confidential project information.

Mitigation: Avoid unnecessary owner PII, security-sensitive plans, confidential lease terms, and other sensitive details in prompts or examples.
