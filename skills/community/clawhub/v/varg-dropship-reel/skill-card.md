## Description:

Creates a 60-second faceless dropshipping guide reel, from product hook and price walkthrough through captions and a comment-to-DM call to action.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[vargai](https://clawhub.ai/user/vargai)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

E-commerce sellers, dropshippers, affiliates, and course creators use the skill to create short product reels that show real product pricing and a dropshipping workflow, then direct viewers to a comment-triggered guide.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Store or order screenshots may include sensitive information when sent to VARG's external generation services; the security evidence notes that redaction guidance is absent.

Mitigation: Use demonstration data or redact sensitive details from screenshots before sending them to external services.

Risk: The security scan only partially inspected two scripts, so their behavior is not fully assessed.

Mitigation: Review the scripts before relying on them with sensitive material or production data.
