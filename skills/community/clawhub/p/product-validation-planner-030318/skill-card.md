## Description:

Helps founders, product managers, makers, and small teams turn rough product ideas into validation plans, prototypes, positioning, checklists, and decision support.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[kyro-ma](https://clawhub.ai/user/kyro-ma)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External founders, product managers, makers, and small teams use this skill to test whether a product idea is worth building before committing major effort or spending. It turns a request into a practical validation workflow, artifact, checklist, lightweight analysis, prototype plan, or positioning support.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Broad activation wording may invoke the skill for general product or startup planning requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the user's intended validation task and keep outputs scoped to the stated product idea, constraints, and success criteria.

Risk: Planning output may be mistaken for validated market evidence or definitive business advice.

Mitigation: State assumptions and recommend independent customer, market, and financial validation before major product decisions.
