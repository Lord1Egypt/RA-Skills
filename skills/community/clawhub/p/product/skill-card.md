## Use Case:

External builders, product managers, and founders use this skill to validate product ideas, prioritize features, create product imagery guidance, optimize marketplace listings, plan pricing, and review manufacturing compliance considerations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Validation experiments can mislead users if unavailable features or offers are presented unclearly.

Mitigation: Use transparent low-risk experiments where possible, disclose unavailable features after clicks, and avoid collecting payment or sensitive information under unclear claims.

Risk: Marketplace, pricing, and compliance facts can change and may affect launch decisions.

Mitigation: Verify compliance, pricing, and platform requirements against current official sources before relying on the guidance.
