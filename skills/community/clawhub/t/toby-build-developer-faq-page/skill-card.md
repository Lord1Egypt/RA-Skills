## Description:

Generate customer-facing answers for common developer questions. Use when working on FAQ content for developers, technical founders, product engineers.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated FAQ answers may include inaccurate product claims, pricing details, technical guarantees, or support commitments.

Mitigation: Review and approve FAQ content before external publication or use in customer-facing materials.
