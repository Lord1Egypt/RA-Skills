## Description:

This skill helps university hard-tech and technology-transfer teams generate Chinese-forward diagnostics, roadmaps, curricula, pitch guidance, case structures, and white paper frameworks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[earthwalking](https://clawhub.ai/user/earthwalking)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

University teachers, student founders, lab venture teams, technology-transfer platforms, incubators, and entrepreneurship educators use this skill to structure lab-to-market venture diagnosis, commercialization roadmaps, teaching materials, pitch narratives, cases, and research-style white papers.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat entrepreneurship methodology output as legal, tax, financial, or equity-agreement advice.

Mitigation: Use qualified professionals for legal, tax, financial, or equity-agreement work before relying on outputs for formal decisions.

Risk: The skill is Chinese-forward and may not match users who expect English deliverables.

Mitigation: State the desired language when invoking the skill.

Risk: The framework is intended for university hard-tech, technology-transfer, and entrepreneurship education contexts.

Mitigation: Check fit before applying it to unrelated channel-only, traffic-led, or generic startup consulting requests.
