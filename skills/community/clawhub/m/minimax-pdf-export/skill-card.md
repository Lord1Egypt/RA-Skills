## Description:

MiniMax PDF helps agents export professionally laid out PDF documents using Playwright for HTML/CSS covers and ReportLab for precise body layout.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[smseow001](https://clawhub.ai/user/smseow001)

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Developers and external users use this skill to guide agents in generating print-ready PDFs for e-books, reports, contracts, forms, and academic papers. The skill describes stable layout, bookmarks, page numbering, hyperlinked tables of contents, cross-references, AcroForms, batch export, cover styles, and font and image fidelity.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may be used to process sensitive documents during PDF generation.

Mitigation: Confirm the publisher provenance and review runtime dependencies and commands before processing confidential or regulated documents.
