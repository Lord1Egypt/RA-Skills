## Description:

NSFC Grant Writer helps users prepare Chinese National Natural Science Foundation grant applications with structure guidance, abstract review, logic checks, formatting checklists, and representative-work selection.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[wylcasia](https://clawhub.ai/user/wylcasia)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

External researchers and grant applicants use this skill for Chinese-language guidance on NSFC youth, general, and related project applications. It helps review proposal structure, abstracts, scientific questions, application logic, formatting requirements, research integrity checks, and representative-work selection.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expose confidential, unpublished, or regulated research details while asking for proposal guidance.

Mitigation: Use only in an environment approved for that data, and redact sensitive research details when approval is unclear.

Risk: NSFC rules, templates, deadlines, and AI-use disclosure requirements may change after the skill content was published.

Mitigation: Verify all submission requirements against current official NSFC guidance before filing.

Risk: Checklist scripts and generated guidance are advisory and may miss institution-specific requirements.

Mitigation: Have the applicant and host institution review the final proposal package before submission.
