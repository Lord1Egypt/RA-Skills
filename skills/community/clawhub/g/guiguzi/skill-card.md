## Description:

This skill turns Guiguzi's 72 strategies into agent guidance for business and finance strategy, negotiation, influence, intelligence analysis, competition, alliances, long-term planning, investment psychology, and deal counterparty analysis.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External business and finance users use this skill to select and apply strategy frameworks for persuasion, negotiation, competitive positioning, alliance building, intelligence analysis, long-range planning, investment behavior review, and financial deal negotiation. The skill is best used as structured decision-support guidance, not as legal, compliance, investment, or market-timing advice.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can steer agents toward aggressive or legally sensitive persuasion, competitive intelligence, recruiting, and finance tactics.

Mitigation: Use it only with explicit legal, compliance, consent, and professional-boundary checks; reject requests involving deception, market manipulation, insider information, bribery, or breach of fiduciary duty.

Risk: The inspected release includes guidance themes around bribery-like incentives, infiltration, rival destabilization, and high-stakes finance decisions.

Mitigation: Constrain outputs to lawful negotiation, public-information analysis, risk framing, and decision support; require qualified professional review before applying guidance in regulated finance or employment contexts.

Risk: The bundled Claude settings allow broad local git commands and web access.

Mitigation: Remove or narrow the bundled .claude/settings.local.json permissions unless those actions are specifically needed in the deployment environment.
