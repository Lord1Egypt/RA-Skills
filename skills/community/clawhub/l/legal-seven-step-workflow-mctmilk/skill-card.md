## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Legal analysis may be jurisdiction-specific, outdated, or unsuitable for a particular case.

Mitigation: Confirm the applicable jurisdiction, provide current statute text, and have a qualified human reviewer check the analysis before relying on it.

Risk: AI-generated case citations or legal authorities may be inaccurate or nonexistent.

Mitigation: Verify cited cases and authorities in official or trusted legal databases before using them.
