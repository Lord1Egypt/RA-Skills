## Description:

Guides agents to derive traceable methods from five classical Chinese sources and combine them into scenario-specific strategy and decision frameworks, with checks for fit, assumptions, and limits.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zengfr](https://clawhub.ai/user/zengfr)

## Use Case:

Agents helping users with strategy, negotiation, execution, organizational, or personal-development questions can consult scenario-specific frameworks with traceable method references. The skill is not intended for locating original passages, quotations, or historical background.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global
