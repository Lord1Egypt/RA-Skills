## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Analytics and user-profiling workflows may involve demographic, behavioral, payment, or feedback data.

Mitigation: Send only approved non-sensitive fields to analytics or third-party tools, and add privacy notice, consent, retention limits, and access controls before tracking users.

Risk: Tool-integration examples for analytics and Notion could be copied into production without credential or data-sharing review.

Mitigation: Treat examples as templates, keep tokens out of source content, store credentials securely, and review each integration before deployment.

Risk: Generated plans, KPI calculations, and recommendations may be incomplete or misleading for a specific business context.

Mitigation: Review assumptions, calculations, legal/privacy implications, and stakeholder constraints before using outputs for operational decisions.
