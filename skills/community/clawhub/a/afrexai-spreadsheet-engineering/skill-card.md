## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Automation examples may send emails to unintended recipients or expose sensitive spreadsheet data.

Mitigation: Review recipient lists, data sensitivity, account permissions, and trigger settings before copying email automation into a business spreadsheet.

Risk: Archive or cleanup scripts may delete or move rows that still need to be retained.

Mitigation: Test scripts on a copy, confirm archive criteria, and keep recoverable backups or version history before running row-deletion automation.
