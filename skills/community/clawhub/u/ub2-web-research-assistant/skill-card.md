## Description:

Conducts structured web research by querying multiple sources, evaluating credibility, synthesizing findings, and producing a cited summary.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, developers, and analysts use this skill to structure web research, compare sources, synthesize findings, and produce cited research summaries.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Research prompts may include sensitive personal, credential, or confidential business details.

Mitigation: Avoid including sensitive details unless they are appropriate to use for web research.

Risk: Research conclusions may depend on source quality or incomplete citations.

Mitigation: Review cited sources before relying on conclusions.
