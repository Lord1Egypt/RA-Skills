## Description:

Generate daily or on-demand medical research briefs for any medical specialty with concise summaries, clinical relevance badges, images when available, and direct links to source articles.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[johnyquest7](https://clawhub.ai/user/johnyquest7)

### License/Terms of Use:


## Use Case:

Clinicians and healthcare-focused users use this skill to request daily, weekly, or on-demand specialty briefs that summarize recent medical research and journal updates from top sources.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Medical research briefs may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect if treated as clinical advice.

Mitigation: Treat outputs as research summaries and verify important clinical claims against the original cited sources.
