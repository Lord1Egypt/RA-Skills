## Description:

Creates full digital personas for deceased figures by distilling personality, appearance, movement, expression, voice, language style, and emotional response patterns into an interactive SOUL.md persona file.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[ygq19901001](https://clawhub.ai/user/ygq19901001)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users and agent builders use this skill to create clearly synthetic deceased-person persona templates from public material or authorized personal source material. The output helps structure a persona's traits, boundaries, interaction rules, and uncertainty labels for use in compatible agent runtimes.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill can help create realistic simulations of deceased people, which may be misused to deceive relatives or the public.

Mitigation: Keep generated personas and outputs clearly labeled as synthetic, and do not present inferred responses as actual statements by the deceased person.

Risk: Persona creation may involve private, sensitive, or unauthorized personal material.

Mitigation: Use only material you have the right to provide, and avoid including private or sensitive information without appropriate authorization.

Risk: Generated personas may blur sourced facts and pattern-based inferences.

Mitigation: Require the SOUL.md file to distinguish verifiable evidence from inferred behavior, uncertainty, and simulation boundaries.
