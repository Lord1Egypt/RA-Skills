## Description:

A comprehensive AI agent skill for managing payroll accurately and on time. Helps small business owners run payroll without a dedicated HR function, explains payroll taxes and compliance requirements, handles contractor vs employee classification, prepares for audits, and ensures every person who works for you gets paid correctly every time.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Small business owners, first-time employers, and operators without dedicated HR use this skill to plan payroll setup, classify workers, calculate payroll obligations, manage filing calendars, and prepare audit-ready payroll records.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Payroll conversations may involve sensitive employee, compensation, tax, and business details.

Mitigation: Limit shared details to what is necessary, avoid unnecessary personal data, and follow the organization's data handling policy for payroll records.

Risk: Payroll tax, worker classification, final pay, and filing obligations vary by jurisdiction and can change over time.

Mitigation: Verify jurisdiction-specific obligations against official sources or a qualified payroll, tax, or legal professional before acting.

Risk: Payroll calculation or deadline mistakes can create penalties, interest, or employee payment issues.

Mitigation: Review calculations, deposit schedules, and filings before submission, especially for overtime, withholding, contractor classification, and late-deposit scenarios.
