## Description:

Audits and fixes accessibility in Android, iOS and Flutter apps against WCAG 2.2 level AA and EN 301 549, and checks whether the European Accessibility Act applies. Covers Compose semantics, TalkBack, SwiftUI modifiers, VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Flutter Semantics, touch targets, contrast, font scaling and automated checks. Engineering guidance, not legal advice. Use for accessibility reviews, EAA readiness, screen reader bugs or a11y tests.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Developers and mobile app teams use this skill to review Android, iOS and Flutter apps for accessibility issues, assess EAA scope, and produce evidence-based findings with platform-specific fixes and test guidance.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Accessibility or EAA guidance may be incomplete or may not fit a particular app and jurisdiction.

Mitigation: Review the relevant platform and standards references, and have the compliance or legal owner confirm EAA scope and any exemptions.
