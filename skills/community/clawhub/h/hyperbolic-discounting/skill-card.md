## Description: <br>
Helps agents identify present-bias preference reversals and design commitment devices for saving, exercise, quitting, subscription lock-in, procrastination, and long-horizon planning. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, and agent builders use this skill to recognize repeated preference reversals and convert vague willpower plans into concrete commitment-device designs. It is especially suited for goals involving saving, health behavior, procrastination, subscriptions, and recurring deferral of long-horizon work. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can over-apply present-bias framing to cases where changed plans reflect new information or real uncertainty. <br>
Mitigation: Use the documented fit checks before applying the process, and do not treat rational updates or bird-in-hand uncertainty as hyperbolic discounting. <br>
Risk: Commitment devices can be too costly, too easy to bypass, or disproportionate to the goal. <br>
Mitigation: Select devices that bind at the moment of temptation, are hard to circumvent, remain acceptable to the present self, and are re-checked at 30, 60, or 90 days. <br>
