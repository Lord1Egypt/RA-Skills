## Description:

Turn habit building into a quest log with daily mission tiers, streak feedback, fallback actions, and recovery guidance after missed days.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[harrylabsj](https://clawhub.ai/user/harrylabsj)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users use this skill to turn one to five habit goals into a daily quest log with concrete actions, fallback quests, streak feedback, and recovery steps after missed days.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may expect reminders, storage, synchronization, or hard accountability from a habit planning skill.

Mitigation: State that the skill is text-only guidance and does not store habits, send reminders, sync data, or enforce accountability.

Risk: Habit plans can include sensitive personal routines or health-related details.

Mitigation: Ask only for the minimum habit context needed and avoid entering sensitive personal details unless the user is comfortable sharing them in the chat session.
