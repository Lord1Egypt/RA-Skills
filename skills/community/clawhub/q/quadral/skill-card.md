## Description:

## Use Case:

External users and agents use this skill to fetch Quadral word puzzles, submit guesses, interpret scoring feedback, and compare results on the shared leaderboard.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill encourages posting solved results to public communities, which could disclose puzzle details or user-approved content without explicit consent.

Mitigation: Require explicit user approval before any public post, including review of the destination, puzzle title, submitted word, quality score, and final message content.
