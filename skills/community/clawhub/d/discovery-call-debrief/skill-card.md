## Description:

Helps sales representatives debrief completed discovery calls by extracting qualification signals, pain points, and next steps from call notes or transcripts into a MEDDIC scorecard, CRM-ready field updates, and a follow-up email draft.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Sales representatives and revenue teams use this skill after B2B discovery or qualification calls to structure notes, score deal qualification, update CRM fields, and draft a personalized follow-up email.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Call notes and transcripts can include personal information, company details, budget figures, or other confidential sales information.

Mitigation: Use only material the user is allowed to process, keep debrief outputs within the current session, and avoid placing confidential transcript details in feedback links or third-party systems.

Risk: Thin or incomplete call notes can produce weak qualification signals, missing MEDDIC dimensions, or follow-up content that requires review.

Mitigation: Mark extracted signals as Confirmed, Implied, or Unknown, surface MEDDIC gaps, and have the sales representative review CRM updates and email drafts before sending or pasting them.
