## Description:

Maoyan provides movie ticketing decision support and box-office data literacy guidance for Maoyan and Maoyan Pro workflows.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Consumers, film-market analysts, and entertainment writers use this skill to choose showtimes and theater formats, interpret Maoyan Pro box-office metrics, and cite movie-market data with clear source and timestamp practices.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Ticket prices, refund eligibility, showtimes, discounts, and box-office figures can change after the skill's static guidance was published.

Mitigation: Confirm current details directly in Maoyan or Maoyan Pro before purchasing tickets, requesting refunds, or publishing market analysis.

Risk: Box-office analysis may be misleading if Maoyan Pro figures are quoted without source, metric definition, or timestamp.

Mitigation: Cite Maoyan Pro explicitly, include the relevant timestamp, and distinguish metrics such as screening share, box-office share, attendance rate, and forecast gross.
